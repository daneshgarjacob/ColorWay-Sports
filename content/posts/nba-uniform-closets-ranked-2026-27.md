The Sixers kicked off Media Day by posting their "threads for the season," all six of them, and it settled a question for us: which NBA team has the best uniform closet for 2026-27? We ranked all 30, judging the whole set together, the core jerseys, the alternates, and whether it all hangs together as one identity.

One pattern jumped out. **About a third of the league now carries three or more black or mostly black jerseys**, between black Statements, black City Editions and the new black Specter set. Black sells, but a closet where three of five jerseys look alike is a closet that wastes its colors, and it cost several big names here.

Many City Editions this year are returning designs that leaked in August and have not been shown by the teams yet. We mark those as leaked or reported.

## 1. Philadelphia 76ers: A

**The closet:** Six jerseys: blue PHILA Icon, white PHILA Association, red Phila script Statement, the cream Phila script City Edition, the new black Specter with spray-painted stars, and the black Iverson-era Hardwood Classic. Patch: Bloom Energy (new).

The deepest closet in the league, and every piece is either classic Sixers or a real idea. The Iverson black is the best throwback anyone is wearing this year. The only knock is two black alternates that overlap.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which NBA team has the best uniforms for 2026-27?**

The Philadelphia 76ers, in our ranking. They have six jerseys, including the black Iverson-era Hardwood Classic, the new Specter and the Phila script pair. The Minnesota Timberwolves, with their full rebrand, are second.

**Which NBA team has the worst uniforms for 2026-27?**

The Sacramento Kings finished 30th. Three of their five jerseys are black, which leaves their purple on only one jersey.

**Which NBA teams have new uniforms for 2026-27?**

The Minnesota Timberwolves (full rebrand) and Houston Rockets (all-new core set). Eight teams add the new Specter uniform: the 76ers, Celtics, Lakers, Knicks, Hawks, Warriors, Kings and Pistons. The Nets add a Classic Edition throwback.

**How many jerseys does each NBA team have?**

Most have four: Association, Icon, Statement and City Edition. Teams with a Classic or Specter uniform have five, and the 76ers have six.

The Sixers win on depth and on the Iverson black, the Wolves win on design, and the Pacers and Hornets prove that using your actual colors still beats another black alternate. At the bottom, the Kings, Mavericks and Cavaliers all have strong colors they barely use.
