**We expect the Steelers in their gold 1933 throwback on Black Friday, and the Broncos are in Summit White.** Pittsburgh hosts Denver at Acrisure Stadium on **Friday, November 27, at 3:00 p.m. Eastern on Prime Video**, the last game of the five-game Thanksgiving week and the first Black Friday game in Steelers history. Team president Art Rooney II has said the 1933 throwback and its **gold helmet** return in 2026, but Pittsburgh has not attached it to a date. Our [Steelers 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/steelers-uniform-schedule-2026) puts it here. Denver's side is not a projection: the Broncos published their jersey for all 17 games, and Week 12 at Pittsburgh is **Summit White**.

## Quick Answers

- **What are the Steelers wearing on Black Friday?** We expect the 1933 throwback: gold helmet with a black stripe, gold jersey with black stripes, beige pants. **Expected.** If the throwback goes to another game, the standard black home jersey takes this one.
- **What are the Broncos wearing?** Summit White. **Scheduled**, from Denver's published 2026 uniform schedule.
- **When is it?** Friday, November 27, 3:00 p.m. ET on Prime Video, at Acrisure Stadium in Pittsburgh.
- **Is this the Steelers' first Black Friday game?** Yes.
- **Our grade:** A- for the matchup if the throwback shows up, B if it does not.

## What The Steelers Are Wearing (Expected)

The 1933 throwback copies what the franchise wore in its first season, when it was still called the Pittsburgh Pirates. Pittsburgh revealed it in July 2025 and first wore it on October 26, 2025 against Green Bay. From the team's own description:

- **The helmet.** Gold, with a black center stripe and a gray facemask. Pittsburgh has worn the same black helmet with a logo on one side since 1962, so a gold shell is the only thing that truly changes how this team looks.
- **The jersey.** Gold with black stripes, large white block numbers on the front and black numbers on the back, and the City of Pittsburgh crest on the front left shoulder.
- **The pants.** Beige, a nod to the khaki pants of 1933.

Why we have it on Black Friday: the Steelers' 2026 themes announcement named no jerseys at all, so this is our call, not theirs. It is a standalone national window on Prime Video, the first Black Friday game in franchise history, and the kind of slot a team dresses up for. Pittsburgh's other alternate, the all-black Color Rush, fits the Sunday night home game against Houston nine days later. We will change this label the day the team posts the uniform.

## What The Broncos Are Wearing (Scheduled)

Summit White is the road half of the Mile High Collection Denver introduced in 2024: **navy numbers outlined in orange**, a **BRONCOS wordmark across the chest**, an **orange collar**, and **orange sleeves cut with a navy mountain-peak graphic**. It is the Broncos' most-used jersey this year, nine games. Denver has paired it with the navy helmet and navy pants on its recent road trips, at San Francisco and Arizona, and opened the season at Kansas City in all white under a white helmet. The helmet and pants for Pittsburgh come with the team's game-week post. Every Denver date is in our [Broncos 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/broncos-uniform-schedule-2026).

## Our Grade

**A- if Pittsburgh wears the throwback.** Gold and beige against white, navy and orange is the brightest game of the holiday week and the most unusual: nobody in the NFL looks like the 1933 Steelers, and the gold helmet in the afternoon light will be the thing people remember from Black Friday. Summit White is a fine road jersey, a little busy at the collar, and it is the right neutral backdrop here.

**B if it is the black home set.** Black and gold against white and orange is a good game. It is also a regular one, on the one Friday a year when a team has a national audience to itself. If the Steelers save the throwback for a lesser date, it is a missed opportunity.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the Steelers wearing on Black Friday 2026?**

We expect the gold 1933 throwback with the gold helmet and beige pants. The Steelers have confirmed it returns in 2026 but have not named the game. If it is not this one, Pittsburgh will be in its black home jersey.

**Is there an NFL game on Black Friday 2026?**

Yes. The Denver Broncos play at the Pittsburgh Steelers on Friday, November 27 at 3:00 p.m. Eastern.

**What channel is the Black Friday NFL game on?**

Prime Video.

**What are the Broncos wearing on Black Friday?**

Summit White, the white road jersey from the Mile High Collection. Denver published it for this game on its 2026 schedule.

**What is the Steelers 1933 throwback?**

A replica of the uniform from the franchise's first season, when the team was the Pittsburgh Pirates: gold helmet with a black stripe, gold jersey with black stripes, City of Pittsburgh crest, and beige pants. It debuted on October 26, 2025 against Green Bay.

**Why is the Steelers' throwback helmet gold?**

It copies the 1933 team's look. The NFL's alternate-helmet rule lets teams wear a second shell a few times a season.

**Have the Steelers ever played on Black Friday?**

No. This is the first Black Friday game in franchise history.

**What other alternates do the Steelers wear in 2026?**

The all-black Color Rush, which we expect for the Sunday night home game against Houston on December 6.

**Will the Broncos wear orange in Pittsburgh?**

No. Denver's published schedule has Summit White for this game. Sunset Orange is on four other dates.

**What time is Broncos vs. Steelers?**

3:00 p.m. Eastern, noon Pacific, Friday, November 27.
