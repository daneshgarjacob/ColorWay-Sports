**The best new jersey you can buy this Black Friday is the Utah Mammoth Winter Classic jersey, an A+. The best new NFL jersey is the Chicago Bears Rivalries set, an A-. The four new jerseys to skip are the Packers, Vikings and Colts Rivalries sets and the Golden Knights Hometown Remix.**

Black Friday is Friday, Nov. 27, and Cyber Monday is Nov. 30. Every year the deals pages tell you what is cheapest. This page tells you what is worth wearing. We graded every jersey that is **new in 2026**: the NFL Rivalries class, the NHL Hometown Remix sweaters, the Winter Classic, the Heat's Vice City uniform, the NBA Specter Edition and the 2026 MLB City Connects. Each one gets a letter grade and the reason, in plain words, and a link to our full story on it.

We do not list prices or discounts here. They change by the hour during Black Friday week, and we would rather you check them on the day. If you want a classic jersey instead of a new one, our [best jerseys to give as gifts in 2026](/stories/best-jerseys-to-give-as-gifts-2026) has the timeless picks, league by league.

## Quick Answers

- **Best new jersey of 2026:** Utah Mammoth Winter Classic, **A+**. Mountain Blue with brown and beige trim.
- **Best new NFL jersey:** Chicago Bears Rivalries, **A-**. Navy with burnt orange numbers.
- **Best new NHL Hometown Remix:** Pittsburgh Penguins, a pop-art penguin on deep blue. No. 1 of 32 in [our ranking](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-jerseys-ranked).
- **Best new NBA jersey:** Miami Heat Vice City, **A-**. Black with neon pink trim. The fan version has not had an online release.
- **Best MLB jersey to buy in the offseason:** Atlanta Braves City Connect, **A+**. Powder blue with an Atlanta script.
- **Worth waiting for:** the Pistons Specter Edition, **A**, which debuts Nov. 29, and the NBA City Editions, which do not hit the floor until **Jan. 14, 2027**.
- **Skip:** the Packers, Vikings and Colts Rivalries sets, all graded **F**, and the Golden Knights Hometown Remix, last of 32.

## The 4 New Jerseys To Skip

Four new jerseys of 2026 failed. Each one took a strong team identity and covered it up.

**Green Bay Packers Rivalries: F.** The stock-certificate idea is the smartest concept in either Rivalries class, and it disappears from your seat. What is left is a green jersey slightly darker than the one Green Bay already wears, and the set benches the gold G helmet for a cream shell. [Every Packers jersey, ranked](/stories/packers-jerseys-2026-ranked).

**Minnesota Vikings Rivalries: F.** A duller purple that drifts toward plum, plus Nordic knots, rune numbers and a new wordmark all at once. The rune numbers are good. Everything around them is noise. [Every Vikings jersey, ranked](/stories/vikings-jerseys-2026-ranked).

**Indianapolis Colts Anvil Strike Rivalries: F.** Anthracite gray is a color Indianapolis has never worn, and the first horseshoe ever on a Colts jersey arrives as a silver mark that barely shows on a gray shirt. The royal blue home is the Colts jersey to buy. [Every Colts jersey, ranked](/stories/colts-jerseys-2026-ranked).

**Vegas Golden Knights Hometown Remix: F.** A black casino sweater with a jade felt-table yoke, card suits, roulette sleeve bands and playing-card numbers, all at once. It is last of 32 in [our Remix ranking](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-jerseys-ranked). [Why it is the ugliest in the NHL](/stories/golden-knights-hometown-remix-jersey-worst).
