The city of Pittsburgh is going full black and gold for the 2026 NFL Draft. Crews are painting streets, putting up banners, and cleaning up downtown all week ahead of Thursday night's opening round at Acrisure Stadium and Point State Park. This is the first time Pittsburgh has hosted the NFL Draft, and the city is treating the event like a home-game weekend for the entire downtown.

The visual treatment matters more than it might seem. NFL Draft host cities have turned the event into a traveling fan festival over the last decade, and the city that leans hardest into the look is the one that wins the week. Detroit in 2024 painted Woodward Avenue, Green Bay in 2025 lit up Lambeau Field and the surrounding Titletown District, and now Pittsburgh is stepping up with a downtown Steelers country takeover.

The Steelers have been pushing the Draft treatment across their own channels. Downtown banners, street paint, directional signage, and cleanup crews are rolling through the city this week. The North Shore, where the main Draft Theater will sit in the lots around Acrisure Stadium, is getting the deepest visual treatment. Point State Park, which will host Steelers Country as the fan festival footprint, is being set up with activations for draft weekend.

This is exactly what you want a host city to do. Pittsburgh has one of the most distinctive visual identities in American sports. Black and gold is a genuinely unique color combination in the NFL, and the city's bridges, skyline, and industrial architecture photograph beautifully on broadcast. Leaning into the palette across downtown gives ESPN, NFL Network, and every visiting fan a consistent visual experience from the hotel to the Main Stage.

## When Is the 2026 NFL Draft

The 2026 NFL Draft runs Thursday, April 23 through Saturday, April 25, 2026 in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Round 1 kicks off Thursday at 8:00 PM Eastern. Rounds 2 and 3 run Friday starting at 7:00 PM Eastern. Rounds 4 through 7 wrap the event Saturday starting at noon Eastern. The Draft Theater and Main Stage are on the North Shore in the Acrisure Stadium parking lot footprint, with Steelers Country at Point State Park.

## Why Pittsburgh Hosting the NFL Draft Is a Big Deal

Pittsburgh has been a Steelers town since the franchise was founded in 1933. Six Super Bowl trophies, a long list of legendary uniforms, and one of the most recognizable color schemes in all of professional sports. But the city has never hosted the NFL Draft before. The Draft rotation has historically gone to cities like Nashville, Cleveland, Las Vegas, Kansas City, Detroit, and Green Bay. Pittsburgh joining that list in 2026 is the league acknowledging one of its foundational franchises and one of its most visually distinctive markets.

Our take: we are absolutely here for it. Pittsburgh going all in on the visual treatment is exactly the energy the Draft needs. Host cities that treat the event as a backdrop get forgotten. Host cities that turn the Draft into a three-day advertisement for their own identity create memories. The painted streets and downtown banners this week tell us Pittsburgh is going with option B. This is going to look incredible on broadcast.

## Host City Momentum Is Building

Each recent NFL Draft host has raised the bar on the visual treatment. Detroit 2024 set a benchmark with Woodward Avenue painted navy and the Campus Martius Park fan festival. Green Bay 2025 took it outdoors with the Lambeau Field backdrop and the Titletown District activations. Pittsburgh in 2026 has a chance to top both with the downtown setting, the confluence of the three rivers, and a fan base that will show up in black and gold whether the team is participating or not.

The 2027 NFL Draft is headed to Washington D.C., according to the NFL's ongoing rotation schedule. Each host city now faces the same question: how hard do you lean into the visual branding of your own market. Pittsburgh's answer this week is setting a high floor.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the 2026 NFL Draft in Pittsburgh

**When is the 2026 NFL Draft?**

The 2026 NFL Draft takes place Thursday, April 23 through Saturday, April 25, 2026. Round 1 begins Thursday at 8:00 PM Eastern. Rounds 2 and 3 run Friday starting at 7:00 PM Eastern. Rounds 4 through 7 close the Draft on Saturday starting at noon Eastern.

**Where is the 2026 NFL Draft being held?**

The 2026 NFL Draft is being held in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. The Draft Theater and Main Stage are set up on the North Shore in the lots surrounding Acrisure Stadium, home of the Pittsburgh Steelers. Steelers Country, the NFL's fan festival footprint, is located at Point State Park at the confluence of the Allegheny, Monongahela, and Ohio Rivers.

**Has Pittsburgh ever hosted the NFL Draft before?**

No. The 2026 NFL Draft is the first time the city of Pittsburgh has hosted the NFL Draft. The Steelers have played in Pittsburgh since 1933, but the Draft rotation had not previously included the city among its host markets.

**Is Pittsburgh painting the city for the NFL Draft?**

Yes. City crews and Steelers staff are painting streets black and gold, installing downtown banners, putting up directional signage, and running general cleanup across downtown Pittsburgh, the North Shore, and Point State Park ahead of Thursday night's opening round. Photos and videos of the preparation have been circulating across NFL social media this week.

**What color are Pittsburgh Steelers and the NFL Draft decorations?**

The Pittsburgh Steelers' team colors are black and gold, and the 2026 NFL Draft is leaning into that palette for the city's Draft week decorations. Street paint, banners, signage, and fan activation spaces are using the black and gold color scheme across downtown Pittsburgh and the North Shore.

**Is the 2026 NFL Draft free to attend?**

Yes. NFL Draft events held in host cities have historically been free to attend for fans. Specific crowd management, seating, and entry details for the 2026 NFL Draft are managed by the NFL and the city of Pittsburgh's Draft operations team.

**Where will the 2027 NFL Draft be held?**

The 2027 NFL Draft is scheduled to be held in Washington D.C. according to the NFL's announced host city rotation.
