**The best record in any jersey in sports right now belongs to the Tampa Bay Lightning: 9-0-0 in their blue home sweater. The worst is the Chicago Blackhawks' white road sweater: 0-8-1.**

*The Uniform Standings is a weekly ColorWay Sports report. Every Monday, we rank every NFL, NBA and NHL jersey by its record this season, from our own game-by-game log. This first edition counts NBA and NHL games through Wednesday, Nov. 18, and NFL games through Week 10.*

We write down what every team wears in every game, then match it to the final score. That gives us 97 NFL jerseys, 92 NBA jerseys and 114 NHL sweaters with a record. Find your team in the full tables at the bottom.

## Quick Answers

- **Hottest NFL jersey:** the Chiefs' red jersey and the 49ers' red jersey, both **5-0**.
- **Coldest NFL jersey:** the Dolphins' white jersey, **0-8**. The Cowboys are **0-6** in their white jersey.
- **Hottest NBA jersey:** the Timberwolves' blue Icon jersey, **8-0**.
- **Coldest NBA jersey:** the Kings' black Icon jersey, **0-5**.
- **Hottest NHL jersey:** the Lightning's blue home sweater, **9-0-0**.
- **Coldest NHL jersey:** the Canucks' blue home sweater, **0-5-0**, and the Blackhawks' white road sweater, **0-8-1**.
- **Biggest league-wide split:** NFL teams are **3-20-1** in white at home and **21-6-1** in a color on the road.
- **How we count:** a jersey needs **3 games** in the NFL and **5 games** in the NBA and NHL to make the hot and cold lists.

## How We Count

Every number here comes from ColorWay Sports' own uniform log: the [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026), the [NBA uniform tracker](/stories/nba-uniform-tracker-2026-27) and the [NHL uniform tracker](/stories/nhl-uniform-tracker-2026-27). We log the jersey each team actually wore, from game photos and the teams' own posts, then pair it with the final score. Every team's full record in every uniform, game by game, is on our [uniform records page](/uniform-records).

This report ranks **jerseys**, not full uniforms. In the NFL, a team's red jersey counts as one jersey whether it is worn with red pants or white pants. (Our [uniform records page](/uniform-records) also splits NFL records by helmet and pants.)

The minimums are set by how often teams play. NFL teams play once a week, so a jersey needs **3 games**. NBA and NHL teams play three or four times a week, so a jersey needs **5 games**. NHL records read wins, regulation losses and overtime or shootout losses (W-L-OTL). The NHL percentage is points percentage, where an overtime loss counts as half. In the NFL, a tie counts as half a win.

A jersey does not win games. Players do. Good teams win in whatever they wear, and some jerseys are worn only for certain games. These records show what happened in each jersey this season, not why.

## Method Note

- **What we log:** the jersey each team wore in every regular-season game, from game photos and the teams' own posts, matched to final scores from ESPN. NFL: 300 team-games (every game through Week 10). NBA: 444 team-games. NHL: 692 team-games. No game in the date range is missing from the log.
- **Date range:** NFL Week 1 through Week 10 (Sept. 9 to Nov. 16). NBA and NHL from opening night through Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026.
- **One jersey, one row:** in the NFL we combine every pants and helmet pairing for the same jersey. For the white-versus-color split, the Jaguars' off-white Bold City Alabaster jersey counts as a color jersey, as in our white-at-home story.
- **What it does not prove:** a jersey does not cause a win or a loss. Teams choose jerseys for reasons (home or road, a promotion, a rivalry game), and small samples swing fast. One game can move a 5-game jersey by 200 points.
- **Updated every Monday:** next Monday's edition adds every game played through Sunday, Nov. 22.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best record in any jersey this season?**

The Tampa Bay Lightning's blue home sweater, 9-0-0 through Nov. 18. In the NBA it is the Timberwolves' blue Icon jersey at 8-0. In the NFL, the Chiefs' red jersey and the 49ers' red jersey are both 5-0.

**What is the worst record in any jersey this season?**

Among jerseys with 5 or more games, the Canucks' blue home sweater (0-5-0) and the Kings' black Icon jersey in the NBA (0-5) have not won. The Blackhawks' white road sweater is 0-8-1. In the NFL, the Dolphins' white jersey is 0-8.

**What is the record of NFL teams wearing white at home in 2026?**

3-20-1 through Week 10. NFL teams wearing a color jersey on the road are 21-6-1.

**Do NBA teams win less in white jerseys?**

So far, yes. NBA teams are 80-100 in their Association jerseys (white for every team but the Bucks, whose is cream) and 82-64 in Icon jerseys through Nov. 18. Teams in white are 34-41 at home and 46-59 on the road.

**How many games does a jersey need to make the hot and cold lists?**

Three games in the NFL. Five games in the NBA and NHL, where teams play several times a week.

**Does an NHL overtime loss count as a loss?**

We list it separately. NHL records read wins, regulation losses and overtime or shootout losses. The NHL percentage is points percentage, where an overtime loss counts as half.

**In the NFL, does a different pants color count as a different jersey?**

Not in this report. Here, a team's red jersey is one row no matter which pants it was worn with. Our [uniform records page](/uniform-records) splits every combination.

**Does a jersey cause a team to win?**

No. Players win games. Good teams win in whatever they wear, and some jerseys are only worn for certain games. These standings show what happened in each jersey, not why.

**Where does this data come from?**

ColorWay Sports' own game-by-game uniform log, matched to final scores. The full log is in our [NFL](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026), [NBA](/stories/nba-uniform-tracker-2026-27) and [NHL](/stories/nhl-uniform-tracker-2026-27) uniform trackers.

**How often are the Uniform Standings updated?**

Every Monday. Each team's [uniform records page](/uniform-records) updates as we log games.
