The Cardinals have four jerseys on the field in 2026, and the team assigned every game before the season started: cardinal red seven times, white seven times, black twice, and the desert Rivalries jersey once. It is a real rotation, with red on the road twice and white at home once. The jerseys themselves are the problem. We put Arizona thirtieth in our [best NFL uniforms of 2026](/stories/best-nfl-uniforms-2026-ranked) at a C+, and nothing in this closet grades higher than that.

Here are all four Arizona Cardinals jerseys for 2026, ranked worst to first and graded, with the helmet and pants each one wears. For the week-by-week jersey schedule 2026, the [Cardinals 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/arizona-cardinals-uniform-schedule-2026) has every game.

The black alternate is worn with the black helmet, and it has two home dates in 2026: the Detroit Lions in Week 5 and the Washington Commanders in Week 12. We expected it to sit out the season. Washington has already published white for its trip and Detroit is projected in white, which is the best case for this jersey, because black against white is the one matchup where it does not blend into the other team.

The trouble is that it does not look like Arizona. Black is not a Cardinals color in any way that matters, and the jersey takes a franchise with one of the best reds in the league and puts that red on the trim. From the upper deck it is a black uniform with a small bird on the helmet, which could belong to several teams.

It grades a C-, the lowest mark in the closet. Two dates is one more than we would give it.

The white jersey is worn under the white helmet, and the team's schedule graphic pairs it with white pants. It has seven dates: six road games, at the Chargers, 49ers, Giants, Rams, Seahawks and Chiefs, plus one at home against the New York Jets in Week 15, which forces New York into green. It opened the season in the all-white version at SoFi Stadium, and Arizona beat the Chargers 26-14.

All white is clean, and it is also the least distinctive thing the Cardinals can wear. With a white helmet, white jersey and white pants, the cardinal red is left to the numbers and the logo, and the odd silver trim on the road set is a detail we would remove first. The Week 1 matchup with the Chargers' powder blue graded a C+ on our [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026), and the Cardinals' all-white side is what held that grade down.

It grades a C. It is tidy, and red pants would help, but right now it reads as unfinished.

Cardinal red is the home jersey, worn under the white helmet, and it has seven dates. Five are at home: the Denver Broncos in Week 7, the Los Angeles Rams in Week 10, the Philadelphia Eagles in Week 13, the Las Vegas Raiders in Week 17 and the San Francisco 49ers in Week 18. Two are on the road: at Dallas on Sunday, November 1, and at New Orleans in Week 16, where both hosts wear white and Arizona chose color.

The red itself is excellent, a saturated shade nobody else in the league quite matches, and the 2023 redesign cleaned up the numbers and removed the old shoulder piping. What it did not fix is the chest. The oversized ARIZONA wordmark takes up the space where a strong jersey would let the color breathe, and it is the first detail we would change. Red on the road in Dallas, against the Cowboys' white, is the best picture this jersey will make all year.

It grades a C+. Great color, weak layout, and it ties the Rivalries jersey on grade. The Rivalries jersey ranks ahead because it has an idea this one does not.

The Rivalries jersey is Arizona's entry in the NFL x Nike Rivalries program from the 2025 NFC West class: a sand-cream jersey with a dust-speckle texture and cardinal red numbers and ARIZONA wordmark, worn with red pants and a cream helmet with a red facemask. It has one date in 2026: at home against the Seattle Seahawks on Sunday, September 20, the team's Hispanic Heritage game.

This is the only jersey in the closet that looks like it comes from a specific place. Cream over red is built on the desert rather than on the usual blackout playbook, and the red pants give the set a strong base where an all-cream version would have gone flat. The speckle is where it slips. It is meant to read as desert dust, and from a distance it reads as a dirty jersey.

It grades a C+, the same mark it holds in our [Rivalries uniforms ranking](/stories/nfl-rivalries-uniforms-2026-ranked), where it places seventh of sixteen. Remove the speckle and it is the best uniform Arizona owns by a clear margin, and it still ranks first here.

## The Bottom Line on the Cardinals Jersey Rankings

The Cardinals jerseys rank by how much each one says Arizona. The Rivalries jersey does it best and grades a C+. Cardinal red has the best color and ties it at C+, white is a C that hides the red, and black is a C- that could belong to anyone.

The rotation is smart. The closet is not. Arizona has a great red and a real desert idea, and until a primary uniform puts them together, the ceiling stays low.

Four: cardinal red, white, the black alternate, and the desert-themed Rivalries jersey. The team announced the jersey for all 17 games on September 1.

We rank the Rivalries jersey first and grade it a C+. Its cream and red design is the only thing in the closet that looks like it comes from Arizona, though cardinal red ties it on grade.
