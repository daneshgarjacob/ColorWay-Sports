The Cleveland Browns are pulling the brown pants back out for the road. On Sunday, October 25, at the Tennessee Titans (1:00 p.m. ET, CBS), Cleveland wears the **orange helmet, white jersey and brown pants**, a look the team announced on X on Thursday, October 22, with a three-word caption: "new road combo."

It is new for 2026, but it is not new to the franchise. According to the Gridiron Uniform Database's game-by-game logs, the last regular-season game in which the Browns wore white jerseys with brown pants was **January 7, 2024**, a 31-14 loss at Cincinnati in Week 18 of the 2023 season. That makes Sunday the first time in 40 regular-season games, spanning all of 2024, all of 2025 and the first six weeks of 2026.

## What the Browns Are Wearing Sunday

The team's graphic shows every piece: the orange helmet with its center stripe, the white jersey with brown numbers and the brown and orange sleeve stripes, brown pants, white socks with brown and orange stripes, and orange cleats. No alternate helmet, no throwback patch. It is the standard white road jersey with a different pair of pants, which is exactly why it reads so well.

In 2026 so far, Cleveland has worn white jerseys with white pants on the road (Week 1 at Jacksonville, Week 5 at the Jets) and the brown jersey with white pants for the trip to Tampa Bay. At home the Browns have worn the brown jersey with orange pants against Carolina and against Baltimore on Crucial Catch day, plus the all-brown Alpha Dawg set against Pittsburgh. Our full grid is on the [Browns 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/browns-uniform-schedule-2026).

## When the Browns Last Wore White Over Brown

The Gridiron Uniform Database logs the Browns in white jerseys and brown pants twice in 2023, both on the road:

- **October 29, 2023, at Seattle.** A 24-20 loss on the day the Seahawks debuted their 1990s throwbacks. Browns Wire previewed the combo the day before: white jerseys "paired with their brown pants."
- **January 7, 2024, at Cincinnati.** The 2023 regular-season finale, a 31-14 loss.

Sports Illustrated's Browns site reached the same conclusion in July 2026, writing that the white-over-brown look "hasn't been used since 2023." Since then, the database shows every Browns road game in white with either white or orange pants.

## A Modern Look, Not a Classic One

It is easy to assume white over brown is a classic Cleveland look, but the record says otherwise. The Gridiron Uniform Database's Browns history notes the team's white pants went unaltered from 1946 until 1975, and the Browns' own uniform timeline on clevelandbrowns.com dates the first orange pants to that 1975 season. White pants returned in 1984 and carried the team through the 1990s and the 1999 return.

Solid brown pants did not appear until **August 18, 2008**, in a preseason game at the Meadowlands against the Giants on ESPN, per the Gridiron Uniform Database. The Browns' timeline says the brown pants then became "a fixture with the white jerseys" in 2009, when Cleveland wore them in every white-jersey game. In 2010 the team went back to white pants with the white jersey.

Brown pants came back in 2013, both with the white jersey on the road and for the first all-brown look in team history, a Thursday night game against Buffalo. From there the Gridiron Uniform Database shows the combination as an occasional road option rather than a default:

- **2013:** at Green Bay, Cincinnati, New England and the Jets
- **2014:** five road games, including the opener at Pittsburgh
- **2015 to 2019:** the Nike redesign's brown pants (the 2015 set launched with brown, orange and white pants) worn sparingly with the white jersey
- **2020:** at Pittsburgh, Tennessee and the Giants
- **2021:** at Minnesota and the Chargers
- **2022:** preseason only
- **2023:** at Seattle and Cincinnati

So the honest version of "they haven't worn it in years" is close: just under two calendar years and two full seasons.

## Our Take

We like it a lot. The white jersey with white pants is clean but flat on TV, and the orange pants version leans loud. Brown pants give the road uniform the same three-color balance the orange helmet gives the whole franchise: brown, orange and white, each in its own place. Against Tennessee's Titans blue jerseys, it should look sharp. If Cleveland keeps calling it a new road combo, we would happily see it again at Pittsburgh in Week 8.

## The Bottom Line

The Browns wear the orange helmet, white jersey and brown pants at Tennessee on Sunday, October 25. Per the Gridiron Uniform Database, it is the first time Cleveland has worn white over brown in a regular-season game since January 7, 2024. The look dates to 2008, not the classic era, and it has been an occasional road option ever since. Every Week 7 combination is in our [NFL Week 7 uniform guide](/stories/nfl-week-7-uniforms-2026).
