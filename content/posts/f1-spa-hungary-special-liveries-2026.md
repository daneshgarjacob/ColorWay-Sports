## 1. Esteban Ocon's Spider-Man Helmet · Haas · Spa and Hungary

Esteban Ocon is running a Spider-Man inspired helmet for both Spa and Hungary, and it is a genuinely great one. The design drops a black web pattern across a deep red shell, with a bold black spider crawling right over the crown, straight out of the classic red-and-black Spidey suit. The gold mirror visor finishes it off and gives the whole thing a premium, comic-book-cover shine. What really makes it work is the restraint: the Haas mark on top, the Toyota Gazoo Racing and Singha sponsor logos, and the Haas Automation band all sit cleanly inside the web instead of fighting it, so the helmet reads as Spider-Man first and a sponsor billboard second. That balance is the hardest part of a themed helmet, and Ocon's designer nailed it.

Themed helmets can tip into gimmick territory fast, but this one lands on the right side of the line. The red base ties it to the Haas colors, the web pattern is instantly readable at any angle, and running it across two race weekends instead of a single one-off gives it real presence heading into the break. It is easily one of our favorite helmet designs of the 2026 season so far.

**Grade: A-** — a clean, confident Spider-Man execution that stays on theme without burying the sponsors. The red-and-black web and gold visor are a fantastic combination, and it just misses an A because the concept, as fun as it is, is a familiar pop-culture pull rather than something wholly original to Ocon.
