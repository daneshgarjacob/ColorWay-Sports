Pitt has one of the cleanest looks in college football: royal blue, gold, and the Pitt script on a gold helmet. The Panthers do not change much from week to week, which makes the uniform schedule easier to call than most, but there are still a few questions on the calendar, from blue pants to the all-white set to a 1976 anniversary game against North Carolina. This is the full Pitt 2026 uniform schedule, with every jersey, all twelve games, and what the Panthers have actually worn so far. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

The current look goes back to 2019, when Pitt dropped navy and "Vegas" gold and returned to the royal blue, the brighter gold and the Pitt script of the Tony Dorsett and Dan Marino years. It was the right call then and it still is. Since that change the program has added only a handful of variations: the all-white set in 2023 and a pair of blue pants with a gold stripe inspired by the Cathedral of Learning, which Pitt paired with the blue jersey at Georgia Tech in November 2025. We have not seen a new 2026 alternate announced. ACC home teams wear color and visitors wear white, so Pitt's home dates point to royal blue and road dates point to white, with the pants as the main variable.

**September 5 vs. Miami (OH).** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **gold helmet, royal blue jersey, gold pants**, the classic Pitt look for the Pittsburgh Day home opener at Acrisure Stadium. Miami came in with red helmets, white jerseys and red pants. Pitt won 59-14, and Mason Heintschel threw for a 53-yard touchdown on the way.

**September 12 vs. UCF.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **gold helmet, royal blue jersey, gold pants** again, this time for Hall of Fame weekend. UCF was in white. It was a defensive game: Pitt led 12-0 at the half, held the Knights to 206 total yards, and won 12-7.

**September 17 vs. Syracuse (Thursday).** Blue expected. The ACC opener is a 7:30 p.m. Eastern kickoff on ESPN, billed as Party on the North Shore, and reports this week say Pitt is staying traditional, which means royal blue and gold. Syracuse, the visitor, should be in white. It is the 82nd meeting in a series played every year since 1955, and Pitt has won 11 straight at home against the Orange. See the [Syracuse 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/syracuse-uniform-schedule-2026) for the other side. We will star this cell once we see the full combination on the field.

**September 26 vs. Bucknell.** Blue expected. Homecoming at Acrisure Stadium with a noon kickoff. Homecoming is a common slot for a pants change, so blue pants are the combination to watch.

**October 2 at Virginia Tech (Friday).** White expected. A 7 p.m. Eastern kickoff on ESPN at Lane Stadium, with the Hokies expected in maroon. Our [Virginia Tech 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/virginia-tech-uniform-schedule-2026) tracks the home side.

**October 10 vs. North Carolina.** Blue expected. This is the 1976 National Championship Golden Anniversary game, with the 1976 team honored at halftime and a Heisman ceremony marking 50 years since Tony Dorsett won the trophy. Pitt has not announced a throwback, but the current set already looks a lot like 1976, so the blue and gold is the logical call. If a special patch or helmet shows up, it will be here. North Carolina should be in white; the [North Carolina 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/north-carolina-uniform-schedule-2026) covers the Tar Heels.

**October 17 at Boston College.** White expected. Kickoff time to be announced at Alumni Stadium, with BC in maroon.

**October 24 at Miami.** White expected. A trip to Hard Rock Stadium, kickoff time to be announced. The [Miami Hurricanes 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/miami-hurricanes-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Canes' side.

**October 31 vs. Georgia Tech.** Blue expected. Hail to Heroes, Pitt's tribute to the military, frontline workers and first responders, on Halloween. A patch or helmet decal is the most likely addition. Georgia Tech should be in white, per our [Georgia Tech 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/georgia-tech-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 7.** Bye.

**November 13 vs. Florida State (Friday).** Blue expected. Senior Night and the home finale, 7 p.m. Eastern on ESPN. Calendar note: ESPN files this game under November 14 because its feed runs on UTC. It is a Friday night game on November 13. The [Florida State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/florida-state-uniform-schedule-2026) tracks the Seminoles.

**November 21 at Louisville.** White expected. It is Senior Day in Louisville, where the Cardinals are expected in red. See the [Louisville 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/louisville-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 28 at California.** White expected. The regular season ends in Berkeley at California Memorial Stadium, against a Cal team that also wears royal blue and gold, so the white jersey matters more here than anywhere else on the schedule.

## The Bottom Line

Two games in, Pitt has worn the same thing twice: gold helmet, royal blue jersey, gold pants. That is the uniform the fan base wants to see, and there is no sign the Panthers plan to drift from it at home. The road games are white, and the real questions are small ones: when the blue pants come out, whether the all-white set gets a night, and what extra touches show up for the 1976 anniversary game on October 10 and Hail to Heroes on October 31. The grid above has every date, and we star each game once the combination is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Pitt wearing this week?**

Pitt hosts Syracuse on Thursday, September 17, and royal blue is expected at home, with reports pointing to the traditional blue and gold. Pitt wore the gold helmet, royal blue jersey and gold pants in both of its first two games. We star the grid cell as soon as the combination is confirmed.

**What are Pitt's football uniforms for 2026?**

A royal blue home jersey with gold numerals, a white road jersey with blue numerals, gold pants, blue pants with a gold Cathedral stripe, and an all-white alternate with a white helmet. The main helmet is gold with a blue center stripe and the blue Pitt script. The look dates to Pitt's 2019 return to royal blue and gold.

**Does Pitt have a new alternate uniform in 2026?**

Nothing new has been announced. The all-white set from 2023 and the blue pants are the variations in the closet. The 1976 National Championship anniversary game against North Carolina on October 10 and Hail to Heroes against Georgia Tech on October 31 are the home dates we are watching for anything special.

**What did Pitt wear against UCF?**

The gold helmet, royal blue jersey and gold pants. UCF wore white. Pitt won 12-7 at Acrisure Stadium on September 12.

**What did Pitt wear against Miami (OH)?**

The same gold, blue and gold combination in the September 5 home opener. Pitt won 59-14.

**Why does ESPN list the Florida State game on November 14?**

ESPN's schedule feed runs on UTC. A 7 p.m. Eastern kickoff on Friday, November 13 is already past midnight in UTC, so the game shows a November 14 date there. It is a Friday night game.

**How many games does Pitt play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven at Acrisure Stadium and five on the road, with a bye on November 7. Two of the games are on Friday nights and one, Syracuse, is on a Thursday. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
