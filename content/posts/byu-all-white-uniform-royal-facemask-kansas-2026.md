**BYU is going all white in Lawrence.** The Cougars posted their uniform for Saturday's game at Kansas on Monday morning: an **all-white uniform**, a **white helmet** and a **royal blue facemask**, with royal blue accents. BYU's caption called it "this week's threads in Lawrence." Kickoff is **Saturday, November 21, at noon Eastern (10 a.m. in Provo) on ABC**, at David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium. It is the first time BYU has worn this exact combination since its 2024 trip to Baylor.

## Quick Answers

- **What is BYU wearing at Kansas?** All white: white helmet, white jersey, white pants, with a royal blue facemask and royal blue accents.
- **When?** Saturday, November 21, noon Eastern (11 a.m. Central, 10 a.m. Mountain) on ABC, in Lawrence.
- **When did BYU last wear it?** At Baylor in 2024. This is the third time BYU has worn it as a Big 12 team, after West Virginia in 2023 and Baylor in 2024.
- **What is Kansas wearing?** Blue is expected. It is Senior Day and the Jayhawks' last home game. Kansas has not posted its look yet.
- **Our grade:** A- for the uniform, B+ for the matchup.

## What BYU Is Wearing At Kansas

BYU posted this one on Monday of game week, the same timing it used for Notre Dame and Arizona State. Here is the look:

- **The helmet.** White, with a **royal blue facemask**. That facemask is the detail that separates this from BYU's other all-white set, which uses a white facemask.
- **The jersey.** White, with royal blue as the trim color.
- **The pants.** White, so the look runs white from top to bottom.

BYU's own Gameday Threads graphic shows every detail: a **royal and white center stripe** on the white helmet with the blue "Y" logo on the side, **royal blue numbers** on the white jersey, **royal and white stripes** on the shoulders, the royal "Y" oval at the collar, and **three royal stripes** down the side of the white pants.

## How It Differs From BYU's Other All-White Look

BYU owns two all-white uniforms, and they are easy to mix up. This is the one people should learn to tell apart.

- **Navy trim, white facemask.** BYU wore this at Colorado State on September 19, and it is the same look the Cougars wore at Kansas in 2023, their first Big 12 game. They lost that one 38-27.
- **Royal trim, royal facemask.** This week's look. Royal is BYU's main blue now, and it reads brighter on TV than navy.

So BYU is going back to Lawrence in white again, three years later, in the other version. The last time these two teams played, in Provo in 2024, BYU wore royal jerseys with white pants and Kansas won 17-13.

## What Kansas Is Wearing

Kansas is the home team and is expected in **blue**. It is Senior Day and the final home game of the season, so a special look is possible, and we'll update this when the Jayhawks post theirs. Blue against white is the cleanest matchup available here, and it means two blues on the same field: Kansas blue on the home side, BYU royal on the facemasks and trim.

Every Kansas date this season is on our [Kansas 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/kansas-uniform-schedule-2026).

## Our Grade: A-

This is BYU's best road uniform, and it isn't close. The white helmet with the royal facemask looks like BYU in a way the navy-trim set doesn't anymore, because royal is the color BYU actually plays in now. One accent color, nothing fighting it, and a facemask you can pick out from the upper deck.

It loses a little because all white can wash out in a noon kickoff in bright sun, and a royal jersey with the same royal facemask would give BYU the same idea with more color on screen. That's a small complaint. **A-** for the uniform.

The matchup gets a **B+**. Kansas blue against BYU white is simple and classic. If Kansas brings something special for Senior Day, it could go higher.
