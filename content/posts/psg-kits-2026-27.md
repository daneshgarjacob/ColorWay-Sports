We ranked every 2026/27 kit at Europe's fifteen biggest clubs and Paris Saint-Germain came out of it with two distinctions: the single best shirt of the season, and the best wardrobe on the continent. They are the only club whose kits both grade A- or better. What is striking is how little they did to get there.

## What Does Paris Saint-Germain's 2026/27 Home Kit Look Like?

Old Royal blue restored, with a bold wide central red Hechter stripe flanked by two crisp white lines, and the stripe carries unbroken onto the back of the shirt.

This is the kit of the 2026/27 season and nothing else was especially close.

What makes it work is that it is simple and exactly to the point. That centre stripe running the full length of the shirt, front to back, is the single best design decision anyone made this year. There is no hidden pattern, no anniversary, no story you need explained in a press release. It is one stripe, correctly placed, on the right shade of blue.

The restoration of Old Royal matters too. PSG have drifted around their blue in recent years, and this is the version that looks like the club.

**Grade: A**

## What Does Paris Saint-Germain's 2026/27 Away Kit Look Like?

Almost an inverted version of the home shirt, drawn from what Nike describe as the sound of Paris, with black at the sleeve tips.

Most clubs treat the away shirt as the place to get weird. PSG treated it as the place to prove the home shirt's idea travels, and it does.

The colours are right and the black at the sleeve tips is the small decision that finishes it. One quirk worth knowing for collectors: the authentic version carries a different crest from the replica.

**Grade: A-**

## Why PSG's Wardrobe Beat Everyone Else's

The pattern across our whole European ranking was that the clubs who did less won. The two best shirts of the season, PSG's home and AC Milan's home, are a stripe and some stripes. Neither has a concept. Meanwhile the shirts built on big ideas, Manchester City's every-shade-of-blue gradient and Real Madrid's crown geometry, both landed in the bottom half.

PSG are the cleanest proof of it. Two shirts, one idea, executed twice.

## Who Makes Paris Saint-Germain's Kits?

Nike, who have supplied Paris Saint-Germain since 1989.

## The Bottom Line

PSG have the best wardrobe in Europe for 2026/27. The home shirt is the kit of the season at an A, built on a single unbroken Hechter stripe and the restored Old Royal blue, and the away is an inverted take on the same idea at an A-. Wardrobe grade: A.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are PSG's 2026/27 kits?**

The home shirt is Old Royal blue with a bold central red Hechter stripe flanked by two white lines, running unbroken onto the back. The away is close to an inverted version of it with black at the sleeve tips. Both are made by Nike. The third kit has not been released.

**What grade do PSG's 2026/27 kits get?**

The home shirt gets an A and is our kit of the season. The away gets an A-. The wardrobe grade is an A, the highest of Europe's fifteen biggest clubs.

**What is the best club kit of 2026/27?**

PSG's home shirt. It is the only kit we graded that combines a restored classic colour with a single, perfectly placed design element, and it does more with less than anything else released this season.

**What is the Hechter stripe on the PSG shirt?**

The wide central stripe that runs down the middle of the shirt, named after Daniel Hechter, the designer and former club president who introduced it in the 1970s. It is PSG's defining design feature, and the 2026/27 version carries it unbroken onto the back.

**Is the PSG authentic shirt different from the replica?**

Yes, on the away kit. The authentic version carries a different crest from the replica, which is worth checking before you buy.

**Who makes PSG's kits?**

Nike, who have supplied Paris Saint-Germain since 1989, one of the longest continuous kit partnerships in European football.
