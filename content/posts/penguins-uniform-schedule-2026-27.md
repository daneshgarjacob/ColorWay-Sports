The Pittsburgh Penguins open 2026-27 on the road against the Philadelphia Flyers on Wednesday, September 30, then play the home opener at PPG Paints Arena on Saturday, October 3. This is the full Penguins uniform schedule: every sweater in the closet, what each one is for, and a game-by-game record of when it goes on the ice. It is the franchise's 60th season, and the Penguins have a new **60th anniversary logo** to mark it, on the helmets all year and on the shoulders for the home opener.

We build these before the season rather than after it, so the closet is here from day one and the dates fill in as they are announced. Third jerseys and any one-off specials get added here as Penguins announce them. The NHL schedules its alternates in blocks rather than all at once, so this page changes through the autumn.

## The Penguins 60th Anniversary Logo and Patch

<img src="/images/posts/penguins-uniform-schedule-2026-27/penguins-60th-anniversary-logo.jpg" alt="Pittsburgh Penguins 60th anniversary logo, a black 60 with the skating penguin inside the 6 and five gold stars arched over the 0, on a cream graphic with black and gold stripes and illustrated Penguins players" style="width:100%;max-width:760px;display:block;margin:1.4em auto 0;border-radius:12px;border:1px solid #ececec;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin-top: 0.5em;">via the Pittsburgh Penguins</p>

The Penguins unveiled the mark on September 29 as part of a season-long 60th anniversary celebration. The team describes it as retro-inspired, with nods to the club and the city: **five stars for the five Stanley Cup championships** and **gold, white and black stripes for the Three Rivers of Pittsburgh**. In the team's graphic, the black 60 carries the skating penguin in its triangle inside the loop of the 6, and the five gold stars arc over the 0.

Where it shows up on the uniform:

- **Helmets, every game.** The logo is on the players' helmets for all home and road games this season, starting tonight, September 30, at Philadelphia.
- **Shoulder patch, home opener.** It goes on the shoulders of the jerseys for the home opener against Montreal on Saturday, October 3, after a pregame ceremony. Those game-worn sweaters are being sold later in the season through Penguins Authentics.
- **Replica patches** go on sale October 3 through the Penguins Foundation, at PPG Paints Arena and online.

The rest of the 60th season is giveaways rather than uniforms: a lapel pin set on November 5 vs Boston, an anniversary apron on November 25 vs Calgary, a poster on December 18 vs Seattle (also 90s Night) and a patterned shirt on March 14 vs Detroit, plus 80s Night on November 3 and Y2K Night on March 9. No throwback or anniversary sweater has been announced, and the Flyers, who also reach season 60, have not announced a logo or patch at all. Source: [the Penguins' announcement on NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/penguins/news/penguins-announce-plans-for-year-long-celebration-in-honor-of-team-s-60th-anniversary-as-an-nhl-franchise).
