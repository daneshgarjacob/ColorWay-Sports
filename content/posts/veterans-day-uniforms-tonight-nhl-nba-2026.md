**Veterans Day and Remembrance Day both fall on Wednesday, November 11, and six NHL teams built a home game around it tonight.** Four U.S. clubs, the Ducks, Golden Knights, Sharks and Sabres, hold Military Appreciation nights. In Canada, the Flames and Oilers play their Remembrance Day games. Two of the six do it in a special sweater: **Anaheim and Calgary both wear their Hometown Remix jerseys.**

Everything below comes from the teams' own posts today and their theme-night schedules. Where a team hasn't shown what it's wearing, we say so instead of guessing.

## Quick Answers

- **Which NHL teams wear something special tonight?** The Ducks (green Hometown Remix vs. Nashville) and the Flames (red and gold Hometown Remix vs. the Rangers, the Saddledome farewell sweater).
- **Is anyone wearing camouflage jerseys?** No team has shown a camo game jersey or camo warmups for tonight as of Wednesday afternoon. The Sharks, Golden Knights and Sabres posted their Military Appreciation nights without showing a special jersey.
- **What about the NBA?** None of the 20 NBA teams playing tonight has announced a military jersey or warmup. Several posted Veterans Day tributes and hosted service members.
- **NFL Salute to Service this weekend:** the Jets, Raiders, Cardinals, Colts and Packers hold their Salute to Service games on Sunday, November 15.

### Nashville Predators at Anaheim Ducks: Hometown Remix

**What the Ducks wear: the green Hometown Remix.** The team called it "the best Hometown Remix jerseys" in its game-day post for Military Appreciation Night. It's the first game of the Ducks' Heroes Series, with a Salute to Those Who Serve presented by GovX. Puck drop is 7 p.m. Pacific on Prime Video.

## NBA: Tributes, No Military Jerseys

Twenty NBA teams play tonight: Wizards at Pacers, Cavaliers at Knicks, Celtics at Raptors, Spurs at Bulls, Nuggets at Grizzlies, Lakers at Bucks, Nets at Pelicans, Magic at Jazz, Warriors at Rockets and Thunder at Trail Blazers. **None of them has announced a military jersey, camo warmup or Veterans Day uniform.** The NBA doesn't run a league-wide military uniform program the way the NFL does with Salute to Service.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What NHL teams have Military Appreciation night on November 11, 2026?**

Four U.S. teams: the Anaheim Ducks (vs. Nashville), Vegas Golden Knights (vs. Utah), San Jose Sharks (vs. Pittsburgh) and Buffalo Sabres (vs. Tampa Bay). In Canada, the Calgary Flames (vs. the Rangers) and Edmonton Oilers (vs. Philadelphia) hold Remembrance Day games the same night.

**Are the Ducks wearing the Hometown Remix tonight?**

Yes. The Ducks said in their game-day post that the Hometown Remix jerseys are "back on the ice at Military Appreciation Night" against Nashville.

**What jersey are the Flames wearing on Remembrance Day?**

The red and gold Hometown Remix, the Saddledome farewell sweater, against the New York Rangers.

**Are NHL teams wearing camo jerseys for Veterans Day 2026?**

Not that any team has shown for tonight's games. In past seasons several teams wore camouflage jerseys in warmups and auctioned them for military charities. If a team posts warmup photos tonight, we'll add them here.

**Do NBA teams wear military jerseys on Veterans Day?**

Not this year. None of tonight's 20 NBA teams announced a military jersey or warmup. The league has no league-wide military uniform program.

**What is the difference between Veterans Day and Remembrance Day?**

They fall on the same date, November 11, the anniversary of the 1918 armistice that ended World War I. Veterans Day in the U.S. honors everyone who has served. Remembrance Day in Canada honors members of the armed forces who died in the line of duty, and the red poppy is its symbol.

**When is NFL Salute to Service in 2026?**

Teams pick their own games. Five of them, the Jets, Raiders, Cardinals, Colts and Packers, play their Salute to Service games on Sunday, November 15. Our [Salute to Service schedule](/stories/nfl-salute-to-service-2026-schedule) has every team's date.
