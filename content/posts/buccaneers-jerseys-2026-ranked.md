The Buccaneers have four jerseys on the field in 2026, and they sit at opposite ends of the color wheel. The red home and white road sets carry the season under the pewter helmet, the Creamsicle throwback brings back the orange and Bucco Bruce of 1976, and the All-Pewter set is the newest look in the closet, head to toe in the franchise's hardest color. We put Tampa Bay sixteenth in our [best NFL uniforms of 2026](/stories/best-nfl-uniforms-2026-ranked) at a B, and the only NFC South team in the top half. The throwback is a big reason why.

Here are all four Tampa Bay Buccaneers jerseys for 2026, ranked worst to first and graded, with the helmet and pants each one wears. For the week-by-week jersey schedule 2026, the [Buccaneers 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/buccaneers-uniform-schedule-2026) has every game.

The All-Pewter set is a pewter jersey with a red Buccaneers wordmark across the chest and red pirate ship logos on both shoulders, worn with matching pewter pants that carry red and white stripes down the sides, solid pewter socks and the standard pewter helmet. Tampa Bay has confirmed it for one game: the NFC South home date against the New Orleans Saints on Sunday, December 20, with the Saints confirmed in white.

Pewter is a hard color to build a whole uniform on. It sits between gray and brown, it goes muddy under stadium lights, and from the upper deck a monochrome pewter player can read as one large smudge. Tampa Bay knows it, which is why the red goes exactly where the eye lands. The shoulder ships break up the mass on a broadcast camera, the chest wordmark does the same from the front, and the red and white pant stripe gives the leg a line on a set that would otherwise have none.

It grades a C+. The details save it from being a problem, but the details are all rescue work. The same jersey over white pants would be a B, because a monochrome set gives up the break at the waist that makes a football uniform readable at speed.

The white road jersey opened the season at Cincinnati under the pewter helmet with pewter pants, a 33-27 loss that still made for one of the cleaner pictures of Week 1, since nothing in Tampa Bay's white and pewter came near the Bengals' orange. It projects to the rest of the road schedule where the host wears color: at the Panthers in Week 7, the Bears in Week 9, the Lions in Week 11, the Ravens in Week 14, the Falcons in Week 16 and the Saints in Week 18. Atlanta and New Orleans have both confirmed color for those games, so the last two are about as safe as a projection gets.

White between two pewter bands is the right way to wear this jersey. The pewter pants give the white a floor, and the pewter shell ties the top and bottom together. The weak point is the jersey itself. The red and pewter trim is thin on a white body, and at a distance the set reads more gray than red, which undersells a franchise that has one of the league's better reds.

It grades a B-. Correct and tidy, and the least distinctive jersey Tampa Bay owns.

The red home jersey is the base of the closet, worn under the pewter helmet with pewter pants as the usual pairing, though pants are a game-week call. It projects to seven home games at Raymond James Stadium: the Cleveland Browns in Week 2, the Minnesota Vikings in Week 3, the Green Bay Packers in Week 4, the Pittsburgh Steelers in Week 6, the Atlanta Falcons in Week 8, the Carolina Panthers in Week 12 and the Los Angeles Rams in Week 17. It also goes on the road once, in Week 5 at Dallas, where the Cowboys wear white at home and Tampa Bay travels in color. None of those dates is confirmed by the team, so treat them as our projection.

This is the jersey where the pewter finally earns its place. Red over pewter pants under a pewter shell gives the uniform three distinct bands, and the red is bright enough to carry the whole look on a sunny afternoon. The pewter helmet is the best piece of the modern set, a shade nobody else in the league wears. What holds it back is that the palette never quite pops on a broadcast. Red and pewter is handsome up close and flat from the press box.

It grades a B, the same mark we gave the team as a whole. It is the jersey that makes Tampa Bay look like the Tampa Bay of the last three decades, which is exactly what a home jersey should do.

Tampa Bay calls it "The '76 Jersey," and everyone else calls it the Creamsicle: a white jersey with orange numbers outlined in red, white pants, and the original white helmet with Bucco Bruce winking on the side and an orange facemask. The Buccaneers wore it from their first season in 1976 through 1996, brought it back in 2023 once the league dropped the one-helmet rule, and have confirmed it for one game in 2026: at home against the Los Angeles Chargers on Sunday, December 6.

This is the warmest, brightest uniform in the NFL, and it is not trying to be anything else. Orange and red on white is a palette nobody else has, Bucco Bruce is one of the great logos in football, and the whole thing looks like Florida in a way the modern set never has. Wearing white at home also lets the Chargers bring their gold Charger Power jersey, so December 6 should be one of the best-looking games of the season.

It grades an A+, the same mark we gave it when the date was announced in our [Chargers and Buccaneers December uniform breakdown](/stories/chargers-bucs-uniforms-december-2026). A+ is a grade we almost never hand out. This one earns it.

## The Bottom Line on the Buccaneers Jersey Rankings

The Buccaneers jerseys rank by how much color they are allowed to show. The Creamsicle is the best uniform the franchise has ever worn and grades an A+. Red Home is a solid B, White Road is a correct but quiet B-, and the All-Pewter set is a C+ that works only because the red details do the heavy lifting.

The two most interesting looks in the closet land two weeks apart in December, the brightest thing Tampa Bay owns on December 6 and the darkest on December 20. That is the stretch to watch.
