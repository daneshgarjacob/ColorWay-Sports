The Dallas Mavericks local broadcast scorebug for 2026 is one of the more polished designs in the NBA this season. The team logo integration is the standout feature here. Instead of just dropping a small logo into the corner of the bug like most networks do, the Mavericks scorebug makes the logo feel like an actual part of the design. It gives the whole thing a branded, intentional look that a lot of other local scorebugs are missing.

The layout is clean and well-proportioned. The score, game clock, and quarter are all easy to read at a glance, and the typography has a modern feel that matches the rest of the Mavericks broadcast package. The navy blue color scheme translates well on screen, giving the bug a deep, premium look without feeling too dark or hard to read. There is enough contrast between the text and background elements to keep everything legible even on smaller screens.

What we like most about this scorebug is that it feels like it was designed specifically for the Mavericks rather than being a generic template with Dallas colors swapped in. That kind of custom approach to local broadcast graphics is becoming rarer as more networks consolidate under companies like FanDuel Sports Network. When a team actually invests in having its own unique scorebug, the difference shows.

The only minor knock is that the color palette is pretty conservative. The Mavericks have silver and blue as their primary colors, and while that looks clean and professional, it does not pop the way some other team color schemes do on screen. A little more contrast or an accent color could push this into elite territory.

## Our Grade: A-

The Dallas Mavericks scorebug earns an A- for 2026. The logo integration, clean layout, and branded feel put it a step above most local NBA scorebugs this season.
