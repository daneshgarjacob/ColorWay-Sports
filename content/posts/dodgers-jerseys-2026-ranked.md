We are going to be upfront about where we are coming from. We are from Los Angeles and we grew up Dodgers fans, so this is our team and this is the closet we know best. It would be easy to assume that tilts the whole list, except the thing we love most about the Dodgers is exactly the thing that makes them easy to rank fairly: they do not overthink it.

Most of this rotation is one idea, drawn once, decades ago, and left alone. The script, the blue, the red numbers, the clean white. Four of these five jerseys are variations on that theme, and they range from very good to the single best uniform in the sport. The fifth steps outside the palette, and it is the one look we still have not fully made peace with.

Here is every Dodgers jersey and uniform for 2026, ranked worst to first and graded.

## 5. City Connect

<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/Dodgers/status/2063462645626515585"></a></blockquote>

This is the cream "City of Dreamers" City Connect, the second one the Dodgers have made, and it is the one look in this closet we genuinely do not love. The Dodgers were the first team in baseball to get a second City Connect, and this one traded the sky-blue "Los Dodgers" set for a cream base with "Los Angeles" in blue block letters, a contrail streaking off the wordmark, and a spray of multicolored flecks the team calls a galaxy of stars, meant to stand for the brilliance and diversity of the city.

The concept has a real idea behind it. The block "Los Angeles" nods to the signage at the Memorial Coliseum, where the Dodgers played their first three seasons in LA, and the jersey is at least trying to say something specific about the city rather than slapping a nickname on a loud costume the way half the program does.

The execution is where it drops. The paint-fleck pattern is busy and a little gimmicky, the cream base does not feel like the Dodgers, and against the cleanest visual identity in the sport the whole thing reads as noise stapled onto a franchise that has never needed any. This is a team whose entire appeal is restraint, and the City Connect is the one time a year they abandon it. The Saturday crowd likes it and it moves merchandise, but as a piece of design it is a clear step below everything else they own, and the C+ reflects that gap rather than the grade of a jersey we would call outright bad.

One scheduling note we had to fix while writing this: the Dodgers wear this City Connect on **Saturday** home games at Dodger Stadium, not Fridays, and it debuted on Saturday, June 22, 2024, against the Angels. If you have seen it listed on a Friday somewhere, that is out of date.

<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/Dodgers/status/2065256902280253539"></a></blockquote>

We will admit we were against this one at first. We are traditionalists about the Dodgers, and the idea of them going to a colored top on the road instead of the gray felt like the exact kind of thing the franchise had always been too disciplined to do. We wrote a [full review when the blue road jersey became official](/stories/dodgers-blue-road-jersey-official-2026), and the honest update is that they pulled it off better than we expected.

The reason it works is that it is not trying to be loud. The royal blue with the white "Los Angeles" script keeps the same lettering language as the rest of the closet, and the gray pants underneath it do a lot of quiet work, keeping it tied to a road look instead of turning the whole thing into a costume. It photographs cleanly under the lights and it gives a team that spends most of its road schedule in muted gray an actual color story when it wants one.

It gets a B+ and not an A because we are still a little hesitant about the Dodgers wearing color on the road at all. That is a preference, not a flaw in the jersey, and we know it. But when the gray sets exist and they are this good, a blue alternate has to clear a very high bar to feel necessary, and this one lands just short of it. It is a good jersey. It is not one of the best they own. We broke down exactly when the team pulls it out in our [Dodgers royal blue alternate jersey guide](/stories/dodgers-royal-alternate-jersey-2026).

This is the gray with "Los Angeles" across the chest instead of "Dodgers," and it used to be the main road uniform before the team moved it into the alternate slot. We are glad they did, because it is not quite as good as the "Dodgers" gray, but we are also glad they kept it, because it is still an excellent jersey and the "Los Angeles" script deserves a place in the rotation.

The whole thing comes down to the wordmark. "Los Angeles" in the same royal blue script, with the red number tucked underneath, is a clean, correct road look with a little more civic weight to it than the "Dodgers" version. It says where the team is from instead of who they are, and there is something to that on the road.

It sits at A- for one small reason: when you own a chest script as good as "Dodgers," putting the city name there instead feels like leaving your best graphic in the drawer. This is a wonderful road gray. It is just the second-best one the Dodgers have, and they are lucky enough to run both.

The "Dodgers" road gray is one of the best road uniforms in baseball and it is not particularly close. Same royal blue script the team has run for generations, same red number under the wordmark, same clean gray. It is timeless in the literal sense, meaning you could show this jersey to someone from 1975 or 2005 or today and none of them would be able to date it.

The script is why it works everywhere the home white works. Royal blue on gray holds its edge under every kind of light, day game or night, and the red number gives the front the one hit of warmth it needs so it never goes cold or flat. This is the jersey the Dodgers wear more than any other on the road, and it never once looks like a placeholder.

If we were ranking road grays across the entire league, this one is top three, maybe top two. It only sits second here because the home white it is based on is perfect, and this is the same idea one step removed.

## 1. Home White

In our eyes this is the best jersey in baseball, and we do not think it is especially close. Clean white, the royal blue "Dodgers" script arced across the chest, and the red number sitting right underneath it. That red number on the front is the detail that makes the whole thing, a small touch almost nobody else in the sport uses, and it turns what could be a plain white jersey into something instantly, unmistakably the Dodgers.

Everything about it is timeless. The blue is the right blue, warm and bright rather than navy. The script has a genuine hand-drawn slant to it, one of maybe three chest wordmarks in all of baseball that resolve as cleanly as this, alongside the Braves and the Tigers. There is no piping fighting it, no secondary logo cluttering it, nothing to age. It has looked like this for decades because there has never been a good reason to touch it.

We know we are Dodgers fans and we know how that sounds. But strip the loyalty out of it and the case still holds. This is a perfect uniform. It is the standard the other four jerseys in this closet are measured against, and it is the reason the Dodgers can run one of the tightest, most classic rotations in the sport. When your home white is this good, you do not need to do much else. Los Angeles figured that out a long time ago and has had the discipline to leave it alone ever since.

## The Bottom Line on the Dodgers Jersey Rankings

The Dodgers own the best home white in baseball and have been disciplined enough to leave it exactly as it is for decades. Everything good about this closet flows from that one jersey. The "Dodgers" road gray is the same idea one step removed and grades nearly as high, the "Los Angeles" alternate gray is a wonderful second road look most teams would kill for, and the royal blue alternate is a genuinely good jersey we are slowly coming around to, even if we still think color on the road is the one thing the Dodgers never needed.

The City Connect finishes last, and this time we do mean it as a knock. The cream "City of Dreamers" set is the one look where the Dodgers abandon the restraint that makes everything else work, and the busy result is a clear step down. The Dodgers do not need a gimmick, and the four jerseys ahead of the City Connect are the reason why.
