LSU and Florida have the best uniforms in the SEC. Alabama is a C. We graded all 16 teams on the full set: the home jersey, the road jersey and every alternate they actually wore this season, then sorted them into tiers and counted down from worst to best.

Every grade is based on what the team wore in 2026, which we track game by game on each team's uniform schedule page. Each entry has the team's own post of its home uniform.

## Quick Answers

- **What is the best uniform in the SEC?** LSU. Gold helmet, white jersey and gold pants at home in Tiger Stadium, with Florida right behind it.
- **What is the worst uniform in the SEC?** Vanderbilt. Three helmets, a black home jersey, a gold alternate and a new chest patch, and none of it adds up to a look you remember.
- **Where is Alabama?** 10th, with a C. A red jersey over white pants and a helmet with nothing on it but a number. Classic, and plain.
- **Where is Texas?** Fourth. The best color in the conference in one of its safest uniforms.

## 16. Vanderbilt: F

Black jersey with white numbers and a gold swoosh, three helmet shells (white with the gold star-V, matte black with a white V, and gold), and black, gold and white pants all in the rotation. Vanderbilt used all three helmets in its first four games. The gold set revealed in August came out once, as a gold jersey over white pants. Add the new SRM Concrete patch on the chest and this is the busiest uniform in the conference with the least identity.

## 15. Mississippi State: F

Maroon jersey with white numbers and white sleeve stripes, white pants with a maroon stripe, a maroon helmet with the interlocking MSU. Bringing the interlocking MSU back was the right call, and fans asked for it for years. Then State keeps walking away from maroon: all white at home for the White Out against Missouri and the black helmet with the State script against Oklahoma. The base set is plain, and the alternates hide the one thing that makes it Mississippi State.

## 14. Missouri: D

Black and gold is a great color pair, and the black helmet with the gold M over a black jersey and gold pants is a good uniform. Missouri does not have a home uniform, though. It rotates black, gold, white and anthracite pieces and announces each week's combination a few days before kickoff. The Memorial Stadium 100th anniversary throwback, with no chest wordmark and all-gold pants, has old-gold trim too muted to read as Mizzou. A team this good-looking on paper should not be this hard to picture.

## 13. Kentucky: C

Royal blue jersey, white numbers, white pants. That base is fine. Then Kentucky put a different helmet on top almost every week: blue, white, chrome, the Power K throwback against LSU, black against Oklahoma, a gray jersey with gray pants against Vanderbilt, a chrome helmet with all white at Tennessee, and now Night Ops, all black, for Florida. Some of those are good on their own. Together it is a program changing clothes every Saturday.

## 12. Arkansas: C

Cardinal helmet with the white running Razorback, cardinal jersey with ARKANSAS across the chest, white pants with a cardinal stripe. Arkansas has worn it at home every week this year with no alternates. It is also a red jersey over white pants, the most common look in college football, and the new Tyson Foods patch on the right shoulder makes it worse. The all-white set at Utah was the best thing it wore all year.

## 11. Oklahoma: C

Crimson helmet with the white interlocking OU, crimson jersey with white numbers, cream or white pants. Crimson and cream is a good pair, but the home set looks slightly different every Saturday because the pants switch between cream and white. The Bud Wilkinson throwback, a plain white helmet with one crimson stripe and no logo, came out against Kentucky and was better than the primary. When the throwback beats the everyday uniform, the everyday uniform has a problem.

## 10. Alabama: C

This will make people mad. Crimson jersey with white block numbers, white pants, a crimson helmet with the player's number on the side and no logo. Alabama has worn some version of this for fifty years, and that is the problem. It is a red jersey over white pants, the most common look in college football, with a helmet that has nothing on it but a number. You respect it. You do not love it. The best thing Alabama wore this year was crimson against Tennessee's orange at Neyland, and that was about the matchup, not the uniform.

## 9. South Carolina: B

Garnet and black is a strong pair, and the garnet home jersey with white Northwestern stripes on the sleeves is a good jersey. In the first year with Nike, South Carolina wore the white helmet over garnet, a gloss black helmet with Cocky over the black GAMECOCKS jersey, head-to-toe garnet against Mississippi State and all black against Tennessee. That is five home games and five different looks. Most of them are good. Pick one and wear it.

## 8. Ole Miss: B

Ole Miss has three of the best looks in the SEC: the red jersey, the navy jersey with gray pants and the full powder blue set it wore against LSU. Then it has four helmet shells, including a Realtree camo one, and mixes all of it freely. This season the Rebels opened in navy over gray, switched to red, went full powder blue and wore the camo helmet against Missouri. Powder blue is the best alternate in the conference. Make it the primary.

## 7. Auburn: B

White helmet with the navy AU outlined in orange, navy jersey with white block numbers in the same font Auburn has used for more than 60 years, white pants with a navy and orange stripe. The move to Nike in 2025 changed almost nothing, which was correct, and Auburn wore navy over white in every home game. It is clean and classic. It is also quiet. From the upper deck, navy and white could be a lot of teams.

## 6. Tennessee: B

White helmet with the orange Power T, orange jersey, white pants, and the checkerboard end zones behind it. Tennessee orange is one of the most recognizable colors in sports, and the home set is great. The first year with adidas added two alternates. The Smokey Grey Neyland tribute is well done, but grey is not orange, and Dark Mode against Kentucky was the first all-black game of the adidas era. Two alternates in one season waters down a home look this strong.

## 5. Georgia: B

Red helmet with the oval G, red jersey, silver britches at home, the same helmet and britches with a white jersey on the road. The silver pants are what make Georgia look like Georgia, and the Bulldogs wear them every week. This year Georgia put a white helmet on the field for the first time in program history, all white against Western Kentucky, and it was a good look. The road set, white over silver, is one of the plainest uniforms in the conference.

## 4. Texas: B

Burnt orange jersey, white pants, a white helmet with the burnt orange Longhorn. On the road, the icy whites, white over white under the same helmet. Athletic director Chris Del Conte has said there is no chance the Longhorns ever add alternates, and they have not. Burnt orange is a color nobody else owns. The problem is that the rest of it is plain: a white helmet, simple sleeve stripes and white pants. It is a great color in a safe uniform.

## 3. Texas A&M: A

Maroon jersey, white pants, and the Aggie Script helmet. Maroon is one of the best colors in college football, and nobody in the SEC wears it better. A&M changes the helmet more than we would like, but every option is maroon, white or a tribute: the desert camo Honor and Support set for the 150th anniversary game against Arkansas is as good as camo gets, and maroon over maroon at LSU looked great. It always looks like Texas A&M.

## 2. Florida: A+

Orange helmet, blue jersey, white pants. Orange and blue is the loudest color pair in the SEC, and it works because the orange sits on top and the blue does the work underneath. Florida also released its entire 2026 uniform schedule in August, helmet, jersey and pants for all twelve games, which is the most fan-friendly thing any program did this year. The Gators mix it up more than most, with a matte blue helmet, an all-blue game against Oklahoma and orange pants on the road, but every combination still looks like Florida. The Spurrier retro helmet against South Carolina was the best single look of the year.

## 1. LSU: A+

Gold helmet, white jersey, gold pants, in Tiger Stadium. LSU is the one program that wears white at home on purpose, which forces every visitor into color and makes LSU home games the best-looking games in the SEC. The tradition goes back to Paul Dietzel in the late 1950s, and Gerry DiNardo petitioned the NCAA in 1995 to bring it back. Purple and gold is an elite pair, and the gold helmet never changes. LSU wore purple at home twice this year, against Louisiana Tech and McNeese. Wear the white. It's still the best uniform in the conference.

## The Bottom Line

The best uniforms in the SEC have a color nobody else owns and the confidence to use it. LSU's white at home, Florida's orange and blue and Texas A&M's maroon all look like nobody else from the upper deck. The middle of the list is full of great colors in safe or crowded closets. The bottom belongs to teams that either look like everyone else (Alabama, Arkansas) or change so often you cannot picture them (Kentucky, Missouri, Vanderbilt).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best uniform in the SEC?**
LSU, graded A+. A gold helmet, white jersey and gold pants at home, the only team in the conference that wears white in its own stadium on purpose. Florida is second, also an A+.

**What is the worst uniform in the SEC?**
Vanderbilt, graded F. Three helmet shells, black, gold and white pants, a gold alternate and a new SRM Concrete chest patch, with a different combination nearly every week.

**Why is Alabama ranked so low?**
Alabama's uniform is a crimson jersey over white pants with a plain crimson helmet carrying only the player's number. It has barely changed in fifty years, which earns respect, but it is the most common color combination in college football. It ranks 10th with a C.

**Why does LSU wear white at home?**
Tradition. LSU started wearing white at home under Paul Dietzel in the late 1950s, lost it to an NCAA rule in 1983, and got it back in 1995 after Gerry DiNardo petitioned the NCAA. Visitors to Tiger Stadium wear color.

**Does Texas have alternate uniforms?**
No. Texas athletic director Chris Del Conte has said there is no chance the Longhorns add alternates. Texas wears burnt orange at home and the all-white icy whites on the road. It ranks fourth.

**Which SEC teams have no alternate uniforms in 2026?**
Alabama, Texas, Arkansas and Auburn have not worn an alternate uniform in 2026. LSU has no alternate either, though it wore its purple jersey at home twice.

**Which SEC teams changed their uniforms for 2026?**
Tennessee and South Carolina both switched to new suppliers, Tennessee to adidas and South Carolina to Nike. Georgia wore a white helmet for the first time in program history, Mississippi State made the interlocking MSU its full-time helmet logo, and Arkansas and Vanderbilt added jersey patches from Tyson Foods and SRM Concrete.
