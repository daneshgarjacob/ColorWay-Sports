## The Series Storylines

**Knicks rest vs Cavs grind.** New York has been off since closing the Sixers sweep, while Cleveland is on a one-day turnaround after the Game 7 win in Detroit. Rest is real in the playoffs but momentum is real too.

**Madison Square Garden home crowd.** The Knicks went 41-0 at home in the regular season Garden-record sense of dominance and are 6-0 at home in these playoffs. The Garden in late May is one of the loudest buildings in basketball.

**The Cavs at the Conference Finals for the first time since LeBron.** Cleveland has not been to the Eastern Conference Finals since the 2018 series against Boston in the LeBron James era. The Statement Edition black road jersey choice is a deliberate callback.

## What to Watch in Game 1

The Knicks Association Edition white is the same jersey that closed the Sixers sweep and the same look New York runs at home through every playoff series. The Cavs Statement Edition black is a swap from the Classic Edition throwback blue that Cleveland leaned on at home in Round 2 against Detroit. Different road look for a different opponent.

Game 2 returns to Madison Square Garden on Thursday night with the Knicks projected to flip to the Statement Edition black at home and the Cavs back in road whites, a wrong-color matchup we graded a 3 out of 10. Series shifts to Rocket Mortgage Fieldhouse for Games 3 and 4 with the Cavs going to the Classic Edition throwback blue at home on the alternate throwback court for both games.
