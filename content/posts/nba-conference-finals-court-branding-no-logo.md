The NBA Finals logo is coming back to center court for the 2026 NBA Finals. Real win for the league. The Finals logo had not appeared at center court since 2015, and the [court logo history](/stories/nba-playoffs-branding-court-logo-history) for the entire decade in between has been a documented absence. The 2026 NBA Finals logo on the court is a return of one of the great traditions in postseason broadcast design.

So why are the Conference Finals courts still naked?

The 2026 Western Conference Finals tipped off Sunday night at Paycom Center in Oklahoma City with zero Conference Finals branding visible on the court. The apron of the floor reads "NBA Playoffs" with the Google and NBC Peacock sponsor logos repeated along the sideline, but the words "Western Conference Finals" appear nowhere on the playing surface. No conference logo at center court. No conference logo on the basket stanchion pad. No painted markings of any kind that signal "this is a Conference Finals game" instead of "this is a Thursday night regular season game in February." Same story at Madison Square Garden for the Eastern Conference Finals Game 1 between the Cavs and Knicks. The NBA Finals court branding is coming back. The Conference Finals court branding is not even on the calendar.

That is the difference. A painted "WESTERN CONFERENCE FINALS" wordmark on the apron in 2021 versus a generic "NBA Playoffs" sponsor-stamp lettering in 2026. The NBA had this exact branding solution figured out five years ago. The painted Conference Finals wordmark on the apron does not interfere with gameplay, does not cost extra production time beyond the regular court setup, and is the kind of small visual upgrade that makes the broadcast feel like the round it is. The 2026 court has the same physical floor space available for the same wordmark. The league just chose not to paint it on. That is the gap we want closed.

## What Every Other Major Sports League Does

Every other major American sports league brands its conference championship round on the playing surface itself.

**Major League Baseball.** The League Championship Series (ALCS and NLCS) field gets the LCS logo painted behind home plate for every game of both series. The logos are big, branded, and unmistakable on every broadcast. MLB has done this every postseason for years.

**National Football League.** The AFC Championship Game and NFC Championship Game field carries the conference logo at midfield with the AFC or NFC shield front and center. Every Sunday in late January, the broadcast cuts to a wide shot of the field with the conference championship branding visible from the blimp.

**National Hockey League.** The Eastern Conference Final and Western Conference Final ice surfaces both feature the conference logo at center ice along with the Stanley Cup Playoffs branding throughout the ice and on the boards. The NHL has had on-ice playoff branding since the late 1990s and has only expanded it since.

The NBA is the only major American professional sports league that does not brand its conference championship round on the playing surface. The Conference Finals courts in San Antonio, Oklahoma City, Cleveland, and New York are running the same regular season floor design they used in November.

## What the NBA Used to Do

The NBA used to do this. From 2010 through 2014, every NBA Playoffs court had a "Playoffs" wordmark at center court along with conference branding for the deeper rounds. The 2010-11 Conference Finals courts featured the league's playoffs branding. The 2013-14 Conference Finals courts had it. The branding got progressively less prominent through 2015 and then disappeared entirely starting in 2016 when the league transitioned to digital broadcast overlays as the primary way to signal playoff context.

The digital broadcast overlays are still there for 2026. Every NBA Conference Finals broadcast on NBC and ESPN includes lower-third graphics that say "Western Conference Finals" and "Eastern Conference Finals" alongside the team logos and score. Those graphics do real work for the broadcast viewer. They do nothing for the fan sitting in the arena. They do nothing for the player walking out of the tunnel. They do nothing for the moment when the camera pans across the floor in a wide pre-tip shot.

## Why the Conference Finals Branding Gap Matters

The court is the stage. The court is what gets photographed. The court is the visual identity of the moment. When the Spurs and Thunder line up for the opening tip of Western Conference Finals Game 1, the wide shot of the court is the visual most viewers will remember from the broadcast. If that court does not signal "Conference Finals," the moment loses something. The MLB ALCS field tells you it is the ALCS. The NFL Conference Championship field tells you it is the Conference Championship. The NHL Eastern Conference Final ice tells you it is the Eastern Conference Final.

The NBA Conference Finals court tells you absolutely nothing.

This is also a missed jersey-and-uniform identity opportunity. The 2026 NBA playoffs are already running with [no consistent home jersey standard](/stories/nba-only-major-league-killed-home-jersey-tradition) because the league killed the home white rule. The court branding gap compounds the problem. When the home team wears a non-white jersey and the court has no playoff identity, the broadcast loses two of the strongest visual signals that distinguish a Conference Finals game from a regular season night.

## What the NBA Could Fix Before the NBA Finals

The fix for the NBA Finals is already in motion. The Finals logo returns to center court for Game 1 of the 2026 NBA Finals on Wednesday, June 3. That alone is a real production upgrade and the league should be commended for getting it back.

The bigger fix is to extend the on-court branding to the Conference Finals starting in 2027. The work is not complicated. The league already has logos for the Eastern Conference Finals and Western Conference Finals (they appear in every broadcast graphic). Painting those logos at center court, on the basket stanchion pad, or as a sideline accent is a one-week production change that every other major American sports league has been doing for decades. The Conference Finals deserve the same visual respect that the LCS, the Conference Championships, and the Stanley Cup Playoffs Eastern Conference Final already get.

The league could also bring back the broader "NBA Playoffs" wordmark at center court for Rounds 1 and 2, the way they did from 2010 through 2014. Make every playoff game look like a playoff game. Stop pretending that the digital broadcast overlay is enough.

## The Bottom Line on NBA Conference Finals Court Branding

The Finals logo on the court for 2026 is the right move. The Conference Finals courts being unbranded is the wrong one. Every other major American sports league has figured out how to brand its conference championship round on the playing surface. The NBA used to do this and stopped. The fix is small, the production cost is minimal, and the visual payoff for the broadcast and the in-arena experience is huge. We would grade the current Conference Finals court branding situation an **F** because it is a complete absence. Bring back the Conference Finals logo on the court for 2027.

## FAQ: NBA Conference Finals Court Branding

**Is there a Western Conference Finals logo on the court?** No. The 2026 NBA Western Conference Finals courts at Paycom Center (Thunder) and Frost Bank Center (Spurs) do not have any Western Conference Finals branding painted on the court. The same is true for the Eastern Conference Finals courts.

**When did the NBA stop putting the playoff logo on the court?** The NBA Playoffs and Finals logos disappeared from center court after the 2014-15 season. The NBA Finals logo is returning to center court for the 2026 NBA Finals starting June 3. The Conference Finals courts remain unbranded.

**Do MLB, NFL, and NHL brand their conference championship rounds?** Yes. The MLB ALCS and NLCS field, the NFL AFC and NFC Championship Game field, and the NHL Eastern and Western Conference Final ice all carry on-surface branding for the conference championship round. The NBA is the only major American sports league that does not.

**Will the NBA add Conference Finals branding to the courts in 2027?** No reports suggest the league is considering extending the on-court playoff branding back to the Conference Finals. The Finals logo return for 2026 is a real win but the Conference Finals remain unbranded.
