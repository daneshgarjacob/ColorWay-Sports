Denver published its full jersey schedule 2026 before the season started, all 17 games, and it makes the ranking easy to put in context. Summit White goes nine times, Sunset Orange four, the Midnight Navy alternate twice, and the Orange Crush throwback twice. The catch is that the two best uniforms in the closet are the two Denver wears least.

Here is every Denver Broncos jersey and uniform for 2026, ranked worst to first and graded, with the helmet and pants each one pairs with and the weeks it is on the field.

Summit White is the road half of the Mile High Collection: navy numbers with an orange outline, a BRONCOS wordmark across the chest, an orange collar, and orange sleeves cut with a navy mountain-peak graphic. The proportions are good and the orange keeps it from going sterile. It shares the B- we gave Denver in our [ranking of every NFL uniform](/stories/best-nfl-uniforms-2026-ranked), where the busy collar and trim were the knock on the whole set.

The pairing is where it slips. Its usual partner is the navy helmet, which gives the uniform its only dark anchor, but Denver opened the season in the monochrome version, white helmet, white jersey, white pants, at Kansas City. With the navy shell gone, the whole uniform sits at one value and the orange trim does all the work. Against the Chiefs' red helmet, red jersey and white pants, the matchup graded a B- on our [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026), and Kansas City won 31-10. Denver's identity is orange and navy, and the all-white look hides both.

When it is worn in 2026: nine games, all on the road and all published by the team. Weeks 1 (at Chiefs, worn), 4 (at 49ers), 5 (at Chargers), 7 (at Cardinals), 9 (at Panthers), 12 (at Steelers, Black Friday), 14 (at Jets), 15 (at Raiders) and 17 (at Patriots). The helmet and pants for each are announced week of.

Sunset Orange is the primary: a bright orange body that stops short of neon, white numbers outlined in navy, the BRONCOS wordmark on the chest, and navy sleeves with the white mountain-peak cut. It pairs with the navy helmet and its 5280 bumper, with white pants as our read for the home dates. The navy numbers outline reads at distance, and orange is the one color no other AFC West team can claim.

It ranks above Summit White because it puts Denver's actual color in front, but it carries the same B- from our [league-wide grades](/stories/best-nfl-uniforms-2026-ranked). The mountain-peak sleeves, the collar trim and the chest wordmark all compete for attention, and the orange itself is darker and heavier than the lighter orange on the throwback, which is the version of this palette that works best.

When it is worn in 2026: four home games, all published by the team. Week 2 against the Jaguars on Sunday, September 20, Week 8 against the Chiefs on Sunday, November 1, Week 11 against the Raiders and Week 13 against the Dolphins.

Midnight Navy is the Mile High Collection alternate, introduced ahead of the 2024 season. The body is deep navy with white numbers, orange piping and orange shoulder panels, and the 5280 bumper on the helmet ties it to the rest of the set. Navy is not a borrowed color for Denver: it was the primary jersey from 1997 through 2011, the era of both John Elway Super Bowl wins.

The orange is what keeps it from the trap most navy alternates fall into, going so dark the uniform turns into a silhouette on television. We graded it an A- on our [Broncos uniform schedule](/stories/broncos-uniform-schedule-2026), and the only thing holding it back is legibility: the numbers get slightly lost against the navy at speed.

The helmet is still undecided. Denver has worn this jersey with the Summit White helmet (2024, against the Browns) and with the navy helmet (2025, against the Bengals), and the team says it will announce the helmet, pants and socks before each game. We prefer the white shell, which gives the set contrast and lets the orange do something.

When it is worn in 2026: twice, both confirmed and both national windows. Week 3 against the Rams on Sunday Night Football on September 27, and Week 16 against the Bills on Christmas Day, Friday, December 25, on Netflix.

The Orange Crush throwback is the best uniform Denver owns and one of the best in the league. It is a complete late-1970s package: a royal blue helmet with the white "D", an orange jersey with white numbers outlined in royal blue and blue-and-white sleeve stripes, white pants, and striped socks nobody else is running. We graded it an A, and in our [league-wide ranking](/stories/best-nfl-uniforms-2026-ranked) we called the lighter orange and lighter blue the good version of this franchise's identity.

Every piece reads from the upper deck, and nothing is decoration for its own sake: no chest wordmark, no sleeve graphic, just stripes and numbers in colors that agree with each other. It is a throwback that does not feel like a costume, because Denver fans never stopped seeing the team this way.

Our only complaint is the allocation. Denver used its full four alternate dates and split them evenly between this set and Midnight Navy, which treats a modern alternate and a genuine classic as equals.

When it is worn in 2026: twice, both confirmed. Week 6 against the Seahawks on Thursday Night Football on October 15, and Week 18 against the Chargers at Empower Field on January 9 or 10, 2027.

## The Bottom Line on the Broncos Jersey Rankings

Denver's closet is upside down. The Orange Crush throwback is an A and Midnight Navy is an A-, and together they get four games. The Mile High Collection primaries, Sunset Orange and Summit White, both sit at B- because the trim, the collar and the chest wordmark crowd a palette that works best when it is left simple, and they get the other thirteen.

The throwback already shows the fix: lighter orange, royal blue, plain stripes. Until the everyday set looks more like it, the best Broncos uniform will keep being the one you only see twice a year.
