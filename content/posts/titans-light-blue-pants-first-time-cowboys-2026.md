**The Tennessee Titans will wear light blue jerseys with light blue pants for the first time in their new uniforms on Sunday, November 22, at the Dallas Cowboys.** The team announced it on Wednesday morning by sharing a post from Jim Wyatt of TennesseeTitans.com: light blue jerseys, light blue pants and white socks for the Week 11 game at AT&T Stadium. Wyatt called it a first for this combo.

## Quick Answers

- **What are the Titans wearing against the Cowboys?** Titans Blue jerseys, light blue pants and white socks, per the team on November 18.
- **Is this the first time the Titans have worn light blue pants with light blue jerseys?** It is the first time in the uniforms Tennessee introduced in March 2026. It is not the first time in franchise history: the Titans wore an all-light-blue Color Rush look against Houston in Week 15 of 2019.
- **Why are the Titans wearing blue on the road?** Dallas wears white at home. A visitor cannot wear white against a host in white, so Dallas is the only road game this season where Tennessee wears its blue jersey.
- **What are the Cowboys wearing?** White. Dallas put white on its published schedule for this game: the silver star helmet, white jersey and silver pants.
- **When and where is the game?** Sunday, November 22, 2026, 1:00 p.m. ET on FOX, at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas.

## What The Titans Will Wear At Dallas

The Titans' 2026 set has two jerseys and two pants colors, and until now the team had never put the blue jersey over the blue pants. At home, the blue jersey has gone with white pants. On the road, the white jersey has worn both pants colors, including light blue pants at Baltimore in Week 4 and at Indianapolis in Week 6. Sunday completes the set: the last of the four pairings is the all-blue one.

- **Jersey:** Titans Blue, with "TITANS" across the chest, college-style numbers and the red 6-String stripe on the sleeves.
- **Pants:** Light blue, with the 6-String stripe down the side.
- **Socks:** White. That is the detail that keeps it from being a full monochrome look, and it breaks up the blue at the shin.
- **Helmet:** Neither post names the helmet. The base set has one helmet, the white shell with a white facemask and the light blue and red stripe; the Titans Blue shell belongs to the separate Music City uniform.

A day earlier, the Titans posted photos from the November 15 debut of their Music City Rivalries uniform, the navy set with the Titans Blue helmet. That uniform is not the one going to Dallas, but it is the other new look Tennessee has worn this season, and its navy jersey over navy pants was the only one-color look Tennessee had worn this season before Sunday.

## Every Light Blue Look In Oilers And Titans History

Light blue has been part of this franchise since 1960, when it started as the AFL's Houston Oilers. The team itself says the 2026 rebrand was built to tap into those Oilers roots. Here is how the color has been used, era by era.

- **Houston Oilers, 1960s:** Columbia blue jerseys at home with white pants, under a Columbia blue helmet with a white oil derrick through 1965. The late 1960s brought silver helmets and silver pants.
- **Houston Oilers, mid-1970s to 1996:** The "Luv Ya Blue" look. A white helmet with the Columbia blue derrick, Columbia blue jerseys with white pants at home, and white jerseys with Columbia blue pants on the road. The light blue pants were a road look, worn with the white jersey, not the blue one.
- **Tennessee Titans, 1999:** The first Titans uniforms were built around navy and white, with light blue as a trim color on the shoulders.
- **2003:** The light blue alternate jersey arrived, introduced at a preseason game, according to the team. It later became the primary color for several seasons and was used as a Color Rush look.
- **December 2019:** The Titans wore what the team called a "rare all-powder-blue uniform" against the Texans in Week 15, light blue jersey over light blue pants. That is the clearest documented all-light-blue game in Titans history.
- **2023 and 2024:** The Oilers throwbacks came back, the Columbia blue jersey with white pants and the white derrick helmet. They were shelved in 2025.
- **2024:** Light blue was worn four times, twice in the regular season, per the team.
- **2025:** Titans blue became the primary home jersey and was worn at every home game.
- **2026:** The rebrand made Titans Blue the home jersey for good, worn with white pants at home, and gave the white road jersey a choice of light blue or white pants.

So the honest version of "first" is this: the all-blue combination has been done before, in 2019 and in different uniforms, but this is the first time the new Titans Blue jersey and the new light blue pants go on the field together.

## The Cowboys Side

Dallas is the reason this happens at all. The Cowboys are the one NFL team that wears white at home by tradition, and they put white on their published schedule for this game on September 2: the silver star helmet, the white jersey and the blue-tinted silver pants. That forces Tennessee into color, and it is the only road date on the Titans' schedule where it happens. We explain the tradition in [why the Cowboys wear white at home](/stories/why-do-the-cowboys-wear-white-at-home).

The matchup on the field should be light blue from helmet stripe to pants against white and silver, two very different blues. Dallas follows this game with the Thanksgiving Classic against the Eagles on Thursday, November 26. Every Cowboys date is on the [Cowboys 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/cowboys-uniform-schedule-2026).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the Titans wearing on Sunday against the Cowboys?**

Light blue jerseys, light blue pants and white socks. The Titans announced it on X on November 18, 2026, by sharing Jim Wyatt's report.

**Have the Titans ever worn all light blue before?**

Yes. The Titans wore an all-powder-blue Color Rush uniform against the Houston Texans in Week 15 of the 2019 season. Sunday is the first time in the uniforms introduced in 2026.

**Why are the Titans wearing light blue pants at Dallas?**

The team has not given a reason. The light blue pants were already part of the 2026 set, worn with the white jersey on the road. Dallas is the only road game where Tennessee wears the blue jersey, which makes it the natural place to try the all-blue pairing.

**Do the Titans have light blue pants?**

Yes. The 2026 set includes light blue pants and white pants. Tennessee wore the light blue pants with the white jersey at Baltimore in Week 4 and at Indianapolis in Week 6.

**Why don't the Cowboys wear blue at home?**

Dallas has worn white at home as a tradition for decades, which forces most visitors into their colored jerseys at AT&T Stadium.

**What helmet will the Titans wear at Dallas?**

The team has not said. The base 2026 set has one helmet, the white shell with a white facemask and the light blue and red stripe.

**What color is Titans Blue?**

Titans Blue is the franchise's light blue, the color the Oilers called Columbia blue. The team says no other NFL team uses this particular shade.

**Did the Houston Oilers wear light blue pants?**

Yes, with the white road jersey during the "Luv Ya Blue" era from the mid-1970s on. At home, the Columbia blue jersey went with white pants.

**What time is Titans vs. Cowboys?**

Sunday, November 22, 2026, at 1:00 p.m. ET on FOX, at AT&T Stadium.
