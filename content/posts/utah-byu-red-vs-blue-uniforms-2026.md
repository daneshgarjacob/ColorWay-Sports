## What Utah Wore: All Red

Utah posted its uniform on Saturday morning with three red circles: **"Home attire 🔴🔴🔴."**

- **Helmet:** red.
- **Jersey:** red, with white numbers and UTAH across the chest.
- **Pants:** red.
- **Socks:** red.

It was also Utah's Military Appreciation game, and the crowd answered the Red Out call. Every photo from the stands is a wall of red.

## Why Neither Team Wore White

In college football the visiting team wears white unless the home team agrees to something else. Utah, at home, chose red for its Red Out, so BYU needed Utah's OK to wear a colored jersey. It got it, and the result is a game with no white jerseys at all.

This is not a one-off. When BYU last played at Utah, in November 2024, it was the same picture: Utah in an all-red throwback and BYU in all royal. In this rivalry, both teams playing in color has become part of the game.

## Our Grade: A+

**A+.** Red and royal blue are about as far apart as two team colors get, and both teams committed all the way, with no white pants or white helmets to break it up. Put that in a full stadium dressed in red, on a sunny November afternoon, with two top-10 teams in the Big 12 race, and it looks like a video game cover. Most rivalry games put one team in white. This one gave fans two full colors, and it is the best-looking matchup of the year so far.

## FAQ

### What did Utah wear against BYU?

All red: a red helmet, a red jersey with white numbers, red pants and red socks. Utah posted it on game day as "Home attire 🔴🔴🔴." It was the Utes' Red Out and Military Appreciation game.

### What did BYU wear at Utah?

All royal blue: a royal helmet with the white Y, a royal jersey and royal pants. BYU called it "all ROYAL head to toe."

### Why didn't BYU wear white on the road?

The visiting team wears white unless the home team agrees otherwise. Utah agreed, so BYU played in royal blue and Utah in red, and neither team wore white.

### Have BYU and Utah worn colors against each other before?

Yes. In their November 2024 game at Rice-Eccles Stadium, Utah wore an all-red throwback and BYU wore all royal. The 2026 game repeated the red-against-royal look.

### What is the Holy War?

It is the nickname for the BYU-Utah rivalry, between two schools about 45 miles apart in Utah. Both teams now play in the Big 12.

### What grade did ColorWay Sports give Utah vs. BYU?

An A+. Two solid colors that never blend, no white anywhere and a full red crowd make it the best color clash of the 2026 college football season.

### What is a color-on-color game?

A game where both teams wear colored jerseys and neither wears white. It is rare in college football, where the road team almost always wears white.
