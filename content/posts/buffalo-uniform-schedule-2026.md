Everything here is Nike, and UB renewed that deal through the summer of 2030, so the template is not going anywhere. What makes Buffalo interesting is the helmet drawer. Most MAC programs run one shell and change jerseys. Buffalo runs three, and the black one with the oversized blue bull is the best looking piece of equipment in the conference. The Bulls have used all three shells in the last two seasons, which means the helmet is genuinely in play every week rather than a fixed starting point.

Buffalo announces the combination each week with a post the program calls Threads. Last November's Week 13 version is a good example of the format and of how the black set gets used, the black shell over the black jersey with white pants.

**October 3 vs. Western Michigan.** Blue expected. MAC play opens at home with a kickoff time still to be assigned.

**October 10 at Toledo.** White expected. Toledo's homecoming in the Glass Bowl, which means Buffalo in white against a midnight blue home team. The Rockets have not worn anything but midnight blue at home this season.

**October 17 vs. UMass.** Blue expected. Buffalo lists this one as the Flagship Cup.

**October 24 vs. Bowling Green.** Blue expected. Senior Day, and the last Saturday home game of the season, because every home game after this is a weeknight.

**November 3 at Miami (OH).** White expected. Tuesday night MACtion at Yager Stadium, the first of three straight midweek games.

**November 11 at Ball State.** White expected. Wednesday night in Muncie.

**November 18 vs. Central Michigan.** Blue expected. Wednesday night at home for the game Buffalo markets as the MACtion Meat Raffle, which is the most Western New York promotion on the 2026 calendar and a natural spot for the black set.

**November 27 at Akron.** White expected. Noon on Black Friday in Akron to close the regular season.

Buffalo put the whole thing out in March, and the six weeknight and Friday dates were baked in from the start.

## Why Buffalo Plays So Many Tuesday and Wednesday Games

MAC programs built their national television identity on midweek November football, and Buffalo carries a full share of it in 2026. The Bulls play at Miami (OH) on a Tuesday, at Ball State on a Wednesday and host Central Michigan on a Wednesday, all inside sixteen days, then finish at Akron on the Friday after Thanksgiving. None of Buffalo's last four games is on a Saturday. That matters for uniforms because the midweek window is when MAC schools reach for alternates: the audience is national, the opponent is familiar, and a black jersey under the lights reads better on television than it does at noon in September.

## The Bottom Line

Two games in, Buffalo has already used two helmets, two jersey colors and two pant colors, which tells you this is not a program that rides one look. Blue is the default at Broadview Stadium, white is locked in on the road under MAC color rules, and the black set is the one everybody is waiting on. Homecoming on September 26 and the Wednesday night Central Michigan game on November 18 are the two dates we would circle. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Buffalo wearing this week?**

Buffalo wore the blue helmet, white jersey and blue pants at Penn State on Saturday, September 19, and lost 55-13 at Beaver Stadium. Penn State wore navy over white pants. The week-by-week grid above lists every game with the expected uniform, and we star each one as it is confirmed.

**What are Buffalo's football uniforms for 2026?**

A blue home jersey with a white BUFFALO wordmark and white numerals, a white road jersey with blue lettering, a black alternate with blue numerals and a graphite alternate. Pants come in blue, white and black. There are three helmets: a black shell with a bright blue charging bull, a white shell with a blue bull and blue center stripe, and a royal blue shell with a white bull. Everything is Nike.

**What did Buffalo wear against UAlbany?**

The black helmet, the blue jersey and black pants in the season opener at Broadview Stadium on Thursday, September 3. Buffalo won 21-17 in a game that kicked off at 9:43 p.m. after a lightning delay of more than two and a half hours.

**Does Buffalo have a black uniform in 2026?**

Buffalo has a black helmet, a black jersey and black pants, and the black helmet and black pants have already appeared this season in the opener. The full black jersey has not been worn yet in 2026. We will star it on this page the moment it shows up.

**Where do the Buffalo Bulls play home games?**

Broadview Stadium on the University at Buffalo's North Campus in Amherst, New York. The stadium carries the Broadview Federal Credit Union name under an athletics partnership that also renamed Alumni Arena. Buffalo has six home games in 2026.

**Why does Buffalo play on Tuesday and Wednesday nights?**

Because the MAC schedules midweek November games for national television. Buffalo has three of them in 2026: at Miami (OH) on Tuesday, November 3, at Ball State on Wednesday, November 11, and at home against Central Michigan on Wednesday, November 18. The finale at Akron is on Friday, November 27.

**How many games does Buffalo play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: six at Broadview Stadium and six on the road, with a single bye on October 31. Eight of the twelve are MAC games. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
