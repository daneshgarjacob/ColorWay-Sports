**Ole Miss is going red, white and blue for Military Appreciation.** The Rebels posted their uniform for Wofford on Thursday night, November 19, with a video captioned "Military Appreciation Threads" and a set of photos a few minutes later. It is a **gray and white camo helmet with the Ole Miss script filled in stars and stripes**, a **navy jersey** and **gray pants**. Kickoff at Vaught-Hemingway Stadium is **Saturday, November 21, at noon Eastern (11 a.m. Central) on SEC Network+**.

## Quick Answers

- **What is Ole Miss wearing against Wofford?** A gray and white camo helmet with a navy and red center stripe and the Ole Miss script in stars and stripes, a navy jersey with white numbers, and gray pants with a red stripe.
- **Why?** It is Ole Miss's Military Appreciation game. The team posted the look as "Military Appreciation Threads."
- **When?** Saturday, November 21, noon Eastern (11 a.m. Central), SEC Network+, at Vaught-Hemingway Stadium in Oxford.
- **Is the field different?** Yes. The team's photos show a stars and stripes Ole Miss logo at midfield, one end zone in navy with white stars and the other in red and white stripes.
- **Our grade:** B.

## What Ole Miss Is Wearing Against Wofford

We went through the team's video and all six photos piece by piece. Here is everything we can see:

- **Helmet:** a **gray and white camo shell**, the same marbled gray and white finish as the Realtree helmet Ole Miss wore against Missouri on October 17. Down the middle is a **navy center stripe with a thin red stripe on each side**. The big change is the side logo. Against Missouri the Ole Miss script was red. This time the script is **filled with the American flag**: "Ole" in **navy with white stars**, with **red and white stripes** behind it, outlined in white. On the gray camo shell the script blends in, and from the side it is hard to read. The front bumper reads **REBS** in red, and the back bumper reads **HOTTY TODDY** in red. The back of the shell carries an **American flag decal**, the **SEC logo** and a round **"CA 91"** decal. The facemask is **gray**.
- **Jersey:** **navy**, with **white numbers**, white shoulder stripes split by a navy stripe, the **white Ole Miss script** on the chest and a white Nike swoosh. The player in the photos, No. 17 Carneiro, also wears an **SEC Graduate** patch on his chest, a player patch for graduates and not part of the uniform design.
- **Pants:** **gray**, with a **red stripe** down each side.
- **Socks and cleats:** white socks and white leg sleeves, and **white Nike cleats with a red swoosh**.

So the helmet is the special piece. The navy jersey and gray pants are the same pair Ole Miss wore against Louisville in the opener and against Georgia on November 7.

## Has Ole Miss Worn A Camo Helmet Before?

Yes, every year since 2022. The camo helmets started with a Realtree partnership: Realtree founder Bill Jordan played receiver for the Rebels.

- **2022:** the first Realtree camo helmet, against Kentucky.
- **2024:** a navy camo helmet against Oklahoma.
- **2025:** a gray and white camo helmet, worn against South Carolina and in the Egg Bowl win at Mississippi State.
- **2026:** the gray and white Realtree helmet with the red Ole Miss script against Missouri on October 17, a 31-3 win. Ole Miss posted that one as "Realtree, red, gray."

The Wofford helmet looks like the same gray and white camo shell as the Missouri helmet, with the red script swapped for the stars and stripes script. That makes Wofford the second camo helmet game of 2026. Every Ole Miss game this season is on our [Ole Miss 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/ole-miss-uniform-schedule-2026).

## Our Take: Grade B

**Jake's take:** "I just don't love the helmet because you could barely see how it reads Ole Miss."

That is the issue. The flag script sits on a gray and white camo shell, so the navy, red and white of the decal have nothing solid to stand out against, and from the side the words almost disappear. The navy jersey and gray pants are the Rebels' standard home pair. **Grade: B.**
