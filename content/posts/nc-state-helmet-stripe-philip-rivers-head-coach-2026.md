**Philip Rivers is home, and he brought a stripe with him.** NC State named its old quarterback head coach on Friday, November 13, on a five-year deal, and introduced him on Monday, November 16. The picture that got the uniform crowd going came the same day: Rivers in a suit, holding a **white NC State helmet with a red center stripe trimmed in black**, the Tuffy wolf head on the side and a **red facemask**.

## Quick Answers

- **Is Philip Rivers NC State's head coach?** Yes. NC State announced the hire on November 13, 2026, and introduced him on November 16. He signed a five-year agreement.
- **What is the new helmet stripe?** A red center stripe with thin black edges on a white helmet, shown in Rivers' first portrait as coach. NC State's white helmet has been plain white, with no stripe, in 2026.
- **When will NC State wear it?** NC State hasn't said. There is no announced date, and the school hasn't said whether this is a new game helmet.
- **Who coaches the rest of 2026?** Interim coach Todd Goebbel, who took over after Dave Doeren was fired on October 24. NC State has two games left, at Florida State and at North Carolina.

## Rivers Is Back At NC State

Rivers started every game at quarterback for NC State from 2000 to 2003, 51 in all, then an NCAA record for career starts at the position. His No. 17 is retired, and he is still the ACC's all-time leading passer. He went on to 17 NFL seasons, 16 of them with the Chargers, and has coached high school football in Alabama since retiring.

NC State fired Dave Doeren on October 24, the day after a 31-28 loss at Stanford, in his 14th season. Athletic director Boo Corrigan announced Rivers on November 13 and called him "one of one." His first game as head coach comes in 2027.

## What The Helmet Looks Like

Here is everything you can see in the photo:

- **The shell.** White.
- **The stripe.** One wide **red center stripe**, edged on both sides by a thin **black** line, running front to back.
- **The logo.** The full-color **Tuffy** wolf head in the red sailor cap that reads "NC STATE," at a normal size, not the oversized Tuffy that nearly covered the side of the shell in recent years.
- **The facemask.** Red.
- **The back.** An American flag decal.

Compare that with the white helmet NC State has worn on the field in 2026. In the school's own photos from the Wake Forest game on October 10, the white helmet is plain white on top, with no stripe at all. So the stripe is the change. Every game this season is on our [NC State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/nc-state-uniform-schedule-2026).

## When Will NC State Wear It?

We don't know yet, and we won't guess a date. NC State hasn't announced the striped helmet as a game helmet, and the photo is a portrait, not a uniform reveal. The two games left in 2026, at Florida State on November 21 and at North Carolina on November 28, are road games under the interim staff. We expect white jerseys for both, and we will log what NC State actually wears. The bigger question is 2027, when Rivers coaches his first game. If this is the helmet his team runs out in, it will be the clearest sign yet of what the Rivers era looks like.

## Our Take: A

I'm a lifelong Chargers fan, so I watched Philip Rivers every Sunday for 16 years. I'm about as big a Rivers fan as you'll find, and I love this helmet.

A center stripe is the oldest trick in college football, and it still works better than anything that's replaced it. It gives the shell a top and a direction. It ties the white helmet to the red jersey. And it lets the Tuffy be a logo again instead of a sticker covering half the helmet. The black edges are the detail that makes it: they keep the red from bleeding into the white on TV, the same way the black outline does on the wolf.

Put it on the field.
