**Miami is wearing its digital camo Military Appreciation uniforms against Virginia Tech on Saturday, November 21**, with College GameDay in Coral Gables and a 7 p.m. Eastern kickoff on ESPN. The Hurricanes posted the combination on Thursday, November 19, with the caption "A tribute to our nation's heroes."

It is the camo set Miami wore against Stanford last season, with **one major change: the pants.** Last year they were olive green. This year they are **black**.

## Quick Answers

- **What is Miami wearing vs. Virginia Tech?** A black helmet with a digital camo U, a digital camo jersey with black numbers, black pants and digital camo cleats.
- **What changed from last season?** The pants. In 2025 the camo jersey went with olive green pants. In 2026 the pants are black.
- **When is the game?** Saturday, November 21, 7 p.m. Eastern on ESPN, at Hard Rock Stadium, with College GameDay on campus.
- **Is this a new uniform?** No. It is the return of the camo set from 2025, updated with black pants.

## Last Year vs. This Year

**2025, vs. Stanford (October 25, 2025).** Miami posted it as "Camo mode: activated." A **black helmet with a digital camo U** outlined in white, a **digital camo jersey with black numbers** outlined in white, **olive green pants**, digital camo cleats and tan laces, and an American flag patch. Miami won that game **42-7**.

**2026, vs. Virginia Tech (November 21, 2026).** The **black helmet with the digital camo U** is back, with MIAMI on the front bumper. The **digital camo jersey** has **black numbers outlined in white**, with the ACC logo and the adidas logo on the chest. The pants are **black**, and the cleats are digital camo with camo laces. The swatch strip in Miami's graphic reads black, camo, black, camo: helmet, jersey, pants, cleats.

One more thing to watch: in Miami's 2026 graphic, the camo pattern reads **greener** than the tan-heavy pattern in last year's graphic. Two different photo shoots can shift color, so we are not calling that a change until we see the jersey on the field on Saturday. The pants are not in doubt. Olive is gone, and black is in.

## Why The Black Pants Work

Last year's set was green from the waist down. Olive pants under a camo jersey looked like a field uniform, which was the point, but from the chest down the whole body ran together into one muddy color. Black pants fix that. They match the black helmet, so the camo jersey becomes the one thing on the uniform that stands out, framed by black at the top and the bottom. It is the same trick teams use with a special jersey: keep the rest plain and let the special part carry it.

It also gives Miami a cleaner night look for a game with the whole country watching. College GameDay is on campus, Miami is ranked No. 5, and kickoff is 7 p.m. on ESPN.

## Virginia Tech's Side

Virginia Tech is the road team and is expected in white. The Hokies wore a maroon helmet, white jersey and white pants at Clemson. We will add Virginia Tech's look once the team posts it. The full Hokies season is on the [Virginia Tech 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/virginia-tech-uniform-schedule-2026).

There is history in this matchup too. Virginia Tech is the team Miami debuted its black Miami Nights uniforms against in November 2017, a 28-10 win. This time the black is on the helmet and the pants, and the jersey is camo.

## How This Fits Miami's 2026 Season

Miami has spent most of 2026 in its base sets: all white on the road at Stanford, Wake Forest, Clemson and North Carolina, orange at home, and the green alternate once, against Central Michigan. The special looks have been the **2001 Tribute** throwbacks, worn against Pittsburgh, at Notre Dame and against Duke. The camo is the first special look this year that is not a throwback. Every game is on the [Miami Hurricanes 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/miami-hurricanes-uniform-schedule-2026), and every look this weekend is in the [college football Week 12 uniform guide](/stories/college-football-week-12-uniforms-2026).

## Our Take

The black pants are the right call. The olive made last year's version look like a costume from the waist down, and black ties the jersey to the helmet. If the camo itself really is greener this year, that is an upgrade too, because a green-based camo reads as Miami in a way the tan did not. **Grade: B+.** We will revisit it after we see it under the lights on Saturday.

**What uniforms is Miami wearing against Virginia Tech?**

Miami is wearing its digital camo Military Appreciation uniform: a black helmet with a digital camo U, a digital camo jersey with black numbers outlined in white, black pants and digital camo cleats. The team posted it on November 19, 2026.

The pants. Last season's camo uniform, worn against Stanford on October 25, 2025, had olive green pants. The 2026 version has black pants.

Yes. Miami wore a digital camo uniform against Stanford in 2025 for its military appreciation game: a black helmet with a digital camo U, a camo jersey with black numbers and olive green pants. Miami won 42-7.
