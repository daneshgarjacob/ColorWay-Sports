**The Dallas Stars wear their Hometown Remix jersey for the first time next Wednesday, Nov. 25, against the Nashville Predators at American Airlines Center.** It is a white sweater with black stripes and neon green trim.

The Stars put it on their countdown graphic on Nov. 18: "1 Week Away," with a player in No. 53 in the full uniform and "Hometown Remix Debut, 11.25 vs. Predators" in neon green. Dallas is 13-6-2 through Nov. 17. Nashville is 11-6-5.

## Quick Answers

- **When do the Stars debut the Remix?** Wednesday, Nov. 25, at home against Nashville.
- **What does it look like?** White, with a black Stars crest outlined and lined in neon green, black sleeve stripes and a black hem band, both trimmed in neon green.
- **Where does the design come from?** The Dallas skyline at night. The lines in the crest are meant to look like the lit-up skyline, and a neon-style "Dallas" sits on the hem.
- **What are the Predators wearing?** By Nashville's posted Remix schedule, its navy and gold Remix. That would make Nov. 25 a Remix-against-Remix game.
- **How many times do the Stars wear it?** Five: three at home, two on the road.
- **Our ranking:** No. 18 of 32 in [our Hometown Remix ranking](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-jerseys-ranked).
- **How do the Stars do at home?** 7-5-0 in the green home sweater, 8-5-0 at home overall.

## What The Remix Looks Like

<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">The Stars' Remix. Studio photo via the NHL</p>

The regular Stars home sweater is Victory Green. The Remix goes the other way: a **white** body, with black and neon green doing the work.

- **Crest:** the Stars star in **black**, with the D cut through it and **neon green** horizontal lines across it, outlined in neon green. The lines are the skyline.
- **Sleeves:** two wide **black stripes** on each arm, each edged in neon green, with a triangle texture pressed into the black.
- **Hem:** a matching black band with the same texture and neon green borders. A neon-style "Dallas" wordmark sits on the hem.
- **Collar:** black.
- **Shoulders:** round black-and-green patches.

The countdown graphic shows the rest of the uniform: a **white helmet** with green numbers, **black pants** with neon green stripes and black socks with white and neon green stripes.

The neon green is not new to Dallas. It is the color of the black-and-neon "Blackout" alternate, and the Remix brings it back on a white base.

## The Inspiration

The Hometown Remix is a league-wide program from the NHL and Fanatics. Every team got one special jersey for 2026-27, built around its city.

Dallas went with its skyline. The team's announcement on Nov. 2 says the design "celebrates your lit skyline and neon energy," with crest texture "inspired by the skyline at night" and the hem wordmark done in "a linear neon look." The reveal post itself was four words: "The fabric of Dallas."

## Our Ranking: No. 18 Of 32

We put it **No. 18**, in the middle of the pack. The neon pops and it will stand out on TV, which is the point. The skyline lines in the crest are a smart way to put the city inside the logo without adding a new one.

We are just not neon fans. Neon green ages fast, and a white base with black stripes is a familiar template. It is a jersey that wants to be noticed more than it wants to last.

**Matchup grade for Nov. 25: B+.** If Nashville wears its navy and gold Remix as scheduled, white and neon against navy and gold is a clean contrast, and two Remix sweaters on the same ice is something most of these games do not have.

## How The Remix Has Done Around The League

Not well. Through Nov. 17, NHL teams were **15-21-2 at home in their Remix jerseys**, against 147-96-26 in their regular home sweaters. Every team's numbers are in our [Hometown Remix record breakdown](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-jerseys-record-2026-27).

Nashville is 1-0-0 in its Remix, a 3-1 win over St. Louis in the Nov. 7 debut. Dallas is one of four teams with a white Remix, along with the Islanders, Canadiens and Avalanche, which is why the home team is in white this time.
