**Update, October 19:** The full Spurs jersey schedule for 2026-27 is out, a call for all 80 dated games. The Icon Edition is the most-worn Spurs jersey this season at **25 games** (12 home, 13 road), ahead of the Association Edition at 22. Opening night, October 20 vs the Thunder, is the **Association Edition**. The City Edition debuts **January 14 vs the Pelicans** and is scheduled for 12 games in all. The Classic Edition is on for nine games. Every game is filled in below.

**Update, October 12 (confirmed):** The September leak was right. The Spurs released their 2026-27 **Classic Edition** on October 12: a silver uniform with the original arched SPURS wordmark that recreates the home uniform worn at HemisFair Arena after the 1973 move to San Antonio, plus a new Classic Edition court with a SAN ANTONIO TEXAS center circle. The Spurs wear it for **nine Classic Nights**, all marked in the table below, starting November 4 against Dallas. The leak story is in our [NBA Hardwood Classics leak post](/stories/nba-hardwood-classics-2026-27-nuggets-spurs-aba-jerseys-leak).

## The Spurs Classic Edition, Graded B (Court A+)

<img src="/images/posts/nba-classic-edition-jerseys-courts-2026-27/spurs-classic-court-arena.jpg" alt="San Antonio Spurs 2026-27 Classic Edition court at Frost Bank Center with a blue apron, red lanes and a SAN ANTONIO TEXAS center circle" style="display: block; width: 100%; max-width: 560px; margin: 1.25em auto 0.4em; border-radius: 10px;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin-top: 0.5em;">Photo: San Antonio Spurs</p>

The silver Classic Edition jersey is a nice silver, and the Spurs keep things simple on purpose, so on its own it gets a **B**. The court is the star: the SAN ANTONIO TEXAS center circle with the map of Texas is incredible, and it gets an **A+**. Together they finished No. 3 in our [ranking of all four ABA Classic Edition jerseys and courts](/stories/nba-classic-edition-jerseys-courts-2026-27).

The nine Classic Nights: November 4 vs Dallas (the uniform and court debut), December 18 vs Chicago, December 23 at Brooklyn, January 6 vs Indiana, February 28 vs Denver, March 3 at Denver, March 18 vs Brooklyn, March 25 at Indiana and April 9 vs Denver. The dates come from the Spurs' October 12 release; home and road are from the NBA schedule.
