## Why Coastal Carolina Wears Teal on the Road

Most college teams treat the colored jersey as a home jersey and the white as a road jersey, and the home team usually gets first choice. Coastal flips that whenever it can, because teal is the brand. Almost nobody else in FBS is built around teal, the field at Brooks Stadium is teal, and a teal jersey against a white home team is instantly recognizable on television. The opener at West Virginia is the model: the home side went all white for its own reasons, which cleared the way for Coastal to go all teal. Any road game where the home team picks a dark jersey pushes Coastal back to white, which is why the rest of the road schedule is marked white above.

## The Bottom Line

Three games in, Coastal has worn three completely different uniforms: all teal at West Virginia, all white at home against Fordham, and teal, white and teal at Delaware. Teal is the default in Conway, but the home calendar is stacked with themes: the Teal Out against Liberty, "Wait 'Til You See This Game" against App State and a Black Out for Homecoming against Old Dominion. October 16 and November 7 are the two dates we would circle. The grid above has every game with the expected set, and we add a star as each uniform is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Coastal Carolina wearing this week?**

Coastal played at Delaware on Saturday, September 19 in a teal helmet, white jersey and teal pants, and lost 22-14 to a Delaware side in blue jerseys and gold pants. Then came the Teal Out against Liberty on Thursday, September 24, in all teal, a 34-17 loss. The week-by-week grid above lists every game with the expected uniform.

**What are Coastal Carolina's football uniforms for 2026?**

A teal jersey with a white COASTAL wordmark and white numerals, and a white jersey with a teal wordmark and teal numerals trimmed in black. Pants come in teal and white, and both have been worn this season. The helmets are a metallic teal shell and a white shell, both with the Chanticleer head logo, and Coastal has used a black helmet in recent seasons. Under Armour makes the uniforms.

**What did Coastal Carolina wear against West Virginia?**

All teal: teal helmet, teal jersey and teal pants in Morgantown on September 5, against West Virginia in all white for its White Out. West Virginia won 31-24, and Deuce Bailey threw for 373 yards and three touchdowns.

**Why did Coastal Carolina wear all white against Fordham?**

The home opener on September 12 was the White Out and Beach Bash, with fans asked to wear white, and the team matched the stands with a white helmet, white jersey and white pants. Coastal won 45-7.

**When is Coastal Carolina's Black Out game?**

Saturday, November 7 against Old Dominion at Brooks Stadium. It is also Homecoming. The Teal Out is Thursday, September 24 against Liberty.

**What color is Coastal Carolina's field?**

Teal. Brooks Stadium in Conway, South Carolina has a teal playing surface, which is part of why the teal uniforms read so strongly at home.

**How many games does Coastal Carolina play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: six at Brooks Stadium and six on the road, with a single bye on October 24. Eight are Sun Belt games, including a Thursday night home game against Liberty on September 24 and a Friday night home game against App State on October 16.
