The Seattle Seahawks are bringing back the white throwback uniform, and the announcement came within hours of the franchise changing hands.

Vice chair Neal Khosla confirmed on Wednesday, September 9, that the 1990s-era throwback set returns for the **2027 season**, with both the home royal blue and the road white in the rotation. The Khosla family made the announcement shortly after closing a **$9.612 billion** purchase of the club from the estate of Paul Allen, which makes this the first uniform decision of the new ownership era and a very deliberate opening statement.

## What the White Throwback Actually Is

Seattle wore the white road jersey from **1983 through 2001**, the last season before the move to the NFC West and the rebrand that followed. It is the road half of the same set most fans know from the royal blue home jersey: the silver helmet with the blue and green logo, big block numbers, and the green and blue sleeve stripes that make the whole thing read at distance.

The blue home version already came back. Seattle reintroduced it in **2023**, and it has been in the rotation ever since, including two dates on the [2026 Seahawks uniform schedule](/stories/seahawks-uniform-schedule-2026). The white road jersey never came with it. That is the gap this closes, and it is why the reaction has been what it has been.

## Why the White One Matters More Than People Expect

Here is the pattern across the league, and it is remarkably consistent. When a team revives a throwback, it revives the colored one. The colored jersey is the one that photographs well in the announcement video, the one that sells, and the one that fills a stadium with matching replicas. The white road version gets left in the archive, and clubs quietly wear their modern road set instead.

That is a mistake, and Seattle is fixing it. A throwback set is only a set if both halves exist. Without the white, the blue throwback can only ever be worn at home, which means the look is limited to eight or nine possible dates a year and never travels. With the white, the whole identity can go on the road, and Seattle gets to show a national away audience something other than the modern navy and grey.

There is also a design argument. White jerseys are where striping does its best work, because there is no dark field swallowing the pattern. The green and blue sleeve stripes that read as a nice detail on the royal blue jersey become the loudest thing on the white one. The same set gets **more** interesting in white, not less, which is the opposite of what most people assume.

## When You Will See It

The set debuts in the **2027 season**. Nothing about Seattle's 2026 rotation changes, so the royal blue throwback stays on its two scheduled dates this year and the white does not appear until next season.

Seattle has opened a notification list for anyone who wants to know when the jerseys go on sale, at `seahawks.com/throwback-uniforms/interest` or by texting THROWBACK to 712-11.

## Our Take, and the Grade We Are Not Giving Yet

We are not putting a letter grade on this one, because the full 2027 set has not been shown. What has been announced is the return, not the final product, and the details that decide whether a throwback lands are exactly the ones nobody has published yet: the pant color, the helmet finish, the number font, and whether the striping is reproduced faithfully or "modernized."

What we will say now is that we like the decision a great deal. Reviving the white half of a throwback is the least glamorous uniform move a team can make and one of the most useful, and it is a genuinely good sign about how this ownership group is thinking about the brand. Ask us again when we can see it.

## The Bottom Line

The Seahawks are bringing the 1983 to 2001 white road throwback back for 2027, completing a set whose blue home half returned in 2023. The announcement was the new ownership group's first public act after a $9.612 billion sale closed. We do not have the final design yet, so there is no grade, but restoring the white half of a throwback instead of only the colored one is the right call and a rarer one than it should be.
