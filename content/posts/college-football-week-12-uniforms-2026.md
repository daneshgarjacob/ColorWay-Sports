Week 12 of the 2026 college football season has the first turquoise game in Florida State history. The Seminoles wear turquoise uniforms for their Seminole Heritage game against **NC State** on Saturday, with turquoise end zones painted at Doak Campbell Stadium to match. The Top 25 game of the night is **No. 1 Georgia at South Carolina** on ABC, College GameDay is in Coral Gables for **Virginia Tech at No. 5 Miami**, and **No. 6 Indiana** makes the trip to Washington for the Huskies' Salute to Service game. **No. 12 BYU** has already posted an all-white look for its noon game at Kansas. On Thursday, **Miami** posted its digital camo Military Appreciation uniform for GameDay night, with black pants replacing last year's olive, and **UCF** went to the gold Knights script on a black helmet for Senior Knight on Friday.

Below is every uniform announced so far, then every game this week involving an AP Top 25 team or one of the programs we track in our college uniform schedules, with kickoff time, TV and what each team is wearing. A look is marked **Confirmed** when the school itself has posted it for this game. When a school has not posted yet, we give our projection from that team's uniform schedule and label it **Expected**. Most schools post their combination between Monday and game day, so this page fills in all week, and on Saturday we check every look against the game broadcasts and team photos, then add final scores. Rankings are from the November 16 AP poll. All times are Eastern. Last week's looks are in our [Week 11 uniform guide](/stories/college-football-week-11-uniforms-2026).

## Quick Answers

- **What is Florida State wearing vs. NC State?** Turquoise, for the first time in a game: a gold helmet, turquoise jersey with garnet numbers and turquoise pants, from the team's own posts.
- **What is BYU wearing at Kansas?** All white: white helmet with a royal blue facemask, white jersey, white pants.
- **What is Kentucky wearing at Missouri?** Blue helmet, white jersey, blue pants.
- **What is Georgia wearing at South Carolina?** Expected: red helmet, white jersey, silver pants, its road set this season.
- **What is Miami wearing vs. Virginia Tech?** Digital camo for Military Appreciation night: black helmet with a camo U, camo jersey with black numbers, black pants. Last year's version had olive pants.
- **What is UCF wearing vs. Iowa State?** Knights Script Mode: black helmet with the gold Knights script, black jersey, black pants.
- **What is Iowa State wearing at UCF?** The cardinal Cyclones script helmet and a white jersey, from the team's "bounce house threads" video.
- **What is TCU wearing vs. Utah?** Purple helmet, black jersey, purple pants, for its home finale.
- **What is Ole Miss wearing vs. Wofford?** Military Appreciation: a gray and white camo helmet with the Ole Miss script in stars and stripes, navy jersey, gray pants.
- **What is Tennessee wearing vs. LSU?** Home threads: white helmet, orange jersey, white pants.
- **What is Washington wearing vs. Indiana?** "Husky Heritage": gold helmet with a purple and white center stripe, purple jersey with white numbers, gold pants.
- **What is Arizona State wearing vs. Oklahoma State?** The Roses &amp; Devils 1986 Rose Bowl throwback: gold helmet with the full-body Sparky, maroon jersey, gold pants.
- **What is Oregon wearing at Michigan State?** Yellow helmet with green wings, white jersey, yellow pants.

## Games of the Week

### NC State at Florida State: Turquoise for the First Time

**Saturday, 3:30 p.m., ACC Network.** **Florida State: Confirmed, turquoise.** On Sunday Florida State posted that "we are representing and honoring our Seminole Heritage on the field in turquoise uniforms for the first time." The Homecoming wallpaper it posted on Wednesday shows the whole set: a **gold helmet**, a **turquoise jersey with garnet numbers outlined in gold**, and **turquoise pants**. The field is getting the treatment too. FSU's rendering shows **turquoise end zones** with SEMINOLES and FLORIDA STATE in garnet and a turquoise Seminole head logo at midfield, and the crew was painting on Wednesday morning. This is the Seminole Heritage game and Homecoming on the same Saturday. The jerseys first showed up on a few players at the 2024 spring showcase, then stayed out of games until now. Our grade: **A+**. [Florida State's turquoise uniforms, explained](/stories/florida-state-turquoise-uniforms-seminole-heritage-2026).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is FSU wearing vs. NC State?**

Turquoise. Florida State wears turquoise uniforms in a game for the first time on Saturday, November 21, for its Seminole Heritage game: a gold helmet, turquoise jersey with garnet numbers and turquoise pants. Kickoff is 3:30 p.m. Eastern on ACC Network.

**Why is Florida State wearing turquoise?**

Florida State says turquoise stands for harmony, friendship and fellowship in Native American communities, and it uses the color to honor its relationship with the Seminole Tribe of Florida. FSU men's basketball has worn turquoise every November since 2013. [The full story](/stories/florida-state-turquoise-uniforms-seminole-heritage-2026).

**Are the end zones turquoise at Florida State?**

Yes. Florida State's field rendering for the Seminole Heritage game shows turquoise end zones with SEMINOLES and FLORIDA STATE in garnet, and a turquoise Seminole head logo at midfield.

**What is NC State wearing at Florida State?**

White is expected, since NC State is the road team. The Wolfpack had not posted their look as of Wednesday.
