Week 12 of the 2026 college football season had the first turquoise game in Florida State history. The Seminoles wore turquoise uniforms for their Seminole Heritage game against **NC State** on Saturday, with turquoise end zones painted at Doak Campbell Stadium to match, and lost 28-10. The Top 25 game of the night was **No. 1 Georgia at South Carolina** on ABC, where the Gamecocks went all black and Georgia won 34-6. College GameDay was in Coral Gables for **Virginia Tech at No. 5 Miami**, a 28-20 Miami win in digital camo, and Washington beat **No. 6 Indiana** 27-24 in overtime in its Husky Heritage look. **No. 12 BYU** won 31-29 in triple overtime at Kansas in all white. On Thursday, **Miami** posted its digital camo Military Appreciation uniform for GameDay night, with black pants replacing last year's olive, and **UCF** went to the gold Knights script on a black helmet for Senior Knight on Friday.

Below is every uniform announced so far, then every game this week involving an AP Top 25 team or one of the programs we track in our college uniform schedules, with kickoff time, TV and what each team is wearing. A look is marked **Confirmed** when the school itself has posted it for this game. When a school has not posted yet, we give our projection from that team's uniform schedule and label it **Expected**. Most schools post their combination between Monday and game day, so this page fills in all week, and on Saturday we check every look against the game broadcasts and team photos, then add final scores. Rankings are from the November 16 AP poll. All times are Eastern. Last week's looks are in our [Week 11 uniform guide](/stories/college-football-week-11-uniforms-2026).

## Quick Answers

- **What did Florida State wear vs. NC State?** Turquoise, for the first time in a game: a gold helmet, turquoise jersey with garnet numbers and turquoise pants. NC State won 28-10.
- **What did BYU wear at Kansas?** All white: white helmet with a royal blue facemask, white jersey, white pants. BYU won 31-29 in triple overtime.
- **What did Kansas wear vs. BYU?** All black for Senior Day: a black helmet with an oversized Jayhawk beak and a blue facemask, black jersey, black pants with red, white and blue stripes.
- **What did Kentucky wear at Missouri?** Blue helmet, white jersey, blue pants. Missouri won 28-14 in black jerseys.
- **What did Georgia wear at South Carolina?** Red helmet, white jersey, silver pants, its road set this season. South Carolina went all black, and Georgia won 34-6.
- **What did Miami wear vs. Virginia Tech?** Digital camo for Military Appreciation night: black helmet with a camo U, camo jersey with black numbers, black pants. Last year's version had olive pants. Miami won 28-20.
- **What did UCF wear vs. Iowa State?** Knights Script Mode: black helmet with the gold Knights script, black jersey, black pants. UCF won 48-17.
- **What did Iowa State wear at UCF?** The cardinal Cyclones script helmet, a white jersey and cardinal pants.
- **What did TCU wear vs. Utah?** Purple helmet, black jersey, purple pants, for its home finale. Utah won 38-17.
- **What did Ole Miss wear vs. Wofford?** Military Appreciation: a gray and white camo helmet with the Ole Miss script in stars and stripes, navy jersey, gray pants. Ole Miss won 59-17.
- **What did Tennessee wear vs. LSU?** Home threads: white helmet, orange jersey, white pants. Tennessee won 27-13.
- **What did Washington wear vs. Indiana?** "Husky Heritage": gold helmet with a purple and white center stripe, purple jersey with white numbers, gold pants. Washington won 27-24 in overtime.
- **What did Arizona State wear vs. Oklahoma State?** The Roses &amp; Devils 1986 Rose Bowl throwback: gold helmet with the full-body Sparky, maroon jersey, gold pants. Oklahoma State won 30-27 in overtime.
- **What did Oregon wear at Michigan State?** Yellow helmet with green wings, white jersey, yellow pants. Michigan State wore all black, and Oregon won 45-7.

## Games of the Week

### NC State at Florida State: Turquoise for the First Time

**Saturday, 3:30 p.m., ACC Network.** **Florida State: Confirmed, turquoise.** On Sunday Florida State posted that "we are representing and honoring our Seminole Heritage on the field in turquoise uniforms for the first time." The Homecoming wallpaper it posted on Wednesday shows the whole set: a **gold helmet**, a **turquoise jersey with garnet numbers outlined in gold**, and **turquoise pants**. The field is getting the treatment too. FSU's rendering shows **turquoise end zones** with SEMINOLES and FLORIDA STATE in garnet and a turquoise Seminole head logo at midfield, and the crew was painting on Wednesday morning. This is the Seminole Heritage game and Homecoming on the same Saturday. The jerseys first showed up on a few players at the 2024 spring showcase, then stayed out of games until now. Our grade: **A+**. [Florida State's turquoise uniforms, explained](/stories/florida-state-turquoise-uniforms-seminole-heritage-2026).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is FSU wearing vs. NC State?**

Turquoise. Florida State wore turquoise uniforms in a game for the first time on Saturday, November 21, for its Seminole Heritage game: a gold helmet, turquoise jersey with garnet numbers and turquoise pants, confirmed from the game broadcast. NC State won 28-10.

**Why is Florida State wearing turquoise?**

Florida State says turquoise stands for harmony, friendship and fellowship in Native American communities, and it uses the color to honor its relationship with the Seminole Tribe of Florida. FSU men's basketball has worn turquoise every November since 2013. [The full story](/stories/florida-state-turquoise-uniforms-seminole-heritage-2026).

**Are the end zones turquoise at Florida State?**

Yes. Florida State painted the end zones turquoise for the Seminole Heritage game, with SEMINOLES and FLORIDA STATE in garnet, as the game broadcast showed.
