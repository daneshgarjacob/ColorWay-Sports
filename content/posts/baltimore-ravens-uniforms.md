## Every Era of Ravens Uniforms

<img src="/images/posts/ravens-uniform-history/ravens-2010s-white-celebrating.jpg" alt="Baltimore Ravens players celebrating in white road uniforms during the 2010s era" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin-top: 0.5em;">Baltimore Ravens Photos</p>

The Ravens have one of the most stable visual identities in the NFL. Purple, black, and white have anchored the franchise since 1996, and every era has built on the foundation Jonathan Ogden, Ray Lewis, and the original 1996 roster set in stone. Our [complete Ravens uniform history](/stories/ravens-uniform-history-every-jersey) covers every jersey Baltimore has worn, from the inaugural set through the 2024 Purple Rising alternates. Below, the highlights from each era.

**1996 Inaugural Set.** Purple jerseys with white numbers outlined in gold, black pants, the original flying raven helmet logo, and the winged shield on the chest. One of the strongest first-day visual identities of the 1990s.

**2000 Super Bowl XXXV.** The Ravens won their first championship in white road uniforms after the 1999 logo redesign swapped the flying raven for the bird-head logo. The number font was updated with an italicized style, and the shield logo moved to the sleeves.

**2004 Black Alternate.** Ray Lewis in number 52, all black, M&T Bank Stadium under the lights. One of the most intimidating uniforms in football and an instant fan favorite.

**2012 Super Bowl XLVII.** A new Nike-era template launched in 2012, and the Ravens wore the white road version when they beat the 49ers in Super Bowl XLVII. The collar, sleeve cuffs, and number font all updated for the modern Nike fit.

**2016 Color Rush.** The Ravens went full purple top to bottom with gold pants for the Color Rush program, one of the more loved Color Rush kits across the league.

**2024 Purple Rising.** A metallic purple alternate helmet with gold talon stripes paired with the existing purple jersey, the first preview of where the rebrand would head a year later.

**2026 The Next Flight.** Four jerseys, three helmets, the new Darkness blackout, and the biggest uniform change in franchise history.

The full visual timeline covers Super Bowl XXXV in 2000, the Reebok era through 2011, the Nike era launching with Super Bowl XLVII in 2012, the Color Rush gold pants in 2016, the 2018 number font update, the 2024 Purple Rising helmet, and the 2026 The Next Flight reveal. We have a photo of every era in the [Ravens uniform history post](/stories/ravens-uniform-history-every-jersey).

## What The Next Flight Means for Baltimore

The Ravens did not need new uniforms. The 1996 visual identity is still strong, the purple-black-gold palette has aged well, and Baltimore was already one of the cleanest-looking teams on broadcast every Sunday. The team chose to redesign anyway, and the result is a collection that respects the foundation while modernizing every detail.

The primary purple home jersey keeps the look fans grew up with but updates the collar, sleeves, and number font. The white road gets the new "Baltimore" wordmark across the chest, the first time the city name has appeared on the jersey since the team arrived in 1996. The Purple Rising alternate becomes the team's primary alternate for 2026, paired with the metallic purple helmet that debuted in 2024. And the Darkness uniform is the statement piece, an all-black blackout look with a matte helmet, glowing red eyes, and a tie-in to the "When Darkness Falls at M&T Bank" campaign that has been part of Ravens marketing for years.

The bigger picture for Baltimore: The Next Flight gives the franchise three distinct uniform identities (the classic purple, the Purple Rising alternate, and the Darkness blackout) that each connect to a different era of the brand. That depth gives the team room to script a full season of jersey rotations the same way the best uniform programs in baseball do. We hope they commit to a schedule.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When did the Baltimore Ravens get new uniforms?**

The Baltimore Ravens unveiled new uniforms on April 16, 2026 at Merriweather Post Pavilion under the campaign name "The Next Flight." The 2026 collection is the biggest uniform change in franchise history and includes four jerseys, three helmets, and an all-new Darkness blackout alternate. We covered every detail in our [full review of the 2026 Ravens uniforms](/stories/ravens-new-uniforms-2026-review).

**What are the new Baltimore Ravens uniforms called?**

The 2026 Ravens uniform collection is branded as "The Next Flight." The set includes four jerseys (Primary Home Purple, White Road with the new Baltimore wordmark, the Purple Rising alternate, and the Darkness blackout), three helmets (primary black, Purple Rising metallic purple, and Darkness matte black with red eyes), plus updated wordmarks, collar patterns, and sleeve shields.

**What is the Ravens Darkness uniform?**

The Darkness uniform is the brand new alternate the Baltimore Ravens introduced in 2026 as part of The Next Flight collection. It is an all-black blackout look with a matte black helmet, glowing red eyes referencing the tunnel above the M&T Bank Stadium player entrance, black talon stripes, and the front-facing two-toned Ravens logo.

**What are the Baltimore Ravens team colors?**

The Baltimore Ravens team colors are purple, black, and gold, with white as a secondary color on the road jersey. The purple is the franchise's signature shade and has anchored every Ravens uniform since the 1996 inaugural season. Gold and white were added as accent colors and have stayed consistent through every redesign.

**How many helmets do the Ravens have for 2026?**

The Ravens have three helmets for the 2026 season: the primary black helmet they have worn for years, the Purple Rising metallic purple helmet with gold talon stripes and a gold facemask (first introduced in 2024), and the new Darkness matte black helmet with two-toned front-facing Ravens logo and glowing red eyes.

**What is the Baltimore wordmark on the new Ravens road jersey?**

The 2026 white road jersey features a new "Baltimore" wordmark across the chest, the first time the city name has appeared on a Ravens jersey since the team's debut in 1996. The wordmark is part of The Next Flight rebrand and is one of the cleanest individual updates in the new collection.

**When was the last time the Ravens changed uniforms?**

Before the 2026 The Next Flight reveal, the most recent meaningful Ravens uniform update was the 2024 introduction of the Purple Rising metallic purple alternate helmet. Before that, the Ravens last refreshed their template when Nike took over the NFL uniform contract in 2012, including the white road set Baltimore wore to win Super Bowl XLVII.
