**The 2026 NHL Heritage Classic jerseys are out.** The Winnipeg Jets and Montreal Canadiens unveiled their outdoor sweaters on Wednesday, October 7, and both teams went back in time. Winnipeg wears a **white** sweater built on the original Jets look of the 1980s and 1990s. Montreal wears a **red** sweater with a single blue chest band, a redrawn felt CH and, for the first time ever on a Canadiens jersey, the words **"Les Habitants."**

Both were designed by Fanatics with the NHL and the two clubs. They get one game: the Tim Hortons NHL Heritage Classic, Montreal at Winnipeg, on Sunday, October 25, 2026, at 7 p.m. ET at Princess Auto Stadium. So it is white against red, outdoors, in late October in Manitoba.

Here is what each team is wearing, and how we grade them.

## The Winnipeg Jets Heritage Classic Jersey

**The Jets' Heritage Classic sweater is white, and it is a direct homage to the franchise's look from the 1980s and 1990s.** The round vintage crest, with "JETS" across a hockey stick and the plane in the red circle, is cut from felt with chain-stitched detailing. The back numbers get a two-layer felt treatment too. Navy and red stripes run around the arms and the hem, and the same stripes show up on the socks. It is worn with red pants.

A few details you will only catch up close. An all-new streaking jet graphic frames the event name along the hem. The loop label inside the collar carries the jet-in-a-circle mark from the team's pants. And the sweater was made to pair with the navy Heritage alternate the Jets already wear this season, so the throwback look now comes in a dark and a white version.

**Grade: A-.** This is a great jersey. The vintage crest is one of the best logos the Jets have ever had, and putting it on a clean white body with navy and red stripes is exactly how it should look. The only reason it lands a notch below Montreal is that the Canadiens did more with less.

## The Montreal Canadiens Heritage Classic Jersey

**The Canadiens' Heritage Classic sweater is red, with one blue band across the chest and arms trimmed in off-white pinstriping.** The trim and the laced collar are a vintage off-white instead of bright white, which gives the whole thing an older feel. The CH has been redrawn to echo past versions of the crest and is cut from felt with running-stitch detailing.

Then come the firsts. **"Les Habitants" appears on a Canadiens jersey for the first time**, on the back of the collar. The event name at the back hem is written in French and sits next to symbols from the flag of the City of Montreal. A fleur-de-lis loop label nods to Quebec.

One blue band instead of the usual stripe set, an off-white collar and a redrawn CH make it different enough that you notice right away. But it is not so different that it stops looking like the Canadiens. It keeps its identity, and that balance is what earns the full A.

Both clubs have been here before. Montreal played in the very first Heritage Classic in Edmonton in 2003 and again in Calgary in 2011. Winnipeg hosted Edmonton in 2016 at the same stadium, then called Investors Group Field, and played Calgary in Regina in 2019. This is the first time the two teams meet in the event.

## The Bottom Line

The Jets went white with their 1980s and 1990s crest and earn an A-. The Canadiens went red with one blue band, a redrawn CH and "Les Habitants" for the first time, and earn an A for changing just enough without losing who they are. Both get worn once, on Sunday, October 25, at Princess Auto Stadium in Winnipeg.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When is the 2026 NHL Heritage Classic?**

The Tim Hortons NHL Heritage Classic is Sunday, October 25, 2026, at 7 p.m. ET. The Montreal Canadiens visit the Winnipeg Jets at Princess Auto Stadium in Winnipeg.

**What are the Jets wearing for the Heritage Classic?**

The Jets wear a white sweater based on their 1980s and 1990s look, with the vintage round Jets crest in chain-stitched felt, navy and red stripes on the arms and hem, red pants and striped socks. A streaking jet graphic frames the event name along the hem. We grade it an A-.

**What are the Canadiens wearing for the Heritage Classic?**

The Canadiens wear a red sweater with a single blue band across the chest and arms, off-white pinstriping, a vintage off-white laced collar and a redrawn felt CH. The event name at the back hem is in French next to symbols from the City of Montreal flag. We grade it an A.

**What does "Les Habitants" mean on the Canadiens jersey?**

"Les Habitants" is the old nickname for the Canadiens, and it is where the "Habs" name comes from. The Heritage Classic sweater is the first Canadiens jersey to carry the words, on the back of the collar.

**How can I watch the 2026 Heritage Classic?**

The game airs on SN and TVA Sports in Canada and on TNT, truTV and HBO Max in the United States. Puck drop is 7 p.m. ET on Sunday, October 25.
