The Vegas Golden Knights advance to the 2026 Stanley Cup Final and the jerseys pick up a brand-new piece of branding for the round. The 2026 Stanley Cup Final patch lands on the right chest of every Knights sweater from Game 1 of the Final forward, paired against the C on captain Mark Stone's left chest in the photo above. We have loved Stanley Cup Final patches in past years. This one is fine. We wish there was more to it.

## What Is on the 2026 Stanley Cup Final Jersey Patch

The 2026 Stanley Cup Final patch is built around the league's official Stanley Cup Final logo for the year, the same mark the NHL has been pushing across broadcasts, social, and arena branding for the postseason. The "2026" year reads clean on the patch and is the single biggest design win, the kind of date stamp that gives the jersey a real timestamp value as a piece of hockey history a year, five years, or twenty years from now. A jersey carrying a Stanley Cup Final patch without a year on it is a jersey that gets confused with the prior decade of Cup jerseys the moment it leaves the broadcast frame. The 2026 lockup avoids that completely and is the right call.

The non-captain look on Mitch Marner's sweater above shows the patch in isolation without the captain C next to it, the clean version that ends up on every Knights player who is not wearing a letter. The Stanley Cup Final patch sits in the same right-chest position the conference round patches lived in during the earlier rounds, the NHL keeping the placement consistent across the entire postseason and letting the patch itself do the round-of-play storytelling for the broadcast.

The problem is the patch itself plays it too safe. The Stanley Cup Final logo gets a literal lift from the broader NHL marketing kit and applied to a jersey patch shape without much in the way of jersey-specific design work, the kind of small adaptive treatment that pushed Final patches in past years above the standard logo-on-fabric level. We have loved Stanley Cup Final patches in past years for that reason, the design teams leaning into the host city, the championship round identity, or some subtle adaptive element that made the patch feel like it was made for a jersey rather than just clipped from the season's marketing system. The 2026 version does not do much of that work, and the result is a patch that earns the year-stamp credit but leaves the rest of the moment on the table.

The 2026 Stanley Cup Final jersey patch hits the year-stamp brief and not much else. The patch is on every Knights sweater on the right chest for the rest of the championship round and will be on every sweater of whichever team comes out of the Eastern Conference Final too, the standard NHL Final placement holding for the year. Worth owning for the year stamp and the Knights run, capped by a design that should have done more with the moment.
