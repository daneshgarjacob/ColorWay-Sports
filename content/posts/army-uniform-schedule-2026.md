Army does not rotate through alternates the way most programs do. The Black Knights wear black at home, white on the road, and a gold helmet with every single one of them, and then once a year in December they throw all of it out and wear something built from scratch. This is the full Army 2026 uniform schedule: the black home set, the white road set, the new USAA patch, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games ending with the 127th Army-Navy Game at MetLife Stadium. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

The closet is short on purpose. Army's base uniform is black and gold with almost no ornament, and the gold helmet has not meaningfully changed in decades. The one visible difference for 2026 is sponsorship: USAA signed on as the preservation partner of Army West Point Athletics in June, a five-year deal reported at around $10 million, and that put a USAA patch on the jersey and the company's name on the building. Michie Stadium is now officially Michie Stadium Preserved by USAA, with the logo painted at the 25-yard lines. The patch debuted in the September 5 opener against Bryant and it is on the jersey in every broadcast frame we have checked since.

**December 12 vs. Navy, MetLife Stadium.** This is the whole season. The 127th Army-Navy Game kicks at 3 p.m. Eastern on CBS, presented by USAA, and it is the first time America's Game has been played at MetLife Stadium since 2021. ESPN lists Army as the designated home team, but that convention means almost nothing here, because Army has not worn its regular black jersey in this game in years. Nike builds a bespoke uniform for the Black Knights every December that honors a unit or a chapter of Army history, and it has become the single most anticipated uniform reveal in college football.

## What Army Wears Against Navy, and What We Know About 2026

Nothing has been announced. That is the honest answer as of the middle of September, and it is what the calendar would predict: Army's Army-Navy uniform reveal usually lands in mid-November, roughly a month out. Last year the unveiling came on November 13.

  <blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/USArmy/status/1988924805089800578"></a></blockquote>
</div>

The recent run gives you the shape of what is coming. Army honored the 25th Infantry Division in 2020, Special Forces Command in 2021, the 1st Armored Division and the 80th anniversary of Operation Torch in 2022, the 3rd Infantry Division and the opening phase of Operation Iraqi Freedom in 2023, and the 101st Airborne Division in 2024, when the helmets carried the card suit markings paratroopers and glider troops used to identify their units after the jump into Normandy. Every one of those was a full head-to-toe build, not a jersey swap.

The 2025 uniform was the largest swing yet. Army and Nike marked 250 years of Army service with an all-white set carrying a subtle marble print on the jersey and pants meant to evoke the headstones at Arlington National Cemetery, a number and nameplate font drawn in the style of the United States Constitution, and purple running through the numerals and along the helmet as a nod to the Badge of Military Merit, the forerunner of the Purple Heart.

Navy lost none of that arms race, which is why the joint reveal has become an event of its own. For 2026 we are not going to guess at a unit, a color or a helmet finish. When the reveal drops we will star the December cell and write the whole thing up.

## The Bottom Line

Army is the rare program where the uniform schedule is almost entirely predictable for eleven games and completely unknowable for the twelfth. Black at home, white on the road, gold helmet every week, with the pants the only real variable and the new USAA patch the only visible change from last season. Then December arrives and Nike hands the Black Knights something nobody has seen. The grid above has every date, and the cell that matters most is the one still waiting on a reveal.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Army wearing this week?**

Army is off this week. The Black Knights are on a bye after losing 28-24 to South Florida on September 12 in the black jersey and black pants. The next game is Friday, September 25 at Temple, where white is expected under the American Conference road convention. The grid above lists every date with the expected set and we star each cell as it is confirmed.

**What are Army's football uniforms for 2026?**

A black home jersey with gold numerals and a gold ARMY wordmark, a white road jersey with black numerals and gold trim, and gold, black or white pants, all from Nike and all worn with the same gold helmet with a black center stripe and no side logo. New for 2026, a USAA patch sits on the jersey as part of the school's preservation partnership with the company.

**Does Army have a new alternate uniform in 2026?**

Nothing has been announced yet. The alternate Army wears every season is the Army-Navy Game uniform, a one-off Nike build that changes every December, and the 2026 version has not been revealed. Last year's reveal came on November 13, so expect the same window this fall.

**What is Army wearing for the Army-Navy Game in 2026?**

Unknown as of September 17. The 127th Army-Navy Game is Saturday, December 12 at 3 p.m. Eastern on CBS at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey. Army has honored a different unit or era each year, from the 101st Airborne Division in 2024 to 250 years of Army service in 2025, and the 2026 design will be unveiled closer to the game. We will update this page the day it drops.

**What did Army wear against South Florida?**

The gold helmet, black jersey and black pants at Michie Stadium on September 12. South Florida wore white and won 28-24. In the opener a week earlier against Bryant, Army wore the gold helmet, black jersey and gold pants and won 59-3.

**How many games does Army play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: six at Michie Stadium, five on the road in the American Conference and at Louisiana Tech, and the neutral-site Army-Navy Game at MetLife Stadium on December 12, with byes on September 19 and November 14. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
