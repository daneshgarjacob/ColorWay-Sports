Oklahoma State does not have a home uniform and a road uniform. It has a closet, and the Cowboys pick from it every week. This is the full Oklahoma State 2026 uniform schedule: the jerseys, pants and helmets in the rotation, what we expect for each of the twelve games in Eric Morris's first season, and a record of what actually goes on the field. The program announces each combination in game week, usually on the @CowboyFB account a day or two before kickoff, and we update this page as each one lands.

Two combinations are on the board. **The opener at Tulsa on September 5 was all white, white helmet with the orange brand, white jersey, white pants**, and **the Oregon game on September 12 was white helmet, orange jersey, white pants, with the Pistol Pete helmet**, both marked with a ★ in the grid. The current Nike set arrived in 2023 with a nod to the Barry Sanders and Thurman Thomas era: "Oklahoma State" over bold numerals on the chest, shoulder numbers for the first time since 2005, and the arm stripes those teams wore. Because helmet, jersey and pants are chosen separately, the Cowboys can put a dozen or more distinct looks on the field in a season, and they usually do. Treat every expectation below as a starting position. Orange at home and white on the road is the default, and Oklahoma State departs from the default more often than almost anyone in the Big 12.

**September 5 at Tulsa.** ★ Confirmed from the announced combination: **white helmet with the orange brand, white jersey, white pants**, the all-white look the program posted on game day for the Turnpike Classic and Eric Morris's first game in charge. It did not bring luck. Tulsa won 24-10, and the Cowboys left Chapman Stadium 0-1 in a uniform that could not have been cleaner.

**September 12 vs. Oregon.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **white helmet, orange jersey, white pants**, with the white Pistol Pete helmet rather than the brand shell, and the Cowboys announced it on Thursday night, early by their standards. This was the classic Stillwater look, orange jersey with white numerals outlined in black over white pants, against the Ducks in a green helmet, white jersey and white pants, and it went about as well as an orange jersey can go. Oklahoma State beat then-No. 6 Oregon 39-31 in the Ducks' first trip to Boone Pickens Stadium, the first win of the Morris era. Coming into the game the white-orange-white combination had gone 16-3 since 2011. Make it 17-3. Oregon's side of the night is on the [Oregon 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/oregon-uniform-schedule-2026).

**September 19 vs. Murray State.** Orange expected. Family Weekend, an FCS visitor making its first trip to Stillwater, and the kind of game where the Cowboys sometimes reach for something other than the default. The combination is announced in game week.

**September 26 at West Virginia.** White expected. Morgantown in late September, the first Big 12 road trip. Orange or black pants under a white jersey are both live options.

**October 3.** Bye.

**October 10 vs. UCF.** Orange expected. Stripe the Stadium night, the crowd alternating orange and white by section, and UCF making its first visit to Stillwater. We would not be surprised by a white-jersey home look on a striped night, but nothing is announced.

**October 17 at Houston.** White expected. On the road the Cowboys pair the white jersey with orange or black pants, and either would fit a mid-October trip to Houston.

**October 24 vs. Colorado.** Orange expected. America's Greatest Homecoming. This is the date to watch: Homecoming has drawn special looks before, including the black Oklahoma A&M throwback for the 125th anniversary in 2024, and nothing has been announced for 2026. If a one-off uniform is coming this season, this is the likeliest week.

**October 31 at Iowa State.** White expected. The first Halloween road game since 2015, in Ames, at the start of the only back-to-back road stretch on the schedule. Black jerseys on Halloween would be very on brand for this program, but that is a guess, not a report.

**November 7 at Kansas State.** White expected. The 17th straight season against Kansas State, the longest active streak against any Cowboy opponent.

**November 14 vs. Texas Tech.** Orange expected. Salute to Service, against Texas Tech, a 2025 College Football Playoff team visiting Stillwater. Military appreciation weeks often bring helmet decals or a special detail rather than a whole new uniform.

**November 21 at Arizona State.** White expected. Tempe, the fifth trip to Arizona in six seasons.

**November 28 vs. Kansas.** Orange expected. Senior Day and the regular-season finale. Bedlam is not on the schedule, so this is the last home look of the year.

## The Bottom Line

Everywhere else in this series the question is which of two jerseys comes out. At Oklahoma State the question is which helmet, which jersey and which pants, and the answer arrives on social media a day or two before kickoff. Two weeks in, the closet has already produced all white and the classic orange over white with the Pistol Pete lid. We log each one here as it lands.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Oklahoma State wearing this week?**

Oklahoma State wore an orange jersey with white pants and the white Pistol Pete helmet to beat Oregon on September 12, one week after an all-white uniform at Tulsa. The Cowboys announce each week's combination in game week, usually on the program's social accounts a day or two before kickoff. The grid above lists every game with the expected look and is updated as each combination is confirmed.

**What are Oklahoma State's uniforms for 2026?**

The current Nike set, introduced in 2023, includes orange, black and white jerseys (two white versions, one with orange numerals and one with black), pants in orange, black and white, and several helmets: white and orange shells with the OSU brand, a black shell, and the white Pistol Pete helmet. The design nods to the 1980s teams with "Oklahoma State" over the numerals and the arm stripes worn by Barry Sanders and Thurman Thomas.

**Does Oklahoma State have an alternate uniform in 2026?**

The black jersey is the standing alternate and it usually appears a few times a season. No one-off throwback or special uniform has been announced for 2026. Homecoming against Colorado on October 24 is the most likely date for one if it comes.

**How many uniform combinations does Oklahoma State have?**

With three jersey colors, three pants colors and at least four helmets chosen independently, the practical count runs well past thirty, and the Cowboys typically use a dozen or more distinct looks in a season. Each is picked week to week rather than set in advance.

**How many games does Oklahoma State play in 2026?**

Twelve regular-season games, six at home and six away, with one bye on October 3. Bedlam against Oklahoma is not on the 2026 schedule. The full slate with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
