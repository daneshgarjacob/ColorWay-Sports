## How Often Is the American Family Field Roof Open?

The roof gets used more than almost any other in baseball, but in both directions. The Brewers open and close it roughly 230 times across a season because Milwaukee weather changes fast. Early-season games in April are often played with the roof closed against the cold, while warm, dry summer evenings get the roof open for outdoor baseball. The team is not committed to open or closed the way the desert and Gulf Coast parks are. They genuinely play it day by day.

## How the Brewers Decide: The Roof Rules

The Brewers describe the roof call as "an art and a science." The main factors are:

- **Temperature**, with the team generally wanting it 60 degrees or warmer during the game
- **Rain or inclement weather** in the forecast, during the day or for the game itself
- **Wind** and the "feels like" temperature
- **Time of year**, since April and late September lean cold and the roof leans closed

The decision is made by the Brewers' stadium operations leadership and the head groundskeeper. As with every MLB park, if the roof starts open it can be closed for impending rain or bad weather, and the postseason call belongs to MLB.

## American Family Field Roof Specs

American Family Field, originally Miller Park, opened in 2001 with its signature fan-shaped retractable roof. The panels pivot from a single point behind home plate and swing open like a folding fan.

- The roof can **open or close in under 10 minutes**, among the fastest in MLB
- The **fan-shaped panel design** is unique in the sport
- The team moves it about **230 times a year**, one of the highest counts in baseball

## American Family Field vs Other MLB Retractable Roofs

American Family Field is one of the more open roofs in the league, near the top alongside Toronto's [Rogers Centre](/stories/rogers-centre-roof-open-blue-jays-2026). The Brewers sit far above the hot-climate parks like the [Houston Astros](/stories/daikin-park-roof-open-astros-2026) at Daikin Park and the [Texas Rangers](/stories/rangers-roof-open-globe-life-field-2026) at Globe Life Field, which keep their roofs shut for most of the year.

The fan-shaped design also makes it the most visually distinctive roof in baseball. When those panels swing open on a warm Milwaukee night, it is one of the best looks in the sport.

## The Bottom Line on the American Family Field Roof

The Brewers will give you open-air baseball whenever the weather cooperates, which is a lot of the summer. Expect the roof closed in the cold of April and on rainy days, and open on warm, dry evenings. For any specific game, call the roof hotline at (414) 902-4636 or check the official Roof Status page, since the Brewers move this roof more than almost anyone.
