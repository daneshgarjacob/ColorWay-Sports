The New York Knicks are NBA champions. For the first time since 1973 — the third title in franchise history, after 1970 and 1973 — the Knicks closed out the San Antonio Spurs 4-1, clinching in Game 5 behind a 45-point Jalen Brunson masterpiece and an OG Anunoby dagger to erase a 16-point hole. And within minutes of the final buzzer, the official 2026 NBA Champions locker room gear was on the players' backs and heads for the trophy presentation: the New Era 9FIFTY snapback and the Nike trophy tee.

We graded the Knicks' [Eastern Conference Champions locker room set](/stories/knicks-2026-eastern-conference-champions-hat-shirt-graded) a few weeks ago — hat A-, shirt B-, with a long complaint about the recycled gray template tee. The championship set is a different story. One of these two pieces is the best championship locker room item we have seen in years. Here is our grade on both.

## New York Knicks 2026 NBA Champions Locker Room Hat

<div style="margin: 2em 0; text-align: center;">
  <img src="/images/posts/knicks-finals-gear/knicks-2026-nba-champions-hat.avif" alt="New York Knicks 2026 NBA Champions locker room New Era 9FIFTY snapback hat in white with a black brim, a gold-trimmed NBA Champions 2026 shield badge with the Knicks roundel on the front, a gold Larry O'Brien Trophy embroidered on the left side, and gold New Era branding" style="max-width: 100%; border-radius: 12px;" />
</div>

The championship hat is a clear step up from the all-black conference cap. New Era went with a white crown and a black flat brim this time, a sharper two-tone base than the default all-black locker room template, and dressed it in gold: a gold-trimmed "NBA CHAMPIONS 2026" shield badge with the Knicks roundel on the front, a gold Larry O'Brien Trophy embroidered on the left side, gold New Era branding on the right, and gold contrast stitching tucked under the brim. The white-black-gold combination reads clean and genuinely champagne-celebration premium, and the little side trophy is a great touch.

The reason it stops at A- and not higher is that it still plays it a touch safe. The front is the same shield-badge layout the league hands every champion, just executed in nicer colors, and for a Knicks team this is the spot to go all the way with Knicks orange and blue rather than letting gold-on-white do most of the talking. It is a very good hat that could have been a more fun, more distinctly New York hat. We wanted a little more creativity to match the moment.

## New York Knicks 2026 NBA Champions T-Shirt

<div style="margin: 2em 0; text-align: center;">

This is the one. Finally. After years of complaining about the heather-gray destination tee every champion gets recycled — the same complaint we made about the Knicks' own conference shirt — Nike actually made a championship shirt with a point of view, and it is a stunner. The base is black, not gray. The "NBA CHAMPIONS 2026" and "NEW YORK KNICKS" lettering is set in a distressed, vintage gothic blackletter that reads like a 1990s concert tour tee, all weathered texture and attitude. The NBA Finals logo and the Knicks roundel flank the centerpiece, and a small gold Nike swoosh sits up top.

But the centerpiece is the centerpiece. The Larry O'Brien Trophy is rendered in a shimmering, almost bedazzled metallic gold that glows against the black and distressed gray around it — the single best design choice on any piece of championship gear this year. It catches the light, it pops on camera, and it makes the whole shirt feel special instead of generic. This is the rare locker room tee you would actually want to wear out, not just collect. It is unique, it is cool, and it is exactly the kind of swing a 53-years-in-the-making championship deserved. Easy A+, and the best championship locker room shirt we have graded.

## Final Take

The Knicks' 2026 NBA Champions locker room set flips the script on our usual complaint. The hat is very good — a sharp white-black-gold New Era 9FIFTY that earns its A- and only misses more because it plays the shield template a touch safe. But the shirt is the triumph: a black, gold-trophy, vintage-gothic Nike tee that finally breaks the recycled-gray mold and lands as the best championship locker room shirt in years. After a 53-year wait, the Knicks got gear worthy of the moment — and the shirt, especially, is one we will be pointing to as the bar for the next champion.
