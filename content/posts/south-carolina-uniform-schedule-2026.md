Then came Mississippi State at Williams-Brice Stadium on September 19, where the Gamecocks went garnet head to toe. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

This is South Carolina's first season with Nike after Under Armour dressed the Gamecocks from 2007 through June 30, 2026, and the athletic department said up front that the changes would be subtle. The stripe pattern and number font carried over almost unchanged. The real differences are in the details: the center stripe on the helmets is thicker, the garnet helmet dropped the small Block C inside a white circle for a large Block C on each side, the SEC patch moved from above the chest wordmark into the collar, and the Block C and maker's logo swapped hips on the pants. The black helmet is the biggest visual change, a gloss finish for the first time since 2004 after most of a decade in matte. Nike has also committed to a full redesign of at least three jerseys by 2030 and an alternate through its program for more than 15 top college football schools, but no 2026 alternate beyond the black set has been shown. SEC home teams usually wear color, so road dates default to white unless the host goes white first.

**September 5 vs. Kent State.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **white helmet, garnet jersey, white pants**. The helmet carried the Block C with the thicker garnet and black center stripe, and the pants had the matching garnet and black side stripe. It was the first game in Nike gear, a 12:45 p.m. Eastern kickoff at Williams-Brice Stadium, and South Carolina won 57-0.

**September 12 vs. Towson.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **glossy black helmet with Cocky on both sides, black jersey, white pants**. The black jersey carried GAMECOCKS across the chest with garnet and white sleeve stripes. The Cocky decal honored the mascot's induction into the Mascot Hall of Fame, and beat reporter Wes Mitchell reported it was a one-night helmet, not a new piece of the rotation. So the new gloss shell debuted with a logo it will not wear again. South Carolina won 45-9 in the 7 p.m. Eastern kickoff.

**September 19 vs. Mississippi State.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **garnet helmet, garnet jersey, garnet pants**, head to toe garnet for the SEC opener at Williams-Brice. We had garnet down for the jersey and white for the pants, and the Gamecocks went garnet below the belt too. Mississippi State came in with the maroon helmet over a white jersey and white pants, and won 41-34 after South Carolina had led 13-0. More: [Mississippi State uniform schedule](/stories/mississippi-state-uniform-schedule-2026).

**September 26 at Alabama.** White expected. Alabama wears crimson at home, so South Carolina goes white at Bryant-Denny Stadium for a 7 p.m. Eastern kickoff. The Tide side is on our [Alabama 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/alabama-uniform-schedule-2026).

**October 3 vs. Kentucky.** Garnet expected. We have [Kentucky](/stories/kentucky-uniform-schedule-2026) in white for the trip to Columbia.

**October 10 at Florida.** Garnet expected. This is the one road game where the host has already told us its look: Florida's released grid has the Gators in a retro helmet, white jersey and white pants, which leaves the visitor in color. Details are on our [Florida 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/florida-uniform-schedule-2026).

**October 17.** Bye.

**October 24 vs. Tennessee.** Garnet expected. First game back from the bye, and a rivalry date where the black set would make sense if the program wants a night to feel different. The Vols' side is on our [Tennessee 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/tennessee-uniform-schedule-2026).

**October 31 at Oklahoma.** White expected. Halloween in Norman, with [Oklahoma](/stories/oklahoma-uniform-schedule-2026) expected in crimson.

**November 7 vs. Texas A&amp;M.** Garnet expected. [Texas A&amp;M](/stories/texas-am-uniform-schedule-2026) should be in white.

**November 14 at Arkansas.** White expected. Road date in Fayetteville, where the home side wears color.

**November 21 vs. Georgia.** Garnet expected. The last home game and the biggest home date on the calendar, so this is the one we are watching for an all-black return. [Georgia](/stories/georgia-uniform-schedule-2026) is expected in white.

**November 28 at Clemson.** White expected. The Palmetto Bowl in Clemson, with the Tigers in orange at home per our [Clemson 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/clemson-uniform-schedule-2026).

## The Bottom Line

Two games into the Nike era, South Carolina has shown two very different looks: the white helmet and garnet jersey for the opener, then black jersey and a gloss black Cocky helmet for the Towson night game. The garnet helmet with the new large Block C has not appeared yet, and neither has the full all-black set with black pants, so both are still in play for the SEC schedule. Garnet is the home default, white is the road default, and the trip to Gainesville flips to garnet because Florida is wearing white. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is South Carolina wearing this week?**

South Carolina wore all garnet against Mississippi State on Saturday, September 19: garnet helmet, garnet jersey and garnet pants for the SEC opener, confirmed from the broadcast, in a 41-34 loss. Last week against Towson the Gamecocks wore a glossy black helmet with Cocky on the sides, black jersey and white pants. The grid above lists every game, and we update it when each combination is confirmed.

**What are South Carolina's football uniforms for 2026?**

Three jerseys from Nike in the program's first season with the brand: a garnet home jersey, a white road jersey and a black alternate with GAMECOCKS across the chest, all with Northwestern-style sleeve stripes. Pants come in white, garnet and black. The helmets are a white shell with the Block C and a garnet and black center stripe, a garnet shell with a large Block C, and a glossy black shell.

**Does South Carolina have a new alternate uniform in 2026?**

The black set is updated rather than new: the helmet moved from matte to gloss, the first gloss black shell since 2004. The only true one-off so far was the Cocky helmet against Towson for the Mascot Hall of Fame induction. Nike's contract includes an alternate through its program for top college teams, but none has been revealed for 2026.

**What did South Carolina wear against Towson?**

A glossy black helmet with the Cocky mascot logo on both sides instead of the Block C, a black jersey and white pants, in a 45-9 win on September 12. The helmet celebrated Cocky's induction into the Mascot Hall of Fame and was meant for that night only.

**What will South Carolina wear at Florida?**

Garnet is expected on October 10. Florida released its full 2026 uniform schedule and will wear a retro helmet with a white jersey and white pants against South Carolina, so the Gamecocks should be in color at Ben Hill Griffin Stadium.

**How many games does South Carolina play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven at Williams-Brice Stadium and five on the road, with a bye on October 17. Nine are SEC games, and the three non-conference games are Kent State, Towson and the rivalry trip to Clemson. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
