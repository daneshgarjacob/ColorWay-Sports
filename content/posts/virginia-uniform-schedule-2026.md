Virginia has three jersey colors, two helmets and a staff that treats every Saturday as a fresh decision. This is the full Virginia 2026 uniform schedule: the orange home set, the all navy look, the white road jersey, the helmet rotation, and a week by week outlook for all twelve games, starting with a Duke's Mayo Classic trip to Charlotte in a combination straight out of the 1980s. We update this page as each uniform is confirmed.

The current Nike package is an update rather than a rebuild. Virginia rolled out enhanced uniforms in July 2024, and head coach Tony Elliott framed the shoulder stripes as the piece that ties the whole thing to the program's history, which is a polite way of saying the Cavaliers are not interested in chasing trends. The two helmets came first, in 2022, when Virginia went back to a clean navy shell with the V-Sabre on both sides and a white shell with an orange and blue center stripe.

What that leaves is a wardrobe with real range. Orange, navy and white jerseys, orange, navy and white pants, and two helmets add up to a lot of Saturdays that look different from each other. ACC home teams wear color and visitors wear white, so every road date on this schedule points to the white jersey and every home date is a question of which color and which helmet. Virginia has already used two completely different home looks in two games.

Virginia won 34-8 and the orange on orange read as loud as the theme suggested. NC State countered with its red helmet, white jersey and red pants, and the smaller Tuffy decal on that red shell was the talking point of the night. **September 5.** Bye. Virginia opened in Week 0, so the first off week landed before most of the country had played a game.

**September 11 vs. Norfolk State.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **navy helmet, navy jersey, navy pants**, the full monochrome look, in a 59-3 win. This was the rarest thing in the wardrobe. Outside of throwbacks, Virginia had not gone navy on navy on navy in 24 games, the last time being against Maryland in September 2024. The date is worth noting too, because this was originally a Saturday game and moved up to Friday night.

## The Bottom Line

Two games in, Virginia has already worn two home looks that share nothing but a logo: orange over orange under the white helmet, then navy head to toe. Week 3 makes it three in a row, and the Charlotte combination is the most interesting of the group because it is the oldest, a white helmet and white pants around an orange jersey that the program wore in its 1980s rise. The road games are settled by ACC rules, so the white jersey is locked in at Florida State, SMU, Wake Forest and Virginia Tech. Everything left to decide happens at Scott Stadium, and the Friday night game against Duke on October 23 is the date to circle. The grid above has every game, and we add the star as each uniform is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Virginia wearing this week?**

Virginia announced a white helmet, orange jersey and white pants for the Duke's Mayo Classic against West Virginia on Saturday, September 19, at Bank of America Stadium in Charlotte, kicking off at 7:30 p.m. Eastern on ACC Network. The Cavaliers are the designated home team, which is why they are in color. It is a combination the program wore regularly in the 1980s and had not used in its previous nine games.

**What are Virginia's football uniforms for 2026?**

Three jerseys and two helmets. The orange home jersey and the navy home jersey both carry white numerals and a shoulder stripe in the other two colors, and the white road jersey uses navy numerals with the same striping. Pants come in orange, navy and white. The helmets are a navy shell with the V-Sabre on both sides and a white shell with an orange and navy center stripe, both introduced in 2022 and carried through the Nike update in 2024.

**What did Virginia wear against NC State and Norfolk State?**

Against NC State on August 29 the Cavaliers wore the white helmet with the orange jersey and orange pants for the Paint the Town Orange opener, winning 34-8. Against Norfolk State on Friday, September 11, they went navy helmet, navy jersey and navy pants in a 59-3 win, the first time outside of throwbacks that Virginia had worn all navy in 24 games.

**Does Virginia have a new alternate uniform in 2026?**

Nothing has been announced. Virginia wore 1995 throwbacks for a Friday night game against Florida State last season to mark the anniversary of the goal line stand, so the program clearly keeps a special set in reserve. The Friday night game against Duke on October 23 and Senior Day against North Carolina on November 21 are the two dates that fit the pattern. We will add anything new to this page the moment it is revealed.

**How many games does Virginia play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven at Scott Stadium, four on the road at Florida State, SMU, Wake Forest and Virginia Tech, and one neutral site game against West Virginia in Charlotte, with byes on September 5 and November 7. The full schedule with every expected uniform is in the grid above.
