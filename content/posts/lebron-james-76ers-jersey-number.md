## LeBron's 76ers Jersey Number Is 23

LeBron will wear **No. 23** for the 76ers, the number that has defined most of his career. He originally chose 23 as a rookie in Cleveland in honor of Michael Jordan, and it is the number he has worn for the majority of his 23-plus NBA seasons. For a signing this big, the Sixers get to hang the most recognizable number in the sport in their arena, and fans get a LeBron No. 23 Philadelphia jersey to line up for.

## Why LeBron Can't Wear No. 6 in Philadelphia

Here is the wrinkle that made this simple. LeBron has worn only two numbers in his entire NBA career: **23 and 6**. The 6 is off the table now for everyone, because the NBA **retired No. 6 league-wide in honor of Bill Russell** in 2022. On top of that, the 76ers have their own **No. 6 retired for Julius Erving**, the franchise icon known as Dr. J. So even setting the league-wide honor aside, 6 was never going to hang in Philadelphia. With his only two career numbers narrowed to one, 23 was the natural and only choice.

## LeBron's Jersey Number History

LeBron's number story is short, which is part of what makes it iconic. He wore **23** through his first Cleveland run, chosen to honor Jordan. He switched to **6** during his four years in Miami, then went back to **23** on his return to Cleveland and into his first stretch with the Lakers. He wore **6** again in a later Lakers stretch before returning to **23**. That is the whole list, just two numbers across a 20-plus-year career, and now the more famous of the two lands in Philadelphia.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What number does LeBron James wear on the 76ers?**
LeBron James wears No. 23 for the Philadelphia 76ers. It is the number he wore for most of his career, originally chosen in Cleveland to honor Michael Jordan.

**Why doesn't LeBron wear No. 6 with the 76ers?**
No. 6 is retired league-wide across the NBA in honor of Bill Russell, so no new player can wear it. The 76ers also have No. 6 retired for franchise legend Julius Erving. Since 23 and 6 are the only numbers LeBron has ever worn, 23 was the natural choice.

**What numbers has LeBron James worn in his career?**
Only two: No. 23 and No. 6. He wore 23 in Cleveland and with the Lakers, and 6 during his Miami years and a later Lakers stretch, before returning to 23.

**Why did LeBron originally pick No. 23?**
He chose 23 as a rookie with the Cleveland Cavaliers in honor of Michael Jordan, who wore 23 for most of his career with the Chicago Bulls.

**Is No. 23 retired by the 76ers?**
No. The 76ers have not retired No. 23, which is why LeBron was able to take his signature number in Philadelphia without a conflict.
