Wondering what jersey Baylor is wearing this week? This is the full Baylor 2026 uniform schedule, game by game, and it has one date circled: **the gold chrome throwback comes out Saturday, September 26, against Colorado at McLane Stadium** for the Traditions Game.

## The Throwback Is the Headline

Baylor announced the Colorado set with one word, "Timeless," and it earns it. Green jersey, white pants, and the gold chrome helmet, which is the piece doing all the work.

The details worth knowing:

- **A gold chrome shell** with a green center stripe flanked by white, and clusters of green stars running along both sides of the stripe.
- **The interlocking BU** in green with a white outline on the sides, in the old proportions rather than the modern compressed version.
- **A green facemask**, which is the choice that keeps the helmet from looking like a novelty. Chrome with a chrome mask reads as a costume. Chrome with a color mask reads as a uniform.
- **The green jersey** with gold-outlined white block numbers and a bear paw on the shoulder where a number usually sits.
- **White pants** with a green and gold stripe down the leg.

Chrome helmets are Baylor's own history rather than a borrowed trend. The Bears wore gold chrome from 2013 to 2017, the best stretch the program has ever had, and the shell has been back in the rotation since 2025. Pairing it with a throwback jersey instead of the anthracite alternate is the version fans have been asking for.

### Our Grade: A+

The helmet and the jersey are both right, which almost never happens on a throwback.

We are not usually a green team. Green is one of the harder colors to make look expensive on a football field and most programs that wear it end up looking like a bowling shirt. The gold chrome helmet on top of the green jersey is the combination that solves it. The metal warms the green up instead of fighting it, and suddenly the whole set looks like money rather than like a color scheme somebody was stuck with.

The stars on the helmet stripe are the detail we keep coming back to. They are small, they are green on gold so they read at distance without shouting, and they give the shell texture that a plain stripe would not. That is a hard thing to pull off on chrome, which usually flattens everything into glare.

White pants under a green jersey is the correct order of operations too. Gold pants would have tipped the whole thing into costume territory. The green facemask does the same job at the other end, keeping a chrome shell from reading as a novelty.

Baylor spends too much of the season in black and anthracite, which any team in the country can wear. This is the one that is only Baylor. It is our favorite college uniform of the offseason.

## How Baylor's Uniform Rotation Actually Works

Baylor does not publish a season-long uniform schedule, and under the current staff the Bears have leaned hard into announcing each week's combination a few days out. What is different in 2026 is that fans get a say: the athletic department's "Fan First Pic 'Em" program let fans submit and then vote on the combination for 254 Night against Prairie View A&M.

The reliable rules:

- **Home games at McLane:** green, white, black or anthracite jerseys, decided week to week.
- **Road games:** white jerseys, per NCAA visitor rules.
- **September 26 vs. Colorado:** the confirmed gold chrome throwback.
- **September 12 vs. Prairie View A&M:** the fan-voted 254 Night combination, built around the green 254 helmet with the area code on the front bumper.

We update this page as each week's combination is announced, so the grid below always reflects the latest confirmed look.

**What jersey is Baylor wearing this week?**

**When does Baylor wear the gold chrome throwback uniform in 2026?**

Saturday, September 26, at home against Colorado for the Traditions Game. Green jersey, white pants, gold chrome helmet.

**What is Baylor's 254 Night uniform?**

It is the combination fans voted on through Baylor's "Fan First Pic 'Em" program, worn September 12 against Prairie View A&M. It is built around the green 254 helmet, which carries the 254 area code on the front bumper.

No. Outside the confirmed throwback date, Baylor announces combinations game by game. This page logs each one as it is announced.

**Who makes Baylor's uniforms?**

Nike. The gold chrome shells are Riddell.
