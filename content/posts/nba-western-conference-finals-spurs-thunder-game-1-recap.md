## What the Spurs Game 1 Win Changes

San Antonio came into the series as the two-seed against the one-seed Thunder and stole home court advantage in the opener. Oklahoma City swept the Lakers in Round 2 and entered the series rested, while the Spurs had to grind out a six-game series against the Wolves with Game 6 at Target Center just last Friday. The Spurs winning on a short rest is the bigger story than the score.

The Thunder still own the next game in the building. Win Game 2 in OKC and the series flips back to even with the next two at Frost Bank Center. Lose Game 2 and the Spurs head home up 2-0 with a chance to close out the conference at home in Games 3 and 4.
