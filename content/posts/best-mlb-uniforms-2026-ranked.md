We have been grading MLB uniforms one team at a time, jersey by jersey, and this is where the best of them all meet. Below is our league-wide ranking of the finest uniforms in baseball for 2026, pulled straight from those team-by-team grades so the order is consistent rather than a gut reaction.

One note up front: we are working through the league in order, so this list covers every team we have fully graded so far and grows as we add more. If your favorite is not here yet, its ranking is coming. For now, here are the best uniforms in the sport, counting down to number one.

## 15. White Sox Home White Pinstripes — A-

Black pinstripes with the Old English SOX, one of the most underrated home sets in the league. Severe, classic, and completely sure of itself. Full breakdown in our [White Sox jersey rankings](/stories/white-sox-jerseys-2026-ranked).

## 14. Dodgers Alternate Road Gray — A-

The "Los Angeles" script gray, once the primary road look and now the alternate. A wonderful road jersey that only ranks this low because the "Dodgers" version above it is even better.

## 13. Phillies Powder Blue — A-

Retro powder blue done right, warm and nostalgic without tipping into costume. One of the best of the modern powder-blue revivals. See the full [Phillies jersey rankings](/stories/phillies-jerseys-2026-ranked).

## 12. Braves Home White — A-

The navy "Braves" script trimmed in red over the tomahawk, essentially unchanged since 1987 because there has never been a reason to touch it. One of the cleanest chest marks in the sport.

## 11. Padres Camouflage — A-

A decades-long tribute to San Diego's military community that never feels like a gimmick, because in that city it is not one. Handsome and heartfelt. More in our [Padres jersey rankings](/stories/padres-jerseys-2026-ranked).

## 10. White Sox Black Alternate — A

The best thing the White Sox wear. Black with the white Old English SOX is menace in uniform form, and it photographs like a million bucks under the lights.

## 9. Red Sox Road Gray — A

Red script with a navy outline on gray, the same trick the Phillies use, and it works for the same reason. A road gray with genuine warmth to it.

## 8. Red Sox Home White — A

The hanging-socks logo, the red trim, Fenway in the afternoon. About as comfortable and correct as a home white gets.

## 7. Dodgers Road Gray — A

The "Dodgers" script in royal blue with the red number, one of the two or three best road grays in baseball. Timeless in the literal sense. It anchors our [Dodgers jersey rankings](/stories/dodgers-jerseys-2026-ranked).

## 6. Yankees Home Pinstripes — A

The most iconic uniform in American sports, and a masterclass in subtraction: no name, no wordmark, just pinstripes and the interlocking NY. It ranks this high on pure design; the discipline behind it is its own story in our [Yankees jersey rankings](/stories/yankees-jerseys-2026-ranked).

## 5. Padres City Connect — A

The navy Dia de los Muertos set, and one of the best City Connects Nike has made for anyone. Premium navy with orange and cream accents that pop harder than anything else in the Padres closet. We would not change a thing.

## 4. Red Sox Red Alternate — A+

The rare alternate that improves on the home look. Boston's red with the navy script is bright, warm, and unmistakably Fenway. Our top-graded Red Sox jersey, and a deserving A+. Full list in the [Red Sox jersey rankings](/stories/red-sox-jerseys-2026-ranked).

## 3. Phillies Cream Alternate — A+

Quite possibly the prettiest jersey in baseball. The cream is warm enough that nobody could mistake it for the home whites, the maroon script glows against it, and it is the look plenty of people would name first if you asked for the best in the sport.

## 2. Braves Powder Blue City Connect — A+

The best City Connect the program has produced, and it is not close. Atlanta reached into its own 1980s TBS-era powder blues instead of inventing a gimmick, built it as a V-neck pullover, and made the single best uniform the franchise owns. The rare City Connect that is not a marketing exercise. See how it topped the [Braves jersey rankings](/stories/braves-jerseys-2026-ranked).

## 1. Dodgers Home White — A+

The best jersey in baseball, and in our view it is not especially close. Clean white, the royal blue "Dodgers" script, and the red number on the front that almost nobody else in the sport uses. It is timeless, it is perfect, and it is the standard every other uniform on this list is measured against. When your home white is this good, you do not need to do anything else, and Los Angeles has had the discipline to leave it alone for decades.

## How We Built This Ranking

Every uniform here comes from our team-by-team jersey rankings, where we grade each of a club's looks worst to first. This master list simply gathers the highest grades across every team we have covered and stacks them against each other, so a jersey's place here matches the grade it earned in its own team's post. We are ranking the league one team at a time, so this list will keep growing. Teams graded so far: the [Dodgers](/stories/dodgers-jerseys-2026-ranked), [Braves](/stories/braves-jerseys-2026-ranked), [Phillies](/stories/phillies-jerseys-2026-ranked), [Red Sox](/stories/red-sox-jerseys-2026-ranked), [White Sox](/stories/white-sox-jerseys-2026-ranked), [Yankees](/stories/yankees-jerseys-2026-ranked), and [Padres](/stories/padres-jerseys-2026-ranked).

## The Bottom Line

The top of baseball's uniform hierarchy is split between two philosophies. There is the timeless camp, led by the Dodgers' perfect home whites and the Yankees' pinstripes, uniforms that win by never changing. And there is the modern camp, led by the Braves' powder blue City Connect and the Phillies' cream, uniforms that win by reaching into a team's own history and pulling out something better than what it wears every day. The Dodgers sit at number one because they do the hardest thing in design, which is to be perfect and know better than to touch it.
