We spent this season grading every uniform in Major League Baseball one team at a time, jersey by jersey, worst to first. That work is finished. All 30 teams, 143 jerseys, every one of them carrying a letter grade we had to defend in its own post.

This is where the best of them meet. The order below is pulled straight from those team-by-team grades rather than assembled from memory, which means a few results surprised us and we left them alone anyway. Only 26 jerseys in the entire sport earned an A or better, and that is the list. We are counting down from 26 to the single best uniform in baseball.

## The 26 Best Uniforms in Baseball, Counting Down

Every jersey below graded an A or an A+ in its own team ranking. Nothing here was promoted or demoted to make the list flow better. Where two jerseys share a grade, we broke the tie the same way we would if they were hanging next to each other in a clubhouse.

The "Nationals" script looks stranded on a white shirt, but on a navy base it finally has something to push against, and the red-and-white lettering glows under the lights. Same wordmark, right base, completely different jersey. Full list in our [Nationals jersey rankings](/stories/nationals-jerseys-2026-ranked).

## 25. Blue Jays Royal-Blue Alternate

Most clubs treat a colored alternate as a treat. Toronto made theirs the house style, and it earns the promotion.

## 24. Cardinals Cream Alternate

<div style="display: flex; align-items: center; gap: 12px; margin: 0.5em 0 1.1em;"><span style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #C41E3A, #0C2340); color: #ffffff; font-size: 1.1em; font-weight: 900; letter-spacing: -0.5px; padding: 7px 0; border-radius: 9px; min-width: 62px; text-align: center;">A</span><span style="font-size: 0.72em; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 2px; color: #6b7180;">ColorWay Sports Grade</span></div>

Reserved for Saturday home games, and it reads completely differently in natural daylight than the home white does, which is exactly why one designated day a week works.

## 23. Brewers Powder Blue Road

<div style="display: flex; align-items: center; gap: 12px; margin: 0.5em 0 1.1em;"><span style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #12284B, #FFC52F); color: #ffffff; font-size: 1.1em; font-weight: 900; letter-spacing: -0.5px; padding: 7px 0; border-radius: 9px; min-width: 62px; text-align: center;">A</span><span style="font-size: 0.72em; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 2px; color: #6b7180;">ColorWay Sports Grade</span></div>

<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/Brewers/status/2083321073433563381"></a></blockquote>

The big move of 2026. Milwaukee retired the road grays outright and made powder blue the primary road uniform, reviving what the franchise wore from 1970 into the mid-1980s. Navy block "Milwaukee" outlined in gold, and the navy cap with the yellow front panel and ball-in-glove crest is the piece that seals it. It is also the only genuinely great uniform the Brewers own, which is its own story in our [Brewers jersey rankings](/stories/brewers-jerseys-2026-ranked).

## 22. Mariners City Connect (Rush-Blue)

<div style="display: flex; align-items: center; gap: 12px; margin: 0.5em 0 1.1em;"><span style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #0C2C56, #005C5C); color: #ffffff; font-size: 1.1em; font-weight: 900; letter-spacing: -0.5px; padding: 7px 0; border-radius: 9px; min-width: 62px; text-align: center;">A</span><span style="font-size: 0.72em; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 2px; color: #6b7180;">ColorWay Sports Grade</span></div>

One of only two City Connects in the entire program we grade this high. Seattle scrapped the royal-and-black version for a rush-blue kit stuffed with real history: gold "Seattle" lettering from the 1969 Pilots, a drop shadow nodding to the 1955 Rainiers, black pants for the 1940s Steelheads, and the old trident on the cap. That is a lot of references, and the reason it works is that none of them fight.

This one is all about the hat. The "Orioles" script in orange and black, paired with the white-front cap topped by the smiling cartoon Oriole Bird, one of the most beloved marks in sports. The clean white lets the script and that cap do the work, and it never gets old. There is nothing here we would touch.

## 18. Mets Home White Pinstripes

<div style="display: flex; align-items: center; gap: 12px; margin: 0.5em 0 1.1em;"><span style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #002D72, #FF5910); color: #ffffff; font-size: 1.1em; font-weight: 900; letter-spacing: -0.5px; padding: 7px 0; border-radius: 9px; min-width: 62px; text-align: center;">A</span><span style="font-size: 0.72em; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 2px; color: #6b7180;">ColorWay Sports Grade</span></div>

One of the most recognizable home uniforms in the National League, and the Mets are smart to leave it almost entirely alone. Blue pinstripes, the gently angled blue "Mets" script, orange trim, and the cap with the interlocking orange NY. It is the correct, classic, cannot-mess-it-up look. We like it a lot without quite loving it, which is the only thing keeping it out of the very top.

## 17. Yankees Home Pinstripes

<div style="display: flex; align-items: center; gap: 12px; margin: 0.5em 0 1.1em;"><span style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #0C2340, #C4CED3); color: #ffffff; font-size: 1.1em; font-weight: 900; letter-spacing: -0.5px; padding: 7px 0; border-radius: 9px; min-width: 62px; text-align: center;">A</span><span style="font-size: 0.72em; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 2px; color: #6b7180;">ColorWay Sports Grade</span></div>

<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/Yankees/status/2080117133472657915"></a></blockquote>

The most iconic uniform in American sports, and it earns the word. No player name, no chest wordmark, no secondary logo, just pinstripes and the interlocking NY over the heart. The genius is subtraction: almost every design decision the Yankees have made in a century has been to leave something off. It lands seventeenth because we are grading jerseys, not legacies, and sixteen of them are simply better looking. The discipline behind it is its own story in our [Yankees jersey rankings](/stories/yankees-jerseys-2026-ranked).

One of the best road uniforms in baseball and not particularly close. Same royal script the team has run for generations, same red number underneath, same clean gray. ## What 143 Grades Actually Told Us

Grading a whole league at once surfaces things you cannot see one team at a time.

**Great uniforms are rarer than they feel.** Out of 143 jerseys, 26 earned an A or better. That is roughly one in six. Most of what teams wear on a given night is perfectly fine and completely forgettable, and the honest middle of this league is a B.

**The City Connect program is the single biggest drag on baseball's wardrobe.** The 29 City Connects we graded average out to a C. Everything else in the sport averages a B+. That gap is not close, and it is the clearest finding in the whole dataset. Nine jerseys graded D+ or worse, and eight of the nine are City Connects. Only two City Connects cracked the A tier at all: the Braves' powder blue and the Mariners' rush-blue, and both did it the same way, by reaching into the team's real history instead of inventing a theme. We ranked the program against itself in our [City Connect rankings](/stories/mlb-city-connect-2026-official-ranking), where a good City Connect can still be a mediocre jersey.

**Two jerseys failed outright.** The Blue Jays' "Night Mode" City Connect and the White Sox' red City Connect are the only F grades in the series.

**The Red Sox have the strongest closet in baseball.** Three of Boston's five uniforms graded A or better, more than any other club. The Mariners have the best closet by average grade, helped by having no true weak link. At the other end, the Reds are the only team in the league without a single jersey above a B.

**The best new idea of 2026 was old.** Nearly every uniform that graded well this year is a revival: Detroit's orange and navy off the 1984 template, Milwaukee's powder blue road set from 1970, Atlanta's TBS-era powder blue, the Angels' California-era V-neck, Miami's 1993 teal. The teams that went looking through their own closets beat the teams that went looking for a concept, every single time.

## The Bottom Line

The top of baseball's uniform hierarchy splits into two camps. There is the timeless group, led by the Dodgers' home whites and the Cubs' pinstripes and the Yankees' interlocking NY, uniforms that win by never changing and by having the discipline not to. And there is the revival group, led by the Braves' powder blue and the Phillies' cream and Detroit's orange, uniforms that win by digging into a team's own history and pulling out something better than what it wears every day.

What almost nothing at the top has in common is invention. Of the 26 uniforms on this list, we count exactly zero that were designed from scratch as a modern concept. That is the whole lesson of grading 143 jerseys. Baseball is a sport with a century of good ideas already sitting in storage, and the teams that look there keep winning.

The Dodgers sit at number one because they do the hardest thing in design, which is to be perfect and know better than to touch it.
