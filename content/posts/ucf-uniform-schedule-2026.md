UCF has three helmets, two jerseys, two pants and a Thursday tradition of telling everyone exactly which ones are coming. This is the full UCF 2026 uniform schedule: the black home set the Knights call Knightmode, the all-white Lightmode road look, the Space Game alternate that lands on Halloween weekend, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

The current jerseys arrived in June 2025, and the number font is the reason to care. Nike calls it Knighted Eras, and it stitches three UCF periods together: the drop shadow from the 1998 Daunte Culpepper season, the notched shapes from the Blake Bortles years, and the Knighted font UCF brought into the Big 12 in 2023. The gold drop shadow is the Culpepper nod. Everything else in the closet is modular, which is the point. Equipment manager Brad Anderson has put the number of possible combinations at 75, the staff maps the season out in the summer, the exact combo gets locked the Monday of game week, and the reveal goes up on social on Thursday. That Thursday post is why this page exists.

**September 3 vs. Bethune-Cookman.** ★ Confirmed: **gold helmet, black jersey, black pants**, the Thursday night opener at the Acrisure Bounce House. The gold shell with the stacked UCF in black is the loudest helmet in the closet and UCF used it first. The Knights won 73-6.

**September 12 at Pittsburgh.** ★ Confirmed: **white helmet, white jersey, white pants**, the full Lightmode set in the rain at Acrisure Stadium against a Pitt team in blue. Lightmode exists because the Knightmode reaction was so strong that the staff went looking for a road version, and the reveal pulled roughly half a million views when it first ran. This one did not go UCF's way. Pitt won 12-7, the Knights' only loss so far.

**September 19 vs. Georgia State.** Knightmode, announced. UCF teased it Wednesday night with a single frame of a black helmet on a throne of swords.

**September 26 vs. TCU.** Black expected. The Big 12 home opener at 3:30 p.m. Eastern on FS1, with TCU in white as the visitor. UCF has three shells to choose from and has already used two, so the helmet is the open question here, not the jersey.

**October 3 at Houston.** White expected. Houston has already told everyone it is wearing its light blue Houston Blue alternate for this one, which makes the visitor's white jersey a lock under Big 12 convention.

**October 10 at Oklahoma State.** White expected, at Boone Pickens Stadium. Oklahoma State has a Stripe the Stadium night planned, so the Knights in white against orange is the likely picture. More: [Oklahoma State schedule](/stories/oklahoma-state-uniform-schedule-2026).

**October 17.** Bye.

**October 24 vs. BYU.** Black expected. Homecoming at the Bounce House against a ranked BYU team. **October 30 vs. Baylor.** Space Game. This is the tenth annual Space Game and the biggest uniform date on the calendar, moved to a Friday night by the Big 12 for the second straight season, 7:30 p.m. Eastern on ESPN. The 2025 version was called HyperSpace, built around a carbon fiber helmet and a center stripe that shifted from Canaveral blue to pink to mimic shock diamonds off a rocket nozzle. Nothing has been revealed for 2026 yet. The Knights also lost their first Space Game ever last year, 30-27 to Houston, so there is some history to reset. More: [Baylor schedule](/stories/baylor-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 7 at Kansas.** White expected. Kansas wears blue at home, so the Knights go white in Lawrence. More: [Kansas schedule](/stories/kansas-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 14 vs. Arizona State.** Black expected, with Arizona State in white. More: [Arizona State schedule](/stories/arizona-state-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 20 vs. Iowa State.** Black expected. Senior Knight, a Friday night, 6 p.m. Eastern on FS1, and the last home game of the year. **November 28 at Colorado.** White expected. The regular season finale at Folsom Field, where Colorado wears black at home. More: [Colorado schedule](/stories/colorado-uniform-schedule-2026).

## The Bottom Line

Two games in, UCF has already shown two of its three helmets and both jerseys, which is exactly how this program operates. Black at home, white on the road, and a gold shell when the mood calls for it. The one date that matters most is October 30, when the tenth Space Game arrives on a Friday night and the Knights unveil whatever Nike has built for it. Everything else gets decided on a Monday and announced on a Thursday, and this grid gets a star the moment we see the uniform on the field.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is UCF wearing this week?**

UCF is wearing Knightmode against Georgia State on Saturday, September 19: black helmet, black jersey and black pants, announced by the team on Thursday. It is Family Weekend at the Acrisure Bounce House with a 7 p.m. Eastern kickoff, and Georgia State will be in white.

**What are UCF's football uniforms for 2026?**

A black jersey with white numbers carrying a gold drop shadow and a white jersey with gold numbers, plus black and white pants with three Polaris stars down each side. Three helmets go with them: gold, black and white, each with the stacked UCF logo on the sides and a sword-shaped center stripe. The set is from Nike and dates to the June 2025 redesign.

**What is Knightmode?**

Knightmode is UCF's all-black look: black helmet, black jersey and black pants. The all-white version is called Lightmode, and UCF built it as the road answer after Knightmode became a fan favorite. UCF wore Lightmode at Pittsburgh on September 12 and wears Knightmode against Georgia State on September 19.

**When is the UCF Space Game in 2026?**

Friday, October 30 against Baylor at 7:30 p.m. Eastern on ESPN. It is the tenth annual Space Game, and the Big 12 moved it off Saturday for the second year in a row. The 2026 alternate uniform has not been revealed. The 2025 edition was the HyperSpace set with a carbon fiber helmet.

**When does UCF announce its uniform each week?**

The staff locks the combination on the Monday of game week and posts the reveal on social media on the Thursday. Equipment manager Brad Anderson has said UCF has around 75 possible combinations to pick from, and the Thursday drop has turned into its own piece of content for the fan base.

**How many games does UCF play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven at the Acrisure Bounce House, five on the road, and a bye on October 17. Two home games are Friday nights, the Space Game against Baylor on October 30 and Senior Knight against Iowa State on November 20. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
