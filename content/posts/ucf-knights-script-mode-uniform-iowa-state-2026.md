**UCF is wearing Knights Script Mode for Senior Knight against Iowa State.** On Thursday, November 19, UCF Equipment posted "Introducing: Knights Script Mode ⬛️⬛️⬛️": a **black helmet with the Knights script in gold**, a **black jersey** and **black pants**. The football account followed with a helmet video captioned "Stormy Knights."

The game is **Friday, November 20, at 6 p.m. Eastern on FS1**, at the Acrisure Bounce House in Orlando. It is UCF's last home game of the season.

**Update, November 20:** Worn as posted, confirmed from the game broadcast: black helmet with the gold Knights script, black jersey, black pants. Iowa State wore its cardinal Cyclones script helmet, a white jersey and cardinal pants. **Final: UCF 48, Iowa State 17.** Alonza Barnett threw four touchdown passes, three of them to Josh Derry, and the win made UCF bowl eligible.

## Quick Answers

- **What is UCF wearing vs. Iowa State?** Black helmet with the gold Knights script, black jersey, black pants.
- **What is "Stormy Knights"?** The caption on UCF Football's helmet video for this game. The equipment staff calls the uniform Knights Script Mode.
- **What is different?** The helmet logo. Every UCF helmet this season has carried the stacked UCF logo. This one has the Knights script.
- **When is the game?** Friday, November 20, 6 p.m. Eastern on FS1, Senior Knight.
- **What is Iowa State wearing?** Confirmed: the cardinal Cyclones script helmet and a white jersey, from the team's "bounce house threads" video. Two script helmets on one field.

## Every Detail Of Knights Script Mode

**The helmet.** A black shell with **Knights** in a gold script across the side, and gold stripes running down the center. The helmet video shows the Big 12 and Monster Energy decal and the Walt Disney World decal on the back.

**The graphic.** UCF framed it as Game 11 with a DRIP U header, the same branding it used for the Homecoming reveal against BYU, sponsored by Orlando Health.

## How It Differs From UCF's Usual Black Helmet

UCF's regular black helmet carries the **stacked UCF logo** in gold, with a gold ORLANDO front bumper. Paired with the black jersey and black pants it is Knightmode, which UCF wore against Georgia State on September 19. Knights Script Mode keeps the black-on-black-on-black and swaps the logo for the script.

The script itself is not brand new. UCF first put the Knights script on a football helmet in 2021, against Bethune-Cookman, on a white shell. What is new is the 2026 version of it, gold script on black, and that it is the first time this season UCF has gone away from the stacked logo.

## UCF's Helmets In 2026

In the games where we have confirmed the helmet, UCF had used three shells this season, all with the stacked UCF logo: **gold** (Bethune-Cookman, TCU, Oklahoma State, BYU, Arizona State), **white** (Pittsburgh, Houston) and **black** (Georgia State), plus the one-off **Mission X** SpaceU helmet for the Space Game against Baylor. Knights Script Mode is the first helmet of the year without the stacked logo on the side. The full season is on the [UCF 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/ucf-uniform-schedule-2026).

## Iowa State's Side

Iowa State posted its look on Thursday afternoon, November 19, as "bounce house threads": the **cardinal helmet with "Cyclones" in white script outlined in gold**, a white and gold center stripe and a **white facemask**, over a **white jersey with IOWA STATE across the chest in cardinal** and cardinal stripes on the sleeves. The video does not show the pants. The Cyclones script helmet is Iowa State's regular helmet, so this is the standard road look, and it means both teams wear a script helmet on Friday night: Knights in gold on black, Cyclones in white on cardinal. Iowa State wore all cardinal with the Cynado helmet against Cincinnati last week.

## Our Take

The script is better than UCF's normal helmet logo, and it should show up more than once a year. The problem is everything around it. This is another all-black uniform, and black and gold are not colors we love on this team. A good helmet on a plain uniform. **Grade: C.**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What uniform is UCF wearing this week?**

Knights Script Mode: a black helmet with the Knights script in gold, a black jersey and black pants, for Senior Knight against Iowa State on Friday, November 20. UCF Equipment posted it on November 19.

**What is the UCF Stormy Knights uniform?**

"Stormy Knights" is the caption UCF Football used on its November 19 helmet video for the Iowa State game. The uniform itself is called Knights Script Mode by UCF's equipment staff: black helmet with the gold Knights script, black jersey, black pants.

**Is the UCF Knights script helmet new?**

The 2026 gold-on-black version is making its first appearance of the season. The Knights script first appeared on a UCF football helmet in 2021, against Bethune-Cookman.

**What time is UCF vs. Iowa State?**

Friday, November 20, 2026, at 6 p.m. Eastern (5 p.m. Central) on FS1, at the Acrisure Bounce House in Orlando.

**Is UCF vs. Iowa State Senior Knight?**

Yes. It is UCF's last home game of the season and its Senior Knight.

**What is Iowa State wearing at UCF?**

The cardinal Cyclones script helmet with a white facemask and a white jersey with IOWA STATE in cardinal, posted by Iowa State on November 19 as "bounce house threads." The pants are not shown in the video. In the game, Iowa State wore cardinal pants.

**What is Knightmode?**

Knightmode is UCF's all-black look with the stacked UCF logo on the black helmet. UCF wore it against Georgia State on September 19. Knights Script Mode is the same black jersey and pants with the script logo on the helmet instead.

**When does UCF announce its uniforms?**

Usually on Thursday of game week. Equipment manager Brad Anderson has said the staff locks the combination on Monday and posts it later in the week.

**Who makes UCF's uniforms?**

Nike. UCF's current set dates to the June 2025 redesign.
