**Only eight NBA teams got a Specter Edition jersey because Nike gave it to the league's oldest franchises.** The eight are the **Sacramento Kings, Detroit Pistons, Boston Celtics, New York Knicks, Golden State Warriors, Atlanta Hawks, Philadelphia 76ers and Los Angeles Lakers**. Nike calls it a collection for "the teams who trace their roots back to the earliest chapters of the league." All eight teams posted official photos of their Specter jerseys on Monday, November 23, and the first four wear them on the court on Sunday, November 29.

The cutoff is easy to see once you line up the founding dates. These eight are **every team still in the league from the first NBA season, 1949-50**. The next-oldest team, the Washington Wizards, did not exist until 1961.

## Quick Answers

- **Why only eight teams?** Nike made the Specter Edition for the NBA's oldest franchises. These eight are the oldest.
- **Which eight?** The Kings, Pistons, Celtics, Knicks, Warriors, Hawks, 76ers and Lakers.
- **How old are they?** The Kings trace back to 1923 and the Pistons to 1937. The other six all started in 1946.
- **What do they have in common?** All eight were in the NBA's first season, 1949-50, after the BAA and NBL merged. The NBA had 17 teams that season, and these are the only eight still around.
- **Why don't the Bulls, Heat or Spurs have one?** They are too young. The Bulls started in 1966, the Spurs in 1967 (in the ABA) and the Heat in 1988.
- **Will more teams get one?** Nike and the NBA have not said. Nothing is announced.
- **When do teams wear it?** Three home games each, 24 games in all, from November 29 to December 29.
- **When does it go on sale?** The Hawks say online on November 30 and in their arena from December 3. The Celtics say "soon" online and in stores.

### Sacramento Kings: 1923

The oldest of the eight. The franchise began as the **Rochester Seagrams**, a semi-pro team in Rochester, New York, in 1923. It was renamed the Rochester Royals and joined the NBL in 1945, won the NBL title in its first season, moved to the BAA in 1948 and became part of the NBA in 1949. It moved to Cincinnati in 1957, to Kansas City (as the Kansas City-Omaha Kings) in 1972 and to Sacramento in 1985. Their Specter jersey is black with a purple glow rising from the bottom, a nod to the purple "Light the Beam" after home wins.

### Detroit Pistons: 1937

Founded in 1937 in Fort Wayne, Indiana, as the **Fort Wayne Zollner Pistons**, a semi-pro team sponsored by Fred Zollner's piston factory. The team turned pro in the NBL in 1941, became the Fort Wayne Pistons and moved to the BAA in 1948, joined the NBA in the 1949 merger and moved to Detroit in 1957. The Specter jersey is black with flames rising up the front and teal trim, which fits the Motor City.

## Where The Line Falls, And Who Just Missed

Nike has never published an official cutoff, a founding year or a rule. It only says these are the teams with roots in "the earliest chapters of the league." What follows is our reading of the list.

The clean way to explain the eight is the **1949 merger**. When the BAA and NBL merged before the 1949-50 season, the new NBA had 17 teams. Nine of them folded or were dropped within a few years. The eight that survived are exactly the eight Specter teams. Nobody else in today's league was there for the first NBA season.

After that, there is a big gap. The NBA did not add a new team that lasted until **1961**, when the Chicago Packers arrived. That team is now the **Washington Wizards**, the ninth-oldest franchise, and it did not get a Specter jersey. So the line sits between the 1946 teams and a 1961 team. There is no close call to argue about.

The Pistons' own September 10 post introducing the jersey opened with "Recognizing our roots," which is the whole idea in three words:

Here is where the famous teams that did not get one fall:

- **Washington Wizards:** 1961, as the Chicago Packers. The closest team to the line, and still 15 years younger than the six 1946 teams.
- **Chicago Bulls:** 1966. Big market, six titles, too young.
- **Houston Rockets and Oklahoma City Thunder:** 1967, as the San Diego Rockets and Seattle SuperSonics.
- **San Antonio Spurs, Indiana Pacers, Brooklyn Nets and Denver Nuggets:** all started in the ABA in 1967 and joined the NBA in 1976.
- **Milwaukee Bucks and Phoenix Suns:** 1968.
- **Miami Heat:** 1988.

Market size, titles and star power had nothing to do with it. The Bulls and Heat are two of the league's biggest draws and they are left out. The Kings and Hawks are not big markets and they are in.

## Will More Teams Get A Specter Edition?

Nobody has said. Nike and the NBA have announced only these eight for 2026-27, and no team outside the eight has said it is getting one. One sneaker site's write-up called them "the first eight teams," which would suggest more are coming. We could not find Nike or the NBA saying that, so treat it as unconfirmed. If the list really is "the oldest franchises," there is no obvious ninth team to add without changing the rule. We will update this page if anything is announced.

## When And Where They Go On Sale

The Hawks' official Specter page says the jerseys and apparel are available **online at the Hawks Shop on November 30** and **in the arena starting December 3**, the night of the Hawks' first Specter game. The Celtics' post says the jerseys are "dropping soon on-court, online and in-store," and the Kings are taking sign-ups for release news at kings.com/Specter. ## Our Take

Picking the oldest teams is a good rule. It is simple, it is easy to explain, and it gives a jersey line a reason to exist beyond selling a fifth shirt. The eight franchises that survived 1949 really are a club of their own, and it is fun to see the Kings and Hawks treated the same as the Celtics and Lakers.

The problem is what Nike did with the idea. A collection built on history has almost no history in it. All eight jerseys are black, all eight have a glowing streak graphic, and none of them uses a Rochester Royals script, a Fort Wayne piston, a Minneapolis Lakers stripe or a Syracuse Nationals logo. The teams were picked because they are old, and then they were dressed like they are brand new. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why did only 8 NBA teams get Specter Edition jerseys?**

Because Nike made the Specter Edition for the league's oldest franchises, "the teams who trace their roots back to the earliest chapters of the league." The eight are every team still in the NBA from its first season, 1949-50.

**How many NBA teams have a Specter Edition jersey?**

Eight: the Sacramento Kings, Detroit Pistons, Boston Celtics, New York Knicks, Golden State Warriors, Atlanta Hawks, Philadelphia 76ers and Los Angeles Lakers.

**Why don't the Bulls have a Specter Edition jersey?**

The Bulls were founded in 1966, 20 years after the youngest Specter teams. The Specter line goes only to the oldest franchises.

**Why don't the Heat have a Specter Edition jersey?**

The Heat joined the NBA as an expansion team in 1988, which makes them one of the youngest teams in the league.

**Why don't the Spurs have a Specter Edition jersey?**

The Spurs started in 1967 as the Dallas Chaparrals of the ABA and joined the NBA in 1976. They are much younger than the eight Specter teams.

**Why don't the Wizards have a Specter Edition jersey?**

The Wizards are the ninth-oldest franchise, but they were founded in 1961 as the Chicago Packers, 12 years after the NBA's first season. Every Specter team dates to 1946 or earlier.

**What is the oldest NBA team?**

The Sacramento Kings, whose franchise traces back to the Rochester Seagrams in 1923. The Pistons are next, from 1937 in Fort Wayne. Among teams that started in the BAA, the Celtics, Knicks and Warriors date to the league's first season in 1946.

**Is the Specter Edition replacing the City Edition?**

No. All 30 teams still have a City Edition this season. The eight Specter teams wear Specter as a fifth uniform on top of Icon, Association, Statement and City Edition.
