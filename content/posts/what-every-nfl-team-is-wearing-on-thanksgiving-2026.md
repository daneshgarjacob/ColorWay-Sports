Three games, six teams, one Thursday. Here is what every NFL team is wearing on Thanksgiving 2026, **Thursday, November 26**, with the helmet, jersey and pants spelled out in colors. The headline look is in Arlington: **the Cowboys wear their 1960s Classic throwback against the Eagles** for the fifth straight Thanksgiving. Detroit opens the day at home against Chicago, and Buffalo hosts Kansas City at night.

Each look carries one of two labels:

- **Scheduled:** the uniform is on the team's own published 2026 uniform schedule. Dallas and Buffalo both put their Thanksgiving jerseys in writing before the season.
- **Expected:** our projection, from the team's 2026 uniform schedule post on this site and how it has dressed all season. Teams show their game-week look in the days before kickoff, and this page switches the label the same day they do.

## Bears At Lions: 1:00 p.m. ET, CBS

### What The Lions Are Wearing (Expected)

**Silver helmet, Honolulu blue jersey, silver pants.** Our [Lions 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/lions-uniform-schedule-2026) has the blue home set for Thanksgiving, and Detroit has paired it with silver pants this season. The other option is blue pants: the Lions went all blue the last time Chicago came to Detroit on Thanksgiving, in 2024. The pants are the one piece to watch on game week.

What Detroit has not put on its 2026 calendar is a throwback. The Lions wore their 1934-style throwbacks, a logo-free silver helmet with a plain blue jersey, on Thanksgiving in 2023 and 2025, and the story behind that uniform is in [Why Do The Lions Wear 1934 Throwback Uniforms On Thanksgiving?](/stories/why-do-the-lions-wear-1934-throwbacks-on-thanksgiving). For 2026, Detroit's alternate dates have gone elsewhere: Rivalries Silver debuted against Minnesota on November 1, and the black alternate is set for Tampa Bay this Sunday. If Detroit shows a throwback during game week, this page changes that day.

**Navy helmet with the orange wishbone C, white jersey.** Chicago wears white on the road, and our [Bears 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/bears-uniform-schedule-2026) has the white set at Ford Field. The Bears have no special uniform on Thanksgiving; their one special date this season is the Rivalries debut against Green Bay on Christmas Day. The pants come with Chicago's game-week post.

## Eagles At Cowboys: 4:30 p.m. ET, FOX

### What The Cowboys Are Wearing (Scheduled)

**White helmet with the vintage star, navy jersey with stars on the white shoulders, white pants with two navy stripes.** This is the Classic, Dallas's 1960s throwback, and it is on the uniform schedule the Cowboys published on September 2. The white helmet replaces the usual silver one, and the navy jersey makes this the one home game all season where Dallas wears a dark jersey. It is the fifth straight Thanksgiving in this uniform, after 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025. Every other Dallas date is in our [Cowboys 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/cowboys-uniform-schedule-2026).

### What The Eagles Are Wearing (Expected)

**Midnight green helmet, white jersey.** With Dallas in navy, Philadelphia has to wear white, so there is no Kelly Green on Thanksgiving. Philadelphia has paired the white jersey with both white and midnight green pants this season. Our [Eagles 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/eagles-uniform-schedule-2026) has every date.

### What The Bills Are Wearing (Scheduled)

**White helmet with the red charging buffalo, royal blue jersey, white pants.** Buffalo published the jersey for every 2026 home game before the season, and Thanksgiving night is the royal blue primary. It is the first Thanksgiving game at the new Highmark Stadium. Buffalo's red helmet is saved for Christmas Day at Denver. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is every NFL team wearing on Thanksgiving 2026?**

Lions: silver helmet, Honolulu blue jersey (expected). Bears: navy helmet, white jersey (expected). Cowboys: the 1960s Classic throwback, white helmet, navy jersey, white pants (on the team's schedule). Eagles: midnight green helmet, white jersey (expected). Bills: white helmet, royal blue jersey, white pants (on the team's schedule). Chiefs: red helmet, white jersey, red pants (expected).

**What are the Lions wearing on Thanksgiving 2026?**

We expect the Honolulu blue home jersey with the silver helmet and silver pants against the Bears. Detroit has no throwback on its 2026 calendar, and the team shows its look during game week.

**Are the Lions wearing throwbacks on Thanksgiving 2026?**

Nothing Detroit has published puts the 1934-style throwback on this game. The Lions wore it on Thanksgiving in 2023 and 2025. If they show it during game week, we will update this page that day.

**What are the Bears wearing on Thanksgiving?**

We expect the white road jersey with the navy helmet at Ford Field. Chicago's only special uniform this season is the Rivalries set on Christmas Day.

**What are the Cowboys wearing on Thanksgiving 2026?**

The Classic, the 1960s throwback: white helmet with the vintage star, navy jersey with stars on the white shoulders, and white pants with navy stripes. It is on the uniform schedule Dallas published September 2.

**Why do the Cowboys wear a throwback on Thanksgiving?**

Dallas has worn the 1960s Classic every Thanksgiving since 2022, the first season the NFL allowed alternate helmets again. The holiday game is the one day a year the team brings out the white helmet.

**What are the Eagles wearing on Thanksgiving?**

We expect white jerseys with the midnight green helmet. The Cowboys wear a navy jersey that day, so Philadelphia has to wear white.

**Will the Eagles wear Kelly Green on Thanksgiving?**

No. Kelly Green is set for Weeks 9 and 16, and a green jersey cannot be worn against the Cowboys' navy throwback.

**What are the Bills wearing on Thanksgiving?**

The royal blue primary: white helmet, royal blue jersey, white pants. Buffalo listed it on its published 2026 home schedule.

**What are the Chiefs wearing on Thanksgiving?**

We expect the red helmet, white jersey and red pants. Kansas City wore white pants with the white jersey once this season, at Seattle in October, so the pants are the one piece to watch.

**What time are the NFL games on Thanksgiving 2026?**

Bears at Lions at 1:00 p.m. ET on CBS, Eagles at Cowboys at 4:30 p.m. ET on FOX, and Chiefs at Bills at 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC, all on Thursday, November 26.

**Do NFL players wear a special patch on Thanksgiving?**

Yes. Every player in the three Thanksgiving games wears the round John Madden Thanksgiving patch on the left chest, confirmed by the NFL on November 19, 2026.
