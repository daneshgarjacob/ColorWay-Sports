Our first list of [hidden details in sports logos](/stories/hidden-details-sports-logos) covered the Brewers' glove, the Maple Leafs' veins and the Jerry West silhouette. We went back for more. Here are 28, across the NBA, NHL, MLB, the NFL, college sports and soccer.

Same rules as Part 1: every detail here is backed by the team, the designer or a major news outlet, museum or university. Where a popular story turns out to be wrong, we say so.

## Quick Facts: More Hidden Logo Details

- **Hidden letters:** the Expos (an e and a b), the Grizzlies (an M in the claws), Inter Miami (an M in the herons' legs), the Islanders (a Y that's a hockey stick).
- **Hidden numbers:** 13 stars for 13 colonies (76ers), seven sun rays for Beckham's No. 7 (Inter Miami), five on five (Trail Blazers), five boroughs (NYCFC).
- **Real places:** Long Island (Islanders), El Palo Alto (Stanford), the Red Triangle (Sharks), Manchester's three rivers (Man City).
- **Real objects:** a 1953 subway token (NYCFC), a 101st Airborne patch (Panthers), a real Bengal tiger's paw (Clemson).
- **Myths busted:** the Packers' G doesn't mean greatness, and the Juventus J isn't a trophy.

## NFL

### Green Bay Packers: The G Stands for Green Bay, Not Greatness

<img src="/images/posts/hidden-logos-part-2/nfl-green-bay-packers.png" alt="Green Bay Packers G logo" style="width:100%;max-width:200px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto;box-shadow:none;border-radius:0;background:transparent;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

You may have heard the G stands for "greatness." It doesn't. Packers team historian Cliff Christl traced that story to Tiki Barber, who said it during media day at Super Bowl XLV. The G means Green Bay. Vince Lombardi asked equipment manager Dad Braisher for a new helmet logo, and John Gordon drew the G for him. The Packers first wore it on August 5, 1961. ## MLB

### Montreal Expos: The M Hides an e and a b

The 1969 Expos logo looks like a tricolor M for Montreal, and it is. But the left side of the M is also a lowercase e for Expos, and the right side is a lowercase b for baseball. Logo designer and historian Todd Radom has called it one of the great case studies in sports design. The twist is that the explanation came long after the logo. The club didn't spell out the e and the b until the late 1980s, about 20 years after the team started wearing it.

### Seattle Mariners: The Compass Points the Wrong Way, on Purpose

<img src="/images/posts/hidden-logos-part-2/mlb-seattle-mariners.png" alt="Seattle Mariners compass rose logo" style="width:100%;max-width:200px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto;box-shadow:none;border-radius:0;background:transparent;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

The compass rose the Mariners introduced in 1993 doesn't point north. It's turned so the main points run east and west, and a team spokesman told The Seattle Times in 1993 that it was done so the words "Seattle" and "Mariners" could read clearly around it. The Mariners' first logo, from 1977, had a trick of its own: the M was an upside-down trident. [Seattle Mariners uniform schedule](/stories/mariners-uniform-schedule-2026)

### Portland Trail Blazers: Five on Five

<img src="/images/posts/hidden-logos-part-2/nba-portland-trail-blazers.png" alt="Portland Trail Blazers pinwheel logo" style="width:100%;max-width:200px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto;box-shadow:none;border-radius:0;background:transparent;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

The pinwheel is a basketball game. Frank Glickman, a cousin of the team's founder, designed it in 1970, and the two sets of five lines, one black and one red, stand for the two teams of five players on the court. ### LA Clippers: A Ship, a Compass and True North

<img src="/images/posts/hidden-logos-part-2/nba-la-clippers.png" alt="LA Clippers ship and compass logo" style="width:100%;max-width:200px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto;box-shadow:none;border-radius:0;background:transparent;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

The Clippers' 2024 logo is a ship's hull with basketball seams, set inside a C that doubles as a compass. The team took the compass literally at its new arena: on the court at Intuit Dome, the N on the center-court compass points to true north, and the arena's coordinates are printed on the floor. Clipper ships are where the name came from in the first place, back in San Diego. [LA Clippers uniform schedule](/stories/clippers-uniform-schedule-2026-27)

### Minnesota Timberwolves: Pointed at True North

<img src="/images/posts/hidden-logos-part-2/nba-minnesota-timberwolves.png" alt="Minnesota Timberwolves logo with the North Star" style="width:100%;max-width:200px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto;box-shadow:none;border-radius:0;background:transparent;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

### Philadelphia 76ers: Thirteen Stars

<img src="/images/posts/hidden-logos-part-2/nba-philadelphia-76ers.png" alt="Philadelphia 76ers logo with 13 stars" style="width:100%;max-width:200px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto;box-shadow:none;border-radius:0;background:transparent;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

### Brooklyn Nets: Black and White Like the Subway

<img src="/images/posts/hidden-logos-part-2/nba-brooklyn-nets.png" alt="Brooklyn Nets B logo" style="width:100%;max-width:200px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto;box-shadow:none;border-radius:0;background:transparent;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

The Nets' black and white identity, created with Jay-Z for the 2012 move to Brooklyn, was modeled on New York City subway signs from 1957, the last year the Dodgers played in Brooklyn, Designboom reported. [Brooklyn Nets uniform schedule](/stories/nets-uniform-schedule-2026-27)

### New Orleans Pelicans: The Bird-de-Lis

<img src="/images/posts/hidden-logos-part-2/nba-new-orleans-pelicans.png" alt="New Orleans Pelicans logo" style="width:100%;max-width:200px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto;box-shadow:none;border-radius:0;background:transparent;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

When the Pelicans name arrived in 2013, one of the new marks folded a pelican into the shape of a fleur-de-lis, the symbol of New Orleans. Fans nicknamed it the "bird-de-lis." [New Orleans Pelicans uniform schedule](/stories/pelicans-uniform-schedule-2026-27)

<img src="/images/posts/hidden-logos-part-2/nhl-winnipeg-jets.png" alt="Winnipeg Jets logo with a jet over a maple leaf" style="width:100%;max-width:200px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto;box-shadow:none;border-radius:0;background:transparent;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

Canada's Department of National Defence owns that roundel, and The Globe and Mail reported in 2011 that the team signed a nine-page licensing contract to use it, which gives the military the final say on how it appears. ### New York City FC: A Subway Token

NYCFC's round crest is shaped like the 1953 New York City subway token, and the design firm behind it, Alfalfa Studio, says the five shapes in the center stand for the city's five boroughs.

### Atletico Madrid: Seven Stars in the Sky

<img src="/images/posts/hidden-logos-part-2/soc-atletico-madrid.png" alt="Atletico Madrid crest with a bear and a tree" style="width:100%;max-width:200px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto;box-shadow:none;border-radius:0;background:transparent;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

The seven stars on Atletico Madrid's crest come from the flag of the Community of Madrid, and LaLiga notes they're also linked to Ursa Major, the Great Bear constellation, which fits the bear and the strawberry tree below them.

### Manchester City: Three Rivers

<img src="/images/posts/hidden-logos-part-2/soc-manchester-city.png" alt="Manchester City round crest" style="width:100%;max-width:200px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto;box-shadow:none;border-radius:0;background:transparent;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

Manchester City's 2016 crest has three wavy lines for the city's three rivers, the Irwell, the Medlock and the Irk. City Football Group said 85% of fans backed keeping the ship, a symbol of Manchester's trading history.

### Arsenal: The Cannon Kept Turning Around

<img src="/images/posts/hidden-logos-part-2/soc-arsenal.png" alt="Arsenal crest with a cannon" style="width:100%;max-width:200px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto;box-shadow:none;border-radius:0;background:transparent;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

Arsenal's cannon has faced both ways. The club's own crest history says it pointed west in 1921-22, east in 1922-23 and west again from 1925-26, the direction it still faces today.

### Juventus: The J Is Not a Trophy

<img src="/images/posts/hidden-logos-part-2/soc-juventus.png" alt="Juventus J crest" style="width:100%;max-width:200px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto;box-shadow:none;border-radius:0;background:transparent;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

A lot of fans read the 2017 Juventus crest as a trophy. The club says otherwise: the J is built from the black and white stripes, the shape of the Scudetto shield and the letter J. It was designed with Interbrand.

### Barcelona: Who Really Drew the Crest

<img src="/images/posts/hidden-logos-part-2/soc-barcelona.png" alt="FC Barcelona crest" style="width:100%;max-width:200px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto;box-shadow:none;border-radius:0;background:transparent;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

The story usually says player Carles Comamala drew Barcelona's crest. The club itself credits Santiago Femenia, who won a design contest in 1910. And when Barcelona tried a simplified crest in 2018 that dropped the letters FCB, it pulled the change back.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What does the G on the Packers helmet stand for?**

Green Bay. The "greatness" story is a myth that team historian Cliff Christl traced to Tiki Barber at Super Bowl XLV media day. John Gordon drew the G in 1961.

**What is hidden in the Expos logo?**

The M for Montreal also contains a lowercase e for Expos and a lowercase b for baseball. The club didn't explain it until the late 1980s.

**Why does the Mariners compass point east and west?**

So the words "Seattle" and "Mariners" read clearly around it, a team spokesman said in 1993.

**What does the Trail Blazers logo mean?**

Two sets of five lines, one black and one red, for the two teams of five players on the court. Frank Glickman designed it in 1970.

**What is hidden in the Grizzlies logo?**

The middle three claw marks form an M for Memphis, the team said in 2018.

**Why does the 76ers logo have 13 stars?**

One for each of the 13 original colonies.

**What does the orange dot in the Flyers logo mean?**

It's a puck. Sam Ciccone designed the winged P in 1967.

**What is hidden in the Islanders logo?**

The tail of the Y is a hockey stick, and the shape behind it is a map of Long Island.

**Who owns the roundel in the Winnipeg Jets logo?**

Canada's Department of National Defence. The team signed a licensing contract in 2011 to use the Royal Canadian Air Force roundel.

**What does the Blue Jackets logo mean?**

It's an Ohio flag curved into a C for Columbus, with a star marking the state capital.

**Where did the Florida Panthers shield come from?**

The patch of the U.S. Army's 101st Airborne Division, where owner Vincent Viola served.

**Why is the Clemson Tiger Paw tilted?**

Clemson says a walking tiger's paw naturally turns out. The logo was made from a cast of a real Bengal tiger's paw.

**What tree is in the Stanford logo?**

El Palo Alto, the redwood that gave Palo Alto its name.

**What is hidden in the Inter Miami crest?**

The herons' legs form an M, and the sun has seven rays for David Beckham's No. 7.

**Why is the NYCFC crest round?**

It's shaped like the 1953 New York City subway token.

**Is the Juventus logo a trophy?**

No. The club says the J is made from the black and white stripes, the Scudetto shield and the letter J.

**What do the three lines on the Manchester City crest mean?**

Manchester's three rivers: the Irwell, the Medlock and the Irk.

**Which way does the Arsenal cannon point?**

West, as it has since 1925-26. It pointed east for the 1922-23 season.

## The Bottom Line

Part 1 was about letters hiding in negative space. Part 2 is about where logos come from: a subway token, an army patch, a napkin on a plane, a real tiger's paw. The best ones still read as one simple shape from the upper deck, and the story is there for anyone who looks closer.
