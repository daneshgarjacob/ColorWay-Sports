**The Bills wore blue over blue on Thanksgiving 2026, the white helmet, royal blue jersey and royal blue pants, and beat the Chiefs 13-10 on Tyler Bass's 57-yard field goal as time expired.** Buffalo wore exactly what it posted on Tuesday in its "Thanksgiving Threads" graphic: **white helmet, royal blue jersey, royal blue pants and white socks**. Kansas City wore its road set: the **red helmet, white jersey and red pants**. It was the first Thanksgiving game at the new Highmark Stadium, and the last of three games on the holiday.

## Quick Answers

- **What did the Bills wear on Thanksgiving 2026?** The white helmet, royal blue jersey, royal blue pants and white socks. Buffalo called it "Blue Blue White."
- **What did the Chiefs wear?** The red helmet, white road jersey and red pants. No alternate: Kansas City does not have one in 2026.
- **Final score?** Bills 13, Chiefs 10. Buffalo is 10-1, Kansas City 8-3.
- **How did it end?** Tyler Bass made a 54-yarder to tie it at 10 early in the fourth quarter, then a 57-yarder as time expired.
- **What is Buffalo's record in blue over blue this season?** 2-0, from our own log: Week 1 at Houston and Thanksgiving night.
- **What is Kansas City's record in white this season?** 2-3. In red it is 6-0. All three Chiefs losses came in the white jersey.
- **Did both teams wear the John Madden patch?** Every player in the three Thanksgiving Day games wore it, per the NFL.
- **Our matchup grade:** B+, the grade we gave it before kickoff.

## What Our Own Log Says

We record the helmet, jersey and pants every NFL team actually wears in every game, from game photos, and pair it with the ESPN final. Here is what that log says about these two teams after Thursday night.

**Buffalo in blue over blue: 2-0.** The Bills have worn the white helmet, royal blue jersey and royal blue pants twice this season, and won both: 36-31 at Houston in Week 1, where the Texans wore white at home, and 13-10 against Kansas City on Thanksgiving. In the royal blue jersey overall, Buffalo is **4-1**. The one loss, 29-26 to New England in Week 4, came with white pants.

**Kansas City in white: 2-3.** The Chiefs are **6-0 in red at home** (plus Miami, where the Dolphins wore white) and **2-3 in the white jersey**. Every one of their three losses this season came in white: 28-24 at Denver, 20-3 at Seattle in white pants, and 13-10 at Buffalo. In the red helmet, white jersey and red pants they are now **2-2**.

| Chiefs look | Record |
|---|---|

That is not a curse. Teams win more at home, and the Chiefs wear red at home. But it does mean Kansas City is 8-3 with every loss on the road in white. ## The Game

Bills 13, Chiefs 10, at Highmark Stadium. Per ESPN, James Cook III scored the only touchdown of the first half on a 5-yard run, and Harrison Butker's 26-yard field goal with two seconds left in the half made it 7-3. Emmett Johnson's 1-yard run gave Kansas City a 10-7 lead in the third quarter. Tyler Bass tied it with a 54-yard field goal with 11:58 left, then won it from 57 yards on the final play. Cook ran 32 times for 117 yards. Josh Allen threw for 81 yards with two interceptions, and Patrick Mahomes threw for 174.

## Our Take

Royal blue against red and white is about as American as football gets on Thanksgiving night, and blue pants made the Bills side better than the white pants Buffalo usually wears with this jersey at home: one solid block of royal under a white helmet. We gave the matchup a **B+** before kickoff and that is where it stays. Two standard uniforms on a holiday when Dallas wore the Classic and the Rams had just worn a one-night alternate is a step down, and Buffalo still has a red helmet it saved for Christmas Day at Denver instead.
