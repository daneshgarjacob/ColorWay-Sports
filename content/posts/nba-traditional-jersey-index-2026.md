For most of NBA history you could tell who was home with one glance: the team in white. That standard quietly died when the league moved to its modern uniform system and let home teams wear whichever edition they want, and the 2026 playoffs are the best measurement yet of how far the league has drifted. This page is the **Traditional Jersey Index**, our running count of how often the 2026 playoff home team wore its classic white uniform, the same index we keep live on the [ColorWay homepage](/). We update it after every playoff game, round by round, through the end of the NBA Finals.

*Updated Wednesday June 10 through the confirmed NBA Finals Game 5 jerseys. The Finals row counts every game with a confirmed uniform assignment, including the Spurs' Association white for Friday's Game 5 at Frost Bank Center.*

The headline number: across 85 playoff games with confirmed uniforms, the home team has worn its traditional white just 31 times. If you grew up on the home-whites era, two out of every three playoff games this spring looked backwards.

## Round by Round: The Index Tells a Story

**Round 1 · 16 of 48 (33%).** The opening round set the tone, with barely a third of home teams in white as City Editions, Icon colors, and Statement uniforms ate up the home slots. We wrote the full first-round autopsy with the team-by-team detail here: [the Round 1 home jersey breakdown](/stories/nba-playoffs-2026-round-1-home-jersey-breakdown).

**Round 2 · 8 of 21 (38%).** A modest bounce, though the round also produced some of the postseason's loudest anti-traditional swings, including black-on-black home nights that we graded at the bottom of the [Round 2 jersey tracker](/stories/nba-playoffs-2026-round-2-jersey-tracker).

**Conference Finals · 3 of 11 (27%).** The low point of the postseason. With four teams playing for a Finals berth, home whites nearly disappeared, a stretch we tracked game by game on the [Conference Finals jersey tracker](/stories/nba-playoffs-2026-conference-finals-jersey-tracker).

**NBA Finals · 4 of 5 (80%).** And then the Finals flipped the whole index on its head. The Knicks and Spurs have combined to put the home team in Association white in four of the five games with confirmed uniforms, by far the most traditional stretch of the 2026 postseason, and the matchup grades on our [NBA Finals jersey tracker](/stories/nba-finals-2026-jersey-tracker-knicks-spurs) have followed: white at home against a color road jersey is the textbook playoff frame, and the Finals keep proving it.

## Why the Biggest Stage Went Traditional

There is a reason the Finals row glows green while every other round runs red. On the league's biggest stage, both franchises leaned into their most timeless looks: the Knicks' white with orange and blue trim at Madison Square Garden, the Spurs' white with the classic black and silver at Frost Bank Center. When the stakes rise, teams reach for the uniform that says franchise history, and the broadcast rewards them for it. Every 10 out of 10 matchup grade we have handed out in this Finals has come with the home team in white.

The rest of the postseason tells the opposite story. Since the league moved to its modern uniform system, home teams designate any edition they want, and the marketing calendar usually wins: City Editions get their home nights, Icon colors anchor brand identity, and the traditional white slot becomes one option among four. The index is not a complaint, it is a measurement. But 36 percent is the kind of number that explains why a Finals run at 80 percent feels so visually right.

## How the Index Works

One rule: did the home team wear its traditional white (Association Edition) uniform? Yes counts, anything else does not, no partial credit for cream, throwback whites count if they are the team's classic white look. We confirm uniforms from the league's official assignments for every playoff game and update this page and the homepage index together after each round of games. The per-game detail, with a grade for every single matchup, lives on the round trackers linked above.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the NBA Traditional Jersey Index?**

The Traditional Jersey Index is ColorWay Sports' running count of how often the home team wears its traditional white uniform in the 2026 NBA Playoffs. The index covers every playoff game with a confirmed uniform assignment, broken out by round, and updates after every game through the end of the NBA Finals. The 2026 index currently stands at 31 of 85 games, or 36 percent.

**How often did NBA home teams wear white in the 2026 playoffs?**

Through the confirmed NBA Finals Game 5 uniforms, home teams have worn their traditional white in 31 of 85 playoff games, 36 percent. Round 1 finished at 16 of 48 (33 percent), Round 2 at 8 of 21 (38 percent), the Conference Finals at 3 of 11 (27 percent), and the NBA Finals lead the postseason at 4 of 5 (80 percent).

**Which 2026 playoff round had the most traditional jersey matchups?**

The NBA Finals, by a wide margin. The Knicks and Spurs have put the home team in Association white in 4 of the 5 games with confirmed uniforms, an 80 percent rate. No other round cleared 40 percent, and the Conference Finals were the least traditional round of the postseason at 27 percent.

**Does the NBA require home teams to wear white?**

No. Under the league's modern uniform system, the home team designates which uniform edition it will wear for each game and the road team selects a contrasting option. The old standard of white at home stopped being a requirement years ago, which is exactly why we track how often teams still choose it.

**Why do NBA teams wear City Edition and colored jerseys at home?**

Each team carries multiple uniform editions every season, and the home team can schedule any of them. City Editions and Icon colors get dedicated home nights for brand and merchandising reasons, which crowds out the traditional white look. The result across the 2026 playoffs: home whites in only about a third of games outside the Finals.
