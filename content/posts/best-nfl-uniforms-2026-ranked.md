Every NFL team's 2026 uniform, graded and counted down from worst to best. We went team by team through all thirty-two closets, home sets, road sets, alternates and throwbacks, and put a letter grade on each one. Only one team failed. Three reached an A, and the team at the top is the one everybody already expects, with two complaints about it we do not hear made often enough.

A note on how we grade. This is about the uniform as a piece of design: the colours, how they are used, the helmet, the pants, and whether the whole thing holds together on a broadcast. Winning is not supposed to matter and we will admit up front that it creeps in anyway, which is a bias worth naming rather than hiding.

## 32. New Orleans Saints &middot; F

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; margin: 1.6em 0;">
<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/Saints/status/2089034456132878795"></a></blockquote>
</div>

The Saints have been our least favourite uniform in the league for a long time, and it comes down to how the pieces are put together rather than the palette. The contrasting collar on the black jersey is too big and it cuts the whole thing in half. Black and gold is a genuinely great colour pair. New Orleans just does not use it correctly, the alternates do not help, and something changed when Nike took over that the Reebok-era Drew Brees sets did not have. Those popped. These do not. Full disclosure: it is harder to love a uniform on a team that is not winning, and the NFC South as a whole is our least favourite division on this list.

## The Bottom Line

The NFC North is the strongest division in the league by uniform, with the Bears at an A and the Packers, Vikings and Lions all landing at A-. The NFC South is the weakest by a distance: Saints, Panthers, Falcons and Buccaneers all sit in the bottom twelve, and the Saints are the only F on the board.

Three teams reached an A and none reached an A+, which is the honest summary of where the league is. The Chargers are the best uniform in football and are still leaving something on the table, between a powder blue that is no longer powder and a Super Chargers set crying out for white pants. The Giants and Bears got there by never needing a rescue in the first place.

The most improved is Houston, which has climbed from the worst uniform in the league to a respectable B-. The biggest missed opportunity is Baltimore, which had a chance to do something real with the best colour in the sport and changed the collar instead.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best NFL uniform in 2026?**

The Los Angeles Chargers, graded A. The powder blue home set is the best-looking uniform in the league, though the current powder blue is noticeably darker than the shade the Chargers wore in the Philip Rivers era.

**What is the worst NFL uniform in 2026?**

The New Orleans Saints, the only F on our board. Black and gold is a great colour pair, but the oversized contrasting collar cuts the jersey in half and the set has not looked right since the Reebok era.

**Which NFL division has the best uniforms?**

The NFC North. The Bears earned an A and the Packers, Vikings and Lions all landed at A-, making it the only division without a single team below the A- tier.

**Which NFL team improved the most?**

The Houston Texans. Their rebrand took them from what we considered the worst uniform in the league to a B-, with numbers that read far better and a near-black navy that works.

**Did any NFL team get an A+?**

No. Three teams reached an A, the Chargers, Giants and Bears, but none earned an A+ in 2026.
