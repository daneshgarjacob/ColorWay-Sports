The NBA is adding a second patch to 2026 NBA Finals jerseys. The new USA 250 patch sits on the right chest above the Nike swoosh on both the New York Knicks and the San Antonio Spurs uniforms for the entire Finals series. The 2026 NBA Finals back patch we covered last week stays where it is, sewn on the back of the jersey just below the collar above the player nameplate. That makes the 2026 Finals the first NBA Finals in league history with two dedicated patches on every jersey at the same time.

The USA 250 patch is not an NBA-only design. The patch is identical to the one the NFL has been wearing across the entire 2025-26 season and into Super Bowl LX. Same shield silhouette, same red and white stripes, same navy blue field with stars, same USA 250 wordmark stacked over the shield. Two leagues, same patch, same designed-for-America's-250th-birthday lockup.

## The Two-Patch Finals Jersey: USA 250 on the Front, Finals Patch on the Back

The 2026 NBA Finals jersey carries two patches at the same time, the first NBA Finals in history to do that. The USA 250 patch lives on the front of the jersey on the right chest directly above the Nike swoosh. The 2026 NBA Finals patch lives on the back of the jersey at the top center just below the collar, the back-of-jersey Finals patch placement we [broke down in detail in the Knicks back patch first-look post](/stories/knicks-2026-nba-finals-jersey-back-patch-first-look) when the NBA released the back patch design earlier in the playoff run.

Front patch is the country, back patch is the round. Both teams in both buildings carry both marks on every game of the Finals at Madison Square Garden and Frost Bank Center.

## The NBA Patch Is the Exact Same Patch the NFL Has Been Wearing All Season

The USA 250 patch is not new in American sports. The NFL has been wearing the same patch across the entire 2025-26 NFL regular season and into the postseason. Every Super Bowl LX jersey in the AFC and NFC will carry the USA 250 patch in the same right-chest placement, the patch sewn above each team's primary chest mark for the entire season. The NBA's 2026 Finals jersey adoption brings the second major American sports league into the same patch program. The two leagues are now visually aligned for America's 250th anniversary celebration through the end of the 2026 NBA Finals and into the Independence Day weekend on July 4, 2026.

We love this design. The USA 250 shield is one of the cleanest commemorative patch lockups in recent memory across any league. Stars and stripes inside a shield outline that reads from across the building, white USA wordmark on the navy top with 250 in white on the red and white stripes below, the whole mark sitting clean on any jersey color. The patch reads as Americana without leaning hokey, the kind of mark that holds up next to a team's primary chest crest or the Nike swoosh on the right chest without overwhelming either side.

## What This Means for MLB and the Rest of American Sports

The NBA and NFL both wearing the same USA 250 patch through 2026 sets up a question every other American league has to answer. MLB is the next obvious candidate. The 2026 MLB season runs through October 2026 and the All-Star Game is in July 2026 directly on top of the 250th anniversary celebration weekend. We expect MLB to adopt the same USA 250 patch for at least the 2026 All-Star Game and the postseason. NHL, MLS, and the WNBA also have summer and fall calendars that overlap with the 250th celebration window.

The visual story the NBA wants is the same one the NFL set up across the 2025-26 season. American sports leagues showing up together for the country's 250th birthday in a single shared patch design. That is the strongest unified branding play across major American sports leagues in years, and the NBA Finals patch placement above the Nike swoosh confirms the league is fully bought in.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the USA 250 patch on the 2026 NBA Finals jerseys?**

The USA 250 patch is a commemorative shield-shaped patch that celebrates the United States of America's 250th anniversary on July 4, 2026. The patch features a shield outline with red and white horizontal stripes, a navy blue field with white stars, the USA wordmark in white on the navy section, and the number 250 in white on the red and white stripes. The patch sits on the front of every 2026 NBA Finals jersey on the right chest directly above the Nike swoosh logo.

**Where is the USA 250 patch on NBA Finals jerseys?**

The USA 250 patch is on the front of the 2026 NBA Finals jerseys on the right chest of the player, directly above the Nike swoosh logo. Both the New York Knicks Association Edition white home jersey and the San Antonio Spurs Icon Edition black road jersey carry the patch in the same right-chest placement above the Nike mark.

**Is the NBA USA 250 patch the same as the NFL Super Bowl USA 250 patch?**

Yes. The USA 250 patch on the 2026 NBA Finals jerseys is identical in design to the patch the NFL has been wearing across the entire 2025-26 NFL regular season and into Super Bowl LX. Same shield silhouette, same red and white stripes, same navy blue field with stars, same USA 250 wordmark lockup. Two leagues, same patch design, same commemorative purpose for America's 250th anniversary.

**How many patches are on the 2026 NBA Finals jerseys?**

The 2026 NBA Finals jerseys carry two patches at the same time, the first time in NBA Finals history that the league has used two dedicated commemorative patches on the same jersey. The USA 250 patch sits on the front right chest above the Nike swoosh. The 2026 NBA Finals patch sits on the back of the jersey at the top center just below the collar above the player nameplate.

**Where is the 2026 NBA Finals back patch?**

The 2026 NBA Finals patch is sewn on the back of the Finals jersey at the top center just below the collar, directly above the player nameplate. The back placement is a change from the upper-left chest position the Finals patch lived in across most recent Finals series. Full breakdown of the back patch placement in our [Knicks 2026 NBA Finals jersey back patch first-look post](/stories/knicks-2026-nba-finals-jersey-back-patch-first-look).

**Will MLB use the USA 250 patch?**

The MLB has not officially confirmed the USA 250 patch for the 2026 season, but the NBA and NFL both adopting the same patch makes the MLB the next obvious candidate. The 2026 MLB All-Star Game in July sits directly on top of the 250th anniversary celebration weekend on July 4, 2026, and the league has historically participated in major patriotic commemorations on jerseys during patriotic holidays. We expect MLB to adopt the same patch for at least the 2026 All-Star Game and the postseason.

**Will the NHL Stanley Cup Final jerseys have the USA 250 patch?**

The Carolina Hurricanes and the Vegas Golden Knights have not been reported wearing the USA 250 patch in the 2026 NHL Stanley Cup Final at the time of writing. The Stanley Cup Final overlaps with the same calendar window as the NBA Finals, so the patch is a possibility, but neither team has confirmed the addition. Full Stanley Cup Final jersey breakdown in our [2026 NHL Stanley Cup Final jersey tracker](/stories/nhl-stanley-cup-final-2026-jersey-tracker-hurricanes-knights).

**When is America's 250th anniversary?**

America's 250th anniversary, also known as the Semiquincentennial, is on July 4, 2026, exactly 250 years after the signing of the Declaration of Independence on July 4, 1776. The full anniversary celebration runs through 2026 with major sports leagues including the NFL and the NBA wearing the USA 250 commemorative patch on jerseys throughout the year.

**Who designed the USA 250 patch?**

The USA 250 patch is the official commemorative patch of the United States Semiquincentennial Commission (also known as America250), the federal commission tasked with leading America's 250th birthday celebration. The same patch design has been adopted across multiple major American sports leagues including the NFL across the 2025-26 season and the NBA for the 2026 Finals.
