Most of our team ranking posts have four or five jerseys to sort through. This one has two, and that is the entire story.

The New York Yankees wear home pinstripes and road grays. That is it. No City Connect, no powder blue Sunday alternate, no black weeknight set, none of the things half the league now rotates through in a single homestand. In 2026 the team technically designated a third uniform, the first "alternate" in its 123-year history, and then did the most Yankees thing imaginable with it: they left it on the rack. We will get to that one, but it does not get a number, because you cannot rank a jersey the team refuses to wear.

Here are the two uniforms the Yankees actually put on, ranked and graded, plus the one they won't.

## 2. Road Gray

Calling this one second is not a knock, because the Yankees road gray is one of the most recognizable uniforms in the sport in its own right. "NEW YORK" arced across the chest in navy block letters, the number underneath, the interlocking NY on the navy cap, and nothing else. No name on the back, no wordmark clutter, no color beyond navy on gray. It is the same discipline that makes the pinstripes work, applied to the road.

The reason it lands at B+ instead of higher is simply that it is the away version of a better idea. The chest wordmark is clean and correct, but it does not carry the same weight as the pinstripe-and-interlocking-NY combination at home. Very few road grays in baseball are this good. It is still a road gray, and a road gray is built to let the home team's colors lead.

What it does perfectly is stay out of its own way. Watch a Yankees road game and the uniform never once asks for attention, which is exactly what it is supposed to do. That is a higher compliment than it sounds.

## 1. Home Pinstripes

<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/Yankees/status/2080117133472657915"></a></blockquote>

This is the most iconic uniform in American sports, and it earns the word. White with navy pinstripes, the interlocking NY over the heart, no player name on the back, no chest wordmark, no secondary logo. It has looked essentially like this for a century, and it is the reference point every other "classic" uniform in baseball gets measured against.

The genius of it is subtraction. Almost every design decision the Yankees have made for a hundred years has been to leave something off. No name so the pinstripe stays uninterrupted. No wordmark so the interlocking NY is the only mark you need. No alternate so the look never dilutes. The result is a uniform that carries more brand weight than any logo in sports while being, on paper, one of the simplest things anyone wears.

It gets an A rather than a literal perfect score only because we hold that ceiling for a jersey doing something a little more daring, and the pinstripes are iconic precisely by refusing to be daring. That is not a criticism. It is the whole philosophy, and it is why this is the standard the rest of the league chases.

## The Alternate They Designated but Won't Wear

Here is the third uniform, and the reason it has no number.

<img src="/images/posts/yankees-first-alternate-uniform-2026/yankees-alternate-jersey.png" alt="New York Yankees navy blue alternate road uniform with NEW YORK wordmark in grey across the chest, the first designated alternate jersey in franchise history" style="width: 100%; max-width: 480px; display: block; margin: 1.5em auto; border-radius: 10px;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin-top: 0.5em;">via Fanatics</p>

Before the 2026 season, the Yankees quietly reclassified their longtime navy spring training and batting practice road top, the one with "NEW YORK" in grey across the chest, from a Spring Training Road Uniform to an official Alternate Road Uniform. On paper, that makes it the first alternate uniform in the 123-year history of the franchise. It happened after players pitched the team on actually wearing navy during the regular season.

And then nothing. As of this writing the Yankees have not worn it in a single 2026 regular season game, and we hope they never do. The full case is in our [Yankees first alternate uniform deep dive](/stories/yankees-first-alternate-uniform-2026), but the short version is this: the Yankees are the Yankees because of what they refuse to wear. The two-uniform closet is not a limitation, it is the brand. A navy alternate would not add anything the pinstripes and grays do not already do better, and it would chip at the one thing no other team in the sport can claim, which is total discipline.

So it exists, technically. It is sold at retail, technically. It is an alternate, technically. It is just not something the Yankees put on, and until they do, it does not belong in a ranking of what they actually wear.

## The Bottom Line on the Yankees Jersey Rankings

The Yankees have the shortest closet in baseball and the strongest, and those two facts are the same fact. Home pinstripes at an A, road grays at a B+, and a century of refusing to add anything else is the reason both grades are that high. When you only wear two things, both of them get to be iconic.

The navy alternate is the exception that proves it. The Yankees gave themselves permission to wear a third uniform and then declined to use it, which is the most Yankees decision of the whole season. Long may it sit in the closet.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many jerseys do the Yankees have in 2026?**

Two that they actually wear: the home pinstripes and the road grays. A navy alternate road uniform was designated for the first time in franchise history before the 2026 season, but the Yankees have not worn it in a regular season game.

**What is the best Yankees jersey?**

The home pinstripes, which we grade an A. White with navy pinstripes, the interlocking NY over the heart, and no name on the back, it is the most iconic uniform in American sports.

**Do the Yankees have an alternate jersey?**

Technically yes, as of 2026. The team reclassified its old navy spring training and batting practice road top with "NEW YORK" in grey as an official alternate road uniform, the first alternate in franchise history. As of this writing they have not worn it in a regular season game.

**Why don't the Yankees have a City Connect?**

By choice. The Yankees have never worn a City Connect and have no alternate rotation the way most teams do. The two-uniform closet, home pinstripes and road grays, is a deliberate part of the brand.
