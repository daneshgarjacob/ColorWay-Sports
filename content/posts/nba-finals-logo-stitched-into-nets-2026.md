A cool new detail on the 2026 NBA Finals at Frost Bank Center: every basketball net at the rim for NBA Finals Game 1 between the New York Knicks and the San Antonio Spurs has the 2026 NBA Finals logo stitched directly into the white nylon net rope. The Finals cursive script wordmark sits in a small white patch woven into the net midway down between the rim and the bottom of the net, the NBA Jerry West silhouette logo and the cursive "Finals" lettering reading cleanly against the white nylon. The patch is the same Finals cursive treatment the league painted onto the floor at center court at both Madison Square Garden and Frost Bank Center for the 2026 NBA Finals, now woven into the actual playing surface at the rim.

The 2026 NBA Finals logo patch is woven into the upper-middle section of the white nylon net, sitting roughly four or five rows of rope down from the orange rim. The placement is the same on every net at every rim at both Frost Bank Center in San Antonio and Madison Square Garden in New York, and the patch carries the same Finals cursive script wordmark and NBA Jerry West silhouette logo treatment the league has run across the entire 2026 NBA Finals visual identity package. The patch is small enough that the rest of the net reads cleanly as a standard NBA playing net on the wide angle, but a tight broadcast camera angle of a swish or a tip-in puts the Finals logo directly in the frame on every made shot.

The Spurs retired jersey banners — Tim Duncan 21, Sean Elliott 32 — visible in the rafters above the rim confirm the photo angle is at Frost Bank Center for the Game 1 home opener. The net-with-patch treatment is now part of the Spurs' Game 1 home presentation along with the floor refresh, the trophy at center court, and the back-patch jerseys we covered separately for both the [Spurs Icon Edition black](/stories/spurs-nba-finals-2026-back-patch-first-look) and the [Knicks Icon Edition blue](/stories/knicks-2026-nba-finals-jersey-back-patch-first-look) Finals jerseys.

## Same Net-Patch Treatment as Both Conference Finals Series

The patch-on-the-net detail is not new for the NBA Finals. The league ran the same nets-with-stitched-patch treatment at every home arena across both the 2026 Eastern Conference Finals between the New York Knicks and the Cleveland Cavaliers and the 2026 Western Conference Finals between the San Antonio Spurs and the Oklahoma City Thunder. Each Conference Finals series had the round's official cursive logo patch — "Eastern Conference Finals" and "Western Conference Finals" — woven into the white nets at Madison Square Garden, Rocket Mortgage Fieldhouse, Frost Bank Center, and Paycom Center for every home game.

Carrying the same format through to the NBA Finals continues the league's net-patch program one level deeper into the postseason, the cursive Finals wordmark now standing in for the Conference Finals wordmark at the same spot on the same white net. The continuity is a small but real detail — every team that survived to the Conference Finals played under the round's logo on the rim, and now the Knicks and the Spurs are playing under the Finals logo on the rim for the championship round at the exact same spot on the net.

The net-stitched patch is the smallest piece of the entire identity refresh and the one most viewers will only catch on a tight rim camera angle, but the same Finals cursive logo is now on the floor, on the jersey, and on the net.

Both rims at both arenas.
