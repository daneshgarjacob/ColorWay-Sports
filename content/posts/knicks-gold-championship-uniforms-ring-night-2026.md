**Update, October 22:** Ring night is in the books. The Knicks wore the gold-trimmed white for the whole game, raised a royal blue **"2025-26 World Champions"** banner to the Garden rafters, and got their rings from Jason of Beverly Hills. Then the 76ers spoiled the party, **106-100**. The banner and the rings are in the new section below.

The New York Knicks raised their championship banner on Tuesday night at Madison Square Garden, and they did it in gold. The team showed off its ring night uniform that afternoon under the caption "gold standard": the home **Association white**, with the trim turned to **metallic gold** almost everywhere you look. We love it, and we are giving it an **A+**.

## What Is Gold on the Knicks' Ring Night Uniform

Here is everything that changed from the regular Association white, from the pictures the Knicks posted:

1. **The "NEW YORK" wordmark is gold.** The letters on the chest are filled in shiny metallic gold, still outlined in Knicks blue and gray.
2. **The names and numbers are gold.** The nameplate on the back, like TOWNS above, is metallic gold twill with gold stitching.
3. **A gold 2025-26 Champions patch sits on the back.** It is a gold basketball with "2025-26" and "Champions" in black script, and the NBA logo below, placed between the collar and the nameplate.
4. **A gold "3" tag sits at the back of the collar.** The small gold patch shows a basketball with a **3** on it, for the franchise's third championship after 1970 and 1973.

Bleacher Report also reported a script "Champs" embroidered on the jersey. The collar keeps its usual blue and orange stripes.

## Our Grade: A+

**Jake Daneshgar, Founder and Editor in Chief:** "I love this. I give it an A+. I'm happy the NBA is doing this again and really making it look special when a team wins the NBA championship."

The reason it works is restraint. The Knicks did not invent a new jersey for ring night. They took their home white, the one that has barely changed since 1973 (we put the two [side by side here](/stories/knicks-1973-vs-2026-championship-uniforms-compared)), and swapped the trim to gold. Gold already sits next to orange and blue without fighting it, so the jersey still reads as the Knicks from the top row of the Garden. The gold "3" at the collar is the best touch: you only see it up close, and it tells the whole story of the franchise in one number.

## Ring Night: What the Knicks Wore and How It Went

- **When:** Tuesday, October 20, 2026. The banner raising and ring ceremony started around 6:30 p.m. ET, with tipoff against the Philadelphia 76ers at 7:00 p.m. ET on NBC and Peacock.
- **Knicks:** the gold-trimmed Association white above, for the full game. The team's own game clips show the white with gold numbers from tipoff on.
- **76ers:** the royal blue Icon, with LeBron James making his regular season debut for Philadelphia.
- **The banner:** it went up next to the 1970 and 1973 banners. Fans in the building got a "2026-27 Champions Night" shirt, and the plaza outside the Garden has been renamed Champions Plaza.
- **Final score:** 76ers 106, Knicks 100, according to ESPN. New York led 58-54 at halftime before Philadelphia won the third quarter 29-18. Jalen Brunson led the Knicks with 21 points, Andre Drummond had 14 rebounds, and Tyrese Maxey scored 28 for the 76ers. Attendance was 19,812.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What did the Knicks wear for ring night?**

The Knicks wore a gold-trimmed version of their white Association uniform for their October 20, 2026 ring night against the 76ers. The "NEW YORK" wordmark, names and numbers are metallic gold, a gold 2025-26 Champions patch sits on the back, and a gold "3" tag sits at the back of the collar.

**Why do the Knicks have gold numbers?**

Gold marks the Knicks as defending NBA champions. New York won the 2026 NBA Finals over the San Antonio Spurs in five games for its first title since 1973, and the gold trim was for the opener where the team received its rings and raised the banner.

**What does the 3 on the Knicks jersey collar mean?**

The gold "3" tag at the back of the collar stands for the Knicks' third NBA championship. The franchise also won in 1970 and 1973.

**What does the Knicks' championship patch say?**

The patch on the back of the Knicks' ring night jersey reads "2025-26 Champions" in black script on a gold basketball, with the NBA logo underneath. It sits between the collar and the player's name.

**Why does the patch say 2025-26 if the Knicks won in 2026?**

NBA seasons span two calendar years. The Knicks won the title at the end of the 2025-26 season, in June 2026, so the patch names that season. That is also why the fan shirts say "2026-27 Champions Night": the ceremony opens the next season.

**Who did the Knicks play on ring night?**

The Knicks hosted the Philadelphia 76ers at Madison Square Garden on Tuesday, October 20, 2026. The ceremony started around 6:30 p.m. ET and the game tipped at 7:00 p.m. ET on NBC and Peacock.

**Did the Knicks win on ring night?**

No. The 76ers beat the Knicks 106-100 at Madison Square Garden on October 20, 2026. New York led 58-54 at halftime, but Philadelphia won the third quarter 29-18. Jalen Brunson scored 21 for the Knicks and Tyrese Maxey had 28 for the 76ers.

**What does the Knicks 2026 championship banner look like?**

The banner is royal blue, matching the Knicks' 1970 and 1973 banners. It has the team's primary logo at the top, the orange basketball with the "KNICKS" wordmark over a blue triangle, then "2025-26" in large white numerals and "WORLD CHAMPIONS" in white below. It was raised to the Madison Square Garden rafters on October 20, 2026.

**What do the Knicks championship rings look like?**

The rings, made by Jason of Beverly Hills, have the Knicks logo on top in yellow gold stones, with "NEW YORK" above the wordmark and royal blue stones filling the triangle behind it, all framed in white diamonds. "CHAMPIONS" runs across the face in raised diamond letters, and "WORLD CHAMPIONS" and a channel of blue stones wrap around the side.

**What did the 76ers wear against the Knicks?**

The 76ers wore their royal blue Icon uniform at the Garden, matching the NBA's official uniform schedule.

**How many championships have the Knicks won?**

Three: 1970, 1973 and 2026.

**Can you buy the Knicks gold championship jersey?**

The gold ring night jersey was a game uniform for the ceremony. For fans, the Knicks and Fanatics have released 2026 championship shirts and hats, which we graded in our [Knicks 2026 NBA champions hat and shirt review](/stories/knicks-2026-nba-champions-hat-shirt-graded).

## The Bottom Line

The Knicks waited 53 years for this banner, and they dressed for it without overdoing it: the same white uniform, now in gold, with a small "3" at the collar that says everything. The 76ers won the game, but the banner is up for good. A+.
