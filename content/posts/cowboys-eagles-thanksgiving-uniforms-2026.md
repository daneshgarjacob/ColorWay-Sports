**The Cowboys wear the Classic throwback on Thanksgiving for the fifth year in a row.** Dallas hosts the Philadelphia Eagles at AT&T Stadium on **Thursday, November 26, at 4:30 p.m. Eastern (3:30 p.m. Central) on FOX**. The team put the Classic on Week 12 when it published all 17 of its 2026 uniform assignments on September 2: a **white helmet with the vintage star**, a **navy jersey with stars on the white shoulders**, and **white pants with two navy stripes**. Because that is a dark jersey, Philadelphia is expected in **white**, not Kelly Green.

## Quick Answers

- **What are the Cowboys wearing on Thanksgiving 2026?** The 1960s Classic throwback: white helmet, navy jersey with stars on the shoulders, white pants with navy stripes. **Scheduled**, from the uniform schedule Dallas published September 2.
- **What are the Eagles wearing?** White jerseys with the midnight green helmet. **Expected.**
- **Will the Eagles wear Kelly Green on Thanksgiving?** No. The Cowboys are in navy, so Philadelphia has to wear white.

## What The Cowboys Are Wearing (Scheduled)

The Classic is a throwback to the Cowboys of the 1960s, and it is the one day a year Dallas changes its silhouette.

- **The helmet.** White, not silver, with the vintage star on the side and a gray facemask. The white shell is the whole trick: it removes the silver that sits under every other Cowboys look.
- **The jersey.** Navy, with oversized stars on a white shoulder yoke and solid block numbers.
- **The pants.** White, with twin navy stripes down the side.

This is also the only home game all season where Dallas wears a dark jersey. ## What The Eagles Are Wearing (Expected)

Normally Dallas in white at home means the visitor gets to pick a color, and Philadelphia used exactly that opening in 2025 to wear Kelly Green at AT&T Stadium. That is off the table on Thanksgiving. The Classic is a navy jersey, so for this one game Dallas is the dark team and the Eagles take **white**, with the **midnight green helmet**. Philadelphia confirmed its three 2026 alternate dates on September 4, Kelly Green in Weeks 9 and 16 and the black alternate in Week 15, and Thanksgiving is not one of them. The Eagles have worn both white and midnight green pants with the white jersey this season; the pants come with their game-week post. ## The Cowboys' Thanksgiving Uniform History

Dallas has hosted a Thanksgiving game nearly every year since 1966, and for most of that time the holiday has been the place for a throwback. The current run:

- **2022 vs. the Giants:** the Classic, its first Thanksgiving after the NFL allowed alternate helmets again.
- **2023 vs. Washington:** the Classic.
- **2024 vs. the Giants:** the Classic.
- **2025 vs. Kansas City:** the Classic, in a 31-28 Dallas win that averaged 57.23 million viewers on CBS, the most-watched regular-season game in NFL history.
- **2026 vs. Philadelphia:** the Classic, fifth straight year.

The white helmet is the reason this works. The NFL's alternate-helmet rule is what brought it back, and Dallas has decided the best use of that rule is one holiday a year. We agree.

## Our Grade

**A-.** The Classic is the best uniform Dallas owns, and putting it against Philadelphia, the division rival Dallas fans care about most, makes this the strongest Thanksgiving matchup of the run. Navy and white with stars against midnight green and white is a clean, dark-versus-light game with no color overlap. What keeps it from an A is the Eagles' side: white is the right call and the only call, but this is the matchup that would have looked best with Kelly Green, and the rules do not allow it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the Cowboys wearing on Thanksgiving 2026?**

The 1960s Classic throwback against the Eagles: white helmet with the vintage star, navy jersey with stars on the shoulders, and white pants with navy stripes. It is on the uniform schedule Dallas published September 2.

**Are the Cowboys wearing throwbacks on Thanksgiving?**

Yes, for the fifth straight year.

**What time is Eagles vs. Cowboys on Thanksgiving?**

Thursday, November 26, at 4:30 p.m. Eastern, 3:30 p.m. Central, at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas.

**What channel is the Cowboys Thanksgiving game on?**

FOX.

**Will the Eagles wear Kelly Green against the Cowboys on Thanksgiving?**

No. The Classic is a navy jersey, so Philadelphia needs to wear white. The Eagles' Kelly Green dates in 2026 are Week 9 against the Giants and Week 16 against the Texans.

**What are the Eagles wearing on Thanksgiving?**

We expect the white road jersey with the midnight green helmet.

**Why do the Cowboys wear a blue jersey on Thanksgiving?**

Because the throwback they wear on Thanksgiving is navy. The rest of the year Dallas wears white at home.

**Why is the Cowboys' Thanksgiving helmet white?**

It copies the white helmet the Cowboys wore in the 1960s. The NFL's alternate-helmet rule lets teams wear a second shell a few times a season, and Dallas uses it on Thanksgiving.

**Why do the Cowboys play on Thanksgiving every year?**

Dallas has hosted a Thanksgiving game nearly every year since 1966, when the team started the tradition to win national attention. Only Detroit has done it longer.

**Do the Cowboys wear the Thanksgiving throwback any other time?**

Not in 2026. Thanksgiving is its only date on the Dallas schedule.

**Do NFL players wear a Madden patch on Thanksgiving?**

Yes. The NFL confirmed on November 19, 2026 that every player in the three Thanksgiving Day games, this one included, wears the [John Madden Thanksgiving patch](/stories/john-madden-thanksgiving-patch-2026): a round patch with a silhouette of Madden in his headset, high on the left chest. It has been worn every Thanksgiving since 2023.
