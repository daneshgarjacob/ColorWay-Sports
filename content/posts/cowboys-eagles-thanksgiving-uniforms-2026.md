**Final: Eagles 39, Cowboys 38.** Dallas wore the **1960s Classic throwback** on Thanksgiving for the fifth year in a row, and for the first time it lost in it. The look was exactly what the Cowboys put on Week 12 when they published all 17 of their 2026 uniform assignments on September 2, and what they posted as their Gameday Threads on November 24: a **white helmet with the navy star**, a **navy jersey with stars on the white shoulders**, and **white pants with two navy stripes**. ESPN's game photos confirm every piece. Because that is a dark jersey, Philadelphia wore **white with midnight green pants** and the midnight green helmet, not Kelly Green. The Eagles trailed 31-10 in the third quarter at AT&T Stadium and won on Jake Elliott's **59-yard field goal with two seconds left**. Every detail of the final is in [what the Cowboys and Eagles wore on Thanksgiving](/stories/cowboys-eagles-thanksgiving-what-they-wore-2026).

## Quick Answers

- **What did the Cowboys wear on Thanksgiving 2026?** The 1960s Classic throwback: white helmet, navy jersey with stars on the shoulders, white pants with navy stripes. It was on the uniform schedule Dallas published September 2, the team posted it as its Week 12 "Gameday Threads" on November 24, and ESPN's game photos confirm it.
- **What did the Eagles wear?** White jerseys with midnight green pants and the midnight green helmet, confirmed by the team's own posts and ESPN's game photos.
- **Did the Eagles wear Kelly Green on Thanksgiving?** No. The Cowboys were in navy, so Philadelphia had to wear white.
- **Who won?** The Eagles, 39-38, on a 59-yard Jake Elliott field goal with two seconds left, Thursday, November 26 on FOX at AT&T Stadium in Arlington.
- **How many Thanksgivings in a row is this?** Five: 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026. The Classic is now 4-1 on the holiday.
- **Our grade:** A- for the matchup.

## What The Cowboys Wore (Worn)

The Classic is a throwback to the Cowboys of the 1960s, and it is the one day a year Dallas changes its silhouette.

- **The helmet.** White, not silver, with the vintage star on the side and a gray facemask. The white shell is the whole trick: it removes the silver that sits under every other Cowboys look.
- **The jersey.** Navy, with oversized stars on a white shoulder yoke and solid block numbers.
- **The pants.** White, with twin navy stripes down the side.

This is also the only home game all season where Dallas wears a dark jersey. The Cowboys wear white at home the rest of the year, which is a story of its own: [why the Cowboys wear white at home](/stories/why-do-the-cowboys-wear-white-at-home). ## What The Eagles Wore (Worn)

Normally Dallas in white at home means the visitor gets to pick a color, and Philadelphia used exactly that opening in 2025 to wear Kelly Green at AT&T Stadium. That was off the table on Thanksgiving. The Classic is a navy jersey, so for this one game Dallas was the dark team and the Eagles wore **white**, with the **midnight green helmet**. Philadelphia confirmed its three 2026 alternate dates on September 4, Kelly Green in Weeks 9 and 16 and the black alternate in Week 15, and Thanksgiving is not one of them. The Eagles have worn both white and midnight green pants with the white jersey this season, and on Thanksgiving it was midnight green: the team showed the white jerseys in its ["Thanksgiving threads" locker-room video](https://x.com/Eagles/status/2125995395587485846) on Thursday morning, and its [first-quarter game photo](https://x.com/Eagles/status/2126081246845354358) shows the midnight green helmet, white jersey and midnight green pants, as do ESPN's game photos. ## The Cowboys' Thanksgiving Uniform History

Dallas has hosted a Thanksgiving game nearly every year since 1966, and for most of that time the holiday has been the place for a throwback. The current run:

- **2022 vs. the Giants:** the Classic, its first Thanksgiving after the NFL allowed alternate helmets again.
- **2023 vs. Washington:** the Classic.
- **2024 vs. the Giants:** the Classic.
- **2025 vs. Kansas City:** the Classic, in a 31-28 Dallas win that averaged 57.23 million viewers on CBS, the most-watched regular-season game in NFL history.
- **2026 vs. Philadelphia:** the Classic, fifth straight year, and its first Thanksgiving loss: Eagles 39, Cowboys 38.

That makes the Classic **4-1 on Thanksgiving** since 2022. In our own [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026), the loss also drops Dallas to 5-7 for the season, 4-1 in its dark and alternate looks and 1-6 in its regular home white.

The white helmet is the reason this works. The NFL's alternate-helmet rule is what brought it back, and Dallas has decided the best use of that rule is one holiday a year. We agree.

## Our Grade

**A-.** The Classic is the best uniform Dallas owns, and putting it against Philadelphia, the division rival Dallas fans care about most, makes this the strongest Thanksgiving matchup of the run. Navy and white with stars against midnight green and white is a clean, dark-versus-light game with no color overlap. What keeps it from an A is the Eagles' side: white is the right call and the only call, but this is the matchup that would have looked best with Kelly Green, and the rules do not allow it. It played to the grade on the field, too: a one-point game decided on the last kick.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the Cowboys wearing on Thanksgiving 2026?**

Dallas wore the 1960s Classic throwback against the Eagles: white helmet with the vintage star, navy jersey with stars on the shoulders, and white pants with navy stripes, confirmed from ESPN's game photos. It was on the uniform schedule Dallas published September 2.

**Are the Cowboys wearing throwbacks on Thanksgiving?**

Yes, for the fifth straight year.

**Who won Eagles vs. Cowboys on Thanksgiving 2026?**

The Eagles, 39-38, on Jake Elliott's 59-yard field goal with two seconds left, after trailing 31-10 in the third quarter.

**What is the Cowboys' record in the Classic on Thanksgiving?**

4-1 since 2022: wins over the Giants, Washington, the Giants and the Chiefs, then the 39-38 loss to the Eagles in 2026.

**What time is Eagles vs. Cowboys on Thanksgiving?**

It kicked off Thursday, November 26, at 4:30 p.m. Eastern, 3:30 p.m. Central, at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas.

**What channel is the Cowboys Thanksgiving game on?**

FOX.

**Will the Eagles wear Kelly Green against the Cowboys on Thanksgiving?**

No. The Classic is a navy jersey, so Philadelphia wore white. The Eagles' Kelly Green dates in 2026 are Week 9 against the Giants and Week 16 against the Texans.

**What are the Eagles wearing on Thanksgiving?**

The white road jersey with midnight green pants and the midnight green helmet, confirmed by the Eagles' Thanksgiving locker-room video, their first-quarter game photo and ESPN's game photos.

**Why do the Cowboys wear a blue jersey on Thanksgiving?**

Because the throwback they wear on Thanksgiving is navy. The rest of the year Dallas wears white at home.

**Why is the Cowboys' Thanksgiving helmet white?**

It copies the white helmet the Cowboys wore in the 1960s. The NFL's alternate-helmet rule lets teams wear a second shell a few times a season, and Dallas uses it on Thanksgiving.

**Why do the Cowboys play on Thanksgiving every year?**

Dallas has hosted a Thanksgiving game nearly every year since 1966, when the team started the tradition to win national attention. Only Detroit has done it longer.

**Do the Cowboys wear the Thanksgiving throwback any other time?**

Not in 2026. Thanksgiving is its only date on the Dallas schedule.

**Do NFL players wear a Madden patch on Thanksgiving?**

Yes. The NFL confirmed on November 19, 2026 that every player in the three Thanksgiving Day games, this one included, wears the [John Madden Thanksgiving patch](/stories/john-madden-thanksgiving-patch-2026): a round patch with a silhouette of Madden in his headset, high on the left chest. It has been worn every Thanksgiving since 2023.
