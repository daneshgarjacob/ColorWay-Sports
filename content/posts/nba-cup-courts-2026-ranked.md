The NBA revealed all 30 **Emirates NBA Cup 2026** courts on October 13 in a [thread on X](https://x.com/NBA/status/2110038191545663961). We graded and ranked every one, counting down from No. 30 to No. 1. Two got an F. One got an A+.

## How the NBA Cup Courts Work

Every Cup court is **painted solid in a team color** with an **oversized tonal wordmark**, the team logo on the **gold NBA Cup trophy** at center court and a gold trophy graphic in each lane. The [NBA confirmed](https://x.com/NBAPR/status/2110039663444427240) all Cup games are played on these painted floors.

The look is **inspired by Nike's "End of Ordinary" NBA Standard Issue Collection**, and teams wear their **Statement Edition uniforms** in every Cup game, per [NBC Sports](https://www.nbcsports.com/nba/news/nba-unveils-nba-cup-courts-bold-colors-are-back-fans-will-know-its-a-cup-game).

Group play starts **October 30**, the quarterfinals are December 4 and 5, and the **final is December 11 at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis** on Prime Video, per [NBA.com](https://www.nba.com/news/emirates-nba-cup-2026-groups-announced). The Knicks are the defending champions, and [ESPN reports](https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/50174098/2026-nba-cup-tournament-court-designs) each winning player gets more than $500,000. Every new jersey and court is in our [NBA uniforms hub](/stories/nba-uniforms-2026-27).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What do the 2026 NBA Cup courts look like?**

Each court is painted solid in team colors, with a big tonal team wordmark and the team logo on a gold NBA Cup trophy at center court.

**Which team has the best NBA Cup court?**

The Utah Jazz, with an A+ for a purple floor and a light blue apron. The Knicks (A) and Pistons (A-) are next.

**Which NBA Cup court is the worst?**

The Milwaukee Bucks (F) are last, and the Houston Rockets (F) are No. 29.

**When were the 2026 NBA Cup courts revealed?**

October 13, 2026, in a team-by-team thread on the NBA's X account.

**When does the 2026 NBA Cup start?**

Friday, October 30, 2026. Group play continues on November 6, 13, 20, 24, 25 and 27.

**When is the 2026 NBA Cup final?**

Friday, December 11, 2026. The quarterfinals are December 4 and 5 and the semifinals are December 8 and 9.

**Where is the NBA Cup final in 2026?**

Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis, streaming on Prime Video.

**Why are NBA Cup courts painted?**

So fans can tell right away that it is a Cup game and not a regular-season game.

**What uniforms do teams wear in NBA Cup games?**

Statement Edition uniforms, in every NBA Cup game.

**What inspired the 2026 NBA Cup court designs?**

Nike's "End of Ordinary" NBA Standard Issue Collection. The big floor wordmarks are styled after the Standard Issue jackets.

**What does "30 Teams. 8 Advance. 1 Champion." mean?**

It is the tournament format printed on every court: 30 teams play group games, eight reach the knockout rounds and one wins the Cup.

**Who won the last NBA Cup?**

The New York Knicks, who beat the San Antonio Spurs in last season's final.

**How much money do NBA Cup winners get?**

More than $500,000 per player on the winning team, according to ESPN.

**Do the Celtics have a parquet floor for the NBA Cup?**

No. The floor is painted kelly green, and the parquet idea is printed on the gray apron.

**Do the Lakers have a gold NBA Cup court?**

Yes. It is a gold floor with a black apron and 17 championship stars on the sideline. We gave it a D+.

The Utah Jazz have the best NBA Cup court of 2026 with an A+, followed by the Knicks and Pistons, whose colors pop. The Bucks and Rockets finished last with F grades. The courts debut October 30, and the final is December 11 at Hinkle Fieldhouse.
