Utah State has worn three different helmets in its first three weeks as a Pac-12 program, and the fourth is already on the calendar. The Aggies opened in the cow print helmet, went to the white shell at Washington, are taking a navy shell to Utah on Saturday, and will bring back their 1992 to 1995 logo for Homecoming against Troy. This is the full Utah State 2026 uniform schedule: the blue home set, the white road set, every helmet in the drawer, the home game themes and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

Nike outfits the Aggies, and the base uniforms did not change much for the move from the Mountain West to the Pac-12 beyond the new conference patch. The helmet drawer is where Utah State has fun. ## The 1992 Retro Helmet for Homecoming

Utah State revealed its Homecoming helmet on August 20: a white shell with the logo the program used from 1992 to 1995, a navy block U with STATE running down the left side and a bull leaping through it, with a small A brand on the bull's hip. It goes on for Homecoming against Troy on September 26 at Maverik Stadium. The jersey and pants for that game have not been announced.

## Why Utah State Wore White at Home

The home team in college football usually picks its color and sends the visitor to white, but it can also choose white, and Utah State did exactly that for the opener. The Ag Day theme asked fans to wear cow print or white, and the team matched it: a white jersey and white pants to set off the white helmet with navy spots. Idaho State wore orange jerseys, so the colors never clashed. With the blue jersey still unused through two games, the October 9 Show Me game against Washington State, a Friday night with the crowd in blue, is our best guess for its debut.

## The Bottom Line

Three weeks, three helmets. Utah State has gone cow print at home, white at Washington, and navy for Saturday's trip to Utah, all over the white jersey, and the Homecoming retro helmet is next on September 26. The blue home jersey has not appeared yet, the Blackout against Colorado State on Halloween is the natural spot for black, and the flex date on November 28 is Senior Day at home. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Utah State wearing this week?**

Utah State plays at Utah on Saturday, September 19 at 1:30 p.m. Mountain on FOX, and the team announced a navy helmet, white jersey and navy pants. The Aggies wore a white helmet, white jersey and navy pants in their last game, a 16-14 loss at Washington on September 12. The week-by-week grid above lists every game with the expected uniform.

**What are Utah State's football uniforms for 2026?**

A navy blue home jersey, a white jersey with a UTAH STATE wordmark and navy sleeve stripes, and black in the closet. Pants come in navy and white, and the Pac-12 patch is new this season. The helmets are a white shell with the navy U State logo, a navy shell with the logo in white, the cow print helmet and a retro homecoming helmet. Nike makes the uniforms.

**Why does Utah State wear a cow print helmet?**

It celebrates the university's agricultural roots. The design began as an April Fools' joke in 2024, fans loved it, and it became a real helmet. Utah State wore it for Ag Day in the 2026 opener against Idaho State, with navy spots hand-placed by the equipment staff so no two helmets match.

**What is Utah State's homecoming helmet in 2026?**

A white helmet with the 1992 to 1995 logo, a bull leaping through a navy block U with STATE down the side. Utah State wears it for Homecoming against Troy on Saturday, September 26 at Maverik Stadium.

**What did Utah State wear against Idaho State?**

The cow print helmet, a white jersey and white pants on September 5 at Maverik Stadium. Idaho State won 29-17.

**Who does Utah State play on November 28?**

A Pac-12 flex game at Maverik Stadium that does not count in the conference standings. The conference sets the matchup late in the season, and Utah State's own home schedule graphic lists Boise State for the date, which is also Senior Day.

**How many games does Utah State play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: six at Maverik Stadium, including the flex game, and six on the road, with a single bye on October 17. It is Utah State's first season in the Pac-12 after thirteen years in the Mountain West.
