**The Lions are 0-5 in their throwback uniform on Thanksgiving.** Fans campaigning for the throwbacks against the Bears this Thursday keep running into the same stat, and we checked every game: Detroit wore the **1934-style throwback** (plain silver helmet with no logo, solid Honolulu blue jersey, silver pants) on Thanksgiving in **2017, 2018, 2020, 2023 and 2025**, and **lost all five**. All five are confirmed from the Gridiron Uniform Database's game-by-game records, and four of them from the Lions' own posts or announcements.

The record is real. It is also not the uniform's fault: in the **other four** Thanksgivings since 2017, in other uniforms, Detroit went **1-3**. Since 2017 the Lions are **1-8 on Thanksgiving** no matter what they wear.

## Quick Answers

- **What is the Lions' record in throwbacks on Thanksgiving?** **0-5** since the current throwback arrived in 2017.
- **Which years?** 2017 (Vikings), 2018 (Bears), 2020 (Texans), 2023 (Packers) and 2025 (Packers).
- **Are 2018 and 2020 really throwback games?** Yes. Gridiron Uniform Database logs the same uniform for 2017, 2018 and 2020, and the Lions' own photos from both games show the logo-free silver helmet and stripeless blue jersey. Neither was the grey Color Rush.
- **Record in other uniforms on Thanksgiving since 2017:** **1-3.** The only win was 2024, in all blue.
- **What are the Lions wearing on Thanksgiving 2026?** **Not announced.** We expect the Honolulu blue home jersey with the silver helmet. In recent years Detroit has announced its Thanksgiving look one to two days before the game.
- **Should they wear the throwbacks?** Yes. Five losses in a uniform is not a curse, and the throwback is the right uniform for this game.

## The Five Throwback Games

### 2017 vs. Vikings: L 30-23

The current throwback was new that season. In July 2017 the Lions [announced](https://www.detroitlions.com/news/lions-to-wear-alternate-uniforms-this-season-19026253) it would be worn on Thanksgiving against Minnesota, describing a solid Honolulu blue jersey with silver block numbers, no sleeve stripes and no shoulder numbers, "recreating the 1934 season uniform." The Vikings won 30-23.

### 2018 vs. Bears: L 23-16

The year that gets disputed. The team's own game-day photo settles it: the plain silver helmet with no logo and the stripeless blue jersey, the same set as 2017. Chicago won 23-16.

## The Four Games Not In Throwbacks

- **2019 vs. Bears, L 24-20:** the grey Color Rush, silver helmet with the logo and grey jersey and pants.
- **2021 vs. Bears, L 16-14:** the regular Honolulu blue home uniform. The Lions posted "home field, home Honolulu blue."
- **2022 vs. Bills, L 28-25:** the grey Color Rush again. "Bringing out the color rush for Thanksgiving," the team posted.
- **2024 vs. Bears, W 23-20:** all blue, which the team called "Blueberries." The only Thanksgiving win in this nine-year stretch.

## What About Before 2017?

The 0-5 stat is about the current throwback, which arrived with the 2017 rebrand. Detroit also wore older throwback designs on Thanksgiving in the 2000s; Sports Illustrated lists 2001 through 2004, 2008 and 2010. Those were different uniforms, and we have not confirmed each one game by game, so they are not in our count.

## What The Lions Are Wearing This Thursday

Detroit hosts the Bears at Ford Field on **Thursday, November 26, at 1 p.m. ET on CBS**, the same matchup as the first Lions Thanksgiving game in 1934. As of Monday the Lions have **not announced** their uniform. Our [Lions 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/lions-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Thanksgiving preview](/stories/lions-bears-thanksgiving-uniforms-2026) have Detroit **expected in the Honolulu blue home jersey with the silver helmet**, because nothing the team has published puts the throwback on the 2026 calendar. The Bears are expected in white. In each of the last three years the Lions announced the Thanksgiving look between Monday and Wednesday of game week, so an answer should come in the next two days. A fan account, @LionsUniforms, has been asking the team daily since Sunday to wear the throwbacks.

## Should They Wear Them? Yes.

The 0-5 is real and it does not matter. The Lions lost those games because of who they were playing and who they were. Three of the five came in 2017, 2018 and 2020, seasons Detroit finished 9-7, 6-10 and 5-11 and missed the playoffs every time. The other two were losses to Green Bay, a division rival that knows them better than anyone. The same stretch has the Lions 1-3 in their other uniforms, including two losses in the grey Color Rush and one in their regular home set. If the throwback is cursed, so is the home jersey.

The case for wearing it is everything else. It is a copy of what Detroit wore in 1934, the year the Lions started the Thanksgiving game, and this year's opponent is the Bears, the same team they played that first Thanksgiving. The full story is in [why the Lions wear 1934 throwbacks on Thanksgiving](/stories/why-do-the-lions-wear-1934-throwbacks-on-thanksgiving). A uniform with that history, on that date, against that opponent, should not be benched over a stat. Wear them, and win in them.
