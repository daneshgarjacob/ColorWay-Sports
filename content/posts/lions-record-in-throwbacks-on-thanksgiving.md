**Thanksgiving 2026 update: no throwback, and the record stays 0-5.** On Thursday, November 26, Detroit wore its standard home set, **Honolulu blue with silver pants and the silver helmet**, as the team posted that morning and as ESPN's game photos confirm, and lost **39-35** to the Bears on a touchdown with 3 seconds left. That game goes in the other column below.

**The Lions are 0-5 in their throwback uniform on Thanksgiving.** Fans campaigning for the throwbacks against the Bears this year kept running into the same stat, and we checked every game: Detroit wore the **1934-style throwback** (plain silver helmet with no logo, solid Honolulu blue jersey, silver pants) on Thanksgiving in **2017, 2018, 2020, 2023 and 2025**, and **lost all five**. All five are confirmed from the Gridiron Uniform Database's game-by-game records, and four of them from the Lions' own posts or announcements.

The record is real. It is also not the uniform's fault: in the **other five** Thanksgivings since 2017, in other uniforms, Detroit went **1-4**. Since 2017 the Lions are **1-9 on Thanksgiving** no matter what they wear. In the **Honolulu blue home jersey** alone, the 2026 look, they are **1-2** over the same span: a loss in 2021, the all-blue win in 2024 and Thursday's loss.

## Quick Answers

- **What is the Lions' record in throwbacks on Thanksgiving?** **0-5** since the current throwback arrived in 2017.
- **Which years?** 2017 (Vikings), 2018 (Bears), 2020 (Texans), 2023 (Packers) and 2025 (Packers).
- **Are 2018 and 2020 really throwback games?** Yes. Gridiron Uniform Database logs the same uniform for 2017, 2018 and 2020, and the Lions' own photos from both games show the logo-free silver helmet and stripeless blue jersey. Neither was the grey Color Rush.
- **Record in other uniforms on Thanksgiving since 2017:** **1-4.** The only win was 2024, in all blue.
- **Record in the Honolulu blue home jersey on Thanksgiving since 2017:** **1-2** (2021 L, 2024 W, 2026 L). With silver pants, as in 2026, it is 0-2.
- **What did the Lions wear on Thanksgiving 2026?** The Honolulu blue home jersey with silver pants and the silver helmet, not the throwback, plus a black "MFF" helmet decal for owner Martha Firestone Ford. The Bears won 39-35.
- **Should they wear the throwbacks?** Yes. It's their history, so they should learn how to win in it.

## The Five Throwback Games

### 2017 vs. Vikings: L 30-23

The current throwback was new that season. In July 2017 the Lions [announced](https://www.detroitlions.com/news/lions-to-wear-alternate-uniforms-this-season-19026253) it would be worn on Thanksgiving against Minnesota, describing a solid Honolulu blue jersey with silver block numbers, no sleeve stripes and no shoulder numbers, "recreating the 1934 season uniform." The Vikings won 30-23.

### 2018 vs. Bears: L 23-16

The year that gets disputed. The team's own game-day photo settles it: the plain silver helmet with no logo and the stripeless blue jersey, the same set as 2017. Chicago won 23-16.

## The Five Games Not In Throwbacks

- **2019 vs. Bears, L 24-20:** the grey Color Rush, silver helmet with the logo and grey jersey and pants.
- **2021 vs. Bears, L 16-14:** the regular Honolulu blue home uniform. The Lions posted "home field, home Honolulu blue."
- **2022 vs. Bills, L 28-25:** the grey Color Rush again. "Bringing out the color rush for Thanksgiving," the team posted.
- **2024 vs. Bears, W 23-20:** all blue, which the team called "Blueberries." The only Thanksgiving win in this ten-year stretch.
- **2026 vs. Bears, L 39-35:** the Honolulu blue home jersey with silver pants and the silver helmet, from the team's ["Thanksgiving Threads" post](https://x.com/Lions/status/2125953219213824130) and ESPN's game photos, with a black "MFF" helmet decal for Martha Firestone Ford. Chicago scored the winner with 3 seconds left.

## What About Before 2017?

The 0-5 stat is about the current throwback, which arrived with the 2017 rebrand. Detroit also wore older throwback designs on Thanksgiving in the 2000s; Sports Illustrated lists 2001 through 2004, 2008 and 2010. Those were different uniforms, and we have not confirmed each one game by game, so they are not in our count.

## What The Lions Wore On Thanksgiving 2026

Detroit hosted the Bears at Ford Field on **Thursday, November 26**, the same matchup as the first Lions Thanksgiving game in 1934, and did **not** wear the throwback. The Lions posted their look that morning, the **silver helmet with the logo, Honolulu blue jersey and silver pants**, and ESPN's game photos confirm it. Chicago wore the navy helmet, white jersey and navy pants and won 39-35. A fan account, @LionsUniforms, had asked the team daily since the Sunday before to wear the throwbacks. Every detail: [what the Lions wore on Thanksgiving 2026](/stories/lions-bears-thanksgiving-uniforms-2026).

## Should They Wear Them? Yes.

We wanted the Lions in the throwbacks this year, and we still want them in it every Thanksgiving. It's their history. This is the uniform Detroit wore in 1934, the year the Lions started the Thanksgiving game, against the same Bears who came to Ford Field in 2026. The full story is in [why the Lions wear 1934 throwbacks on Thanksgiving](/stories/why-do-the-lions-wear-1934-throwbacks-on-thanksgiving).

The 0-5 is no reason to put them away. Detroit is 1-4 in everything else over the same stretch, including Thursday's loss in the regular blue, so the jersey isn't the problem. It's their history, so they should learn how to win in it. Thanksgiving 2027 is a good place to start.
