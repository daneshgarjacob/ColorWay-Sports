Toledo changed helmets before this season even started, and the rest of the closet is deeper than most people outside the MAC realize. This is the full Toledo 2026 uniform schedule: the midnight blue home jersey, the white road set, the gold and anthracite alternates, the new glossy shell, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games, including three weeknight kickoffs and a trip to Sacramento. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

Start with the helmet, because that is the piece that actually changed. Toledo retired its matte midnight blue shell in May and went glossy, keeping the white TOLEDO wordmark with the gold rocket streaking out of it and giving the equipment staff a gold facemask and a blue facemask to choose from. The Rockets posted the new lids from around campus and the Maumee riverfront.

The jerseys are all Nike, and the 2026 update moved the white road jersey onto the same Vapor F.U.S.E. template the midnight blue home, gold alternate and anthracite alternate already used, so every Toledo jersey now carries the same TOLEDO wordmark across the chest. Media day in July gave the clearest look at the two base sets side by side, the blue at home with its gold collar V and the white on the road with gold trim on the numerals.

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; margin: 2em 0;">

There is one more thing hanging over the season. In August the athletic department unveiled the Glass City Edition, an apparel and uniform program built around a new light blue called Glass City Blue and a Glass City Script pulled from the city's flag, outlined in the school's Midnight Blue. Toledo said it will appear at select home events during the season. No football game has been attached to it yet, so it is not in the grid below, but a light blue Toledo football jersey would be the biggest uniform story in the MAC if it lands.

**September 4 at Michigan State.** ★ Confirmed from game photography: **midnight blue helmet, white jersey, midnight blue pants**, the standard road look under Friday night lights at Spartan Stadium. Michigan State wore green over white and won 30-20 in Pat Fitzgerald's first game as the Spartans head coach. You can see the other side of that game on our [Michigan State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/michigan-state-uniform-schedule-2026). One calendar note: ESPN files this game under September 5 because its feed runs on UTC and an 8 p.m. Eastern kickoff lands after midnight there. The correct local date is Friday, September 4.

**September 12 vs. Central Connecticut.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **midnight blue helmet, midnight blue jersey, gold pants**, the classic Glass Bowl combination for a game the school billed as the Biggest Party in Toledo. The Rockets won 63-10.

**September 19 vs. Temple.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **midnight blue helmet with the gold facemask, midnight blue jersey, gold pants**, the same combination the Rockets wore against Central Connecticut a week earlier. The helmet carries the Glass City bumper. Temple answered in all white. Band Day in the Glass Bowl went to overtime and Toledo won 49-48.

**September 26 vs. San Diego State.** Blue expected. A noon start against a Mountain West opponent on UToledo Family Weekend, and the last non-conference home date of the year.

**October 3 at Ball State.** White expected. MAC play opens in Muncie with a kickoff time still to be assigned.

**October 10 vs. Buffalo.** Blue expected. Homecoming, which is the traditional slot for an alternate if one exists, so this is the home date we are watching hardest. Buffalo comes in as the designated visitor, so the Bulls should be in white.

**October 17 at Eastern Michigan.** White expected. A 3:30 p.m. kickoff at Rynearson Stadium.

**October 24 vs. Western Michigan.** Blue expected. Toledo lists this as Senior Day, which makes sense once you look at the calendar: it is the last Saturday home game of the season, because everything after it is a weeknight or a Friday.

**October 31.** Bye. Toledo's only open date of the year, and it lands on Halloween.

**November 4 at Sacramento State.** White expected. This is the strangest date on any MAC schedule in 2026. Sacramento State joined the conference as a football-only affiliate on July 1 after moving up from the FCS, and Toledo drew the cross-country Wednesday night trip to Hornet Stadium in the national MACtion television window.

**November 11 vs. UMass.** Blue expected. A Wednesday night Salute to Service game in the Glass Bowl, and military appreciation nights are another place a special uniform or helmet decal can appear.

**November 20 vs. Bowling Green.** Blue expected. The Battle of I-75 on a Friday night at home, the biggest date on the Toledo calendar and the last home game of the season.

**November 27 at Ohio.** White expected. A noon kickoff in Athens on Black Friday to close the regular season.

## What About the Gold Helmet?

Toledo's gold shell is the one piece of this closet with real history attached, and it usually comes out with the gold or anthracite jerseys rather than on its own. The most famous version was the 2017 MAC Championship Game at Ford Field, when the Rockets announced gold helmets with the midnight blue uniforms the night before kickoff and then won the title.

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; margin: 2em 0;">
  <blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/ToledoFB/status/936658018460229643"></a></blockquote>
</div>

Nothing like that has been announced for 2026, and through two games Toledo has worn the blue helmet both times. If the gold shell or the anthracite jersey shows up this fall, Homecoming on October 10 and the Bowling Green game on November 20 are the two dates that fit.

## The Bottom Line

Two games in, Toledo has used one helmet, two jerseys and two pant colors, which is exactly the pace you would expect from a program that saves its variety for November. Midnight blue is the default in the Glass Bowl, white is locked in on the road under MAC color rules, and the gold jersey, the anthracite jersey and the gold helmet are the wild cards. The real unknown is the Glass City Edition and whether a light blue football jersey is part of it. The grid above has every date with the expected set, and we add a star as each uniform is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Toledo wearing this week?**

Toledo wore the midnight blue helmet with the gold facemask, the midnight blue jersey and gold pants to beat Temple 49-48 in overtime on September 19 in the Glass Bowl, with Temple in all white. It is the same combination the Rockets wore in a 63-10 win over Central Connecticut on September 12. The week-by-week grid above lists every game with the expected uniform.

**What are Toledo's football uniforms for 2026?**

A midnight blue home jersey with a gold TOLEDO wordmark and white numerals outlined in gold, a white road jersey with blue lettering, a gold alternate and an anthracite alternate. Pants come in midnight blue, gold and white. The helmet is a new glossy midnight blue shell with a white TOLEDO wordmark and a gold rocket, with a gold or blue facemask, and Toledo also keeps a white shell and a gold shell. Everything is Nike.

**Did Toledo get new uniforms in 2026?**

Not a full redesign. Toledo switched from a matte midnight blue helmet to a glossy one and moved the white road jersey onto the same Nike Vapor F.U.S.E. template as the rest of the closet. The athletic department also introduced a new custom typeface called 1872 and a Glass City Edition collection built around a light blue called Glass City Blue, which points to a bigger change coming.

**What is the Toledo Glass City Edition uniform?**

An alternate program the athletic department unveiled on August 20, 2026. It introduces Glass City Blue, a light blue drawn from the Toledo city flag and skyline, paired with a Glass City Script outlined in Midnight Blue. Toledo said the look will appear at select home events during the season. No football game date has been announced, so we have not added it to the schedule grid.

**When does Toledo play Bowling Green in 2026?**

Friday, November 20 at 7:30 p.m. Eastern in the Glass Bowl. The Battle of I-75 is Toledo's last home game of the season, and the Rockets are expected in midnight blue with Bowling Green in white.

**Why does Toledo play on Wednesday nights?**

Because it is a MAC program, and the conference built its television identity on midweek games. Toledo has two Wednesday dates in 2026, at Sacramento State on November 4 and at home against UMass on November 11, plus Friday games against Bowling Green on November 20 and at Ohio on November 27.

**How many games does Toledo play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven in the Glass Bowl and five on the road, with a single bye on October 31. Eight of the twelve are MAC games, including the new trip to Sacramento State. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
