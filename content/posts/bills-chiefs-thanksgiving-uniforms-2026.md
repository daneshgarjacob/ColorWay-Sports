**Final: Bills 13, Chiefs 10.** Buffalo wore **royal blue over royal blue pants with the white helmet** on Thanksgiving night, the last of three games on Thanksgiving Day, at the new Highmark Stadium, and won on Tyler Bass's **57-yard field goal as time expired**. Buffalo published the jersey for every 2026 home game before the season, and this one was the **royal blue primary**. On Tuesday, November 24, the Bills posted the full combination as "Thanksgiving Threads": **white helmet, royal blue jersey, royal blue pants and white socks**, a graphic the team labeled "Blue Blue White," and that is what went on the field. Kansas City wore its standard road look, **red helmet, white jersey, red pants**. Every detail of the final is in [what the Bills and Chiefs wore on Thanksgiving](/stories/bills-chiefs-thanksgiving-what-they-wore-2026).

## Quick Answers

- **What did the Bills wear on Thanksgiving 2026?** The royal blue home jersey with royal blue pants, white socks and the white helmet, as the Bills posted on November 24 and as the game footage shows.
- **What did the Chiefs wear?** White jersey, red pants, red helmet.
- **Who won?** The Bills, 13-10, on a 57-yard Tyler Bass field goal as time expired, Thursday, November 26, on NBC at Highmark Stadium in Orchard Park.
- **Did the Bills wear an alternate on Thanksgiving?** No. The red alternate, the gray Nickel City set and the white Cold Front Rivalries uniform all have other dates.
- **Our grade:** B+ for the matchup.

The royal blue primary is the uniform most people picture when they picture the Bills: the **white helmet** with the red charging buffalo and a **royal blue jersey** with white numbers. Buffalo gave it the four biggest non-alternate home dates on its calendar, the Week 2 opener at the new stadium against Detroit, Crucial Catch against New England, Native American Heritage against Baltimore, and Thanksgiving night against Kansas City. Saving the base uniform for the biggest nights is the right call, and opening a new stadium in it was the tell.

It was also the first Thanksgiving game at the new Highmark Stadium. Every Buffalo date is in our [Bills 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/bills-uniform-schedule-2026), and the full grades are in [every Bills jersey, ranked](/stories/bills-jerseys-2026-ranked).

## What The Chiefs Wore (Worn)

Kansas City has worn white on the road all season except at Miami in Week 3, where the Dolphins wore white at home and the Chiefs wore red. With Buffalo in blue, there was no reason to change, and it did not: **red helmet, white jersey, red pants**, as the team's own pregame photos and the game footage show. The Chiefs run the simplest closet in the league, home and road and nothing else, so there is no alternate to wonder about. Our [Chiefs 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/chiefs-uniform-schedule-2026) has every date, including the next Thursday at the Rams on December 3, where Los Angeles wears its gold Classic Sol.

## Both Teams On Thanksgiving

- **Buffalo** last played on Thanksgiving in 2022, a 28-25 win at Detroit on a Tyler Bass field goal in the final seconds. 2026 was its first Thanksgiving at home in the new building, and another last-second Bass field goal won it.
- **Kansas City** played on Thanksgiving last year, losing 31-28 at Dallas in the most-watched regular-season game in NFL history, 57.23 million viewers on CBS. This year the Chiefs got the night slot instead, and lost by three again.

## Our Grade

**B+.** Royal blue against red and white is about as American as football gets on Thanksgiving, and both earned an A- in our [NFL uniform rankings](/stories/best-nfl-uniforms-2026-ranked), Kansas City ninth and Buffalo eleventh. Under the lights in late November in western New York, the colors looked great. What holds it back from an A is that it is two standard uniforms on a holiday where Dallas is in a throwback and the Rams just wore a one-night alternate. Buffalo has a red helmet it saves for holiday games now, and it went to the Christmas Day trip to Denver instead. We would have split them.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the Bills wearing on Thanksgiving 2026?**

Blue over blue: the white helmet, royal blue jersey, royal blue pants and white socks. Buffalo listed the royal blue jersey on its published 2026 home schedule, posted the full combination as "Thanksgiving Threads" on November 24, and wore it.

**Who won Chiefs vs. Bills on Thanksgiving 2026?**

The Bills, 13-10, on Tyler Bass's 57-yard field goal as time expired.

**What time is Chiefs vs. Bills on Thanksgiving?**

It kicked off Thursday, November 26, at 8:20 p.m. Eastern, at Highmark Stadium in Orchard Park, New York.

**What channel is the Thanksgiving night game on?**

NBC, with a stream on Peacock.

**What are the Chiefs wearing on Thanksgiving?**

The red helmet, white jersey and red pants, Kansas City's road look for every trip this season except Miami (and white pants once, at Seattle).

**Are the Bills wearing the red helmet on Thanksgiving?**

No. Buffalo's red helmet is confirmed for the Christmas Day game at Denver, with the white jersey and white pants.

**Do the Bills have an alternate uniform in 2026?**

Three: the red alternate (Week 18 against the Jets), the gray Nickel City set (Weeks 3 and 15) and the all-white Cold Front Rivalries uniform (Week 11 against Miami).

**Is this the first Thanksgiving game at the new Highmark Stadium?**

Yes. The stadium opened this season, with the Week 2 home opener against Detroit.

**Do the Chiefs have an alternate uniform?**

Not in 2026. Kansas City wears red at home and white on the road.

**When did the Bills last play on Thanksgiving?**

2022, a 28-25 win at Detroit.

**Do NFL players wear a special patch on Thanksgiving?**

Yes. The NFL confirmed on November 19, 2026 that every player in the three Thanksgiving Day games, this one included, wears the [John Madden Thanksgiving patch](/stories/john-madden-thanksgiving-patch-2026): a round patch with a silhouette of Madden in his headset, high on the left chest. It has been worn every Thanksgiving since 2023.
