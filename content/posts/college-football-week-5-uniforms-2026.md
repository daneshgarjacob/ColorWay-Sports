Week 5 of the 2026 college football season is the week the pre-announced alternates finally come out of the closet. **Tennessee wears its Smokey Grey Neyland Legacy set against Auburn**, the one-game centennial tribute to General Robert Neyland that the Vols revealed in August. **Texas A&M wears its desert camo Honor and Support uniform against Arkansas** on the day the university turns 150, and **Air Force debuts its matte black B-21 Raider uniform against Navy** in the first leg of the Commander-in-Chief's Trophy. Rutgers has called **The Blackout** for No. 6 Indiana under the lights, and Houston's **Houston Blue** Oilers tribute is lined up for UCF. On top of all that, Northwestern opens the new Ryan Field on Friday night against Penn State, exactly 100 years to the day after the original stadium hosted its first game.

Below is every game this week involving an AP Top 25 team or one of the programs we track in our college uniform schedules, with kickoff time, TV and what each team is expected to wear. A look is marked **Confirmed** when the school itself has announced it, and **Expected** when it has not, in which case we give the set the program normally wears in that spot. As of Thursday night, more than 30 teams on this page have posted or announced their Week 5 look, and we have marked each one Confirmed with the team's own post. **Update, Sunday, October 4:** every game below now has its final score, verified against the line score, and what was actually worn, marked **★ Worn** wherever we could confirm it from the broadcast or the schools' own game photos. The biggest surprises: UConn debuted its red alternate jersey for Homecoming, South Carolina wore black instead of garnet, South Florida went white at home, LSU wore purple instead of white, Kansas wore red and Kentucky brought out a chrome helmet. ESPN and the AP poll call this Week 5, and the rankings below are from this week's AP poll. All times are Eastern. Last week's looks and final scores are in our [Week 4 uniform guide](/stories/college-football-week-4-uniforms-2026).

## Games of the Week

### Auburn at No. 17 Tennessee: Smokey Grey

**Saturday, 3:30 p.m., ESPN.** The best uniform date of the weekend. **Tennessee: Confirmed, Smokey Grey.** Tennessee revealed the 2026 Smokey Grey on August 20 as a tribute to General Robert Neyland, 100 years after his first season as head coach, and named Auburn as the game. The details: Neyland's signature in script across the back helmet bumper, a white star on the shell, Ayres Hall printed in tonal grey across the shoulder yokes, the vintage interlocking UT on the collar and sleeves, orange numbers with a white outline, and cream pants with an orange, cream and grey stripe. It is the first Smokey Grey of the adidas era, and it is worn once. **Auburn: Expected in white**, the road set, which gives us white against grey at Neyland Stadium. More: [Tennessee schedule](/stories/tennessee-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Auburn schedule](/stories/auburn-uniform-schedule-2026). **Final: Tennessee 24, Auburn 14.** The Smokey Grey went on exactly as revealed, and Auburn came in all white.

### Arkansas at Texas A&M: Honor and Support Camo

**Saturday, 7 p.m., ESPN2.** The Southwest Classic at Kyle Field, on the day Texas A&M marks its 150th anniversary. **Texas A&M: Confirmed, Honor and Support camo.** The Aggies revealed the desert camouflage set on August 14 and put exactly one date on it, this one: a camo jersey, a camo helmet with a maroon facemask and the Aggies script, and maroon pants. We graded the whole set in our [Texas A&M camo breakdown](/stories/texas-am-camo-honor-and-support-uniforms-2026). **Arkansas: Expected in white**, the road set, so it is desert tan and maroon against white. More: [Texas A&M schedule](/stories/texas-am-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Arkansas schedule](/stories/arkansas-uniform-schedule-2026). **Final: Texas A&M 34, Arkansas 7.** The camo was worn as announced, against Arkansas in the cardinal helmet over all white.

### Navy at Air Force: B-21 Raider

**Saturday, noon, CBS.** The first leg of the Commander-in-Chief's Trophy, at Falcon Stadium. **Air Force: Confirmed, B-21 Raider.** Air Force and Northrop Grumman announced the 2026 Air Power Legacy Series uniform on August 25 for this game: matte black almost head to toe, a black shell with a dark camouflage wrap and B-21 printed above the facemask, a black jersey with chrome silver numerals and an outlined AIR FORCE wordmark, and patches for the units behind the bomber. Our full grade is in [Air Force's B-21 Raider uniform](/stories/air-force-b21-raider-uniform-2026). **Navy: Expected in white**, the road set under the gold helmet that never changes. Matte black against plain white is about as clean a contrast as this rivalry gets. More: [Air Force schedule](/stories/air-force-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Navy schedule](/stories/navy-uniform-schedule-2026). **Final: Air Force 14, Navy 9.** The B-21 Raider was worn as announced, and Navy came in white under the gold helmet.

### No. 5 Ohio State at No. 14 Iowa

**Saturday, 3:30 p.m., CBS.** College GameDay is in Iowa City, and it is the ANF Black and Gold Spirit Game, with the crowd striping Kinnick in black and gold by section. **Iowa: Expected in black**, the black Tigerhawk helmet, black jersey and gold pants at home. Iowa rarely strays from the classic home set, and a 3:30 kickoff makes a black-pants night look less likely. **Ohio State: Expected in white**, the silver helmet over the white road jersey. Eleven Warriors reported on Wednesday, citing a source, that Ohio State will wear its all white "Sub Zero" alternates in Iowa City, the first all white look with the Buckeye stripes. The Buckeyes post their full combination at 3:30 p.m. Friday, 24 hours before kickoff, so it stays Expected until then. Iowa had not posted as of Thursday night. More: [Ohio State schedule](/stories/ohio-state-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Iowa schedule](/stories/iowa-uniform-schedule-2026). **Final: Ohio State 31, Iowa 14.** Iowa went all black, black pants and all, and Ohio State wore the silver helmet over all white.

### No. 4 Miami at Clemson

**Saturday, 7:30 p.m., ABC.** Prime time in Death Valley and Miami's biggest road test of the first half. **Clemson: Expected in orange**, the orange home set that Clemson wears at Memorial Stadium almost every week. **Miami: Confirmed, all white.** The Hurricanes posted "Business trip attire" on Wednesday: white helmet, white jersey with orange and green trim, white pants, the same road look they wore at Stanford and Wake Forest. With Miami in white the two oranges never touch. More: [Miami schedule](/stories/miami-hurricanes-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Clemson schedule](/stories/clemson-uniform-schedule-2026). **Final: Miami 41, Clemson 13.** Both teams wore what we listed: Miami all white, Clemson in orange over white pants.

### Penn State at Northwestern: The New Ryan Field Opens

**Friday, 8 p.m., FOX.** The new Ryan Field opens on October 2, one hundred years to the day after the original stadium hosted its first game on October 2, 1926. **Northwestern: Confirmed, purple helmet, purple jersey, white pants.** "Classic threads at our new home," the Wildcats posted on Wednesday, so the stadium is the novelty and the uniform is not. The new Purple Gothics alternate keeps its announced debut on October 24 against Rutgers. **Penn State: Expected in white**, the road set in its first adidas season. ## Alternates and Special Looks This Week

- **Tennessee, Smokey Grey Neyland Legacy (Confirmed).** Grey jersey with Ayres Hall on the shoulders, orange numbers, cream pants, Neyland's signature on the helmet bumper, against Auburn.
- **Texas A&M, Honor and Support camo (Confirmed).** Camo helmet, camo jersey, maroon pants against Arkansas on the university's 150th anniversary.
- **Air Force, B-21 Raider (Confirmed).** Matte black with a camouflage helmet wrap and chrome silver numerals against Navy.
- **Wisconsin, Red Out (Confirmed).** The alternate red helmet with a large white Motion W, red jersey with white numbers, red pants and red cleats for Homecoming against Michigan State.
- **North Carolina, 1980 throwbacks (Confirmed).** Powder blue helmet with the stairstep UNC, powder blue jersey with "Carolina" across the chest, white pants against No. 3 Notre Dame.

- **Houston, Houston Blue (Confirmed).** The program's official UCF game preview counts a win as Houston's second in Houston Blue: light blue jersey with red numbers, white helmet with the light blue Houston script, white pants with a red stripe, for the Built By Houston Game.
- **Rutgers, The Blackout (Confirmed).** Black helmets and black uniforms for Homecoming against Indiana, per the school's Blackout announcement.
- **Iowa State, Homecoming all cardinal (Confirmed).** Cardinal helmet with "Cyclones" in script on the side, cardinal jersey, cardinal pants against West Virginia.
- **Arizona State, Maroon Monsoon (Confirmed).** A maroon chrome helmet with the gold pitchfork over the maroon jersey for the Big 12 home opener against Baylor.
- **Utah State, the Jumpin' Cow (Confirmed).** All white with the throwback Jumpin' Cow logo on the white helmet at Boise State.
- **Wake Forest, all black (Confirmed).** Black helmet with the gold Deacs script, black jersey, black pants against Stanford.
- **UAB, Smile-A-Mile names (Confirmed).** For the fourth annual Smile-A-Mile childhood cancer game against Samford, every Blazer wears the name of a Smile-A-Mile patient on the back of his jersey.
- **Ohio State, Sub Zero (Reported).** Eleven Warriors reported the all white alternate for Iowa; the official reveal is Friday afternoon.
- **Colorado, Homecoming (watch).** The university's 150th anniversary and a black and gold stripe out against Texas Tech. Black is expected, and it is the likeliest home date for a gold-on-gold look.
- **Illinois, Hail to the Orange Out (watch).** Homecoming against Purdue for the Cannon. Navy is expected, but the all orange set is a real possibility.

**Coming up:** Oregon is off this week. The Ducks' new [Origin of Speed uniform](/stories/oregon-origin-of-speed-uniforms-2026), a Hayward Field tribute in Night Forest with Green Strike speed-streak wings, debuts October 10 against UCLA.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are college football teams wearing in Week 5?**

Most top teams are in standard sets for Week 5: home teams in color and visitors in white. The confirmed specials are Tennessee's Smokey Grey Neyland Legacy uniform against Auburn, Texas A&M's Honor and Support camo against Arkansas, Air Force's B-21 Raider uniform against Navy, Florida's orange helmet, white jersey and blue pants at Missouri from its published season schedule, Wisconsin's all-red Red Out with the red helmet against Michigan State, and North Carolina's 1980 throwbacks against Notre Dame. Rutgers is confirmed in black helmets and black uniforms for The Blackout, and Houston in Houston Blue against UCF. Iowa State goes all cardinal for Homecoming, Arizona State brings a maroon chrome helmet, Utah State wears its Jumpin' Cow throwback logo, Wake Forest is all black and Miami is all white at Clemson. Every game is listed above with its confirmed or expected uniform.

**What is Tennessee wearing against Auburn?**

Tennessee wears its 2026 Smokey Grey uniform against Auburn on Saturday, October 3, at Neyland Stadium. The set honors General Robert Neyland 100 years after his first season: grey jersey with Ayres Hall printed on the shoulders, orange numbers with a white outline, cream pants, a white star on the helmet and Neyland's signature on the back bumper. It is worn once. Auburn is expected in white.

**Why is Texas A&M wearing camo against Arkansas?**

The desert camo uniform is Texas A&M's Honor and Support set, and the Aggies scheduled it for one game only, Arkansas on October 3, the same day the university celebrates its 150th anniversary. It is a camo helmet and camo jersey over maroon pants. Texas A&M revealed it on August 14.

**What is Wisconsin wearing against Michigan State?**

The Red Out, which Wisconsin confirmed on October 1: an alternate red helmet with a large white Motion W, white stripes and a white facemask, a red jersey with white numbers, red pants and red cleats, for Homecoming at Camp Randall Stadium. Michigan State is expected in white.

**What is North Carolina wearing against Notre Dame?**

The 1980 throwbacks, confirmed by the program on October 1: a powder blue helmet with the stairstep UNC logo, a powder blue jersey with "Carolina" across the chest and white pants. It honors the 1980 ACC championship team in the 100th season of Kenan Stadium. Notre Dame is expected in white with the gold helmet.

**What is Air Force wearing against Navy?**

Air Force wears its B-21 Raider uniform from the Air Power Legacy Series against Navy on October 3: matte black with a camouflage helmet wrap, B-21 above the facemask and chrome silver numerals. It was announced on August 25. Navy is expected in white with its gold helmet.

**What is Rutgers wearing against Indiana?**

Rutgers has named the Indiana game The Blackout, for Homecoming and Family Weekend at 8 p.m. on October 3, and the school has confirmed that the team will bring out its black uniforms and helmets, the all-black set built for the Blackout. Indiana is expected in white.

**What is Miami wearing against Clemson?**

All white. Miami posted its Week 5 look on September 30: white helmet, white jersey and white pants, the same road set it wore at Stanford and Wake Forest. Clemson is expected in orange at home on Saturday night.

**What is Ohio State wearing against Iowa?**

Eleven Warriors reported, citing a source, that Ohio State will wear its all white Sub Zero alternates at Iowa. The Buckeyes post their full combination at 3:30 p.m. Eastern on Friday, so we have it as reported, not confirmed, until then.

**Is Houston wearing Houston Blue against UCF?**

Yes. Houston has branded the October 3 UCF game the Built By Houston Game with a Wear Houston Blue theme, and the program's official game preview counts a win as its second in Houston Blue. It is the fourth time Houston has worn the light blue Oilers tribute set. UCF is expected in white.

**Is Oregon playing in Week 5?**

No. Oregon has a bye on October 3. The Ducks return October 10 against UCLA in the new [Origin of Speed uniform](/stories/oregon-origin-of-speed-uniforms-2026). Texas, Ole Miss, Utah, Oklahoma State and UCLA are also off.
