The Cardinals run one of the most disciplined uniform rotations in all of baseball, and that is exactly what makes them so satisfying to rank. There are no random alternates thrown on for a Tuesday afternoon and no closet full of gimmicks. St. Louis ties a specific look to a specific day and leaves the whole thing alone, and for 2026 they cleaned it up even further, bringing back the classic Majestic-style cut and restoring real braid piping on the throwbacks.

What you are left with is five uniforms that range from very good to genuinely great, plus one bold swing that the whole fan base still argues about. Here is every St. Louis Cardinals jersey and uniform for 2026, ranked worst to first and graded.

"The Lou" is the boldest thing St. Louis owns and the one look we keep coming back to with mixed feelings. It carries real weight on every panel: the chest reads "The Lou" in chain-stitch as a nod to the nickname Nelly gave the city, and Nelly himself co-designed it, with red pinstripes for the Mississippi River and a fleur-de-lis from the city flag on the sleeve. It is also a genuine piece of history, the first red jersey the franchise has ever worn in the regular season, which is wild for a team whose entire identity is the color red.

We respect the storytelling more than we expected to. As a jersey to actually look at, though, all-red on red with the red cap is a lot, and it is the one Cardinals look we do not reach for. It lands at a B-, and that is not a knock so much as a reflection of how strong everything ahead of it is.

The road gray is the quiet workhorse of the whole rotation. It swaps the birds-on-the-bat for a clean "St. Louis" script, keeps the navy cap, and travels for nearly every road game that is not a Saturday. There is nothing flashy here and nothing that needs fixing. It exists to do a job and it does it without any clutter.

It sits at a B+ for a simple reason: it is very good, but it is the most replaceable-feeling look in a closet full of standouts. When the cream, the powder blue, and that home white are all sharing the same rotation, a solid gray is going to be the one that grades a touch lower. That is a compliment to everything else more than a hit on this.

Powder blue is undefeated and the "Victory Blue" is proof. It is a throwback to the road look the Cardinals wore from 1976 to 1984, revived as a Saturday-road alternate, and it comes with the one piece of headwear that breaks the navy pattern, a red cap that finishes it off perfectly. For 2026 the team restored the real braid piping here too, sharpening a jersey that already had plenty of nostalgia behind it.

We love this one, and an A- is high praise. It gives St. Louis a genuine splash of color on the road without ever tipping into costume territory, and tying it to Saturdays keeps it feeling special instead of routine. It only misses the A tier because the cream does the vintage thing just slightly better, and because a powder-blue road alternate, as good as it is, still lives one notch below the timeless stuff at the top of this list.

The cream alternate is the standout of the entire standard set. It carries the "St. Louis" script, leans hard into a vintage, Stan Musial-era feel, and the restored braid piping makes it sing up close, the kind of old-school craftsmanship the off-white fabric was built for. Reserved for Saturday home games at Busch Stadium, it reads completely differently under natural daylight than the home white does, which is exactly why giving it one designated day a week works.

This is the Cardinals at their most timeless, and it earns a clean A. We would happily see it more than once a week if the system allowed it, but part of what makes it this good is the restraint. A weekly cream keeps its charm in a way an everyday jersey never could.

This is the one you picture when you think of St. Louis baseball, and it is our favorite look the team owns. The two cardinals perched on the yellow bat have anchored this franchise for generations, the red script sits perfectly against the clean white, and there is not a single thing we would change about the jersey itself. It is a top-five uniform in all of baseball, full stop.

Here is where the grade gets interesting, and it comes down to the cap. When the Cardinals pair the home white with the classic navy "StL" cap, this is an A+, one of the best-looking uniforms in the sport and the exact look that makes us love this team's restraint. When they swap in the red cap instead, it drops to an A- for us. Still excellent, just a notch busier, with the red up top pulling attention away from the birds on the bat. Average the two out and the home white lands at a rock-solid A overall, but make no mistake: with the blue hat on, nothing else in this closet touches it.

## The Bottom Line on the Cardinals Jersey Rankings

There is not a bad jersey in this closet, which is the whole point of St. Louis. The birds-on-the-bat home white is a top-five uniform in baseball and gets even better with the navy cap on top, the cream and the powder blue are two of the best throwback alternates any team runs, and even the road gray is a clean, correct look that most teams would be glad to own.

The City Connect finishes last, and we mean that gently. "The Lou" is a fun, meaningful swing with real St. Louis storytelling behind it, but all-red on red is the one time a year the Cardinals step away from the restraint that makes everything else so good. When the rest of the rotation is this timeless, the boldest look is always going to be the one fighting uphill.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many jerseys do the Cardinals have in 2026?**

Five in active rotation: the birds-on-the-bat home white, the road gray with the "St. Louis" script, the cream Saturday-home alternate, the powder blue "Victory Blue" Saturday-road throwback, and the all-red "The Lou" City Connect. It is one of the most disciplined, day-by-day uniform systems in baseball.

**What is the best Cardinals jersey?**

We rank the home white first and grade it an A overall, rising to an A+ when it is paired with the classic navy "StL" cap. The two cardinals on the yellow bat make it a top-five uniform in all of baseball, and it is the standard every other look in the closet is measured against.

**Why is the Cardinals City Connect ranked last?**

We grade "The Lou" a B-, the lowest mark in the closet. It carries real meaning, chain-stitched for the city with red pinstripes for the Mississippi and a fleur-de-lis on the sleeve, and it is the first red jersey in franchise history. But all-red on red with the red cap is a lot to look at, and it is a clear step below the five classic looks ahead of it.

**What is the Cardinals "Victory Blue" jersey?**

It is a powder-blue road alternate, a throwback to the look St. Louis wore from 1976 to 1984, worn for Saturday road games and paired with a red cap. The 2026 version restored real braid piping, and we grade it an A-.
