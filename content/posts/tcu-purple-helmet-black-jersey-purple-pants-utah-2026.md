**TCU is wearing purple and black for its home finale.** The Horned Frogs posted their uniform for No. 2 Utah on Friday morning, November 20, with four photos and the caption "🟪 & ⬛️ under The Carter's lights." It is a **purple helmet, a black jersey and purple pants**. Kickoff at Amon G. Carter Stadium is **Saturday, November 21, at 9 p.m. Eastern (8 p.m. Central) on FOX**.

## What TCU Is Wearing Against Utah

TCU shot all four photos under heavy purple light, so we went piece by piece. Here is everything we can see:

- **Helmet:** **glossy purple**, with **TCU in white block letters** on the side over a dark **horned frog head**. The front bumper reads **TCU in purple letters outlined in white**, with a **white horned frog head** on each side of it. The facemask is dark.
- **Jersey:** **black**, with **white numbers trimmed in purple** on the chest and white numbers on the shoulders, a small TCU wordmark under the collar, the Big 12 patch on the chest and a white Nike swoosh.
- **Pants:** **purple**. The photos crop at the thigh, so we cannot see whether the pants carry a stripe.
- **Gloves:** black with purple trim, with a **white horned frog** across the back of the hand.

It is not the all-black look from Halloween against Kansas, which put a black helmet over black pants, and it is not the Frog Horn black from Dublin, which had a purple collar ringed in spikes. Here the black sits in the middle and purple caps both ends.

## When Has TCU Worn This Before?

The **purple helmet over the black jersey** came out once already in 2026, for **Homecoming against West Virginia on October 24**, confirmed from the team's game photos. TCU lost that one 24-20. Saturday is the second time this season.

Every look TCU has worn this year is on our [TCU 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/tcu-uniform-schedule-2026), including the [Freedom Frogs white helmet](/stories/tcu-freedom-frogs-white-helmet-horned-frog-kansas-state-2026) from last week's win over Kansas State.

## Our Grade: A-

This is really nice. Purple and black is the best color pairing TCU has, and putting the black in the middle lets the purple helmet and pants frame it instead of getting lost. The white block TCU on the helmet and the white numbers are the only bright pieces, so your eye goes right where it should. Under the lights at the Carter, on FOX in prime time against the No. 2 team in the country, it is going to look great.

It stops short of an A only because we have seen the helmet and jersey together already this year. It is still one of the best looks TCU has worn in 2026.
