The 2026-27 NBA season tipped off on Tuesday, October 20, and it brings a lot of new looks. Two teams rebranded. Nike added a fifth uniform for the eight oldest franchises. Four former ABA teams went back to the 1970s for the 50th anniversary of the merger. Every team brought back a favorite City Edition, every team got a painted NBA Cup court, and nine teams changed the sponsor patch on the front of the jersey.

This page is every one of those items in one place. For each one we give you what it is, our grade where we have one, and **when** you will see it on the floor, taken from the NBA's official game-by-game uniform schedule published October 19. Each item links to our full post with the photos.

For the season hub, start with our [NBA Uniforms 2026-27 guide](/stories/nba-uniforms-2026-27). For every jersey on every date, see the [NBA uniform schedule for 2026-27](/stories/nba-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

## The Big Three Changes

### Minnesota Timberwolves Rebrand: A+

Minnesota brought back the brighter blue and green of its 1989 expansion era, the WOLVES wordmark, the pine tree print and a modernized wolf head crest. We graded the full rebrand an **A+**: A+ for the white Association, A+ for the blue Icon and A for the black Statement, where the tree print wraps the collar, the yokes and the shorts. The Wolves debut the blue Icon on Opening Night, **Wednesday, October 21 at Miami**, and the black Statement first appears **Friday, October 30 vs the Clippers**. Full review: [Timberwolves new uniforms, officially released](/stories/timberwolves-officially-release-new-uniforms-2026-27).

### Houston Rockets Rebrand: B

Houston went back to ketchup and mustard. The rebrand brings a new Nose Cone badge logo, an updated R icon, the Dunkstronaut as a permanent secondary mark and Championship Yellow on the uniform for the first time in 30 years. We graded the white, red and black jerseys a **B+** each and the full package a **B**, because the badge logo holds it back. The Rockets open in the new red Icon at home **Wednesday, October 21 vs Dallas**, and the black Statement debuts **Saturday, October 31 vs Oklahoma City**. Full review: [Rockets 2026-27 rebrand, graded](/stories/houston-rockets-2026-rebrand-graded).

### The Specter Edition: D+ as a Collection

Nike's new Specter Edition is a fifth uniform for the eight oldest franchises: the Lakers, Celtics, Warriors, Knicks, 76ers, Pistons, Hawks and Kings. These are the dark designs that leaked in the spring as "Christmas jerseys." We graded the collection a **D+**, with Detroit on top at an **A** and the Lakers last at a **C**. All 24 Specter games are home games between **Sunday, November 29 and Tuesday, December 29**. Full breakdown: [NBA Specter Edition jerseys](/stories/nba-specter-edition-jerseys-2026-27).

## New Logos

**Houston Rockets.** The new Nose Cone global logo is an inverted-triangle badge inspired by space mission patches, with the HOUSTON ROCKETS wordmark above an updated R icon. The Dunkstronaut is now a permanent secondary mark. We are not fans of badge logos, which is a big part of why the rebrand lands at a B. [Rockets rebrand](/stories/houston-rockets-2026-rebrand-graded).

**Minnesota Timberwolves.** The new crest is a modernized take on the original wolf head: a howling white wolf inside a silver basketball with a green star, ringed in the throwback green. It is the center of the A+ rebrand. [Timberwolves release](/stories/timberwolves-officially-release-new-uniforms-2026-27) and [the logo leak](/stories/timberwolves-leaked-new-logo-2026).

**2027 NBA All-Star logo.** Phoenix hosts the 2027 All-Star Game on **Sunday, February 21, 2027** at Mortgage Matchup Center. The logo, unveiled February 15, 2026, uses the Suns sunburst in a sunset palette with four stars for the city's four All-Star Games: 1975, 1995, 2009 and 2027, per the [NBA's All-Star 2027 page](https://nbaevents.nba.com/allstar2027) and [azfamily](https://www.azfamily.com/2026/02/16/phoenix-host-76th-annual-nba-all-star-game-2027/). For every logo before it, see our [NBA All-Star logo history](/stories/nba-all-star-logo-history-2010-2026).

## New Courts

**NBA Cup courts.** All 30 teams play Cup games on a floor painted solid in a team color with an oversized tonal wordmark, inspired by Nike's "End of Ordinary" Standard Issue collection. Our top five: the **Jazz (A+)**, **Knicks (A)**, **Pistons (A-)**, **Magic (B+)** and **76ers (B)**. The **Rockets** and **Bucks** both got an **F**. Group play starts **Friday, October 30**, and the final is **Friday, December 11 at Hinkle Fieldhouse** in Indianapolis. [All 30 Cup courts, ranked](/stories/nba-cup-courts-2026-ranked).

**ABA classic courts.** Each of the four ABA teams has a new classic floor. The Nets and Spurs earned an **A+**, with San Antonio's SAN ANTONIO TEXAS center circle the standout, the Pacers' plaid-lane floor an **A**, and the Nuggets' orange court an **A-**. [Classic Edition courts](/stories/nba-classic-edition-jerseys-courts-2026-27).

**Timberwolves.** A new Core Court with the wolf head at center and a gray key (**A+**), plus a Statement Court with the WOLVES wordmark across stained dark wood (**A-**). [Timberwolves release](/stories/timberwolves-officially-release-new-uniforms-2026-27).

**Rockets.** Two new Toyota Center floors: the Core Court (**C+**) and the Dunkstronaut Statement Court (**C**). [Rockets courts, graded](/stories/houston-rockets-court-2026-27-graded).

**Nuggets.** The regular Ball Arena floor dropped the navy borders, added mountains and shrank the center logo. We graded it a **C**. [Nuggets court](/stories/nuggets-court-2026-27).

**Lakers.** The new floor with the LA logo is at the UCLA Health Training Center, the practice facility, not the game floor at Crypto.com Arena. It is very dark, and we gave it a **C+**. [Lakers new court](/stories/lakers-new-court-2026).

**Heat.** Miami plays on a custom Vice City court for one night, November 18. It has not been revealed yet.
