**The Chicago Blackhawks wore their Centennial jersey on Tuesday night, 100 years to the day after their first NHL game, and beat the Toronto Maple Leafs 6-3 at the United Center.**

The Black Hawks, as the name was spelled then, played their first game on Nov. 17, 1926, against the Toronto St. Pats, the team that became the Maple Leafs a few months later. Chicago won that one 4-1 at the Chicago Coliseum. On Nov. 17, 2026, the same two franchises met again, and Chicago won again.

## Quick Answers

- **What did the Blackhawks wear on Nov. 17, 2026?** The Centennial jersey from their 100th season (2025-26): red with black and white stripes, a crest and numbers outlined in gold, a lace-up collar and a Centennial patch on the shoulder.
- **Why that night?** It was 100 years to the day since the franchise's first NHL game, against the Toronto St. Pats.
- **What did Toronto wear?** Its white road sweater.
- **Who won?** Chicago, 6-3. Frank Nazar and Tyler Bertuzzi scored twice each.
- **Is it a new jersey?** No. It was Chicago's home jersey for every home game last season except the black alternate nights. It had not been worn this season until Tuesday.
- **Why was it Patrick Kane's first time in it?** Kane played for Detroit last season. He signed back with Chicago on July 23, 2026.
- **Our matchup grade:** A.

## The Jersey

From a distance, the Centennial jersey looks like the regular Blackhawks red. That is on purpose. The team built it on the classic home sweater: a red body, black-white-black stripes on the sleeves and hem, and the chain-stitched Chief Black Hawk crest. One fan reply under the NHL's post put it simply: "Dawg these look the same."

The differences are in the details, and most of them are gold. From the team's unveiling in June 2025:

- **Crest:** still chain-stitched, now outlined in a thin line of **metallic gold**. The team says this honors the gold-trimmed sweaters from the earliest days of the franchise.
- **Numbers:** the back numbers are outlined in gold too.
- **Shoulders:** a **Centennial patch** on one shoulder and the secondary logo in metallic gold on the other.
- **Collar:** a **lace-up collar**, with "Always an Original" written inside.
- **Hem:** the six Stanley Cup years printed inside, plus a gold Centennial jock tag.

The team made it for one season only, the 2025-26 centennial year, and it was the home sweater at the United Center all of last season. This season the standard red returned. Tuesday was the one-night comeback.

The game, per ESPN's play-by-play and the AP recap:

- **Toronto 1-0:** Easton Cowan at 3:22 of the first period.
- **1-1:** Frank Nazar at 13:17 of the second.
- **Chicago 2-1:** Nazar again at 17:54 of the second.
- **2-2:** Teddy Blueger at 18:16 of the second.
- **Chicago 3-2:** Connor Bedard at 18:51 of the second. It was the winner.
- **Chicago 4-2:** Tyler Bertuzzi tipped one in at 9:07 of the third. Patrick Kane had an assist.
- **4-3:** John Tavares at 11:09 of the third.
- **Chicago 5-3:** Oliver Moore 12 seconds later.
- **Chicago 6-3:** Bertuzzi again at 12:10.

Sam Rinzel had three assists and Spencer Knight made 31 saves. The crowd was 19,988. Chicago is now 6-13-2. Toronto has lost seven in a row, its longest skid since March 2015.

The bad news: Bedard left 54 seconds into the third period after a faceoff and did not return. The AP reported that his right hand was hit by Nick Paul's stick. Bedard missed nearly a month last season with a right shoulder injury from a faceoff.

Patrick Kane spent the 2025-26 season, Chicago's centennial year, with the Detroit Red Wings. On July 23, 2026, he signed a two-year, $16 million contract to come back to the Blackhawks, the team he won three Stanley Cups with. So Tuesday was the first time he wore the Centennial jersey.

Blackhawks reporter Mario Tirabassi called it "the one and only time" Kane will wear it. The same goes for anyone who joined Chicago this season. Tirabassi pointed out it was also the first Centennial game for Roman Kantserov, Cole Smith and Ian Cole.

Kane picked up an assist on Bertuzzi's first goal, his 10th assist of the season.

## Is This The Last Time?

Probably. The team built the Centennial jersey as a one-season design and has not announced another date for it. Fans and reporters covering the team treated Tuesday as the jersey's final game. If Chicago brings it back again, we will add the date to the [Blackhawks uniform schedule](/stories/blackhawks-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

## The 1926 Class

Three NHL teams joined the league for the 1926-27 season, and all three are marking 100 years this week:

- **New York Rangers:** first game Nov. 16, 1926, a 1-0 win over the Montreal Maroons. They wore their lighter blue Centennial jersey on Nov. 16, 2026, and beat Montreal 3-1. [Read the story](/stories/rangers-centennial-jerseys-100th-anniversary-canadiens-2026).
- **Chicago Black Hawks:** first game Nov. 17, 1926, a 4-1 win over the Toronto St. Pats. They wore the Centennial jersey on Nov. 17, 2026, and beat Toronto 6-3.
- **Detroit Cougars (now the Red Wings):** first game Nov. 18, 1926, a 2-0 loss to the Boston Bruins in Windsor, Ontario. Detroit hosted Boston again on Nov. 18, 2026, in its red Centennial sweater, and Boston won 4-1. [What the Cougars wore in 1926](/stories/red-wings-100-years-first-game-bruins-detroit-cougars-uniforms).

That is three Original Six anniversaries in three nights, and two centennial jerseys back on the ice.

## Our Take

The Centennial jersey is the best kind of anniversary jersey. It does not try to be something new. It takes the best sweater in hockey and adds a little gold, a lace-up collar and the Cup years inside the hem. Wearing it on the exact date of the first game, against the franchise Chicago played that night, is how this should be done.

The gold trim is easy to miss on TV, and that is the one knock. **Matchup grade: A**, the same as the Rangers' Centennial night against Montreal. Every grade is on the [NHL uniform tracker](/stories/nhl-uniform-tracker-2026-27).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey did the Blackhawks wear against the Maple Leafs on Nov. 17, 2026?**

The Centennial jersey from their 100th season: red with black and white stripes, a gold-outlined crest and numbers, a lace-up collar and a Centennial shoulder patch. Toronto wore white.

**Why did the Blackhawks wear the Centennial jersey on Nov. 17?**

The franchise played its first NHL game on Nov. 17, 1926. Tuesday's game was exactly 100 years later.

**Who did the Blackhawks play in their first ever game?**

The Toronto St. Pats, at the Chicago Coliseum. Chicago won 4-1. The St. Pats became the Toronto Maple Leafs a few months later.

**Did the Blackhawks win the 100th anniversary game?**

Yes, 6-3 over the Maple Leafs. Frank Nazar and Tyler Bertuzzi each scored twice, and Connor Bedard and Oliver Moore scored once.

**What is the difference between the Blackhawks Centennial jersey and the regular home jersey?**

The body and stripes are the same. The Centennial adds a gold outline around the crest and the numbers, a gold secondary logo on one shoulder, a Centennial patch on the other, a lace-up collar and the six Stanley Cup years printed inside the hem.

**When did the Blackhawks first wear the Centennial jersey?**

It was unveiled in June 2025 and debuted at the 2025-26 home opener. It was Chicago's home jersey for that season.

**Will the Blackhawks wear the Centennial jersey again?**

The team has not announced another date. It was made for the 2025-26 season only, and people covering the team treated Tuesday as its last game.

**Why had Patrick Kane never worn the Centennial jersey?**

He played for the Detroit Red Wings in 2025-26, the season Chicago wore it. He signed back with the Blackhawks on July 23, 2026, on a two-year, $16 million deal.

**What happened to Connor Bedard against Toronto?**

He scored the winning goal late in the second period, then left 54 seconds into the third after a faceoff and did not return. The AP reported his right hand was hit by a stick.

**Which other NHL teams are turning 100 this season?**

The New York Rangers (first game Nov. 16, 1926) and the Detroit Red Wings, who started as the Detroit Cougars on Nov. 18, 1926.

**Who designed the original Black Hawks sweaters?**

Irene Castle, the dancer married to team founder Frederic McLaughlin, is credited with the original black and white sweaters and the first Black Hawk crest.
