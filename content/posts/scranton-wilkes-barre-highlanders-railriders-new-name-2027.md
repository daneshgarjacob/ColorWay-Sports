**The Scranton/Wilkes-Barre RailRiders are now the Scranton/Wilkes-Barre Highlanders.** The New York Yankees' Triple-A affiliate unveiled its new name, logos and uniforms on **Saturday, November 7**, at a fall festival at **PNC Field** in Moosic, Pennsylvania. The **SWB Highlanders** take the field for the first time in the **2027 season**, with the first home game on **April 6 against Lehigh Valley**.

The name is a callback. Before the Yankees were the Yankees, they were the **New York Highlanders**, and the club says today's SWB Highlanders "are becoming tomorrow's Bronx Bombers."

## Quick Answers

- **What is the RailRiders' new name?** The Scranton/Wilkes-Barre Highlanders, or SWB Highlanders.
- **When was it announced?** November 7, 2026, at The Adventure Begins Fall Festival at PNC Field.
- **Why did the RailRiders change their name?** The team announced in August that it would retire the RailRiders name after 2026 and build a new identity around the region's outdoors and its Yankees connection.
- **What does Highlanders mean?** Two things: the wooded hills around PNC Field, and the New York Highlanders, the Yankees' name from 1903 to 1912.
- **What are the new colors?** Navy, light blue, green, gray and white, from the team's reveal graphics.
- **Who is the new mascot?** A bear, still unnamed, in a cap with an H, carrying a hiking pack and a bat as a walking stick. CHAMP stays too.
- **When do the Highlanders debut?** The 2027 season. The first home game is April 6, 2027, at 4:05 p.m. against Lehigh Valley.

## Why Did The RailRiders Change Their Name?

The team announced on **August 11, 2026**, that it would retire the **RailRiders** name after the 2026 season and unveil a new identity in November. The RailRiders name had been in place **since 2013**, a run that included the **2016 International League title and Triple-A National Championship** and a future three-time American League MVP in Aaron Judge.

Team president and general manager **Shawn Reilly** said in August that the rebrand was about "building an identity that our community can rally around for generations to come," rooted in the region "while honoring the incredible tradition of baseball that has defined this franchise for decades."

In the November release, the club also pointed to the wider trend in Minor League Baseball, with teams taking on "bold and distinctive identities" built around local culture.

## What Does Highlanders Mean?

The team says the name has a **dual meaning**:

1. **The place.** PNC Field sits among wooded hills and trails in Northeastern Pennsylvania. The identity is built around NEPA's mountains, water and outdoor life.
2. **The Yankees.** The **New York Highlanders** played at **Hilltop Park** in Manhattan's Washington Heights from **1903 to 1912**, before the franchise became the Yankees.

"Before they were the Yankees, they were the Highlanders," Reilly said at the reveal. "Once again, you are a Highlander before you are a Yankee!"

The Yankees' own pinstripe tradition still runs through the new set. Why the big club wears no names on the back is in [our explainer](/stories/why-yankees-have-no-names-on-jerseys), and every look the Yankees wore in 2026 is in the [Yankees uniform schedule](/stories/yankees-uniform-schedule-2026).

## The New Highlanders Logo

The primary logo, from the team's reveal graphic, is a **shield** with a gray outline. **SCRANTON/WILKES-BARRE** runs across the top in **green**, and **HIGHLANDERS** is set in white block letters on **navy**. Below it, the new **bear mascot** looks out from under a light blue cap with an **H**, with a rolled bedroll and a pack on his back, a bat over his shoulder and **green pine trees** behind him.

There is also a stand-alone **wordmark**: the same green SCRANTON/WILKES-BARRE over a white HIGHLANDERS on a navy panel.

The team describes the bear as "equal parts ballplayer and trail guide." He wears a classic cap with a bold H, carries a hiking backpack, and uses a well-worn wooden bat as a walking stick. He will be named before the 2027 season. **CHAMP**, the franchise's longtime mascot, is staying.

## Our Take

Highlanders is the right name. It ties the farm club to the one piece of Yankees history most fans half-know, and it works for Northeastern Pennsylvania without a pun. "You are a Highlander before you are a Yankee" is a line the team will get years out of.

The uniforms are the safe part, and that is fine for a Yankees affiliate. A white pinstripe home with an interlocking monogram is the Yankees' look with new letters, and gray with a navy arched wordmark is as classic as road jerseys get. The light blue alternate is the one with personality, and it keeps a little of the baby blue fans liked on the Baby Bombers vest.

The weak spot is the type. The HIGHLANDERS lettering has jagged notches cut into it, which fits a hiking-and-woods logo but looks busier on a jersey chest than the clean block letters around it. The bear logo is good minor league art. The jerseys would have been stronger if they leaned on it more.

## When Do The Highlanders Debut?

- **First game as the Highlanders:** the 2027 season opens on the road on **March 26** with a nine-game trip to **Worcester and Buffalo**.
- **First home game:** **Tuesday, April 6, 2027, at 4:05 p.m. vs. Lehigh Valley** at PNC Field.
- **Season:** 150 games, 75 at home. Lehigh Valley, Buffalo, Charlotte, Jacksonville, Rochester, Syracuse, Toledo and Worcester all visit PNC Field.
- **The bear's name:** announced before the season starts.
