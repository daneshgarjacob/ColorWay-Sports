Most top teams in Week 3 of the 2026 college football season are in their standard sets: home teams in color, visitors in white. The exceptions are Texas Tech's all-black Patrick Mahomes "Let's Ride" uniform against Houston on Friday, Missouri's Memorial Stadium 100th anniversary throwback against Troy, Utah's white Ute Proud helmet against Utah State, BYU's navy-trimmed whites at Colorado State and Florida's orange pants at Auburn, all announced. Since Wednesday, Miami, Houston, Oregon, Kentucky, Louisville, Tennessee, TCU, Virginia and Purdue have also posted their Week 3 combinations. Ole Miss and Florida State, the two biggest open questions, had not as of Thursday morning.

Below is every game this week involving an AP Top 25 team or one of the 43 programs we track in our college uniform schedules, with kickoff time, TV, the final score and what each team actually wore. A look is marked **Confirmed** when we have seen it in a broadcast frame or in the school's own game photography, and left as **expected** when we never got a clear look, which happened more often than usual this week because so many of these games were on networks that feed ESPN nothing. ESPN and the AP poll call this Week 3. All times are Eastern.

## Games of the Week

### No. 7 LSU at No. 8 Ole Miss

**Saturday, 7:30 p.m., ABC. College GameDay was in Oxford. Ole Miss won 32-24.** Lane Kiffin brought LSU back to the program he left at the end of 2025, and this was the most-watched uniform decision of the weekend. **Ole Miss answered it with POWDER BLUE**: powder blue helmet, powder blue jersey and white pants, the full set, with the end zones painted to match. Red and navy were both live and the Rebels never announced it. **LSU wore white** with the gold helmet and gold pants. The Rebels wore the full powder blue set (powder blue helmet, powder blue jersey, white pants) against LSU in 2025 and won 24-19. Through two games in 2026 they have worn navy and then red, both under the navy helmet with gray pants. Ole Miss had called a Stripe Out in the stands, which is a fan theme and not a uniform, and the Rebels never posted a combination graphic at all. More: [LSU schedule](/stories/lsu-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Ole Miss schedule](/stories/ole-miss-uniform-schedule-2026).

### Florida State at No. 10 Alabama

**Saturday, 3:30 p.m., ABC. Alabama won 50-36.** This was the return game from FSU's 31-17 win in Tallahassee last season, and it carried the week's second real uniform question. **Florida State answered it with GARNET PANTS**, under the gold helmet and the white road jersey. Gold is the traditional road pairing and the Seminoles never posted the combination in game week, so the broadcast settled it. It is the same call FSU made at Stanford last season. **Alabama wore crimson**, the only home look it owns: crimson helmet with the player's number, crimson jersey, white pants. More: [Alabama schedule](/stories/alabama-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Florida State schedule](/stories/florida-state-uniform-schedule-2026).

### No. 2 Georgia at Arkansas

**Saturday, noon, ABC. Georgia won 45-17.** **Georgia: confirmed in white**, the road set of red helmet with the oval G, white jersey and silver britches. The all-white look with the first white helmet in program history was a one-game alternate, worn against Western Kentucky last week. **Arkansas: confirmed in cardinal**, with white pants and the cardinal helmet. Arkansas called a Stripe Out in the stands, with fans in alternating sections wearing red or white, and did not change the uniform. More: [Georgia schedule](/stories/georgia-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Arkansas schedule](/stories/arkansas-uniform-schedule-2026).

### Florida at Auburn

**Saturday, 7 p.m., ESPN. Florida won 44-39.** Florida's first trip to Jordan-Hare since 2011, and both sides wore exactly what the week said they would. **Florida wore the orange helmet, white jersey and orange pants**, straight off the full-season uniform schedule the Gators published in August, one of only two dates on it that carry the orange pants. **Auburn wore the home default**, white helmet with the navy AU, navy jersey, white pants. Navy against white and orange under the lights, as billed. More: [Florida schedule](/stories/florida-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Auburn schedule](/stories/auburn-uniform-schedule-2026).

### No. 22 Houston at No. 13 Texas Tech

**Friday, 8 p.m., FOX. Texas Tech won 28-26.** **Texas Tech wore the Patrick Mahomes "Let's Ride" set**, the third Mahomes uniform from adidas in three years: all black, red block numbers, black pants, black helmet, the Gladiator logo worked in and his name across the back bumper, with the State Farm logo on the jersey that Texas Tech had confirmed on the Wednesday. **Houston wore the Icy Whites**, the set it posted as ["Icy whites for Game 3"](https://x.com/UHCougarFB/status/2100278212085383592): white helmet with the red interlocking UH and a red facemask, white jersey with the red Houston script, white pants. Both held. More: [Texas Tech schedule](/stories/texas-tech-uniform-schedule-2026).

### No. 16 SMU at No. 23 Louisville

**Saturday, 3:30 p.m., ESPN2. Louisville won 41-31.** The ACC opener for both teams and the only ranked game in the afternoon window. **Louisville wore the red set it posted on the Thursday** under ["Keeping it classic"](https://x.com/LouisvilleFB/status/2100585568203837775): white helmet, red jersey, white pants. The week before, Louisville had paired the red jersey with a Salute to Service helmet; the all-black For The Ville alternate is still held for Stanford on October 31. **SMU went all white**, which is what we had down for it without an announcement. More: [SMU schedule](/stories/smu-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Louisville schedule](/stories/louisville-uniform-schedule-2026).

## Alternates and Special Looks This Week

- **Texas Tech, Let's Ride (Confirmed).** The Mahomes all-black set, worn Friday against Houston.
- **Missouri, 100th anniversary throwback (Confirmed).** Mizzou revealed it on August 20 for the Memorial Stadium 100th Anniversary Game and wore it against Troy exactly as unveiled: black helmet with a narrow block M and gold and white center striping, a black jersey with no wordmark on the chest, and old gold pants.
- **BYU, navy-trimmed whites (Confirmed).** BYU announced it on Monday and wore it at Colorado State: white helmet with a white facemask, white jersey with navy numbers, white pants. It was BYU's first navy of 2026.
- **Florida, orange pants (Confirmed).** Orange helmet and white jersey over orange pants at Auburn, one of only two dates on Florida's published season schedule that carry them.
- **Utah, Ute Proud (Confirmed).** Utah [revealed it on Wednesday](https://x.com/Utah_Football/status/2100329327091237185) as white, red, red and wore it in a 33-0 shutout of Utah State: a white helmet with the tribal interlocking U's on the side and a black, white and red tribal stripe down the center, a red jersey and red pants, for the Ute Proud and Hall of Fame game.
- **TCU, new helmet look (Confirmed).** TCU teased a ["new look this weekend"](https://x.com/TCUFootball/status/2100626024555753749) on Thursday, [posted the full set](https://x.com/TCUFootball/status/2100631176251674710) and then wore it against Arkansas State: a white helmet with a Frogs script, purple jersey and white pants.
- **Oregon, all green (Confirmed).** Oregon [posted three green circles](https://x.com/oregonfootball/status/2100381723284693132) and then wore the green helmet, green jersey and green pants against Portland State on Friday night, matching the Wear Green theme in Autzen.
- **Baylor, Helmet of Hope decal (Confirmed).** Baylor [showed the decal on Thursday](https://x.com/BUFootball/status/2100628122806665700): a pink and teal bear head reading Helmet of Hope on the green helmet, plus a gold childhood cancer ribbon, the design from a young patient at McLane Children's according to Baylor Bears On SI. The jersey was the open question and the Bears answered it with gold over gold under the green shell, matching the Gold Rush theme in the stands rather than leaving it to the crowd.

## The Bottom Line

This was billed as a week of standard uniforms with big games, and the standard part did not entirely hold. Both of the real open questions got answered, and both went the less obvious way: **Ole Miss came out in the full powder blue set** for LSU on GameDay night, with the end zones painted to match, and won 32-24; and **Florida State put garnet pants under the gold helmet** at Bryant-Denny rather than the traditional gold. What the afternoon and night windows actually produced was a run of pants changes nobody announced: South Carolina went garnet head to toe instead of white pants, Washington swapped gold pants for white, East Carolina put purple under the road whites, USF wore green over green for the first time this year, Liberty skipped its red home set for the navy alternate, and Oklahoma State pulled out the orange shell for the first time in 2026. Two more looks arrived without a reveal at all: **Colorado State's green home set**, which the Rams had never shown publicly in their first adidas season and simply wore against BYU, and **Tulsa's red throwback**, which the program unveiled without naming a game and then put on three days later. The announced specials all held: Texas Tech's Let's Ride, Missouri's centennial throwback, Utah's Ute Proud helmet, TCU's Frogs script helmet, BYU's navy-trimmed whites, Florida's orange pants and UCF's Knightmode. San Diego State's Age of the Jaguar took an extra day, because The CW fed no frames and the only images in existence on Saturday night were the reveal shoot, but the school's own game photography landed on Sunday morning and the red over red is confirmed.
