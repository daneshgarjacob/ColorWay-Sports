Most top teams in Week 3 of the 2026 college football season are in their standard sets: home teams in color, visitors in white. The exceptions are Texas Tech's all-black Patrick Mahomes "Let's Ride" uniform against Houston on Friday, Missouri's Memorial Stadium 100th anniversary throwback against Troy, BYU's navy-trimmed whites at Colorado State and Florida's orange pants at Auburn, all four announced.

Below are every game this week involving an AP Top 25 team or one of the 43 programs we track in our college uniform schedules. Each has kickoff time, TV and each team's look, marked **Announced** when the school has confirmed it and **Expected** when it has not. ESPN and the AP poll call this Week 3, and we update this page as reveals land through Saturday. All times are Eastern.

## Games of the Week

### No. 7 LSU at No. 8 Ole Miss

**Saturday, 7:30 p.m., ABC. College GameDay is in Oxford.** Lane Kiffin brings LSU back to the program he left at the end of 2025, and this is the most-watched uniform decision of the weekend. **LSU: white expected**, gold helmet and gold pants. Ole Miss wears color at home, and LSU wore white in Oxford last season. **Ole Miss: home color expected, not announced.** Red, navy and powder blue are all possible. The Rebels wore the full powder blue set (powder blue helmet, powder blue jersey, white pants) against LSU in 2025 and won 24-19. Through two games in 2026 they have worn navy and then red, both under the navy helmet with gray pants. Ole Miss has called a Stripe Out in the stands. More: [LSU schedule](/stories/lsu-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Ole Miss schedule](/stories/ole-miss-uniform-schedule-2026).

### Florida State at No. 10 Alabama

**Saturday, 3:30 p.m., ABC.** This is the return game from FSU's 31-17 win in Tallahassee last season. **Alabama: crimson expected**, the only home look it wears: crimson jersey, white pants, crimson helmet. **Florida State: white expected** under the gold helmet. The pants are the open question. Gold is the traditional road pairing, but FSU also wore garnet pants with the white jersey at Stanford last season. FSU usually posts the full combination during game week, and we will update this game once it does. More: [Alabama schedule](/stories/alabama-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Florida State schedule](/stories/florida-state-uniform-schedule-2026).

### No. 2 Georgia at Arkansas

**Saturday, noon, ABC.** **Georgia: white expected**, the road set of red helmet with the oval G, white jersey and silver britches. The all-white look with the first white helmet in program history was a one-game alternate, worn against Western Kentucky last week. **Arkansas: cardinal expected**, with white pants and the cardinal helmet. Arkansas has announced a Stripe Out, with fans in alternating sections wearing red or white, but it has not announced a uniform change. More: [Georgia schedule](/stories/georgia-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Arkansas schedule](/stories/arkansas-uniform-schedule-2026).

### Florida at Auburn

**Saturday, 7 p.m., ESPN.** It is Florida's first trip to Jordan-Hare since 2011. **Florida: Announced**, orange helmet, white jersey, orange pants. It comes from the full-season uniform schedule the Gators released before the season, and this is one of only two games with the orange pants. **Auburn: navy expected**, with the striped white pants and the white helmet, the home default for the SEC opener. That makes it navy against white and orange under the lights. More: [Florida schedule](/stories/florida-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Auburn schedule](/stories/auburn-uniform-schedule-2026).

### No. 22 Houston at No. 13 Texas Tech

**Friday, 8 p.m., FOX.** **Texas Tech: Announced, the Patrick Mahomes "Let's Ride" set**, the third Mahomes uniform from adidas in three years. It is an all-black set with red block numbers, black pants and a black helmet, with Mahomes' Gladiator logo worked in and his name across the back bumper. Texas Tech confirmed on Wednesday that a State Farm logo will appear on the jersey. **Houston: white expected** as the visitor, in the script Houston road set. More: [Texas Tech schedule](/stories/texas-tech-uniform-schedule-2026).

### No. 16 SMU at No. 23 Louisville

**Saturday, 3:30 p.m., ESPN2.** It is the ACC opener for both teams and the only ranked game in the afternoon window. **Louisville: red expected**, with white pants and the white helmet with the red Cardinal head. Last week Louisville paired that set with a Salute to Service helmet. The all-black For The Ville alternate is saved for Stanford on October 31. **SMU: white expected**, not announced. More: [Louisville schedule](/stories/louisville-uniform-schedule-2026).

## Alternates and Special Looks This Week

- **Texas Tech, Let's Ride (Announced).** The Mahomes all-black set, Friday against Houston.
- **Missouri, 100th anniversary throwback (Announced).** Mizzou revealed it on August 20 for the Memorial Stadium 100th Anniversary Game. It has a black helmet with a narrow block M and gold and white center striping, a black jersey with no wordmark on the chest, and old gold pants.
- **BYU, navy-trimmed whites (Announced).** BYU announced it on Monday: white helmet with a white facemask, white jersey with navy numbers, white pants. It is BYU's first navy of 2026.
- **Florida, orange pants (Announced).** Orange helmet and white jersey over orange pants at Auburn.
- **Utah, Ute Proud (Expected, not revealed).** Utah lists this as its Ute Proud and Hall of Fame game. The Ute Proud helmet has been white with the tribal interlocking U's in each of the last two seasons.
- **Baylor, Helmet of Hope decal.** Baylor's childhood cancer awareness game adds a helmet decal designed by a young patient at McLane Children's, according to Baylor Bears On SI. The jersey has not been announced. Gold Rush is a fan color theme, not a confirmed uniform.

## The Bottom Line

This is a week of standard uniforms, but the games are big. Four of the five biggest games are SEC home dates, where the home team wears color and the visitor wears white, so LSU, Florida State, Georgia and Florida are all in white. That leaves two real uniform questions: which of three colors Ole Miss picks for GameDay, and what pants FSU wears at Bryant-Denny. The confirmed specials are Texas Tech's Let's Ride on Friday, Missouri's centennial throwback, BYU's navy-trimmed whites and Florida's orange pants.
