Most top teams in Week 3 of the 2026 college football season are in their standard sets: home teams in color, visitors in white. The exceptions are Texas Tech's all-black Patrick Mahomes "Let's Ride" uniform against Houston on Friday, Missouri's Memorial Stadium 100th anniversary throwback against Troy, Utah's white Ute Proud helmet against Utah State, BYU's navy-trimmed whites at Colorado State and Florida's orange pants at Auburn, all announced. Since Wednesday, Miami, Houston, Oregon, Kentucky, Louisville, Tennessee, TCU, Virginia and Purdue have also posted their Week 3 combinations. Ole Miss and Florida State, the two biggest open questions, had not as of Thursday morning.

Below are every game this week involving an AP Top 25 team or one of the 43 programs we track in our college uniform schedules. Each has kickoff time, TV and each team's look, marked **Announced** when the school has confirmed it and **Expected** when it has not. ESPN and the AP poll call this Week 3, and we update this page as reveals land through Saturday. All times are Eastern.

## Games of the Week

### No. 7 LSU at No. 8 Ole Miss

**Saturday, 7:30 p.m., ABC. College GameDay is in Oxford.** Lane Kiffin brings LSU back to the program he left at the end of 2025, and this is the most-watched uniform decision of the weekend. **LSU: white expected**, gold helmet and gold pants. Ole Miss wears color at home, and LSU wore white in Oxford last season. **Ole Miss: home color expected, not announced.** Red, navy and powder blue are all possible. The Rebels wore the full powder blue set (powder blue helmet, powder blue jersey, white pants) against LSU in 2025 and won 24-19. Through two games in 2026 they have worn navy and then red, both under the navy helmet with gray pants. Ole Miss has called a Stripe Out in the stands, and that is a fan theme, not a uniform. As of Thursday morning the Rebels had not posted a uniform graphic for this game. More: [LSU schedule](/stories/lsu-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Ole Miss schedule](/stories/ole-miss-uniform-schedule-2026).

### Florida State at No. 10 Alabama

**Saturday, 3:30 p.m., ABC.** This is the return game from FSU's 31-17 win in Tallahassee last season. **Alabama: crimson expected**, the only home look it wears: crimson jersey, white pants, crimson helmet. **Florida State: white expected** under the gold helmet. The pants are the open question. Gold is the traditional road pairing, but FSU also wore garnet pants with the white jersey at Stanford last season. FSU usually posts the full combination during game week, but it had not as of Thursday morning. We will update this game once it does. More: [Alabama schedule](/stories/alabama-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Florida State schedule](/stories/florida-state-uniform-schedule-2026).

### No. 2 Georgia at Arkansas

**Saturday, noon, ABC. Georgia won 45-17.** **Georgia: confirmed in white**, the road set of red helmet with the oval G, white jersey and silver britches. The all-white look with the first white helmet in program history was a one-game alternate, worn against Western Kentucky last week. **Arkansas: confirmed in cardinal**, with white pants and the cardinal helmet. Arkansas called a Stripe Out in the stands, with fans in alternating sections wearing red or white, and did not change the uniform. More: [Georgia schedule](/stories/georgia-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Arkansas schedule](/stories/arkansas-uniform-schedule-2026).

### Florida at Auburn

**Saturday, 7 p.m., ESPN.** It is Florida's first trip to Jordan-Hare since 2011. **Florida: Announced**, orange helmet, white jersey, orange pants. It comes from the full-season uniform schedule the Gators released before the season, and this is one of only two games with the orange pants. **Auburn: navy expected**, with the striped white pants and the white helmet, the home default for the SEC opener. That makes it navy against white and orange under the lights. More: [Florida schedule](/stories/florida-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Auburn schedule](/stories/auburn-uniform-schedule-2026).

### No. 22 Houston at No. 13 Texas Tech

**Friday, 8 p.m., FOX.** **Texas Tech: Announced, the Patrick Mahomes "Let's Ride" set**, the third Mahomes uniform from adidas in three years. It is an all-black set with red block numbers, black pants and a black helmet, with Mahomes' Gladiator logo worked in and his name across the back bumper. Texas Tech confirmed on Wednesday that a State Farm logo will appear on the jersey. **Houston: Announced, all white.** The Cougars posted ["Icy whites for Game 3"](https://x.com/UHCougarFB/status/2100278212085383592) on Wednesday: white helmet with the red interlocking UH and a red facemask, white jersey with the red Houston script, white pants. More: [Texas Tech schedule](/stories/texas-tech-uniform-schedule-2026).

### No. 16 SMU at No. 23 Louisville

**Saturday, 3:30 p.m., ESPN2.** It is the ACC opener for both teams and the only ranked game in the afternoon window. **Louisville: Announced, red.** The Cardinals posted the combination on Thursday under ["Keeping it classic"](https://x.com/LouisvilleFB/status/2100585568203837775): white helmet, red jersey, white pants. ## What Was Worn Friday and in the Early Window

Every Friday night reveal held. ## The Bottom Line

This was billed as a week of standard uniforms with big games, and the standard part did not entirely hold. Of the two real open questions, FSU answered one by putting garnet pants under the gold helmet at Bryant-Denny, and Ole Miss still has GameDay to itself. What the afternoon and night windows actually produced was a run of pants changes nobody announced: South Carolina went garnet head to toe instead of white pants, Washington swapped gold pants for white, East Carolina put purple under the road whites, USF wore green over green for the first time this year, Liberty skipped its red home set for the navy alternate, and Oklahoma State pulled out the orange shell for the first time in 2026. The announced specials all held: Texas Tech's Let's Ride, Utah's Ute Proud helmet, TCU's Frogs script helmet, BYU's navy-trimmed whites, Florida's orange pants and UCF's Knightmode. We update this page as each game finishes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are college football teams wearing this week?**

Most top teams are in standard sets for Week 3: home teams in color, visitors in white. Texas Tech (all-black Mahomes "Let's Ride" set against Houston), Missouri (100th anniversary throwback against Troy), Utah (Ute Proud helmet with red jersey and red pants), BYU (white with navy trim at Colorado State) and Florida (orange helmet, white jersey, orange pants at Auburn) have announced their looks. Miami and Kentucky are in all white, Oregon is in all green, and Houston, Louisville, Tennessee, TCU, Virginia, Wake Forest and Purdue have also posted their combinations. Every game is listed above with its announced or expected uniform.

**What is LSU wearing at Ole Miss?**

LSU is expected in white with gold pants and the gold helmet on Saturday, September 19, because Ole Miss wears color at home. LSU wore white in Oxford last season. Ole Miss had not announced its uniform as of Thursday morning. Red, navy and powder blue are all possible, and the Rebels wore powder blue against LSU in 2025.

**What is Florida State wearing at Alabama?**

Florida State wore the gold helmet, white jersey and garnet pants at Bryant-Denny Stadium, confirmed from the broadcast. The pants were the open question all week: gold is the traditional road pairing, and FSU went with the garnet instead, the same call it made at Stanford last season. Alabama wore its crimson home set with white pants.

**Which teams are wearing alternates in Week 3?**

Texas Tech wears its new Patrick Mahomes "Let's Ride" uniform on Friday against Houston. Missouri debuts its Memorial Stadium 100th anniversary throwback against Troy. Utah wears its Ute Proud set against Utah State, and TCU debuts a white helmet with a Frogs script against Arkansas State. BYU's navy-trimmed all-white set and Florida's orange pants are also one-off looks this week.

**What is Utah wearing for the Ute Proud game?**

Utah announced a white helmet, red jersey and red pants for its Ute Proud game against Utah State on Saturday, September 19. The white helmet carries the interlocking U's with tribal elements and a black, white and red tribal stripe down the center. The game honors the Ute Indian Tribe and doubles as Utah's Hall of Fame game.

**What did Miami wear at Wake Forest?**
