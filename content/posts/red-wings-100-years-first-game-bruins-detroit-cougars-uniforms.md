**The Detroit Red Wings played their first NHL game 100 years ago tonight, on Nov. 18, 1926. They were called the Detroit Cougars, they played in Canada, and they lost 2-0 to the Boston Bruins.**

Tonight Detroit hosts Boston again at Little Caesars Arena (7:30 p.m. ET, TNT), and the Red Wings are calling it The Original Faceoff. No special sweater has been announced, so Detroit is expected in its regular red home sweater. We will update this story if that changes.

## Quick Answers

- **When was the Red Wings' first game?** Nov. 18, 1926, as the Detroit Cougars.
- **Who did they play?** The Boston Bruins. Boston won 2-0.
- **Where?** Border Cities Arena in Windsor, Ontario, across the Detroit River. Detroit's Olympia was still being built.
- **What did the Cougars wear?** A white sweater with a single red stripe across the body and an Old English D on the chest.
- **What did the Bruins wear?** A white sweater with brown and gold stripes, the first brown and gold era of the team.
- **When did they become the Red Wings?** Before the 1932-33 season, after the Cougars (1926-30) and the Falcons (1930-32).
- **What are the Red Wings wearing tonight?** The regular red home sweater is expected. No special jersey has been announced.

Detroit's franchise came from the West. The Victoria Cougars of the Western Hockey League shut down in April 1926, and the NHL awarded a new Detroit club to a group led by Charles A. Hughes. The new team bought the Victoria players' contracts and kept the name, so Detroit's first NHL team was the Cougars.

The new Olympia in Detroit was not ready, so the Cougars played their whole first season at Border Cities Arena in Windsor. About 6,000 fans filled it for the first game. Per NHL.com:

- **Boston 1-0:** Duke Keats scored at 1:45 of the first period.
- **Boston 2-0:** Archie Briden scored at 2:40, with Keats assisting.
- **In net:** Detroit's starter, Hap Holmes, got sick about two hours before the game. Herb Stuart replaced him, gave up those two early goals and nothing after. Boston's Charles "Doc" Stewart made 21 saves for the shutout.

The Cougars finished that first season 12-28-4, last in the American Division. They moved into the Olympia for their second season.

## What The Cougars Wore In 1926

The first Detroit sweater was white with one wide red stripe across the body. On that stripe sat an Old English D. In the team photo at the top of this page you can also see stripes on the sleeves and the socks.

There was no winged wheel yet. That came six years later.

The Red Wings have brought this sweater back more than once:

- **2009 Winter Classic:** Detroit wore a replica of the 1926-27 Cougars sweater, white with the single red stripe and the Old English D, and beat Chicago 6-4 at Wrigley Field.
- **2025-26 Centennial uniform:** the Cougars D was redrawn as a secondary mark. It sits on the front of the red pants, on the jock tag and on the hem loop on the back.

## The Barber Pole, Then The Falcons

The Cougars did not keep one look for long. In 1927-28 they wore what the team calls its "Barber Pole" uniforms, white with red stripes running around the sweater. The Red Wings remixed that look for their 2022 Reverse Retro, in red with black stripes and DETROIT across the front, and the numbers on the 2025-26 Centennial sweater use the Barber Pole font.

In 1930 the team became the Detroit Falcons. According to the Detroit Historical Society, the name came from a fan contest, and Falcons beat Ambassadors, Dynamics and Wanderers. The Falcons lasted two seasons. Their mark on today's team is the striping: the Red Wings say the stripes on the hem, sleeves and socks of the Centennial uniform come from the 1930-32 Falcons sweaters.

## 1932: The Red Wings And The Winged Wheel

James Norris bought the club in 1932 and renamed it the Red Wings for the 1932-33 season. Norris had played for the Montreal Amateur Athletic Association, whose emblem was a winged bicycle wheel. Its teams were known as the Winged Wheelers. Norris turned that into a winged wheel for the Motor City.

The first Red Wings sweater was dark red with the new crest. The Red Wings say the first chain-stitched Winged Wheel was introduced in 1932 and used through 1948.

## How Close Is Today's Sweater To The 1930s One?

Closer than almost any other team in the league. The crest on tonight's home sweater is still a red winged wheel on a red sweater, 94 years after it first appeared. The details around it have moved, with white trim and names on the back added over the decades, but the idea has not changed.

The closest modern copy is the Centennial sweater the team made for its 100th season: solid red, a cream chain-stitched 1930s Winged Wheel, a lace-up collar and Falcons-style stripes. We covered its return in [Red Wings Centennial Sweater Returns](/stories/red-wings-centennial-sweater-returns-2026).

Boston was only in its third season in 1926, and it was still brown and gold, the colors of owner Charles F. Adams' First National Stores grocery chain. The Bruins' own sweater history on NHL.com describes the 1926-33 sweater as white, with gold and brown stripes along the full length of the sleeves and a wider brown band near the bottom. That is the sweater Boston wore when it won its first Stanley Cup in 1929.

Brown turned to black in the 1930s, but it has kept coming back. This season's Hometown Remix, the brown Varsity Sweater, is the first all-brown Bruins uniform since 1924. The full story is in our [Bruins uniform history](/stories/boston-bruins-uniform-history).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When was the Red Wings' first game?**

Nov. 18, 1926. The team was called the Detroit Cougars at the time.

**Who did the Red Wings play in their first game?**

The Boston Bruins. Boston won 2-0.

**Did the Red Wings win their first game?**

No. The Cougars lost 2-0. Duke Keats and Archie Briden scored in the first three minutes, and Boston goalie Doc Stewart made 21 saves.

**Where did the Red Wings play their first game?**

At Border Cities Arena in Windsor, Ontario. The Olympia in Detroit was not finished, so the Cougars played their entire first season in Windsor.

**What did the Detroit Cougars wear?**

In 1926-27, a white sweater with a single red stripe across the body and an Old English D on the chest, with striped sleeves and socks. In 1927-28 they switched to white "Barber Pole" sweaters with red stripes.

**Why were the Red Wings called the Cougars?**

The franchise was built from the Victoria Cougars of the Western Hockey League, which folded in 1926. The Detroit club bought the Victoria players' contracts and kept the name.

**When did the Detroit Cougars become the Falcons?**

In 1930. The Detroit Historical Society says the name came from a fan contest.

**When did the Falcons become the Red Wings?**

For the 1932-33 season, after James Norris bought the team.

**Where does the Red Wings' winged wheel come from?**

From the Montreal Amateur Athletic Association, whose emblem was a winged wheel. Norris had played for the club and adapted the logo for a team in the Motor City.

**What color was the first Red Wings sweater?**

Dark red, with the winged wheel crest. The Red Wings say the first chain-stitched Winged Wheel was used from 1932 through 1948.

**What did the Bruins wear in 1926?**

A white sweater with gold and brown stripes along the sleeves and a wider brown band near the bottom, according to the Bruins' sweater history on NHL.com.

**Why were the Bruins brown and gold?**

Owner Charles F. Adams used the colors of his First National Stores grocery chain.

**What are the Red Wings wearing against the Bruins on Nov. 18, 2026?**

Detroit is expected in its regular red home sweater. No special jersey has been announced. We will update this story if that changes.

**What is The Original Faceoff?**

It is the Red Wings' name for tonight's home game against Boston, exactly 100 years after the franchise's first NHL game, also against the Bruins.

**Have the Red Wings ever worn the Detroit Cougars jersey again?**

Yes. They wore a replica of the 1926-27 Cougars sweater at the 2009 Winter Classic at Wrigley Field and beat Chicago 6-4. Their 2022 Reverse Retro remixed the 1927-28 Barber Pole sweater in red and black.
