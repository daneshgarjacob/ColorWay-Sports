**The Iron Bowl is in Tuscaloosa this year, so Alabama wears crimson and Auburn wears white.** Auburn visits Bryant-Denny Stadium on **Saturday, November 28, on ABC**, at either **3:30 or 7:30 p.m. Eastern** (2:30 or 6:30 Central). The SEC sets the final time after this weekend's games. Alabama has worn the **crimson helmet, crimson jersey and white pants** in every home game so far this season. The last time Auburn came to Tuscaloosa, in 2024, it wore **all white**. Neither school has posted its uniform for this game yet. Both post during game week, and this page changes the day they do.

## Quick Answers

- **What is Alabama wearing in the Iron Bowl?** Expected: crimson helmet, crimson jersey, white pants.
- **What is Auburn wearing at Alabama?** Expected: the white road set. It wore white helmet, white jersey and white pants in Tuscaloosa in 2024.
- **Any alternates?** None announced by either school for 2026.
- **When is the Iron Bowl?** Saturday, November 28, on ABC, at 3:30 or 7:30 p.m. ET. The exact time is set after the November 21 games.
- **What did they wear last year?** At Jordan-Hare in 2025, Auburn wore navy at home and Alabama wore white. Alabama won 27-20.

## What We Know

- **Alabama is the home team.** The game is at Bryant-Denny Stadium on November 28. Auburn's athletics site listed it as a flex game that is either an afternoon or an evening kickoff ([Auburn Athletics](https://auburntigers.com/news/2026/06/10/sec-announces-tv-windows-for-2026-auburn-football-games)), and on November 16 the network was set as ABC, at 2:30 or 6:30 p.m. Central ([Touchdown Alabama](https://tdalabamamag.com/2026/11/16/kickoff-time-slot-options-network-announced-for-iron-bowl/)).
- **Neither school has announced an alternate for 2026.** Alabama has worn only its crimson home set and white road set this season. Auburn has worn only its navy home set and its white road set.
- **Both teams post their uniforms before kickoff.** Alabama posts a locker room photo coded by color, such as "crimson, white, white" for Vanderbilt on November 14. Auburn posted a game-number uniform graphic for its Georgia game in October.

## What We're Watching For

This is our read until the schools post.

- **Auburn's pants.** Auburn's road set this year has been white over white every time we have seen it, at Tennessee, Georgia and Mississippi State. White pants are the expected answer.
- **Alabama's pants.** Alabama has paired the crimson jersey with white pants every time this season. Crimson pants would be news.

## Why The Home Team Wears Color

SEC home teams wear their color jersey and the visitor wears white, so the Iron Bowl flips each year with the site: navy against white at Jordan-Hare, crimson against white at Bryant-Denny. A home team can choose to wear white, which would put the visitor in color, so the labels here stay Expected until the schools post.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Alabama wearing in the 2026 Iron Bowl?**

The crimson helmet, crimson jersey and white pants are expected. Alabama is the home team and has worn that set in every home game so far this season.

**What is Auburn wearing in the 2026 Iron Bowl?**

The white road set is expected: white helmet, white jersey and white pants, which Auburn wore at Tennessee, Georgia and Mississippi State this season and in Tuscaloosa in 2024.

**What time is the 2026 Iron Bowl?**

Either 3:30 or 7:30 p.m. Eastern (2:30 or 6:30 Central) on ABC. The SEC picks the slot after the November 21 games.

**Where is the Iron Bowl in 2026?**

Bryant-Denny Stadium in Tuscaloosa. The 2025 game was at Jordan-Hare Stadium in Auburn.

**Does Alabama wear alternate uniforms?**

Alabama has not worn one in 2026. Its season has been the crimson home set and the white road set, and it wore crimson on the road only at Tennessee and LSU.

**Does Auburn have an orange jersey?**

Auburn has not worn one in 2026 and has not announced one. Its 2026 games have all been navy at home or white on the road.

**What did Auburn wear in the 2025 Iron Bowl?**

The white helmet, navy jersey and white pants at Jordan-Hare Stadium. Alabama won 27-20.

**What did Alabama wear at Auburn last year?**

The crimson helmet, white jersey and white pants.

**Why does Alabama have numbers on its helmet?**

Alabama's helmet carries the player's number on the side in place of a logo. It is the same crimson helmet in both the home and road sets.

**Who wears white in the Iron Bowl?**

The visiting team, under the SEC's home-color convention. In 2026 that is Auburn.
