## The 2027 NHL Winter Classic Logo

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr; gap: 12px; margin: 1.5em 0; max-width: 360px; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;">
  <div style="text-align: center;"><img src="/images/posts/nhl-winter-classic-2027-logo-history-ranked/2027.jpg" alt="2027 NHL Winter Classic logo: a hexagon with snow-capped mountains at the top, red rock canyon walls at the bottom, the Winter Classic script, Discover and UTAH" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 8px;" /></div>
</div>

The 2027 logo is a hexagon. Snow-capped mountain peaks fill the top, red rock canyon walls rise up both sides at the bottom, and the white Winter Classic script runs across the middle with a long curved swoosh off the "W" that coverage of the reveal describes as a subtle mammoth tusk. CLASSIC is in sand-colored block letters, Discover sits under it, and UTAH is in light blue at the bottom point. The colors are the Mammoth's light blue, black, sand and red rock orange. There is no year on it, because the league already played a "2026" Winter Classic in Miami in January and this game falls on Dec. 31, 2026. The sky and stadium behind it in our image come from the NHL's reveal video. They are not part of the logo.

We like it. Mountains on top, desert below is Utah in one picture, and the hexagon is a fresh shape for the program. Two knocks keep it out of the A range. It packs two landscapes into one badge, so it gets busy at patch size, and it has nothing for Colorado, the other team in the game. Still strong overall.

## Every NHL Winter Classic Logo, 2008 to 2027

<img src="/images/posts/nhl-winter-classic-2027-logo-history-ranked/history-grid.jpg" alt="Comprehensive chronological grid of every NHL Winter Classic logo from 2008 through 2027 organized year by year for the complete outdoor game branding history" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

Twenty Winter Classic years, 18 games played, 18 game logos. The 2013 game (Maple Leafs at Red Wings, Michigan Stadium) was cancelled by the NHL lockout, and the 2021 game (Blues at Wild, Target Field) was called off by the COVID-19 pandemic. Both matchups were played the next time around, in 2014 and 2022.

## 2008 · Penguins at Sabres · Ralph Wilson Stadium

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr; gap: 12px; margin: 1.5em 0; max-width: 320px; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;">
  <div style="text-align: center;"><img src="/images/posts/nhl-winter-classic-2027-logo-history-ranked/2008.png" alt="2008 NHL Winter Classic logo for the inaugural outdoor game between Penguins and Sabres at Ralph Wilson Stadium in Buffalo" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 8px;" /><p style="font-size: 0.85em; color: #666; margin: 0.4em 0 0;">2008 · Grade B+</p></div>
</div>

The original. The Winter Classic began on Jan. 1, 2008, with Sidney Crosby's shootout winner in the snow against the Sabres at Ralph Wilson Stadium in Orchard Park, New York, in front of 71,217 fans. The logo is an ice-blue oval with the NHL shield on top, the white Winter Classic script, a puck tucked into the "C" and 2008 under CLASSIC, with small stars and snowflakes around the edge. It does not try to do much because it did not need to. The concept was the headline. It also set the look every logo since has built on: the shield on top and the script wordmark. B+.

## 2009 · Red Wings at Blackhawks · Wrigley Field

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr; gap: 12px; margin: 1.5em 0; max-width: 320px; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;">
  <div style="text-align: center;"><img src="/images/posts/nhl-winter-classic-2027-logo-history-ranked/2009.png" alt="2009 NHL Winter Classic logo for Red Wings at Blackhawks at Wrigley Field with the iconic Wrigley marquee shape" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 8px;" /><p style="font-size: 0.85em; color: #666; margin: 0.4em 0 0;">2009 · Grade A-</p></div>
</div>

The Wrigley Field marquee is one of the most recognizable signs in American sports and the 2009 logo borrowed it. The red top with 2009 is the marquee, the blue banner underneath reads WRIGLEY FIELD, and the Winter Classic script runs across the middle. The first time the Winter Classic visited a baseball cathedral and the logo absolutely understood the moment. One of the strongest outdoor-game marks in the run. A-.

## 2010 · Flyers at Bruins · Fenway Park

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr; gap: 12px; margin: 1.5em 0; max-width: 320px; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;">
  <div style="text-align: center;"><img src="/images/posts/nhl-winter-classic-2027-logo-history-ranked/2010.png" alt="2010 NHL Winter Classic logo for Flyers at Bruins at Fenway Park with the green Fenway palette and stadium silhouette" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 8px;" /><p style="font-size: 0.85em; color: #666; margin: 0.4em 0 0;">2010 · Grade A-</p></div>
</div>

The follow-up to Wrigley landed at Fenway, and the logo goes old Boston. A brick-red badge with an old-fashioned street lamp on top, a Fenway-green panel behind the Winter Classic script, BOSTON · 2010 across the bottom and crossed sticks under that. It feels like a ballpark sign from 1912. Less of an instant landmark than the Wrigley marquee, but every piece is about the place. A-.

## 2011 · Capitals at Penguins · Heinz Field

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr; gap: 12px; margin: 1.5em 0; max-width: 320px; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;">
  <div style="text-align: center;"><img src="/images/posts/nhl-winter-classic-2027-logo-history-ranked/2011.png" alt="2011 NHL Winter Classic logo for Capitals at Penguins at Heinz Field with Pittsburgh gold and black colors" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 8px;" /><p style="font-size: 0.85em; color: #666; margin: 0.4em 0 0;">2011 · Grade B</p></div>
</div>

Heinz Field hosted in 2011, and the logo leans hard into Pittsburgh black and gold. A yellow steel bridge arches over the top, a nod to the City of Bridges, with a black-and-silver plate reading PITTSBURGH 2011 under the wordmark and ice dripping off the bottom. HBO's first 24/7 series turned this game into an event, but the logo does not quite match the heat. The color story works, the composition is a little busy, and the typography is a touch dated. B.

## 2012 · Rangers at Flyers · Citizens Bank Park

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr; gap: 12px; margin: 1.5em 0; max-width: 320px; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;">
  <div style="text-align: center;"><img src="/images/posts/nhl-winter-classic-2027-logo-history-ranked/2012.png" alt="2012 NHL Winter Classic logo for Rangers at Flyers at Citizens Bank Park Philadelphia with Bridgestone title sponsor branding" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 8px;" /><p style="font-size: 0.85em; color: #666; margin: 0.4em 0 0;">2012 · Grade B</p></div>
</div>

The Liberty Bell sits behind the Winter Classic script, with a brick building on top and PHILADELPHIA 2012 curved along the bottom. This is the first logo in our set with the Bridgestone title-sponsor lockup built in, stamped big under the wordmark. The bell is the right symbol for Philadelphia, but the sponsor lockup takes up as much room as the landmark, and that pulls some of the heritage feel out of the mark. B.

## 2013 · CANCELLED · The Lockout Year

The 2012-13 NHL lockout wiped out the originally scheduled 2013 Winter Classic between the Toronto Maple Leafs and the Detroit Red Wings at Michigan Stadium. A logo had already been shown in August 2012: a red, white and blue shield with Michigan Stadium and no year. The 2014 logo is essentially that design with a 2014 tab added, so we rank it once, under 2014.

## 2014 · Maple Leafs at Red Wings · Michigan Stadium

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr; gap: 12px; margin: 1.5em 0; max-width: 320px; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;">
  <div style="text-align: center;"><img src="/images/posts/nhl-winter-classic-2027-logo-history-ranked/2014.png" alt="2014 NHL Winter Classic logo The Big House Big Chill Toronto Maple Leafs at Detroit Red Wings at Michigan Stadium" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 8px;" /><p style="font-size: 0.85em; color: #666; margin: 0.4em 0 0;">2014 · Grade A</p></div>
</div>

The Big House. 105,491 fans inside Michigan Stadium on New Year's Day 2014, the biggest crowd in NHL history, watched the Maple Leafs win in a shootout. The logo is a blue shield with a red border, part of Michigan Stadium up top next to a 2014 tab, the Winter Classic script across the front, and THE BIG HOUSE stamped underneath like a sub-brand of its own. Easily one of the most iconic Winter Classic marks because the design committed to the venue and let the venue do the rest. A.

## 2015 · Blackhawks at Capitals · Nationals Park

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr; gap: 12px; margin: 1.5em 0; max-width: 320px; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;">

</div>

Washington's first Winter Classic, at Nationals Park, got a round badge with the U.S. Capitol dome on top, a field of stars behind the script, red-and-white stripes around the bottom and WASHINGTON, D.C. under CLASSIC. It is the flag and the Capitol in one mark, and it reads as D.C. right away. B+.

## 2016 · Canadiens at Bruins · Gillette Stadium

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr; gap: 12px; margin: 1.5em 0; max-width: 320px; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;">
  <div style="text-align: center;"><img src="/images/posts/nhl-winter-classic-2027-logo-history-ranked/2016.png" alt="2016 NHL Winter Classic logo for Canadiens at Bruins at Gillette Stadium Foxborough with stadium silhouette" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 8px;" /><p style="font-size: 0.85em; color: #666; margin: 0.4em 0 0;">2016 · Grade C+</p></div>
</div>

The Canadiens-Bruins Original Six matchup at Gillette Stadium got an ice-blue frame with icicles across the top, a little rink with crossed sticks and a puck under the wordmark, 2016 in a serif face, and FOXBORO on a brown ribbon at the bottom. Clean and wintry, but there is no landmark. Nothing in it says Gillette or New England beyond the word on the ribbon. With no landmark at all, it is the weakest logo in the run. C+.

## 2017 · Blackhawks at Blues · Busch Stadium

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr; gap: 12px; margin: 1.5em 0; max-width: 320px; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;">
  <div style="text-align: center;"><img src="/images/posts/nhl-winter-classic-2027-logo-history-ranked/2017.png" alt="2017 NHL Winter Classic logo for Blackhawks at Blues at Busch Stadium St Louis with the Gateway Arch shield" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 8px;" /><p style="font-size: 0.85em; color: #666; margin: 0.4em 0 0;">2017 · Grade A-</p></div>
</div>

The Gateway Arch rises over the NHL shield, with the Winter Classic script across the middle and a red banner reading 2017 ST. LOUIS underneath. The game was played Jan. 2, 2017. The arch is one of the cleanest civic landmarks any Winter Classic logo has had to work with and the design committed to it the way the 2014 mark committed to the Big House. Simple, regional, instantly readable. A-.

## 2018 · Rangers at Sabres · Citi Field

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr; gap: 12px; margin: 1.5em 0; max-width: 320px; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;">
  <div style="text-align: center;"><img src="/images/posts/nhl-winter-classic-2027-logo-history-ranked/2018.png" alt="2018 NHL Winter Classic 10th Anniversary logo for Rangers at Sabres at Citi Field New York" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 8px;" /><p style="font-size: 0.85em; color: #666; margin: 0.4em 0 0;">2018 · Grade B+</p></div>
</div>

The Winter Classic turned 10 in 2018 and the logo carries a 10TH ANNIVERSARY ribbon across the top. Behind it is a New York skyline with the Statue of Liberty, a big gold X behind the wordmark, and the Bridgestone lockup over CITI FIELD 2018. The Sabres were the designated home team, but the game was in Queens and the Rangers won 3-2 in overtime. The anniversary stamp and the skyline carry it, even if the mark is crowded. B+.

## 2019 · Bruins at Blackhawks · Notre Dame Stadium

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr; gap: 12px; margin: 1.5em 0; max-width: 320px; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;">
  <div style="text-align: center;"><img src="/images/posts/nhl-winter-classic-2027-logo-history-ranked/2019.png" alt="2019 NHL Winter Classic logo for Bruins at Blackhawks at Notre Dame Stadium, a green shamrock with a NOTRE DAME STADIUM banner" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 8px;" /><p style="font-size: 0.85em; color: #666; margin: 0.4em 0 0;">2019 · Grade A-</p></div>
</div>

The first Winter Classic on a college campus, with the Blackhawks as the home team against the Bruins, and the logo committed to Notre Dame. The whole badge is a green shamrock, with the white script across it and NOTRE DAME STADIUM on a gold ribbon at the bottom. The campus-stadium setting was a swing for the league and the logo backed it up. One of the most distinct Winter Classic marks in the run on the strength of the color story alone. A-.

## 2020 · Predators at Stars · Cotton Bowl

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr; gap: 12px; margin: 1.5em 0; max-width: 320px; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;">
  <div style="text-align: center;"><img src="/images/posts/nhl-winter-classic-2027-logo-history-ranked/2020.png" alt="2020 NHL Winter Classic logo for Predators at Stars at Cotton Bowl Dallas with Bridgestone title sponsor" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 8px;" /><p style="font-size: 0.85em; color: #666; margin: 0.4em 0 0;">2020 · Grade A-</p></div>
</div>

Dallas hosted at the Cotton Bowl in front of 85,630 fans, and the logo is a Texas belt buckle. Silver with engraved scrollwork, a gold Winter Classic script, 2020 on a gold bar and DALLAS ★ TEXAS on a ribbon at the bottom. It is the most original shape in the run and nobody could mistake it for any other city. The engraving gets muddy at small sizes, but no other Winter Classic logo took a swing like this. A-.

## 2021 · CANCELLED · The COVID Year

The 2021 Winter Classic between the Blues and the Wild at Target Field was called off because of the COVID-19 pandemic. The league kept the matchup and the venue and played it as the 2022 Winter Classic.

## 2022 · Blues at Wild · Target Field

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr; gap: 12px; margin: 1.5em 0; max-width: 320px; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;">
  <div style="text-align: center;"><img src="/images/posts/nhl-winter-classic-2027-logo-history-ranked/2022.png" alt="2022 NHL Winter Classic logo for Blues at Wild at Target Field, a shield with pine trees and a frozen lake, Discover and MINNESOTA 2022" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 8px;" /><p style="font-size: 0.85em; color: #666; margin: 0.4em 0 0;">2022 · Grade B-</p></div>
</div>

The Discover era begins. Bridgestone is out as title sponsor and Discover is in. The 2022 logo is a shield with a row of pine trees and a snowy, frozen lake at the top, the Winter Classic script and Discover across the middle, and MINNESOTA 2022 at the bottom. It is pond-hockey Minnesota, which is the right idea. The trees are a little generic and the shield is plain, and nothing points to Target Field or the Twin Cities. But the frozen lake is real Minnesota pond hockey, and that is more identity than the 2016 logo had. B-.

Fenway Park's second Winter Classic, played Jan. 2, 2023, with the Bruins beating the Penguins 2-1. The logo is the Green Monster. CLASSIC is spelled out one letter per box like the hand-operated scoreboard in left field, the badge is Fenway green with 2023 in the corner, and BOSTON sits on a black plate at the bottom under Discover. A smart way to get a second, different logo out of the same ballpark. B+.

It fits a port city and a team named after a sea monster, and it gave the young Kraken a real visual moment. B+.

This time the logo uses Wrigley's other icon: the ivy-covered brick outfield wall. Ivy spills off both sides, a brick base runs along the bottom, and the badge is Cubs-and-Blackhawks red with CHICAGO under Discover. Where 2009 was the marquee, 2025 is the bleachers. A-.

## 2027 · Avalanche at Mammoth · Rice-Eccles Stadium

The new entry, covered up top: a hexagon with snow-capped mountains above and red rock canyon walls below, a tusk-like swoosh off the "W," Discover, and UTAH at the bottom point, with no year. ## The Bottom Line on NHL Winter Classic Logo History

The Winter Classic has produced far more strong logos than weak ones over 18 games, which is unusual for an annual event mark. The pattern holds: the logos built around one real landmark (the Wrigley marquee, the Big House, the Gateway Arch, the Notre Dame shamrock, the Green Monster, Wrigley's ivy) beat the ones that lean on a generic frame (2016 Foxboro, 2022 Minnesota). The 2027 Utah logo sits in the first group, with real Utah scenery doing the work, which is why it lands a B+ before we see how Rice-Eccles looks on New Year's Eve. Big sponsor lockups, Bridgestone and now Discover, have taken some heritage feel out of the program. Our advice to the league stays the same: make the venue the hero. The logos that do that win every time.

## Frequently Asked Questions About NHL Winter Classic Logos

**When did the NHL Winter Classic start?**

The first NHL Winter Classic was played on January 1, 2008 between the Pittsburgh Penguins and the Buffalo Sabres at Ralph Wilson Stadium in Orchard Park, New York. Sidney Crosby scored the shootout winner in falling snow. The league has played a Winter Classic on or around New Year's Day every season since, except the cancelled 2013 and 2021 games.

**Why were the 2013 and 2021 Winter Classics cancelled?**

The 2013 Winter Classic between the Toronto Maple Leafs and the Detroit Red Wings at Michigan Stadium was cancelled by the 2012-13 NHL lockout. The game was played on Jan. 1, 2014, with essentially the same logo plus a 2014 tab. The 2021 Winter Classic between the St. Louis Blues and the Minnesota Wild at Target Field was cancelled by the COVID-19 pandemic and rescheduled to 2022.

**What is the 2027 NHL Winter Classic matchup?**

The Colorado Avalanche visit the Utah Mammoth on Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026, at Rice-Eccles Stadium on the University of Utah campus in Salt Lake City. It is the first outdoor game in Mammoth history and the first NHL outdoor game in Utah.

**Which Winter Classic logo is the best?**

Our pick is the 2014 Winter Classic logo for the Toronto Maple Leafs vs Detroit Red Wings game at Michigan Stadium. The Big House subtitle, the stadium silhouette, the 105,491 attendance moment, and the visual commitment to the venue all combine into the strongest mark of the 18. The 2009 Wrigley Field mark and the 2017 St. Louis Gateway Arch mark are the next two on our list.

**Who is the title sponsor of the NHL Winter Classic?**

Discover has been the title sponsor since the 2022 game, so the event is the Discover NHL Winter Classic, and the Discover name is on every logo from 2022 through the new 2027 Utah logo. Before that, Bridgestone was the title sponsor through the 2020 game, and its name is built into the 2012 and 2018 logos. The first game in 2008 was the AMP Energy NHL Winter Classic.

**Where has the NHL Winter Classic been played?**

The Winter Classic has been hosted at Ralph Wilson Stadium (Buffalo), Wrigley Field (Chicago, twice), Fenway Park (Boston, twice), Heinz Field (Pittsburgh), Citizens Bank Park (Philadelphia), Michigan Stadium (Ann Arbor), Nationals Park (Washington, D.C.), Gillette Stadium (Foxborough), Busch Stadium (St. Louis), Citi Field (New York), Notre Dame Stadium (Notre Dame, Indiana), the Cotton Bowl (Dallas), Target Field (Minneapolis), T-Mobile Park (Seattle) and loanDepot park (Miami). The 2027 game at Rice-Eccles Stadium will be the first Winter Classic in Utah.

**Will the Winter Classic come back to Wrigley Field or Fenway Park?**

Wrigley Field has hosted twice already, in 2009 and 2025, and Fenway Park has hosted twice as well, in 2010 and 2023. The league has not announced a third visit to either. Both produced strong logos both times, and each second logo used a different landmark from the same park: Wrigley's ivy in 2025 after the marquee in 2009, and the Green Monster in 2023 after the old ballpark sign in 2010.

**When is the 2027 NHL Winter Classic?**

The 2027 Discover NHL Winter Classic is on Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026, New Year's Eve, at 6 p.m. ET (4 p.m. Mountain), with the Colorado Avalanche at the Utah Mammoth at Rice-Eccles Stadium in Salt Lake City. It airs on TNT, truTV and HBO Max in the U.S.

**What was the first NHL Winter Classic logo?**

The first NHL Winter Classic logo was designed for the 2008 game between the Pittsburgh Penguins and the Buffalo Sabres at Ralph Wilson Stadium in Orchard Park, New York. It is an ice-blue oval with the NHL shield on top, the white Winter Classic script, a puck and 2008 under CLASSIC. We grade it B+ on the strength of laying the visual foundation every subsequent Winter Classic logo has built on.

**How many NHL Winter Classic logos are there?**

There are 18 Winter Classic game logos from 2008 through 2027, one for each game played. The years 2013 and 2021 had no game: the 2013 Maple Leafs-Red Wings game was cancelled by the lockout and the 2021 Blues-Wild game by COVID-19. Both matchups were played the next time, in 2014 and 2022. A logo for the 2013 game was shown in 2012, and the 2014 logo is essentially that design with the year added.

**Which NHL Winter Classic logo is the worst?**

Our pick for the weakest NHL Winter Classic logo in the 2008-2027 run is the 2016 Canadiens vs Bruins mark at Gillette Stadium. It is clean and wintry, with an ice-blue frame and icicles, but it is the only one with no landmark at all. Nothing in it says Foxborough or New England beyond the word on the ribbon. We grade it C+.

**What is the 2027 Winter Classic logo?**

The 2027 Discover NHL Winter Classic logo is a hexagon with snow-capped mountains at the top and red rock canyon walls at the bottom. The white Winter Classic script runs across the middle, with a curved swoosh off the "W" described as a mammoth tusk, then CLASSIC in sand-colored letters, Discover, and UTAH at the bottom point. The NHL revealed it on May 13, 2026.

**Where is the 2027 Winter Classic?**

Rice-Eccles Stadium, the University of Utah's football stadium in Salt Lake City. The Utah Mammoth are the home team against the Colorado Avalanche. It is the first NHL outdoor game in Utah.

**Why is it called the 2027 Winter Classic if it is on Dec. 31, 2026?**

The league already played a 2026 Winter Classic, Rangers at Panthers in Miami on Jan. 2, 2026. This game is in the 2026-27 season, so it is billed as 2027, and the logo itself carries no year.

**Is the Winter Classic logo on the jerseys?**

Yes. The Avalanche jersey wears the 2027 Winter Classic logo as a shoulder patch. Utah's shoulder carries a new round Utah Mammoth patch.

**Which team has played in the most Winter Classics?**

The Chicago Blackhawks, with five: 2009, 2015, 2017, 2019 and 2025. The Boston Bruins are next with four: 2010, 2016, 2019 and 2023.

**Which Winter Classic had the biggest crowd?**

The 2014 game at Michigan Stadium, with 105,491 fans for Maple Leafs vs. Red Wings. It is the largest crowd in NHL history. The 2020 game at the Cotton Bowl is second at 85,630.

**Has the Winter Classic ever been played outside the U.S.?**

No. Every Winter Classic has been in an American stadium. The NHL's outdoor games in Canada have been the Heritage Classic, a separate event with its own logos.
