Seattle's closet is a strange one to rank, because the best jersey in it is the oldest one. The modern college navy and white set carries the ordinary weeks, the Rivalries uniform gets Christmas Day, and the royal blue throwback gets two primetime nights. The throwback is the jersey everybody wants the team to wear every week, and the defending Super Bowl LX champions only bring it out twice.

Here is every Seattle Seahawks jersey and uniform for 2026, ranked worst to first and graded, with when each one is worn and what it pairs with.

The white road jersey projects to all eight away games: at the Cardinals (Week 2), Commanders (Week 3), Broncos (Week 6), Raiders (Week 10), 49ers (Week 12), Eagles (Week 15), Panthers (Week 17), and the Week 18 trip to the Rams. None of those is a team confirmation. It is the standard road convention, and it pairs with the college navy helmet, with navy pants as our read for Week 2.

The jersey is college navy numbers with an action green outline on white, navy shoulder panels carrying the "SEAHAWKS" wordmark, and the feather pattern running up the collar. The numbers are the best part, because the green outline reads cleanly on white in a way it cannot quite on navy. The shoulder panels are the dated part: the swooping cut is very 2012 Nike, and the feather trim is too fine to register from the stands. It is a perfectly functional road set that looks like a decade-old template, so it grades a B-.

College navy is the everyday home jersey, and it projects to the six home dates not claimed by a special uniform: the Patriots (Week 1), Chargers (Week 4), 49ers (Week 5), Bears (Week 8, Monday night), Cardinals (Week 9), and Giants (Week 14). Week 1 is the only one already on the field: Seattle opened the season against New England in full navy, navy helmet over navy jersey over navy pants, and the matchup earned a B on our [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026). The usual pairing is the navy helmet with wolf grey pants.

The jersey itself is wolf grey numbers with an action green outline on navy, wolf grey shoulder panels, and a green shoulder cap. The green is the part that works, an aggressive accent nobody else in the league runs, and the wolf grey pants give the combination room to breathe. The weak spot is the numbers: grey on navy is low contrast, and the green outline is doing all the work to make them readable. We grade the uniform a B, the same mark we gave the whole Seattle set in our [best NFL uniforms ranking](/stories/best-nfl-uniforms-2026-ranked). Great colors, dated execution. The all-navy monochrome version from Week 1 is the one we would retire.

The Rivalries uniform gets one wearing in 2026, and it is the best slot any team has given the program: Week 16 at home against the Rams on Friday, December 25, Christmas Day. The Rams confirmed they are wearing their all-black Midnight Mode Rivalries set for the same game, so it is the first Rivalries-on-Rivalries matchup the program has had. This is the uniform's second season, since Seattle joined with the NFC West wave in 2025.

The helmet is the star. The iridescent green shell with the chrome hawk shifts color as the light moves, and it is the one finish that makes action green look expensive instead of loud. The jersey is built around the 12s: a soundwave pattern radiating from the collar across the shoulders for the noise at Lumen Field, and a 12 worked into the number fill and the back of the neck, all on a wolf grey base with navy trim. We ranked it second in our [NFL Rivalries uniform ranking](/stories/nfl-rivalries-uniforms-2026-ranked) and it keeps its B+ here. What holds it back is the body: strip the shoulders and it is a plain grey jersey, and the helmet can drift toward oil slick under the wrong lights.

The royal blue throwback is confirmed for two home games, both in primetime: Week 7 against the Chiefs on Sunday, October 25 on Sunday Night Football, and Week 13 against the Cowboys on Monday, December 7 on Monday Night Football. It pairs with the silver helmet carrying the original blue and green hawk, the look Seattle wore at home from 1983 through 2001 and brought back in 2023.

This is the best thing Seattle owns, and it is not close. The jersey is simple in the right places: big white block numbers on royal blue, a green and white striped V-neck, and the old hawk logo sitting on a blue, white and green sleeve stripe. Every element reads from the upper deck, which is exactly what the modern set's fine feather trim does not do. The silver helmet also matters, because a helmet that does not match the jersey gives the uniform a second color story instead of one dark block.

It grades an A-, and it is the clearest signal that the current set needs a refresh. When a throwback beats the modern primary this comfortably, it should be the primary. Two national windows is the most the team has ever given it, and Kansas City in red and Dallas in white are both clean contrasts for royal blue.

## The Bottom Line on the Seahawks Jersey Rankings

Seattle's top two are excellent and they are both special-occasion uniforms. The royal blue throwback is an A- with bold numbers and a silver helmet that the modern set cannot match, and the Rivalries set is a B+ carried by the best helmet in the program. Both got primetime or holiday slots, which is the best staging in the NFC West.

The everyday uniforms are where the closet sags. College navy is a B with great colors held back by low-contrast grey numbers, and the white road set is a B- that looks like the 2012 template it is. The fix is already on the way: the team's new owners are [bringing back the white throwback in 2027](/stories/seahawks-white-throwback-uniforms-2027), which would give the best uniform in the closet a road half.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jerseys do the Seahawks have in 2026?**

Four: the college navy home jersey, the white road jersey, the royal blue throwback, and the grey Rivalries uniform. The throwback is scheduled for Weeks 7 and 13 and Rivalries for Week 16 on Christmas Day.

**Did the Seahawks retire the Action Green uniform?**

It looks that way. The all-green Color Rush set first appeared in 2016, but it is not on Seattle's 2026 uniform schedule, and the team spent its alternate dates on the royal blue throwback and the Rivalries set instead. We have left it out of this ranking and will add it back if Seattle brings it out again.

**What is the best Seahawks jersey?**

We rank the royal blue throwback first and grade it an A-. Its white block numbers, striped collar and old hawk sleeve logo read from anywhere in the stadium, and the silver helmet gives it a second color the modern navy set does not have.

**What jersey are the Seahawks wearing this week?**

Week 2 is at the Arizona Cardinals on Sunday, September 20 at 4:25 ET. Seattle is projected in the white road jersey under the navy helmet, with navy pants as our read, against Arizona's confirmed desert Rivalries uniform. The [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026) logs the real combination once the game is played.

**Do the Seahawks have a new jersey in 2026?**

No. Every 2026 uniform is a returning set, and the Rivalries jersey is in its second season. The next new addition is the white throwback road jersey, which the team has announced for the 2027 season.

**When do the Seahawks wear the royal blue throwback?**

Twice in 2026, both at Lumen Field and both in primetime: Week 7 against the Chiefs on Sunday, October 25 on Sunday Night Football, and Week 13 against the Cowboys on Monday, December 7 on Monday Night Football.

**When do the Seahawks wear the Rivalries uniform?**

Once, on Christmas Day, Friday, December 25, at home against the Los Angeles Rams. The Rams wear their Rivalries uniform in the same game.
