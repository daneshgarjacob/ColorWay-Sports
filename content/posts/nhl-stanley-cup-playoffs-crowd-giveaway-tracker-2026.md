## Frequently Asked Questions About NHL Playoff Crowd Giveaways

**Do NHL teams give out free t-shirts and towels to fans during the playoffs?**

Yes. Most NHL home teams run crowd giveaway programs through the Stanley Cup Playoffs, with t-shirts at every seat or rally towels handed out at the entrances of the arena. Programs vary team to team. Some teams commit to a full color takeover with t-shirts at every seat, others run a simpler towel-only giveaway, and some teams skip the giveaway entirely.

**Which NHL teams ran the best crowd giveaways in the 2026 Stanley Cup Playoffs Round 1?**

We are tracking every reveal as it lands. Through Wednesday Round 1, the Vegas Golden Knights gold towels coordinated with their home gold sweater are the cleanest color match of the round. The Philadelphia Flyers orange shirt giveaway against their iconic orange home sweater has a chance to land at the top of the round once the shirt design is confirmed.

**What is the difference between a crowd t-shirt giveaway and a rally towel giveaway?**

A t-shirt giveaway puts a shirt on every seat for fans to wear during the game, which loads the entire lower bowl with one coordinated color for the full broadcast. A rally towel giveaway hands fans a towel they wave during stoppages and goals. Towels deliver bigger movement moments during scoring chances but never hit the visual ceiling of the all-shirt color takeover. Most teams choose one or the other based on the marketing budget for that specific game.

**Why do home teams coordinate the giveaway color with the home jersey?**

Coordinating the giveaway color with the home jersey gives the broadcast a single coherent in-arena visual. When the crowd, the jersey, and the rink graphics all share a color story, the home arena reads tight on TV and the road team's contrasting color pops against it. Vegas gold towels with the Vegas gold sweater is a textbook example. The Philadelphia orange shirts with the Flyers orange Icon home sweater is another.
