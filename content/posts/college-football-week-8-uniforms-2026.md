**Update, October 24/25:** Every game below now has its final score, plus what each team actually wore wherever we could confirm it from the school's own posts and game photos. The big results: **Miami won 52-23 in the 2001 Tribute**, **UCF beat No. 2 BYU 39-31 in double overtime** in its all black Drip U set, **Illinois beat Oregon 21-10** in the military appreciation helmet with a navy jersey and orange pants, and **Arizona State finished its eclipse look with black pants** in a 28-17 win over Kansas State. Stanford's all red really was all red, with cardinal pants, in a 31-28 Friday night win over NC State. Texas beat No. 7 Ole Miss 34-27, South Carolina beat No. 11 Tennessee 27-24 in all black, Utah went all black to beat No. 14 Houston 21-13, and No. 6 Indiana won 13-6 in overtime at Michigan.

Week 8 of the 2026 college football season opens with one of the best throwbacks of the year. **No. 1 Miami wears its 2001 Tribute uniform against Pitt**, the orange jersey and white helmet of the last Hurricanes team to win a national title, 25 years later. **Arizona State goes black on black for Kansas State** and is asking its crowd to "Enter the eclipse," **No. 9 UCLA breaks out the powder blue for Homecoming** against Michigan State, and **Illinois puts a military appreciation helmet on against Oregon**, which is wearing yellow helmets with green chrome wings. SMU goes full Dallas against Cal, Stanford goes all red on Friday night, UCF calls its black Homecoming set "Drip U" against No. 2 BYU, and No. 21 Boise State brings a white helmet to Pullman. On the field, No. 7 Ole Miss visits No. 19 Texas, Texas A&M visits No. 13 Alabama and No. 14 Houston closes the night at No. 10 Utah.

Below is every game this week involving an AP Top 25 team or one of the programs we track in our college uniform schedules, with kickoff time, TV and what each team is wearing. A look is marked **Confirmed** when the school itself has posted it. When a school has not posted yet, we say so and do not guess. Most schools post their combination between Monday and game day, and on Saturday we confirm the rest from the game broadcasts and team photos, then add final scores. The rankings below are from this week's AP poll. All times are Eastern. Last week's looks and final scores are in our [Week 7 uniform guide](/stories/college-football-week-7-uniforms-2026).

## Games of the Week

### Pitt at No. 1 Miami: The 2001 Tribute

**Saturday, noon, ESPN.** **Miami: Confirmed, 2001 Tribute.** Miami posted "A legendary look for Week 8" on Thursday with a graphic titled 2001 TRIBUTE. It is a white helmet with the orange and green U and an orange center stripe trimmed in green, an orange jersey with white numbers trimmed in green and green trim on the shoulders and sleeves, and white pants with a green and orange stripe down the side. The graphic puts the new set next to photos of the 2001 team in the same look, under the line "25 years later, the 2001 uniform represents the Miami brand."

**Our take:** this is the right throwback for this team. The 2001 Hurricanes are the standard every Miami roster gets measured against, and this year's team is 6-0 and No. 1. The uniform is not a costume either. It is Miami's own colors in the cleanest arrangement the program has ever worn, and the white helmet with the stripe looks better than anything on the current rotation. **Pitt: Confirmed**, gold helmet, white jersey, gold pants. Pitt posted its combination as gold, white, gold on Saturday morning with locker room photos: the gold helmet with the blue Pitt script and a blue facemask, white jerseys with blue numbers and gold pants with a blue stripe hanging in the stalls. **Final: Miami 52, Pitt 23**, both teams in the looks they posted. More: [the full 2001 throwback story](/stories/miami-2001-throwback-uniforms), [Miami schedule](/stories/miami-hurricanes-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Pitt schedule](/stories/pitt-uniform-schedule-2026).

### Michigan State at No. 9 UCLA: Powder Blue for Homecoming

**Saturday, 3:30 p.m. (12:30 p.m. Pacific), CBS, at the Rose Bowl.** **UCLA: Confirmed, powder blue Homecoming set.** UCLA posted "Homecoming threads" with Jordan Brand on Wednesday. The photos show a lighter powder blue jersey with white shoulder stripes and white numbers, gold pants and the gold helmet with a gray facemask. That is the powder blue look honoring the 1954 national champions, the same idea UCLA wore for Homecoming in 2023 and 2025, with white stripes where the regular home jersey has gold.

**Our take:** we have been waiting on this one since August. The regular blue and gold home set is great, but the powder blue with white stripes is the UCLA uniform people picture when they close their eyes, and the gold pants keep it from looking washed out. UCLA is 6-0. **Michigan State: Confirmed**, green helmet, white jersey, green pants. Michigan State posted "Gameday Threads" coded green, white, green on Thursday night: the green helmet with the Spartan stripe and STATE on the front bumper, a white jersey with MICHIGAN STATE across the chest and green numbers, and green pants. **Final: UCLA 28, Michigan State 10**, and the Bruins are 7-0 after a powder blue Homecoming. More: [UCLA schedule](/stories/ucla-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Michigan State schedule](/stories/michigan-state-uniform-schedule-2026).

### Oregon at Illinois: Military Helmet vs. Yellow Wings

## The Bottom Line

Week 8 belongs to Miami. The 2001 Tribute is one of the best throwbacks of the season, second only to UCLA's powder blue in our [throwback rankings](/stories/best-throwback-uniforms-2026-every-sport), because it is not really a throwback at all, just Miami's own orange, green and white in the order the greatest team in school history wore them, and this year's 6-0 team gets to wear it at No. 1. Right behind it are UCLA's powder blue, the most beautiful jersey in the sport, finally back for Homecoming, and Illinois' military helmet, which saves its best work for the back bumper. Arizona State's eclipse black and SMU's Dallas set are the two new looks of the week, and SMU's Peruna inside the D is a detail we would keep forever. The matchup to watch for contrast is Oregon's yellow wings against the orange Illinois helmet in Champaign. On the scoreboard the special looks did their job: Miami, UCLA, Illinois, Arizona State, SMU, UCF, Arizona and Stanford all won in them. The night belonged to black, with South Carolina, Utah and Arizona State all going head to toe and all winning.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are college football teams wearing in Week 8?**

The specials, all posted by the schools, are Miami's 2001 Tribute against Pitt, Arizona State's eclipse black against Kansas State, UCLA's powder blue Homecoming set against Michigan State, Illinois' military appreciation helmet against Oregon, SMU's Dallas set against Cal, Stanford's all red on Friday night, UCF's Drip U Homecoming set against BYU and Arizona's classic Homecoming look against Iowa State. Every one of those teams won. Every game is listed above with what each team wore and the final score.

**What is Miami wearing against Pitt?**

Miami wears its 2001 Tribute uniform on Saturday, October 24: a white helmet with the orange and green U and an orange center stripe trimmed in green, an orange jersey with white numbers, and white pants with a green and orange side stripe. Miami won 52-23.

**Why is Miami wearing a 2001 throwback?**

The 2001 Hurricanes went undefeated and won the national title, and 2026 is the 25th anniversary. Miami's graphic says the 2001 uniform still represents the Miami brand 25 years later. Our [full 2001 throwback story](/stories/miami-2001-throwback-uniforms) has the history.

**What is Arizona State wearing against Kansas State?**

Arizona State wore a black helmet, a black jersey with gold lettering and black pants, posted as "Enter the eclipse" and confirmed from the team's game photos. Fans were asked to wear black. Arizona State won 28-17. Kansas State wore a purple helmet, white jersey and purple pants.

**What is UCLA wearing for Homecoming?**

UCLA wears its powder blue Homecoming set against Michigan State: a lighter powder blue jersey with white shoulder stripes and white numbers, the gold helmet and gold pants. It honors the 1954 national champions. UCLA won 28-10 to move to 7-0.

**What helmet is Illinois wearing against Oregon?**

Illinois wears a military appreciation helmet: orange with the ILLINI wordmark, flag decals, a navy and white center stripe, and a white rear bumper with the seals of the military branches. Illinois wore it with a navy jersey and orange pants, confirmed from the team's game photos. Oregon wore a yellow helmet with green chrome wings, a white jersey and white pants. Illinois won 21-10.

**What is SMU wearing against Cal?**

SMU wears a Dallas themed set, posted as "Putting on for the Triple D": a white helmet with the red Peruna inside a blue D, a royal blue jersey with Dallas in white script, and white pants. SMU won 59-20.

**What is Stanford wearing against NC State?**

Stanford went all red on Friday night, posted as "All red for FNL": a cardinal helmet with a white stripe and the Stanford script, the cardinal jersey and cardinal pants, confirmed from the team's game photos. Stanford won 31-28.

**What is Boise State wearing at Washington State?**

Boise State wore a white helmet, white jersey and blue pants, confirmed by the team. Washington State wore all crimson for Homecoming. Boise State won 31-10.

**What did BYU wear against UCF?**

BYU wore its road whites: royal blue helmet, white jersey and white pants, posted by the team as "road whites looking clean." UCF wore a gold helmet, black jersey and black pants for Homecoming and won 39-31 in double overtime.

**What did Utah wear against Houston?**

Utah went all black: black helmet, black jersey and black pants, from the team's game photos. Houston wore a white helmet, white jersey and red pants. Utah won 21-13.

**Who is off in Week 8?**

No. 3 Ohio State, No. 4 Notre Dame, No. 5 Georgia, No. 17 Wake Forest, No. 20 Florida and No. 22 Missouri are off, along with Penn State, Nebraska, Washington, Louisville, Florida State and several others.

**Is this Week 8 or Week 9?**

Week 8. ESPN's schedule and the AP poll both count the October 22 to 24 games as Week 8, and Miami's own post calls its uniform "a legendary look for Week 8." Some schools count games played instead, which is why UCF and Baylor call this their Game 7.
