Week 8 of the 2026 college football season opens with the best throwback of the year. **No. 1 Miami wears its 2001 Tribute uniform against Pitt**, the orange jersey and white helmet of the last Hurricanes team to win a national title, 25 years later. **Arizona State goes black on black for Kansas State** and is asking its crowd to "Enter the eclipse," **No. 9 UCLA breaks out the powder blue for Homecoming** against Michigan State, and **Illinois puts a military appreciation helmet on against Oregon**, which is wearing yellow helmets with green chrome wings. SMU goes full Dallas against Cal, Stanford goes all red on Friday night, UCF calls its black Homecoming set "Drip U" against No. 2 BYU, and No. 21 Boise State brings a white helmet to Pullman. On the field, No. 7 Ole Miss visits No. 19 Texas, Texas A&M visits No. 13 Alabama and No. 14 Houston closes the night at No. 10 Utah.

Below is every game this week involving an AP Top 25 team or one of the programs we track in our college uniform schedules, with kickoff time, TV and what each team is wearing. A look is marked **Confirmed** when the school itself has posted it. When a school has not posted yet, we say so and do not guess. Most schools post their combination between Monday and game day, and on Saturday we confirm the rest from the game broadcasts and team photos, then add final scores. The rankings below are from this week's AP poll. All times are Eastern. Last week's looks and final scores are in our [Week 7 uniform guide](/stories/college-football-week-7-uniforms-2026).

## Games of the Week

### Pitt at No. 1 Miami: The 2001 Tribute

**Saturday, noon, ESPN.** **Miami: Confirmed, 2001 Tribute.** Miami posted "A legendary look for Week 8" on Thursday with a graphic titled 2001 TRIBUTE. It is a white helmet with the orange and green U and an orange center stripe trimmed in green, an orange jersey with white numbers trimmed in green and green trim on the shoulders and sleeves, and white pants with a green and orange stripe down the side. The graphic puts the new set next to photos of the 2001 team in the same look, under the line "25 years later, the 2001 uniform represents the Miami brand."

**Our take:** this is the right throwback for this team. The 2001 Hurricanes are the standard every Miami roster gets measured against, and this year's team is 6-0 and No. 1. The uniform is not a costume either. It is Miami's own colors in the cleanest arrangement the program has ever worn, and the white helmet with the stripe looks better than anything on the current rotation. **Pitt:** not posted yet. Pitt wore all white at Boston College last week. More: [the full 2001 throwback story](/stories/miami-2001-throwback-uniforms), [Miami schedule](/stories/miami-hurricanes-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Pitt schedule](/stories/pitt-uniform-schedule-2026).

### Michigan State at No. 9 UCLA: Powder Blue for Homecoming

**Saturday, 3:30 p.m. (12:30 p.m. Pacific), CBS, at the Rose Bowl.** **UCLA: Confirmed, powder blue Homecoming set.** UCLA posted "Homecoming threads" with Jordan Brand on Wednesday. The photos show a lighter powder blue jersey with white shoulder stripes and white numbers, gold pants and the gold helmet with a gray facemask. That is the powder blue look honoring the 1954 national champions, the same idea UCLA wore for Homecoming in 2023 and 2025, with white stripes where the regular home jersey has gold.

**Our take:** we have been waiting on this one since August. The regular blue and gold home set is great, but the powder blue with white stripes is the UCLA uniform people picture when they close their eyes, and the gold pants keep it from looking washed out. UCLA is 6-0. **Michigan State:** not posted yet. More: [UCLA schedule](/stories/ucla-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Michigan State schedule](/stories/michigan-state-uniform-schedule-2026).

### Cal at No. 23 SMU: Putting On for the Triple D

**Saturday, noon (11 a.m. Central), The CW.** **SMU: Confirmed, Dallas set.** SMU posted "Putting on for the Triple D" on Wednesday night with photos shot around downtown Dallas. The helmet is white with blue stripes down the middle, a blue facemask and a new side logo: the red Peruna inside a big blue D. The jersey is royal blue with Dallas in white script across the chest and white numbers, and the pants are white with a blue stripe.

**Our take:** Dallas across the chest works for a school whose campus sits in University Park, surrounded on all sides by Dallas, and the royal blue is SMU's best color. The D around Peruna is the kind of helmet detail that should become permanent. **Cal:** not posted yet. Cal lost 41-7 to Wake Forest in its Grateful Dead helmet last week. **What are college football teams wearing in Week 8?**

The specials, all posted by the schools, are Miami's 2001 Tribute against Pitt, Arizona State's eclipse black against Kansas State, UCLA's powder blue Homecoming set against Michigan State, Illinois' military appreciation helmet against Oregon, SMU's Dallas set against Cal, Stanford's all red on Friday night, UCF's Drip U Homecoming set against BYU and Arizona's classic Homecoming look against Iowa State. **What is Miami wearing against Pitt?**

Miami wears its 2001 Tribute uniform on Saturday, October 24: a white helmet with the orange and green U and an orange center stripe trimmed in green, an orange jersey with white numbers, and white pants with a green and orange side stripe. Kickoff is noon Eastern on ESPN at Hard Rock Stadium.

**Why is Miami wearing a 2001 throwback?**

The 2001 Hurricanes went undefeated and won the national title, and 2026 is the 25th anniversary. Miami's graphic says the 2001 uniform still represents the Miami brand 25 years later. Our [full 2001 throwback story](/stories/miami-2001-throwback-uniforms) has the history.

**What is Arizona State wearing against Kansas State?**

Arizona State wears a black helmet and a black jersey with gold lettering, posted as "Enter the eclipse." The pants have not been shown yet. Fans are asked to wear black. Kickoff is 3:45 p.m. Eastern, 12:45 p.m. in Tempe, on ESPN2.

**What is UCLA wearing for Homecoming?**

UCLA wears its powder blue Homecoming set against Michigan State: a lighter powder blue jersey with white shoulder stripes and white numbers, the gold helmet and gold pants. It honors the 1954 national champions. Kickoff is 3:30 p.m. Eastern on CBS at the Rose Bowl.

**What helmet is Illinois wearing against Oregon?**

Illinois wears a military appreciation helmet: orange with the ILLINI wordmark, flag decals, a navy and white center stripe, and a white rear bumper with the seals of the military branches. Illinois has not shown the jersey or pants yet. Oregon wears a yellow helmet with green chrome wings, a white jersey and white pants.

**What is SMU wearing against Cal?**

SMU wears a Dallas themed set, posted as "Putting on for the Triple D": a white helmet with the red Peruna inside a blue D, a royal blue jersey with Dallas in white script, and white pants. Kickoff is noon Eastern on The CW.

**What is Stanford wearing against NC State?**

Stanford goes all red on Friday night: a cardinal helmet with a white stripe and the Stanford script, and the cardinal jersey, posted as "All red for FNL." Kickoff is 7:30 p.m. Pacific on ESPN.

**What is Boise State wearing at Washington State?**

Boise State wears a white helmet, white jersey and blue pants, confirmed by the team. Kickoff is 3 p.m. Pacific on USA Network at Martin Stadium.

Week 8. ESPN's schedule and the AP poll both count the October 22 to 24 games as Week 8, and Miami's own post calls its uniform "a legendary look for Week 8." Some schools count games played instead, which is why UCF and Baylor call this their Game 7.
