**The Commanders painted a black helmet on their field for Monday night.** Washington posted a 35-second "MNF field reveal" video on Saturday morning, November 21, walking the grass at Northwest Stadium two days before it hosts the **Cincinnati Bengals on Monday Night Football, November 23, at 8:15 p.m. Eastern on ESPN**. The new piece is a huge **black helmet drawn in white outline, with the Spear W across the top in burgundy and gold**. It is the helmet from the all-black **Hail Raiser** uniform, which Washington wears in a game for the first time that night.

## Quick Answers

- **What does the Commanders' Monday Night Football field look like?** A giant black helmet is painted on the grass, outlined in white, with the Spear W logo in burgundy and gold running across the top, plus the burgundy W logo outlined in gold.
- **When was it revealed?** Saturday, November 21, in a video the Commanders posted with the caption "MNF field reveal."
- **Why a black helmet?** Washington wears the Hail Raiser, its all-black alternate with a black helmet and the Spear W, for the first time in this game.
- **When is the game?** Monday, November 23, 8:15 p.m. Eastern, ESPN, Commanders vs. Bengals at Northwest Stadium.
- **What are the teams wearing?** Washington: black helmet, black jersey, black pants. Cincinnati, confirmed by the Bengals: orange helmet, white jersey, white pants.
- **Our grade:** pending.

## What The Field Shows

We went through the team's video frame by frame. Here is what it shows, and only what it shows:

- **The helmet.** A football helmet in profile, drawn as **thick white outlines on black**: the shell, the ear hole and the bars of the facemask, all in white. The black fills the shell and the circle around it, so it reads as a black helmet sitting on the grass.
- **The Spear W.** Running across the top of the helmet is the **Spear W**, the W with spear points on both ends, filled in **burgundy and outlined in gold**, with gold spear tips. It is the logo on the side of the real Hail Raiser helmet.
- **The W logo.** The video also passes over the **burgundy W logo outlined in gold** painted on the grass, and a large **gold** shape painted in the turf near it.
- **The walls.** The burgundy wall behind the end zone reads **RAISE HAIL** in white letters with gold W logos.

The video is shot at ground level and never shows the whole field from above, so it does not make clear exactly where on the field each piece is painted. We will add that once the Commanders or ESPN show it from overhead on Monday.

## What Washington Is Wearing On Monday Night

This field is built around the uniform. The **Hail Raiser** is Washington's all-black alternate from the April redesign: a **black helmet with the Spear W**, a **black jersey** with burgundy numbers outlined in gold, and **black pants**. Monday is its first game, and it gets one more date this season, at home against the Falcons in Week 15 on December 20. Every detail and our grade of the uniform itself are in our [Hail Raiser story](/stories/commanders-hail-raiser-uniform-bengals-monday-night-football-2026).

The Bengals have confirmed **orange helmets, white jerseys and white pants**, so it is black against white under the lights.
