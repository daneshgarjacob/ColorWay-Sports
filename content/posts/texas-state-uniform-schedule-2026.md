Texas State is playing its first Pac-12 season, and the Bobcats have already used two different gold helmets in San Marcos. This is the full Texas State 2026 uniform schedule: the maroon home jersey, the white road set, the gold and white helmets, the Bobcats script shell that showed up for Family Weekend, every game played so far, including the 31-29 loss in the Pac-12 opener at San Diego State on October 3, and the expected uniform for the rest of the year. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

The uniforms are Adidas, and the base look is simple: maroon and gold at home, white on the road. What makes Texas State interesting this season is the helmet rotation. The Bobcats wore the white shell in Austin, the gold Bobcat head shell for their first two home games, and then a gold shell with a maroon Bobcats script across the side for Family Weekend against UIW, paired with maroon pants for the first maroon-on-maroon look of the year. The program did not announce the script helmet in advance, and we have not seen it tied to any other date.

This is also a new conference. Texas State spent 13 seasons in the Sun Belt before joining the Pac-12 for 2026, and it comes in off three straight bowl wins, the last a 41-10 win over Rice in the Armed Forces Bowl on January 2. Our [Rice 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/rice-uniform-schedule-2026) covers the other side of that bowl.

**September 5 at Texas.** ★ Confirmed from Texas State's game photography, dated to the day of the game: **white helmet with the Bobcat head, white jersey, white pants**, all white at DKR-Texas Memorial Stadium. Texas wore its only home look, the white Longhorn helmet, burnt orange jersey and white pants, and won 59-7. Our [Texas 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/texas-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Longhorns side.

**September 12 vs. UTSA.** ★ Confirmed from Texas State's game photography, dated to the day of the game: **gold helmet with the Bobcat head, maroon jersey, gold pants** for the I-35 Showdown and home opener. UTSA came in all white with the white helmet. The Bobcats led 17-7 at halftime, but UTSA scored 24 points in the second half and won 31-26. Our [UTSA 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/utsa-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Roadrunners side.

**September 19 vs. North Texas.** ★ Confirmed from Texas State's game photography, dated to the day of the game: **gold helmet with the Bobcat head, maroon jersey, gold pants**, the same set as the week before, for the First Responder Bowl rematch and Faculty and Staff Appreciation Day. The same photos show North Texas in its announced Icy Whites, white helmet, white jersey and white pants. Texas State won a shootout 49-35. More on our [North Texas 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/north-texas-uniform-schedule-2026).

**September 26 vs. UIW.** ★ Confirmed from Texas State's game photography, dated to the day of the game: **gold helmet with a maroon Bobcats script, maroon jersey, maroon pants**, a new helmet and the first maroon-over-maroon look of the season for Family Weekend. Texas State scored 28 in the first quarter and won 63-10.

**October 3 at San Diego State**, Snapdragon Stadium, 7:30 p.m. Pacific (10:30 p.m. ET) on The CW. ★ Confirmed by Texas State: **white helmet, white jersey, maroon pants.** The program posted it as its Week 05 threads with the line Keepin' it cool. It is white over white up top, as it was at Texas, with maroon pants in place of the white ones, the second straight game in maroon pants after the Family Weekend win over UIW. It is the Pac-12 opener for both teams, and San Diego State is expected in black at home, which should make for a sharp white and maroon against black pairing. See our [San Diego State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/san-diego-state-uniform-schedule-2026). The CW broadcast matches: **white helmet, white jersey, maroon pants**, worn as announced, and San Diego State wore a red helmet, black jersey and black pants. Texas State led 17-3 early in the third quarter, but Jaylon Hawkins returned the second-half kickoff 96 yards and Cameron Cooper added a 32-yard pick-six, and short-handed San Diego State won 31-29. Brad Jackson threw for 372 yards and three touchdowns in the loss, and Texas State is 2-3.

## Which Helmet Is Next?

Texas State has worn three helmets in five games, and the script shell is the one to watch. It appeared for Family Weekend with the maroon pants and has not been announced for anything else. The Bobcat head gold shell looks like the default at home, and the white shell has been the road helmet in both road games, at Texas and at San Diego State. If the script helmet comes back, the Bobcats Blackout against Colorado State on October 15 and Homecoming against Utah State on October 24 are the two home dates that fit.

## The Bottom Line

Five games into its Pac-12 era, Texas State has run through white, gold and maroon without ever looking like anyone but Texas State. The maroon and old gold palette is one of the more distinctive in the conference, and the Bobcats have leaned on it: gold pants for the first two home games, maroon pants and a new script helmet for the third. The road helmet question at San Diego State was settled the same way, with the white helmet, white jersey and maroon pants worn as announced in the 31-29 loss. The open question now is whether the Blackout night on October 15 is a fan theme or a uniform. The grid above has every date, and we add a star as each uniform is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Texas State wearing this week?**

Texas State wore the white helmet, white jersey and maroon pants at San Diego State on Saturday, October 3, as announced and confirmed on the broadcast, in a 31-29 loss. After a bye on October 10, the Bobcats host Colorado State on Thursday, October 15, where maroon is expected for the Bobcats Blackout night.

**What helmet did Texas State wear against UIW?**

A gold helmet with a maroon Bobcats script on the side, instead of the Bobcat head logo the team wore in its first two home games. Texas State paired it with the maroon jersey and maroon pants for Family Weekend on September 26 and beat UIW 63-10.

**What are Texas State's football uniforms for 2026?**

A maroon home jersey and a white road jersey, both with a TEXAS STATE wordmark, plus gold, maroon and white pants. Texas State has worn three helmets: a gold shell with the Bobcat head, a gold shell with a Bobcats script and a white shell with the Bobcat head. The uniforms are made by Adidas.

**Is Texas State in the Pac-12?**

Yes. 2026 is Texas State's first season in the Pac-12 after 13 seasons in the Sun Belt. Its Pac-12 opener was at San Diego State on October 3, a 31-29 loss, and the Pac-12 Championship Game is Friday, December 4 at the home of the highest seed.

**Does Texas State have a blackout game in 2026?**

Yes, as a game theme. The Bobcats Blackout is Thursday, October 15 against Colorado State at UFCU Stadium. Texas State has not announced a black uniform for it, so our grid lists maroon until the team says otherwise.

**When is Texas State's homecoming in 2026?**

Saturday, October 24 against Utah State at UFCU Stadium, 6:30 p.m. Central on CBS Sports Network. Texas State is expected in maroon.
