The 2026 NBA Playoffs logo is the same legacy mark the NBA has been using on courts, broadcasts, and playoff branding for years. It pairs the red, white, and blue NBA logoman (the Jerry West silhouette) with a bold black "PLAYOFFS" wordmark. No rebrand, no modernized glyph, no year attached to the mark itself. The league keeps it simple, and the identity carries year over year.

## What the 2026 NBA Playoffs Logo Looks Like

The NBA Playoffs logo is a two-element lockup. On the left, the official NBA logoman in the red-and-blue badge. On the right, "PLAYOFFS" set in a heavy condensed black sans-serif, all caps. Same weight and style you see on regular season branding, just swapped from "NBA" to "PLAYOFFS."

Across the 2026 Playoffs you'll see it applied two primary ways:

- Sideline paint on the court next to each team's bench
- Corner graphics on TV broadcasts (ABC, ESPN, NBC, Amazon Prime Video)

## The 2026 NBA Finals Logo Is a Different Mark

When the Finals arrive, the Playoffs logo retires for the round. The 2026 NBA Finals logo is a full, ornate mark: the NBA logoman on the left, "Finals" in black cursive script across the center, and the Larry O'Brien Championship Trophy rendered in gold stretching behind the wordmark. Totally different visual system. Script instead of block lettering, gold instead of black, a trophy instead of flat type.

## Where the 2026 NBA Playoffs Logo Appears

Across Round 1 of the 2026 Playoffs, the mark has shown up in four places:

- **Courts:** Painted on the sideline next to each team's bench at every arena.
- **Broadcasts:** Integrated into the corner scorebug on ABC, ESPN, TNT Sports, and NBC.
- **League merchandise:** Apparel drops, Statement-type merch, and Fanatics playoff collections.
- **Social and marketing:** NBA social posts, team accounts, and league promos.

One notable absence: the logo has not been painted on center court since 2015. That's 11 consecutive playoffs without the Playoffs mark at midcourt. We wrote a [full visual history of NBA Playoffs court branding](/stories/nba-playoffs-branding-court-logo-history) that traces every change.

## How the 2026 Logo Compares to Past Years

The NBA Playoffs logo has been essentially stable since the early 2010s. Comparing 2026 to 2019, 2022, or 2014, the design language has held: logoman plus "PLAYOFFS" wordmark, same red, white, blue, and black palette.

What has changed is the usage, not the logo itself. From 2010 to 2014, the mark appeared on center court at every playoff arena. Starting in 2015, the league replaced it with YouTube TV sponsor branding on the sideline. The NBA Cup added its own centered court logo in 2024, which proves the league is still willing to paint a mark at midcourt when it wants to. For 2026, the Playoffs logo stays on the sideline, and the Finals mark still gets the full broadcast treatment.

## Our Take

The 2026 NBA Playoffs logo is a fine mark. The logoman lockup is iconic, the typography is clean, and it reads well on broadcast. The problem isn't the logo, it's the placement. The league has moved the mark off of center court, which is the one place that would actually communicate "this is the playoffs" at a glance. As long as regular season games and playoff games share the same court, the logo on the sideline is doing less work than it could.
