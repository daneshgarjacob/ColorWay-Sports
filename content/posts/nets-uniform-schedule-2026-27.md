The Brooklyn Nets have already picked a jersey for every game of the 2026-27 season. It is not in a press release. It is on the team's own schedule page at nba.com/nets/schedule, where every one of the 80 regular season games carries a jersey tag, and we counted all of them as of September 28, 2026. Thanks to the reader who sent it in.

The headline is the **Classic Edition**. Brooklyn has it booked for **16 games**, more than the Icon black (14), and it opens the season on October 21 against Charlotte. The City Edition does not show up until January 23.

That covers all 80 games the league has dated. Two more December dates are still waiting on the NBA Cup knockout draw, and we will add them when Brooklyn does. Team schedule pages can change, so we recheck this one through the season and flip each row to what was actually worn the morning after.

A few things jump out of the count:

- **The Classic Edition runs the Eras Nights.** Opening Night, ABA Night (November 18), 80s Night (December 18), 90s Night (January 9) and 00s Night (March 15) are all Classic games. The leaked [2026-27 Nets wordmark](/stories/nba-wordmarks-2026-27) is the red script from the New Jersey era, which fits a season built around the franchise's history. The team has not officially shown the full uniform yet.
- **The white is a road jersey now.** 14 of the 22 Association games are on the road. At home, Brooklyn wears it just eight times.
- **The City Edition waits until winter.** The Biggie Coogi set debuts January 23 against Toronto, then goes on a run: 11 games in about nine weeks, including back to back home nights against Memphis and Golden State on March 28 and 29.
- **The Statement owns February at Barclays.** Six straight home Statement games from January 27 to February 25, Phoenix through Philadelphia.
