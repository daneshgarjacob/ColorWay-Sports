Most of them made their first appearance in the new number at [NBA Media Day](/stories/nba-media-day-2026-new-jerseys) on September 28.

## Devin Booker: No. 1 to No. 15

The biggest number change of the year is one where the player did not even move. Booker announced on Father's Day, June 21, that he was switching from the No. 1 he has worn his entire career to **No. 15**, in honor of his father, Melvin, who wore 15 at Missouri. It is also the number he wears for Team USA. Mark Williams, who had 15, gave it up.

Klay is back in **No. 11**, the number he made famous in Golden State, after wearing 31 in Dallas. Bobby Portis Jr. has the most creative number of the year: **No. 95**, for the I-95 South signs in Miami and the year he was born.

## Same Number, New Jersey

Some of the biggest moves kept their numbers. **LeBron James** is still **No. 23** in Philadelphia, and he is joined by **Jaylen Brown** in **No. 7**. **Kawhi Leonard** is back in Toronto in **No. 2**, the number from the 2019 title. **Julius Randle** kept **No. 30** in Brooklyn, a number he wears for his mother. **LaMelo Ball** is still **No. 1**, now in the new Timberwolves uniform.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What number is Giannis wearing on the Heat?**

No. 7. He wore No. 34 for his entire Milwaukee career and picked 7 after the trade to Miami.

**Why did Devin Booker change his number to 15?**

To honor his father, Melvin Booker, who wore No. 15 at Missouri. Booker announced the switch from No. 1 on Father's Day, June 21, 2026. He also wears 15 for Team USA.

**What number is Ja Morant wearing on the Trail Blazers?**

No. 1. He wore No. 12 in Memphis.

**What number is Anthony Davis wearing on the Wizards?**

No. 23, back to his original career number after wearing No. 3 in Dallas.

**What number is Paul George wearing on the Celtics?**

No. 13, the number he wore for most of his career before wearing No. 8 in Philadelphia.

**What number is Bobby Portis wearing on the Heat?**

No. 95, a nod to Interstate 95 in Miami and the year he was born, 1995.

**What number is LeBron James wearing on the 76ers?**

No. 23, the same number he wore with the Lakers. The full story is in our [LeBron 76ers jersey number post](/stories/lebron-james-76ers-jersey-number).

## The Bottom Line

The new numbers to know for 2026-27: Giannis 7, Booker 15, Ja 1, AD 23, PG 13, Klay 11 and Portis 95. LeBron, Jaylen Brown, Kawhi, Randle and LaMelo kept theirs.
