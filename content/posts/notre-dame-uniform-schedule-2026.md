## The Lambeau Uniform Is the Best Story of the Opener

The thirteenth Shamrock Series uniform is not a Notre Dame tribute. It is a Green Bay one, which is a strange and rather good decision.

The jersey is a deep blue drawn from the 1920s **Acme Packers**, the company-sponsored name Green Bay carried before it was the Packers. The sleeve striping echoes the Packers set you would recognize today. The numerals take a hard block shape from the Paul Hornung era, and inside each numeral is a custom mesh texture carrying the silhouette of Green Bay's oval G.

On the collar sit **eleven gold shamrocks**, one for each of Notre Dame's consensus national championships. There is also an American flag patch marking the nation's 250th anniversary in 2026.

The history it is reaching for is real rather than invented. Paul Hornung won a Heisman at Notre Dame and a championship in Green Bay. Curly Lambeau, who the stadium is named after, played at Notre Dame under Knute Rockne before he founded the Packers, and he took the Notre Dame box formation north with him. A uniform built on that is doing something more interesting than putting shamrocks on a black jersey, which is the trap this series has fallen into before.

## The Detail Almost Nobody Is Calling a Uniform Change

There is one more change on every Notre Dame jersey this season, and it is the largest of them in dollar terms: **a SoFi patch.**

Notre Dame signed a six-year jersey-patch deal reported to average **$18 to 20 million a year**, which makes it the most valuable jersey sponsorship in the history of college athletics. SoFi is the first non-apparel brand to appear on Irish uniforms, and the patch runs across all sports in 2026-27. Its football debut is the Lambeau game.

We are going to reserve judgment on how it looks until we see it on the field at full size. What is worth saying now is that a program which has kept the same uniform silhouette since 1984, and which markets itself on exactly that refusal to change, just put a corporate mark on the front. That is a bigger break with Notre Dame's own tradition than any green jersey has ever been.

## The Bottom Line

Two confirmed alternates before the opener is more certainty than almost any program in the country offers, and both of them are good: a green-out with the Leprechaun promoted to the shoulder, and a Lambeau one-off that actually understands the building it is played in.

The rest is the most predictable wardrobe in college football, which is the point. Navy at home, white on the road, gold helmet always. The only genuinely open date is Navy at Gillette, and we will have that answered in game week.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When does Notre Dame wear green in 2026?**

Saturday, October 10, against Stanford at Notre Dame Stadium, with a 3:30 p.m. kickoff. It is the annual Irish Wear Green game. The 2026 set is green jersey, green pants and the traditional gold helmet, with the Leprechaun logo replacing the ND monogram on the shoulders.

**What uniform is Notre Dame wearing against Wisconsin at Lambeau Field?**

The 2026 Shamrock Series uniform, a deep blue set honoring the 1920s Acme Packers. It has Packers-style sleeve striping, block numerals from the Paul Hornung era with the Green Bay oval G worked into the mesh, eleven gold shamrocks on the collar for Notre Dame's consensus national titles, and an American flag patch for the nation's 250th anniversary.

**What are Notre Dame's regular uniforms?**

Navy jersey with gold numerals at home, white jersey with navy numerals on the road, gold pants and the gold helmet with both. The silhouette has not meaningfully changed since 1984.

**How many alternate uniforms does Notre Dame wear in 2026?**

Two are confirmed: the Shamrock Series set on September 6 and the Irish Wear Green set on October 10. Every other game is expected to be the standard navy or white.

**Why is there a SoFi patch on Notre Dame's jersey?**

Notre Dame signed a six-year jersey-patch sponsorship with SoFi reported to average $18 to 20 million a year, the most valuable in college athletics. The patch appears on Notre Dame uniforms across all sports in 2026-27 and debuts in football at Lambeau Field on September 6.

**Is Notre Dame the home team at Lambeau Field?**

Yes. Notre Dame is the designated home team for the Shamrock Series game against Wisconsin on September 6, even though it is played at Lambeau Field in Green Bay. It is the first game the Irish have ever played there.

**What does Notre Dame wear against Navy in 2026?**

Not yet confirmed. The October 31 game is at Gillette Stadium in Foxborough, a neutral site, and Navy has been listed as the designated home team, which would put Notre Dame in white. We confirm this page in game week rather than guessing.
