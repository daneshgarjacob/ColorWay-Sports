Northwestern ran through ten different uniform combinations last season, so "purple at home, white on the road" only gets you halfway. This is the full Northwestern 2026 uniform schedule: the purple home set, the white road set, the black Gothics, the brand new Purple Gothics alternate that has a debut date already circled, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games. The season also splits across two stadiums, because the new Ryan Field opens in October. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

Under Armour builds all of it, and the base sets are simple enough. The purple jersey and the white jersey carry the same slab NORTHWESTERN wordmark, the same three-stripe shoulder treatment and the same stylized N at the collar. What makes Northwestern interesting is the mixing. Last season the Wildcats logged ten different helmet, jersey and pants combinations, including an all-white look, an all-purple look, purple over black and two versions of the black Gothics. There is no such thing as a default Saturday here.

The Gothics are the program's signature costume. Northwestern first introduced a Gothic alternate in October 2014, built around the campus architecture and the Arnold Weber Arch, and brought the black version back most memorably for the Wrigley Field game in November 2024.

The details are worth knowing before you see it on television. The purple is a deep royal shade with a silver flake in the shell, closer to the Baltimore Ravens than to the flat purple Northwestern usually wears. A metallic gold center stripe runs over the crown and an oversized gold N sits on each side, with the Weber Arch printed across the back of the helmet and WILDCATS on the front bumper in Gothic type. The jersey carries Gothic script on the shoulders, Northwestern on one side and University on the other, over gold and black stripes, and the numerals are textured metallic gold outlined in black. The pants repeat the stripe and add a gold arch and N on the left hip. It is a monochrome uniform, which usually reads better in person than on a broadcast, and it is going to divide people. That is clearly the point.

One more thing shapes this schedule, and it is not a jersey. Northwestern played its first two home games of 2026 at the temporary lakefront stadium, Northwestern Medicine Field at Martin Stadium, and moves into the new Ryan Field on October 2. Everything from that date forward is in the new building.

**September 5 vs. South Dakota State.** ★ Confirmed from game photos: **purple helmet, purple jersey, white pants**, the cleanest version of the base home set, under the lights at Northwestern Medicine Field at Martin Stadium. Northwestern won 34-18 in Aidan Chiles's debut at quarterback and in Chip Kelly's first game as offensive coordinator. The opponent had a subplot of its own, since Wildcats receiver Griffin Wilde transferred in from South Dakota State.

**September 12.** Bye. An off week in Week 2 is unusual, and it gives Northwestern two full weeks before Colorado.

**September 19 vs. Colorado.** Purple expected. A 7:30 p.m. Eastern kickoff on FOX and the last game at the lakefront stadium, with Deion Sanders bringing Colorado to Evanston. Colorado is in white as the visitor. Our [Colorado 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/colorado-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Buffaloes' side, and this is the home date most likely to get something other than the plain purple set, because it is the last night in the temporary building.

**September 25 at Indiana.** White expected. The Big Ten and FOX flexed this one to Friday night, 8 p.m. Eastern in Bloomington, against the defending national champion. Indiana wears crimson at home and has not lost there in three seasons, so Northwestern is in white. The open question is the helmet and the pants, since the Wildcats have paired the white jersey with the purple helmet, the white helmet, white pants and purple pants all within the last calendar year.

**October 2 vs. Penn State.** Purple expected. This is the big one. The new Ryan Field opens on a Friday night, 8 p.m. Eastern on FOX, exactly one hundred years to the day after the original stadium hosted its first game on October 2, 1926. If Northwestern has a special uniform planned for anything in 2026 that has not been announced yet, it is this game, and we will update the cell the moment anything surfaces. ## The Bottom Line

Northwestern is the rare program where the uniform is genuinely part of the week. Purple and white are the base, but the Wildcats mixed helmets, jerseys and pants ten different ways a year ago, and 2026 adds the Purple Gothics to a closet that already held two black Gothic sets. We know one date for certain: Rutgers on October 24 gets the new purple and gold look. The date we are watching hardest is October 2, when the new Ryan Field opens on the hundredth anniversary of the old one, because that is the sort of night a program dresses for. The grid above has every game, and we star each cell as the combination is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Northwestern wearing this week?**

Northwestern hosts Colorado on Saturday, September 19 at 7:30 p.m. Eastern on FOX, and the purple home jersey is expected with Colorado in white. The Wildcats opened the season in purple helmet, purple jersey and white pants against South Dakota State. The week-by-week grid above lists every game with the expected set, and we confirm each one as it is worn.

**What are Northwestern's football uniforms for 2026?**

A purple home jersey and a white road jersey, both with the NORTHWESTERN wordmark, white or purple shoulder stripes and the stylized N, plus purple, white and black helmets and purple, white and black pants. The alternates are the black Gothics and the new Purple Gothics, which was revealed on June 30. Under Armour makes all of it.

**What are Northwestern's Purple Gothics uniforms?**

A deep royal purple alternate with metallic gold trim, inspired by the Gothic architecture on Northwestern's campus and specifically the Arnold Weber Arch. The helmet has a gold center stripe, oversized gold N logos, the arch across the back and WILDCATS in Gothic type on the front bumper. The jersey carries Gothic lettering on the shoulders and textured gold numerals outlined in black, and the purple pants add a gold arch and N on the left hip. Northwestern wears them for the first time against Rutgers on October 24.

**When does the new Ryan Field open?**

Friday, October 2, 2026, against Penn State, with an 8 p.m. Eastern kickoff on FOX. The date is exactly one hundred years after the original stadium on the site opened on October 2, 1926. Northwestern played its first two home games of 2026 at the temporary Northwestern Medicine Field at Martin Stadium and plays its final five home games at the new Ryan Field.

**How many games does Northwestern play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven at home, two at the lakefront Martin Stadium and five at the new Ryan Field, and five on the road at Indiana, Michigan State, Oregon, Ohio State and Minnesota, with a bye on September 12.
