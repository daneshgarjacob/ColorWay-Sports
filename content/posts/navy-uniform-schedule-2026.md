Navy owns two of the biggest uniform dates on the college football calendar and it has already announced what it is wearing for one of them. This is the full Navy 2026 uniform schedule: the navy home jersey, the white road set, the gold helmet that never changes, the 1926 centennial throwback the Midshipmen will wear against Notre Dame at Gillette Stadium, and the December tribute uniform that still has not been revealed. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

The base is as traditional as it gets. Navy wears a gold helmet with no logo on the sides, a navy jersey at home over gold pants, and a white jersey on the road, and all of it comes from Under Armour. What separates this program from almost every other roster in the sport is what sits on top of that base: Navy builds elaborate one-off uniforms for its two signature games, and the storytelling on them is genuinely detailed rather than decorative.

For 2026 the first of those two is already on the books. On September 15 Navy announced a commemorative uniform honoring the 100th anniversary of the 1926 national championship team, the only national title in program history, which went 9-0-1 with a 10-0 win over Michigan and a 21-21 tie against Army in front of a crowd of more than 110,000 at Soldier Field. The jersey is a modern interpretation of the 1926 Chevron Stripe design, with nine stripes across the upper body, four on the front and five on the back, one for each win that season. The numerals borrow typography from the Class of 1926 pin, a back-neck locker tag reproduces that pin with its eagle, trident and year, the interior collar reads "Navy Blue and Gold" after the alma mater first performed publicly in April 1926, and a 100th anniversary patch sits on the uniform. Navy wears it against Notre Dame on October 31.

## What Navy Wears Against Army, and What We Know About 2026

Nothing has been announced. Navy's Army-Navy uniform reveal has landed in the second half of November in recent seasons, and last year it dropped on November 18.

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; margin: 2em 0;">

</div>

Navy's version of this tradition leans hard on the fleet. The Midshipmen went NASA-themed in 2022, honored the submarine force with a Silent Service look in 2023, and built the 2024 uniform around the Jolly Rogers. The 2025 set marked the 250th anniversary of the United States Navy and was the most intricate one yet: a copper helmet with a wooden plank stripe in tribute to the USS Constitution, six ropes wrapping the shell for the original six frigates, 63 knots on the front and rear for Navy's 63 wins over Army, a hand-painted USS Constitution on parchment on one side and a copper USN monogram on the other, a jersey whose wordmark and numerals were drawn from the typography of the Act to Provide Naval Armament with six ropes carrying 250 knots in total, dark navy sleeves for the hull of Old Ironsides, heritage red cuffs for cannon carriage colors, and stone-colored pants with 126 knots for the 126th game.

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; margin: 2em 0;">

</div>

There is a second layer to Navy's Army-Navy uniform that does not get enough attention. Navy and Marine Corps players wear individual unit patches on the jersey, chosen for units they have a personal connection to, and more than 100 units are represented in a given year. In 2025 that included a patch for the 160th Special Operations Aviation Regiment worn in memory of four special operations aviators killed in a September helicopter crash, a USS Truxton patch worn by a player whose cousin was deployed on the ship, and a Mercury-Atlas 6 patch worn by a great-nephew of John Glenn. That is the part of this uniform you cannot buy in a store.

Navy also comes into December holding the Commander-in-Chief's Trophy after winning the 126th Army-Navy Game.

## The Bottom Line

Navy's regular season uniform schedule is simple: navy and gold at home, white on the road, gold helmet every single week. What makes this the best uniform program in college football is the two dates it builds something for. One of them is already public, the 1926 Chevron Stripe centennial against Notre Dame at Gillette Stadium on October 31, and it is a real throwback with real math built into the stripes. The other is the Army-Navy Game on December 12, and nobody outside Annapolis and Under Armour has seen it yet. The grid above has every date, and we star each cell as the combination is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Navy wearing this week?**

Navy is off this week. The Midshipmen are on a bye after losing 38-30 at Florida Atlantic on September 12 in the white jersey and navy pants. Next up is a Friday night game at UAB on September 25, where white is expected. The grid above lists every game with the expected set and we star each one as it is confirmed.

**What are Navy's football uniforms for 2026?**

A navy blue home jersey with gold numerals and gold and white shoulder stripes over gold pants, a white road jersey with navy numerals worn with navy or white pants, and the gold helmet with no side logo that Navy wears with everything. All of it is Under Armour. On top of the base sets, Navy has announced a 1926 centennial throwback for the Notre Dame game and will build a separate one-off uniform for the Army-Navy Game.

**Does Navy have a new alternate uniform in 2026?**

Yes. Navy announced on September 15 that it will wear a commemorative Under Armour uniform honoring the 100th anniversary of the 1926 national championship team against Notre Dame on October 31 at Gillette Stadium. The jersey is a modern take on the 1926 Chevron Stripe design with nine stripes across the body for the team's nine wins, numerals drawn from the Class of 1926 pin, a locker tag reproducing that pin, "Navy Blue and Gold" inside the collar, and a 100th anniversary patch. A separate Army-Navy Game uniform has not been revealed.

**What is Navy wearing for the Army-Navy Game in 2026?**

Unknown as of September 17. The 127th Army-Navy Game is Saturday, December 12 at 3 p.m. Eastern on CBS at MetLife Stadium. Navy has honored a different part of the fleet each year, from the Jolly Rogers in 2024 to the USS Constitution and the Navy's 250th anniversary in 2025, and players wear individual unit patches on the jersey. We will update this page the day the 2026 design is revealed.

**What did Navy wear against Florida Atlantic?**

The gold helmet, white jersey and navy pants at Flagler Credit Union Stadium on September 12. Florida Atlantic wore navy at home and won 38-30. In the opener a week earlier, Navy wore the gold helmet, navy jersey and gold pants and beat Towson 42-15.

**How many games does Navy play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: five at Navy-Marine Corps Memorial Stadium, five on the road including the trip to Air Force, and two neutral-site games, Notre Dame at Gillette Stadium on October 31 and Army at MetLife Stadium on December 12, with byes on September 19 and November 14.
