## New Orleans Is the HOME Team in Paris (Week 7)

The Saints host Pittsburgh at the Stade de France on **Sunday, October 25**, in the 8:30 a.m. Central window, and this is the detail worth getting right: **New Orleans is the designated home team.** NFL.com lists the game as Steelers at Saints.

So the Saints wear black and Pittsburgh wears white, and the game counts as one of New Orleans' home dates rather than a neutral one. Both clubs' schedule widgets render it as "vs", which is why it gets miscounted; a useful independent check is that Pittsburgh's own home-game themes announcement lists eight games at Acrisure Stadium and skips October 25 entirely.

It is also the single most obvious place on the calendar for New Orleans to do something with a uniform. A club hosting a game in Europe, in the early window with no competing games, is exactly the situation that produces an alternate. Nothing has been announced.

## The Saints Closet

**Black at home.** The base, with either gold or black pants, and it remains one of the best-balanced uniforms in the league. Black and old gold is a combination almost nobody else uses, and the fleur-de-lis is a genuinely great mark, simple enough to read at any distance and tied to the city rather than invented for a football team.

**White on the road.** Same set with the values flipped.

**The gold alternate.** New Orleans owns a gold jersey and it is the loudest thing they wear. No 2026 date has been announced.

**The 1967 throwback.** The original expansion-era look, worn periodically in recent seasons. Also unassigned.

## Where an Alternate Would Fit

Not announced, and this is a read rather than a report.

The Saints have one standalone primetime home game, **Week 4 on Monday night against Atlanta on October 5**, which is both a division rival and the biggest home audience of the season. That and the Paris game are the two obvious slots for the gold jersey or the throwback.

If either gets named, it goes on this page the day it does.

## Black at the Superdome (7 Games, Plus Paris)

Weeks 3, 4, 5, 9, 10, 13 and 18, plus Week 7 in Paris. Week 16 against Arizona comes off this list: the Cardinals published their full 2026 uniform schedule with red in New Orleans, which only works if the Saints are in white, so that one is a white home game. The Monday nighter against Atlanta in Week 4 and the Week 18 close against Tampa Bay carry the most weight.

The Superdome is one of the few stadiums where a black uniform genuinely looks better than it does anywhere else. It is a fully enclosed dome with controlled lighting, so the black reads as black rather than as a dark shape, and the gold actually picks up highlights instead of going flat.

## White on the Road (7 Games)

Weeks 1, 6, 11, 12, 14, 15 and 17. Four of them are NFC South games, and the Week 17 trip to Atlanta closes out a home-and-home that started in Week 4.

## Black in Baltimore (Week 2)

The one road game where New Orleans wears black, and it is not the Saints' choice. Baltimore has confirmed its **White Noise** game for September 20, the all-white set at home, and with the home team in white the visitor wears its color. Black and gold against Baltimore's white is a strong way to open the road schedule. Our [Ravens 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/ravens-uniform-schedule-2026) has their side.

## How the Saints Rotation Works in 2026

1. **Black at home, white on the road**, unless the team designates otherwise.
2. **Paris is a HOME game.** Black, not white, and it counts toward the home slate.
3. **No alternate date has been announced** for the gold jersey or the 1967 throwback.
4. **Pants and helmet combinations** are a week-of call, as they are for every club.

## The Bottom Line

New Orleans has a uniform that does not need changing and a schedule with two obvious places to change it anyway. Black and old gold under a dome is about as good as this sport looks, and the fleur-de-lis does not need help from anybody.

The thing to watch is Paris. Hosting a game in Europe is the sort of occasion clubs build a jersey moment around, and the Saints have both a gold alternate and a throwback sitting unused. So far they have said nothing.
