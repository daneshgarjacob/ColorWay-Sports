**Vancouver's Hometown Remix jersey makes its debut tonight.** The Canucks wear it for the first time against the New York Rangers at Rogers Arena on **Friday, Nov. 13, at 7 p.m. Pacific (10 p.m. Eastern)**. It is **all navy**, with the orca crest in **tonal dark blues**, and the team calls it the city after dark. New York is expected in its **road whites**.

## Quick Answers

- **What are the Canucks wearing tonight?** Their Hometown Remix jersey, for the first time: navy with a tonal orca and bright blue sleeve bands.
- **When?** Friday, Nov. 13, 7 p.m. Pacific, at Rogers Arena in Vancouver.
- **What are the Rangers wearing?** Road white is expected.
- **How many times will Vancouver wear it?** Six, all at home.
- **Our grade:** D+.

## What The Canucks' Hometown Remix Jersey Looks Like

The base is **navy**. The orca crest is done in **tonal dark blues**, with only a **silver eye** and a **bright blue outline** to pick it out. The **bright blue sleeve bands** carry a debossed Vancouver **skyline reflected in the water**, and the **lions of the Lions Gate Bridge** sit inside the hem.

## Our Grade: D+

The details are good. The skyline in the sleeve bands and the Lions Gate lions are real Vancouver touches. The problem is that you can only find them up close. From the stands or on TV, a navy sweater with a navy orca reads as a practice jersey, and Vancouver already owns one of the best color stories in hockey in blue and green. The Remix leaves the green at home.

We ranked it [No. 30 of all 32 Hometown Remix jerseys](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-jerseys-ranked). Tonight's debut is the chance to prove it looks better under the lights than it does in the studio photos.
