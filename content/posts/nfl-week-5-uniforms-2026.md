Week 5 of the 2026 NFL season runs from Thursday, October 8 to Monday, October 12, with 15 games and the first byes of the year for the Kansas City Chiefs and Carolina Panthers. It is the best throwback weekend of the season so far. New England brings out the red **Pat Patriot** throwback against Las Vegas on the day it honors the 2001 Super Bowl team, Green Bay debuts its brand-new **Rivalries** uniform against Chicago at Lambeau, Tampa Bay wears **red** at Dallas on Thursday night, Arizona goes to the **black** alternate against Detroit, and Atlanta stays in **red** for Sunday Night Football against Baltimore. Jacksonville hosts Philadelphia in London, and it is the second weekend of [Crucial Catch](/stories/nfl-crucial-catch-2026-schedule).

Below is every Week 5 look we know, starting with the special ones and then all 15 games in order. **Confirmed** means the team has announced the jersey or the full combination. **Expected** is our read from that team's uniform schedule, and it gets replaced with what was actually worn in our [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026) once the game is played. Helmets and jerseys project reliably; pants are the piece teams change the week of the game, so treat every expected pants call as the least certain part of the line.

### Green Bay Packers: The Rivalries Debut Against the Bears

<div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:1fr 1fr;gap:12px;margin:1em 0 0.6em;"><div style="background:#ececf0;border-radius:10px;height:190px;display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:center;padding:10px;box-sizing:border-box;"><img src="/images/posts/nfl-tracker-jerseys/bears-white-road.jpg" alt="Chicago Bears white road jersey, confirmed at Green Bay in Week 5 of 2026" style="max-height:170px;max-width:100%;object-fit:contain;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></div><div style="background:#ececf0;border-radius:10px;height:190px;display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:center;padding:10px;box-sizing:border-box;"><img src="/images/posts/nfl-tracker-jerseys/packers-green-rivalries.jpg" alt="Green Bay Packers Rivalries jersey, debuting against the Bears in Week 5 of 2026" style="max-height:170px;max-width:100%;object-fit:contain;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></div></div>

**Sunday, 1:00 p.m. ET, FOX. Both jerseys confirmed.** Green Bay's Rivalries set was revealed with the rest of the 2026 class on August 25, and the debut date came with it: this game. It is a darker hunter green jersey with cream and gold trim, numbers modeled on the lettering of paper currency, striping taken from the borders of Packers stock certificates, and an interlocking GB logo. The helmet is **cream with the first matte finish in team history** and the first matte green facemask, which means the gold helmet with the G sits this one out, and the pants are **cream** too. Chicago is in its road set, **navy helmet, white jersey**, with the pants still to be announced.

Chicago gets its own turn on Christmas Day, when the Bears debut their Rivalries set against Green Bay at Soldier Field. Full details in the [Packers 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/packers-uniform-schedule-2026) and the [Bears 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/bears-uniform-schedule-2026), and every set in the class is ranked in our [NFL Rivalries uniforms ranking](/stories/nfl-rivalries-uniforms-2026-ranked).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What uniforms are NFL teams wearing in Week 5 of 2026?**

Most teams are in their standard sets, home in color and visitors in white. The special looks are the Patriots' red Pat Patriot throwback against the Raiders, the Packers' Rivalries debut against the Bears, the Buccaneers in red at the Cowboys on Thursday night, the Bengals in an orange combination they have never worn before at Miami, the Cardinals in black with the black helmet against the Lions, the Falcons in red over black pants for Sunday Night Football against the Ravens, and the Chargers in powder blue over powder blue pants against the Broncos.

**What are the Patriots wearing in Week 5?**

The red Pat Patriot throwback, with the Pat Patriot logo on a white helmet, against the Las Vegas Raiders on Sunday, October 11 at Gillette Stadium. New England confirmed the date on August 31. It is the day the team honors the 25th anniversary of the 2001 Super Bowl champions, and the throwback returns once more on Thursday, December 10 against the Vikings.

**What are the Packers wearing against the Bears in Week 5?**

The new Rivalries uniform, which debuts at Lambeau Field on Sunday, October 11. It is a darker hunter green jersey with cream and gold trim, currency-style numbers and an interlocking GB logo, under a cream helmet with the first matte finish in team history, with cream pants. The Bears are in their white road set under the navy helmet.

**What are the Cowboys and Buccaneers wearing on Thursday Night Football?**

Dallas wore its standard white jersey with the silver star helmet and silver-blue pants for the Thursday, October 8 game at AT&T Stadium. Because the Cowboys wear white at home, Tampa Bay wore color: pewter helmet, red jersey, white pants. The Buccaneers won 24-16.

**What are the Falcons wearing on Sunday Night Football in Week 5?**

Red with black pants, under the black helmet with the silver facemask, against the Baltimore Ravens on Sunday, October 11 at 8:20 p.m. Eastern on NBC. Atlanta posted the full combination on October 7, and it is the first time the new red jersey has been worn with the black pants. Red became the Falcons' primary home jersey again with the April 2026 redesign. Baltimore confirmed the black helmet, white jersey and purple pants.

**What are the Eagles and Jaguars wearing in London?**

Jacksonville is the designated home team at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, so the Jaguars wear teal with black pants under the black helmet, and the Eagles go all white under the midnight green helmet. Both clubs confirmed their looks for the game, which kicks off at 9:30 a.m. Eastern on Sunday, October 11, on NFL Network.

**What are the Bengals wearing at the Dolphins in Week 5?**

Orange helmet, orange jersey, white pants with orange stripes and white socks, against the Dolphins on Sunday, October 11 at 1:00 p.m. Eastern on FOX. The Bengals announced it on October 6 and say they have never worn this combination before. It is also the first time Cincinnati has worn orange on the road since the 2021 redesign. Miami is all white at home, so it is orange against white.

**Which NFL teams are on bye in Week 5?**

The Kansas City Chiefs and the Carolina Panthers. They are the first byes of the 2026 season, so 30 teams play in Week 5, starting with Buccaneers at Cowboys on Thursday, October 8 and ending with Bills at Rams on Monday, October 12.

**Which NFL teams have Crucial Catch games in Week 5?**

Eight clubs have named a Week 5 home game: the Seahawks, Steelers, Commanders, Dolphins, Jets, Cardinals, Chargers and Titans. The Cowboys, Falcons, Saints and Rams have not named theirs, and Week 5 is one of the possible dates for each. Crucial Catch does not change the jerseys, only the hats, gloves and sideline gear.
