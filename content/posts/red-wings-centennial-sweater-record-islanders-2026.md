**The Red Wings have worn the Centennial sweater three times this season, and they are 1-2 in it.** Detroit put the solid red sweater with the cream 1930s Winged Wheel on again Saturday, November 21, against the New York Islanders at Little Caesars Arena, three nights after wearing it for the 100th anniversary game against Boston. The team posted the warmup photos with "Back in the Centennials." The Islanders won **3-2**.

## Quick Answers

- **What did the Red Wings wear Saturday?** The **Centennial sweater**: solid red, a **cream chain-stitched 1930s Winged Wheel**, several **cream stripes** on the sleeves and hem, cream numbers, a lace-up collar and **red helmets**. The Islanders wore their road white.
- **How many times this season?** Three: October 29 vs. Chicago, November 18 vs. Boston and November 21 vs. the Islanders.
- **Record in it:** 1-2. A 4-1 win over Chicago, then a 4-1 loss to Boston and a 3-2 loss to the Islanders.
- **Are there more dates?** Detroit has not published a Centennial date list for 2026-27.

## How The Centennial Sweater Differs From The Regular Home Red

Both are red, which is why they can look alike on a small screen. The details are where they split:

- **Crest:** the regular sweater has the modern Winged Wheel in **white**. The Centennial uses the **1930s version**, chain-stitched in **cream**, so it has texture you can see on TV.
- **Stripes:** the regular red has **one wide white stripe** on each sleeve and one at the hem. The Centennial has **several thin cream stripes** in both places.
- **White against cream:** every light color on the Centennial is cream instead of bright white, which is what makes it look old.
- **Collar:** the Centennial has a **lace-up collar** with "Hockeytown" inside the neck.

## Will The Red Wings Wear The Centennial Sweater Again?

Probably, but Detroit has not said when. The team's 2026-27 promotional schedule, published in July, lists the November 18 Centennial Game and a run of theme nights, and it does not include any Centennial sweater dates. All three wears this season have been home games.

Last season, the franchise's 100th, Detroit published a list of Centennial nights and wore it at more than a dozen home games, starting with the home opener against Montreal. This season the sweater has shown up without a list.

The next Red Wings home games are:

- **Wednesday, November 25** vs. Vancouver
- **Saturday, November 28** vs. Nashville
- **Tuesday, December 1** vs. Calgary
- **Thursday, December 3** vs. Colorado
- **Friday, December 11** vs. the Rangers

The Red Wings also have not posted dates for their [Hometown Remix jersey](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-jerseys-ranked), the red and white sweater with HOCKEYTOWN arched over the Winged Wheel.
