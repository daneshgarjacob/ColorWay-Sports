UTSA is in its second season with Nike. The Roadrunners wore Adidas from 2013 through 2025 and moved to Nike and Game One under a six-year partnership that started in June 2025, so the jerseys and the 210 helmet are all Nike product now.

The piece that got national attention this season is the helmet UTSA wore for its annual Hispanic Heritage Game against Colorado State on September 26. ESPN picked it for its best uniforms of the week and called out the City Connect-style idea: standard navy jersey and white pants below, but a white helmet splashed with Fiesta confetti, a multicolored center stripe, the 210 area code on the front and San Antonio on one side. UTSA revealed it earlier that week.

**September 5 vs. UTRGV.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **navy helmet with the 210 bumper, navy jersey, navy pants**, all navy to match the Navy Out theme for the opener at the Alamodome. UTSA won 45-16.

**September 12 at Texas State.** ★ Confirmed from Texas State's game photography, dated to the day of the game, and the broadcast: **white helmet, white jersey, white pants**, all white for the I-35 Showdown in San Marcos. Texas State wore the gold helmet, maroon jersey and gold pants. UTSA trailed 17-7 at halftime, scored 24 points after the break and won 31-26. Our [Texas State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/texas-state-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Bobcats side.

**September 19 at Texas.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **white helmet, white jersey, navy pants**, the same helmet and jersey as the week before with navy swapped in below the belt. Texas wore its only home look, the white Longhorn helmet, burnt orange jersey and white pants, and won 30-6. More on our [Texas 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/texas-uniform-schedule-2026).

**September 26 vs. Colorado State.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **the Hispanic Heritage Fiesta helmet, navy jersey, white pants**. Colorado State wore the green helmet, white jersey and green pants. The game turned into a shootout, UTSA scored 17 in the fourth quarter and won 59-45 to finish non-conference play 3-1. See our [Colorado State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/colorado-state-uniform-schedule-2026) for the Rams.

**October 3 at Rice.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **navy helmet with orange trim, white jersey, white pants**. We expected the white helmet from the first two road games, and the Roadrunners went with the dark shell instead. Rice wore a navy helmet, navy jersey and navy pants. Michael Petro kicked three field goals and Will Henderson III ran for a touchdown, Rice cut it to 16-14 with 13:35 left, and UTSA held on to win 16-14 in the American Conference opener. UTSA is 4-1. Our [Rice 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/rice-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Owls side.

**October 8 vs. South Florida.** Navy expected, with a caveat. A Thursday night at the Alamodome on ESPN, 7:30 p.m. Eastern, and UTSA's game theme is Lights Out. A national weeknight window and a lights-out theme is exactly the spot a program saves a special look for, so this is the home date we are watching hardest. Lights Out is also the name of the black uniforms UTSA debuted on November 2, 2024, when it beat No. 25 Memphis for its first ever win over a ranked team. Nothing has been announced. USF should be in white; see our [South Florida 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/south-florida-uniform-schedule-2026).

**October 17 vs. Navy.** Navy expected. UTSA's Salute to Armed Forces and Veterans game, presented by USAA, and a service academy opponent on the same day. Military appreciation games are a common place for a special helmet or decal. Our [Navy 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/navy-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Midshipmen.

**October 24 at Tulane.** White expected. Yulman Stadium in New Orleans, kickoff time still to be set. See our [Tulane 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/tulane-uniform-schedule-2026).

**October 31.** Bye.

**November 5 at Florida Atlantic.** White expected. A Thursday night in Boca Raton, 8 p.m. Eastern.

**November 14 vs. North Texas.** Navy expected. Homecoming at the Alamodome. North Texas should be in white; our [North Texas 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/north-texas-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Mean Green.

**November 21 at UAB.** White expected. Protective Stadium in Birmingham, kickoff time still to be set.

**November 27 or 28 vs. Tulsa.** Navy expected. Senior Day, with the exact date still to be set by the conference. More on our [Tulsa 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/tulsa-uniform-schedule-2026).

## Will the Fiesta Helmet Come Back?

Nothing says it will. UTSA built the helmet for its annual Hispanic Heritage Game and has not attached it to any other date. What the season has shown is a fairly clear system everywhere else: the navy 210 helmet at home, the white helmet on the first two road trips before the navy shell went to Rice, and the jersey and pants mixed from there. If UTSA has one more special look in the closet this fall, the Thursday night Lights Out game against South Florida on October 8 and the Salute to Armed Forces game against Navy on October 17 are the two dates that fit.

## The Bottom Line

Five games in, UTSA has worn five different combinations and one genuinely great helmet. The Fiesta shell worked because it was about San Antonio, not about a trend, with the 210 and the city's name carrying the idea and the uniform below staying clean. The rest of the closet runs on a simple rule, navy helmet at home and white jersey away, and the open questions are which helmet and pants go with the white jersey, after the navy shell showed up at Rice, and whether the Lights Out night brings something new. The grid above has every date, and we add a star as each uniform is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is UTSA wearing this week?**

UTSA wore the navy helmet with orange trim, white jersey and white pants at Rice on Saturday, October 3, confirmed from the broadcast, and won the American Conference opener 16-14 to move to 4-1. Next is South Florida at the Alamodome on Thursday, October 8, the Lights Out game, where navy is expected.

**What helmet did UTSA wear for the Hispanic Heritage game?**

A white helmet decorated with Fiesta colors, worn against Colorado State on September 26, 2026. It has confetti details, a center stripe with spots of pink, teal, yellow and orange, the 210 area code on the front and a San Antonio wordmark on one side. UTSA paired it with the navy jersey and white pants and won 59-45.

**What does 210 on the UTSA helmet mean?**

It is San Antonio's area code. UTSA puts 210 on the front bumper of its navy helmet, and the number was also on the front of the Hispanic Heritage helmet.

**Who makes UTSA's uniforms?**

Nike. UTSA began a six-year apparel partnership with Nike and Game One in June 2025 after wearing Adidas from 2013 through 2025, so 2026 is the Roadrunners' second football season in Nike.

**What are UTSA's home game themes in 2026?**

Navy Out against UTRGV on September 5, Hispanic Heritage against Colorado State on September 26, Lights Out against South Florida on October 8, Salute to Armed Forces and Veterans against Navy on October 17, Homecoming against North Texas on November 14 and Senior Day against Tulsa on November 27 or 28.

**When did UTSA play Rice in 2026?**

Saturday, October 3 at 7 p.m. Eastern at Rice Stadium in Houston, on ESPN+. It was the American Conference opener for both teams, and UTSA won 16-14 in a navy helmet, white jersey and white pants, with Rice in navy.
