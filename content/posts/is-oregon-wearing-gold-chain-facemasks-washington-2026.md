**Short answer: there is no sign of it.** The photos of an Oregon helmet wrapped in gold rope chain come from Oregon's **"Mighty Metallic"** post on **Wednesday, November 25**, which presented the team's new **Nike Vaporposite Pro** cleats. Oregon has not said the chain is going on a facemask for a game, and the uniform Oregon posted for Washington shows the **chrome helmet with a plain silver facemask**. Oregon hosts Washington on **Saturday, November 28, at 7:30 p.m. Eastern (4:30 p.m. Pacific) on NBC**.

## Where The Gold Chain Photos Came From

On Friday morning, November 27, Yahoo Sports posted three photos of a silver Oregon helmet with thick gold rope chain draped over the facemask, with the line "Oregon gave fans a sneak peek at their gold rope chain facemasks (via @oregonfootball)." The post passed 470,000 views by Friday afternoon. Yahoo's graphic carries a tag that reads "oregonfootball, Mighty Metallic."

The helmet picture is in the **Instagram version** of the same post. On Instagram, ["Mighty Metallic"](https://www.instagram.com/p/DgL6rCmD62n/) is a six-photo collaboration post from Oregon Football, GoDucks, Oregon's equipment staff (Ducks Equipment) and the artist **Victor Solomon**. Five of the six photos are the cleats. The fifth is a close-up of a silver helmet with a green O, with gold rope chain wrapped across the facemask. The caption is the same as on X and is about the cleats. It does not mention the helmet or the facemask.

Yahoo's graphic also has two photos of hands holding the chain against a helmet. We did not find those two photos in Oregon's posts on X or Instagram.

## What Oregon Has And Has Not Said

- **Oregon has not said the chain will be worn in a game.** Neither Oregon Football, GoDucks nor Ducks Equipment has called it a facemask, a game-day item or part of the Washington uniform in any post we found on X or Instagram.
- **Oregon has not said it is only for the photo, either.** The chain helmet appears in a post presenting cleats, next to cleats with gold rope laces, and that is all Oregon has said about it.
- **The uniform Oregon posted for Saturday does not include it.** Details below.

Victor Solomon is the same artist behind Ohio State's cleats for The Game, red "Nike x Victor Solomon Vaporposites" with a gold chain hanging from the laces ([what Ohio State and Michigan are wearing](/stories/ohio-state-michigan-the-game-uniforms-2026)). So gold chain is the theme of this week's Nike cleats, on both teams.

## What The Game 12 Uniform Video Shows

Oregon posted its uniform for Washington 23 minutes before the cleats, also on November 25: "Protect Autzen. Game 12 uniform combo." We went through the 14-second video frame by frame. Around the five-second mark a player stands in the tunnel in the **chrome helmet with apple green wings**, and the facemask is a **standard silver metal cage**. Two seconds later the helmet is carried by the facemask, which is again plain silver. There is no chain anywhere in the video.

## What Oregon Is Wearing Against Washington

**Silver chrome helmet with apple green wings, apple green jersey with yellow numbers, apple green pants**, with the green and gold Vaporposite cleats. Fans call it **Crystal Apple**. Washington wears all white. Every piece, and how it compares with Oregon's other 11 looks this season, is in our story on [Oregon's Crystal Apple uniform against Washington](/stories/oregon-crystal-apple-uniforms-washington-2026).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Oregon wearing gold chain facemasks against Washington?**

Oregon has not said so. The chain photos are from Oregon's "Mighty Metallic" cleat post, and Oregon's Game 12 uniform video shows the chrome helmet with a plain silver facemask.

**Where did the Oregon gold chain helmet photo come from?**

From the Instagram version of Oregon's "Mighty Metallic" post on November 25, which presented its Nike Vaporposite Pro cleats. Yahoo Sports reposted it on November 27 as a "sneak peek" at gold rope chain facemasks.

**Did Oregon say the gold chain facemask is real?**

Oregon has not addressed it. The post's caption is about the cleats and does not mention the helmet or a facemask.

**Is the gold chain helmet just a prop?**

Oregon has not said either way. What is established is that it appears in a cleat post, and that it is not in the uniform Oregon posted for Washington.

**What facemask is Oregon wearing against Washington?**

In the Game 12 uniform video and photos, a silver facemask on the chrome helmet.

**What is Oregon wearing against Washington?**

A silver chrome helmet with apple green wings, an apple green jersey with yellow numbers and apple green pants, posted by Oregon on November 25.

**What are Oregon's cleats against Washington?**

Green Nike Vaporposite Pro cleats with a gold swoosh and gold rope laces, which Oregon presented as "Mighty Metallic."

**Who is Victor Solomon?**

An artist who designs trophy-style objects. He is credited on Oregon's "Mighty Metallic" Instagram post and on Ohio State's Nike x Victor Solomon cleats for The Game.

**What time is Oregon vs. Washington?**

Saturday, November 28, 2026, at 7:30 p.m. Eastern (4:30 p.m. Pacific) on NBC, at Autzen Stadium.

**What is Washington wearing at Oregon?**

All white: a white helmet with the purple W, a white jersey and white pants.
