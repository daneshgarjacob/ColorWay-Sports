## How Often Is the loanDepot Park Roof Open?

Almost never. loanDepot Park has the lowest open-roof rate in baseball, well under a handful of games in a typical season. The Miami climate gives the team almost no comfortable windows. Daytime heat and humidity are punishing, and summer evenings bring pop-up thunderstorms, so the roof stays closed to protect fans and keep games from being rained out. An open roof at loanDepot Park is a genuine event, usually reserved for a rare cool, dry stretch.

## How the Marlins Decide: The Roof Rules

The home club makes the call in the regular season, and the Marlins close the roof for heat and the constant threat of rain. The MLB rules apply:

- The roof is **closed for impending rain or other adverse weather**, which in Miami is most days
- If the game **starts open**, it can be closed at any time when weather threatens
- The **opening of the roof only happens between innings**, never mid-inning
- The decision is made by the home club in consultation with the umpire crew chief, and by MLB in the postseason

In practice, the heat and humidity alone are usually enough to keep the roof down before rain is even a factor.

## loanDepot Park Roof Specs

loanDepot Park opened in 2012 as the sixth retractable-roof stadium in MLB, built on the site of the old Orange Bowl. Its roof is one of the heaviest and most complex in the sport.

- The roof has **three panels**, one upper and two lower
- The panels move a combined **19 million pounds** in about **13 to 15 minutes**
- They travel at roughly **39 feet per minute** along the track
- When closed, the ballpark is fully climate controlled against the Miami heat

## loanDepot Park vs Other MLB Retractable Roofs

loanDepot Park is the most-closed retractable roof in baseball, at the very bottom of the league for openness alongside the Texas parks. The [Houston Astros](/stories/daikin-park-roof-open-astros-2026) at Daikin Park and the [Texas Rangers](/stories/rangers-roof-open-globe-life-field-2026) at Globe Life Field also lean heavily closed, but even they open more than Miami does. At the other extreme, Toronto's [Rogers Centre](/stories/rogers-centre-roof-open-blue-jays-2026) and Milwaukee's [American Family Field](/stories/american-family-field-roof-open-brewers-2026) open their roofs most of the summer.
