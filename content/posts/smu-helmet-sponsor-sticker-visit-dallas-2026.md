**SMU is putting a sponsor on its football helmets.** The logo of Visit Dallas, the city's tourism and convention marketing group, goes on the back of every Mustangs helmet for the last two games of the regular season: **Saturday, November 21 at No. 3 Notre Dame** and **Saturday, November 28 at Stanford**. SMU announced it on Tuesday, November 17. It is the first helmet sponsorship in SMU history, according to The Dallas Morning News.

So, are sponsor patches coming to college football helmets? They are already here. The NCAA has allowed a commercial logo on equipment since August 1, and at least three other programs sold the back of their helmets before SMU did.

## Quick Answers

- **Why is there a Visit Dallas logo on SMU's helmet?** It is a paid sponsorship. SMU Athletics and Mustang Partners, the school's revenue and brand unit, signed Visit Dallas as a partner, with the deal arranged through Learfield.
- **Which games?** At Notre Dame on November 21 and at Stanford on November 28. Both are road games.
- **Where does the sticker go?** On the back bumper of the helmet, below the center stripe. The photo shows "VISIT DALLAS" in blue with a white star inside the D, on the white helmet.
- **Is that allowed in college football?** Yes. Since August 1, 2026, NCAA Division I rules allow one additional commercial logo on equipment, up to 4 square inches, in the regular season.
- **Is SMU the first school with a helmet sponsor?** No. Utah (ALKEME), Minnesota (Visible) and UCF (Walt Disney World) put sponsor logos on their helmet bumpers earlier this season. It is SMU's first.
- **How much is SMU getting?** Not disclosed.
- **Will SMU keep it next year?** SMU's Brian Ullmann told The Dallas Morning News the two sides are already working on extending it.

## What SMU Announced

SMU's release says the Visit Dallas logo will be on the helmets for the Mustangs' trips to "two of college football's most iconic venues," Notre Dame Stadium and Stanford Stadium. The idea is national reach: SMU is on NBC in prime time in South Bend, and Visit Dallas gets its name in front of a college football audience well outside Texas.

Ullmann, SMU's executive deputy athletic director, framed it as a geography lesson. He told the Morning News that too many people still do not realize SMU is in Dallas, and that the school wants to make that connection as directly as it can. He also said 80% of SMU's fans travel 50 miles or less to games, and the school wants to draw more people from around the region for a weekend in the city.

Visit Dallas chief marketing officer Jennifer Walker said in SMU's release that the university's "growing national profile" is a chance to show the city to new audiences.

The deal is SMU's third big uniform-area sponsor this year. Texas Capital, the Dallas bank, has the all-sports jersey patch and an end zone, a deal the Morning News called the largest sponsorship in SMU athletics history. Progressive signed on as a field sponsor at the start of the season. Ullmann said SMU picked jersey, helmet and field sponsorships as its marquee offerings when Mustang Partners launched in December 2025.

## What The Sticker Looks Like

From the photo SMU provided to the Morning News:

- **Helmet:** White shell.
- **Stripe:** A red center stripe trimmed in blue, running down to the back.
- **Sticker:** "VISIT" in small blue capitals, then "DALLAS" in large blue letters, with a white star set into a blue D. It sits across the back bumper, below the stripe.
- **Also on the back:** The American flag decal and the standard warning label, above the bumper.

SMU has not said whether the sticker would also go on a different helmet if it changes combinations. Both games are on the road, where we expect SMU in white jerseys. Our [SMU uniform schedule](/stories/smu-uniform-schedule-2026) has every combination this season.

## What Are College Jersey Patch Deals Worth?

Most schools have not disclosed their numbers. The figures that have been reported this year, collected by Front Office Sports:

- **Notre Dame and SoFi:** about $18 million to $20 million a year over six years, reported by Ross Dellenger, the biggest school deal on record.
- **Tennessee and First Horizon:** about $120 million over 10 years for football and Olympic sports, reported by ESPN's Pete Thamel.
- **Michigan State and MSU Federal Credit Union:** in the range of $40 million over 10 years, per The Detroit News.
- **Illinois and Busey Bank:** $30 million over five years, per Sports Business Journal.
- **Big 12 and Monster Energy:** $20 million a year for a conference-wide deal.
- **Wyoming and Tallgrass:** $4.5 million over five years, per Sports Business Journal.

SMU has not disclosed what Texas Capital or Visit Dallas pays. Helmet values have not been reported for any school.

SMU's trip to South Bend puts two of these deals on one field: Notre Dame wears the SoFi patch at home, and SMU arrives with Texas Capital on the jersey and Visit Dallas on the helmet.

## Our Take

A helmet is the one piece of a college uniform that still reads as pure school: a color, a logo, a stripe. The back bumper is the least bad place to sell, and a city tourism board is a far easier sell to fans than an energy drink. Visit Dallas also fits SMU's whole pitch that it is Dallas' team. But it is also how it starts. One sticker at 4 square inches is small. The test is whether schools stop at one, and whether the logo ever moves from the bumper to the side of the shell, where the school's logo lives. If that happens, the helmet becomes a jersey sleeve.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is on the back of SMU's helmet?**

A Visit Dallas sticker, on the back bumper below the center stripe, for the games at Notre Dame on November 21 and at Stanford on November 28.

**Why is SMU wearing a Visit Dallas sticker?**

It is a sponsorship with Visit Dallas, the city's official destination marketing organization. SMU says it ties the program to its home city on a national stage, and Visit Dallas gets in front of fans across the country.

**Is this SMU's first helmet sponsor?**

Yes. The Dallas Morning News reported it is the first helmet sponsorship in program history.

**Can college football teams put sponsors on helmets?**

Yes. Since August 1, 2026, NCAA Division I teams can wear one additional commercial logo on equipment during the preseason and regular season, up to 4 square inches.

**Which college football teams have a sponsor on their helmets?**

Utah (ALKEME), Minnesota (Visible), UCF (Walt Disney World) and now SMU (Visit Dallas), all on the back bumper.

**What is SMU's jersey patch?**

Texas Capital, a Dallas-based bank, on all SMU sports. The Morning News reported it is the largest sponsorship deal in SMU athletics history and that SMU was the first power-conference school in Texas with an all-sports jersey patch.

**How much do college football jersey patches pay?**

The biggest reported school deal is Notre Dame's with SoFi, about $18 million to $20 million a year. Tennessee's First Horizon deal is reported at about $120 million over 10 years. Many schools, including SMU, have not disclosed their numbers.

**Can teams wear sponsor patches in the College Football Playoff?**

The NCAA rule covers non-NCAA championship competition, and the CFP is not an NCAA championship, so patches are expected to be allowed, CBS Sports reported. The NCAA has not approved logos in its own championships.

**Will SMU wear the Visit Dallas sticker next season?**

Nothing is official. SMU's Brian Ullmann told the Morning News the school is already working on extending the partnership.
