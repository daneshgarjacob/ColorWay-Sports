**Do the Vikings announce one uniform and wear another?** Sometimes. On Sunday night, before kickoff in Mexico City, the uniform account [@fashion_nfl](https://x.com/fashion_nfl) said Minnesota's favorite thing this season is listing one combination in its Game Preview and switching it at game time. We went back through every 2026 Vikings game and checked the team's own words against the team's own game photos.

Of the ten games played, the Vikings published a uniform ahead of time for seven. **Two of those seven did not match, and both times it was the pants.** In Week 5 at New Orleans, the team's Game Preview said white pants and Minnesota wore purple. In Week 7 against the Colts, the team's season schedule said purple pants and Minnesota wore white. The other five matched helmet, jersey and pants.

## Quick Answers

- **Do the Vikings wear what they announce?** Usually. Five of the seven announced looks matched; two did not.
- **Which games didn't match?** **Week 5 at the Saints** (listed white pants, wore purple pants) and **Week 7 vs. the Colts** (listed purple pants, wore white pants).
- **Was the helmet or jersey ever different?** No. Both changes were the pants only.
- **Where do the Vikings announce uniforms?** Two places: the 2026 home game themes article on vikings.com (July 28), which named a jersey and pants for every home game, and a short "Uniform" line inside some of the weekly Game Preview articles on vikings.com.
- **What did they wear in Week 11?** Purple helmet, white jersey, white pants in Mexico City, the exact combination the Game Preview listed.
- **What are the Vikings wearing this week?** Week 12 against Atlanta on Sunday, Nov. 29: **purple jersey with white pants**, per the team's home schedule.

## The Two Mismatches

### Week 5 at New Orleans: Listed White Pants, Wore Purple Pants

The Game Preview on vikings.com, published two days before the game, had a one-line uniform note: "The Vikings will wear their road uniforms: purple helmets with white jerseys and pants." An archived copy from Friday, Oct. 9 shows the same wording, so the line was there before kickoff.

On Sunday in the Superdome, Minnesota wore the purple helmet and white jersey with **purple pants**. The team's own game action gallery shows it from the first series. The Saints were in the gold helmet, black jersey and black pants, so the switch put more color on the Vikings' side of the field. Minnesota won 23-13.

The Vikings have not said why. We found no team or beat-writer explanation for the change.

### Week 7 vs. Colts: Listed Purple Pants, Wore White Pants

This one is the season schedule. When the Vikings published their home game themes on July 28, the Colts game had no theme of its own, and the team wrote that it was "scheduled to wear Purple-on-Purple (jersey and pants)." It was meant to be the one all-purple home game of the year.

By game week the plan had changed. The Game Preview, archived on Saturday, Oct. 24, the day before the game, read: "Normal home uniform combination of purple helmets and jerseys with white pants." That is what Minnesota wore. So the team did update its own article before kickoff, but the July schedule, which is the page most fans and most uniform sites work from, was never corrected. [Athlon Sports](https://athlonsports.com/nfl/minnesota-vikings/announcers-referees-and-jersey-combination-for-vikings-week-7-game) repeated purple-on-purple the night before. The Vikings won 16-10.

The team did not explain the change. All-purple did turn up a week later, at Detroit in Week 8, where the Game Preview announced it and the Vikings wore it.

## Week 11 In Mexico City Matched

@fashion_nfl's post went up about 20 minutes before kickoff on Sunday night. The Week 11 Game Preview had listed white jerseys, white pants and purple helmets, and Minnesota wore exactly that: purple helmet, white jersey, white pants. [Athlon Sports](https://athlonsports.com/nfl/announcers-referees-and-jersey-combination-for-vikings-week-11-game-vs-49ers) quoted the same combination on Nov. 21.

One detail we can't settle: vikings.com shows that Game Preview was last edited at 6:29 p.m. Central on Sunday, about 50 minutes before kickoff. We could not find an earlier saved copy, so we can't say whether the uniform line changed in that edit. Every version we can see says white over white, and that is what was worn.

## Our Take

Two misses in seven announcements is a small number. It is still two too many for a team that puts its uniform plan in writing. The Vikings publish a jersey for every home game before the season starts, and that is worth keeping. The fix is small: if the equipment staff changes the pants, update the page that said otherwise. The July schedule still lists purple pants for a game that was played in white a month ago.

Fans pay attention to this, and uniform sites, broadcasters and fan accounts copy the team's listing word for word. When the listing is wrong, the mistake spreads. A single game-day post with the full combination, helmet to pants, would solve it, the way many teams already post a uniform graphic every week.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the Vikings wearing this week?**

**What did the Vikings wear in Mexico City?**

Purple helmet, white jersey and white pants against the 49ers on Nov. 22. San Francisco was the designated home team in scarlet. The 49ers won 35-10. More: [what both teams wore in Mexico City](/stories/49ers-vikings-mexico-city-uniforms-2026).

**Do the Vikings wear the uniform they announce?**

Five of the seven times they announced one in 2026, yes. The two exceptions were the pants in Week 5 at New Orleans and Week 7 against Indianapolis.

**Why did the Vikings switch uniforms against the Saints?**

The team has not said. The Game Preview listed white pants; Minnesota wore purple pants with the white jersey against a Saints team in all black.

**Why didn't the Vikings wear all purple against the Colts?**

The team has not explained it. The July schedule listed purple-on-purple, but by game week the Game Preview said white pants, and that is what they wore.

**Have the Vikings worn all purple in 2026?**

Once: Week 8 at Detroit on Nov. 1, purple helmet, purple jersey and purple pants, against the Lions' silver Rivalries uniform. Detroit won 19-15.

**Where do the Vikings announce their uniforms?**

In the 2026 home game themes article on vikings.com, which lists a jersey and pants for every home game, and in a short "Uniform" line in some weekly Game Preview articles. They also posted the Classic opener on X the day before.

**Do the Vikings announce road uniforms?**

Sometimes. The Game Preview listed a road uniform for Week 5 at New Orleans, Week 8 at Detroit and Week 11 in Mexico City, and none for the Bears, Buccaneers or Packers games.

**What pants do the Vikings wear with the white jersey?**

Both. In 2026 they have worn purple pants with the white jersey three times (at Chicago, New Orleans and Green Bay) and white pants once (Mexico City).

**What is the Vikings' record by uniform in 2026?**

7-3 overall: 3-1 in purple over white, 3-0 in white over purple, 1-0 in the Classic, 0-1 in all purple and 0-1 in white over white.

**When do the Vikings wear the Rivalries uniform?**

Week 15 against the Lions on Sunday Night Football, Dec. 20. It is the debut of the deep purple set with rune-carved numbers and a gold facemask.

**When do the Vikings wear the Winter Warrior uniform in 2026?**

Week 16 at home against Washington for the "Winter Whiteout" game, with the date still to be set, and we expect it again at the Jets in Week 17.
