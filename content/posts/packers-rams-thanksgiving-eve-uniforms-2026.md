**The Rams wear Fearsome White on Thanksgiving Eve, and it is the only time you will see it this season.** Los Angeles hosts the Green Bay Packers at SoFi Stadium on **Wednesday, November 25, at 8:00 p.m. Eastern (5:00 p.m. Pacific) on Netflix**, the first NFL game ever played the night before Thanksgiving. The Rams put Fearsome White on this date when they published all 17 of their 2026 uniform assignments on September 1: a **white jersey with royal blue numbers**, a royal stripe that runs over the shoulder like a horn, **white pants**, and a **royal blue helmet with white horns and a white facemask**. The Rams **confirmed** it on Tuesday, November 24, in a "Fearsome White Uniform Look Book" of player photos. Green Bay has **confirmed** its look: the Packers posted their Week 12 uniform on Monday, November 23, and it is the **green jersey with gold pants and the gold helmet**.

## Quick Answers

- **What are the Rams wearing on Thanksgiving Eve?** Fearsome White: white jersey with royal blue numbers, white pants, royal helmet with white horns and a white facemask. **Confirmed** by the Rams on November 24, matching the uniform schedule they published September 1.
- **What are the Packers wearing?** The green jersey with gold pants and the gold helmet. **Confirmed** by the Packers on November 23. Green Bay is in green on the road because the Rams took white first.
- **When is it?** Wednesday, November 25, 8:00 p.m. ET (5:00 p.m. PT) on Netflix, at SoFi Stadium.
- **How many times will the Rams wear Fearsome White in 2026?** Once. This is the only date.
- **Is this the first NFL game on Thanksgiving Eve?** Yes.
- **Our grade:** A- for the uniform, A- for the matchup.

## What The Rams Are Wearing (Confirmed)

**Update, Tuesday, November 24:** the Rams posted a "Fearsome White Uniform Look Book," seven photos of players in the full uniform with the caption "Fearsome's in the fabric." It matches the schedule: the **royal blue helmet with white horns and a white facemask, white jersey with royal blue numbers and the royal shoulder stripe, and white pants**.

Fearsome White was revealed in July alongside the gold Classic Sol set, and it is named for the Fearsome Foursome, the Rams defensive line of the 1960s ([who they were](/stories/who-were-the-fearsome-foursome)). Here is every piece:

- **The jersey.** White, with royal blue numbers and a single royal stripe that sweeps up and over the shoulder to mirror the curve of the horn on the helmet. TV numbers sit centered on the sleeve.
- **The helmet.** The regular royal blue shell, but the gold horns are swapped for **white horns** and the facemask is **white**. That is the 1960s Rams helmet color scheme on the modern shell. The front bumper carries a retro "R" shaped like a ram horn.
- **The pants.** White, so the look runs white from the shoulders down with royal as the only color.

It is a home game, and the Rams are choosing white at home. That is the whole point of the date: a standalone national window on the biggest week of the regular season, and the cleanest uniform in the closet. ## What The Packers Are Wearing (Confirmed)

**Update, Monday, November 23:** the Packers posted their Week 12 uniform with the caption "On Wednesdays we wear Green & Gold." It is the **gold helmet, green jersey and gold pants**, with green socks, the same combination they wore at the Jets in Week 2. The team's graphic also shows the round John Madden silhouette patch; the NFL's release lists that patch for the three Thursday games, so we will check Wednesday's game photos before saying Green Bay wears it.

Two teams cannot both wear white, so Green Bay brings its **green home jersey** to Los Angeles. The Packers normally wear white on the road, and this is one of three road games this season where the host took white first. The other two were at the Jets in Week 2 and at Tampa Bay in Week 4.

## The Packers On Thanksgiving

Green Bay has spent the last few Thanksgivings in Detroit. The Packers won there in 2023 and again in 2025, 31-24, both times against a Lions team in its throwbacks. This year Green Bay is off on Thursday and plays the night before instead, which makes Wednesday a clean first: a new holiday window, a new uniform, and a home team in white.

## Is Fearsome White Any Good?

Yes. It is the best new uniform the Rams have made since the 2020 rebrand. The shoulder stripe is the one idea in the Rams closet nobody else could wear, and the white facemask is the detail we have wanted from this team for years. The only flaw is the black outline on the numbers and the black swoosh, which pull the jersey a step toward Indianapolis. Drop the black and it is close to perfect. **A-**, the same grade we gave it at the [reveal](/stories/rams-leaked-alternate-jerseys-2026).

The matchup gets an **A-** too. Royal and white against green and gold is four strong colors with no overlap, under the lights, on a night with no other football. Our only complaint is that Fearsome White gets one game a year.

## When You Can See It Again

Not in 2026. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the Rams wearing against the Packers on Thanksgiving Eve?**

Fearsome White: a white jersey with royal blue numbers and a royal shoulder stripe, white pants, and the royal helmet with white horns and a white facemask. It is on the uniform schedule the Rams published September 1, and the team confirmed it with a Fearsome White look book on November 24.

**What time is Packers vs. Rams on Thanksgiving Eve?**

Wednesday, November 25, at 8:00 p.m. Eastern, 5:00 p.m. Pacific, at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood.

**What channel is the Packers-Rams game on?**

Netflix, which streams the game nationally.

**Is there an NFL game the night before Thanksgiving?**

Yes, for the first time ever. Packers at Rams on Wednesday, November 25 opens a five-game holiday week that ends with Broncos at Steelers on Black Friday.

**What are the Packers wearing against the Rams?**

The green jersey with gold pants and the gold helmet. The Packers confirmed it on November 23 ("On Wednesdays we wear Green & Gold"). They are in green because the Rams are in white at home, the same reason Green Bay wore that combination at the Jets in Week 2.

**What is Fearsome White named after?**

The Fearsome Foursome, the Rams defensive line of the 1960s: Deacon Jones, Merlin Olsen, Rosey Grier and Lamar Lundy. Our [Fearsome Foursome explainer](/stories/who-were-the-fearsome-foursome) has the history.

**Is the Fearsome White helmet white?**

No. The shell is the regular royal blue. The horns and the facemask are white instead of gold and royal.

**How many times will the Rams wear Fearsome White in 2026?**

Once, on Thanksgiving Eve against Green Bay.

**What other alternates do the Rams wear in 2026?**

Classic Sol, the gold 1951 tribute, against the Giants in Week 2 and the Chiefs on December 3, and the black Midnight Mode Rivalries uniform at Seattle on Christmas Day.

**Do the Packers wear a throwback in 2026?**

Yes, but not here. Green Bay has its navy 1923 Classic in Week 14 against Buffalo, per our Packers schedule.
