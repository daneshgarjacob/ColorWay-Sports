The Wild Card Series starts today, and every team still playing is wearing the **2026 postseason cap patch** on the side of its New Era cap. Take a good look at it, because you have seen it before. MLB is using the same **MLB postseason logo** for the third straight October, and that felt like the right moment to go back and rank every one of them.

MLB posted its own history of the postseason mark last year, 15 logos in a row from 2011 on:

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; margin: 2em 0;">
  <blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/MLB/status/1972791135283315129"></a></blockquote>
</div>

We added 2026 and graded all 16. Our rules were simple. Color and personality win. A **playoff patch** lives on a cap or a sleeve for a few weeks, so it has to read at small size, but it also has to make October feel different from the regular season. The logos that did that, 2011, 2019 and 2022, sit at the top. The ones that could be any month of the year sit at the bottom, and the three-year repeat from 2024 to 2026 costs the current design a lot.

A quick bit of history before the countdown. From 2013 through 2019, every playoff team wore the postseason logo as a sleeve patch on its jerseys. Since 2020 the postseason patch has been cap-only, stitched on the right side of the New Era on-field cap. The two pennant winners then switch to a World Series cap patch and add a small World Series chest patch for the Fall Classic. For what teams are wearing this year, see our [2026 MLB postseason uniforms guide](/stories/mlb-postseason-uniforms-2026), the [playoff helmet and Strauss logo breakdown](/stories/mlb-playoff-helmet-strauss-logo-2026), and the game-by-game [MLB uniform tracker](/stories/mlb-uniform-tracker-2026).

We are counting down from 16 to 1, worst to best.

The one we cannot get behind. The 2021 logo is a navy POSTSEASON wordmark with a white baseball peeking up from behind the letters and the year split around the MLB silhouette underneath. That is it. There is no color beyond navy and a sliver of red, the ball reads as a gray smudge at cap-patch size, and the whole thing looks like a placeholder someone forgot to replace. A postseason logo has one job, which is to make October feel different from the other six months, and this one feels like a regular season graphic with a baseball clip-art layer. It is the only logo on the board that we would call lifeless. F.

Here is the 2026 MLB postseason logo, and if it looks familiar, that is the problem. It is the same serif POSTSEASON wordmark MLB introduced in 2024, the same MLB logo floating on top, the same thin gold rules, with a gold 2026 swapped in underneath. This is the third straight October with the same design. The league confirmed it in its own 2026 postseason schedule graphic:

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; margin: 2em 0;">
  <blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/MLB/status/2086855575074132455"></a></blockquote>
</div>

Nothing about the mark is broken. It is clean, it reads on a cap, and the beveled lettering still looks expensive. But the entire appeal of a postseason logo is that each year gets its own, and three straight years of the same design is honestly disappointing. The **2026 postseason cap patch** sits on the right-wear side of every playoff team's New Era cap, the same spot it has lived since 2020. New Era put the [2026 postseason side patch 59FIFTY](https://www.neweracap.com/products/los-angeles-dodgers-2026-postseason-side-patch-59fifty-fitted) on sale on September 15, 2026, before a single playoff spot was clinched.

The 2026 bracket: the Wild Card Series presented by AbbVie runs September 29 to October 1, the ALDS presented by Booking.com runs October 3 to 10 and the NLDS runs October 3 to 9, the NLCS runs October 11 to 19, the ALCS runs October 12 to 20, and the World Series presented by Capital One runs October 23 to 31. D plus, and the plus is for the craft, not the idea.

This was the sleeve patch that year, worn by every playoff club from 2013 through 2019, and it is the weakest of that sleeve era. D plus.

## Frequently Asked Questions About MLB Postseason Logos

**What is the best MLB postseason logo?**

We rank the 2011 MLB postseason logo as the best, with an A. It sets POSTSEASON in gold and olive and frames it with autumn leaves, which makes it the only postseason logo that actually looks like fall. The 2019 black diamond with a rainbow edge (A minus) and the 2022 gold and silver pennant (B plus) round out our top tier.

**What is the worst MLB postseason logo?**

The 2021 postseason logo is the worst in our ranking, with an F. It is a plain navy wordmark with a baseball rising behind it, no real color, and nothing that says October. The 2018 bunting ribbon and the 2026 repeat tie for the next spot up at D plus.

**Is the 2026 MLB postseason logo the same as 2025?**

Yes. The 2026 MLB postseason logo is the same serif POSTSEASON wordmark with gold rules that MLB introduced in 2024 and carried over in 2025, with the year changed to 2026. That makes 2026 the third straight postseason with the same design. MLB's own 2026 postseason schedule graphic shows it.

**Where is the 2026 postseason cap patch?**

The 2026 postseason cap patch is on the right-wear side of the New Era on-field cap for every playoff team. New Era released the 2026 postseason side patch 59FIFTY caps on September 15, 2026. The two World Series teams switch to a World Series cap patch for the Fall Classic.

**Do MLB teams wear a postseason patch on their jerseys?**

Not anymore. From 2013 through 2019, every playoff team wore the postseason logo as a jersey sleeve patch. Since 2020 the playoff patch has been cap-only. The two pennant winners do add a small World Series chest patch to their jerseys for the Fall Classic.

**Who designed the current MLB postseason logo?**

The postseason identity MLB introduced in 2024 was built by Fanbrandz with SKYE Design. It uses custom beveled lettering called Flare Extended and a color system that steps up by round as the playoffs go on. MLB has used the same design in 2024, 2025 and 2026.

**When is the 2026 MLB postseason?**

The 2026 Wild Card Series presented by AbbVie runs September 29 to October 1. The ALDS presented by Booking.com runs October 3 to 10 and the NLDS runs October 3 to 9. The NLCS runs October 11 to 19 and the ALCS runs October 12 to 20. The World Series presented by Capital One runs October 23 to 31.

**Is the 2026 World Series logo new?**

Barely. The 2026 World Series logo keeps the cursive World Series script, gold diamond, Capital One and gold year banner from 2025. It is the first back-to-back World Series logo repeat since 1998 and 1999. We gave it a B minus: we love the script and dislike the repeat.

## The Bottom Line

Sixteen years of MLB postseason logos tell a clear story. When the league took a swing, with the autumn leaves in 2011, the oak leaf shield in 2012, the rainbow diamond in 2019 and the gold pennant in 2022, it made marks people still remember. When it played it safe, it got 2018, 2020 and 2021. The 2024 design is a well-made logo, but running it three straight years turns a postseason identity into a template with a date field, and that is a real letdown for the most important month of the baseball calendar. We would take one bold swing that misses over a third year of the same patch. Here is hoping 2027 brings back some color.
