## Series Storylines

**The Hurricanes have been waiting for this run.** Carolina has been one of the best regular-season teams in the East for five straight years but has never broken through to the Stanley Cup Final under the current core. Round 2 was a four-game sweep of the Flyers. The Canes have not lost a home game in these playoffs.

**The Habs are the playoff story of the year.** Montreal entered the postseason as a wild card and has knocked off two Atlantic Division teams in seven-game series to reach the Conference Final. The bleu-blanc-rouge running deep in May is the kind of run that lifts every Canadian sports fan regardless of team allegiance.

**Bell Centre versus Lenovo Center.** Two of the loudest playoff buildings in the East. Montreal sells out every playoff night with the most engaged fan base in hockey. Carolina has built one of the best home-ice atmospheres in the league under the Storm Surge era and has lost only twice at home in the entire 2026 playoffs.
