Every Titans jersey is new in 2026. Tennessee unveiled a full rebrand in March, its first uniform change since 2018, built on two jerseys, Titans Blue at home and white on the road, and added a third in August with the Music City Rivalries uniform. There is no throwback on the calendar and no Oilers set this year. We graded the new jerseys an [A- when the rebrand landed](/stories/tennessee-titans-rebrand-2026) and put Tennessee eighteenth in our [best NFL uniforms of 2026](/stories/best-nfl-uniforms-2026-ranked) at a B, and the gap between those two grades is mostly about how much the new set pops on a field.

Here are all three Tennessee Titans jerseys for 2026, ranked worst to first and graded, with the helmet and pants each one wears. For the week-by-week jersey schedule 2026, the [Titans 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/titans-uniform-schedule-2026) has every game.

Music City is Tennessee's entry in the NFL x Nike Rivalries program, revealed on August 25. The jersey is navy with "Music City" in embroidered script across the chest, a nod to the honky-tonk signs downtown, and neon-inspired numbers. The pant striping is drawn from six guitar strings, the collar tab carries the Tennessee tristar, and red is almost entirely gone. The helmet is the headline: a Titans Blue shell with navy racing stripes and a circular navy T on the side, the first time the new era has put light blue on a helmet. It debuts at home against the Jacksonville Jaguars on Sunday, November 15.

Dropping red and letting two blues carry the uniform was the right instinct, and the script is a great touch. The helmet is the best thing Tennessee has produced since the rebrand, good enough that we would happily see it more than once a year. The jersey never catches up. The navy body is quiet, the neon numbers carry more weight than they should have to, and the guitar-string stripes read better on a detail card than from the stands.

It grades a B-, the same mark we gave it in our [Rivalries uniforms ranking](/stories/nfl-rivalries-uniforms-2026-ranked), where it placed fourth of sixteen. It is an A-grade helmet on a B-grade jersey, and this list ranks the jersey.

Titans Blue is the new home jersey: a lighter blue with TITANS across the chest in a clean block, white numbers outlined in red, a red and navy sleeve stripe, and three navy stars at the back of the collar for the state's three grand divisions. It goes under the white helmet with a white facemask and a light blue and red stripe, with white or light blue pants. It opened the season against the New York Jets with white pants and blue socks, and it projects to the other six home dates: the Philadelphia Eagles in Week 2, the Houston Texans, Cleveland Browns, Washington Commanders, Indianapolis Colts and Pittsburgh Steelers. It should also travel once, to Dallas in Week 11 on Sunday, November 22, because the Cowboys wear white at home. Only Week 1 is team-confirmed, so the rest is our projection.

The color is the whole argument. The old navy went nearly black under stadium lights, and this blue holds its identity in the same conditions. It points back to the Oilers without dressing up as them, and almost nobody else in the league wears blue at that value. White pants are the way to wear it, because they break the blue up and make the jersey the loudest thing on the field. The light blue pants give you a monochrome version we would use sparingly.

It grades an A-, our grade on the rebrand. The one reason it sits second is the one note we have had since March: it plays it a little safe, and the white jersey does more with the same pieces.

The white road jersey has TENNESSEE across the chest in light blue and light blue numbers outlined in red, worn under the same white helmet with light blue or white pants. It projects to eight of the nine road games: at the Giants, Ravens, Colts, Bengals, Jaguars, Lions, Raiders and Texans. The Titans have not announced road jerseys game by game, so these follow the standard convention.

Putting the state name on the road jersey instead of the team name is a small idea done well, and the light blue numbers give it a warmth most white road sets never get near. With the light blue pants it is the best combination Tennessee owns, which was our favorite look at the March reveal and still is. The date to circle is Week 14 at Detroit, where light blue against Honolulu blue should be one of the prettier road pictures of the year.

It grades an A-, level with Titans Blue, and it takes the top spot because it is the one Titans jersey with a real idea on its chest.

## The Bottom Line on the Titans Jersey Rankings

The two new base jerseys are the strength of this closet, and they grade the same. The white road set edges Titans Blue because TENNESSEE in light blue is the best single idea in the rebrand, and Titans Blue is the color that finally makes this franchise readable at distance.

Music City is a B- because its best piece sits on top of the head. If Tennessee ever puts that Titans Blue helmet on the regular set, this whole ranking gets better.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jerseys do the Titans have in 2026?**

Three: the Titans Blue home jersey, the white road jersey, and the Music City Rivalries uniform. All three are new, since the Titans rebranded in March.

**What is the best Titans jersey?**

We rank the white road jersey first with an A-. TENNESSEE across the chest in light blue with light blue numbers gives it more character than most white sets, and with light blue pants it is the best look Tennessee has. Titans Blue also grades an A-.

**What jersey are the Titans wearing this week?**

Week 2 is at home against the Philadelphia Eagles on Sunday, September 20 at 1:00 p.m. ET, and Tennessee is projected in Titans Blue under the white helmet with the white pants it wore in Week 1. Philadelphia is projected in white. That is a projection until kickoff, so check the [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026) after the game.

**Do the Titans have a new jersey in 2026?**

Yes, all of them. The Titans Blue and white sets are from the March rebrand, and the Music City Rivalries uniform was revealed on August 25.

**When do the Titans wear the Music City uniform in 2026?**

Once, at home against the Jacksonville Jaguars on Sunday, November 15. It is worn with the Titans Blue helmet and stays in the rotation for three seasons.

**Will the Titans wear an Oilers throwback in 2026?**

No. Tennessee wore the Houston Oilers throwbacks in 2023 and 2024, shelved them in 2025, and has no throwback on the 2026 schedule.
