**The Senators wore black and gold on Saturday because the Hometown Remix sweater came back.** Ottawa put on its Remix, the black sweater with the brushed gold centurion crest, against the LA Kings at Canadian Tire Centre on Saturday, November 21, and beat them **4-2**. It was the second time the Senators have worn it, and the date was never announced: when Ottawa revealed the sweater on November 2, it said the Remix would be worn nine times at home and named only one night, the November 12 debut against Colorado. After the win, the team posted the line that sums up the season so far: "UNDEFEATED IN THE HOMETOWN REMIX JERSEYS."

## Quick Answers

- **What did the Senators wear Saturday?** The black **Hometown Remix** sweater: black body, a brushed metallic gold centurion crest, gold numbers outlined in red, and gold and red bands on the sleeves and hem. The Kings wore their road white.
- **Was it on the schedule?** Ottawa has not published its Remix dates. The only date it announced was the November 12 debut against Colorado.
- **How many times have they worn it?** Twice: November 12 against Colorado and November 21 against the Kings.
- **Record in it:** 2-0. A 4-3 overtime win over Colorado and a 4-2 win over Los Angeles.
- **How many are left?** Seven. Ottawa said nine home games, and two are done.

## What The Senators Wore Against The Kings

Every piece we could see in the team's photos and the Sportsnet broadcast:

- **Sweater:** **black**, with the centurion crest done in **brushed metallic gold** instead of the regular red and gold.
- **Numbers:** **gold**, outlined in **red**, on the back and sleeves. The back lettering uses a new font based on the Gothic arched windows and doors of Parliament Hill.
- **Stripes:** **gold and red bands** on the sleeves and hem.
- **Shoulders:** the English and French versions of the logo on opposite shoulders.
- **Pants and helmets:** **black**, with gold and red trim on the pants.
- **Socks:** black with gold and red stripes.

The Senators also call out the **Peace Tower clock** on the sleeves and the **Alexandra Bridge**, which connects Ottawa and Gatineau, worked into the hem. The Kings were in their **white road sweater**, so it was black against white all night.

## How It Compares With The Regular Senators Black

The everyday Ottawa home sweater is also black, which is why the Remix is easy to miss on TV at first. The difference is the trim. The regular home black, from the 2020 return to the 1992 look, has the 2D centurion crest bordered in gold, **two red arm bars framing white numbers**, a red collar and a wide red hem stripe. Red does most of the work.

The Remix flips that. The numbers are **gold instead of white**, the crest is **all gold**, and gold leads the stripes with red as the accent. Two nights before the Kings game, against Philadelphia on November 19, Ottawa wore the regular black with the red and white stripes, so the switch is easy to see side by side.

## Was Saturday's Remix Game Announced?

No, and that is why it caught fans off guard. The Senators' own announcement on November 2 said the black alternate would be worn **nine times at home, beginning November 12 against the Colorado Avalanche**, and stopped there. No list of the other eight dates has been published by the team, so every Remix night after the debut is a game-day reveal.

Saturday was also the Senators' Celebrating the Ottawa Charge Night, a tribute to the city's PWHL team. The team has not said whether the theme night is why the Remix came out, so we are not going to guess.

## How Many Remix Nights Are Left?

**Seven.** Ottawa has 30 home games left after Saturday, so roughly one in four of them will be a Remix night. The next five home dates are:

- **Monday, November 23** vs. Calgary
- **Thursday, December 3** vs. New Jersey
- **Saturday, December 5** vs. Tampa Bay
- **Tuesday, December 8** vs. Anaheim
- **Thursday, December 10** vs. Vegas

None of those are announced as Remix nights. We will mark each one in the [Senators 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/senators-uniform-schedule-2026-27) the morning after, the full league picture is in the [NHL uniform tracker](/stories/nhl-uniform-tracker-2026-27), and everything we have on Ottawa is on the [Senators team hub](/teams/nhl/ottawa-senators).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why did the Senators wear black and gold jerseys?**
They were wearing their Hometown Remix sweater, a black alternate with a brushed gold centurion crest and gold numbers. Ottawa wore it against the LA Kings on Saturday, November 21, 2026, the second time this season.

**What jersey did the Senators wear against the Kings?**
The black and gold Hometown Remix. The Kings wore white. Ottawa won 4-2.

**Was the Senators Remix game on November 21 announced?**
No. The Senators said the Remix would be worn nine times at home and only named the November 12 debut against Colorado. November 21 was not announced ahead of time.

**How many times will the Senators wear the Hometown Remix?**
Nine times at home in 2026-27, according to the team. Two are done, November 12 and November 21, so seven are left.

**What is the Senators' record in the Hometown Remix jersey?**
2-0. They beat Colorado 4-3 in overtime on November 12 and the Kings 4-2 on November 21.

**What is the difference between the Senators Remix and the regular black jersey?**
The regular home black has white numbers, red arm bars and a red hem stripe. The Remix has gold numbers outlined in red, an all-gold brushed crest, and gold and red stripes. Both sweaters are black.

**What does the Senators Hometown Remix jersey look like?**
Black, with a brushed metallic gold centurion crest, gold numbers in a font based on Parliament Hill's Gothic arches, the Peace Tower clock on the sleeves, the Alexandra Bridge in the hem, and English and French logos on opposite shoulders.

**When do the Senators wear the Hometown Remix next?**
The team has not announced it. Ottawa's next home games are November 23 against Calgary and December 3 against New Jersey.

**Who scored for the Senators against the Kings?**
Tim Stützle scored twice and added an assist, and Stephen Halliday also scored in the 4-2 win.

**Does the Senators Remix jersey come with black pants?**
Yes. Black pants with gold and red trim, black helmets and black socks with gold and red stripes.

**Is the Senators Remix jersey the same as the red third?**
No. The red third, called Ignite the Red, is a red sweater with a black yoke. Ottawa has worn it twice at home this season, against St. Louis and the Rangers. The Remix is black and gold.

**How many jerseys do the Senators have this season?**
Four: the home black, the road white, the red third and the black and gold Hometown Remix.
