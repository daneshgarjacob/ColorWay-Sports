**Final: Bears 39, Lions 35.** Detroit wore **Honolulu blue with silver pants and the silver helmet** on Thanksgiving 2026, **not the 1934 throwback**, and lost to Chicago at Ford Field on Caleb Williams' 6-yard touchdown pass to Rome Odunze with 3 seconds left. The Lions posted the look on Thursday morning in the team's ["Thanksgiving Threads" post](https://x.com/Lions/status/2125953219213824130), and ESPN's game photos confirm it: silver helmet, Honolulu blue jersey, silver pants and blue socks, the home set our [Lions 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/lions-uniform-schedule-2026) had for this game. The Bears wore their road set, the **navy helmet, white jersey and navy pants**, also confirmed from ESPN's game photos.

**One addition for Martha Firestone Ford.** The Lions' owner from 2014 to 2020 died this week, and Detroit wore a black **"MFF" decal on the back of the helmet**, beside the American flag decal, with the WCF decal for her late husband, William Clay Ford, just below, [per the team's post on Thursday](https://x.com/Lions/status/2125992981895495707). The Lions also marked her with a pregame tribute on the field ([video](https://x.com/Lions/status/2126012767953981557)), and the team says a jersey patch debuts on Sunday, December 6, at Atlanta.

## Quick Answers

- **What did the Lions wear on Thanksgiving 2026?** The Honolulu blue home jersey with the silver helmet and silver pants, plus an "MFF" helmet decal for Martha Firestone Ford. **Confirmed** by the team on Thanksgiving morning and by ESPN's game photos. Chicago won 39-35.
- **Did the Lions wear throwbacks on Thanksgiving 2026?** No. Detroit wore the standard Honolulu blue home set. The Lions wore the 1934 throwbacks against Green Bay in 2023 and 2025, and they are 0-5 on Thanksgiving in them since 2017: [the full record](/stories/lions-record-in-throwbacks-on-thanksgiving). This loss came in the standard blue.
- **What did the Bears wear?** The navy helmet, white jersey and navy pants, confirmed from ESPN's game photos.
- **When was it?** Thursday, November 26, 1:00 p.m. ET (noon in Chicago) on CBS, at Ford Field. Final: Bears 39, Lions 35.
- **Our grade:** B+ for the matchup.

## What The Lions Have Worn On Thanksgiving

Detroit does not have one fixed Thanksgiving uniform, which is why people search this question every November.

- **2026 vs. Chicago: Honolulu blue, silver pants, silver helmet.** No throwback, and an "MFF" helmet decal for Martha Firestone Ford. Chicago won 39-35.
- **2025 vs. Green Bay: throwbacks.** The logo-free silver helmet, a plain Honolulu blue jersey and silver pants, per [WILX](https://www.wilx.com/2025/11/26/lions-return-classic-throwback-look-thanksgiving-game/). Green Bay won 31-24.
- **2024 vs. Chicago: all blue.** Honolulu blue jersey and blue pants, and Detroit won 23-20, ending a seven-game Thanksgiving losing streak. It was the previous time these two teams met on the holiday.
- **2023 vs. Green Bay: throwbacks.** The same logo-free throwback set.
- **The 2000s:** Detroit wore throwbacks often on the holiday in that decade.

So the last two times the Bears came to Detroit on Thanksgiving, the Lions were in blue: all blue in 2024, blue over silver in 2026.

## Our Grade

**B+.** Honolulu blue against navy and orange on white is a good-looking game: three strong colors and no overlap. What held it back was two standard uniforms on the one day a year Detroit owns. The Lions have a logo-free throwback that has become a Thanksgiving favorite, and leaving it in the closet on the biggest stage of their season was a miss. It would have been an A-.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the Lions wearing on Thanksgiving 2026?**

The Honolulu blue home jersey with the silver helmet and silver pants against the Bears, confirmed by the team's Thanksgiving Threads post and ESPN's game photos, with a black "MFF" helmet decal for Martha Firestone Ford. It was not the 1934 throwback. Chicago won 39-35.

**Are the Lions wearing throwbacks on Thanksgiving this year?**

No. In 2026 Detroit wore Honolulu blue with silver pants and the silver helmet, not the throwback, and lost 39-35 to the Bears. The Lions wore their throwbacks on Thanksgiving in 2023 and 2025, both against Green Bay, and all blue against Chicago in 2024.

**What time is Bears vs. Lions on Thanksgiving?**

Thursday, November 26, at 1:00 p.m. Eastern, noon Central, at Ford Field in Detroit.

**What channel is the Lions Thanksgiving game on?**

CBS.

**What are the Bears wearing on Thanksgiving?**

The white road jersey with the navy helmet and navy pants, Chicago's standard road look, confirmed from ESPN's game photos. The Bears won 39-35.

**Why do the Lions always play on Thanksgiving?**

Detroit has hosted a Thanksgiving game every year since 1934, except for the World War II years. It is the oldest tradition in the NFL schedule.

**What color are the Lions?**

Honolulu blue and silver. Owner George A. Richards picked blue and silver in 1934 when he moved the team to Detroit, and our [Honolulu blue explainer](/stories/why-do-the-lions-wear-honolulu-blue) has the full story.

**When did the Lions and Bears last play on Thanksgiving?**

Thursday, November 26, 2026: Bears 39, Lions 35, with Detroit in Honolulu blue over silver pants and Chicago in white over navy. Before that, 2024, a 23-20 Lions win in Detroit with the Lions in all blue.

**What is the Lions' Rivalries uniform?**

Rivalries Silver: a pale silver jersey with DETROIT across the chest, carbon-fiber numbers and an electric blue helmet. It was worn once, against Minnesota on November 1.

**Do NFL players wear a patch on Thanksgiving?**

Yes. Both the Lions and Bears wore the [John Madden Thanksgiving patch](/stories/john-madden-thanksgiving-patch-2026), a round silhouette of Madden in his headset on the left chest, confirmed by the NFL on November 19. It has been worn every Thanksgiving since 2023.
