**The Lions are wearing Honolulu blue on Thanksgiving, not a throwback, and the team has confirmed it.** Detroit hosts the Chicago Bears at Ford Field on **Thursday, November 26, at 1:00 p.m. Eastern on CBS**, the first of three games on Thanksgiving Day. Our [Lions 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/lions-uniform-schedule-2026) had the **Honolulu blue home jersey with the silver helmet**, and Detroit confirmed it on Thursday morning in the team's ["Thanksgiving Threads" post](https://x.com/Lions/status/2125953219213824130) on Thursday morning: silver helmet, Honolulu blue jersey, silver pants and blue socks. The Bears are expected in their **white road jersey with the navy helmet**; Chicago has not posted its full look.

## Quick Answers

- **What are the Lions wearing on Thanksgiving 2026?** The Honolulu blue home jersey with the silver helmet and silver pants. **Confirmed** by the team on Thanksgiving morning.
- **Are the Lions wearing throwbacks on Thanksgiving?** No. Detroit's game-day uniform post shows the standard Honolulu blue home set. They wore them against Green Bay in 2023 and 2025, but there is no throwback on the 2026 calendar. Detroit is 0-5 on Thanksgiving in the throwback: [the full record](/stories/lions-record-in-throwbacks-on-thanksgiving).
- **What are the Bears wearing?** The white road jersey with the navy helmet. **Expected.**
- **When is it?** Thursday, November 26, 1:00 p.m. ET (noon in Chicago) on CBS, at Ford Field.
- **Our grade:** B+ for the matchup.

## What The Lions Are Wearing (Confirmed)

Honolulu blue jersey, the silver helmet with the leaping lion, and silver pants with blue socks, the 1934 formula. The Lions posted it on Thanksgiving morning under the line "Bringing out the fine silver for Thanksgiving."

## What The Lions Have Worn On Thanksgiving

Detroit does not have one fixed Thanksgiving uniform, which is why people search this question every November.

- **2025 vs. Green Bay: throwbacks.** The logo-free silver helmet, a plain Honolulu blue jersey and silver pants, per [WILX](https://www.wilx.com/2025/11/26/lions-return-classic-throwback-look-thanksgiving-game/). Green Bay won 31-24.
- **2024 vs. Chicago: all blue.** Honolulu blue jersey and blue pants, and Detroit won 23-20, ending a seven-game Thanksgiving losing streak. That is the last time these two teams met on the holiday.
- **2023 vs. Green Bay: throwbacks.** The same logo-free throwback set.
- **The 2000s:** Detroit wore throwbacks often on the holiday in that decade.

So the last time the Bears came to Detroit on Thanksgiving, the Lions were in blue, and that is our call again.

## Our Grade

**B+.** Honolulu blue against navy and orange on white is a good-looking game: three strong colors and no overlap. What holds it back is that it is two standard uniforms on the one day a year Detroit owns. The Lions have a logo-free throwback that has become a Thanksgiving favorite, and leaving it in the closet on the biggest stage of their season is a miss. If Detroit announces it during game week, this game goes up to an A-.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the Lions wearing on Thanksgiving 2026?**

We expect the Honolulu blue home jersey with the silver helmet against the Bears. Detroit has no throwback on its 2026 calendar.

**Are the Lions wearing throwbacks on Thanksgiving this year?**

Nothing Detroit has published says so. The Lions wore their throwbacks on Thanksgiving in 2023 and 2025, both against Green Bay, and all blue against Chicago in 2024.

**What time is Bears vs. Lions on Thanksgiving?**

Thursday, November 26, at 1:00 p.m. Eastern, noon Central, at Ford Field in Detroit.

**What channel is the Lions Thanksgiving game on?**

CBS.

**What are the Bears wearing on Thanksgiving?**

We expect the white road jersey with the navy helmet, Chicago's standard road look.

**Why do the Lions always play on Thanksgiving?**

Detroit has hosted a Thanksgiving game every year since 1934, except for the World War II years. It is the oldest tradition in the NFL schedule.

**What color are the Lions?**

Honolulu blue and silver. Owner George A. Richards picked blue and silver in 1934 when he moved the team to Detroit, and our [Honolulu blue explainer](/stories/why-do-the-lions-wear-honolulu-blue) has the full story.

**When did the Lions and Bears last play on Thanksgiving?**

2024, a 23-20 Lions win in Detroit with the Lions in all blue.

**What is the Lions' Rivalries uniform?**

Rivalries Silver: a pale silver jersey with DETROIT across the chest, carbon-fiber numbers and an electric blue helmet. It was worn once, against Minnesota on November 1.

**Do NFL players wear a patch on Thanksgiving?**

Yes. Both the Lions and Bears wear the [John Madden Thanksgiving patch](/stories/john-madden-thanksgiving-patch-2026), a round silhouette of Madden in his headset on the left chest, confirmed by the NFL on November 19. It has been worn every Thanksgiving since 2023.
