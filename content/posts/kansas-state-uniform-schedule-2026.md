K-State is one of the most disciplined uniform programs in the Power Four. Athletic director Gene Taylor said in June that he is "a proponent of being true to your colors" and that new football designs take a couple of years to develop, so we are not expecting a brand-new alternate in 2026. What the Wildcats do have is a white helmet in the closet. They wore it with purple jerseys and white pants to beat Rutgers 44-41 in the Rate Bowl in December 2024, and they wore all white, helmet included, at home against North Dakota to open 2025, the first white-jersey home game at Bill Snyder Family Stadium since 1991. The one visible change this fall is the Purple Wave Auction patch, part of the first season Division I allows sponsor patches, which appears on both the home and road jerseys. Big 12 home teams wear color and visitors wear white, which makes the road dates easy to call.

**September 5 vs. Nicholls.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **silver helmet, purple jersey, silver pants**, the standard home set for Collin Klein's debut on a Stripe Out night at Bill Snyder Family Stadium. Nicholls was in white with red numerals. K-State won 71-3, its most points since 2000, and Avery Johnson became the program's career leader in touchdown passes on the opening drive.

**September 12 vs. Washington State.** ★ Confirmed from game photos: **silver helmet, purple jersey, silver pants** again, this time for Fort Riley Day and an 11 a.m. kickoff. Washington State went all white, crimson Coug on a white shell, which made for a clean purple-against-white look. K-State won 34-7.

**September 19 vs. Tulane.** Purple expected. An 11 a.m. Central kickoff on ESPN2 to close non-conference play, on a Harley Day, All-Star Band and University Family Weekend date. This is the only home date left before Big 12 play, and the last time Tulane came to Manhattan in 2022 was the day K-State debuted its white Walking Willie helmet, so we are watching the equipment room's social accounts this week.

**September 26 at Cincinnati.** White expected. The Big 12 opener at Nippert Stadium kicks off at 6 p.m. Central, 7 p.m. Eastern, and the visitor wears white.

**October 3.** Bye.

**October 10 vs. Houston.** Purple expected. The Celebrate Ag game in Manhattan, back from the off week.

**October 17 vs. Kansas.** Purple expected. The Sunflower Showdown is also Homecoming, and Kansas will be in white, so this should be the purple-and-silver look against the Jayhawks' white road set. Homecoming is the traditional slot for a surprise, which is why we flag it.

**October 24 at Arizona State.** White expected. Arizona State wears color at home in Tempe, so K-State is in white.

**October 31 at Colorado.** White expected. Halloween at Folsom Field, where Colorado is expected in black, which sets up white against black for the Wildcats.

**November 7 vs. Oklahoma State.** Purple expected. The Cowboys visit in white, and this is the 17th straight season the two schools have met.

**November 14 at TCU.** White expected. TCU is expected in its own purple at Amon G. Carter Stadium, so this is the purple-on-purple matchup where the road white matters most.

**November 21 vs. Arizona.** Purple expected. The final home game of the regular season.

**November 28 at Iowa State.** White expected. Farmageddon at Jack Trice Stadium closes the regular season, the same matchup that opened 2025 in Dublin.

## The Bottom Line

Kansas State is the rare program where the expected uniform is usually the right one: silver helmet, purple jersey and silver pants at home, white on the road, and both 2026 games so far have followed that script exactly. The wild card is the white helmet, which has shown up in a bowl game and a season opener in the last two years, and Tulane week and Homecoming against Kansas are the two home dates where a change would make the most sense. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Kansas State wearing this week?**

Kansas State hosts Tulane on Saturday, September 19, at 11 a.m. Central, and the purple home jersey with silver helmet and silver pants is expected. The Wildcats have worn that exact combination in both games this season. We will star the grid cell once the uniform is confirmed.

**What are Kansas State's football uniforms for 2026?**

A purple home jersey with white numerals and white shoulder stripes, a white road jersey with purple numerals and purple shoulder stripes, and silver pants with a purple and white side stripe. The primary helmet is silver with an oversized purple Powercat, a purple and white center stripe and WILDCATS across the front bumper. A white helmet and white pants are available as alternates, and a Purple Wave Auction sponsor patch is on the jerseys this season.

**Does Kansas State have a new alternate uniform in 2026?**

Not that has been announced. Athletic director Gene Taylor said in June 2026 that new football designs take a couple of years to develop and that he prefers staying true to the school's colors. The white helmet K-State wore in the 2024 Rate Bowl and the 2025 opener is the most likely change if one comes.

**What did Kansas State wear against Washington State?**

Silver helmets, purple jerseys and silver pants, the standard home set, on Fort Riley Day in Manhattan on September 12. Washington State wore all white. Kansas State won 34-7.

**Has Kansas State ever worn white at home?**

Yes. K-State opened its 2025 home schedule against North Dakota in all white, helmet included, ending a run of 219 straight home games in purple that dated to 1991. The school said part of the reason was logistics, since the purple set had just made the trip to and from Dublin for the opener against Iowa State.

**How many games does Kansas State play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven at Bill Snyder Family Stadium and five on the road in the Big 12, with a bye on October 3. The grid above lists every game with the expected uniform.
