YES Network has always been one of the stronger regional sports networks when it comes to on-screen graphics, and the 2026 Nets scorebug keeps that standard going. The design is sharp, modern, and built around contrast in a way that makes the score easy to read from across the room. For a team that leans heavily into a black and white color scheme, that kind of readability is not always easy to pull off in a scorebug.

The layout is tight and well-organized. Team logos and abbreviations are positioned cleanly, with the score displayed prominently in the center. The game clock and quarter information are easy to find without having to scan around the bug looking for them. The black and white palette gives the whole thing a sleek, premium feel that fits the Nets brand well. There is enough contrast between the elements that nothing gets lost, which is the biggest challenge when you are working with a monochrome color scheme.

YES Network clearly puts effort into their graphics package beyond just the scorebug. The transitions, lower thirds, and stat overlays all feel cohesive with the scorebug design. That kind of consistency across an entire broadcast is something a lot of regional networks do not prioritize, and it makes a noticeable difference when you are watching a full game.

The only thing holding this back from a higher grade is the limited color palette. The black and white works, but it can feel a little flat compared to scorebugs from teams with more vibrant colors. That is more of a branding issue than a design issue, though. YES Network is working with what the Nets give them, and they are doing it well.

## Our Grade: A-

The Nets YES Network scorebug is one of the cleanest in the NBA for 2026. The contrast is strong, the layout is modern, and YES Network continues to put out some of the best regional broadcast graphics in sports.
