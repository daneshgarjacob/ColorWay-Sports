New Era's 2026 NFL Sideline collection is out, and the headline is that every team got the same treatment this year. One silhouette, the 9SEVENTY Stretch-Snap with the notched brim, one construction, and one idea: a crown in the team's primary color with a raised rubberized logo on the front and a thin sliver of a second color tucked under the brim.

It is a real step up from the busy embroidered sideline caps of the last few years. It is also a treatment that exposes a team's logo completely, because when everything else is stripped away the only thing left to look at is the mark on the front. Some teams have a mark built for that. Some very much do not.

We graded all 32, worst to first. One rule decided most of this list, and you will see it coming by about the fifth hat.

## The Rule That Decides This Whole Ranking

Tonal is the defining choice of the 2026 collection, meaning the logo is molded in the same color as the crown it sits on, with a thin keyline around the edge for definition. When a team's logo is filled in its own crown color, it disappears. When it is given a genuinely different color, it sings.

That is nearly the entire ranking in one sentence. The bottom of this list is full of teams whose logos were painted the same shade as their hats, and the top is full of teams who used white, or gold, or a real second color. A few teams beat the pattern in both directions, and we will point those out as they come.

Counting down from 32.

## The Bottom Line

The 2026 Sideline collection is the best-constructed set New Era has made for the NFL in years, and the tonal-with-a-keyline formula genuinely looks premium in person. The problem is that it was applied to all 32 teams as a rule rather than a choice.

For teams whose logo is a bold silhouette or already an outline, the Colts, the Raiders, the Cowboys, it costs nothing. For teams whose logo lives on internal contrast, the Texans, the Falcons, the Cardinals, the Buccaneers, it removes the entire thing. The teams at the top of this list are almost all teams that broke the rule. The Rams went gold, the Jets went white, the Chiefs went white, Philadelphia kept its logo in full color, and Denver changed its crown color entirely.

The lesson is the same one uniform designers keep relearning. A house style is only a good idea if you are willing to break it for the teams it does not fit.

**What is the best NFL sideline hat for 2026?**

The Los Angeles Rams. We grade it an A and rank it first out of all 32. A royal blue crown with the LA ram horn in solid gold, it is the clearest, boldest use of the 2026 template in the league.

**What hat do NFL teams actually wear on the sideline in 2026?**

The New Era 9SEVENTY Stretch-Snap, identifiable by the notched front brim. Every team gets a primary version in team color, a white version, and most get a historic throwback version.

**Why do the 2026 sideline hats all look the same?**

New Era used one template across the league this year: a tonal crown in the team's primary color, a raised rubberized logo, and a contrasting sliver under the brim edge. The teams that stand out are the ones that gave their logo a genuinely different color from the crown.

The Tampa Bay Buccaneers, which we grade a C-. A red flag logo on a red crown leaves the skull and crossed swords almost invisible, and the Dolphins finish just above it for similar reasons.
