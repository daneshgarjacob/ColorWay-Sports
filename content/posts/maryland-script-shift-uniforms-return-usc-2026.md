**Maryland is wearing its Script Shift uniforms at USC.** The Terps announced on Wednesday, November 18, that the limited-edition Under Armour set returns for **Saturday, November 21 at No. 18 USC** in the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum (6:15 p.m. Eastern, 3:15 p.m. Pacific, on BTN).

It is only the second time Maryland football has worn it. The first was **November 9, 2024, at No. 1 Oregon** in Autzen Stadium.

## Quick Answers

- **What is Maryland wearing at USC?** The Script Shift set: white helmet with the Terps script, white jersey, white pants, all trimmed in an iridescent blend of gold, black and red.
- **What are Script Shift uniforms?** A limited-edition Under Armour uniform with an icy white base. The numbers, the Terps chest script and the logos are iridescent, so they shift between gold, black and red as the light hits them.
- **When did Maryland wear them before?** Once, at No. 1 Oregon on November 9, 2024. Oregon won 39-18.
- **Do other Maryland teams wear Script Shift?** Yes. Men's and women's basketball wore their own version in February and March 2026.
- **What is USC wearing?** Expected: cardinal at home, for Senior Day at the Coliseum.

## Every Detail Of The Script Shift Uniform

From Maryland's announcements in 2024 and this week, and the photos and video the team posted:

- **Helmet:** All white with a white facemask. The cursive "Terps" script on the side is outlined in black and filled with the iridescent finish, which reads gold in the team's 2024 helmet photo.
- **Jersey:** Icy white. "Terps" is spelled out across the chest in script and the numbers are iridescent. The Under Armour and Big Ten logos on the jersey match the iridescent marks. Maryland says the white base and white trim are meant to create "a minimalist, stark silhouette."
- **Pants:** White, with the Maryland M-Bar logo and the Under Armour logo in the same iridescent blend.
- **Iridescence:** Maryland says the finish is "owned exclusively by the University" and is an uninterrupted blend of gold, black and red. The 2024 release described it as gold and red. This week's release adds black.
- **Gloves and cleats (2024):** Flooded red, with the same color-shifting Under Armour logo and a tonal terrapin shell print. The 2024 game was the debut of the UA Blur Pro, Spotlight Pro and Spotlight Pro Mid cleats.

The 2024 release also said Maryland's four-quadrant flag was "reimagined and reproportioned" in the design, and that the numerals and marks nod to historical uniforms. Maryland has not said whether the gloves and cleats for USC match the 2024 versions.

In 2024 Maryland did not call it Script Shift. The release called it an exclusive special edition, and the team's posts called it Script Terps. The Script Shift name arrived with the basketball version in February 2026, and Maryland now uses it for the football set too.

## Script Shift In Basketball

Maryland women's basketball wore the Script Shift uniforms first, against Purdue on February 22, 2026, and won 99-66 on Senior Day. The men wore them against Rutgers on March 1 and lost 69-65. The basketball version uses the same icy white base and the same gold, black and red iridescence, with "Maryland" across the chest instead of "Terps," and the M-Bar on the shorts. Maryland sold Script Shift shirts, pullovers and sweatshirts through its team shop.

## Why Maryland Wears It On The Road

Both football games in Script Shift have been road trips to the West Coast, to two of the most famous stadiums in the sport: Autzen in 2024 and the Coliseum this week. Maryland's release leans on Under Armour, calling Maryland "the original Under Armour school." UA founder Kevin Plank was a special teams captain for the Terps and graduated in 1996.

At USC it is a road game with a white jersey either way. The difference from Maryland's normal road set is the helmet: Maryland has worn a red script helmet with the white jersey and red pants on the road this season. Saturday is white from top to bottom.
