**Cold Front comes out once this season, and this is the day.** The Bills wear their all-white Rivalries uniform when they host the Miami Dolphins on **Sunday, November 22, at 1:00 p.m. Eastern on CBS**, Buffalo's Salute to Service game. That is a **white helmet with a metal-flake finish** and a cracked-ice center stripe, a **white jersey** with frosted silver numbers outlined in royal blue, and **white pants**. Buffalo put this game on its home schedule as "Rivalries" before the season started, and it is the only Cold Front date in 2026.

**Update, November 22, final:** the Bills beat the Dolphins 26-9 in Cold Front. ESPN's game photos match the team's: all white for Buffalo, aqua over aqua under the white helmet for Miami. We keep our B- for the uniform.

**Update, November 22:** it is on the field. The Bills' own pregame and first-half photos show the full set as described below, the metal-flake white helmet, white BUFFALO jersey and white pants, and Miami in the **white helmet, aqua jersey and aqua pants**.

## What The Bills Are Wearing Against The Dolphins

Cold Front is Buffalo's entry in the NFL and Nike Rivalries program, from the first class of 2025. It debuted on Sunday Night Football against the Patriots on October 5, 2025. Here is every piece, from the team's own description:

- **The helmet.** The same white shell as Buffalo's regular helmet, with small metal flakes through the paint so it sparkles. The center stripe was redesigned to look like cracked ice, and the charging buffalo is filled in a frosty metallic silver with a bright royal blue outline.
- **The jersey.** All white, with BUFFALO across the chest instead of BILLS. The shoulders have a cracked-ice texture with a speckled white pattern, meant to look like fresh snow on a bison's fur.
- **The numbers.** The same frosty metallic fill as the helmet logo, with a royal blue border so they read on TV.
- **The sleeves.** A shiny chrome charging buffalo, the first time the logo has been stitched onto a Bills sleeve.
- **White pants.**

The design team took every bit of red off the uniform on purpose. Red reads as warm, and the whole idea was cold.

## What The Dolphins Are Wearing

With the home team in white, Miami is in **aqua** on the road, as it was at the Jets in Week 7, and the Dolphins' own game photos show **aqua pants** with it under the white helmet. Aqua against an all-white team in late November in Buffalo is the warm-weather team against the cold-weather team, which is the whole idea of this uniform.

## Is Cold Front Any Good?

It's good with a ceiling. The concept is the best in the Rivalries program: a team that plays through lake-effect snow, dressed like the weather. The metal-flake helmet looks great up close, the chrome sleeve buffalo is a smart detail, and pulling the red out entirely took discipline.

It sits at a **B-** because of what happens on a clear afternoon. Silver numbers on a white jersey have very little contrast, and from a distance the royal blue outline does most of the work. The cracked-ice texture looks better in a photo shoot than from the upper deck. It's fifth of sixteen in our [ranking of every NFL Rivalries uniform](/stories/nfl-rivalries-uniforms-2026-ranked), and fourth of five in [every Bills jersey, ranked](/stories/bills-jerseys-2026-ranked).

The matchup gets an **A-**. All white against aqua is one of the brightest color pairings the league can put on a field, and if it snows, Cold Front finally gets the conditions it was made for.

## Salute To Service And Cold Front

Most teams wear their regular uniforms for Salute to Service and keep the tribute on sideline gear, helmet decals and the field. Buffalo and Baltimore are the two clubs that put an announced alternate on their Salute to Service date this year, the Ravens with Purple Rising on November 5 and the Bills with Cold Front here. Every team's date is in our [2026 Salute to Service schedule](/stories/nfl-salute-to-service-2026-schedule).
