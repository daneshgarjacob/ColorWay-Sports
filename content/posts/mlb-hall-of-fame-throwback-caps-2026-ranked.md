For one weekend in late July, every team in baseball reaches into its own closet and pulls out a cap almost nobody remembers. It is the best league-wide event of the season, and for [2026 Hall of Fame Weekend](/stories/mlb-throwback-caps-hall-of-fame-weekend-2026) all 30 clubs wore a vintage throwback lid, most of them a forgotten regular-season design, a couple of old spring training caps, and one dusted-off primary logo. Every one of them even got a green underbrim, the kind of small detail that makes a big leaguer feel like a big leaguer.

So we did the thing nobody else does with them: we graded all 30. This is every 2026 Hall of Fame Weekend throwback cap ranked from worst to best, judged on color, logo, how deep the cut is, and how good it actually looks on the field. Number one is at the bottom.

Texas dug up its brief early-2000s flirtation with black, a royal-blue crown with a black brim and a white T. Even Chris Creamer called the choice head-scratching, and it is easy to see why: there is nothing wrong with it, but there is nothing especially Texan about it either. Of everything on this board, this is the cap that most reads like a forgettable in-between era rather than a piece of history worth reviving.

## The Bottom Line on the 2026 Hall of Fame Weekend Caps

The best caps of the weekend all had one thing in common: they were brave. The Angels' halo, the Pirates' gold, the Brewers' "Motre Bame," and the Mariners' teal all reached for something specific and a little strange, and that is what a throwback weekend is for. The caps that landed at the bottom were not ugly so much as safe, either nearly identical to a current cap or a black-era design with no real story behind it. Green underbrims on all 30 were the perfect finishing touch, and honestly, we would not mind seeing this event grow into full throwback uniforms next year.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why did every MLB team wear throwback caps this weekend?**
All 30 teams wore vintage throwback caps to mark [2026 Hall of Fame Weekend](/stories/mlb-throwback-caps-hall-of-fame-weekend-2026), the late-July stretch when the newest class is inducted into the National Baseball Hall of Fame in Cooperstown. The caps were worn across games from Friday, July 24 through Sunday, July 26.

**What was the best Hall of Fame Weekend cap?**
Our top pick was the Los Angeles Angels' original 1960s cap, with the red cursive LA and a gold halo ringing the top of the crown. The halo is a detail no other team can match, and it earned our only A+.

**What was the worst throwback cap?**
The Texas Rangers' early-2000s cap ranked last. It celebrates a brief, forgettable flirtation with black and reads more like an in-between era than a piece of team history worth reviving.

**Do the 2026 Hall of Fame caps have green underbrims?**
Yes. Every one of the 30 caps was finished with a green underbrim, a vintage touch that ties the whole program together.

**Did the teams wear throwback jerseys too?**
No. For 2026 the throwbacks were on the caps only. Each team paired its vintage cap with one of its normal 2026 jerseys, including City Connects and alternates, so the caps were the shared thread across the league.

**When is Hall of Fame Weekend in 2026?**
The 2026 induction weekend ran Friday, July 24 through Sunday, July 26, with the throwback caps worn all three days.

**Where can I buy the MLB Hall of Fame Weekend caps?**
The 2026 Hall of Fame Weekend and Cooperstown Collection caps are sold for all 30 teams through the usual retailers, so you can pick up your team's vintage design.
