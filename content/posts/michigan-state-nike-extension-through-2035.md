**Michigan State is staying with Nike through June 30, 2035.** The school and Nike announced an **eight-year extension** of their all-sports partnership on Monday, November 23, 2026. The new term runs from **July 1, 2027 to June 30, 2035**, according to Nike's own release and Michigan State Athletics. The money was not disclosed. What was disclosed matters more for anyone who cares about uniforms: **custom football and basketball alternate uniforms** are written into the deal.

Michigan State Athletics posted the news at 1 p.m. Eastern, saying the two have partnered "for 26 years." The fan account [@MSU_Uniforms](https://x.com/MSU_Uniforms) was first to put a length on it, posting "an additional 8 years" about 25 minutes later. Nike's release confirms that figure.

## Quick Answers

- **Is Michigan State staying with Nike?** Yes. An eight-year extension was announced November 23, 2026.
- **How long is the new deal?** Eight years, **July 1, 2027 through June 30, 2035**.
- **How much is it worth?** **Not disclosed.** Athletic director Dan Bartholomae called it "a substantially increased level of support from Nike."
- **How long has MSU been with Nike?** Since **2000**. That is 26 years, and the new deal takes it to 35.
- **What does it mean for uniforms?** The deal includes **custom alternate uniforms for football and basketball** and **Michigan State-specific basketball shoes**.
- **Did the school confirm eight years?** Yes. Nike's release and the school's announcement both give the July 1, 2027 to June 30, 2035 term.

## What Is In The New Michigan State Nike Deal

From Nike's release and Michigan State's announcement, the extension includes:

- **Custom alternate uniforms** for basketball and football.
- **Michigan State-specific basketball footwear.**
- **Access to the Nike Sport Research Lab** for Spartan athletes.
- **Preferred Nike testing partner** status for Michigan State.

It is an all-sports deal, so Nike keeps outfitting every Spartan team, not only football and basketball.

The three statements in the release came from Bartholomae, Nike executive vice president of global sports marketing **Ann Miller**, and basketball coach **Tom Izzo**. Izzo tied the relationship back to the start: he said the original 2000 deal gave his program credibility, and that Nike has become "part of our DNA." The school's post used the same phrase.

## How Much Does Nike Pay Michigan State?

Neither side said. Here is what has been reported about earlier deals, with who reported it:

- **2015:** a 10-year deal worth **$34 million** in total, as reported by The Detroit News at the time.
- **2024:** an extension through 2027. **Stephen Brooks of 247Sports**, citing the contract obtained through a public records request, reported Nike paying **$550,000 for 2023-24 and $700,000 a year for the next three years**, as Sports Illustrated summarized it in July 2024.

Sports Illustrated's Spartans beat writer Jacob Cotsonika wrote on Monday that the new figure is likely higher, and that he has asked for the contract. We will add the number if the school releases it or a named outlet reports it.

For scale, two rivals' Nike deals were made public years ago. **Ohio State** signed a 15-year, **$252 million** extension with Nike in January 2016, running to 2033, as Fox Sports reported from school documents. **Michigan's** Nike deal, which put Wolverines football in Jordan Brand, was reported at **$169 million** when it was announced in 2015, with a term through 2027 and a school option to extend it.

## How Long Has Michigan State Been With Nike?

**Since 2000.** Nike's release calls the two "all-sports partners since 2000," and Sports Illustrated reports that 2000 was the first year the swoosh appeared on Spartan uniforms. Izzo's team won the national championship that spring. Through June 30, 2035, the partnership will have run 35 years.

## Our Take

Michigan State got the right partner and the wrong priority list. Staying with Nike was never in doubt: Izzo's program is built into the brand, and no school walks away from 26 years of that over money it will not even disclose. The part of this deal fans will actually see is the custom alternate clause, and the Nike record here is mixed at best. The best Spartan look of the Nike era is the plainest one, the green jersey and the green helmet. The worst was the year they tried neon.

So spend the custom budget on the past. Michigan State has a deep green-and-white history and has dipped into it once, for a helmet. A full throwback set, helmet to socks, would do more for this program than another black uniform. Black is not a Michigan State color, and four seasons of it have not changed that. In our [Big Ten uniforms ranking](/stories/big-ten-football-uniforms-ranked-2026) we have the current set **15th, a C+**, mostly for the Greek key repeated three times. The new deal gives Michigan State eight years to fix that. It should start with fewer patterns, not more alternates.
