**The Chargers are going powder on powder against the Jets.** Los Angeles hosts the **New York Jets** at **SoFi Stadium** on **Sunday, November 22, at 4:05 p.m. Eastern (1:05 p.m. Pacific) on FOX**, and the team's own game-week post confirms the **powder blue jersey, powder blue pants and white socks**. The Jets wear **White on Green**: the green helmet, white jersey and green pants. Powder blue from the shoulders to the shins is a bold look, and it is also our least favorite Chargers combination.

## Our Take: Our Least Favorite Chargers Combination

Powder on powder is the Chargers combination I like least. The powder blue jersey is the best thing the Chargers own, and it looks its best over white pants, the way they wore it on opening day against Arizona. White pants give the powder blue something to stand out against. With powder blue pants, the whole uniform runs together into one block of color, and the numbers and stripes have to do all the work.

The matchup itself is fine. Powder blue against the Jets' white jersey and green pants is bright, and the two teams look nothing alike, so it will be easy to follow on TV. That is what keeps the grade up.
