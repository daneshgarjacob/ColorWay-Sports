Both of these are the right call. Denver had four alternate games to hand out and it spent two of them on the only two nights the whole country is watching. The Rams game is the first home Sunday night game Denver has had since 2023, and the Christmas date is a rematch of a Divisional Round game that people are still arguing about. If you are going to break out the best thing in the closet, break it out when the audience is biggest.

## The Helmet Is Genuinely Undecided

Here is the part worth being precise about, because it is already getting reported wrong.

The announcement graphic shows two players in Midnight Navy, one in a navy helmet and one in a white helmet. That has been read in a few places as a confirmed split, one helmet per game. It is not. The Broncos' own release says plainly that the team "will announce the corresponding helmet, pant and sock colors ahead of the matchups." Nothing about the helmets has been assigned to a date yet.

What the graphic is actually doing is showing you both options, and both have real precedent:

- **In 2024**, Denver paired the **Summit White helmet** with an all-Midnight Navy uniform and beat the Browns in it.
- **In 2025**, Denver went the other way, pairing its **primary metallic satin navy helmet** with the same all-blue set against the Bengals.

So the team has already run this jersey with each helmet once, and won both times. Denver is 2-0 in Midnight Navy since the Mile High Collection arrived ahead of the 2024 season. Reading the graphic as a schedule is guessing. Reading it as a menu is closer to the truth.

For what it is worth, we think the white helmet is the better of the two. The navy-on-navy version is handsome but it flattens out under stadium lights, and the white shell is what gives the set its contrast and lets the orange do something. If Denver wants the Christmas game to look like an event on Netflix, white is the shell that photographs.

The Midnight Navy set works because it is not a costume. Navy is not a color Denver borrowed for a one-off blackout gimmick, it is a color the team wore as its primary from 1997 through 2011, which covers both Super Bowl wins of the John Elway era and most of what a whole generation of fans grew up on. The jersey has history behind it in a way that most modern alternates do not.

The execution is clean, too. The deep navy base, the orange piping, the 5280 bumper on the helmet, and the number treatment all read from distance, and the set avoids the trap most navy alternates fall into, which is going so dark that the whole uniform becomes a silhouette on television. The orange is doing real work here.

We are holding it at an A- rather than an A for one reason, and it is the same note we would give a lot of the Mile High Collection: the numbers get slightly lost against the navy at speed. That is a legibility problem, not a taste problem, and it is the only thing between this and a flat A.

Denver's navy also has some of the best moments in franchise history attached to it. Peyton Manning threw his record-setting 509th career touchdown pass in navy, and the Broncos beat the Packers on Sunday Night Football in navy during the Super Bowl 50 run.

That is a well-built slate. Two navy games in the biggest windows, two throwback games spread to the other end of the calendar, and no week where the two looks step on each other. Compare it to a team like the [Chiefs, who have no alternate at all in 2026](/stories/chiefs-uniform-schedule-2026), and Denver looks like it actually thought about the calendar.

It also sets up a good week for uniform watchers in Week 3. The Rams are in their white road set that night, which our [Rams 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/rams-uniform-schedule-2026) has them wearing at Denver, so navy against white with the orange trim is a clean matchup rather than a color clash. Week 16 works the same way with the [Bills in their white road set](/stories/bills-uniform-schedule-2026) on Christmas.

## The Bottom Line

Denver got this right. The Midnight Navy is the best jersey the Broncos own that is not a throwback, it has real history rather than manufactured edginess, and the team put it in the two windows where the most people will see it. The only thing left hanging is the helmet, and anyone telling you today which shell goes with which game is reading a graphic, not a release. The Broncos said they will tell us closer to each date, and that is where it stands.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey are the Broncos wearing today?**

Denver's alternates are locked to four dates in 2026: Midnight Navy in Week 3 against the Rams and Week 16 against the Bills, and the Orange Crush throwbacks in Week 6 against the Seahawks and Week 18 against the Chargers. Any other week is the standard rotation, which means Summit White or Sunset Orange at home and the white set on the road.

**What jerseys are the Broncos wearing this week?**

Check the week against the four alternate dates above. If it is not Week 3, 6, 16 or 18, Denver is in its standard home or road set rather than an alternate.

**What uniform did the Broncos wear last week?**

For the uniform we logged in their most recent game, see the [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026), which we update through the week. The dates above are the season plan, and the tracker is the record of what actually went on the field.

**What are the Broncos wearing next week?**

Same rule. Week 3 and Week 16 are Midnight Navy, Week 6 and Week 18 are the Orange Crush throwbacks, and everything else is the standard set.

**When do the Broncos wear the Midnight Navy uniform in 2026?**

Twice. Week 3 against the Los Angeles Rams on Sunday, September 27 at 6:20 p.m. MT on Sunday Night Football, and Week 16 against the Buffalo Bills on Christmas Day, December 25 at 2:30 p.m. MT on Netflix.

**What helmet do the Broncos wear with the Midnight Navy jersey?**

Not announced yet. The Broncos said they will confirm the helmet, pant and sock colors ahead of each game. Denver has worn this jersey with the Summit White helmet in 2024 against the Browns and with the primary metallic satin navy helmet in 2025 against the Bengals, so both are live options and the announcement graphic shows both.

**What uniform are the Broncos wearing on Christmas Day 2026?**

The Midnight Navy jersey, against the Buffalo Bills at 2:30 p.m. MT on Netflix. The helmet has not been confirmed.

**How many alternate uniform games do the Broncos have in 2026?**

Four, which is the maximum the team is allotted. Two are Midnight Navy (Weeks 3 and 16) and two are the Orange Crush throwbacks (Weeks 6 and 18).

**Is Midnight Navy a throwback?**

No. It is a modern alternate from the Mile High Collection, introduced ahead of the 2024 season. It does reference real history, since navy was Denver's primary jersey color from 1997 through 2011, but it is not a reproduction of an old uniform. The Orange Crush set is the actual throwback.
