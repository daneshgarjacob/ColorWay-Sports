**Almost every NFL team wears its color at home. The Dallas Cowboys wear white, and they have since the 1960s.** On Sunday the San Francisco 49ers come to AT&T Stadium in their red 1994 throwbacks, and Dallas is scheduled in white, the same look it has assigned to eight of its nine home games in 2026.

So why do the Cowboys wear white at home? The short answer is Tex Schramm, the team's first president and general manager, who wanted Dallas fans to see the visiting team's colors. The Texas heat is the other half of the story, and a Super Bowl loss in blue gave Dallas one more reason never to change. Here is what the record supports, where the accounts disagree, and what Dallas wears the rest of this season.

## Quick Answers

- **Why do the Cowboys wear white at home?** Tex Schramm wanted home crowds to see the opponent in its own colors instead of the same white-shirted visitors every week. The team's own site leads with that reason.
- **Was it the heat?** Partly. Longtime equipment director Mike McCord said in 2017 that the heat at the Cotton Bowl started it: white stays cooler in a day game, and the visitors are stuck in the dark jersey.
- **When did it start?** Most accounts say 1964, after Dallas wore blue at home for its first four seasons. A few say 1960.
- **Is the blue jersey cursed?** That idea came later, after Dallas lost Super Bowl V in blue. All five Cowboys Super Bowl wins came in white.
- **What do the Cowboys wear at home in 2026?** White eight times in nine home games, counting the Rio de Janeiro game and two all-white Color Rush dates. The exception is the Thanksgiving Classic against the Eagles.
- **Our take (opinion):** white is the right call. The Cowboys' white with the silver-blue pants is one of the best home looks in the sport, and it has nothing to do with superstition.

In the NFL, the home team picks white or its color, and the visitor wears the opposite. We explain how that works in [who decides which uniforms teams wear each game](/stories/who-decides-which-uniforms-teams-wear-each-game). For decades that choice was not really a choice: home teams wore dark, so every visitor at every stadium showed up in white.

Schramm did not want every home game to look the same. If Dallas wore white, the fans at the Cotton Bowl would see the Packers in green, the Redskins in burgundy and the Browns in brown. DallasCowboys.com has described the white jerseys as Schramm's idea and a choice the team kept, not a rule it had to follow.

## Reason Two: The Texas Heat

</div>
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">Dallas in white at home against Arizona on November 1, 2026, via <a href="https://x.com/dallascowboys" target="_blank" rel="noopener">@dallascowboys</a></p>

The other explanation is simpler. Early-season afternoon games at the Cotton Bowl were brutally hot, white reflects sun, and a dark jersey soaks it up. If the home team wears white, the visitors wear the hot one.

Mike McCord, the Cowboys' longtime equipment director, told Fox Sports in 2017 that the heat is what started it. NBC 5 Dallas has listed both reasons. We think both are true: the heat made white practical, and Schramm's idea about showing off the visitors' colors is why Dallas kept it long after the team left the Cotton Bowl.

## Blue At Home, Then White: When It Started

Dallas joined the NFL in 1960 and wore blue at home for its first four seasons. Most accounts, including the Cowboys' own history and the Uni Watch coverage cited by Wikipedia, put the switch to white at home in **1964**, when the NFL began letting home teams choose white. A few accounts say Dallas wore white at home as early as 1960, and some describe the old dark-at-home rule as an unwritten custom rather than a written rule. Either way, the Cowboys have been a white-at-home team for six decades.

## Super Bowl V And The Blue Jersey Jinx

<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/dallascowboys/status/2119537912391880958"></a></blockquote>
</div>
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">Dallas in navy at Indianapolis on November 8, 2026, via <a href="https://x.com/dallascowboys" target="_blank" rel="noopener">@dallascowboys</a></p>

The white came first. The superstition came after.

- **Super Bowl V, January 1971.** Dallas was the designated home team, and the rule then required the home team to wear dark, so the Cowboys wore blue. They lost to the Baltimore Colts, **16-13**, and the talk of a blue jersey jinx started there.
- **Super Bowl XIII, January 1979.** This was the first Super Bowl where the home team could pick its jersey. Dallas was the first team to use the choice and took white. The Cowboys lost to Pittsburgh anyway, **35-31**.
- **NFC Championship, January 1981.** The Philadelphia Eagles had not worn white at home in five years. For the 1980 NFC title game they did, forcing Dallas into blue in Philadelphia, and the Eagles won **20-7**. In public, Schramm shrugged it off. The Eagles' own history says he was furious.

All five Cowboys Super Bowl wins came in white. Through 2013, NBC 5 Dallas counted Dallas at **1-6** in the playoffs in blue. Schramm himself told The New York Times in 1981 that he did not believe in a curse, and the record backs him up on the order of events: the white jerseys were already a Dallas tradition years before anyone called blue unlucky.

## Which Other NFL Teams Wear White At Home?

Dallas does it more than anyone. In 2026 the Cowboys wear white at home eight times, the Miami Dolphins six and the Tampa Bay Buccaneers five, all teams that play early-season games in the heat. We counted all 32 teams in [which NFL teams wear white at home](/stories/nfl-teams-that-wear-white-at-home-2026), and we explained Miami's version in [why the Dolphins wear white at home](/stories/why-do-the-dolphins-wear-white-at-home).

## Our Take

The heat story explains how it started, and the jinx story makes for good television, but the real reason white works for Dallas is that it is the better uniform. The navy jersey is fine. The white jersey, with the blue stripes, the silver star helmet and the silver-blue pants, is one of the most recognizable looks in American sports. Wearing it at home also gives Dallas fans the best version of every visitor: the 49ers in red this Sunday, the Commanders in burgundy, the Giants in blue. Schramm was right, and 60 years later nobody in Dallas wants to change it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why do the Cowboys wear white at home?**

Tex Schramm, the team's first president and general manager, wanted Dallas fans to see visiting teams in their own colors. The heat at the Cotton Bowl was a second reason: white stays cooler in a day game, and it puts the visitor in the hot dark jersey.

**When did the Cowboys start wearing white at home?**

Most accounts say 1964, after Dallas wore blue at home in its first four seasons (1960 to 1963). A few sources say the white started as early as 1960.

**Do the Cowboys ever wear blue at home?**

Rarely. In 2026 the only home game not in white is the Thanksgiving Classic against the Eagles on November 26. The navy primary is worn three times, all on the road.

**Is the Cowboys blue jersey cursed?**

That is the legend, not the history. It started after Dallas lost Super Bowl V to the Colts, 16-13, in blue. The white jerseys came first, all five Super Bowl wins came in white, and Tex Schramm said he did not believe in a curse.

**What are the Cowboys wearing against the 49ers?**

White, per the team's 2026 schedule, with the silver star helmet. The 49ers wear their red 1994 throwback. The game is Sunday, November 15, at 4:25 p.m. ET on FOX.

**How many home games do the Cowboys wear white in 2026?**

Eight of nine: six in the standard white jersey (counting the Rio de Janeiro game against Baltimore) and two in the all-white Arctic White Color Rush. The ninth is the Thanksgiving Classic.

**Who decides which team wears white in the NFL?**

The home team. It chooses white or its team color, and the visitor wears the opposite. That is why Dallas wearing white puts the visitors in color.

**Did the Cowboys win their Super Bowls in white?**

Yes. All five Cowboys Super Bowl wins (VI, XII, XXVII, XXVIII and XXX) came in white jerseys.

**What color are the Cowboys' pants?**

With the white home jersey, Dallas usually wears its metallic silver-blue pants. We break the pants down in [what color are the Cowboys' pants](/stories/what-color-are-the-cowboys-pants).

**What other NFL teams wear white at home?**

In 2026, 15 teams wear white for at least one home game. The Cowboys lead with eight, then the Dolphins with six and the Buccaneers with five.
