**The 49ers wore scarlet and gold in Mexico City, and the Vikings wore white.** San Francisco was the designated home team against the Minnesota Vikings at **Estadio Banorte**, the stadium most fans still call Estadio Azteca, on **Sunday night, November 22**, and won **35-10**. The 49ers wore the **gold helmet, scarlet jersey and gold pants**, the look their own 2026 schedule had listed since July, with the **80th anniversary patch**. Minnesota wore the **purple helmet, white jersey and white pants**. Neither team brought an alternate or a throwback. The one thing made for the game was a **Mexico City patch** on both teams' jerseys.

## Quick Answers

- **What did the 49ers wear in Mexico City?** The gold helmet, the scarlet home jersey with white numbers and gold pants, with the 80th anniversary patch.
- **What did the Vikings wear in Mexico City?** The purple helmet, the white road jersey with purple numbers and white pants.
- **Did either team wear a special uniform?** No. Both wore their standard sets.
- **Was there a Mexico City patch?** Yes. Both teams wore a small shield-shaped patch on the chest: the NFL shield on top, "MEXICO CITY" on a blue band and a green, white and red bottom like the Mexican flag.
- **Why were the 49ers in red at a neutral site?** San Francisco was the designated home team, so the game counted as one of its home dates.
- **Final:** 49ers 35, Vikings 10, Sunday, November 22, on NBC.
- **Our grade:** **B** for the matchup. Scarlet and gold against purple and white is a clean, classic read with nothing new in it.

## What The 49ers Wore

San Francisco published every jersey for 2026 on 49ers.com in July, and Week 11 read "vs. Minnesota Vikings (Sunday Night Football in Mexico City, Mexico) — Red Primary." That is what it wore, confirmed in ESPN's game photos and the team's own:

- **Helmet:** **gold**, with the red SF oval.
- **Jersey:** **scarlet**, with **white numbers** and three white sleeve stripes.
- **Pants:** **gold**, the same pants the 49ers have worn with scarlet in every home game this season.
- **Patches:** the **80th anniversary patch** and the **Mexico City game patch** on the chest.

## What The Vikings Wore

Minnesota publishes a look for every home game but not for road games, and this one counted as a road game. It wore the standard away set, the same one from its 30-10 win at Green Bay the week before:

- **Helmet:** **purple**, with the white and gold horn.
- **Jersey:** **white**, with **purple numbers**.
- **Pants:** **white**, with purple and gold stripes. Not the purple pants Minnesota wore with white at Chicago in Week 2 and at New Orleans in Week 5.
- **Patch:** the same **Mexico City game patch** on the chest.

## Was There A Special Look For Mexico City?

No alternate, no throwback and no Spanish nameplates. The only addition was the game patch. From the teams' photos it is a small **shield**: the **NFL shield** at the top, **"MEXICO CITY"** in white letters on a **blue** band, and a bottom split into **green, white and red**. Neither team nor the league published anything about it before the game, so that is all we can confirm.

A patch for a Mexico City game is not new. In the first one, in 2005, both teams wore a "MEXICO" game patch on the chest, according to the Cardinals' own photo archive.

## How The Game Went

San Francisco led 28-10 at halftime. **Brock Purdy** threw two touchdown passes to **George Kittle** and one to **Mike Evans**, **Ji'Ayir Brown** returned an interception 18 yards for a touchdown, and **Christian McCaffrey** ran for the last score in the fourth quarter. Minnesota scored on an Aaron Jones Sr. touchdown run and a Will Reichard field goal.

A few things stand out:

- **The 49ers have worn three different looks there.** Red over gold as the visitor in 2005, white over gold as the visitor in 2022, and scarlet over gold as the home team this year. They are 2-1 in Mexico City.
- **Only one home team has worn white.** The Cardinals went all white in 2005. Every home team since has worn its color.
- **The best-looking Mexico City game is still 2019.** The Chargers wore powder blue against the Chiefs' white and red, in the first season powder blue was the Chargers' main home jersey.
- **The Raiders hosted twice,** in black both times.

A game between the Chiefs and Rams was also planned for Mexico City in 2018, but the league moved it to the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum because of the condition of the Estadio Azteca field.

## Was It A Good Uniform Matchup?

It was solid. Scarlet and gold against white and purple is two classic color schemes with no clash, and the 49ers' red looked great in a full stadium at night. We grade both jerseys a **B+** on their own, in [every 49ers jersey ranked](/stories/49ers-jerseys-2026-ranked) and [every Vikings jersey ranked](/stories/vikings-jerseys-2026-ranked). Nothing about the matchup was new, though. Two standard sets for one of the biggest stages of the season leaves the grade at a **B**.

Purple pants would have helped. White over purple gives the Vikings' side of the field more color than white on white.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What did the 49ers wear against the Vikings in Mexico City?**

The gold helmet, the scarlet home jersey and gold pants, with the 80th anniversary patch and the Mexico City game patch. It was the look the 49ers put on this game in their 2026 uniform schedule.

**What did the Vikings wear against the 49ers in Mexico City?**

The purple helmet, the white road jersey and white pants, with the Mexico City game patch.

**Did the 49ers or Vikings wear special uniforms in Mexico City?**

No. Both teams wore their standard uniforms. The only addition was a Mexico City patch on the chest.

**What does the NFL Mexico City patch look like?**

A small shield with the NFL shield on top, "MEXICO CITY" on a blue band and a green, white and red bottom like the Mexican flag.

**Who was the home team for 49ers vs. Vikings in Mexico City?**

The San Francisco 49ers. That's why they wore their scarlet home jersey and the Vikings wore white.

**Who won 49ers vs. Vikings in Mexico City?**

The 49ers, 35-10, on Sunday, November 22, 2026, on NBC's Sunday Night Football.

**What stadium was the Mexico City game in?**

Estadio Banorte, the current name of Estadio Azteca, in Mexico City.

**Have the Vikings ever played in Mexico City before?**

No. This was the Vikings' first game in Mexico.

**How many times have the 49ers played in Mexico City?**

Three regular-season games: 2005 and 2022 as the visitor against Arizona, and 2026 as the home team against Minnesota. They are 2-1.

**How many NFL games have been played in Mexico City?**

Six regular-season games: 2005, 2016, 2017, 2019, 2022 and 2026. A 2018 game was moved to Los Angeles because of the field.

**What did the 49ers wear in Mexico City in 2022?**

The gold helmet, white jersey and gold pants. They beat the Cardinals 38-10. Arizona wore red jerseys with white helmets and white pants.

**What did the Chargers wear in Mexico City?**

Powder blue jerseys with white helmets and white pants against the Chiefs in 2019. Kansas City wore white jerseys with red helmets and red pants and won 24-17.

**What did the Raiders wear in Mexico City?**

Silver helmets, black jerseys and silver pants, both in 2016 against Houston and in 2017 against New England.

**When do the 49ers wear an alternate next?**

Week 14 against the Rams in the black Rivalries uniform, then the white 1994 throwback at the Chargers in Week 15 and the red 1994 throwback against the Eagles in Week 17.
