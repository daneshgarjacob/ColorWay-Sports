**The 49ers are the home team in Mexico City, and they're wearing red.** San Francisco hosts the Minnesota Vikings at **Estadio Banorte**, the stadium most fans still call Estadio Azteca, on **Sunday, November 22, at 8:20 p.m. Eastern (7:20 p.m. in Mexico City) on NBC**. The 49ers' own 2026 uniform schedule has the **scarlet** home jersey for this game, under the **gold helmet** and with the **80th anniversary patch** on the chest. Minnesota is expected in its **white** road jersey under the **purple helmet**.

## Quick Answers

- **What are the 49ers wearing in Mexico City?** The scarlet home jersey, from the team's own 2026 schedule, with the gold helmet and the 80th anniversary patch.
- **What are the Vikings wearing in Mexico City?** White is expected. Minnesota hasn't announced its look for this game.
- **Why are the 49ers in red at a neutral site?** San Francisco is the designated home team. The game counts as one of its home dates.
- **When?** Sunday, November 22, 8:20 p.m. ET (7:20 p.m. local) on NBC, at Estadio Banorte in Mexico City.
- **Is there a special Mexico City uniform or patch?** Neither team has announced one as of Monday, November 16.
- **Our grade:** B for the matchup. Scarlet and gold against purple and white is a clean, classic read with nothing new in it.

## What The 49ers Are Wearing

San Francisco published every jersey for 2026 on 49ers.com in July, and Week 11 reads "vs. Minnesota Vikings (Sunday Night Football in Mexico City, Mexico) — Red Primary." That is the **scarlet** home jersey with white numbers and the three white sleeve stripes, under the **gold helmet** with the SF oval.

Every 49ers jersey this season carries the **80th anniversary patch**, which the team announced on April 9: a white "80" with black and gold trim, a red "YEARS" banner inside the zero, and the SF oval over "EST. 1946." Our full breakdown is in [the 49ers 80th anniversary patch](/stories/49ers-80th-anniversary-patch-2026).

The pants haven't been announced. San Francisco has worn **gold pants** with the scarlet jersey in all five home games this season, against Miami, Arizona, Denver, Washington and Las Vegas. Gold is the likely call again.

This is a change from last week. The 49ers wore the red 1994 throwback in their 46-40 win at Dallas, and we covered that in [49ers wear the red 1994 throwback at Dallas](/stories/49ers-1994-throwback-uniform-cowboys-2026). Mexico City is back to the modern scarlet set.

## What The Vikings Are Wearing

Minnesota published a jersey and pants combination for every home game in 2026, but not for its road games, and this one counts as a road game. We expect the **white road jersey** under the **purple helmet**, the standard away look and the one the Vikings have worn on most trips this season.

The pants are the open question. Minnesota wore white with **purple pants** at Chicago in Week 2 and at New Orleans in Week 5, and white with **white pants** in its 30-10 win at Green Bay on Sunday. Either one is possible in Mexico City. See the [Vikings 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/vikings-uniform-schedule-2026).

## Is There A Special Look For Mexico City?

Not that either team has announced. The 49ers' Mexico City events, including the Faithful Fan Rally and gameday watch party at their La Casa de Los Fieles fan house, haven't come with a uniform change, and the Vikings haven't said anything about the trip's uniforms. No Spanish nameplates, no game patch and no alternate jersey have been shown by either team as of Monday. If the league or either team posts one this week, we'll add it here and in the [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026).

## The 49ers And Vikings In Mexico City

This is the first time the Vikings have played in Mexico. It's the 49ers' third regular-season game in Mexico City and their first as the home team. They were the visitors against Arizona in 2005, the NFL's first regular-season game in Mexico City, and again in 2022, a 38-10 win at Estadio Azteca on Monday night. San Francisco being the host is why this one is scarlet.

## Is It A Good Uniform Matchup?

It's solid. Scarlet and gold against white and purple is two classic color schemes with no clash, and the 49ers' red will look great in a full Azteca at night. We grade both jerseys a **B+** on their own, in [every 49ers jersey ranked](/stories/49ers-jerseys-2026-ranked) and [every Vikings jersey ranked](/stories/vikings-jerseys-2026-ranked). Nothing about the matchup is new, though. Two standard sets for one of the biggest stages of the season leaves the grade at a **B**.

Purple pants would help. White over purple gives the Vikings' side of the field more color than white on white.
