**The Red Deer Rebels went back to the beginning, and they are not going back.** The WHL team wore a recreation of its **original 1992 sweater** for Retro Night on **Friday, November 20**, at the Marchant Crane Centrium: **black, white and grey**, with the **original Rebels script** and the **original skate blade logo**. Red Deer beat the **Lethbridge Hurricanes 6-2** in it. The team calls it a **second alternate**, and it says the look is in the rotation **permanently**: "Black and grey… here to stay."

## Quick Answers

- **What is the Red Deer Rebels retro jersey?** A recreation of the sweater the Rebels wore in their **first five seasons, 1992-93 through 1996-97**, with the original Rebels script and skate blade logo.
- **What colors is it?** **Black, white and grey.** No burgundy, which is in the current sweaters.
- **Why now?** It is the franchise's **35th WHL season**. The Rebels entered the league as an expansion team in 1992-93.
- **When did they wear it?** Retro Night, Friday, November 20, 2026, against Lethbridge. **Red Deer won 6-2.**
- **Is it a one-off?** No. The team says it is "(permanently) re-adding a classic look to our jersey palette," and on November 21 it posted that "the throwback threads will be back again soon." No next date has been announced.
- **Can you buy one?** Replicas went on sale on game night at the team store in the arena, along with retro shirts and hats. The team has not published a price.
- **Are the game-worn sweaters being auctioned?** The team has not announced an auction.
- **Our grade:** **B+.**

## What The Retro Sweater Looks Like

From the team's game photos against Lethbridge:

- **Sweater:** **black**.
- **Crest:** **"Rebels"** in the original script, in **white**, over a **skate blade**, with a **silver star** beside it and **RED DEER** underneath.
- **Stripes:** **white and grey** bands on the sleeves and the hem.
- **Pants and socks:** **black** pants, with **black, white and grey** striped socks.
- **Helmet:** **black**.

"Rebels fans have been asking a long time to see these jerseys again," Rebels vice president of business development **Dean Williams** said in the team's announcement. The team's November 14 post set it up:

The retro drops the burgundy entirely and swaps the skull for a script wordmark. It is the cleaner of the two by a distance.

## The Rebels' First Season, 1992-93

The team's own history says the Rebels "entered the Western Hockey League in 1992-93 as an expansion team," owned by brothers **Terry and Wayne Simpson**. **Brent Sutter** bought the club in May 1999 and led it to the **2001 Memorial Cup**. Count 1992-93 as season one and 2026-27 is season 35. The black, white and grey look from that first season lasted through 1996-97, five seasons, before the bull skull era began.

## The Game: Rebels 6, Hurricanes 2

The team's recap opens with Retro Night looking good "not just because of the new (or old) jerseys." Lethbridge tied the game twice in the first two periods before **Cole Temple** scored a shorthanded goal early in the third to put Red Deer ahead for good. **Poul Andersen** had a **hat trick**, and Jake Banicevic and Grayden Peterson also scored. Red Deer outshot Lethbridge 42-21 in front of **3,455** at the Centrium. The Hurricanes wore white.

## Our Grade: B+

This is the best sweater the Rebels own. Black, white and grey with a script crest is a clean, confident look, and it has the one thing no new design can buy: it is what the team wore when it was new. It loses a little because the script and star crest is busy for its size, a very 1990s problem. Our take: the Rebels should make it the home sweater. A "permanent" alternate that shows up a few nights a year is not permanent enough.
