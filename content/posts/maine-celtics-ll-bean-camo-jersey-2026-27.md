**The Maine Celtics are going camo.** The G League affiliate of the Boston Celtics unveiled its **2026-27 L.L.Bean "Amazing Maine" uniform** on Monday, November 16: the **classic L.L.Bean camouflage pattern, redone in a Celtics green colorway**. The team says the pattern was first introduced in **1959** with the **Bean's Camouflage Parka**.

It is the **fourth season** of the Amazing Maine series, and each year the uniform has borrowed from a different piece of L.L.Bean's catalog. The Celtics will wear the camo set for **five games**, starting **Sunday, November 22, against Capital City** at 1 p.m.

## Quick Answers

- **What is the Maine Celtics L.L.Bean jersey?** A specialty uniform that puts L.L.Bean's classic camouflage pattern on a basketball uniform, in a green colorway inspired by the Celtics.
- **Where does the pattern come from?** L.L.Bean's camouflage, which the team says debuted on the Bean's Camouflage Parka in 1959.
- **When do the Maine Celtics wear it?** Five games in 2026-27: Nov. 22, Dec. 11, Jan. 22, Feb. 27 and Mar. 10.
- **What is on the patch?** This season's jersey patch recognizes the WinterKids Education Foundation.
- **Who benefits?** Each Amazing Maine game recognizes a different Maine nonprofit (full list below).
- **Is this the first L.L.Bean uniform?** No. It is the fourth. The first was Maine Hunting Plaid in 2023-24, and last season's was based on the L.L.Bean Norwegian Sweater.

## What The 2026-27 Uniform Looks Like

Here is what the team's announcement says about the design, and nothing more:

**The pattern.** L.L.Bean's **classic camouflage**, the pattern that first appeared on the **Bean's Camouflage Parka in 1959**.

**The color.** The camo is **reimagined in a new green colorway inspired by the Maine Celtics**, so instead of hunting browns, the uniform reads as Celtics green.

**The patch.** The jersey carries a **patch recognizing the WinterKids Education Foundation**, the nonprofit tied to this season's uniform.

The team's announcement does not describe the wordmark, the numbers or the trim, so we are not going to guess at them. We will add the details once the Celtics wear it on November 22.

"Fans now anticipate each new uniform design year over year," Maine Celtics team president **Dajuan Eubanks** said in the announcement, calling the collaboration "a staple at the Portland Expo."

**Gabe DiGristina** of L.L.Bean brand partnerships said the series "continues to celebrate Maine's outdoor heritage" and that the company expects this season's jersey "to be very popular with fans."

## Our Take

This is the right kind of minor league partnership. A sponsor uniform usually means a logo slapped on a jersey. Here the sponsor's product is the design, and the product happens to be one of the most Maine things you can put on a basketball court.

Turning L.L.Bean camo green is the smart move. Straight hunting camo would have looked like a costume. In Celtics green it still looks like the Celtics. The open question is how busy an allover pattern reads from the stands, and November 22 will answer it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What does the Maine Celtics L.L.Bean jersey look like in 2026-27?**

It uses L.L.Bean's classic camouflage pattern, reworked in a green colorway inspired by the Maine Celtics, with a jersey patch recognizing the WinterKids Education Foundation.

**What L.L.Bean product inspired the Maine Celtics camo uniform?**

The Bean's Camouflage Parka. The team says L.L.Bean's camouflage pattern was first introduced with that parka in 1959.

**When will the Maine Celtics wear the L.L.Bean camo uniform?**

At five games: November 22 vs. Capital City, December 11 vs. Greensboro, January 22 vs. Cleveland, February 27 vs. Raptors 905 and March 10 vs. Windy City.

**When is the first Amazing Maine game of 2026-27?**

Sunday, November 22, at 1 p.m. against Capital City.

**What is the Amazing Maine campaign?**

A Maine Celtics series, presented by L.L.Bean, in which the team wears a specialty L.L.Bean-inspired uniform at select games and recognizes a different Maine nonprofit at each one. 2026-27 is its fourth season.

**What were the earlier Maine Celtics L.L.Bean uniforms?**

The first, in 2023-24, used L.L.Bean's Maine Hunting Plaid. The 2025-26 uniform was inspired by the L.L.Bean Norwegian Sweater. The 2026-27 uniform uses L.L.Bean camouflage.

**What charity is on the Maine Celtics jersey patch?**

The WinterKids Education Foundation.

**Which nonprofits are recognized at the Amazing Maine games?**

The Dempsey Center (Nov. 22), WinterKids Education Foundation (Dec. 11), Make-A-Wish Maine (Jan. 22), Teens to Trails (Feb. 27) and Maine Adaptive (Mar. 10).

**Can you buy the Maine Celtics L.L.Bean camo jersey?**

The team's announcement does not say when or where the jersey will be sold. Check the Maine Celtics team store.

**What NBA team are the Maine Celtics affiliated with?**

The Boston Celtics. The Maine Celtics are Boston's NBA G League team and play at the Portland Expo in Portland, Maine.
