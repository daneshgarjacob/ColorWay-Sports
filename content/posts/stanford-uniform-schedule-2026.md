Stanford is the most restrained uniform program in the ACC and it still found a way to open 2026 with something new. This is the full Stanford 2026 uniform schedule: the cardinal home jersey, the white road set, the black alternate, the cardinal script helmet that only comes out for special occasions, the tribal-pattern helmet stripe the Cardinal wore in the season opener, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games in Tavita Pritchard's first year as head coach. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

Stanford's Nike set barely changes from year to year, and that is the point. No chest wordmark, no shoulder striping, one logo, one font. Everything the program does to break the routine happens on the helmet, and that is exactly how 2026 started.

For the Week 0 opener against Hawai'i, Stanford paired the traditional white shell with a center stripe carrying a tribal pattern celebrating Pacific Islander heritage, part of a collaboration with Island Avenue for the Pacific Islander Heritage Celebration. The athletics department also confirmed the small detail uniform people notice first: a red facemask on the white helmet for the first time since November 2017.

The bigger swing in the helmet rack is the cardinal one. Stanford had never worn a red shell in program history until late 2023, when it broke out a cardinal helmet with a white Stanford script.

## The Bottom Line

Two games in, Stanford has worn the same thing twice: white helmet, cardinal jersey, white pants. That consistency is the brand, and the program has never apologized for it. The variety comes from the helmet rack and from the calendar. A tribal-pattern stripe and a red facemask for the opener, a Red Out under the lights against Georgia Tech, a Friday night against NC State, and a Big Game in Berkeley that could pull the cardinal script shell out of storage. The black jersey is the one thing that would genuinely change the picture, and it has not been seen since 2024. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Stanford wearing this week?**

Stanford plays at Duke on Saturday, September 19 at 1 p.m. Pacific on The CW, and the white road jersey is expected because ACC visitors wear white. The open question is the pants, cardinal or white. Stanford wore the cardinal jersey with the white helmet and white pants in both home games so far. The week-by-week grid above lists every game with the expected uniform, and we update it as combinations are confirmed.

**What are Stanford's football uniforms for 2026?**

A cardinal home jersey with white numerals, a white road jersey with cardinal numerals, and a black alternate. All three are deliberately plain: no chest wordmark, no shoulder stripes, the block S tree mark at the collar. Pants come in white and cardinal. The default helmet is a white shell with the cardinal block S, and Nike makes the whole set.

**What was the special helmet Stanford wore against Hawai'i?**

For the Week 0 Pacific Islander Heritage Celebration, Stanford put a unique center stripe with a tribal pattern on its traditional white helmet, a collaboration with Island Avenue that also produced sideline apparel for coaches and staff. The same helmet carried a red facemask, which the athletics department noted was the first red facemask on the white shell since November 2017.

**Does Stanford have a red helmet?**

Yes, and it is newer than most people think. Stanford wore a cardinal shell with a white Stanford script for the first time in program history in November 2023. It is a special-occasion helmet rather than a weekly option, so the white block S shell remains the default for 2026.

**How many games does Stanford play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: six at Stanford Stadium against Hawai'i, Miami, Georgia Tech, Elon, NC State and SMU, and six on the road at Duke, Wake Forest, Notre Dame, Louisville, Virginia Tech and California, with byes on September 12 and November 7. The season opened in Week 0 on August 29 and ends on November 28. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
