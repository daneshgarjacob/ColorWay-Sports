The Milwaukee Bucks have already assigned a jersey to every game of the 2026-27 season. It was not announced. It sits in the data behind the team's own schedule page at nba.com/bucks/schedule, where each of the 80 regular season games carries a jersey, and we counted all of them as of September 28, 2026.

The Association is the workhorse: **29 games**, and 18 of them are on the road. The Cream City City Edition does not appear until January 28.

That covers all 80 games the league has dated. Two more December dates are still waiting on the NBA Cup knockout draw. Team schedule data can change, so we recheck it through the season and flip each row to what was actually worn the morning after.

A few things jump out of the count:

- **The Association travels.** 18 of its 29 games are road games, starting with the opener at Washington on October 21. At Fiserv Forum the home games split almost perfectly, 11 Association, 11 Icon, 11 Statement and 7 City.
- **The Statement gets the NBA Cup nights.** Both home Cup group games, Toronto on November 6 and Brooklyn on November 25, are Statement games.
- **The City Edition is a winter and spring jersey.** It debuts January 28 against Miami and all 11 City games fall between then and April 8. The team's data pairs every home City game with the City court.
- **Nothing is left blank.** Unlike the [Pacers](/stories/pacers-uniform-schedule-2026-27), who left 26 dates off their list, Milwaukee has a call for every game.
