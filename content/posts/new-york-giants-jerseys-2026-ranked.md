The Giants carry four uniforms in 2026 and not one of them is new. That is the whole point of this closet. The royal blue home set and the white road set do most of the work, and two throwbacks from the club's 1980s and 1990s teams, Legacy Blue and Vintage White, get two games each. We already rank the Giants as the second-best uniform in the league in our [best NFL uniforms of 2026](/stories/best-nfl-uniforms-2026-ranked), and the individual pieces hold up just as well.

Here is every New York Giants jersey for 2026, ranked worst to first and graded, with the helmet and pants each one wears and every date it comes out.

The standard road jersey is white with red numbers, a small lowercase "ny" on the chest, and three red stripes on each sleeve. It pairs with white pants and the blue NY helmet. The Giants wear it five times in 2026, all on the road: Week 2 at the Rams on Monday night, September 21, Week 7 at Houston, Week 12 at Indianapolis, Week 14 at Seattle, and Week 16 at Detroit on Monday night.

The red numbers are the choice that defines it. They give the road set a bright, loud front that no other Giants uniform has, and the sleeve stripes carry the same red, so the jersey reads as one idea from any camera angle. The problem is that red is the Giants' secondary color, and on a blue helmet above white pants the red numbers leave the blue with almost nothing to do below the neck. The uniform looks sharp and still feels a step removed from the franchise.

That is why it is a B+ and last in a closet with no bad options. It is also the look with the least history behind it, which is why the Week 7 trip to Houston is the one to watch. The Texans wear Battle Red at home that day, and white with red numbers against a red home jersey will be the most red on one field in the Giants' season.

The royal blue jersey is the primary, and it is the most-worn uniform on the calendar: eight games. It carries plain white numbers, the lowercase "ny" on the chest, and no sleeve stripes at all, and it pairs with the blue NY helmet and white pants with a red and blue leg stripe. The Giants opened in it on Sunday night, September 13, against Dallas, and it returns against the Titans in Week 3, at Washington on October 11, against the Saints in Week 6, against the Jaguars on November 22, against the Browns in Week 15, at Dallas on January 3, and against the Eagles in the Week 18 finale.

This is the jersey we graded when we put the Giants second in the league, and the grade stands at an A. The case for it is how little is on it. A solid royal body, white numbers without an outline, a bare sleeve, and a helmet with one logo on it. The only red on the whole uniform lives in the pants stripe, which is exactly where a little of it does the most good. Week 1 proved the point: against the Cowboys' silver and white under the MetLife lights, royal against white was the only contrast on the field, and it was all the game needed. We gave that matchup an A- in our [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026).

The two road dates are worth circling. Washington wears white at home in Week 5 and Dallas always does, so the Giants get to take blue on the road twice, and blue on white at AT&T Stadium is one of the better pictures the division makes.

Vintage White is the road twin of the Legacy set. The Giants built it in 2016 for the league's Color Rush program, and it reproduces the road look the club wore for two decades: a white jersey with blue numbers outlined in red, a blue and red striped collar and sleeve cuffs, white pants, and the navy helmet with the GIANTS wordmark. It comes out twice, back to back: Week 9 at Philadelphia on Sunday, November 8, and Week 10 against Washington on Thursday night, November 12.

Put it next to the white road jersey above and the difference is the numbers. Blue with a red outline keeps the primary color in charge and uses red as trim, which is the balance the modern road set gets backwards. The striped cuffs finish the sleeve instead of cutting across it, and the wordmark helmet turns a plain white uniform into something you can identify from the upper deck.

It grades an A, level with the royal home set and ahead of it here because of what it does with the calendar. November 8 is the best-dressed game on the Giants' schedule: the Eagles wear Kelly Green that day, so it is Vintage White against Kelly Green in Philadelphia, both clubs in the looks their best 1980s teams wore. Four days later it gets a national Thursday night against Washington in burgundy.

The Legacy set is the uniform the Giants wore from 1980 through 1999, including Super Bowl XXI. It is a royal blue jersey with white numbers outlined in red, red and white stripes on the sleeves and collar, and Wellington Mara's line "Once a Giant, Always a Giant" inside the neck. It pairs with a navy helmet carrying a white facemask, a solid scarlet center stripe, and the raised, underlined GIANTS wordmark, plus white pants with red and blue stripes. It is worn twice at MetLife Stadium: Sunday, October 4 against Arizona, when the 1986 team is honored at halftime for the fortieth anniversary of Super Bowl XXI, and Sunday, December 6 against San Francisco.

This is the best uniform the Giants own, and it is not close. It has the two things the modern set gave up. The first is the wordmark helmet, a design that has almost vanished from the league, so it reads as distinct rather than nostalgic. The second is stripes that resolve at distance: the red and white sleeve bands and the striped pants give the eye a pattern to follow down the whole body, where the primary asks the solid blue to carry everything alone.

It grades an A, and it tops a closet where three jerseys earn one. If we had the pen, Legacy Blue would be the primary, and two games a year is fewer than it deserves.

## The Bottom Line on the Giants Jersey Rankings

The Giants have one of the few closets in the NFL where the worst uniform is a B+. The royal blue home set is an A because it does almost nothing and does it perfectly, and the white road set only trails because its red numbers pull the jersey away from the blue that defines the club.

The throwbacks are the real story. Legacy Blue is an A and the best thing New York owns, and Vintage White is an A too, that gets the best game on the schedule, Kelly Green in Philadelphia on November 8. Four throwback dates is the right direction. More would be better.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jerseys do the Giants have in 2026?**

Four: the royal blue home jersey, the white road jersey with red numbers, the Legacy Blue throwback, and the Vintage White throwback. The two throwbacks wear the navy GIANTS wordmark helmet, and the two standard sets wear the blue NY helmet. All four pair with white pants.

**What is the best Giants jersey?**

We rank Legacy Blue first and grade it an A. It is the 1980 to 1999 uniform, with the underlined GIANTS wordmark on a navy helmet, red and white sleeve stripes, and striped white pants, and it is the uniform the Super Bowl XXI team wore.

**What jersey are the Giants wearing this week?**

Week 2 is at the Los Angeles Rams on Monday Night Football, September 21. The Giants are scheduled for the white road jersey with white pants and the blue NY helmet, and the Rams have confirmed their gold Classic Sol jersey. Check the [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026) after kickoff for what was actually worn.

**Do the Giants have a new jersey in 2026?**

No. All four uniforms are returning. The change for 2026 is how often the throwbacks appear: Legacy Blue and Vintage White get two games each. Century Red was the 2024 alternate for the franchise's 100th season and is not on the 2026 schedule.

**When do the Giants wear the Legacy uniform in 2026?**

Twice, both at MetLife Stadium: Sunday, October 4 against the Arizona Cardinals, the Super Bowl XXI fortieth anniversary game, and Sunday, December 6 against the San Francisco 49ers.

**When do the Giants wear Vintage White in 2026?**

Back to back in Weeks 9 and 10: Sunday, November 8 at the Philadelphia Eagles, who wear Kelly Green that day, and Thursday, November 12 against the Washington Commanders.
