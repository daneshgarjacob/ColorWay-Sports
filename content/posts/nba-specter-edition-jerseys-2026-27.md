Nike and the NBA confirmed a new uniform line on September 10, 2026, and it answers a question this site has been circling since April. The eight dark, neon-streaked designs that leaked in the spring as "Christmas jerseys," then looked like City Editions, are neither. They are the **Nike Specter Edition**, a fifth uniform for eight of the league's oldest franchises, and it arrives later this fall.

## What the Specter Edition Is

Specter is a program for eight clubs, chosen for age rather than market: the **Los Angeles Lakers, Boston Celtics, Golden State Warriors, New York Knicks, Philadelphia 76ers, Detroit Pistons, Atlanta Hawks and Sacramento Kings**. Sacramento is the oldest continuously operating franchise in the league, and the other seven all trace back to the 1940s and 1950s. **It is only these eight.** Nike's own framing is a collection for "the teams who trace their roots back to the earliest chapters of the league," and the other 22 clubs do not get one this season.

Nike's own description is that the collection reimagines "team identity for the league's oldest franchises through their history, culture and the energy of today's game," and that the designs are "inspired by the next generation of hoopers." The company says it built the line with feedback from players and younger fans, with the stated goal of making team gear more wearable away from the court. Read that as a streetwear brief with a basketball jersey as the canvas.

It sits inside a wider **Nike NBA Standard Issue** collection, which is where the confusion about dates comes from. Standard Issue drops on **September 15 and October 12**. The Specter uniforms themselves reach retail and the court **later in the fall**, with the Pistons' version pegged for November, which puts the on-court debuts close to the holiday stretch that made everyone assume "Christmas" in the first place.

## What It Is Not

It is not a replacement for anything. The eight Specter clubs keep the full set: Icon, Association, Statement and City Edition, with Specter as a fifth. The league-wide **City Edition reveal on September 15** goes ahead exactly as planned, and we track all thirty of those on our [2026-27 City Edition page](/stories/nba-city-edition-jerseys-2026-27). The Hawks, for example, have a confirmed City Edition (the returning MLK uniform) and a Specter Edition, and they are two completely different uniforms. That mismatch was the clue we had in August that the leak was not what it said it was.

## The Design, and Why the Reaction Has Been Brutal

The eight jerseys share a template, and the template is the story. Every one runs a **dark base**, black or near-black, with the team's color reduced to streaks, gradients or piping. The **Swoosh moves toward the centre of the chest**, the **wordmark shrinks**, and the **player name sits low across the back, below the number**, rather than in the usual arch above it. Several of the designs pull historical marks into the graphic: the Celtics' clover, the Knicks' NYK mark, a blue and white stripe on the Lakers' shoulder that references the Minneapolis years.

The two designs Nike has shown most fully set the tone. The **Lakers'** is black and purple with yellow lettering and number outlines, the Swoosh sitting above a smaller "Lakers" wordmark. The **Pistons'** is a black base with teal outlining and red, teal and orange streaks running through the front and hem, a smoky, flame-like treatment that has nothing to do with red, white and blue.

The reaction has been rough, and not only from the usual corners. "Middle school basketball," "low effort," and "Nike ruined NBA jerseys" were the polite versions. Our read is that the problem is structural rather than any single design: one dark template applied to eight of the most recognizable color schemes in American sport produces eight jerseys that look like each other and not like their teams. A Celtics jersey that is mostly black is a Celtics jersey in the way a shadow of a thing is the thing.

## What We Got Wrong, in Order

We owe readers the record. In April we published the eight designs as the 2026 Christmas jerseys, because that is how they leaked. In August the NBA schedule showed three of the eight clubs were not playing on December 25, so we called the label wrong and guessed City Edition, since the motifs were city landmarks. The Hawks' confirmed City Edition being a different uniform was the fact that did not fit, and we said so on the page rather than burying it. On September 10 Nike named the line, and both of our earlier reads were wrong about the program and right about one thing: these were never holiday uniforms. The reviews stand because the designs do; the labels are corrected across all nine posts.

## The Bottom Line

The Specter Edition is a new fifth uniform for the Lakers, Celtics, Warriors, Knicks, 76ers, Pistons, Hawks and Kings, built by Nike on one dark, streetwear-first template with the Swoosh centred and the player name dropped below the number. It debuts on court and at retail later in the fall of 2026, separately from the September 15 City Edition reveal, which is unaffected. Our first-look grades run from an A for Detroit down to a C for the Lakers, with the collection at a D+, and the fairest summary of the set is that it makes eight famous teams look like one brand.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the NBA Specter Edition?**

A new Nike uniform line for the 2026-27 season, given to eight of the league's oldest franchises as a fifth uniform alongside Icon, Association, Statement and City Edition. Nike describes it as reimagining team identity through history, culture and "the energy of today's game."

**Which teams have a Specter Edition jersey?**

The Los Angeles Lakers, Boston Celtics, Golden State Warriors, New York Knicks, Philadelphia 76ers, Detroit Pistons, Atlanta Hawks and Sacramento Kings.

**When do the Specter Edition jerseys come out?**

Later in the fall of 2026, with the on-court debut and retail release landing around the holiday stretch. The wider Nike NBA Standard Issue collection they belong to drops on September 15 and October 12.

**Are the Specter Edition jerseys the same as the City Edition jerseys?**

No. City Edition is a separate program for all thirty teams, revealed on September 15, 2026. The eight Specter clubs get both.

**Were these the leaked 2026 NBA Christmas jerseys?**

Yes, the same eight designs leaked in April labelled as Christmas jerseys. The label was wrong. Three of the eight teams are not on the Christmas Day schedule at all.

**Why is the player name below the number on the Specter jerseys?**

It is a template choice shared across the collection, part of the streetwear-first construction Nike describes. It is one of the most criticized details of the set.
