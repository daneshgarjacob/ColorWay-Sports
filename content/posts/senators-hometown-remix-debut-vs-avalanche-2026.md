**Ottawa's Hometown Remix jersey makes its debut on Thursday.** The Senators wear it for the first time against the Colorado Avalanche at Canadian Tire Centre on **Thursday, Nov. 12, at 7 p.m. Eastern**. It is **black and gold**, with a **brushed metallic gold centurion crest** and details taken from **Parliament Hill**. Colorado is expected in its **road whites**.

## What the Senators' Hometown Remix Jersey Looks Like

Ottawa built it around the capital. Here is what the team and the league describe, and what the studio photo shows:

- **Color:** black, with gold and red trim. The league's description opens with "Black and gold, elevated."
- **Crest:** the centurion, done in **metallic gold lurex with a brushed finish**.
- **Sleeves:** gold and red stripes, with geometric patterns based on the architecture of **Parliament Hill**. The team points to the Peace Tower clock in the sleeve design.
- **Numbers:** a new number font drawn from the **Gothic arched windows and doors** of the Parliament buildings.
- **Shoulders:** the **English logo on one shoulder and the French logo on the other**, for a bilingual capital.
- **Hem:** an **OTTAWA** wordmark inside the hem, framed by the bridges across the river to Gatineau.

## What the Avalanche Wear

Colorado has its own Hometown Remix, a frosted white sweater with a mountain-elevation pattern in the C, but Thursday is not one of its six Remix dates. The Avalanche are expected in their regular **white road jerseys**, which puts white against the Senators' black. We will confirm it from the game photos after the game.

Colorado's Remix games are Nov. 7 vs. Red Wings (worn, the debut), Nov. 27 at Wild, Nov. 28 vs. Sharks, Dec. 12 vs. Capitals, Jan. 8 vs. Kraken and Feb. 22 vs. Golden Knights. See the [Avalanche 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/avalanche-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

## How Many Times the Senators Wear It

**Nine times**, all at home, starting Thursday. Ottawa has not published the other eight dates in one place yet. We will add them to the [Senators 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/senators-uniform-schedule-2026-27) as soon as we confirm them.

## Our Grade: C

**C.** The details are good. Gothic-arch numbers, the Peace Tower in the sleeves and bilingual shoulders are things only Ottawa could put on a jersey. The problem is that you cannot see most of them from the stands. From a distance it is a black sweater with a gold crest, which is close to what the Senators already wear. We have it [No. 26 of all 32 Remix jerseys](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-jerseys-ranked). A red or a Parliament green base would have made the same ideas stand out.

**The matchup: B-.** Black against white is clean and easy to follow, but it is the look of a normal Senators home game.

## FAQ

### When do the Senators debut their Hometown Remix jersey?

Thursday, Nov. 12, 2026, against the Colorado Avalanche at Canadian Tire Centre. Puck drop is 7 p.m. Eastern.

### What does the Senators Hometown Remix jersey look like?

Black with gold and red trim, a centurion crest in brushed metallic gold, sleeve patterns and numbers drawn from the Gothic architecture of Parliament Hill, English and French logos on opposite shoulders and an OTTAWA wordmark inside the hem.

### What is the Parliament Hill detail on the Senators jersey?

The sleeve design includes the Peace Tower clock and geometric striping from the Parliament buildings, and the numbers use a font based on their Gothic arched windows and doors.

### Why does the Senators jersey have English and French logos?

Ottawa is a bilingual capital, so the English logo sits on one shoulder and the French logo on the other.

### How many times will the Senators wear the Hometown Remix jersey?

Nine times in 2026-27, all at home, starting Nov. 12.

### What are the Avalanche wearing against the Senators?

Colorado is expected in its white road jerseys. Nov. 12 is not one of the Avalanche's six Hometown Remix dates.

### What does the Avalanche Hometown Remix jersey look like?

White with blues and metallic silver, and a flag-style C filled with a mountain elevation pattern that shades from light to dark blue. Colorado debuted it on Nov. 7 against Detroit.

### Where does the Senators Remix jersey rank?

We have it No. 26 of 32. The details are smart, but from a distance it looks like a regular black Senators sweater.

### Is the Senators Remix jersey the same as their red alternate?

No. The Remix is black and gold. Ottawa's other alternate is red.
