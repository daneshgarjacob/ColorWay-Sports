**The New York Rangers wore their Centennial jerseys on Monday night, exactly 100 years after their first NHL game, and beat the Montreal Canadiens 3-1.**

The Rangers played their first game on Nov. 16, 1926, a 1-0 win over the Montreal Maroons at the old Madison Square Garden. On Nov. 16, 2026, they marked the date at the current Garden against the other team from Montreal, in the lighter blue sweater built for the 100th season.

## Quick Answers

- **What did the Rangers wear on Nov. 16, 2026?** The Centennial jersey: a lighter blue sweater with the diagonal RANGERS in cream, a cream collar and cream-red-cream stripes on the sleeves and hem.
- **Why that night?** It was 100 years to the day since the Rangers' first NHL game.
- **Who won?** The Rangers, 3-1. Will Cuylle scored twice and Scott Morrow got his first goal as a Ranger.
- **Where does the blue come from?** The original 1926 Rangers shield, according to the team.
- **When did the Centennial jersey first come out?** It was unveiled on Sept. 18, 2025, for 10 themed home games in the 2025-26 season. The first was Opening Night against Pittsburgh on Oct. 7, 2025.
- **Will the Rangers wear it again this season?** The team has not said. If it does, we will add the date to the [Rangers uniform schedule](/stories/new-york-rangers-uniform-schedule-2026-27).
- **Our take (opinion):** it is the best jersey the Rangers own, and it should not be a one-night return.

The Centennial sweater keeps the Rangers' famous diagonal RANGERS across the chest, but changes almost everything around it. The blue is a shade lighter than the regular Broadway blue home jersey; the team says it comes from the original 1926 shield. The letters are cream instead of red, and they stand straight up instead of leaning, a font based on the 1926 original. The collar is cream, the sleeve and hem stripes are cream-red-cream, the numbers are cream, and the 100th anniversary shield sits on the left shoulder. Inside the collar is a Garden marquee with the date of the first game, Nov. 16, 1926.

The cream is the detail that makes it work. It gives the jersey an older, softer look without turning it into a costume. Put it next to the regular home jersey and it looks like the same team 100 years apart.

Here is how it compares with the rest of the 2026-27 closet:

| Jersey | Body | RANGERS lettering | Trim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Centennial | Lighter blue from the 1926 shield | Cream, diagonal, upright letters | Cream and red |
| Home | Broadway blue | Red with a white shadow, diagonal | Red and white |
| Road | White | Blue with a red shadow, diagonal | Blue and red |
| Navy Shield third | Navy | Shield crest, no wordmark | Red, white and blue |
| Hometown Remix | Navy | NEW YORK in stair-step letters | Red sleeve band |

Every one of them, with dates, is on the [Rangers uniform schedule for 2026-27](/stories/new-york-rangers-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

Before the game, Sam Rosen hosted a ceremony with five Rangers whose numbers hang in the Garden: Henrik Lundqvist, Mark Messier, Adam Graves, Mike Richter and Brian Leetch. Family members of Rod Gilbert, Jean Ratelle, Andy Bathgate, Ed Giacomin, Harry Howell and Vic Hadfield were there too.

Then the game, per the NHL's recap:

- **Montreal 1-0:** Phillip Danault tipped in a Lane Hutson shot at 7:59 of the second period.
- **1-1:** Will Cuylle tied it at 9:47 of the second when the puck went in off his skate.
- **Rangers 2-1:** Cuylle scored again short-handed with 57 seconds left in the second. It was the winner.
- **Rangers 3-1:** Scott Morrow scored from the point at 6:44 of the third, his first goal as a Ranger.

Joonas Korpisalo made 24 saves in his first home start for New York. The Rangers are 13-8-1 and have won six of their last seven.

## Our Take

The regular Rangers home jersey is close to perfect, so a special jersey has a hard job. This one does it by changing very little: a lighter blue, cream where the regular jersey has red and white, and the upright 1926 letters. That restraint is why it looks better than most anniversary jerseys in any sport.

The Rangers should make it a regular third jersey. They already have a navy third and a navy Hometown Remix, which is two navy specials. A lighter blue one would give them something nobody else in the league has.

The Centennial jersey, a lighter blue sweater with the diagonal RANGERS in cream and cream-red-cream stripes. Montreal wore its white road jersey. The NHL posted photos of it before the game.

**Why did the Rangers wear the Centennial jersey on Nov. 16?**

The Rangers played their first NHL game on Nov. 16, 1926. Monday's game was exactly 100 years later.

The Montreal Maroons, at Madison Square Garden III. The Rangers won 1-0.

Yes, 3-1 over the Montreal Canadiens. Will Cuylle scored twice, Scott Morrow scored once and Joonas Korpisalo made 24 saves.

A lighter blue than the regular home jersey, with cream lettering, numbers and collar and cream-red-cream stripes. The team says the blue was taken from the original 1926 shield.
