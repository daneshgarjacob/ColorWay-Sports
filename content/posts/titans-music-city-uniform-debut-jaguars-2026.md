**The Tennessee Titans wore their Music City uniform for the first time on Sunday, November 15, against Jacksonville at Nissan Stadium.** It was the debut of Tennessee's entry in the NFL's Rivalries uniform program, and it went on the field exactly as the team revealed it in August: a Titans Blue helmet, a navy jersey with "Music City" across the chest and navy pants. Jacksonville won the game 40-23.

## Quick Answers

- **What did the Titans wear against the Jaguars?** The Music City Rivalries uniform: Titans Blue helmet with a circular navy T, navy jersey with "Music City" in script across the chest, navy pants with a light blue stripe and light blue socks. Confirmed from the Titans' game photos.
- **Was this the first time?** Yes. Week 10 against Jacksonville was the announced debut.
- **What did Jacksonville wear?** The black helmet, white jersey and black pants.
- **Who won?** Jacksonville, 40-23.
- **Our grade:** B-. An A helmet on a B jersey.

## What The Music City Uniform Looks Like

The Music City set is Nashville from top to bottom. The jersey is navy with "Music City" in embroidered script across the chest, a nod to the honky-tonk signs on Lower Broadway, and numbers styled after neon. The pant stripes are drawn from guitar strings, and the collar carries the Tennessee tristar. There is almost no red anywhere. It is a two-blue uniform: navy body, light blue accents and a light blue helmet.

The helmet is the headline. It is a Titans Blue shell with navy racing stripes and a circular navy T on the side, the first time Tennessee's new era has put light blue on a helmet.

## What Is The Rivalries Uniform Program?

Rivalries is the NFL's program of alternate uniforms built around a team's city or region. Tennessee's arrived in 2026, the first season of its rebrand, and the team chose Nashville's music identity over anything tied to its football history. We explain how the program works in [what is the NFL Rivalries uniform program](/stories/what-is-the-nfl-rivalries-uniform-program) and rank every set in [NFL Rivalries uniforms ranked](/stories/nfl-rivalries-uniforms-2026-ranked).

## Our Take

The helmet is the best thing Tennessee has worn in years. Light blue with a navy circled T and navy stripes is clean, original and unmistakably the Titans, and it deserves more than one game a season. The jersey under it is quieter: a plain navy body leaning on the script and the neon numbers. Dropping red was the right call, because it lets the two blues carry the whole look. **Grade: B-.**
