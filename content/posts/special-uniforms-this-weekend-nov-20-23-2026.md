These are the special uniforms being worn this weekend, Friday, November 20 through Monday, November 23, 2026, in the NFL, college football, the NBA and the NHL, all on one page. **Florida State wears turquoise in a game for the first time**, **Miami** brings back its digital camo with new black pants, **UCF** puts the gold Knights script on a black helmet, the **Bills** go all white in **Cold Front**, the **Commanders** wear the all-black **Hail Raiser** for the first time on Monday night, the **Pacers** and **Nets** bring out their ABA throwbacks, and seven NHL teams wear their **Hometown Remix** sweaters.

Each entry says who is wearing it, when, against whom, what it looks like by color, why it matters, and our grade where we have already published one. A look is **Confirmed** when the team has posted it for this game. **Scheduled** means it is on the jersey schedule the team or league published. **Reported** means someone else reported it and the team has not posted it. Where the team posted the look, its post is embedded under the entry.

Thursday night is already covered: the Texans debut the white-helmet HOUSTON/US Rivalries uniform against the Colts, the first white helmet in team history. We graded the uniform a **B**. [Every detail](/stories/texans-houston-us-rivalries-uniform-colts-thursday-night-football-2026).
