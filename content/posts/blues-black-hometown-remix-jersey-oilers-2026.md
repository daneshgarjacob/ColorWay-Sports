**The St. Louis Blues are 2-0 in their black Hometown Remix jersey.** They wore it for the second time on Sunday, November 22, at Enterprise Center, fell behind the Edmonton Oilers 4-1 early in the third period, then scored three goals in just over five minutes and won **5-4 in a shootout**. The team says it was the first time in Blues history they came back from three goals down in the final 10 minutes of regulation to win. The debut, on November 17, was an 8-6 win over Anaheim. Eight Remix nights are left, and the team has published every one of them.

## Quick Answers

- **What color is the Blues Hometown Remix jersey?** **Black**, with **blue** stripes and trim and **silver** accents. The team says it is the first Blues uniform to put black and silver together.
- **How many times have the Blues worn it?** Twice: November 17 against Anaheim and November 22 against Edmonton.
- **Record in it:** **2-0**. An 8-6 win over the Ducks and a 5-4 shootout win over the Oilers.
- **How many Remix games are left?** **Eight**, starting Wednesday, November 25 at the Islanders, the only road date.
- **Our grade:** **C+.** A good crest on a jersey in a color the Blues have never really owned.

## What The Blues Wore Against The Oilers

The Blues' Remix in plain colors, from the team's game photos on Sunday night:

- **Jersey:** **black** all over.
- **Crest:** the **round "ST. LOUIS BLUES" crest**, a blue ring with white lettering around the Blue Note. It comes from the navy alternate the Blues wore from 2008 to 2016, updated with **three wave lines** for the Mississippi River.
- **Stripes:** **blue** bands on the sleeves and hem, with a **debossed wave pattern** pressed into the black sleeve bands.
- **Numbers:** **light blue**, in a new beveled style the team says echoes the metal finish of the Gateway Arch.
- **Hem:** the city name framed as if you were looking up from under the Arch.
- **Helmets and pants:** **black**.

The Oilers wore their **white road sweater** with orange and navy stripes, so it was black against white all night. The team ties the black to "the dark waters of the Mississippi," in its own words from the November 2 reveal.

## How The Comeback Happened

Edmonton led 2-0 after goals by Kasperi Kapanen and Leon Draisaitl, and 4-1 after Colton Dach scored 4:40 into the third. Then the Blues came back:

- **10:27** of the third: **Philip Broberg**, a slap shot. 4-2.
- **15:12**: **Pavel Buchnevich**. 4-3.
- **15:38**: **Jimmy Snuggerud**, 26 seconds later. 4-4.

Nobody scored in overtime. In the shootout, Connor McDavid scored for Edmonton, **Jake Neighbours** answered, **Joel Hofer** stopped Draisaitl, **Mason McTavish** scored and Hofer stopped Owen Michaels to end it.

## Our Grade: C+

The round crest is a good logo, and it was a good call to bring it back from the 2008-16 navy alternate. The wave lines and the beveled numbers are smart details. The problem is the color. **Black has never been a Blues color**, and on TV this reads as a black jersey with blue trim that could belong to several teams. The navy the crest came from would have made it a B. We have it **No. 20 of 32** in our [ranking of every NHL Hometown Remix jersey](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-jerseys-ranked): nothing wrong with it, nothing exciting about it either.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What does the Blues Hometown Remix jersey look like?**
It is black, with blue stripes and trim, silver accents, a round "ST. LOUIS BLUES" crest with three river-wave lines, debossed waves on the sleeve bands and beveled light blue numbers inspired by the Gateway Arch.

**Why are the Blues wearing black jerseys?**
They are wearing their Hometown Remix sweater, part of the NHL and Fanatics special edition every team got in 2026-27. The Blues picked black for the dark water of the Mississippi River.

**Have the Blues ever worn black before?**
Not as a main color. The team says the Remix is the first Blues uniform to pair black and silver.

**What jersey did the Blues wear against the Oilers?**
The black Hometown Remix, on Sunday, November 22, 2026. The Oilers wore white. The Blues won 5-4 in a shootout.

**What is the Blues' record in the Hometown Remix jersey?**
2-0. They beat Anaheim 8-6 on November 17 and Edmonton 5-4 in a shootout on November 22.

**How many times will the Blues wear the Hometown Remix?**
Ten times in 2026-27, according to the team's sweater schedule. Two are done, so eight are left.

**When do the Blues wear the Hometown Remix next?**
Wednesday, November 25 at the New York Islanders, then Friday, November 27 at home against Dallas.

**Do the Blues wear the Hometown Remix on the road?**
Once. Nine of the 10 dates are at home. The road date is November 25 at the Islanders.

**When did the Blues debut the Hometown Remix jersey?**
Tuesday, November 17, 2026, at home against the Anaheim Ducks. The Blues won 8-6.

**Where does the round crest on the Blues Remix come from?**
The navy alternate the Blues wore from 2008 to 2016. The Remix version adds three wave lines for the Mississippi.

**Who scored the shootout winner for the Blues against the Oilers?**
Mason McTavish. Jake Neighbours also scored in the shootout, and Joel Hofer stopped Leon Draisaitl and Owen Michaels.

**How did the Blues come back against the Oilers?**
Down 4-1 in the third period, they got goals from Philip Broberg, Pavel Buchnevich and Jimmy Snuggerud, the last two 26 seconds apart, then won the shootout.

**Where does the Blues Remix rank among all 32?**
No. 20 in our ranking. Our grade is C+.

**Is the Blues Hometown Remix the same as the Blues' third jersey?**
No. The Blues' third jersey is the royal blue former home sweater. The Remix is a separate special edition in black.
