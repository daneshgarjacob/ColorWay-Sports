It comes down to two. The 2026 World Cup Final is set: **Spain vs Argentina**, Sunday, July 19, kicking off at 3 p.m. ET at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey. It is a final between two of the most iconic color stories in the sport, and the good news for anyone who cares how a final looks is that both teams get to wear the kits they are famous for. Here is exactly what each side will wear, and why this is one of the cleaner kit matchups a final could ask for.

## What Argentina Will Wear: The Light-Blue-and-White Stripes

Argentina wears home, which means the **albiceleste**: the instantly recognizable light-blue-and-white vertical stripes, paired with **white shorts and white socks**. It is one of the most famous jerseys on earth and needs no introduction, the same sky-blue-and-white the country has worn through its greatest moments. This is the adidas home shirt, and it carries the three stars above the crest for Argentina's 1978, 1986, and 2022 titles. Win on Sunday and a fourth star is coming. There is nothing to overthink here. When Argentina reaches a final, the stripes come out, and they remain one of the best-looking kits in international football.

## What Spain Will Wear: La Roja in Red

Spain wears its home **red jersey**, worn with **navy blue shorts and socks**. La Roja's red is as much a part of the country's football identity as the stripes are to Argentina, a deep, confident red that reads beautifully under the lights. This is also an adidas kit, carrying the single star for Spain's 2010 World Cup triumph. The navy shorts ground the red and keep the look from going one-note, and against Argentina's pale stripes it gives the final exactly the contrast a showpiece wants.

## The Matchup: Why This Final Looks So Good

Red against light-blue-and-white is close to a perfect final pairing. The two kits never clash, they read cleanly from every seat and every camera angle, and they put two of the most storied identities in the sport on the same field with no compromise needed. No third-choice change kit, no muddy color conflict, just two home shirts that fans around the world would recognize in a silhouette. On looks alone, this is a **9 out of 10** matchup, the kind of visual final the tournament deserves. ## The Bottom Line on the World Cup Final Kits

The 2026 World Cup Final gives us the version everyone hoped for: Argentina in the timeless stripes, Spain in the bold red, both in the kits that made them. It is a clean, high-contrast, deeply recognizable pairing with real history stitched into every crest and star. Whoever lifts the trophy, the final is going to look the part.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What kit will Spain wear in the 2026 World Cup Final?**
Spain wears its home kit: the red La Roja jersey with navy blue shorts and socks. It is an adidas kit and carries the single star for Spain's 2010 World Cup title.

**What kit will Argentina wear in the 2026 World Cup Final?**
Argentina wears its home kit: the light-blue-and-white vertical stripes (the albiceleste) with white shorts and white socks. The adidas shirt carries three stars for Argentina's 1978, 1986, and 2022 titles.

**When and where is the 2026 World Cup Final?**
The final is Sunday, July 19, 2026, kicking off at 3 p.m. ET at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey, just outside New York City.

**Will there be a kit clash in the final?**
No. Spain's red and Argentina's light-blue-and-white stripes do not clash, so both teams wear their home kits. Neither side needs to switch to an away or third kit.

**Why does Argentina wear light blue and white?**
The light-blue-and-white stripes are Argentina's national colors, drawn from the country's flag, and the albiceleste has been the team's identity for over a century. It is considered one of the most iconic kits in world football.

**Who makes the Spain and Argentina jerseys?**
Both national teams are outfitted by adidas for the 2026 World Cup, which sets up an all-adidas final on the kit front.
