Thanksgiving week 2026 has five NFL games in three days: the league's first Thanksgiving Eve game on Wednesday, November 25, three games on Thanksgiving Thursday, November 26, and a Black Friday game on November 27. The two biggest looks are already on the teams' own published schedules. **The Rams wear their new Fearsome White alternate against the Packers on Wednesday night**, its only date of the season, and **Dallas wears its 1960s Classic throwback against Philadelphia** for the fifth straight Thanksgiving, as published on the Cowboys' own uniform schedule. Detroit hosts Chicago in the early game, Buffalo hosts Kansas City at night in blue over blue, and Pittsburgh hosts Denver the next afternoon in the **all-black Color Rush**, which the Steelers announced on November 24.

Every look below carries one of three labels, and we keep them separate:

- **Confirmed:** the team has announced the uniform for this game.
- **Scheduled:** the uniform is on the team's own published 2026 schedule.
- **Expected:** our projection, based on the team's usual home and road pattern and our schedule post for that team. It changes to Confirmed or Scheduled when the team posts it.

## Thanksgiving Eve: Packers at Rams, 8:00 p.m. ET on Netflix

**Rams: Fearsome White (Confirmed).** Los Angeles assigned all 17 of its 2026 games on September 1, and this is the only date for Fearsome White: a white jersey with royal blue numbers, a continuous horn stripe over the shoulder, white pants, and a royal blue helmet with white horns and a white facemask. The Rams confirmed the full set on November 24 in a "Fearsome White Uniform Look Book." It is named for the Fearsome Foursome defensive line of the 1960s ([who they were](/stories/who-were-the-fearsome-foursome)), and we graded it an A- when it [first leaked](/stories/rams-leaked-alternate-jerseys-2026). Full season in our [Rams 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/rams-uniform-schedule-2026).

**Packers: green jersey (Confirmed).** Two teams cannot both wear white, so Green Bay brings the green home jersey to SoFi Stadium with the gold helmet and gold pants, as it did at the Jets in Week 2. The Packers confirmed it on November 23 ("On Wednesdays we wear Green & Gold"). It is one of three road games this season where the host took white first. See our [Packers 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/packers-uniform-schedule-2026).

## Eagles at Cowboys, 4:30 p.m. ET on FOX

**Cowboys: Thanksgiving Classic throwback (Confirmed).** Dallas published all 17 of its 2026 uniform assignments on September 2 and confirmed this one with its Week 12 "Gameday Threads" post on November 24. It is the Classic, the 1960s throwback: a **white helmet with the vintage star and a gray facemask**, a navy jersey with stars on the white shoulder yoke and solid block numbers, and white pants with twin navy stripes. The Cowboys wore it on Thanksgiving in 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025. Full details are in our [Cowboys 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/cowboys-uniform-schedule-2026). Dallas wearing a dark jersey at home is the exception: [why the Cowboys wear white at home](/stories/why-do-the-cowboys-wear-white-at-home) the rest of the year.

**Eagles: white road jersey (Expected).** Dallas usually wears white at home, which normally lets a visitor bring a colored jersey to AT&T Stadium; Philadelphia wore Kelly Green there in 2025. That option is closed this year because the Classic is a navy jersey, so the Eagles take white. Philadelphia confirmed its three 2026 alternate dates on September 4 (Kelly Green in Weeks 9 and 16, the black alternate in Week 15), and Thanksgiving is not one of them. Our [Eagles 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/eagles-uniform-schedule-2026) has the midnight green helmet and white jersey for this game. The full game page: [What Are The Cowboys Wearing On Thanksgiving 2026?](/stories/cowboys-eagles-thanksgiving-uniforms-2026)

## Thanksgiving Uniform History, Briefly

- **Dallas and the Classic.** The 1960s throwback has been the Cowboys' Thanksgiving uniform every year since 2022, and 2026 makes five in a row.
- **Detroit's mix.** The Lions do not have one fixed Thanksgiving look. They wore their throwbacks (logo-free silver helmet, plain Honolulu blue jersey, silver pants) against Green Bay in 2023 and again in 2025, per [WILX](https://www.wilx.com/2025/11/26/lions-return-classic-throwback-look-thanksgiving-game/), and went all blue against the Bears in 2024. They also wore throwbacks often on the holiday in the 2000s.
- **The Madden patch is back.** The NFL confirmed on November 19 that every player in the three Thursday games wears the round [John Madden Thanksgiving patch](/stories/john-madden-thanksgiving-patch-2026), a silhouette of Madden in his headset, high on the left chest. It is the fourth year of the patch, since 2023, and the fifth John Madden Thanksgiving Celebration.

We will update this page as each team posts its game-week uniform, and the [2026 NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026) logs what all ten teams actually wear.

## FAQs

**What are the Lions wearing on Thanksgiving 2026?**

We expect Honolulu blue at home against the Chicago Bears on Thursday, November 26, with the silver helmet. Detroit has announced its uniforms week by week this season and has no throwback on its 2026 calendar.

**What are the Cowboys wearing on Thanksgiving 2026?**

The 1960s Classic throwback against the Eagles: white helmet with the vintage star, navy jersey with stars on the shoulders, and white pants with navy stripes. It is on the uniform schedule Dallas published on September 2, the team confirmed it in its game-week post on November 24, and it is the fifth straight Thanksgiving in that uniform.

**Will the Eagles wear Kelly Green on Thanksgiving?**

No. The Cowboys' Classic throwback is a navy jersey, so Philadelphia needs to wear white. The Eagles' two Kelly Green dates in 2026 are Week 9 against the Giants and Week 16 against the Texans.

**What are the Rams wearing on Thanksgiving Eve?**

Fearsome White, the new white alternate with the white-horn helmet, against the Packers on Wednesday, November 25. It is on the uniform schedule the Rams published on September 1, the team confirmed it with a look book on November 24 (royal helmet with white horns, white jersey, white pants), and it is the jersey's only game this season. Green Bay has confirmed its green jersey, gold pants and gold helmet.

**Is there an NFL game the night before Thanksgiving in 2026?**

Yes. The Green Bay Packers play at the Los Angeles Rams on Wednesday, November 25 at 8:00 p.m. ET on Netflix, the first Thanksgiving Eve game in NFL history.

**What time are the NFL Thanksgiving games in 2026?**

Bears at Lions at 1:00 p.m. ET on CBS, Eagles at Cowboys at 4:30 p.m. ET on FOX, and Chiefs at Bills at 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC, all on Thursday, November 26.

**What are the Bills wearing against the Chiefs on Thanksgiving?**

Blue over blue: the white helmet, royal blue jersey and royal blue pants with white socks, confirmed in the Bills' "Thanksgiving Threads" post on November 24. We expect Kansas City in white jerseys with red pants.

**Is there a Black Friday NFL game in 2026?**

Yes. The Denver Broncos play at the Pittsburgh Steelers on Friday, November 27 at 3:00 p.m. ET on Prime Video.

**What are the Steelers wearing on Black Friday?**

The all-black Color Rush: black helmet, black jersey with gold numbers, black pants, as the Steelers announced on November 24. It is not the 1933 throwback. Denver wears Summit White, per its published schedule.

**What are the Bears wearing on Thanksgiving?**

We expect white jerseys with the navy helmet at Detroit, Chicago's standard road look. The Bears' only special uniform date this season is the Rivalries debut on Christmas Day.

**Do NFL players wear a special patch on Thanksgiving?**

Yes. The NFL confirmed on November 19, 2026 that every player in the three Thanksgiving Day games wears the [John Madden Thanksgiving patch](/stories/john-madden-thanksgiving-patch-2026), a round patch with a silhouette of Madden on the left chest. It has been worn every Thanksgiving since 2023.
