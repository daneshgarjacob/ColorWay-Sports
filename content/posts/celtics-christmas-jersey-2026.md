We already covered the [full 2026 NBA Christmas jersey collection](/stories/nba-christmas-jerseys-2026-leaked) and gave the overall set a pretty rough grade. Most of those designs missed the mark. But when you pull the Celtics jersey out on its own and really look at it, this one actually holds up better than the rest of the group.

The neon green on a black base just works for Boston. The Celtics have always owned that shade of green, and when you light it up against a dark background, it pops in a way that feels natural for the brand. It does not feel forced the way some of the other teams' Christmas jerseys do. The green aurora-style graphics woven into the fabric have an energy to them that actually fits the Celtics aesthetic.

## What Works

The color combination is the biggest win here. Neon green on black is not something the Celtics wear in their regular rotation, so it actually feels special. That is supposed to be the whole point of a Christmas jersey. It should look different from what you see on a random Tuesday night in January. This one does. The green has enough glow to it that it will read well on camera during the Christmas Day broadcast, and it still feels unmistakably Celtics.

The overall shape of the design is solid too. The graphic elements flow across the jersey in a way that does not look as busy as some of the other leaked designs. For a jersey that is trying to do a lot, it stays relatively clean.

## What Doesn't Work

The number placement is too high on the chest. It sits awkwardly close to the neckline and throws the whole balance of the jersey off. When you look at where the number sits on a standard Celtics jersey, there is a natural spacing that gives everything room to breathe. This one feels cramped up top.

And then there is the Nike swoosh sitting dead center on the chest. We have said this before with other designs and we will keep saying it. The swoosh in the middle of the jersey never looks right. It competes with the number and the team wordmark for attention, and the whole front of the jersey ends up feeling cluttered. Move it back to the shoulder where it belongs.
