Houston published one of the more useful uniform announcements in the league this year: three of its eight home games come with a named jersey, and as of August 25 all three have a face. The Texans open in Liberty White, run the Battle Red game in Week 7, and debuted the brand-new HOUSTON/US Rivalries uniform, the first white helmet in franchise history included, in a 26-3 win over the Colts on Thursday night in Week 11. Here is the full Houston Texans 2026 uniform schedule: the confirmed jersey dates first, then a week-by-week outlook for the Deep Steel Blue home set and the white road set.

**Update, November 27: Week 12 is confirmed, all navy.** Houston (4-6) hosts the **Baltimore Ravens at NRG Stadium on Sunday, November 29, 1:00 p.m. ET on CBS**, and the team's "Deep Steel Sunday" post on November 26 shows the look: **navy helmet, Deep Steel Blue jersey and navy pants**, with the 25th season patch on the chest. It is the first time this season Houston has worn the navy jersey with navy pants. Baltimore's side is on our [Ravens uniform schedule](/stories/ravens-uniform-schedule-2026), where we have the Ravens in white. **Next after that: Week 13 at the Pittsburgh Steelers on Sunday Night Football, December 6, 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC.** Houston has not posted that uniform yet; we expect **white**, because Pittsburgh hosts in color (our [Steelers schedule](/stories/steelers-uniform-schedule-2026) has the 1933 throwback that night, our projection, not a team announcement).

## Liberty White (Week 1 vs. Bills, Confirmed)

Houston opens the season at home against Buffalo on Sunday, September 13, and the Texans wore Liberty White with fans asked to wear white to match. The team's game-week graphic pointed to the red Battle Red helmet, but on the field Houston went with the **standard navy helmet, white jersey and white pants**, the Liberty White set under the navy shell. Buffalo won the opener 36-31.

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; margin: 2em 0;">
  <blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/HoustonTexans/status/2098049349074710805"></a></blockquote>
</div> That is a genuinely notable choice, because a home team wearing white is giving something up.

Here is why it matters beyond the aesthetics. NFL convention lets the home team pick, and the visitor takes the contrasting jersey. By choosing white, Houston hands Buffalo the option of a colored jersey in Week 1, which is the opposite of how almost every home opener in the league is staged. We have updated our [Bills 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/bills-uniform-schedule-2026) to reflect it, and Buffalo has since confirmed its side of it: white helmet, blue jersey and blue pants. The opener is Liberty White against all blue.

As a uniform, Liberty White is the cleanest thing Houston owns. The navy and red striping does enough without crowding, and against a September afternoon under the NRG Stadium roof it should read sharply. Whether it is the right call for an opener is a separate question. The white set is the quiet one, and openers usually want volume.


## The 25th Season Game: 2002 Colors (Week 4 vs. Cowboys, Confirmed)

Houston celebrates its 25th season at home against Dallas on Sunday, October 4, and the Texans are wearing the colors of the night it all started. It is the **navy helmet, white jersey, navy pants and red socks**, the same color blocking Houston wore on September 8, 2002, when it beat the Cowboys 19-10 in the first game in franchise history. The end zones go back to 2002 too, blue paint with white lettering and no logo, as the [Houston Chronicle reported](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/houston-texans-wear-throwback-color-180010351.html) on Wednesday, September 30.

It is a throwback in color rather than cloth. NFL rules on uniform changes keep the actual 2002 jerseys off the field, so Houston recreated the look with what it already owns, and the red socks are the detail that gives it away. Dallas is in its navy jersey for this game, published on its own schedule on September 2, so it is white and navy against navy under the NRG Stadium roof. Our [Cowboys 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/cowboys-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Dallas side, and the [NFL Week 4 uniform guide](/stories/nfl-week-4-uniforms-2026) has every other game.

We like it. White over navy pants is the most balanced version of the Texans uniform, and a flash of Battle Red at the ankle is exactly the right amount of red for a jersey that already carries it in the trim.

## Battle Red (Week 7 vs. Giants, Confirmed)

The Battle Red game returns for Week 7 against the New York Giants on Sunday, October 25, with the crowd asked to wear red and the team in the Battle Red uniforms. It is the best atmosphere on the Houston home schedule and it has been for years.

Battle Red is also the uniform that gives the Texans an identity. The navy home set is fine and slightly anonymous, a dark jersey with red trim in a league full of them. The red set is the one that looks like nobody else, and pairing it with a full-stadium red out is the sort of coordinated staging that most teams talk about and few actually pull off.

Our only complaint is the frequency. One Battle Red game a season is not enough for a uniform this strong, especially when the alternative is another afternoon in navy.


## The "HOUSTON/US" Rivalries Uniform, Revealed (Week 11 vs. Colts)

<img src="/images/posts/texans-uniform-schedule-2026/rivalries-htown-group.jpg" alt="Houston Texans HOUSTON/US Rivalries uniform reveal, players in the Liberty White jersey and helmet with H-Town Blue and Battle Red tile stripes" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px; margin-bottom: 0.5em;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 2em;">via the Houston Texans</p>

The wait is over. Houston's Rivalries uniform dropped Tuesday morning under the name HOUSTON/US, with the tagline "By the H. For the H.," and it is built from the city itself: Liberty White as the base, H-Town Blue and Battle Red doing the accent work, and the blue-and-white street-tile mosaics you see all over Houston running through the stripes, the nameplate, and the numbers. The "Be Someone" bridge inspired the jersey numbers, the chrome facemask is a nod to slab culture and candy paint, and the inner collar reads HOUSTON in tiled letters.

The headline piece is the helmet, the first white shell in Texans history, a tribute to the white prototype the late Robert C. McNair held up in 1999 before the franchise settled on Deep Steel Blue. It carries a gothic H-Town "H" with a star on the side, a tiled red-and-blue center stripe, and an H-TOWN front bumper with a clean border around it, which is exactly the kind of small detail this program is supposed to deliver.

Our first take: we like this a lot. The palette is the win, light, bright, and completely different from the navy-heavy base set, and the chrome blue facemask is the single best facemask in the entire 2026 Rivalries class, even if the shell it is bolted to does not crack our top five. Putting the bullhead logo on the jersey chest is a smart move that keeps the primary mark on the uniform. Our one real quibble is the helmet logo: the gothic H is cool, but we would have preferred the primary bullhead on that white shell, because a first-ever white Texans helmet with the classic logo would have been an instant icon.

## Deep Steel Blue at Home (The Base Set, Expected)

The navy home jersey projects to three home dates: the Bengals in Week 2, the Ravens in Week 12, and the Jaguars in Week 15. **Week 2 was worn:** the navy helmet, Deep Steel Blue jersey and white pants, a 20-6 loss to Cincinnati. **Week 12 against Baltimore is now confirmed by the team** as the navy helmet, Deep Steel Blue jersey and navy pants. Salute to Service against Baltimore is among the announced themes, but none of those dates names a jersey. The regular-season finale against the Titans in Week 18 goes to the Blue Alternate, Houston's second alternate after Battle Red. **Week 4 against the Cowboys is the exception: Dallas published navy for the trip to Houston, and the Texans wear white at home with navy pants and red socks, the colors of their 2002 debut,** while they celebrate the 25th Season.

We said the 25th Season game was the most plausible place for a heritage nod, and Houston delivered one: not the 2002 jerseys, which league rules keep off the field, but the 2002 color combination, detailed in its own section above.


## The White Road Set (Expected)

The white road uniform projects to all nine away games: at the Colts, Titans, Chargers, Browns, Steelers, Commanders, Eagles, Packers, and the Week 6 meeting with Jacksonville at Wembley.

That London game is the wrinkle worth understanding. Houston and Jacksonville meet in Week 6 on Sunday, October 18 at Wembley Stadium, and the Jaguars are the designated home team, which means Houston does not surrender one of its eight home dates and travels as the visitor. Two of the road games are also worth circling for contrast: Week 9 at the Chargers is Los Angeles's [Super Chargers navy game](/stories/chargers-uniform-schedule-2026), and Week 3 at Indianapolis is the Colts' Rivalries debut.


## How the Texans Rotation Works in 2026

Houston's system is three jerseys and a plan. Navy is the default at home, white travels, and the two showcase dates get carved out early: white for the opener, red for the October home game with the best atmosphere. The Rivalries jersey then arrives in November as a fourth look, and the Blue Alternate closes the regular season against Tennessee in Week 18, which gives the season a genuine build rather than front-loading everything.

What we like about it is the spacing. Weeks 1, 7, and 11 are far enough apart that each one lands as an event, and none of them competes with another. What we would change is the balance. Battle Red gets one date and navy gets three, and that ratio is backwards for a team whose red uniform is the one people actually want to see.
