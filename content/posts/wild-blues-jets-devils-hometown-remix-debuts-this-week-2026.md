**Four more Hometown Remix jerseys hit the ice this week, and three of them come out on the same night.** The **Minnesota Wild**, **St. Louis Blues** and **Winnipeg Jets** all wear their Remix sweaters for the first time on **Tuesday, Nov. 17**, and the **New Jersey Devils** follow on **Wednesday, Nov. 18** against Montreal. All four are dark jerseys: black for Minnesota, black and blue for St. Louis, navy for Winnipeg and black and red for New Jersey. All four debuts are home games, so every visitor is expected in white.

After this week, only three of the 32 teams have not worn their Remix yet: Dallas (Nov. 25) and the Red Wings and Rangers, who have not posted a date.

## Quick Answers

- **Which NHL teams debut Hometown Remix jerseys this week?** Wild, Blues and Jets on Tuesday, Nov. 17. Devils on Wednesday, Nov. 18.
- **Jets vs. Senators:** Tuesday, 7 p.m. Eastern (6 p.m. Central) at Canada Life Centre. Jets in navy, Senators expected in white.
- **Blues vs. Ducks:** Tuesday, 8 p.m. Eastern (7 p.m. Central) at Enterprise Center. Blues in black, Ducks expected in white.
- **Wild vs. Predators:** Tuesday, 8 p.m. Eastern (7 p.m. Central) at Grand Casino Arena. Wild in black, Predators expected in white.
- **Devils vs. Canadiens:** Wednesday, 7:30 p.m. Eastern at Prudential Center. Devils in black and red, Canadiens expected in their regular white road sweater. Nov. 18 is not on Montreal's Remix list.
- **Best of the four:** the Devils. **Worst:** the Jets.

## Minnesota Wild: Black, With a Night Sky Logo

The Wild's Remix is **black** with green and white trim. The team says the idea is Minnesota at night:

- **Crest:** the Wild logo, with a **crescent moon** where the sun usually sits.
- **Sleeves:** the **Big Dipper and Little Dipper** (Ursa Major and Ursa Minor) are debossed into the fabric.
- **Numbers:** two small stars in every number, one for each of the **Twin Cities**.
- **Trim:** the studio photo shows a green lace-up collar, green and white stripes on the sleeves and hem, and **MINNESOTA** printed inside the hem.

Minnesota wears it **10 times**. After Tuesday: Nov. 27 vs. Colorado, Dec. 10 at Columbus, Dec. 12 vs. Winnipeg, Dec. 26 vs. Winnipeg, Jan. 2 vs. St. Louis, Jan. 14 vs. Florida, Jan. 21 vs. Detroit, Feb. 23 vs. Pittsburgh and March 19 vs. Calgary. Full list on the [Wild uniform schedule](/stories/minnesota-wild-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

## St. Louis Blues: Black, Blue and Silver

The Blues' Remix is **black**, which the team ties to the dark water of the Mississippi. The team says it is the **first time black and silver have appeared together on a Blues uniform**.

- **Crest:** the round **ST. LOUIS BLUES** crest from the navy alternate the Blues wore from 2008 to 2016, updated with the **Gateway Arch** around the Blue Note and **three wave lines** for the river.
- **Sleeves:** a debossed wave pattern, with blue and silver stripes.
- **Numbers:** a new beveled style meant to look like the Arch's steel.
- **Hem:** the city name sits under a drawing of the Arch, as if you were looking up at it.

St. Louis wears it **10 times**, nine at home: Nov. 17 vs. Anaheim, Nov. 22 vs. Edmonton, Nov. 25 at the Islanders (the one road date), Nov. 27 vs. Dallas, Dec. 2 vs. Pittsburgh, Dec. 13 vs. Tampa Bay, Jan. 26 vs. Chicago, Jan. 30 and Feb. 25 vs. Vancouver, and March 13 vs. Anaheim. So the Blues wear it **twice this week**. Full list on the [Blues uniform schedule](/stories/blues-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

## Winnipeg Jets: Navy With Northern Lights Sleeves

Winnipeg's Remix is the **northern lights over Manitoba**. The body is a dark night-sky **navy**.

- **Crest:** the Jets roundel, lit up in bright blue as if the aurora were shining on it.
- **Sleeves:** a fade from **blue to light green**, like the aurora moving across a winter sky.
- **Hem:** a full aurora scene printed inside.
- **Names and numbers:** a soft, blended effect so they look like they glow through the fabric.

Winnipeg wears it **eight times, all at home**: Nov. 17 vs. Ottawa, Nov. 21 vs. Vegas, Nov. 23 vs. Utah, Dec. 10 vs. Chicago, Jan. 19 vs. Minnesota, Jan. 21 vs. the Rangers, March 23 vs. St. Louis and March 25 vs. Colorado. That is **three games in seven days** to start. Full list on the [Jets uniform schedule](/stories/winnipeg-jets-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

## New Jersey Devils: Black and Red, No Circle

The Devils' Remix is **black with red**. It takes the logo from the black sweater New Jersey wore at the February 2024 Stadium Series against the Flyers, reverses the colors and makes a few changes. The big **red NJ** comes out of its circle and sits alone on the chest. The studio photo shows **angled red stripes** on both sleeves and a red hem. The team's teaser called it the **"Black Parade."**

It takes over the black alternate slot from the black "Jersey" sweater, which the Devils retired. New Jersey wears it **10 times, all at home**: Nov. 18 vs. Montreal, Nov. 27 vs. Calgary, Nov. 28 vs. Winnipeg, Dec. 15 vs. the Rangers, Dec. 17 vs. Toronto, Jan. 16 vs. the Islanders, Jan. 19 vs. Dallas, Feb. 23 vs. Carolina, March 23 vs. Philadelphia and March 29 vs. Carolina. Full list on the [Devils uniform schedule](/stories/devils-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

## Our Grades

**Devils: C+.** The best of the four, and it is not close. Red on black is the Devils' best color combination, the NJ is big enough to read from the upper deck, and the angled sleeve stripes give it some edge. It still plays it safe. A team with a green-and-red history had room to try something bolder. We have it [No. 23 of 32](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-jerseys-ranked).

**Blues: C+.** Clean, and the round Arch crest is a good logo. But it is a black jersey with blue stripes, and black was never a Blues color. Swap it for the navy the crest came from and it is a B. No. 20 in our ranking.

**Wild: C.** The moon and the Dipper are clever details nobody will see on TV. From the stands it is a black Wild jersey with the usual logo. Minnesota has a great red-and-green history and skipped it. No. 25.

**Jets: C-.** The best idea of the four on paper and the worst result. The northern lights should have been the whole jersey. Instead it is a dark navy sweater, close to the Jets' regular home blue, with a little color on the arms. No. 29.

**Best matchup of the week: Devils vs. Canadiens, B.** Black and red against Montreal's red, white and blue reads well, and it is the only debut against an Original Six team. **Worst: Jets vs. Senators, C-,** a dark navy jersey that will look a lot like Winnipeg's normal home look on TV.

## FAQ

### Which NHL teams debut their Hometown Remix jerseys this week?

The Minnesota Wild, St. Louis Blues and Winnipeg Jets on Tuesday, Nov. 17, 2026, and the New Jersey Devils on Wednesday, Nov. 18.

### When do the Wild wear their Hometown Remix jersey for the first time?

Tuesday, Nov. 17, 2026, at home against the Nashville Predators at 7 p.m. Central.

### What does the Wild Hometown Remix jersey look like?

Black, with a crescent moon in place of the sun in the Wild logo, the Big Dipper and Little Dipper debossed on the sleeves, two stars in every number for the Twin Cities, and green and white stripes.

### When do the Blues debut their Hometown Remix jersey?

Tuesday, Nov. 17, 2026, at home against the Anaheim Ducks at 7 p.m. Central. They wear it again Sunday, Nov. 22 against Edmonton.

### What does the Blues Hometown Remix jersey look like?

Black with blue and silver trim, a round ST. LOUIS BLUES crest with the Gateway Arch and three Mississippi River wave lines, debossed waves on the sleeves and beveled numbers. The team says it is the first Blues uniform to pair black and silver.

### When do the Jets wear their northern lights jersey?

The debut is Tuesday, Nov. 17, 2026, against the Ottawa Senators at Canada Life Centre. Winnipeg wears it eight times, all at home, including Nov. 21 vs. Vegas and Nov. 23 vs. Utah.

### What colors is the Jets Hometown Remix jersey?

Night-sky navy, with sleeves that fade from blue to light green like the northern lights, a crest and numbers that look lit up, and an aurora scene inside the hem.

### When do the Devils debut their black Hometown Remix jersey?

Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026, at Prudential Center against the Montreal Canadiens, at 7:30 p.m. Eastern. It is worn 10 times, all at home.

### Is the Devils Remix the same as the Stadium Series jersey?

No. It is based on the black jersey from the February 2024 Stadium Series against Philadelphia, with the colors reversed and the NJ logo pulled out of its circle.

### Are the Canadiens wearing their Remix jersey at New Jersey?

No. Nov. 18 is not one of Montreal's 10 Remix dates. The Canadiens are expected in white.

### Which NHL teams have not worn their Hometown Remix yet?

After this week, the Dallas Stars (debut Nov. 25 vs. Nashville), Detroit Red Wings and New York Rangers. Detroit and New York have not posted a debut date.

### Which of this week's Remix jerseys is the best?

We like the Devils' black and red best and the Jets' navy least.
