## Why Bringing Back Mustard Yellow Is the Right Call for Houston

The original Houston Rockets uniform from 1972 through 1995, red and mustard yellow and white with the iconic stretched HOUSTON wordmark on the chest, is the most beloved single look in Houston Rockets uniform history. Fans call it the ketchup and mustard era. The mustard yellow was the layering color that gave those uniforms their identity. Without the mustard, the look is just red and white. With it, the Rockets uniforms read as one of the most distinctive looks in modern NBA uniform history.

The current Houston Rockets identity has been red, black, white, and silver since the franchise updated the look ahead of the 2019-20 season. The black and silver direction modernized the uniforms but pulled the Rockets away from the warmth that defined the franchise's championship era. Adding mustard yellow back to the rotation puts the actual Houston Rockets color story front and center again, the layered red and mustard combination that ran on Hakeem's chest during back-to-back NBA title runs.
