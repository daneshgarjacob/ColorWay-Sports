**Purple is back in Louisville.** The **Louisville Bats**, the Triple-A affiliate of the Cincinnati Reds, unveiled a **City Connect uniform for 2027** on Tuesday, November 17: a **purple jersey with white pinstripes**, a **Derby City** wordmark across the chest and a **white fleur-de-lis** underneath. It is the first time since **2015** that purple will be a regular part of the Bats' on-field look.

The Bats will wear it at **every Friday home game in 2027**, a promotion the team is calling **Purple Fridays**. The first one is **Friday, April 2, against the Indianapolis Indians** at Louisville Slugger Field.

## Quick Answers

- **What is the Louisville Bats City Connect jersey?** A purple jersey with white pinstripes, a Derby City wordmark, a white fleur-de-lis, a bourbon barrel stripe across the middle and diamond-shaped jockey silk patches on both sleeves.
- **What color is it?** Purple, with white pinstripes and white lettering. The cap is purple too.
- **When do the Bats wear it?** Every Friday home game in 2027. The debut is April 2 against Indianapolis.
- **Why purple?** Purple was a staple of the Bats' uniforms in the team's early years at Louisville Slugger Field, and the team says fans have been asking for it back.
- **Who designed it?** Bats graphic designer Max Oxley, who also created the Louisville Humidity alternate identity that debuted in 2026.
- **Can you buy it?** Yes. City Connect merchandise is on sale online and at the team store at Louisville Slugger Field.

## What The Derby City Uniform Looks Like

Every detail here comes from the Bats' announcement.

**The jersey.** A **purple base with white pinstripes**. The team calls it a nod to its earlier **white jerseys with purple pinstripes**, so the new jersey is the old one flipped: the purple moves from the stripes to the body. **Derby City**, a nickname tied to Louisville for more than a century, runs across the chest. The team says it is the first time that wordmark has appeared on a primary jersey.

**The fleur-de-lis.** A **white fleur-de-lis** sits under the wordmark. It is one of Louisville's best-known symbols and the centerpiece of the city's official seal.

**The bourbon barrel stripe.** A **horizontal stripe runs across the middle of the jersey** and crosses the pinstripes. The team says the crossing lines are meant to look like the metal hoops and wooden staves that hold a bourbon barrel together.

**The sleeves.** Both sleeves carry **diamond-shaped patches** with the Bats lettering and secondary logos, modeled on the colorful **jockey silks** worn in the Kentucky Derby.

**The cap.** A **purple cap** with a purple version of the Bats' new secondary logo. A version of this cap was first sold as the team's batting practice cap before the 2026 season.

The Bats have not posted a side-by-side of the old purple-pinstripe jersey, and we are not going to put a retail or fan photo in its place. We will add the team's own picture of the old set if they share one.

"Purple is a big part of our history, and we felt this was the time to bring it back," Bats president **Greg Galiette** said in the announcement. "Every detail tells a piece of Louisville's story."

Designer **Max Oxley** said the goal was to bring purple back "while incorporating our city," calling it "a modern design take on the purple from our past."

## Our Take

This is what a minor league City Connect should be. Every piece points at one city: Derby City, the fleur-de-lis from the seal, a bourbon barrel and jockey silks. None of it could be moved to another team.

The flip from white-with-purple-pinstripes to purple-with-white-pinstripes is the smartest move in the set. Fans who remember the old jersey will recognize it right away, and it still looks new. The one thing to watch is the barrel stripe. A horizontal band through pinstripes can get busy, and how it reads from the stands on April 2 will decide whether this is very good or great.

It also gives Louisville three distinct looks in two years: the regular Bats set, the Louisville Humidity weather identity from 2026 and now purple on Fridays. The Bats were already one of the most creative clubs in Triple-A. Their [Nothing Night, with no music and no ads](/stories/louisville-bats-nothing-night-mlb-should-copy-2026), is still the best idea we saw in the minors this year. The parent club's look is in our [Reds 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/reds-uniform-schedule-2026), and the Reds' own City Connect is in [our breakdown of it](/stories/reds-city-connect-2026-official).

## Purple Fridays In 2027

- **First Purple Friday:** April 2, 2027, vs. the Indianapolis Indians.
- **Opening Night:** Tuesday, March 30, vs. Indianapolis at Louisville Slugger Field.
- **Every Friday at home:** live music, drink specials, giveaways and fireworks. The team's purple page adds that $1 beer specials and postgame fireworks return every Friday.
- **Purple Plan:** a ticket package for the first 10 Purple Fridays that includes a replica jersey with each ticket.
