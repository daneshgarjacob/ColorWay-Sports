**The Governor's Cup is a color rush this year: Kentucky in all blue, Louisville in all red.** Kentucky Football confirmed it on **Friday, November 27**, posting a "first look of tomorrow's color rush Governor's Cup matchup" in EA Sports College Football, with the Wildcats in **blue helmets, blue jerseys and blue pants** and the Cardinals in **red helmets, red jerseys and red pants**. The game is **Saturday, November 28, at noon Eastern on ESPN2**, at Kroger Field in Lexington. Usually the road team in this rivalry wears white. Not this time.

## Quick Answers

- **What is Kentucky wearing against Louisville?** All blue: blue helmet, blue jersey, blue pants. **Confirmed** by Kentucky Football on November 27.

## What Kentucky Is Wearing

**Blue helmet, blue jersey, blue pants.** In Kentucky's preview, the helmet is blue with the white UK logo, the jersey is royal blue with white numbers, and the pants are blue. It is the Wildcats' home jersey, but the full blue set is a change from how Kentucky usually dresses at Kroger Field. From our [Kentucky 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/kentucky-uniform-schedule-2026), the home looks so far have included the **Power K throwback** with white pants against LSU, **gray over gray** against Vanderbilt and the all-black **Night Ops** set against Florida. Kentucky last played at Missouri on November 21, in a blue helmet, white jersey and blue pants, and lost 28-14.

## What Louisville Is Wearing

**Red helmet, red jersey, red pants.** Kentucky's preview shows the Cardinals in red from top to bottom, with LOUISVILLE across the chest. Louisville has not posted its own uniform graphic yet; its game-week post on Friday was a teaser, "Next chapter of the Governor's Cup." We will add the team's own post when it lands.

Here is why this one is different. Our [Louisville 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/louisville-uniform-schedule-2026) logs every game, and the Cardinals have worn **white in every true road game this season**:

| Date | Game | Louisville wore |
|---|---|---|
| Oct. 3 | at NC State | Red helmet, white jersey, white pants |
| Oct. 17 | at Syracuse | White helmet, white jersey, red pants |
| Nov. 7 | at Georgia Tech | Black helmet, white jersey, black pants |
| Nov. 14 | at North Carolina | Red helmet, white jersey, red pants |
| **Nov. 28** | **at Kentucky** | **Red helmet, red jersey, red pants** |

So the red jersey travels for the first time this season, in the one road game where both fan bases will notice.

## Our Grade

**A-.** Blue against red is the whole rivalry in two colors, and a color-on-color game is the right way to play it. Both sets are clean, both teams go monochrome, and nobody wears white to dull it. It is not an A because the looks themselves are each team's standard colors with matching pants, not anything new. It is the matchup that makes it.
