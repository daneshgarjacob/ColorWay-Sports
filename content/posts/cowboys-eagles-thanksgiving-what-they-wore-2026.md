**The Cowboys wore the 1960s Classic throwback on Thanksgiving for the fifth year in a row, and for the first time they lost in it: Eagles 39, Cowboys 38.** Dallas wore exactly what it posted in game week: the **white helmet with the navy star**, the **navy jersey with navy stars on white shoulders**, and **white pants**. Philadelphia wore the **midnight green helmet with silver wings, the white road jersey and midnight green pants**. The Eagles trailed **31-10** late in the third quarter and won on Jake Elliott's **59-yard field goal with two seconds left**. Both uniforms are in the Eagles' final-score photo below.

## Quick Answers

- **What did the Cowboys wear on Thanksgiving 2026?** The 1960s Classic: white helmet with a navy star and navy-white-navy center stripe, navy jersey with white shoulders carrying navy stars, white pants.
- **Is that the Cowboys' Thanksgiving throwback every year?** Five straight years now: 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026.
- **What was Dallas's record in the Classic on Thanksgiving?** **4-0** going in. After Thursday it is **4-1**.
- **What did the Eagles wear?** Midnight green helmet with silver wings, white jersey with midnight green numbers, midnight green pants. Not Kelly Green.
- **Did both teams wear the John Madden patch?** Yes. It is on both jerseys in the Eagles' final-score photo.
- **Final score?** Eagles 39, Cowboys 38. Philadelphia is 4-7, Dallas 5-7.
- **Our matchup grade:** A-, the grade we gave it before kickoff.

## The Classic Finally Lost On Thanksgiving

The Cowboys brought the Classic back for Thanksgiving in 2022, the first season the NFL allowed a second helmet again. Until Thursday it had never lost on the holiday. Scores from ESPN:

| Year | Opponent | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 2022 | Giants | **W 28-20** |
| 2023 | Commanders | **W 45-10** |
| 2024 | Giants | **W 27-20** |
| 2025 | Chiefs | **W 31-28** |
| 2026 | Eagles | **L 38-39** |

So the Classic is now **4-1 on Thanksgiving**. Dallas had a 21-point lead in it this time and still lost.

## How Each Team Did In These Uniforms This Season

From our own [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026), before Thursday:

- **Cowboys:** 1-6 in the white home jersey, 2-0 in the all-white Color Rush, 2-0 in navy. The Classic loss makes Dallas **4-1 in a dark or alternate look and 1-6 in its regular home white**. The full numbers are in [the NFL uniforms that haven't lost in 2026](/stories/nfl-uniforms-undefeated-and-winless-2026).
- **Eagles:** 0-2 in the white jersey with midnight green pants, at Chicago and at Washington. Thursday was the first win in that combination, making it **1-2**.

## The Game

Eagles 39, Cowboys 38, at AT&T Stadium. Per ESPN, Dallas led 28-10 at halftime and 31-10 after Brandon Aubrey's 59-yard field goal in the third quarter. Joe Milton threw for 331 yards and three touchdowns, two of them to DeVonta Smith (11 catches, 200 yards). Kenny Moore II returned a blocked punt for a touchdown, Milton ran one in, and his 24-yard touchdown pass to Makai Lemon cut it to 38-36 with 2:10 left. Elliott won it from 59 yards with two seconds on the clock. Javonte Williams scored three touchdowns for Dallas, and CeeDee Lamb had 134 receiving yards.

## Our Take

This is the best-looking game on the Thanksgiving schedule every year Dallas wears the Classic. Navy and white with stars on the shoulders against midnight green and white has no clash anywhere, and the white helmet is the right call for the one day a year people who never watch the Cowboys are watching. We gave it an **A-** before kickoff and it played to it. Our one complaint is the same one we have every year: the Classic is better than anything else in the Dallas closet, and it gets one game.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What did the Cowboys wear on Thanksgiving 2026?**

The 1960s Classic throwback: a white helmet with the navy star, a navy jersey with navy stars on white shoulders, and white pants.

**Why do the Cowboys wear throwbacks on Thanksgiving?**

Dallas has worn the Classic on Thanksgiving every year since 2022, when the NFL started allowing a second helmet shell again, because the Classic needs its own white helmet. More in [why NFL teams wear throwbacks on Thanksgiving](/stories/why-do-nfl-teams-wear-throwbacks-on-thanksgiving).

**What is the Cowboys' record in the Classic throwback on Thanksgiving?**

4-1: wins in 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025, and the 39-38 loss to the Eagles in 2026.

**What did the Eagles wear against the Cowboys on Thanksgiving?**

The midnight green helmet with silver wings, the white road jersey and midnight green pants.

**Why didn't the Eagles wear Kelly Green on Thanksgiving?**

Because the Cowboys were in a navy jersey, Philadelphia had to wear white. The Eagles wore Kelly Green in Dallas in 2025 only because the Cowboys were in white that year.

**Who won Eagles vs. Cowboys on Thanksgiving 2026?**

The Eagles, 39-38, on a 59-yard Jake Elliott field goal with two seconds left, after trailing 31-10.

**Did the Cowboys and Eagles wear the John Madden patch?**

Yes. Both teams' jerseys carry the round Madden patch in the Eagles' final-score photo.

**Is the Cowboys' Thanksgiving helmet white?**

Yes. The Classic helmet is white with a navy star and a navy-white-navy center stripe. The regular Cowboys helmet is silver.

**How many Thanksgivings in a row have the Cowboys worn the Classic?**

Five: 2022 through 2026.
