The 2026 NBA Finals are set on one side. The New York Knicks swept the Cleveland Cavaliers 4-0 in the Eastern Conference Finals to lock the franchise's first NBA Finals trip since 1999. The other side is still up for grabs. The Oklahoma City Thunder lead the San Antonio Spurs 3-2 in the Western Conference Finals with Game 6 tonight at Frost Bank Center, Spurs in black trying to force a Game 7 back in Oklahoma City. We previewed what Game 1 of the 2026 NBA Finals at Madison Square Garden looks like under both possible jersey and uniform matchups: Knicks vs Thunder or Knicks vs Spurs.

## The Eastern Conference Champion Is Locked

The Knicks are the 2026 Eastern Conference Champions and the confirmed home team for Game 1 of the 2026 NBA Finals at Madison Square Garden. The home Game 1 uniform pairing for New York is the Association Edition white jersey, the team's traditional home uniform with the cursive NEW YORK wordmark and orange and blue trim. The 2026 NBA Finals patch debuts on the back of the jersey at the top center just below the collar, a placement change from the upper-left chest position the Finals patch has lived in for most recent Finals. We broke down the back-patch placement in detail in our [Knicks 2026 NBA Finals jersey first-look post](/stories/knicks-2026-nba-finals-jersey-back-patch-first-look).

The Madison Square Garden floor is already painted with the 2026 NBA Finals court branding as well, the league bringing the dedicated Finals court logo back at center court after a multi-year break, full breakdown in our [2026 NBA Finals court branding post](/stories/nba-finals-court-logo-painted-2026). The home half of the Finals matchup is fully designed and ready. The visitor's half decides tonight at Frost Bank Center.

## Tonight Decides the West

Game 6 of the Western Conference Finals tips off tonight at Frost Bank Center with the Thunder leading the series 3-2 and the Spurs facing elimination at home. San Antonio is wearing the Icon Edition black uniform for the elimination night per Spurs convention for high-stakes home games at the Frost Bank Center. The Thunder are on the road in Association road colors (the league's road white was modified to allow Thunder home-color road games but Oklahoma City typically holds the Icon blue or Association white for the WCF road slots).

A Thunder win tonight clinches the Western Conference and locks in Scenario A: Knicks vs Thunder at MSG for Game 1 of the 2026 NBA Finals. A Spurs win forces Game 7 back in Oklahoma City Sunday night with the Western Conference Champion (and the Finals visitor for Game 1 at MSG) decided there.

Regardless of whether Thunder or Spurs survives the West, the home half of the Finals matchup at Madison Square Garden is fully locked in design language. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**Who are the 2026 NBA Finals teams?**

The New York Knicks are the 2026 Eastern Conference Champions after a 4-0 sweep of the Cleveland Cavaliers in the Eastern Conference Finals. The Western Conference Final is still ongoing with the Oklahoma City Thunder leading the San Antonio Spurs 3-2 entering Game 6 on 2026-05-28 at Frost Bank Center. The Western Conference Champion will be the Knicks's 2026 NBA Finals opponent.

**Where will Game 1 of the 2026 NBA Finals be played?**

Game 1 of the 2026 NBA Finals will be at Madison Square Garden in New York City. The Knicks have home court advantage for the 2026 NBA Finals as the higher-seeded team out of the Eastern Conference, so the Finals begin and end at MSG with the 2-2-1-1-1 home-away series format.

**What jersey will the Knicks wear in Game 1 of the 2026 NBA Finals?**

The New York Knicks will wear the Association Edition white home uniform with the 2026 NBA Finals patch on the back of the jersey at the top center, just below the collar and directly above the player nameplate. The Association white is the Knicks traditional home uniform with the cursive NEW YORK wordmark in blue and orange trim.

**What jersey will the Thunder wear in Game 1 if they win the Western Conference Finals?**

If the Oklahoma City Thunder advance to the 2026 NBA Finals, they will wear the Icon Edition blue road uniform in Game 1 at Madison Square Garden. The Icon blue is the Thunder's primary color road jersey with the THUNDER wordmark across the chest in white with orange trim.

**What jersey will the Spurs wear in Game 1 if they win the Western Conference Finals?**

If the San Antonio Spurs come back to win the 2026 Western Conference Finals, they will wear the Icon Edition black road uniform in Game 1 at Madison Square Garden. The Icon black is the Spurs traditional road jersey with the SPURS wordmark in white and silver trim, the same uniform San Antonio is wearing for Game 6 elimination night tonight at Frost Bank Center.

**Will the Spurs wear the Fiesta City Edition jersey in the 2026 NBA Finals if they advance?**

The San Antonio Spurs have rotated the Fiesta colorway City Edition jersey at home in San Antonio across the 2026 Western Conference Finals, but City Edition uniform usage in the NBA Finals depends on league approval per game. If the Spurs advance to the 2026 NBA Finals, they could request to wear the Fiesta City Edition for select games, but the default expectation is the Icon Edition black on the road in New York.
