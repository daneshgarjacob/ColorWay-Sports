The Carolina Hurricanes are Stanley Cup champions. For the first time since 2006, and only the second time in franchise history, Carolina lifted the Cup — closing out the Vegas Golden Knights 4-2 with a 3-0 shutout in Game 6 on the road at T-Mobile Arena, Brandon Bussi stopping all 22 shots, Taylor Hall and Jackson Blake scoring and Nikolaj Ehlers sealing it into the empty net. The Canes finished the playoffs 16-3, the fewest games any team has needed to win the Cup since the 1988 Oilers.

The official 2026 Stanley Cup Champions locker room gear — the New Era championship caps and Fanatics tees the players pulled on as they passed the Cup around the visitors' room in Las Vegas — is on shelves now. Below is where to buy each piece, plus our first-look read on the set. Championship gear moves fastest in the first 48 hours, and Raleigh has waited 20 years for this one.
