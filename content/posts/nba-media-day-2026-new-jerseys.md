NBA Media Day 2026 is the day the offseason becomes real. Every team opened its doors on Monday, September 28, and for the first time we got to see the summer's biggest moves in their new jerseys: LeBron James in Philadelphia blue, Giannis Antetokounmpo in Miami white, Kawhi Leonard back in Toronto red and Ja Morant in Portland. Here is every first look we saw (the full list of number changes is in our [NBA new jersey numbers guide](/stories/nba-new-jersey-numbers-2026-27)), with the numbers, the uniform each player wore, and the new jersey patches that showed up with them.

Every photo below comes from the team's own account.

## LeBron James, Philadelphia 76ers: No. 23, Icon Blue

The headline act. LeBron posed in the royal blue "PHILA" Icon jersey with **No. 23**, the number we explained [when he signed](/stories/lebron-james-76ers-jersey-number). The Sixers' group shot put him next to **Jaylen Brown in No. 7**, Joel Embiid and Tyrese Maxey, all in blue, and the caption was just "no caption needed." Fair.

The other new thing on that jersey is the patch. **Bloom Energy is the Sixers' new jersey patch partner**, announced Monday morning, and it is on every blue jersey in the photo. LeBron wore the Icon, not the new Specter uniform Philadelphia gets this season. We break that one down in our [Specter Edition guide](/stories/nba-specter-edition-jerseys-2026-27).

## Giannis Antetokounmpo, Miami Heat: No. 7, Association White

Giannis in anything other than Bucks green still looks strange, and that is the point of media day. He wore the white "HEAT" Association jersey with **No. 7**, walking in next to Bam Adebayo.

## LaMelo Ball, Minnesota Timberwolves: No. 1, the New White

This was the uniform story of the day. It was the first look at Minnesota's [full rebrand](/stories/timberwolves-officially-release-new-uniforms-2026-27) on actual players, and LaMelo modeled the new white "WOLVES" Association with blue and green trim and the new belt buckle. The rebrand is the best work any NBA team has done in years, and it looks even better on a body than on a hanger.

## Julius Randle, Brooklyn Nets: No. 30, Icon Black

Randle is Brooklyn's new frontcourt anchor, and the Nets posted their first look at him in the black "BROOKLYN" Icon in **No. 30**. ## Our Verdict: The Best New Look of Media Day

**LaMelo Ball in the new Timberwolves white.** Everyone else is a great player in a familiar jersey. Minnesota is the only team that showed up with a genuinely new uniform, and it was the best-looking thing in the league on Monday. Runner-up: LeBron in Sixers blue, if only because it still does not look real.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When is NBA Media Day 2026?**

Monday, September 28, 2026, for most teams. Training camp practices start the next day, and the regular season opens October 20.

**What number is LeBron James wearing on the 76ers?**

No. 23. He wore the blue Icon jersey with No. 23 at Sixers media day.

**What number is Giannis wearing on the Heat?**

No. 7. He wore the white Association jersey at Heat media day.

**What number is Ja Morant wearing on the Trail Blazers?**

No. 1. He wore No. 12 in Memphis and switched to 1 in Portland.

**What is the new 76ers jersey patch?**

Bloom Energy. The Sixers announced it on Media Day, and it was on the jerseys in their team photos.

Alibaba, starting with the 2026-27 season, the Nets' 60th.
