The 2026-27 season is the tenth year of Nike's NBA City Edition program, and instead of thirty new designs, every team is bringing back a favorite from its own first nine years. That makes this collection a league-wide vote on which City Editions actually worked, decided by the teams themselves.

The full lineup has now surfaced. Atlanta and Washington confirmed their picks earlier this year, Indiana's has been reported, and the rest appeared in a leaked set of Nike renders at the end of August. We graded all thirty and ranked them from worst to best, so the best City Edition in the collection is at the bottom of the page.

## When Do the 2026-27 NBA City Edition Jerseys Come Out?

Not on September 15, which is what nearly everyone expected. Every NBA club teased that date in August, and it turned out to be the launch of Nike's **Standard Issue** apparel collection, the warmup and practice gear, rather than the City Edition uniforms. We broke that down in [what the 09.15.26 teaser actually was](/stories/nba-september-15-2026-uniform-reveal).

The City Edition uniforms are expected to reach stores in **January 2027**, later than the usual November drop. Clubs have been confirming their designs one at a time, and we will update each entry below as they do.

Two picks stretch the brief. Boston and Cleveland both went back to their **2018-19 Earned Edition** uniforms, the alternates teams won by making the playoffs, rather than an actual past City Edition.

And the leaked designs from the spring are not in this ranking at all. The eight dark uniforms that circulated as "Christmas jerseys" are the new **Specter Edition**, a separate fifth uniform for eight of the oldest franchises, which we grade on its own page: [NBA Specter Edition Jerseys 2026-27](/stories/nba-specter-edition-jerseys-2026-27).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best NBA City Edition jersey for 2026-27?**

The Indiana Pacers' Moments Mixtape, which we grade an A. It is a white jersey with a royal blue diagonal band and gold PACERS lettering that remixes two of the franchise's best eras. The Detroit Pistons' red Mixtape set is second at B+, and the Denver Nuggets' white Rainbow Skyline is third.

**What is the worst NBA City Edition jersey for 2026-27?**

The Dallas Mavericks' white and gold "Legacy Is Everything" set is last, graded F. The Atlanta Hawks' MLK uniform is 29th, also an F: the tribute behind it is meaningful, but the uniform itself is a plain black shirt with a wordmark.

**When were the 2026-27 NBA City Edition jerseys revealed?**

There has been no single league-wide reveal. The Hawks confirmed their pick on MLK Day in January 2026 and the Wizards confirmed theirs in March. Most of the other designs surfaced in a leaked lineup at the end of August. The September 15 date every team teased was the Nike Standard Issue apparel launch, not City Edition.

**When do the 2026-27 City Edition jerseys go on sale?**

January 2027, according to reporting on the anniversary collection. That is later than the usual November City Edition retail drop.

**Are the 2026-27 City Edition jerseys new designs?**

No. 2026-27 is the tenth season of the program, and every team is bringing back a design from its own history instead of creating a new one. The 2026-27 versions add an iridescent aluminium-finish Swoosh and move the NBA logo to the back neck.

**Why are the Celtics and Cavaliers City Editions Earned Editions?**

Both teams chose to bring back their 2018-19 Earned Edition uniforms, the alternates Nike gave to playoff teams that season, rather than a past City Edition. Boston's is kelly green with gold lettering, and Cleveland's is white fading into light blue with a navy script.

**Which NBA City Edition jerseys are officially confirmed for 2026-27?**

Two: the Atlanta Hawks' MLK uniform and the Washington Wizards' Cherry Blossom uniform, now called City in Bloom. The Indiana Pacers' Moments Mixtape has been reported. The rest come from the leaked lineup and have not been shown by the teams yet.

**Are the leaked NBA Christmas jerseys City Editions?**

No. The eight dark designs that leaked in April as "2026 Christmas jerseys" are the new Nike Specter Edition, a separate uniform for the Lakers, Celtics, Warriors, Knicks, 76ers, Pistons, Hawks and Kings. Those teams still have a City Edition as well.

## The Bottom Line

A greatest-hits year should produce the best City Edition collection ever, and at the top it does. Indiana, Detroit, Denver and San Antonio all picked designs that are local, rooted in their own history and still look like their teams.

The bottom of the list is a reminder of what the program got wrong for nine years. Too many clubs went back to black shirts with thin outline lettering, and a few picked designs that hide their own colors. The anniversary gave every team a free choice, and the ones that chose their own identity won.
