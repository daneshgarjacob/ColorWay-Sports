**There have been 18 NHL Winter Classics, and 36 jerseys. We ranked every one.** The new 2027 sweaters for the Utah Mammoth and Colorado Avalanche came out today, so this is the full list, from the first game in the snow in Buffalo on New Year's Day 2008 to New Year's Eve 2026 at Rice-Eccles Stadium in Salt Lake City.

Every jersey is shown with the team's own post or a free-license photo of a player wearing it in the game, except the 2008 Sabres and 2009 Red Wings, where we could not find one we are allowed to use. The full breakdown of the new Utah and Colorado sweaters is in our [2027 Winter Classic jerseys story](/stories/utah-mammoth-colorado-avalanche-winter-classic-jerseys-2027), and every event logo is ranked in our [Winter Classic logo history](/stories/nhl-winter-classic-2027-logo-history-ranked).

How we ranked them: design first, history second. A straight copy of an old jersey loses points to a new idea that looks old. Wearing white to a party loses points. Doing the same thing twice loses points. Disagree? That's the point.

## Quick Answers

- **What is the best Winter Classic jersey ever?** The 2008 Pittsburgh Penguins powder blue, worn by Sidney Crosby when he won the first Winter Classic in a shootout in the snow in Buffalo.
- **What is the worst Winter Classic jersey ever?** Our pick is the 2025 St. Louis Blues sweater, the first Blues jersey with no Blue Note on the chest.
- **Who plays in the 2027 Winter Classic?** The Utah Mammoth host the Colorado Avalanche on Dec. 31, 2026, at Rice-Eccles Stadium in Salt Lake City, at 6 p.m. ET.
- **Where do the new 2027 jerseys rank?** Utah's Mountain Blue sweater is No. 3 of 36. Colorado's cream sweater is No. 24.
- **How many Winter Classic jerseys are there?** 36, two for each of the 18 games. The 2013 game was canceled by the lockout and the 2021 game by the pandemic.

## 36. St. Louis Blues, 2025: Where's the Note?

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; margin: 1.5em 0;">
<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/StLouisBlues/status/1874230478678745286"></a></blockquote>
</div>

**The game:** Blues 6, Blackhawks 2, Dec. 31, 2024, at Wrigley Field.

**The look:** cream, with blue and gold stripes and a felt wordmark in a font taken from a 1966 prototype the team never wore. The Blue Note is moved to the shoulder. The NHL said it was the first time in team history the chest wasn't a Blue Note.

**Why it's last:** the Blue Note is one of the best logos in hockey, and the Blues took it off the chest for a font from a jersey nobody ever wore. They won the game by four goals. The jersey lost.

## 24. Colorado Avalanche, 2027: Fine, and That's It

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; margin: 1.5em 0;">

## What Makes a Great Winter Classic Jersey?

The best ones look like they were dug out of an old trunk but never really existed: Boston's 2010 gold, Montreal's 2016 band, Minnesota's 2022 "MPLS. ST. PAUL" and now Utah's Mountain Blue. The worst ones are white, already worn or both. Of the bottom ten on our list, seven are white or cream.

Color wins. Eight of our top 10 are a color, not white. If a team is going to wear white at a Winter Classic, it had better be the 1974 Capitals.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best NHL Winter Classic jersey ever?**

The 2008 Pittsburgh Penguins powder blue jersey, worn at the first Winter Classic at Ralph Wilson Stadium near Buffalo. Sidney Crosby scored the shootout winner in the snow. It was the first time the Penguins had worn powder blue since 1973.

**What is the worst NHL Winter Classic jersey ever?**

Our pick is the 2025 St. Louis Blues jersey from Wrigley Field. It was the first Blues jersey without the Blue Note on the chest, using a font from a 1966 prototype the team never wore.

**Who plays in the 2027 Winter Classic?**

The Utah Mammoth host the Colorado Avalanche on Dec. 31, 2026, at 6 p.m. ET at Rice-Eccles Stadium in Salt Lake City. It's the first outdoor NHL game in Utah and Colorado's first Winter Classic.

**What jersey will the Utah Mammoth wear in the Winter Classic?**

A Mountain Blue jersey with brown and beige trim, "UTAH" arched over a vintage walking mammoth, brown pants and gloves, and stripes based on Hygeia Iceland, Utah's first ice rink. It was revealed on Nov. 18, 2026.

**What jersey will the Colorado Avalanche wear in the Winter Classic?**

A cream jersey with blue trim and a vintage burgundy "C" with blue mountains inside, plus burgundy helmets with a Colorado flag, the first burgundy helmets in team history. "5280" is on the loop label for the Mile High City.

**When is the 2027 Winter Classic?**

Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026, at 6 p.m. ET, on HBO Max, truTV and TNT in the U.S. and Sportsnet and TVA Sports in Canada.

**How many Winter Classics have there been?**

Seventeen have been played, from 2008 to 2026, and the 2027 game is the 18th. Two were canceled: the 2013 game in Ann Arbor because of the lockout and the 2021 game in Minneapolis because of the pandemic. Both were played the next time.

**Which team has played in the most Winter Classics?**

The Chicago Blackhawks, with five: 2009, 2015, 2017, 2019 and 2025. The Bruins are next with four: 2010, 2016, 2019 and 2023.

**Why do teams wear throwback jerseys at the Winter Classic?**

The game is built around old-time outdoor hockey, so each team wears a one-time jersey based on its own history or its city's hockey history. Teams with little history, like Utah, Seattle and Vegas, borrow from older local teams or make a vintage-style design.

**Did both teams ever wear dark jerseys at a Winter Classic?**

Yes. In 2014 at Michigan Stadium, the Maple Leafs wore royal blue and the Red Wings wore red, the first time neither team wore white.

**Which Winter Classic had the biggest crowd?**

The 2014 game between the Maple Leafs and Red Wings at Michigan Stadium, with 105,491 fans, the NHL record.

**Which is the best Bruins Winter Classic jersey?**

The 2010 gold and brown jersey from Fenway Park, designed by Cam Neely. It's No. 6 on our list.

**Which is the best Penguins Winter Classic jersey?**

The 2008 powder blue, No. 1 overall. Pittsburgh's 2011 skating penguin jersey is No. 17, and the 2023 Pirates jersey is No. 29.
