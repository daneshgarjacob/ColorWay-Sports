## Frequently Asked Questions

**What did NHL teams wear last night?**

The newest day is always at the top of this page. Every game from the night before gets a card with both sweaters, home team on the right, and the final score, logged the next morning once we have seen the game photos. Through the first three nights of 2026-27, all 16 games used the standard sweaters: dark at home, white on the road.

**What jersey is my NHL team wearing tonight?**

Open your club's [2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/nhl-uniform-schedule-2026-27). Each one lists the full closet and every date announced for a third, throwback or Hometown Remix sweater. If a game is not on that list, the default applies: the home team wears its dark and the visitor wears white.

**Do NHL teams wear dark jerseys at home?**

Yes. Since 2003-04 the home team wears its dark sweater and the visitor wears white, and that is the rule this tracker checks against every night. Thirds, throwbacks and Hometown Remix sweaters are worn at home in place of the dark, and a few clubs occasionally swap the order for a special game, which is exactly the kind of night we flag here.

**How do you know what each team wore?**

We look at the game photos and highlight frames from every game the morning after and confirm both sweaters before anything is logged. A game we have not seen does not get a card. Scores come from the NHL's own scoreboard.

**Where can I see every game a team has played this season?**

On its [team uniform calendar](/nhl-tracker). This page keeps the five most recent nights; the team pages keep the whole season, with the sweater from every confirmed game and running home and road counts.
