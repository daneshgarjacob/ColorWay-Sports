**Every National Lacrosse League team wears a new jersey this season, and the first ones hit the floor tonight.** The NLL's 40th season opens on **Friday, November 27**, with the **Saskatchewan Rush at the Oshawa FireWolves**, and all **12 clubs** are in new **Capelli Sport** uniforms, the league's first season with Capelli after it replaced Castore as the official uniform supplier in April. The league revealed the full set on **October 21**, one post per team. Some clubs used the switch for a real redesign: **Rochester moved to teal and purple**, **Buffalo brought back the crest from its 2008 title team**, **Colorado put a mountain range across the chest**, and **Las Vegas added a sunset alternate**. Here is every team's new jersey, from the team's own reveal.

## Quick Answers

- **Does every NLL team have a new jersey in 2026-27?** Yes. All 12 clubs wear new Capelli Sport uniforms this season. The NLL revealed them on October 21, 2026.
- **Who makes NLL jerseys now?** **Capelli Sport**, the league's official uniform through 2029. It replaced **Castore**.
- **Why 12 teams and not 14?** The **Ottawa Black Bears** and **Philadelphia Wings** discontinued operations and will not play in 2026-27, the NLL announced on September 10.
- **When is the first game in the new jerseys?** Tonight, **Friday, November 27, 7:30 p.m. ET**: Saskatchewan at Oshawa, on TSN and FloLacrosse.
- **Which teams changed the most?** **Rochester** (a full switch to teal and purple), **Buffalo** (the classic crest returns), **Colorado** (a mountain range and tree line) and **Las Vegas** (new home, away and a sunset alternate).
- **What is different about the jersey itself?** The league says it is built from **doubleknit and mesh** with **reinforced stitching**. Every jersey has a holographic NLL crest at the collar.
- **Our top 3:** Colorado, Oshawa, Rochester.

The best idea in the league. The Mammoth kept their **burgundy** home base and put a **linear mountain range** right under the logo, drawn from Colorado's **Front Range**, with a **black tree line** along the hem that the team says is inspired by the trails up to Colorado's 14ers. The mountains read light gray up close and white from the stands. The collar is black with a burgundy layer inside, **player names are now stitched** rather than pressed, the back numbers are slightly larger, and the shorts are black. The team designed it in house with Kroenke Sports & Entertainment. The Mammoth play at Las Vegas on December 5, and the home opener is **January 1 against Georgia** at Ball Arena.

## Our Take: The Top 3

**1. Colorado Mammoth.** No jersey in the league does more to tell you where the team plays, and it does it without a word. The mountain line is thin enough that the burgundy still does the work, and the tree line at the hem finishes it.

**2. Oshawa FireWolves.** Black and gold with one big logo is the safest formula in sports, and the fang pattern on the cuffs is what keeps it from being plain. The maroon numbers are a good third color.

**3. Rochester Knighthawks.** The most meaningful change in the league. Teal and purple is a big swing, and the Haudenosaunee flag on the back gives it a reason beyond the color.

**The one we want to see more of:** Halifax. The league's close-up shows purple with an orange emblem, and that combination is either the best jersey in the league or too much. We will know Saturday.

The bigger point is the reset. Every team changed at once, which almost never happens in a pro league, and the teams that used it for a real redesign, Rochester, Buffalo, Colorado and Las Vegas, are the ones people will remember from this season.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Did the NLL get new jerseys for 2026-27?**

Yes. All 12 NLL teams wear new Capelli Sport jerseys in the 2026-27 season. The league revealed them on October 21, 2026, and they debut as the season opens on November 27.

**Who makes NLL jerseys?**

Capelli Sport, the NLL's official uniform through 2029. It replaced Castore before the 2026-27 season.

**How many teams are in the NLL in 2026-27?**

12: Buffalo, Calgary, Colorado, Georgia, Halifax, Las Vegas, Oshawa, Rochester, San Diego, Saskatchewan, Toronto and Vancouver.

**What happened to the Ottawa Black Bears and Philadelphia Wings?**

The NLL announced on September 10, 2026, that both clubs discontinued operations and will not play in 2026-27.

**When does the 2026-27 NLL season start?**

Friday, November 27, 2026, with the Saskatchewan Rush at the Oshawa FireWolves at 7:30 p.m. ET on TSN and FloLacrosse. It is the league's 40th season.

**Why do the Rochester Knighthawks have new colors?**

The new jerseys are part of a rebrand to teal and purple. The Seneca Nation bought the Knighthawks in August 2025, and the jersey carries the Seneca Nation logo on the shoulders and the Haudenosaunee flag on the back.

**What is on the back of the Knighthawks jersey?**

The Haudenosaunee flag, modeled after the Hiawatha Belt, representing the Cayuga, Mohawk, Oneida, Onondaga, Seneca and Tuscarora nations, the team says.

**What do the Bandits' new jerseys look like?**

A black home and a white away, a modernized version of the look Buffalo wore in its 2008 championship season, with the classic primary logo back as the crest.

**What are the mountains on the Colorado Mammoth jersey?**

A linear drawing of Colorado's Front Range under the logo, with a tree line at the hem inspired by the trails up to the state's 14ers.

**What is new about the Capelli NLL jersey?**

The league says it combines doubleknit and mesh fabric with reinforced stitching. Every jersey has a holographic NLL crest at the collar, and some teams, like Colorado, now stitch player names into the jersey.

**Can you buy the new NLL jerseys?**

Yes. Teams including Buffalo, Colorado, Rochester and Las Vegas opened pre-orders after the reveal.
