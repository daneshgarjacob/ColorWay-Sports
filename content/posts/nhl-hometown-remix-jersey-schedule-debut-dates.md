The NHL and Fanatics revealed all 32 Hometown Remix jerseys on Monday, Nov. 2, and the first three hit the ice that same night. The rest roll out over the next three weeks. By Nov. 25, 30 of the 32 teams will have worn theirs at least once.

Below is every debut in date order, with the opponent, a link to that team's full uniform schedule and where the jersey landed in [our ranking of all 32](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-jerseys-ranked). For what each design looks like, see [our guide to every Hometown Remix jersey](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-2026-27).

## Hometown Remix Debut Dates, in Order

Every debut is a home game except Utah's, which comes on the road in Montreal. The Mammoth's first home game in the Remix is Thursday, Nov. 12 against St. Louis. The Red Wings and Rangers have not posted their Remix dates yet.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When do NHL teams wear their Hometown Remix jerseys?**
The first three debuted Monday, Nov. 2: the Blackhawks vs. Colorado, the Panthers vs. Detroit and the Sharks vs. Calgary. Every team with a posted schedule debuts by Wednesday, Nov. 25, when the Stars host Nashville. After the debut, each team wears it between five and 13 times through the end of the regular season.

**Which team wore the Hometown Remix jersey first?**
Chicago, Florida and San Jose, all on Monday, Nov. 2. Columbus, Montreal, Philadelphia and Pittsburgh followed on Tuesday, Nov. 3.

**Which team debuts its Hometown Remix jersey last?**
Of the teams with posted dates, Dallas, on Wednesday, Nov. 25 against Nashville. Detroit and the Rangers have not announced their dates yet.

**When do the Red Wings and Rangers wear their Hometown Remix jerseys?**
Neither team has posted its Remix dates yet. We will add them to this calendar and to the [Red Wings](/stories/red-wings-uniform-schedule-2026-27) and [Rangers](/stories/new-york-rangers-uniform-schedule-2026-27) uniform schedules once they do.

**Which team wears the Hometown Remix jersey the most?**
Columbus and Philadelphia, 13 games each. Boston is next with 12.

**Which team wears the Hometown Remix jersey the fewest times?**
Dallas and Vegas, five games each. Toronto has five themed nights on the calendar so far, and Utah has five dates posted.

**Do teams wear the Hometown Remix jersey on the road?**
Mostly at home. Twelve teams have at least one road date: Boston, Buffalo, Colorado, Dallas, Minnesota, Montreal, Nashville, the Islanders, Philadelphia, Pittsburgh, St. Louis and Utah. Utah's debut is on the road, at Montreal on Nov. 5.

**When does Pittsburgh's Hometown Remix jersey debut?**
Tuesday, Nov. 3 against Vegas at home. The Penguins' pop-art sweater is No. 1 in [our ranking](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-jerseys-ranked), and they wear it 10 times.

**When does the Golden Knights' Hometown Remix jersey debut?**
Sunday, Nov. 15 against Vancouver. Vegas wears it five times, and it finished last in our ranking. [Here is why](/stories/golden-knights-hometown-remix-jersey-worst).

**Where can I find every date for my team?**
Each team's link in the tables above goes to its full 2026-27 uniform schedule, with every Remix game marked in the game log.
