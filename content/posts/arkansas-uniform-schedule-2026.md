The base sets are what Arkansas fans call the D-Mac era uniforms, a modern take on the look the Hogs wore when Darren McFadden was running wild in the mid-2000s. The cardinal jersey returned in 2019 and was worn for every home game that season, and the white version followed in 2020, so 2026 is the sixth straight year with the full set. The one visible change this season is on the shoulders. Arkansas signed a jersey patch deal with Tyson Foods, announced March 4, that puts the Tyson logo on the right shoulder of every varsity uniform. The SEC patch moves to the collar, replacing the small Razorback logo that sat there since 2024, and the Nike swoosh stays on the left shoulder. First-year head coach Ryan Silverfield has also signaled restraint, telling 247Sports that "year one we're gonna stick with the traditional things." SEC home teams wear color and visitors wear white, which makes every date below predictable until Arkansas says otherwise.

**September 5 vs. North Alabama.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **cardinal helmet, cardinal jersey, white pants**, the standard home set for Silverfield's first game in Fayetteville. North Alabama came in wearing purple helmets, white jerseys and purple pants. Arkansas won 31-14 at Donald W. Reynolds Razorback Stadium.

**September 12 at Utah.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **white helmet, white jersey, white pants**, the same all-white look Arkansas wore for the 2022 Southwest Classic. No. 20 Utah wore red helmets, red jerseys and red pants at Rice-Eccles Stadium, so the night game in Salt Lake City was a clean red-against-white picture. Utah won 43-10. One calendar note: ESPN files this game under September 13 because its feed runs on UTC. It kicked off at 8:30 p.m. Mountain on Saturday, September 12.

**September 19 vs. Georgia.** Cardinal expected. No. 2 Georgia visits at 11 a.m. Central on ABC, and Arkansas has called a stripe out, with fans in alternating sections wearing red or white. The program has not announced a uniform change for the game, and the stripe out asks for both colors in the stands, so the default home set is the call. Georgia's side is on our [Georgia 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/georgia-uniform-schedule-2026), where white is expected.

**September 26 vs. Tulsa.** Cardinal expected. A 7 p.m. Central kickoff and the last non-conference game of the season.

**October 3 at Texas A&M.** White expected. The Southwest Classic moves to Kyle Field, and Arkansas will be the white half of one of the loudest matchups of the season: Texas A&M has confirmed its desert camo Honor and Support uniform for this game, covered on our [Texas A&M 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/texas-am-uniform-schedule-2026). The Southwest Classic has produced Arkansas specials before, including Cowboys-inspired jerseys in 2017 and white helmets over cardinal jerseys in 2023, so this is a date to watch.

**October 10 vs. Tennessee.** Cardinal expected. The Vols are down for white on our [Tennessee 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/tennessee-uniform-schedule-2026), which means cardinal against white with orange trim.

**October 17 at Vanderbilt.** White expected. Road SEC game in Nashville at FirstBank Stadium.

**October 24.** Bye.

**October 31 vs. Missouri.** Cardinal expected. The Battle Line Rivalry on Halloween in Fayetteville, with Missouri expected in white on our [Missouri 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/missouri-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 7 at Auburn.** White expected. Auburn is listed in navy at Jordan-Hare on our [Auburn 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/auburn-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 14 vs. South Carolina.** Cardinal expected.

**November 21 at Texas.** White expected. The old Southwest Conference rivalry in Austin, with Texas in burnt orange at home. Details on our [Texas 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/texas-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 28 vs. LSU.** Cardinal expected. The Battle for the Golden Boot closes the regular season in Fayetteville, the final home game of the year. LSU's side of the day is on our [LSU 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/lsu-uniform-schedule-2026).

## The Bottom Line

Two games in, Arkansas has used the cardinal home set and the all-white road look, and the white helmet has already made one appearance. Nothing else is on the calendar: no anthracite, no throwback, and a new head coach who says year one is about the traditional looks. The only new detail on the jersey this season is the Tyson Foods patch on the right shoulder. The Southwest Classic at Kyle Field is the game where Arkansas has broken form before, and we will update the grid if it does again. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Arkansas wearing this week?**

Arkansas hosts No. 2 Georgia on Saturday, September 19, and the cardinal home jersey is expected, with white pants and the cardinal helmet. Fans have been asked to stripe out Razorback Stadium in red and white. Last week at Utah, Arkansas wore all white. The grid above lists every game with the expected set, and we update it as combinations are confirmed.

**What are Arkansas's football uniforms for 2026?**

A cardinal home jersey with white numerals and the Arkansas wordmark, a white road jersey with cardinal numerals, and white pants with a cardinal stripe set. The helmets are a cardinal shell with the white running Razorback and a white shell used for select games. These are the D-Mac era uniforms Arkansas has worn since 2019, made by Nike, now with a Tyson Foods patch on the right shoulder and the SEC patch on the collar.

**Does Arkansas have a new alternate uniform in 2026?**

No new alternate has been announced. The only change for 2026 is the patch layout: Tyson Foods on the right shoulder and the SEC logo moved to the collar. Ryan Silverfield has said the program will stick with traditional looks in his first season, so an anthracite or throwback set would be a surprise this year.

**What did Arkansas wear against Utah?**

All white: white helmet, white jersey and white pants at Rice-Eccles Stadium on September 12. Arkansas was the visitor, and Utah wore all red. Utah won 43-10.

**What is the Tyson patch on the Arkansas jersey?**

It is part of a sponsorship deal between Arkansas and Tyson Foods, announced March 4, 2026, that puts the Tyson logo on the jerseys of all 19 Razorback varsity teams starting with the 2026-27 season. On the football uniform it sits alone on the right shoulder. Arkansas is one of the first schools with an all-sport jersey patch deal.

**How many games does Arkansas play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven at Donald W. Reynolds Razorback Stadium and five on the road, with one bye on October 24. The SEC slate includes Georgia, Tennessee, Missouri, South Carolina and LSU at home, and Texas A&M, Vanderbilt, Auburn and Texas on the road.
