## How Often Is the Daikin Park Roof Open?

Almost never during the season. Houston summers are among the hottest and most humid in baseball, so the roof stays closed from late spring through September. In a typical year the roof is open for fewer than 10 percent of home games, putting Daikin Park near the very bottom of the league for roof openness alongside Texas's Globe Life Field and Miami's loanDepot Park.

The open-roof window is short and front-loaded. The roof is most likely to be open in **April and into early May**, on dry nights in the 70s before the Texas heat takes over for the summer. A handful of cool, clear evenings in late September can also get the roof open as the season winds down.

## How the Astros Decide: The Daikin Park Roof Rules

In the regular season the home team makes the call, and the Astros close the roof if any of these are true:

- **Air temperature or heat index above about 88 degrees** for a night game, or about 84 degrees for a day game
- **Temperature below 65 degrees** for a night game
- **Sustained winds above 30 mph**
- **Any threat of rain** before the anticipated end of the game

If the roof starts open, it can be closed mid-game only for impending rain or other adverse weather. The roof can be moved only between innings, and it takes roughly 14 to 20 minutes to slide fully open or closed. During the postseason, MLB has the final say on the roof.

## Daikin Park Roof Specs

Daikin Park opened in 2000 as the second retractable-roof ballpark in Major League Baseball, after Chase Field in Phoenix. The roof was built for exactly one reason: Houston's summer is too hot and too wet for comfortable outdoor baseball from June through September.

- The roof is made up of large sliding steel panels that park beyond left field when open
- It takes about **14 to 20 minutes** to fully open or close
- It can be moved **only between innings**, never mid-inning
- When the roof is shut, the ballpark is climate controlled for fan comfort

## Minute Maid Park Is Now Daikin Park

If you are searching for the Minute Maid Park roof, you are in the right place. The Astros' ballpark was renamed **Daikin Park** on January 1, 2025, after the team signed a 15-year naming-rights deal with Daikin, the air-conditioning company, that runs through the 2039 season. The building, the roof, and the policies are all the same. Only the name on the front changed, so "Minute Maid Park roof open today" and "Daikin Park roof open today" point to the exact same retractable roof.

## Daikin Park vs Other MLB Retractable Roofs

Daikin Park sits at the closed end of the league right next to its in-state rival. The Astros and the Texas Rangers at [Globe Life Field](/stories/rangers-roof-open-globe-life-field-2026) share the same climate-controlled-over-atmosphere philosophy, and both keep the roof shut for roughly four out of five home games. At the opposite end, the Arizona Diamondbacks at [Chase Field](/stories/chase-field-retractable-roof-diamondbacks) open their roof every chance they get.

The seven retractable-roof ballparks, from most open to least, run roughly: Rogers Centre (Blue Jays), American Family Field (Brewers), Chase Field (Diamondbacks), T-Mobile Park (Mariners), Globe Life Field (Rangers), Daikin Park (Astros), and loanDepot Park (Marlins), where the roof is essentially never open.
