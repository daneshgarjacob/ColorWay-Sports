## Quick Answers

- **When do the Heat play on the Vice City court?** Wednesday, November 18, 2026, against the Bucks at Kaseya Center, 7 p.m. ET on ESPN.
- **Is it only for one game?** Yes. The Heat's post says "one night only."
- **What does it look like?** Natural wood, pink around the paint, a blue paint, a pink "Vice City" script at center court and a black border with "I ♥ VICE CITY" on the baselines.
- **Is GTA 6 on the court?** Yes. The white GTA VI logo, with palm trees inside the letters, sits on the pink area at each end.
- **Our grade:** B+.

## What the Vice City Court Looks Like

The Heat posted three photos. Here is everything you can see in them.

**Pink around the paint.** At each end, a big pink half circle fills the area around the lane, out to a thin blue line at its edge. Look closely and there are faint palm leaf shapes in a lighter pink inside it.

**A blue paint.** The lane itself is a bright royal blue, with black lines. Pink outside and blue inside is the whole idea of this floor, and it is what you will notice first on TV.

**The GTA VI logo.** On the pink, just outside the lane, is Rockstar's white "VI" logo for Grand Theft Auto VI, with pink palm trees inside the letters. It is the same mark that sits on the fan version of the Vice City jersey.

**Vice City at center court.** There is no Heat logo at center court. Instead it is "Vice City" in a big pink script, in the style of the old game's logo. A small pink "Vice City" signature also sits in the corner of each paint.

**A black border with I ♥ VICE CITY.** The out-of-bounds area is black. On the baselines it reads "I ♥ VICE CITY" in big white letters, with a pink heart.

**Kaseya Center on the wood.** The arena's name and logo appear in black on the wood on both sides of half court.

What you will not see: we did not spot a Cactus Jack logo or a backwards Nike logo anywhere on the court in the Heat's photos. Travis Scott's marks stay on the uniform. The arena's center-hung video board in the first photo also shows the GTA VI logo.

## The Same Night as the Vice City Jerseys

The court and the uniform are one package. The Heat wear the black Nike x Cactus Jack uniform with neon pink trim, a backwards Nike logo and six pink stars on the shorts. On court the jersey says HEAT, and the "Vice City" script version is the one fans can buy. Every detail is in our [Heat Vice City jersey story](/stories/miami-heat-vice-city-jerseys-gta-6-2026).

The pink on the floor matches the pink on the jersey. The blue paint is the one color on the court that isn't on the uniform.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When will the Heat play on the Vice City court?**

On Wednesday, November 18, 2026, against the Milwaukee Bucks at Kaseya Center in Miami. Tipoff is 7 p.m. ET on ESPN.

**Is the Heat Vice City court only for one game?**

Yes. The Heat's own post says you can see it "one night only," against Milwaukee. After that, Miami goes back to its regular floor.

**What jerseys are the Heat wearing with the Vice City court?**

The black Nike x Cactus Jack Vice City uniform, with neon pink trim, a backwards Nike logo and six pink stars on the shorts. The players' version says HEAT on the chest. The "Vice City" script version is the fan jersey.

**What colors are on the Heat Vice City court?**

Natural wood, pink, royal blue, black and white. The area around each paint is pink, the paint is blue, the center-court script is pink and the border is black with white letters.

**Is the GTA 6 logo on the Heat court?**

Yes. The white Grand Theft Auto VI "VI" logo, with palm trees inside it, is painted on the pink area at each end of the floor.

**Is there a Heat logo on the Vice City court?**

Not at center court. Center court has a pink "Vice City" script instead. We did not see a Heat logo anywhere on the floor in the team's photos.

**Why do the Heat have a Vice City court?**

It is part of "A Night in Vice City," the Heat's event with Rockstar Games for the release of Grand Theft Auto VI. GTA VI is set in Vice City, the game's version of Miami.

**When does GTA 6 come out?**

Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. That is the day after the Heat play on the Vice City court.

**Is this the same as the Heat's old Vice courts?**

No. The Heat's Vice City Edition uniforms from 2017-18 to 2020-21, and the courts that went with them, were inspired by the 1980s Miami Vice TV show. This floor is a one-night court for the GTA VI release, though it shares the pink.
