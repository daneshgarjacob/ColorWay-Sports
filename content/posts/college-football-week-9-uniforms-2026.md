Halloween falls on a Saturday this year, and college football is dressing for it. Week 9 of the 2026 season has the best set of Halloween uniforms we have seen in years. **Nebraska debuts its new black alternate against Washington** in the second Blackout game at Memorial Stadium. **Syracuse wears an Otto the Orange helmet for the first time ever** against No. 19 SMU. **No. 8 BYU goes all navy** for Arizona State, **UCF wears its tenth Space Game uniform** against Baylor on Friday night, and **Navy wears its 1926 national championship throwback against No. 5 Notre Dame** at Gillette Stadium. Iowa State, UNLV, East Carolina and Louisville have all announced something different, and Fresno State is teasing a new look. On the field, No. 3 Georgia and No. 17 Florida meet in Atlanta, No. 2 Ohio State visits USC on Saturday night, and No. 1 Miami goes to North Carolina.

Below is every Halloween uniform announced so far, then every game this week involving an AP Top 25 team or one of the programs we track in our college uniform schedules, with kickoff time, TV and what each team is wearing. A look is marked **Confirmed** when the school itself has posted it. When a school has not posted yet, we give our projection from that team's uniform schedule and label it **Expected**. Most schools post their combination between Monday and game day, so this page fills in all week, and on Saturday we confirm every look from the game broadcasts and team photos, then add final scores. Rankings are from this week's AP poll. All times are Eastern. Last week's looks and final scores are in our [Week 8 uniform guide](/stories/college-football-week-8-uniforms-2026).

## Games of the Week

### Washington at Nebraska: The Black Alternate Debuts on Halloween

**Saturday, 8 p.m. (7 p.m. Central), BTN.** **Nebraska: Confirmed, black alternate.** Nebraska Athletics unveiled the uniform on August 15 under the theme "Our Red Burns Brighter," with Nebraska Football posting it as "Darkness Returns," and the school said then that it would debut in the Halloween home game against Washington, the second annual Blackout game at Memorial Stadium. On Monday of game week the team doubled down with an "Embrace Darkness" game graphic and a call for fans to wear black.

Here is what Nebraska has shown. The **helmet** is black, and it carries the Huskers script in metallic red, the first time the script has been on the helmet, plus a metallic red center stripe with a crop-row pattern, a nod to the state's farms, and GBR on the front bumper. Nebraska says the back of the helmet has an "A Winning Tradition" emblem. The **jersey** is black with NEBRASKA across the chest and white numbers trimmed in red. Nebraska says the metallic red details are inspired by the flames, fireworks and light shows at Memorial Stadium, that the back hem has a perforated Block N for the pregame drone show, and that the collar is lined with "A Winning Tradition." Our [Nebraska schedule](/stories/nebraska-uniform-schedule-2026) has the pants as black with red stripes. Nebraska is also using the night to celebrate the 25th anniversary of Eric Crouch's Heisman season.

### Baylor at UCF: The Tenth Space Game

**Friday, 7:30 p.m., ESPN.** **UCF: Confirmed, Mission X Space Game uniform.** UCF revealed the 2026 Space Game set on September 29, posted as "The 2026 UCF SpaceU uniform." The Big 12 moved the game to Friday night, and it is the tenth Space Game, the tradition built on UCF's origins as a school founded to supply talent to the space program at nearby Kennedy Space Center. This year's theme, Mission X, looks back to NASA's earliest crewed programs, Mercury, Gemini and Apollo.

The reveal photo shows a **white helmet with a red center stripe and a chrome gold facemask**, a **white jersey** with navy numbers trimmed in light blue and a light blue V at the collar, and **white pants**. It is a long way from last year's HyperSpace set and its carbon fiber helmet. UCF comes in off what it called the highest ranked win in program history, 39-31 in double overtime over a BYU team that was No. 2.

**New Tuesday: the full SpaceU helmet.** UCF posted "The 2026 SpaceU helmet" on October 27, and the 360-degree video shows details the September reveal did not. The UCF logo on the side is filled with a silver stars-and-stripes pattern, stars on top and stripes below, outlined in black. The chrome gold facemask carries a matching gold visor, the front bumper reads SPACEU, and the back bumper reads Walt Disney World, above a "26" with a flag decal and a cartoon astronaut. It is the most detailed helmet UCF has made for the Space Game, and it is the one piece of this set worth seeing up close.

### No. 5 Notre Dame vs. Navy at Gillette Stadium: The 1926 Throwback

**Saturday, noon, ABC, at Gillette Stadium in Foxborough, Mass.** **Navy: Confirmed, 1926 centennial uniform.** Navy announced on September 15 that it would wear a uniform honoring the 100th anniversary of its 1926 national championship team, the only national title in program history, and that it would wear it once, in this game. The jersey is navy with gold chevron stripes, nine in all, four on the front and five on the back, one for each win by the 9-0-1 team of 1926. The numbers are gold, there is a 100th anniversary national championship patch, and it goes with Navy's plain gold helmet and gold pants. Navy is the designated home team.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What college football teams are wearing Halloween uniforms in 2026?**

The announced Halloween weekend looks are Nebraska's new black alternate against Washington, Syracuse's first-ever Otto helmet against SMU, BYU's all navy against Arizona State, UCF's Mission X Space Game set against Baylor, Navy's 1926 throwback against Notre Dame, Iowa State's black "halloween threads" against Oklahoma State, UNLV's black jersey against Northern Illinois, East Carolina's marching PeeDee helmet against Temple and Louisville's For The Ville set against Stanford. Fresno State is teasing a new look for Oregon State.

**What is Nebraska wearing against Washington?**

Nebraska debuts its black alternate: a black helmet with a metallic red Huskers script, a metallic red crop-row center stripe and GBR on the bumper, a black jersey with NEBRASKA across the chest and white numbers, and black pants. Nebraska revealed it on August 15 under the theme "Our Red Burns Brighter," and it is the second annual Blackout game at Memorial Stadium. Kickoff is 7 p.m. Central on BTN.

**Is Nebraska's black alternate new for 2026?**

Yes. Nebraska unveiled it on August 15, 2026, and Halloween against Washington is its first game. Last season's Blackout game against USC kept the white helmet. This one goes to a black shell and puts the Huskers script on the helmet for the first time. Our [Nebraska 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/nebraska-uniform-schedule-2026) has every Nebraska look this season.

**What helmet is Syracuse wearing against SMU?**

A white helmet with a cartoon Otto the Orange decal, an orange center stripe trimmed in navy and a white facemask. Syracuse says it is the first time ever it has worn helmets featuring Otto. The jersey and pants have not been announced. Our [Syracuse Otto helmet story](/stories/syracuse-otto-helmet-halloween-smu-2026) has every detail.

**What is BYU wearing against Arizona State?**

All navy: a navy helmet with a navy and white stripe and the white oval Y, a navy jersey with white numbers and navy pants with white side stripes. BYU announced it on Monday of game week. More in [BYU goes all navy vs. Arizona State](/stories/byu-all-navy-uniforms-arizona-state-2026).

**When is the UCF Space Game in 2026, and what is UCF wearing?**

Friday, October 30, at 7:30 p.m. Eastern on ESPN, against Baylor. It is the tenth Space Game. UCF's 2026 set, Mission X, has a white helmet with a red stripe, a stars-and-stripes UCF logo and a chrome gold facemask and visor, a white jersey with navy numbers and a light blue collar, and white pants.

**What is Navy wearing against Notre Dame?**

The 1926 centennial uniform: a navy jersey with nine gold chevron stripes, one for each win by Navy's 1926 national championship team, gold numbers and an anniversary patch, with the gold helmet and gold pants. Navy wears it once, at Gillette Stadium on October 31. Our [full review](/stories/navy-1926-centennial-uniform-2026) has the grade.

**Is Iowa State wearing black on Halloween?**

Yes. Iowa State posted "halloween threads" with three black circles for its game against Oklahoma State, and the video shows a black helmet with a white stripe and the white Cynado logo, a black jersey with IOWA STATE in white, and black pants.

**What is UNLV wearing against Northern Illinois?**

UNLV posted "Black is back": a red helmet with the UNLV wordmark, a black jersey with REBELS across the chest and white numbers trimmed in red, and black pants. Kickoff is 7:30 p.m. Pacific at Allegiant Stadium.

**Is Louisville wearing the For The Ville uniform against Stanford?**

That is the plan Louisville announced in August, when it said the uniform "will make its on-field debut Oct. 31 against Stanford." It is a black uniform built around Muhammad Ali's boxing gear, with an oversized white LOUISVILLE across the chest. We will confirm it from the game on Saturday.

**What are Georgia and Florida wearing in Atlanta?**

Florida's published season schedule lists the orange helmet, white jersey and white pants, which would put Georgia in red. Neither team has posted a game-week look yet. The game is at Mercedes-Benz Stadium because EverBank Stadium is being renovated.

**Is this Week 9 or Week 10 of college football?**

Week 9. ESPN's schedule and this week's AP poll both count the October 29 to 31 games as Week 9. Some schools count games played instead, which is why BYU calls this its Game 8.

**Who is off in Week 9?**

No. 9 Kentucky, No. 10 Alabama, No. 15 LSU, No. 16 Tennessee and No. 18 Houston are off, along with Texas A&M, Vanderbilt, Michigan State, Virginia Tech and several others.
