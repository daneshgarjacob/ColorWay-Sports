Wondering what jersey Ohio State is wearing this week? This is the full Ohio State 2026 uniform schedule, game by game, plus the home dates where an alternate is most likely to show up. Big Ten programs confirm most combinations in game week, so we update this page as each one lands.

## The Ohio State 2026 Uniform Sets

Ohio State runs one of the most disciplined wardrobes in college football, and the helmet is the reason people care.

- **Scarlet jersey, silver pants.** The home set at Ohio Stadium.
- **White jersey, silver pants.** The road set.
- **The silver helmet.** Unchanged in either set, and the only helmet in the sport that gets more decorated as the season goes on, because the buckeye leaf stickers accumulate with every big play.

**No alternate uniform has been assigned to any 2026 game.** Ohio State reaches for one rarely, usually around The Game, and nothing has been announced. If that changes we will add the date here.

## The Full Ohio State 2026 Uniform Schedule, Week by Week

Big Ten home teams wear colour and visitors wear white, which makes the road dates close to automatic.

**September 5 vs. Ball State**, Ohio Stadium. Home opener, and the Jim Tressel Ring of Honor tribute. Scarlet expected.

**September 12 at Texas**, Austin. The biggest non-conference game on either team's schedule. Texas wears burnt orange at home, so Ohio State goes white. See our [Texas 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/texas-uniform-schedule-2026) for the other side of this one.

**September 19 vs. Kent State**, Ohio Stadium. The Helmet Stripe Game returns, though that is a crowd theme rather than a uniform change: fans in the horseshoe wear grey and students dress to match the stripes on the helmet. Scarlet expected on the field.

**September 26 vs. Illinois**, Ohio Stadium. Hall of Fame Weekend and the Buckeyes for a Cancer Free World game. Scarlet expected.

**October 3 at Iowa**, Iowa City. Road. White expected.

**October 10 vs. Maryland**, Ohio Stadium. Homecoming. Scarlet expected.

**October 17 at Indiana**, Bloomington. Road. White expected.

**October 24.** Bye week.

**October 31 at USC**, Los Angeles. Road, and worth watching. USC wears cardinal at home, so Ohio State should be in white, but a Halloween night game in the Coliseum is exactly the kind of stage a program uses for something unannounced.

**November 7 vs. Oregon**, Ohio Stadium. The first meeting since the 2024 Rose Bowl, at home, against the one program in the country with a bigger uniform closet than anybody. Scarlet expected, and if Ohio State is going to answer Oregon with something, this is the date.

**November 14 vs. Northwestern**, Ohio Stadium. **Military Appreciation Game**, part of the Our Honor Defend programme. Military appreciation games are one of the most common triggers for a patch or a special uniform element in college football, so this is the most likely date on the schedule for something beyond the base set. Nothing confirmed yet.

**November 21 at Nebraska**, Lincoln. Road. White expected.

**November 28 vs. Michigan**, Ohio Stadium. The Game, at home. Scarlet expected. Ohio State has broken out alternates for this fixture before, and if anything is being held back for one Saturday, it is this one.

## The Three Dates Worth Watching

Everything on this schedule follows the Big Ten pattern except three home games. **Oregon on November 7** is the marquee non-rivalry matchup. **Northwestern on November 14** is the Military Appreciation game, historically the likeliest slot for a special element. And **Michigan on November 28** is The Game, where Ohio State has gone off-script before.

Ohio State's 2026 uniform schedule is scarlet at home, white on the road, and the silver buckeye-leaf helmet in every game, with no alternate announced.
