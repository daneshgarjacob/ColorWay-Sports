**The John Madden Thanksgiving patch is back.** The NFL announced on Thursday, November 19 that its fifth annual **John Madden Thanksgiving Celebration** runs on Thanksgiving Day, **Thursday, November 26, 2026**, and that "a patch featuring a silhouette of Coach Madden will be included on the jersey of each player at all three games," per the league's release on [NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/news/fifth-annual-john-madden-thanksgiving-celebration-on-thursday-nov-26). That means six teams: the **Detroit Lions and Chicago Bears**, the **Dallas Cowboys and Philadelphia Eagles**, and the **Buffalo Bills and Kansas City Chiefs**.

If you are watching on Thursday and wondering what the round orange-and-brown patch on every jersey is, this is it: a tribute to John Madden, the Hall of Fame coach and broadcaster who called 20 Thanksgiving Day games and died in December 2021.

## Quick Answers

- **What is the patch on NFL jerseys on Thanksgiving?** The John Madden Thanksgiving patch, a round patch with a silhouette of Madden.
- **Is it back in 2026?** Yes. The NFL confirmed it on November 19 for all three Thanksgiving Day games.
- **Which teams wear it?** All six Thanksgiving Day teams: Lions, Bears, Cowboys, Eagles, Bills and Chiefs.
- **Where does it go?** On the front of the jersey, high on the player's left chest, toward the shoulder.
- **Since when?** The tribute started in 2022 with a helmet sticker. The jersey patch has been worn every Thanksgiving since 2023, so 2026 is its fourth year.

## What The 2026 Patch Looks Like

The patch image the NFL sent out with this year's release is a **circle with a dark brown field and an orange ring**, carrying an **orange silhouette of Madden in profile wearing his broadcast headset**, the microphone in front of his mouth. There is no lettering on it. It is the same artwork as the patch image in the league's 2025 release, so the design has not changed.

It matches the rest of the celebration's look. The 2026 John Madden Thanksgiving logo, in the NFL Media post above, is a brown shield with an orange THANKSGIVING band and Madden's signature, and the coin-toss coins use the same headset silhouette.

On the field, embroidered versions have read a little more gold than orange depending on the jersey. Here is how it looked sewn onto the Lions' jersey for Thanksgiving 2024, posted by the team:

## Where The Patch Sits On The Jersey

**On the front, on the player's left chest, high up and toward the shoulder, below the collar.** On the Lions' 2024 jersey above, the Madden patch sits on the left chest while the captain's "C" patch is on the opposite side of the chest. The Packers' photo gallery of team seamstresses [sewing the patch onto Green Bay's white jerseys](https://www.packers.com/photos/photos-packers-seamstresses-affix-madden-patch-ahead-of-week-13-thanksgiving-game-2025) before Thanksgiving 2025 shows the same spot.

That location matters for the six 2026 uniforms. Dallas is scheduled to wear its navy **1960s Classic throwback**, so on the Cowboys the patch will sit on navy. Detroit is expected in Honolulu blue, Buffalo is scheduled in royal blue, and the three visitors are expected in white.

## Everything Else In The 2026 Madden Celebration

From the NFL's November 19 release:

- **A coin for each game.** The coin toss uses a coin with Madden's silhouette on "Heads" and a **six-legged turducken** on "Tails," with a separate coin for Bears-Lions, Eagles-Cowboys and Chiefs-Bills.
- **Field stencils.** John Madden Thanksgiving Celebration stencils go **in the end zones and on the sidelines** at Ford Field, AT&T Stadium and Highmark Stadium, with stadium wall panels showing Madden from his coaching days.
- **A new MVP trophy.** Each network names a Madden Thanksgiving MVP, who gets a $10,000 NFL Foundation donation to a high school or youth football program. For the trophy's third year, the NFL commissioned a new one in the shape of a **football turkey leg**, designed by Victor Solomon.
- **Broadcast tributes.** CBS, FOX and NBC run Madden tributes during each game, and each broadcast opens with an NFL-produced piece in Madden's own words.
- **An auction.** Game-used items from all three games go to NFL Auction to benefit the John Madden Foundation, for the third straight year.

**2022: the first year, on the helmet.** The NFL announced the John Madden Thanksgiving Celebration on November 11, 2022, as "a yearly, multi-faceted celebration." The uniform piece that year was not a jersey patch: per the league's [announcement on NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-honors-john-madden-with-thanksgiving-day-commemoration), there was a sticker on the back of each player's helmet showing the well-known image of Madden with his fist up in the air, plus a John Madden Thanksgiving logo at the 25-yard lines.

**2023: the patch arrives.** For the second year, every player wore a jersey patch with Madden's silhouette, and the coin toss used the Madden-and-turducken coin for the first time, as [ESPN reported](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/38964598/nfl-thanksgiving-tribute-john-madden-includes-coin-toss) on Thanksgiving morning.

**2024: the patch again, and an MVP trophy.** The Lions posted their patch the day before the game with the line "All teams playing on Thanksgiving Day wear a John Madden jersey patch." The 2026 release says this year is the trophy's third, which puts the first Madden Thanksgiving MVP trophy in 2024.

**2025: the fourth celebration.** The NFL's [2025 release](https://www.nfl.com/news/fourth-annual-john-madden-thanksgiving-celebration-on-thursday-nov-27) used the same wording as this year's: a patch with a silhouette of Coach Madden on the jersey of each player at all three games.

**2026: the fifth.** Same patch, a new turkey-leg trophy, and stencils in the end zones as well as on the sidelines.

## Our Take

This is how a memorial patch should work. It is small, it has no words on it, and the headset silhouette says "Madden" to anyone who watched football in the 1980s, 1990s or 2000s. The brown and orange are Thanksgiving colors rather than team colors, so it does not fight with any of the six jerseys. Keeping it the same every year is the right call too. It is becoming part of what a Thanksgiving uniform looks like.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the patch on NFL jerseys on Thanksgiving?**

It is the John Madden Thanksgiving patch, a round patch with a silhouette of the late Hall of Fame coach and broadcaster wearing his broadcast headset. Every player in the three Thanksgiving Day games wears it, as part of the NFL's John Madden Thanksgiving Celebration.

**Is the Madden Thanksgiving patch back in 2026?**

Yes. The NFL confirmed on November 19, 2026 that a patch with Madden's silhouette will be on the jersey of every player at all three Thanksgiving games on Thursday, November 26.

**Which teams wear the Madden patch in 2026?**

Six: the Detroit Lions and Chicago Bears (1:00 p.m. ET, CBS), the Dallas Cowboys and Philadelphia Eagles (4:30 p.m. ET, FOX), and the Buffalo Bills and Kansas City Chiefs (8:20 p.m. ET, NBC).

**What does the Madden Thanksgiving patch look like?**

A circle with a dark brown background and an orange border, with an orange silhouette of Madden in profile wearing his headset and microphone. There is no text on it. The NFL's 2026 patch image is the same design as 2025.

**Where is the Madden patch on the jersey?**

On the front of the jersey, high on the player's left chest toward the shoulder, below the collar. Team photos from the Lions in 2024 and the Packers in 2025 show it there.

**When did the NFL start the Madden Thanksgiving patch?**

The John Madden Thanksgiving Celebration started in 2022, when the tribute was a sticker on the back of every helmet. The jersey patch started in 2023, so 2026 is the fourth year with the patch and the fifth year of the celebration.

**Why does the NFL honor John Madden on Thanksgiving?**

Madden called 20 Thanksgiving Day NFL games as a broadcaster and made the holiday part of his on-air identity, from the turkey legs he handed out to the turducken. The league says the celebration brings together what mattered to him: family, football, food, tradition and fun.

**Do the Thanksgiving Eve and Black Friday games have the Madden patch?**

The NFL's release only covers the three Thursday games. It does not mention Packers at Rams on Wednesday, November 25, or Broncos at Steelers on Friday, November 27.

**What is on the Madden Thanksgiving coin?**

A silhouette of Madden on "Heads" and a six-legged turducken on "Tails." There is a game-specific coin for each of the three Thanksgiving games.

**What is the Madden Thanksgiving MVP trophy?**

Each network picks a Madden Thanksgiving MVP, who gets a $10,000 NFL Foundation donation to a youth or high school football program. In 2026 the trophy is new: a football turkey leg designed by Victor Solomon.

**Can you buy a jersey with the Madden Thanksgiving patch?**

The NFL's release does not mention retail jerseys with the patch. Game-used items from the three games are auctioned through NFL Auction to benefit the John Madden Foundation.
