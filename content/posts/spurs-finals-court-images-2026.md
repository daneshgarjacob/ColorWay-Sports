## Compared to the 2009 Lakers Finals Court

This is the first NBA Finals court with the Larry O'Brien Trophy painted at center court since the 2009 NBA Finals between the Lakers and the Magic at Staples Center. We pulled up the 2009 court alongside the 2026 Spurs floor in our [2026 vs 2009 NBA Finals court compare-and-contrast post](/stories/2026-nba-finals-court-vs-2009-lakers-finals-court-compared) earlier this week. The 2026 version is a different execution. The trophy itself is the new design that Tiffany and Co. unveiled in 2022 for the league's 75th anniversary. The trophy faces a different direction at center court. The center court team logo is smaller on the 2026 court because it sits inside the trophy silhouette instead of next to it. And the four-corner The Finals cursive wordmarks on the 2009 court are gone, replaced by the new NBA Finals primary logo on the sidelines directly above and below center court.

We graded the 2026 NBA Finals court an A+ when the league released the official renderings last week in our [2026 NBA Finals court reveal post](/stories/nba-finals-court-2026-knicks-spurs-revealed). The in-arena photos of the actual painted Spurs floor at Frost Bank Center hold that grade. The renders did not lie. The court looks even better in the building than it did on the official PDF.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What does the 2026 NBA Finals Spurs court at Frost Bank Center look like?**

The 2026 NBA Finals Spurs court at Frost Bank Center is a natural hardwood floor with black painted borders, the Larry O'Brien Trophy painted in gold at center court with the Spurs primary spurs mark embedded inside the trophy silhouette, The Finals cursive script wordmark painted in both keys on both sides of the floor, the new NBA Finals primary logo painted on both sidelines directly above and below the center court trophy, the Frost Bank Center sponsor logo above and below center, the SAN ANTONIO SPURS wordmark in white running vertically along both baselines, and Michelob Ultra and Coinbase sponsor branding on the sidelines and ad strips.

**Is the Larry O'Brien Trophy painted at center court on the Spurs 2026 NBA Finals court?**

Yes. The Larry O'Brien Trophy is painted in gold at center court on the 2026 NBA Finals Spurs floor at Frost Bank Center, with the Spurs primary spurs mark embedded inside the trophy silhouette in black. This is the first time since the 2009 NBA Finals between the Lakers and the Magic at Staples Center that the trophy has been painted at center court on an NBA Finals floor. That is a 17-year absence.

**What is the new NBA Finals logo on the Spurs court?**

The new NBA Finals primary logo on the 2026 Spurs court is the league's current NBA Finals primary mark, a vertical lockup that stacks the NBA Jerry West silhouette logo next to the Finals cursive script. The logo is painted on both sidelines of the floor directly above and below the center court trophy. This sideline placement is brand new for the 2026 Finals and has not appeared on any previous NBA Finals court.

**Where is The Finals cursive script on the Spurs 2026 NBA Finals court?**

The Finals cursive script wordmark is painted in both keys on both sides of the 2026 Spurs Finals floor at Frost Bank Center. This is the first time the cursive script has appeared in the keys on an NBA Finals court since the 2014 Miami Heat vs San Antonio Spurs NBA Finals, which was also the last Finals series the Spurs hosted at home. The 2026 court brings the cursive script back to the Spurs home floor after a 12-year absence.

**When does the 2026 NBA Finals start at Frost Bank Center?**

The 2026 NBA Finals starts Thursday with Game 1 at Madison Square Garden between the New York Knicks and the San Antonio Spurs. Frost Bank Center hosts Games 3 and 4 in San Antonio under the 2-2-1-1-1 home-away format, with Game 6 also returning to Frost Bank Center if necessary. Full game-by-game jersey grading in our [2026 NBA Finals jersey tracker](/stories/nba-finals-2026-jersey-tracker-knicks-spurs).

**When was the last NBA Finals game at Frost Bank Center?**

The 2026 NBA Finals at Frost Bank Center is the first NBA Finals at the renamed San Antonio Spurs home arena. The Spurs last hosted an NBA Finals game in 2014 during the Heat vs Spurs Finals at the AT&T Center, which has since been renamed Frost Bank Center.

**Are the 2026 NBA Finals court logos painted or decals?**

Painted. Adam Silver confirmed on the Pardon My Take podcast earlier this year that the NBA is painting the 2026 NBA Finals logos directly into the hardwood instead of using adhesive court decals. The change addresses player safety concerns from previous Finals series, where the raised edges of decals at center court created slipping issues. The in-arena photos of the Spurs court at Frost Bank Center confirm the painted finish.
