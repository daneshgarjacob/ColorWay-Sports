This is the full Colorado State 2026 uniform schedule, game by game, and it opens a brand-new chapter: **the Rams revealed their first adidas uniforms on August 24**, days before their first season in the Pac-12 kicks off at home against Wyoming on September 5.

## The New Adidas Whites Are the Headline

Colorado State ended its Under Armour era and signed a long-term deal with adidas in early August, and this is the first look at what that partnership produces. The reveal is the white set, and it is a confident first statement.

The details worth knowing:

- **Oversized green numbers with a Vegas gold outline**, front and shoulder. They read from the last row of Canvas Stadium, and on a white shirt they carry the whole design.
- **A stitched COLORADO STATE wordmark plate** across the chest. Sewn-on lettering instead of printed, which is the kind of build quality that separates a real uniform from a template.
- **Green over gold shoulder stripes**, a clean nod to the program's classic striping.
- **The curled-horn Ram logo** at the collar button, with the Pac-12 shield on the chest, because this is a Pac-12 uniform now.
- **A stitched orange A** tucked at the collar, with a matching A on the pant hip. That is the Aggies A, the letter that stood for this school before the Rams were the Rams, in the Aggie Orange that still gets its own day in Fort Collins every fall.

### Our Grade: A-

We love this uniform, and the reasons are specific.

The numbers are the star. College football has spent a decade shrinking numerals to make room for design gimmicks, and Colorado State went the other way: big, bold, green with a gold edge, the kind of numbers that make broadcast shots look better. The stitched wordmark plate is the other piece we keep coming back to. Sewn-on lettering costs more and reads richer, and against the white it gives the shirt a varsity weight that printed graphics never have.

The orange A is the detail that earns the hidden-gem points. It is small, it is in the right place, and it means something: the agricultural school heritage that Ag Day celebrates every season. A brand-new manufacturer honoring the oldest thing about the program is exactly how a first impression should work.

Why not the full A? We want to see the green home set before crowning the wardrobe, and a white uniform this restrained lives or dies by what it pairs with. But as a debut, adidas is putting real pressure on Nike this year, and this is one more piece of evidence.

## What Is Confirmed and What Is Not

The reliable rules for reading the grid below:

- **Only the white set has been revealed.** Colorado State has not yet released its green home uniform or this year's Ag Day set. Both are coming, and we will grade them here the day they drop.
- **Home games at Canvas Stadium** project as the green home set, per the standard home-dark convention.
- **Road games are white**, per NCAA visitor rules, and now we know exactly what that white looks like.
- **September 5 vs. Wyoming** is the Border War, the season opener, the Pac-12 debut and the first game in adidas, all at once.
