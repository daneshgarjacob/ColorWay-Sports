The 2026 FIFA World Cup away kits might be even more interesting than the home kits. This is where brands take risks, push creative boundaries, and sometimes completely miss. Adidas dropped 24 away jerseys featuring the retro trefoil logo, Nike brought 16, and Puma has 10 (Czech Republic and Iceland only have home kits so far). That gives us 50 away kits to rank.

We already ranked [every 2026 World Cup jersey overall](/stories/every-2026-world-cup-jersey-ranked) and [every home kit](/stories/every-2026-world-cup-home-jersey-ranked). This ranking focuses only on away kits. All images are courtesy of Nike, Adidas, and Puma.

---

## 50. Qatar (Adidas): F

<img src="/images/posts/adidas-world-cup-away-kits/qatar-front.jpg" alt="Qatar 2026 World Cup away kit by Adidas" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

There is just nothing here. White kit, maroon accents, no pattern, no personality. This is the most boring away kit in the entire 2026 World Cup. It looks like a plain training top you would grab off a clearance rack.

## 49. Algeria (Adidas): D-

<img src="/images/posts/adidas-world-cup-away-kits/algeria-front.jpg" alt="Algeria 2026 World Cup away kit by Adidas" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

The two-tone green is not it. The collar is strange, and the overall look just feels off. It looks like two different jerseys stitched together.

## 48. Egypt (Puma): D

<img src="/images/posts/puma-world-cup-kits/egypt-away.jpg" alt="Egypt 2026 World Cup away kit by Puma" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

<img src="/images/posts/wc-china-away.jpg" alt="China 2026 World Cup away kit by Nike" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

Venezuela takes the top Adidas spot. The white base is minimal, but the pops of yellow, red, and blue in the three stripes and gold detailing on the collar are perfect. Understated, sophisticated, and the retro block lettering on the crest ties the whole thing together.

## 3. Norway (Nike): A

<img src="/images/posts/wc-norway-away.jpg" alt="Norway 2026 World Cup away kit by Nike Viking warrior" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

The all-black Viking warrior kit is aggressive and clean. Paired with Norway's incredible home kit, this makes them one of the strongest overall packages from Nike. The dark palette is a bold choice and it works.

## 2. Brazil (Nike): A

<img src="/images/posts/wc-brazil-away.jpg" alt="Brazil 2026 World Cup away kit by Nike Jordan Brand" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

The darker palette through the Jordan Brand collab adds something different to Brazil's identity. The colors still pop in that way that only Nike seems to pull off. A strong complement to the iconic yellow home kit.

## 1. France (Nike): A+

<img src="/images/posts/wc-france-away.jpg" alt="France 2026 World Cup away kit by Nike Statue of Liberty green" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

The best away kit in the 2026 World Cup, and it's not close. That green jersey inspired by the Statue of Liberty is the most creative thing any brand did this cycle. The patina color is unique, it tells a story connecting France to the United States where this World Cup is being held, and it's going to look incredible on the pitch this summer. This is the kit everyone will remember from 2026. France wins.
