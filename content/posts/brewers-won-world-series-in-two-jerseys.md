**The Milwaukee Brewers carried five uniforms this season and won the World Series wearing two of them.** Cream at home. Navy on the road. Thirteen playoff games, 11 wins, and not one night in anything else.

No powder blue, even though it was their main road jersey all year. No pinstripes. No City Connect. The Brewers picked two looks in October and never blinked, and they finished it with a 4-1 win at Tropicana Field on Wednesday for the first title in franchise history.

Here's our take: **that's the right way to do it, and every team in baseball should copy it.** For every game, both teams' jerseys and the score, see [what the Brewers wore in every World Series game](/stories/world-series-uniforms-2026). This story is about why it worked.

## Quick Answers

- **What did the Brewers wear in the 2026 postseason?** Only two uniforms: the cream home jersey in all six home games and the navy alternate in all seven road games.
- **What was the Brewers' record in each jersey?** 6-0 in cream at home and 5-2 in navy on the road, 11-2 overall.
- **What did the Brewers wear to win the World Series?** The navy alternate with gray pants, the yellow-front ball-in-glove cap and the World Series patch, in a 4-1 Game 5 win at Tampa Bay.
- **Did the Brewers wear powder blue in the playoffs?** No. It was their most-worn road jersey in the regular season, 43 games, and it didn't play once in October.
- **Our take:** two jerseys in October is the right call. Teams should pick a home look and a road look for the playoffs and stop rotating.

**Navy on the road: 5-2.** The navy alternate with gray pants and the yellow-front ball-in-glove cap. Two games at San Diego, two at Dodger Stadium for the sweep, three at Tropicana Field, including the clincher. That's the jersey in every title photo now.

## Why Two Jerseys Is the Right Call

**It's who they are.** Navy and gold and a ball-in-glove is Milwaukee. When a team plays its biggest games, it should look like the team, not like this week's alternate. Fans tuning in for the first time all year saw the same Brewers every single night.

**It looks better on TV.** The World Series is the biggest stage in the sport, and a lot of those viewers don't follow a team every day. Cream against the Rays' road gray, then navy and gold against the Rays' home white, is two clean, simple matchups. Nobody had to ask which team was which.

**It stops the guessing.** Rotation teams make every playoff game a jersey debate. The Brewers took it off the table. Cream at home, navy on the road, and the only question left was the score.

**And yes, it's a little superstitious.** Baseball players don't change what's working. Once the cream went 2-0 against the Padres, nobody in that clubhouse was going to suggest the pinstripes.

## The Jersey That Got Left Behind: Powder Blue

This is the controversial part. The Brewers retired their road grays before this season and made a new powder blue set the main road jersey, a revival of the look the team wore from 1970 into the mid-1980s. It was the most-worn road look all year, 43 games, and fans loved it.

It didn't play a single October game. The navy alternate took every road start.

We think the Brewers were right anyway. Powder blue is a great regular-season jersey: it's fun, it's retro, it sells. But the navy is the stronger look, and it plays better against a home white. Powder blue against the Rays' white at the Trop would have been two light jerseys on one field. Navy against white is the matchup you want in a clincher.

The catch: the last time the Brewers played in the World Series, in 1982 against the Cardinals, powder blue was their road jersey. So the new set got the history and none of the October. That will be the first question in 2027. We'd say it can have all the summer it wants.

## Which Teams Should Copy the Brewers?

**Every team with more than three uniforms.** Wear the alternates all summer. Run the City Connect on Fridays. Then in October, pick one home look and one road look and stop.

That's the hot take, and the Brewers aren't the only proof. The Rays bounced between three looks in the Series: road gray in Milwaukee, the white Devil Rays throwback in Game 3 and the home white in Games 4 and 5. They lost the throwback game 10-1. ## The Bottom Line

Five uniforms in the closet, two on the field, one title. The Brewers went 6-0 in cream and 5-2 in navy, left the powder blue at home and the pinstripes on the hanger, and won the first World Series in franchise history looking like themselves every night. Teams love to rotate. In October, they shouldn't. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many jerseys did the Brewers wear in the 2026 postseason?**

Two. The Brewers wore the cream home jersey in all six home playoff games and the navy alternate in all seven road playoff games. They didn't wear the powder blue road jersey, the white pinstripe home alternate or the "Wisco" City Connect at any point in October.

**What was the Brewers' record in the cream jersey this postseason?**

6-0. The Brewers won both NLDS home games against the Padres, both NLCS home games against the Dodgers and World Series Games 1 and 2 against the Rays in the cream.

**What was the Brewers' record in the navy jersey this postseason?**

5-2. They went 1-1 at San Diego in the NLDS, 2-0 at Dodger Stadium in the NLCS and 2-1 at Tropicana Field in the World Series, including the Game 5 clincher.

**What jersey did the Brewers win the World Series in?**

The navy alternate, with gray pants, the yellow-front ball-in-glove cap and the 2026 World Series patch, in a 4-1 win over the Rays in Game 5 on October 28 at Tropicana Field.

**Why didn't the Brewers wear powder blue in the playoffs?**

The team didn't say. Powder blue was their most-worn road jersey in the regular season, 43 games, but the navy alternate started every road game in October. Our guess is the Brewers stuck with what was winning: the navy went 2-0 at Dodger Stadium and they never changed it.

**How many uniforms do the Brewers have in 2026?**

Five: the cream home, the white pinstripe home alternate, the navy alternate, the powder blue road and the "Wisco" City Connect. The gray road uniforms were retired before the season.

**Did the Brewers wear powder blue in the 1982 World Series?**

Yes. Powder blue was the Brewers' road uniform in 1982, when they lost the World Series to the Cardinals in seven games. The 2026 powder blue set is a revival of that look.
