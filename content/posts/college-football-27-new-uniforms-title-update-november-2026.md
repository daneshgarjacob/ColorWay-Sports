**EA Sports College Football 27's Tuesday, Nov. 17 title update adds seven new helmets that we can confirm: six that EA showed (Oregon, Texas A&M, Michigan State, Kentucky, Iowa State and Fresno State) and Syracuse's Otto helmet, which the team announced on its own.**

EA posted the preview on Monday afternoon, Nov. 16, with the line "Swipe to see the new uniforms and helmets dropping in Tuesday's Title Update." The post has one cover image and six team slides, and each slide names the helmet. On Tuesday morning Syracuse posted that the Otto helmet "is now available" in the game, which tells us the update has more in it than EA's six slides. EA has not published written patch notes for this update on its news page as of Tuesday, so the list below is what EA and the teams have shown, not the full list.

## Quick Answers

- **When did the College Football 27 November title update come out?** Tuesday, Nov. 17, 2026. EA called it "Tuesday's Title Update."
- **Which teams got new helmets?** From EA's preview: Oregon, Texas A&M, Michigan State, Kentucky, Iowa State and Fresno State. Syracuse confirmed its Otto helmet separately.
- **How many new helmets?** Seven confirmed. There may be more; EA has not posted a full written list.
- **How do I use the new helmets?** Update the game, then go to Edit Uniforms on the Team Select screen, per Syracuse.
- **Are these real helmets?** Most of them match helmets the teams wore this fall. Details for each are below.
- **Is there a new Syracuse helmet in College Football 27?** Yes. The Otto helmet Syracuse wore for the first time on Oct. 31 against SMU.
- **Our take (opinion):** Oregon's black helmet with white wings is the best one in the update, and Michigan State's Gruff Sparty is the one fans will use most.

## Every New Helmet In The Update

These are the names EA used on its slides, in the order EA posted them.

### Texas A&M: Special Edition USA Helmet

A white helmet with the ATM logo filled in as an American flag, red and white stripes with a blue field and white stars, plus "Aggies" in script near the front. EA shows it with a white jersey with maroon numbers.

This matches a helmet Texas A&M actually wore this season. Against Arizona State at Kyle Field on Sept. 12, the Aggies wore a white helmet with a red, white and blue flag-style ATM decal over a maroon jersey and white pants, and won 48-20. Every Aggies game this year is in our [Texas A&M 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/texas-am-uniform-schedule-2026).

### Michigan State: Green Gruff Sparty Helmet

A green helmet with the white Gruff Sparty logo, the snarling, helmeted Spartan head that Michigan State used in the 1980s and has brought back on helmets in recent seasons. EA pairs it with a green facemask and the green jersey with the Greek key on the shoulders.

Michigan State has not worn Gruff Sparty in a game this season, based on our tracking: every 2026 game so far has been the standard Spartan helmet in green or white. So this one is a look the game gives you before the team does. Our [Michigan State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/michigan-state-uniform-schedule-2026) has every game.

### Kentucky: Black Helmet

A black shell with the blue interlocking UK logo on the side. On the back, EA's slide shows an American flag, the SEC logo and a Kentucky state outline. EA shows it with Kentucky's white jersey.

That is the same description as the helmet Kentucky wore at Oklahoma on Oct. 17: black helmet with the blue UK, white jersey, black pants. It is a different helmet from the matte black Night Ops shell with the blue and white stripe that Kentucky wore against Florida on Nov. 14. More in our [Kentucky 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/kentucky-uniform-schedule-2026) and our [Night Ops breakdown](/stories/kentucky-night-ops-all-black-uniforms-florida-2026).

### Iowa State: Black Punching Cy Helmet

A black helmet with a white Cy logo on the side, worn in the game with a black jersey with white sleeve stripes. "Punching Cy" is EA's name for it.

Iowa State did go black this season, for Halloween against Oklahoma State on Oct. 31, and won 51-49 on the last kick. That helmet had a white center stripe and the white Cynado logo. We cannot tell from EA's slides whether the game's version is meant to be that exact helmet, so we are treating it as its own look. The full season is in our [Iowa State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/iowa-state-uniform-schedule-2026).

### Oregon: Black Helmet With White Wings

A black helmet with big white wings down the side and a black facemask. EA shows it with a black jersey with diamond-plate shoulders and silver numbers outlined in green.

This is the helmet Oregon wore against Northwestern on Halloween: a matte black helmet with white wings and a black facemask, black jersey, white pants. Oregon posted it as "Bold by design." EA used it as the cover image of the whole preview. Every Ducks look this year is in our [Oregon 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/oregon-uniform-schedule-2026).

### Fresno State: New Blackout Helmet

A matte black helmet with a gray Bulldog decal with red eyes and a red mouth, a crossbones stripe down the middle, "DOGS" on the front bumper, and the Green V on the back. The Green V has been on the back of Fresno State helmets since 1997 and stands for the Central Valley.

This lines up with the "Dark mode" black helmet with silver crossbones that Fresno State teased for its Fright Night game against Oregon State on Oct. 31. Our [Fresno State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/fresno-state-uniform-schedule-2026) has the details.

### Syracuse: Otto Helmet

This one was not in EA's preview. Syracuse posted on Tuesday morning that "The Otto Helmet is now available in EA SPORTS College Football 27," and told players to update the game today.

Syracuse wore it for the first time on Oct. 31 against No. 19 SMU, with an orange jersey and orange pants, and won 30-27 with 17 seconds left. ## How To Equip The New Helmets In College Football 27

1. **Update the game.** The helmets come with the Nov. 17 title update, so download it first.
2. **Pick your team on the Team Select screen.**
3. **Choose Edit Uniforms** and select the new helmet. Syracuse's post gives this exact path: "Equip it under Edit Uniforms at the Team Select screen."

EA did not say in its post whether the new helmets also show up for teams in Dynasty or Ultimate Team, so we are not going to guess.

## What We Could Not Confirm

- **The full list.** EA's post says "new uniforms and helmets," and Syracuse's Otto helmet proves there is more in the update than the six slides. EA has not posted written patch notes for it on its news page as of Tuesday.
- **Full uniforms.** Every EA slide names a helmet only. The jerseys in the photos may be ones already in the game.
- **Which game each helmet is based on.** The matches above are our comparisons with what the teams wore this season. EA did not say.

## Our Take (Opinion)

Oregon's black helmet with white wings is the best thing in the update. It was one of the best helmets of Halloween weekend in real life, and the wings read clearly at any camera distance.

Gruff Sparty is the one people will actually use. It is a fan favorite, and Michigan State has not worn it this season, so this is the only way to see it on the field in 2026 so far.

Texas A&M's flag helmet is the weakest. The flag fills the ATM letters, so the logo loses its shape from a distance.

Syracuse's Otto helmet is the most fun, and it is a good sign that a one-game Halloween helmet reached the game two and a half weeks after it was worn.
