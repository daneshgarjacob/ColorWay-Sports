**The Seattle Torrent finally have their logo on their chest.** The PWHL club unveiled its **first jerseys featuring the team logo** on Tuesday, November 17: new **crested home and away jerseys** built around a textured version of the Torrent's **wave "S"**, in **slate green, river blue and foam**. They replace the sweaters the team wore in its inaugural season.

The **away jersey debuts Saturday, December 5, in Vancouver**, when the Torrent open the 2026-27 season against the Goldeneyes at noon Pacific. The **home jersey debuts Sunday, December 20, at Climate Pledge Arena** in the home opener at 4 p.m. Pacific.

## Quick Answers

- **Do the Seattle Torrent have new jerseys?** Yes. The PWHL unveiled the Torrent's first crested home and away jerseys on November 17, 2026.
- **What is on the front?** A textured version of the Torrent's "S" logo, which the league describes as both a letter S for Seattle and a rush of water.
- **What colors are they?** Slate green, river blue and foam (a cream white).
- **What makes the design different?** Curved, wave-like color blocking on the lower body and sleeves. The PWHL calls it the first non-linear design on a PWHL jersey.
- **When do they debut?** The away jersey on December 5 in Vancouver, the home jersey on December 20 at Climate Pledge Arena.
- **Who makes them?** Bauer, the PWHL's official jersey partner.
- **Can you buy one?** Yes, home and away jerseys with player options are on sale now at the PWHL's official shop in the U.S. and Canada.

## What The New Torrent Jerseys Look Like

Every detail here comes from the PWHL's announcement and the league's jersey graphic.

**The crest.** A **textured "S"** that doubles as a rush of water, the logo the team has used since its identity launch on November 6, 2025. On the slate green jersey in the league's graphic, worn by forward **Abbey Murphy**, **TORRENT** runs across the middle of the S in white.

**The color blocking.** The lower part of the body, extending into the sleeves, is broken up by **curved, wave-like bands** of color instead of straight stripes. The PWHL says it is the **first non-linear design** on any PWHL jersey.

**The numbers.** The perforation pattern on the back numbers includes **rising wave symbols**. The league's graphic calls it "an organic perforated pattern" that evokes the rhythm of waves.

**The colors.** **Slate green, river blue and foam.** The league ties them to Seattle as a seaport between Puget Sound and Lake Washington, and to the Duwamish River, which runs from south Seattle into Elliott Bay, where "cream-crested waves lap the shores of the downtown Seattle waterfront."

Seattle and Vancouver joined the PWHL as expansion teams for the 2025-26 season, and the league unveiled the Torrent name and logo on November 6, 2025. The team played its first season in sweaters that did not carry the new crest. The league's release does not describe those sweaters, so we have not guessed at them here, and we will add the team's own picture of the old set if it posts one.

## What They Said

"Debuting our official jerseys is an exciting step in our evolution as an organization," Torrent forward **Alex Carpenter** said. "The colors and wave imagery represent the connection that this team has to our region."

**Amy Scheer**, the PWHL's executive vice president of business operations, said Seattle turned out "in record-breaking numbers" in the inaugural season and that the crested jerseys mark "an exciting next chapter" for the team.

## Our Take

This is a strong first real jersey. The wave "S" was always the best part of the Torrent identity, and it finally gets the chest. The curved color blocking is the detail that sets it apart. Almost every hockey sweater uses straight stripes across the hem and arms, and bending them into waves makes the Torrent recognizable from across the rink without adding clutter.

Slate green and river blue also give the Torrent a look of their own in a building they share with the [Seattle Kraken](/stories/seattle-kraken-uniform-schedule-2026-27), whose full jersey schedule is in our guide.

The question is how the wave bands read on TV. Subtle curves can flatten into plain stripes on a broadcast. We will know on December 5 in Vancouver.

## Where To Buy Seattle Torrent Jerseys

The home and away jerseys, including personalized player versions, are on sale at the PWHL's official shop in the U.S. and Canada. In Seattle they are sold, while quantities last, at the **Kraken Community Iceplex**, **The Armory at Seattle Center** and, on game days, **The Team Store at Climate Pledge Arena**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Did the Seattle Torrent get new jerseys?**

Yes. On November 17, 2026, the PWHL unveiled the Torrent's first crested home and away jerseys. They replace the sweaters from the team's inaugural season.

**What do the Seattle Torrent jerseys look like?**

A textured wave "S" crest on the front, curved wave-shaped color blocking across the lower body and sleeves, and wave symbols in the number perforation, in slate green, river blue and foam.

**What are the Seattle Torrent's colors?**

On the new jerseys: slate green, river blue and foam. The team's full identity palette, announced in 2025, is Slate Green, Shadow Blue and Glacier Blue, with Foam, Haze Grey and Basalt Black as accent colors.

**What does the Torrent "S" logo mean?**

The PWHL says the S is both a letter for Seattle and a rush of water, with curves that mirror river channels. The name Torrent comes from the waterways that shape Washington.

**When will the Seattle Torrent wear the new jerseys?**

The away jersey debuts on Saturday, December 5, 2026, at noon Pacific in Vancouver. The home jersey debuts on Sunday, December 20, at 4 p.m. Pacific at Climate Pledge Arena.

**Why didn't the Torrent's first jerseys have the logo?**

The league did not say why in the release. The Torrent name and logo were unveiled on November 6, 2025, and the new set is the first to carry the logo.

**Who makes the Seattle Torrent jerseys?**

Bauer, the PWHL's official jersey partner.

**Where can I buy a Seattle Torrent jersey?**

At the PWHL's official online shop in the U.S. and Canada, and in person at the Kraken Community Iceplex, The Armory at Seattle Center and The Team Store at Climate Pledge Arena on game days.

**Is there a Seattle Torrent away jersey?**

Yes. This is the first time a Torrent away jersey has been sold to fans, and it is the one the team wears first, on opening day in Vancouver.

**Who designed the Seattle Torrent logo?**

The PWHL led the design and worked with the creative agency Flower Shop on the name and logo.
