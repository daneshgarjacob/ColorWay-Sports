The Chiefs have two jerseys in 2026 and nothing else: no alternate, no throwback, no alternate helmet, and no Rivalries uniform, because the program has not reached the AFC West yet. That makes this the shortest ranking we will write, and one of the easiest to defend. We put Kansas City ninth in our [best NFL uniforms of 2026](/stories/best-nfl-uniforms-2026-ranked) at an A-, and both jerseys are a big part of why.

The white road jersey has red numbers, red and gold sleeve stripes and the AFL patch on the chest, worn with red pants under the red arrowhead helmet. It projects to all nine road games: at the Dolphins, Raiders, Seahawks, Broncos, Falcons, Bills, Rams, Bengals and Chargers. The two to circle are back to back: Thanksgiving night at Buffalo on Thursday, November 26, and the following Thursday at the Rams on December 3, where Kansas City is the plain half of the frame against Buffalo's royal blue and the Rams' gold Classic Sol.

Red pants are what make this work. With a red helmet and red pants, the white jersey sits between two blocks of the team color, so the road uniform reads as red and white rather than mostly white. The gold is used as a thin accent and never becomes a second main color, which is the right call.

It grades an A-. It is one of the better road uniforms in the league. It sits second only because the home jersey puts that red in the biggest space it has.

The red home jersey has white numbers with gold trim, the same striped sleeves and the AFL patch, worn under the red helmet, usually with white pants. It is the jersey for all eight home games at Arrowhead: the Denver Broncos, Indianapolis Colts, Los Angeles Chargers, New York Jets, Arizona Cardinals, New England Patriots, San Francisco 49ers and Las Vegas Raiders. It opened the season on Monday night against Denver with white pants, and Kansas City won 31-10. The December pair, the Patriots on Monday, December 21 and the 49ers the following Sunday, is the best stretch on the calendar.

This jersey also covers the one variation the Chiefs allow themselves: red pants with the red jersey, the all-red look they bring out for select prime-time home games. It has no announced 2026 date. It is still the red jersey, not a separate uniform, and it is one of the few monochrome looks in the league that works, because the red is deep enough to hold its shape under the lights.

It grades an A, the top mark in the closet. The red is rich without being loud, the gold is used as trim instead of a second main color, and the arrowhead reads from anywhere in the stadium. It has barely changed in sixty years because it has never needed to.

## The Bottom Line on the Chiefs Jersey Rankings

The Chiefs jerseys rank by how much red each one shows. The red home jersey grades an A and the white road jersey an A-, and there is almost nothing between them.

Two jerseys is the whole closet, and that restraint is the point. Kansas City has never needed an alternate to look like itself, which is more than most of the league can say.
