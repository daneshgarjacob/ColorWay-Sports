**The Lakers' Specter Edition jersey is black with purple and gold.** The team posted its official photos on Monday, November 23, with the caption "Built for the spotlight. Specter Edition - Taking center stage 12/14." The base is **black**, with **purple light streaks** running across the front and down the sides, **black numbers outlined in gold**, a **gold LAKERS wordmark** and **gold trim** around the neck and arms. The Lakers wear it **three times, all at home**: December 14 vs the Pelicans, December 21 vs the Pacers and December 23 vs the Nuggets.

## Quick Answers

- **What does the Lakers Specter jersey look like?** Black, with purple light streaks across the front, black numbers outlined in gold, a gold LAKERS wordmark, a black Nike logo outlined in gold and gold trim.
- **When do the Lakers wear it?** December 14 vs the Pelicans, December 21 vs the Pacers and December 23 vs the Nuggets. All three are home games.
- **When is the first game?** Monday, December 14, against New Orleans. The Lakers are the last of the eight Specter teams to debut theirs.
- **When does it go on sale?** November 30, the date the Specter jerseys go on sale (per the Hawks' official page). The Lakers have not posted their own sale details yet.
- **Why do the Lakers have one?** Nike made the Specter Edition only for the NBA's eight oldest franchises. The Lakers date back to the 1946 Detroit Gems. [Why only eight teams](/stories/why-only-8-nba-teams-got-specter-edition-jerseys).
- **Our grade:** C. No. 5 of 8 in [our Specter ranking](/stories/nba-specter-edition-jerseys-ranked).

## What The Jersey Looks Like

Here is every piece, from the Lakers' own photos:

- **The base.** Black. This is a black jersey first, with the team colors added on top.
- **The purple.** Lakers purple, in blurry horizontal light streaks, like a long-exposure photo. They are brightest across the chest and down the left side, and they fade toward the hem. On the back they show up low, across the bottom and the side panels.
- **The numbers.** Black, with a thick gold outline. From a distance the number reads as a gold outline on black.
- **The wordmark.** The classic LAKERS script in gold with a black outline, smaller than on the regular jerseys, sitting below the Nike logo.
- **The Nike logo.** Black, outlined in gold, moved toward the middle of the chest like on every Specter jersey.
- **The trim.** Thin gold piping around the neck and arm holes.
- **The back.** The player's name sits below the number, in gold, the same Specter layout every team has.
- **The patch.** The gold Albert patch on the left chest, the team's sponsor patch this season ([more on the Albert deal](/stories/lakers-albert-jersey-patch-2026)).
- **The shorts.** Black with the same purple streaks and gold trim, from the Lakers' photos.

## When It Goes On Sale

The Specter jerseys go on sale **November 30**. The Hawks' official Specter page gives that date for online sales, with in-arena sales starting December 3, and the Celtics say theirs are "dropping soon on-court, online and in-store." The Lakers have not posted their own sale details yet. Nike has not announced a price, so we are not guessing at one.

## Why The Lakers Got One

The Specter Edition is only for the league's eight oldest franchises, and the Lakers are one of them. The team started in 1946 as the **Detroit Gems** of the National Basketball League, moved to Minneapolis in 1947, joined the BAA in 1948 and the NBA in its first season, 1949-50, and moved to Los Angeles in 1960. The other seven are the Kings, Pistons, Celtics, Knicks, Warriors, Hawks and 76ers. ## Our Grade: C

The official photos look better than the leak images did. There is more purple than we thought, and the light streaks have some motion to them. The gold outline on the numbers looks sharp up close.

It is still a black jersey for a team that should never need one. Purple and gold is one of the best color combinations in sports, and here the purple is a blur and the gold is an outline. From the upper deck or on a TV screen, this is going to look like a black jersey with a little color in it. The Lakers also already have a black City Edition this season, so this is the second black jersey in the closet. We gave it a **C** when it leaked in April ([our first review](/stories/lakers-christmas-jersey-2026)), and the photos do not change that. In [our ranking of all eight](/stories/nba-specter-edition-jerseys-ranked) it is No. 5, right in the middle.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the Lakers Specter Edition jersey?**

A fifth Lakers uniform for 2026-27, part of Nike's Specter Edition line for the NBA's eight oldest franchises. It joins the Icon, Association, Statement and City Edition jerseys.

**What color is the Lakers Specter jersey?**

Black, with purple light streaks across the front and sides, black numbers outlined in gold, a gold LAKERS wordmark and gold trim.

**When do the Lakers wear the Specter Edition jersey?**

Three home games: December 14 vs the Pelicans, December 21 vs the Pacers and December 23 vs the Nuggets.

**When is the Lakers Specter jersey debut?**

Monday, December 14, 2026, at home against the New Orleans Pelicans. The Lakers' post on November 23 said "Taking center stage 12/14."

**When can I buy the Lakers Specter jersey?**

The Specter jerseys go on sale November 30, per the Hawks' official page. The Lakers have not posted their own sale details yet.

**How much is the Lakers Specter jersey?**

**Is the Lakers Specter jersey a Christmas jersey?**

No. It leaked in April labeled as a Christmas jersey, but the Lakers' three Specter games are December 14, 21 and 23. No Specter game falls on Christmas Day.

No. The Lakers have a separate black City Edition with gold LAKERS this season, worn 12 times starting January 14. The Specter is its own jersey.

The Specter Edition goes only to the eight oldest franchises. The Lakers date to 1946. The Clippers started in 1970 as the Buffalo Braves.

Five: Association (white), Icon (gold), Statement (purple), City Edition (black) and Specter Edition. The count for each is in our [Lakers uniform schedule](/stories/lakers-uniform-schedule-2026-27).
