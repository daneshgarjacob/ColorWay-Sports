The Cowboys run their uniforms backwards from the rest of the league, and they have done it for so long that it reads as tradition rather than defiance. Dallas wears **white at home**, and here is the part most people get wrong: they wear white on the road too. Because the home team picks first and almost every host chooses its dark jersey, Dallas ends up in white nearly every week. The navy jersey came out just **three times in all of 2025**. Add the 1960s Thanksgiving throwback, which has owned the holiday four years running, and you have the whole 2026 closet. Here is the Cowboys uniform schedule week by week.

## The White Set (Almost Every Week)

The white jersey with the metallic-silver-blue pants is the uniform Dallas has built its identity on, and it draws all nine home dates: the Week 2 home opener against Washington on September 20, a Thursday-night game against Tampa Bay in Week 5, the Week 10 visit from San Francisco, and a New Year's weekend date with the Giants in Week 17 among them.

It also projects to the eight road games, which is the part people find surprising. Dallas is not choosing white on the road so much as receiving it: the host picks first, the host almost always picks dark, and white is what is left. The practical effect is that the Cowboys wear the same jersey roughly sixteen times a year.

The white set is the better of the two primaries and it is not particularly close. The navy star on the white shell, the blue-and-white sleeve stripe, and the silver-blue pants make a combination nobody else in the league is running, and the fact that Dallas insists on wearing it at home means the good version is the one their own crowd sees.

## The Navy Jersey (Rare, and Undated)

This is the part of the Dallas closet that gets misunderstood most often, including by us until we checked. The navy jersey is labelled the road uniform, but it is not what Dallas wears on the road. In 2025 the Cowboys wore white in **eleven** games and the standard navy in only **three**, and their trips to Philadelphia, Chicago, and Carolina were all in white.

The reason is the rule, not the wardrobe. The home team picks its jersey and the visitor takes the contrasting one. Nearly every host in the league picks its dark color, which puts Dallas in white. So the Cowboys wear white at home by choice and white on the road by consequence, and the navy only surfaces on the handful of dates when a host wears white or when Dallas is staging something. In 2025 that meant Detroit in early December and the Christmas Day game against Washington.

None of those dates are known for 2026 yet. Expect roughly three, most likely attached to a nationally televised game, and expect Dallas to tell us in late August.

As a uniform, the navy is the plainer of the two primaries. The silver-blue that makes the home whites distinctive gets swapped for a flatter silver, and the shoulder stripe loses most of its contrast against the darker base. There is an old superstition about the navy being unlucky for Dallas. We have no opinion on that, but scarcity is doing the jersey a favor.

## The Thanksgiving Throwback (Week 12, Projected)

The best uniform Dallas owns comes out once a year. The 1960s throwback pairs a **white helmet** with the navy jersey, oversized stars on the white shoulder yoke, solid block numbers, and white pants with twin navy stripes. The Cowboys have worn it on Thanksgiving in 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025, and 2026 puts Philadelphia at AT&T Stadium on Thursday, November 26.

If it happens, and everything about the pattern says it will, it is the marquee uniform game of the Dallas season. The white helmet is the whole trick: it removes the silver that anchors every other Cowboys look and leaves a cleaner, older, more graphic uniform underneath.

One popular hope for this game does not survive contact with the rules, so it is worth heading off. Because Dallas wears white at home, a visiting team is normally free to bring a coloured jersey to AT&T Stadium, and Philadelphia used exactly that opening in 2025 to wear **Kelly Green** on a trip to Dallas, the first road wearing since the set returned. That will not happen on Thanksgiving. The 1960s throwback is a **navy** jersey, not a white one, so for this one date Dallas is the dark team and Philadelphia takes white. A Cowboys throwback against a Kelly Green Eagles is not a combination the league permits.

The Eagles have not announced their 2026 Kelly Green dates, and our [Eagles 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/eagles-uniform-schedule-2026) has them in white for the Thanksgiving trip for exactly this reason. The green will have to come out on a week when Dallas is in white.

## The Arctic White Helmet

Dallas added an all-white helmet combination on Christmas Day 2025, pairing the Arctic White shell with the navy jersey and white pants. It is a separate look from the Thanksgiving throwback, which uses a different white helmet with the classic stripe. No 2026 date has been announced. If the club brings it back, a nationally televised December game is the natural slot, which points at Week 15 at the Rams or Week 16 against Jacksonville.
