The Cowboys published their full 2026 uniform schedule on Wednesday, September 2, and it confirms the thing this page has argued from the start: Dallas is a white-jersey team. **White goes ten times**, at home by choice and on the road because the host almost always picks dark and leaves the visitor in white. The **navy primary gets three road dates**, each one a trip to a host that wears white at home. The **all-white Color Rush goes three times**, including a Monday night in Philadelphia. And the **Thanksgiving Classic is official**, Week 12 against the Eagles on November 26. Here is the whole schedule, game by game, with the seven dates that are not plain white marked.

## The White Set (10 Games, Confirmed)

The white jersey with the metallic-silver-blue pants is the uniform Dallas has built its identity on, and it draws ten of the seventeen games. Six are at home: the Rio de Janeiro game against Baltimore in Week 3, which counts as a Dallas home date, the Thursday-night visit from Tampa Bay in Week 5, Arizona in Week 8, San Francisco in Week 10, Tennessee in Week 11, and the New Year's weekend date with the Giants in Week 17. Four are on the road: the Sunday-night opener at the Giants in Week 1, Lambeau in Week 6, a Monday night in Seattle in Week 13, and the finale at Washington in Week 18.

Those four road whites are not a choice so much as a consequence. The host picks first, the host almost always picks dark, and white is what is left for the visitor. The three road games where Dallas does not wear white are the three where the host is wearing white itself, which is covered below.

The white set is the better of the two primaries and it is not particularly close. The navy star on the white shell, the blue-and-white sleeve stripe, and the silver-blue pants make a combination nobody else in the league is running, and the fact that Dallas insists on wearing it at home means the good version is the one their own crowd sees.

## The Navy Jersey (3 Games, All on the Road)

This is the part of the Dallas closet that gets misunderstood most often, and the 2026 schedule settles it. The navy jersey is labelled the road uniform, but Dallas wears it in only **three** of the nine road games, and each of the three is a trip to a host that wears white at home:

- **Week 4 at the Texans, Sunday, October 4.** Houston is celebrating its 25th season at this game, and with Dallas confirmed in navy the Texans will be in white at home. Our [Texans 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/texans-uniform-schedule-2026) has the detail.
- **Week 9 at the Colts, Sunday, November 8.** Indianapolis announced its all-white White Out for this game back in the summer, which is why Dallas is in navy. The [Colts 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/colts-uniform-schedule-2026) covers it.
- **Week 15 at the Rams, Sunday, December 20.** Los Angeles published white at home for the Dallas game on its own schedule, and the [Rams 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/rams-uniform-schedule-2026) was the first place this navy date showed up.

The reason is the rule, not the wardrobe. The home team picks its jersey and the visitor takes the contrasting one. Nearly every host in the league picks its dark color, which puts Dallas in white. So the Cowboys wear white at home by choice and white on the road by consequence, and the navy only surfaces when a host chooses white. In 2025 that happened three times as well, at Detroit in early December and on Christmas Day at Washington among them.

As a uniform, the navy is the plainer of the two primaries. The silver-blue that makes the home whites distinctive gets swapped for a flatter silver, and the shoulder stripe loses most of its contrast against the darker base. There is an old superstition about the navy being unlucky for Dallas. We have no opinion on that, but scarcity is doing the jersey a favor.

## The Color Rush (3 Games, All-White)

The all-white Color Rush is the fan favourite of the Dallas wardrobe, and three dates is the most the team has given it in years. It is a different uniform from the standard whites, not just a pants swap: a **white helmet** with the current star and a white facemask, a white jersey with **navy shoulders carrying the twin stars** in the manner of the 1990s double-star jersey, navy block numbers, and white pants with two navy stripes down the side. Head to toe, it is the whitest uniform in the league.

- **Week 2 vs. the Commanders, Sunday, September 20.** The home opener, at 4:25 Eastern. Washington has burgundy pencilled in for this trip on our [Commanders 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/commanders-uniform-schedule-2026), and burgundy against all-white is a good-looking game.
- **Week 7 at the Eagles, Monday, October 26.** A Monday night in Philadelphia, and Dallas is in white, which means the Eagles are free to wear anything. Kelly Green has not been announced for this date, but a national-television home game against Dallas is exactly the kind of night Philadelphia picks for it. Our [Eagles 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/eagles-uniform-schedule-2026) tracks it.
- **Week 16 vs. the Jaguars, Sunday, December 27.** Sunday Night Football at AT&T Stadium in the last week of the calendar year, and the last of the three.

## The Thanksgiving Classic (Week 12, Confirmed)

The best uniform Dallas owns comes out once a year, and the team has now confirmed the date. The 1960s throwback, which the club labels the Classic, pairs a **white helmet** with the navy jersey, oversized stars on the white shoulder yoke, solid block numbers, and white pants with twin navy stripes. The Cowboys wore it on Thanksgiving in 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025, and it is official for a fifth straight year: **Week 12 against Philadelphia at AT&T Stadium on Thursday, November 26.**

It is the marquee uniform game of the Dallas season. The white helmet is the whole trick: it removes the silver that anchors every other Cowboys look and leaves a cleaner, older, more graphic uniform underneath.

One popular hope for this game does not survive contact with the rules, so it is worth heading off. Because Dallas wears white at home, a visiting team is normally free to bring a coloured jersey to AT&T Stadium, and Philadelphia used exactly that opening in 2025 to wear **Kelly Green** on a trip to Dallas, the first road wearing since the set returned. That will not happen on Thanksgiving. The 1960s throwback is a **navy** jersey, not a white one, so for this one date Dallas is the dark team and Philadelphia takes white. A Cowboys throwback against a Kelly Green Eagles is not a combination the league permits.

The Eagles have confirmed only one Kelly Green date so far, Week 9 against the Giants, and our [Eagles 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/eagles-uniform-schedule-2026) has them in white for the Thanksgiving trip for exactly this reason. The green will have to come out on a week when Dallas is in white, and the Week 7 Monday-night game in Philadelphia, with Dallas in the Color Rush, is the obvious candidate.

## The White Helmet

Dallas has one white helmet shell and it appears on four dates in 2026, in two guises. For the three Color Rush games it carries the current star and a white facemask. For the Thanksgiving Classic it switches to the vintage star and a gray facemask. The team swaps the decals between looks rather than carrying two separate helmets.

The one combination missing from the 2026 schedule is the one Dallas debuted on Christmas Day 2025 at Washington: the white helmet with the **navy jersey** and white pants, a pairing the franchise had never worn before. It was a one-off for a holiday road game, and with all three 2026 navy dates assigned as the standard navy look, it does not return this season unless the team changes a listing.
