New Era's 2026 NFL Sideline collection has a white version, and it is a completely different exercise from the primary caps we [ranked last week](/stories/nfl-sideline-hats-2026-ranked). Same 9SEVENTY Stretch-Snap silhouette, same notched brim, but the formula flips: a white crown, a brim in the team's colour, and the logo rendered in its actual full colours rather than tonally.

That change fixes the single biggest problem with the primary set. When a logo sat on a crown in its own colour, it vanished. Tampa Bay's pirate flag was the worst hat in that collection for exactly that reason. On white it is one of the best things here.

But white creates a brand-new problem, and it decides the bottom of this list. **A white crown punishes any team whose primary mark is white, silver or very light**, because there is suddenly nothing behind it. Kansas City put a white arrowhead on a white hat. Philadelphia and Detroit both went with silver. Those three are the bottom of the ranking and none of them had to be.

We graded all 32, worst to first.

A white arrowhead, outlined in red, on a white crown. That is the whole problem in one sentence. Kansas City's mark is one of the most recognisable in the league and it is the only logo in this set rendered in the same colour as the fabric behind it, so from any distance it disappears into the hat. The red brim is doing every bit of the work. Whatever the tonal caps got wrong last year, this manages to repeat on a white crown, which should have been the easiest canvas in the collection.

## What The White Caps Tell Us

**Contrast beat colour, again.** The four A grades all went to teams whose logo has a hard outline: the Chargers gold edge on the powder blue bolt, the Saints intricate fleur-de-lis, the Steelers black ring, the Packers gold boundary around the G. Every one of them gives the mark somewhere to end. The three worst hats are the three whose marks just fade out.

**Gold on white is the hardest brief in the set,** and it split the field completely. New Orleans and Green Bay solved it with an outline. Washington did not, and the W goes soft at the edges as a result. Same colour, same crown, opposite outcomes.

**The tonal failures got fixed.** Tampa Bay went from the worst hat in the primary collection to one of the best here, and Miami made almost the same jump. Neither team changed its logo. That is worth remembering the next time a cap gets called ugly, because the treatment does more of the work than the mark does.

## The Bottom Line

The Chargers win, with the powder blue bolt and the gold outline on a bright white crown, the best cap New Era made this year. Pittsburgh, New Orleans and Green Bay are the other A grades. Kansas City finish last for putting a white arrowhead on a white hat, with Philadelphia and Detroit just above them for choosing silver. The collection averages a B, which is a full step better than the primary set.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the 2026 NFL white sideline hats?**

A white-crown version of New Era's 2026 NFL Sideline collection, built on the same 9SEVENTY Stretch-Snap silhouette as the primary caps. Each hat pairs a white crown with a brim in the team's colour and a raised logo rendered in the team's full colours rather than tonally.

**Which 2026 NFL white sideline hat is the best?**

The Los Angeles Chargers. The powder blue bolt with its gold outline against a bright white crown is the most striking cap in the collection, and the gold edge is what stops the light blue from washing out. It is one of four caps we graded an A, alongside Pittsburgh, New Orleans and Green Bay.

**Which 2026 NFL white sideline hat is the worst?**

Kansas City. The Chiefs arrowhead is rendered in white with a red outline on a white crown, so the logo effectively disappears into the hat. Philadelphia and Detroit are next worst, both for using silver marks that wash out against white.

**How are the white sideline hats different from the primary ones?**

The primary caps use a crown in the team's colour with the logo rendered tonally in the same colour family, which caused several marks to disappear. The white caps use a white crown with the logo in full colour, which gives almost every logo more contrast. Tampa Bay and Miami improved dramatically as a result.

They are part of New Era's 9SEVENTY Stretch-Snap Sideline line, sold through New Era and Fanatics. Prices vary by retailer and change through the season, so check the current listing rather than relying on a figure here.

**Do NFL teams actually wear the white sideline hats in games?**

Yes. The Sideline collection is the on-field range coaches and players wear during games and on the sideline, and the white version is worn as an alternate to the primary colourway through the season.
