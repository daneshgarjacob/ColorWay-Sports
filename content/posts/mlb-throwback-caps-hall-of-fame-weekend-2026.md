If you flipped on any Major League Baseball game the weekend of July 24 and noticed your team, and every other team, wearing a different, old-school cap, you are not imagining it. Every one of the 30 MLB clubs swapped their normal hats for **throwback caps** across the weekend of Friday, July 24 through Sunday, July 26, 2026. It is one of the best cap weekends of the season, and there is a single reason behind it: the National Baseball Hall of Fame induction.

## The Short Answer: It's Hall of Fame Weekend

MLB holds a league-wide throwback-cap tradition to coincide with **Hall of Fame Weekend**, the late-July stretch when the newest class is inducted into the National Baseball Hall of Fame in Cooperstown, New York. To celebrate the sport's history while its legends are enshrined, all 30 teams reach back into their own past and wear a vintage cap design for that weekend's games. It is a nod to heritage across the entire league at the exact moment baseball is honoring its history.

## What the Teams Wore

The caps are not uniform from team to team, which is what makes the weekend fun. Of the 30 clubs, the large majority, 27 teams, wore a **throwback version of a previous regular-season cap** design. Two teams reached even further off the beaten path and wore old **spring training or batting practice caps**, and one team went with an **old primary logo** on its lid. The jerseys stayed current for the most part, so the throwback caps were the through-line tying the whole league together.

A few stood out. The Los Angeles Angels, for example, paired their road grays with a vintage "Los Angeles" cap, a clean nod to the franchise's California-era identity that we named our Jersey of the Day for July 24. Because each team dips into its own archive, the weekend doubles as a quick tour of 30 different chapters of baseball history, all on the field at once. ## Why It Matters for Uniform Fans

Cap weekends like this are a reminder that the hat is doing more identity work than any other piece of a baseball uniform. A single throwback lid can flip a team's entire look decades into the past without touching the jersey, and seeing all 30 clubs do it at once is the closest the sport gets to a league-wide heritage night. If you have ever wanted to see what your team looked like a generation ago without digging through old photos, Hall of Fame Weekend puts it right on the field.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why are all the MLB teams wearing throwback caps?**
All 30 MLB teams wear throwback caps to celebrate Hall of Fame Weekend, when the newest class is inducted into the National Baseball Hall of Fame in Cooperstown, New York. In 2026 the weekend ran Friday, July 24 through Sunday, July 26, and every club wore a vintage cap design from its own history.

**What is MLB Hall of Fame Weekend?**
It is the late-July weekend when the National Baseball Hall of Fame holds its annual induction ceremony in Cooperstown. MLB marks the occasion league-wide, and one of the visible traditions is having all 30 teams wear throwback caps during that weekend's games.

**When is Hall of Fame Weekend in 2026?**
The 2026 Hall of Fame induction weekend ran from Friday, July 24 through Sunday, July 26. The throwback caps appeared across all three days.

**Do all 30 MLB teams wear throwback caps?**
Yes. For Hall of Fame Weekend 2026, all 30 clubs wore throwbacks. Twenty-seven wore a throwback of a previous regular-season cap, two wore old spring training or batting practice caps, and one wore an old primary logo.

**Why is my team wearing a different hat this weekend?**
Because it is Hall of Fame Weekend. Your team is wearing a throwback cap from its own past as part of a league-wide tribute to baseball history during the Cooperstown induction. It is a one-weekend look and the team returns to its normal caps afterward.
