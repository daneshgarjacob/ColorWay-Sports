Hawaii has already worn all white and all black at home this season, a week apart, which makes the Rainbow Warriors one of the few programs in the country with no fixed home jersey. This is the full Hawaii 2026 uniform schedule: the black set, the white set, the two helmets, the Senior Night White Out on the calendar, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games, including early morning kickoffs in Laramie and DeKalb. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

Hawaii went back to Nike in the summer of 2025, its first Nike deal since 2007, and the current closet comes from that return: a black set and a white set, both built around tapa triangle patterns and a feathered helmet stripe. The designs look back to the Colt Brennan era of the program, and the tapa detailing runs through the shoulders, the pant stripes and the helmet. What makes Hawaii hard to predict is that neither jersey is the home jersey. The Rainbow Warriors pick the color by the promotion, and in 2026 they have used white for a day game and black for a night game at the Ching Complex.

The white set debuted at Mountain West Media Days in Las Vegas in July 2025.

## Why Hawaii Plays at 9 a.m.

Hawaii does not observe daylight saving time, so it sits two or three hours behind the Pacific time zone and five or six hours behind the East, so road games in the Mountain and Central time zones land early on the island. The Wyoming game kicks off at 9 a.m. Hawaii time and the Northern Illinois game at 9:30 a.m. Home games run the other direction: a 6 p.m. kickoff at the Ching Complex is 11 p.m. Central in September, which is why Hawaii home games are so often the last game on the national scoreboard.

## The Bottom Line

Three games in, Hawaii has worn all white at home, all black at home and white over black on the road, which tells you the Rainbow Warriors choose the uniform for the moment rather than the calendar. White is locked in on the road. At home, black at night is the pattern so far, and the Senior Night White Out on November 28 should bring the all white set back. Homecoming against New Mexico on October 17 is the date we would circle for anything new. The grid above has every game with the expected set, and we add a star as each uniform is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Hawaii wearing this week?**

Hawaii is off this week on its September 19 bye. The next game is at Wyoming on Saturday, September 26 at 9 a.m. Hawaii time on The CW, and the white jersey is expected. The Rainbow Warriors wore the black helmet, black jersey and black pants in their last game against New Mexico State on September 12.

**What are Hawaii's football uniforms for 2026?**

A black set and a white set from Nike. The black jersey has HAWAI&#8216;I across the chest and green tapa on the shoulders, and the white jersey has a green wordmark, dark green numerals and green shoulder yokes banded in tapa. Pants come in black and white. There are two helmets, a white shell and a glossy black shell, both with a green feathered stripe.

**What did Hawaii wear against UNLV?**

The white helmet, white jersey and white pants for the White Out at the Ching Complex on September 5. UNLV wore red helmets and red jerseys and won 21-6.

**Does Hawaii have a green jersey?**

Green is the lead color on both uniforms, but Hawaii has not worn a green jersey in 2026. The two jerseys in use are black and white. We will star it on this page if a green jersey shows up.

**When did Hawaii go back to Nike?**

In the summer of 2025. It was Hawaii's first Nike deal since 2007, and the black and white sets were revealed that July.

**Where does Hawaii play home games?**

The Clarence T.C. Ching Athletics Complex on the University of Hawai&#8216;i at M&#257;noa campus in Honolulu. Hawaii has six home games there in 2026.

**How many games does Hawaii play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: six at the Ching Complex and six on the road, with byes on September 19 and October 31. Eight of the twelve are Mountain West games. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
