**NFL teams wear throwback uniforms on Thanksgiving because the holiday is the league's oldest tradition, and the two teams that host it every year have chosen to dress the part.** The Lions have played on Thanksgiving since 1934 and the Cowboys since 1966. Detroit has worn some version of its early blue-and-silver look on the holiday at least ten times since 2001. Dallas wore its 1960s white-helmet throwback every Thanksgiving from 2004 through 2012, lost it to a league helmet rule for nine seasons, and has worn it every Thanksgiving since 2022.

This year the Cowboys' 1960s Classic is on the schedule again, for the Eagles game on **Thursday, November 26**. Detroit has not announced a throwback for the Bears. Below is the whole history, year by year, with a source for every date.

## Quick Answers

- **Why do NFL teams wear throwbacks on Thanksgiving?** The day is built on old traditions: the Lions' Thanksgiving game began in 1934, the Cowboys' in 1966. Old uniforms fit a holiday about tradition, and the Thanksgiving games draw some of the biggest TV audiences of the season, so teams use them to show off.
- **When did it start?** The Lions and the Packers both wore throwbacks on Thanksgiving 2001, and the Lions have worn them on and off since. Before that, 1994, the league's 75th season, was the year of throwbacks across the NFL.
- **Why do the Cowboys wear a throwback on Thanksgiving?** Dallas honored its original 1960s uniform every Thanksgiving from 2004 through 2012, according to the Cowboys, and has worn its 1960s Classic every Thanksgiving since 2022.
- **Why did the Cowboys stop from 2013 to 2021?** The NFL did not allow alternate helmets, and the throwback needs a white helmet. In 2013 Dallas wore its regular blue jerseys at home instead.
- **Why do the Lions wear throwbacks on Thanksgiving?** Their throwback copies the 1934 team, the one that played the first Lions Thanksgiving game. The full story is in [why do the Lions wear 1934 throwbacks on Thanksgiving](/stories/why-do-the-lions-wear-1934-throwbacks-on-thanksgiving).
- **Who wears a throwback on Thanksgiving 2026?** The Cowboys, scheduled. The Lions have not announced one.

## Two Teams, Two Old Traditions

Every Thanksgiving throwback story starts with the hosts. Detroit played its first Thanksgiving game on November 29, 1934, losing to the Bears 19-16, and has hosted one every year since except 1939 through 1944, according to the Pro Football Hall of Fame. Dallas began hosting in 1966 and has done it nearly every year since. Because the games come back every November with the same home teams, a uniform worn on Thanksgiving gets compared with every Thanksgiving before it. That makes the day a natural stage for old uniforms.

## 1994: The NFL's 75th Season

The league turned 75 in 1994, and teams marked it with throwback uniforms. The Lions say they wore throwback jerseys that season "while the NFL was celebrating their 75th anniversary," a design meant to copy the jersey of 1935, the year Detroit won its first title.

Dallas used Thanksgiving that year to debut something new. The Cowboys' white jersey with blue stars on the shoulders, the "Double-Star," made its first appearance on Thanksgiving 1994 against the Packers, according to the Dallas Morning News. It was the day backup Jason Garrett, filling in for the injured Troy Aikman and Rodney Peete, led Dallas to a 42-31 win, as theScore recounts.

## 2001 To 2004: Throwbacks Become A Thanksgiving Habit

The modern run of Thanksgiving throwbacks begins in 2001. Sports Illustrated's year-by-year list of Lions Thanksgivings has Detroit in throwbacks every year from 2001 through 2004, and the visitors joined in.

- **2001, Packers at Lions.** Green Bay flashed back to 1939: plain gold helmets and white jerseys with no stripes, according to Packers.com. The Packers won 29-27.
- **2002, Patriots at Lions.** Both teams in throwbacks: Detroit in a clean, classic blue look and New England in its red throwbacks. Sports Illustrated calls it one of the best uniform matchups in Patriots history. New England won 20-12.
- **2003, Packers at Lions.** Green Bay wore a throwback to Vince Lombardi's 1967 team: three broad green sleeve stripes split by two gold ones, lace-up pants and green socks with white and gold stripes. Detroit wore "uniforms in the style of their 1934 team," Packers.com reported at the time. The Lions won 22-14.
- **2004, Bears at Cowboys.** Chicago wore orange throwbacks celebrating the orange jerseys it wore in 1933 and 1934, according to the Bears. Dallas wore its 1960s throwback for the first time on Thanksgiving.

Photos of the 2003 Packers, the 2004 Bears and other holiday looks are in our [best NFL Thanksgiving uniforms bracket](/stories/best-nfl-thanksgiving-uniforms-bracket-2026).

## 2004 To 2012: The Cowboys' 1960s Throwback

"From 2004 until the 2012 season, the Cowboys honored the original uniform each Thanksgiving Day Game," the team wrote in 2022. The original was the uniform of Dallas's first seasons, 1960 through 1964: a white helmet with a single navy star, which the team later traded for silver. On Thanksgiving it came with a navy jersey with white shoulders.

That run covers the last Thanksgivings at Texas Stadium and the first at the Cowboys' new stadium in Arlington. In 2009, the first Thanksgiving there, the Raiders wore white and the Cowboys wore "their blue throwback jersey and white helmets," per DallasCowboys.com.

Detroit kept wearing throwbacks in this stretch too. The franchise adopted a throwback for its 75th anniversary in 2008 and made it the permanent alternate in 2009, according to Pro Football Network. Sports Illustrated's list has the Lions in throwbacks on Thanksgiving 2008 against Tennessee and 2010 against New England, and NFL.com noted in 2017 that the 2010 Patriots game was the last time Detroit had worn them.

## 2013 To 2021: The Helmet Rule

In 2013 the throwback disappeared. "Teams are not allowed to wear an alternate helmet," DallasCowboys.com explained that Thanksgiving, a rule tied to the league's focus on concussions. Without a second helmet, the white-helmet throwback "was not an option," so Dallas wore its regular blue jerseys at home against Oakland, for the first time since its Cotton Bowl days, the team wrote.

Thanksgiving found other looks in those years:

- **2015, Panthers at Cowboys.** One of the first Color Rush games. Dallas went all white and Carolina all blue, per Sports Illustrated. Our bracket has both.
- **2017, Vikings at Lions.** Detroit brought back a throwback with its 2017 rebrand, a copy of the 1934 uniform, and wore it on Thanksgiving, per DetroitLions.com.
- **2019 and 2022, Lions in wolf gray.** Detroit wore its gray Color Rush set on Thanksgiving in 2019 and 2022, per CBS Sports.

## 2022 To Now: The White Helmet Comes Back

In 2021 the NFL said teams could wear a second helmet starting in 2022, to pair with throwback, Color Rush or alternate uniforms. Dallas announced the next summer that its throwback helmet, the white helmet with a single navy star, would return for the Thanksgiving game against the Giants on November 24, 2022.

It has been the Cowboys' Thanksgiving uniform every year since: 2022 against the Giants, 2023 against Washington, 2024 against the Giants and 2025 against Kansas City. Counting the 2004-2012 run, that is 13 Thanksgivings in the 1960s look, with a 14th scheduled for this week.

## Our Take

The Cowboys figured out Thanksgiving: one uniform, every year, and now nobody pictures the holiday without the white helmet. Detroit has the better story and keeps wearing something else. The Lions' throwback is a copy of the team that played the first Thanksgiving game, the Bears are on the schedule again, and the team still has not committed. Of all the Thanksgivings in the table above, the best uniform matchups were the ones where both teams went back in time, Patriots red against Lions blue in 2002 and Packers 1967 against Detroit in 2003. The league should push visitors to do that again.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why do NFL teams wear throwbacks on Thanksgiving?**

Thanksgiving football is the oldest tradition in the NFL, and the two hosts, Detroit (since 1934) and Dallas (since 1966), use old uniforms to match it. The games also draw some of the biggest TV audiences of the season, which makes them a showcase for special looks.

**Why do the Cowboys wear throwbacks on Thanksgiving?**

To honor their original 1960s uniform. Dallas wore a version of it every Thanksgiving from 2004 through 2012 and has worn the 1960s Classic every Thanksgiving since 2022.

**Why didn't the Cowboys wear throwbacks on Thanksgiving from 2013 to 2021?**

The NFL did not allow teams to wear an alternate helmet, and the throwback needs a white helmet instead of the usual silver. The league allowed second helmets again starting in 2022.

**What did the Cowboys wear on Thanksgiving 2013?**

Their regular blue jerseys, at home against the Raiders, for the first time since the team played at the Cotton Bowl.

**When did the Cowboys start wearing throwbacks on Thanksgiving?**

2004, against the Bears, who wore orange throwbacks of their own.

**Why do the Lions wear throwbacks on Thanksgiving?**

The Lions' throwback copies their 1934 uniform, and 1934 is the year their Thanksgiving game began. Our [full explainer](/stories/why-do-the-lions-wear-1934-throwbacks-on-thanksgiving) has the history.

**What years did the Lions wear throwbacks on Thanksgiving?**

2001 through 2004, 2008, 2010, 2017, 2018, 2020, 2023 and 2025, based on Sports Illustrated's year-by-year list and the 2025 game-day reports.

**Are the Cowboys wearing throwbacks on Thanksgiving 2026?**

Yes. The 1960s Classic is on the schedule Dallas published September 2, for the Eagles game on November 26.

**Are the Lions wearing throwbacks on Thanksgiving 2026?**

Not announced. We expect the Honolulu blue home uniform against the Bears.

**What was the first Thanksgiving throwback in the NFL?**

We can't name a single first. The earliest holiday throwbacks we could confirm are from 2001, when the Packers wore 1939 uniforms at Detroit. The league-wide throwback year came earlier, in 1994, the NFL's 75th season.

**What is the Cowboys Double-Star jersey?**

A white jersey with blue stars on the shoulders that debuted on Thanksgiving 1994 against the Packers, the day Jason Garrett led Dallas to a 42-31 win. Dallas brought back an all-white Double-Star look for the Color Rush game on Thanksgiving 2015.

**Have the Bears worn throwbacks on Thanksgiving?**

Yes. Chicago wore orange throwbacks at Dallas on Thanksgiving 2004, honoring its 1933 and 1934 teams.

**Do players wear a patch on Thanksgiving?**

Yes. Every player in the three Thanksgiving Day games wears the [John Madden Thanksgiving patch](/stories/john-madden-thanksgiving-patch-2026), worn every Thanksgiving since 2023.
