**Georgia is the home team in Athens, so the Bulldogs wear red and Georgia Tech wears white.** Georgia Tech visits Sanford Stadium on **Saturday, November 28**, at **3:30 or 7:30 p.m. Eastern on ABC, or 7 p.m. on ESPN**. The SEC sets the time after this weekend's games. Georgia's home set is the **red helmet with the oval G, red jersey and silver britches**. The last three meetings tell you something about this rivalry: **Georgia wore red in all three, and Georgia Tech wore white in all three**, including 2023 and 2025, when Tech was the home team. Neither school has posted its uniform for this game yet, and we update this page the day either one does.

## What Georgia And Georgia Tech Wore In The Last Three Games

Checked against ESPN's game photos, plus Georgia Tech's announcements in 2023 and 2024.

- **2025, Mercedes-Benz Stadium, Atlanta: Georgia 16, Georgia Tech 9.** Georgia Tech was the designated home team and wore a **white jersey with gold pants**. Georgia wore the **red helmet, red jersey and silver britches**. Tech's helmet is not clear in the frames we checked, so we are not calling it.
- **2024, Sanford Stadium: Georgia 44, Georgia Tech 42, in eight overtimes.** Georgia wore **red over silver** at home. Georgia Tech wore a **gold helmet, white jersey and gold pants**, the combination it announced that week ([Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/college/georgiatech/football/georgia-tech-football-yellow-jackets-reveal-uniform-combination-for-matchup-with-georgia-bulldogs-01jdqjdgcn2w)).
- **2023, Bobby Dodd Stadium: Georgia 31, Georgia Tech 23.** Georgia Tech was at home and still wore a **white helmet, white jersey and gold pants**, as it announced ([Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/college/georgiatech/football/georgia-tech-reveals-uniform-combination-for-game-vs-no-1-georgia)). Georgia wore **red**.

When the home team picks white, the visitor wears color, and that is how Georgia ended up in red in Atlanta. In Athens there is no reason for either team to switch.

## Why The Home Team Wears Color

In the SEC and the ACC the home team wears its color jersey and the visitor wears white. The home team can choose white instead, and then the visitor wears color. Georgia Tech has used that choice twice in this rivalry, in 2023 and 2025, which is why Georgia wore red three years running. With the game in Athens in 2026, the convention and the recent history point the same way: Georgia in red, Tech in white.
