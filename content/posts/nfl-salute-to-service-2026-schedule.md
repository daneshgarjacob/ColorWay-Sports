## What Salute to Service Is

Salute to Service is the NFL's year-round military appreciation program, created in **2011** and presented by USAA. Its public face is November, when all 32 clubs dedicate a home game to service members, veterans and their families, and the league dresses fields, sidelines and helmets in military-inspired details.

The money side is real. The NFL says the program has raised **more than $75 million** since 2011 for its five charitable partners:

- **Bob Woodruff Foundation**
- **Pat Tillman Foundation**
- **Tragedy Assistance Program for Survivors (TAPS)**
- **USO**
- **Wounded Warrior Project**

The program also carries an award. The **Salute to Service Award presented by USAA** honors a member of the NFL community for supporting the military. USAA donates $25,000 to military aid societies in the winner's name, and the NFL Foundation matches with $25,000 to the winner's chosen military nonprofit. The 2025 award went to 49ers running back **Christian McCaffrey**, the second straight year a 49er won it after tight end George Kittle.

## What Teams Wear for Salute to Service

The short version: **teams wear their normal game uniforms**, and the military theme shows up on everything around them. That is why the searches read "why are the Chiefs wearing camo" or "why are the Ravens wearing army green." What people are seeing is the sideline, not the jersey.

Here is what the league has described in its recent announcements:

- **Sideline apparel.** Nike hoodies and long sleeves in olive and camo, worn by players and coaches, plus New Era caps and beanies. The 2025 New Era caps used an army green crown, a digital camo underside and a team logo shield on the undervisor.
- **Helmet decals.** The 2024 release listed Salute to Service helmet decals among the on-field elements.
- **Field and stadium markings.** Salute to Service stencils on the field, field wall banners and goal post wraps.
- **Game balls and gear.** The 2024 package also included Salute to Service game balls and custom Wilson, New Era and Nike gear.

A few clubs go further with the uniform itself. The Cowboys' red helmet stripe is the best-known example, a nod to the stripe Dallas wore in the 1976 Bicentennial season. In 2026 two clubs have put confirmed alternates on their Salute to Service dates: **Baltimore's Purple Rising** set on November 5 and **Buffalo's Cold Front** Rivalries set on November 22.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why are NFL teams wearing camo?**

It is the NFL's Salute to Service program, which honors service members, veterans and their families every November. Players and coaches wear olive and camo sideline apparel from Nike and New Era, and fields carry Salute to Service stencils, banners and goal post wraps. The jerseys themselves are usually the team's regular uniforms.

**Why are NFL coaches wearing green?**

The green hoodies, jackets and caps are Salute to Service sideline gear. The olive and army green colors are a military tribute, and the NFL donates its proceeds from the fan versions to partners including the USO, Wounded Warrior Project and the Pat Tillman Foundation.

**When is Salute to Service in 2026?**

The NFL has not announced its official 2026 weeks. Team-announced dates run from October 25 (Titans) to November 30 (Buccaneers), with ten games in Week 9, November 5 through 9. The league used Weeks 9 through 11 in 2025 and Weeks 10 through 12 in 2024.

**What is my team's 2026 Salute to Service game?**

Twenty-one teams have named theirs, listed week by week above. The Bengals, Broncos, Browns, Chargers, Cowboys, Eagles, Falcons, Lions, Panthers, Rams and Saints have not announced a 2026 date yet.

**Do NFL teams wear special jerseys for Salute to Service?**

Usually not. The tribute lives on sideline apparel, helmet decals and field markings. In 2026 the Ravens will wear their Purple Rising uniform and the Bills their Cold Front Rivalries uniform on their Salute to Service dates, but those are team alternates, not camo jerseys.

**Why do the Cowboys have a red stripe on their helmets?**

Dallas adds a red center stripe for its Salute to Service game, a throwback to the 1976 Bicentennial season. The Cowboys have brought it back every year since 2021, and the 2026 game has not been announced.

**When does the 2026 Salute to Service collection come out?**

No date has been announced, and NFL Shop is still selling 2025 product. The 2025 New Era caps arrived October 16, 2025, so a mid-October 2026 release is the best bet.

**Where does the money from Salute to Service go?**

To the program's five partners: the Bob Woodruff Foundation, Pat Tillman Foundation, TAPS, the USO and Wounded Warrior Project. The NFL says it has raised more than $75 million for those partners since 2011.
