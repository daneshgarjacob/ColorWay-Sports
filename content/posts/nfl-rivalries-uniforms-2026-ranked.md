Sixteen NFL teams now own a Rivalries uniform, and every one of them will wear it in 2026. The AFC East and the NFC West got theirs in 2025, the AFC South and the NFC North were revealed together on August 25, and the league's full wearing calendar puts all sixteen on the field between Week 2 and the first weekend of January. So we ranked all sixteen against each other, both classes in one list, graded worst to best.

The pattern that decided this list held across both years: the clubs that dug into their own history, or dressed like the place they play, finished at the top, and the clubs that decorated over a strong identity finished at the bottom. Four sets failed. One belongs in a regular rotation. Counting down from sixteen.

## 16. Miami Dolphins, Dark Water — F

<img src="/images/posts/dolphins-rivalries-2026/dolphins-rivalries-dark-water.jpg" alt="Miami Dolphins Dark Water Rivalries uniform, a near-black jersey with aqua numbers, an orange MIAMI wordmark and a dark helmet with an aqua and orange center stripe, shown on the Week 17 game graphic against the Bills" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px; margin-bottom: 0.5em;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.6em;">via the Miami Dolphins</p>

The one franchise in football whose colours need no help went dark. Miami's entire visual identity is aqua and orange in sunlight, and Dark Water takes both of those and shrinks them to trim on a near-black base: aqua numbers, an orange MIAMI wordmark, a thin aqua-and-orange stripe on a matte dark shell. Aqua numerals on a black body is a look Jacksonville wore for years and abandoned, and it reads that way from any distance.

The detail work is not the problem. The 305 bumper and the sunburst dolphin on the helmet are fine. The problem is the premise, that a South Florida team is improved by looking like it plays at night in a warehouse. It closes the home schedule against Buffalo on January 3, and we have [said our piece on it at length](/stories/dolphins-dark-water-rivalries-bills-january-2027).

## 15. Minnesota Vikings — F

<img src="/images/posts/vikings-uniform-schedule-2026/rivalries-group.jpg" alt="Minnesota Vikings Rivalries uniform, deep purple with ivory and gold rune-carved numbers and Nordic knot shoulder trim" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px; margin-bottom: 0.5em;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.6em;">via the Minnesota Vikings</p>

Four ideas fighting each other on top of the best base uniform in the division. The "Purple Dynasty" shade is deeper and duller than Minnesota's regular purple and drifts toward a flat plum under stadium light, which takes the team's single greatest asset and turns the contrast down. The gold facemask closes down the front of a helmet that works because of its light cage. Then the Nordic knots run the shoulders and the pants, the numbers are rune-carved, the wordmark is new, and the bumper is an inscription almost nobody in the building can read.

The rune numbers are genuinely good and worth saving. Everything around them is noise. [Full breakdown in the Vikings schedule post](/stories/vikings-uniform-schedule-2026).

## 14. Green Bay Packers — F

<img src="/images/posts/packers-uniform-schedule-2026/rivalries-hero-collage.jpg" alt="Green Bay Packers 1919 Rivalries uniform, hunter green with cream trim and a cream matte helmet with the interlocking GB logo" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px; margin-bottom: 0.5em;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.6em;">via the Green Bay Packers</p>

The smartest concept in either class, rendered so quietly you cannot see it from your seat. Green Bay is the only fan-owned franchise in American professional sport, and the design turns that into currency-engraved numerals and hairline borders lifted from real stock certificates. On a detail card it is superb. At broadcast distance it disappears, and what is left is a green jersey slightly darker than the one they already wear.

Then there is the cost. This set benches the gold helmet with the G, the most recognizable helmet in football, for a cream shell carrying a mark the audience has never been taught. That trade has to buy something enormous. It buys a quieter version of the normal uniform. [More in the Packers schedule post](/stories/packers-uniform-schedule-2026).

## 13. Indianapolis Colts, Anvil Strike — F

<img src="/images/posts/colts-uniform-schedule-2026/rivalries-anvil-strike-group.jpg" alt="Indianapolis Colts Anvil Strike Rivalries uniform, anthracite jersey and pants with royal blue numbers" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px; margin-bottom: 0.5em;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.6em;">via the Indianapolis Colts</p>

A gorgeous helmet marooned on someone else's uniform. Take the metallic blue shell off and what remains is anthracite in a color Indianapolis has never worn, tied to an anvil that appears nowhere in the club's history. This is a franchise whose entire visual power is subtraction: royal blue, white, a horseshoe, and the discipline to stop. A dark neutral is the one direction that identity cannot absorb.

The details make it worse rather than better. Putting the horseshoe on a Colts jersey for the first time ever should be a landmark, and it arrives as a silver mark on a grey shirt where it barely separates from the fabric. The finished silhouette, bright lid over grey body, belongs to Detroit. [More in the Colts schedule post](/stories/colts-uniform-schedule-2026).

## 12. San Francisco 49ers, For the Faithful — D

<img src="/images/posts/nfl-rivalries-uniforms-2026-ranked/rivalries-2025-49ers.jpg" alt="San Francisco 49ers Rivalries jersey, all black with red saloon-style numbers outlined in gold, red and black sleeve stripes, a gold Faithful script above the number and Faithful to the Bay inside the collar" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px; margin-bottom: 0.5em;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.6em;">via Nike</p>

The players designed this one, and it shows in the two things they got right and the one thing they got wrong. The saloon numerals, red with a gold outline in a letterform pulled from the club's Gold Rush wordmark, are the best numbers in the 2025 class, and the gold-coated facemask is a genuinely good piece of hardware. The Faithful script above the number is a nice sentiment and a small one.

The wrong thing is everything else, because everything else is black. San Francisco's identity is scarlet and gold, one of the three or four most valuable colour pairings in American sport, and this set reduces it to stripes on a black jersey, black pants and a black helmet. From the upper deck it is a dark uniform with red numbers, which describes Atlanta and Arizona as easily as it describes the 49ers. The Faithful have been arguing about it since the day it dropped, and it returns in Week 14 against the Rams. [More in the 49ers schedule post](/stories/49ers-uniform-schedule-2026).

## 11. Los Angeles Rams, Midnight Mode — C-

<img src="/images/posts/nfl-rivalries-uniforms-2026-ranked/rivalries-2025-rams.jpg" alt="Los Angeles Rams Midnight Mode Rivalries uniform, a matte near-black jersey with white numbers outlined in blue, sol yellow and royal blue horn shoulders, and a black helmet with a yellow horn and RAMS bumper" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px; margin-bottom: 0.5em;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.6em;">via the Los Angeles Rams</p>

Midnight Mode is built on SoFi Stadium at night, a matte near-black base with a hint of royal, the sol yellow horn on a black shell, and the shoulder horns rendered in yellow and blue over a halftone fade. The helmet is the good part. A black shell with a single bright yellow horn is clean, and the RAMS bumper in white finishes it properly.

The rest is a dark alternate for a team whose best asset is royal blue and sol yellow in Southern California daylight, and the Rams already carry more uniforms than any club in the league. The dotted gradient under the shoulder horns is the modern treatment most people wanted gone, the white numbers with a thin blue outline sit flat on the black body, and the whole thing lands closer to a Color Rush leftover than a rivalry uniform. It gets its one date on Christmas Day at Seattle, across from the set ranked second on this list, which will not help it. [More in the Rams schedule post](/stories/rams-uniform-schedule-2026).

## 10. New England Patriots, Nor'easter — C

<img src="/images/posts/nfl-rivalries-uniforms-2026-ranked/rivalries-2025-patriots.jpg" alt="New England Patriots Nor'easter Rivalries uniform on Drake Maye, a weathered Storm Blue jersey with white numbers, textured shoulders and an NE sleeve mark, white pants, and a white helmet with a navy stripe and star" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px; margin-bottom: 0.5em;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.6em;">via the New England Patriots</p>

Storm Blue is a real colour, not just a darker navy, and the weathered finish on the jersey gives it a texture the regular set has never had. The regional idea is honest: a nor'easter is the one weather event every New Englander has a story about, and the six stars stand for six states. The white helmet with the single navy stripe and the star is the best-looking Patriots lid since the Pat Patriot shell.

What holds it at a C is that it solves a problem New England does not have. The club already wears a blue jersey with white numbers and white pants at home, and from the stands this is that uniform with the contrast turned down and a texture you cannot see. The details you can see, the NE on the sleeve and the shoulder pattern, are the sort of thing you need to be told about. It comes out in Week 13 against Buffalo. [More in the Patriots schedule post](/stories/patriots-uniform-schedule-2026).

## 9. Detroit Lions — C

<img src="/images/posts/lions-uniform-schedule-2026/rivalries-hero.jpg" alt="Detroit Lions Rivalries uniform, bone white jersey with DETROIT wordmark and carbon fiber numbers under an electric blue helmet" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px; margin-bottom: 0.5em;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.6em;">via the Detroit Lions</p>

The first set on this list with real ideas that survive contact with a football field. The bone shade is a warmer, richer base than a standard white and gives the whole thing texture, and filling italicized numerals with a carbon-fiber weave is a clever way to say Motor City without writing it out. The electric blue helmet is the best alternate lid Detroit has ever worn.

What holds it at a C is the jersey underneath. Strip out the carbon fill and you have a white shirt with black shoulder wedges and blue trim, a silhouette Carolina already owns, and none of the Detroit-specific thinking lives above the waist where the camera sits. [More in the Lions schedule post](/stories/lions-uniform-schedule-2026).

## 8. New York Jets, Gotham City Football — C+

<img src="/images/posts/nfl-rivalries-uniforms-2026-ranked/rivalries-2025-jets.jpg" alt="New York Jets Gotham City Football Rivalries uniform, a dark green jersey with UCLA-style shoulder stripes, a black gloss helmet with an italic JETS wordmark, shown on a player holding a football under green light" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px; margin-bottom: 0.5em;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.6em;">via the New York Jets</p>

The best Jets shoulder stripes in decades on a jersey that keeps getting darker. The UCLA stripes are the detail that carries this set, a real piece of the franchise's 1960s and 1970s look brought back with confidence, and the black gloss helmet with the italic JETS wordmark is a legitimate idea rather than a colour swap. The gothic numbers fit the theme without tipping into costume.

The theme is the trouble. Gotham City Football leans on a comic book for a franchise that already has a perfectly good nickname, and the green is pushed so dark that under stadium lights it reads swamp rather than Jets green. Put the stripes and the helmet on the standard Gotham Green body and this is a B. As it stands it is a good uniform buried in its own lighting. It closes the home schedule against New England in Week 16. [More in the Jets schedule post](/stories/jets-uniform-schedule-2026).

## 7. Arizona Cardinals — C+

<img src="/images/posts/arizona-cardinals-uniform-schedule-2026/rivalries-week-2.jpg" alt="Arizona Cardinals desert Rivalries uniform, a sand-cream jersey with dust-speckle texture, cardinal red numbers and ARIZONA wordmark, red pants, and a cream helmet with a red facemask, on the team's Week 2 graphic against the Seahawks" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px; margin-bottom: 0.5em;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.6em;">via the Arizona Cardinals</p>

The right palette for the place. A sun-baked cream base with cardinal red numbers, red pants, and a cream helmet with a red cage is specific to Arizona in a way nothing else in the closet is, and the red pants give the set legs where an all-cream version would have gone flat. This is the first Rivalries uniform built on a colour the club had not worn before that actually looks like it belongs to the club.

The dust speckle is where it slips. The texture is meant to read as desert and at distance it reads as a dirty jersey, which is a strange effect to design in on purpose. Take the speckle off and the cream-over-red combination is essentially a warmer version of the road look the Cardinals wore for decades, which is fine, and fine is what a C+ is. It opens the whole 2026 Rivalries calendar in Week 2 against Seattle. [More in the Cardinals schedule post](/stories/arizona-cardinals-uniform-schedule-2026).

## 6. Jacksonville Jaguars, Bold City — C+

<img src="/images/posts/jaguars-uniform-schedule-2026/rivalries-bold-city-group.jpg" alt="Jacksonville Jaguars Bold City Rivalries uniform, alabaster jersey with cursive Jaguars script, teal helmets and spotted black pants" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px; margin-bottom: 0.5em;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.6em;">via the Jacksonville Jaguars</p>

A well-researched uniform that keeps reaching for references instead of leaning on the one asset nobody else in football owns. The gloss teal helmet with the gold cage is one of the best individual pieces of the year and it carries this ranking almost by itself.

Below it the reasoning gets thinner. Alabaster and gold together read closer to New Orleans than Jacksonville. The 904 bumper is the third area-code reference we have graded this year and the trend has gone from local to lazy. The jaguar-spot pants look sharp in a studio and turn to noise from the upper deck. Teal is why this franchise looks like nobody else, and Bold City spends most of its ideas somewhere else. [More in the Jaguars schedule post](/stories/jaguars-uniform-schedule-2026).

## 5. Buffalo Bills, Cold Front — B-

<img src="/images/posts/nfl-rivalries-uniforms-2026-ranked/rivalries-2025-bills.jpg" alt="Buffalo Bills Cold Front Rivalries uniform, an all-white jersey and pants with silver and frost-blue numbers and cracked-ice shoulder texture, and a white metal-flake helmet with a cracked-ice center stripe and silver charging buffalo, shown in falling snow" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px; margin-bottom: 0.5em;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.6em;">via the Buffalo Bills</p>

The best-received set of the 2025 wave, and the reason is simple: Buffalo dressed like the weather. All white with silver and frost-blue accents, a cracked-ice texture across the shoulders, and a white metal-flake helmet with an ice stripe down the middle and a silver charging buffalo on the side. Put it on the field in late November with snow coming down and the concept explains itself from the top row, which is more than most of the sets on this list can say.

It stops at B- because of what happens without the snow. Silver and pale blue numbers on white have almost no contrast, and on a bright afternoon the numerals fade into the jersey. The cracked-ice texture is a photography idea before it is a uniform idea. It is a Color Rush all-white with an excellent story attached, and the story does a lot of the work. It gets Salute to Service against Miami in Week 11. [More in the Bills schedule post](/stories/bills-uniform-schedule-2026).

## 4. Tennessee Titans, Music City — B-

<img src="/images/posts/titans-uniform-schedule-2026/rivalries-music-city-hero.jpg" alt="Tennessee Titans Music City Rivalries uniform, navy jersey with Music City script and neon numbers under a Titans Blue helmet" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px; margin-bottom: 0.5em;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.6em;">via the Tennessee Titans</p>

An A-grade helmet bolted to a B-grade jersey. Dropping red entirely and letting two blues carry the uniform was the correct instinct, and it gives the set a clear identity instead of a costume. The Titans Blue shell with the circled T is the best thing Tennessee has produced since the rebrand and the only helmet in the 2026 class we would happily see promoted beyond one game a year.

The jersey never catches up. The navy body is quiet, the neon numbers carry more weight than they should have to, and the guitar-string pant striping reads better on a detail card than at field distance. It edges Buffalo because its best piece is a helmet you can see from anywhere, and Buffalo's best piece is a texture you cannot. [More in the Titans schedule post](/stories/titans-uniform-schedule-2026).

## 3. Houston Texans, HOUSTON/US — B

<img src="/images/posts/texans-uniform-schedule-2026/rivalries-htown-group.jpg" alt="Houston Texans HOUSTON/US Rivalries uniform, Liberty White with H-Town Blue and Battle Red tile striping and white helmets" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px; margin-bottom: 0.5em;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.6em;">via the Houston Texans</p>

The best palette in the 2026 class and the most complete piece of city-specific design work in either year. Liberty White with H-Town Blue and Battle Red is light, bright and nothing like the navy-heavy base set. The street-tile mosaics running the stripes and the nameplate are real Houston rather than a generic nod, the "Be Someone" numerals come from an actual bridge, and the chrome blue facemask is the single best facemask anyone made this year.

One decision keeps it off the top two. The first white helmet in franchise history, a tribute to the prototype Robert McNair held up in 1999, is a once-ever canvas, and Houston put a secondary gothic H on it instead of the bullhead. That mark on that shell would have been instantly iconic. [More in the Texans schedule post](/stories/texans-uniform-schedule-2026).

## 2. Seattle Seahawks — B+

<img src="/images/posts/nfl-rivalries-uniforms-2026-ranked/rivalries-2025-seahawks.jpg" alt="Seattle Seahawks Rivalries uniform on Kenneth Walker III, a white and wolf grey jersey with a soundwave pattern radiating across the shoulders, navy trim, and an iridescent green helmet with a chrome hawk logo" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px; margin-bottom: 0.5em;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.6em;">via the Seattle Seahawks</p>

The best set of the 2025 class and the best helmet the whole program has produced. The iridescent green shell with the chrome hawk shifts colour as the light moves, which sounds like a gimmick and turns out to be the one finish that makes Seattle's action green look expensive instead of loud. The uniform is built around the 12s: a soundwave pattern radiating out from the ears and across the shoulders for the noise at Lumen Field, and a 12 worked into the number fill and the back of the neck.

The reason it works where so many concept uniforms on this list fail is that the concept is visible. The soundwave reads from the upper deck as movement across the shoulders, and the white-and-wolf-grey base gives the helmet a clean stage. What keeps it under Chicago is the body: strip the shoulders and it is a white jersey, and an iridescent helmet can drift toward oil slick under certain lights. It gets Christmas Day against the Rams, both teams in their Rivalries sets, and it will be the best-dressed team on the field by a distance. [More in the Seahawks schedule post](/stories/seahawks-uniform-schedule-2026).

## 1. Chicago Bears, Return of the Monsters — A-

<img src="/images/posts/bears-uniform-schedule-2026/rivalries-monsters-group.jpg" alt="Chicago Bears Return of the Monsters Rivalries uniform, navy jersey with burnt orange numbers, the GHS patch, orange pants and striped socks" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px; margin-bottom: 0.5em;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.6em;">via the Chicago Bears</p>

The best set in either class and it is not particularly close. Everything the concept promises actually shows up on the field: burnt orange numbers drawn from 1948, three-stripe sleeve trim from the 1930s, orange pants, navy-and-orange striped socks, and an orange helmet carrying a revamped classic BEARS wordmark plus the four stars of the Chicago flag.

The GHS patch is the detail of the entire program. It is a faithful replica of the memorial patch Chicago wore in 1983 for George Halas, put back exactly where it lived, and it is the clearest example of the difference between honoring history and decorating with it. "Monsters of the Midway" stitched inside the collar is the kind of touch nobody sees and everybody appreciates.

What keeps it from an A is the ceiling the brief sets: this is a heritage uniform executed almost perfectly, so Chicago was never going to surprise anyone, and the orange pants will divide a room. Small price. [More in the Bears schedule post](/stories/bears-uniform-schedule-2026).

## The Bottom Line

Four failing grades out of sixteen, and the four clubs that earned them have something in common: Miami, Indianapolis, Green Bay and Minnesota all walked in holding elite, long-established identities and traded them for set dressing that only works in close-up. Every one of those uniforms has a good idea in it. None of the good ideas are visible from row 40.

The two classes came out with the same shape. Each produced one set we would wear every week, a couple of honest B's, a run of C's, and failures at the bottom, and the 2026 group is a little more extreme at both ends. Chicago won the whole thing by going into its own archive and pulling out a patch from 1983 and a number font from 1948. Seattle came second by building a uniform around a real thing that happens in its own stadium. Neither needed a nickname to explain itself, and that is the entire premise of this program.

The helmets tell a different story from the uniforms. Two of the 2026 clubs at the bottom of this list built genuinely excellent shells, and we ranked [all eight 2026 Rivalries helmets separately](/stories/nfl-rivalries-helmets-2026-ranked).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which NFL Rivalries uniform is the best?**

Chicago's "Return of the Monsters" set, which we grade an A-. Navy with burnt orange 1948-inspired numbers, the 1983 GHS patch for George Halas, orange pants, striped socks, and an orange helmet with the four stars of the Chicago flag. Seattle's 2025 set is second at a B+.

**Which NFL Rivalries uniforms are the worst?**

Four sets failed for us: Miami's Dark Water from the 2025 class, and Minnesota's, Green Bay's and Indianapolis's from 2026. In each case a club with a strong existing identity replaced it with a dark base or detail work that does not read at broadcast distance.

**How many NFL teams have Rivalries uniforms?**

Sixteen. The AFC East and NFC West (Bills, Dolphins, Jets, Patriots, 49ers, Cardinals, Rams, Seahawks) got theirs in 2025, and the AFC South and NFC North (Texans, Colts, Jaguars, Titans, Bears, Lions, Packers, Vikings) were revealed on August 25, 2026. The NFC East and AFC West are next, in 2027. Each set stays in its team's rotation for three seasons.

**Which 2025 Rivalries uniform is the best?**

Seattle's, at a B+. Buffalo's Cold Front is next at a B-, then the Cardinals and Jets at C+, the Patriots at C, the Rams at C-, the 49ers at D and Miami's Dark Water at F.

**When does each team wear its Rivalries uniform?**

Once a season, at a home game against a division rival. The full 2026 calendar for all sixteen Rivalries teams is in our [Rivalries program explainer](/stories/what-is-the-nfl-rivalries-uniform-program).

**Do the helmet rankings match these uniform grades?**

No, deliberately. Houston finishes third here and sixth on helmets; Indianapolis has a top-five helmet on a uniform we failed. The [helmet ranking](/stories/nfl-rivalries-helmets-2026-ranked) covers the eight 2026 shells on their own.
