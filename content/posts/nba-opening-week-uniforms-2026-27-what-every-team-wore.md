The 2026-27 NBA season tipped off on Tuesday, October 20, and by the end of Wednesday night 28 of the league's 30 teams had played. We went through every game against the NBA's official game-by-game uniform schedule, then checked what each team actually wore in the teams' own game photos (and, for the Lakers, our own from the stands). Four storylines stand out:

1. **The Knicks raised their banner in gold.** New York wore a one-night version of its home white with metallic gold lettering, a gold 2025-26 Champions patch and a gold "3" tag at the collar. Then the 76ers won, **106-100**.
2. **The Bulls wore white in Toronto when the schedule said Statement.** The NBA's schedule had Chicago in its **Statement Edition**. Every Bulls photo from the game shows the **white Association Edition**. It is the only clear schedule miss of the week, and it came in a **135-92** loss.
3. **The Nets debuted their Classic Edition at home.** Brooklyn opened in the white jersey with the red "Nets" script and red, white and blue piping, a night the team billed as "Classic Edition Night."
4. **The Lakers opened in gold.** We were at Crypto.com Arena for the [Lakers home opener](/stories/lakers-opening-night-2026-from-the-stands): Lakers in the gold Icon, Warriors in royal blue, Lakers by **127-105**.

**How to read this list.** "Scheduled" is the call from the NBA's official game-by-game uniform schedule, released October 19. **Confirmed** means we saw the jersey in the team's or the NBA's own game photos. **As scheduled (not yet confirmed)** means the game has not been played or we have not seen a photo yet. Every team links to its full [2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/nba-uniforms-2026-27), where each row flips to what was actually worn.

## The Biggest Surprises

**1. The Bulls threw out the schedule on day one.** Twenty-eight teams played through Wednesday, and 27 of them wore exactly what the NBA said they would. Chicago was the exception. The league had the Bulls in the Statement Edition at Toronto, and they came out in white. We have not seen an explanation from the team yet. Our hot take: it was the right call. Black against the Raptors' red is a dark, muddy matchup on TV. White against red is the cleanest look in basketball.

**2. Ring night gold was the best uniform of the week, and it was not a new uniform.** The Knicks did not invent a jersey. They took the home white and turned the trim to gold. That restraint is why it worked, and it is why we gave it an A+. Losing to Philadelphia does not change the grade.

**3. The Nets went Classic on night one.** Most teams save their Classic and City Editions for later in the season. Brooklyn put its Classic Edition on the floor in the very first home game, and the red, white and blue piping looked great under the Barclays Center lights.

**4. Home teams wore color more than white.** Of the 14 games through Wednesday, the home team wore color in eight: the Magic, Wizards, Raptors, Grizzlies, Pelicans, Rockets, Lakers and Clippers. The NBA stopped requiring home whites years ago, and opening week showed it.

**5. Two Icon-on-Icon games, zero clashes.** Mavericks royal against Rockets red and Warriors royal against Lakers gold both worked because the colors are so far apart. That is how the color-versus-color era is supposed to look.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What did the Knicks wear on ring night?**

A special version of their home Association white with metallic gold "NEW YORK" lettering, gold names and numbers, a gold 2025-26 Champions patch on the back and a gold "3" tag at the collar for the franchise's third title. The 76ers won the game 106-100.

**Did the Bulls wear the wrong jersey on opening night?**

They wore a different jersey than the official schedule listed. The NBA had Chicago in the Statement Edition at Toronto on October 21. The Bulls' own game photos show the white Association Edition. They lost 135-92.

**What did the Lakers and Warriors wear on opening night?**

Both wore their Icon Editions, as scheduled: the Lakers in gold and the Warriors in royal blue. The Lakers won 127-105 at Crypto.com Arena.

**What is the Nets' Classic Edition jersey?**

It is white with the red "Nets" script and red, white and blue piping, tied to the 50th anniversary of the ABA-NBA merger. Brooklyn debuted it on opening night against Charlotte on October 21.

**Did any NBA team wear a City Edition in opening week?**

No. No team was scheduled for a City Edition from October 20 through October 22, and none of the 28 teams that played through October 21 wore one. The City Edition debuts start later in the season. Each team's schedule post lists its City Edition dates.

**What are the 76ers wearing against the Cavaliers on October 22?**

The NBA schedule has Philadelphia in its royal blue Icon Edition and Cleveland in the Association white. We will confirm it once the game is played.

**What are the Thunder and Nuggets wearing on October 22?**

The schedule has Oklahoma City in the blue Icon Edition and Denver in the Association white. Tip-off is 9:30 p.m. Eastern.

**Where can I find what every NBA team is wearing for every game?**

Every team has a 2026-27 uniform schedule post with its full game-by-game jersey list, linked from our [NBA uniforms 2026-27 guide](/stories/nba-uniforms-2026-27). The rows update to what was actually worn the morning after each game.

**How do you confirm what a team wore?**

We check the official game photos the teams post, from either side of the matchup, for every game. If we have not seen a photo, we label the jersey "as scheduled" instead of guessing.

**Who won the NBA opening night games?**

On October 20, the Pistons beat the Celtics 121-113, the 76ers beat the Knicks 106-100, and the Thunder beat the Spurs 116-104. On October 21, the Hawks, Wizards, Timberwolves, Hornets, Raptors, Grizzlies, Pacers, Rockets, Lakers, Trail Blazers and Clippers all won.

## The Bottom Line

Opening week was almost perfectly on schedule. Twenty-seven of the 28 teams that played through Wednesday wore exactly what the NBA said they would, and the one that did not, Chicago, swapped black for white at Toronto. The look of the week belonged to the Knicks, whose gold ring night white was the best uniform on any floor, even in a loss. The Nets brought out their Classic Edition early, and the Lakers opened in gold with Dieter on the organ.
