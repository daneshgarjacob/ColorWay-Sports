## Matchup 1: Cowboys 1960s Classic Vs. Rams Fearsome White

**1A. Cowboys 1960s Classic throwback (No. 1 seed).** A white helmet with a single navy star and a gray facemask, a navy jersey with white shoulders, and white pants. It copies the uniform Dallas wore from 1960 through 1964. The Cowboys wore a version of it on Thanksgiving every year from 2004 through 2012, per the [Dallas Cowboys](https://www.dallascowboys.com/news/cowboys-unveil-alternate-helmet-for-2022-season), including Tony Romo's five-touchdown game against Tampa Bay in 2006. The NFL's one-helmet rule shelved it after 2012; it came back on Thanksgiving 2022 against the Giants and has been the Thanksgiving uniform every year since. It is on the schedule again for the Eagles game this year: [What Are The Cowboys Wearing On Thanksgiving 2026?](/stories/cowboys-eagles-thanksgiving-uniforms-2026)

**1B. Rams Fearsome White (No. 8 seed).** The newcomer. A white jersey with royal blue numbers and a royal stripe that runs over the shoulder like a horn, white pants, and a royal blue helmet with white horns and a white facemask. The Rams put it on one date in 2026: Wednesday, November 25, against the Packers, the first NFL game ever played on Thanksgiving Eve. It has not been worn in a game yet, which is exactly why it is the 8 seed. Every detail: [What Are The Rams Wearing On Thanksgiving Eve 2026?](/stories/packers-rams-thanksgiving-eve-uniforms-2026)

**Jake's Pick:** Cowboys 1960s Classic over the Rams Fearsome White.

## Matchup 2: Bears Orange Vs. Packers 1967

**2A. Bears orange throwback (No. 4 seed).** Thanksgiving 2004 at Dallas. Chicago wore orange throwbacks honoring its 1933 and 1934 teams, per the [Chicago Bears](https://www.chicagobears.com/news/chalktalk/chalk-talk-what-s-bears-record-in-orange-jerseys), against a Cowboys team in its own 1960s throwbacks: orange against navy on national TV. Dallas won 21-7, and it is still the last time the Bears wore orange jerseys on the road, per the [team](https://www.chicagobears.com/news/chalktalk/chalk-talk-when-was-last-time-bears-wore-orange).

**2B. Packers 1967 throwback (No. 5 seed).** Thanksgiving 2003 at Detroit. Green Bay honored Vince Lombardi's 1967 team, per [Packers.com](https://www.packers.com/news/packers-will-don-throwback-uniforms-against-detroit-2474380): three broad green sleeve stripes split by two broad gold ones, lace-up gold pants with thinner side stripes, green socks with white and gold stripes, black shoes, and the gold helmet with a **gray facemask** instead of green. The 1967 team won the Ice Bowl and Super Bowl II. The Packers' own photos from that game are in their [Throwback Thursday alternate-uniform gallery](https://www.packers.com/photos/throwback-thursday-packers-showcase-alternate-uniforms) (the three 2003 at Lions shots).

**Jake's Pick:** Bears orange over the Packers 1967 throwback. The Packers' 1967 throwback is nice, but I don't see much difference between it and their normal jersey.

## Matchup 3: Lions 1934 Throwback Vs. Panthers All Blue

**3A. Lions 1934 throwback (No. 2 seed).** The uniform from Detroit's first season, the one that started Honolulu blue: a plain Honolulu blue jersey with silver block numbers, no sleeve stripes and no shoulder numbers, silver pants, and a silver helmet with no logo. The [Detroit Lions](https://www.detroitlions.com/news/lions-to-wear-alternate-uniforms-this-season-19026253) wore it against the Vikings on Thanksgiving 2017, and it came back against Green Bay in 2023 and again in 2025, per [WILX](https://www.wilx.com/2025/11/26/lions-return-classic-throwback-look-thanksgiving-game/). Why that blue is called Honolulu blue: [Why Do The Lions Wear Honolulu Blue?](/stories/why-do-the-lions-wear-honolulu-blue) And why the 1934 look belongs on Thanksgiving: [Why Do The Lions Wear 1934 Throwback Uniforms On Thanksgiving?](/stories/why-do-the-lions-wear-1934-throwbacks-on-thanksgiving)

**3B. Panthers all blue (No. 7 seed).** Thanksgiving 2015 at Dallas, one of the first Color Rush games. Carolina wore head-to-toe blue, per [Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/nfl/2015/11/20/thanksgiving-color-rush-jerseys-cowboys-panthers), and beat the Cowboys 33-14 to stay unbeaten. The Panthers went on to reach the Super Bowl that season.

**Jake's Pick:** Lions 1934 throwback over the Panthers all blue.

## Matchup 4: Cowboys Double-Star White Vs. Lions Wolf Gray

**4A. Cowboys Double-Star white (No. 3 seed).** Thanksgiving 2015 against Carolina, the other half of that Color Rush game. Dallas went head-to-toe white: a mostly white jersey with navy shoulders carrying stars, and white pants with navy piping, per the [Dallas Cowboys](https://www.dallascowboys.com/news/cowbuzz-cowboys-unveil-new-colorrush-uniform-for-thanksgiving-day-game-393151), who called it a nod to the Double-Star jersey of the 1990s.

**4B. Lions wolf gray (No. 6 seed).** Detroit's Color Rush: a dark steel gray (anthracite) jersey with white numbers trimmed in Honolulu blue and blue sleeve stripes, per the [Lions](https://www.detroitlions.com/news/lions-to-wear-alternate-uniforms-this-season-19026253). Detroit wore it on Thanksgiving in 2019 against the Bears and in 2022 against the Bills, per [CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/nfl/news/lions-choose-not-to-wear-traditional-uniform-on-thanksgiving-in-favor-of-this-controversial-choice). Gray over blue on the holiday: some fans love it, plenty do not.

**Jake's Pick:** Cowboys Double-Star white over the Lions wolf gray.

## Why These Eight

We looked for looks that were worn on the holiday, that fans still argue about, and that each say something different: two true throwbacks (Lions 1934, Cowboys 1960s), two that brought back a color the team had dropped (Bears orange, Packers 1967 stripes), three Color Rush sets from the years the NFL used Thanksgiving to show them off, and one brand-new uniform that gets its only game of the season the night before Thanksgiving.

Left out, and fair to argue about: the Lions in all blue in 2024, the Steelers' gold 1933 throwback (expected on Black Friday this year, not yet worn on the holiday), and the Cowboys' standard white-at-home set.

## FAQs

**What is the best NFL Thanksgiving uniform ever?**

That is what this bracket decides. Our No. 1 seed is the Cowboys' 1960s Classic throwback, the white-helmet look Dallas has worn on 13 Thanksgivings (2004 through 2012 and 2022 through 2025), but the fan vote picks the winner on Thanksgiving morning.

**How do I vote in the Thanksgiving uniform bracket?**

Tap your pick under each matchup on this page, or comment your pick (for example "1A") on our TikTok bracket post at @colorway.sports. Website votes and TikTok comments are added together. Round 1 runs from Friday, November 20 to Saturday, November 21 at 11:59 p.m. ET.

**Why do the Cowboys wear a throwback on Thanksgiving?**

Dallas wore its 1960s throwback on Thanksgiving every year from 2004 through 2012, stopped when the NFL limited teams to one helmet a season, and brought it back in 2022 after the league allowed alternate helmets again.

**What throwback do the Lions wear on Thanksgiving?**

The 1934 throwback: a plain Honolulu blue jersey with silver numbers, silver pants and a silver helmet with no logo. They wore it on Thanksgiving in 2017, 2023 and 2025.

**Have the Bears ever worn orange on Thanksgiving?**
