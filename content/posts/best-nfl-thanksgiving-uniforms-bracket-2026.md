## The Eight Uniforms

### Cowboys 1960s Classic Throwback

A white helmet with a single navy star and a gray facemask, a navy jersey with white shoulders, and white pants. It copies the uniform Dallas wore from 1960 through 1964. The Cowboys wore a version of it on Thanksgiving every year from 2004 through 2012, per the [Dallas Cowboys](https://www.dallascowboys.com/news/cowboys-unveil-alternate-helmet-for-2022-season), including Tony Romo's five-touchdown game against Tampa Bay in 2006. The NFL's one-helmet rule shelved it after 2012; it came back on Thanksgiving 2022 against the Giants and has been the Thanksgiving uniform every year since. It is on the schedule again for the Eagles game this year: [What Are The Cowboys Wearing On Thanksgiving 2026?](/stories/cowboys-eagles-thanksgiving-uniforms-2026)

### Lions 1934 Throwback

The uniform from Detroit's first season, the one that started Honolulu blue: a plain Honolulu blue jersey with silver block numbers, no sleeve stripes and no shoulder numbers, silver pants, and a silver helmet with no logo. The [Detroit Lions](https://www.detroitlions.com/news/lions-to-wear-alternate-uniforms-this-season-19026253) wore it against the Vikings on Thanksgiving 2017, and it came back against Green Bay in 2023 and again in 2025, per [WILX](https://www.wilx.com/2025/11/26/lions-return-classic-throwback-look-thanksgiving-game/). It stayed in the closet on Thanksgiving 2026: Detroit wore Honolulu blue with silver pants and the silver helmet against the Bears and lost 39-35. Why that blue is called Honolulu blue: [Why Do The Lions Wear Honolulu Blue?](/stories/why-do-the-lions-wear-honolulu-blue) And why the 1934 look belongs on Thanksgiving: [Why Do The Lions Wear 1934 Throwback Uniforms On Thanksgiving?](/stories/why-do-the-lions-wear-1934-throwbacks-on-thanksgiving)

### Cowboys Double-Star White

Thanksgiving 2015 against Carolina, the other half of that Color Rush game. Dallas went head-to-toe white: a mostly white jersey with navy shoulders carrying stars, and white pants with navy piping, per the [Dallas Cowboys](https://www.dallascowboys.com/news/cowbuzz-cowboys-unveil-new-colorrush-uniform-for-thanksgiving-day-game-393151), who called it a nod to the Double-Star jersey of the 1990s.

### Bears Orange Throwback

Thanksgiving 2004 at Dallas. Chicago wore orange throwbacks honoring its 1933 and 1934 teams, per the [Chicago Bears](https://www.chicagobears.com/news/chalktalk/chalk-talk-what-s-bears-record-in-orange-jerseys), against a Cowboys team in its own 1960s throwbacks: orange against navy on national TV. Dallas won 21-7, and it is still the last time the Bears wore orange jerseys on the road, per the [team](https://www.chicagobears.com/news/chalktalk/chalk-talk-when-was-last-time-bears-wore-orange).

### Packers 1967 Throwback

Thanksgiving 2003 at Detroit. Green Bay honored Vince Lombardi's 1967 team, per [Packers.com](https://www.packers.com/news/packers-will-don-throwback-uniforms-against-detroit-2474380): three broad green sleeve stripes split by two broad gold ones, lace-up gold pants with thinner side stripes, green socks with white and gold stripes, black shoes, and the gold helmet with a **gray facemask** instead of green. The 1967 team won the Ice Bowl and Super Bowl II. The Packers' own photos from that game are in their [Throwback Thursday alternate-uniform gallery](https://www.packers.com/photos/throwback-thursday-packers-showcase-alternate-uniforms) (the three 2003 at Lions shots).

**Jake's Take:** The Packers' 1967 throwback is nice, but I don't see much difference between it and their normal jersey.

### Lions Wolf Gray

Detroit's Color Rush: a dark steel gray (anthracite) jersey with white numbers trimmed in Honolulu blue and blue sleeve stripes, per the [Lions](https://www.detroitlions.com/news/lions-to-wear-alternate-uniforms-this-season-19026253). Detroit wore it on Thanksgiving in 2019 against the Bears and in 2022 against the Bills, per [CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/nfl/news/lions-choose-not-to-wear-traditional-uniform-on-thanksgiving-in-favor-of-this-controversial-choice). Gray over blue on the holiday: some fans love it, plenty do not.

### Panthers All Blue

Thanksgiving 2015 at Dallas, one of the first Color Rush games. Carolina wore head-to-toe blue, per [Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/nfl/2015/11/20/thanksgiving-color-rush-jerseys-cowboys-panthers), and beat the Cowboys 33-14 to stay unbeaten. The Panthers went on to reach the Super Bowl that season.

### Rams Fearsome White

The newcomer. A white jersey with royal blue numbers and a royal stripe that runs over the shoulder like a horn, white pants, and a royal blue helmet with white horns and a white facemask. The Rams put it on one date in 2026: Wednesday, November 25, against the Packers, the first NFL game ever played on Thanksgiving Eve. It was worn as announced on Thanksgiving Eve, its only date this season, and the Rams won 26-13. Every detail: [What Are The Rams Wearing On Thanksgiving Eve 2026?](/stories/packers-rams-thanksgiving-eve-uniforms-2026)

## Why These Eight

We looked for looks that were worn on the holiday, that fans still argue about, and that each say something different: two true throwbacks (Lions 1934, Cowboys 1960s), two that brought back a color the team had dropped (Bears orange, Packers 1967 stripes), three Color Rush sets from the years the NFL used Thanksgiving to show them off, and one brand-new uniform that gets its only game of the season the night before Thanksgiving.

Left out, and fair to argue about: the Lions in all blue in 2024, the Steelers' gold 1933 throwback (never worn on the holiday; Pittsburgh wears the Color Rush on Black Friday this year), and the Cowboys' standard white-at-home set.

That is what this fan vote decides. The most-worn look in the field is the Cowboys' 1960s Classic throwback, the white-helmet look Dallas has worn on 13 Thanksgivings (2004 through 2012 and 2022 through 2025), but the fan vote picks the winner, revealed Thanksgiving morning.

**Why do the Cowboys wear a throwback on Thanksgiving?**

Dallas wore its 1960s throwback on Thanksgiving every year from 2004 through 2012, stopped when the NFL limited teams to one helmet a season, and brought it back in 2022 after the league allowed alternate helmets again.

**What throwback do the Lions wear on Thanksgiving?**

The 1934 throwback: a plain Honolulu blue jersey with silver numbers, silver pants and a silver helmet with no logo. They wore it on Thanksgiving in 2017, 2018, 2020, 2023 and 2025 ([the full record](/stories/lions-record-in-throwbacks-on-thanksgiving)). They did not wear it in 2026: against the Bears it was Honolulu blue with silver pants and the silver helmet, a 39-35 loss.

**Have the Bears ever worn orange on Thanksgiving?**

Yes. Chicago wore orange throwbacks at Dallas on Thanksgiving 2004, honoring its 1933 and 1934 teams. Dallas won 21-7, and it is the last time the Bears wore orange on the road.
