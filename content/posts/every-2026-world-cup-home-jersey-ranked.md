The 2026 FIFA World Cup home kits are here from all three major brands. Adidas has 23 home jerseys, Nike has 16, and Puma has 12. That gives us 51 home kits to rank from worst to best.

We already ranked [every 2026 World Cup jersey overall](/stories/every-2026-world-cup-jersey-ranked) and each brand individually. This ranking focuses only on home kits, the jerseys each team will wear most often on the World Cup stage this summer. All images are courtesy of Nike, Adidas, and Puma.

---

## 51. Qatar (Adidas): D+

<img src="/images/posts/adidas-world-cup-home-kits/qatar.jpg" alt="Qatar 2026 World Cup home kit by Adidas" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

The maroon base with a subtle pattern down the center looks more like a training top than a World Cup kit. Qatar hosted the last World Cup and this is the best they could come up with. Easily the weakest home kit in the 2026 batch.

## 50. Egypt (Puma): D

<img src="/images/posts/puma-world-cup-kits/egypt-home.jpg" alt="Egypt 2026 World Cup home kit by Puma" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

The pyramid-inspired graphic on red just doesn't come together. Egypt has so much visual history to pull from and neither the concept nor the execution takes advantage of it.

## 49. China (Nike): D+

<img src="/images/posts/wc-china-home.jpg" alt="China 2026 World Cup home kit by Nike" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

Red and gold should be an easy win. Somehow this kit makes it boring. There is nothing here that grabs your attention, and for a country with that much visual culture, this feels like a missed opportunity.

## 48. Poland (Nike): C-

<img src="/images/posts/wc-poland-home.jpg" alt="Poland 2026 World Cup home kit by Nike" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

The white home with the eagle feather graphic is subtle to the point of being invisible. Poland needs more personality in their home kits.

## 47. Czech Republic (Puma): C

<img src="/images/posts/puma-world-cup-kits/czech-republic-home.jpg" alt="Czech Republic 2026 World Cup home kit by Puma" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

The dark cherry red is a nice base color, but the button collar feels out of place on a soccer jersey. Not enough going on here.

## 46. Turkey (Nike): C

<img src="/images/posts/wc-turkey-home.jpg" alt="Turkey 2026 World Cup home kit by Nike" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

The red home with the chest band is a standard Nike template. Turkey's colors should pop more than this.

## 45. UAE (Adidas): C

<img src="/images/posts/adidas-world-cup-home-kits/uae.jpg" alt="UAE 2026 World Cup home kit by Adidas" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

The white base with maroon accents is clean, but it doesn't really look like a soccer jersey. More like a cricket kit or a polo shirt.

## 44. Northern Ireland (Adidas): C

<img src="/images/posts/adidas-world-cup-home-kits/northern-ireland.jpg" alt="Northern Ireland 2026 World Cup home kit by Adidas" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

The two-tone green gradient doesn't work. Northern Ireland has worn green forever, and there are plenty of ways to make a green kit interesting. This isn't one of them.

## 43. Iceland (Puma): C+

<img src="/images/posts/puma-world-cup-kits/iceland-home.jpg" alt="Iceland 2026 World Cup home kit by Puma" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

A solid blue with the snowflake crest. Puma says it's inspired by volcanic landscapes and glaciers, but that doesn't translate to the actual jersey.

## 42. Switzerland (Puma): C+

<img src="/images/posts/puma-world-cup-kits/switzerland-home.jpg" alt="Switzerland 2026 World Cup home kit by Puma" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

A standard red kit with a subtle wave pattern. It's clean, but it's boring. Switzerland as a country deserves better.

## 41. Scotland (Adidas): C+

<img src="/images/posts/adidas-world-cup-home-kits/scotland.jpg" alt="Scotland 2026 World Cup home kit by Adidas" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

The navy blue with the subtle tartan-style pattern is fine. It looks like a Scotland kit. But that's about it. Nothing here makes you look twice.

## 40. Chile (Adidas): C+

<img src="/images/posts/adidas-world-cup-home-kits/chile.jpg" alt="Chile 2026 World Cup home kit by Adidas" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

The subtle chevron pattern is hard to see unless you're up close. We expected more from one of the most recognizable colors in international soccer.

## 39. Peru (Adidas): C+

<img src="/images/posts/adidas-world-cup-home-kits/peru.jpg" alt="Peru 2026 World Cup home kit by Adidas with diagonal sash" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

The iconic diagonal sash is there and it always looks good on Peru. But this version doesn't hit the same way as some of their older kits.

## 38. Canada (Nike): C+

<img src="/images/posts/wc-canada-home.jpg" alt="Canada 2026 World Cup home kit by Nike" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

## 37. South Korea (Nike): C+

<img src="/images/posts/wc-south-korea-home.jpg" alt="South Korea 2026 World Cup home kit by Nike" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

The white tiger print is fine. Everything about this kit is just fine. Nothing wrong, nothing exciting. South Korea has had better.

## 36. Sweden (Adidas): B-

<img src="/images/posts/adidas-world-cup-home-kits/sweden.jpg" alt="Sweden 2026 World Cup home kit by Adidas" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

Yellow and blue, same as always. The subtle pattern gives it texture, but it looks too similar to Ukraine's kit. Solid but not exciting.

## 35. Spain (Adidas): B-

<img src="/images/posts/adidas-world-cup-home-kits/spain.jpg" alt="Spain 2026 World Cup home kit by Adidas" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

## 34. South Africa (Adidas): B-

<img src="/images/posts/adidas-world-cup-home-kits/south-africa.avif" alt="South Africa 2026 World Cup home kit by Adidas" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

The yellow base with green accents is a classic Bafana Bafana look, but the execution is a little plain compared to what the away kit delivers.

## 33. Saudi Arabia (Adidas): B-

<img src="/images/posts/adidas-world-cup-home-kits/saudi-arabia.jpg" alt="Saudi Arabia 2026 World Cup home kit by Adidas" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

## 32. Mexico (Adidas): B-

<img src="/images/posts/adidas-world-cup-home-kits/mexico.jpg" alt="Mexico 2026 World Cup home kit by Adidas Aztec calendar" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

The Aztec calendar design covering the entire front is bold. We like the concept. But the green-on-green pattern is hard to see from a distance, and Mexico has had better home kits in World Cup history.

## 31. Japan (Adidas): B-

<img src="/images/posts/adidas-world-cup-home-kits/japan.jpg" alt="Japan 2026 World Cup home kit by Adidas" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

The radiating lines from the crest are a cool concept and the dark navy is a strong base. But we like brighter colors on soccer kits, and this one is just too dark and subdued for our taste.

## 30. Slovenia (Nike): B-

<img src="/images/posts/wc-slovenia-home.jpg" alt="Slovenia 2026 World Cup home kit by Nike" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

The mountain silhouettes in the grid pattern are a nice touch. Slovenia clearly put thought into this one. It just isn't flashy enough to crack the top half.

## 29. Australia (Nike): B-

<img src="/images/posts/wc-australia-home.jpg" alt="Australia 2026 World Cup home kit by Nike" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

The yellow-green is a callback to their 2006 kit, which is a nice touch. Decent, but not going to turn heads.

## 28. Nigeria (Nike): B

<img src="/images/posts/wc-nigeria-home.jpg" alt="Nigeria 2026 World Cup home kit by Nike" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

The green is vibrant and the design is a bit busy, but when the colors are this good, you can get away with it. Nigeria always brings energy to their kits.

## 27. Austria (Puma): B

<img src="/images/posts/puma-world-cup-kits/austria-home.jpg" alt="Austria 2026 World Cup home kit by Puma" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

Red body, black sleeves and shoulders. It's clean but doesn't take any risks. Gets the job done without being memorable.

## 26. New Zealand (Puma): B

<img src="/images/posts/puma-world-cup-kits/new-zealand-home.jpg" alt="New Zealand 2026 World Cup home kit by Puma" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

## 25. Paraguay (Puma): B

<img src="/images/posts/puma-world-cup-kits/paraguay-home.jpg" alt="Paraguay 2026 World Cup home kit by Puma" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

## 24. Greece (Adidas): B

<img src="/images/posts/adidas-world-cup-home-kits/greece.jpeg" alt="Greece 2026 World Cup home kit by Adidas" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

White base with blue collar and shoulder stripes. Classic and clean. Greece doesn't push the envelope but the colors work.

## 23. Hungary (Adidas): B

<img src="/images/posts/adidas-world-cup-home-kits/hungary.jpg" alt="Hungary 2026 World Cup home kit by Adidas" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

## 22. Algeria (Adidas): B

The white home with green and red trim is actually pretty nice. The subtle vertical panel design adds texture, and the crescent and star crest looks great against the white base. The home kit is way better than Algeria's terrible away kit.

## 21. Ukraine (Adidas): B

<img src="/images/posts/adidas-world-cup-home-kits/ukraine.jpg" alt="Ukraine 2026 World Cup home kit by Adidas floral pattern" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

## 20. Venezuela (Adidas): B

The deep burgundy with the geometric triangle pattern is different from anything else in the Adidas batch. The gold and blue shoulder stripes add just enough contrast.

## 18. Morocco (Puma): B+

## 17. Senegal (Puma): B+

<img src="/images/posts/puma-world-cup-kits/senegal-home.jpg" alt="Senegal 2026 World Cup home kit by Puma" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

## 16. Uruguay (Nike): B+

<img src="/images/posts/wc-uruguay-home.jpg" alt="Uruguay 2026 World Cup home kit by Nike" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

## 15. Wales (Adidas): B+

The retro chevron pattern with black, red, and gold is a callback to some of Germany's most iconic World Cup kits from the late 80s and early 90s. One of the best design concepts in the entire Adidas batch.

## 13. Costa Rica (Adidas): B+

<img src="/images/posts/adidas-world-cup-home-kits/costa-rica.jpg" alt="Costa Rica 2026 World Cup home kit by Adidas" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

That red and purple swirl pattern is wild. Costa Rica came to play. The design is loud, bold, and completely different from anything else in the Adidas 2026 batch.

## 12. England (Nike): B+

<img src="/images/posts/wc-england-home.jpg" alt="England 2026 World Cup home kit by Nike" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

England has a way of keeping things classic without being boring. There's a reason the Three Lions kits always sell well. They just look right.

## 11. Netherlands (Nike): A-

<img src="/images/posts/wc-netherlands-home.jpg" alt="Netherlands 2026 World Cup home kit by Nike" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

You can't go wrong with Dutch orange. It's one of the most recognizable colors in international soccer, and this home kit does it justice. The colors just pop in that way that Nike does best.

<img src="/images/posts/wc-usa-home.jpg" alt="USA 2026 World Cup home kit by Nike" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

Nike got it right. The stars and stripes feel is there without making it look like a costume. It's patriotic and clean at the same time. For a World Cup on home soil, the home jersey delivers.

## 9. Ghana (Puma): A-

<img src="/images/posts/puma-world-cup-kits/ghana-home.jpg" alt="Ghana 2026 World Cup home kit by Puma" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

## 8. Portugal (Puma): A-

## 7. Colombia (Adidas): A

<img src="/images/posts/adidas-world-cup-home-kits/colombia.jpg" alt="Colombia 2026 World Cup home kit by Adidas floral pattern" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

## 6. Belgium (Adidas): A

<img src="/images/posts/adidas-world-cup-home-kits/belgium.jpg" alt="Belgium 2026 World Cup home kit by Adidas" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

## 5. Ivory Coast (Puma): A

## 4. Argentina (Adidas): A

<img src="/images/posts/adidas-world-cup-home-kits/argentina.jpg" alt="Argentina 2026 World Cup home kit by Adidas classic blue and white stripes" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

Classic Argentina. The light blue and white vertical stripes are one of the most iconic looks in all of soccer. This 2026 version nails it. Argentina doesn't need to reinvent the wheel, and they didn't. Timeless.

## 3. Norway (Nike): A

<img src="/images/posts/wc-norway-home.jpg" alt="Norway 2026 World Cup home kit by Nike Viking cross" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

That tonal Viking cross is absolutely incredible. It's one of those kits where you have to look closely to see the detail, and when you do, it hits different. Norway doesn't get enough credit for consistently putting out great home kits.

## 2. Brazil (Nike): A

<img src="/images/posts/wc-brazil-home.jpg" alt="Brazil 2026 World Cup home kit by Nike" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

It's Brazil. The yellow, green, and blue is one of the most iconic color combinations in all of sports, and Nike nailed it. The colors pop off the screen in a way that only Nike pulls off. This is exactly what you want from a Brazil home kit.

## 1. France (Nike): A+

<img src="/images/posts/wc-france-home.jpg" alt="France 2026 World Cup home kit by Nike metallic copper crest" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />

Our number one home kit for the 2026 World Cup. The white base with the metallic copper crest is clean, elegant, and modern all at once. Nike gave France something that looks as good hanging in your closet as it does on the pitch. When you pair this with the Statue of Liberty away kit that we ranked as the [best jersey overall](/stories/every-2026-world-cup-jersey-ranked), France owns the 2026 kit cycle.
