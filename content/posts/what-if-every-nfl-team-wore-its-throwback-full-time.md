**Last Sunday the Falcons, Jets and Seahawks all wore throwbacks, and it was the best-looking NFL Sunday of the year.** So here's the obvious question: why not every Sunday?

What if every NFL team threw out its current uniform and wore its throwback full time? Some teams would instantly look better. Some would lose the look they won championships in. We picked seven teams with real throwbacks in the closet right now and gave each one a verdict.

This is a hypothetical. No team has announced a full-time switch. It's our call, and you're going to disagree with at least one of these.

## Quick Answers

- **Better full time:** Seahawks (royal blue), Patriots (red Pat Patriot), Jaguars (teal Prowler), Falcons (1966 red helmet).
- **No real change:** Vikings (Classic).
- **Worse full time:** Buccaneers (creamsicle), Steelers (1933 gold).
- **Why are there so many throwbacks now?** The NFL let teams use a second helmet shell again starting in 2022, which made real throwback helmets possible.
- **Hottest take on this list:** the creamsicle should stay a once-a-year treat.

## Why Throwbacks Took Over

From 2013 through 2021, NFL teams could use only one helmet shell all season. That killed most throwbacks: you can't do a real 1980s look with a 2020s helmet. The league dropped the one-shell rule for the 2022 season, [the Boston Globe reported](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2021/06/25/sports/nfl-teams-will-be-able-use-alternate-helmets-again-2022-season), and the floodgates opened. The Patriots and Falcons brought back their old helmets in 2022, the Seahawks, Vikings and Buccaneers followed in 2023, the Jaguars in 2024 and the Steelers' gold helmet in 2025.

The league still limits how many alternate and throwback games a team gets each season. Which is exactly why they feel special. Now imagine the limit was gone and the throwback was the uniform.

We ranked the throwbacks themselves in [the best throwback uniforms in sports right now](/stories/best-throwback-uniforms-2026-every-sport). This is a different question: not "is it good," but "is it better than what they wear now."

Seattle wore royal blue from its first season in 1976 through 2001, then switched to navy in 2002. The throwback came back in 2023 with royal jerseys and silver helmets, and it debuted in a 24-20 win over the Browns on October 29, 2023, [NFL.com reported](https://www.nfl.com/news/seahawks-unveil-1990s-inspired-throwback-uniforms). Seattle wore it again on Sunday Night Football last weekend against the Chiefs, with silver pants.

**Verdict: better.** The navy era has the titles, and we know that's the counterargument. But the royal and silver look is brighter, cleaner and more unique. Navy is everywhere in the NFL. Royal blue with silver is Seattle's alone. [Seahawks 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/seahawks-uniform-schedule-2026)

The Patriots wore Pat Patriot, the hiking minuteman, on a white helmet from 1961 through 1992, [the team's own history says](https://www.patriots.com/news/history-of-the-uniform-and-logo). **Verdict: better.** This is the one Patriots fans will fight us on, because all six Super Bowl titles came after the team switched to the "Flying Elvis" logo in 1993. We don't care. Red jersey, white helmet, Pat Patriot crouched on the side: it's one of the best uniforms the NFL has ever had. The navy set is fine. Fine isn't the standard. The Prowler throwbacks recreate the Jaguars' original 1995 expansion uniform: a black helmet with the original jaguar-head logo, a teal jersey with white numbers trimmed in gold and black, and white pants. **Verdict: better, and it's not close.** When the Prowlers came out, plenty of Jaguars fans asked the team to make them permanent. They were right. ## Atlanta Falcons: 1966 Throwback. Better.

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; margin: 1.2em 0 1.6em;"><div style="background: #ececf0; border-radius: 12px; padding: 12px; max-width: 260px; width: 100%; box-sizing: border-box; text-align: center;"><img src="/images/posts/nfl-tracker-jerseys/falcons-black-throwback.jpg" alt="Atlanta Falcons black 1966 throwback jersey with white numbers and red trim" style="max-height: 260px; max-width: 100%; object-fit: contain; box-shadow: none; border-radius: 0;" loading="lazy" /><p style="margin: 6px 0 0; font-size: 0.72em; font-weight: 800; letter-spacing: 1px; text-transform: uppercase; color: #5b6474;">1966 throwback: black, under a red helmet</p></div></div>

The Falcons wore red helmets from 1966 until switching to black in 1990, [the Atlanta Journal-Constitution notes](https://www.ajc.com/sports/2025/10/as-the-falcons-unveil-those-iconic-red-helmets-once-more-an-ode). The 1966 throwback pairs that red helmet with a black jersey and white pants. Atlanta wore it last Sunday against the 49ers and won 29-20, and it's back in Week 13 against the Lions.

**Verdict: better.** Atlanta just [redesigned its uniforms in April](/stories/falcons-new-uniforms-2026) and made red the primary jersey again, which helps. But the red helmet is the piece the new set doesn't have, and it's the best helmet in the franchise's history. Red helmet, black jersey, every week. The Bucs wore orange and white with Bucco Bruce on the helmet from 1976 through 1996. ## The Pattern

The throwbacks that win are the ones that replaced something with **more color and more personality**: royal over navy, red over navy, teal done right, a red helmet over a plain one. The throwbacks that lose are the ones that would replace a look that's already iconic. Pittsburgh's black and gold doesn't need saving. Tampa's creamsicle needs to stay a holiday.

That's our seven. Which team did we get wrong? Patriots fans, we're looking at you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which NFL teams have throwback uniforms in 2026?**

Among them: the Seahawks (royal blue), Patriots (red Pat Patriot), Jaguars (teal Prowler), Falcons (1966), Vikings (Classic), Buccaneers (creamsicle), Steelers (1933) and 49ers (1994). Our [best throwback uniforms](/stories/best-throwback-uniforms-2026-every-sport) ranking has more, with dates.

**Why can NFL teams wear throwback helmets again?**

From 2013 through 2021 the NFL limited teams to one helmet shell per season. The league ended that rule for 2022, so teams can wear a second shell with an alternate or throwback uniform.

**Can an NFL team wear its throwback every week?**

Not as a throwback. The league caps how many alternate and throwback games a team gets each season. A team that wanted to wear it every week would have to make it the official uniform, which is a full redesign.

**When did the Seahawks switch from royal blue to navy?**

In 2002. Seattle wore royal blue from 1976 through 2001. The royal throwback returned in 2023.

**When did the Patriots stop using Pat Patriot?**

After the 1992 season. Pat Patriot was on the helmet from 1961 through 1992, and the "Flying Elvis" logo replaced it in 1993. The red throwback with Pat Patriot came back in 2022.

**What years did the Buccaneers wear the creamsicle uniforms?**

From 1976 through 1996. Tampa Bay switched to red and pewter in 1997. The creamsicle has returned as a throwback in 2009 through 2012 and again since 2023.

**When did the Jaguars wear the Prowler uniforms?**

The original look dates to the Jaguars' first season in 1995. The Prowler throwback returned for the team's 30th season in 2024.

**When did the Falcons wear red helmets?**

From 1966 to 1989. They switched to black helmets in 1990. The red helmet returned with the 1966 throwback in 2022.

**What is the Steelers 1933 throwback?**

A uniform honoring the franchise's first season, when it was called the Pittsburgh Pirates. It has a gold helmet, black-striped jersey, white block numbers and beige pants, and it debuted in 2025.

**Is any NFL team switching to its throwback full time?**

No team has announced it. This story is a hypothetical.
