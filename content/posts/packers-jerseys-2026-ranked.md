Green Bay runs one of the least-changed wardrobes in American sport, and that restraint is exactly why we put the Packers fourth in our [best NFL uniforms of 2026](/stories/best-nfl-uniforms-2026-ranked) at an A-. The club's 2026 lineup names four uniforms: Vintage Green, Classic White, the 1923 throwback and the new Rivalries set. Three of them have games on the 2026 calendar, and those are the three we rank here. The 1923 navy and gold throwback is back in the closet, but the team has not given it a date yet.

Here are the Green Bay Packers jerseys on the 2026 schedule, ranked worst to first and graded, with the helmet and pants each one wears. For the full jersey schedule 2026, week by week, see the [Packers 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/packers-uniform-schedule-2026).

The Packers' Rivalries set, revealed on August 25, is a vintage 1919 package built around the club's fan ownership. The jersey is a darker hunter green with cream and gold trim, the numbers are modeled on the lettering of paper currency, and the striping echoes the borders on Packers stock certificates. An interlocking GB logo from Lombardi-era sideline gear sits on the chest. The helmet is cream with the first matte finish in team history, a matte green facemask and "GO PACK GO" on the rear bumper, and cream pants finish it.

It is the best story in the 2026 Rivalries class attached to a uniform you cannot read from your seat. The currency numerals and certificate borders are superb on a detail card and invisible at broadcast distance, and what is left is a green jersey slightly darker than the green jersey Green Bay already wears. To get there, it benches the gold helmet with the G, one of the most recognizable objects in American sport.

It grades an F, the grade we gave it at the reveal, and it ranked fourteenth of sixteen in our [Rivalries uniforms ranking](/stories/nfl-rivalries-uniforms-2026-ranked). Its one 2026 date is Week 5 against the Chicago Bears, Sunday, October 11 at 3:25 p.m. CT at Lambeau Field, and it stays in the rotation for three seasons.

Classic White is the road jersey, with green numbers and green and gold sleeve stripes, worn with the gold helmet and gold pants. That is exactly how Green Bay opened the season at Minnesota, against the Vikings in their purple throwback, in a 39-22 loss. It projects to six of the eight road games: at the Vikings, Buccaneers, Lions, Patriots and Saints, and on Christmas Day at the Bears, where Chicago debuts its own Rivalries set. The two other road trips, Week 2 at the Jets and Week 12 at the Rams, are white games for the home team, so the Packers wear green.

The gold pants are what make this jersey work. White between a gold shell and gold pants reads as three clean bands, and it is the brightest look in a closet built on dark green. Very few road uniforms in the league are as instantly identifiable from a wide camera shot.

It grades an A-. The only thing keeping it behind the home jersey is that the green does less work here, and green is the color this franchise owns.

Vintage Green is the home jersey, dark green with white numbers and gold and white sleeve stripes, worn with the gold helmet and gold pants. It projects to eight home games at Lambeau Field: the Atlanta Falcons in Week 3, the Dallas Cowboys in Week 6, the Carolina Panthers in Week 8, the Minnesota Vikings in Week 10, then the Buffalo Bills, Miami Dolphins, Houston Texans and Detroit Lions to close the season. It also goes on the road twice. The Packers have confirmed it for Week 2 at the Jets, with the gold helmet and gold pants against New York's White Out, and it is the expected look in Week 12 at the Rams, who wear Fearsome White that night.

This is an all-time home uniform. Green Bay never chased the skin-tight modern cut, so the jersey still looks like a football jersey, and the gold helmet is a brave piece of equipment that a hundred other teams would have talked themselves out of. It also looks better in bad weather than almost anything in the league, which is convenient for a jersey that works in December in Wisconsin.

It grades an A, the top mark in the closet. It is also the best argument against the Rivalries set: the darker green on that jersey is chasing a feeling this one already delivers every week.

## The Bottom Line on the Packers Jersey Rankings

The Packers jerseys rank by how close they stay to the gold helmet. Vintage Green is the identity and grades an A. Classic White, with the same helmet and gold pants, is an A-. The Rivalries set gives up that helmet for details nobody can see from the stands, and it grades an F.

The gap between the top two and the bottom one is the widest in the NFC North. Green Bay's best quality is restraint, and the one jersey that abandons it is the one that misses.
