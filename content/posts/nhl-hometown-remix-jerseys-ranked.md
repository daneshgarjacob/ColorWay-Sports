The NHL and Fanatics revealed all 32 Hometown Remix jerseys on Monday morning, and we ranked every one of them, counted down from the worst to the best. Each entry has the team's own reveal post, so you can see the sweater on a player.

A few of these picks go against the early consensus. We have the Panthers' coral, a favorite on most lists, at No. 28, and the Blackhawks' green, last on most lists, at No. 15. (Want the details on every design, A to Z? See [every Hometown Remix jersey and its debut date](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-2026-27).)

## Quick Answers

- **What is the best Hometown Remix jersey?** The Pittsburgh Penguins' pop-art sweater, with the Washington Capitals' Capitol Dome sweater at No. 2.
- **What is the worst Hometown Remix jersey?** The Vegas Golden Knights' casino sweater. It is not close.
- **What do the worst ones have in common?** Blue that does nothing (Jets, Canucks, Lightning), dark bases with one pop of color (Devils, Oilers, Wild, Senators) and gray (Flyers, Blue Jackets).
- **What do the best ones have in common?** A big logo in the middle of the chest and a strong team color: Pittsburgh, Washington, Calgary and Buffalo.

## 32. Vegas Golden Knights

Vegas went all in on a casino theme: a black sweater, a jade shoulder yoke with a felt-table texture and card suits, red-and-black roulette bands on the sleeves and numbers in a playing-card font. Every one of those ideas is on the sweater at the same time, and none of them work together. This is by far the ugliest jersey in the program and one of the worst sweaters the NHL has put on the ice in years.

**Our full take:** [The Golden Knights' Hometown Remix jersey is the ugliest in the NHL](/stories/golden-knights-hometown-remix-jersey-worst).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best NHL Hometown Remix jersey?**
In our ranking, the Pittsburgh Penguins' pop-art sweater, a tribute to Pittsburgh native Andy Warhol that reminds us of the Penguins' 2008 Winter Classic jersey. The Capitals are second and the Flames third.

**What is the worst NHL Hometown Remix jersey?**
The Vegas Golden Knights' casino sweater, with its jade felt-table yoke, card suits and roulette sleeve stripes. The Lightning and Canucks are 31st and 30th.

**Where do the Panthers rank?**
28th of 32. Most early rankings have Florida's coral sweater near the top. We think the pink-coral base is an eyesore.

**Where do the Blackhawks rank?**
15th. ESPN and The Athletic both put Chicago's green sweater last. We think green works for Chicago, even better than it does for Anaheim.

**Where do the Ducks rank?**
16th. The Mighty Ducks green is the fan favorite, and we like it, but we think the Blackhawks wear green better.

**Which Hometown Remix jerseys use gray?**
The Flyers, Blue Jackets and Hurricanes. Carolina's Stormy logo lifts it to 13th; the Flyers (21st) and Blue Jackets (22nd) sit in our bottom half.

**When did the Hometown Remix jerseys come out?**
All 32 were revealed and went on sale Monday, November 2, 2026. The Panthers, Blackhawks and Sharks wore theirs first that night.

**How many games will teams wear the Hometown Remix jersey?**
It varies by team, from five games to 13. Every team's dates are on [our Hometown Remix tracker](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-2026-27) and on each team's uniform schedule page.
