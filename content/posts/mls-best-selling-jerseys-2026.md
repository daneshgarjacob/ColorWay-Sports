MLS released its **top 10 best-selling player jerseys of 2026** on Tuesday, November 3, based on sales on the league's online store from January 1 through October 30. **Lionel Messi is No. 1 for the fourth season in a row.** But the biggest story is right behind him: **Thomas Müller** in his first full MLS season, and summer signings **Robert Lewandowski and Antoine Griezmann**, all made the top five.

**The quick version:** Messi and Son Heung-Min held the top two spots from 2025. Inter Miami has three players in the top 10, LAFC has two, and six of the ten names are new to the list.

## The Big Moves

**Messi, again.** Four straight years at No. 1, every year since he arrived in 2023. Nobody else is close to that.

**Lewandowski has been the best-seller since he got here.** He's No. 4 for the full year even though he signed with Chicago at the end of June. Since then, his Fire jersey has outsold every jersey in MLS, Messi's included. That's the number to watch in 2027.

**The World Cup winners came to sell jerseys.** Müller (Germany, 2014), Griezmann (France, 2018) and Messi and De Paul (Argentina, 2022) give the top 10 four World Cup winners. Müller is No. 3 in his first full MLS season, and Griezmann made the top five a few months after arriving.

**A 17-year-old made the list.** Cavan Sullivan of the Philadelphia Union is No. 6. In September he became the youngest MLS Player of the Month ever, and in October he became the youngest scorer in U.S. men's national team history.

**Who dropped out:** Hirving Lozano (San Diego FC, No. 3 in 2025), Miguel Almirón, Jordan Morris, Darlington Nagbe, Wilfried Zaha and Marco Reus are all off the list.

## The Bottom Line

MLS's list is a star list more than a club list. The three new names in the top five tell you the league's jersey business is built on signings, and the next one is easy to predict: if Lewandowski keeps outselling Messi month to month, 2027 could be the first year since 2022 that someone else finishes No. 1.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best-selling MLS jersey in 2026?**

Lionel Messi's Inter Miami jersey, for the fourth straight year, on the list MLS released November 3, 2026.

**Who is in the top 5 of MLS jersey sales?**

Lionel Messi (Inter Miami), Son Heung-Min (LAFC), Thomas Müller (Vancouver Whitecaps), Robert Lewandowski (Chicago Fire) and Antoine Griezmann (Orlando City).

**Is Lewandowski outselling Messi?**

Since he joined the Chicago Fire in June, yes. Lewandowski's jersey has been the best-selling in MLS since his arrival, but Messi is still No. 1 for the full year.

**Which MLS team has the most players in the top 10?**

Inter Miami, with three: Messi, Luis Suárez and Rodrigo De Paul. LAFC has two, Son and Denis Bouanga.

**How is the MLS jersey list decided?**

It's based on sales on the official MLS online store from January 1 through October 30, 2026.
