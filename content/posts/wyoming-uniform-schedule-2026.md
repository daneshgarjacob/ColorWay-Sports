Wyoming's colors are among the most distinctive in the country. Very few programs wear brown, and the Cowboys pair it with a bright gold that reads as yellow from the stands. Through five games, the helmet has been the constant. Everything below it has changed: all white at Colorado State, brown over gold at home against Northern Colorado, white over brown at Central Michigan, gold over white for Homecoming and white over gold at North Dakota State.

That last one is the look of the season so far. The athletic department billed the Hawaii game as a Gold Out of War Memorial Stadium, and the team matched the crowd in the gold jersey. Wyoming won 27-10 to open Mountain West play and took back the Paniolo Trophy.

**September 5 at Colorado State.** ★ Confirmed from Colorado State's official game photography, dated to the day of the game: **white helmet, white jersey, white pants**, all white for the Border War in Fort Collins. Colorado State wore green at home. It was a rough opener: the Cowboys turned it over four times and Colorado State won 35-13 and took home the Bronze Boot. Our [Colorado State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/colorado-state-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Rams side.

**September 12 vs. Northern Colorado.** ★ Confirmed from Wyoming's own game photography, dated to the day of the game: **white helmet, brown jersey, gold pants**, the classic Wyoming home look and the first home game of the season at War Memorial Stadium. Northern Colorado wore white. The Cowboys rallied to win 21-13.

**September 19 at Central Michigan.** ★ Confirmed from Wyoming's own game photography, dated to the day of the game: **white helmet, white jersey, brown pants** on a rainy afternoon at Kelly/Shorts Stadium. Central Michigan wore maroon over white. The Chippewas ran for 339 yards and won 24-10.

**September 26 vs. Hawaii.** ★ Confirmed from Wyoming's own game photography, dated to the day of the game: **white helmet, gold jersey, white pants** for Homecoming and the Gold Out at War Memorial Stadium. Hawaii came in all black. Wyoming won 27-10 to open Mountain West play and reclaim the Paniolo Trophy, with Markell Holman running for two touchdowns. More on our [Hawaii 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/hawaii-uniform-schedule-2026).

**October 3 at North Dakota State.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **white helmet, white jersey, gold pants**. NDSU wore its green helmet and green jersey at home in the Fargodome. The Bison intercepted Wyoming on the first play from scrimmage and scored two plays later, and North Dakota State won 28-0 in its first Mountain West home game and the first Battle of the Basins. Wyoming managed 162 yards and fell to 2-3.

## Brown, Gold or White Pants?

The pants are where Wyoming's closet gets interesting. The Cowboys have worn white, gold and brown pants in five games and have not repeated a full combination yet. At home, gold pants went under the brown jersey and white pants went under the gold jersey. On the road, the white jersey went over white pants in Fort Collins, brown pants in Mount Pleasant and gold pants in Fargo. Wyoming has not published a season uniform grid, so the grid above only names the expected jersey. We will not guess the pants until each one is confirmed.

## The Bottom Line

Five games in, Wyoming has kept one helmet and spread everything else around: brown at home, white on the road and gold for the Homecoming Gold Out, which was the best look of the bunch and came with the first Mountain West win of the season. Brown and gold is one of the most recognizable color pairs in college football, and the Cowboys are getting a lot out of it. The questions left are whether the gold jersey comes back for another home date and which pants show up each week. The grid above has every date, and we add a star as each uniform is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Wyoming wearing this week?**

Wyoming wore the white helmet, white jersey and gold pants at North Dakota State on Saturday, October 3, confirmed from the broadcast, in a 28-0 loss that dropped the Cowboys to 2-3. Next is San Jose State on the road on Friday, October 9, where white is expected.

**What did Wyoming wear for the Gold Out?**

The white helmet, the gold jersey and white pants for Homecoming against Hawaii on September 26. The athletic department billed the game as a Gold Out of War Memorial Stadium, and Wyoming won 27-10.

**What are Wyoming's football uniforms for 2026?**

A brown home jersey, a white road jersey and a gold alternate, with white, gold and brown pants. The helmet in every game so far has been white with a brown and gold center stripe and the bucking horse logo on the side.

**What is the bucking horse on Wyoming's helmet?**

It is the Steamboat, the bucking horse and rider that is the symbol of the University of Wyoming and appears on the state's license plates. It sits on both sides of the Cowboys' white helmet.

**What is the Battle of the Basins?**

A new traveling trophy between Wyoming and North Dakota State, starting with their October 3, 2026 game in Fargo, which North Dakota State won 28-0. The trophy has a wooden base with both schools' logos and an oil field drill bit on top, a nod to the petroleum industry in both states.

**When does Wyoming play Air Force in 2026?**

Friday, October 23 at 9 p.m. Eastern at War Memorial Stadium in Laramie, on The CW. Wyoming is expected in its brown home jersey.
