Manchester United have the best away shirt of the 2026/27 season and it is not particularly close. That is the headline, and it is unusual, because United's away kits have been the least reliable part of their wardrobe for years. The home shirt is solid without being exciting. Together they make the third-best wardrobe in Europe.

## What Does Manchester United's 2026/27 Home Kit Look Like?

Clean red with a polo collar and banded cuffs, marking fifty years since the 1977 FA Cup final.

It is a bit boring, and it is also unmistakably a classic Manchester United shirt, which is most of what a United home kit needs to be.

The retro polo collar is the right amount of reaching. It gives the shirt a period feel without turning it into a costume, and the banded cuffs finish it properly. ESPN had it 18th of every Premier League shirt and called it crisp, which is fair. There is nothing here to complain about and nothing that will make you stop and look.

**Grade: B+**

## What Does Manchester United's 2026/27 Away Kit Look Like?

Blue and red, drawing on the 1988 away shirt and the nearby River Irwell, with echoes of the 2016/17 away.

This is the best change kit of the season anywhere in Europe.

The blue is superb, and the pop of red at the collar is the whole thing. It is the sort of decision that sounds trivial written down and completely transforms a shirt in practice: one contrasting colour, in one place, doing all the work. The River Irwell reference is the rare heritage nod that produced a genuinely better-looking shirt rather than just a better press release.

**One caveat before you buy.** Multiple owners have reported fabric pulls and snagged threads on this shirt after light wear. It is worth knowing about, and it is the only reason we would hesitate to recommend it outright.

**Grade: A**

## The Away Shirt Bucked The Season's Trend

Across our full European ranking, away kits had a bad year. Five of the six B- grades were change shirts and so was the only C+. Just two aways reached A- and only one reached an A.

That one was United's. In a season where clubs used the change shirt as a place to experiment and mostly got it wrong, United's worked precisely because it did not experiment. Blue base, red at the collar, done.

## Who Makes Manchester United's Kits?

adidas, who have supplied Manchester United since 2015.

## The Bottom Line

Manchester United's 2026/27 away shirt is the best change kit in Europe, an A, thanks to a superb blue and a single pop of red at the collar. The home shirt is a solid B+ carrying a polo collar for the fiftieth anniversary of the 1977 FA Cup final. Buyers have reported fabric snagging on the away. Wardrobe grade: A-.
