## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the MLB daily uniform tracker?**

It is a running log of what every MLB team actually wore in every game, updated each morning after the previous night's slate. Every jersey and uniform gets recorded, from home whites and road grays to alternates, City Connects, and throwbacks, and every matchup gets a ColorWay grade for how the two looks paired on the field.

**How do you know what uniform each team wore?**

We check every game the morning after using game photos and broadcast footage from the night before, then log both teams' uniforms. When something is ambiguous, like a City Connect versus a similar regular alternate, we verify against multiple dated game photos before recording it.

**How often do MLB teams wear their City Connect uniforms?**

Most City Connect uniforms follow a fixed weekly slot. The Pirates wear their all-black 2026 City Connect for Friday home games, the Reds wear their pinstriped City Connect 2.0 on Saturdays, and most other clubs use similar weekend anchors. The running counts on this page will show the real frequency as the season plays out.

**Do MLB teams always wear white at home and gray on the road?**

No, and that is exactly why this tracker exists. White at home and gray on the road is the default, but on last night's slate alone 12 of the 30 teams wore an alternate instead. Some clubs, like the Phillies, follow a strict published rotation, while others swap in alternates whenever the mood or the starting pitcher calls for it.

**When do MLB teams wear their alternate jerseys?**

It varies by team. Some alternates have fixed days, like Friday reds or Sunday creams, while others are manager's choice or a starting pitcher's preference. That day-of-week data is exactly what this tracker is built to surface, and the pattern section above fills in with evidence as the days accumulate.
