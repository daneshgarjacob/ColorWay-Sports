The Chargers are changing more than the uniform this week. For **Super Chargers week**, Sunday, November 8 against the Texans, the field at SoFi Stadium gets a navy makeover. The midfield logo is **the navy Chargers helmet**, not the white bolt the team used for its Super Chargers games last season.

## What Changed at Midfield

In 2025 the Super Chargers field put a **white bolt** at midfield, outlined in navy and gold. This year the team swapped it for the **navy Super Chargers helmet** itself, with the white bolt on the side. The end zones are still navy with "CHARGERS" in white block letters, the San Diego-era lettering.

That is the right call. The helmet is the best part of the Super Chargers set, and now it sits in the middle of the field in every wide TV shot.

## What the Chargers Wear Against the Texans

The all-navy **Super Chargers** uniform:

- **Helmet:** navy with a navy facemask and a white bolt.
- **Jersey:** navy, with white numbers trimmed in gold and "Super Chargers" stitched at the collar.
- **Pants:** navy, with a white bolt down the side.

It is a modern version of the navy uniforms the franchise wore from 1992 through 2006. The game kicks off at 1:05 p.m. Pacific (4:05 p.m. ET) on CBS. Houston is expected in white.

## Our Take

Teams rarely change the field for an alternate uniform, and when they do it is usually just the end zones. Putting the helmet at midfield ties the whole stadium to the uniform. A navy team on a field with a navy helmet in the middle looks like one idea, not a jersey swap. More teams should do this for their throwback weeks.
