**The Texans wore a white helmet for the first time on Thursday night, and they won in it.** Houston debuted its **HOUSTON/US** Rivalries uniform against the **Indianapolis Colts** on Thursday Night Football and beat them **26-3**. It went on the field exactly as the team revealed it in August: a **white helmet** with a **chrome blue facemask**, a **white jersey** with H-Town Blue numbers and blue-and-red tile stripes, **white pants** and **white socks**. The Colts wore the **white helmet, royal blue jersey and white pants**. Every piece below is confirmed from the game photos the Texans, the Colts and ESPN posted after the final.

## Quick Answers

- **What did the Texans wear Thursday night?** The HOUSTON/US Rivalries uniform: white helmet, chrome blue facemask, white jersey with H-Town Blue numbers, white pants, white socks.
- **Was that the first white helmet in Texans history?** Yes. Houston had never worn a white helmet in a game before Thursday.
- **What did the Colts wear?** The white helmet with the blue horseshoe, the royal blue jersey with white numbers, white pants with blue stripes and royal blue socks.
- **What was the score?** Texans 26, Colts 3. C.J. Stroud threw three touchdown passes, per ESPN.
- **Did it look like the reveal?** Yes, piece for piece. Nothing changed between August and the game.
- **Our grades:** HOUSTON/US uniform B. Matchup B+.

## What The Texans Wore, Piece By Piece

All of this is from the game photos: ESPN's game frames and the Texans' own first-half and post-game photos.

- **Helmet:** white, with a red center stripe edged in light blue tiles, the gothic H-Town "H" on the side with a red star, **H-TOWN** on the front bumper and **TEXANS** on the back bumper.
- **Facemask:** chrome, in H-Town Blue. Under the stadium lights it threw back the light like the reveal photos promised.
- **Jersey:** white, with H-Town Blue numbers in the "Be Someone" bridge stencil, the bullhead on the chest, the 25 Seasons patch, and a Battle Red stripe with blue tile edging on each sleeve. The nameplates were in the same tiled lettering.
- **Pants:** white, with the red-and-blue tile stripe down the side and the gothic H on the hip.
- **Socks:** white, so the look ran white from the helmet to the cleats.
- **Gloves:** H-Town Blue on most of the skill players.

Nothing on the field differed from what Houston announced. The only thing that looked different from the preview was the field, covered below.

## How It Looked In The Game: Our Take

It looked as good on the field as it did in August. The facemask is the best part of the whole thing, and on TV it was even better than in the reveal. The white helmet with the red stripe is clean. Light blue, white and red is a bright, happy look for a team whose normal uniform is dark navy, and it is the most Houston thing the Texans have ever worn.

Our one problem with it has not changed. The first white helmet in team history has the gothic H on it instead of the bullhead, and the bullhead on that white shell would have been the look people remember. **Grade: B.**

Indianapolis wore what its published schedule said: **royal blue** on the road, for the second time this season after Week 8 at Jacksonville. The **white helmet** with the blue horseshoe and gray facemask, the **royal blue jersey** with white numbers and white shoulder stripes, **white pants** with blue stripes and **royal blue socks**. That gave Houston the white side of the field all to itself, which is the whole point of a Rivalries debut.

## The Matchup: Royal Blue Against White

Royal blue against an all-white uniform with chrome blue and red trim was a clean, bright game to watch. Two blues on the field could have been a problem, but the Colts' royal is dark enough and the Texans' H-Town Blue light enough that the two never ran together. **Matchup grade: B+.**

## The New Field

Houston played on the field it painted for the night: the gothic H-Town "H" at midfield and tile-style end zones spelling out H-TOWN and TEXANS. One thing looked different from the painting photos the Texans posted on Wednesday, which made the end zones look mostly white. In the game frames from ESPN and the Texans, the end-zone paint around the goal line showed up as bright H-Town Blue. The full layout is in our story on the [Texans' new Rivalries field](/stories/texans-new-field-rivalries-colts-thursday-night-football-2026).

## The Game

Texans 26, Colts 3. Per ESPN, C.J. Stroud threw for 194 yards and three touchdowns, to Nico Collins from 42 yards, Kayshon Boutte from 24 and rookie Marlin Klein from 13. Will Anderson Jr. sacked Daniel Jones in the end zone for a safety, and Kamari Lassiter intercepted Jones. It was Houston's first home win of the season, and the Texans are now 4-6. The Colts, at 3-8, did not score a touchdown.

## Where HOUSTON/US Ranks Among This Year's Rivalries Uniforms

Third of sixteen in our [ranking of every NFL Rivalries uniform](/stories/nfl-rivalries-uniforms-2026-ranked), and second of the eight new 2026 sets, behind only the Bears' Return of the Monsters. It is the best palette in the 2026 class. On helmets alone it sits sixth in our [Rivalries helmet ranking](/stories/nfl-rivalries-helmets-2026-ranked), and the gothic H is the reason. Inside Houston's own closet it is second, behind Battle Red, in our [Texans jerseys ranking](/stories/texans-jerseys-2026-ranked).

## When Will The Texans Wear It Again?

Not this season. Thursday was the only HOUSTON/US date on the [Texans 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/texans-uniform-schedule-2026). Each Rivalries uniform stays in a team's rotation for three seasons, so it comes back in 2027 and 2028. Houston is home against Baltimore next, on Sunday, November 29, where Deep Steel Blue is the projection. The Colts host the Giants that same day in royal blue, per the [Colts 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/colts-uniform-schedule-2026).
