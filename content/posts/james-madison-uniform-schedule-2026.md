The Nike base set is purple and gold in the most literal sense: gold helmet, purple jersey, gold numerals. What makes the JMU wardrobe fun is the third color. Black is not in the school palette at all, and the Dukes wear it anyway, usually head to toe under that gold shell. They did it against Georgia Southern last September and the announcement came the way it always does, as three emoji and nothing else.

Read it left to right and it is helmet, jersey, pants. Gold, black, black. That is the JMU combination post in its entire form, and it is the single best reason to keep an eye on this account during the week of a home game. Sun Belt home teams wear color and visitors wear white, which locks every road date into the white jersey and turns every home date into a choice between purple and the blackout.

**September 26 at Old Dominion.** White expected. The TowneBank Royal Rivalry Challenge in Norfolk, 6 p.m. on ESPN+, and the first Sun Belt game of the year for both sides.

**October 3 vs. Marshall.** Purple expected. Family Weekend in Harrisonburg and the Sun Belt home opener.

**October 10 at Georgia Southern.** White expected. A trip to Statesboro with kickoff still to be set.

**October 17 vs. Georgia State.** Purple expected, with an asterisk. This is Homecoming Weekend, which is historically the date programs save an alternate for, and JMU has already shown it will wear black in September. If the Dukes go to the closet again, this is the most likely home date.

**October 22 at App State.** White expected. A Thursday night at Kidd Brewer Stadium, 7 p.m., and JMU in white against a black home side is one of the better looking road games on the schedule.

**October 29 vs. Troy.** Purple expected. Thursday night at 7:30 p.m., a rematch of last season's Sun Belt Championship Game, which JMU hosted and won.

## The Bottom Line

Two games into 2026 and James Madison has already used two of its three jerseys, both times under the gold helmet. Purple over white is the default at home, white is locked in for all six road games by Sun Belt rule, and the black set is the wild card that shows up when the program wants a night to feel bigger than the opponent. Homecoming against Georgia State is the date we are watching hardest. The grid above has every game, and we add the stars as each uniform is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is James Madison wearing this week?**

James Madison plays at San Diego State on Saturday, September 19, at 10 p.m. Eastern, and as the visiting team the Dukes are expected in the white jersey. The last confirmed combination was the gold helmet, black jersey and black pants against Wagner on September 12. The grid above lists every game with the expected set, and we update it as combinations are confirmed.

**What are James Madison's football uniforms for 2026?**

Three jerseys and two helmets from Nike. The purple home jersey carries gold numerals outlined in white with JAMES MADISON across the chest, the white road jersey uses purple numerals and a purple wordmark, and the black alternate pairs a black jersey with black pants and gold numerals. The helmets are a gold shell with purple striping, which is the default, and a white shell with the JMU mark.

**What did James Madison wear against Liberty and Wagner?**

Against Liberty on September 5, the Dukes wore the gold helmet, purple jersey and white pants in a 20-13 win. Against Wagner on September 12 they went to the blackout, gold helmet with black jersey and black pants, and won 87-3.

**Does James Madison have a new alternate uniform in 2026?**

Nothing new has been announced. The black jersey and black pants combination is the existing alternate and it has already been worn once this season. Homecoming against Georgia State on October 17 and the home finale against Coastal Carolina on November 28 are the two dates that would make sense for a reveal, and we will add it to this page if it happens.

**Why does ESPN list the San Diego State game on September 20?**

ESPN's schedule feed runs on UTC. A 10 p.m. Eastern kickoff in San Diego on Saturday, September 19 is already Sunday morning in London, so the game shows a September 20 date there. The correct local date is Saturday, September 19.

**How many games does James Madison play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: six at Bridgeforth Stadium against Liberty, Wagner, Marshall, Georgia State, Troy and Coastal Carolina, and six on the road at San Diego State, Old Dominion, Georgia Southern, App State, Southern Miss and UConn, with a bye on November 21. The full schedule with every expected uniform is in the grid above.
