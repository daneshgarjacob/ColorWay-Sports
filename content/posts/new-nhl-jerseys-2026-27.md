**What are the new NHL jerseys for 2026-27?** The big one is **Hometown Remix**: every one of the 32 teams gets a new special edition jersey from Fanatics this season, built around its city and local culture. Beyond that, the new looks are mostly throwbacks and returning alternates. The **Calgary Flames** bring back their 2004 Stanley Cup Final sweater, the **New York Rangers** return the navy shield third, the **St. Louis Blues** carry five sweaters, and Winnipeg, Utah, Colorado, Dallas and Vegas all have outdoor-game uniforms still to be unveiled. No team has changed its home or road set.

The season opens tonight, Tuesday, September 29, with the Hurricanes raising their Stanley Cup banner against Florida. It is the earliest start in years and the first 84-game schedule since 1993-94, so the uniform calendar is longer than usual too. Below is every new or returning sweater we can source, team by team, plus the clubs that changed nothing. For the game-by-game record, every club has its own page in our [NHL uniform schedule hub](/stories/nhl-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

## Hometown Remix: A New Jersey for All 32 Teams

<img src="/images/posts/nhl-hometown-remix-2026-27/hometown-remix-logo.jpg" alt="NHL Hometown Remix program logo, HOMETOWN in white block capitals with REMIX below it in a green graffiti script and an arrow, on black" style="width:100%;max-width:640px;display:block;margin:1.4em auto;border-radius:12px;border:1px solid #ececec;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin-top: 0.5em;">via the NHL and Fanatics</p>

The NHL and Fanatics announced the program on August 19. It is the first 32-team special edition uniform, apparel and headwear program Fanatics has designed since becoming the league's on-ice outfitter in 2024, and the brief is to celebrate the fans, city and local culture of each club. The league said more information would follow shortly after the season begins, which is now.

**No team has officially shown its design yet.** What clubs have started confirming is how often they will wear it, and the numbers are bigger than a typical one-off special:

- **[Buffalo Sabres](/stories/sabres-uniform-schedule-2026-27):** 8 games, starting November 4 against Ottawa.
- **[St. Louis Blues](/stories/blues-uniform-schedule-2026-27):** 10 games, starting November 17 against Anaheim.
- **[Winnipeg Jets](/stories/winnipeg-jets-uniform-schedule-2026-27):** 8 games, starting November 17 against Ottawa.
- **[Toronto Maple Leafs](/stories/maple-leafs-uniform-schedule-2026-27):** nine "Remix Nights," starting November 7 against Montreal.
- **[Calgary Flames](/stories/flames-uniform-schedule-2026-27)**, **[Edmonton Oilers](/stories/oilers-uniform-schedule-2026-27)** and **[Washington Capitals](/stories/capitals-uniform-schedule-2026-27)** have confirmed a Remix jersey is coming, with dates still to be published for Edmonton.

Early November is the pattern so far, which lines up with a league-wide reveal in October. We are tracking and grading every design as it lands on our [Hometown Remix tracker](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-2026-27).

## Every NHL Team With Something New for 2026-27

### Calgary Flames: The 2004 Throwback Returns

The Flames announced their 2026-27 jersey schedule on September 22 during a preseason game against Vancouver, and the headline is the return of the **2004 home sweater**: red, the black Flaming C crest, the flaming horse Blasty on the shoulders, chevron waist striping and a drawstring collar. It is a replica of the red home Calgary wore from 2003-04 through 2006-07, the sweater from the run to Game 7 of the 2004 Stanley Cup Final.

Calgary wears it **four times**, once against each of its four 2004 playoff opponents: October 24 vs Detroit, December 26 vs Vancouver, December 29 vs San Jose and January 9 vs Tampa Bay. It is the final season at the Saddledome, and the club also confirmed a Hometown Remix jersey. Daily Faceoff reads the throwback as taking the black Blasty third's place as the main alternate this season. [Flames uniform schedule](/stories/flames-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### New York Rangers: The Navy Shield Third Is Back

The Rangers confirmed on September 25 that the **navy third** with the Rangers shield crest returns for **four games**, all at Madison Square Garden: October 11 vs Vancouver, November 1 vs Edmonton, December 5 vs Colorado and January 17 vs Dallas. The team's own copy calls it a sweater inspired by the city after dark. Last season's light-blue centennial sweater and the Winter Classic white do not return. [Rangers uniform schedule](/stories/new-york-rangers-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### St. Louis Blues: Five Sweaters in One Season

The Blues published their full sweater schedule today, September 29, and they carry **five**: the blue home (28 games), the white road (41 road games plus one at home, December 27 against the Rangers), a **third** that the club describes as the home sweater from the 2018-19 Cup season (3 games: October 29, November 14, March 28), a **1990s retro** in blue, yellow and red (2 games: January 3 vs Calgary and January 28 vs Colorado) and the **Hometown Remix** (10 games). That '90s retro is the only full throwback we have seen confirmed for 2026-27 outside Calgary. [Blues uniform schedule](/stories/blues-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### Buffalo Sabres: Goathead 10 Times, Remix 8 Times

Buffalo announced on September 23 that the black-and-red **Goathead** alternate goes **10 times**, down from 15 last season: October 10 vs Utah, November 8 vs Vegas, November 27 vs Pittsburgh, December 26 vs New Jersey, January 14 vs Colorado (Hall of Fame Night), January 21 vs Seattle, February 18 vs Carolina, March 15 vs San Jose, March 25 vs Toronto and April 10 vs Detroit. The Hometown Remix fills 8 more dates, including a road game on January 20 at the Islanders. [Sabres uniform schedule](/stories/sabres-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### Winnipeg Jets: Four Sweaters, One Outdoors

The Jets' schedule, reported by The Hockey News today, has four looks: the home (26 games), the **Heritage** blue alternate (7 games, starting October 7 vs Colorado), the **Hometown Remix** (8 games) and the **Heritage Classic** sweater for the outdoor game against Montreal on October 25. Neither the Remix nor the Heritage Classic design had been unveiled when the schedule came out. [Jets uniform schedule](/stories/winnipeg-jets-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### Edmonton Oilers: Oil Country Returns

The Oilers announced on September 28 that the **cream Oil Country alternate**, introduced last season, goes **eight times**, including three home games. The club said its Hometown Remix dates will be announced soon. [Oilers uniform schedule](/stories/oilers-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### Toronto Maple Leafs: Nine Remix Nights

Toronto was first to put Hometown Remix dates on a calendar, naming nine Remix Nights back in August: November 7 vs Montreal, November 14 vs Edmonton, November 19 vs Los Angeles, November 30 vs Philadelphia, December 13 vs Anaheim, December 19 vs Tampa Bay, January 18 vs St. Louis, February 8 vs Columbus and February 16 vs Carolina. The **St. Pats** throwback returns for March 17. The black reversible third from 2022-23 through 2025-26 has not been confirmed for this season. [Maple Leafs uniform schedule](/stories/maple-leafs-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### Vegas Golden Knights: Season X, and Steel Grey Steps Back

It is the Golden Knights' 10th season, marked with an X anniversary logo and five Anniversary Knights. The Las Vegas Review-Journal and The Hockey News reported in August that the original **steel grey** leaves the regular rotation and becomes a throwback, with the Hometown Remix taking the third slot. The club has not put out its own release confirming that. Vegas also plays outdoors at Dallas in February (below). [Golden Knights uniform schedule](/stories/golden-knights-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### Vancouver Canucks: A New Sponsor Patch

The only change to a primary sweater anywhere in the league: an **RBC** patch replaces TD on the Canucks' home jersey under a multi-year deal announced in June. The black **Skate** alternate is expected back; Vancouver usually publishes its dates in October. [Canucks uniform schedule](/stories/canucks-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### Minnesota Wild: A Mystery Fourth Sweater

The Wild's uniform schedule in the team app lists the green home, the white road and the **78s** throwback, plus a fourth page that does not load. The Hockey News counted 10 games that none of the three listed sweaters covers, nine at home and one on the road, which is where a Hometown Remix would sit. [Wild uniform schedule](/stories/minnesota-wild-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### Philadelphia Flyers: Independence Edition Nights

The Flyers' promotional schedule includes **10 Independence Edition** home games from November through mid-March, tied to the country's 250th anniversary. Philadelphia has not said which sweater goes with them, and the black alternate was not worn once last season. It is also the Flyers' 60th season. [Flyers uniform schedule](/stories/flyers-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### Washington Capitals: Screaming Eagle 10 Times, Remix on the Calendar

Washington announced on September 30 that the red **Screaming Eagle** third goes **10 times**, all at home, down from 15 in its debut season, as reported by Russian Machine Never Breaks from the team's release: October 9 vs the Rangers, October 17 vs New Jersey, November 1 vs Minnesota, November 14 vs New Jersey, December 26 vs Columbus, January 16 vs Colorado, January 22 vs the Islanders, February 16 vs Anaheim, March 26 vs Boston and April 3 vs Columbus. The promotional schedule also carries Hometown Remix items: a rally towel on November 7 vs Florida, a beanie on December 1 vs Vancouver, an Alex Ovechkin Hometown Remix bobblehead on January 12 vs Vegas and a cowboy hat on January 14 vs Carolina. [Capitals uniform schedule](/stories/capitals-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### Anniversaries: Kings 60, Flyers 60, Penguins 60, Red Wings 100

- **[Los Angeles Kings](/stories/los-angeles-kings-uniform-schedule-2026-27):** a 60th anniversary logo (a black 60 with the original silver crown) and a "60 Seasons" ring at center ice. No anniversary patch or throwback sweater has been announced. The black crown alternate from last season is expected back.
- **[Pittsburgh Penguins](/stories/penguins-uniform-schedule-2026-27):** a new retro-inspired **60th anniversary logo**, unveiled September 29: a black 60 with the skating penguin inside the 6, five gold stars for the five Stanley Cups and gold, white and black stripes for the Three Rivers. It is on the helmets for every game this season and on the jersey shoulders for the home opener against Montreal on October 3. Pittsburgh's gold third, signed up for at least three seasons, returns.
- **[Philadelphia Flyers](/stories/flyers-uniform-schedule-2026-27):** the other 1967 expansion club reaches season 60 too, opening against the Penguins on September 30. The Flyers have not announced an anniversary logo or patch.
- **[Detroit Red Wings](/stories/red-wings-uniform-schedule-2026-27):** the **Centennial Game** against the **[Boston Bruins](/stories/bruins-uniform-schedule-2026-27)** on November 18, 100 years to the day after the Detroit Cougars' first NHL game, a 2-0 loss to Boston. Neither team has said what it will wear.

### Returning Thirds (Not New, Still Worth Knowing)

These alternates were new last season or earlier and are expected back, with dates still to be published: the **[Ottawa Senators](/stories/senators-uniform-schedule-2026-27)** red, the **[Seattle Kraken](/stories/seattle-kraken-uniform-schedule-2026-27)** glow-in-the-dark Abyss black, the **[Chicago Blackhawks](/stories/blackhawks-uniform-schedule-2026-27)** 1996-2009 black, the **[Carolina Hurricanes](/stories/hurricanes-uniform-schedule-2026-27)** red and Whalers green (Whalers Night is January 28), the **[Dallas Stars](/stories/stars-uniform-schedule-2026-27)** "The 99" black and the **[Columbus Blue Jackets](/stories/blue-jackets-uniform-schedule-2026-27)** cannon third, which the club has not confirmed.

## Outdoor Games and Global Series Jerseys for 2026-27

The NHL has three outdoor games this season. **None of the six outdoor sweaters has been unveiled.**

- **Heritage Classic, Sunday, October 25:** Montreal at Winnipeg, Princess Auto Stadium. The first outdoor game of the season and the next uniform reveal to watch, since it is less than four weeks away.
- **Winter Classic, Thursday, December 31:** Colorado at Utah, Rice-Eccles Stadium in Salt Lake City, the first outdoor NHL game in the state. The logo is out (see our [Winter Classic logo history](/stories/nhl-winter-classic-2027-logo-history-ranked)); the commissioner said specialty sweaters are planned. Colorado Hockey Now, citing Icethetics, reported on September 28 that the Avalanche will wear an off-white sweater with burgundy as the main accent. The team has not confirmed it.
- **Stadium Series, Saturday, February 20, 2027:** Vegas at Dallas, AT&T Stadium in Arlington, on ABC.

The **Global Series** sends Seattle and Carolina to Helsinki (November 12 and 14) and Chicago and Ottawa to Dusseldorf (December 18 and 20). No special sweaters have been announced for either trip.

## The Bottom Line

The 2026-27 NHL jersey story is almost entirely **Hometown Remix**: 32 new sweaters, none of them shown yet, with teams already committing to 8 to 10 dates each. Outside the program, the league is in a throwback mood rather than a rebrand mood. Calgary's 2004 sweater, the Blues' '90s retro and the Rangers' navy shield are the notable returns, three outdoor sweaters per side are still to come, and nobody touched a home or road set. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the new NHL jerseys for 2026-27?**

Every team gets a new Hometown Remix special edition jersey designed by Fanatics. The other new looks are throwbacks and returning alternates, led by the Flames' 2004 home sweater, the Rangers' navy shield third and the Blues' 1990s retro. Six outdoor-game sweaters are also still to be unveiled.

**Are any NHL teams changing their home or road jerseys in 2026-27?**

No. No club has announced a new home or road set. The only change to a primary sweater is the RBC sponsor patch replacing TD on the Vancouver Canucks' home jersey.

**When will the NHL Hometown Remix jerseys be revealed?**

The NHL said more information would come shortly after the season begins on September 29, 2026. Teams that have posted dates start wearing them in early November, so a reveal in October is the reasonable expectation.

**How many times will teams wear the Hometown Remix jersey?**

It varies by team. Buffalo and Winnipeg have 8 dates, St. Louis has 10 and Toronto has nine Remix Nights. The league has not set a league-wide number.

**Which NHL teams are wearing throwback jerseys in 2026-27?**

Calgary (the 2004 home, four games), St. Louis (a 1990s retro, two games), Toronto (the St. Pats on March 17), Buffalo (the Goathead, 10 games), Minnesota (the 78s) and Vegas, which reportedly moves its original steel grey to throwback status.

**What outdoor games are on the 2026-27 NHL schedule?**

The Heritage Classic (Montreal at Winnipeg, October 25), the Winter Classic (Colorado at Utah, December 31 at Rice-Eccles Stadium) and the Stadium Series (Vegas at Dallas, February 20, 2027 at AT&T Stadium). None of the jerseys has been unveiled.

**When does the 2026-27 NHL season start?**

Tuesday, September 29, 2026, with Panthers at Hurricanes, Rangers at Bruins, Canadiens at Maple Leafs, Canucks at Oilers and Blackhawks at Golden Knights. It is an 84-game season.
