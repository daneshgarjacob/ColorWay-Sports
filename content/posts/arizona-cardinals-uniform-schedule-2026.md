## The Desert Rivalries Jersey (Week 2 vs. Seahawks, Confirmed)

Arizona wears its Rivalries uniform against Seattle on Sunday, September 20, paired with the team's Hispanic Heritage game day. This is the second season for the set, which Arizona unveiled as part of the program's first wave alongside the rest of the NFC West and the AFC East.

It remains one of the better things the Rivalries program has produced. Where a lot of entries reach for a blackout, Arizona built its around the desert: sand and copper tones against the cardinal red, drawing on the landscape rather than on the usual alternate-uniform playbook. The result actually looks like it comes from Arizona, which is a low bar that most alternate uniforms in the NFL still fail to clear.

Pairing it with the Seahawks is also correct. Seattle is the division rival Arizona has the most history with in that stadium, and putting the uniform in Week 2 rather than saving it for December means it gets a warm-weather afternoon where the copper actually reads on camera. ## Cardinal Red at Home (The Base Set, Expected)

The red home jersey projects to the eight home dates not claimed by the Rivalries game: the Lions in Week 5, the Broncos in Week 7, the Rams in Week 10, the Commanders in Week 12, the Eagles in Week 13, the Jets in Week 15, the Raiders in Week 17, and the 49ers in Week 18.

Cardinal red is a genuinely good base and an underrated one, mostly because Arizona has spent a decade doing odd things around it. The 2023 redesign cleaned up the numerals and dropped the piping that used to clutter the shoulders, and what is left is a strong, saturated red that nobody else in the league quite matches. ## The White Road Set (Expected)

The white road uniform projects to seven of the eight away games: at the Chargers, 49ers, Giants, Rams, Seahawks, Chiefs, and Saints.

The eighth is Week 8 at Dallas on Sunday, November 1, and it is the one road date where Arizona has a real choice. The Cowboys wear white at home as a matter of tradition, and a visiting team can only wear a colored jersey when the host goes white, so red at AT&T Stadium is available to the Cardinals if they want it. Philadelphia has used the same opening to wear Kelly Green in Dallas, so there is precedent for a visitor treating it as an occasion rather than a formality.

## How the Cardinals Rotation Works in 2026

Arizona's system is red at home, white on the road, and one Rivalries game in September. That is a thin plan for a team with nine home dates, and it is thinner than it needs to be given what is sitting unused.

The Rivalries set is the best uniform Arizona owns and it gets one wearing. The black alternate gets none, which we are fine with. What we would like to see is the desert uniform treated as a recurring look rather than a one-off, because it does something the base set cannot: it makes the Cardinals look like a team from a specific place. Nine home games is a lot of red.
