**The Texans wear white helmets for the first time on Thursday night.** Houston debuts its **HOUSTON/US** Rivalries uniform when it hosts the Indianapolis Colts on **Thursday, November 19, at 8:15 p.m. Eastern on Prime Video**. That is a **white helmet** with a **chrome blue facemask**, a **white jersey** with bright blue numbers and blue-and-red stripes built from Houston's street tiles, and **white pants**. It is the uniform's first game, and the Texans named this date for it the day they revealed it.

## Quick Answers

- **What are the Texans wearing on Thursday Night Football?** The HOUSTON/US Rivalries uniform: white helmet, chrome blue facemask, white jersey with blue numbers, white pants.
- **When?** Thursday, November 19, 8:15 p.m. ET (7:15 p.m. in Houston) on Prime Video, against the Colts at home.
- **What are the Colts wearing?** Royal blue jerseys are expected. Indianapolis has not posted its game-week look yet.
- **Is this the first time the Texans wear it?** Yes. The team revealed it on August 25 and named this game as its debut.
- **Have the Texans ever worn a white helmet?** Never in a game. This is the first white helmet in franchise history.
- **Our grade:** B for the uniform, B+ for the matchup.

## What The Texans Are Wearing Against The Colts

HOUSTON/US is Houston's entry in the NFL and Nike Rivalries program, revealed on August 25 with the line "By the H. For the H." Here is every piece, from the team's own description:

- **The helmet.** The first Liberty White helmet the Texans have ever made, with a glossy finish the team compares to candy paint. The side carries a white version of the blackletter "H" from the 2024 rebrand, and the front and back bumpers spell out "H-Town" and "Texans" in street tiles.
- **The facemask.** Chrome, in H-Town Blue. It is the detail you'll notice first on TV.
- **The jersey.** White, with the bullhead logo over the heart for the first time in team history. The numbers are chrome-finished H-Town Blue, drawn from the stencil lettering on the "Be Someone" bridge in Houston.
- **The stripes.** The sleeve, collar and pants stripes use Houston's blue street tiles next to a two-tone Battle Red stripe.
- **The collar.** Inside, a Battle Red "Houston" wordmark in the bridge's lettering, with the "US" picked out in blue. That's where the name comes from.
- **White pants**, so the look runs white from the helmet down, with the blue and red living in the trim.

The Texans have asked fans to wear H-Town Blue to the game.

## What The Colts Are Wearing

With Houston in white at home, Indianapolis is expected in its **royal blue** jersey on the road, the same call it made at Jacksonville in Week 8 when the Jaguars wore their own Rivalries uniform. The Colts haven't posted their game-week uniform yet, so we'll update this once they do. Royal blue against a white uniform with blue numbers keeps the whole field in blue and white, with Houston's red stripes as the only warm color out there.

## Is HOUSTON/US Any Good?

Mostly, yes. It's the best color palette in the 2026 Rivalries class: light, bright and nothing like Houston's dark navy base set. The street tiles are something you can actually find in Houston, and the bridge numbers come from a real landmark, which is more than most city-themed uniforms can say. The chrome blue facemask is the best facemask anyone made this year.

What keeps it at a **B** is the helmet logo. The first white helmet in team history was the chance to put the bullhead on a color it has never worn, and Houston used the blackletter H instead. The H is fine. The bullhead on that shell would have been the one people remembered. It ranks third of sixteen in our [ranking of every NFL Rivalries uniform](/stories/nfl-rivalries-uniforms-2026-ranked).

The matchup gets a **B+**. Royal blue against a mostly white uniform under the lights is a clean, bright Thursday night, and the white helmet will be the thing everyone talks about.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the Texans wearing against the Colts?**

The HOUSTON/US Rivalries uniform: a white helmet with a chrome blue facemask, a white jersey with H-Town Blue numbers and street-tile stripes, and white pants. Houston named this game for the debut when it revealed the uniform on August 25.

**What time is Colts vs. Texans on Thursday Night Football?**

Thursday, November 19, at 8:15 p.m. Eastern (7:15 p.m. Central) on Prime Video, in Houston.

**What is HOUSTON/US?**

It's the name of the Texans' Rivalries uniform. It comes from the inside collar, which reads "Houston" with the "US" in blue. The team says it stands for the people who call the city home.

**Is this the Texans' first white helmet?**

Yes. The Texans have never worn a white helmet in a game. Their regular helmet is dark navy, and their Battle Red games use a red one.

**What logo is on the Texans' white helmet?**

A white version of the blackletter "H" from Houston's 2024 rebrand. The bullhead logo is on the jersey chest instead, the first time it has sat over the heart.

**Where do the Texans' jersey numbers come from?**

The "Be Someone" bridge, the painted freeway bridge near downtown Houston. The numbers copy its stencil-style block lettering.

**What are the Colts wearing at Houston?**

Royal blue is expected, the same road look Indianapolis wore at Jacksonville in Week 8 when the Jaguars debuted their Rivalries uniform. The Colts haven't announced their game-week look yet.

**How many times will the Texans wear the Rivalries uniform?**

Once in 2026. The uniform stays in Houston's rotation for three seasons.

**What is the NFL Rivalries program?**

A Nike program that gives teams a city-themed alternate uniform worn at home against a division rival. The AFC East and NFC West got theirs in 2025, and the AFC South and NFC North, including Houston, got theirs in 2026. Our [Rivalries explainer](/stories/what-is-the-nfl-rivalries-uniform-program) covers how it works.
