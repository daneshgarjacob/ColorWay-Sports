**Houston has a new look.** On Wednesday, November 11, the Cougars posted "New uni unlocked: Icy Houston Blues" with a photo shoot and a video. It is a white uniform built around the light Houston Blue that the program brought back this season, with the red trim that ties it to the rest of the closet.

Our grade: **B**. I think the Icy Houston Blues are great as a concept. I would have liked to see the helmet have more of a pop of color, and for that reason I don't think it's going to look as good on the field as it does in the photos.

## Quick Answers

- **What is the Icy Houston Blues uniform?** A white helmet, white jersey and white pants, with "Houston" in Houston Blue script outlined in red on the helmet, the chest and the hip, a Houston Blue V-neck collar insert with a white interlocking UH, Houston Blue stripes on the pants and Houston Blue gloves.
- **When did Houston reveal it?** Wednesday, November 11, 2026, on the team's X account.
- **When will Houston wear it?** Houston did not say in the post. Its next game is **Friday, November 13 at Colorado** (9:15 p.m. Central, ESPN), and it was revealed that week, so Boulder is the likely debut. We will confirm from game photos.
- **Our grade:** **B**.

## What The Icy Houston Blues Look Like

Everything here comes from Houston's own photos.

- **Helmet:** white shell with **"Houston" in Houston Blue script outlined in red**, a red center stripe and a red facemask.
- **Jersey:** white, with a **Houston Blue V-neck collar insert** carrying a white interlocking UH, **"Houston" across the chest in Houston Blue script outlined in red**, and a red Nike swoosh. The Big 12 patch sits on the shoulder.
- **Pants:** white, with **Houston Blue stripes** down the leg and a **"Houston" script patch** on the hip.
- **Gloves:** Houston Blue.

It is an all-white uniform where the blue does the talking. That is the idea behind the name: white first, then icy blue on top.

## Our Grade: B

I like the overall concept a lot. The script is the best part. Houston Blue with a red outline is one of the best color combinations in college football, and putting it on the chest, the hip and the helmet ties the whole uniform together. The blue collar is a nice touch too.

My problem is the helmet. From a distance it reads as a plain white helmet with a little bit of script on the side. I would have liked to see more of a pop of color up top, like a Houston Blue facemask or a bigger blue stripe, so the helmet matches how much blue is on the rest of the uniform. On TV, under the lights, I think a lot of this is going to read as plain white, which is why it lands at a **B** and not higher.
