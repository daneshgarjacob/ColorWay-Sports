No franchise in baseball is wearing its own situation as plainly as this one. The Athletics have no city on their chest, play in a minor league park in Sacramento, and have a jersey that thanks the town taking them in while carrying an advertising patch for the city they are leaving it for. All four uniforms are legible as a story about a team between homes. The good news, and it is genuinely good news, is that the best thing in the closet is the piece nobody has been allowed to touch in fifty years. Here is every Athletics jersey and uniform for 2026, ranked worst to first and graded.

Revealed in September 2025 and the newest thing the A's own, this replaced the old gold "A's" top entirely. Gold base, "Sacramento" across the chest in green script trimmed in white, a green A's mark on the left sleeve, and green sleeve piping. It anchors "Sacramento Saturdays" at Sutter Health Park, and players can wear it on other days if they want.

It grades a B, and finishing last says more about the company than the jersey. On pure design this is good work: the script is handsome, the gold is the right gold, and shooting it against the Tower Bridge was the correct instinct because the whole point is civic. What complicates it is what else is on the fabric. This is a uniform built as a thank-you to the region that took the team in, and it carries a Las Vegas tourism patch on the sleeve while doing it. That is not a design flaw, and it is not something the equipment staff chose, but you cannot look at the jersey without seeing it. A genuinely warm gesture wearing an advertisement for the exit.

The home white is a clean white base with green and gold trim and "Athletics" arched across the chest in green script. No city name, just the team. It takes most of the homestand, everything the Friday greens and the Saturday golds do not claim.

It grades a B+. Green and gold is one of the great colour pairings in the sport, and a white base is the most generous canvas you can give it, so the trim does all the work without the uniform ever raising its voice. The chest is the interesting part though. That missing city name is not a design choice, it is a franchise with nowhere to put one, and it leaves the wordmark looking slightly stranded in a way a normal home white never does. A very good jersey carrying a blank space it did not ask for.

On the road it is the standard gray set with "Athletics" across the chest, names on the back, and the green cap with the interlocking gold "A." The default for most away games.

It grades a B+ and edges the home white, which is not where a road gray usually finishes. The reason is that gray suits this team's predicament better than white does. A home uniform is supposed to announce where you are, and the A's cannot, so the whites carry an absence. Grays were never in that business. They exist to be the away kit, and "Athletics" with no city reads as deliberate here rather than incomplete. Add that the green-and-gold cap pops harder against gray than white, and the road set quietly becomes the better-resolved uniform. Not by much, but by enough.

The kelly green is the fan favourite and the closest thing this team has to a hard rule. Bright kelly green base, "Athletics" across the chest in gold, gold numbers, green-and-gold cap, pulled straight from the Charlie Finley era. It has owned Friday home games since Green Friday began in 2018, and it made the move to Sacramento intact, with only the old "Oakland" wordmark swapped for "Athletics." The A's leaned further into that heritage in 2026 with a [125th anniversary patch reviving the elephant logo](/stories/athletics-125th-anniversary-patch-elephant-logo-2026).

It grades an A, the highest mark in this closet, and the shade is the whole argument. This is not a muted forest green or a corporate hunter green, it is a bright, unapologetic kelly that no other team in baseball is brave enough to wear, and the gold sitting on top of it is exactly the right amount of loud. Nothing else in the league looks like it. It is also the one uniform here with no asterisk attached: no missing city, no patch working against the message, no interim anything. Fifty years of arguments about where this franchise plays and the answer to what it should look like has not changed once. Give it a standing Friday slot and get out of the way, which is precisely what they have done.

## The Bottom Line on the Athletics Jersey Rankings

This is a four-jersey closet with no bad uniform in it, which is remarkable for a franchise this unsettled. Nothing here grades below a B.

What separates them is how much each one is carrying. The gold Sacramento is a genuinely nice jersey doing an impossible job, thanking one city while advertising another on its own sleeve. The home whites are handsome but wear a blank space where a city name should sit. The grays edge them because a road uniform was never supposed to say where you are from, so the absence reads as intent rather than loss.

And then the kelly greens, which are simply great. No caveat, no interim status, no compromise. The single loudest shade in baseball, worn with total confidence, unchanged in any meaningful way since the 1970s. A franchise that has lost its city and its ballpark still has not lost its colour, and on Fridays you can see exactly what this team is supposed to look like.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best Athletics jersey for 2026?**

We rank the kelly green alternate No. 1 with an A. It is a bright kelly base with "Athletics" in gold, pulled from the Charlie Finley era, and it has anchored Friday home games since the Green Friday tradition started in 2018. No other team in baseball wears a green this bright.

**How many uniforms do the Athletics have in 2026?**

Four: the home whites, the road grays, the kelly green alternate, and the gold "Sacramento" alternate. They do not have a City Connect.

**Why don't the Athletics have a city name on their jerseys?**

Because the franchise dropped its city label while it moves between homes. Having left Oakland and with a Las Vegas ballpark still ahead, the team plays as simply the "Athletics," and both the home whites and the road grays carry only the team name across the chest.

**What is the gold Sacramento jersey?**

An alternate revealed in September 2025 that replaced the old gold "A's" top. It is a gold jersey with "Sacramento" in green script, designed as a thank-you to the region hosting the team at Sutter Health Park, and it anchors "Sacramento Saturdays." It also carries a Las Vegas tourism patch, which is the tension a lot of fans noticed immediately.

**When do the Athletics wear their green jerseys?**

Friday home games. The Green Friday tradition dates to 2018 and survived the move to Sacramento intact, with only the chest wordmark changing from "Oakland" to "Athletics."
