Gold is the Lakers home jersey. Everybody knows it. The Forum, Magic, Kobe, the Showtime highlight reels: gold at home is the most famous look in the NBA. So here is the strange part of the 2026-27 schedule. **The Lakers wear gold at home just 9 times in 40 home games.** The purple Statement jersey gets **13** home games, the most of anything in the closet.

The NBA published its official game-by-game uniform schedule on October 19, and we charted every Lakers home date below. Tonight's home opener against the Warriors is one of the rare gold nights.

Gold has not disappeared. It just moved out. The Icon Edition is still the most-worn Lakers jersey of the season at **28 games**, but **19 of those are on the road**. With most NBA teams now wearing color at home, the Lakers bring gold on the road to avoid a color-on-color game. The gold travels, and the purple stays home.

## The Patterns Hiding in the Schedule

**Sunday white is alive.** Nine of the Lakers' 10 white Association games at home fall on a Sunday. The only white home game that is not on a Sunday is Christmas Day against the 76ers. If you have tickets to a Sunday home game, plan on white: every Sunday home game this season is a white game.

**Purple Fridays.** Nine of the 13 purple Statement home games are on a Friday. Friday night at Crypto.com Arena this season is a purple night.

**No gold at home in December.** The Lakers play six home games in December and none of them are gold: three Specter Edition nights (Pelicans, Pacers, Nuggets), white on Christmas and December 27, and purple on December 29. After the Jazz on November 18, the next gold home game is **January 7 against the Celtics**. That is eight straight home games and seven weeks without gold in the building.

**Purple opens the home stand.** After tonight's gold opener, the next two home games are both purple: the Clippers on Friday, October 23, and the Trail Blazers on Tuesday, October 27.

**The City Edition stays home late.** The black City Edition is on five home dates: January 14 vs the Magic, February 10 vs the Clippers, February 18 vs the Rockets, March 17 vs the Suns and April 7 vs the Timberwolves. Our [City Edition guide](/stories/nba-city-edition-jerseys-2026-27) has the design and all 30 teams.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many times do the Lakers wear gold at home in 2026-27?**

Nine. The gold Icon Edition is scheduled for 9 of the Lakers' 40 dated home games, per the NBA's official uniform schedule: October 21, November 2, November 18, January 7, January 26, February 16, March 6, March 11 and April 6.

**What jersey do the Lakers wear most at home this season?**

The purple Statement Edition, scheduled for 13 home games. Then the white Association Edition with 10, the gold Icon with 9, the City Edition with 5 and the Specter Edition with 3.

**What are the Lakers wearing tonight?**

For the home opener on Wednesday, October 21 against the Warriors, the Lakers wear the gold Icon Edition. Our [Lakers uniform schedule](/stories/lakers-uniform-schedule-2026-27) shows the jersey for every game.

**Do the Lakers still wear white on Sundays?**

Yes. Nine of the Lakers' 10 white home games in 2026-27 are on Sundays, and every Sunday home game this season is a white game. The one white home game not on a Sunday is Christmas Day against the 76ers.

**Why do the Lakers wear purple at home?**

The NBA lets teams choose from their full wardrobe at home, and the Lakers have given the purple Statement jersey the most home nights this season, mostly on Fridays. Gold moves to the road: 19 of the Lakers' 28 gold games are road games, since most home teams now wear color and the Lakers need a contrasting jersey.

**Is gold still the Lakers' most-worn jersey?**

Yes, across the whole season. The gold Icon Edition is scheduled for 28 of 80 dated games, the most of any Lakers jersey. It is just mostly worn on the road (19 road, 9 home).

**When do the Lakers wear the Specter Edition?**

Three home games in December: December 14 vs the Pelicans, December 21 vs the Pacers and December 23 vs the Nuggets. See our [Specter Edition guide](/stories/nba-specter-edition-jerseys-2026-27).

**When do the Lakers wear the City Edition at home?**

Five times: January 14 vs the Magic, February 10 vs the Clippers, February 18 vs the Rockets, March 17 vs the Suns and April 7 vs the Timberwolves.

**What do the Lakers wear on Christmas Day 2026?**

The white Association Edition, at home against the Philadelphia 76ers on Friday, December 25.

**Can the Lakers' jersey schedule change?**

It can. Dates move for national television, and two more games per team are added after the NBA Cup knockout bracket is set. We update this page and the Lakers schedule if a call changes.

## The Bottom Line

Gold is still the Lakers' most-worn jersey, but not at home. In 2026-27 the purple Statement owns Crypto.com Arena with 13 home nights, Sundays stay white, and gold shows up at home just nine times, starting tonight against the Warriors. If you are going to a game and want gold, circle those nine dates.
