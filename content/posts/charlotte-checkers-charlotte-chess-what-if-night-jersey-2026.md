**Chess, not checkers.** The **Charlotte Checkers** have heard the joke for decades, and on **Friday, November 27** they are leaning into it. The Florida Panthers' AHL affiliate will play one game as the **Charlotte Chess** for **What If? Night**, against the **Hershey Bears** at **4 p.m.** at **Bojangles Coliseum**.

"What if we listened to everyone's favorite joke on social media?" is how the club put it in its announcement. The team will wear special Charlotte Chess jerseys for the game, and the game-worn and game-issued jerseys are **already up for auction**, with bidding open until **6 p.m. Friday**.

## Quick Answers

- **What is the Charlotte Chess?** A one-night alternate identity for the AHL's Charlotte Checkers, built on the saying "playing chess, not checkers."
- **When do the Checkers play as the Charlotte Chess?** Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, at 4 p.m. Eastern.
- **Who do they play?** The Hershey Bears, at Bojangles Coliseum in Charlotte.
- **What does the jersey look like?** White, with black stripes on the sleeves and hem, a black collar and a shield crest with a chess knight, a crown, "CLT" and "CHESS."
- **Can you get one?** The game-worn and game-issued jerseys are being auctioned on the Checkers' Dash page until 6 p.m. Friday. The team says a line of Chess merchandise will be sold at the merch store during the game.
- **Who are the Checkers affiliated with?** The Florida Panthers, since before the 2020-21 season.

## What The Charlotte Chess Jersey Looks Like

Everything here comes from the club's own announcement and the jersey photo it posted with it. The team's written description is short: **"special white and black jerseys emblazoned with the Charlotte Chess logo across the chest."** Here is what the photo shows.

**The body.** A **white** sweater. Each sleeve has **two black stripes** running around the arm, and the sleeves end in **black cuffs**. A **wide black stripe** runs around the hem.

**The collar.** A **black V-neck collar** with a black insert at the throat. No laces in the photo.

**The crest.** A **shield** with a **crown** sitting on top, a bar that reads **"CLT"**, a **white chess knight** (the horse-head piece) in the middle, and a bar across the bottom that reads **"CHESS."** The club's photo is shot in black and white, so we are going only on the team's words for color: white and black.

**The shoulders.** Round patches on both shoulders with a small **crown** in the middle and the **Charlotte Checkers** name around it. So the real team name is still on the sweater, just moved to the shoulders.

**The promo art.** The team's Chess Night graphic shows Chubby, the Checkers' mascot, holding a king piece over a chessboard, with the CLT Chess crest beside the words "Chess Night."

The club has not posted a back view, numbers or names, so we are not going to describe them. We will update this after the game.

## How To Buy Or Bid On A Charlotte Chess Jersey

Per the team:

- **The auction.** Signed Charlotte Chess memorabilia, including **game-worn and game-issued jerseys**, **locker room nameplates**, **pucks** and **mini sticks**, is on the **Checkers' Dash auction page** (fans.winwithdash.com/team/checkers). Bidding opened at **9 a.m. Tuesday, Nov. 24** and closes at **6 p.m. Friday, Nov. 27**. The game starts at 4 p.m., so bidding stays open through most of it.
- **Merch.** The team says **a line of Chess merch** will be available at the merch store **during the game**. It has not said what is in that line, or whether a replica jersey is part of it.
- **Tickets.** Sold through the Checkers' ticket page on Fevo.

## This Is The Third What If? Night, And All Three Are Against Hershey

Here is the detail nobody else has put together. The Charlotte Chess is the Checkers' **third** What If? Night, and **every one of them has come against the Hershey Bears**:

- **Charlotte River Rats**, Saturday, **Feb. 22, 2025**, 4 p.m. vs. Hershey. The team's first What If? Night. The idea: what if the franchise had kept the name of the **Albany River Rats**, the AHL club it bought and moved to Charlotte in 2010. Game-worn jerseys were auctioned that day.
- **Queen City Grits**, Saturday, **Jan. 17, 2026**, vs. Hershey. A nod to an original team name suggestion, per the team. The jerseys were auctioned to benefit Novant Health Hemby Children's Hospital.
- **Charlotte Chess**, Friday, **Nov. 27, 2026**, 4 p.m. vs. Hershey.

The first two were real "what if" questions about names the team could have had. The Chess is different. Nobody ever proposed it. It comes straight from the internet.

The Chess is also the first of **six specialty jerseys** the Checkers have planned for 2026-27. Next are Racing Night (Dec. 19), Pink In The Rink (Jan. 16), Florida Panthers-inspired sweaters for Affiliate Weekend (Feb. 5 and 6), Bojangles Night (March 6) and BBQ Night (April 3). The team says every one of them gets an online auction.

## Why Are They Called The Checkers?

The name is older than the AHL team, by more than 50 years. From the team's own history page:

- **1956:** The Eastern Hockey League's **Baltimore Clippers** lose their rink to a fire and finish the season at the new Charlotte Coliseum, the building now called **Bojangles Coliseum**. They stay and become the **Charlotte Clippers**.
- **Late 1950s:** A name contest. The team narrows hundreds of entries down to seven and asks **Maurice "Rocket" Richard** to pick by telegram. He picks **Checkers** over Crowns, Vikings, Sabres, Wildwings, Furies and Satans.
- **1977:** That team folds with the Southern Hockey League.
- **1993:** The **ECHL** brings hockey back. Another name contest, and "Checkers" beats "Blades."
- **2010:** Owner Michael Kahn buys the AHL's **Albany River Rats** and moves them to Charlotte. The franchise takes the Checkers name and starts in the AHL as the **Carolina Hurricanes'** affiliate.
- **2015-16:** Back to Bojangles Coliseum after 10 seasons uptown.
- **2019:** First **Calder Cup**.
- **2020-21 on:** Top affiliate of the **Florida Panthers**. The Checkers' new primary jerseys, introduced in 2025, use a stripe pattern borrowed from the Panthers' sweaters.

One more thing about the crowns on the Chess jersey: Crowns was one of the names Richard turned down, and Charlotte is the Queen City. The team has not said the crowns are a reference to either, so take that as our reading, not theirs.

## Our Take

This is the best of the three Charlotte What If? Nights, and it is not close. River Rats and Queen City Grits were fine ideas for people who know the team's history. The Chess works for anyone who has ever heard the phrase, which is everyone. A team taking the oldest joke about its own name and turning it into a jersey is exactly what minor league hockey should be doing.

The jersey itself is clean. White with black stripes and a black hem is the most classic hockey template there is, and on a night about chess, black and white is the obvious answer. The knight is the best possible pick for the crest, since it is the one chess piece everyone recognizes on sight. Putting the real Checkers name on the shoulder patches is a smart touch too.

Our complaint: the crest is busy. A crown, a shield, two text bars and a knight is a lot for one chest logo, and the "CLT" bar adds nothing that the shoulder patches do not already say. A big knight on its own would have been stronger. And we wish the team had used a checkerboard somewhere on the sweater. The whole joke is chess vs. checkers, and both games are played on the same board.

**Grade: B+.** Great idea, good jersey, a crest that tries to do too much. Tell us we are wrong.
