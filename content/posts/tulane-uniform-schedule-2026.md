Tulane has more fun with uniforms than anyone else in the American Conference, and the fans have the receipts to prove it. This is the full Tulane 2026 uniform schedule: the green home jersey, the white road set, the sky blue alternate, the one-night Mardi Gras City Edition the Green Wave already burned in Week 2, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

The base is a Nike package that got a light refresh before the 2025 season: a bolder chest wordmark replacing the thinner 2019 version, the same green, white and sky blue jersey colors, and a Welcome To Uptown line stitched inside the collar. Helmets come in white with the green Angry Wave, olive green, and sky blue, and Tulane mixes and matches all of it without much of a pattern.

Tulane launched its City Edition series in 2025 and then built the 2026 version entirely out of New Orleans: argyle and beads and king cake and a jester, in the Mardi Gras purple, green and gold. We graded the whole thing when it dropped in [Tulane's Mardi Gras City Edition uniforms](/stories/tulane-city-edition-uniforms-2026), and the short version is that it is the most committed city uniform anyone has made in college football.

## The Sky Blue Question

Tulane has three jersey colors and has only used two of them so far. The sky blue set, jersey and helmet both, was all over last season. It has not appeared in 2026, and the two home dates with the most air under them are the Friday night Memphis game in October and Homecoming against Tulsa in November.

It is also worth saying what the City Edition series is and is not. Tulane introduced its first City Edition uniform in October 2025 for a November debut, then made the Mardi Gras build for 2026. That is two City Edition sets in two seasons, both one-offs, both retired immediately after the game. If there is a third piece coming this year it would be a separate reveal, and nothing has been announced.

## The Bottom Line

Tulane spent its best uniform of the year in Week 2 and that was clearly the plan, because the Mardi Gras set was built for a home night in New Orleans and nothing else on the schedule would have suited it. What is left is a straightforward color pattern with one open question: green at home, white on the road, and a sky blue alternate sitting in the equipment room waiting for a Friday night or Homecoming. The grid above has every date with the expected jersey, and we add a star as each uniform is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Tulane wearing this week?**

Tulane plays at Kansas State on Saturday, September 19 at 11 a.m. Central on ESPN2, and the white road jersey is expected with Kansas State in purple at home. The Green Wave last wore the Mardi Gras City Edition against South Alabama on September 12. The week-by-week grid above lists every game with the expected uniform, and we update it as combinations are confirmed.

**What are Tulane's football uniforms for 2026?**

An olive green home jersey, a white road jersey and a sky blue alternate, all Nike, all carrying the bolder Tulane chest wordmark introduced in 2025 and a Welcome To Uptown mark inside the collar. Helmets come in white with the green Angry Wave, olive green and sky blue. On top of that sits the one-night Mardi Gras City Edition set worn against South Alabama.

**Will Tulane wear the Mardi Gras City Edition uniform again in 2026?**

No. Tulane said the King of Mardi Gras set was a one-game uniform, worn against South Alabama at Yulman Stadium on September 12 and then sold, with proceeds going to the Green Wave Talent Fund. Our full breakdown and grade is in [Tulane's Mardi Gras City Edition uniforms](/stories/tulane-city-edition-uniforms-2026).

**Does Tulane still have the sky blue uniforms?**

Yes. The sky blue jersey and the sky blue helmet are both part of the current closet and were used several times last season. Neither has appeared in 2026 through two games, and the Friday night home game against Memphis on October 16 is the most likely spot for them.

**When is Tulane's Homecoming game in 2026?**

Saturday, November 7 against Tulsa at Yulman Stadium, with kickoff still to be announced. Green is expected. Homecoming and the October 16 Memphis game are the two home dates we are watching most closely for an alternate.

**How many games does Tulane play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: six at Yulman Stadium and six on the road, with a single bye on October 3. The American Conference Championship Game on December 5 would be a thirteenth if the Green Wave qualify. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
