## The Nuggets Classic Edition: Throwback Nights, Graded B

<img src="/images/posts/nba-classic-edition-jerseys-courts-2026-27/nuggets-throwback-nights-jersey-players.jpg" alt="Denver Nuggets 2026-27 Throwback Nights Classic Edition jersey, orange with an arched black DENVER wordmark" style="display: block; width: 100%; max-width: 480px; margin: 1.25em auto 0.4em; border-radius: 10px;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin-top: 0.5em;">Photo: Denver Nuggets</p>

The Throwback Nights jersey brings back the orange, black and white of the Denver Rockets for the 50th anniversary of the ABA-NBA merger. We like that it is a simple throwback, but it looks nothing like the navy and gold the Nuggets usually wear, so the jersey gets a **B**. The new ABA court, with an orange apron, orange lanes and an arched DENVER at center court, is a big step up from Denver's regular floor and gets an **A-**. Denver finished No. 4 in our [ranking of all four ABA Classic Edition jerseys and courts](/stories/nba-classic-edition-jerseys-courts-2026-27).

Denver wears it seven times, all at Ball Arena: December 27 vs Indiana, January 21 vs Washington, February 4 vs Dallas, February 11 vs Chicago, March 1 vs Brooklyn, March 3 vs San Antonio and April 8 vs the Clippers.
