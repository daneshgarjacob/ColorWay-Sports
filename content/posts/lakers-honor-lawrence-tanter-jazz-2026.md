**The Lakers honored Lawrence Tanter on Monday night, and we were in the building.** Tanter was the Lakers' public address announcer for 43 seasons, from 1982 until he retired in June after a stroke. He died on September 23 at 76. Before the Lakers beat the Jazz 109-94 at Crypto.com Arena on November 23, the team put a commemorative banner on Star Plaza for fans to sign. During the game it played a video tribute, brought Michael Cooper and Mychal Thompson onto the court to speak about him, and showed a plaque with a headset resting on it at the announcer's spot on the big screen.

## Quick Answers

- **When did Lawrence Tanter die?** September 23, 2026, at 76. The Lakers announced it that night.
- **How long was he the Lakers' PA announcer?** 43 seasons, starting in the 1982-83 season. He is the longest-tenured PA announcer in Lakers history.
- **When did the Lakers honor him?** Monday, November 23, 2026, at the home game against the Utah Jazz.
- **What did the Lakers do?** A commemorative banner on Star Plaza where fans left messages from 4 to 8 p.m., a video tribute in the arena, Michael Cooper and Mychal Thompson speaking on the court, and a plaque with a headset shown on the scoreboard.
- **Did the Lakers wear a Tanter patch?** Not that we saw, and the team has not announced one. The Lakers wore their regular purple Statement Edition jerseys.
- **Who won?** The Lakers, 109-94.
- **Who is the Lakers' PA announcer now?** Derrin Horton, named on October 1, 2026.

## What We Saw at Crypto.com Arena

The tribute that stopped us came at the break before the second quarter. The center-hung scoreboard went purple, with "Remembering Lawrence Tanter" on every panel and on the ribbon boards around the bowl. In the middle was a live shot of a black plaque with the Lakers logo, standing courtside where the public address announcer works, with a pair of headphones and a microphone resting on it.

The plaque reads "Lakers," "Remembering Lawrence Tanter," "Public Address Announcer" and "43 seasons." The score bug underneath said Lakers 30, Jazz 20. The Lakers led after one quarter by the same score, per ESPN.

It was a simple picture. A headset with no one wearing it said what it needed to say. For anyone who has been to Lakers games over the last four decades, the building has always sounded like his voice.

The video tribute ran during the game. Per the New York Post's Melissa Rohlin, it showed Tanter introducing Magic Johnson, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Shaquille O'Neal, Kobe Bryant, LeBron James and Luka Doncic, along with his famous "Laker Girls" call, and ended with a banner marking his 43 years. Cooper and Thompson, who both won titles in the 1980s with Tanter on the microphone, then spoke on the court. "As much as you guys love him, we love him even more," Thompson told the crowd, per the Post.

Before the game, Lakers coach JJ Redick called Tanter "an iconic voice" and said he "made a mark not only on the Lakers but the NBA," per USA Today. Redick also said that hearing Tanter's voice at his first game at Staples Center was the moment he felt he had made the NBA.

Outside, the Lakers set up a commemorative banner on Star Plaza and invited fans to write messages to "LT" from 4 to 8 p.m.

## Who Lawrence Tanter Was

Tanter grew up in the Chicago area and played basketball at Thornton High School, then earned a basketball scholarship to the University of Dubuque in Iowa. He got his start in radio there, answering an ad for a Sunday night jazz host. He moved to Los Angeles in 1971 and spent decades as a jazz radio DJ and programmer, starting at KJLH in 1972 and later working at stations including KUTE, KACE, KKJZ and KTWV.

The Lakers hired him as their PA announcer for the 1982-83 season, during Showtime at the Forum. He went with the team to Staples Center in 1999, the building now called Crypto.com Arena, and stayed through 43 seasons. He stepped away from 1993 to 1995, then came back. Over that run he called 10 Lakers championships, per the team, and more than 2,100 games, including 14 NBA Finals series and four All-Star Games, per USA Today.

His style was the opposite of most modern arena announcers. He used a low, smooth voice and kept it level for most of the night, which made the big moments land. He stretched names on the big plays. His best-known call was the long "Laker Girls" after the dance team's routines.

Two moments stand out. At the first Lakers home game after Kobe Bryant died in January 2020, Tanter introduced every Lakers starter as Kobe Bryant: "6-6, 20th year out of Lower Merion High School, Kobe Bryant." And during the 2020 bubble in Orlando, with no fans and no arena, he recorded the Lakers' player introductions at a home studio in Porter Ranch so they could be played before games at Walt Disney World, per ESPN. The Lakers won the title that season.

In March 2026 Tanter had a stroke and missed the last six home games of the regular season and the playoffs. Jason Barquero, the PA announcer for the Lakers' G League team, filled in, per ESPN. We wrote about it when [Tanter first missed a game](/stories/lawrence-tanter-lakers-pa-announcer-illness-2026). On June 16 the Lakers announced [his retirement after 43 seasons](/stories/lawrence-tanter-retires-lakers-pa-announcer-2026) and named him special advisor for game presentation. He held that job until he died on September 23.

The Lakers' statement said, "For 43 years, LT's voice served as the soundtrack to Lakers games at the arena." The team posted this the night he died:

## How the Lakers Honored Him

Here is everything the Lakers did on November 23 that we could confirm:

- **Banner on Star Plaza.** A commemorative banner outside the arena before the game, with fans invited to write messages from 4 to 8 p.m. The Lakers announced it on the morning of the game.
- **The plaque and the headset.** A black Lakers plaque reading "Remembering Lawrence Tanter, Public Address Announcer, 43 seasons," with a headset resting on it, shown live on the scoreboard and ribbon boards before the second quarter. We saw this ourselves. USA Today also reported that the Lakers unveiled a plaque in the arena.
- **Video tribute.** His introductions of Lakers greats from Magic to Luka, his "Laker Girls" call and a closing banner for his 43 years.
- **Michael Cooper and Mychal Thompson.** Two Showtime-era Lakers spoke on the court.

This was not the first moment for him this season. The Lakers held a moment of silence for Tanter before their first home preseason game, against the Kings on October 8, per a USA Today photo caption. That was also Derrin Horton's first game as the new PA announcer.

### Was There a Jersey Patch?

No. We found no announcement of a Tanter patch, armband or warm-up shirt from the Lakers, and we did not see one on Monday. The Lakers' purple jerseys carried the usual Albert sponsor patch and the Jordan logo on the chest, and nothing else that we could see from our seats or in the NBA TV highlights. The Lakers honored Tanter through the arena itself: the scoreboard, the plaque at his old spot, the video and the banner outside.

## The Game

The Lakers beat the Jazz 109-94 in front of 19,057, per ESPN and the Associated Press. Five days earlier, Utah had won 109-97 in the same building and ended the Lakers' nine-game winning streak. Utah opened the second half on a 15-3 run on Monday before the Lakers started the fourth quarter on a 17-5 run. Luka Doncic had 28 points and eight assists, Jake LaRavia added 15 points and 10 rebounds, and Matisse Thybulle scored 14. Kyle Filipowski led the Jazz with 21 points and 12 rebounds.

## How Other Teams Have Honored Their Voices on the Uniform

Memorial patches for announcers are mostly a baseball tradition. Four we could confirm:

- **Yankees, Bob Sheppard (2010).** The Yankees' PA announcer from 1951 to 2007 died in July 2010. The team wore a patch for him on the left sleeve of the home and road uniforms for the rest of the season, alongside a patch for George Steinbrenner, who died two days later.
- **Dodgers, Vin Scully (2022).** After Scully died on August 2, 2022, the Dodgers wore a black "VIN" patch with a microphone on their jersey sleeves for the rest of the season.
- **Brewers, Bob Uecker (2025).** The Brewers wore a patch with Uecker's signature over a baseball ringed in plaid, a nod to his sport coats, for the whole 2025 season.
- **Yankees, John Sterling (2026).** After the longtime radio voice died in May, the Yankees added a round sleeve patch with his name and a microphone over pinstripes, per MLB.com.

A PA announcer is the only one of these voices you hear only in the building. Sheppard is the closest match to Tanter, and he got a sleeve patch.

## Our Take

The Lakers got this right. The plaque and the headset at his spot were the best part of the night: quiet and specific to the job he did. The video of him calling Magic, Kareem, Shaq, Kobe, LeBron and Luka covered every era of the franchise, and he was the one constant through all of it.

The one thing missing is on the uniform. The Yankees put a microphone on their sleeve for Bob Sheppard. The Lakers could have done the same with a small "LT" patch for one night, or for the season. Lawrence Tanter was the greatest PA announcer in sports, and his voice is part of what the Lakers are, the same as the purple and gold.
