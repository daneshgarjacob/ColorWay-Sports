**Ten years ago this week, on Tuesday, November 22, 2016, owner Bill Foley stood outside T-Mobile Arena and introduced the Vegas Golden Knights.** The name came with a logo that has not changed since: a knight's helmet on a black and gold shield, with a **V** cut into the visor. In the decade since, Vegas has worn **seven different sweaters**, from the steel grey original to this season's casino-themed Hometown Remix, and it just retired the best one.

Here is every logo and jersey in Golden Knights history, in plain colors, with our grade for each. The cover photo is the one that started it all: the **steel grey** home, on goalie Robin Lehner in 2022.

## Quick Answers

- **When were the Golden Knights' name and logo revealed?** Tuesday, November 22, 2016, at Toshiba Plaza outside T-Mobile Arena, five months to the day after Las Vegas was awarded the team.
- **Why are they called the Golden Knights?** Foley is a West Point graduate, and "Knights" nods to Army's Black Knights. Gold is there because Nevada is the largest gold producer in the United States, the NHL said at the reveal.
- **What is hidden in the logo?** A **V**, for Vegas, formed by the opening in the knight's visor.
- **What are the team colors?** **Steel grey, gold, red and black.**
- **Did the Army sue over the name?** The Army opposed the team's trademark in 2018, and the two sides settled with a coexistence agreement that July. The Army's parachute team keeps the Golden Knights name for skydiving.
- **What was the first jersey?** **Steel grey** at home with black, gold and red, and **white** on the road, unveiled June 20, 2017.
- **When did gold become the home jersey?** The gold sweater arrived as a third jersey in 2020-21 and became the primary home in **2022-23**.
- **Is the steel grey jersey retired?** Yes, from the regular rotation. It was worn one last time on October 10, 2026.
- **Best Golden Knights jersey ever:** the steel grey original, **B+**. **Worst:** the 2026-27 Hometown Remix, **F**.

## The 2016 Reveal: Name And Logo

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; margin: 1.5em 0;">
<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/NHL/status/801268880077271040"></a></blockquote>
</div>
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: -0.5em 0 2em;">The NHL's post on reveal night, via <a href="https://x.com/NHL" target="_blank" rel="noopener">@NHL</a></p>

Foley revealed the name at an event at **Toshiba Plaza, just outside T-Mobile Arena**, on November 22, 2016, [according to NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/news/vegas-golden-knights-official-team-name-283981522). It came five months to the day after the league awarded Las Vegas its expansion team.

**Why "Knights":** Foley called knights "the epitome of the warrior class." He is a graduate of West Point, whose teams are the Army Black Knights, and he had wanted "Black Knights" before dropping it, as the Las Vegas Review-Journal and the Washington Post reported at the time. He also mentioned Nighthawks and Sidewinders as names that had been in the running.

**Why "Golden":** gold is the top precious metal, and **Nevada is the largest producer of gold in the United States**, the NHL noted at the reveal.

**Why "Vegas" and not "Las Vegas":** locals say Vegas, and a four-word name was too long.

**The logo:** a knight's **helmet** in **steel grey and gold** on a **black and gold shield**. The opening in the visor forms a **V** for Vegas. The secondary logo, which you see on the shoulders, is two **crossed swords** forming a **star**, borrowed from the star on the "Welcome to Fabulous Las Vegas" sign. The primary logo has not changed in 10 years.

**The colors, as the team explained them:** **steel grey** for strength and durability, **gold** for Nevada's gold, **red** from the Las Vegas skyline, the desert and Red Rock Canyon, and **black** for power and intensity.

**Logo grade: B+.** The hidden V is the rare logo trick that works on first look, and a helmet on a shield is a mark you can draw from memory. It is a little generic as a knight, and a lot of college logos look like it.

## The Trademark Fights

The name was not a sure thing. In **December 2016**, the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office initially **refused** "Vegas Golden Knights," citing likely confusion with the **College of Saint Rose Golden Knights** in Albany, New York. Vegas appealed, and the name was approved in **August 2017**. The club also cleared the name with Clarkson University, whose teams are also the Golden Knights.

Then came the **U.S. Army**. Its parachute demonstration team has been the **Golden Knights since 1959**, and the Army filed an **opposition** to the team's trademark, which the Trademark Trial and Appeal Board instituted on **January 10, 2018**. On **July 19, 2018**, the two sides [announced a trademark coexistence agreement](https://www.nhl.com/goldenknights/news/golden-knights-and-u-s-army-enter-into-trademark-coexistence-agreement-299533494) and the Army dropped its opposition. The Army keeps the name for its parachute team, and the hockey team keeps it for hockey.

## Every Golden Knights Jersey

### Steel Grey Home (2017-2022), Then Alternate (2022-2026)

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; margin: 1.5em 0;">
<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/GoldenKnights/status/877343682013216768"></a></blockquote>
</div>
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: -0.5em 0 2em;">The first two jerseys on unveiling night, June 20, 2017, via <a href="https://x.com/GoldenKnights" target="_blank" rel="noopener">@GoldenKnights</a></p>

The first Golden Knights uniform was unveiled on Tuesday, **June 20, 2017**, at Intrigue nightclub at Wynn Las Vegas, the same night adidas unveiled its new jerseys for every NHL team.

- **Sweater:** **steel grey**, a dark, slightly metallic charcoal.
- **Stripes:** **gold** and **black** sleeve bands edged in **red**. The NHL.com reveal story noted that the gold trim on the sleeves carries a pattern of **sagebrush**, Nevada's state flower.
- **Crest:** the helmet-and-shield logo. **Shoulders:** the crossed-swords star.
- **Numbers:** white, trimmed in gold.
- **Pants and helmet:** **black**.

It was the home jersey for five seasons, including the run to the 2018 Stanley Cup Final in the team's first year. When gold took over the home slot in 2022-23 it became the alternate, and this fall Vegas took it out of the regular rotation. Its last game was **October 10, 2026**, the anniversary of the franchise's first home game, a 2-1 overtime win over the Kings.

**Grade: B+.** The best sweater Vegas has ever worn. Steel grey was a color nobody else in sports owned, it made the gold and red look expensive, and it gave the team an identity on day one. Retiring it was a mistake.

- **Sweater:** **white**, with **steel grey** on the shoulders and upper sleeves.

- **Pants:** **steel grey**. **Helmet:** **white**.

It has not changed since 2017.

**Grade: C+.** Fine and forgettable. It is a white version of a good idea, and it never had the grey's presence.

### Metallic Gold (Third 2020-22, Home 2022-Present)

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; margin: 1.5em 0;">
<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/GoldenKnights/status/1312082934501785600"></a></blockquote>
</div>
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: -0.5em 0 2em;">The gold jersey's unveiling, October 2, 2020, via <a href="https://x.com/GoldenKnights" target="_blank" rel="noopener">@GoldenKnights</a></p>

Unveiled **October 2, 2020**, and billed by the team as **"the first all-metallic gold jersey in the history of the National Hockey League."** It started as the third jersey. On September 20, 2022, the team announced gold would be the **home** jersey for 2022-23, and it has been ever since, including in the 2023 Cup win and the 2026 Final.

- **Sweater:** **metallic gold**.
- **Stripes:** **black, steel grey and red** bands on the sleeves and hem.
- **Crest:** the helmet-and-shield logo, with the sword star on the shoulders.
- **Pants:** **black**. **Helmet:** **gold**.

**Grade: C-.** The same grade we gave it in [the most overrated uniforms in sports](/stories/most-overrated-uniforms-in-sports-2026-ranked). Metallic gold never looks the same twice: mustard under some arena lights, brass under others, and the black, grey and red stripes crowd on top of it.

### Red Reverse Retro (2020-21)

<img src="/images/posts/golden-knights-logo-and-jersey-history/red-reverse-retro-2020.jpg" alt="Vegas Golden Knights 2020-21 red Reverse Retro jersey with a crossed swords star crest and V-shaped steel grey, gold and black stripes" style="display:block;width:100%;max-width:560px;margin:1.2em auto 0;border-radius:10px;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">The red Reverse Retro at its unveiling, November 16, 2020. Photo via the team</p>

Unveiled **November 16, 2020**, as part of the league-wide adidas Reverse Retro program. Vegas had no history of its own to flip, so it borrowed the city's: the **red** comes from the ECHL's **Las Vegas Wranglers** (2003-2014), and the **V-shaped stripes** come from the IHL's **Las Vegas Thunder** (1993-1999), [according to the team](https://www.nhl.com/goldenknights/news/vegas-golden-knights-and-adidas-unveil-all-new-reverse-retro-jersey-319636918).

- **Sweater:** **red**.
- **Crest:** the **crossed-swords star** in white, gold and black. The first Golden Knights jersey without the helmet crest.
- **Stripes:** a big **V** of **steel grey, gold and black** across the bottom and the sleeves.

**Grade: B-.** A smart way to give a four-year-old team a "retro" look, and the swords-star crest is better as a chest logo than anyone expected. The red was never really a Golden Knights color, though.

### Black Reverse Retro (2022-23)

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; margin: 1.5em 0;">
<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/GoldenKnights/status/1596659169301385216"></a></blockquote>
</div>
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: -0.5em 0 2em;">The black Reverse Retro on debut night, November 26, 2022, via <a href="https://x.com/GoldenKnights" target="_blank" rel="noopener">@GoldenKnights</a></p>

Unveiled **October 20, 2022**, and first worn **November 26, 2022**, against Vancouver. It was the team's **first black-based jersey**, another nod to the Las Vegas Thunder.

- **Sweater:** **black**.
- **Crest:** a diagonal **VEGAS** wordmark across the chest.
- **The trick:** the lettering and numbers **glow in the dark**, which the team showed off with the arena lights down.

**Grade: C+.** The glow was a fun one-night gimmick. As a sweater, it is a black jersey with a diagonal word on it, and it could belong to anyone.

<img src="/images/posts/golden-knights-logo-and-jersey-history/winter-classic-2024.jpg" alt="Jack Eichel of the Vegas Golden Knights in the cream 2024 Winter Classic sweater with gold stripes, crashing toward Seattle Kraken goalie Joey Daccord" style="display:block;width:100%;max-width:560px;margin:1.2em auto 0;border-radius:10px;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

Worn **January 1, 2024**, at T-Mobile Park in Seattle. The team said it imagined what a Vegas hockey team would have worn in the Wild West of the 1910s.

- **Sweater:** **vintage white**, almost cream, the first Golden Knights jersey on that base.
- **Stripes:** wide **heritage gold** bands on the sleeves and body.
- **Crest:** a **grey felt "V"** taken from the V in the logo.
- **Pants:** **steel grey** with "Vegas" in script. **Socks:** white, gold and grey barber-pole stripes.

Seattle won **3-0** in front of 47,313, with Joey Daccord making the shutout.

**Grade: B.** The best special jersey Vegas has worn. One color story, a simple crest, and it looked right outdoors. A plain V is thin as a crest, which keeps it out of the B+ range.

## Our Take

The Golden Knights got their identity right in 2016 and 2017 and have spent the decade drifting away from it. The name works, the hidden V works, and steel grey was the most original color an expansion team has picked in a generation. Then gold went from novelty to home jersey, the specials got louder, and this season the grey was retired the same fall Vegas put a casino on a sweater. For their 10th anniversary, the Golden Knights should bring back the steel grey as the home jersey. Gold can be the third.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When did the Vegas Golden Knights reveal their name and logo?**

November 22, 2016, at Toshiba Plaza outside T-Mobile Arena, five months after Las Vegas was awarded the team.

**Why are they called the Golden Knights?**

Owner Bill Foley is a West Point graduate, and the Army teams are the Black Knights. Gold was added because Nevada produces more gold than any other state. Foley said knights are "the epitome of the warrior class."

**What names did the Golden Knights consider?**

Foley mentioned Nighthawks and Sidewinders, and he had wanted Black Knights before dropping it.

**Why is it Vegas and not Las Vegas Golden Knights?**

Locals call the city Vegas, and a four-word name would have been too long.

**Is there a hidden V in the Golden Knights logo?**

Yes. The opening in the knight's visor forms a V for Vegas.

**What are the Golden Knights' colors?**

Steel grey, gold, red and black. The team said steel grey stands for strength, gold for Nevada, red for the Las Vegas skyline and Red Rock Canyon, and black for power and intensity.

**Did the Army sue the Golden Knights?**

The Army filed a trademark opposition, instituted in January 2018, because its parachute team has been the Golden Knights since 1959. The two sides settled with a coexistence agreement on July 19, 2018.

**What was the Golden Knights' original jersey?**

A steel grey home jersey with gold, black and red stripes and a white road jersey, unveiled June 20, 2017, at Wynn Las Vegas.

**When did the Golden Knights' gold jersey come out?**

It was unveiled October 2, 2020, as a third jersey, and became the home jersey in 2022-23.

**Did the Golden Knights retire the grey jersey?**

Yes, from the regular rotation. It was worn for the last time on October 10, 2026, a 2-1 overtime win over Los Angeles.
