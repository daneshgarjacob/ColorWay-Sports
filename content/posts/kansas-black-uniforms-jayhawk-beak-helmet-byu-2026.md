**Kansas went all black for Senior Day.** The Jayhawks posted their uniform combo for No. 12 BYU on Saturday morning, November 21, with the caption "⚫️⚡️": a **black helmet with an oversized Jayhawk**, zoomed in so the yellow beak takes over the side of the shell, a **blue facemask**, a **black jersey** and **black pants**. Kickoff at David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium was **noon Eastern (11 a.m. Central) on ABC**, and it was the final home game of the season. **Final: BYU 31, Kansas 29 in triple overtime**, after the game was tied 16-16 at the end of regulation.

## Quick Answers

- **What is Kansas wearing against BYU?** All black: a black helmet with an oversized Jayhawk and a blue facemask, a black jersey with KANSAS in white and blue numbers, and black pants with red, white and blue stripes down the side.
- **What is the beak helmet?** The regular Kansas Jayhawk logo, blown up and cropped so the bird's yellow beak and red head wrap around most of the black shell.
- **Has Kansas worn it before?** Yes. The same black helmet and black uniform came out at Utah on October 10 this season, and Kansas wore the oversized Jayhawk on a black helmet against West Virginia in September 2025.
- **What is BYU wearing?** All white with a royal blue facemask.
- **Our grade:** B+.

## What Kansas Is Wearing Against BYU

Kansas's "Uniform Combo, Game 11" graphic shows the full set, and the team's game photos match it. Here is every piece:

- **Helmet:** **black**, with the Jayhawk logo **blown up and cropped** so the **yellow beak** points forward across the side of the shell and the red head sits at the back. It is the regular Jayhawk, not the angrier 1941 bird, just zoomed in. The facemask is **blue**.
- **Jersey:** **black**, with **KANSAS across the chest in white** in the circus-style lettering, **blue numbers outlined in white and red**, a **blue V-neck collar** and a **Jayhawk on each sleeve**.
- **Pants:** **black**, with a **red, white and blue stripe** down each leg. The combo graphic shows the pants plain, but the game photos show the stripes clearly.
- **Cleats:** black.

## When Has Kansas Worn This Before?

This is the **second time this season**. Kansas wore the same black helmet with the oversized Jayhawk, the blue facemask, the black jersey and the black pants **at Utah on October 10**, shown in the team's own locker room photos before kickoff at Rice-Eccles Stadium.

The black set goes back further. Kansas first wore an all-black uniform against Illinois in September 2023, the first completely black uniform in program history, and that version carried the **1941 Jayhawk** on the helmet. The black came back against UNLV in 2024, and for the 2025 Big 12 opener against West Virginia on September 20, 2025, Kansas wore it with the **oversized Jayhawk on the black helmet**, the same helmet it is wearing today.

## Our Grade: B+

We love the helmet. Blowing the Jayhawk up until the beak is the whole side of the shell is a great idea, and that yellow beak against a black helmet is the brightest thing on the field. It pops.

The rest of the uniform is what keeps it at a B+. We are not big fans of all black in general, and head to toe black hides a lot of what makes Kansas look like Kansas. The red, white and blue pant stripes and the blue numbers help, but the helmet is doing most of the work here.

So we love the helmet and the pop it brings, and we don't love the all black around it. B+ overall.

## What BYU Is Wearing At Kansas

BYU is in **all white**: a **white helmet with a royal blue facemask** and a royal and white center stripe, a **white jersey with royal numbers** and royal stripes on the shoulders, and **white pants**.
