**The 89 patch on the Bears' jerseys is a memorial for Mike Ditka.** Ditka, the Hall of Fame tight end who wore No. 89 for Chicago and later coached the Bears to the Super Bowl XX title, died in October 2026 at 86. The Bears put a **football-shaped patch with his 89** on the chest for the game at Atlanta on **October 18**, which would have been his 87th birthday, per NBC 5 Chicago. The week before, at Green Bay, they wore **89 stickers on the back of their helmets**. The patch was on the jersey again on Thursday, when Chicago won 39-35 in Detroit on Thanksgiving.

## Quick Answers

- **What does the 89 on the Bears' jersey mean?** It is Mike Ditka's number. The patch honors him after his death in October 2026.
- **What does the patch look like?** A football shape with the number 89, in Bears navy, orange and white, on the chest of the jersey.
- **When did the Bears start wearing it?** October 18, 2026, at Atlanta, on what would have been Ditka's 87th birthday, per NBC 5 Chicago.
- **Did the Bears wear anything for Ditka before that?** Yes. Stickers on the back of the helmets at Green Bay the week before, per NBC 5 Chicago.
- **What is the Bears' record with the patch?** **6-0**, from our own game-by-game uniform log. They were 3-2 before it.
- **Is 89 retired by the Bears?** Yes. Chicago retired Ditka's No. 89 in 2013.

## Who Was Mike Ditka?

Ditka played tight end for the Bears from 1961 to 1966 and changed the position. Before him, tight ends mostly blocked. He caught passes like a receiver and still blocked like a lineman, and he was the first tight end voted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame. He came back as head coach in 1982 and won Super Bowl XX with the 1985 Bears, the team most fans still call the best defense ever. He coached Chicago through 1992. The franchise retired No. 89 in 2013, at a Monday night game at Soldier Field.

That makes 89 a number every Bears fan already knows on sight, which is why the patch does not need a name on it.

## What The Patch Looks Like On The Uniform

The patch sits on the left chest, over the heart. On the white road jersey you can see it plainly in the Bears' Thanksgiving photo above: a navy football with the 89, opposite the round John Madden patch the NFL put on every team for Thanksgiving ([the Madden patch explained](/stories/john-madden-thanksgiving-patch-2026)).

It is small and it is the right size, and the shape is not new. When founder George S. Halas died in the middle of the 1983 season, the Bears wore a **football-shaped patch** for him on the chest for the rest of that year, then moved his **GSH** initials to the left sleeve in 1984, where they still are ([why the Bears wear GSH](/stories/why-bears-wear-gsh-on-sleeves)). The Ditka patch uses the same football shape 43 years later, and it sits on the same jersey as the GSH without crowding it.

## The Bears Are 6-0 With The Patch On

This is the part nobody else is counting. We log the helmet, jersey and pants the Bears wear in every game in our [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026). Line that up with the date the patch went on, and the split is clean:

| | Record | Games |
|---|---|---|
| Before the patch (Weeks 1-5) | **3-2** | W Carolina, L Minnesota, W Philadelphia, W NY Jets, L Green Bay |
| With the 89 patch (Week 6 on) | **6-0** | W Atlanta, W New England, W Seattle, W Tampa Bay, W New Orleans, W Detroit |

The only game with the helmet stickers and no jersey patch was the 31-7 loss at Green Bay. Since the patch, Chicago has won six straight, three in the navy home jersey and three in the white road jersey, and is 9-2 and alone in first place in the NFC North.

A patch does not win football games. The Bears were getting better before Ditka died. But 6-0 is 6-0, and in Chicago they will not be taking it off.

## Where Else The Bears Honored Ditka

The patch was not the only tribute. For the Thursday night home game against New England in Week 7, the Bears painted Ditka's **89 inside a football logo on the field** at Soldier Field and held a tribute through the game, which the Bears beat the Patriots 30-24 in. The full uniform for that night, navy helmet, navy jersey and white pants, is in our [Week 7 uniforms](/stories/nfl-week-7-uniforms-2026) guide.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why do the Bears have an 89 patch?**

To honor Mike Ditka, who wore No. 89 as a Bears tight end, coached the team to the Super Bowl XX title, and died in October 2026 at 86.

**When did the Bears add the Ditka patch?**

October 18, 2026, at Atlanta, on what would have been Ditka's 87th birthday, according to NBC 5 Chicago.

**What do the 89 stickers on the Bears' helmets mean?**

They were the Bears' first tribute to Ditka, worn on the back of the helmets at Green Bay the week before the jersey patch debuted.

**Where is the 89 patch on the Bears' jersey?**

On the left chest, over the heart. On Thanksgiving it sat opposite the round John Madden patch.

**What is the Bears' record with the Ditka patch?**

6-0 through Thanksgiving: Atlanta, New England, Seattle, Tampa Bay, New Orleans and Detroit. Chicago was 3-2 before it.

**Did the Bears retire Mike Ditka's number?**

Yes. No. 89 was retired in 2013.

**What other memorial do the Bears wear?**

The GSH initials of founder George S. Halas, on the left sleeve of every Bears jersey since 1984, the season after Halas died.

**Did the Bears wear the 89 patch on Thanksgiving?**

Yes. The Bears' post-game photo of Rome Odunze shows both the 89 patch and the John Madden Thanksgiving patch on the white road jersey.

**Can you buy the Bears' Ditka patch?**

Replica 89 memorial patches are sold by Chicago-area retailers.

**Who won the Bears-Lions Thanksgiving game?**

The Bears, 39-35, on Caleb Williams' 6-yard touchdown pass to Odunze with three seconds left. What both teams wore is in our [Lions-Bears Thanksgiving uniform story](/stories/lions-bears-thanksgiving-what-they-wore-2026).
