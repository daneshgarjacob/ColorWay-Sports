**NHL teams are 13-20-2 at home in their new Hometown Remix jerseys. That is 13 wins, 20 regulation losses and 2 overtime or shootout losses.**

That works out to a .400 points percentage. In their regular home sweaters, NHL teams are 143-93-25, a .596 points percentage. We log what every NHL team wears in every game and match it to the final score, so we can see this before anyone else. Every number here runs through Monday, Nov. 16.

## Quick Answers

- **Hometown Remix record at home:** **13-20-2** in 35 games, a .400 points percentage.
- **Regular home sweater record:** **143-93-25** in 261 games, a .596 points percentage.
- **Same 25 teams, regular home sweaters:** **104-75-20**, a .573 points percentage. The gap holds even when you compare teams with themselves.
- **Scoring:** teams score 2.83 goals a game at home in the Remix and allow 3.23. In regular home sweaters they score 3.30 and allow 3.16.
- **On the road:** **2-0-1**. The Canadiens won at Toronto and Boston in their white Remix. Utah lost in overtime at Montreal.
- **Best Remix record:** the Blue Jackets, **2-2-0**, with the most wins. Seven teams are 1-0.
- **Worst Remix record:** the Bruins, **0-2-0** in brown, after going 8-1-0 in their regular black.
- **Teams that have worn it:** 25 of 32.
- **Our take (opinion):** 35 games is not enough to call a jersey cursed. But it is enough to stop calling it a coincidence if the gap is still this big at Christmas.

## What Are The Hometown Remix Jerseys?

The Hometown Remix is a new program from the NHL and Fanatics for the 2026-27 season. Every team got one new jersey built around its city. The league revealed all 32 on Monday, Nov. 2, and the first three were worn that night. We cover every design in our [guide to all 32 Hometown Remix jerseys](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-2026-27), and every debut date is in our [Hometown Remix schedule](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-jersey-schedule-debut-dates).

Most teams wear theirs at home. In our log, 35 of the 38 Remix games so far were home games.

## How We Count

Every record comes from our [NHL uniform tracker](/stories/nhl-uniform-tracker-2026-27). We log the sweater each team wore, confirm it from game photos, then pair it with the final score. A Remix game is any game where a team wore its Hometown Remix jersey. The Bruins call theirs the Varsity Sweater, and it counts.

NHL records are wins, regulation losses and overtime or shootout losses. Points percentage is points earned (2 for a win, 1 for an overtime or shootout loss) divided by points possible. A .500 points percentage is break-even. The full team-by-team numbers in every jersey are on our [uniform records page](/uniform-records).

Thirteen wins in 35 home games. In their regular home sweaters, NHL teams win more than half their home games. In the Remix, they win about 37% of them.

The fairest comparison is the bottom row. Those are the same 25 teams that have worn a Remix, playing at home in their regular sweaters. They are 104-75-20, a .573 points percentage. That is lower than the league-wide number, because a few weaker teams are in the group. It is still far above .400.

## Finding 2: The Goals Dried Up

At home in the Remix, teams score 2.83 goals a game. In their regular home sweaters they score 3.30. That is almost half a goal less every night.

The defense barely changed. Teams allow 3.23 goals a game in the Remix and 3.16 in their regular home sweaters. For the same 25 teams the defense is actually a little better in the Remix: they allow 3.30 a game at home in their regular sweaters. So the losing is about scoring, not about giving up more. The same 25 teams score 3.32 a game in their regular home sweaters and 2.83 in the Remix.

Three Remix teams were shut out at home: the Lightning (2-0 to Detroit), the Maple Leafs (4-0 to New Jersey) and the Mammoth, who lost to St. Louis in a shootout without scoring a goal.

## Big Caveats

- **The sample is small.** 35 home games, and 23 of the 25 teams have worn it at home only once or twice. One bad night swings a team's record from perfect to winless.
- **It is early.** The first Remix game was Nov. 2. Every Remix game so far came in a two-week stretch.
- **The opponents were good.** The teams that visited Remix home games have a combined .564 points percentage this season. That is above average, and it explains some of the gap.
- **The teams wearing it are a little below average.** The 25 Remix teams have a combined .541 points percentage this season, below the .573 they post in their regular home sweaters.
- **A jersey cannot score goals.** We are reporting a record, not a cause.

Even with all of that, a .400 points percentage against a .573 for the same teams in their normal sweaters is a big gap.

## Our Take

The Hometown Remix is a good idea. Some of the designs are among the best new sweaters in the league, and the Bruins' brown and the Ducks' green are the kind of jerseys fans actually want to buy. But teams are losing in them, and they are losing at home, where they should be strongest.

We do not think a jersey decides a hockey game. We do think fans notice a losing jersey fast, and teams notice what fans say. Until then, the honest read is simple: so far, these jerseys have been bad luck.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the NHL Hometown Remix jersey record?**

Through Nov. 16, NHL teams are 13-20-2 at home in their Hometown Remix jerseys. That is 13 wins, 20 regulation losses and 2 overtime or shootout losses, a .400 points percentage. On the road they are 2-0-1.

**What does 13-20-2 mean in the NHL?**

NHL records list wins first, then regulation losses, then losses in overtime or a shootout. So 13-20-2 means 13 wins, 20 regulation losses and 2 overtime or shootout losses. Teams get a point for an overtime or shootout loss.

**Are the NHL Hometown Remix jerseys bad luck?**

So far, yes. Teams have a .400 points percentage at home in them and a .573 points percentage at home in their regular sweaters. But it is only 35 games, the opponents have been above average, and a jersey cannot score goals.

**Which NHL team has the best Hometown Remix record?**

The Columbus Blue Jackets have the most wins at 2-2-0, including a 7-2 win over Colorado in the debut. Seven teams are 1-0-0: the Hurricanes, Kings, Avalanche, Predators, Capitals, Senators and Kraken.

**Which NHL team has the worst Hometown Remix record?**

The Boston Bruins are 0-2-0 in their brown Varsity Sweater, losing 5-2 to Montreal and 6-2 to Minnesota. Ten more teams are 0-1-0 at home in theirs.

**How do NHL teams do in their regular home sweaters?**

NHL teams are 143-93-25 at home in their regular home sweaters this season, a .596 points percentage. The 25 teams that have worn a Remix are 104-75-20 at home in their regular sweaters, a .573 points percentage.

**When do NHL teams wear the Hometown Remix jerseys?**

Mostly at home, on dates each team picks. The first Remix games were Nov. 2, and the Stars debut theirs Nov. 25. Every team's dates are in our [Hometown Remix schedule](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-jersey-schedule-debut-dates) and the [NHL uniform schedule](/stories/nhl-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

**How many NHL teams have worn the Hometown Remix?**

25 of the 32 teams, through Nov. 16. The Wild, Blues, Jets, Devils, Stars, Red Wings and Rangers have not worn theirs yet.

**Who makes the Hometown Remix jerseys?**

Fanatics designed them. The Hometown Remix is a league-wide program from the NHL and Fanatics, with one jersey for each of the 32 teams.

**Do NHL teams wear the Remix on the road?**

Rarely. Only three of the 38 Remix games so far were road games: the Canadiens at Toronto and Boston, and the Mammoth at Montreal. The road teams went 2-0-1.

**Why are NHL teams scoring less in the Remix?**

We do not know, and the sample is too small to say. Teams have scored 2.83 goals a game at home in the Remix and 3.30 in their regular home sweaters. They have allowed about the same in both.

**Which NHL teams wear white Hometown Remix jerseys?**

The Islanders, Canadiens, Avalanche and Stars. All four wear them mostly at home, which puts the home team in white. Read more in [why NHL teams wear dark jerseys at home](/stories/why-do-nhl-teams-wear-dark-jerseys-at-home).
