**Update, October 19, 2026: every jersey for every game is now set.** The NBA's official uniform schedule is out for all 30 clubs, a call for each of the 1,200 dated games, and every club page below has its full game-by-game table, a count of how many times each jersey is worn, and every City Edition date. Opening night is Tuesday, October 20.

## The 2026-27 NBA Uniform Schedule by the Numbers

- **The white Association jersey is still the league's most-worn uniform**, 815 team-games of 2,400, then the Icon (652), the Statement (567) and the City Edition (293). The Classic Edition gets 49 and the new [Specter Edition](/stories/nba-specter-edition-jerseys-2026-27) 24, three nights each for its eight teams.
- **The [Knicks](/stories/knicks-uniform-schedule-2026-27) wear white 40 times**, the most in the NBA, including 24 road games. The [Cavaliers](/stories/cavaliers-uniform-schedule-2026-27) are next at 38.
- **The [Rockets](/stories/rockets-uniform-schedule-2026-27) are the league's most color-heavy team**, 37 Icon Edition games, and the [Thunder](/stories/oklahoma-city-thunder-uniform-schedule-2026-27) wear their blue Icon 33 times.
- **Three teams build their season around an alternate:** the [Pacers](/stories/pacers-uniform-schedule-2026-27) wear their gold pinstriped Statement 31 times, the [Wizards](/stories/wizards-uniform-schedule-2026-27) wear their Statement 28 times and the [Pelicans](/stories/pelicans-uniform-schedule-2026-27) 25.
- **The [Heat](/stories/miami-heat-uniform-schedule-2026-27) wear their City Edition the most, 18 games**, with the [Wizards](/stories/wizards-uniform-schedule-2026-27) at 17. The [Rockets](/stories/rockets-uniform-schedule-2026-27) wear theirs only three times.
- **33 games are City Edition against City Edition**, and 11 are Classic against Classic.
- **The [Lakers](/stories/lakers-uniform-schedule-2026-27) wear white just 18 times**, 10 of them at home. The Icon Edition leads with 28.

**Update, September 18, 2026:** the Indiana Pacers published its own 2026-27 uniform schedule, the first club we have seen do it this year: 31 Statement games, 13 Association and 10 Icon. We counted every date, home and road, in the [Pacers uniform schedule](/stories/pacers-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

**Update, September 28, 2026:** the Brooklyn Nets went further and tagged all 80 games with a jersey on their own schedule page: 22 Association, 17 Statement, 16 Classic, 14 Icon and 11 City, with the Classic throwback on Opening Night. Every date is in the [Nets uniform schedule](/stories/nets-uniform-schedule-2026-27). The Milwaukee Bucks did the same in their schedule data: 29 Association, 21 Icon, 19 Statement and 11 City, all in the [Bucks uniform schedule](/stories/bucks-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

## How These Pages Work

Every club page carries the full closet and the scheduled jersey for all 80 dated games, from the NBA's official uniform schedule and the clubs' own announcements. Nothing on a club page is a guess, and once a game is played we flip the row to what was actually worn, the morning after. The [daily uniform tracker](/stories/nba-uniform-tracker-2026-27) collects every game from the night before in one place, and each team's [uniform calendar](/nba-tracker) shows its whole season at a glance.

It is the same format we run for [every NFL club](/stories/nfl-uniform-schedule-2026) and [every MLB club](/stories/mlb-uniform-schedule-2026), which are the two most-read things on this site.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where can I find an NBA team's uniform schedule for 2026-27?**

Every club has its own page, linked above. Each one lists the full closet for the season and the game-by-game record as it fills in.

**How many uniforms does an NBA team have?**

Four in a normal season: Association, Icon, Statement and City Edition, plus a Classic Edition in the years a club runs one.

**When are the 2026-27 NBA uniforms announced?**

The City Edition collection was revealed league-wide on September 15, 2026, and the full game-by-game uniform schedule for all 30 clubs was out by October 19, the day before opening night. Every date is on the club pages above.

**Which NBA team wears white the most in 2026-27?**

The New York Knicks, 40 Association Edition games, 16 at home and 24 on the road. The Cavaliers are second with 38 and the Jazz third with 36.

**How many City Edition games are there in the 2026-27 NBA season?**

293 team-games across all 30 clubs. The Heat wear theirs the most, 18 times, and 33 games are City Edition against City Edition.
