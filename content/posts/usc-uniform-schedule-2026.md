## The Bottom Line

Cardinal at home, white on the road, gold pants always. USC has one of the three or four best uniforms in the sport and the sense to leave it alone.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is USC wearing this week?**

USC wears cardinal at home and white on the road in 2026. The week-by-week grid above lists every game with the expected set, and we update it as combinations are confirmed.

**What are USC's uniforms for 2026?**

Cardinal jersey with gold numerals, gold pants, cardinal helmet. White jersey with cardinal numerals, gold pants, same helmet. Cardinal shell with the interlocking SC on the side and a gold stripe.

**Does USC have an alternate uniform in 2026?**

None announced for 2026. USC has resisted them about as firmly as anyone.

**How many games does USC play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
