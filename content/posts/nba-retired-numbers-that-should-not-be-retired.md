**A retired number should mean something.** It should mean a Hall of Famer, or a player so important to a franchise that nobody else should ever wear his number again. Lately, NBA teams hand them out for being a good teammate for a long time. These six banners are the ones we would take down.

This is my list, built from the players I actually watched. Every one of them was a real player, and most were fan favorites. None of them belongs in the rafters. Tell us we're wrong.

## Quick Answers

- **Which NBA retired numbers shouldn't be retired?** Our six: **Zydrunas Ilgauskas** (Cavaliers No. 11), **Tony Allen** (Grizzlies No. 9), **Nick Collison** (Thunder No. 4), **Shawn Marion** (Suns No. 31), **Andre Iguodala** (Warriors No. 9) and **Muggsy Bogues** (Hornets No. 1).
- **Are any of them in the Hall of Fame?** No. None of the six has been inducted as a player.
- **What is our standard?** Hall of Famers, plus the rare player who truly changed a franchise. A long, likable career is not enough.
- **What is the newest one?** Muggsy Bogues. Charlotte announced it on October 27, 2026, and the ceremony is February 11, 2027. [Full story here](/stories/hornets-retire-muggsy-bogues-number-2026).

## Why the NBA Hands Out Too Many

Retired numbers used to be rare. Now teams use them as a thank-you card: for loyalty, for a beloved era, for moving with the franchise. The cost is real. Every banner takes a number out of circulation forever, and every new one makes the old ones mean a little less. Save the rafters for the players who earned them.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which NBA team retired Nick Collison's number?**

The Oklahoma City Thunder retired his No. 4 on March 20, 2019. It was the first number the franchise retired after moving from Seattle.

**When did the Grizzlies retire Tony Allen's jersey?**

March 15, 2025, after a home game against the Miami Heat. His No. 9 joined Zach Randolph's No. 50 and Marc Gasol's No. 33.

**When did the Warriors retire Andre Iguodala's number?**

February 23, 2025, before a game against the Dallas Mavericks.

**When did the Suns retire Shawn Marion's jersey?**

In December 2023, the same season Phoenix retired Amar'e Stoudemire's No. 32.

**When did the Cavaliers retire Zydrunas Ilgauskas' number?**

March 8, 2014, at halftime of a home game. He was the seventh player in team history to have his number retired.

**Are any of these players in the Hall of Fame?**

No. None of the six has been inducted into the Naismith Basketball Hall of Fame as a player.

**Who decides when a team retires a number?**

The team does. There is no league rule or standard, which is exactly why the list keeps getting longer.
