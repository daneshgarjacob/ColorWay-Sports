The Milwaukee Brewers are World Series champions for the first time. They beat the Tampa Bay Rays 4-1 in Game 5 on Wednesday night, October 28, at Tropicana Field to win the Series 4-1, 57 seasons after the franchise moved to Milwaukee. Jacob Misiorowski threw 5 2/3 scoreless innings and William Contreras was named World Series MVP. They won it in the navy alternate, and every game's uniforms are in our [2026 World Series uniform guide](/stories/world-series-uniforms-2026).

Then came the gear. The players pulled on a black Fanatics locker room T-shirt and a black New Era locker room 9FORTY as soon as they got to the clubhouse, and the full champions collection went on sale the same night. My verdict on the locker room set: **a bit boring, but I like it.
