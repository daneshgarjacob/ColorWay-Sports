**The Lions wore Honolulu blue on Thanksgiving, not the throwback, and lost anyway: Bears 39, Lions 35.** Detroit played the Chicago Bears at Ford Field in the **silver helmet, Honolulu blue jersey and silver pants**, the look the team posted Thursday morning as its "Thanksgiving Threads." Every Lions helmet also carried a small **black "MFF" decal** for owner Martha Firestone Ford, who died this week. Chicago wore the **navy helmet, white jersey and navy pants**, with the **No. 89 patch** for Mike Ditka on the chest. Caleb Williams threw a 6-yard touchdown pass to Rome Odunze with **three seconds left** for the Bears' first lead of the day. Everything below is confirmed from the photos both teams posted during and after the game.

## Quick Answers

- **What did the Lions wear on Thanksgiving 2026?** Silver helmet with a Honolulu blue facemask, Honolulu blue jersey, silver pants with blue stripes. The standard home set.
- **Did the Lions wear throwbacks on Thanksgiving?** No. The 1934 throwback, worn on Thanksgiving in 2023 and 2025, stayed in the closet, and the team never announced it.
- **Did the Lions honor Martha Firestone Ford?** Yes. The team announced "MFF" helmet decals on November 25, and the decal is visible in Detroit's own final-score photo: a black circle reading MFF on the back of the helmet, next to the American flag. A jersey patch is planned from December 6 against Atlanta.
- **What did the Bears wear?** Navy helmet with the orange wishbone C, white jersey with navy and orange sleeve stripes and the GSH initials, navy pants with an orange stripe.
- **Did the Bears wear any patches?** Yes: the round John Madden Thanksgiving patch and a football-shaped No. 89 patch for Mike Ditka, both visible in the Bears' post-game photo of Odunze.
- **Final score?** Bears 39, Lions 35. Chicago is 9-2 and alone in first in the NFC North. Detroit is 8-3.
- **Our matchup grade:** B+, the grade we gave it before kickoff. It held up.

## Why No Throwback This Year

Fans asked for the logo-free 1934 throwback all week, and the Lions said no on Thursday morning. They never put it on the calendar: Detroit's two special uniforms this season already had dates. The **Rivalries Silver** set debuted against Minnesota on November 1, and the **black alternate** was worn against Green Bay in Week 7 and Tampa Bay last Sunday. Neither throwback nor alternate was assigned to Thanksgiving.

The superstition argument was there for anyone who wanted it. The Lions were **0-5 in the throwback on Thanksgiving** (2017, 2018, 2020, 2023 and 2025), a record we checked game by game in [the Lions' record in throwbacks on Thanksgiving](/stories/lions-record-in-throwbacks-on-thanksgiving). Leaving it in the closet did not fix anything.

## Detroit's Thanksgiving Record By Uniform, Updated

Here is the number nobody else has. Since the throwback arrived in 2017, counting Thursday:

| Uniform on Thanksgiving | Record since 2017 |
|---|---|
| 1934 throwback | **0-5** |
| Everything else (home blue, grey Color Rush, all blue) | **1-4** |
| **Total** | **1-9** |

The one win, 23-20 over Chicago in 2024, came in **all blue**: Honolulu blue jersey with Honolulu blue pants. Thursday was silver pants. In our own 2026 log the Lions are now **2-0 in blue pants and 2-1 in silver pants** with the Honolulu blue jersey, and 8-3 overall. If the uniform were the problem, Detroit would have its answer: the only look that has won on this holiday since 2017 is the one with blue pants. We do not think the pants lost this game. Detroit led by 11 with seven minutes left. But it is the only Thanksgiving uniform with a win, and nobody else is keeping this count.

## Did The Bears' Uniform Win?

Chicago is now **4-1 in white this season**, all on the road, with the white jersey and navy pants every time, per our [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026). The one loss was at Green Bay in Week 5. The Bears are 5-1 in navy at home. A 9-2 team wins in everything, but the road white has been as good as anything in the NFC North this season.

## The Game

Bears 39, Lions 35. Per ESPN and the AP, Jahmyr Gibbs ran for 205 yards and three touchdowns, including a 70-yard run that put Detroit up 35-24 with 8:20 left. Williams answered with a 41-yard touchdown to Odunze and ran in the two-point conversion, then won it with the 6-yarder with three seconds left after the Lions went for it on fourth down and missed. Williams threw for 345 yards and two touchdowns. Chicago became the third team to come back from 11 or more points down in the fourth quarter to win on Thanksgiving, per the AP, and the first since the 1980 Bears did it, also in Detroit.

## Our Take

The Honolulu blue and silver against Chicago's road white was the right look on paper and a great-looking game on TV: three strong colors and no overlap. We gave the matchup a **B+** before kickoff, and it held. It is not higher because the Lions still had the throwback in the closet on their one national holiday. The MFF decal was handled the right way: small, black, and only noticeable if you looked.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What did the Lions wear on Thanksgiving 2026?**

The silver helmet with the Honolulu blue facemask, the Honolulu blue home jersey and silver pants. The team posted it Thursday morning as its "Thanksgiving Threads."

**Did the Lions wear throwback uniforms on Thanksgiving 2026?**

No. Detroit wore its standard home uniform. The 1934 throwback, worn on Thanksgiving in 2023 and 2025, was not on the 2026 schedule.

**Why didn't the Lions wear throwbacks on Thanksgiving?**

The team never assigned the throwback to this game. Its special uniform dates this season went to the Rivalries Silver set (November 1) and the black alternate (Weeks 7 and 11). Detroit was also 0-5 in the throwback on Thanksgiving.

**What is the MFF decal on the Lions' helmets?**

A memorial decal for Martha Firestone Ford, the Lions' former owner, who died this week. It is a small black circle with "MFF" on the back of the helmet, next to the American flag. The Lions announced it on November 25.

**Will the Lions wear a Martha Firestone Ford patch?**

Yes. The team says a jersey patch debuts on Sunday, December 6, against the Atlanta Falcons.

**What did the Bears wear against the Lions on Thanksgiving?**

The navy helmet, the white road jersey with navy and orange stripes, and navy pants, with the John Madden patch and the No. 89 Mike Ditka patch on the chest.

**What is the 89 patch on the Bears' jerseys?**

A memorial for Mike Ditka, the Hall of Fame tight end and Super Bowl XX coach, who wore No. 89. The Bears added it in October after his death.

**Who won Lions vs. Bears on Thanksgiving 2026?**

The Bears, 39-35, on a 6-yard touchdown pass from Caleb Williams to Rome Odunze with three seconds left.

**What is the Lions' record on Thanksgiving in throwbacks?**

0-5 since 2017. In all other uniforms they are 1-4 over the same span after Thursday, so 1-9 overall.

**What uniform did the Lions last win a Thanksgiving game in?**

All blue: Honolulu blue jersey and Honolulu blue pants, 23-20 over the Bears in 2024.

**Did both teams wear the John Madden patch?**

Yes. The NFL put the Madden patch on every team's jersey for all three Thanksgiving games. It is visible on the Bears' post-game photos.
