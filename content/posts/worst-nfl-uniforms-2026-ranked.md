**Updated November 1, after Week 8: this is our new list.** These are the 10 worst uniforms in the NFL right now, the ones teams are wearing in 2026, counted down from 10 to the worst. Every entry has the team's own photo of the full uniform on the field or in a team shoot, so you can see exactly what we mean.

Six of the ten come from the NFL's Rivalries program, the new alternates that were supposed to be the fun part of the season. (Across all four leagues? See [the 10 worst uniforms in sports right now](/stories/worst-uniforms-in-sports-2026-ranked).)

## Quick Answers

- **What is the worst uniform in the NFL right now?** The Buffalo Bills' Nickel City gray alternate.
- **Which Rivalries uniforms made the list?** Six: the Vikings, Dolphins Dark Water, Colts Anvil Strike, 49ers For the Faithful, Packers and Jaguars Bold City.
- **What is the best thing on this list?** The helmets. The Bills' Charge, the Colts' metallic blue and the Jaguars' teal shell are all good. The uniforms under them are the problem.

## 10. Jacksonville Jaguars Bold City Rivalries

Jacksonville debuted Bold City today and beat the Colts 42-7, so the team will love it. The look is an alabaster jersey with a cursive Jaguars script outlined in gold, numbers printed with a jaguar-spot pattern, black spotted pants and a teal helmet with a gold facemask. The teal helmet is great. Below it, cream and gold read more like New Orleans than Jacksonville, and the spotted pants turn into noise on TV.

**Worn:** November 1 vs. the Colts, its debut. [Every Jaguars jersey, ranked](/stories/jaguars-jerseys-2026-ranked).

## 9. Green Bay Packers Rivalries

The Packers' Rivalries set is built around the team's fan ownership: a darker hunter green jersey, numbers modeled on paper currency, striping from old stock certificates, cream pants and a matte cream helmet. It is a great story you cannot see from your seat. From a distance it is a green jersey slightly darker than the one Green Bay already wears, and it benches the gold helmet with the G to get there.

**Worn:** October 11 vs. the Bears at Lambeau Field. [Every Packers jersey, ranked](/stories/packers-jerseys-2026-ranked).

## 8. San Francisco 49ers Black Rivalries ("For the Faithful")

All black from helmet to pants, with red numbers outlined in gold and a gold facemask. The numbers are good. The problem is the black. Scarlet and gold is one of the best color pairs in sports, and this turns it into trim on a black shirt. From the upper deck it could be the Falcons or the Cardinals.

**Worn:** last season against the Seahawks. Next: December 13 vs. the Rams. [Every 49ers jersey, ranked](/stories/49ers-jerseys-2026-ranked).

## 6. Los Angeles Chargers Charger Power Gold

The first yellow jersey in Chargers history, a nod to the Air Coryell era: solid gold with white numbers outlined in powder blue and powder blue bolts on the shoulders. Last season it was gold on gold from collar to cleats, and it looked like a costume. The Chargers already have the best uniform in the league in powder blue. They don't need this.

**Worn:** in 2025 with gold pants. Next: December 6 at Tampa Bay, with white pants. [Every Chargers jersey, ranked](/stories/chargers-jerseys-2026-ranked).

A dark anthracite gray jersey and pants with royal blue numbers and, for the first time, a silver horseshoe on the jersey itself, under a metallic blue helmet. The helmet is beautiful. Everything below it has nothing to do with Indianapolis. The Colts have never worn gray, the horseshoe disappears into the fabric, and a gray body under a blue helmet looks like the Lions.

**Worn:** September 27 vs. the Texans. [Every Colts jersey, ranked](/stories/colts-jerseys-2026-ranked).

## 4. Miami Dolphins Dark Water Rivalries

A near-black jersey with aqua numbers and an orange MIAMI wordmark, under a dark helmet with a 305 bumper. Miami has aqua, orange and white, the brightest colors in the league, and there is no black anywhere in them. Dark Water shrinks all of it down to trim. A South Florida team should not look like it plays at night in a warehouse.

**Next:** January 3 vs. the Bills, the last home game of the season. [Every Dolphins jersey, ranked](/stories/dolphins-jerseys-2026-ranked).

All black, with gold numbers and gold trim. It is Pittsburgh's colors, so it is not wrong, but it is the darkest uniform in the league and the gold is the only thing you can see. On a night game it is a black blob with numbers on it. The Steelers' regular black home jersey with gold pants already does this better.

A deep "Purple Dynasty" jersey with ivory and gold, chiseled rune-style numbers and wordmark, a Nordic knot pattern on the shoulders and pants, and a matte purple helmet with a gold facemask and SKOL in runes on the bumper. Four ideas fighting on top of one of the best base uniforms in the NFL. The duller purple goes flat under the lights, and none of the details get room to stand out.

## Why So Many Rivalries Uniforms?

Six of the ten are Rivalries sets, and they all make the same mistake: a strong local story that does not survive the trip to a TV screen. Darker versions of colors a team already owns, textures and patterns you only see in a studio photo, and details you have to read about to notice. The best uniforms in the league are simple and bright, and almost nothing on this list is either.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the worst uniform in the NFL in 2026?**

The Buffalo Bills' Nickel City alternate, in our ranking: a gray jersey and gray pants under the royal blue Charge helmet. It takes the brightest colors in the AFC and turns them gray.

**When do the Bills wear Nickel City?**

They wore it on September 27 against the Chargers, and it is scheduled again for Saturday, December 19, against the Bears.

**Did the Jaguars wear their Rivalries uniforms against the Colts?**

Yes. Jacksonville debuted the Bold City Rivalries set on November 1 against Indianapolis and won 42-7.

**When do the Vikings wear their Rivalries uniform?**

Sunday, December 20, on Sunday Night Football against the Detroit Lions.

**When do the Steelers wear the Color Rush?**

Pittsburgh wore the all-black Color Rush on Monday Night Football against the Dolphins in December 2025. For 2026 we project it for the Week 13 Sunday night game against the Texans.

**Why are the Rivalries uniforms so bad?**

Not all of them are, but the worst ones bury a team's best colors under darker shades and small details that disappear on TV. Six of the ten uniforms on this list are Rivalries sets.

**Is the 49ers' black uniform a Rivalries uniform?**

Yes. The all-black "For the Faithful" set is San Francisco's Rivalries uniform. It returns December 13 against the Rams.

## The Bottom Line

The worst uniforms in the NFL are not the old ones. They are the new ones. The Bills, Vikings and Dolphins each took one of the brightest identities in football and turned it dark or gray.
