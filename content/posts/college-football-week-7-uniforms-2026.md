Week 7 of the 2026 college football season is the best uniform week of the year so far, and most of the big looks were posted before Thursday night. **Cal wears a Grateful Dead helmet against No. 21 Wake Forest** for its Grateful Bears game, **Coastal Carolina goes full 1990s on Friday night against App State**, and **Oklahoma brings back its Bud Wilkinson era throwback against No. 15 Kentucky**, which is wearing a black helmet and black pants of its own. No. 11 Utah runs back its icy white set at Colorado, Stanford breaks out its throwback helmet for Reunion Homecoming, Ole Miss puts a Realtree camo helmet on against No. 13 Missouri, and Alabama and Tennessee meet in home colors for the first time since 1970. On top of that, No. 4 Ohio State visits No. 5 Indiana, No. 2 Notre Dame goes to No. 8 BYU and Florida State visits No. 3 Miami.

Below is every game this week involving an AP Top 25 team or one of the programs we track in our college uniform schedules, with kickoff time, TV and what each team is wearing. A look is marked **Confirmed** when the school itself has posted it, and **Expected** when it has not, in which case we give the set the program normally wears in that spot. Most schools post their combination between Monday and game day, and on Saturday we confirm the rest from the game broadcasts and team photos, then add final scores. The rankings below are from this week's AP poll. All times are Eastern. Last week's looks and final scores are in our [Week 6 uniform guide](/stories/college-football-week-6-uniforms-2026).

## Games of the Week

### No. 21 Wake Forest at Cal: The Grateful Bears Game

**Saturday, 10:30 p.m., ESPN2.** **Cal: Confirmed, Grateful Dead helmet.** Cal revealed the helmet on Monday night: a gold shell with the Cal script inside a Steal Your Face skull, a blue center stripe with a white lightning bolt, and the dancing bears across the white back bumper in blue-gray, pink, orange and green, with Oski in a CAL sweater in the middle. On Thursday Cal posted the rest of the set, and its own caption sums it up: the lids are yellow and the jerseys are blue. That is the blue jersey with gold numbers and gold pants, plus towels with a gold dancing bear in a Cal cap. Cal says the Grateful Bears game celebrates the band's 29 shows at the Hearst Greek Theatre on campus.

**Our take:** this is the best special helmet of the season so far, and the reason is restraint. Cal kept its own blue and gold and let the Dead show up in the details, the stealie, the bolt and the bears on the back. A tie-dye jersey would have been a costume. This is a uniform.

**Wake Forest: Confirmed**, gloss black helmet, white jersey, black pants, from the team's Week 7 threads post. More: [Cal schedule](/stories/cal-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Wake Forest schedule](/stories/wake-forest-uniform-schedule-2026).

### App State at Coastal Carolina: The '90s Night

**Friday, 8 p.m., ESPN2.** **Coastal Carolina: Confirmed, '90s uniform.** This is Coastal's annual "Wait 'Til You See This" game, and the school says the limited edition set was inspired by 1990s TV, naming Saved By The Bell, Boy Meets World, The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air and Friends. In the team's photos it is a white jersey with COASTAL in a teal graffiti script, teal numbers outlined in pink sitting on a hot pink triangle, and confetti, squiggles and triangles in pink, purple, lime and orange all over the front and back. It goes with a teal helmet and teal pants, with "Chants" in script on the back bumper. Coastal is selling game-worn jerseys after the game, and fans were asked to dress for the decade.

**Our take:** we love it, and it is a little funny. Coastal Carolina did not play football in the 1990s. The program played its first season in 2003 and moved up to FBS in 2017. So this is not a throwback to anything the Chanticleers wore. It is a tribute to a decade, the Trapper Keeper and the Saturday morning lineup, and it is exactly the kind of swing a Friday night on ESPN2 is for. Keeping teal on the helmet and pants stops it from looking like a costume.

**App State: Confirmed**, black helmet, black jersey, gold pants, from the team's Splash Warning post. App State spoiled the party, 35-32. More: [Coastal Carolina schedule](/stories/coastal-carolina-uniform-schedule-2026) and [App State schedule](/stories/appalachian-state-uniform-schedule-2026).

### No. 15 Kentucky at Oklahoma: Throwback vs. Black

**Saturday, 7:30 p.m., SEC Network.** **Oklahoma: Confirmed, Bud Wilkinson throwback.** Oklahoma listed Kentucky as its Throwback game when it released its 2026 game themes in July, and on Thursday it posted the look: a plain white helmet with a single crimson stripe and no logo, a crimson jersey with white numbers and striped sleeves, and white pants. It honors Oklahoma's look from 1946 to 1956, the Bud Wilkinson era, when the Sooners won three national titles.

**Kentucky: Confirmed, black helmet and black pants.** Kentucky posted a "new look for week seven" on Tuesday: a black helmet with the blue interlocking UK, the white jersey with KENTUCKY and the numbers in blue, and black pants with a blue and white side stripe and a blue UK on the hip. It is the third different helmet in three weeks for Kentucky, after the chrome shell at South Carolina and the Power K throwback against LSU.

**Our take:** two very different ideas on one field. Oklahoma's throwback is as plain as a uniform gets, and that is the point. Kentucky's black and white is sharp, but next to that crimson it will look like the modern team it is. **Kentucky 21, Oklahoma 13.** More: [Oklahoma schedule](/stories/oklahoma-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Kentucky schedule](/stories/kentucky-uniform-schedule-2026).

### No. 6 Alabama at No. 14 Tennessee: The Third Saturday in Full Color

**Saturday, 3:30 p.m., ABC.** **Both teams: Confirmed in home colors.** Alabama and Tennessee announced in August that both will wear home jerseys for the Third Saturday in October in 2026 and 2027, crimson against orange, the first color vs. color meeting in this rivalry since 1970. Tennessee is in its orange home set at Neyland Stadium, and Alabama is in the crimson helmet and crimson jersey it would normally save for Tuscaloosa. Tennessee called it "a historic look" in a Tuesday video. **Tennessee 26, Alabama 10.** Our full story: [Alabama and Tennessee go color vs. color](/stories/alabama-tennessee-color-vs-color-third-saturday-october-2026). More: [Alabama schedule](/stories/alabama-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Tennessee schedule](/stories/tennessee-uniform-schedule-2026).

### Elon at Stanford: Reunion Homecoming Throwback Helmet

**Saturday, 7:30 p.m. (4:30 p.m. Pacific), ACC Network Extra.** **Stanford: Confirmed, throwback helmet.** Stanford posted "Breaking out the throwback threads" on Thursday, and the photos match what it announced: a white helmet with a smaller block S, a single cardinal stripe down the middle and a gray facemask, with the regular cardinal home jersey and white pants. It is Reunion Homecoming, and the same small S and gray mask Stanford used in 2021 to honor Jim Plunkett and the 1970 and 1971 Rose Bowl teams.

**Our grade: A-.** The small block S and the gray mask are a great look, and Stanford's cardinal and white is one of the best color pairings in the sport. It stops short of an A because the throwback stops at the helmet. **Stanford 34, Elon 7.** Full breakdown on our [Stanford schedule](/stories/stanford-uniform-schedule-2026).

## The Bottom Line

This is the week college football remembered uniforms are supposed to be fun. Cal's Grateful Dead helmet is the best special helmet of the season so far because it keeps Cal's own blue and gold and lets the stealie, the bolt and the dancing bears do the talking. Coastal Carolina is honoring a decade it never played in, and we are fine with that on a Friday night. Oklahoma's Bud Wilkinson throwback against Kentucky's black helmet and black pants is the best contrast of styles on the board, Stanford's small S and gray mask earned an A- from us, and Alabama in crimson at Tennessee is a picture this rivalry has not had since 1970. The noon window went to the road teams in the big games: Ohio State wore its silver helmet and road whites and won 19-15 at Indiana, and Florida came out in the blue helmet, not the orange one its season schedule listed, and won 27-24 at Texas. Then the afternoon got wild. Auburn, in all white, beat No. 1 Georgia 27-24 in Athens, Tennessee won the color vs. color game 26-10, and at night Kentucky's black helmet and black pants beat Oklahoma's throwback 21-13 and BYU, in royal, beat No. 2 Notre Dame 28-24. The surprises on the uniform side were Colorado in gray against Utah and Southern Miss in gold pants for the first time this season. The West Coast games were still going at press time, and we will add those final scores when they end.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are college football teams wearing in Week 7?**

The specials, all posted by the schools, are Cal's Grateful Dead helmet against Wake Forest, Coastal Carolina's '90s uniform against App State, Oklahoma's Bud Wilkinson throwback against Kentucky, Kentucky's black helmet and black pants, Utah's icy white at Colorado, Stanford's throwback helmet against Elon, Ole Miss's Realtree helmet against Missouri and Clemson's purple Military Appreciation set. Alabama and Tennessee both wear home colors. Every game is listed above with what each team is wearing.

**What is Cal wearing against Wake Forest?**

Cal wears its Grateful Dead helmet for the Grateful Bears game on Saturday, October 17: a gold shell with the Cal script in a Steal Your Face skull, a lightning bolt center stripe and the dancing bears on the back bumper. It goes with the blue jersey and gold pants. Kickoff is 7:30 p.m. Pacific on ESPN2.

**Why is Cal wearing a Grateful Dead helmet?**

Cal says the Grateful Bears game celebrates the Grateful Dead's 29 shows at the Hearst Greek Theatre on the Berkeley campus, and the band's deep roots in the Bay Area.

**What is Coastal Carolina wearing against App State?**

Coastal Carolina wears a limited edition '90s uniform on Friday, October 16: a white jersey with a graffiti COASTAL wordmark, teal numbers outlined in pink and '90s confetti and triangle graphics, with a teal helmet and teal pants. The school says it was inspired by shows like Saved By The Bell, Boy Meets World, The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air and Friends.

**Did Coastal Carolina play football in the 1990s?**

No. Coastal Carolina played its first football season in 2003 and became a full FBS member in 2017, so the '90s uniform is a tribute to the decade, not a throwback to an old Coastal uniform.

**What is Oklahoma wearing against Kentucky?**

Oklahoma wears its Bud Wilkinson era throwback: a plain white helmet with a crimson stripe and no logo, a crimson jersey with white numbers and striped sleeves, and white pants. Kentucky wears a black helmet, white jersey and black pants. Kickoff is 7:30 p.m. Eastern on SEC Network.

**Are Alabama and Tennessee both wearing colored jerseys?**

Yes. The schools agreed to wear home jerseys for the Third Saturday in October in 2026 and 2027, so Alabama is in crimson at Tennessee, which is in orange. It is the first color vs. color meeting in the rivalry since 1970.

**What is Stanford wearing against Elon?**

Stanford wears its throwback helmet for Reunion Homecoming: white with a smaller block S, a single cardinal stripe and a gray facemask, with the cardinal home jersey and white pants. We graded it A-.

**What is Utah wearing against Colorado?**

Utah wears its icy white set for a second straight week: a white helmet with a frosty mountain graphic, a white jersey and white pants. Utah wore the same look in its 41-24 win over Kansas.

**Who is off in Week 7?**

No. 17 Iowa, No. 19 USC and No. 23 Boise State are off, along with Arizona, Illinois, Iowa State, Minnesota, NC State, South Carolina, UCF, UConn and several others.
