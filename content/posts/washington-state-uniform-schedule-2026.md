Washington State builds its uniforms the way you build a wardrobe, four helmet shells, four jersey colors, four pants colors, two facemask colors, and a new arrangement almost every Saturday. The Cougars have worn 53 different combinations since the 2017 redesign, and they added the newest one this week. This is the full Washington State 2026 uniform schedule: the crimson home set, the Icy White road look, the Ketchup Bottle combo, the gray throwback helmets, and a week by week outlook for all twelve games in Kirby Moore's first season. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

The reason Washington State can do this is the 2017 Nike redesign. Nike stripped the shoulder stripes, the number outlines and the chest wordmark off the 2012 jerseys and left a big block number and a WSU logo on the collar, which sounds like subtraction until you realize it means every top matches every bottom matches every helmet. Reviews were mixed at the time. Nine seasons later it is the most flexible closet on the West Coast.

The throwback history is the good part. The script Cougars helmet logo lived on the shells from 1976 through 1999, came back out for the Apple Cup wins of 2005, 2007 and 2008, and got a full turn back the clock treatment for the 2018 Apple Cup with a gray shell, crimson facemask and an all crimson uniform. Then in October 2024, against Hawaii, Washington State debuted something even older: a gray helmet carrying the WSC cougar in a freshman beanie, a mark drawn from the 1920s, 1930s and 1940s, rendered in crimson and white over crimson jerseys and gray pants. Nothing has been announced for 2026, but both of those lids exist, and the Cougars have plenty of dates left to use them.

**September 19 vs. Duquesne.** Announced: **white helmet, crimson jersey, crimson pants**, the combination Coug fans named the Ketchup Bottle. This one has a crimson facemask, which by Washington State's own bookkeeping makes it the 53rd distinct combination since the 2017 redesign.

## The Bottom Line

Two road games, two crimson and white looks, and the crimson jersey did not appear until the home opener. That is the shape of Washington State's 2026: white on the road, crimson at Gesa Field, and the gray and anthracite pieces held back for the dates that mean something. The Ketchup Bottle against Duquesne already counts as a new combination on a technicality, which tells you how finely the Cougars slice this. Homecoming against Boise State and Senior Day against Oregon State are the two we are watching hardest. The grid above has every game, and we add a star as each one is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Washington State wearing this week?**

The Ketchup Bottle: white helmet with a crimson facemask, crimson jersey and crimson pants, for the home opener against Duquesne at Gesa Field on Saturday, September 19, at 12:30 p.m. Pacific. The game is a crimson out. The week by week grid above lists every game with the expected set, and we update it as combinations are confirmed.

**What are Washington State's football uniforms for 2026?**

Crimson, white, gray and anthracite jerseys and pants, all interchangeable, with big block numerals, no chest wordmark and a WSU logo at the collar. Helmets come in crimson, white, anthracite and gray, each available with a white or a crimson facemask, and there are two throwback gray shells, one with the old Cougars script and one with the 1920s WSC beanie cougar. The system is Nike and dates to 2017.

**What did Washington State wear in the 2026 Apple Cup?**

A crimson helmet with a white facemask, a white jersey and crimson pants at Husky Stadium on Sunday, September 6. It is the same combination the Cougars wore in their 2024 Apple Cup win. Washington wore gold helmets, purple jerseys and gold pants and won 24-10.

**Does Washington State have a throwback uniform in 2026?**

Nothing has been announced yet. The two throwback helmets in the closet are the gray shell with the script Cougars logo that the program wore from 1976 to 1999, and the gray shell with the WSC cougar in a freshman beanie that debuted against Hawaii in October 2024. We will add either to this page the moment one is confirmed.

**What is the Ketchup Bottle uniform?**

A white helmet over a crimson jersey and crimson pants, named by Washington State fans for the obvious reason. It first appeared in the 2018 Alamo Bowl win over Iowa State, returned in 2021 against Portland State and was last worn in 2022 against Cal. The 2026 version swaps the white facemask for a crimson one.

**How many games does Washington State play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven at Gesa Field at Martin Stadium, five on the road, with a bye on November 7. Four non-conference games opened the season, the seven game Pac-12 round robin runs from October 3 through November 21, and the November 28 flex game against a Pac-12 opponent counts as a non-conference game. The Pac-12 championship game is Friday, December 4. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
