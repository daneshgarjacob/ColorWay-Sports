**There is real gold on every Notre Dame football helmet.** Not gold-colored paint, actual gold: Notre Dame says the paint carries 23.9-karat gold flakes, collected when the Golden Dome on the Main Building was regilded. The Irish wear it again on Saturday against SMU at Notre Dame Stadium, with the navy home jersey expected.

So why are Notre Dame's helmets painted with real gold? Because the helmet is meant to match the Dome, and for most of the last 60 years the program has treated that as literally as it can. Here is the full story: the plain helmet, the gold in the paint, the student managers who painted them by hand, and what changed in 2011.

## Quick Answers

- **Is there real gold on Notre Dame's helmets?** Yes. Notre Dame says the paint has 23.9-karat gold flakes in it, and that tradition continued after the painting process changed in 2011.
- **Where does the gold come from?** The flakes were collected when the Golden Dome was regilded. The Dome is covered in 23.9-karat gold leaf.
- **Why gold at all?** The helmet is meant to match the Dome. When Ara Parseghian rebuilt the uniform in 1964, TIME reported the helmet color was "keyed exactly to the Golden Dome."
- **Who paints them?** For decades, student managers painted them by hand, often late on the Friday night before a home game. Since 2011, an outside company, Hydro Graphics, has done the paint.
- **Do the managers still touch the helmets?** Yes. Every game week they inspect them, clean them and remove scuff marks.
- **Why is there no logo?** Notre Dame tried shamrocks (1959-62) and numbers (1963). Parseghian went to a plain gold helmet in 1964, and the shell has carried no logo, stripe or number since. (The ND only appears on the small bumper above the facemask.)
- **Our take (opinion):** it is the best helmet in college football. One color, no logo, and the only one with a building's worth of story painted into it.

## No Logo, No Stripe, No Number

Look at a Notre Dame helmet from the side and there is nothing on it. No logo, no stripe down the middle, no player number. Almost every other major program puts something on the shell. Notre Dame puts gold.

It was not always that way. Notre Dame's own athletics history lists **shamrocks on the helmets from 1959 to 1962** and **player numbers on the sides in 1963**. Then Ara Parseghian arrived in 1964. His first season brought new gold pants, plain blue jerseys and, in TIME's words that November, helmets "whose color was keyed exactly to the Golden Dome itself." The plain gold helmet has been the standard ever since. Some one-off Shamrock Series helmets have broken the rule, but the regular helmet is still bare.

## There Is Real Gold In The Paint

Notre Dame has said it for years: the paint on the game helmets contains **23.9-karat gold flakes**. Pure gold is 24 karat, so 23.9 is about as close as gold gets.

The gold is not a marketing line added later. In the managers' era, the paint was mixed on campus, and Notre Dame's history describes gold dust bought from the O'Brien Paint Company in South Bend being mixed with lacquer and lacquer thinner, plus gold leaf from the Dome added to the mix. When the process changed in 2011, Notre Dame said the 23.9-karat gold flakes would stay, and in 2018 it said the tradition was still going.

## The Gold Comes From The Golden Dome

The Dome on Notre Dame's Main Building is covered in real gold leaf, **23.9 karat** and about 3 microns thick, according to the university. It has to be redone every couple of decades. Notre Dame lists regildings in 1886, 1893, 1904, 1912, 1924, 1933, 1948, 1961, 1971, 1988 and 2005, and the most recent, in 2023, was the 12th.

The connection to the helmets is direct. Notre Dame Athletics wrote in 2011 that the gold flakes in the helmet paint were **collected when the Dome was regilded**. The university's own 2005 story on the regilding says the football players wear gold helmets "in deference to" the Dome.

What we could not confirm: whether flakes from the 2023 regilding have gone into the helmet paint since. Notre Dame's 2023 coverage of the regilding does not mention the helmets.

## Student Managers Painted Them By Hand

For decades, the helmets were painted by Notre Dame's student managers, the students who run the equipment side of the program. Notre Dame's own account of how it worked:

- Painting started **after the Friday pep rally** before home games and often ran **past midnight**.
- At the peak, **50 to 80 managers** took part. Facemasks were taped off, scuffs buffed out, a coat of gold paint went on, then a second coat of lacquer with the gold dust.
- Fans watched through the stadium concourse gates, usually 50 to 75 a game and as many as 250 before USC.
- Cold weather late in the season made drying hard, and by the end of a year a helmet could carry **as many as 12 coats**.
- The managers also painted before bowl games and on road trips.

Two changes ended it. In **2005**, the team started using separate practice and game helmets, which moved painting off Friday night to Monday through Wednesday. Then came 2011.

## 2011: The Managers Stopped Painting

Athletic director Jack Swarbrick was unhappy with the helmet color for a couple of seasons. Notre Dame's own 2011 story says the program tried **five different shades of gold** in one season while it hunted for the right one, and Swarbrick handed the job to head equipment manager Ryan Grooms.

The fix was **Hydro Graphics Inc.**, an Oregon company, which made more than 12 versions before Notre Dame settled on one. The new helmets debuted on **October 22, 2011**, the night game against USC, Notre Dame Stadium's first home night game since 1990. Notre Dame said the new color was closer to the Dome than any helmet it had worn and would stay the same from week to week and year to year.

The cost was the tradition. Notre Dame said the new process was too detailed to repeat each week, so **the student managers no longer paint the helmets**. They still inspect them, clean them and remove scuff marks every game week. **The 23.9-karat gold flakes stayed in the paint.** The decision was made by the football staff, Swarbrick and head coach Brian Kelly.

Helmets have moved on since. By 2018, Notre Dame said about 52 players wore Vicis helmets, with the gold finish done by Velocity Chrome, a Florida company that hand-paints and spray-paints them. Practice helmets are a base gray. We could not confirm which company finishes the 2026 helmets.

## Our Take

Plenty of programs have a great helmet. Notre Dame has the only one where the color is a building. It has no logo because it does not need one, and it has real gold in it because the people who made it decided the helmet should match the Dome exactly, then kept doing that for 60 years. Losing the managers' Friday-night paint shop in 2011 was a real loss, but keeping the flakes was the right call. **Best helmet in college football.**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why are Notre Dame helmets gold?**

They are meant to match the Golden Dome on the Main Building. When Ara Parseghian rebuilt the uniform in 1964, TIME reported the helmet color was keyed exactly to the Dome, and Notre Dame says the football players wear gold helmets in deference to it.

**Is there real gold on Notre Dame's football helmets?**

Yes. Notre Dame says the helmet paint contains 23.9-karat gold flakes. It confirmed the flakes stayed in 2011 when the painting moved to an outside company, and said the tradition was continuing in 2018.

**How many karats is the gold on Notre Dame's helmets?**

23.9 karat, according to Notre Dame. That matches the 23.9-karat gold leaf on the Golden Dome.

**Does the gold on Notre Dame's helmets come from the Golden Dome?**

Notre Dame Athletics said in 2011 that the gold flakes were collected when the Dome was regilded. Its 2018 history also says gold leaf from the Dome was part of the paint for decades. We could not confirm whether flakes from the 2023 regilding have been used since.

**Do student managers still paint Notre Dame's helmets?**

No. Student managers painted them by hand for decades, usually after the Friday pep rally before home games. That ended in 2011, when Notre Dame moved to a more complex paint process. Managers still inspect, clean and remove scuffs from the helmets every game week.

**Who paints Notre Dame's helmets now?**

In 2011 the paint moved to Hydro Graphics Inc., an Oregon company. By 2018, Notre Dame said Velocity Chrome, a Florida company, did the finish on its Vicis helmets. We could not confirm the current vendor for 2026.

**When did Notre Dame change how its helmets are painted?**

In 2011. The new helmets debuted on October 22, 2011, in the night game against USC at Notre Dame Stadium. Earlier, in 2005, a separate set of practice helmets moved painting off Friday nights to early in the week.

**Why did the student managers stop painting the helmets?**

Athletic director Jack Swarbrick wanted a color that matched the Dome and stayed the same every week. The new process was too detailed for the managers to repeat weekly, so the staff picked color accuracy over the tradition.

**How many coats of paint were on a Notre Dame helmet?**

In the managers' era, a helmet could carry as many as 12 coats by the end of a season, according to Notre Dame.

**Why is there no logo on Notre Dame's helmet?**

Notre Dame tried shamrock decals from 1959 to 1962 and numbers in 1963. Ara Parseghian went to a plain gold helmet in 1964, and the shell has had no logo, stripe or number since. The ND monogram appears only on the small bumper above the facemask.

**Did Notre Dame ever have numbers on its helmets?**

Yes, in 1963, according to Notre Dame's athletics history. They were gone when Parseghian's plain gold helmet arrived in 1964.

**How often is the Golden Dome regilded?**

Roughly every one to two decades. The 2023 project was the 12th regilding, and the one before it was in 2005.

**What kind of gold is on the Golden Dome?**

23.9-karat gold leaf, about 3 microns thick, according to Notre Dame. The 2005 project covered about 3,500 square feet.

**Does Notre Dame wear the gold helmet with the green jersey?**

Yes. On October 10, 2026, against Stanford, Notre Dame wore a green jersey and green pants with the gold helmet and won 58-7.

**What will Notre Dame wear against SMU?**

The navy home jersey with the gold helmet and gold pants is expected for Saturday, November 21, at Notre Dame Stadium. Updates are on the [Notre Dame 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/notre-dame-uniform-schedule-2026).

**What are Notre Dame's colors?**

Blue and gold. Notre Dame says its original colors were yellow and blue, and gold and blue became official after the Dome and the statue of Mary were gilded. Green is an alternate.
