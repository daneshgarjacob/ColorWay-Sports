**The Ravens went all black on Monday night, and on their own field it was just no contrast.** Baltimore wore its **Darkness Falls** uniform against the Los Angeles Chargers at M&T Bank Stadium: the matte black helmet with the red-eyed raven, a black jersey with white numbers, and black pants. The field was dressed to match, with black end zones and a dark midfield logo. On TV, the uniform and the turf blended together. The Chargers, in white jerseys over powder blue pants, were the best-looking thing in the building, except for one detail we've never liked: the gold facemask. Final: Chargers 23, Ravens 20.

## What The Ravens Wore: Darkness Falls

This was the third and last of Baltimore's 2026 theme nights, and the first regular-season game for the Darkness helmet from **The Next Flight**, the collection the Ravens revealed in April. Here is every piece, as the team showed it on Monday:

- **The helmet.** Matte black, with the new front-facing raven head on the side and its glowing red eyes, a nod to the red eyes above the M&T Bank Stadium tunnel.
- **The jersey.** Black, with white numbers and a dark purple-and-black shield patch on the chest.
- **The pants.** Black, so the look runs black from the helmet to the cleats.
- **The field.** Black paint in the end zones and a dark field logo, so the stadium was themed along with the team.

The team's helmet photos carried a line from Edgar Allan Poe's "The Raven," which gave the franchise its name: "Darkness there and nothing more."

## Our Take On Darkness Falls: Just No Contrast

We liked this uniform on paper. When the Ravens revealed it in April we gave it an A-, the best grade in The Next Flight, and the helmet is still a great piece of design up close. The red eyes are the kind of detail you only get from a team that actually thought about where it plays.

The problem is what happened when they put it on the field. A black uniform needs something to stand against. On Monday it had black end zones and a dark logo at midfield, and the camera couldn't separate one from the other. Every time Baltimore had the ball near the goal line, the players were black shapes on a black background. The white numbers were the only thing doing any work. The purple, which is the whole identity of this franchise, was down to a patch on the chest you'd only see in a close-up.

Blackout uniforms are a look-at-it-in-the-locker-room idea. The photos the team posted are great. The broadcast was not. **Grade: C.** Wear it on the road, or wear it at home on a normal green field, and it moves right back up.

Los Angeles published this call back on September 1, and it showed up exactly as planned:

- **The helmet.** White, with the gold bolt, the numbers on the side, and the gold facemask.
- **The jersey.** White, with powder blue numbers.
- **The pants.** Powder blue.

White over powder blue is the road look built entirely from the Chargers' two best colors, and it's one of our favorite combinations in the league. Powder blue is the best color in the NFL. Against an all-black opponent, it was the only light, bright thing on the field all night.

## Our Take On The Chargers: Great Uniform, Wrong Facemask

We love this uniform from the shoulders down. The one thing we've never liked is the gold facemask.

The Chargers made the gold facemask full time in 2019, when they moved powder blue back to the primary home jersey, and the 2020 rebrand kept it on the white helmet. We've disliked it ever since. On a white shell, gold is hard to see from the broadcast angle, so it reads as a faint yellow smudge in front of the face instead of a clean outline. And it's the only gold on the uniform tonight. Nothing else on a white jersey with powder blue numbers and powder blue pants picks it up, so it looks a little weird, like a piece from a different set.

**Grade: B+.** Everything except the facemask is an A.

## The Fix: A White Or Gray Facemask

Make it something mute. A **white** facemask on the white helmet would let the gold bolt be the only gold on the shell, and the whole helmet would read clean and bright with the powder blue numbers. A **gray** facemask would do the same job with a little more definition around the face. Either one lets the bolt and the powder blue do the talking, which is what they're good at.

It isn't a new idea for this franchise. The Chargers' own uniform history says that when they added a white helmet in 2000, it came with a **white facemask**. We made the bigger case for how we'd rebuild the Chargers' look in [Jake's Fix No. 1: Make Super Chargers the full-time uniform](/stories/chargers-should-make-super-chargers-full-time). The facemask is the smaller fix, and it's the easiest one in the league to make.

## The Matchup Grade

The two teams contrasted with each other fine. Black against white and powder blue is about as far apart as two uniforms get. What hurt this game was the Ravens against their own field, not against the Chargers. **Matchup grade: B.**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What did the Ravens wear against the Chargers on Monday Night Football?**

The Darkness Falls uniform: a matte black helmet with the red-eyed raven head, a black jersey with white numbers and a dark chest shield patch, and black pants.

**What did the Chargers wear against the Ravens?**

A white jersey with powder blue numbers, powder blue pants, and the white helmet with the gold facemask, the combination the team announced on September 1.

**Who won Chargers vs. Ravens on Monday Night Football?**

The Chargers won 23-20 in Baltimore.

**Why were the Ravens' end zones black?**

The field was painted for the Darkness Falls theme night, with black end zones and a dark field logo to match the all-black uniform.

**What is Darkness Falls?**

The Ravens' all-black theme game. In 2026 it uses the black uniform and the matte black Darkness helmet from The Next Flight, the collection Baltimore revealed in April.

**Why do the Ravens' helmet eyes glow red?**

The red eyes on the Darkness helmet's raven reference the red eyes above the player tunnel at M&T Bank Stadium.

**When did the Chargers start wearing a gold facemask?**

The gold facemask went full time in 2019, when the Chargers made powder blue their primary home jersey. Before that it was used with their Color Rush uniforms starting in 2016, and the team first wore a gold facemask in the 1970s. The 2020 rebrand kept it.

**Have the Chargers ever worn a white facemask?**

Yes. According to the team's uniform history, the white helmet the Chargers added in 2000 had a white facemask.

**What should the Chargers' facemask be?**

Our take: white or gray. Something mute that lets the gold bolt and the powder blue stand out.

**When do the Ravens wear black again?**

The black jersey is scheduled for Week 14 against Tampa Bay and Week 18 against Pittsburgh, both at home. Baltimore hasn't said which helmet goes with it.
