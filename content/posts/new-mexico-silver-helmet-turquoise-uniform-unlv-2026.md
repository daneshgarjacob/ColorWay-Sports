**New Mexico is going silver.** On Wednesday, November 11, the Lobos posted "New lids for this weekend" with three photos of a brand-new **silver helmet**: a red **Lobos** script on the side, a red facemask and a **turquoise and red diamond-pattern stripe** running down the back. A day earlier, the team said it is **back in turquoise** for Saturday's home game against UNLV, so the new helmet debuts with the turquoise uniform.

Our grade: **B+**. I love teams that use silver, and the turquoise design on the back of the helmet is the best part. I like this a lot.

## Quick Answers

- **What did New Mexico unveil?** A new silver helmet with a red Lobos script, a red facemask and a turquoise and red diamond-pattern center stripe, posted November 11, 2026.
- **When do the Lobos wear it?** Saturday, November 14 at home against UNLV, per the team ("New lids for this weekend"). Kickoff is 7:30 p.m. Eastern (5:30 p.m. Mountain) on The CW.
- **What jersey goes with it?** The **turquoise** uniform. New Mexico posted "Back in Turquoise Saturday at home" on November 10.
- **Our grade:** **B+**.

## What The Silver Helmet Looks Like

Everything here comes from New Mexico's photos.

- **Shell:** silver, with a sparkle finish under the lights.
- **Side:** **"Lobos" in red script**.
- **Facemask:** red.
- **Center stripe:** a **turquoise and red diamond pattern** running front to back, outlined in white and red. From behind it is the boldest thing on the helmet.
- **Back:** an American flag decal and a New Mexico state flag decal on one side, a **turquoise Mountain West** decal on the other, and **"GRIT"** in red on the rear bumper.
- **Visor clip:** turquoise.

## The Turquoise Uniform Is Back

The helmet pairs with New Mexico's turquoise jersey, which has white **LOBOS** lettering and red and white trim in the team's video. This is not the turquoise set's first appearance:

- **December 2025:** New Mexico wore turquoise "with purpose" around the Rate Bowl, per a December 26, 2025 team post.
- **October 24, 2026:** "Turquoise Time in the 505," a home game in Albuquerque.
- **November 14, 2026:** back in turquoise against UNLV, now with the silver helmet.

## Our Grade: B+

I love people that use silver. It is one of the most underused colors in college football, and it makes the red Lobos script and the turquoise stripe stand out more than a red or white helmet would. The turquoise design on the back is the detail that makes it: it feels like New Mexico and nobody else. It is a uniform only New Mexico could wear. **B+**.
