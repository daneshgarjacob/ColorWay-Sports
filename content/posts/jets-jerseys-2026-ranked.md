The Jets have four jerseys on the field in 2026, and three of them lean on history: the Legacy Green and Legacy White sets built on the Sack Exchange era of 1979 to 1989, the Classic throwback that nods to the 1968 champions, and the Gotham City Football Rivalries jersey. The biggest change this year is not a jersey at all. It is the new all-white helmet that turns the Legacy White set into the White Out for the home opener. We put New York fourteenth in our [best NFL uniforms of 2026](/stories/best-nfl-uniforms-2026-ranked) at a B+, and almost nobody else in the league is wearing green, which is a head start.

Here are all four New York Jets jerseys for 2026, ranked worst to first and graded, with the helmet and pants each one wears. For the week-by-week jersey schedule 2026, the [Jets 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/jets-uniform-schedule-2026) has every game.

Gotham City Football is the Jets' entry in the NFL x Nike Rivalries program, a dark green jersey with UCLA-style shoulder stripes and gothic numbers, worn under a black gloss helmet with an italic JETS wordmark. It debuted in December 2025 with the AFC East wave and has one date in 2026: the home finale against the New England Patriots on Sunday, December 27.

The shoulder stripes are the best Jets stripes in decades, a real piece of the franchise's 1960s and 1970s look, and the black helmet is a genuine idea rather than a color swap. The trouble is the base. The green is pushed so dark that under stadium lights it reads swamp rather than Jets green, and the comic book theme is doing work a franchise with a good nickname does not need.

It grades a C+, the same mark we gave it in our [Rivalries uniforms ranking](/stories/nfl-rivalries-uniforms-2026-ranked), where it placed eighth of sixteen. Put those stripes and that helmet on a Legacy Green body and it is a B.

The Classic is a white jersey worn with white pants, a tribute to what the Jets wore against Baltimore in Super Bowl III. The front is clean with no patch, the number and letter fonts come from the 1968 set, and the green trim is Legacy Green. The helmet is the throwback white shell with the green and white center stripe, a gray facemask copied from 1968, and the oval logo the team used from 1998 to 2018. It is confirmed for one game: the First Responders Appreciation game against the Miami Dolphins on Sunday, October 25.

The helmet is the star here. A gray cage on a white shell with a center stripe is pure 1960s football, and nothing else in the Jets closet looks like it. The jersey underneath is the weaker half. It is handsome and correct, but it is the second white jersey in a four-jersey closet, and with a plain front and white pants, the green has very little space to work with.

It grades a B. Pairing it with a divisional game against Miami is the right call, and a sunny October afternoon is where a white throwback looks its best.

Legacy White is the road jersey, with green numbers and the double shoulder stripes the team wore from 1979 to 1989. It opened the season at Tennessee under the green helmet with green pants, and the Jets won 23-10. It projects to all eight road games where the host wears color: at the Titans, Lions, Bears, Patriots, Chiefs, Chargers and Dolphins, and the Week 18 trip to Buffalo. The one road exception is Week 15 at Arizona, where the Cardinals wear white at home and the Jets switch to green.

This jersey is also the base of the White Out. For the home opener against the Green Bay Packers on Sunday, September 20, New York pairs it with a brand-new white helmet with a white facemask and white pants. It is the first time the Jets have put a white helmet on their primary white set, and with the Packers confirmed in green and gold, it should be one of the cleanest pictures of Week 2.

It grades a B+. The double stripes are the right stripes for this franchise, and the green numbers read from anywhere. With green pants it is the sharpest road look the Jets have had in years. The White Out is a strong idea, but that is the helmet doing the work, not a new jersey.

Legacy Green is the home jersey, with white numbers and the same Sack Exchange double shoulder stripes. It projects to the five home games not claimed by an alternate: the Cleveland Browns in Week 5, the Las Vegas Raiders in Week 8, the Buffalo Bills in Week 10, the Denver Broncos in Week 14 and the Minnesota Vikings in Week 17. It also goes on the road once, in Week 15 at Arizona. None of those is confirmed by the team, so treat them as our projection.

The color is the whole argument. Nobody else in the NFL wears this shade of green, and in an AFC full of blue, red and navy it reads instantly on a broadcast. The double stripes give the shoulders structure without clutter, and the green shell with the jet-over-JETS logo from the 1980s ties the whole thing together. The white collar is the one piece we go back and forth on. It is a nice period detail that occasionally reads loud.

It grades an A-, the top mark in the closet. This is the jersey that makes the Jets look like the Jets, and it is the one the Gotham City set should have been built on.

## The Bottom Line on the Jets Jersey Rankings

The Jets jerseys rank by how much green they are allowed to show. Legacy Green is the identity and grades an A-. Legacy White is a strong B+ with the right stripes, the Classic is a B carried by its helmet, and Gotham City is a C+ that buries good stripes under a green too dark to read.

The helmet is the real 2026 story. Between the green shell, the new all-white shell and the throwback white with the center stripe, New York has more variety up top than almost any team in the league.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jerseys do the Jets have in 2026?**

Four: Legacy Green at home, Legacy White on the road, the Classic throwback, and the Gotham City Football Rivalries jersey. The White Out is the Legacy White jersey with the new white helmet and white pants, not a separate jersey.

**What is the best Jets jersey?**

We rank Legacy Green first and grade it an A-. No other NFL team wears that shade, and the Sack Exchange double stripes give it a clean, confident shoulder.

**What jersey are the Jets wearing this week?**

Week 2 is at home against the Green Bay Packers on Sunday, September 20 at 1:00 p.m. ET, and the Jets have confirmed the White Out: the new white helmet with a white facemask, the white jersey and white pants. **Do the Jets have a new jersey in 2026?**

No. All four jerseys are returning. The new piece is the all-white alternate helmet with a white facemask, which debuts with the White Out in Week 2.

**When do the Jets wear the Classic uniform in 2026?**

Once, at home against the Miami Dolphins on Sunday, October 25, the team's First Responders Appreciation game. It is the white jersey and white pants with the throwback white helmet and gray facemask.

**When do the Jets wear the Gotham City jersey in 2026?**

Once, in the home finale against the New England Patriots on Sunday, December 27. Rivalries uniforms are worn at home against a division rival.
