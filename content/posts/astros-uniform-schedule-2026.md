The Houston Astros carry five uniforms in 2026, and while their rotation isn't as rigidly scheduled as a team like the Phillies, there's a clear logic to it once you know the anchors. The one hard rule lives at home: the new white "Stros" City Connect owns Monday home games at Daikin Park, full stop. Around that, the orange and navy alternates tend to claim their own nights, and the whites and grays handle the day-to-day work. Here's the full Astros closet for 2026 and exactly when each jersey comes out.

The Astros carry five uniforms in 2026, and the set is the same one they settled into for 2025 after a notable change: the beloved navy "Space City" City Connect was retired and an all-new white "Stros" City Connect took its place. Everything else — the navy-and-orange home, road, and alternate looks that date to the 2013 rebrand — carries straight over. It's a closet that knows exactly what it is, anchored by a brand identity Houston has run cleanly for more than a decade.

## Home Whites (Most Home Games)

The home white is Houston's foundation: a clean white jersey with orange and navy trim, "Astros" arched across the chest in orange script with the H-and-star logo tucked into the wordmark, and the navy cap with the orange-outlined "H-star." It's worn for the bulk of every homestand — essentially any home game that isn't a Monday (which belongs to the City Connect) or a day the team reaches for the navy alternate. It's the most recognizable look the franchise owns, and the Astros are smart to leave it almost entirely alone. The navy-orange-white scheme has aged beautifully since 2013, and a classic home flannel doesn't need tinkering.

## Road Grays (Most Away Games)

On the road, the Astros wear the standard gray set with "HOUSTON" across the chest rather than the team name — the city, not the club, the way most road sets are built — in navy lettering with orange trim, names on the back, and the navy cap with the orange brim that's reserved for the road. It's the workmanlike default for most away games, the look that lets the home team's colors be the star. Nothing flashy, and it doesn't need to be.

## Orange Alternate (Frequent Friday Look)

The orange alternate is the splash of color in the rotation: a vivid orange jersey with navy and white piping, "Astros" across the chest, paired with white pants and the road-style navy cap with the orange brim. Unlike a strict day-of-week jersey, it's a true alternate that travels — the Astros wear it both at home and on the road — but it's leaned on heavily for Friday games, giving the end of the week its own jolt of Houston orange. It's the boldest everyday top in the closet and a direct line back to the team's brightest color.

## Navy Alternate (Customary Sunday Jersey)

The navy alternate is the dark counterpart: a navy-blue jersey with "Astros" stitched across the chest in orange — a nod to the 1970s, when the team last wore the club name in orange on a colored top — with white or gray pants and the navy "H-star" cap. In practice it's become Houston's go-to Sunday look at Daikin Park, the deep-navy bookend to a week that opens in the white City Connect. It's the most understated of the three colored options and a clean, heritage-tinged alternate that rounds out the navy-and-orange identity.

## City Connect — The White "Stros" Kit (Monday Home Games)

The newest piece, unveiled in March 2025, is a complete reinvention of Houston's City Connect program. Gone is the all-navy "Space City" design; in its place is an almost entirely white uniform with "Stros" across the chest — the first time the organization has formally embraced the fan-driven nickname. A broken orange star anchors the front, with digital-style navy lettering and numbers placed to the lower right of it, lunar-pattern trim and pinstriping running through the details, and "Space City" set on a moon-surface texture along the back of the collar. "HTX" is stitched along the beltline, a Union Station mission patch sits on the sleeve, and the only non-white elements on the body are the "afterburner" socks — a fiery orange-and-yellow gradient that recalls the classic tequila-sunrise rainbow. It debuted on March 31, 2025, and it now owns every Monday home game. Tying the loudest, most place-specific jersey in the closet to a guaranteed weekly slot is a smart move — fans know exactly when to come see it.

## What Happened to the Navy "Space City" City Connect?

Retired. The all-navy "Space City" City Connect — the one with "SPACE CITY" stenciled in NASA-worm-style orange and the rainbow gradient piping, first worn in 2022 — was one of the most popular alternates in the sport, but Houston shelved it after the 2024 season to make room for the new white "Stros" design. If you're looking for the navy City Connect in 2026, you won't find it on the field anymore; the navy you'll see is the regular navy alternate, which is a different jersey entirely.

## How the Astros Rotation Actually Works

Be honest about it: Houston's system is looser than the truly scheduled teams. The Phillies and [Marlins](/stories/marlins-uniform-schedule-2026) treat their closets like a published timetable; the Astros really only have one hard anchor — **the white "Stros" City Connect on Monday home games** — plus two soft habits, the orange alternate as a frequent Friday look and the navy alternate as the customary Sunday jersey. Everything else is the default: home whites for the rest of the homestand, road grays for the rest of the trip, with the orange top free to travel whenever the staff wants extra color.

It still works, because the anchor is the part fans care about. The City Connect is the jersey people buy tickets and merch to see, and giving it a standing Monday slot builds anticipation even without a rigid system everywhere else. The whites and grays are happy to do the quiet work in between, and the orange and navy alternates keep the week from ever feeling monochrome.

One more constant: the entire rotation runs on the navy, orange, and white identity Houston adopted in its 2013 rebrand, when the franchise returned to its traditional colors and the "H-star" logo. More than a decade later, it's one of the most cohesive brand packages in baseball — every jersey in the closet, City Connect included, reads instantly as the Astros.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many uniforms do the Astros have in 2026?**
Five: the home white, the road gray, the orange alternate, the navy alternate, and the white "Stros" City Connect. The set carried over from 2025, when the old navy "Space City" City Connect was retired and the new white version took its place.

**When do the Astros wear the City Connect uniform?**
The white "Stros" City Connect is Houston's every-Monday home jersey at Daikin Park. It debuted on March 31, 2025, and replaced the navy "Space City" design that the team wore from 2022 through 2024.

**What happened to the navy "Space City" City Connect?**
It was retired after the 2024 season. The all-navy kit with "SPACE CITY" across the chest and rainbow-gradient piping was swapped out for the new white "Stros" City Connect, so the navy version is no longer worn.

**When do the Astros wear the orange jersey?**
The orange alternate is a true alternate that the Astros wear both at home and on the road, and it's leaned on most often for Friday games. It's the brightest everyday top in the rotation.

**When do the Astros wear the navy alternate jersey?**
The navy alternate — navy with "Astros" in orange across the chest — has become Houston's customary Sunday jersey at home. It's a different uniform from the retired navy "Space City" City Connect.

**Do the Astros have a strict uniform rotation like the Phillies?**
No. Outside of the City Connect on Monday home games, the Astros pick their jerseys with more flexibility — home whites and road grays handle most days, with the orange and navy alternates floating into their customary Friday and Sunday slots rather than following a rigid published schedule.
