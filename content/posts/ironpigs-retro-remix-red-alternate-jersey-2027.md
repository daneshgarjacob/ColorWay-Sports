**The IronPigs have a new red alternate.** The **Lehigh Valley IronPigs**, the Triple-A affiliate of the Philadelphia Phillies, added the **"Retro Remix"** to their Authentic Collection on Monday, November 16. It is a **red jersey with navy blue piping**, and it joins the **regular rotation starting in 2027**.

The set is built out of the team's **Friday Fauxbacks**, the cream 1950s-style uniforms the IronPigs introduced in 2025. Same script, same cartoon pig, new color.

You can see the team's own photo of the jersey and cap on [the IronPigs' announcement](https://www.mlb.com/milb/lehigh-valley/news/retro-remix-uniform-added-to-ironpigs-authentic-collection).

## Quick Answers

- **What is the IronPigs Retro Remix jersey?** A red alternate jersey with navy piping, a Lehigh Valley script wordmark, a pig sleeve patch and a matching red cap with a navy brim.
- **When will the IronPigs wear it?** In regular rotation as an alternate starting with the 2027 season. The team has not tied it to a specific day of the week.
- **Where does the design come from?** The Friday Fauxback uniforms the IronPigs added in 2025.
- **Can you buy it?** Yes. The team says a full line of Retro Remix merchandise is at the IronPigs Clubhouse Store and online.
- **Who are the IronPigs affiliated with?** The Philadelphia Phillies.

## What The Retro Remix Looks Like

Every detail here comes from the IronPigs' announcement.

**The jersey.** An **IronPigs Retro Red** base with **navy blue piping up the front** and **navy blue piping on the sleeves**.

**The wordmark.** **"Lehigh Valley"** in an **arcing script**, the same font as the Friday Fauxbacks, finished with a **curled pig tail** at the end of the word.

**The sleeve patch.** The **Fauxback swinging pig**, shown as a head, sits in a patch on the **right sleeve**.

**The cap.** A **red crown with a navy blue brim**. The logo is the **full-bodied pig, legs crossed, leaning on a bat**.

The IronPigs say the Fauxbacks have become fan favorites since they arrived. The team created them because a club founded in 2008 has no real throwbacks to wear, so it imagined what the IronPigs might have looked like in the 1950s.

## Our Take

This is a sensible move more than an exciting one. The Fauxbacks worked, so the IronPigs took the best parts of them, the script, the pig tail and the cartoon pig, and put them on a red jersey that can be worn any night instead of only on Fridays.

The best detail is the cap. A red crown with a navy brim is a classic baseball combination, and a pig leaning on a bat with its legs crossed is a better logo than most big league alternates have. Putting "Lehigh Valley" on the chest instead of "IronPigs" is also the right call for a road-style look.

The weak spot is that it is a recolor. Red with navy piping is the kind of uniform a lot of teams already own, and the new pieces here are small. Fans who already have a Fauxback may not see much reason to buy a second one. **Grade: B.**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What does the IronPigs Retro Remix jersey look like?**

A red jersey with navy blue piping down the front and on the sleeves, "Lehigh Valley" in an arcing script with a curled pig tail, and the Fauxback swinging pig head on a right-sleeve patch.

**What does the Retro Remix cap look like?**

A red cap with a navy blue brim and the full-bodied pig on the front, legs crossed, leaning on a bat.

**When do the IronPigs start wearing the Retro Remix?**

With the 2027 season. The team says it will be worn in regular rotation as a new alternate.

**What is the difference between the IronPigs Fauxback and the Retro Remix?**

The Fauxback is a cream jersey with red piping and an IronPigs script, worn on Friday home games since 2025. The Retro Remix is red with navy piping, uses a Lehigh Valley script in the same font and is a regular alternate.

**Why is it called the Retro Remix?**

Because it reworks the team's retro-style Fauxback uniforms in a new color, according to the IronPigs' announcement, which says the whole set draws heavy inspiration from the Fauxbacks.

**What is the pig on the IronPigs Retro Remix?**

Two versions of the cartoon pig introduced with the Fauxbacks: a swinging pig head on the sleeve patch, and a full-bodied pig leaning on a bat on the cap.

**Can you buy the IronPigs Retro Remix jersey?**

The team says a full line of merchandise, including T-shirts and more, is available at the IronPigs Clubhouse Store and online at the team's shop.

**Who are the Lehigh Valley IronPigs affiliated with?**

The Philadelphia Phillies. The IronPigs are their Triple-A club in the International League.

**Where do the IronPigs play?**

Jefferson Park in Allentown, Pennsylvania, formerly known as Coca-Cola Park.

**Will an MLB lockout affect the IronPigs in 2027?**

The team says the IronPigs will play their full 2027 season as scheduled, unaffected by any Major League Baseball labor dispute or work stoppage.
