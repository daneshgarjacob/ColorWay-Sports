**Florida State men's basketball wore turquoise on Friday, November 20, for its Seminole Heritage game against Green Bay** at the Tucker Center in Tallahassee. The team posted the uniform that morning as "Special day. Special unis." and the Seminoles won **87-63** in a noon tip on ACC Network Extra. It opened Homecoming weekend, a day before the football team wears turquoise in a game for the first time.

## Quick Answers

- **When did Florida State basketball wear turquoise?** Friday, November 20, 2026, against Green Bay, its Seminole Heritage game. Florida State won 87-63.
- **What does the jersey look like?** Turquoise, with SEMINOLES across the chest in garnet trimmed in gold, garnet numbers trimmed in gold, and garnet and cream side panels.
- **What about the shorts?** Turquoise, with the same garnet and cream side panels and the Seminole head logo on the waistband.
- **What shoes did they wear?** Nike N7 shoes in turquoise with a black geometric pattern, black soles and an N7 heel tab.
- **Why turquoise?** Florida State wears it in tribute to its relationship with the Seminole Tribe of Florida. Men's basketball started it with Nike's N7 jerseys in 2013.

## The Jersey

The base is a light, bright turquoise, the same family of color the football team wears on Saturday. Across the chest is **SEMINOLES** in garnet block letters outlined in gold, with the **number in garnet trimmed in gold** below it. On the back, the player's name sits in garnet over the number.

The sides are where the jersey gets its character. Each side runs a **garnet and cream panel** with pointed, feather-like edges, and the panel carries down onto the shorts so the whole uniform reads as one piece from the side.

The patches are the same as the regular jerseys: an **American flag** and the **ACC logo** on the chest, a **ReliaQuest** patch at the neckline, and a garnet Nike swoosh. The **shorts are turquoise** with the garnet and cream side panels and the Seminole head logo on the waistband.

## The N7 Shoes

The night before the game, Florida State posted the shoes: "Got something special to go with tomorrow's unis," tagged to Nike N7. They are **turquoise with a black geometric pattern** over the upper, **black soles**, a turquoise swoosh and an **N7 logo on the heel**. N7 is Nike's program supporting sport in Native American and Indigenous communities, and it is where Florida State's basketball turquoise began.

## Where Turquoise At Florida State Comes From

Florida State's men's team first wore turquoise in an 89-61 win over UT-Martin on November 17, 2013, and has worn it every season since, always for a game in November, which is Native American Heritage Month. Florida State says turquoise represents harmony, friendship and fellowship, and wears it in tribute to its relationship with the Seminole Tribe of Florida.

For most of that run, turquoise was a basketball thing. That changes this weekend. On Saturday, November 21, Florida State football wears turquoise in a game for the first time, with a gold helmet, a turquoise jersey and turquoise pants against NC State, on turquoise end zones at Doak Campbell Stadium. The full story is in [Florida State's turquoise football uniforms, explained](/stories/florida-state-turquoise-uniforms-seminole-heritage-2026).

## The Game

Florida State beat Green Bay **87-63** in a noon tip at the Tucker Center, with free admission to open Homecoming weekend.

## Our Grade

The turquoise is the right shade, bright enough to look nothing like a regular home or road jersey on TV, and garnet with a gold outline is the cleanest way to put Florida State's colors on top of it. The garnet and cream side panels are the detail that makes it look like Florida State and not a generic N7 template. The one thing holding it back is the chest: SEMINOLES in plain block letters is a little flat on a uniform this special. Grade: **A-**.
