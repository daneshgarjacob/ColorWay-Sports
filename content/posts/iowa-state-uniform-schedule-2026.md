Iowa State wears a cardinal jersey with white pants at Jack Trice Stadium and a white jersey with white pants on the road, both under the cardinal Cyclones script helmet. That covers all three games so far in 2026: cardinal over white in the 38-10 opener against Southeast Missouri, all white under the cardinal lid in the 16-13 loss at Iowa, and cardinal over white again in the 55-7 win over Bowling Green. This is the full Iowa State 2026 uniform schedule, with every jersey and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games in Jimmy Rogers' first season. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

The current closet comes from the Nike redesign of 2024, which gave Iowa State four uniforms: cardinal home, white road, and black and white alternates. The bigger change came on the helmet. Iowa State brought back the Cyclones script midway through 2024, a nod to the lids of 1987 to 1994, then unveiled a cardinal version in June 2025 and wore a script helmet in every game of 2025 through November, per the Iowa State Daily. The block I-State logo, the primary mark from 2008 through 2023, still has a cardinal helmet but has not been the default since. Rogers arrived from Washington State with 83 newcomers on the roster, and the wardrobe has not changed with him. Both jerseys also carry the Big 12 chest patch, which in 2026 includes a Monster Energy logo. Big 12 home teams wear color and visitors wear white, so every date below follows that rule until Iowa State announces otherwise.

**September 5 vs. Southeast Missouri.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **cardinal Cyclones script helmet, cardinal jersey, white pants**, the standard home look for Rogers' debut on a muggy afternoon in Ames. The white facemasks and the white and gold center stripe in the ESPN+ frames mark it as the script helmet, not the I-State helmet, which has a cardinal facemask. Iowa State won 38-10, with Jaylen Raynor accounting for four touchdowns and the defense logging six sacks.

**September 12 at Iowa.** ★ Confirmed from game photos: **cardinal Cyclones script helmet, white jersey, white pants**. The white jersey carries cardinal numerals outlined in gold and cardinal and gold sleeve stripes. Per the pregame report, it was the first time since 2012 that Iowa State wore all white in the Cy-Hawk game. Iowa answered with its all-black alternate for the prime-time NBC game at Kinnick Stadium, so the night was cardinal and white against solid black, and we have the Hawkeyes' side on the [Iowa 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/iowa-uniform-schedule-2026). Iowa won 16-13 on a 42-yard field goal with 15 seconds left, after Iowa State had won the previous two meetings on late kicks of its own.

**September 19 vs. Bowling Green.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **cardinal helmet, cardinal jersey, white pants**, the same home look Iowa State opened the season in, for an 11 a.m. Central kickoff on ESPNU. Bowling Green answered in a white helmet and white jersey over orange pants, which gave Jack Trice Stadium a clean cardinal against white read. Iowa State won 55-7 in the last nonconference game before Big 12 play.

**September 26 vs. Utah.** ★ Confirmed by Iowa State: the **Whiteout**, all white with black lettering and accents, the script Cyclones helmet and black stripes across the shoulders. We had cardinal down. The Big 12 opener at Jack Trice Stadium, 2:30 p.m. Central on FOX, with Utah reported in all red; the Utes are on the [Utah 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/utah-uniform-schedule-2026).

**October 3 vs. West Virginia.** Cardinal expected. This is homecoming, the most likely home date for an alternate if Iowa State plans one, so we are watching it closely. West Virginia is expected in white on the [West Virginia 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/west-virginia-uniform-schedule-2026). Kickoff time is still TBA.

**October 9 at BYU.** White expected. A Friday night game at LaVell Edwards Stadium, 8:15 p.m. Mountain (9:15 p.m. Central), and it falls during BYU's homecoming week. BYU is expected in royal blue at home on the [BYU 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/byu-uniform-schedule-2026). ESPN's feed lists this game on October 10 because it runs on UTC. It is a Friday, October 9 game.

**October 17.** Bye.

**October 24 at Arizona.** White expected. Iowa State's second trip west this month, to Casino Del Sol Stadium in Tucson.

**October 31 vs. Oklahoma State.** Cardinal expected. A Halloween game in Ames, with Oklahoma State expected in white.

**November 7 at Baylor.** White expected. It is Baylor's homecoming at McLane Stadium, which usually means a green-heavy look from the Bears.

**November 14 vs. Cincinnati.** Cardinal expected.

**November 20 at UCF.** White expected. A Friday game at the Acrisure Bounce House in Orlando, 5 p.m. Central on FS1.

**November 28 vs. Kansas State.** Cardinal expected. Farmageddon closes the regular season at Jack Trice Stadium, a rematch of the game that opened 2025 in Dublin. Kansas State is expected in white on the [Kansas State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/kansas-state-uniform-schedule-2026), and the last home game of the season.

## The Bottom Line

Three games in, Iowa State has worn a cardinal helmet every week and changed only the jersey: cardinal at home, white on the road, white pants every time. That fits a program that just replaced almost its entire roster and kept the closet the way it was. The script helmet is now the default for a third straight season, and the black and all-white alternates from the 2024 Nike set have not shown up yet. Homecoming against West Virginia and the Halloween game against Oklahoma State are the two home dates where that is most likely to change. The grid above has every date, and we add a star as each uniform is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Iowa State wearing this week?**

Iowa State wore a cardinal helmet, cardinal jersey and white pants for the 55-7 win over Bowling Green at Jack Trice Stadium on Saturday, September 19, the same home look it opened the season in. Bowling Green answered in a white helmet and white jersey over orange pants. Next up is Utah in Ames on September 26, the Big 12 opener, and Iowa State has confirmed an all white Whiteout for it.

**What are Iowa State's football uniforms for 2026?**

A cardinal home jersey with white numerals outlined in gold, a white road jersey with cardinal numerals outlined in gold, white pants with both, and black and all-white alternates from the 2024 Nike redesign. The primary helmet is cardinal with a white Cyclones script outlined in gold and a white and gold center stripe. A cardinal helmet with the I-State logo is also in the closet.

**Does Iowa State have a new alternate uniform in 2026?**

Not that has been announced. The one visible addition is the Big 12 chest patch, now allowed under the NCAA's 2026 patch rule. The existing black and all-white alternates have not been worn yet this season, and we will add any new look to this page as soon as it is revealed.

**What did Iowa State wear against Iowa?**

Iowa State wore the cardinal Cyclones script helmet, a white jersey and white pants at Kinnick Stadium on September 12. It was the Cyclones' first all-white look in the Cy-Hawk game since 2012, per the pregame report. Iowa wore its all-black alternate, and the Hawkeyes won 16-13 on a field goal with 15 seconds left.

**What helmet does Iowa State wear?**

The cardinal Cyclones script helmet, which Iowa State revealed in June 2025 and has used as its primary helmet since, including the first two games of 2026. Iowa State wore a cardinal helmet again against Bowling Green. The script is a throwback to the Cyclones helmets of 1987 to 1994. It replaced the block I-State logo, which was the primary helmet mark from 2008 through 2023.

**How many games does Iowa State play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven at Jack Trice Stadium and five on the road, with one bye on October 17. Two of the road games are on Fridays, at BYU on October 9 and at UCF on November 20. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
