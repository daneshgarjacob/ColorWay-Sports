Baseball has a jersey. Football has a combination. A team can wear the same jersey three weeks running and look like three different teams depending on the helmet and the pants underneath it, and that combination is the thing nobody keeps a proper record of.

So we are keeping it. This is a running log of what all 32 teams actually wear, game by game, for the whole 2026 season. It starts here with the preseason, because the preseason is where teams quietly test combinations they are thinking about using when it counts, and it is where throwbacks and one-offs turn up with no announcement at all.

Every game below gets a card with both teams. Each card shows three bars per team: helmet, jersey, pants. Once a game kicks off we log the actual combination that was worn and the bars update to match it.

## How This Works

We log **helmet, jersey and pants separately** because that is how a football uniform is actually built, and because the interesting decisions live in the pants. Monochrome, white-over-white, colour-over-colour, a throwback shell with a modern jersey: none of that shows up if you only record "they wore navy."

Games are listed earliest first while the preseason is still ahead of us. Once the season is underway the most recent games move to the top, the same way our [MLB uniform tracker](/stories/mlb-uniform-tracker-2026) runs.

Nothing below is a prediction. The bars show each team's identity colours until the game is played, and the label under every team says so. We do not guess a combination and we do not backfill one from a press photo we cannot verify.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the 2026 NFL uniform tracker?**

A running log of the helmet, jersey and pants combination every NFL team wears in every game of the 2026 season, starting with all 49 preseason games. We update it after games are played.

**When does the 2026 NFL preseason start?**

Thursday, August 6, with the Hall of Fame Game between Carolina and Arizona in Canton. Preseason Week 1 runs August 13 to 15, Week 2 runs August 20 to 23, and Week 3 runs August 27 to 29.

**How many preseason games does each NFL team play in 2026?**

Three, except Carolina and Arizona, who play four because they meet in the Hall of Fame Game.

**Why do you track pants separately from the jersey?**

Because in football the combination is the uniform. The same jersey looks completely different over white pants than over colour pants, and monochrome sets only exist as a pants decision. Logging just the jersey misses most of what actually changes week to week.

**Do teams wear special uniforms in the preseason?**

Often, yes, and with far less warning than in the regular season. The preseason is where clubs trial combinations they are considering for real games, and where throwbacks sometimes appear with no announcement.

**Where can I see what a team wore last night?**

Every game is logged on this page by date. For baseball, our [MLB uniform tracker](/stories/mlb-uniform-tracker-2026) does the same thing daily, with per-team calendars.
