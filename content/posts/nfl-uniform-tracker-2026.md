If you came here to find out what an NFL team is wearing tonight, that is exactly what this page is for.

Baseball has a jersey. Football has a combination. A team can wear the same jersey three weeks running and look like three different teams depending on the helmet and the pants underneath it, and nobody keeps a proper record of it. So we are keeping it: what all 32 teams actually wear, game by game, all season, with a grade on every matchup.

It starts with the preseason, because the preseason is where teams quietly test combinations they are thinking about using when it counts, and where throwbacks and one-offs turn up with no announcement at all.

## How This Works

Every game gets its own entry with both teams. Each team shows three bars, labelled **helmet**, **jersey** and **pants**, because that is how a football uniform is actually built and because the interesting decisions live in the pants. Monochrome, white over white, colour over colour, a throwback shell under a modern jersey: none of that shows up if you only record "they wore navy."

Once a game kicks off we do three things. We log the actual combination and the bars update to match it. We add the team's own post showing the uniform, so you can see it rather than take our word for it. And we grade the matchup, because two good uniforms can still make an ugly game to look at, and that is worth saying.

Games run earliest first while the preseason is ahead of us. Once the season starts the newest games move to the top, the same way our [MLB uniform tracker](/stories/mlb-uniform-tracker-2026) runs.

Nothing here is a prediction. Until a game is played the bars show that team's **standard look**, every team reads **Not yet worn**, and the bars are replaced with the real combination once we log it. We do not guess a combination, and we do not backfill one from a photo we cannot verify.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jerseys are NFL teams wearing tonight?**

Every game on this page is logged with the exact helmet, jersey and pants each team wore, added after kickoff. Find tonight's date, then the matchup, and the card shows both teams' combinations.

**What is the 2026 NFL uniform tracker?**

A running log of the helmet, jersey and pants combination every NFL team wears in every game of the 2026 season, with a grade on each matchup. It starts with all 49 preseason games.

**How do I find what one specific team wore?**

Every game has its own heading in the form "Detroit Lions at Cincinnati Bengals," so you can search this page for a team name and see every game they have played and what they wore in each.

**When does the 2026 NFL preseason start?**

Thursday, August 6, with the Hall of Fame Game between Carolina and Arizona in Canton. Preseason Week 1 runs August 13 to 15, Week 2 runs August 20 to 23, and Week 3 runs August 27 to 29.

**How many preseason games does each NFL team play in 2026?**

Three, except Carolina and Arizona, who play four because they meet in the Hall of Fame Game.

**Do NFL teams wear throwbacks in the preseason?**

Often, and with far less warning than in the regular season. The preseason is where clubs trial combinations they are considering for real games, and where throwbacks sometimes appear with no announcement at all.

**Why do you track pants separately from the jersey?**

Because in football the combination is the uniform. The same jersey looks completely different over white pants than over colour pants, and monochrome sets only exist as a pants decision. Logging just the jersey misses most of what changes week to week.

**What is a monochrome NFL uniform?**

One where the jersey and pants are the same colour, usually with a matching helmet. We flag them on the cards because they are the most divisive look in football. Our full case against them is in [Why Monochrome NFL Uniforms Look Bad](/stories/monochrome-nfl-uniforms-why-they-look-bad).

**How are the grades decided?**

We grade the matchup, not just one team, on a letter scale. Two strong uniforms can still clash badly, and a great look opposite a plain one can flatten the whole broadcast. The grade reflects how the game actually looked.

**Where can I see what a baseball team wore last night?**

Our [MLB uniform tracker](/stories/mlb-uniform-tracker-2026) does the same thing daily for all 30 clubs, with per-team calendars.
