**The Commanders are going all black next Monday night.** Washington wears its **Hail Raiser** alternate when it hosts the Cincinnati Bengals on **Monday, November 23, at 8:15 p.m. Eastern on ESPN**. That is the **black helmet with the Spear W**, a **black jersey** with burgundy numbers outlined in gold, and **black pants**. It is the first game for the uniform Washington revealed in April, and the first of only two dates it gets in 2026.

## Quick Answers

- **What are the Commanders wearing on Monday Night Football?** The Hail Raiser: a black helmet with the Spear W logo, a black jersey and black pants, with burgundy and gold trim.

## Why This Game Got The Hail Raiser

Washington published every 2026 jersey on July 16 and gave the Hail Raiser two home dates. The team picked this one first, calling it a rematch of the 2024 Monday night game in Cincinnati, a 38-33 Washington win in which neither team punted or turned the ball over. Holding the uniform to two dates keeps it special. Alternates lose their pull when they show up every month.

## Is The Hail Raiser Any Good?

Yes. Black alternates are usually the laziest thing a team can do: swap the color for black and call it new. This one works because burgundy and gold stay in charge. The numbers are burgundy, the outlines and stripes are gold, and the franchise colors read from the upper deck.

The helmet is what makes it. The Spear W replaces the primary logo, it has a real link to the team's history, and from the front the two spearheads give the shell a look no other team has. We graded the Hail Raiser an **A** at the reveal and ranked it first in [every Commanders jersey ranked](/stories/commanders-jerseys-2026-ranked). The helmet got an A- in our [2026 alternate helmet rankings](/stories/nfl-new-alternate-helmets-2026-ranked). Against road white on national TV, the matchup is an **A-**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the Commanders wearing against the Bengals?**

The Hail Raiser: the black helmet with the Spear W, a black jersey with burgundy numbers outlined in gold, and black pants with burgundy and gold stripes. Washington put the Hail Raiser on this game in its 2026 jersey schedule on July 16.

**What time is Bengals vs. Commanders on Monday Night Football?**

Monday, November 23, at 8:15 p.m. Eastern on ESPN, at the Commanders' home stadium in Landover, Maryland.

**What is the Commanders' Hail Raiser uniform?**

Washington's all-black alternate, revealed April 15, 2026, with the rest of the team's redesign. It has its own black helmet with the Spear W alternate logo, burgundy and gold sleeve stripes, solid block numbers, and black pants.

**What is the Spear W?**

The Commanders' alternate logo: the W with a spear running through it, pulled from the franchise's 1960s uniforms. It appears only on the Hail Raiser helmet.

**Have the Commanders worn the Hail Raiser before?**

No. Monday night against the Bengals is its first game.

**How many times will the Commanders wear the Hail Raiser in 2026?**

Twice, both at home: Week 11 against the Bengals and Week 15 against the Falcons on Sunday, December 20.

**What are the Bengals wearing against the Commanders?**

The striped orange helmet, the white jersey and white pants, with black socks, confirmed by the Bengals for this game.

**How many uniforms do the Commanders have in 2026?**

Three: the white primary, the burgundy heritage jersey, and the black Hail Raiser alternate. The white goes six times, burgundy nine, and the Hail Raiser twice. There are two helmets: the burgundy primary and the black Hail Raiser helmet.

**Why is it called the Hail Raiser?**

It plays on "Raise Hail," the team's rallying cry and one of the wordmarks in the new brand. Washington describes the uniform as the identity of the Commander, "a leader of warriors."
