**The Yankees' High-A team is turning into gnomes.** The **Hudson Valley Renegades** announced the **Hudson Valley Mountain Gnomes** on Tuesday, November 17: a new alternate identity for **2027**, built on a piece of local folklore about **Henry Hudson's 1609 trip up the river** and the bearded gnomes his crew supposedly met in the **Catskill Mountains**.

The Renegades will wear **Mountain Gnomes uniforms for four games** next season, starting **Friday, May 21, against the Wilmington Blue Rocks** at Heritage Financial Park. The team says every jersey in the set hides its gnome in a **different spot**, and calls it the first known case of a minor league team making a unique design for each jersey in an identity.

## Quick Answers

- **What are the Hudson Valley Mountain Gnomes?** A one-season alternate identity the Hudson Valley Renegades will use at four home games in 2027.
- **Where does the name come from?** A Hudson Valley legend: Henry Hudson's crew drinks the Catskill gnomes' magic liquor, plays nine-pins with them and briefly turns into gnomes.
- **What is special about the jerseys?** Each jersey places its gnome in a different location, a nod to gnomes hiding in the mountains.
- **When is the first Mountain Gnomes game?** Friday, May 21, 2027, vs. the Wilmington Blue Rocks.
- **Is there a mascot?** Yes. Rally the Mountain Gnome.
- **Who are the Renegades affiliated with?** The New York Yankees, as their High-A club since 2021.

## What The Mountain Gnomes Look Like

Everything here comes from the Renegades' announcement and the team's Mountain Gnomes page. The team has not published a written color breakdown, so we are not going to guess at shades.

**The primary logo.** A **mountain range covered in evergreen trees**, meant to look like the **snow-capped Catskill peaks** Hudson's crew would have seen more than 400 years ago. The wordmark sits inside a **home plate**.

**The hidden letters.** There is an **HV hidden in the mountain peak**. The team describes it as subtle and impossible to miss once you see it.

**The gnome.** **Rally the Mountain Gnome**, a friendly, expressive gnome with a **baseball bat on his shoulder**. He is also the new mascot.

**The jerseys.** This is the part that is new. The team says the gnomes appear in a **different location on every jersey**, so no two players' shirts are the same, playing off the idea that gnomes hide in the mountains.

**Who drew it.** The team's Mountain Gnomes page credits local designer **Justin Arundell of Just Art** with the logo, and the team's video above credits its in-house artists with the full set.

## The Legend Behind The Name

The story, as the Renegades tell it, goes like this. On **September 3, 1609**, Hudson sailed the **Half Moon** into the river that now carries his name. The Mohican people he traded with told him about a group of gnomes who lived in the mountains and worked in metals.

On the way back down the river, anchored near the Catskills, Hudson heard music coming off the mountains. He and part of his crew went ashore and found the gnomes, with long bushy beards, dancing around a fire. The gnomes shared their homemade liquor and a game of nine-pin bowling. When Hudson went to leave, his crew had turned into gnomes. The chief told him it would wear off with the liquor, and it did.

Locals say Hudson's crew returns to the Catskills every 20 years to bowl with the gnomes. The team notes that the warning about the gnomes' liquor is the basis for Washington Irving's **Rip Van Winkle**, and that the next return is due in **September 2029**.

## Our Take

This is the best minor league identity of the off-season so far, and the reason is the jerseys, not the gnome. Plenty of teams have a funny mascot. Nobody has tried hiding the logo in a different spot on every shirt. It turns a uniform into a game fans play from the seats, and it means every jersey a fan buys is a little different from the next one.

The story behind it also holds up. A lot of minor league alternates are food puns with no connection to anything. This one comes from a real Hudson Valley legend that most people have never heard, tied to one of the most famous short stories in American writing. The HV hidden in the mountain is a nice touch too.

The open question is how it reads on the field. Four games is not much, and a set where every jersey is different can look like a mistake from far away if the gnomes are small. We will judge that on May 21. On the idea alone: **Grade: A-.**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the Hudson Valley Mountain Gnomes?**

A new alternate identity for the Hudson Valley Renegades, the New York Yankees' High-A affiliate. The team will play four home games as the Mountain Gnomes in 2027.

**When will the Renegades play as the Mountain Gnomes?**

Four games in 2027. The first is Friday, May 21, against the Wilmington Blue Rocks. The team says the other three will be in June, July and August, with dates announced later.

**Why are the Hudson Valley Renegades called the Mountain Gnomes?**

The name comes from a local legend in which Henry Hudson's crew meets a band of bearded gnomes in the Catskill Mountains in 1609, drinks their magic liquor, bowls nine-pins with them and briefly turns into gnomes.

**What does the Mountain Gnomes logo look like?**

A mountain range covered in evergreens, modeled on the snow-capped Catskills, with the wordmark inside a home plate and an HV hidden in the mountain peak. Rally the Mountain Gnome carries a bat on his shoulder.

**What is special about the Mountain Gnomes jerseys?**

Each jersey shows its gnome in a different location, so no two are the same. The team says it is the first known example of a minor league team creating a unique jersey for every jersey in an identity.

**Who is Rally the Mountain Gnome?**

The new gnome mascot introduced with the identity, and the character in the logo.

**Who designed the Mountain Gnomes logo?**

The team's Mountain Gnomes page credits local designer Justin Arundell of Just Art. The team's behind-the-scenes video credits its in-house artists for the design work.

**Can you buy Mountain Gnomes merchandise?**

Yes. It is on sale online at the Renegades team store now, including a $26 T-shirt, a $49.99 hoodie and a $40 hat. The first 500 buyers get two free tickets to the May 21 debut, according to the team.

**Is there a Mountain Gnomes ticket package?**

Yes. The team is selling a package with tickets to all four Mountain Gnomes games, a free ticket to Opening Day on Friday, April 2, a Mountain Gnomes T-shirt and a pint glass, starting at $70 plus taxes and fees.

**What does the Mountain Gnomes legend have to do with Rip Van Winkle?**

In the legend, anyone who drinks the spirits' magic liquor sleeps for 20 years. The Renegades say that warning is the basis for Washington Irving's short story Rip Van Winkle.

**Are the Mountain Gnomes replacing the Renegades?**

No. The Renegades stay the Renegades. The Mountain Gnomes are an alternate identity for four games, and the team says the brand will also show up around the ballpark at all home games, including a signature cocktail.

**What other alternate identities do the Hudson Valley Renegades have?**

The team's site lists the Big Apples, Cider Donuts, Fenómenos Enmascarados and Newburgh Gorhams, along with the new Mountain Gnomes.

**Where do the Hudson Valley Renegades play?**

Heritage Financial Park in Wappingers Falls, New York.
