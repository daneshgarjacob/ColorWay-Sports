## Why Fans Are Calling It "Goldfinger"

Oregon did not give this one a name. "Goldfinger" comes from **Jonah Henderson**, a Salem, Oregon, graphic designer who tracks every Oregon uniform, and who named the look in his post about it on Wednesday. The yellow helmet, yellow pants and the pointing finger in the team's photo do the rest.

## How Often Has Oregon Worn Yellow, White and Yellow?

According to Henderson's count:

- **Third year in a row** Oregon has worn a yellow helmet, white jersey and yellow pants.
- **10th time this century.**
- **Third time** with the current white Generation O jersey, but the **first time the helmet has had wings**.
- **First time the yellow pants have been out since Week 1.**

The parts we can check line up. Oregon wore a yellow helmet, the white "Mighty Oregon" Generation O jersey and yellow pants **at Northwestern on September 13, 2025**, according to [Oregon Ducks On SI](https://www.si.com/college/oregon/football/oregon-ducks-uniforms-nike-flagship-program-nil-phil-knight-combination-dan-lanning-dante-moore-northwestern-big-ten), which also described it as a repeat of the 2024 combination. That helmet had no wings, just a green O on the side. And the yellow pants last appeared in the **September 5 opener against Boise State**, with a yellow helmet and green jersey, as logged in our [Oregon 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/oregon-uniform-schedule-2026). We could not independently confirm the "10th time this century" count, so that number is Henderson's.

The winged yellow helmet is not new this season. Oregon wore it, with green chrome wings, over a white jersey and **white** pants at Illinois on October 24. Friday swaps in the yellow pants.

## Our Grade: A-

This is Oregon at its most Oregon. Green and yellow are the school colors, and a white jersey in the middle lets both of them pop instead of fighting each other. The wings are the upgrade. The 2025 version used a plain yellow helmet with an O, which looked fine and a little flat. The wings give the helmet the movement it was missing.

It loses a little for being familiar. Oregon has worn some version of yellow-white-yellow three years running, and it wore this exact helmet four weeks ago. But under the lights on a Friday night against Michigan State's green, it will be the brightest thing on television that night.
