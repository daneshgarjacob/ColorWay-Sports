**November 27 at Florida State** (away). Blue helmet, white jersey, white pants.

## The Three Games That Matter

**October 10 vs. South Carolina is the one to circle.** It is the only retro helmet of the season, and the cover art gives it away: blue shell, orange and white striping, and the block F inside a circle rather than the modern script. White jersey and white pants underneath keep the helmet as the entire story, which is the correct call.

**November 7 vs. Oklahoma is the all-blue.** Blue helmet, blue jersey, blue pants, the only mono-colour game Florida runs all year. Mono looks are a risk and this is the right way to spend one: a marquee home game against Oklahoma, in the colour the programme actually owns.

**September 12 vs. Campbell is the only orange jersey.** Florida wears orange exactly once, in week two, against a team they have never played. That is an unusual place to spend your rarest jersey.

## What the Grid Tells You About Florida

Three things stand out once you lay the twelve games side by side.

**The orange helmet is the default.** Eight of twelve games. Blue appears three times and the retro once. Florida has essentially settled on orange as the base shell and treats blue as the accent, which is the inverse of how most people picture the Gators.

**They never wear blue on the road.** Every away game is a white jersey. All four road trips and the Georgia neutral come in white, which means the blue jersey is a Swamp exclusive in 2026.

**Orange pants are a road-only device.** They appear twice, at Auburn and at Kentucky, and nowhere else. Both are SEC road games, and both pair with the orange helmet and white jersey for a look that is close to a full orange-and-white road set.

## The Bottom Line

Florida is the rare college programme that will tell you in August what it is wearing in November, and the 2026 grid is a well-built one. Orange helmet as the base, blue reserved for The Swamp, one retro helmet against South Carolina on October 10 and one all-blue against Oklahoma on November 7. The only odd note is spending the season's single orange jersey on Campbell in week two.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Florida wearing this week?**

Florida released a full 2026 uniform schedule in advance. Blue jerseys at home, white jerseys on the road every week, and one orange jersey, against Campbell on September 12.

**When does Florida wear the retro helmet in 2026?**

Saturday, October 10 against South Carolina at Ben Hill Griffin Stadium. It is the only retro helmet game of the season, worn with a white jersey and white pants.

**Does Florida wear an all-blue uniform in 2026?**

Yes, once. November 7 against Oklahoma at home is blue helmet, blue jersey and blue pants, the only mono-colour combination on the schedule.

**What does Florida wear against Georgia?**

Orange helmet, white jersey and white pants for the October 31 neutral-site game at Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta.

**What does Florida wear against Florida State?**

Blue helmet, white jersey and white pants for the November 27 road game in Tallahassee.

**How many times does Florida wear the orange helmet?**

Eight of twelve games. The blue helmet appears three times and the retro helmet once.
