If you watched around Major League Baseball on Friday, July 24, you saw a wild night of uniforms: Hall of Fame Weekend throwback caps on all 30 teams, and City Connects and alternates almost everywhere you looked. Except one game. At Citi Field, the New York Mets and Los Angeles Dodgers both took the field in their plain, classic primary uniforms, the only matchup all night with no City Connect and no alternate in sight. There is a very good reason for that, and it has nothing to do with baseball: a Hollywood movie was filming at the ballpark.

## The Movie: Emma Stone's "The Catch"

The film shooting at Citi Field is **"The Catch,"** a baseball romance directed by **Dave McCary** that has been described as "Bull Durham meets Notting Hill" and is set for theaters on **May 21, 2027**. The production shot on location at the Mets' ballpark during the team's late-July homestand, including the Dodgers series, with an earlier round of filming reported around the mid-July Red Sox series. It stars **Emma Stone**, who reportedly plays "the most hated woman in baseball," alongside **Chris Pine** as a baseball player, with **Juan Soto** appearing in a scene as himself. The cast also reportedly includes **Tim Robbins** as Stone's father, **Sissy Spacek** as an aunt, and **Ashley Padilla**. Mets fans were even invited to fill the stands as paid extras for the ballpark scenes.

When a feature film shoots a baseball game, the production almost always wants the most timeless, recognizable version of each team on camera. Splashy City Connects and modern alternates date a scene and complicate licensing, while classic home whites and road grays read as baseball in any era. So instead of the Mets' usual Friday-night look, both clubs wore their signature primaries for the cameras.

## Why the Mets Broke Their Friday-Night Habit

Here is the detail that makes this a real uniform story. The Mets almost always break out an alternate for **Friday home games** at Citi Field, most often their fan-favorite black jersey, and on some Friday nights the gray "NYC" City Connect. Either way, a Friday under the lights at Citi Field is usually an alternate night rather than a plain-pinstripes night, which is what fans plan tickets and merchandise around.

But not this Friday. With "The Catch" filming, the Mets went with the **home white pinstripes** instead, the clean, classic look that plays best on screen. The Dodgers matched the energy in their road grays with the script Dodgers across the chest. It gave the broadcast something you almost never see anymore on a City Connect Friday: two of the most timeless uniforms in the sport, sharing a field with nothing flashy in the frame. Sometimes the simplest look is the best one, and this time it was a movie's call, not a manager's.
