Tulsa is 2-0 for the first time since 2015, it has beaten Oklahoma State two years running, and on Wednesday the program dropped a red jersey that nobody saw coming. This is the full Tulsa 2026 uniform schedule: the royal blue home set, the white road set, the gold helmet that goes with everything, the new red throwback, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games, including a Thursday night game against North Texas and a Friday night game against Army. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

Tulsa is an adidas school, and the base look is one of the more settled in the American: gold helmet, gold pants, and a blue or white jersey depending on who is home. The script wordmark carries across the helmet and the chest, which gives the whole thing a vintage feel even in the standard set. Both games so far have used the same gold helmet and the same gold pants, so on a normal Saturday the only real variable is the jersey color.

The opener was the second straight Tulsa win over Oklahoma State and the first home win in the series since 1998. Tulsa wore blue at home and the Cowboys came in all white.

## The New Red Throwback

On Wednesday, September 16, Tulsa posted a photo set with the caption "Nothing like a classic look," and the uniform in it is red. The jersey is a bright red with plain white block numerals, a royal blue band on the sleeve cap framed by thin gold stripes, and the adidas mark on the chest. The pants are gold. The shoot was staged at a vintage filling station with an old Phillips 66 pump and a classic Chevy convertible, which is hard to miss in the year Route 66 turns 100. The Mother Road runs straight through Tulsa.

Crimson is one of Tulsa's three school colors alongside royal blue and gold, but it usually lives in the trim and the logo, not the body of the jersey, which is what makes this one land. Tulsa has not said which game it is for. The same week the program posted a video titled "Stepping into a time machine" and another called "The making of the helmet," so there is likely a matching helmet we have not seen yet. The next home game is Saturday against East Texas A&M, which is also Senior Day and Parent and Family Weekend. That is the date we are watching, but until Tulsa says so it stays a watch, not a call.

Tulsa also brought back its city flag helmet for 918 Day on September 18, the gold shell with the script filled in with the colors of the Tulsa flag that the program first showed off last September.

## The Bottom Line

Two games, two wins, one helmet and one pair of pants. Tulsa has been as disciplined as it gets with the base set, gold over blue at home and gold over white on the road, and there is no reason to expect that to change on a normal Saturday. The red throwback is the story, and it is a good one: a real school color that rarely gets a jersey of its own, shot on a vintage Route 66 filling station set in the year the highway turns 100. Saturday against East Texas A&M is the first chance to see it, with Military Appreciation Night against Army and Homecoming against Florida Atlantic still on the board if Tulsa is saving it. The grid above has every game, and we add a star as each combination is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Tulsa wearing this week?**

Tulsa hosts East Texas A&M on Saturday, September 19 at 7 p.m. Central on ESPN+. The royal blue home jersey with gold pants and the gold helmet is the expected look, but Tulsa revealed a red throwback jersey on September 16 without naming a game, and this is the next home date. We will confirm it here once the combination is posted.

**What are Tulsa's football uniforms for 2026?**

A royal blue home jersey and a white road jersey, both with the Tulsa script, worn with gold pants, plus a new red throwback jersey. The main helmet is gold with a blue Tulsa script and a blue facemask. Tulsa also has a white helmet and a gold city flag helmet with the script filled in the colors of the Tulsa flag. The uniforms are made by adidas.

**Does Tulsa have a red uniform?**

Yes. Tulsa revealed a red jersey with white block numerals, a blue and gold sleeve stripe and gold pants on September 16, 2026, under the caption "Nothing like a classic look." Crimson is one of Tulsa's three school colors along with royal blue and gold. The program has not announced which game it will be worn in.

**What did Tulsa wear against Oklahoma State?**

The gold helmet, the royal blue home jersey and gold pants for the 24-10 win on September 5 at H.A. Chapman Stadium. Oklahoma State wore all white. It was Tulsa's second straight win over the Cowboys and its first home win in the series since 1998.

**When do Tulsa and North Texas play in 2026?**

Thursday, October 1 at 8 p.m. Central on ESPN at H.A. Chapman Stadium. It is the American opener for both teams. Tulsa should be in royal blue at home, with North Texas in white.

**How many games does Tulsa play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: six at H.A. Chapman Stadium and six on the road, with one bye on October 31. Eight are American games. The finale at UTSA will be played on either November 27 or November 28. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
