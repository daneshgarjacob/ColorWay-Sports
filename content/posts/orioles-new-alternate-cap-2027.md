**The Orioles have a new alternate cap for 2027.** Baltimore revealed it on Wednesday, November 18: an **all-black cap**, black crown and black brim, with a **full-body oriole** on the front in orange, black and white, and an orange New Era flag on the side. The team says it "joins the lineup" as an on-field alternate for 2027, and it is already on sale at the Team Store at Oriole Park.

## Quick Answers

- **What is the Orioles' new cap?** An all-black on-field alternate cap with a full-body orange, black and white oriole on the front.
- **When will they wear it?** In 2027. The team has not said which games.
- **Can you buy it?** Yes. The Orioles say it is available now at the Team Store at Oriole Park.
- **What else is changing?** The sleeve trim moves back to the edge of the sleeves, and the left sleeve patch changes from an orange ring to a black ring around the Maryland flag.
- **Our grade:** **B-**.

## The New Cap, Detail By Detail

- **Crown:** solid black, front panels included. There is no white front like the home cap.
- **Brim:** black. Every other cap in the Orioles' 2027 on-field set has an orange brim, so this is the only one without it.
- **Logo:** a full-body oriole standing in profile, facing left. The head, back and wing are black, the chest and tail are orange, and the wing has white bars. The bird has a white beak and is outlined in orange so it stands out on the black crown.
- **Side:** an orange New Era flag.

The bird is a realistic oriole, not the smiling cartoon bird the Orioles wear on their home and road caps. That style of bird is the one Baltimore wore on its caps before the cartoon bird came back in 2012, so the new cap reads as a nod to that era more than a brand-new logo.

## How It Compares To The Other Orioles Caps

The team's 2027 uniform graphic lines up every on-field cap in one row. With the new one, Baltimore has six:

| Cap | Crown | Brim | Logo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home | White front, black back | Orange | Cartoon bird |
| Road | Black | Orange | Cartoon bird |
| Alternate | Black | Orange | Orange "O's" script |
| Alternate | Black | Orange | Orange script "B" |
| **Alternate (new for 2027)** | **Black** | **Black** | **Full-body oriole** |

Three of the alternates now share a black crown. The new one is the only cap in the set with a black brim, which is the biggest visual change. The "O's" cap pairs with the black alternate jersey, and the script "B" cap was the new addition in 2026. ## The 2027 Sleeve Changes

Baltimore is also making two small changes to the jerseys for 2027, first reported by Paul Mancano, who posted the team's "Baltimore Orioles 2027 Uniform Updates" graphic.

<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/PaulMancano/status/2123069302555475975"></a></blockquote>

<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: -0.5em 0 2em;">The Orioles' 2027 uniform updates graphic, via <a href="https://x.com/PaulMancano" target="_blank" rel="noopener">@PaulMancano</a></p>

**1. The sleeve trim moves back to the edge.** In 2026 the black, orange and black stripes sat a little above the end of the sleeve, with a band of the jersey color below them. For 2027 the stripes run right at the edge of the sleeve. Per the graphic:

- **Home white:** black, orange, black at the edge.
- **Road gray:** black, orange, black at the edge.
- **Black alternate:** a single orange stripe at the edge.
- **Orange alternate:** black, orange, black at the edge.

**2. A new left sleeve patch.** The Maryland flag patch stays, but the ring around it changes. The 2026 patch had an **orange ring** with "BALTIMORE ORIOLES" in white. The 2027 patch has a **black ring** with "BALTIMORE" across the top and "ORIOLES" across the bottom in white, plus a thin orange outer border.

Both changes are small. The trim at the edge of the sleeve is the more traditional baseball look, and the black ring makes the patch match the black in the rest of the uniform instead of adding more orange.

## Our Grade: B-

Jake's take: "I actually like their other cap more. I like the style, and it's an alternate, so it's fine, but it's not my favorite look for them. I think it's a bit bland and boring."

The realistic bird is a good logo and a fair nod to the team's history. The problem is everything around it. With a black brim, the cap loses the orange that makes every other Orioles cap look like the Orioles, and from a distance it is a black cap with a small bird on it. The script "B" cap does the alternate job better. As a third black alternate it is fine, and that is where the **B-** comes from. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the Orioles' new alternate cap for 2027?**

It is an all-black on-field cap, with a black crown and a black brim, and a full-body oriole on the front in orange, black and white with an orange outline. The New Era flag on the side is orange. The Orioles revealed it on November 18, 2026.

**When will the Orioles wear the new alternate cap?**

During the 2027 season. The team has not said which games or which jerseys it goes with.

**Where can I buy the new Orioles alternate cap?**

The Orioles say it is available now at the Team Store at Oriole Park at Camden Yards.

**What bird is on the new Orioles cap?**

A realistic, full-body oriole standing in profile, not the cartoon bird on the home and road caps. It has a black head, back and wing, an orange chest and tail, and white bars on the wing.

**Is the new Orioles cap a throwback?**

The team did not call it a throwback. The realistic bird is the style Baltimore wore on its caps before the cartoon bird returned in 2012, so it looks back to that era, but the all-black cap with a black brim is new.

**How many caps do the Orioles have for 2027?**

Six on-field caps, per the team's graphic: the home cap with a white front, the black road cap, three black alternates (the "O's" script, the script "B" and the new full-body bird), and the cream City Connect cap.

**What color is the brim on the new Orioles cap?**

Black. It is the only Orioles on-field cap without an orange brim.

**Are the Orioles changing their uniforms in 2027?**

Only slightly. The sleeve trim moves back to the edge of the sleeves, and the left sleeve patch switches from an orange ring to a black ring around the Maryland flag. The jerseys, wordmarks and numbers stay the same.

**What is the new Orioles sleeve patch?**

A round Maryland flag patch with a black ring that reads "BALTIMORE" on top and "ORIOLES" on the bottom in white, with a thin orange border. The 2026 version had an orange ring.

**What is changing with the Orioles' sleeve trim?**

In 2026 the stripes sat above the end of the sleeve. In 2027 they move to the edge: black, orange and black on the home white, road gray and orange alternate, and a single orange stripe on the black alternate.

**What is the Orioles' best cap?**

We still say the home cap with the white front, orange brim and cartoon bird. It is one of the best caps in baseball.

**What grade did we give the new Orioles cap?**

A **B-**. The bird is good, but the black brim drops the orange that makes the other Orioles caps work, and it reads plain.
