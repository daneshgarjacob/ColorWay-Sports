East Carolina has worn two completely different uniforms in two games: a white helmet with the old Pirates script at Alabama, then purple from head to toe for the home opener against App State. That is the ECU closet in a nutshell, deep enough that the helmet changes as often as the jersey. This is the full East Carolina 2026 uniform schedule: the purple home set, the white road set, the black alternate that comes out for Night of the Boneyard, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games, including two Thursday nights and a Friday night in Greenville. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

**September 5 at Alabama.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **white helmet with the purple Pirates script and a gold facemask, white jersey, white pants**, the full white road set in Tuscaloosa. Alabama wore crimson with white pants and won 48-10 on a brutally hot afternoon at Bryant-Denny Stadium. Our [Alabama 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/alabama-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Tide side.

**September 12 vs. App State.** ★ Confirmed from the ESPNU broadcast: **purple helmet with the pirate logo, purple jersey, purple pants**, all purple for the Paint it Purple home opener at Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium. App State came in all white and won 27-24, its fourth straight win in the series. See our [App State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/appalachian-state-uniform-schedule-2026).

**September 19 at Old Dominion.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **white helmet with the purple Jolly Roger, white jersey, purple pants**, the road whites broken up with purple below the belt rather than the all-white look the Pirates used at Alabama. A 6 p.m. kickoff at S.B. Ballard Stadium in Norfolk, with the Monarchs in their home colors. East Carolina won 20-17 on a late field goal after trailing 17-10 at the half.

**September 26 vs. NC Central.** Purple expected. Family Weekend and Junior Pirate Club Day, 4 p.m. in Greenville.

**October 3.** Bye. ECU's only Saturday off before conference play.

**October 10 vs. Rice.** Purple expected. Homecoming and Letterwinners Weekend opens American Conference play, kickoff time to be announced. Homecoming is one of the two dates on any schedule where a throwback is most likely, and ECU has the helmets to do it.

**October 15 at UAB.** White expected. Thursday night at Protective Stadium in Birmingham, 7:30 p.m. Eastern on ESPN or ESPN2, against a UAB team in green at home.

**October 22 at Memphis.** White expected. A second straight Thursday night, 7:30 p.m. Eastern on ESPN2 at Simmons Bank Liberty Stadium, with Memphis in blue. Our [Memphis 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/memphis-uniform-schedule-2026) tracks the Tigers.

**October 31 vs. Temple.** Purple expected. Halloween in Greenville for Fan Appreciation, kickoff time to be announced, with Temple expected in white. See our [Temple 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/temple-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 6 vs. South Florida.** Purple or black. A Friday night at 7:30 on ESPN2 that ECU has tagged as Paint It Black, Night of the Boneyard and Military Appreciation Day. When the crowd is told to wear black for a Boneyard night, the team has usually followed, so this is the date we would bet on for the black set. It is not confirmed. USF is expected in white on our [South Florida 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/south-florida-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 14 at Charlotte.** White expected. The in-state road trip to Jerry Richardson Stadium, kickoff time to be announced, with Charlotte in green.

**November 21 at Army.** White expected. Noon on CBS Sports Network at Michie Stadium, with Army expected in black for its final home game. Our [Army 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/army-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Black Knights side.

**November 27 or 28 vs. Florida Atlantic.** Purple expected. Senior Day to close the regular season, with the date listed as Friday or Saturday of Thanksgiving weekend and the kickoff still to be set. The American Conference championship game follows on December 5 at the highest seed's campus.

ECU found out it was opening in Tuscaloosa a year out, and announced it with a helmet graphic that put the pirate logo on a purple shell next to Alabama's crimson.

## Why East Carolina Plays on Thursday and Friday Nights

The American sells weeknight games to ESPN, and ECU carries three of them in 2026: at UAB on Thursday, October 15, at Memphis on Thursday, October 22, and home against South Florida on Friday, November 6. Weeknight games are where Group of Five programs reach for alternates, because the audience is national and there is nothing else on. ECU's black uniform was built for exactly this kind of game, and the program's own history backs it up.

## The Bottom Line

Two games in, East Carolina has used two helmets, two jerseys and two pant colors, and both looks were good ones. White is the call for the six road games, starting Saturday at Old Dominion, and purple is the default in Greenville, with the pants and helmet genuinely in play each week. The dates we would circle are homecoming against Rice on October 10, when a throwback helmet would fit, and the Friday night Boneyard game against South Florida on November 6, the most likely spot for the black set. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is East Carolina wearing this week?**

East Carolina wore the white helmet, white jersey and purple pants at Old Dominion on Saturday, September 19, a 20-17 win, confirmed from the broadcast. The Pirates wore white with the white script helmet at Alabama and all purple at home against App State, so this is a third different pants call in three games. The week-by-week grid above lists every game with the expected uniform.

**What are East Carolina's football uniforms for 2026?**

A purple home jersey with PIRATES across the chest and gold numerals, a white road jersey with gold numerals outlined in purple, and a black alternate for Night of the Boneyard games. Pants come in purple, gold and white, plus black for the all black set. Helmets include a purple shell with the pirate logo, a white shell with the Pirates script, a black shell, and a purple shell with the ECU letters that returned in 2025. Everything is adidas.

**What did East Carolina wear against App State?**

The purple helmet, purple jersey and purple pants for the Paint it Purple home opener at Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium on September 12. App State wore all white and won 27-24.

**Does East Carolina have a black uniform?**

Yes. ECU wears a black alternate for Night of the Boneyard, most recently shown off by the team in 2023. The 2026 Night of the Boneyard game is Friday, November 6 against South Florida, which ECU has also billed as Paint It Black. The team has not confirmed the uniform yet.

**When is Night of the Boneyard in 2026?**

Friday, November 6 against South Florida at Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium, 7:30 p.m. Eastern. The game is also ECU's Military Appreciation Day and a Paint It Black crowd theme.

**Where does East Carolina play home games?**

Bagwell Field at Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium in Greenville, North Carolina, with a capacity of 50,000. ECU has six home games in 2026.

**How many games does East Carolina play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: six at home and six on the road, with a single bye on October 3. Eight are American Conference games. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
