The teaser image features a real raven with the Ravens logo and the date 4.16.26, and the team posted a hype video on social media to go with it.

## What We Know So Far

The Ravens have not leaked any images of the actual jerseys yet. All we have is the "The Next Flight" branding and the April 16 date. No fabric, no colors, no wordmarks. Just a raven and a promise.

That said, the timing lines up with the broader NFL uniform cycle this offseason. The Falcons already dropped their new set on April 2, the Commanders are revealing theirs on April 15, and now the Ravens are going the day after on April 16. It is shaping up to be the busiest uniform week in recent NFL history.

## What We Want to See

The Ravens have had essentially the same uniform template since the franchise started in 1996, with some tweaks along the way. The purple, black, and white color scheme has always been strong, but the jersey designs themselves have never quite matched the quality of the logo and helmet. The shield crest on the chest has looked dated for a while, and the number font has been unchanged since day one.

We want to see the Ravens modernize without overthinking it. A cleaner chest design, updated number fonts, and maybe a fresh take on the sleeve striping. The Color Rush gold pants they wore a few years ago proved that this team can pull off a bold look when they want to. If the new set takes some of that energy and applies it to the full uniform, we are in for something good.
