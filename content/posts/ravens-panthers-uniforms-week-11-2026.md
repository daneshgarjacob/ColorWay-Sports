**Both teams have shown their uniforms for Sunday, and it is a look straight out of the 1990s.** The **Baltimore Ravens** visit the **Carolina Panthers** at **Bank of America Stadium** on **Sunday, November 22, at 1:00 p.m. Eastern on FOX**. Baltimore wears the **black helmet, white jersey, purple pants and white socks**. Carolina wears the **silver helmet, black jersey, silver pants and black socks**. Both combinations come from the teams' own game-week posts.

## Quick Answers

- **What are the Ravens wearing against the Panthers?** The black helmet, white jersey, purple pants and white socks.
- **What are the Panthers wearing against the Ravens?** The silver helmet, black jersey, silver pants and black socks.
- **Is it confirmed?** Yes. Both teams posted their uniforms this week.
- **What time is the game, and what channel?** Sunday, November 22, 1:00 p.m. Eastern on FOX.
- **Records:** Ravens 4-6, Panthers 4-5, per ESPN.

## What The Ravens Are Wearing

Baltimore's "Gameday Threads" graphic lists four pieces: the **primary helmet**, which is the black helmet with the raven head, the **white jersey** with purple numbers, **purple pants** and **white socks**.

## What The Panthers Are Wearing

Carolina's "Game Threads" graphic spells it out: **silver helmet, black jersey, silver pants, black socks**. The Panthers' schedule, published in September, had the black jersey for this game, and the helmet, pants and socks came with Friday's post.

The silver pants are the news. This is the first time in the 2026 regular season that Carolina has worn them. In both of its earlier black-jersey games, against Detroit in Week 4 and Denver in Week 9, the Panthers wore black pants. Silver pants were the Panthers' standard pants when the team started in 1995, which is why this combination looks like the 1990s. Every Panthers date is on the [Panthers 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/panthers-uniform-schedule-2026).

## How It Will Look On The Field

Each team brings a color the other does not have. Carolina is dark in the middle, with black jerseys between a silver helmet and silver pants. Baltimore is light in the middle, with a white jersey between a black helmet and purple pants. The silver and the purple are the two pieces that will stand out on TV, and nothing clashes.

## Our Grade

**Matchup: B (provisional).** Both teams picked combinations with a contrast color in the pants instead of matching everything, and the silver pants give Carolina's black jersey a lift it did not have in its all-black games.
