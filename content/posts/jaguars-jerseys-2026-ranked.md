The Jaguars have three jerseys on the 2026 schedule: teal at home, white on the road, and the new Bold City Rivalries uniform for one November game. The black alternate and the Prowler throwback are still in the closet, but neither has a 2026 date. Jacksonville owns the one color in the NFL nobody else has, and how much of it each jersey shows decides this entire ranking. We put the Jaguars thirty-first in our [best NFL uniforms of 2026](/stories/best-nfl-uniforms-2026-ranked) at a C, and that grade is mostly about how rarely the uniform leans on the teal.

Here are all three Jacksonville Jaguars jerseys for 2026, ranked worst to first and graded, with the helmet and pants each one wears. For the week-by-week jersey schedule 2026, the [Jaguars 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/jaguars-uniform-schedule-2026) has every game.

White is the road jersey, worn under the black helmet. It projects to eight of the nine road games: at the Broncos in Week 2, the Bengals, Ravens, Titans, Giants, Bears and Texans, and the Week 18 finale at Indianapolis. The ninth, Week 16 at Dallas on Sunday, December 27, is open, because the Cowboys wear white at home and that is the one road game where Jacksonville could wear teal. None of the road jerseys is team-confirmed, so these follow the standard convention and are our projection.

The problem is what the white jersey does with the franchise's best asset. Black carries most of the design, and teal shows up only in small doses, so the jersey reads as black and white with a few shades of teal thrown in. The jaguar logo on the side of the jersey has not worked for years either. Put this set on a broadcast next to a team in color and it could belong to almost anyone.

It grades a C. Jacksonville owns one of the best color stories in the league, and on the road it leaves nearly all of it at home.

Bold City is Jacksonville's entry in the NFL x Nike Rivalries program, revealed on August 25 under the motto "Fortune Favors the Bold." The jersey is alabaster, an off-white cream, with a cursive Jaguars script outlined in gold and numbers printed with a jaguar-spot pattern, gold trim and a teal shadow. The inner collar reads Bold City and the outer collar reads Since '95. The pants are black with an all-over spot print and a teal and gold stripe, and the helmet is a gloss teal shell with a metallic gold face mask and 904 on the front bumper. It debuts at home against the Indianapolis Colts on Sunday, November 1.

The helmet is the best thing here by a wide margin, and it makes the case for more teal headwear in Jacksonville all by itself. Below it, the reasoning gets thinner. Alabaster and gold read closer to New Orleans than Jacksonville, the 904 bumper is the third area-code reference we have graded this year, and the spotted pants look sharp in a studio and turn to noise from the upper deck.

It grades a C+, the same mark we gave it in our [Rivalries uniforms ranking](/stories/nfl-rivalries-uniforms-2026-ranked), where it placed sixth of sixteen. It ranks above the white jersey because at least it puts teal on the most visible piece of the uniform.

Teal is the home jersey and the one Jacksonville actually wears. It opened the season against the Cleveland Browns under the black helmet with white pants, and the Jaguars won 34-10. It projects to the six other home dates not claimed by Bold City: the New England Patriots in Week 3, back-to-back London games against the Philadelphia Eagles on Sunday, October 11 and the Houston Texans on Sunday, October 18 (Jacksonville is the designated home team for both), the Tennessee Titans in Week 12, the Pittsburgh Steelers on Monday Night Football in Week 14, and the Washington Commanders in Week 17. It could also travel to Dallas in Week 16. Only Week 1 is confirmed by what was worn, so the rest is our projection.

Teal is the only part of the Jaguars' identity nobody else in the NFL has, and the years Jacksonville leaned on black instead were the years the brand looked most anonymous. Gold trim gives it a second note, and the black shell keeps it from reading as one pastel block. White pants are the version to wear. Teal over teal is the monochrome option the team used last season, and it works better in small doses.

It grades a B-, the top of a thin closet. It is the right jersey, and it still does less with the teal than the color deserves. The Maurice Jones-Drew era sets showed how far this franchise can push it.

## The Bottom Line on the Jaguars Jersey Rankings

The Jaguars jerseys rank by how much teal they show. The teal home jersey is a B- and the only one that looks unmistakably like Jacksonville. Bold City is a C+ held up by a beautiful teal helmet, and the white road set is a C that barely uses the color at all.

This franchise has the rarest asset in the league and a closet that keeps hiding it. The fix is not complicated: more teal, and a road set that treats it as the main color instead of trim.
