**TCU has a new helmet for Saturday.** The Horned Frogs posted the look for their **Freedom Frogs** game on Friday morning, a day before Kansas State visits Amon G. Carter Stadium on **Saturday, November 14, at 2:00 p.m. Eastern on ESPNU**. The jersey is TCU's purple. The helmet is the story: **white, with a purple diamond stripe down the middle and the horned frog head on the side**. TCU's own caption: "everything old is new again."

## Quick Answers

- **What is TCU wearing against Kansas State?** A white helmet with a purple diamond center stripe and the horned frog head logo, and the purple jersey with silver numbers.
- **When?** Saturday, November 14, 2:00 p.m. Eastern, ESPNU, at Amon G. Carter Stadium in Fort Worth.
- **What is the Freedom Frogs game?** TCU's military appreciation game.
- **Our grade:** B.

## What TCU Is Wearing

The jersey is the standard **purple** home jersey with **silver numbers trimmed in white**, the purple we expected for this game on our TCU schedule, with a patch on the chest for the occasion.

The helmet is the change. It is **white**, with a center stripe made of **purple diamonds**, and the side logo is the **horned frog head**, the snarling frog with its horns swept back, in purple. The bumper reads "Give 'Em Hell." TCU is calling it a look back ("everything old is new again"), and it is a sharp turn from the purple helmets the Frogs wear most weeks.

## Our Grade: B

The purple jersey is TCU at its best. That purple is one of the most distinct colors in college football, and silver numbers on it look right. It earns the grade on its own.

The helmet is where it slips. The frog head is a great logo, but a **white shell with a busy diamond stripe** pulls your eye away from the purple, and the whole thing reads louder than it needs to. Put the same frog on a purple helmet and this is an A. As it is, it's a **B**.
