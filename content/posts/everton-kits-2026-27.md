## The Home Shirt: B+

Royal blue with white logos and yellow on the collar and cuffs, finished with white shorts and blue socks. The reference is maritime signal flags, the ones flown along the city's historic docks, which is a more specific idea than most home shirts bother with. The yellow is the piece worth watching. Everton home kits usually sit in blue and white alone.


**Grade: B+**

## The Away Shirt: A-

The best shirt in the wardrobe. A crisp white base with fine navy vertical pinstripes, a navy V-neck and thick navy cuffs, each trimmed in a thin yellow line. It revives a rare 1950s Everton design worn by Dave Hickson, the centre-forward known as the Cannonball Kid. Pinstripes on a white away shirt is a hard thing to get right and this one is right. Out July 17, 2026.


**Grade: A-**
