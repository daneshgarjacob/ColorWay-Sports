**When the Lions wear their throwback on Thanksgiving, they are dressing like the team that started the tradition.** The plain Honolulu blue uniform with silver numbers copies what Detroit wore in 1934, its first season in the city. That was also the year the Lions played their first Thanksgiving game, against the Chicago Bears, on November 29, 1934.

So the throwback and the holiday share a birth year. Detroit wore the copy on Thanksgiving in 2017, 2023 and 2025. Here is the story of that first game, what the 1934 Lions actually looked like, how the throwback matches it, and what we know about this year's Thanksgiving game against the Bears.

## Quick Answers

- **Why do the Lions wear a 1934 throwback on Thanksgiving?** The throwback copies Detroit's first-season uniform from 1934, the same year the Lions began hosting a Thanksgiving game. Wearing it on the holiday ties the uniform to the tradition it started with.
- **When was the first Lions Thanksgiving game?** Thursday, November 29, 1934, against the Bears at University of Detroit Stadium.
- **Who won?** The Bears, 19-16, according to the Pro Football Hall of Fame.
- **How big was it?** All 26,000 tickets sold out two weeks before the game, and owner George A. Richards set up a 94-station NBC radio network to carry it nationally.
- **What does the throwback look like?** A solid Honolulu blue jersey with silver block numbers, no sleeve stripes and no shoulder numbers, with silver pants and a silver helmet with no logo.
- **When have the Lions worn it on Thanksgiving?** 2017 against Minnesota, and 2023 and 2025 against Green Bay.
- **Are they wearing it on Thanksgiving 2026?** It has not been announced. We expect the Honolulu blue home uniform against the Bears on November 26.
- **Our take (opinion):** It should be the Lions' Thanksgiving uniform every year.

## 1934: A New Team Needs A Big Game

The Lions were brand new in 1934. George A. Richards, a Detroit radio executive who owned station WJR, had bought the Portsmouth Spartans of Ohio and moved them to Detroit, as the Pro Football Hall of Fame and WDET tell it. The Spartans had worn purple and gold. Detroit got blue and silver (the story of that blue is in [why do the Lions wear Honolulu blue](/stories/why-do-the-lions-wear-honolulu-blue)).

The new team had a problem: Detroit was a Tigers town, and the Lions were an afterthought. Richards decided a game on Thanksgiving Day would draw attention, even though he knew scheduling a holiday game carried some risk, according to the Hall of Fame. He booked the defending world champion Chicago Bears.

## November 29, 1934: The First Thanksgiving Game

It was a showdown before kickoff. The Bears came in unbeaten at 11-0 and the Lions were 10-1, according to History.com. All 26,000 tickets at University of Detroit Stadium sold out two weeks before the game, the Hall of Fame reports.

The Bears won 19-16. The Hall of Fame calls the game "an all-time classic." Detroit lost, and the date stuck anyway.

## 94 Stations: Thanksgiving Football On National Radio

Richards was a radio man, and he treated the game like a broadcast event. Working with NBC Radio, he set up a 94-station network to carry the Lions and Bears across the country, according to the Pro Football Hall of Fame. A team in its first season in a new city was on national radio on a holiday afternoon.

The game has been played every year since, with one break: no Lions Thanksgiving game was played from 1939 through 1944, the Hall of Fame notes. It is the oldest tradition on the NFL schedule.

## What The 1934 Lions Actually Wore

The first Detroit uniform, as the Lions' own team history describes it, was a blue jersey with silver numbers and silver pants. You can see the jersey on the cover of the team's 1934 game program: Glenn Presnell, No. 3, in a blue jersey with light numbers. Presnell is also the halfback who said, years later, that he and his wife helped pick blue and silver from the color samples on Richards' table.

One honest caveat about the helmet. The modern throwback uses a plain silver helmet. The 1935 card art shows leather-colored helmets, so we are not going to tell you the 1934 team wore silver helmets on Thanksgiving. The jersey and pants are the part of the throwback the record clearly supports.

## 1935: Champions In Year Two

The look that started Thanksgiving also won Detroit its first title. In their second season, the Lions beat the New York Giants 26-7 for the 1935 NFL championship on December 15, 1935, according to Pro-Football-Reference. Michigan Governor Frank Fitzgerald then proclaimed April 18, 1936, a "Day of Champions," with the Lions named in the proclamation.

## 2017: The 1934 Look Comes Back

The current throwback arrived with Detroit's 2017 rebrand. The team said it "replicates what the team wore in their inaugural season in Detroit (1934)": a solid Honolulu blue jersey with silver block numbers, no sleeve stripes and no shoulder numbers. The Lions wore it on Thanksgiving, November 23, 2017, against the Minnesota Vikings, according to [DetroitLions.com](https://www.detroitlions.com/news/lions-to-wear-alternate-uniforms-this-season-19026253).

## Why Thanksgiving Is The Right Day For It

Detroit has plenty of chances to wear an alternate. The throwback makes the most sense on Thanksgiving for one reason: the date. The uniform is a copy of 1934, and 1934 is the year the Lions' Thanksgiving game began. On any other Sunday it is a nice old uniform. On Thanksgiving it is the uniform of the team that played the first one.

That is our read of why it fits, and we seeded it No. 2 in our [best NFL Thanksgiving uniforms bracket](/stories/best-nfl-thanksgiving-uniforms-bracket-2026), where readers are voting now.

## Our Take

This should be Detroit's Thanksgiving uniform every year. The Cowboys have made their 1960s Classic the standard on the holiday, and it has become part of how Thanksgiving looks. The Lions have a better story than Dallas: the Thanksgiving game is Detroit's, it started in 1934, and the team already owns a uniform from 1934. The plain blue jersey, the silver numbers and the logo-free silver helmet look good on their own. Against the Bears, the team that played the first one, it would be a perfect fit. Wear it every year.
