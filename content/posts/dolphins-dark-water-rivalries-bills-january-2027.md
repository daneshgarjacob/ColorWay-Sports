The Dolphins have circled the date. Miami announced it will wear its Dark Water Rivalries uniform for exactly one game this season, and it is the one you would have picked: a Week 17 home date against the Buffalo Bills on **Sunday, January 3, at 1:00 PM ET on CBS** at Hard Rock Stadium. One uniform, one game, and it happens to fall on the division rival that has owned the AFC East for most of the last decade.

That is the whole point of the Rivalries program, and Miami is using it exactly the way Nike drew it up.

## What the Dark Water Uniform Actually Is

The Dark Water set is Miami's entry in the [Nike and NFL Rivalries program](https://about.nike.com/en/newsroom/releases/nike-nfl-rivalries-program-announcement), the alternate initiative that gives a group of teams a special uniform to wear once a year against a division rival. Miami was part of the inaugural AFC East and NFC West group, and the Dolphins built the loudest, most fully realized look of the bunch.

It reads as black, but it is not. The base is a deep, near-black shade Nike calls "pitch blue," and the entire idea of the uniform is a team that hunts in dark water. The helmet is pitch blue with a matching blacked-out dolphin, and the "305" area code sits on the bumper where a number usually goes. From there the two classic Miami colors do all the accent work: the numbers are in the trademark aqua, and the "MIAMI" wordmark and the nameplate are in orange. Aqua and orange striping runs down the pants and the sleeve cuffs, so nothing about the color story leaves the franchise's own palette. It just turns all the lights down.

The result is the rare all-dark alternate that still looks unmistakably like the team wearing it. Plenty of clubs put on a blackout set and become anonymous. Miami went dark and somehow got more Miami, because the aqua glows harder against pitch blue than it ever does against white.

## Why the Bills Game Is the Right Stage

A Rivalries uniform only gets one appearance, so the matchup is the entire statement. Buffalo has been the class of the AFC East, and Miami handing its single best alternate to that specific Sunday is a way of saying this is the game the season is measured against. Week 17 in early January also means real stakes on the field, with playoff seeding usually still alive for both teams by then, and a prime-window home crowd at Hard Rock ready for a night look in the middle of the afternoon.

It is also just good scheduling for the aesthetic. The Dark Water set wants lights and contrast, and a January game under a low winter sun in South Florida is going to make the aqua pop on every broadcast angle. If you are going to wear it once, wear it here.

## The Bottom Line

The Dolphins own one of the best uniforms the Rivalries program has produced, and they have handed it to the one game on the schedule that deserves it. Mark January 3. Dark Water against the Bills is the look of the year in Miami, and it only comes out once.
