The 2026-27 NBA season tips off on **Tuesday, October 20**, with a three-game Opening Night on NBC and Peacock, and 22 more teams open on **Wednesday, October 21**. That is 14 games across the first two nights and 28 of the 30 clubs. The other two, Cleveland and Denver, open on Thursday.

The headline is at Madison Square Garden. The New York Knicks get their **championship rings** and raise their **first title banner since 1973**, and then they play **LeBron James** in his first regular season game for the Philadelphia 76ers. On Wednesday, Brooklyn opens in its **ABA Classic Edition throwback**, Houston plays its first regular season game in the **new ketchup and mustard uniforms**, Minnesota brings its **rebranded uniforms** to Miami for Giannis Antetokounmpo's Heat debut, and Indiana wears **white on the road** in New Orleans.

**Update, October 19:** Every Opening Night game now carries our matchup grade, in the game list below. The NBA's official game-by-game uniform schedule is out, and all 28 Opening Night calls on this page are now confirmed. Nine games changed from our earlier read. Atlanta wears white at Orlando, with the Magic in their Icon. Washington wears its navy Statement, "The District of Columbia" jersey, against Milwaukee. Chicago wears its black Statement at Toronto, with the Raptors in their Icon. Utah wears white at Memphis, with the Grizzlies in their Statement. New Orleans wears its Statement against Indiana. Houston opens in its new Icon at home, so it is Icon against Icon with Dallas. The Lakers wear their gold Icon at home against the Warriors' blue Icon. Phoenix wears its Statement at Portland. Sacramento wears white at the Clippers, with LA in its Icon. Thursday flipped too: the 76ers and Thunder wear their Icons at home, and the Cavaliers and Nuggets wear white.

Below is every look for both nights, starting with the special ones. Every call is **confirmed** by the NBA's official uniform schedule, published October 19, and matches each team's [2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/nba-uniform-schedule-2026-27) on our site. Teams can still change a jersey on the night, but it rarely happens. We will log what was actually worn the morning after.

The official schedule also shows how freely NBA teams pick home jerseys compared with NFL teams. Eight of the 14 home teams over the first two nights wear a colored jersey, and Brooklyn wears its Classic Edition. That is the same split as the first two nights of last season, when the official matchups had eight of the 14 home teams in their colored Icon jersey and one more in its Classic Edition.

## The Opening Night Storylines

### Philadelphia 76ers at New York Knicks: Ring Night at the Garden

**Tuesday, 7:00 p.m. ET, NBC and Peacock. Confirmed on both sides.** This is the Knicks' first banner raising and ring ceremony at Madison Square Garden in 53 years, and the opponent is a 76ers team that added LeBron James and Jaylen Brown over the summer. The NBA's official schedule has New York in the **Association white** at home and Philadelphia in its **royal blue Icon**, the jersey LeBron wore in **No. 23** at [Media Day](/stories/nba-media-day-2026-new-jerseys).

That settles the biggest question of the night. Champions sometimes go to their color for ring night, as Oklahoma City did with its blue Icon at home for its 2025 ring night, but the Knicks raise their banner in white. On Tuesday the Knicks showed that white with a twist: gold lettering, gold numbers, a gold 2025-26 Champions patch and a gold "3" tag at the collar. Every detail, and our A+ grade, is in our [Knicks gold ring night uniform breakdown](/stories/knicks-gold-championship-uniforms-ring-night-2026).

### Boston Celtics at Detroit Pistons: A 3 p.m. Tip to Start the Season

**Tuesday, 3:00 p.m. ET, NBC and Peacock. Confirmed on both sides.** The season starts in the afternoon with last season's top two seeds in the East. Detroit wears the **Association white** at Little Caesars Arena, and Boston its **green Icon**, with Paul George and former Knick Mitchell Robinson in green for the first time in a game that counts. Both clubs are in the new Specter Edition program, but those uniforms arrive later in the fall, with Detroit's pegged for November, so neither wears Specter on Tuesday. Our [Specter Edition guide](/stories/nba-specter-edition-jerseys-2026-27) has the details. See the [Pistons 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/pistons-uniform-schedule-2026-27) and the [Celtics 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/celtics-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### Oklahoma City Thunder at San Antonio Spurs: The Rematch

**Tuesday, 9:30 p.m. ET, NBC and Peacock. Confirmed on both sides.** San Antonio beat Oklahoma City in Game 7 of last season's Western Conference Finals, and the Spurs open at home against the Thunder. The Spurs wear their **Association white** at Frost Bank Center and the Thunder their **blue Icon**. The Spurs' new silver ABA Classic Edition does not debut until **November 4 against Dallas**, the team has said, so Tuesday is a regular uniform night. Every Classic date is in our [NBA Classic Edition rankings](/stories/nba-classic-edition-jerseys-courts-2026-27), and the rest is in the [Spurs 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/spurs-uniform-schedule-2026-27) and the [Thunder 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/oklahoma-city-thunder-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### Charlotte Hornets at Brooklyn Nets: The Classic Edition Opens the Season

**Wednesday, 7:30 p.m. ET. Confirmed on both sides.** Brooklyn tagged a jersey for all 80 games on its own schedule page, and Opening Night is the **Classic Edition**: white, with the red Nets script and its long underline, red numbers outlined in navy, and red, white and blue piping. It is the first of **16 Classic nights**, built around the ABA-NBA 50th anniversary and 60 seasons of Nets basketball, and the Nets say the jersey goes on sale October 19. We graded it an **A-**. With Brooklyn in white, Charlotte wears its **teal Icon**. It is also Julius Randle's first regular season game for the Nets. See the [Nets 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/nets-uniform-schedule-2026-27) and the [Hornets 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/hornets-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### Dallas Mavericks at Houston Rockets: Ketchup and Mustard Is Back

**Wednesday, 8:30 p.m. ET. Confirmed on both sides.** This is Houston's home opener and the first regular season game for the [Rockets' 2026-27 rebrand](/stories/houston-rockets-2026-rebrand-graded): new white, red and black uniforms with Championship Yellow back for the first time in 30 years. We graded all three jerseys a **B+**. The NBA's official schedule has the Rockets in their **new Icon Edition** at home, not the white we first expected, and Dallas in its **Icon** too, so both teams wear their Icon. It is also Houston's first regular season game on the new [Core Court](/stories/houston-rockets-court-2026-27-graded), which we graded a **C+**. See the [Rockets 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/rockets-uniform-schedule-2026-27) and the [Mavericks 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/mavericks-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### Minnesota Timberwolves at Miami Heat: Giannis and LaMelo Debut

**Wednesday, 7:30 p.m. ET, ESPN. Confirmed on both sides.** Two of the summer's biggest moves meet in Miami: Giannis Antetokounmpo's first regular season game for the Heat and LaMelo Ball's first for the Timberwolves. Both wore new jerseys at Media Day, Giannis in **No. 7** in Heat white and LaMelo in **No. 1** in the new Wolves white. The NBA's official schedule has Miami in the **Association white** at home and Minnesota in the **new blue Icon**, the jersey we called maybe the best blue in the league when the [Timberwolves rebrand](/stories/timberwolves-officially-release-new-uniforms-2026-27) dropped. We graded the full rebrand an **A+**. See the [Heat 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/miami-heat-uniform-schedule-2026-27) and the [Timberwolves 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/timberwolves-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### Five Teams in White on the Road

Five visiting teams wear white on Wednesday, which puts their hosts in color.

- **Atlanta at Orlando, 7:00 p.m. ET.** The Hawks wear their **Association white** and the Magic their **Icon Edition** at home. [Hawks 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/hawks-uniform-schedule-2026-27) and [Magic 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/orlando-magic-uniform-schedule-2026-27).
- **Milwaukee at Washington, 7:00 p.m. ET.** The Bucks wear the **Association white** for the opener in D.C., the first of 18 road games in white, and the Wizards answer in their **navy Statement**, "The District of Columbia" jersey. It is Anthony Davis and Trae Young's first opener as Wizards. [Bucks 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/bucks-uniform-schedule-2026-27) and [Wizards 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/wizards-uniform-schedule-2026-27).
- **Indiana at New Orleans, 8:00 p.m. ET.** The Pacers published their uniform schedule on September 18, and the opener is the **Association white** on the road. The Pelicans answer in their **Statement Edition**. The Pacers' gold pinstriped Statement, booked for 31 games, waits until Friday at home against Dallas. [Pacers 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/pacers-uniform-schedule-2026-27) and [Pelicans 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/pelicans-uniform-schedule-2026-27).
- **Utah at Memphis, 8:00 p.m. ET.** The Jazz wear their **Association white** and the Grizzlies their **Statement Edition**. [Jazz 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/utah-jazz-uniform-schedule-2026-27) and [Grizzlies 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/grizzlies-uniform-schedule-2026-27).
- **Sacramento at LA, 10:30 p.m. ET.** The Kings wear their **Association white** and the Clippers their **Icon Edition**. [Kings 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/sacramento-kings-uniform-schedule-2026-27) and [Clippers 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/clippers-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

- **Kawhi Leonard back in Toronto.** Kawhi is a Raptor again, and his first game back is the opener at home against Chicago. Toronto wears its **Icon Edition** and the Bulls their **black Statement**. [Raptors 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/raptors-uniform-schedule-2026-27) and [Bulls 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/bulls-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

[Trail Blazers 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/trail-blazers-uniform-schedule-2026-27) and [Suns 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/suns-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

Both clubs are Specter Edition teams too, but neither wears Specter on Opening Night. [Lakers 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/lakers-uniform-schedule-2026-27) and [Warriors 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/warriors-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

**When is NBA Opening Night 2026?**

Tuesday, October 20, 2026, with three games on NBC and Peacock: Celtics at Pistons at 3:00 p.m. Eastern, 76ers at Knicks at 7:00 p.m. and Thunder at Spurs at 9:30 p.m. Eleven more games follow on Wednesday, October 21, and the Cavaliers and Nuggets open on Thursday, October 22.

No. 23, in the 76ers' royal blue Icon at Madison Square Garden, the jersey LeBron wore at Media Day. The NBA's official uniform schedule confirms it, with the Knicks in white.

The Classic Edition, the white throwback with the red Nets script, against Charlotte on Wednesday, October 21, at 7:30 p.m. Eastern. It is on the Nets' own schedule page and Classic Edition schedule, and it is the first of 16 Classic nights this season.

No. The Spurs' Classic Edition debuts on November 4 against Dallas. Against the Thunder on October 20, San Antonio wears its regular Association white (confirmed).

**What are the Lakers wearing on Opening Night?**

The gold Icon at home against the Warriors on Wednesday, October 21, at 10:00 p.m. Eastern on ESPN. Golden State wears its blue Icon, so neither team is in white.
