The 2026-27 NBA season tips off on **Tuesday, October 20**, with a three-game Opening Night on NBC and Peacock, and 22 more teams open on **Wednesday, October 21**. That is 14 games across the first two nights and 28 of the 30 clubs. The other two, Cleveland and Denver, open on Thursday.

The headline is at Madison Square Garden. The New York Knicks get their **championship rings** and raise their **first title banner since 1973**, and then they play **LeBron James** in his first regular season game for the Philadelphia 76ers. On Wednesday, Brooklyn opens in its **ABA Classic Edition throwback**, Houston plays its first regular season game in the **new ketchup and mustard uniforms**, Minnesota brings its **rebranded uniforms** to Miami for Giannis Antetokounmpo's Heat debut, and Indiana wears **white on the road** in New Orleans.

Below is every look we know for both nights, starting with the special ones. **Confirmed** means the team has published the jersey for that game. **Expected** is our read from that team's [2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/nba-uniform-schedule-2026-27), which follows the standard pattern of white at home and color on the road. NBA LockerVision, the league's official jersey assignment site, had not posted any 2026-27 games when we checked on October 17, so most of these are still expected. We will flip each one to what was actually worn the morning after.

One warning on the expected calls. NBA teams pick home jerseys far more freely than NFL teams do. Over the first two nights of last season, LockerVision had eight of the 14 home teams in their colored Icon jersey and one more in its Classic Edition, so treat every expected home white as the softest part of this page.

## The Opening Night Storylines

### Philadelphia 76ers at New York Knicks: Ring Night at the Garden

**Tuesday, 7:00 p.m. ET, NBC and Peacock. Expected on both sides.** This is the Knicks' first banner raising and ring ceremony at Madison Square Garden in 53 years, and the opponent is a 76ers team that added LeBron James and Jaylen Brown over the summer. Our Knicks schedule has New York in the **Association white** at home, which sends Philadelphia out in its **royal blue Icon**, the jersey LeBron wore in **No. 23** at [Media Day](/stories/nba-media-day-2026-new-jerseys). Neither team has announced a jersey for the game.

The white is the call we are least sure of all night. Champions sometimes go to their color for ring night: Oklahoma City wore its blue Icon at home for its 2025 ring night, per LockerVision. If the Knicks go orange and blue instead, Philadelphia would switch to white. We will update this as soon as either team shows its lockers.

LeBron's number and why it is not 6 are in our [LeBron 76ers jersey number explainer](/stories/lebron-james-76ers-jersey-number). If you want to see how little the Knicks' look has changed since the last banner, our [1973 vs 2026 Knicks championship uniforms](/stories/knicks-1973-vs-2026-championship-uniforms-compared) puts them side by side. More dates in the [Knicks 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/knicks-uniform-schedule-2026-27) and the [76ers 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/76ers-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### Boston Celtics at Detroit Pistons: A 3 p.m. Tip to Start the Season

**Tuesday, 3:00 p.m. ET, NBC and Peacock. Expected on both sides.** The season starts in the afternoon with last season's top two seeds in the East. Detroit is expected in the **Association white** at Little Caesars Arena, and Boston in its **green Icon**, with Paul George and former Knick Mitchell Robinson in green for the first time in a game that counts. Both clubs are in the new Specter Edition program, but those uniforms arrive later in the fall, with Detroit's pegged for November, so do not expect them on Tuesday. Our [Specter Edition guide](/stories/nba-specter-edition-jerseys-2026-27) has the details. See the [Pistons 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/pistons-uniform-schedule-2026-27) and the [Celtics 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/celtics-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### Oklahoma City Thunder at San Antonio Spurs: The Rematch

**Tuesday, 9:30 p.m. ET, NBC and Peacock. Expected on both sides.** San Antonio beat Oklahoma City in Game 7 of last season's Western Conference Finals, and the Spurs open at home against the Thunder. We expect the Spurs in **white** at Frost Bank Center and the Thunder in their **blue Icon**. The Spurs' new silver ABA Classic Edition does not debut until **November 4 against Dallas**, the team has said, so Tuesday is a regular uniform night. Every Classic date is in our [NBA Classic Edition rankings](/stories/nba-classic-edition-jerseys-courts-2026-27), and the rest is in the [Spurs 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/spurs-uniform-schedule-2026-27) and the [Thunder 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/oklahoma-city-thunder-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### Charlotte Hornets at Brooklyn Nets: The Classic Edition Opens the Season

**Wednesday, 7:30 p.m. ET. Nets confirmed, Hornets expected.** Brooklyn tagged a jersey for all 80 games on its own schedule page, and Opening Night is the **Classic Edition**: white, with the red Nets script and its long underline, red numbers outlined in navy, and red, white and blue piping. It is the first of **16 Classic nights**, built around the ABA-NBA 50th anniversary and 60 seasons of Nets basketball, and the Nets say the jersey goes on sale October 19. We graded it an **A-**. With Brooklyn in white, Charlotte is expected in its **teal Icon**. It is also Julius Randle's first regular season game for the Nets. See the [Nets 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/nets-uniform-schedule-2026-27) and the [Hornets 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/hornets-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### Dallas Mavericks at Houston Rockets: Ketchup and Mustard Is Back

**Wednesday, 8:30 p.m. ET. Expected on both sides.** This is Houston's home opener and the first regular season game for the [Rockets' 2026-27 rebrand](/stories/houston-rockets-2026-rebrand-graded): new white, red and black uniforms with Championship Yellow back for the first time in 30 years. We graded all three jerseys a **B+**. Our schedule has the Rockets in the **new Association white** and Dallas in its **Icon**. It is also Houston's first regular season game on the new [Core Court](/stories/houston-rockets-court-2026-27-graded), which we graded a **C+**. See the [Rockets 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/rockets-uniform-schedule-2026-27) and the [Mavericks 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/mavericks-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

### Minnesota Timberwolves at Miami Heat: Giannis and LaMelo Debut

**Wednesday, 7:30 p.m. ET, ESPN. Expected on both sides.** Two of the summer's biggest moves meet in Miami: Giannis Antetokounmpo's first regular season game for the Heat and LaMelo Ball's first for the Timberwolves. Both wore new jerseys at Media Day, Giannis in **No. 7** in Heat white and LaMelo in **No. 1** in the new Wolves white. Our schedule has Miami in the **Association white** at home, which puts Minnesota in the **new blue Icon**, the jersey we called maybe the best blue in the league when the [Timberwolves rebrand](/stories/timberwolves-officially-release-new-uniforms-2026-27) dropped. We graded the full rebrand an **A+**. See the [Heat 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/miami-heat-uniform-schedule-2026-27) and the [Timberwolves 2026-27 uniform schedule](/stories/timberwolves-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When is NBA Opening Night 2026?**

Tuesday, October 20, 2026, with three games on NBC and Peacock: Celtics at Pistons at 3:00 p.m. Eastern, 76ers at Knicks at 7:00 p.m. and Thunder at Spurs at 9:30 p.m. Eleven more games follow on Wednesday, October 21, and the Cavaliers and Nuggets open on Thursday, October 22.

**What are the Knicks wearing on ring night?**

The Knicks have not announced it. Our schedule has them in the Association white at home against the 76ers on Tuesday, October 20, at 7:00 p.m. Eastern on NBC. Champions sometimes wear their color on ring night, as Oklahoma City did in 2025, so we will update this as soon as the Knicks show their lockers.

**What jersey is LeBron James wearing in his 76ers debut?**

No. 23. We expect the 76ers in their royal blue Icon at Madison Square Garden, the jersey LeBron wore at Media Day, because the Knicks are expected in white. If New York wears a colored jersey instead, Philadelphia would switch to white.

**What are the Nets wearing on Opening Night?**

The Classic Edition, the white throwback with the red Nets script, against Charlotte on Wednesday, October 21, at 7:30 p.m. Eastern. It is on the Nets' own schedule page and Classic Edition schedule, and it is the first of 16 Classic nights this season.

**When do the Rockets wear their new uniforms for the first time?**

Their home opener against Dallas on Wednesday, October 21, at 8:30 p.m. Eastern at Toyota Center is their first regular season game in the new uniforms, which were revealed on June 4. We expect the new white at home.

**Are the Spurs wearing their ABA throwback on Opening Night?**

No. The Spurs' Classic Edition debuts on November 4 against Dallas. Against the Thunder on October 20 we expect San Antonio in its regular white.

**What are Giannis Antetokounmpo and LaMelo Ball wearing in their debuts?**

Giannis wears No. 7 for Miami and LaMelo No. 1 for Minnesota. We expect the Heat in white at home and the Timberwolves in their new blue Icon on Wednesday, October 21, at 7:30 p.m. Eastern on ESPN. Neither team has announced the jerseys.

**Which NBA teams have announced their Opening Night jerseys?**

Three so far: the Bucks (Association white at Washington), the Nets (Classic Edition against Charlotte) and the Pacers (Association white at New Orleans). Everything else on this page is expected until the teams or NBA LockerVision post it.
