The Panthers wear three jerseys in 2026, black, Panther Blue and white, and for the first time we know exactly when each one comes out. On September 4 Carolina published a jersey for all seventeen games: black six times at home, Panther Blue three times, and white on every road trip. There is no throwback and no Rivalries set this year, so all of the variety comes from mixing two helmets and three pairs of pants. We put Carolina twenty-third in our [best NFL uniforms of 2026](/stories/best-nfl-uniforms-2026-ranked) at a B-, and the ranking below explains most of that grade: the team's best jersey is the one it wears least.

Here are all three Carolina Panthers jerseys for 2026, ranked worst to first and graded, with the helmet and pants each one wears. For the week-by-week jersey schedule 2026, the [Panthers 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/panthers-uniform-schedule-2026) has every game.

Black is Carolina's primary home jersey this season, confirmed for six games: the Detroit Lions on Sunday Night Football in Week 4, the Denver Broncos in Week 9, the Baltimore Ravens in Week 11, the Cincinnati Bengals in Week 15, the Seattle Seahawks in Week 17 and the Atlanta Falcons in the Week 18 finale. The helmet and pants are announced during game week, and Carolina has black and silver helmets and black, silver and white pants to pick from.

The jersey is well made and completely anonymous. Half the league owns a black jersey, and in a division where Atlanta just went back to red and New Orleans owns black and gold outright, black is the least distinctive thing Carolina could wear. The all-black version has its fans and it does look sharp under lights. It just does not look like Carolina specifically.

It grades a C+. Handing it the Sunday night game against Detroit, the biggest home audience of the year, is the call we would argue with most.

White is the road jersey, confirmed for all eight away games: at the Falcons in Week 2, the Browns, the Eagles, the Packers on Thursday night, the Saints, the Buccaneers on Monday night, the Vikings and the Steelers. The helmet and pants are announced during game week, and for Week 2 our read is the silver helmet with white pants.

This is where the Panther Blue does its best work outside the blue jersey itself. On white, the process blue trim and the blue cat logo get room to read, and with silver pants the whole look ties back to the silver helmet the way a Carolina uniform should. What holds it down is the same thing that holds the whole set down: every piece is a notch quieter than the colors deserve, and on a broadcast it can pass for several other teams.

It grades a B-. It is a solid road jersey and a more Carolina look than the black one, because the blue is doing the talking.

Panther Blue is confirmed for three home games: the Chicago Bears in Week 1, the Tampa Bay Buccaneers in Week 7 and the New Orleans Saints in Week 14. Two of the three are division games, which reads like a deliberate choice to save the most distinctive shirt for the teams Carolina plays twice.

The opener showed how to wear it. Carolina came out in the black helmet, Panther Blue jersey and black pants, the same combination it used in the 2024 Germany game and the 2025 home opener, and the black at both ends gives the loud blue hard edges. Chicago won 59-37, but the uniform had a much better day than the defense. With the silver helmet instead, the blue gets a lighter frame that is just as good and more classically Carolina.

It grades a B+, the top of the closet. Panther Blue is a process blue, brighter and cooler than the navies all over the NFC, and it is the only look Carolina owns that could not belong to another team. We think it should be the primary. Three games is not enough.

## The Bottom Line on the Panthers Jersey Rankings

Carolina has its order upside down. Panther Blue is a B+ and the only jersey that is unmistakably Carolina, white is a B- that works because of the blue trim, and black is a C+ that gets six home games, including the Sunday night showcase.

The colors are among the best in the league. The uniform is a notch below where that palette says it should be, and the easiest fix does not need a redesign. Wear the blue more.
