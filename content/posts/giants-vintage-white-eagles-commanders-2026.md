## What the Giants' Vintage White Uniform Looks Like

It is the road uniform the Giants wore through the 1980s and 1990s, rebuilt in 2016 as their Color Rush set:

- **Helmet:** navy, with the italic "GIANTS" wordmark in white with a red outline, a red center stripe and a white facemask.
- **Jersey:** a slightly warm white, with royal blue numbers outlined in red and blue and red stripes on the sleeves.
- **Pants:** white, with a blue and red stripe down the side.

The Giants also posted the color palette behind it: Royal Blue, Impact Red, Vintage White and the Legacy Blue of the helmet.

## When the Giants Wear Vintage White in 2026

- **Week 9: at Philadelphia, Sunday, November 8, 1:00 p.m. ET, FOX.** The Eagles wear **Kelly Green**, so this game is two 1980s uniforms against each other.
- **Week 10: vs. Washington, Thursday, November 12, 8:15 p.m. ET, Prime Video.** The Commanders come to MetLife in burgundy.

Those are the only two Vintage White games this season. The Giants wear the Legacy Blue throwback, its home twin, one more time, on December 6 against the 49ers. Every other week is in our [Giants 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/new-york-giants-uniform-schedule-2026).

## Our Grade: A

This is the best uniform the Giants own, and it is not close. The red outline on the blue numbers gives the jersey a little warmth that the regular white set doesn't have. The navy wordmark helmet looks great with it. And the white isn't stark: it reads like a jersey that has been through a few seasons.

It is even better because of who it's against. **Vintage White at Lincoln Financial Field against Kelly Green** is the best-looking game of Week 9, and maybe of the season. Four days later it gets a national Thursday night against burgundy and gold.

Our only complaint is the count. Two games a year is not enough. This should be the Giants' full-time road uniform.

A white jersey with royal blue numbers outlined in red, white pants and the navy "GIANTS" wordmark helmet. It brings back the road look the Giants wore in the 1980s and 1990s, and it was introduced as their Color Rush uniform in 2016.

**Is Vintage White the same as the Giants' Legacy uniform?**
They are a pair. The Legacy uniform is the royal blue home version with the same wordmark helmet. Vintage White is the road version.
