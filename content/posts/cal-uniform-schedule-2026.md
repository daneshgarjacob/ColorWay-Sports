Cal opened the Tosh Lupoi era by pulling the single best uniform in its closet out of storage, and the season has been about blue and gold ever since. This is the full Cal 2026 uniform schedule: the blue home set, the white road set, the Joe Roth throwback that came back for UCLA, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

Everything here is Nike, and the base jerseys date to the 2024 redesign that stripped out the Sather Stripe Cal had carried for a decade and replaced it with plain shoulder striping pulled straight from the program's older sets. There is one new piece of real estate on the 2026 jersey that has nothing to do with striping: Cal added a jersey patch sponsor over the summer, and every one of the school's thirty sports is wearing it.

The throwback is the set people care about. Joe Roth was Cal's quarterback in 1975 and 1976, finished ninth in the Heisman voting, and died of melanoma in February 1977 at 21 years old. His No. 12 is the only retired number in the history of Cal football, and the hip patch on these pants is a gold football outline with a blue block C and a 12 inside it. Cal used to save the set for the home game against UCLA or USC. Then both schools left for the Big Ten, Cal left for the ACC, and the uniform effectively went into a closet with the rivalry. UCLA landed on the 2026 schedule in Week 1, and the uniform came back with it.

The reveal is worth a close look, because the blue is the whole argument. It is a brighter royal than the Berkeley navy Cal has worn in recent seasons, and against the gold helmet and gold pants it reads like a photograph from 1976 rather than a modern jersey with a retro sticker on it.

Cal published its home themes in July, and they double as a decent map of where a special uniform could land. Gold Out for Clemson on a Friday night, Homecoming for Virginia Tech, a first-ever Grateful Dead theme game against Wake Forest, and the 129th Big Game against Stanford.

**September 5 vs. UCLA.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **gold helmet, royal blue jersey, gold pants**, the Joe Roth throwback for Lupoi's first game at California Memorial Stadium. UCLA won 45-24. One calendar note: ESPN files this game under September 6 because its schedule feed runs on UTC and a 7:30 p.m. Pacific kickoff is already the next day in London. The correct local date is Saturday, September 5.

**September 12 at Syracuse.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **blue helmet, white jersey, white pants**, the standard road set with the gold Cal script on the navy shell. Cal won 21-18 in the dome. Syracuse hosted in orange helmets, navy jerseys and orange pants, which we track on the [Syracuse 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/syracuse-uniform-schedule-2026).

**September 19 vs. Wagner.** Blue expected. Rooted in the Bay Day, 12:30 p.m. Pacific, and a noon kickoff against an FCS opponent is the least likely slot on the calendar for anything unusual.

**September 25 vs. Clemson.** Blue expected. This is the Friday night ACC opener, it is the Gold Out, and it is the one home date where we would not be surprised by the gold Big C helmet instead of the navy shell. Clemson in white against Berkeley Blue is one of the cleaner color pairings Cal gets all year.

**October 3 at UNLV.** White expected. A rare non-conference road trip in October, played indoors at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas with UNLV as the home team.

**October 10 vs. Virginia Tech.** Blue expected. Homecoming, which at most programs is the traditional slot for an alternate if one exists.

**October 17 vs. Wake Forest.** Blue expected, with an asterisk. This is Cal's first Grateful Dead theme game, built around the band's long history of playing on and around campus. Nothing has been announced on the uniform side, but if there is one date in 2026 where Cal puts something new on the field, this is it.

**October 24 at SMU.** White expected. Cal visits Gerald J. Ford Stadium, where SMU has Fan Appreciation Day and is asking its own fans to wear red, so this could be the rare road game where Cal's white gets a red backdrop instead of a blue one.

**October 31 at NC State.** White expected. Halloween in Raleigh.

**November 7.** Bye.

**November 14 at Virginia.** White expected. The last road game of the season, in Charlottesville.

**November 21 vs. Stanford.** Blue expected. The 129th Big Game and the battle for the Axe, the oldest rivalry on the West Coast, back in Berkeley.

**November 28 vs. Pitt.** Blue expected. Hometown Heroes and the final home game of the year.

## The Bottom Line

Two games in, Cal has worn the best throwback of the 2026 season and its plainest road set, which is a fair preview of how the rest of the fall should go. Blue is the default at home, white is locked in for all five road dates, and the Joe Roth uniform is the one that moves the needle. The open questions are whether the gold Big C helmet surfaces for the Clemson Gold Out and whether the Grateful Bears game gets a jersey to match the theme. The grid above has every date, and we fill in the stars as each uniform is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Cal wearing this week?**

Cal hosts Wagner on Saturday, September 19 at 12:30 p.m. Pacific for Rooted in the Bay Day, and the blue home set is expected. The Bears wore the white road uniform in their last game, a 21-18 win at Syracuse on September 12. The week-by-week grid above lists every game with the expected set, and we update it as combinations are confirmed.

**What are Cal's football uniforms for 2026?**

A Berkeley Blue home jersey with California Gold numerals and gold and white shoulder stripes, a white road jersey with blue numerals trimmed in gold, and the royal blue Joe Roth throwback. The helmets are a blue shell with the gold Cal script, a gold shell with the blue Big C, and a gold throwback shell with the blue script. All of it is Nike, and every jersey carries the school's new jersey patch sponsor.

**What are the Joe Roth uniforms?**

They are Cal's tribute to quarterback Joe Roth, who played in 1975 and 1976, finished ninth in the 1976 Heisman voting and died of melanoma in February 1977 at 21. His No. 12 is the only retired number in Cal football history. The set is a gold helmet with the blue script, a royal blue jersey, gold pants and a gold football patch on the hip with a blue C and a 12 inside it. Cal wore it against UCLA on September 5.

**Does Cal have a new alternate uniform in 2026?**

Nothing new has been announced. The Joe Roth throwback is the special set this season, and it has already been worn. The Grateful Bears theme game against Wake Forest on October 17 and the Gold Out against Clemson on September 25 are the two home dates we are watching, and we will add anything that gets revealed to this page.

**Why does ESPN list the UCLA game on September 6?**

ESPN's schedule feed runs on UTC. A 7:30 p.m. Pacific kickoff on Saturday, September 5 is already Sunday in London, so the game shows a September 6 date there. It was a Saturday night game in Berkeley.

**How many games does Cal play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven at California Memorial Stadium and five on the road, with a bye on November 7. Four of the five road trips are ACC games and the fifth is at UNLV. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
