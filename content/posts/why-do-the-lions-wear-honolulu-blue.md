**Detroit is about 4,000 miles from Honolulu, and its football team has worn a color named for the city since the 1930s.** The blue is official, it is on every Lions uniform in 2026, and the story of how it got its name is one of the least settled in the NFL.

The short version: the Lions started in Ohio as the Portsmouth Spartans, in purple and gold. When radio man George A. Richards bought the team in 1934 and moved it to Detroit, he dropped those colors for blue and silver. Who actually picked the blue, and where the word "Honolulu" came from, depends on which account you trust. Here are both stories, what the record supports, and how the colors have changed since.

## Quick Answers

- **Why do the Lions wear Honolulu blue?** Owner George A. Richards chose blue and silver in 1934 to replace the Portsmouth Spartans' purple and gold when he moved the team to Detroit.
- **Why is it called Honolulu blue?** The most repeated story, from an old Lions media guide, is that the color reminded Richards of the ocean he saw on a trip to Hawaii. It is reported, not documented.
- **Who picked it?** Disputed. Tailback Glenn Presnell said he and his wife picked blue and silver from the color options on Richards' table. A 1993 team history credits general manager Cy Huston.
- **What are the Lions' official colors?** Honolulu blue and silver. White and black are also in the current set.
- **What is the hex code for Honolulu blue?** #0076B6 (RGB 0, 118, 182) is the widely published value. Silver is #B0B7BC.
- **Have the Lions always worn silver pants?** Yes, from the first Detroit uniform in 1934: blue jersey, silver numbers, silver pants, silver helmet.
- **The 2026 hook:** Detroit is 3-0 this season in silver pants, including the 35-10 win over New England in Munich on Sunday.
- **Our take (opinion):** Honolulu blue and silver is the best color pairing in the NFC, and the 2024 redesign finally treated it that way.

## From Portsmouth Purple To Detroit Blue

The franchise joined the NFL in 1930 as the Portsmouth Spartans, from a small city on the Ohio River. The Spartans wore purple and gold. They were good on the field (they lost the 1932 league title playoff game to the Bears, 9-0) and they never made money. Historian Drew Feight told WDET in 2024 that ticket sales were never large enough to cover star salaries.

In the spring of 1934, George A. Richards, who owned Detroit radio station WJR, bought the team for less than $8,000, according to WDET. He moved it to Detroit, named it the Lions to pair with the city's baseball Tigers, and wanted new colors that belonged to the new city. The first Detroit uniform, as the Lions' own history describes it, was a blue jersey with silver numbers, silver pants and a silver helmet. Detroit won the NFL championship in its second season, 1935, in that look.

## Origin Story One: The Hawaii Trip

This is the version most fans know. An old Lions media guide, cited by WDET, says Richards named the blue "Honolulu" because it reminded him of ocean water he had seen on a trip to Hawaii. ClickOnDetroit and Wikipedia repeat it, and Wikipedia hedges with "supposedly."

The problem is the paper trail. The Detroit Historical Society, in its piece on the story of Honolulu blue, notes that the Lions' 1950 media guide describes the uniform without using the name "Honolulu Blue" at all. The society's search of the Detroit Free Press turned up the phrase in a classified ad for a Buick Special painted "Honolulu Blue," which suggests the name was already a known paint color in Detroit before it was firmly attached to the football team. The Hawaii trip may have happened. Nobody has produced a document from 1934 that says so.

## Origin Story Two: The Color Samples And Glenn Presnell's Wife

The other story comes from someone who was in the room. Glenn Presnell, a halfback who led the league in scoring in 1933 and was the last surviving member of the 1934 Lions, told journalist Bill Dow in a 1999 interview that Richards had a table of uniform color options, including orange and black and red and white. Richards invited Presnell and his wife to look. "My wife saw that Honolulu blue and silver," Presnell said, as quoted by WDET, and they told Richards that was the one they liked. Richards went with it.

Bill Keenist, the Lions' longtime communications vice president and team historian, told ClickOnDetroit the same story: color swatches on Richards' table, Presnell and his wife invited in, and the couple loving the blue. Joe Horrigan, the Pro Football Hall of Fame's senior advisor, told the Detroit Free Press in 2023 that Presnell's wife "went right to the blue and silver."

## A Third Name: Cy Huston

There is one more claim. Mike Murray's 1993 team history, *Lions Pride: 60 Years of Detroit Lions Football*, quoted by the Detroit Free Press, credits Cy Huston, the new vice president and general manager, with going after blue uniforms and looking at many shades before landing on one that would work with silver. Huston is quoted calling it the kind of blue that "will match with silver." He does not call it Honolulu blue.

**What we can say for certain:** Richards made the final call in 1934, blue and silver replaced purple and gold, and the silver was part of the plan from the start. **What is disputed:** whether Presnell's wife, Huston or Richards himself picked the exact shade, and whether a Hawaii trip had anything to do with the name. The two stories do not have to cancel each other out. Richards could have liked the blue Presnell's wife pointed to and named it after a trip he remembered. Nobody can prove it either way.

The Lions' official colors are Honolulu blue and silver, and the current uniform set also uses white and black. The digital value published by color-reference sites such as Team Color Codes is **#0076B6**, RGB 0, 118, 182, listed at PMS 7462 C. Silver is **#B0B7BC**, RGB 176, 183, 188. Print values (CMYK and Pantone) vary from source to source, and the team does not publish a public brand guide, so treat the hex as the reference value rather than a team-issued spec.

The Detroit Free Press describes Honolulu blue as a medium-dark cyan blue, somewhere between royal and light blue on one side and navy and steel blue on the other. That is why it reads so differently from every other blue in the league: it is brighter than the Colts, Giants or Bills, and greener than the Chargers' powder blue.

## Silver Pants Came First

Silver is not a later addition. The 1934 uniform already had silver pants and a silver helmet, and silver has been the base of the Lions' look ever since, with one break.

From 1948 to 1950, coach Bo McMillin, who came from 14 seasons at Indiana, put Detroit in scarlet with silver pants and used black for some rivalry games, according to WDET and Wikipedia. Hall of Fame curator Jason Aikens told WDET that McMillin thought the team would look better in his old school's crimson. Buddy Parker restored blue and silver in 1951, and the Lions won back-to-back championships in 1952 and 1953. The team's own history says the organization kept Honolulu blue and silver as its official colors through the scarlet years.

## How The Colors Have Changed Since

- **1960 and 1961:** The new AFL Buffalo Bills wore Honolulu blue, silver and white. Horrigan told the Free Press that first Bills coach Buster Ramsey, a former Detroit assistant, and owner Ralph Wilson, a minority Lions owner, borrowed the look. We cover that in [how every NFL team got its colors](/stories/how-every-nfl-team-got-its-colors).
- **1994:** Throwbacks modeled on the 1935 championship uniforms for the NFL's 75th season.
- **1998:** A white jersey with blue pants, worn that one season.
- **2003:** Black trim added to the logo and jerseys, with black facemasks.
- **2005 to 2007:** The first black alternate jersey, introduced under general manager Matt Millen. WDET counts the Lions at 2-4 in black from 2005 to 2007, and 15-33 overall in that stretch. For 2008 the black jersey was dropped in favor of a 75th-anniversary throwback, and in 2009 the throwback replaced it as the permanent alternate.
- **2009:** A redesigned logo with a flowing mane and fangs.
- **2017:** A full rebrand that removed black from the identity entirely, with a new typeface, a silver logo border, an all-gray alternate and an all-blue combination (Honolulu blue jersey and blue pants).
- **2017 Color Rush:** The 2016 design Nike gave the Lions was all black, and the team never wore it because its only Thursday game was Thanksgiving, which was exempt. The first Lions Color Rush game was December 16, 2017, against the Bears, in a steel gray (anthracite) uniform.
- **2021:** White pants with the white jersey against Green Bay, a combination last worn in 1948 and 1949.
- **2023:** A blue alternate helmet with the 1960s logo, the first blue Lions helmet since 1955.

## The 2024 Redesign

The Lions unveiled their current set on April 18, 2024, at Ford Field. Nike moved Honolulu blue to a richer tone, closer to the early-1990s shade. The home jersey is Honolulu blue with white numbers trimmed in silver and silver sleeve stripes, worn with silver or blue pants. The road jersey has DETROIT across the chest for the first time in franchise history. Black came back too: team president Rod Wood had promised coach Dan Campbell, who wore black as a Lions player, that the black jersey would return if Detroit won the division, and the Lions won the NFC North in 2023.

It is the first Lions jersey built on silver instead of blue or white. Detroit wore it once, against Minnesota on November 1, and won 19-15. ## Our Take

The name may be a paint-chip legend, but the color is the real thing. No other NFL team owns a blue anywhere near Honolulu blue, and paired with silver it gives Detroit a look that is bright without being loud and old without looking dated. The black years (2003 to 2016) were the weakest stretch of the franchise's uniform history, and the best thing the 2024 redesign did was push blue and silver back to the front. The silver pants are the proof: Honolulu blue over silver is what the Lions wore in 1934, and it is still the best version of them.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why do the Detroit Lions wear Honolulu blue?**

Owner George A. Richards picked blue and silver in 1934, when he bought the Portsmouth Spartans and moved them to Detroit. He wanted new colors to replace Portsmouth's purple and gold.

**Why is it called Honolulu blue?**

An old Lions media guide says the color reminded Richards of the ocean he saw on a trip to Hawaii. That story is widely repeated but not documented from 1934, and the Detroit Historical Society found the phrase used for a Buick paint color in a Free Press classified ad.

**Who picked the Lions' colors?**

It is disputed. Glenn Presnell said in 1999 that he and his wife chose blue and silver from color options on Richards' table, and team historian Bill Keenist tells the same story. A 1993 team history credits general manager Cy Huston with choosing the shade. Richards made the final decision.

**Did the Lions' owner really go to Hawaii?**

The team's media guide said so, and the story has been repeated for decades, but no source we found documents the trip or ties it to the color at the time.

**What color were the Lions before Honolulu blue?**

Purple and gold, as the Portsmouth Spartans of Ohio from 1930 to 1933.

**When did the Portsmouth Spartans become the Detroit Lions?**

In 1934, when George A. Richards, owner of Detroit radio station WJR, bought the team for less than $8,000 and moved it to Detroit.

**What are the Detroit Lions' official colors?**

Honolulu blue and silver. The current uniform set also uses white and black.

**What is the hex code for Honolulu blue?**

#0076B6, or RGB 0, 118, 182, is the widely published value. The Lions' silver is #B0B7BC.

**What Pantone is Honolulu blue?**

Color-reference sites most often list PMS 7462 C, but others list 7461 C. The team does not publish its print spec.

**Is Honolulu blue the same as royal blue?**

No. It is a medium-dark cyan blue, brighter and greener than royal blue and much lighter than navy. The 2024 redesign moved it to a richer tone.

**Have the Lions always worn silver pants?**

Silver pants were part of the very first Detroit uniform in 1934, and they have been the standard pants with the blue jersey for most of the team's history. The current set pairs the blue home jersey with either silver or blue pants.

**What is the Lions' record in silver pants in 2026?**

3-0: wins over the Jets (31-24) and Patriots (35-10) in Honolulu blue over silver, and over the Vikings (19-15) in the Rivalries Silver uniform with silver pants.

**Did the Lions ever wear red?**

Yes. From 1948 to 1950, coach Bo McMillin put the team in scarlet with silver pants, borrowing from his Indiana teams. Buddy Parker brought back blue and silver in 1951.

**When did the Lions first wear black jerseys?**

In 2005. The black alternate was worn from 2005 to 2007, dropped for 2008 in favor of a 75th-anniversary throwback, and replaced by that throwback as the alternate in 2009.

**Why did the Lions bring back black jerseys?**

Team president Rod Wood promised Dan Campbell the black jersey would come back if the Lions won the division. They won the NFC North in 2023, and the black alternate returned with the 2024 uniforms.

**Did the Lions have an all-blue Color Rush uniform?**

No. Their original 2016 Color Rush design was all black and never worn, and the one they did wear, starting December 16, 2017, against the Bears, was steel gray. The all-blue look, with Honolulu blue pants, is a regular combination from the 2017 and 2024 sets.

**When did the Lions get new uniforms?**

The current set was unveiled on April 18, 2024. The Rivalries Silver uniform was added on August 25, 2026.

**What is Rivalries Silver?**

The Lions' Nike Rivalries uniform: a pale silver jersey with DETROIT on the chest, carbon-fiber numbers and an electric-blue helmet. Detroit wore it against the Vikings on November 1, 2026, and won 19-15.

**Why did the Buffalo Bills wear Lions colors?**

In 1960 and 1961 the Bills wore Honolulu blue, silver and white. Pro Football Hall of Fame advisor Joe Horrigan credits first coach Buster Ramsey, a former Detroit coach, and owner Ralph Wilson, who had been a minority owner of the Lions.

**What do the Lions wear on Thanksgiving in 2026?**

Honolulu blue, against the Bears at Ford Field on Thursday, November 26. The Bears wear white.

**What are the Lions wearing against the Buccaneers?**

We expect the black alternate on Sunday, November 22, at Ford Field, the second of three projected black games. The team had not announced it as of Monday.
