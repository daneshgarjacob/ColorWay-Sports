**The Las Vegas Desert Dogs have a new look for their fifth season.** The National Lacrosse League team revealed **new home, away and alternate uniforms** at a fan event on Tuesday, November 17, at The George Sportsmen's Lounge at Durango Resort. The **home jersey is black and the away jersey is white**, the team's colors since day one, both stripped down from the old set. The headline is the **alternate**: a **black jersey with a sunset gradient**, orange fading into pink and purple, across the shoulders and down the sleeves. It is the team's first full-time alternate.

## Quick Answers

- **What are the Desert Dogs' new uniforms?** A new **black home**, a new **white away** and a new **black alternate with a sunset gradient**, for the 2026-27 NLL season.
- **When were they revealed?** Tuesday, November 17, 2026, at a fan event at Durango Resort in Las Vegas, co-hosted with Lexicon Bank.
- **What changed?** The "LAS VEGAS DESERT DOGS" wordmark under the chest logo is gone, and so are the big side panels and contrast shoulders. The new home and away use simple hockey-style stripes on the sleeves and hem.
- **What does the alternate look like?** Black, with the wolf-and-sticks logo in white on the chest and a gradient that runs from **orange to pink to purple** on the shoulders and sleeves. The team says the colors come from the **Las Vegas sunset**.
- **When do they wear the alternate?** No game has been announced. Replicas are on presale and ship **January 30, 2027**, so expect it on the floor in the second half of the season.
- **When is the home opener?** **Saturday, December 5, 7 p.m.**, against the **Colorado Mammoth** at **Lee's Family Forum** in Henderson.
- **Our grades:** home **B-**, away **B-**, alternate **B**.

## The Alternate: A Las Vegas Sunset

This is the jersey people will buy. The base is **black**, the chest logo stays **white**, and the color is all in a gradient: the **shoulders** are a band of **pink fading to orange**, and the **sleeves** fade from **purple** at the elbow through **pink and red** to **bright orange** at the cuffs. The collar picks up the same colors, and **LAS VEGAS** is inside the neck. The cover image at the top of this page is the alternate, from the team's store.

Chief business officer **Jonah Haas** told [FOX5 Las Vegas](https://www.fox5vegas.com/2026/11/18/las-vegas-desert-dogs-unveil-new-uniforms-fifth-season/) that the alternate is **"inspired by the Las Vegas sunset"** and adds a spin "a lot of our fans have been asking about." The team's own detail post goes further: the gradient is "inspired by the vibrant colors of a Las Vegas desert sunset," and the alternate gets its own **black helmet** with an **oversized Desert Dogs logo** and sunset-gradient accents. Nobody has published exact color names, so the shades above are what you can see in the team's images.

The old set was black and white too, but busier:

- **Wordmark gone.** The old jerseys stacked **LAS VEGAS** and a big **DESERT DOGS** under the logo. The new ones put the logo alone on the chest.
- **Panels gone.** The old black home had **white side panels** and **white lower sleeves**. The old white away had **black shoulders**, black side panels and black lower sleeves. The new jerseys are one solid color with stripes.
- **Stripes added.** Simple hockey-style stripe bands on the sleeves and hem replace the panels.
- **The "22" Nevada mark** at the hem is new.
- **The alternate** is new. The team never had a full-time third jersey before.

## Who Are The Las Vegas Desert Dogs?

The Desert Dogs are the National Lacrosse League's box lacrosse team in Las Vegas. The [NLL awarded the franchise](https://www.nll.com/news/national-lacrosse-league-awards-las-vegas-15th-franchise-announces-wayne-gretzky-dustin-johnson-steve-nash-and-joe-tsai-as-co-owners/) on **June 21, 2021**, as its 15th team, with **Wayne Gretzky, Dustin Johnson, Steve Nash and Joe Tsai** as co-owners, and the team started play in the **2022-23** season at Michelob ULTRA Arena at Mandalay Bay. The name was picked from fan submissions and revealed in April 2022. After two seasons on the Strip, the team [moved to Lee's Family Forum in Henderson](https://leesfamilyforum.com/las-vegas-desert-dogs-announce-lees-family-forum-as-new-home-in-2024-2025/) for 2024-25. The team's own description says the black and white color scheme "resembles the desert night." The Desert Dogs have not made the playoffs yet: they went 5-13, 5-13 and 4-14 in their first three seasons and a best-yet 8-10 last season, missing the final spot on a tiebreaker.

## Our Grades

**Home: B-.** Dropping the wordmark and the side panels was the right call. The wolf logo is good, and it looks better alone on a clean black jersey. But black and white with white stripes is the most generic look in sports, and nothing on the jersey says Las Vegas except a tiny "22" at the hem.

**Away: B-.** Same jersey, same verdict. The solid black logo on white is strong and easy to read from the stands.

**Alternate: B.** The best jersey the team has had. The sunset gradient is the one idea on this set that could only belong to Las Vegas, and it is done with restraint: color on the shoulders and sleeves, black everywhere else, the logo left alone. It loses a little for the gradient trend, which every league has leaned on for five years.

**Our take:** the alternate should be the home jersey. A team that plays in the desert, in a city known for neon, chose black and white as its everyday look and saved the only color for a third jersey that does not ship until January. Wear the sunset as often as the league allows.
