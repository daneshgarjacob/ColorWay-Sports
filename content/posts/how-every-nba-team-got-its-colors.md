Most NBA color schemes have a reason behind them, and some of the reasons are strange. Two owners took their colors from their old high schools. One team's color is named after a brick. One came from a subway sign, and one from a baseball team's merchandise sales. Here is where all 30 came from, with the stories we could verify and a note on the ones we couldn't. For football, see [how every NFL team got its colors](/stories/how-every-nfl-team-got-its-colors).

## Quick Facts: NBA Team Colors

- **Chicago Bulls:** red and black from founder Dick Klein's high school in Fort Madison, Iowa.
- **Cleveland Cavaliers:** wine and gold from founder Nick Mileti's high school in Cleveland.
- **Sacramento Kings:** purple, partly because the Colorado Rockies' purple gear was selling so well.
- **Oklahoma City Thunder:** an orange picked to be "not too red and not too orange," to stay neutral between OU and OSU.
- **Milwaukee Bucks:** "Cream City Cream," named for Milwaukee's cream-colored 1800s bricks.
- **Los Angeles Lakers:** the owner hated the word purple, so it was officially "Forum Blue."
- **Toronto Raptors:** purple for the Jurassic Park era, with "Naismith silver" for basketball's Canadian inventor.
- **Brooklyn Nets:** black and white from the New York City subway signs.

## Atlantic Division

### Boston Celtics

The green is about as old as the name. Founder Walter Brown called the team the Celtics in 1946 for Boston's huge Irish population, and green came with it. Gold and black are accents, and the green has barely changed in 80 years.

### Brooklyn Nets

When the Nets moved from New Jersey to Brooklyn in 2012, they dropped red, white and blue for black and white, taken from the classic signs of the New York City subway. Part-owner Jay-Z was heavily involved in the design. It is one of only two NBA color schemes with no color at all, alongside the Spurs.

### New York Knicks

Blue, orange and white are New York City's official colors, from the Dutch flag that New Amsterdam's settlers flew. The team name comes from the same place: "Knickerbockers" was a nickname for the city's Dutch settlers.

### Philadelphia 76ers

Red, white and blue, because the name honors 1776 and the Declaration of Independence, signed in Philadelphia. No hidden story here, just the flag.

<img src="/images/posts/how-every-nba-team-got-its-colors/76ers-colors.png" alt="Philadelphia 76ers team colors" style="width:100%;max-width:100%;border-radius:10px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto 1.2em;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

### Toronto Raptors

The 1995 expansion team was named in the middle of the Jurassic Park craze and launched in purple, a color picked to stand out in a hockey town. Its silver was officially "Naismith silver," after James Naismith, the Canadian who invented basketball. The team later moved to red and black.

### Detroit Pistons

The team began as the Fort Wayne Zollner Pistons, a company team for Fred Zollner's auto-piston factory, and it has worn red and blue since the 1940s. The name came from the factory; the colors have no story of their own.

### Indiana Pacers

Navy and gold come from the blue and gold of the Indiana state flag.

### Milwaukee Bucks

"Cream City Cream" is named for Milwaukee's bricks. In the 1800s the city's clay fired a pale cream color instead of red, so many of its buildings were cream, and Milwaukee got the nickname Cream City. The Bucks made cream an official color in 2015, and no other NBA team has it.

## Southeast Division

### Atlanta Hawks

Red has been the Hawks' color since they arrived in Atlanta in 1968. The 2020 rebrand added "Legacy Yellow" as a tribute to the team's best eras.

<img src="/images/posts/how-every-nba-team-got-its-colors/hawks-colors.png" alt="Atlanta Hawks team colors" style="width:100%;max-width:100%;border-radius:10px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto 1.2em;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

### Charlotte Hornets

Menswear designer Alexander Julian of Chapel Hill picked teal and purple and the pinstripes for the 1988 expansion team. The look became a '90s fad copied across sports, and the colors came back when the Bobcats became the Hornets again in 2014.

<img src="/images/posts/how-every-nba-team-got-its-colors/hornets-colors.png" alt="Charlotte Hornets team colors" style="width:100%;max-width:100%;border-radius:10px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto 1.2em;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

### Miami Heat

Red, black and yellow came with the 1988 flaming basketball logo, and they fit the name. No hidden story.

<img src="/images/posts/how-every-nba-team-got-its-colors/heat-colors.png" alt="Miami Heat team colors" style="width:100%;max-width:100%;border-radius:10px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto 1.2em;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

### Orlando Magic

The name points to Disney's Magic Kingdom. We could not find a reliable source for why the team picked blue and black, so we won't repeat the stories that float around.

<img src="/images/posts/how-every-nba-team-got-its-colors/magic-colors.png" alt="Orlando Magic team colors" style="width:100%;max-width:100%;border-radius:10px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto 1.2em;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

### Washington Wizards

After the name changed from Bullets to Wizards in 1997, the team wore blue, bronze and black. Owner Ted Leonsis brought back red, white and blue in 2011 because those were the colors the Bullets won in, the same move he made with the Capitals.

<img src="/images/posts/how-every-nba-team-got-its-colors/wizards-colors.png" alt="Washington Wizards team colors" style="width:100%;max-width:100%;border-radius:10px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto 1.2em;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

### Denver Nuggets

The 2018 palette is named after Colorado: Midnight Blue, Sunshine Yellow, Skyline Blue and Flatirons Red, after the red rock formations near Boulder.

The 2017 rebrand renamed the green "Aurora Green" for the Northern Lights over Minnesota, and the lighter blue "Lake Blue" for the state's lakes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why are the Bulls red and black?**

Founder Dick Klein gave the team the colors of his high school, Fort Madison High School in Iowa, when he started the Bulls in 1966.

**Why are the Cavaliers wine and gold?**

Founder Nick Mileti used the colors of his alma mater, John Adams High School in Cleveland, for the 1970 expansion team.

**Why are the Lakers purple and gold?**

Owner Jack Kent Cooke switched the Lakers from powder blue to purple and gold in 1967. He disliked the word purple, so the team called the color "Forum Blue" for years.

**What is Cream City Cream?**

It's the Milwaukee Bucks' cream color, added in 2015. It's named for the pale cream bricks that gave Milwaukee its "Cream City" nickname in the 1800s.

**Why are the Kings purple?**

In 1994 the Kings wanted new colors, struggled to get a consistent burgundy that didn't look like the 49ers, and chose purple partly because the Colorado Rockies' purple merchandise was selling well.

**Why are the Nets black and white?**

When they moved to Brooklyn in 2012, the Nets took black and white from the New York City subway signs, with Jay-Z heavily involved in the design.

**Why are the Thunder blue and orange?**

The blue matches the Oklahoma state flag. The orange-red was chosen to be "not too red and not too orange," so the team wouldn't look like it sided with Oklahoma or Oklahoma State.

**Why are the Jazz purple when they play in Utah?**

The Jazz started in New Orleans in Mardi Gras purple, green and gold, and kept the colors after moving to Utah in 1979.

**What color were the Raptors originally?**

Purple, with red, black and "Naismith silver," when the team launched in 1995. The team moved to red and black later.

**Which NBA teams have no color in their color scheme?**

The Brooklyn Nets (black and white) and the San Antonio Spurs (silver, black and white).
