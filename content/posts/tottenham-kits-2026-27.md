Tottenham are the least complete wardrobe of Europe's fifteen biggest clubs going into 2026/27. The home and away shirts have both launched and are in shops, and the third is still a leak. Two shirts to grade, and the better of them is the one that took the longest to reach the shelves.

## The Home Shirt: B-

Lilywhite with a textured diagonal tonal stripe patterned directly into the fabric, and navy blue on the collar and cuffs. The diagonal treatment references the iconic mid-1980s Hummel shirts.

The reference is a good one. Those Hummel kits are among the best Tottenham have ever worn, and a modern club nodding to them is the right instinct. The problem is that the nod is so quiet you need to be told it is there, and once you strip it out you are left with a white shirt with navy trim, which is what Tottenham wear every single season.

ESPN ranked it 34th of every Premier League kit and described it as plain by design, and called the diagonal hash marks a reference to far superior Hummel kits. That is harsh but it is not wrong. This is not a bad shirt. It is an interchangeable one, and for a club with a genuinely distinctive archive to draw on, that is the disappointment.

**Grade: B-**
