The NHL's Hometown Remix jerseys stop being photos this week. Ten teams wear theirs for the first time between Tuesday and Thursday, and the week starts with the best possible matchup: **the Penguins debut the best Remix jersey in the league tonight against the Golden Knights, whose Remix we ranked dead last.** Vegas is on the road, so it will be in white, but the timing is perfect.

Here is every debut this week, with where each jersey landed in our [Hometown Remix ranking](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-jerseys-ranked).

## Tuesday, November 3

### Penguins vs. Golden Knights: No. 1 of 32

<img src="/images/posts/nhl-daily-tracker/penguins-remix.jpg" alt="Pittsburgh Penguins Hometown Remix jersey, deep blue with a pop-art penguin crest, light blue trim and halftone dots" style="display:block;width:100%;max-width:340px;margin:0.5em auto;border-radius:10px;" loading="lazy" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.5em;">Image: NHL</p>

A deep blue sweater with an Andy Warhol-style pop-art penguin, light blue trim and halftone dots. It is our favorite Remix in the league, and it looks a lot like Pittsburgh's 2008 Winter Classic jersey, which is about as good as a hockey sweater gets. Ten games this season. [Why it's the best in the NHL](/stories/penguins-hometown-remix-jersey-best).

### Canadiens vs. Jets: No. 9

<img src="/images/posts/nhl-daily-tracker/canadiens-remix.jpg" alt="Montreal Canadiens Hometown Remix jersey, white with the five symbols of the Montreal flag down the sleeves and a stonework CH crest" style="display:block;width:100%;max-width:340px;margin:0.5em auto;border-radius:10px;" loading="lazy" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.5em;">Image: NHL</p>

White, with the five symbols from Montreal's flag down the sleeves and a CH that looks carved in stone. We really like it. Our one wish is that it had been a color instead of white. Ten games.

### Flyers vs. Wild: No. 21

<img src="/images/posts/nhl-daily-tracker/flyers-remix.jpg" alt="Philadelphia Flyers Hometown Remix jersey, gray with six orange arm stripes and the Liberty Bell inscription" style="display:block;width:100%;max-width:340px;margin:0.5em auto;border-radius:10px;" loading="lazy" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.5em;">Image: NHL</p>

Gray, with six orange stripes on the arms and the Liberty Bell's inscription stamped between them. Great story, but the gray drags it down. Thirteen games, tied for the most in the league.

### Blue Jackets vs. Avalanche: No. 22

<img src="/images/posts/nhl-daily-tracker/blue-jackets-remix.jpg" alt="Columbus Blue Jackets Hometown Remix jersey, gray with the cannon logo on the chest" style="display:block;width:100%;max-width:340px;margin:0.5em auto;border-radius:10px;" loading="lazy" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.5em;">Image: NHL</p>

A gray sweater with the cannon on the chest. A team called the Blue Jackets wearing a gray jacket is still a strange choice. Thirteen games, so you will see a lot of it.

## Wednesday, November 4

### Sabres vs. Senators: No. 4

Royal blue with the throwback goat head, big, right in the middle of the chest. Buffalo just does uniforms well. Eight games.

### Kings vs. Sharks: No. 6

<img src="/images/posts/nhl-daily-tracker/kings-remix.jpg" alt="Los Angeles Kings Hometown Remix jersey, purple and gold with black stripes and a Hollywood-style crest" style="display:block;width:100%;max-width:340px;margin:0.5em auto;border-radius:10px;" loading="lazy" />

Purple and gold with black stripes and a Hollywood-style crest. Purple belongs in the Kings' everyday uniforms, and we are glad to at least get it here. Seven games, all on Wednesday home dates.

### Kraken vs. Flames: No. 12

<img src="/images/posts/nhl-daily-tracker/kraken-remix.jpg" alt="Seattle Kraken Hometown Remix jersey, deep sea blue with two cream stripes and the anchor logo on the chest" style="display:block;width:100%;max-width:340px;margin:0.5em auto;border-radius:10px;" loading="lazy" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.5em;">Image: NHL</p>

Deep sea blue, two cream stripes and the anchor logo finally on the chest. Simple and clean. Six games.

Also Wednesday: the Panthers wear their coral Remix again, at home against Chicago. They debuted it Monday against Detroit, and it is still our [most overrated Remix](/stories/panthers-hometown-remix-jersey-overrated).

## Thursday, November 5

### Oilers vs. Ducks: No. 24

<img src="/images/posts/nhl-daily-tracker/oilers-remix.jpg" alt="Edmonton Oilers Hometown Remix jersey, black with orange outlines, an Oil Country shoulder patch and warning stripes in the hem" style="display:block;width:100%;max-width:340px;margin:0.5em auto;border-radius:10px;" loading="lazy" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.5em;">Image: NHL</p>

Black with orange outlines, an "Oil Country" patch and warning stripes at the hem. Fun up close. From the stands, it is a black jersey with orange trim. Seven games.

### Islanders vs. Hurricanes: No. 19

<img src="/images/posts/nhl-daily-tracker/islanders-remix.jpg" alt="New York Islanders Hometown Remix jersey, white with the Fisherman crest and wavy teal, orange and navy stripes" style="display:block;width:100%;max-width:340px;margin:0.5em auto;border-radius:10px;" loading="lazy" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.5em;">Image: NHL</p>

The Fisherman is back, cleaned up, on a white sweater with wavy teal, orange and navy stripes. Islanders fans have wanted this for years. We still don't love the logo. Eleven games.

### Utah at Canadiens: No. 8

<img src="/images/posts/nhl-daily-tracker/mammoth-remix.jpg" alt="Utah Mammoth Hometown Remix jersey, bright mountain blue with black and white tusk-shaped stripes" style="display:block;width:100%;max-width:340px;margin:0.5em auto;border-radius:10px;" loading="lazy" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 1.5em;">Image: NHL</p>

The only road debut of the week. Bright mountain blue with black and white stripes shaped like rising tusks, and the first Mammoth jersey that isn't black or white. It reminds us a little of the Capitals' sweater, which is a compliment.

Also Thursday: the Blue Jackets wear their Remix for the second time, at home against Dallas.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When do the Penguins wear their Hometown Remix jersey for the first time?**
Tuesday, November 3, 2026, at home against the Vegas Golden Knights. It is one of 10 Remix games for Pittsburgh this season.

**Which NHL teams debut their Hometown Remix jerseys this week?**
Tuesday: Penguins, Canadiens, Flyers and Blue Jackets. Wednesday: Sabres, Kings and Kraken. Thursday: Oilers, Islanders and Utah.

**Will the Golden Knights wear their Remix jersey in Pittsburgh?**
No. Vegas is the road team, so it wears white. Its Remix is for home games.

**Which Remix jersey debuting this week is the best?**
The Penguins', our No. 1 of all 32. The Sabres (No. 4) and Kings (No. 6) are next.

**Which team wears its Remix jersey the most?**
The Blue Jackets and Flyers, with 13 games each.
