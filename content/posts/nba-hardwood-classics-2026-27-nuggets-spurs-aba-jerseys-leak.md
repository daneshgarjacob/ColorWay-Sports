## Why Four Former ABA Teams Are Going Retro in 2026-27

On June 17, 1976, the ABA and NBA agreed to merge, and four ABA clubs came over: the **Denver Nuggets, Indiana Pacers, New York Nets and San Antonio Spurs**. The Kentucky Colonels and the Spirits of St. Louis were folded instead. The 2026-27 season is the **50th anniversary of that merger**, and it is also **season 60** for all four franchises, which all date back to the ABA's first season in 1967-68.

That is the reported reason for the set: the four clubs that survived the ABA, dressed as their ABA selves, in the same season.

## The Leaked Nuggets ABA Jersey: The Denver Rockets in Orange

The leaked Nuggets jersey is **bright orange** with **DENVER** arched across the chest in black block letters outlined in white, black numbers outlined in white, and black and white trim around the collar and arm holes. The back carries the name and number in the same black and white.

**The history.** The franchise was a charter member of the ABA in 1967. It was going to be the Denver Larks, but owner Bill Ringsby renamed the club the **Denver Rockets** before it played a game, after his Ringsby Rocket trucking company. The team's **black, orange and white** came straight off those trucks, and the Rockets wore them from 1967-68 through 1970-71 before switching to blue and gold. The Rockets name had to go when the club prepared for the NBA, which already had a team in Houston, and a fan contest produced **Nuggets** in 1974, after Denver's old NBL club.

The orange era is the Spencer Haywood era. Haywood came out of the University of Detroit and in 1969-70, his only ABA season, won MVP and Rookie of the Year while averaging 30 points and 19.5 rebounds. Denver then reached the final ABA Finals in 1976, losing to Julius Erving's New York Nets in six games, which makes a Nuggets vs Nets ABA night this season a genuine rematch.

**Why it matters for the closet.** Denver's current set is white, midnight navy, the royal Statement and the white Rainbow Skyline City Edition. Orange appears nowhere in that closet, so this would be the only jersey in the closet that looks nothing like the rest of it. That is exactly what a throwback should do.

## The Leaked Spurs ABA Jersey: San Antonio's First Silver

The leaked Spurs jersey is **silver** with the 1970s **SPURS** wordmark across the chest, the one with a cowboy spur standing in for the U, in black outlined in white, with black numbers and black and white trim.

**The history.** The Spurs began as the **Dallas Chaparrals** in 1967, in red, white and blue. A San Antonio group led by Angelo Drossos took over the club in 1973, briefly named it the Gunslingers, and then settled on **Spurs** before a game was played. The new name came with **black, silver and white**, starting in 1973-74, and the Spurs have kept those colors ever since. This jersey is the look from those first San Antonio seasons, the George Gervin years, before the club entered the NBA in 1976.

San Antonio's most recent Classic Edition was a black 1970s-inspired uniform for the franchise's 50th season in San Antonio in 2022-23. The 2026-27 version is the silver one.

**Why it matters for the closet.** San Antonio already wears a silver San Antonio Statement, so an ABA silver would put two silver alternates in one closet. We flagged that in our [closet ranking](/stories/nba-uniform-closets-ranked-2026-27) before the leak, and the leak confirms it. The saving grace is the wordmark, which is one of the best in basketball.
