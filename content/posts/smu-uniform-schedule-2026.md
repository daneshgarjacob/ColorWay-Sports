SMU has one helmet, three jersey colors, and a habit of wearing white pants with all of them. This is the full SMU 2026 uniform schedule: the blue home set, the all-white icy whites that opened the season at Florida State, the red and black alternates sitting in the closet, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

The white helmet with the red Peruna is the anchor of the whole thing, and it has been since 2008, when SMU walked away from the blue shells it had worn since 2003 and went back to the look of the mid-1980s Pony Express teams. Everything since has been built around that one decision. There is no second helmet, no chrome, no matte black shell. SMU changes the jersey and leaves the head alone.

The season opened at Florida State with the set SMU calls the icy whites, an all-white combination the program has been running since 2024.

**September 7 at Florida State.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **white helmet, white jersey, white pants**, the icy whites, on Labor Day night in Tallahassee. SMU won 27-24 in a game that was stopped by a power outage at Doak Campbell Stadium. Florida State hosted in gold helmets, garnet jerseys and gold pants, which we cover on the [Florida State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/florida-state-uniform-schedule-2026).

**September 12 vs. UC Davis.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **white helmet with the red facemask, blue jersey, white pants**, the standard home look for a 3 p.m. Central home opener. SMU won 56-10. The stands were the white part of the equation: the school ran a White Out and handed crowns to the first 2,000 students for Kevin Jennings' Heisman campaign.

**September 19 at Louisville.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **white helmet, white jersey, white pants**, all white for the first ACC road game. Louisville wore the white helmet with a red jersey and white pants and won 41-31, so the clean red-on-white matchup arrived as expected. We track the other side on the [Louisville 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/louisville-uniform-schedule-2026).

**September 26 vs. Missouri State.** Blue expected. Family Weekend, the start of a four-game homestand, and SMU is asking its fans to wear blue.

**October 3 vs. Boston College.** Blue expected, with one thing worth watching. The ACC home opener is a Blue theme day, but Boston College is also the opponent SMU picked for its all-black set the last time the two met in Dallas in 2024. If the black comes out in 2026, this is a candidate date.

**October 10.** Bye.

**October 17 vs. Virginia.** Blue expected. This is the Salute to Service game and the Hall of Fame game, with a Stripe Out in the stands. SMU has aimed the blackout at its Salute to Service date before, so this is the other candidate for the black jersey.

**October 24 vs. Cal.** Blue or red. This is the one home date where SMU is pointing its own fans at a color other than blue or white: Fan Appreciation Day comes with a "wear red" instruction, which puts the red jersey in play for the first time this season. Cal will be in white.

**October 31 at Syracuse.** White expected. Halloween in the dome, and orange against the icy whites is one of the better looking games on the SMU calendar. See the [Syracuse 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/syracuse-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 6 vs. Virginia Tech.** Blue expected. A Friday night ACC game at 6 p.m. Central on ESPN, built around Club Takeaway Night, with light-up sticks for the first 5,000 students. More on the visitor at the [Virginia Tech 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/virginia-tech-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 14 vs. Wake Forest.** Blue expected. Homecoming and Senior Day, and the last home game of the season.

**November 21 at Notre Dame.** White expected. Prime time on NBC in South Bend, with Notre Dame in navy, which is as good a stage as the icy whites are going to get this year. See the [Notre Dame 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/notre-dame-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 28 at Stanford.** White expected. Regular season finale on the West Coast.

## The Bottom Line

Two games in, SMU has used two of its jerseys and the only helmet it owns, which is the whole story of this uniform program: the white shell with the red Peruna never leaves, and the variable is what goes under it. Blue at home, icy whites on the road, and two alternates that need a reason to come out. The red has a natural landing spot on October 24, when the school is already asking the crowd to wear it. The black needs a night game, and Boston College and the Salute to Service date against Virginia are the two obvious candidates. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is SMU wearing this week?**

SMU plays at Louisville on Saturday, September 19 at 3:30 p.m. Eastern on ESPN2, and the white road set is expected against a red-wearing home team. The Mustangs wore the blue home jersey with white pants in their last game, a 56-10 win over UC Davis on September 12. The week-by-week grid above lists every game with the expected set, and we update it as combinations are confirmed.

**What are SMU's football uniforms for 2026?**

One white helmet with the red Peruna on both sides, a red center stripe flanked by blue and MUSTANGS on the rear bumper. Under it: a blue home jersey with white numerals outlined in red, a white jersey with light blue numerals and a red SMU wordmark, and red and black alternates. White pants go with everything.

**What are SMU's icy whites?**

That is SMU's own name for the all-white combination, white helmet, white jersey and white pants, which the program introduced in 2024 and wore for the 2026 opener at Florida State. The white version also swaps the red facemask for a white one, which is what makes the look read as one solid piece rather than a white jersey with a red trim.

**Will SMU wear red or black in 2026?**

Neither has been announced. The red jersey is the more likely of the two, and October 24 against Cal is the date to watch, because SMU has already told fans to wear red for Fan Appreciation Day. The all-black set has appeared roughly once a year since 2022 and tends to show up for a night game, which points at Boston College on October 3 or the Salute to Service game against Virginia on October 17.

**Why does ESPN list the Virginia Tech game on November 7?**

ESPN's schedule feed runs on UTC. A 6 p.m. Central kickoff on Friday, November 6 is already Saturday in London, so the feed shows a November 7 date. The Missouri State game on September 26 has the same one-day shift for the same reason. Both are correct in Dallas as listed in our grid.

**How many games does SMU play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven at Gerald J. Ford Stadium and five on the road, with a bye on October 10. Nine of them are ACC games, plus a home game against UC Davis, a home game against Missouri State and a trip to Notre Dame. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
