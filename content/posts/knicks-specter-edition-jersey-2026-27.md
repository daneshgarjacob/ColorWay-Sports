**The Knicks' Specter Edition jersey is royal blue and black with orange.** The team posted its official photos on Monday, November 23, with the caption "gameday threads don't get more intense than this," adding "we'll wear these for the first time nov 29." The jersey has **grainy royal blue and black vertical stripes** from the neck to the hem, **black numbers outlined in orange**, an **orange NEW YORK wordmark** and **orange piping** around the neck and arms. The Knicks wear it **three times, all at Madison Square Garden**: November 29 vs the Hawks, December 23 vs the Jazz and December 27 vs the Nets.

## Quick Answers

- **What does the Knicks Specter jersey look like?** Grainy royal blue and black vertical stripes, black numbers outlined in orange, an orange NEW YORK wordmark in a small black box, orange piping and an orange BRUNSON across the back in the team's photos.
- **When do the Knicks wear it?** November 29 vs the Hawks, December 23 vs the Jazz and December 27 vs the Nets. All three are home games.
- **When is the first game?** Sunday, November 29, against Atlanta. The Knicks are one of four Specter teams that debut theirs that night.
- **When does it go on sale?** November 30, the date the Specter jerseys go on sale (per the Hawks' official page). The Knicks have not posted their own sale details, and the jersey was not yet listed on the MSG team shop on November 24.
- **Why do the Knicks have one?** Nike made the Specter Edition only for the NBA's eight oldest franchises. The Knicks were founded in 1946 as an original BAA team and have never left New York. [Why only eight teams](/stories/why-only-8-nba-teams-got-specter-edition-jerseys).
- **Our grade:** B+. No. 6 of 8 in [our Specter ranking](/stories/nba-specter-edition-jerseys-ranked).

## What The Jersey Looks Like

Here is every piece, from the Knicks' own six photos:

- **The base.** Black, with royal blue sprayed over it. In the team's photos there is about as much blue as black, which makes it the lightest of the eight Specter jerseys.
- **The stripes.** Wide vertical stripes of royal blue and black, with a grainy, spray-paint texture so the edges blur into each other. They run the full length of the front and the back. Think of classic pinstripes, blown up and smudged.
- **The numbers.** Black, with a thick orange outline and a thin black edge outside the orange. On the blue stripes the black number pops; on the black stripes the orange outline does the work.
- **The wordmark.** NEW YORK in orange block letters, inside a small black box with an orange border, sitting between the Nike logo and the number. It is much smaller than the arched NEW YORK on the regular jerseys.
- **The Nike logo.** Black, outlined in orange, moved to the middle of the chest like on every Specter jersey.
- **The trim.** Orange piping around the neck and both arm holes, and down the sides.
- **The back.** The number sits high, with **BRUNSON** in orange block letters below it, the same name-under-the-number layout every Specter team has. The NBA logo at the back of the neck is orange.
- **The patch.** The gray "experience abu dhabi" sponsor patch on the left chest.
- **The shorts.** The same royal blue and black spray stripes, with orange piping down the sides and around the hem, an orange Nike logo, an orange NBA logo and the orange Knicks ball logo on the black waistband. They match the retail preview of the shorts that surfaced in September ([details in our Specter guide](/stories/nba-specter-edition-jerseys-2026-27)).

All three are at Madison Square Garden, from the NBA's official 2026-27 game-by-game uniform schedule. The Knicks' own post says "we'll wear these for the first time nov 29," which matches. Every opponent is in white, so all three nights are blue and black against white, the best possible setting for a dark jersey.

Two things stand out on the schedule:

- **It brackets Christmas.** The Knicks wear it on December 23 and December 27, but on Christmas Day itself, December 25 against the Spurs, the NBA's schedule has them in their **white Association Edition**. So the jersey that leaked in April as a "Christmas jersey" is worn two days before and two days after Christmas, and not on it.
- **It is the only blue the Garden gets.** The royal blue Icon Edition is scheduled for 12 games this season, and all 12 are on the road. That makes these three Specter nights the only home games on the NBA's schedule where the Knicks wear blue at all, even if it is blue sprayed over black.

That is three of the Knicks' 80 dated games, the fewest of their five jerseys. Every other Knicks game, and what they wore, is in our [Knicks uniform schedule](/stories/knicks-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

## How It Compares To The Other Knicks Jerseys

The Knicks have five jerseys this season. Here is the count for each, from the NBA's schedule:

- **Association Edition (white):** 40 games, 16 at home and 24 on the road. The everyday look, and the one they wear on Christmas.
- **Statement Edition (black):** 14 games.
- **Icon Edition (royal blue):** 12 games, all on the road.
- **City Edition (black with an orange NEW YORK):** 11 games, all at home, starting January 14 vs the Heat.
- **Specter Edition (royal blue and black stripes):** 3 games, all at home.

So the Specter is the third Knicks jersey this season built on black, after the Statement and the City Edition. What sets it apart is the blue. The Statement and the City Edition are black jerseys with orange on them. The Specter is the only one where royal blue covers half the jersey.

## When It Goes On Sale

The Specter jerseys go on sale **November 30**. The Hawks' official Specter page gives that date for online sales, with in-arena sales starting December 3, and the Celtics say theirs are "dropping soon on-court, online and in-store." The Knicks have not posted their own sale details, and the jersey was not on the MSG team shop's jersey page when we checked on November 24. Nike has not announced a price, so we are not guessing at one.

## Why The Knicks Got One

The Specter Edition is only for the league's eight oldest franchises, and the Knicks are one of them. They were founded in **1946** as one of the original teams of the Basketball Association of America, played in the BAA's first game, at Toronto on November 1, 1946, and have been in New York the whole time. The other seven are the Kings, Pistons, Celtics, Warriors, Hawks, 76ers and Lakers. ## Our Grade: B+

The official photos are the best case for this jersey so far. The blue is brighter than it looked in the leaks and the flat product shot, and there is a lot more of it. Next to the Lakers, where the team color is a blur on black, the Knicks actually look like the Knicks: blue, orange and New York on the chest.

The stripes are the best idea in the whole Specter set. Vertical stripes read as a nod to classic pinstripes, a very New York look, so the graphic means something instead of just looking fast. The orange outline on the numbers and the orange BRUNSON on the back are sharp.

What keeps it from an A is the same thing as every Specter jersey. Half of it is still black, the spray texture gets muddy from a distance, and the NEW YORK wordmark is shrunk down to a label. We gave it a **B+** when it leaked in April ([our first review](/stories/knicks-christmas-jersey-2026)), and the official photos back that up. In [our ranking of all eight](/stories/nba-specter-edition-jerseys-ranked) it came in No. 6, docked for how it reads from the upper deck; up close, it is one of the better ones.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the Knicks Specter Edition jersey?**

A fifth Knicks uniform for 2026-27, part of Nike's Specter Edition line for the NBA's eight oldest franchises. It joins the Association, Icon, Statement and City Edition jerseys.

**What color is the Knicks Specter jersey?**

Royal blue and black, in grainy vertical stripes, with black numbers outlined in orange, an orange NEW YORK wordmark and orange piping.

**When do the Knicks wear the Specter jersey?**

Three home games at Madison Square Garden: November 29 vs the Hawks, December 23 vs the Jazz and December 27 vs the Nets.

**When is the Knicks Specter jersey debut?**

Sunday, November 29, 2026, at home against the Atlanta Hawks. The Knicks' post on November 23 said "we'll wear these for the first time nov 29."

**When can I buy the Knicks Specter jersey?**

The Specter jerseys go on sale November 30, per the Hawks' official page. The Knicks have not posted their own sale details yet, and it was not on the MSG shop as of November 24.

**How much is the Knicks Specter jersey?**

No price has been announced by Nike or the Knicks yet. We will add it when one is posted.

**Is the Knicks Specter jersey a Christmas jersey?**

No. It leaked in April labeled as a Christmas jersey, but the Knicks wear it on December 23 and December 27. On Christmas Day against the Spurs, they are scheduled to wear the white Association Edition.

**Is the Knicks Specter jersey the same as the City Edition?**

No. The Knicks' City Edition is a separate black jersey with an orange NEW YORK, worn 11 times starting January 14 vs the Heat. The Specter is its own jersey, with royal blue stripes.

**Why do the Knicks have a Specter jersey and the Nets don't?**

The Specter Edition goes only to the eight oldest franchises. The Knicks date to 1946. The Nets started in 1967 in the ABA, and their throwback this season is the white Classic Edition, which they wear at the Garden on December 27, the Knicks' third Specter night.

**Whose name is on the Knicks Specter jersey in the photos?**

Jalen Brunson's. The photos show No. 11 on the front and BRUNSON in orange below the number on the back.

**How many jerseys do the Knicks have in 2026-27?**

Five: Association (white), Icon (royal blue), Statement (black), City Edition (black with orange NEW YORK) and Specter Edition. The count for each is in our [Knicks uniform schedule](/stories/knicks-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

**Is the Knicks Specter jersey any good?**

We grade it a B+. It has the most team color of any Specter jersey, and the stripes are a nod to classic pinstripes, but it is still half black and gets muddy from a distance.
