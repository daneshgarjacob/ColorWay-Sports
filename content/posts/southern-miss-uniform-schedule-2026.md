The uniforms are Adidas, and the look is the black-first set Southern Miss leaned into last season, when the program showed off what it called a new look before a September 2025 game. What changed in 2026 is everything around it. Blake Anderson is in his first season as head coach, and the home stadium is now Renasant Stadium after a 10-year, $13.7 million naming-rights deal, though everybody in Hattiesburg still calls it The Rock.

Two habits stand out so far. At home, Southern Miss has gone black from helmet to cleats both times, with no gold pants in sight. On the road, the white jersey has been the constant and the helmet and pants have changed to fit the night: black helmet and black pants under the lights at Auburn, then white from top to bottom at Tulane.

**September 5 vs. Alcorn State.** ★ Confirmed from Southern Miss's own game photography, dated to the day of the game: **black helmet, black jersey, black pants** for the first game of the Blake Anderson era at Renasant Stadium. Alcorn State wore white with purple trim. The Golden Eagles won 49-3.

**September 12 at Auburn.** ★ Confirmed from Southern Miss's own game photography, dated to the day of the game: **black helmet, white jersey, black pants**, a night game at Jordan-Hare Stadium. Auburn won 43-8. Our [Auburn 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/auburn-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Tigers side.

**September 19 vs. UConn.** ★ Confirmed from Southern Miss's own game photography, dated to the day of the game: **black helmet, black jersey, black pants**, all black again for 601 Night at The Rock. The game-week poster showed gold pants, but the team wore black. UConn came in all white and won 48-20. More on our [UConn 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/uconn-uniform-schedule-2026).

**September 26 at Tulane.** ★ Confirmed from Southern Miss's own game photography, dated to the day of the game: **white helmet, white jersey, white pants**, the first appearance of the white helmet this season, for the Battle for the Bell at Yulman Stadium. Tulane answered in all sky blue. Southern Miss led 21-14 after three quarters, but Tulane scored the last 10 points and won 24-21 to keep the Bell. Our [Tulane 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/tulane-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Green Wave.

**October 6 at Troy.** White expected. A Tuesday night Sun Belt opener at Veterans Memorial Stadium, 8 p.m. Eastern on ESPN2, coming off the bye week. Troy is expected in cardinal at home.

**October 17 vs. Arkansas State.** Black expected. Kids Takeover at The Rock and M-Club Hall of Fame and Letterwinners Day. Kickoff time still to be set.

**October 24 vs. Louisiana.** Black expected. Homecoming at Renasant Stadium, and one of the home dates where gold pants or a special touch would fit if Southern Miss has one planned. Kickoff time still to be set.

**October 31 at UL Monroe.** White expected. Malone Stadium in Monroe, kickoff time still to be set.

**November 5 vs. James Madison.** Black expected. Thursday Night Under the Lights and a Blackout, 8 p.m. Eastern on ESPN or ESPN2. Black is the only call for a Blackout. JMU is expected in white; our [James Madison 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/james-madison-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Dukes.

**November 14 at Louisiana Tech.** White expected. Joe Aillet Stadium in Ruston.

**November 21 vs. South Alabama.** Black expected. Senior Day and Military Appreciation, the home finale and another date where a special helmet decal or patch is common.

**November 28 at Old Dominion.** White expected. The regular season finale at S.B. Ballard Stadium in Norfolk, kickoff time still to be set.

## Will Southern Miss Wear Gold Pants in 2026?

It is the open question in the Southern Miss closet. Gold pants are part of the set, and the school's own game-week poster for UConn showed a player in a black jersey over gold. But in both home games so far the Golden Eagles have worn black pants, and on the road they have gone black at Auburn and white at Tulane. We will not put gold in the grid until we see it. Homecoming against Louisiana on October 24 and Senior Day against South Alabama on November 21 are the home dates where a change would make the most sense. The November 5 Blackout against JMU is the one date we would bet stays black.

## The Bottom Line

Four games into the Blake Anderson era, Southern Miss has worn black at home and white on the road, with the black helmet three times and the white helmet once. All black at The Rock is a strong, simple look, and it fits a program that wants its home field to feel like a hard place to play. The questions left are whether gold pants ever make it onto the field and what the Golden Eagles wear for Homecoming, the Thursday night Blackout and Senior Day. The grid above has every date, and we add a star as each uniform is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Southern Miss wearing this week?**

Southern Miss is off this week and plays at Troy on Tuesday, October 6, where the white road jersey is expected. The Golden Eagles wore white jerseys in both road games so far, with the black helmet and black pants at Auburn and an all-white look at Tulane. Southern Miss has not announced its combination for the Troy game.

**What color does Southern Miss wear at home?**

Black. Southern Miss wore the black helmet, black jersey and black pants in both home games of 2026, against Alcorn State and UConn.

**Does Southern Miss have a white helmet?**

Yes. The Golden Eagles wore a white helmet with a gold stripe and the gold SOUTHERN MISS wordmark with an all-white uniform at Tulane on September 26. The black helmet has been worn in the other three games.

**Who makes Southern Miss's uniforms?**

Adidas. The three stripes are on the jerseys and pants, and both the black and white sets are Adidas.

**What is Southern Miss's homecoming game in 2026?**

Louisiana on Saturday, October 24 at Renasant Stadium in Hattiesburg, with kickoff time still to be set. Southern Miss is expected in its black home set.

**What is the name of Southern Miss's stadium?**

Renasant Stadium, with the field named Carlisle-Faulkner Field. The stadium was M.M. Roberts Stadium until a 10-year naming-rights agreement with Renasant Bank in 2026. Fans still call it The Rock.

**When is the Southern Miss Blackout game in 2026?**

Thursday, November 5 against James Madison at Renasant Stadium, at 8 p.m. Eastern. The school has billed it as Thursday Night Under the Lights and a Blackout.
