Throwbacks are the best thing happening in sports uniforms right now, and this weekend proves it. On **Saturday, October 24**, UCLA wears its 1954 powder blue, No. 1 Miami wears its 2001 Tribute and Calgary pulls out its 2004 red. On **Sunday, October 25**, the Falcons wear their 1966 throwback, the Jets wear the Classic, the Seahawks wear royal blue on Sunday Night Football, the Pacers and Nets meet in ABA throwbacks, and the Jets and Canadiens play the Heritage Classic outdoors in Winnipeg. That is the deepest throwback weekend we have ever tracked.

So we ranked all of them. Every notable throwback being worn in the 2026-27 seasons, college football first, then the NFL, NBA, NHL and a quick look back at MLB, with our grade and every date we can confirm. Every uniform below is shown from the team's own post or release. Some of these are the best uniforms in their sport. One of them is a throwback that forgot to bring any color.

Tell us who we got wrong.

## The Short List: Our Five Best Throwbacks Right Now

1. **UCLA 1954 powder blue** (college football), Saturday vs Michigan State
2. **Tampa Bay Buccaneers creamsicle** (NFL), December 6 vs the Chargers
3. **Indiana Pacers Classic Threads** (NBA), starting Sunday at Brooklyn
4. **Cal Joe Roth uniforms** (college football), worn September 5 vs UCLA
5. **Denver Broncos Orange Crush** (NFL), Week 18 vs the Chargers

The full rankings, league by league, are below.

## College Football Throwbacks 2026, Ranked

College football is where the throwback is best right now, because schools are not limited to one alternate and they tie them to real teams. Here are the six that matter this season, counting down.

**Worn: Saturday, October 24 vs Iowa State, Homecoming (7:30 p.m. ET, ESPN2).** Arizona posted "classic look for homecoming" on Thursday: a white helmet with the red block A and red and navy stripes down the middle, a navy jersey with white numbers trimmed in red, and white pants. **Our take:** it looks great, and the white helmet with the red and navy center stripes is a sharp piece. But "classic look" is doing a lot of work. ### 5. Florida Spurrier Throwback <span style="display:inline-block;background:#2f6bed;color:#fff;font-weight:800;border-radius:999px;padding:2px 12px;margin-left:6px;font-size:0.9em;">A-</span>

**Worn: October 10 vs South Carolina.** Florida's "Retro unlocked" set honors the 60th anniversary of Steve Spurrier's 1966 Heisman season: a royal blue helmet with an orange and white center stripe and the block F inside a white oval, a white jersey with blue-orange-blue shoulder stripes, and white pants.

<img src="/images/posts/florida-gators-throwback-2026/cover.jpg" alt="Florida Gators 2026 throwback uniform honoring Steve Spurrier's 1966 Heisman season, blue helmet with the oval F logo, white jersey with blue and orange shoulder stripes" style="display:block;width:100%;max-width:560px;margin:1.5em auto 0.6em;border-radius:12px;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

**Our take:** the jersey is gorgeous and the shoulder stripes are exactly right. The helmet is from the wrong year. Spurrier won the Heisman in a white helmet, and the oval F does not show up until 1967. ### 3. Cal Joe Roth Uniforms <span style="display:inline-block;background:#2f6bed;color:#fff;font-weight:800;border-radius:999px;padding:2px 12px;margin-left:6px;font-size:0.9em;">A</span>

<img src="/images/posts/cal-joe-roth-uniforms-2026/full-uniform.jpg" alt="Cal Golden Bears Joe Roth throwback uniform for 2026, gold helmet with the blue Cal script, royal blue jersey with gold and white shoulder stripes, gold pants" style="display:block;width:100%;max-width:560px;margin:1.5em auto 0.6em;border-radius:12px;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align:center;font-size:0.78em;color:#7C8696;margin:0 0 2em;">via Cal Football</p>

**Worn: September 5 vs UCLA.** Cal brought back the uniforms of the Joe Roth era for the opener: a gold helmet, a royal blue jersey and gold pants, with a No. 12 patch. Roth was Cal's quarterback in 1975 and 1976, finished ninth in the Heisman voting in 1976 while playing with melanoma, and died in February 1977 at 21. His No. 12 is the only retired number in Cal football history.

**Our take:** the most meaningful throwback of the year and one of the best looking. Gold helmets and gold pants on both teams made the Cal-UCLA opener a beauty. [Our Joe Roth uniform grade](/stories/cal-joe-roth-uniforms-2026).

### 1. UCLA 1954 Powder Blue <span style="display:inline-block;background:#2f6bed;color:#fff;font-weight:800;border-radius:999px;padding:2px 12px;margin-left:6px;font-size:0.9em;">A+</span>

This should not be a once-a-season homecoming treat. It should be the home uniform. ## The Bottom Line

The best throwback in sports right now is UCLA's 1954 powder blue, and it is not even UCLA's regular uniform, which is the whole problem. The same goes for the Broncos' Orange Crush, the Dolphins' white and the Bucs' creamsicle: the best uniform each team owns, rationed to a game or two. The misses are rare and they come from the same mistake. The Jets' Classic goes all white for a team famous for green, and Miami picked the orange half of a set whose white and green half is better. Start with this weekend: UCLA and Miami on Saturday, the Falcons, Seahawks, Pacers and the Heritage Classic on Sunday.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What throwback uniforms are being worn this weekend, October 24-25, 2026?**

Saturday: UCLA's 1954 powder blue vs Michigan State, Miami's 2001 Tribute vs Pitt, Arizona's classic Homecoming look vs Iowa State and the Calgary Flames' 2004 red vs Detroit. Sunday: the Falcons' 1966 throwback vs San Francisco, the Jets' Classic vs Miami, the Seahawks' royal blue vs Kansas City on Sunday Night Football, the Pacers and Nets in ABA Classic Editions in Brooklyn, and the Jets and Canadiens in Heritage Classic sweaters in Winnipeg.

**What is UCLA's throwback uniform?**

The powder blue jersey with white shoulder stripes and white numbers, worn with the metallic gold helmet and gold pants, honoring UCLA's 1954 national champions. UCLA wears it for Homecoming against Michigan State on Saturday, October 24, at 12:30 p.m. Pacific on CBS.

**Why is Miami wearing a 2001 throwback?**

2026 is the 25th anniversary of the 2001 Hurricanes, the last Miami team to win a national title. Miami wears the orange home version, white helmet, orange jersey and white pants, against Pitt on October 24 at noon Eastern on ESPN. The white road version with green pants has no date yet.

**When do the Falcons wear the 1966 throwback in 2026?**

Twice: Sunday, October 25 against the 49ers and Sunday, December 6 against the Lions. It is the red helmet, black jersey and white pants, and they are the only two games all season Atlanta wears black.

**When do the Seahawks wear the royal blue throwback?**

Three times, all in primetime: Week 6 at Denver, which they already wore, Sunday, October 25 against Kansas City on Sunday Night Football, and Week 13 against Dallas on Monday night.

**When do the Buccaneers wear the creamsicle uniforms in 2026?**

Sunday, December 6 against the Los Angeles Chargers at 1 p.m. Eastern. It is the only creamsicle date on Tampa Bay's 2026 schedule.

**When do the Eagles wear Kelly Green in 2026?**

Twice, both at home: Week 9 against the Giants on Sunday, November 8, and Week 16 against the Texans on Thursday, December 24.

**What are the NBA Classic Edition jerseys for 2026-27?**

The four former ABA teams, the Pacers, Nets, Spurs and Nuggets, wear ABA-era throwbacks for the 50th anniversary of the ABA-NBA merger, each with a matching classic court. We rank the Pacers' blue Classic Threads first.

**What are the 2026 NHL Heritage Classic jerseys?**

The Winnipeg Jets wear a white sweater built on their 1980s and 1990s look, and the Montreal Canadiens wear red with a single blue chest band and "Les Habitants" on the jersey for the first time. The game is Sunday, October 25, at 7 p.m. Eastern at Princess Auto Stadium in Winnipeg.

**What is the best throwback uniform in sports right now?**

Our pick is UCLA's 1954 powder blue. In the NFL it is the Buccaneers' creamsicle, in the NBA the Pacers' Classic Threads, and in the NHL the Canadiens' Heritage Classic sweater.

**When does Arizona State wear the Roses & Devils throwback?**

Arizona State has not announced a date. The set, a gold helmet with the full-body Sparky and a maroon jersey with ARIZONA STATE across the chest, marks 40 years since the 1987 Rose Bowl win over Michigan.
