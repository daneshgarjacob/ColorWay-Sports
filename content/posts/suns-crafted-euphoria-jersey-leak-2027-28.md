**This is a leak, not an announcement.** On Friday, November 20, the uniform account Basketball Jersey Archive posted what it called the Phoenix Suns' **2027-28 Crafted "Euphoria" jersey**, crediting the fan account **@SunsUniTracker**. The image shows an **orange jersey with SUNS across the chest in black, black numbers and black trim**, with the Suns' sunburst logo on the shorts. The Suns, the NBA and Nike have not said anything about it.

## Quick Answers

- **Is it real?** Unconfirmed. It comes from a uniform news account and a Suns fan account, not from the team, the league or Nike.
- **What is "Crafted"?** The reported name of the alternate uniforms that replace City Edition in 2027-28. That report is also unconfirmed. [What we know](/stories/nba-city-edition-ending-crafted-uniforms-2027-28).
- **What does the leaked Suns jersey look like?** Orange, with SUNS in black block letters, black numbers, black trim around the neck and arms, and curved black lines down the sides.
- **When would it be worn?** The 2027-28 season, if the leak is right. That is two seasons from now.

## What The Leak Shows

Basketball Jersey Archive's follow-up post, "Nike Crafted Jerseys are coming," has two images: its own graphic reading "Crafted Alternate Program" next to the Nike and NBA logos, and a close crop of the Suns jersey on a player.

Here is everything we can see in the image:

- **Jersey:** **orange** base.
- **Wordmark:** **SUNS in black block letters**, arched across the chest.
- **Numbers:** **black**, with no outline.
- **Trim:** a **black V-neck collar** and **black trim around the arm openings**.
- **Side lines:** a **curved black line** on each side of the jersey, running from under the arm down to the hem.
- **Shorts:** **orange** with a black hem, the **Suns' sunburst basketball logo** on the legs and a small version of it on the waistband.
- **Swoosh:** a black Nike swoosh on the chest.

The link card on Basketball Jersey Archive's article labels the image "Phoenix Suns Euphoria Uniform." The article itself describes it as a preview of Nike's new Crafted concept that follows City Edition.

## What We Do Not Know

- **Whether it is real.** No one from the Suns, the NBA or Nike has confirmed it.
- **Where the image came from.** It shows a player on a plain orange background. It does not look like a photo of a jersey in a store or in a game, and the posts do not say whether it comes from Nike material or was made by someone else from a description.
- **What "Euphoria" refers to.** The name comes from the leak. Nothing explains it yet.
- **Whether the details are final.** Uniforms change between early design and release, and 2027-28 is still almost a year away from the usual reveal window.

## How Solid Is This Leak?

**Take it as a rumor for now.** The bigger story, that Nike and the NBA are replacing City Edition with "Crafted" alternates in 2027-28, was first reported by Basketball Jersey Archive on October 30, and a second uniform outlet said its own sources backed it up. That part has some support. The Suns jersey is a step further out: one image, from a fan account, passed along by one outlet, with no second source. We will update this page if the Suns, Nike or another outlet confirms it.
