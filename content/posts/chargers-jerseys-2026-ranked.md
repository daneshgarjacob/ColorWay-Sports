We graded the Chargers the best uniform in the NFL this year, and the closet is the reason. Four jerseys, three pant colors, and every piece comes from the same palette of powder blue, navy, gold and white. The team also published its full jersey schedule 2026 on September 1, jersey and pants for all 17 games, so there is no guessing about when each look appears.

Here is every Los Angeles Chargers jersey and uniform for 2026, ranked worst to first and graded, with the helmet and pants each one pairs with and the weeks it is on the field.

Charger Power arrived in 2025 as the first yellow jersey in franchise history, a nod to the Air Coryell era, when fans wore gold Charger Power T-shirts. The jersey is solid gold with white numbers outlined in powder blue and powder blue bolts on the shoulders. The powder blue is what saves it, because it gives the eye somewhere to land on a jersey that is otherwise one bright color.

In 2025 it was worn gold on gold, and we graded that version a C-. A single column of gold from collar to cleats erases the break at the waist and reads more like a costume than a uniform. For 2026 the team announced white pants instead, which fixes the proportion and pulls the white helmet down into the rest of the look. That moves it up to the B- we gave it in our [Chargers at Buccaneers preview](/stories/chargers-bucs-uniforms-december-2026). It is still a lot of gold, and gold is a hard color to build a football uniform around.

When it is worn in 2026: once, on the road, in Week 13 at Tampa Bay on Sunday, December 6, with the white helmet and white pants. The Buccaneers are wearing their white 1976 creamsicle throwback, and that is what allows a visiting team to wear a colored jersey.

Super Chargers is a modern take on the navy jerseys the franchise wore from 1992 to 2006, the era of the 1994 AFC Championship team. The deep navy body carries white numbers outlined in gold and white-and-gold bolts on the shoulders. Navy is dark enough that the gold trim glows instead of blending in, and the whole set looks like it belongs to this team rather than to a merchandising plan.

It pairs with a glossy navy helmet and navy pants, and the pants are the problem. Head-to-toe navy has the same missing waistline that held the gold set back. We graded Super Chargers an A- in our [full breakdown](/stories/chargers-super-chargers-2026) and said it would be an A+ with white pants. The team gave the gold jersey white pants this year and left the navy set alone, so the A- stands.

When it is worn in 2026: three home games at SoFi Stadium. Week 9 against the Texans on Sunday, November 8, Week 12 against the Patriots on Sunday, November 29 on Sunday Night Football, and Week 17 against the Chiefs, whose date has not been set.

The white road jersey takes the powder blue set and turns it inside out: powder blue numbers outlined in gold on a white body, with powder blue and gold bolts on the shoulders. Powder blue numbers on white are about as readable as numbers get, and the bolts give the shoulders a shape most white road jerseys lack.

What lifts it to an A- is the pants. The team rotates three colors under it, and white over powder blue pants is the strongest version: a road uniform built only from the franchise's two signature colors, with a clean break at the waist. The all-white version is fine but loses that break, and white over gold pants is the loudest of the three. It ranks just behind powder blue because the home jersey carries the identity and the road set borrows it.

When it is worn in 2026: eight games, every road date except Tampa Bay. White over powder pants goes three times, at Seattle (Week 4), at Baltimore on Monday night (Week 10) and at Denver in Week 18. All white goes three times, at Buffalo (Week 3), against the Rams at SoFi, where the Chargers are the visiting team (Week 8), and at Las Vegas (Week 14). White over gold pants goes twice, at Kansas City (Week 6) and at Miami (Week 16). The team's schedule lists jersey and pants only, so we log the helmet on the [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026) after each game.

Powder blue is the best uniform in the NFL, and it is why we ranked the Chargers first in our [league-wide ranking](/stories/best-nfl-uniforms-2026-ranked) with an A. The jersey carries white numbers outlined in gold and gold bolts on the shoulders, and no other team in football owns this color. Powder blue over white pants is the version we would pick over any uniform in the league: the jersey does the talking, the white pants give it a floor, and the gold cuts cleanly against the blue.

It is an A and not an A+ for one reason. The current powder blue is noticeably darker than the shade of the Philip Rivers era, and that lighter color was the whole point. Bring it back and this is the best uniform in sports.

When it is worn in 2026: five home games, with three different pants. It pairs with the white helmet the team wore in Week 1 and has confirmed again for Week 2. Powder over white pants appears once, in Week 1 against Arizona. Powder over gold pants goes twice, against the Raiders in Week 2 and the 49ers on Thursday night in Week 15. All powder goes twice, against the Broncos in Week 5 and the Jets in Week 11. The Week 1 matchup with Arizona's all-white set earned a C+ on our tracker, a grade that reflects the Cardinals' side, not this uniform.

## The Bottom Line on the Chargers Jersey Rankings

The best closet in the league has three sets at A- or better. Powder blue is an A and the best uniform in football. The white road set is an A- at its best over powder pants. Super Chargers is an A- that white pants would make perfect. Charger Power sits last at a B-, better than it was in 2025, and still the one look that feels more like a costume than a uniform.

Two changes would make this the best closet in sports: return powder blue to its lighter original shade, and give the navy set white pants. The team fixed the gold set this year, so the second change is not far off.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jerseys do the Chargers have in 2026?**

Four: the powder blue home jersey, the white road jersey, the navy Super Chargers alternate, and the gold Charger Power alternate. All four are on the 2026 schedule, which the team published on September 1 with jersey and pants for every game.

**What is the best Chargers jersey?**

We rank powder blue first with an A, and we consider it the best uniform in the NFL. Its white numbers with gold trim and gold shoulder bolts are at their best over white pants, the combination the Chargers wore in Week 1.

**What jersey are the Chargers wearing this week?**

In Week 2, the Chargers host the Las Vegas Raiders at SoFi Stadium on Sunday, September 20, at 4:05 ET. The team's schedule has powder blue with gold pants, and the white helmet it wore in Week 1 is expected again. The Raiders are projected in white. In Week 1 against Arizona, Los Angeles wore the white helmet, powder blue jersey and white pants.

**Do the Chargers have a new jersey in 2026?**

No. All four jerseys are returning sets. The change for 2026 is the pants: the gold Charger Power jersey is paired with white pants for the first time, replacing the gold-on-gold look from 2025.

**When do the Chargers wear the Super Chargers navy uniform?**

Three times, all at home: Week 9 against the Texans on Sunday, November 8, Week 12 against the Patriots on Sunday, November 29 on Sunday Night Football, and Week 17 against the Chiefs, whose date is still to be set.

**What happened to the Chargers' royal blue alternate?**

It is not on the 2026 uniform schedule. The team's four jerseys this season are powder blue, white, Super Chargers navy and Charger Power gold, so we have left the royal blue jersey out of this ranking and will add it back if Los Angeles brings it out.
