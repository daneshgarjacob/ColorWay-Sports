**The best jersey to give this year is the Los Angeles Chargers powder blue. It is our No. 1 uniform in the NFL. If you want the luckiest gift instead, buy the Tampa Bay Lightning blue home sweater: 8-0-0 this season.**

Every gift guide tells you to buy the best-selling jersey. We do not. We grade every uniform in the NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB and college football, and we log what every pro team wears in every game. So every pick below is either a jersey we ranked at the top or one that has not lost this season. Most of them are both good-looking and winning.

Every pick is a jersey on sale this season. For the NBA, every pick is the Nike Swingman, the standard fan version.

## Quick Answers

- **Best NFL jersey to give:** Chargers powder blue, our **No. 1 uniform in the NFL**.
- **Best NBA jersey to give:** Timberwolves blue Icon Edition Swingman, **7-0** this season.
- **Best NHL jersey to give:** Blackhawks red home, our **No. 1 sweater in the NHL**.
- **Best college jersey to give:** UCLA powder blue, the **most beautiful color pair in college football**.
- **Best MLB jersey to give:** Dodgers home white, our **No. 1 jersey in baseball**.
- **Luckiest jersey to give:** Lightning blue home, **8-0-0**.
- **For the fan who has everything:** the Rangers Centennial jersey, worn on their 100th birthday on Nov. 16.
- **Skip for now:** the 2026-27 NBA City Editions. They do not hit the floor until **Jan. 14, 2027**.
- **How we picked:** our own [rankings](/stories/best-nfl-uniforms-2026-ranked) and our game-by-game [uniform records](/uniform-records), through Monday, Nov. 16.

## College

### UCLA Bruins Powder Blue Jersey

UCLA's powder blue and gold is a color pair nobody else in the sport owns. The 1954 powder blue throwback is the **No. 1 throwback in our [throwback rankings](/stories/best-throwback-uniforms-2026-every-sport)**, an A+, and we think powder blue should be every fan's first UCLA jersey. [UCLA 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/ucla-uniform-schedule-2026).

### USC Trojans Cardinal Jersey

The **No. 1 uniform in our [Big Ten uniform rankings](/stories/big-ten-football-uniforms-ranked-2026)**, an A. Cardinal with gold numbers, unchanged for decades, and USC wore it to beat No. 2 Ohio State 30-18 on Halloween. [USC 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/usc-uniform-schedule-2026).

### Michigan Wolverines Navy Jersey

Our No. 2 in the Big Ten, an A. The navy jersey with maize numbers is the base of the best-looking set in the conference, under the best helmet in college football. [Michigan 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/michigan-uniform-schedule-2026).

### LSU Tigers White Jersey

The **No. 1 uniform in our [SEC uniform rankings](/stories/sec-football-uniforms-ranked-2026)**, an A+. LSU is the one program that wears white at home on purpose, so the white jersey is the real Tiger Stadium look. Buy the white, not the purple. [LSU 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/lsu-uniform-schedule-2026).

## How We Picked

Every pick had to pass two tests. First, it is a jersey on sale this season. Second, it is either at the top of one of our rankings or unbeaten in our [uniform records](/uniform-records), which come from our game-by-game [NFL](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026), [NBA](/stories/nba-uniform-tracker-2026-27) and [NHL](/stories/nhl-uniform-tracker-2026-27) trackers. Records run through Monday, Nov. 16, 2026.

We left out the 2026-27 NBA City Editions. They do not hit the floor until Jan. 14, 2027, and most are returning designs. Our grades for all 30 are in the [City Edition ranking](/stories/nba-city-edition-jerseys-2026-27).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best NFL jersey to buy in 2026?**

The Los Angeles Chargers powder blue jersey. It is the No. 1 uniform in our 2026 NFL uniform rankings. The Chicago Bears Rivalries jersey is the best new NFL jersey of 2026.

**What is the best NBA jersey to buy in 2026?**

The Minnesota Timberwolves blue Icon Edition Swingman. The Wolves are 7-0 in it this season. For the best overall closet, the 76ers are No. 1 in our ranking, and their blue Icon jersey is 6-0.

**What is the best NHL jersey to buy in 2026?**

The Chicago Blackhawks red home sweater, the No. 1 sweater in our 2026-27 NHL rankings. For something new this season, the Pittsburgh Penguins Hometown Remix is the best of all 32 Remix jerseys.

**What is the luckiest jersey to give as a gift?**

The Tampa Bay Lightning blue home sweater, at 8-0-0 through Nov. 16. In the NBA, the Timberwolves blue is 7-0. In the NFL, the Chiefs in red over white are 5-0.

**What jersey should I buy for a fan who has everything?**

The New York Rangers Centennial jersey, worn on the club's 100th birthday on Nov. 16, 2026. The Seattle Seahawks Rivalries jersey is another good pick, because Seattle wears it on Christmas Day.

**What is a Swingman jersey?**

The Swingman is Nike's standard fan version of an NBA jersey. It is the one most fans wear, and it costs less than the Authentic. Every NBA pick in this guide is a Swingman.

**Are the 2027 NBA City Edition jerseys available for Christmas?**

We do not recommend them as a Christmas gift. The 2026-27 City Editions do not hit the floor until Jan. 14, 2027. An Icon Edition Swingman is the safer gift.

**What is the best college football jersey to give?**

UCLA powder blue. Our throwback rankings put UCLA's 1954 powder blue at No. 1 in college football. USC's cardinal and LSU's white are the best in the Big Ten and the SEC.

**What MLB jersey should I buy in the offseason?**

The Los Angeles Dodgers home white, our No. 1 jersey in baseball. The Cubs home pinstripes and the Mets black alternate are our next two picks.
