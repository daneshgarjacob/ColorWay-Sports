App State runs one of the cleanest uniform programs in the Sun Belt: two jerseys, two helmets, gold on everything, and almost no gimmicks. This is the full Appalachian State 2026 uniform schedule, the black home set, the white road look, the helmets that go with each, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games. Two are already confirmed. We update this page as each combination is locked in.

The current look is the one App State showed off in its 2024 uniform drop, and the appeal is how little it tries to do. The numerals are a clean block with a gold outline, the shoulder stripes are two thin gold bars, and the pants carry a small gold A on the hip. Nothing is chrome. Nothing is a gradient. It is a black and gold uniform that looks like a black and gold uniform, which in the Sun Belt is its own kind of statement.

The helmet is where App State gets its only real decision each week, because the mountain A logo reads completely differently on a black shell than on a white one. On black it is a gold stamp you can see from the top row of Kidd Brewer Stadium. On white it is gold with a black outline, thinner and older looking, closer to the helmets the program wore through its FCS title run. Two games in, App State has used one of each: black at home, white on the road.

The program also runs a weekly uniform reveal video with fans, presented by Beech Mountain Resort, which is where the Week 1 look against Maine went public two days before kickoff. It is a small thing, but it means App State tells you what it is wearing before the broadcast does.

The stands were the gold part, since the athletic department had already announced the opener as a gold out for fans. App State won 55-3 and led 31-0 in the third quarter.

**September 19 vs. Charlotte.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **black helmet with the gold mark, black jersey with gold numerals, black pants**, head to toe black for Black Saturday (Under the Lights). Nothing new appeared, so the helmet we were watching for turned out to be the standard black shell. The game was delayed and moved to a 9 p.m. Eastern kickoff, and App State won 26-21 after Charlotte scored twice in the fourth. Charlotte wore white.

**September 26.** White expected at NC State. A 7:30 p.m. Eastern kickoff on ESPNU in Raleigh, and the [NC State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/nc-state-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Wolfpack in red.

**October 3.** Bye.

**October 10 vs. Old Dominion.** Black expected. Homecoming at Kidd Brewer Stadium, 1 p.m., and homecoming is the other date worth circling if App State has anything held back.

**October 16 at Coastal Carolina.** White expected. A Friday night on ESPN2 in Conway, and the calendar note matters here: this one is Friday, not Saturday, so it shows up in a different week than most of the Sun Belt slate. **October 22 vs. James Madison.** Black expected. Thursday night at 7 p.m. on ESPNU for Family Weekend, the biggest home game on the schedule, and the [James Madison 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/james-madison-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Dukes in white for the trip to Boone.

**October 31 at Georgia Southern.** White expected. Halloween in Statesboro.

**November 7 vs. Georgia State.** Black expected. Heroes Day, which App State builds around military, veterans and first responders, and historically the program leans into the day with its messaging rather than a costume uniform.

**November 14 at Marshall.** White expected. This is the heaviest date on the schedule for reasons that have nothing to do with App State: Marshall hosts its annual Play For The 75 game on the exact anniversary of the 1970 plane crash, and the Thundering Herd will be in black at home. App State goes as the visitor in white.

## The Bottom Line

App State is not a uniform program in the Oregon sense and does not want to be. Black at home, white on the road, a matching helmet either way, gold on the edges of everything. Two games in, the Mountaineers have already used both sets exactly as you would guess, which makes the three home night and event games the ones worth watching: Black Saturday against Charlotte, homecoming against Old Dominion, and the Thursday nighter against James Madison. If anything new is coming in 2026, it shows up on one of those three. The grid above has every date, and we add the stars as each look is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is App State wearing this week?**

App State wore all black against Charlotte on Saturday, September 19: black helmet, black jersey and black pants, confirmed from the broadcast, in a 26-21 win. The game was Black Saturday (Under the Lights) at Kidd Brewer Stadium, delayed to a 9 p.m. Eastern kickoff, and it makes three straight home games in the same all-black combination.

**What are App State's football uniforms for 2026?**

Two jerseys and two helmets. The black home jersey has white numerals outlined in gold and two gold shoulder stripes, worn with black pants and a gold belt. The white road jersey has black numerals trimmed in gold, worn with white pants. The helmets are a black shell with a gold mountain A and a white shell with the same A outlined in black.

**What did App State wear against Maine and East Carolina?**

Black helmet, black jersey and black pants for the 55-3 win over Maine on September 5, then white helmet, white jersey and white pants for the 27-24 win at East Carolina on September 12. Both are confirmed from broadcast footage.

**Does App State have an alternate uniform in 2026?**

Nothing has been announced. App State has stayed with the same two Nike sets through the first two games. If an alternate appears, the most likely spots are the Black Saturday night game against Charlotte on September 19, homecoming against Old Dominion on October 10, or the Thursday night game against James Madison on October 22.

**What conference is App State in for 2026?**

The Sun Belt Conference. App State plays eight Sun Belt games in 2026 and carries the Sun Belt patch on the jersey, with non-conference dates against Maine, East Carolina, Charlotte and NC State.

**How many games does App State play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: six at Kidd Brewer Stadium against Maine, Charlotte, Old Dominion, James Madison, Georgia State and ULM, and six on the road at East Carolina, NC State, Coastal Carolina, Georgia Southern, Marshall and South Alabama, with a bye on October 3.
