**The Steelers wore the all-black Color Rush on Black Friday and beat the Broncos 27-5.** Pittsburgh wore exactly what it announced on November 24: the **black helmet, the black jersey with gold block numbers and gold sleeve stripes, and black pants**, on a field with **both end zones painted black**. Denver wore what it posted on Thursday: the **navy helmet, the Summit White jersey and orange pants**. It was the first Black Friday game in Steelers history and the first time Pittsburgh has worn the Color Rush this season.

## Quick Answers

- **What did the Steelers wear on Black Friday 2026?** The all-black Color Rush: black helmet, black jersey with gold numbers and gold sleeve stripes, black pants.
- **What did the Broncos wear?** The navy helmet, the Summit White jersey and orange pants, with white socks.
- **Final score?** Steelers 27, Broncos 5. Pittsburgh is 7-4, Denver 8-3.
- **Were the end zones black?** Yes. Both end zones at Acrisure Stadium were painted solid black with gold lettering for the game.
- **Steelers at home this season, from our log:** 5-0.
- **Broncos in Summit White this season, from our log:** 3-3. In every other jersey Denver is 5-0.
- **Our grades:** the Steelers' look, C+. The matchup, B-.

## The Game

Steelers 27, Broncos 5, at Acrisure Stadium. Per ESPN, T.J. Watt recovered a fumble in the end zone for the first touchdown, and Mason Rudolph hit DK Metcalf for 22 yards to make it 14-0 after one quarter. Denver's only points were an Alex Singleton safety and a 44-yard Wil Lutz field goal. Jalen Ramsey returned an interception 36 yards for a touchdown with 1:33 left in the half, right after a Chris Boswell field goal, to make it 24-5, and Boswell added a 33-yarder in the fourth. Rudolph went 9 of 15 for 142 yards. Bo Nix threw for 169 yards and two interceptions.

## Our Take

We gave this matchup a **B-**, and the Steelers' side is the reason it is not higher. The all-black Color Rush is a **C+** for us: a bit bland. Black on black with gold numbers reads as one dark block, especially on a gray, wet afternoon, and the regular black jersey with gold pants is the better Steelers uniform because the gold pants break it up. The black end zones were a good touch, and they did more for the day than the uniform did. Denver's side was the better half: navy, white and orange is a strong road look, and orange pants under Summit White is a combination we would like to see again.
