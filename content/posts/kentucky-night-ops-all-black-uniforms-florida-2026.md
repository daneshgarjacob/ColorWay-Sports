**Kentucky is going black.** The Wildcats posted their **Night Ops** uniform on Wednesday afternoon, three days before No. 11 Kentucky hosts Florida at Kroger Field on **Saturday, November 14, at 6:15 p.m. Eastern on ESPN**. It is a matte black helmet, a black jersey and black pants, and the one thing that keeps it from disappearing at night is the **white numbers**.

## What The Night Ops Uniform Looks Like

Everything below is from the four photos in Kentucky's post.

- **Helmet:** matte black shell with a **royal blue center stripe between two thin white stripes**, the blue interlocking UK on each side outlined in white, and a black facemask.
- **Jersey:** black, with **KENTUCKY** arched across the chest in light blue trimmed in royal blue, **white numbers outlined in royal blue**, a royal blue stripe across the shoulder and the blue Wildcat head on the sleeve.
- **Pants:** black, with a **royal blue and white stripe** down the side.
- **Cleats:** some players are in blue, some in black.

One note on the photos: X shows a **"Made with AI"** label on Kentucky's post, which usually means the images were edited with an AI tool. The uniform pieces match across all four photos, but we will confirm every detail from game photos on Saturday night.

## Our Grade: A-

<!-- Grade line below: Jake's take, confirm with Jake before changing -->
**A-.** We usually do not like all black. Most blackout uniforms turn a team into one dark shape on a TV screen, and nothing on the field tells you who is who. This one works because of the numbers. **White numbers on a black jersey are the brightest thing in the uniform**, and they pop under stadium lights in a way a black-on-black or gray number never does. The blue stripe on the helmet and the blue shoulder stripe carry just enough Kentucky blue that it still reads as Kentucky and not a generic black set.

What keeps it from an A: the pants. Black pants under a black jersey with a thin side stripe is the safe choice, and a blue-and-white stripe this narrow will vanish from the upper deck. A bolder stripe, or chrome or blue pants, would have made it a complete look.

Against Florida in white, it is a strong color-versus-white matchup for a Saturday night game on ESPN.

## Kentucky's History In Black

Kentucky has worn black before, but not often, and this is the first time the team has called a uniform **Night Ops**.

- **2011:** Kentucky wore black uniforms for the first time in program history for a "Blackout" game, according to UK Athletics. On3 dates that first blackout to the Mississippi State game on October 29, 2011.
- **2014:** Kentucky paired all black with chrome helmets, a look that was named a Uniform of the Year, per UK Athletics.
- **2023:** Kentucky wore black uniforms with chrome helmets at Kroger Field against Missouri, according to On3.
- **2026:** before Night Ops, Kentucky's only black this season was a **black helmet and black pants with the white jersey** at Oklahoma on October 17. Every game is in our [Kentucky 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/kentucky-uniform-schedule-2026).

Kentucky's other special looks this year: the [ICE COLD chrome helmet and all white at Tennessee](/stories/kentucky-ice-white-chrome-helmet-tennessee-2026) on November 7 and the Power K throwback against LSU on October 10.
