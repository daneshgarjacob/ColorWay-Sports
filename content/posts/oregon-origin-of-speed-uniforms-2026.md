Oregon just did the most Oregon thing possible: it made a football uniform about running. Nike and the Ducks revealed the **"Origin of Speed"** uniform on September 29, a near-black green set with electric green and yellow wings that look like they are still moving, built as a tribute to Hayward Field and the track history of Eugene. It is the third and final uniform in Oregon's **Generation O** series, and the Ducks debut it on **Saturday, October 10 against UCLA at Autzen Stadium.**

Our first look: we like it. The reveal photos are shot in near-darkness with green light everywhere, so it is honestly hard to judge the true colors yet, but what we can see is one of the better "concept" uniforms Oregon has done in years, because every loud idea on it actually means something.

*Every uniform photo in this post, including the cover image, comes from Oregon Football and Nike. These are the school's official reveal images, not ColorWay Sports originals.*

## What Oregon Revealed

The set is built on two colors, one old and one brand new:

- **Night Forest**, which Nike calls its darkest green. It is the base of the jersey and pants, and in most light it reads as black.
- **Green Strike**, a new color that debuts on this uniform, running across the jersey shoulders and down the sides of the helmet, with Volt and reflective silver as the accents.

The pieces:

- **The helmet** is a dark shell with a "kinetic wing" of green and yellow streaks on each side, angled at **26.2 degrees**, a nod to the marathon distance.
- **The jersey** is Night Forest with the same streaking wings sweeping across both shoulders and into a V at the collar, a small duck mark at the neck, and a green number outlined in silver.
- **The numbers** are wider at the top and leaner at the bottom, which Nike says is meant to mimic a superhero pose, and they are punched with **waffle-print perforation**, the pattern from Nike's original running shoe.
- **The pants** are dark to match, with a Nike swoosh and a side wordmark that leans *forward* instead of back, meant to capture a first-place finish at the tape.
- **The cleats** are black with volt laces, with the Hayward lanes and a clay-colored hue built in.

## The Hidden Details, and Why They Matter

The best part of this uniform is on the shoulder, and you would never catch it on TV. The Oregon "O" patch is drawn as **the original Hayward Field track**, with the stadium's outline forming the O and **six running lanes** inside it. Two of those lanes, **lanes 4 and 5**, are highlighted in a different color to honor two Ducks who died young: **Spencer Webb**, the tight end who died in 2022, and **Khyree Jackson**, the former Oregon cornerback who died in 2024 after being drafted by the Minnesota Vikings.

That is the difference between this and a lot of college "special" uniforms. It is not a random color swap chasing a recruit's Instagram. The whole thing, the streaks, the marathon angle, the waffle numbers, the forward-leaning wordmark, the track in the O, is about one idea: Oregon is where Nike and American running both started, and a lot of Oregon's great football players (LaMichael James, De'Anthony Thomas, Devon Allen) were track guys too. The uniform took about two years to design, with current players in the room.

## What Works

**The concept is airtight.** Every detail ties back to Hayward Field, and it is the rare uniform where the "story" is actually visible in the design rather than buried in a press release.

**The wings.** The streaked green and yellow across the shoulders is the signature, and it gives the Ducks' wing motif real motion. It should pop under the Autzen lights in a way a flat wing never does.

**The shoulder O.** The track-lane O with the two highlighted lanes is one of the best small details anyone has put on a college jersey this year.

## What We Want to See in Daylight

**The actual colors.** Night Forest is so dark that from the upper deck this will likely look like a black uniform with green trim, and the reveal photography, all green light and long exposure, does not tell us how it reads under stadium lights. We will know on October 10.

**The numbers.** Green on near-black with a silver outline could either glow or disappear on a broadcast. The silver edge should save it, but that is the thing we will be watching.

## When Oregon Wears the Origin of Speed Uniform

Once, as of now: **Saturday, October 10, 2026, vs. UCLA at Autzen Stadium.** Oregon is off on October 3, so this is the next game on the schedule. Fan apparel went on sale the same day as the reveal, September 29, on Nike's site and at select retailers. Every other combination Oregon wears this year is logged on our [Oregon uniform schedule 2026](/stories/oregon-uniform-schedule-2026).

## The Bottom Line

Oregon's Origin of Speed uniform is a dark, fast-looking set with real meaning in the details, most of all the Hayward Field "O" with lanes for Spencer Webb and Khyree Jackson. First look, we like it a lot. Our full grade comes when we see it in real light against UCLA on October 10.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Oregon's Origin of Speed uniform?**

It is a new Nike football uniform for the Oregon Ducks, revealed September 29, 2026, that honors Hayward Field and Oregon's track history. It uses a Night Forest (Nike's darkest green) base with new Green Strike and yellow speed-streak wings on the shoulders and helmet.

**When will Oregon wear the Origin of Speed uniforms?**

Oregon debuts the uniform on Saturday, October 10, 2026, against UCLA at Autzen Stadium.

**What do the two highlighted lanes on the Oregon shoulder patch mean?**

The Oregon "O" on the shoulder is drawn as the original six-lane Hayward Field track. Lanes 4 and 5 are highlighted to honor Spencer Webb and Khyree Jackson, two former Ducks who died in 2022 and 2024.

**Why is the Oregon helmet wing at 26.2 degrees?**

The kinetic wing on the helmet is angled at 26.2 degrees as a nod to the 26.2-mile marathon distance, part of the uniform's running theme.

**What is Oregon's Generation O series?**

Generation O is a three-year series of Nike special uniforms for Oregon football. Origin of Speed is the third and final uniform in the series.

**Can you buy the Oregon Origin of Speed jersey?**

Yes. Nike put Origin of Speed fan apparel on sale on September 29, 2026, at nike.com and select retail partners.
