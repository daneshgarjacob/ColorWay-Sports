FC Barcelona just dropped the eighth El Clasico jersey takeover from Spotify, and this one belongs to Olivia Rodrigo. A yellow butterfly logo built from the singer's initials replaces the Spotify wordmark on the front of Barcelona's iconic red-and-blue Nike home kit for the May 10 Clasico against Real Madrid at Spotify Camp Nou, with the women's team getting first wear in their May 6 Liga F match against Levante UD. At 23 years old Rodrigo is the youngest artist ever placed on the Barcelona shirt, joining a list that runs Drake, Rosalia, the Rolling Stones, Karol G, Coldplay, Travis Scott, and most recently Ed Sheeran for last October's Clasico against Madrid.

## The Olivia Rodrigo Butterfly Logo on the FC Barcelona Kit

The Rodrigo logo is a yellow butterfly formed from the artist's stylized initials, placed on the front of the Nike-made Barcelona home jersey in the same chest position normally held by the Spotify wordmark. The yellow against Barcelona's classic red-and-blue vertical stripes gives the kit a soft pop-color contrast that reads cleanly on broadcast and ties to Rodrigo's visual world, the same approach Spotify and Barcelona have run on every artist takeover since Drake's October 2022 OVO owl debut. The kit is the same Nike home jersey Barcelona wore for last fall's Ed Sheeran Clasico against Real Madrid and the rest of the 2025-26 La Liga season, with the artist logo as the only difference from the standard match-day uniform.

## When FC Barcelona Wears the Olivia Rodrigo Kit at Spotify Camp Nou

The Olivia Rodrigo Barcelona shirt makes its on-field debut on Wednesday, May 6, 2026, when FC Barcelona Femeni hosts Levante UD in Liga F at Spotify Camp Nou, the women's team becoming the first Barcelona side to wear an artist takeover before the men. The men's team then wears the same Rodrigo jersey for the El Clasico against Real Madrid on Sunday, May 10, 2026, also at Spotify Camp Nou, the second Clasico of the 2025-26 La Liga season after Ed Sheeran's October Play-album takeover at the same stadium. Both matches are home games for Barcelona at the renamed Spotify Camp Nou, reinforcing the streaming partner's footprint across the venue branding, the kit branding, and the wider 2026 Clasico moment.

## Where to Buy the Olivia Rodrigo FC Barcelona Limited Edition Kit

Barcelona released the Olivia Rodrigo capsule on Friday, May 1, 2026, at 10 a.m. CEST through Barca Official Stores and the club's online shop. The drop includes 1,899 limited-edition match-quality jerseys, the kit count a deliberate nod to FC Barcelona's 1899 founding year and the same scarcity move Barcelona ran on the Ed Sheeran Play kit drop last fall. The capsule extends past the jersey to a full Olivia Rodrigo lifestyle line built around T-shirts, fleece crews, hoodies, bucket hats, scarves, travel mugs, sticker packs, and tote bags, all carrying the yellow butterfly logo against Barcelona's club colors. A separate ultra-limited tier puts 22 match-worn shirts signed by the men's and women's starting eleven on sale alongside 11 shirts signed by Olivia Rodrigo herself, the signed pieces priced at 3,000 euros each and aimed squarely at the high-end Barcelona collector market.

## Olivia Rodrigo's Billions Club Concert in Barcelona on May 8

The kit drop runs alongside an exclusive Olivia Rodrigo concert at Billions Club Live in Barcelona on Friday, May 8, 2026, two days before the men's Clasico, the show staged for top fans celebrating Olivia's tracks in Spotify's Billions Club tier. Spotify's Billions Club program flags songs that have crossed one billion streams on the platform, and Olivia Rodrigo currently has multiple eligible tracks across her Sour and Guts albums. The concert is a private invite-only event for Spotify listeners rather than a public show, and lines up with Olivia Rodrigo's broader Guts world tour visibility ahead of the 2026 Clasico.

## Every Artist on the FC Barcelona El Clasico Kit Through the Spotify Jersey Takeover

Olivia Rodrigo is the eighth musician to land on a Barcelona jersey through the Spotify partnership that started in October 2022, the program running roughly twice a season around the two La Liga Clasicos and one of the most-watched kit-branding traditions in world football. Here is the full list, in order, of every artist FC Barcelona has worn for an El Clasico through the Spotify takeover.

### Drake on the FC Barcelona El Clasico Kit, October 16, 2022

Drake was the first artist on a Barcelona Clasico jersey, the OVO owl logo landing on the chest of the 2022-23 home kit for the El Clasico against Real Madrid at the Santiago Bernabeu on October 16, 2022. The Drake takeover was tied to the rapper crossing 50 billion Spotify streams, the first artist in the platform's history to hit that mark, and the OVO kit also carried the number 50 on the back as a streaming milestone callout. Real Madrid won the match 3-1, the first Clasico in the Spotify shirt-takeover era ending in Real Madrid's favor.

### Rosalia on the FC Barcelona El Clasico Kit, March 19, 2023

Rosalia followed Drake with the second Spotify artist takeover, the Motomami album logo replacing the Spotify wordmark for the second Clasico of the 2022-23 season on March 19, 2023. The Rosalia kit was the first time a Spanish-language artist took the Barcelona shirt and the first time a woman appeared on the Clasico jersey, the same match Barcelona won 2-1 to take the season series.

### The Rolling Stones on the FC Barcelona El Clasico Kit, October 28, 2023

The Rolling Stones brought the band's iconic tongue-and-lips emblem to the front of the 2023-24 Barcelona home kit for the October 28, 2023 Clasico, the first non-solo act in the Spotify takeover program and the first rock band on a Barcelona jersey. Real Madrid won the match 2-1, but the Stones tongue logo on the Barcelona shirt remains one of the most-replicated images of the entire artist takeover series.

### Karol G on the FC Barcelona El Clasico Kit, April 21, 2024

Karol G's barbed-wire heart logo took the front of the Barcelona kit for the April 21, 2024 Clasico, the second Latin artist on the shirt after Rosalia and a deliberate move by Spotify and Barcelona to keep the Latin music representation rolling year over year. Real Madrid won 3-2 in a high-scoring Clasico that became one of the most-watched matches of the 2023-24 La Liga season.

### Coldplay on the FC Barcelona El Clasico Kit, October 26, 2024

Coldplay brought the Moon Music album logo to the Barcelona home jersey for the October 26, 2024 Clasico, the first British rock band to take the kit and the first artist takeover with a logo built around an album rollout rather than a brand mark. Barcelona won 4-0 on the road in one of the most lopsided Clasico results in recent memory, the Coldplay kit becoming an instant collector item after the win.

### Travis Scott on the FC Barcelona El Clasico Kit, May 11, 2025

Travis Scott's Cactus Jack logo landed on the Barcelona kit for the May 11, 2025 Clasico, the second American hip-hop artist on the shirt after Drake and the second time the Cactus Jack logo crossed into football merchandise after Travis Scott's earlier Nike collaborations. Barcelona won 4-3 in a Clasico thriller that closed out the 2024-25 La Liga title race.

### Ed Sheeran on the FC Barcelona El Clasico Kit, October 26, 2025

Ed Sheeran took the kit for the October 26, 2025 Clasico, the first solo male pop artist on the Barcelona shirt and the kit that locked in the extended Spotify-Barcelona partnership through 2030 and the Spotify Camp Nou naming rights through 2034. The logo was the Play album wordmark from Sheeran's 2025 release, the album branding running across the chest in place of the Spotify mark.

### Olivia Rodrigo on the FC Barcelona El Clasico Kit, May 10, 2026

Olivia Rodrigo closes the 2025-26 Spotify takeover slate with the May 10, 2026 Clasico kit, the yellow butterfly logo making her the youngest artist on a Barcelona shirt and the third American solo artist on the front of the Barcelona jersey after Drake and Travis Scott. The Rodrigo kit also lines up with the women's match against Levante UD on May 6, the first time a Spotify artist takeover has run across both the men's and women's Barcelona squads in the same week.

## Why FC Barcelona Keeps Putting Artists on the El Clasico Kit

The FC Barcelona Spotify artist takeover started as a single one-off Drake kit in October 2022 and turned into a recurring twice-a-season tradition that now drives some of the highest-volume jersey sales on the Barcelona Official Store calendar. Each artist takeover lines up with a Clasico against Real Madrid, the most-watched club football match of the calendar year, and the limited 1,899-kit drop almost always sells out within hours, the scarcity tied directly to FC Barcelona's 1899 founding year. The artist takeovers also extend Spotify's brand presence well past the standard front-of-shirt sponsor placement, the artist logos dominating broadcast coverage, social-media chatter, and global news cycles in a way no static sponsor mark ever does.

The Olivia Rodrigo kit is the eighth and youngest entry in the program, a 23-year-old American pop star joining a list that already includes the biggest active artists in hip-hop, Latin music, rock, and now teen-leaning pop. The Spotify-Barcelona partnership is locked in through 2030, with the Spotify Camp Nou naming deal extending to 2034, so expect more artist takeovers across the next four La Liga seasons. The Rodrigo butterfly is a soft-pop entry that contrasts visually against the harder-edged Drake OVO owl, Travis Scott Cactus Jack, and Rolling Stones tongue marks that came before, the program now spanning a full decade of music genres on the Barcelona Clasico jersey.
