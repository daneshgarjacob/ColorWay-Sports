The Carolina Hurricanes open 2026-27 at Lenovo Center on Tuesday, September 29 against the Florida Panthers. This is the full Hurricanes uniform schedule: every sweater in the closet, what each one is for, and a game-by-game record of when it goes on the ice.

We build these before the season rather than after it, so the closet is here from day one and the dates fill in as they are announced. Third jerseys and any one-off specials get added here as Hurricanes announce them. The NHL schedules its alternates in blocks rather than all at once, so this page changes through the autumn.
