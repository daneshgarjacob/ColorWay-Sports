The 2026 Field of Dreams jerseys have leaked, and they leaked the way these things always seem to now: hanging on a store rack before anyone made it official. The Minnesota Twins host the Philadelphia Phillies at the Field of Dreams site in Dyersville, Iowa on August 13, 2026, live on Netflix, and both throwback uniforms turned up early. We pulled the images, sized up both jerseys, and graded them. One of these is a genuinely great throwback. The other is good but plays it safe.

Nothing here is officially unveiled yet, so treat the finer details as leak-stage until MLB and the teams confirm them. The grades below are on the looks as they appear in these first images.

## The Phillies Throwback: Our Grade

This is the stronger of the two, and it is not particularly close. The Phillies went with a pale gray-cream base, the classic cursive "Phillies" script across the chest in red with a navy drop shadow, and clean red-and-navy piping running down the placket and around the collar. The detail that makes it is the round "Baseball Centennial" sleeve patch, the same 1839 to 1939 mark the sport wore to celebrate baseball's supposed 100th birthday. It is a real, specific piece of history rather than a generic old-timey flourish, and it gives the whole uniform a reason to exist.

Everything on this jersey is pulling in the same direction. The script is the Phillies' best asset and they let it lead, the piping frames it without shouting, and the patch ties the design to an actual era instead of a vibe. The only thing keeping it out of the top tier is a slightly busy collar, where the red, navy, and cream all meet and the eye has to work for a second. That is a small ding on an otherwise excellent throwback jersey.

**Grade: A-**

## The Twins Throwback: Our Grade

The Twins look good too, just a full tier more cautious. Their Field of Dreams uniform is a cream jersey with black pinstripes, the old-school "Twins" script across the chest in red with a black outline, and a small vintage mascot patch on the sleeve. It is a handsome, warm, unmistakably retro jersey, and the cream-and-black combination photographs beautifully.

Two things hold it to a B. The first is that pinstripes plus a throwback script is the safest move in the entire throwback playbook. It is nearly impossible to make it look bad, but it is also hard to make it feel special, and this one lands closer to tasteful than memorable. The second is a history wrinkle: the Twins did not exist until 1961, so a "period" throwback for a franchise this young is more nostalgia costume than heritage. It is a lovely uniform. It just is not saying as much as the Phillies' is.

**Grade: B**

## What Era Are These Throwbacks Actually From?

This is where the two jerseys separate, and it is the whole reason for the grade gap. The Phillies patch points to a real season. Baseball celebrated its centennial in 1939, and the Phillies were there for it, so a 1939-styled uniform with that patch is honest throwback design. It commemorates something.

The Twins have a harder job, because the franchise only landed in Minnesota in 1961 after moving from Washington. A vintage cream pinstripe with a cartoon mascot evokes the era without belonging to it, which is fine for a fun one-off but leaves the design doing an impression rather than telling a story. Both teams are dressing up for a game staged inside a cornfield built for a movie about baseball nostalgia, so a little theater is the entire point. The Phillies just grounded their theater in an actual date, and that earns the higher grade.
