Every NBA team has four, five or six jerseys this season. We asked a simpler question: if each team could keep only one, which one should it be, and how do those 30 stack up against each other? This is Jake's list, one jersey per team, from the shirts teams are actually wearing in 2026-27.

The rules: one pick per team, and it has to be in the rotation this season, including the throwbacks and alternates that come out after New Year's. Every entry shows the jersey, says what makes it work or not, and tells you how many games it gets on the NBA's official schedule. We counted down from 30, so you will have to scroll for the top. Tell us who we got wrong.

## Quick Answers

- **How we picked:** each team's single best jersey in this season's rotation, then all 30 ranked against each other.
- **The pattern:** not one black jersey made the top 10, and the three black picks on the list all finished 23rd or lower.
- **The most-worn pick:** the Rockets' red, at 37 games. The least: the Clippers' light blue, at five.
- **Every team's full schedule:** in our [NBA uniform schedule hub](/stories/nba-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best NBA jersey in 2026?**

The Los Angeles Lakers' gold jersey with LAKERS in purple, in our ranking. It has barely changed in decades and no team in any sport owns a color the way the Lakers own that gold. The Utah Jazz's purple mountains UTAH jersey is second and the Indiana Pacers' blue ABA throwback is third.

**What is the best NBA jersey for 2026-27?**

The Lakers' gold. The Lakers wear it 28 times on this season's schedule.

**What is the worst NBA jersey?**

On our list of every team's best jersey, the weakest is the Milwaukee Bucks' black BUCKS jersey at No. 30. It is the best thing in the Bucks' closet, and it still hides Milwaukee's green under black. The New Orleans Pelicans' red CRESCENT CITY jersey is 29th.

**Which NBA team has the best jerseys?**

For a single jersey, the Lakers, for the gold. For the whole closet, we ranked all 30 separately in [every NBA team's uniform closet, ranked](/stories/nba-uniform-closets-ranked-2026-27), where the 76ers finished first.

**Did the Utah Jazz bring back the purple mountain jersey?**

Yes. The purple jersey with mountains fading up the chest behind UTAH is in the rotation for 2026-27, on the schedule for 24 games. It is No. 2 on our list.

**Do the Timberwolves have new jerseys for 2026-27?**

**What is the Pacers' blue ABA jersey?**

The Indiana Pacers' blue ABA Classic Edition throwback, No. 3 overall. The best in the Western Conference is the Lakers' gold.
