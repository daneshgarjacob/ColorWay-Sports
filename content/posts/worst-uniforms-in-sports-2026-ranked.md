We grade every uniform in the NFL, NBA, MLB and NHL, every jersey in every closet. Most of sports looks better than it ever has. These ten do not.

The rules: uniforms teams are actually wearing in 2026, across all four leagues, ranked by the grades we have already given them. No grudges, no throwbacks, no retired sets. Three get an F. A team playing in the World Series is on here. So is a two-time Stanley Cup champion. And we are Lakers fans, and the Lakers are on here too.

We are counting down from 10 to the single worst uniform in sports. Tell us who we got wrong.

## Why the NFL Owns the Bottom of This List

The three F grades all come from the NFL, and two of them come from one place: the new Rivalries program, which lets teams add a one-game uniform against a division rival. It was supposed to be the most fun uniform idea of the year. Instead, teams with some of the best base looks in sports (Green Bay, Minnesota) used it to bury their own identities under texture, runes and numbers you cannot read. Baseball's version of the problem is the City Connect program, where Milwaukee, Washington and Cincinnati all traded what makes them recognizable for a concept. The NBA's is the Specter Edition, eight black-based alternates that mute the league's best color schemes.

The Minnesota Vikings Rivalries uniform, which we grade an F. It stacks a duller purple, a gold facemask, rune numbers and knot details on top of one of the best base uniforms in the NFL. It debuts December 20 on Sunday Night Football against Detroit.

**What is the worst uniform in the NHL?**

The Vikings Rivalries uniform. The ten worst in the NFL alone are in our [worst NFL uniforms of 2026](/stories/worst-nfl-uniforms-2026-ranked) ranking.

**Why are the Lakers on this list?**

Because the Specter Edition buries purple and gold under dark satin on black. We are Lakers fans, and we still gave it a C. The Lakers' regular closet is fine; this is about one alternate worn three times in December.

**How did you grade these uniforms?**

Every uniform here was graded in its own review or team ranking on ColorWay Sports, on design, color, fit with the team's identity and how it reads from the stands and on TV. This list pulls the lowest grades across the three leagues.

The Washington Nationals' District Blueprint City Connect and the Milwaukee Brewers' Wisco City Connect share our lowest grade among the baseball uniforms on this list, a D-. **Are the Brewers wearing the Wisco City Connect in the World Series?**

## The Bottom Line

The worst uniforms in sports right now are not ugly by accident. They are good franchises trying too hard: too many ideas on one jersey, too little of the colors fans love. The Vikings Rivalries uniform is the worst of all of them. Think we are wrong about your team? That is the point. Tell us.
