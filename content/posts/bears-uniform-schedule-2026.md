## The Rivalries Uniform (Week 16, Christmas Day, Confirmed)

Chicago is part of the 2026 class of the NFL x Nike Rivalries program, which is rolling through the league division by division. The NFC North gets its turn this year, and the Bears will wear their set **one time**: Friday, December 25, at noon Central, at home against Green Bay on Netflix.

The uniform itself has not been fully revealed yet. We will grade it the day it drops.

The scheduling here is close to perfect. Rivalries jerseys are supposed to be worn against the rival they were designed around, and Bears versus Packers is the oldest rivalry in the NFL, 200-plus meetings deep. Putting the debut on Christmas Day, in the noon window, on a streaming platform that has made the holiday slate its own event, is the single largest audience Chicago will have all year. If you are only going to wear a uniform once, wear it then.

### The Wrinkle: Both Teams Get a Rivalries Game, in Different Weeks

Here is the part that makes the 2026 NFC North schedule unusual. Green Bay is also in this year's Rivalries class, and the Packers are debuting **their** set against the Bears in Week 5 at Lambeau Field on October 11.

So the same rivalry produces two Rivalries uniforms in two different games, and neither team wears its set in the other's game. Chicago is in white at Lambeau in Week 5 while Green Bay debuts. Green Bay is in white at Soldier Field in Week 16 while Chicago debuts. Each club gets its own moment at home, which is the sensible way to split it, and it means Bears-Packers is worth watching twice this year for reasons that have nothing to do with the standings. Our [Packers 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/packers-uniform-schedule-2026) has the other half of it.

## Navy at Soldier Field (9 Games)

The navy jersey with the burnt orange numbers is the base of the whole thing, and it draws every home date except Christmas: the Vikings in Week 2, a Monday night against Philadelphia in Week 3, the Jets in Week 4, a Thursday nighter against New England in Week 7, Sunday night against Tampa Bay in Week 9, the Saints in Week 11, the Jaguars in Week 13, and the Lions in Week 17.

That is four standalone national windows in navy, which is a lot for a team with almost no wardrobe. Chicago does not need one. The navy-and-orange combination is one of the three or four most recognizable uniforms in American sports, the wishbone C has not needed a redesign since 1962, and the whole set works because nothing about it is trying. Wearing the same thing for sixty years is the point.

## White on the Road (8 Games)

Weeks 1, 5, 6, 8, 12, 14, 15 and 18. The road white is the same uniform with the values flipped, navy numbers on white, and it holds up just as well.

The Week 12 game is the one to circle. Chicago plays at Detroit on Thanksgiving, which puts the white set in the biggest daytime window of the regular season, and against a Lions team that has become one of the better-dressed in the league. The Week 8 trip to Seattle on Monday night is the other good one, since our [Seahawks 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/seahawks-uniform-schedule-2026) has them in College Navy for it, which makes for a genuinely handsome navy-on-white frame.

## Is the Orange Jersey Coming Back?

This is the question Bears fans actually have, and the honest answer is that nothing has been announced.

Chicago has an orange alternate in the closet and has used it in recent seasons, but the team has not named a 2026 date for it, and the reporting around the Rivalries rollout suggests the new set is taking the alternate slot this year rather than sharing it. We are not going to put an orange game on the grid above on a hunch. If a date gets announced, we will add it here the day it lands.

## How the Bears Rotation Works in 2026

1. **Navy at home, white on the road.** No exceptions on the current schedule except Christmas.
2. **One alternate game all season**, the Rivalries debut in Week 16 against Green Bay.
3. **No orange date announced**, and no throwback on the calendar.
4. **Pants and helmet combinations** are a week-of call, as they are for every team.

## The Bottom Line

The Bears are running one of the smallest closets in the NFL and it does not cost them a thing, because the thing they own is already great. Navy and orange, the wishbone C, and sixty years of not messing with it.

The one swing they are taking is well placed. A brand-new Rivalries uniform, debuting against Green Bay, on Christmas Day, at Soldier Field, on Netflix. That is the biggest stage the franchise will have this season and they are using it on the rivalry that defines them. Whatever the uniform turns out to look like, the scheduling is an A.
