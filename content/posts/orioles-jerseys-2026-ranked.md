The Orioles run a deep, loose rotation in 2026, and it is one of the more fun closets in baseball to work through. There is no rigid day-by-day script the way the Phillies or Cardinals run one, just a stack of good options the team picks from game to game, with the lone fixed rule being the City Connect on Friday home games. Orange, black, cream, and the classic Baltimore white all get their turns.

And at the center of it is one of the best home uniforms in the sport. Here is every Baltimore Orioles jersey and uniform for 2026, ranked worst to first and graded.

The brand-new 2026 City Connect is the one Orioles look we just do not like next to the rest of the closet. Built around a "From the Stoop to the Yard" theme, it is a cream jersey with green sleeves and an orange-trimmed green "BMORE" wordmark, with warehouse-brick accents nodding to the B&O Warehouse. There are real ideas in it, and it debuted fittingly on "410 Day."

But cream and green is a strange direction for a franchise whose whole identity is orange and black, and against the sharp, classic looks ahead of it, the City Connect feels busy and off-brand. It is anchored to Friday home games, so you will see plenty of it, but for us it is the clear low point of the rotation and gets a C.

We will admit we are not big black-jersey guys in general, but this one is pretty cool. The black alternate carries an orange "Orioles" wordmark and pairs with the sleeker black-crown "O's" cap instead of the cartoon bird, and the orange-on-black contrast reads sharp under the lights. It is a moody night-game option that the team pulls at its own discretion.

It lands at a B-, which for a black alternate from us is genuine praise. It is a good-looking jersey, it just is not one of the looks that makes the Orioles the Orioles, and in a closet with this much orange-and-black character, a black top is always going to sit a notch below the classics.

The road gray is a clean, classic travel set with "Baltimore" in script across the chest, the city name on the road to the team name at home, which is exactly the traditional baseball touch we love. The orange-and-black trim keeps it unmistakably Orioles even in muted gray.

It grades a B+, a genuinely strong road uniform that does its job without a wasted detail. It sits behind the color jerseys and the home white only because a gray, however well done, is rarely going to be the most exciting thing in a closet this colorful. When it appears, it is a reminder of how good a simple, correct road set can look.

The orange alternate is a wall of Baltimore orange with the "Orioles" script, and it is a genuine highlight of the rotation. Orange is the franchise's signature color, and leaning all the way into it gives the team a jolt of energy that most clubs cannot pull off. It can show up at home on no set day, which is part of the fun. We love it and grade it an A-.

Bonus points for the look this jersey unlocks: a few times a year the Orioles pair it with orange pants for the head-to-toe orange-on-orange retro combo, a throwback to the 1971 to 1972 Brooks Robinson era. Keeping that full-orange statement rare is exactly right, and it ties the modern Orioles back to one of the franchise's golden runs. The everyday orange top earns the A-, and the retro combo is the cherry on top.

This is the best thing the Orioles own, and it is all about the hat. The home white carries the "Orioles" script in orange and black and pairs with the classic white-front cap topped by the smiling cartoon Oriole Bird, one of the most beloved marks in all of sports. We love the cap and we love the jersey, and together they make one of the friendliest, most instantly recognizable home looks in baseball.

There is nothing to fix here and nothing we would touch. The clean white lets the orange-and-black script and that perfect cartoon-bird cap do all the work, and it never gets old. It is the everyday look at Camden Yards, the uniform the whole closet is built around, and a very easy A at the top of this list.

## The Bottom Line on the Orioles Jersey Rankings

The Orioles have one of the deeper, more likable closets in baseball, and it all revolves around that home white and the cartoon-bird cap, a genuine top-tier home look that anchors everything else. The orange alternate is a fantastic color-pop that unlocks the beloved orange-on-orange throwback, the road gray is a clean, classic B+ travel set, and even the black alternate, not usually our thing, is a sharp night-game option.

The only real miss is the new "BMORE" City Connect, where the cream and green pulls the team away from the orange-and-black identity that makes the rest of the rotation so strong. Lose that one look and this is one of the most consistent, most fun closets in the sport. Baltimore keeps it loose, and when the everyday jersey is this good, they can afford to.
