**Texas A&M is the home team on Black Friday, so the Aggies wear maroon and Texas wears white.** Texas visits Kyle Field on **Friday, November 27, at 6:30 p.m. Central (7:30 Eastern) on ABC**. The last time this game was in College Station, in 2024, it was **maroon against the Longhorns' all-white "icy whites,"** and that is the expected look again. A&M has already used its two special sets this season, the camo on October 3 and the all-black set on November 14. Neither school has posted its uniform for this game yet, and we update this page the day either one does.

## What Texas A&M And Texas Wore In 2024 And 2025

The rivalry came back in 2024, the first meeting in 13 years, after Texas joined the SEC ([CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/college-football/news/texas-vs-texas-a-m-football-rivalry-renewed-longhorns-aggies-set-to-reignite-feud-on-2024-sec-schedule)). Both games are checked against ESPN's game photos.

- **2025, Austin: Texas 27, Texas A&M 17.** Texas was at home in the **white helmet, burnt orange jersey and white pants**. A&M wore a **maroon helmet, white jersey and maroon pants**.
- **2024, Kyle Field: Texas 17, Texas A&M 7.** A&M wore a **maroon helmet, maroon jersey and white pants**. Texas wore the **icy whites**: white helmet, white jersey, white pants.

The 2026 game is back at Kyle Field, so the expected answer is the 2024 picture: maroon against all white.

## Why The Home Team Wears Color

SEC home teams wear their color jersey and visitors wear white. That is why this rivalry has been maroon against white in College Station and burnt orange against white in Austin. The one exception Texas has hit this season was LSU, which chose white at home and put Texas in burnt orange. If A&M ever picked white at Kyle Field, the same thing would happen, which is why both looks stay Expected until the schools post.
