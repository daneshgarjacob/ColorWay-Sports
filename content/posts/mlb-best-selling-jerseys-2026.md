MLB only tells us who sells the most jerseys a few times a year, and each list gets about a day of attention. We keep every official list here and compare each one to the last, so you can see whose jersey is rising and whose is cooling off. We will add the next one the day it comes out.

**The quick version:** Shohei Ohtani finished No. 1 for the fourth straight season, Pete Crow-Armstrong jumped from 10th to 3rd, and Ronald Acuña Jr. climbed 13 spots to 4th.

## The 2026 MLB Jersey Sales Top 10

MLB and MLB Players Inc. released two lists this year, one on Opening Day (March 26) and one at the end of the regular season (September 25). Both count Nike jerseys sold through the Fanatics network, including MLBShop.com, so the two lists compare cleanly.

Ohtani is the first player to finish No. 1 four seasons in a row since MLB started tracking in 2010. The real story is underneath him. **Pete Crow-Armstrong went from 10th to 3rd**, the biggest jump inside the top 10, and **Ronald Acuña Jr. climbed 13 spots** to 4th. Yamamoto, who opened the year 2nd on the back of his World Series run, slid to 9th as the season went on.

Rounding out the top 20 on the September list: Corey Seager, Vladimir Guerrero Jr., Adley Rutschman (now with the Red Sox), Kyle Schwarber, Paul Skenes, Cal Raleigh, Elly De La Cruz, Jackson Merrill, Nolan Arenado and Munetaka Murakami of the White Sox. Raleigh and Arenado both opened the year in the top 6 and finished outside the top 15.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best-selling MLB jersey in 2026?**

Shohei Ohtani's Dodgers jersey, No. 1 on MLB's September 25 list for the fourth straight season.

**Who is in the top 5 of MLB jersey sales in 2026?**

Shohei Ohtani, Aaron Judge, Pete Crow-Armstrong, Ronald Acuña Jr. and Fernando Tatis Jr.

**How many times has Ohtani led MLB in jersey sales?**

Four straight seasons. MLB says he is the first player to finish No. 1 four years in a row since it started tracking in 2010.

**Whose MLB jersey sales went up the most in 2026?**

Inside the top 10, Ronald Acuña Jr. climbed the most, from 17th on Opening Day to 4th. Pete Crow-Armstrong rose from 10th to 3rd, and Fernando Tatis Jr. went from outside the top 20 to 5th.

**Whose MLB jersey sales dropped in 2026?**

Yoshinobu Yamamoto fell from 2nd to 9th and Mookie Betts from 5th to 8th. Cal Raleigh and Nolan Arenado both started the year in the top 6 and finished outside the top 15.

**Is Pete Crow-Armstrong's jersey popular?**

Yes. His Cubs jersey was the third best-selling jersey in baseball on the end-of-season list, behind only Ohtani and Judge.

**How many Dodgers are in the top 10 of MLB jersey sales?**

Four: Ohtani (1st), Freddie Freeman (6th), Mookie Betts (8th) and Yoshinobu Yamamoto (9th).

**Who made the MLB jersey sales top 20 in 2026?**

After the top 10, the list continued with Corey Seager, Vladimir Guerrero Jr., Adley Rutschman, Kyle Schwarber, Paul Skenes, Cal Raleigh, Elly De La Cruz, Jackson Merrill, Nolan Arenado and Munetaka Murakami.

**How does MLB measure jersey sales?**

The list ranks Nike player jerseys sold across the Fanatics network, including MLBShop.com, and is released jointly by MLB and MLB Players Inc.

**When does MLB release its most popular jerseys list?**

Usually on Opening Day, around the All-Star break and at the end of the regular season. The 2026 lists came out March 26 and September 25.
