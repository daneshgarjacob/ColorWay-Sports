**The Bengals have tiger stripes on their helmets because their first helmet looked too much like the Browns'.** From 1968 through 1980, Cincinnati wore a plain orange helmet with "BENGALS" arched across the side. Paul Brown, who founded both teams, said that from a distance you could not read the word, and the helmet looked like Cleveland's. In 1981 the lettering came off and six black tiger stripes went on. That same season, the Bengals made their first Super Bowl.

Forty-five years later the stripes are still there. They are the only thing on the helmet: no logo, no letters. Here is how that happened, what has changed around the helmet since, and where it stands in 2026.

## Quick Answers

- **Why do the Bengals have stripes on their helmets?** Paul Brown wanted a helmet people could recognize from far away. The old one, plain orange with "BENGALS" on the side, looked like Cleveland's at a distance.
- **When did the stripes start?** 1981. Before that, from 1968 to 1980, the helmet said BENGALS in block letters.
- **Who designed the striped helmet?** Paul Brown chose it, the team says. The drawing is credited to Bruce Claypool, a designer at NFL Properties in 1981.
- **How many stripes are on the helmet?** Six black tiger stripes, running from one side of the helmet to the other.
- **Did the helmet work?** The Bengals went 12-4 in 1981 and reached Super Bowl XVI, their first, in the first season with stripes.
- **Has the helmet changed since?** Barely. The 1997, 2004 and 2021 uniform changes all left the striped helmet alone. The big addition is the White Bengal, a white version of the striped helmet, in 2022.
- **Our take (opinion):** it is the best helmet in the NFL. It turned the team's name into a pattern, and nobody else can copy it.

Paul Brown coached the Cleveland Browns, the team named after him, until Art Modell fired him in 1963. He came back as the founder of an expansion team in Cincinnati, announced the Bengals name and colors in 1967, and the team began play in 1968.

The Bengals' own uniform history describes the first helmet this way: orange, with a gray facemask, and "Bengals" arched across both sides in block letters, black with a thin white outline. The uniform around it stayed almost the same for more than a decade. In 1980 the facemask changed from gray to black, and the team added numbers on the back of the helmet so equipment staff could tell whose helmet was whose.

Put that next to Cleveland. The Browns wore an orange helmet too, with no logo at all, only stripes down the middle. From the stands or on a small TV, an orange helmet with thin black lettering and an orange helmet with a thin center stripe were hard to tell apart. Both teams were also coached and built around the same man, so the resemblance was not an accident of color. (We explain why Cleveland never added a logo in [what if the Browns put a logo on their helmet](/stories/what-if-browns-put-a-logo-on-their-helmet).)

## "When You Were Far Away, It Looked Like Cleveland's Helmet"

By 1981 Brown was the Bengals' vice president and general manager, and he wanted a helmet that would be identified with Cincinnati at a glance. The team's uniform history quotes him directly: "You couldn't read 'Bengals' on our helmet from a distance. When you were far away, it looked like Cleveland's helmet."

Bengals.com also says Brown wanted something in the spirit of the Baltimore Colts' horseshoe and the San Diego Chargers' lightning bolt, helmets you recognize before you can read anything. His goal, in the team's words, was a helmet that would be "instantly recognizable even from a distance."

## 1981: Six Tiger Stripes

The 1981 uniform was a full makeover, and the helmet was the loudest part. Per the Bengals, the shell stayed orange and kept its black facemask, and the block-letter "Bengals" was replaced by **six black tiger stripes that run from one side of the helmet to the other**. The same tiger pattern went on the jersey shoulders and straight down the full length of each pant leg.

**Who designed it?** The team's history says the design was chosen by Paul Brown. The drawing itself is credited to **Bruce Claypool**, who worked for NFL Properties, the league's design and licensing arm, in 1981. Adweek described the helmet in 2022 as his best-known work and said the wrap-around design of the Los Angeles Rams' horns helmet was his inspiration. Claypool has also said publicly that he has no paperwork proving it, only his own account, so we treat it as credited rather than documented.

Not everyone loved it at first. The Bengals' history recalls that the Cincinnati Enquirer quoted an NFL Properties source saying the design "was not our first choice" among several shown to the team, then adding that "these helmets will definitely get recognized." That part turned out to be right.

## The First Striped Season Ended In The Super Bowl

The 1981 Bengals went **12-4**, won the AFC Central and beat the San Diego Chargers 27-7 in the AFC Championship Game at Riverfront Stadium on January 10, 1982, the Freezer Bowl, still the coldest game in NFL history by wind chill. It sent Cincinnati to its first Super Bowl.

Super Bowl XVI was played on January 24, 1982, at the Pontiac Silverdome in Michigan. The Bengals lost to the San Francisco 49ers, **26-21**, after a third-quarter goal-line stand kept them out of the end zone. Ken Anderson brought them back within one score, but they could not recover the late onside kick.

The helmet did not win that game, but it arrived with the best season the franchise had ever had, and every Super Bowl the Bengals have played since has been in stripes: Super Bowl XXIII after the 1988 season and Super Bowl LVI after the 2021 season, both losses.

## What Changed Around The Helmet: 1991, 1997, 2004 And 2021

Photo: <a href="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kevin_Huber_w_helmet.jpg" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Tarcil</a> / <a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">CC BY-SA 3.0</a></p>

The jerseys have changed several times. The helmet has not, beyond small details.

- **1991:** small NFL shield logos were added to the helmet, jerseys and pants, per the team's history.
- **1997:** a light update the team called "evolutionary, not revolutionary." The helmet was unchanged; the jerseys picked up a leaping tiger logo on the sleeves where the numbers used to be.
- **2004:** a complete redesign of the jerseys and pants, only the second in team history, which added an orange jersey and a second set of pants. The Bengals' media guide says the tiger-striped helmet "was not altered."
- **2021:** the Bengals unveiled new uniforms on April 19, 2021, their first major change in 17 years, built with Nike from the white Color Rush set. The team's announcement said the striped helmet was unchanged.

That 2021 season ended in Super Bowl LVI. Both times the Bengals rebuilt the uniform around the helmet, in 1981 and 2021, they made the Super Bowl in the first year.

When the NFL began allowing a second helmet shell again in 2022, Cincinnati did not invent a new logo. It took the stripes and put them on white. The **White Bengal** helmet, black tiger stripes on a white shell, debuted on September 29, 2022, in a 27-15 Thursday night win over Miami, and it is worn with the white jersey and white pants.

It is now 6-1 in that uniform after beating Pittsburgh 38-18 on Sunday Night Football on November 15, 2026, its only date this season. Every game, score and detail is in [the White Bengal vs. Steelers on Sunday Night Football](/stories/bengals-white-bengal-uniform-steelers-snf-2026).

## Our Take

The striped helmet is the best in the NFL, and the reason is simple: it is the name. Most teams put a logo on a helmet. Cincinnati made the whole shell into the animal, so you know who it is from the upper deck, from a phone screen, from a blurry photo. That is exactly what Paul Brown asked for in 1981. The fact that the team has redesigned everything else three times and left the helmet alone every time tells you the Bengals know it too. The White Bengal proves the point: the stripes work on any color.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why do the Bengals have stripes on their helmets?**

Because Paul Brown wanted a helmet you could recognize from a distance. The original helmet, plain orange with "BENGALS" in block letters, looked like the Cleveland Browns' helmet from far away. In 1981 the letters were replaced by tiger stripes.

**When did the Bengals get tiger stripes on their helmets?**

In 1981. The Bengals wore the lettered helmet from their first season, 1968, through 1980.

**What did the Bengals helmet look like before 1981?**

Orange, with "Bengals" arched across both sides in black block letters with a thin white outline. The facemask was gray until 1980, when it switched to black.

**Who designed the Bengals helmet?**

Paul Brown chose it, according to the team. The design is credited to Bruce Claypool, a designer at NFL Properties in 1981, though Claypool himself has said he has no documents proving it.

**How many stripes are on the Bengals helmet?**

Six black tiger stripes, running from one side of the helmet to the other, per the Bengals' uniform history.

**Why did the Bengals helmet look like the Browns helmet?**

Both were orange, and Paul Brown built both teams. Brown coached Cleveland until 1963, then founded the Bengals, who started play in 1968. In his words, "When you were far away, it looked like Cleveland's helmet."

**Did the Bengals go to the Super Bowl the year they got the stripes?**

Yes. The 1981 Bengals went 12-4, won the Freezer Bowl against San Diego and reached Super Bowl XVI, where they lost to the 49ers, 26-21, on January 24, 1982.

**Have the Bengals ever changed their striped helmet?**

Not in any major way. Small NFL shield logos were added in 1991, but the uniform changes in 1997, 2004 and 2021 all kept the same helmet. The only new helmet is the White Bengal alternate, added in 2022.

**Does the Bengals helmet have a logo?**

No. The stripes are the whole design. The Browns are the other NFL team with no logo on the helmet, and theirs has only a center stripe.

**What is the White Bengal helmet?**

A white helmet with the same black tiger stripes, worn with the all-white White Bengal uniform. It debuted on September 29, 2022, and the Bengals are 6-1 in it.

**When did the Bengals last change their uniforms?**

April 19, 2021, their first major uniform change in 17 years. The jerseys and pants changed. The helmet did not.

**What helmet are the Bengals wearing this week?**

The orange striped helmet. Cincinnati plays at Washington on Monday Night Football, November 23, and we expect the white road jersey. Every week is on the [Bengals 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/bengals-uniform-schedule-2026).

**Have the Bengals won a Super Bowl?**

No. They have played in three, all in the striped helmet: Super Bowl XVI (1981 season), XXIII (1988 season) and LVI (2021 season).
