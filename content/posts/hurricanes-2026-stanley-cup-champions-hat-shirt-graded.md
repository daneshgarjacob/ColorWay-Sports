The Carolina Hurricanes are Stanley Cup champions. For the first time since 2006, and only the second time in franchise history, Carolina lifted the Cup — closing out the Vegas Golden Knights 4-2 with a 3-0 shutout in Game 6 on the road, Brandon Bussi stopping all 22 shots, Taylor Hall and Jackson Blake scoring and Nikolaj Ehlers sealing it. And as the team passed the Cup around the visitors' room in Las Vegas, they were in the official 2026 Stanley Cup Champions locker room gear: the Fanatics structured cap and the authentic locker room tee.

We just graded the [Knicks' 2026 NBA Champions set](/stories/knicks-2026-nba-champions-hat-shirt-graded) and came away impressed; the league did good work on championship gear this year, and Carolina's set keeps the streak going. Here is our grade on both pieces.

## Carolina Hurricanes 2026 Stanley Cup Champions Hat

<div style="margin: 2em 0; text-align: center;">
  <img src="/images/posts/hurricanes-champions-gear/hurricanes-2026-stanley-cup-champions-hat.avif" alt="Carolina Hurricanes 2026 Stanley Cup Champions Fanatics structured adjustable hat in charcoal with 2026 Stanley Cup over a large Champions wordmark, the Hurricanes warning-flag logo, cream piping on the brim, and a textured satin finish" style="max-width: 100%; border-radius: 12px;" />

This is the piece to beat. The Fanatics structured adjustable cap is a sleek charcoal-graphite, and the texture is what sells it: a subtle satin-finish fabric with a clean cream piping line tracing the brim and crown that gives the whole thing a premium, understated feel. "2026 STANLEY CUP" sits above a big, bold "CHAMPIONS" wordmark in silver-white, with the Hurricanes warning-flag crest centered below and a small Fanatics flag on the side. It is genuinely wearable — the kind of championship hat you would actually wear out, not just store in a closet next to the ticket stubs. That is the highest compliment we can pay a locker room cap.

It misses a perfect score on two small things. It could have been more colorful and fun — the charcoal-and-cream is tasteful but safe, and there was room to bring more Carolina red into it. And the "Stanley Cup" line above "Champions" is set quite small; bumping that text up would have given the front more balance and more punch. Neither knock changes the verdict. This is a really good hat and an easy A.

The shirt is very cool too, and it does more than the standard locker room tee. The base is the familiar heather gray Fanatics authentic, but the graphics give it real personality: the front runs a dynamic, angled "2026 Stanley Cup Champs" lockup with "CANES" in big bold red, the Stanley Cup illustrated above it, the warning-flag logo and red accents threading through, and the back carries a vertical "RAISE" treatment with the Cup. It is energetic and unmistakably Carolina — a clear step up from the recycled wordmark-over-watermark template a lot of these tees settle for.

It lands at A- rather than higher because the heather-gray base still anchors it to the standard authentic-tee formula, and the front graphic, while fun, is a touch busy — a little more breathing room and it sings. They did a good job here. It is just a notch behind the hat, which is the star of this set.

## Final Take

Carolina's 2026 Stanley Cup Champions locker room set is a strong one, and it is the hat that leads it — a textured charcoal Fanatics cap with cream piping that is genuinely wearable and earns its A, missing more only because it plays the colors a little safe and shrinks the "Stanley Cup" line. The heather-gray authentic tee is very cool in its own right, with real Canes personality in the angled Champs-Canes front graphic, and grades a clean A-. There is a nice irony in all that red on the merch, too: the Hurricanes [won the Cup on the ice in black and white without their iconic red sweater ever appearing](/stories/hurricanes-won-2026-stanley-cup-without-iconic-red-sweater) — but the red identity is alive and well on the championship gear.
