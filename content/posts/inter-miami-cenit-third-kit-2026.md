Inter Miami is known for one thing above all in the kit world: pink. So when the club and Adidas rolled out the 2026 third kit, the interesting part is what they did not do. Instead of another pink-forward look, the new "Cénit" jersey goes clean and bright, and it is a smart change of pace. We like it.

## The Design: Miami White, Not Miami Pink

The Cénit kit is built on a luminous "Miami White" base rather than the club's signature pink. The pink is still here, along with a mint green Adidas calls "Easy Mint" and touches of black, but all three now live as accents rather than running the whole jersey. There is also a subtle all-over pattern across the shirt, drawn from the architectural lines of the canopy on Inter Miami's new home, Nu Stadium. It reads clean from distance and rewards a closer look, which is exactly what you want from a third kit.

The name is the tell. "Cénit" means zenith in Spanish, the highest point, and the club is leaning into the idea that a new stadium and a new era call for a look that feels like an arrival rather than a repeat.

## The Change of Pace

This is the part we like most. Inter Miami's pink home kit is one of the most recognizable jerseys in the sport, but leaning on pink for every release would water it down. Pulling pink back to an accent on the third kit keeps the identity intact while giving the club a genuinely different third option. Keeping the mint in the mix is the right call too, because that pink-and-mint pairing is a big part of what makes Miami feel like Miami. This is a uniform that knows what the brand is and edits it rather than copying it.

## What Holds It at a B

The white base is clean, but it is also the safer canvas. Plenty of clubs run a white third kit, so on the color alone this does not stand out the way the pink home shirt does. What saves it is the detailing: the Nu Stadium pattern and the mint-and-pink accents give it enough Miami personality to matter. Push those details a touch harder and this jumps a grade. As it stands, it is a clean, likable third kit that lands at a solid B.

## The Bottom Line

Inter Miami made a smart move here. Rather than force pink onto everything, the Cénit third kit hands the spotlight to a bright Miami White and lets the pink and mint do their work as accents, all tied together by a pattern pulled from the club's new home. It is not the boldest jersey they have ever released, but it is a sharp, wearable look that gives Inter Miami a real third option. We grade it a B.
