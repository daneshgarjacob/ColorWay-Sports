Ole Miss dresses more like a closet than a uniform. Nike supplies three primary jerseys (red, navy, white), a powder blue alternate, gray, white and navy pants, and four helmet shells, and the Rebels mix them freely. The powder blue look is the one fans ask about most: it grew out of the powder blue helmet that returned in 2014 as a tribute to Chucky Mullins, and by 2025 the full powder blue set was the choice for the biggest home dates and for the Fiesta Bowl. The Realtree camo helmet comes from a partnership with Realtree founder Bill Jordan, a former Rebels receiver. It debuted against Kentucky in 2022, came back in navy camo against Oklahoma in 2024, and in 2025 turned into a gray and white camo shell worn against South Carolina and in the Egg Bowl. No 2026 Realtree helmet has been unveiled yet, but the program's fan color schedule tags the Missouri game as "White - Realtree," which is the best hint we have at the camo date. One caution on that schedule: it tells fans what to wear, not the team. Fans were told powder blue for Louisville, and the Rebels wore navy.

**September 6 vs. Louisville, Nissan Stadium, Nashville.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **navy helmet, navy jersey, gray pants**, with the red and navy stripe running down the gray pants and the red Ole Miss script on the helmet. Ole Miss was the designated home team for the Music City Kickoff, a Sunday night game, and Louisville wore white. Ole Miss won 41-38 in Pete Golding's first game as head coach.

**September 12 vs. Charlotte, Oxford.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **navy helmet, red jersey, gray pants**, the home opener at Vaught-Hemingway Stadium with Charlotte in white. Ole Miss won 41-9. That makes two games, two different jerseys, and the same navy helmet and gray pants both times.

**September 19 vs. LSU, Oxford.** Home color expected, with no uniform announced yet. This is the game of the season in Oxford: Lane Kiffin brings LSU back to the program he left at the end of the 2025 regular season, both teams are in the top ten, and College GameDay is in the Grove for the first time since 2021. Kickoff is 6:30 p.m. Central on ABC, and the fan theme is a Stripe Out. Red, navy and powder blue are all live options. When LSU visited in 2025, the Rebels wore the full powder blue set, powder blue helmet, powder blue jersey and white pants, and won 24-19. The LSU side of the matchup is on our [LSU 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/lsu-uniform-schedule-2026).

**September 26 at Florida, Gainesville.** White expected. SEC road opener at 2:30 p.m. Central, and Florida wears blue at home, so the Rebels travel in white. The powder blue helmet over white jersey and white pants is the road look they used at Kentucky, Georgia and Oklahoma in 2025. Florida's side is on our [Florida 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/florida-uniform-schedule-2026).

**October 3.** Bye.

**October 10 at Vanderbilt, FirstBank Stadium, Nashville.** White expected. The Rebels' second trip to Nashville this season, this time as the visitor.

**October 17 vs. Missouri, Oxford.** Home color expected, but this is the date to watch for the Realtree helmet, since the fan schedule labels it "White - Realtree." The white fan theme also leaves room for the Rebels to wear white at home, which they did with the camo helmet in 2025. If that happens, Missouri would need to bring a color. Missouri's side is on our [Missouri 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/missouri-uniform-schedule-2026).

**October 24 at Texas, Austin.** White expected. Texas wears burnt orange at home, so the only open question is the helmet. The [Texas 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/texas-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Longhorns' side.

**October 31 vs. Auburn, Oxford.** Home color expected. Halloween in Oxford with a powder blue fan theme, which makes this a candidate date for the powder blue set.

**November 7 vs. Georgia, Oxford.** Home color expected, with Georgia in white. The Rebels wore white at Georgia in 2025, so the colors flip this time. The Bulldogs' side is on our [Georgia 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/georgia-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 14 at Oklahoma, Norman.** White expected. Oklahoma wears crimson at home. The Sooners' side is on our [Oklahoma 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/oklahoma-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 21 vs. Wofford, Oxford.** Home color expected. The FCS tune-up before the Egg Bowl and the kind of game where a helmet or pants swap is low risk.

**November 27 vs. Mississippi State, Oxford.** Home color expected. The Egg Bowl moves to the Friday after Thanksgiving in Oxford. In 2025 the Rebels won it in Starkville in white with the gray and white Realtree helmet, so a camo encore is possible here too if it is not used earlier.

## The Bottom Line

Two games in, Ole Miss has shown the navy and red jerseys, both under the navy helmet and both over gray pants, which is the most traditional version of this team. The powder blue set, the white road look and a new Realtree helmet are all still to come. The LSU game on September 19 is the obvious stage for a statement uniform, Missouri on October 17 is the likeliest camo date, and every road trip projects white. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Ole Miss wearing this week?**

Ole Miss hosts LSU on Saturday, September 19, and has not announced its uniform yet. As the home team, the Rebels are expected in a color jersey, red, navy or powder blue, with LSU in white. Last week against Charlotte they wore the navy helmet, red jersey and gray pants. We update the grid above as soon as the combination is known.

**What are Ole Miss's football uniforms for 2026?**

A red home jersey, a navy home jersey, a white road jersey with powder blue numerals and stripes, and a powder blue alternate jersey, all from Nike. Pants come in gray, white and navy. The helmets are a navy shell with the red Ole Miss script, a powder blue shell, a white shell, and a Realtree camo alternate.

**Does Ole Miss have a new alternate uniform in 2026?**

Not one that has been revealed. Ole Miss has introduced a new Realtree camo helmet each year recently, navy camo in 2024 and gray and white camo in 2025, and the 2026 fan schedule tags the October 17 Missouri game as a Realtree game. We will add the new helmet to this page when it is unveiled.

**What did Ole Miss wear against Louisville?**

A navy helmet, navy jersey and gray pants in the Music City Kickoff at Nissan Stadium in Nashville on Sunday, September 6. Ole Miss was the designated home team and won 41-38. Louisville wore white.

**Why did Ole Miss wear navy against Louisville when fans were told to wear powder blue?**

The fan color schedule is a guide for the crowd, not the team's uniform plan. Ole Miss asked fans to wear powder blue in Nashville, and the Rebels still chose the navy jersey with gray pants. Use the fan schedule as a hint, not a confirmation.

**Does Ole Miss wear powder blue at home or on the road?**

Mostly at home. In 2025 the full powder blue set appeared against Arkansas, LSU and Florida, plus the Fiesta Bowl against Miami. On the road the powder blue usually shows up on the helmet, over the white jersey and white pants.

**How many games does Ole Miss play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven at Vaught-Hemingway Stadium, four SEC road games, and a neutral-site opener against Louisville in Nashville, with a bye on October 3. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
