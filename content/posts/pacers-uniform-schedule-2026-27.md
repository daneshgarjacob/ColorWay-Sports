The Indiana Pacers did something most NBA teams never do: on September 18, 2026 they published their own uniform schedule for the 2026-27 season, jersey by jersey and date by date. We counted every game on it. The gold pinstriped Statement jersey is the story. It is scheduled for **31 games**, more than the Association white (13) and the Icon navy (10) combined.

Below is the full count, home and road, followed by the game-by-game Pacers jersey schedule for all 80 dated games with every announced night filled in.

That is 54 of the 80 games the league has dated so far. Two more December dates are still waiting on the NBA Cup knockout draw, so 28 games in all are not on the Pacers' schedule yet. Our best guess is that most of those belong to the City Edition, which Indiana has not officially shown for 2026-27, but the team has not said so and we are not filling them in until it does.

A few things jump out of the count:

- **The Statement is the real home jersey now.** It is booked for 17 of the 28 announced home games. The Association white, the traditional home uniform, gets six.
- **The white goes on the road more than it stays home.** Seven of the 13 Association games are road games, including the season opener at New Orleans on October 21 and the entire February West Coast swing at Golden State, Sacramento and Portland.
- **The Icon navy barely shows up.** Ten games all season, split evenly, five home and five road.

## Our Take: Indiana Is Leaning All the Way Into the Gold

We love this. The gold pinstriped Statement is our favorite jersey in the Pacers closet, and it is the one the team is wearing the most, by a lot. It is the 1990s look that made the Pacers famous, and 31 nights of it is exactly the right call.

The flip side is that Indiana is quietly moving away from the circle jerseys, the Association and Icon sets that wrap INDIANA PACERS around the chest in a ring. Ten Icon games and 13 Association games is a small share of a season for the two uniforms that are supposed to be the base of the closet. The ring logo is not going anywhere, but on the court this season the Pacers look like a gold, pinstriped, INDIANA-across-the-chest team first.
