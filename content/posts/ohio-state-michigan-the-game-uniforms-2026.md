**Ohio State is the home team for The Game, so the Buckeyes wear color and Michigan wears white.** Michigan visits Ohio Stadium on **Saturday, November 28, at noon Eastern on FOX**. Ohio State's home set this season is the **silver helmet with the scarlet jersey**, and Michigan wore white the last time it played in Columbus, in 2024. One part is still open. Ohio State has three alternates and has not put any of them on a date. The school says it names each game's uniform during that game's week, so this page gets updated the day Ohio State posts.

## Quick Answers

- **What is Ohio State wearing against Michigan?** Expected: silver helmet, scarlet jersey. Ohio State is the home team.
- **What is Michigan wearing at Ohio State?** **Confirmed:** the maize winged helmet, white jersey and white pants, all white, posted by Michigan as "Saturday's Threads" on November 27.
- **Is Ohio State wearing an alternate?** Not announced. Scarlet Rush, Sub Zero and Tunnel Vision all exist, and none has been assigned a game.
- **When is The Game?** Saturday, November 28, noon ET on FOX, at Ohio Stadium in Columbus.
- **What did they wear last year?** In Ann Arbor in 2025, Michigan wore its "Big Game Blues," navy over navy, and Ohio State wore white. Ohio State won 27-9.

## What We Know

These are confirmed, from the schools, the network or the game photos:

- **The game is in Columbus.** Ohio State announced on May 11 that The Game kicks off at noon on FOX at Ohio Stadium, so Ohio State is the home team. ([Ohio State Athletics](https://ohiostatebuckeyes.com/news/2026/5/11/football-the-game-set-to-air-on-fox-at-noon))
- **Ohio State has five combinations in 2026 and picks one each week.** The May 15 uniform release lists the core home set, the core road set and three alternates, and says the uniform for each 2026 game "will be announced each game week." ([Ohio State Athletics](https://ohiostatebuckeyes.com/news/2026/5/15/ohio-state-football-honors-past-embraces-future-via-comprehensive-uniform-evolution-and-stadium-turf-modernization))
- **The three alternates, as Ohio State lists them:** **Scarlet Rush** (silver helmet, scarlet jersey, scarlet pants), **Sub Zero** (silver helmet, white jersey, white pants) and **Tunnel Vision** (black helmet, black jersey, black pants). Tunnel Vision was introduced in 2015 and last worn in 2022.
- **Michigan posted its uniform on Friday, November 27: all white under the maize winged helmet.** Earlier in the week neither team had posted one. As of Thursday, November 19, Ohio State still has a road game at Nebraska and Michigan hosts UCLA first. Michigan has posted its uniform during game week this season (Indiana, Oregon), and Ohio State says it names each one in game week.

## What We're Watching For

This is our read. None of it is confirmed until a school posts it.

- **Which scarlet look Ohio State wears.** Every Ohio Stadium game we have logged this season has been the silver helmet and scarlet jersey. What changes is the pants. They were gray against Illinois, Maryland and Northwestern, and scarlet against Oregon on November 7, which is the same helmet, jersey and pants Ohio State lists as Scarlet Rush.
- **Whether Tunnel Vision comes out.** The black set has not been worn since 2022 and has no 2026 date. The Michigan game is Ohio State's last regular-season home date.
- **Michigan's pants.** Answered on Friday: white. Michigan's white jersey had been worn with blue pants at Minnesota and maize pants at Oregon this season; for Columbus it is white on white, as in 2024.
- **The weather.** The Game is played in late November in Columbus. If it is cold, look for long sleeves and gloves, not a different uniform.

From our [Michigan 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/michigan-uniform-schedule-2026). Michigan has gone off script more than usual this year:

On Monday, November 23, Michigan posted "Rivalry Week." with a promo photo for the game: a player in the **white jersey and the maize winged helmet**, shot inside the Ohio Stadium rotunda, with "Nov. 28, Noon ET, on FOX" at the bottom. ## What Ohio State And Michigan Wore In The Last Three Games

Checked against ESPN's game photos for each meeting.

- **2025, Ann Arbor: Ohio State 27, Michigan 9.** Michigan was the home team and wore its **"Big Game Blues," navy jersey with navy pants** under the winged helmet, as it announced that week ([ClickOnDetroit](https://www.clickondetroit.com/all-about-ann-arbor/2025/11/28/michigan-football-to-bring-out-signature-uniforms-for-the-game-against-no-1-ohio-state/)). Ohio State wore the **silver helmet and white jersey**.
- **2024, Columbus: Michigan 13, Ohio State 10.** Ohio State wore the **silver helmet and scarlet jersey** at home. Michigan wore the **winged helmet with white jerseys and white pants**, all white, as reported that week ([Eleven Warriors](https://www.elevenwarriors.com/forum/ohio-state-football/2024/11/151137/michigan-to-wear-all-white-uniforms-vs-ohio-state)).
- **2023, Ann Arbor: Michigan 30, Ohio State 24.** Michigan wore the **navy jersey** at home and Ohio State wore **white with gray pants**.

The last time The Game was in Columbus it was scarlet against white, and that is the expected look again.

## Why The Home Team Wears Color

Big Ten home teams wear their color jersey and visitors wear white. That is why the answer flips every year in this series: navy against white in Ann Arbor, scarlet against white in Columbus. The home team can choose to wear white, and the visitor then wears color, which is why every look here stays Expected until Ohio State posts its uniform.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Ohio State wearing against Michigan in 2026?**

The silver helmet and scarlet jersey are expected, because The Game is at Ohio Stadium and Ohio State is the home team. Ohio State has not announced the combination yet and says it names each game's uniform during that game's week.

**What is Michigan wearing against Ohio State?**

All white under the maize winged helmet: white jersey and white pants, confirmed by Michigan's "Saturday's Threads" post on November 27. It is the same look Michigan wore the last time it played in Columbus, in 2024.

**What cleats is Ohio State wearing against Michigan?**

Red Nike x Victor Solomon Vaporposites with a gold swoosh and a gold chain on the laces, which Ohio State posted on November 27.

**Is Ohio State wearing black against Michigan?**

Not announced. The all-black Tunnel Vision set has a black helmet, black jersey and black pants. It was last worn in 2022, and Ohio State has not assigned it a 2026 game.

**What time is Ohio State vs. Michigan in 2026?**

Noon Eastern on Saturday, November 28, on FOX.

**Where is The Game in 2026?**

Ohio Stadium in Columbus. The game rotates every year, and 2025 was in Ann Arbor.

**What did Ohio State wear against Michigan last year?**

The silver helmet and white jersey at Michigan Stadium on November 29, 2025, when Ohio State won 27-9. Michigan wore navy over navy.

**What are Michigan's Big Game Blues?**

The navy jersey with navy pants under the winged helmet. Michigan wore them at home against Ohio State in 2025.

**What is Ohio State's Scarlet Rush uniform?**

The silver helmet with a scarlet jersey and scarlet pants. Ohio State wore that combination against Oregon on November 7, 2026.

**What did Michigan wear the last time it played at Ohio State?**

All white with the winged helmet, in 2024, when Michigan won 13-10.

**Who wears white in Ohio State vs. Michigan?**

The visiting team, under the Big Ten's home-color convention. In 2026 that is Michigan.
