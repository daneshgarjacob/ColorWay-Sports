The 2026 Indian Premier League brought a fresh wave of kits, and with ten franchises — plus Royal Challengers Bengaluru's special "Go Green" edition — there are **eleven jerseys** on the field this year, each one a different color story. The IPL has the boldest, most saturated kit palette in world cricket: this is the league of canary yellow, royal purple, blazing orange and hot pink, where every shirt is a statement and no team is afraid of color.

This is ColorWay's design-first ranking of all **eleven IPL 2026 jerseys, from worst to best** — judged purely on color, contrast, crest and how each kit reads under a packed stadium's lights. We're not ranking trophies; we're ranking the shirts. Here's the full board, counting down to the best kit in the league.

## Frequently Asked Questions About IPL 2026 Jerseys

**Which IPL team has the best jersey in 2026?**

In our ranking, Sunrisers Hyderabad have the best IPL jersey of 2026. Their blazing orange kit, with a new black zig-zag print and a black collar, is the most saturated and energetic shirt in the league and reads brilliantly under stadium lights. Mumbai Indians (royal blue and gold) and Gujarat Titans (navy and gold) round out the top three.

**What are all 10 IPL teams in 2026?**

The ten IPL 2026 franchises are: Chennai Super Kings (yellow), Mumbai Indians (blue and gold), Royal Challengers Bengaluru (navy and red), Sunrisers Hyderabad (orange), Kolkata Knight Riders (purple and gold), Rajasthan Royals (blue and pink), Punjab Kings (red), Gujarat Titans (navy and gold), Lucknow Super Giants (red and blue), and Delhi Capitals (blue and red).

**Who are the IPL 2026 jersey sponsors?**

The 2026 front-of-shirt sponsors are: CSK – Etihad Airways, Mumbai Indians – Lauritz Knudsen, RCB – Nothing, Sunrisers Hyderabad – Shree Cement, Kolkata Knight Riders – Vida, Rajasthan Royals – Waaree Solar, Punjab Kings – CP Plus, Gujarat Titans – Birla Estates, Lucknow Super Giants – JK Super Cement, and Delhi Capitals – Hero Fincorp. Several teams changed sponsors for 2026 after real-money fantasy gaming brands stepped back.

**Why does RCB wear a green jersey?**

Royal Challengers Bengaluru wear their "Go Green" jersey for one home game each season as part of a sustainability initiative they have run since 2011. The shirts are made from recycled material, and the team swaps its usual red home kit for the green edition to raise environmental awareness. Their main 2026 kit is still the navy-and-red home shirt.

**What color is each IPL team's jersey in 2026?**

Chennai Super Kings wear yellow, Mumbai Indians wear royal blue and gold, Sunrisers Hyderabad wear orange, Kolkata Knight Riders wear purple and gold, Royal Challengers Bengaluru wear navy and red, Rajasthan Royals wear blue and pink, Gujarat Titans wear navy and gold, Delhi Capitals wear blue and red, Punjab Kings wear red, and Lucknow Super Giants wear red and blue.

**Why did several IPL teams change sponsors for 2026?**

Several IPL franchises signed new front-of-shirt sponsors for 2026 after real-money fantasy gaming brands — long a major presence on IPL kits — stepped back amid regulatory pressure in India. That opened the door for new principal sponsors across the cement, energy, aviation, and tech sectors, including Shree Cement on Sunrisers Hyderabad, Etihad Airways on Chennai Super Kings, and Waaree Solar on Rajasthan Royals.
