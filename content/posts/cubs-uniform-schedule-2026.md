The Cubs carry four uniforms in 2026, and the rotation is the same one they settled into for 2025 after a notable shake-up: the navy "Wrigleyville" City Connect was retired and a brand-new powder-blue alternate took its slot. The bigger story for 2026 is the patchwork on every sleeve — this is the franchise's **150th anniversary** season, so a commemorative "150" mark joins the **Motorola** advertising patch on all four jerseys. There's no new template change to the core looks; the classics carry over untouched.

## Home Pinstripes (Most Home Games)

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; margin: 1.5em 0 2em;">

The home pinstripe is Chicago's signature and one of the most recognizable looks in the sport: a white jersey with royal-blue pinstripes, the famous red Cubs "bullseye" roundel on the left chest — the enclosed "C" with "UBS" tucked inside, ringed in blue and red — and a blue cap with the red "C." It's worn for the bulk of the homestand, essentially every home game that isn't a summer Friday (those belong to the Blues alternate). The Cubs have leaned on this template since pinstripes arrived at Wrigley in 1957, and there's nothing here that needs touching. It's a clean, timeless home flannel, and the ivy-and-pinstripes pairing is as classic as baseball gets.

## Powder-Blue "Blues" Alternate (Friday Home Games)

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; margin: 1.5em 0 2em;">
<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/Cubs/status/2073142530716119367"></a></blockquote>

This is the jersey people circle on the calendar. Introduced for 2025, the powder-blue "Blues" alternate is a love letter to Chicago's electric-blues heritage and to the team's own past — the Cubs were the first MLB club to wear baby blue back in 1941, and the color hadn't been a staple since the early 1980s. The chest mark blends early-Cubs typography with a blues-instrument feel; the left sleeve carries a guitar-pick patch split by a lightning bolt — a nod to electrifying the blues — and the socks read "Sweet Home Chicago" with six royal stripes for the six strings on a guitar. The cap pairs a royal crown and brim with a white front panel and a red button. It debuted on April 5, 2025, and it's worn at home on Fridays during the summer months. Tying the loudest jersey in the closet to a guaranteed weekly slot is the smart move every team should make — fans know exactly when to come see it.

## What Happened to the City Connect?

Gone — and not coming back in the same form. The navy "Wrigleyville" City Connect that Chicago wore from 2021 was retired after the 2024 season, and the powder-blue Blues alternate took its place in the rotation. Notably, neither the team nor Nike classifies the Blues look as part of the City Connect program, which means the Cubs are one of the rare clubs heading into 2026 without an active City Connect uniform at all. If you're hunting for the navy "Wrigleyville" marquee top on the field this year, you won't find it.

## How the Cubs Rotation Actually Works

Be honest about it: Chicago's system is looser than the truly scheduled teams. The [Phillies](/stories/phillies-uniform-schedule-2026) treat their closet like a published timetable; the Cubs really only have one hard anchor — **the powder-blue Blues alternate on Friday home games in the summer**. Everything else is the default: home pinstripes for the rest of the homestand, road grays for the rest of the trip, with no strict day-of-week assignment beyond that Friday hook. With only four jerseys and no City Connect to slot in, there simply isn't much to schedule.

It still works, because the anchor is the part fans care about. The Blues alternate is the jersey people buy tickets to see, and giving it its own weeknight builds the anticipation. The pinstripes and grays are happy to do the quiet, classic work in between.

Two constants tie the whole 2026 closet together. Every jersey carries the **Motorola** "batwing" advertising patch — the first sponsor patch in franchise history, on the uniforms since August 2024 — and every jersey also wears the **150th anniversary** sleeve patch this season, a red "C" with "150" inside it framed by the years 1876 and 2026. It's a collector's-item year, and the sleeves make sure you know it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey are the Chicago Cubs wearing today?**

The Cubs keep a loose closet with only one hard rule, so start with the day and the location. The powder-blue "Blues" alternate is worn at home on Fridays during the summer months, and that is the only fixed slot. Outside that Friday hook, home games are the white pinstripes and road games are the grays, with the royal-blue alternate dropped in at the manager's discretion. If it is a summer Friday at Wrigley, expect the Blues; otherwise check home or away and assume the pinstripes or grays.

**What jersey did the Cubs wear last night?**

A summer Friday home night at Wrigley was the powder-blue "Blues" alternate, which is the Cubs' only fixed slot. Any other home night was the white pinstripes and any road night was the grays, with the royal-blue alternate dropped in at the manager's discretion rather than on a schedule.

**What uniform are the Cubs wearing tonight?**

If it is a Friday at Wrigley during the summer months, it is the powder-blue "Blues." Outside that one hook the Cubs keep a loose closet: pinstripes at home, grays on the road, and the royal-blue alternate whenever the manager wants it.

**What are the Cubs wearing tomorrow?**

The only date you can call with confidence is a summer Friday at Wrigley, which is the powder-blue alternate. Otherwise check whether the Cubs are home or away and assume the white pinstripes or the road grays.

**How many uniforms do the Cubs have in 2026?**
Four: the home white pinstripes, the road grays, the royal-blue alternate, and the powder-blue "Blues" alternate. There's no City Connect in the mix — the navy "Wrigleyville" City Connect was retired after the 2024 season.

**Do the Cubs have a City Connect uniform in 2026?**
No. The Cubs retired the 2021 navy "Wrigleyville" City Connect and replaced it with the powder-blue Blues alternate, which the team and Nike do not consider part of the City Connect program. That leaves Chicago as one of the few clubs without an active City Connect this season.

**When do the Cubs wear the powder-blue uniform?**
The Blues alternate is worn at home on Fridays during the summer months. It debuted on April 5, 2025, and brought baby blue back to the Cubs for the first time since the early 1980s.

**What is the Cubs' powder-blue jersey about?**
It celebrates Chicago's electric-blues music heritage. The design features blues-era typography, a guitar-pick sleeve patch split by a lightning bolt, "Sweet Home Chicago" on the socks with six stripes for a guitar's six strings, and a royal-and-white cap with a red button.

**What patches are on the Cubs' 2026 jerseys?**
Two. The Motorola "batwing" advertising patch appears on all home, road, and alternate jerseys — the first jersey ad in franchise history — and a 150th anniversary commemorative patch marking 1876–2026 is worn on the sleeves all season.

**Do the Cubs wear player names on their home pinstripes?**
The home pinstripes keep the classic clean front and the iconic bullseye logo; the road grays carry "CHICAGO" across the chest with player names on the back for away games.
