Auburn wears navy jerseys at home and white jerseys on the road, always under the same white helmet, and in 2026 that has not changed. This is the full Auburn 2026 uniform schedule: the navy home set, the white road set, the orange jersey that has not been worn since 1980, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games in Alex Golesh's first season. Two games in, Auburn has worn navy over white pants both times. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

Auburn moved from Under Armour to Nike in July 2025 and used the switch to change almost nothing. The jerseys moved to Nike's Vapor F.U.S.E. template with the v-shaped chest seam and a larger chest wordmark, but the block numerals are the same font Auburn has used for more than 60 years. The pants got their full-length stripe back and an AU logo on the right hip. The helmet traded the big side numbers from 2021 for small numbers on the center stripe, a Tuberville-era detail, and the bumper lettering switched to Copperplate. We have not seen a 2026 alternate announced. SEC home teams wear color and visitors wear white, so the grid below is navy at Jordan-Hare Stadium and white everywhere else.

**September 5 vs. Baylor, Mercedes-Benz Stadium, Atlanta.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **white helmet, navy jersey, white pants**, the home set for the Aflac Kickoff Game. This date was originally an Auburn home game and moved to Atlanta, and Auburn stayed the designated home team, so the Tigers kept navy. Baylor wore a green helmet, white jersey and green pants with a gold stripe. Auburn won 17-16 in Golesh's debut, and the navy and orange against green and gold made it one of the sharpest color matchups of opening weekend.

**September 12 vs. Southern Miss.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **white helmet, navy jersey, white pants** again, this time at Jordan-Hare Stadium for the first home game of the Golesh era. Southern Miss came in wearing a black helmet with a gold stripe, a white jersey and black pants. Auburn won 43-8. Kickoff was on Saturday night, and ESPN's feed lists the game under September 13 because it runs on UTC. The correct local date is Saturday, September 12.

**September 19 vs. Florida.** Navy expected, the home default for the SEC opener. It is the first time Florida has played at Jordan-Hare since 2011. Florida already published its whole season: the Gators come in wearing an orange helmet, white jersey and orange pants, so it will be navy against white and orange under the lights. See the [Florida 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/florida-uniform-schedule-2026) for every Gators combination.

**September 26 vs. Vanderbilt.** Navy expected. This is Auburn's homecoming game, which is the traditional home date for an alternate if the program ever breaks from its standard sets.

**October 3 at Tennessee.** White expected, because Tennessee is at home in color. The Vols have already announced they will wear their new Smokey Grey Neyland Legacy uniform for this game, so Auburn in white against gray is the combination we expect at Neyland Stadium. See the [Tennessee 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/tennessee-uniform-schedule-2026) for the full Smokey Grey breakdown.

**October 10.** Bye.

**October 17 at Georgia.** White expected. The Deep South's Oldest Rivalry in Athens, and Auburn in white against Georgia's red jerseys and silver pants.

**October 24 vs. LSU.** Navy expected, the home default, in an 11 a.m. Central kickoff.

**October 31 at Ole Miss.** White expected. Halloween in Oxford.

**November 7 vs. Arkansas.** Navy expected.

**November 14 at Mississippi State.** White expected, at Davis Wade Stadium.

**November 21 vs. Samford.** Navy expected. The final home game of the regular season.

**November 28 at Alabama.** White expected. The Iron Bowl in Tuscaloosa, with Alabama in crimson at home and Auburn in the all-white road set, which is how this game has looked in Tuscaloosa for years.

## The Bottom Line

Auburn's 2026 uniform schedule is the simplest in the SEC: navy jersey and white pants at Jordan-Hare, white jersey and white pants on the road, and the same white helmet with the orange and navy stripe every Saturday. Both games so far have gone exactly that way. The only real question is whether Golesh's first season brings the orange jersey back after 46 years, and nothing points to it yet. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Auburn wearing this week?**

Auburn plays Florida at Jordan-Hare Stadium on September 19, and the navy home jersey with white pants and the white helmet is expected. Florida has already confirmed an orange helmet, white jersey and orange pants for the game. Auburn wore navy over white pants against Southern Miss on September 12. The week-by-week grid above lists every game.

**What are Auburn's football uniforms for 2026?**

A navy home jersey with white block numerals and orange and white sleeve stripes, a white road jersey with navy numerals and navy and orange sleeve stripes, and white pants with a full-length navy and orange stripe for both. The helmet is white with an orange center stripe bordered in navy and the navy AU logo. Nike makes the uniforms, and 2026 is the second season of that deal.

**Does Auburn have a new alternate uniform in 2026?**

Not that has been announced. Auburn is one of the few SEC programs without an alternate jersey in rotation. Its last colored alternate, an orange jersey, was worn four times from 1978 to 1980 and dropped when Pat Dye became head coach.

**Has Auburn ever worn orange jerseys?**

Yes. Auburn wore orange in the early years under Shug Jordan in the 1950s, then brought orange back as an alternate under Doug Barfield. Auburn Uniform Database counts four orange-jersey games in that stretch: Georgia in 1978 (a 22-22 tie), Mississippi State in 1979 (a 14-3 win), Southern Miss in 1980 (a 31-0 win) and Georgia in 1980 (a 31-21 loss). Pat Dye did away with them after 1980.

**What did Auburn wear against Baylor?**

The navy home set: white helmet, navy jersey, white pants. The game was played in Atlanta at Mercedes-Benz Stadium as the Aflac Kickoff Game, but Auburn was the designated home team, so the Tigers wore navy and Baylor wore white. Auburn won 17-16.

**How many games does Auburn play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: six at Jordan-Hare Stadium, five on the road in the SEC, and the neutral-site opener against Baylor in Atlanta, with a bye on October 10. The Iron Bowl at Alabama closes the regular season on November 28.
