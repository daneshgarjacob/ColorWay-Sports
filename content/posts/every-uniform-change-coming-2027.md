**Last Updated: Tuesday, November 17, 2026.** This page will change all offseason. Every time a team leaks, teases or reveals a new look for 2027, it goes here.

Here is every uniform, logo and jersey change we know of for 2027, league by league. Some are official. Some are only reports. We label every one so you know which is which.

**How We Track This:** we only list a change if a team, a league or a trusted outlet has said it. **Confirmed** means the team or league announced it. **Reported** means a credible outlet or a team executive has said it, but there is no official reveal. **Rumored** means a single leak or speculation that nobody with the team has backed up. Every item names its source.

## Quick Answers

- **Which NFL teams are getting new uniforms in 2027?** The Seahawks are bringing back their white 1990s throwback. All eight teams in the AFC West and NFC East get a new Rivalries uniform: the Broncos, Chiefs, Raiders, Chargers, Cowboys, Eagles, Giants and Commanders.
- **Is NBA City Edition ending?** Reportedly, yes. City Edition is reported to be replaced by new "Crafted" alternate uniforms in 2027-28. The NBA and Nike have not announced it.
- **Which NBA teams are getting new uniforms in 2027-28?** The Wizards (executives on the record), the Spurs (new wordmark, confirmed) and, per an unconfirmed leak, the Raptors and Pacers.
- **Which NHL team has a new jersey for 2027-28?** The Islanders. Fans voted in the Navy Lighthouse third jersey, and it debuts in 2027-28.
- **What is new in MLB for 2027?** The Royals' 1969 throwback for the Field of Dreams Game against the Red Sox on August 12, 2027, and the Rays' first full season in their new gray road uniform.
- **Any college changes?** Alabama and Tennessee both wear their home jerseys again in 2027, crimson against orange in Tuscaloosa.

### AFC West And NFC East: New Rivalries Uniforms

**Status: Confirmed.** The NFL and Nike roll out the Rivalries program one pair of divisions at a time. The **NFC East and AFC West** are the 2027 group. That means a brand new Rivalries uniform for the **Broncos, Chiefs, Raiders, Chargers, Cowboys, Eagles, Giants and Commanders**. Each team wears its new look for one home game against a division rival. The designs have not been revealed.

- **Source:** [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/44858300/nfl-nike-announce-rivalries-uniform-gear-program), [Broncos Wire via Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/broncos-add-uniform-wardrobe-2027-140026898.html)

### 2025 And 2026 Rivalries Uniforms: Back Again

**Status: Confirmed.** Every Rivalries uniform stays in its team's rotation for three seasons. So the AFC East and NFC West sets from 2025, and the AFC South and NFC North sets from 2026, can all come back in 2027.

## NBA 2027-28

### League-Wide: City Edition Ends, "Crafted" Begins

**Status: Reported.** Nike and the NBA are reported to be ending City Edition after 10 seasons. A new set of **"Crafted"** alternate uniforms would replace it for all 30 teams in 2027-28. The report came from the Basketball Jersey Archive on October 30, 2026. Neither the NBA nor Nike has announced it. If it holds, this season's City Editions, which debut January 14, 2027, are the last ones.

**Status: Confirmed.** The Rays revealed a new gray road uniform with "Tampa Bay" across the chest on September 21, 2026, a modern take on the 1998 Devil Rays road set. It debuted the next night. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are the Seahawks getting new uniforms in 2027?**
Yes. The white 1990s throwback returns in 2027, so the full throwback set, royal blue and white, is back. The team's new owners announced it on September 9, 2026.

**Are the Broncos getting a new uniform in 2027?**
Yes. Denver is one of eight teams, with the rest of the AFC West and the NFC East, getting a new Rivalries uniform in 2027. The design has not been revealed.

**Are the Cowboys getting a Rivalries uniform?**
Yes, in 2027. The NFC East is in the 2027 Rivalries group, so the Cowboys, Eagles, Giants and Commanders each get one.

**Is NBA City Edition ending?**
It is reported, not confirmed. Uniform outlets say City Edition ends after 2026-27 and "Crafted" alternates replace it in 2027-28. The NBA and Nike have not announced it.

**Are the Wizards getting new uniforms in 2027?**
Probably. Team executives told The Athletic they are aiming for 2027-28. A new logo may take longer, and the Bullets name is not coming back.

**Are the Raptors going back to purple?**
It is only a rumor. A leak account reported a purple Statement jersey for 2027-28. The Raptors have not said anything.

**Are the Spurs changing their uniforms?**
A little. The team has said the "Go Spurs Go" wordmark goes on all of its core uniforms starting in 2027-28.

**Are the Islanders getting a new jersey?**
Yes. The fan-designed Navy Lighthouse third jersey debuts in 2027-28.

**Are the Jaguars playing in Orlando in 2027?**
That is the plan reported by local outlets, at Camping World Stadium, while their Jacksonville stadium is rebuilt. It does not change their uniforms.

**What uniforms will the Royals and Red Sox wear at the 2027 Field of Dreams Game?**
The Royals will wear a throwback to their 1969 home uniform. The Red Sox have teased a cap but have not shown a jersey yet.

**Is Alpine changing its F1 livery in 2027?**
Yes. Alpine races as Gucci Racing in 2027, in Gucci colors instead of BWT pink. The livery reveal is expected before the season.

**When are new 2027 uniforms revealed?**
It depends on the league. NFL and college looks usually come out in the spring and summer. NBA uniforms come out between the draft and the start of the season, with alternates in the fall. NHL alternates often come out in the fall. We will update this page with every one.
