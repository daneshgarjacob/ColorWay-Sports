The base sets come from the 2024 Nike redesign, which brought back the crescent shoulder stripe and put the Don Nehlen double stripe on the gold and navy pants. Rich Rodriguez added the glossy navy helmet on his return in 2025, and this summer the program replaced the matte white helmet it had used since 2013 with a gloss version. Every piece is built to match any other, so WVU can run a different helmet, jersey, and pants combination nearly every week, and it announces each one before kickoff. Home games are the variable: navy is the default, gold shows up for Gold Rush, and black belongs to Coal Rush. On the road, WVU wears white. The 2025 old gold 1965 throwback has not been scheduled for 2026.

**September 5 vs. Coastal Carolina.** ★ Confirmed from the program's game photos: **white helmet, white jersey, white pants**, the first appearance of the new gloss white helmet, with WEST VIRGINIA across the chest and the navy, gold, navy stripe down the pants. The White Out was "All White for 5," the day West Virginia retired Pat White's No. 5, and fans in Milan Puskar Stadium wore white too. Coastal Carolina wore all teal. West Virginia won 31-24.

**September 12 vs. UT Martin.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **navy helmet, navy jersey, gold pants**, the glossy navy shell with the gold Flying WV over a navy jersey with gold numerals and gold pants with navy side stripes. UT Martin wore white. West Virginia won 52-7.

**September 19 vs. Virginia, Bank of America Stadium, Charlotte.** White expected. This is the Duke's Mayo Classic, a 7:30 p.m. Eastern kickoff on ACC Network, and ESPN lists Virginia as the home team, which puts West Virginia in the visitor's white. WVU has not announced the combination yet, and the helmet is the open question: navy or the new gloss white.

**September 26 vs. Oklahoma State.** Gold expected. Gold Rush is the Big 12 home opener, and West Virginia wore gold jerseys with gold pants for Gold Rush last season, so we expect the gold jersey again with Oklahoma State in white. Kickoff is 7 p.m. Eastern on FS1. See what the Cowboys pack on the [Oklahoma State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/oklahoma-state-uniform-schedule-2026).

**October 3 at Iowa State.** White expected. First Big 12 road game, at Jack Trice Stadium.

**October 10 vs. Arizona.** Coal Rush black expected. WVU has made Arizona its Coal Rush game, with fans asked to wear black, and it wore the black set for the two previous Coal Rush games. It is also Hall of Fame weekend.

**October 17 vs. Cincinnati.** Navy or gold expected. Homecoming doubles as Stripe the Stadium, with even-numbered sections and students in gold and odd-numbered sections in blue, so the uniform could lean either way. Navy is the home default.

**October 24 at TCU.** White expected. TCU wears purple at home, so we expect a clean white-on-purple matchup in Fort Worth. More on the [TCU 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/tcu-uniform-schedule-2026).

**October 31.** Bye.

**November 7 at Texas Tech.** White expected. The Red Raiders pick red or black at home, and WVU should be in white either way. The [Texas Tech 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/texas-tech-uniform-schedule-2026) tracks their side.

**November 14 vs. Kansas.** Navy expected. This is Heroes Day during Mountaineer Week, and local coverage expects a helmet decal with the American flag filling the state outline behind the Flying WV. Kansas should be in white, as listed on the [Kansas 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/kansas-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 21 vs. Houston.** Navy expected. Senior Day and the final home game. WVU closed its 2025 home slate in navy helmet, navy jersey, navy pants for True Blue weekend, so all navy is a real possibility.

**November 27 at Utah.** White expected. A Friday night finale, 9 p.m. Eastern (7 p.m. Mountain) on FOX at Rice-Eccles Stadium. ESPN files it under November 28 because its feed runs on UTC. West Virginia wore the old gold 1965 throwback against Utah last season. Nothing special has been announced for the rematch, and Utah wears red at home, so we expect white.

## The Bottom Line

Two games in, West Virginia has shown two very different looks: head-to-toe white for the Pat White retirement and navy over gold pants for UT Martin. That is how this closet works. No two WVU weeks need to match, and the program's promotional calendar tells you where the special looks land: gold for Oklahoma State, black for Arizona, a flag decal for Kansas. Road games should stay white. The one set we are still waiting on is Country Roads, which has not shown up yet in 2026. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is West Virginia wearing this week?**

West Virginia plays Virginia in the Duke's Mayo Classic in Charlotte on Saturday, September 19, and white is expected because ESPN lists Virginia as the home team. WVU has not announced the helmet or pants yet. Last week against UT Martin it wore the navy helmet, navy jersey, and gold pants. The grid above lists every game with the expected set.

**What are West Virginia's football uniforms for 2026?**

Navy, gold, and white jerseys with the crescent shoulder stripe, plus navy, gold, and white pants that can be paired any way the staff likes. The helmets are glossy navy with the gold Flying WV, a new gloss white shell with a navy state outline, and a gold shell. The alternates are the all-black Coal Rush set and the gold Country Roads jersey with the state roadmap in the numerals.

**Does West Virginia have a new alternate uniform in 2026?**

No new jersey has been announced. The new piece for 2026 is the gloss white helmet, which replaced the matte white helmet used since 2013 and debuted in the White Out opener. Coal Rush returns against Arizona on October 10, and a flag helmet decal is expected for Heroes Day against Kansas on November 14.

**Why did West Virginia wear all white against Coastal Carolina?**

The September 5 opener was "All White for 5," the game where West Virginia retired Pat White's No. 5. The team wore the gloss white helmet, white jersey, and white pants, and fans were asked to wear white. Coastal Carolina wore all teal, and WVU won 31-24.

**When is the West Virginia Coal Rush game in 2026?**

October 10 against Arizona in Morgantown, with fans asked to wear black. The Coal Rush uniform is all black with reflective silver numerals and a flat black helmet, and WVU wore it against Iowa State in 2024 and against TCU in 2025.

**How many games does West Virginia play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven at Milan Puskar Stadium, four Big 12 road games, and a neutral-site game against Virginia in Charlotte, with a bye on October 31. The season ends Friday, November 27 at Utah.
