## What Oregon Is Wearing Against Washington

Oregon Athletics posted three photos with the caption "Lead the way. Game 12 uniform combo," and the football account posted a short video with "Protect Autzen." Here is every piece we can see in the team's own photos:

- **Helmet:** a **silver chrome shell** with **apple green wings** sweeping back from the front, a silver facemask and a small green O on the front.
- **Jersey:** **apple green**, the bright, almost neon shade, not Oregon's darker forest green. **Yellow numbers**, a yellow collar, the Duck on the shoulder and the Big Ten patch on the chest.

## Why It Is Called "Crystal Apple"

Oregon did not name this uniform. The team calls it the Game 12 uniform combo. **"Crystal Apple" comes from Jonah Henderson**, a graphic designer who logs every Oregon uniform, and who named it in his post about the reveal on Wednesday with a diamond and a green apple. The crystal is the chrome helmet. The apple is apple green, the bright green on the jersey, the pants and the helmet wings. It is the same way the [Goldfinger name](/stories/oregon-goldfinger-yellow-white-yellow-uniforms-michigan-state-2026) for last week's yellow, white and yellow look came about.

## Has Oregon Worn This Before?

According to Henderson's count:

- A chrome helmet with a green jersey and green pants is out for the **fifth time ever**, but only the **second time in apple green**. The first was the **2017 Las Vegas Bowl**.
- It is the **second time** apple green wings have been on a chrome helmet. The first was the **Rose Bowl after the 2024 season**, played January 1, 2025.
- It is Oregon's **first green jersey against Washington since 2014**.

Those counts are Henderson's. We have not checked them against game photos from those years.

## How It Fits Oregon's 2026 Season

We have logged every Oregon uniform this season on our [Oregon 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/oregon-uniform-schedule-2026). Crystal Apple is new in three ways against what the Ducks have worn so far:

- **The first chrome or silver helmet at Autzen this season.** Oregon has worn a silver helmet once, in **Warp Speed at USC** on September 26, and a dark chrome helmet once, in **[Mummy Mode 2.0 at Ohio State](/stories/oregon-mummy-mode-2-uniforms-ohio-state-2026)** on November 7. Both were road games.
- **Only the third green jersey of the season.** The others were the **Boise State opener** on September 5 (yellow helmet, green jersey, yellow pants) and **Portland State** on September 18 (all green with a green helmet and yellow wing). Oregon won both. The near-black Night Forest green of **[Origin of Speed against UCLA](/stories/oregon-origin-of-speed-uniforms-2026)** is a different color and is not counted.
- **The first green jersey since September.** In the eight games since Portland State, Oregon has worn white four times, black three times and Origin of Speed once.

Green pants have been out twice before: with the all green look against Portland State, and in bright green under the black Fly Era jersey against Nebraska on October 17.
