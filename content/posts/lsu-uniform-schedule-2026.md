Here is the part almost nobody states plainly. **The home team is normally required to wear dark jerseys**, and that rule ended LSU's white tradition in 1983. In 1995 head coach Gerry DiNardo petitioned the NCAA to let home teams choose, the petition succeeded, and LSU went straight back to white. The tradition itself started under Paul Dietzel in the late 1950s and stuck after a national championship, for no better reason than superstition.

▶ **The consequence is what makes this page useful: a visitor to Tiger Stadium wears COLOR, not white.** That is the opposite of every other SEC road game, and it is the recurring date to watch on every LSU opponent's schedule.

**September 5 vs. Clemson.** ★ Confirmed: **white jersey, gold pants, white helmet**, the home white set. Clemson came to Baton Rouge and LSU wore white, which is the quirk this whole page turns on, and it pushed **Clemson into orange** rather than the road white they wear almost everywhere else. Orange against white under the lights in Tiger Stadium is a far better television game than orange against white would have been the other way round.

**September 12 vs. Louisiana Tech.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast, and it is the first surprise of LSU's season: **purple jersey, gold pants, gold helmet** in Tiger Stadium. We had the home whites down, the look LSU wore against Clemson a week earlier, and LSU went purple for the FCS visitor instead. Purple at home is the exception in Baton Rouge, not the rule, which is why it is worth a line here. LSU won 45-14.

**September 19 at Ole Miss.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **gold helmet, white jersey, gold pants**, the standard road set. Ole Miss wears a color at home and chose the full powder blue set, powder blue helmet and jersey over white pants, the same look it wore against LSU in Oxford last season. The Rebels never announced it in game week, so powder blue over red or navy was the open question of the weekend. LSU lost 32-24. White at home, white on the road against SEC hosts in color, purple only when the other side is in white, gold helmet and gold pants throughout, and a rule change from 1995 underneath all of it. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is LSU wearing this week?**

LSU wears white at home and, because its SEC hosts wear color, white on most road trips too; it was in white at Ole Miss and at Alabama last season. Next up is Ole Miss in Oxford on September 19, white expected. The week-by-week grid above lists every game with the expected set, and we update it as combinations are confirmed.

**What are LSU's uniforms for 2026?**

White jersey, gold pants, gold helmet. The standard look in Tiger Stadium. The dark jersey. Because SEC hosts wear color at home, LSU is usually in white on the road too; purple comes out when a host wears white, or for the odd home game. Constant in every set, with the LSU mark and the number on the side.

**Does LSU have an alternate uniform in 2026?**

None announced for 2026. The home whites already do the work an alternate would.

**Why does LSU wear white at home?**

The tradition started under head coach Paul Dietzel in the late 1950s and stuck after a national championship. A rule requiring home teams to wear dark jerseys ended it in 1983, until head coach Gerry DiNardo successfully petitioned the NCAA in 1995 to let home teams choose. It is why visitors to Tiger Stadium wear color, like Clemson in orange on September 5.

**Why does LSU wear purple and gold?**

The colors go back to 1893. Before LSU's first football game, coach Charles Coates and his players went looking for ribbon to decorate the uniforms, and a Baton Rouge store stocked for Mardi Gras had purple and gold but no green yet, so they bought those. A second account credits baseball captain E.B. Young with picking the colors that spring.

**How many games does LSU play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
