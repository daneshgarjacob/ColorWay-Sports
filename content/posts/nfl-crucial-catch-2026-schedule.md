NFL Crucial Catch 2026 runs almost entirely across three October Sundays: October 4, October 11 and October 18, which are Weeks 4, 5 and 6 of the season. The league has not put out a single team-by-team calendar, so we built one from each club's own 2026 home game theme announcements.

Here is where it stands: **21 of the 32 teams have named their Crucial Catch game.** The other 11 have not, and for those we list the October home games where it is most likely to land, clearly marked as expected. One team breaks the pattern entirely. Jacksonville holds its Crucial Catch game in September, because the Jaguars spend Weeks 5 and 6 in London.

The 2026 Crucial Catch hats and hoodies are also already on sale, a few weeks before the first game. More on that below.

## What Crucial Catch Is, and How It Changed

Crucial Catch is the NFL's cancer screening campaign with the American Cancer Society. It launched in 2009 as a breast cancer awareness push, which is why so many fans still picture a pink October.

In 2017 the league and the ACS broadened it. After nearly a decade focused on breast cancer, the campaign expanded to early detection and risk reduction for multiple cancers, starting with breast, colorectal and cervical, under the name "Crucial Catch: Intercept Cancer." Today every club supports the league's early detection mission and each year picks one or more cancers to highlight. The league's apparel now carries a multi-colored logo meant to represent all cancers.

Pink has not vanished. The Bears still pair their game with Bears Care's Real Bears Fans Wear Pink, and pink is one of the two main colorways on the 2026 retail line. It is just no longer the whole story. The Steelers describe 2026 as the 18th consecutive season of the partnership.

## When Is Crucial Catch in 2026?

The pattern is steady. The NFL and the ACS activated Crucial Catch in **Weeks 4 through 6** in both 2024 and 2025, per the American Cancer Society's September 26 releases in each of those years.

The 2026 team announcements match it exactly. Week 1 opened Thursday, September 10, which puts the three Crucial Catch Sundays here:

- **Week 4:** Sunday, October 4 (plus Thursday, Oct. 1 and Monday, Oct. 5)
- **Week 5:** Sunday, October 11 (plus Thursday, Oct. 8 and Monday, Oct. 12)
- **Week 6:** Sunday, October 18 (plus Thursday, Oct. 15)

We have not found a 2026 league release that names the weeks outright. Every confirmed game except Jacksonville's falls on one of those three weekends, so we treat Weeks 4 to 6 as the 2026 window.

## What Teams Wear for Crucial Catch

Crucial Catch does not change the jersey. Teams wear their normal home or road uniform, which is why every uniform note above is simply the club's regular look for that week. What changes is everything around the jersey.

In its 2025 release, the NFL listed the on-field elements as Crucial Catch game balls, goal post wraps, field-wall banners, and Nike and New Era apparel for players, coaches and officials carrying the multi-colored all-cancers logo. The sideline hats and hoodies are the most visible pieces on a broadcast. In 2024 the league's hats went to a rainbow plaid.

In the breast cancer years the list ran much longer and was all pink: cleats, gloves, wristbands, chin straps, captains' patches, helmet decals and towels. Some players still work pink or multi-colored accessories into their Crucial Catch games, but the league has not published a 2026 equipment list.

Game-worn Crucial Catch gear goes up on NFL Auction after the games, with proceeds going to the American Cancer Society.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is NFL Crucial Catch?**

Crucial Catch is the NFL's cancer screening campaign run with the American Cancer Society. It started in 2009 as a breast cancer awareness initiative and expanded in 2017 to early detection and risk reduction for multiple cancers. Every team holds one Crucial Catch home game each season, and money from merchandise and auctions goes to the ACS.

**When is NFL Crucial Catch 2026?**

Mostly Weeks 4 through 6, which in 2026 means the weekends of October 4, October 11 and October 18. That matches the Weeks 4 to 6 window the league used in 2024 and 2025. Jacksonville is the exception, holding its game on September 27 against New England.

**Has the NFL released the 2026 Crucial Catch schedule?**

No. Since 2017 it has covered multiple cancers, and each team chooses which cancers to highlight. The league's logo is now multi-colored to represent all cancers. Pink is still part of the retail line, and some teams, like the Bears, still hold a pink-themed game.
