## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the 2026 NBA Finals patch?**

The 2026 NBA Finals patch features the Larry O'Brien Trophy mark with the "2026 NBA Finals" wordmark in a cream-and-white design, and both teams in the Finals wear it. For 2026 the NBA moved the patch from the upper-left chest to the top center of the back of the jersey, just below the collar.

**Where is the NBA Finals patch on the Knicks 2026 Finals jersey?**

The 2026 NBA Finals patch on the New York Knicks Finals jersey sits at the top center of the back of the jersey, just below the collar and directly above the player nameplate. This is a placement change from the upper-left front chest position that has been the standard NBA Finals patch placement for most of the past several Finals series.

**Is the Knicks 2026 NBA Finals jersey blue or orange?**

The Knicks 2026 NBA Finals jersey is the Icon Edition blue jersey, the team's signature primary look with a Knicks blue base, white NEW YORK wordmark, orange number, and contrasting orange and white side panels and shoulder piping.

**Where can I buy the Knicks 2026 NBA Finals jersey?**

The Knicks 2026 NBA Finals jersey is available now at Fanatics, the league's official Finals jersey retail partner. Jalen Brunson, Karl-Anthony Towns, OG Anunoby, Mikal Bridges, and the rest of the Knicks roster all have their numbers available for ordering ahead of the Finals opener on the road at Frost Bank Center in San Antonio.

**What is on the back of the Knicks 2026 NBA Finals jersey?**

The back of the Knicks 2026 NBA Finals jersey carries the 2026 NBA Finals patch at the top center just below the collar, the player name arched across the upper back, and the large player number in Knicks orange below the name. The orange and white side piping continues onto the back panel from the sides.

**When did the New York Knicks last reach the NBA Finals?**

The New York Knicks last reached the NBA Finals in 1999, when they made the run as the eighth seed and lost to the San Antonio Spurs four games to one. The 2026 NBA Finals is the Knicks' first NBA Finals appearance since that 1999 series, the trip clinched by a 4-0 sweep of the Cleveland Cavaliers in the 2026 Eastern Conference Finals.

**Who will the Knicks play in the 2026 NBA Finals?**

The New York Knicks face the winner of the 2026 NBA Western Conference Finals between the Oklahoma City Thunder and the San Antonio Spurs in the 2026 NBA Finals. Home court advantage for the series is set once the Western Conference Champion is decided. If the Spurs advance from the West they hold home court advantage and Game 1 is at Frost Bank Center in San Antonio. If the Thunder advance from the West the Knicks hold home court advantage and Game 1 is at Madison Square Garden in New York.
