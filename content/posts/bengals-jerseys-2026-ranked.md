The Bengals build every uniform they own on one idea, the tiger stripe, and in 2026 they wear it four ways: black at home, white on the road, orange for the home opener, and the all-white White Bengal with the white helmet twice in primetime. We put Cincinnati twelfth in our [best NFL uniforms of 2026](/stories/best-nfl-uniforms-2026-ranked) at a B+, and the individual looks show where that grade comes from. The top of this closet is as good as anything in the league.

Here is every Cincinnati Bengals jersey for 2026, ranked worst to first and graded, with the helmet and pants each one wears and every date it comes out.

The road jersey is white with black numbers outlined in orange, an orange BENGALS wordmark across the chest, and black tiger stripes on the shoulders. It pairs with the striped orange helmet and white pants, which is how we have the Bengals projected for Week 2 at Houston. It projects to all nine road games: at the Texans on Sunday, September 20, at the Steelers in Week 3, at the Dolphins in Week 5, at the Ravens in Week 7, against Atlanta in Madrid on Sunday, November 8 (the Falcons are the home team), at the Commanders in Week 11, at the Browns in Week 13, at the Panthers in Week 15, and at the Colts in Week 16.

The shoulder stripes are the best part, and they are also the only part doing any work. On white, the orange is limited to a thin number outline, the wordmark, and the helmet, so the jersey itself reads mostly black and white, and the brightest color in the franchise ends up on the smallest details.

It grades a B. It is clean and it is unmistakably Cincinnati, but it is the one uniform in the closet where the stripe is not the loudest thing on the field. The best road date is Week 16 at Indianapolis, where the Colts wear their black Indiana Nights alternate, which gives this jersey the dark opponent it needs.

The black home jersey carries white numbers outlined in orange, the orange BENGALS wordmark, and orange tiger stripes on the shoulders. It pairs with the striped orange helmet, and Cincinnati picks the pants during game week. It projects to the five home games not claimed by a special uniform: the Jaguars in Week 4, the Titans on November 1, the Saints on November 29, the Chiefs in Week 14, and the Browns in Week 18. None of those is confirmed by the team yet, so treat them as our projection.

This is the look the franchise is known for, and the 2021 redesign left it alone where it mattered. Orange stripes on black are the highest-contrast version of the stripe in the whole closet, and the white numbers with an orange outline stay readable from anywhere in the stadium. The Week 14 game against Kansas City, with the Chiefs in their white road set, is the best test of it all season.

It grades a B+, the same as the closet overall. What keeps it below the top two is the base. Black is a color Cincinnati uses well, but it is not the color that makes the Bengals the Bengals. That is orange, and the two uniforms ahead of this one put it front and center.

The orange jersey has white numbers outlined in black, a black BENGALS wordmark, and black stripes on the shoulders. In 2026 it had one confirmed date, Open In Orange, the home opener tradition Cincinnati started in 2024: Sunday, September 13 against Tampa Bay, worn with the orange helmet and white pants. That is the version the team settled on in 2025, after the first Open In Orange went orange from helmet to pants.

The white pants are why this ranks so high. With the orange helmet and orange jersey stacked on top, the white pants give the stripes somewhere to stop, so the uniform reads as a block of orange with black stripes rather than a wall of one color. On the Week 1 broadcast it worked exactly that way against Tampa Bay's white and pewter, a matchup we graded a B+ in the [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026).

It grades an A-. This is the jersey that puts the franchise's defining color in the biggest space it has, and the stripe pattern gets better the more orange you put around it.

The White Bengal uses the same white jersey as the road set, with white pants and the white helmet that debuted in 2022, and the black tiger stripes run on the shell at full strength. Cincinnati wears it twice in 2026, both at home and both in primetime: Week 10 against Pittsburgh on Sunday Night Football, November 15, and Week 17 against Baltimore on Thursday, December 31. The New Year's Eve game is billed as White Bengal Stripe, with the crowd split into white and black sections, but the uniform is the same White Bengal set, not a new jersey.

The helmet is the entire difference, and it changes everything. With a white shell, the black stripes become the boldest graphic on the uniform instead of trim, and the head-to-toe white makes the stripe pattern read harder than it does on the black home set. Most white alternates are a road uniform with the contrast turned down. This one turns it up.

It grades an A, the top mark in the closet. Wearing white at home also lets Pittsburgh and Baltimore bring their dark jerseys to Paycor Stadium, so both nights are white against black under the lights, the best-looking divisional games on the Bengals' schedule.

## The Bottom Line on the Bengals Jersey Rankings

The Bengals closet is ranked by how much room each uniform gives the stripe. The White Bengal gives it the whole helmet and grades an A. The orange alternate gives it the franchise color and grades an A-. The black home set is the identity and a strong B+, and the white road set, where the stripe shrinks to the shoulders, is a B.

There is no weak uniform here, and Cincinnati uses its best two well: orange to open the season and the White Bengal for two primetime divisional nights. The only thing we would change is the count. One orange game is not enough for a jersey this good.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jerseys do the Bengals have in 2026?**

Four looks: the black home jersey, the white road jersey, the orange alternate worn for Open In Orange, and the White Bengal, which is the white jersey worn with white pants and the white helmet.

**What is the best Bengals jersey?**

We rank the White Bengal first and grade it an A. The white helmet puts the black tiger stripes front and center, and the all-white look makes the stripe pattern stand out more than any other Bengals uniform.

**What jersey are the Bengals wearing this week?**

Week 2 is at the Houston Texans on Sunday, September 20 at 1:00 p.m. ET. The Bengals are projected in the white road jersey with the orange helmet and white pants, and Houston is projected in its Deep Steel Blue home jersey. Neither team has confirmed, so check the [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026) after kickoff.

**Do the Bengals have a new jersey in 2026?**

No. All four looks are returning. White Bengal Stripe on New Year's Eve is the name of the game and the crowd design, not a new uniform, and the Bengals do not have a Rivalries uniform in 2026 because the AFC North has not had its turn in that program.

**When do the Bengals wear the White Bengal uniform in 2026?**

Twice, both at home: Sunday, November 15 against the Pittsburgh Steelers on Sunday Night Football, and Thursday, December 31 against the Baltimore Ravens, the White Bengal Stripe game.

**When do the Bengals wear orange in 2026?**

The only confirmed orange date is Open In Orange, the home opener against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers on Sunday, September 13, worn with the orange helmet and white pants.
