This is the full Temple 2026 uniform schedule: the cherry jersey, the white jersey, the cherry helmet, the white helmet, the diamond striping that runs through all of it, and a week by week outlook for every game. Two games in, the Owls have worn the cherry jersey twice under two different helmets. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

Temple's kit is Nike, and the current version arrived in July 2025 for K.C. Keeler's first season. It was the first redesign since the school's Nike partnership began in 2020, and the idea behind it is a single motif repeated everywhere: diamonds, for the corner of 10th and Diamond Streets where Temple's football complex sits.

</div>

The diamonds run down the center of both helmets, down the outside of both pants, and around the collar of both jerseys. The secondary logo moved to the chest plate, and the front bumper above the facemask, which used to read TUFF, now reads OWLS. That is the whole system. Two jerseys, two helmets, two pants, one shape holding it together, and no black set, no alternate, nothing extra bolted on.

Which means the variety here is all in the mixing, and Temple mixes a lot. In 2025 alone the Owls ran all cherry with the white helmet, white over cherry with the cherry helmet, and the white on white look the equipment staff calls the Icy Whites. The program announces the pick most game weeks with a graphic on its own account.

**November 14 vs. UAB.** Cherry expected. Senior weekend territory, and a good candidate for the cherry pants if Temple wants the full monochrome.

**November 19 vs. Rice.** Cherry expected. A Thursday night at the Linc on ESPN, the last home game of the season, and the single most likely date for something special.

**November 27 or 28 at Memphis.** White expected. The regular season finale, and the date genuinely is not locked: the conference still has to sort the Thanksgiving weekend windows, and [Memphis](/stories/memphis-uniform-schedule-2026) lists it as November 27 or 28 on its own schedule.

## The Bottom Line

Temple does not need an alternate. The diamond system gives the equipment staff four clean combinations out of two jerseys and two helmets, and the Owls have already used two of them in two weeks. Cherry is the home default, white is locked in on the road, and the interesting question every Saturday is which shell goes on top and whether the cherry pants come out. The two dates we are watching hardest are the Friday afternoon against Army on ESPN and the Thursday night finale at home against Rice, because those are the windows programs save their best looks for. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Temple wearing this week?**

Temple plays at Toledo on September 19, and white is expected under the American Conference road convention. The Owls wore the white helmet with the cherry jersey and white pants against Rhode Island in the opener, then went cherry on cherry with white pants against Penn State. The grid above lists every game with the expected set, and we update it as combinations are confirmed.

**What are Temple's football uniforms for 2026?**

A cherry jersey with white numerals and a white diamond collar, a white jersey with cherry numerals and a cherry diamond collar, a cherry helmet with white diamonds down the center, a white helmet with cherry diamonds down the center, and two pants in the same two colors. It is all Nike, and the design dates to the July 2025 redesign that brought the diamond motif in.

**Why does Temple have diamonds on its uniforms?**

Temple's football complex, Edberg-Olson Hall, sits at the corner of 10th and Diamond Streets in North Philadelphia. The 2025 Nike redesign turned that into the identity: diamonds down the helmet center, down the outside of the pant legs and around the jersey collars.

**Does Temple have an alternate uniform in 2026?**

Nothing announced. Temple's closet is two jerseys, two helmets and two pants, all in cherry and white, and the Owls get their variety by mixing rather than by adding a third color. If a special set shows up, the most likely landing spot is the Thursday night home finale against Rice on November 19, and we will add it to this page.

**What did Temple wear against Penn State?**

The cherry helmet with the white diamond stripe, the cherry jersey and white pants, at Lincoln Financial Field on September 12. Penn State wore its all white road set and won 27-9.

**How many games does Temple play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven at Lincoln Financial Field and five on the road, with a bye on October 24. The full slate with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
