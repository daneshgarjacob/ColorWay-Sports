**Mississippi State finally put the white helmet with the maroon jersey.** For Senior Day against Tennessee Tech on Saturday, November 21, the Bulldogs wore a **white helmet with the maroon interlocking MSU**, a **maroon jersey** and **white pants**, and won **34-16** at Davis Wade Stadium. In ten games before Saturday, Mississippi State had worn the white helmet only with white jerseys. This was the first time all season the white shell went over maroon.

## Quick Answers

- **What did Mississippi State wear against Tennessee Tech?** A **white helmet** with the **maroon interlocking MSU**, a **maroon jersey** with white numbers and white sleeve stripes, and **white pants** with a maroon stripe.
- **Is that new?** For this season, yes. It is the first time in 11 games the Bulldogs have paired the white helmet with the maroon jersey.
- **What is the normal home look?** A **maroon helmet**, maroon jersey and white pants.
- **Final:** Mississippi State 34, Tennessee Tech 16, on Senior Day in the last home game of the season.

## What Mississippi State Wore On Senior Day

From the team's game photos and the broadcast:

- **Helmet:** **white**, with the **maroon interlocking MSU** on each side. It is the same white shell Mississippi State has worn on the road this season.
- **Jersey:** **maroon**, with **white numbers** and **white stripes** on the sleeves. This is the regular adidas home jersey.
- **Pants:** **white**, with a **maroon stripe** down the leg.

Tennessee Tech wore a **gold helmet**, **white jersey** with purple numbers and **white pants**, so the Bulldogs' maroon jersey carried most of the color on the field.

## How It Compares With The Usual Home Look

Mississippi State's standard home uniform this season is maroon on maroon: a **maroon helmet** with the **white interlocking MSU**, a maroon jersey and white pants. That is what the Bulldogs wore for the opener against UL Monroe, against Alabama, against Vanderbilt and, with gray pants, against Auburn.

Swapping only the helmet changes the whole look. The maroon helmet makes the top half of the uniform one solid block of maroon. The white helmet splits it, white on top, maroon in the middle, white on the bottom, and it makes the interlocking MSU, in maroon on white, the easiest thing to read on the field.

The white helmet has now been worn four times: three times with the all-white set (at Minnesota, the White Out against Missouri and at Texas) and once with maroon. Saturday also snapped a six-game losing streak. Mississippi State is 5-6 going into the Egg Bowl.

Mississippi State plays at **Ole Miss on Friday, November 27**, at noon Eastern on ABC. Ole Miss wears its home color, so the Bulldogs are expected in **white jerseys** in Oxford. A win makes them bowl eligible at 6-6.
