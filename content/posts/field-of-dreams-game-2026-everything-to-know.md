Baseball goes back to the corn on August 13. After a three-year gap, MLB at Field of Dreams returns to Dyersville, Iowa, with the Philadelphia Phillies and Minnesota Twins, and this one streams live on Netflix.

We care about this game for one reason above all the others, which is that it produces uniforms teams would never otherwise build. Both clubs have now unveiled what they are wearing, and one of them is the best throwback either franchise has put on a field. Here is everything worth knowing, starting with the part we actually grade.

## The Uniforms

<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/Phillies/status/2079207284308058302"></a></blockquote>

**Philadelphia wears its 1939 to 1941 road set,** and it is the deepest cut in the entire history of this game. The red "Phillies" script slants up across the chest with a navy outline, red and navy striping runs the collar, sleeves, and placket, and the cap carries a bold red P in period lettering. The detail that makes it is the sleeve patch, a replica of the mark the Phillies wore in 1939 for what was then believed to be baseball's 100th anniversary. It is a uniform commemorating a commemoration, and it is anchored to a real dated event rather than a vague sense of oldness.

<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/Twins/status/2079214024139350432"></a></blockquote>

**Minnesota wears 1961, its first season in Minnesota.** It is a white home jersey with navy pinstripes and no trim, a navy "Twins" script outlined in red, and the tail of the "s" curling back to underline the wordmark, which is the best detail on the jersey. The sleeve carries the Minnie and Paul patch, created in 1961 and worn on Twins home uniforms through 1986. That makes it the team wearing its own history rather than dressing up as somebody else's. It is a safer swing than Philadelphia's, and it is a good deal better than the leaked retail-rack photos made it look.

Minnesota is the home team, which is why Philadelphia is in a road set and the Twins are in home pinstripes. The ballpark beside the movie site holds roughly 8,000 people, which is why tickets are what they are. Resale has been running well north of a thousand dollars a seat, so for almost everybody this is a Netflix game.

## Why There Was No Game From 2023 to 2025

The game ran in 2021 and 2022, then stopped. MLB paused it while a larger development project went up around the movie site, including a youth complex and permanent facilities, and the 2026 edition is the first one back. That gap is part of why this year's uniforms matter more than usual. Two clubs get the stage after three years of nobody having it.

## The Matchup

The Twins host, the Phillies visit, and the uniform contrast is the appeal. Philadelphia's 1939 road grays against Minnesota's 1961 home pinstripes is a genuine period frame, two real throwbacks facing each other rather than one team dressing up while the other wears its normal clothes. That is the version of this game that works, and it is what the 2021 and 2022 editions got right too.

## The Bottom Line on the 2026 Field of Dreams Game

The reason to care is the clothes. A regular season game between the Phillies and Twins is a Thursday, but a Phillies team in a 1939 road set with a 100th-anniversary sleeve patch, against a Twins club in 1961 pinstripes with the Minnie and Paul patch on the sleeve, in a cornfield, on Netflix, is the only night all season that looks like this.

Philadelphia is bringing the best uniform in the history of the event. That is worth 7:30 on a Thursday.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When is the 2026 Field of Dreams Game?**

Thursday, August 13, 2026, with first pitch at 7:30 p.m. ET, which is 6:30 p.m. CT local time in Iowa.

**Who is playing in the 2026 Field of Dreams Game?**

The Philadelphia Phillies and the Minnesota Twins. Minnesota is the designated home team.

**How can I watch the Field of Dreams Game?**

The game streams live on Netflix.

**What uniforms are the Phillies wearing at the Field of Dreams Game?**

Philadelphia wears a throwback of its 1939 to 1941 road set: a slanted red "Phillies" script with navy outline, red and navy striping on the collar, sleeves, and placket, gray pants, and a navy cap with a red P. The sleeve carries a replica of the 1939 baseball centennial patch.

**What uniforms are the Twins wearing at the Field of Dreams Game?**

Minnesota wears a 1961 throwback from the franchise's first season in Minnesota: a white home jersey with navy pinstripes, a navy "Twins" script outlined in red, navy back numbers, a navy cap with the interlocking "TC," and the Minnie and Paul sleeve patch worn on Twins uniforms from 1961 to 1986.

**Where is the Field of Dreams Game played?**

At the ballpark built beside the Field of Dreams movie site in Dyersville, Iowa. It holds roughly 8,000 people.

**Why was there no Field of Dreams Game between 2023 and 2025?**

MLB paused the event while a larger development went up around the movie site. The 2026 game is the first since 2022.

**Which Field of Dreams uniform is the best ever?**

We rank the Phillies' 2026 1939 road set first out of all six uniforms worn across the three games. Our [full ranking](/stories/field-of-dreams-uniform-history-ranked) has the complete order.
