Wondering what jersey Alabama is wearing this week? This is the full Alabama 2026 uniform schedule, game by game. The short version: crimson at home, white on the road, and almost nothing else, because Alabama guards its uniform more carefully than any program in the sport.

## Alabama Does Not Do Alternates

The Crimson Tide uniform has barely changed in fifty years, and that is the point. Crimson jersey with white block numbers at home, white jersey with crimson numbers on the road, white pants and the crimson helmet with the player's number on the side. No nameplates on the back. No throwback weeks. No blackouts.

Alabama has not assigned an alternate uniform to any 2026 game, and the program's history suggests it will not. If that changes we will add the date here.

- **Crimson jersey, white pants, crimson helmet.** The home set at Bryant-Denny Stadium.
- **White jersey, white pants, crimson helmet.** The road set.

The numbered helmet is the detail that makes it. No logo, no wordmark, just the player's number in crimson on a plain shell.

SEC home teams wear color and visitors wear white, so the road games below are close to automatic.

**September 5 vs. East Carolina**, Tuscaloosa. Home opener. Crimson.

**September 12 at Kentucky**, Lexington. Road. White.

**September 19 vs. Florida State**, Tuscaloosa. The marquee non-conference game, at home. Crimson.

**September 26 vs. South Carolina**, Tuscaloosa. Crimson.

**October 3 at Mississippi State**, Starkville. Road. White.

**October 10 vs. Georgia**, Tuscaloosa. The biggest home game of the year. Crimson.

**October 17 at Tennessee**, Knoxville. **Crimson, not white.** Announced August 19: Alabama and Tennessee will both wear their home jerseys for the Third Saturday in October, crimson against orange, for the first time since 1970. The same arrangement holds when the game returns to Tuscaloosa in 2027. This is the one road game all season where Alabama is not in white. **October 31.** Bye week.

**November 7 at LSU**, Baton Rouge. Road, and the one to watch. LSU wears white at home for selected games, and when the host goes white the visitor wears color. That is the single date all season where Alabama could wear crimson on the road.

**November 14 at Vanderbilt**, Nashville. Road. White.

**November 21 vs. Chattanooga**, Tuscaloosa. Crimson.

**November 28 vs. Auburn**, Tuscaloosa. The Iron Bowl at home. Crimson.

## The LSU Game Is the Only Real Question

LSU is the one program in the SEC that regularly wears white at home, and Alabama's trip to Baton Rouge on **November 7** is the only game on this schedule where the Tide might not be in the expected jersey. If LSU goes white, Alabama wears crimson on the road. Nothing has been confirmed and we will update this page when it is.

## The Bottom Line

Alabama's 2026 uniform schedule is the most predictable in the SEC. Crimson at home, white on the road, the numbered crimson helmet in every game, and no alternates announced or expected. The November 7 trip to LSU is the only date where the answer is genuinely open.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Alabama wearing this week?**

Alabama wears crimson at home and white on the road. The one date that could break the pattern is November 7 at LSU, because LSU sometimes wears white at home, which would put Alabama in crimson.

**Does Alabama have alternate uniforms in 2026?**

No alternate uniform has been assigned to any 2026 Alabama game. The program has historically avoided alternates entirely, running only the crimson home set and the white road set.

**Why does Alabama have numbers on its helmets?**

Alabama's crimson helmet carries the player's jersey number on the side rather than a logo or wordmark. It is one of the oldest continuously used helmet designs in college football.

**Does Alabama put names on the back of its jerseys?**

No. Alabama does not wear nameplates on its game jerseys, one of the few remaining programs in major college football that does not.

**What does Alabama wear in the Iron Bowl?**

The 2026 Iron Bowl is at Bryant-Denny Stadium on November 28, so Alabama is the home team and wears crimson.
