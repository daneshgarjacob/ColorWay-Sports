**The Utah Mammoth and Colorado Avalanche revealed their Winter Classic jerseys on Wednesday, November 18.** They will wear them on **Thursday, December 31, at Rice-Eccles Stadium in Salt Lake City**, at 6 p.m. Eastern (4 p.m. Mountain) on TNT, truTV and HBO Max. It is the first NHL outdoor game in Utah and the Mammoth's first outdoor game ever.

The NHL says both sweaters are "inspired by outdoor hockey played at the base of ski hills." Utah wears **Mountain Blue with brown and beige**. Colorado wears **cream-white with blue stripes and a burgundy "C"**, plus burgundy helmets for the first time in team history.

## Quick Answers

- **What Utah wears:** Mountain Blue jersey, beige stripes, an arched "UTAH" over a brown walking mammoth, brown pants and gloves, Mountain Blue helmet and socks.
- **What Colorado wears:** Cream-white jersey with blue stripes and shoulder yoke, a burgundy Tuscan-style "C" with blue mountains inside, burgundy pants, gloves and helmets.
- **Where Utah's look comes from:** Hygeia Iceland, Utah's first ice rink, in Sugar House. The stripes honor the rink and the "UTAH" lettering copies the old Hygeia Ice Co. signs.
- **Where Colorado's look comes from:** 19th-century Rocky Mountain town signs and the railway lines that tied them to Denver, plus vertical yoke stripes from historic Colorado hockey jerseys.
- **When:** Thursday, December 31, 2026, 6 p.m. ET, Rice-Eccles Stadium.
- **Our grades:** Utah **A+**. Colorado **C+**.

**The color.** The jersey is **Mountain Blue**, the light sky blue from the Mammoth's palette, with **brown and beige** trim. The NHL says the brown logo, gloves and pants are a nod to a woolly mammoth's fur. Up close, the brown on the sleeve numbers and nameplate letters has a reddish tint.

**The crest.** "UTAH" arches across the chest in thick beige letters outlined in brown, over a **throwback walking mammoth**, tusks curled up, in brown. In the team's photos the crest and numbers have a soft, felt-like texture rather than shiny twill.

**The stripes: Hygeia Iceland.** This is where the history is. The team says the striping "pays homage to Hygeia Iceland," Utah's first ice skating facility, in the Sugar House neighborhood of Salt Lake City. It was a popular outdoor rink through the 1950s and later became a practice rink for the **Salt Lake Golden Eagles**, the city's minor-league team, starting in 1969. The sleeves carry wide beige bands, the hem has a beige stripe and the collar is beige.

**The lettering.** The arched "UTAH" and the player names and numbers are drawn from the early signs of **Hygeia Ice Co.**, the ice company behind the rink. The team calls out the "thick letterforms and swooping serifs." The back number is beige outlined in brown, the name is brown on a beige nameplate, and the sleeve numbers are brown with beige trim.

**Inside the collar and hem.** The interior collar and hem have a repeating **snowflake pattern from vintage ski sweaters**, in brown on beige. The hem tag is a version of the **Utah state flag with a snowflake in the center**, set in a hexagon.

**The shoulder patch.** A new round "Utah Mammoth" patch with the mammoth logo.

**The helmet.** Mountain Blue, with "Utah" in script on the side. The NHL says a tusk forms the crossbar of the "T."

**Pants, gloves and socks.** Brown pants with a Utah-shaped "UTAH" logo, brown gloves, and Mountain Blue socks with beige stripes to match the sleeves.

## The Colorado Avalanche Winter Classic Jersey

<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">Via the <a href="https://x.com/Avalanche" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Colorado Avalanche</a></p>

**The color.** The NHL calls it white with blue accents. In the team's photos it reads as **cream**, with a **blue shoulder yoke**, blue sleeve bands and a blue stripe at the waist. **Burgundy** is the accent, on the crest, numbers, name, pants, gloves and helmet.

**The crest.** A vintage **Tuscan-style "C" in burgundy** with **blue mountain peaks** inside it. The C is stitched with a zigzag and snowflake pattern in the same ski-sweater spirit as Utah's collar. The NHL says the lettering is inspired by **19th-century signage in Rocky Mountain towns** and the railway lines that connected them to Denver.

**The shoulder yoke.** The NHL says the vertical stripes across the yoke are drawn from **historic Colorado hockey jerseys**. The Winter Classic logo patch sits on the shoulder.

**Numbers and name.** Burgundy, one color, on the back and the sleeves, with the name in burgundy above the number.

**The burgundy helmet.** The Avalanche will wear burgundy helmets for the first time. The NHL says they carry a custom waving Colorado flag inspired by the old patches on hockey coaches' cardigans. The team's close-up also shows a gold-trimmed version of the mountain "C" on the shell.

**Pants and gloves.** Burgundy. The left pant leg has a **diamond logo**, a nod to the black-diamond difficulty ratings on ski runs.

**The loop label.** It reads **"5280,"** Denver's elevation in feet, the Mile High City.

## Our Grade

**Utah Mammoth: A+.** I absolutely love it. It gives me the winter vibe I want from an outdoor game. My favorite Winter Classic jersey ever is the Pittsburgh Penguins' light blue one from a while back (the powder blue from the 2008 game in Buffalo), and this is similar to that. I love the blue with the brown, which reads almost maroon on the numbers. And the Hygeia Iceland story makes it more than a color: it is a real Utah rink, a real Utah sign and a real piece of the state's hockey history.

**Colorado Avalanche: C+.** It's nice. It's just a bit plain. A cream jersey with blue stripes and a burgundy C is a bit boring to me, and the best ideas, the 5280 label and the ski-sweater stitching, are things you will never see from the stands. The burgundy helmets are the one big swing. Definitely, the Utah one is better.

Which one would you wear on New Year's Eve?

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What do the Utah Mammoth Winter Classic jerseys look like?**

Mountain Blue with beige stripes, an arched beige "UTAH" over a brown walking mammoth, brown numbers and nameplate letters, brown pants and gloves, a Mountain Blue helmet with "Utah" in script and Mountain Blue socks with beige stripes.

**What do the Colorado Avalanche Winter Classic jerseys look like?**

Cream-white with a blue shoulder yoke and blue stripes, a burgundy Tuscan-style "C" with blue mountains inside, burgundy numbers, burgundy pants with a diamond logo, burgundy gloves and burgundy helmets.

**When is the 2027 Winter Classic?**

Thursday, December 31, 2026, New Year's Eve, at 6 p.m. ET (4 p.m. MT). The NHL calls it the 2027 Winter Classic because it belongs to the 2026-27 season.

**Where is the 2027 Winter Classic?**

Rice-Eccles Stadium in Salt Lake City, home of University of Utah football and the site of the 2002 Winter Olympics opening ceremony.

**What channel is the Winter Classic on?**

TNT, truTV and HBO Max in the United States. Sportsnet and TVA Sports in Canada.

**Who is playing in the 2027 Winter Classic?**

The Utah Mammoth host the Colorado Avalanche.

**What is Hygeia Iceland?**

Utah's first ice skating facility, an outdoor rink in Sugar House run by the Hygeia Ice Co. It was a popular gathering place through the 1950s and became a practice rink for the Salt Lake Golden Eagles starting in 1969. The Mammoth's Winter Classic stripes honor it, and the "UTAH" lettering is based on the company's signs.

**What do the Mammoth Winter Classic stripes mean?**

They pay homage to Hygeia Iceland, Utah's first rink, according to the team.

**What is on the hem of the Mammoth Winter Classic jersey?**

A tag with the Utah state flag and a snowflake in the center, and a repeating snowflake pattern inside the collar and hem taken from vintage ski sweaters.

**What does 5280 mean on the Avalanche Winter Classic jersey?**

It is Denver's elevation in feet, 5,280, one mile. It is printed on the jersey's loop label as a nod to the Mile High City.

**Is this the first time the Avalanche have worn burgundy helmets?**

Yes. The NHL says the Winter Classic is the first time the Avalanche will wear burgundy helmets.

**What does the diamond on the Avalanche pants mean?**

It is a nod to ski-run difficulty ratings, like a black diamond trail.

**What inspired the Avalanche Winter Classic jersey?**

The NHL says the lettering comes from 19th-century Rocky Mountain town signs and the railway lines that connected them to Denver, and the vertical yoke stripes come from historic Colorado hockey jerseys.

**Is this the Mammoth's first outdoor game?**

Yes. It is also the first NHL outdoor game in Utah.

**Are the Winter Classic jerseys the same as the Hometown Remix jerseys?**

No. Hometown Remix is the league-wide special edition series revealed Nov. 2. The Winter Classic jerseys are separate, made only for the New Year's Eve game.

**Who made the Winter Classic jerseys?**

Fanatics, the NHL's authentic outfitter. The league and Fanatics unveiled them together.

**Can I buy the Utah Mammoth Winter Classic jersey?**

Yes. It went on sale November 18 at the Mammoth team store and across the Fanatics network, including NHLShop.com and NHLShop.ca.

**How well are the 2027 Winter Classic jerseys selling?**

Better than any before them. Andrew Low Ah Kee, CEO of Fanatics Commerce, said on November 20 that it was the biggest Winter Classic launch in history, at more than double the previous best, and that 8 of the top 10 products across every league on Fanatics were Winter Classic items.

**Which Winter Classic jersey is better, Utah or Colorado?**

Ours is Utah, A+ to a C+. The Mountain Blue and brown feel like winter and the Hygeia Iceland history is real. Colorado's is clean but plain.
