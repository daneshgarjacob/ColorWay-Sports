**Final: Canadiens 3, Devils 2.** The New Jersey Devils wore their Hometown Remix jersey for the first time on Wednesday, Nov. 18, against the Montreal Canadiens at Prudential Center, and the debut ended in a loss. We checked the game's highlight frames: the Devils wore the black Remix with red trim and red numbers, and the Canadiens wore their regular white road sweater.

Jake Evans scored the go-ahead goal and Nick Suzuki ended Montreal's power-play drought, handing Connor Hellebuyck his first loss as a Devil. Luke Evangelista and Timo Meier scored for New Jersey. It was also the end of a seven-game home winning streak, all of it in red.

It was Newark Celebration Night, with live music and dance from Newark artists before the game and during both intermissions. New Jersey came in at 15-6-0. Montreal came in at 12-5-4.

## Quick Answers

- **What did the Devils wear?** The Hometown Remix jersey, for the first time: black, with a large red NJ that has no circle around it and angled red stripes on the sleeves.
- **What did the Canadiens wear?** Their regular white road sweater. Nov. 18 is not one of Montreal's Remix dates.
- **Where does the design come from?** The black sweater the Devils wore at the February 2024 Stadium Series against Philadelphia, with the colors flipped and the NJ pulled out of its circle.
- **What is the theme?** The team's reveal line: "pride without permission," "loyalty without question," "the fabric of New Jersey."
- **How many times do the Devils wear it?** 10 times, all at home.
- **What jersey does it replace?** The black "Jersey" script alternate, which the Devils retired after last season.
- **Our grade:** C+, No. 23 of 32 in [our Hometown Remix ranking](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-jerseys-ranked). It is the best of the dark-base, one-color-pop group, and it still plays it safe.
- **Who won?** Montreal, 3-2. The Devils are 0-1-0 in the Remix and 9-2-0 at home in red.

## What The Remix Looks Like

<div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:1fr;max-width:280px;margin:1em auto 0.4em;"><img src="/images/posts/nhl-daily-tracker/devils-remix.jpg" alt="New Jersey Devils Hometown Remix jersey: black body with a large red NJ logo with no circle, angled red sleeve stripes and a red hem" style="width:100%;border-radius:10px;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></div>
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">The Devils' Remix. Studio photo via the NHL</p>

Start with the color. The regular Devils home sweater is red with black shoulders. The Remix flips that: the body is **black** and the red goes into the details.

- **Crest:** the NJ monogram with the horns and tail, in **red**, made bigger and taken out of the circle it normally sits in. It is the only thing on the chest.
- **Sleeves:** sharp, **angled red stripes**, not the straight three-stripe bands on the regular jerseys.
- **Hem:** red.
- **Lettering:** new names and numbers that mix curves with hard cuts.
- **Where it started:** the black Stadium Series sweater from Feb. 17, 2024, at MetLife Stadium against the Flyers, with the colors reversed.

The team teased it as the "Black Parade" before the November reveal. It fills the black alternate slot that the cursive "Jersey" sweater left open when it was retired after a farewell run in 2025-26.

On the ice, the black body and the red NJ read clearly from a distance. That is the strength of this jersey. Against Montreal's white, red and blue, it was one of the cleaner-looking matchups of the Remix season so far.

## The Inspiration

The Hometown Remix is a league-wide program from the NHL and Fanatics. Every team got one special jersey for 2026-27, built around its city or region. Most teams leaned on a landmark or a local symbol. New Jersey went with an attitude.

The Devils revealed theirs on Nov. 2 with four lines: "It's pride without permission. It's loyalty without question. It's a force moving as one. It's the fabric of New Jersey." Fanatics' product description uses the same idea, a team and its fans "moving forward as one force."

So there is no turnpike, no shoreline and no Jersey Devil legend on the sweater. The New Jersey part is the black and red, the NJ itself and the Stadium Series look fans already liked.

## Our Grade: C+

We ranked it **No. 23 of 32**, at the top of our "boring" tier. Red on black is the Devils' best color combination, and freeing the NJ from its circle makes the logo big enough to read from the upper deck. The angled sleeve stripes give it some edge.

The problem is that it is a safe jersey. The Devils had a red-and-green era from 1982 to 1992 that fans still love, and a Remix program built for local stories was the perfect place to bring it back. Instead, New Jersey reused an outdoor game look from 2024. It looked good on TV. It did not surprise anyone.

**Matchup grade: B.** Black and red against Montreal's white, red and blue is clear and sharp. It was the only Remix debut this week against an Original Six team.

## The Devils At Home In Red

Before Wednesday, every Devils home game had been in the red home sweater, and Prudential Center had been a fortress. From our [uniform records](/stories/devils-uniform-schedule-2026-27):

| | Record |
|---|---|
| At home in red | 9-2-0 |
| At home in the Remix | 0-1-0 |
| Home winning streak before the debut | 7 games (since Oct. 15) |
| In red overall, home and road | 11-2-0 |
| Current winning streak in red | 9 games |
| In white on the road | 4-4-0 |

The two red wins away from Newark came in Vegas on Oct. 30, when the Golden Knights wore white at home, and in Toronto on Nov. 8, when the Maple Leafs debuted their cream Remix.

So the Remix took the ice in the middle of a nine-game run in red, and the black sweater is the one that broke the home streak. Before Wednesday, the last home loss was Oct. 12 against Ottawa. Connor Hellebuyck started in goal after winning all seven of his starts for New Jersey, and took his first loss as a Devil.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When did the Devils first wear their Hometown Remix jersey?**

Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026, against the Montreal Canadiens at Prudential Center in Newark. Montreal won 3-2.

**What does the Devils Hometown Remix jersey look like?**

It is black, with a large red NJ logo that has no circle around it, angled red stripes on the sleeves, a red hem and new names and numbers that mix curves with hard cuts.

**Is the Devils Remix red or black?**

Black. The red is in the logo, the sleeve stripes and the hem. The red jerseys with black shoulders and a round "Property Of New Jersey" badge in the team's Tuesday practice photos are practice jerseys, not the game sweater.

**What is the "Property Of New Jersey" jersey?**

A practice jersey the Devils wore on Tuesday, Nov. 17, ahead of the Remix debut. It comes in red with black shoulders and in white, with a round "Property Of New Jersey" badge around the Devils logo. It is not worn in games.

**What inspired the Devils Hometown Remix jersey?**

The black sweater from the February 2024 Stadium Series against the Flyers, flipped so the NJ is red on black. The team's theme is "pride without permission" and "loyalty without question," which it calls "the fabric of New Jersey."

**How many times will the Devils wear the Hometown Remix jersey?**

10 times, all at home. After the debut: Nov. 27 vs. Calgary, Nov. 28 vs. Winnipeg, Dec. 15 vs. the Rangers, Dec. 17 vs. Toronto, Jan. 16 vs. the Islanders, Jan. 19 vs. Dallas, Feb. 23 vs. Carolina, March 23 vs. Philadelphia and March 29 vs. Carolina.

**Does the Remix replace the Devils' black "Jersey" alternate?**

Yes. The cursive "Jersey" sweater was retired after a farewell run in 2025-26, and the Remix takes over the black alternate slot.

**What did the Canadiens wear at New Jersey?**

Their regular white road sweater, confirmed from the game's highlight frames. Montreal wears its own white Remix 10 times this season, and Nov. 18 at New Jersey is not one of them.

**What is Newark Celebration Night?**

A Devils theme night for Newark's arts community. The Suyat Band, with Newark's Destiny Autumn, was scheduled to play before the game and during both intermissions, the EVO Dance Crew on the main concourse in the first intermission, and DJ MORAE' and the Arts High School Jazz Band were also on the lineup.

**What is the Devils' home record this season?**

9-3-0. They are 9-2-0 in the red home sweater and 0-1-0 in the Remix, after the loss to Montreal ended a seven-game home winning streak. They have won nine straight in red counting two road games.

**Where does the Devils Remix rank among all 32?**

No. 23 in [our ranking](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-jerseys-ranked), with a C+. The Penguins' pop-art sweater is No. 1 and the Golden Knights' casino sweater is last.

**Can I buy the Devils Hometown Remix jersey?**

Yes. It has been on sale through Fanatics, NHLShop.com and the Devils' team store since the Nov. 2 reveal.
