**Look closely at a Dallas Cowboys player in the home whites and you can count four colors that do not belong together: a silver helmet, a green-tinted silver pant, royal blue stripes and a navy blue star.** Most NFL teams wear one silver and one blue. Dallas wears two of each, at the same time, on the same player.

We already answered the single most-asked part of this in [what color are the Cowboys' pants](/stories/what-color-are-the-cowboys-pants). This story is the whole uniform: every color, sampled from a real photo and laid side by side, what the team itself calls each one, where the mismatch came from and what the Cowboys have said about it. One detail surprised us: the team's own 2026 media guide does not list one set of colors. It lists two.

## Quick Answers

- **Why don't the Cowboys' uniform colors match?** Because Dallas wears pieces from two different color sets at once. The helmet's Silver and navy blue belong with the navy jersey. The white jersey's colors are Royal Blue and Silver-Green. The home uniform mixes them.
- **What are the Cowboys' official colors?** The 2026 Dallas Cowboys Media Guide lists them in two groups. Helmet/Blue Jersey: Silver (PMS 8240) and Blue (PMS 282). White Jersey: Royal Blue (PMS 661) and Silver-Green (PMS 8280).
- **Are the Cowboys' helmet and pants the same color?** No. The helmet is Silver. The pants worn with the white jersey are Silver-Green, and they read green next to the helmet.
- **Why are there two blues?** The helmet star and the navy jersey are a dark navy. The stripes, numbers and socks on the white uniform are royal blue. The team's equipment director has said navy took over because it sold better.
- **Who picked the pants color?** Tex Schramm, the team's founding president and general manager, according to longtime equipment director Mike McCord, who said Schramm saw the color inside a car.
- **Have the Cowboys ever fixed it?** No. In 2010 a team executive said Dallas was testing new pants shades. Nothing changed, and in 2023 the team said its uniforms were the same as always.
- **Our take (opinion):** leave it. The mismatch is the most recognizable thing about the uniform.

Two silvers: the helmet sample is a neutral gray, and the pants sample has more green in it than red or blue. Two blues: the royal on the sock is clearly blue, and the star on the helmet is so dark it reads almost black under the stadium lights. Put the four chips next to each other and you can see what fans have argued about for decades.

## The Helmet: Silver

The Cowboys' helmet shell is a metallic silver that most guides call **metallic silver-blue**. The team's own media guide calls it simply **Silver, PMS 8240**. On it sit a navy star with a white border and a blue-white-blue stripe down the crown.

The helmet is the oldest part of the modern look. Dallas wore white helmets with a plain blue star from 1960 through 1963 and switched to the silver-blue shell in 1964, according to the team's uniform history as summarized by Wikipedia. It has been silver ever since, apart from the white helmets on the Thanksgiving Classic and the Color Rush set.

## The Pants: Silver-Green

The pants Dallas wears with its white jersey are the color everyone notices. The team lists them as **Silver-Green, PMS 8280**. Equipment director Mike McCord called the shade "Cowboys Star Blue" in a 2017 interview with FOX Sports. In person it reads silver-blue. On TV it often reads green.

Whatever the name, it is not the helmet's silver. Stand a player in the home whites under stadium lights and the pants are greener and darker than the helmet above them. We go deeper on the pants, including why HD television made the green more obvious, in [what color are the Cowboys' pants](/stories/what-color-are-the-cowboys-pants).

## Two Blues: Royal Trim, Navy Star

The second mismatch is blue. On the white home uniform, the sleeve stripes, numbers, pants stripes and socks are **royal blue (PMS 661)**. The star on the helmet is a dark navy, which the team lists as **Blue (PMS 282)**, the same blue as the navy road jersey.

Uni Watch's Paul Lukas pointed this out in a 2007 ESPN column: the royal on the uniform and the navy on the helmet, he wrote, "clash." The Cowboys' explanation for why navy took over the dark jersey is a business one. McCord told FOX Sports in 2017 that the team's blue was originally royal, and that navy "has been more popular in retail."

## The Team's Own Media Guide Lists Two Color Sets

This is the part we had not seen anyone point out. On the "Fingertip Information" page of the **2026 Dallas Cowboys Media Guide**, published by the team and the NFL, the colors are not listed as one palette. They are split under two headings:

Read that way, the mismatch is not an accident in a single palette. It is two complete color sets. The navy uniform matches itself: navy jersey, Silver helmet, Silver pants. The white uniform matches itself too: royal trim, Silver-Green pants. The clash happens because Dallas wears **one helmet with both**, so the white uniform always carries a Silver and navy helmet on top of royal and Silver-Green.

## The Navy Jersey Gets A Different Silver

The road pants are the third silver in the story. "On the road, the Cowboys wear a different color silver," FOX Sports wrote in 2017, a silver that matches the helmet and the navy jersey "far better." The media guide backs that up by putting the helmet and the navy jersey in the same Silver group.

Some color references list the road pants as their own shade, a pearl-like metallic silver with a separate Pantone number, which would make three silvers. Lukas counted three in 2007 too: the helmet, the home pants and the road pants, and he wrote that the helmet matched neither. The team's current guide lists two. We go with the team's list and note that the count depends on who you ask.

You can see the navy set three times in 2026, all on the road: at Houston in Week 4, at Indianapolis in Week 9 and at the Rams on December 20. Every date is on our [Cowboys 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/cowboys-uniform-schedule-2026).

## Why: Tex Schramm, A Car Interior And Television

The fullest explanation on record comes from the team's own equipment staff. Mike McCord, the Cowboys' equipment director, told FOX Sports in 2017 that the pants color started with **Tex Schramm**, the club's founding president and general manager, who "saw a car with that color interior and fell in love with it."

Two more pieces of the history are documented:

- **The color changed over time.** McCord said the pants looked closer to a plain gray in the team's early years and through most of the 1970s, and that the current shade was matched in the late '70s and early '80s, when Russell made the uniforms. A summary of the team's uniform history on Wikipedia says silver-blue pants arrived with the silver helmet in 1964. The accounts do not fully agree on when the pants became today's color.
- **Television.** A 2010 NBC 5 Dallas report, based on a Dallas Observer story by Richie Whitt, said Schramm's color mix was designed to come across on television as silver. Schramm knew TV: before joining Dallas he spent three years as an assistant sports director at CBS in the late 1950s, according to the Pro Football Hall of Fame, which inducted him in 1991. We could not find Schramm or the team saying the TV reason in their own words, so treat it as a reported explanation, not a confirmed one.

Why the helmet and pants were never made to match is not documented anywhere we could find. Lukas wrote in 2007 that no reasonable explanation had ever been offered for the two sets of silver pants. That is still true.

## What The Cowboys Have Said

The team has addressed the colors on the record a handful of times, and every time the answer has been to keep them.

- **2010:** Bill Priakos, then the team's vice president of merchandising, said the green pants were the "No. 1 grief" from fans at home games, and that HD had brought the same complaints from TV viewers. He said the team was experimenting with different shades in the pants, and that Dallas would never change the home white jerseys or the helmets. The pants did not change.
- **2017:** McCord explained the car-interior origin and the switch from royal to navy for retail, and said what fans see on HD television is close to the real color on the field.
- **2023:** The NFL's style guide showed royal stripes on the silver road pants, and fans wondered whether Dallas would start wearing silver pants with the white jersey. McCord told KRLD there was nothing to it and the uniforms were exactly the same as in past years. The team called the style guide difference possibly human error.

## Where The Mismatch Takes A Day Off: Thanksgiving

On Thanksgiving against the Eagles on November 26, Dallas wears the Classic, its 1960s throwback: a white helmet with the vintage star, a navy jersey with stars on the shoulders and white pants with navy stripes. No silver anywhere, so nothing clashes. The all-white Arctic White Color Rush does the same thing with a white helmet and white pants. Both are cleaner than the everyday uniform. Neither is what anyone pictures when they picture the Cowboys. Full details are in our [NFL Thanksgiving uniforms guide](/stories/nfl-thanksgiving-uniforms-2026).

## Our Take

Leave it. A Cowboys uniform with one silver and one blue would be tidier, and it would look like a dozen other teams. The green-tinted pants under a neutral silver helmet, with royal on the body and navy on the star, are why you can spot Dallas from across a room. Schramm already proved the point when he put the team in white at home ([why the Cowboys wear white at home](/stories/why-do-the-cowboys-wear-white-at-home)): the Cowboys win by looking like nobody else, not by following the rules. The mismatch has lasted more than 40 years. It is the brand now.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why don't the Cowboys' uniform colors match?**

Dallas wears pieces from two color sets at once. The team's 2026 media guide lists Silver and Blue (navy) for the helmet and navy jersey, and Royal Blue and Silver-Green for the white jersey. The home uniform puts the Silver-and-navy helmet on top of the royal-and-Silver-Green white uniform.

**What are the Dallas Cowboys' official colors?**

Per the 2026 Dallas Cowboys Media Guide: Helmet/Blue Jersey, Silver (PMS 8240) and Blue (PMS 282); White Jersey, Royal Blue (PMS 661) and Silver-Green (PMS 8280). White is the base of the home jersey.

**Do the Cowboys' helmet and pants match?**

No. The helmet is Silver (PMS 8240). The pants worn with the white jersey are Silver-Green (PMS 8280). Sampled from the same photo, the helmet reads neutral gray (#B7B7B3) and the pants read green-gray (#8D998E).

**Why do the Cowboys have two different blues?**

The white uniform is trimmed in royal blue, while the helmet star and the dark jersey are navy. Equipment director Mike McCord said in 2017 the team's blue started as royal and navy took over because it was more popular in retail.

**Why are the Cowboys' pants green?**

The color is a custom silver with a green-blue tint. McCord said it came from Tex Schramm, who saw the color inside a car and loved it. The team lists it as Silver-Green.

**Did Tex Schramm pick the Cowboys' pants color for TV?**

A 2010 NBC 5 Dallas report, based on the Dallas Observer, said the color mix was designed to look silver on television. Schramm worked in TV at CBS before joining Dallas. We found no statement from Schramm or the team confirming the TV reason, so it remains a reported explanation.

**Do the Cowboys wear different pants with the navy jersey?**

Yes. With navy, Dallas wears a different silver pant that matches the helmet. The team groups the helmet and navy jersey under one Silver.

**How many silvers do the Cowboys use?**

The team's current media guide lists two, Silver and Silver-Green. Some color references and Uni Watch's Paul Lukas (2007) count three, treating the road pants as a separate shade from the helmet.

**Have the Cowboys ever tried to change the pants color?**

In 2010 the team said it was experimenting with different pants shades after fans complained the pants looked green in HD. No change was made. In 2023 the team said its uniforms were exactly the same as in past years.

**When did the Cowboys get silver helmets?**

In 1964. The first Cowboys helmets, from 1960 through 1963, were white with a blue star.

**What do the Cowboys wear on Thanksgiving 2026?**

The Classic 1960s throwback against the Eagles on November 26: white helmet, navy jersey with stars on the shoulders and white pants. Along with the all-white Color Rush, it is one of two Dallas uniforms with no silver on it.

**When do the Cowboys wear the navy jersey in 2026?**

Three times, all on the road: at the Texans in Week 4, at the Colts in Week 9 and at the Rams in Week 15 on December 20.

**Should the Cowboys make their colors match?**

Our opinion: no. One silver and one blue would make the uniform look like many others. The mismatch is what makes it recognizable.
