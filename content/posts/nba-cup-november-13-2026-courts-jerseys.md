The Emirates NBA Cup is back on Friday, November 13, with **nine group play games**, and every one is played on the home team's painted Cup court. Here is what everybody wears, from the NBA's official game-by-game uniform schedule, published October 19.

1. **Every home team wears its Statement jersey.** All nine: the Pacers, Magic, Heat, Bulls, Pelicans, Rockets, Spurs, Kings and Clippers.
2. **Eight of the nine road teams wear white.** The exception is Golden State, which wears its **royal blue Icon jersey** at San Antonio, so Warriors-Spurs is the only game of the night with no white jersey on the floor.
3. **Two games are on Prime Video:** Knicks at Pacers at 7:00 p.m. ET and Warriors at Spurs at 9:30 p.m. ET.

That first point corrects a common line about the Cup. When the courts were unveiled, the coverage said teams wear Statement Edition uniforms in every Cup game. On the official schedule it is the **home team** that wears Statement. The visitors wear whatever the schedule gives them, which on Friday is almost always white.

Every court is graded in our [NBA Cup courts ranking](/stories/nba-cup-courts-2026-ranked), and every jersey for every team is in our [NBA uniforms hub](/stories/nba-uniforms-2026-27). We log what was actually worn the next morning in the [NBA uniform tracker](/stories/nba-uniform-tracker-2026-27).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there an NBA Cup game on Friday, November 13, 2026?**

Yes, nine: Knicks at Pacers, Bucks at Magic, Cavaliers at Heat, Hawks at Bulls, Grizzlies at Pelicans, Jazz at Rockets, Warriors at Spurs, Trail Blazers at Kings and Thunder at Clippers, all group play games in the Emirates NBA Cup.

**What jerseys do NBA teams wear in NBA Cup games?**

The home team wears its Statement Edition jersey. On November 13 all nine home teams are in Statement, and eight of the nine road teams are in their white Association jersey. The Warriors wear their royal blue Icon jersey at San Antonio.

**Do both teams wear Statement jerseys in the NBA Cup?**

No. The official uniform schedule has only the home team in Statement. The visitors wear white in eight of Friday's nine games.

**Are all NBA Cup games played on the special courts?**

Yes. The NBA confirmed every Cup game is played on the painted Cup courts, so each home team uses its own Cup floor for its group games.

**Which NBA Cup games are on national TV on November 13?**

Two, both on Prime Video: Knicks at Pacers at 7:00 p.m. ET and Warriors at Spurs at 9:30 p.m. ET. The other seven are on local broadcasts.

**What is the best NBA Cup court in use on November 13?**

The Magic's, a B+ and fourth of all 30 in our ranking. The Pacers' and Clippers' courts (both B-) are next.

**What is the worst NBA Cup court in use on November 13?**

The Rockets'. We graded it an F, last of all 30 Cup courts.

**When are the remaining NBA Cup group play nights?**

After November 13, group play continues on November 20, 24, 25 and 27. The quarterfinals are December 4 and 5, the semifinals December 8 and 9, and the final is December 11 at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis.

**Who won the NBA Cup last season?**
