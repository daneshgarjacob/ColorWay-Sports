**Look at the left sleeve of any Bears jersey and you will find three letters in the stripes: GSH.** They are the initials of George Stanley Halas, the man who started the franchise in 1920, played for it, coached it for 40 seasons and owned it until the day he died.

So why do the Bears wear GSH on their sleeves? Halas died on Oct. 31, 1983, in the middle of a season. The Bears wore a football-shaped patch for him on the chest for the rest of that year, and in 1984 they moved his initials onto the sleeve, where they have been ever since. Here is the full story, the colors and the C that came from the same man, and what Chicago wears next Thursday at Detroit.

## Quick Answers

- **What does GSH stand for on the Bears jersey?** George Stanley Halas, the Bears' founder, player, coach and owner.
- **When did GSH go on the sleeve?** In 1984, the season after Halas died. Most accounts say 1984; a few say 1985.
- **Which sleeve?** The left one, inside the stripes.
- **When did George Halas die?** Oct. 31, 1983, at his home in Chicago. He was 88.
- **What did the Bears wear in 1983?** An orange football-shaped patch on the front of the jersey, with a big H between the G and the S.
- **Is GSH on every Bears jersey?** Since 2024, yes. That year the team added it to the throwback, the one jersey that had been missing it.
- **Where did the Bears' colors come from?** Halas brought navy and orange from the University of Illinois, where he played.
- **What are the Bears wearing on Thanksgiving?** The white road jersey with the navy helmet at Detroit, Thursday, Nov. 26. **Expected.**
- **Our take (opinion):** it is the best detail on any NFL uniform. It is small, it is permanent, and it means something.

## Who Was George Stanley Halas?

<img src="/images/posts/nfl-uniform-mysteries-explained/halas-1950.jpg" alt="Black and white photo of Bears founder and coach George Halas in a cap and sweatshirt, holding a football, in 1950" style="width:100%;max-width:420px;border-radius:10px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto 0;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">George Halas in 1950. Photo: public domain</p>

Halas did almost every job a football team has. In 1920 he ran the A.E. Staley company's team in Decatur, Illinois, and represented it at the meeting in Canton, Ohio, where the league that became the NFL was organized. The team moved to Chicago in 1921 and became the Bears in 1922.

He played end for the team in its early years. He coached it for 40 seasons across four separate stints, the last ending in 1967, and won six NFL championships as head coach, with 324 total wins. He was a charter member of the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 1963. And he owned the team from the start until his death.

That is the reason the initials are on the jersey. They do not honor a star player or a single season. They honor the person the whole franchise grew out of.

## Oct. 31, 1983: The Patch Comes First

Halas died at his home in Chicago on Oct. 31, 1983. He was 88 and still the team's principal owner. The 1983 season was half over.

For the rest of that year the Bears wore a memorial patch on the front of the jersey: an orange football with a monogram of his initials, a large H in the middle with the G and the S on either side. The Chicago Tribune's history of Bears uniforms describes it as a monogram-style football patch, and the Bears' own photo caption calls it the "Original 1983 GSH patch."

## 1984: Onto The Left Sleeve

The patch came off after the season. The initials did not. In 1984 the Bears put GSH into the stripes on the left sleeve of their jerseys, a permanent memorial instead of a one-year patch. NBC Chicago and most other accounts put it in 1984. A few say 1985. The team has not published a date that we could find, so we go with the majority.

Since then:

- **2012:** Nike took over the NFL uniform contract, and the Bears' Nike update made the GSH larger, according to the team's 2012 uniform release as cited by Wikipedia. We have not seen that release directly.
- **2024:** The Bears added GSH to the throwback jersey, which had gone without it. With that change, every jersey the team wears carries the memorial.
- **2026:** The Rivalries uniform debuts on Christmas Day against Green Bay with a replica of the 1983 football patch on the chest. We have not confirmed whether it also has GSH on the sleeve.

Most teams wear a memorial patch for one season and then retire it. The Bears have worn this one for more than 40 years.

## The Same Patch, For His Daughter

In 2025 the Bears used the same idea again. Virginia Halas McCaskey, George Halas's daughter and the team's owner for more than four decades, died on Feb. 2, 2025, at 102. The team wore an orange football-shaped **VMH** patch on the upper left of the jersey all season, with the M for McCaskey in the middle, which the Bears called "a near-identical design" to the 1983 patch. The team also kept its alternates and throwbacks in the closet that year in her honor.

## The Colors Came From Illinois

Halas played football, baseball and basketball at the University of Illinois, and he took the school's navy and orange with him to his pro team from the start, before it was even called the Bears. The Bears' own site says he "brought the school's navy and orange colors with him," and the Chicago Tribune's uniform history says the same. Illinois picked navy and orange in 1894. We tell that story, and 31 others, in [how every NFL team got its colors](/stories/how-every-nfl-team-got-its-colors).

## The Wishbone C: 1962

The orange C on the navy helmet came later, after Halas had been running the team for 40 years. The Bears put the wishbone C on the helmet in 1962. It was white at first and turned orange in the 1970s; most accounts say 1974, a few say 1973.

The shape is not a Bears invention. Uni Watch traced the wishbone-style C to the University of Chicago around 1898, and the Cincinnati Reds were the first pro team to wear one, in 1905.

There is history in this one, too. Detroit's Thanksgiving tradition started against the Bears. On Nov. 29, 1934, the Lions hosted Chicago at the University of Detroit Stadium in their first Thanksgiving game, and the Bears won, 19-16, per the Pro Football Hall of Fame. Halas was the Bears' coach that day.

## Our Take

The best uniform details are the ones nobody had to invent. GSH is not a marketing idea or a reveal-video moment. It is a memorial that a team put on its sleeve in 1984 and simply never took off, and the Bears have treated it that way: bigger in 2012, added to the throwback in 2024, copied for Halas's daughter in 2025. In a league where special patches come and go every season, the Bears found one that belongs on the uniform permanently. We think it is the best sleeve detail in the NFL. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What does GSH mean on the Bears jersey?**

GSH stands for George Stanley Halas, who started the franchise in 1920 as the Decatur Staleys, played for it, coached it for 40 seasons and owned it until his death in 1983.

**Why do the Bears have GSH on their sleeve?**

To honor Halas. After he died on Oct. 31, 1983, the Bears wore a memorial patch on the chest for the rest of that season, then moved his initials to the left sleeve in 1984 as a permanent tribute.

**What year did the Bears add GSH to the jersey?**

The sleeve initials started in 1984, according to most accounts. A few sources say 1985. The football-shaped chest patch came first, in 1983.

**Which sleeve is GSH on?**

The left sleeve, set inside the sleeve stripes.

**When did George Halas die?**

Oct. 31, 1983, at his home in Chicago. He was 88.

**How old was George Halas when he died?**

88. He was born in 1895.

**Did George Halas found the Bears?**

Yes. He ran the A.E. Staley company team in Decatur, Illinois, in 1920, represented it at the meeting that organized the NFL, moved it to Chicago in 1921 and renamed it the Bears in 1922.

**How many championships did George Halas win?**

Six NFL championships as the Bears' head coach, with 324 total wins in 40 seasons, according to the Pro Football Hall of Fame.

**Is GSH on every Bears jersey?**

Yes, since 2024, when the team added it to the throwback. The navy home, white road and throwback jerseys all carry it. The 2026 Rivalries jersey carries a replica of the 1983 football patch on the chest.

**What was the 1983 Bears patch?**

An orange football-shaped patch on the front of the jersey with Halas's initials, a large H between the G and the S. The Bears are bringing a replica back on their Rivalries uniform on Christmas Day.

**What does VMH mean on the Bears jersey?**

Virginia Halas McCaskey, George Halas's daughter and the team's longtime owner, who died on Feb. 2, 2025, at 102. The Bears wore a VMH football patch, nearly identical to the 1983 GSH patch, throughout the 2025 season.

**Did the GSH ever change?**

The letters stayed in the same place. The Bears' 2012 Nike update made them larger, according to the team release as cited by Wikipedia, and in 2024 the team added them to the throwback jersey.

**Why are the Bears navy and orange?**

Halas brought the colors from the University of Illinois, where he played. The team has used them since its first season.

**When did the Bears get the wishbone C?**

1962. It started out white and turned orange in the 1970s.

**What are the Bears wearing on Thanksgiving 2026?**

The white road jersey with the navy helmet at Detroit, Thursday, Nov. 26, at 1:00 p.m. ET on CBS. Expected until the team posts its game-week look.

**Did the Bears play in the Lions' first Thanksgiving game?**

Yes. The Bears beat the Lions 19-16 on Thanksgiving 1934 at the University of Detroit Stadium, the first game of Detroit's Thanksgiving tradition.
