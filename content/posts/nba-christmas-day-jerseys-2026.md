**There are no special NBA Christmas jerseys in 2026.** All 10 teams playing on December 25 are scheduled to wear their regular Icon, Association or Statement jerseys, per the NBA's official game-by-game uniform schedule as of November 27. No Christmas edition, no Specter Edition and no City Edition on the holiday.

Below: all five Christmas Day games with tip times, the jersey each team wears with a picture of it, why the Specter Edition keeps getting called a Christmas jersey, and a look back at the adidas Christmas uniforms from 2012 to 2016.

## Quick Answers

- **Are there NBA Christmas jerseys in 2026?** No. Neither Nike nor the NBA has announced a Christmas set, and the league's uniform schedule has every Christmas team in a regular jersey.
- **What is each team wearing on Christmas Day?** Spurs black at Knicks white (noon ET). Heat black at Celtics green (2:30 p.m.). 76ers royal blue at Lakers white (5 p.m.). Thunder blue at Timberwolves black (8 p.m.). Nuggets royal blue at Warriors white (10:30 p.m.).
- **Will anyone wear Specter on Christmas?** Not on the schedule. Five of the eight Specter teams play on December 25, and all five wear Specter on other nights in November and December.
- **When was the last NBA Christmas jersey?** 2016, the last season adidas made the league's uniforms. Nike took over in 2017 and has not made a Christmas set.

## Are NBA Christmas Jerseys Coming Back in 2026?

Not this year. In early November, reports spread that Nike was bringing back Christmas uniforms for the first time since 2016, with eight teams and a December 14 release: the Celtics, Knicks, 76ers, Hawks, Lakers, Warriors, Kings and Pistons. Here is what has happened since:

- **Those eight teams are the Specter Edition**, the dark fifth uniform Nike and the NBA confirmed on September 10. The teams posted their official Specter photos on November 23.
- **The Specter debuts on Sunday, November 29**, and the league schedule has 24 Specter games, three per team, all at home, between November 29 and December 29. None of them is on December 25.
- **The on-sale date is November 30, not December 14.** The Hawks' official Specter page says online at the team shop on November 30 and in the arena from December 3.
- **Three of the eight teams (the Hawks, Kings and Pistons) do not play on Christmas at all.**

So the "Christmas jerseys" in those reports are the Specter Edition, and the Specter Edition is worn around Christmas, not on it.

The Specter got called a Christmas jersey twice. When the designs leaked in April, the leak was labeled as the 2026 Christmas set, and [we covered it that way](/stories/nba-christmas-jerseys-2026-leaked). In September, Nike revealed them as the [Specter Edition](/stories/nba-specter-edition-jerseys-2026-27), and we explained [why only eight teams got one](/stories/why-only-8-nba-teams-got-specter-edition-jerseys). The holiday timing is close: the Hawks, Kings, Knicks and Lakers all wear Specter on **December 23**, two days before Christmas.

What about City Edition? No team wears its 2026-27 City Edition on Christmas either. Most clubs debut theirs from [January 14, 2027](/stories/nba-city-edition-jerseys-2026-27). Miami is the exception among the Christmas teams: its Vice City jersey has been on the floor since November 18, but the Heat are in black Icon at Boston.

## NBA Christmas Jerseys History: 2012 to 2016

The NBA wore dedicated Christmas uniforms for five straight years under adidas. Each year had its own idea:

- **2012, "Big Color":** one-color uniforms, the jersey and shorts in the same team color. Miami's all-red set for its Christmas game against the Thunder is the one most fans remember.
- **2013, "Big Logo":** the sleeved Christmas jerseys. A large logo sat in the middle of the chest, but the short sleeves got all the attention. Adidas said they were lighter than a normal jersey, and some players complained the sleeves affected their shooting.
- **2014, "First-Name Basis":** the players' first names on the back of the jersey, on a panel below the numbers.
- **2015, "Christmas Cards":** cursive script lettering inspired by holiday greeting cards. ABC7 calls it the most beloved set of the five.
- **2016:** the script lettering came back for a second and final year.

Nike took over the NBA's uniforms in 2017 and stopped the Christmas sets. In 2021 Nike said, "We like the assortment we have now." (Source for this history: [ABC7's look back at the Christmas jerseys](https://abc7ny.com/post/looking-back-best-christmas-day-nba-jerseys/12605873/).)

## Our Take: Put the Specter Teams in Specter on Christmas

People wanted the Christmas report to be true, and we get it. Some adidas Christmas jerseys were bad (the sleeved ones), but Christmas felt like an event. Now the NBA's biggest regular-season day looks like any other night of the season.

The fix was sitting right there. Five Specter teams play on the 25th: the Knicks, Celtics, Lakers, 76ers and Warriors. Put all five in Specter that day and the league has a Christmas collection without making a single new jersey. Instead the Specter nights land on December 22 and 23 and the holiday gets the home whites.
