**Texas A&M is changing two things at Kyle Field this week: the field and the uniform.** On Wednesday morning the team posted photos of crews painting a **script "Aggies"** into the end zones, and that afternoon it posted a **black uniform** with the same script on the helmet under the words "Paint it black." Tennessee visits on **Saturday, November 14, at 3 p.m. Eastern on ESPN**.

## The Script Aggies End Zones

The post, titled "Script Aggies," shows the word **Aggies in a flowing script** painted across the end zone in a dark fill with a white outline, in place of the block lettering. The 50-yard line and the rest of the field markings are unchanged in the photos.

## The Black Uniform

From the team's photos:

- **Helmet:** black, with **"Aggies" in white script** on the side, the same script as the end zones.
- **Jersey:** black, with **TEXAS A&M** across the chest and the numbers in white.
- **Pants:** black, with a white Texas-shaped logo on the hip.

The post does not name the game, but it came during Tennessee week, the same day as the end zone photos, and Tennessee is A&M's next game. We will confirm the full look from game photos on Saturday.

## What Tennessee Wears

Tennessee has worn all white on the road at Georgia Tech, Arkansas and South Carolina this season, and we expect white again at Kyle Field. Tennessee wore its all-black Dark Mode at home against Kentucky on November 7. Black against white would make this one of the cleaner color matchups of the week.

Texas A&M's 2026 uniform schedule had one announced alternate, the Honor and Support camo, worn against Arkansas on October 3 ([our grade](/stories/texas-am-camo-honor-and-support-uniforms-2026)).
