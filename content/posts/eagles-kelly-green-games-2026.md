The record tells a clear story. Philadelphia won its first five Kelly Green games, the Dolphins and Bills in 2023, the Jaguars and Cowboys in 2024 and the Giants in October 2025. It has lost the three since: at Dallas in November 2025, the Week 18 finale against Washington with backups playing, and this month against the Giants. That is 5-3 across all eight games, or 5-2 if you only count the seven we can confirm from photos.

The Dallas game in 2025 is the only road Kelly Green game since the uniform came back. It was possible because the Cowboys wear white at home, and a visiting team can only wear a colored jersey when the home team is in white.

## What The Kelly Green Uniform Looks Like

The current set recreates the uniform the Eagles wore through the 1985-95 era, before the switch to midnight green in 1996:

- **Helmet:** kelly green with silver wings.
- **Jersey:** kelly green with white numbers.
- **Pants:** silver, with a green-white-green stripe down the side.

The Eagles could not bring it back for years because of the NFL's one-helmet rule, which made teams use the same helmet shell for every uniform. Once the league allowed a second helmet again, Philadelphia brought back Kelly Green in 2023, the first Kelly Green game since 2010. How the club ended up with midnight green in the first place is in [how every NFL team got its colors](/stories/how-every-nfl-team-got-its-colors).
