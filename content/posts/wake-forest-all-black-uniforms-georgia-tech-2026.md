**Wake Forest wore all black at Georgia Tech, and it was a road game.** The Demon Deacons played at Bobby Dodd Stadium on Saturday, November 21, in a **black helmet with the gold WF**, a **black jersey** with old gold numbers and **black pants**, and won **50-29**. Visitors in the ACC usually wear white. Georgia Tech chose **all white** at home for its last home game, which left the dark jersey to Wake Forest. It was the first time this season Wake has worn its black jersey away from Winston-Salem.

The team's post after the game called it "History makers." Wake Forest says it is the first team in the College Football Playoff era with **six power conference road wins in one season**: Purdue, Louisville, NC State, Cal, SMU and Georgia Tech. The player at the bottom right of that graphic is in the black helmet and black jersey. Wake wore white in the other five road games, so that photo is from Atlanta.

## Quick Answers

- **What did Wake Forest wear at Georgia Tech?** All black: a **black helmet with the gold WF**, a **black jersey** with **old gold numbers** and the WF on the shoulders, and **black pants**.
- **Why black on the road?** Georgia Tech wore all white at home, so Wake Forest wore its dark jersey.
- **What did Georgia Tech wear?** A white helmet with the gold GT, a white jersey with gold numbers and white pants.
- **How many times has Wake gone all black this season?** Three that we have confirmed: Miami, Stanford and Georgia Tech. The Deacons are 2-1 in them.
- **Final:** Wake Forest 50, Georgia Tech 29.

## What Wake Forest Wore At Georgia Tech

From the broadcast and Wake Forest's own game photos:

- **Helmet:** **black**, with the **gold block WF** on each side. This is the WF helmet, not the gold Deacs script helmet Wake wore against Stanford.
- **Jersey:** **black**, with **old gold numbers**, an old gold nameplate and the **WF** on each shoulder.
- **Pants:** **black**.

It is the full monochrome version of the Wake Forest home set. The usual home look puts **old gold pants** under the black jersey, and that black over gold is the closest thing Wake has to a signature uniform. Black pants take the gold off the bottom half and leave the numbers and the helmet logo as the only color.

## Why Wake Forest Wore Black On The Road

The home team picks first. ACC home teams usually take their color and send the visitor out in white, and that is what we expected at Georgia Tech, where the Yellow Jackets wear gold at home this season. Instead, Georgia Tech went **all white** for its final home game: a **white helmet with the gold GT**, a **white jersey with gold numbers** and **white pants**. With the hosts in white, Wake Forest wore black.

Wake Forest has worn white in all five of its other road games this season. Black against white also made it one of the cleanest contrasts of the weekend. Georgia Tech's side is on our [Georgia Tech 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/georgia-tech-uniform-schedule-2026).
