**NFL teams that wear white at home this season are 3-20-1. That means 3 wins, 20 losses and 1 tie in 24 games.**

We log the uniform every NFL team wears in every game, then match it to the final score. Nobody else keeps this. Through Week 10, 11 teams have chosen white for a home game. Those games have gone almost all one way. And the same 11 teams have a better record on the road than they do at home in white.

## Quick Answers

- **What is the record of NFL teams wearing white at home in 2026?** 3-20-1 (3 wins, 20 losses, 1 tie) through Week 10.
- **How many games is that?** 24 home games in white, by 11 teams.
- **Which teams wear white at home the most?** The Cowboys (5 games) and Buccaneers (5), then the Dolphins (4). The Texans and Jets have done it twice. The Ravens, Bengals, Colts, Saints, Giants and Commanders once each.
- **Which teams won at home in white?** Only three: the Cowboys over the Commanders in Week 2 (37-20, in the Arctic White Color Rush), the Buccaneers over the Falcons in Week 8 (16-0) and the Bengals over the Steelers in Week 10 (38-18). The Commanders tied the Giants 27-27 in Week 5.
- **How do those teams do in other games?** 12-17 at home in color and 23-26-1 on the road. So they win more often on the road than at home in white.
- **What about the rest of the league at home?** The other 21 teams are 56-41 at home.
- **Is white at home cursed?** No. The sample is small and several of these teams are losing teams. But the split between home white and road games is hard to ignore.
- **Our take (opinion):** a team that loses in white at home should try its color at home before it tries anything else.

## How We Count

Every number here comes from ColorWay Sports' game-by-game uniform log, the [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026). We record the look each team actually wore, from game photos, and pair it with the ESPN final score. Every team's full record in every uniform is on our [uniform records page](/uniform-records).

A **white home game** is any game where the team is the designated home team and wears a white jersey. That includes white alternates, white throwbacks and all-white Color Rush sets. International games count for the designated home team, so the Cowboys' Week 3 game against the Ravens in Rio de Janeiro is a Dallas home game. Records run through Week 10, which ended Monday, Nov. 16.

For which teams wear white at home and how often, see our full list: [which NFL teams wear white at home in 2026](/stories/nfl-teams-that-wear-white-at-home-2026).

## Finding 1: 3-20-1 Is The Worst Home Split We Track

Three wins in 24 games is a winning percentage of .146 (counting the tie as half a win). For comparison:

- **The other 21 NFL teams at home:** 56-41 (.577).
- **The same 11 teams at home in color:** 12-17 (.414).
- **The same 11 teams on the road:** 23-26-1 (.470).
- **Every NFL road team in a white jersey:** 57-65 (.467).

That last line matters. White jerseys are not losing everywhere. Road teams in white win close to half their games, about what you would expect from road teams. The problem is white at home, and it is a big one. The visiting teams in those 24 games, almost all of them in their color, went 20-3-1.

## Why Do Some NFL Teams Wear White At Home?

In the NFL the home team picks its jersey, and the visitors wear the opposite. Most teams pick color. A few pick white, for a few reasons.

- **Dallas: tradition.** Sports Illustrated's Cowboys site has reported that Tex Schramm, the club's original president and general manager, put Dallas in white at home so Cowboys fans would see visiting teams in their own colors. Mike McCord, the team's longtime equipment director, told Fox Sports in 2017 that it also "started with the heat," especially at Texas Stadium.
- **Miami: the heat, most likely.** The Dolphins have not given an official reason. The explanation most often given is the South Florida sun: white stays cooler, and it puts the visitors in the dark jersey. Miami's color games in 2026 are bunched in December and January.
- **Tampa Bay: probably the heat too.** The Bucs have not said why. But they wear white from September into November, the hottest part of the year in Tampa, and switch to red once it cools off.
- **Everyone else: special looks.** Most of the one-off white home games were throwbacks or theme nights, like the Jets' White Out against Green Bay and the Giants' Vintage White against Washington.

The full history is in our story on [which NFL teams wear white at home](/stories/nfl-teams-that-wear-white-at-home-2026).

## Does This Happen In Other Leagues?

Yes, in a smaller way. We log the NBA and NHL the same way.

- **NBA:** home teams in color are 73-60 (55 percent wins). Home teams in white are 33-39 (46 percent). NBA teams used to wear white at home by rule. Here is [why NBA teams don't wear white at home](/stories/why-nba-teams-dont-wear-white-at-home) anymore.
- **NHL:** home teams in white are 2-5. Home teams in their dark sweater are 174-151. In hockey, white at home is rare by design. Here is [why NHL teams wear dark jerseys at home](/stories/why-do-nhl-teams-wear-dark-jerseys-at-home).

NBA and NHL records run through Monday, Nov. 16. In all three leagues, the home team in white wins less often than the home team in color. In the NBA and NHL the gap is small. In the NFL it is enormous.

## What This Does Not Prove

Be careful with this number.

- **The sample is small.** 24 games is about a month and a half of football.
- **Many of these teams are losing.** The Dolphins are 1-8, the Jets 2-8, the Colts 3-7, the Buccaneers 3-6, the Texans and Saints 3-6, and the Cowboys 4-6. Bad teams lose in any color.
- **The schedule matters.** Some of these were tough opponents: the Chiefs, Packers, Lions and 49ers.

What the record does show is the split. The same teams are 23-26-1 on the road and 3-20-1 at home in white. Team quality alone would not explain that. The heat could cut both ways, and so could luck. Our honest read: something is off for these teams in white at home this season, and we do not know yet whether it is the jersey, the schedule or chance.

## Our Take

The home team gets to pick. That is a real edge, and these teams are giving it away. White at home made sense for Dallas in the Schramm era, when TV was new and fans wanted to see the other team's colors. It makes less sense now, when every fan can see every team, every week.

We do not think a jersey wins or loses a football game. But if we ran the Cowboys or the Dolphins, 2-12 at home would be enough to try the color. Dallas has a navy jersey it is 2-0 in. Miami has an aqua jersey that won its only game. There is no rule that says they cannot wear them at home.
