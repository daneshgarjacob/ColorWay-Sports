Washington has worn the gold helmet in four of five games in 2026: purple over gold pants in the Apple Cup win over Washington State and the 16-14 escape against Utah State, purple over white pants against Eastern Washington, and white over white pants in the 25-21 loss at USC. The all-black Dawgs alternate came out once, for the Minnesota blackout. This is the full Washington Huskies 2026 uniform schedule: the purple home set, the white road set, the all-purple option, the new all-black Dawgs alternate, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

The core set dates to the adidas refresh of June 2025, which brought back the gold outline on the numerals that the final Nike jerseys used to carry, switched the purple jersey's shoulder stripes to gold and white, and reworked the pant stripes so the gold pants match the helmet stripe. The same release included an all-purple look, purple jersey over purple pants, in the spirit of the 2023 team that reached the national title game. The only 2026 alternate announced so far is the black Dawgs jersey, revealed in August. It is the third straight season with a blackout look: the first version debuted on Senior Day against UCLA in 2024, then returned for the Sun Bowl against Louisville and for Rutgers in 2025. Big Ten home teams wear color and visitors wear white, so the road dates below are predictable. The home dates are where the athletic department's game themes give us clues.

**September 6 vs. Washington State.** ★ Confirmed from the program's own postgame photo: **gold helmet, purple jersey, gold pants**, the base home set for a season-opening Apple Cup at Husky Stadium. The trophy celebration shows the white numerals with gold outline and the gold pants with the purple and white stripe. Washington won 24-10. Washington State confirmed its own side in a reveal video: crimson helmet with a white facemask, white jersey, crimson pants. One calendar note: this was a Sunday game, 1 p.m. Pacific on NBC, not a Saturday.

**September 12 vs. Utah State.** ★ Confirmed from the Big Ten Network broadcast: **gold helmet, purple jersey, gold pants** again, on Band Day. Utah State wore white helmets, white jerseys and navy pants. Washington won 16-14 in a game that was 10-7 at halftime and was not settled until the fourth quarter. More: [Utah State uniform schedule](/stories/utah-state-uniform-schedule-2026).

**September 19 vs. Eastern Washington.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast, with one change from what we had down: **gold helmet, purple jersey, white pants**. We expected gold pants, the pairing Washington used in each of its first two games, and the Huskies switched to white below the belt for Community Day. Eastern Washington wore white with red and black, and Washington won 48-16.

**September 26 vs. Minnesota.** ★ Confirmed by Washington and from the game photos: the new all-black **Dawgs** set, **matte black helmet, black jersey with DAWGS in gold script, black pants**, with a purple and gold stripe on the helmet and shoulders. Minnesota wore a maroon helmet, white jersey and white pants. This Big Ten opener was the "Unleashed" blackout game, with fans asked to wear black, 8 p.m. Pacific on FOX. Minnesota won 27-24: Demond Williams Jr. threw for 239 yards and two touchdowns and ran for a third with 1:53 left, and Washington fell to 3-1. The Dawgs jersey is a one-off, so this is the only game it is scheduled to appear. ESPN lists this game on September 27 because its feed runs on UTC.

**October 3 at USC**, 7:30 p.m. ET on NBC. ★ Confirmed by Washington: **gold helmet, white jersey, white pants**. The Huskies posted it as "Week Five Threads vs USC." After the all-black Dawgs blackout against Minnesota, it is back to the gold helmet, now over the white road jersey and white pants for the first Big Ten road trip, to the Coliseum. USC is expected in cardinal at home, so it is cardinal against white with gold helmets on the Washington side. ★ Washington's own game photos match: **gold helmet, white jersey, white pants**, worn as posted, and USC wore the cardinal helmet, cardinal jersey and gold pants. Washington led by double digits in the second half, but USC scored the last 15 points, the go-ahead score a 48-yard fumble return by Jahkeem Stewart, and won 25-21. Demond Williams Jr. drove Washington to the USC 12 in the final minute, but his last pass fell incomplete. The Huskies are 3-2.

## The Bottom Line

Five games in, Washington has stayed close to the base set, with one big exception: the new Dawgs jersey came out for the Minnesota blackout night, as the athletic department's theme suggested it would. Penn State's Purple Reign homecoming is the natural stage for the all-purple set, and Salute to Service against Indiana is where a camo surprise showed up last year. On the road, Washington opened with the gold helmet, white jersey and white pants at USC, and white is the jersey for the four trips left. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Washington wearing this week?**

Washington wore the gold helmet, white jersey and white pants at USC on Saturday, October 3, as posted and confirmed in the team's game photos, in a 25-21 loss. The week before, against Minnesota, the Huskies wore the all-black Dawgs uniform for the "Unleashed" blackout game, a 27-24 loss. Next is Iowa at Husky Stadium on Friday, October 9, where purple is expected.

**What are Washington's football uniforms for 2026?**

A purple home jersey with gold-outlined white numerals and gold-and-white shoulder stripes, worn with gold pants; a white road jersey with gold-outlined purple numerals, built to go with purple pants; an all-purple combination; and a new all-black alternate with DAWGS in gold script. The metallic gold helmet with the purple W is the standard shell. Everything is made by adidas.

**Does Washington have a new alternate uniform in 2026?**

Yes. Washington revealed an all-black uniform in August with DAWGS in gold script across the chest, purple numerals outlined in gold, and purple and gold piping. It is the third straight season with a blackout look, and it is a one-off for one home game. Washington wore it for the September 26 blackout game against Minnesota, confirmed from the game photos.

**What did Washington wear against Washington State in the 2026 Apple Cup?**

The standard home set: gold helmet, purple jersey, gold pants. Washington won 24-10 at Husky Stadium on Sunday, September 6. The Huskies wore the same combination a week later in the 16-14 win over Utah State.

**Does Washington wear white pants on the road?**

They have in 2026. The 2025 adidas refresh paired the white road jersey with purple pants, but for the first road trip of 2026, at USC on October 3, Washington wore white pants with the white jersey and the gold helmet. The Huskies also wore white pants with the purple jersey at home against Eastern Washington.

**How many games does Washington play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven at Husky Stadium and five on the road, with a bye on October 24. Two of the games are on Friday nights, at home against Iowa on October 9 and at Purdue on October 16, and the opener against Washington State was played on a Sunday.
