Duke keeps its jerseys simple and plays with its helmets. The blue home jersey and the white road jersey do most of the work, and the variety comes from the shell: a block D one week, Gothic Duke lettering the next, black when the night calls for it. This is the full Duke 2026 uniform schedule: the blue home set, the white road set, the black alternate, every helmet in the rotation, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games. Two games in, the defending ACC champions have worn two different helmets, and we update this page as each combination is confirmed.

Nike outfits Duke, and the base jerseys have barely moved in years. The helmet is where the program experiments. The Gothic Duke lettering borrowed from the basketball program's Brotherhood look debuted on a white shell for the 2023 opener against Clemson, and it has since shown up in white, black, and a chrome blue version. There is also a Blue Devil head decal on both white and black shells, and for the 2025 ACC Championship Game Duke wore a white helmet with a blue center stripe and a DUKE wordmark. We have not seen a new 2026 alternate announced, so the grid below works from the closet we know. ACC home teams wear color and visitors wear white, which makes the road dates predictable and leaves the helmet as the weekly question.

**September 5 vs. Tulane.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast and Duke's own game photos: **white helmet with the blue block D, blue jersey, white pants**, the most traditional look in the closet for the opener at Brooks Field at Wallace Wade Stadium. Tulane wore white jerseys with green pants. Duke won 17-3.

**September 12 at Illinois.** ★ Confirmed from Duke's game photos: **white helmet with the blue Gothic Duke lettering, white jersey, white pants**, the full all-white road set, against Illinois in navy jerseys, orange pants, and orange helmets. Duke won 31-27 at Gies Memorial Stadium after trailing 24-14 late in the second quarter. That made six straight wins.

**September 19 vs. Stanford.** Blue expected. The ACC opener, a 4 p.m. Eastern kickoff on The CW, with Stanford in white.

**September 26 vs. William & Mary.** Blue expected. A 3:30 p.m. Eastern kickoff on ACC Network Extra against the FCS Tribe, and the last home game before the October stretch.

**October 3.** Bye.

**October 10 at Georgia Tech.** White expected, with Georgia Tech in its home colors at Bobby Dodd Stadium. See the [Georgia Tech 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/georgia-tech-uniform-schedule-2026) for the Yellow Jackets' side.

**October 17 vs. North Carolina.** Blue expected. The Victory Bell game in Durham, with the Tar Heels in white on the [North Carolina 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/north-carolina-uniform-schedule-2026). Rivalry week is when Duke tends to reach for something beyond the block D.

**October 23 at Virginia.** White expected. A Friday night game at Scott Stadium, 7 p.m. Eastern on ESPN.

**October 31 vs. Boston College.** Blue expected. A Halloween home game, and the kind of date where a black alternate would not surprise us.

**November 7 at NC State.** White expected. The Tobacco Road trip to Carter-Finley Stadium is NC State's Military Appreciation game, and the Wolfpack could bring back their camo Wolfpack Warriors set; we cover that on the [NC State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/nc-state-uniform-schedule-2026). Worth remembering: on its last trip to Raleigh in 2024, Duke wore the black helmet with white Gothic lettering.

**November 14 at Miami.** White expected, with Miami in orange at Hard Rock Stadium, per the [Miami Hurricanes 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/miami-hurricanes-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 20 vs. Clemson.** Blue expected. The final home game, a Friday night kickoff at 7:30 p.m. Eastern on ESPN, with Clemson in white. ESPN files this game under November 21 because its feed runs on UTC, but it is a Friday, November 20 game. See the [Clemson 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/clemson-uniform-schedule-2026) for the Tigers' side.

**November 28 at Wake Forest.** White expected. The regular season finale in Winston-Salem.

## The Bottom Line

Duke's jersey pattern is about as predictable as it gets: blue at home, all white on the road, black when the moment calls for it. The helmet is the real storyline. Two games have produced two shells already, the block D against Tulane and the Gothic lettering at Illinois, and Friday night against Clemson plus the rivalry dates with North Carolina and NC State are the likeliest spots for black. The grid above has every date, and we add a star as each uniform is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Duke wearing this week?**

Duke hosts Stanford on Saturday, September 19, and the blue home jersey with white pants is expected, with Stanford in white. Last week at Illinois, Duke wore the all-white road set with the white Gothic Duke helmet. The week-by-week grid above lists every game with the expected set, and we update it as combinations are confirmed.

**What are Duke's football uniforms for 2026?**

A Duke blue home jersey with white numerals and shoulder stripes, a white road jersey with the same design in blue, and a black alternate jersey. The helmets include a white shell with the blue block D, a white shell with blue Gothic Duke lettering, a black shell with white Gothic lettering, and a black shell with the Blue Devil head. Nike makes the uniforms.

**What helmet did Duke wear against Illinois?**

The white helmet with blue Gothic Duke lettering, the basketball-inspired design that first appeared in the 2023 opener against Clemson. Duke paired it with a white jersey and white pants in the 31-27 win. In the opener against Tulane, Duke wore the white helmet with the blue block D.

**Does Duke have a new alternate uniform in 2026?**

Not that has been announced. Duke's variety comes mostly from its helmet rotation rather than new jerseys. If a new set is revealed, we will add it to this page along with the date it is worn.

**Why does ESPN list the Clemson game on November 21?**

ESPN's schedule feed runs on UTC. A 7:30 p.m. Eastern kickoff on Friday, November 20 is already Saturday in UTC, so the game shows a November 21 date there. Duke's other Friday game, at Virginia on October 23, kicks off at 7 p.m. Eastern.

**How many games does Duke play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: six at Brooks Field at Wallace Wade Stadium and six on the road, with a bye on October 3. Two are Friday night games, at Virginia on October 23 and against Clemson on November 20. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
