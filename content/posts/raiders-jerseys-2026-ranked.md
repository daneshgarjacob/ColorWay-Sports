The Raiders have two jerseys in 2026, the same two they have worn for most of six decades: black at home and white on the road, both over silver pants and under the silver helmet. There is no alternate, no throwback, no alternate helmet, and no Rivalries uniform, since the program has not reached the AFC West yet. We put Las Vegas twenty-second in our [best NFL uniforms of 2026](/stories/best-nfl-uniforms-2026-ranked) at a B, lower than the reputation, and the order of these two jerseys is part of the reason.

Here are both Las Vegas Raiders jerseys for 2026, ranked and graded, with the helmet and pants each one wears. For the week-by-week jersey schedule 2026, the [Raiders 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/raiders-uniform-schedule-2026) has every game.

The black home jersey has white numbers with a silver outline, worn with silver pants under the silver helmet and the shield-and-swords logo. It is the jersey for all eight home games at Allegiant Stadium: the Miami Dolphins, Kansas City Chiefs, Buffalo Bills, Los Angeles Rams, Seattle Seahawks, Los Angeles Chargers, Denver Broncos and Tennessee Titans. The team's home themes include Viva Los Raiders against Kansas City, Crucial Catch against Buffalo and Salute to Service against Seattle, but none of them comes with a different uniform. It opened the season against Miami, and Las Vegas won 27-13.

Silver and black is one of the strongest identities in American sports, and the dome helps. Under controlled light the black reads as a color and the silver pants and helmet catch the highlights. The weakness is the jersey itself. Black with white numbers gives the silver very little room above the waist, so at distance the uniform can read as a dark block with silver at both ends, and the Nike cut has never looked as sharp as the old one.

It grades a B. It is classic, and nobody confuses it for another team, but the reputation is doing some of the work.

The white road jersey has black numbers with a silver outline, worn with the same silver pants and silver helmet. It projects to all nine road games: at the Chargers, Saints, Patriots, Jets, 49ers, Broncos, Browns, Cardinals and Chiefs. The best of them is Week 5 at New England on Sunday, October 11, when the Patriots wear their red Pat Patriot throwback. Week 3 at New Orleans, where the Saints have confirmed black, is a close second.

This is the better of the two, and it is the uniform most of the franchise's best-known photos were taken in. Black numbers on white give the jersey real contrast, and the silver pants and helmet finally have something light to sit next to, so the uniform reads as three clean pieces instead of one dark one. Against a colored opponent it is one of the sharpest road looks in the league.

It grades a B+, the top mark in the closet. The white jersey is where silver and black looks the way the reputation says it does.

## The Bottom Line on the Raiders Jersey Rankings

The Raiders jerseys rank by how much room each one gives the silver. White does it better and grades a B+, and black, the more famous of the two, grades a B.

Two jerseys and no plans for a third is a deliberate choice, and a defensible one. The uniform does not need more pieces. It needs the white jersey on the field more often.
