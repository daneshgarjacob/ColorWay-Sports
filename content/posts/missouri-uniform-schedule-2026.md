Wondering what jersey Missouri is wearing this week? This is the full Mizzou 2026 uniform schedule, game by game, and it comes with the date every Tigers fan is asking about: **the Memorial Stadium 100th anniversary throwback uniform debuts Saturday, September 19, against Troy at Faurot Field**.

## The 100th Anniversary Throwback Is the Headline

Memorial Stadium turns 100 in 2026, and Mizzou built a uniform for it rather than a patch. The set is a 1970s throwback, and it is the most restrained thing the program has put on the field in a decade.

What makes it work is what is missing:

- **No wordmark on the chest.** No "Missouri," no "Mizzou," just numbers. That is the nod to everything the program wore before the 1980s, and it is the single decision the whole uniform rests on.
- **A black helmet with the block M** and a gold and white stripe running down the center.
- **The same gold and white striping on the shoulders**, carried down onto the sleeves the way the 1970s jerseys did.
- **All-gold pants**, which is the piece that turns a black jersey into a throwback instead of a blackout.
- **The block M at the base of the collar**, a reference to the Rock M built into the north end zone in 1927.
- **"STP" sewn into the back collar**, for the "Something To Prove" mantra the staff has used since 2023.

### Our Grade: B-

The old-timey look is genuinely well executed. It just does not give us Missouri.

Start with what works. Taking the wordmark off a college football jersey is a real risk, and the chest does not look empty here, because the shoulder striping does the identifying work. Gold and white bands on a black sleeve is one of the oldest tricks in the sport and it still reads from the upper deck, which is more than most modern alternates manage. Tying the block M at the collar to the Rock M that has sat in the north end zone since 1927 is a real connection to the building being honored rather than a manufactured backstory.

The problem is the palette. Black with a muted old-gold and silver-grey trim is handsome and it is also bland, and it is the same well roughly a dozen programs go to when they want to look vintage. Missouri's identity is a brighter, harder gold than this. Wash that out and you are left with a uniform that could belong to half the country.

The all-gold pants are the one place the set gets loud, and they are carrying more weight than they should have to. If the jersey striping had been pulled toward that same brighter gold, the whole thing would read as Mizzou instead of as a nice generic throwback.

A centennial deserves the loudest version of a team's own colors. This is the quietest.

## How Missouri's Uniform Rotation Actually Works

Missouri does not publish a season-long uniform schedule. Under Nike the Tigers rotate black, gold, white and anthracite pieces and announce each week's combination a few days before kickoff, usually on Thursday or Friday.

The reliable rules:

- **Home games at Faurot Field:** black or gold jerseys, decided game by game.
- **Road games:** white jerseys, per NCAA visitor rules.
- **September 19 vs. Troy:** the confirmed 100th anniversary throwback.
- **Anthracite** shows up once or twice a year, almost always in a night game.

We update this page as each week's combination is announced, so the grid below always reflects the latest confirmed look.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Missouri wearing this week?**

Mizzou announces each week's combination a few days before kickoff, usually Thursday or Friday. This page is updated as each one is confirmed. Home games are black or gold, road games are white.

**When does Missouri wear the 100th anniversary throwback uniform?**

Saturday, September 19, at home against Troy, for the Memorial Stadium 100th Anniversary Game.

**What does the Missouri throwback uniform look like?**

Black helmet with the block M and a gold and white center stripe, a black jersey with gold and white shoulder and sleeve striping and no chest wordmark, and all-gold pants. It is modeled on what Mizzou wore in the 1970s.

**What does STP mean on the Missouri jersey?**

"Something To Prove," the team mantra introduced by head coach Eli Drinkwitz in 2023. It is sewn into the back collar of the throwback jersey.

**Who makes Missouri's uniforms?**

Nike. A throwback-inspired Nike merchandise line launched alongside the uniform reveal.

## The Bottom Line

Missouri's 2026 uniform schedule has one date that matters: September 19 against Troy, when the Memorial Stadium 100th anniversary throwback gets its night. We grade it a B-. The 1970s construction is right and the empty chest is a brave, correct call, but the palette is washed out enough that it reads as a generic vintage set rather than a Missouri one. Around it, expect the usual Columbia rhythm, black or gold at home and white on the road, with each combination announced days out and logged here when it is.
