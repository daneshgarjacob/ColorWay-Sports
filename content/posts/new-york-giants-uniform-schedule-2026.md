## The Legacy Throwback (Week 4, October 4, Confirmed)

New York wears the Legacy uniform against Arizona at 1:00 p.m. Eastern on CBS, and the occasion is specific: it is forty years since the **Super Bowl XXI** win over Denver. Members of the 1986 team reunite for a halftime ceremony, and the first 25,000 fans through the gates get a Mark Bavaro bobblehead.

The uniform is the one that team actually wore. A navy helmet carrying the underlined **GIANTS** wordmark on the sides with a white facemask, a royal blue jersey with classic striping at the collar and cuffs, and white pants with red and blue stripes.

It is the best thing in the Giants' closet and it is not particularly close. The current primary set is fine, but the Legacy look has the two things the modern one gave up: a wordmark helmet instead of a logo helmet, and stripes that actually resolve at distance. Wordmark helmets have almost vanished from the league, which is exactly why this one still reads as distinct rather than nostalgic.

Pairing it with the fortieth anniversary of the Parcells-Simms-Taylor team is the correct use of a throwback. The uniform and the ceremony are the same story, which is not something every team manages when it digs into the archive.

### Will the Legacy Set Come Back a Second Time?

Possibly. League rules let a club combine alternate and throwback wearings across a handful of games in a season, and the Giants have historically used the Legacy uniform for more than one date when they schedule Legacy Games.

Only October 4 has been announced. If a second date is added we will put it on the grid the day it lands, and we are not going to guess at one in the meantime.

## Royal Blue at MetLife (8 Games)

The blue jersey takes every home date except the throwback: Dallas in the Week 1 opener, the Titans in Week 3, New Orleans in Week 6, Washington on Thursday night in Week 10, Jacksonville in Week 11, San Francisco in Week 13, Cleveland in Week 15, and Philadelphia to close the season in Week 18.

Five of those nine home games are against NFC East opponents or genuine national draws, which is a good year for a uniform that photographs well under lights. The royal blue and red combination has been essentially untouched since the 2000s and does not need help.

## White on the Road (7 Games, Plus One Open)

Weeks 2, 5, 7, 9, 12, 14 and 16. The Week 7 trip to Houston is worth noting because the Texans have confirmed **Battle Red** for that game, per our [Texans 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/texans-uniform-schedule-2026), which puts the Giants in white against one of the loudest home uniforms in the league.

## The Week 17 Dallas Wrinkle

Dallas is the one club in the NFL that wears white at home by tradition, which means a trip to AT&T Stadium is the rare road date where the visiting team can wear a coloured jersey.

That makes **Week 17 at Dallas** the single most open game on the Giants' calendar. New York could wear royal blue there, and blue-on-white in that building is one of the better looks the division produces. Nothing has been announced either way, so the grid above leaves it open rather than assuming. Our [Cowboys 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/cowboys-uniform-schedule-2026) has Dallas's side of it.

## Is the Century Red Uniform Coming Back?

No. This question comes up constantly and the answer is that Century Red belonged to the **2024** season, the Giants' 100th, and articles about it still surface in search results as though they were current. They are not. There is no 2026 Century Red game, and nothing about that uniform has been announced for this season.

## How the Giants Rotation Works in 2026

1. **Royal blue at home, white on the road.**
2. **One throwback game confirmed**, the Legacy set in Week 4 against Arizona.
3. **Week 17 at Dallas is genuinely open**, because Dallas wears white at home.
4. **No Century Red.** That was 2024.
5. **Pants and helmet combinations** are a week-of call, as they are for every club.

## The Bottom Line

The Giants have one of the most stable wardrobes in the league and one genuinely great alternate, and this year they are using it for the right reason on the right weekend. A fortieth-anniversary ceremony for the 1986 team, in the uniform the 1986 team wore, with the men who wore it standing on the field at halftime.

The rest of the season is royal blue and white and does not need to be anything else. If there is an argument to be made, it is that the Legacy set is better than the primary and should be seen more than once. October 4 is the only date on the books.
