**Final: Steelers 16, Saints 10.** Pittsburgh changed its pants for Paris and won. Against the New Orleans Saints at the Stade de France on **Sunday, October 25** (Week 7), the Steelers wore the **black helmet, white jersey and black pants** with a gold stripe, plus black socks, in front of 78,488 fans in the NFL's first regular-season game in France. The Saints, the designated home team, wore **black jerseys, gold helmets and gold pants**. The team posted the Pittsburgh look the morning before with two words: "Tomorrow's kit."

## How It Looked in Paris

It looked sharp, and it came with a win. The Steelers beat the Saints **16-10** at the Stade de France on Sunday, October 25, in the black helmet, white jersey, **black pants** with the gold stripe and black socks, exactly the kit the team posted on Saturday. ESPN's game photos show Aaron Rodgers in it on his late scramble: white No. 8 with the black and gold sleeve stripes, black pants with the gold stripe down the leg. New Orleans wore its black home jersey with gold numbers, the gold helmet and **gold pants**, so the field read black and gold on one side and white and black on the other.

### Steelers vs Saints in Paris: What Happened

- **Final score:** Steelers 16, Saints 10. Pittsburgh is 5-2 with three straight wins; New Orleans fell to 2-5.
- **The touchdown:** Rodgers hit tight end **Darnell Washington** from 4 yards out for a 10-3 lead, set up by a 51-yard catch by DK Metcalf. It was Rodgers' 540th career touchdown pass, moving him past Peyton Manning for third in NFL history. Washington is the same player who told the team site the black-pants look was "unique for us to wear."
- **The turning point:** linebacker Payton Wilson intercepted Tyler Shough in the red zone early in the third quarter, and Chris Boswell's 39-yard field goal made it 16-10. Boswell kicked three field goals in all.
- **The numbers:** Jaylen Warren ran for 101 yards on 18 carries. Rodgers went 19 of 30 for 212 yards. The Saints ran for just 18 yards.
- **Attendance:** 78,488 at the Stade de France.

Scoring and stats are from ESPN's game recap.

## The Paris Look, Piece by Piece

- **Helmet:** the standard black shell with the Steelers logo on one side, unchanged.
- **Jersey:** the white road jersey with black numbers and the gold and black sleeve stripes.
- **Pants:** **black**, with a gold stripe down each leg. Steelers.com describes them as the team's black Color Rush pants.
- **Socks:** black, in the team's photos.
- **Patch:** the team's photos show a shield-shaped NFL Paris patch on the chest, opposite the Steelers logo.

The normal road set is white over **gold pants**, under the black helmet. That is exactly what Pittsburgh wore at Cleveland in Week 4 this season, with black socks, and at New England in Week 2.

## A Short History of Steelers Pants

<img src="/images/posts/steelers-black-pants-white-jerseys-paris-2026/steelers-uniforms-2025-illustration.png" alt="Illustration of the Pittsburgh Steelers uniforms: black home and white away over gold pants, the black alternate with black pants and a gold stripe, and the 1933 throwback with beige pants" style="width:100%;border-radius:12px;margin:1.4em 0 0.8em;background:#ffffff;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align:center;font-size:0.78em;color:#7C8696;margin:-0.4em 0 1.6em;">The Steelers' current closet, home, away, Color Rush alternate and 1933 throwback. Illustration: <a href="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pittsburgh_Steelers_Uniforms_2025.png">DaRealConMan</a> / <a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/">CC BY-SA 4.0</a></p>

- **1968 onward: gold.** The modern design, as [the uniform history on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logos_and_uniforms_of_the_Pittsburgh_Steelers) summarizes it, is gold pants with either the black or the white jersey.
- **1970 and 1971: white.** For those two seasons the Steelers paired white pants with their white jerseys.
- **2007 to 2011: white, striped.** The throwback set of that era came with white pants and a single gold stripe down each leg.
- **2016: black.** The Color Rush uniform introduced black pants with a thick gold stripe, the reverse of the usual gold pants. It debuted on Christmas against Baltimore, as an all-black look.
- **2025: beige.** The 1933 throwback came with beige pants under the gold helmet.

So the black pants are not new. Wearing them under the white jersey is.

## Is It the First Time in Team History?

The team's word is "new," in both its X post and on Steelers.com, and the published history of the team's pants above shows no earlier white-over-black set. A SteelersNOW report carried by Pittsburgh's WPXI calls it "never-before-worn." But neither the team nor the NFL has said "first time in franchise history" in so many words, so we describe it as what the sources support: a combination the Steelers have rarely, if ever, worn. If the team confirms it, we will update this story.

## Our Take

We give it a **B-**. It's a new look for a big game, and the gold stripe down the black pants keeps the team's colors in it. But we prefer the Steelers in gold pants. White over gold is one of the cleanest road looks in the league, and the black pants make this one heavier and darker without making it better. Fun for one game in Paris; we'd keep the gold pants the rest of the way.
