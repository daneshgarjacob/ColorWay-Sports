**Final: Texans 26, Colts 3.** Thursday night was the night the Texans put on a white helmet for the first time. Houston hosted the **Indianapolis Colts** on **Thursday Night Football on Prime Video** in the debut of the **HOUSTON/US** Rivalries uniform, and wore it exactly as announced, confirmed from the game photos: a **white helmet** with a **chrome blue facemask**, a **white jersey** with H-Town Blue numbers and Houston street-tile stripes, **white pants** and white socks, on a new field painted to match. The Colts wore **royal blue** with the white helmet and white pants. How it all looked on the field, with our take, is in [what the Texans wore in the white helmet debut](/stories/texans-white-helmet-houston-us-rivalries-uniform-colts-what-they-wore-2026).

## Quick Answers

- **What did the Texans wear?** The HOUSTON/US Rivalries uniform: white helmet, chrome blue facemask, white jersey with H-Town Blue numbers, white pants, white socks. Worn as announced, confirmed from the game photos.

## What The Texans Wore

HOUSTON/US is Houston's entry in the NFL and Nike Rivalries program, and Thursday was its first game. Every piece, from the team's own description, and all of it matched the game photos:

- **Helmet:** Liberty White, the first white helmet the Texans have ever worn in a game, with a glossy finish. The side carries a white version of the gothic H-Town "H" with a red star, and the center stripe is tiled in red and blue. The front bumper reads "H-Town."
- **Facemask:** chrome, in H-Town Blue. On TV it was the first thing you noticed.
- **Jersey:** white, with the bullhead logo over the heart for the first time in team history. The numbers are H-Town Blue, drawn from the stencil lettering on Houston's "Be Someone" bridge.
- **Stripes:** the sleeves, collar and pants use Houston's blue-and-white street tiles next to a Battle Red stripe.
- **Collar:** a "Houston" wordmark inside, with the "US" picked out in blue. That is where the name comes from.
- **Pants:** white, with white socks, so the look ran white from top to bottom.

The Texans asked fans to wear H-Town Blue to the game. Every detail of the uniform is in our [HOUSTON/US Rivalries uniform story](/stories/texans-houston-us-rivalries-uniform-colts-thursday-night-football-2026).

## What The Colts Wore

The Colts' published season plan had **royal blue** for this road trip, one of only two road games where Indianapolis wears blue. The other was Week 8 at Jacksonville, when the Jaguars wore their own Rivalries uniform. Indianapolis wore it as scheduled, confirmed from ESPN's game photos and the team's own: the **white helmet** with the blue horseshoe, the **royal blue jersey** with white numbers, **white pants** with blue stripes and **royal blue socks**.

## The New H-Town Field

Houston did not stop at the uniform. On Wednesday the Texans posted photos of their grounds crew painting NRG Stadium for the game: the gothic H-Town "H" with a red star at midfield, **white end zones** painted like the city's street-tile mosaics with **"H-TOWN"** and **"TEXANS"** spelled out in blue, and the 25 Seasons logo redone in the same tile style. In the game frames from ESPN and the Texans, the end-zone paint around the goal line showed up as bright H-Town Blue, bluer than the painting photos suggested. Not this season. Thursday was the only HOUSTON/US date on the [Texans 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/texans-uniform-schedule-2026). ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What did the Texans wear on Thursday Night Football?**

The HOUSTON/US Rivalries uniform, for the first time: a white helmet with a chrome blue facemask, a white jersey with H-Town Blue numbers and blue-and-red street-tile stripes, white pants and white socks.

**What did the Colts wear against the Texans?**

Royal blue, as their published schedule had it: the white helmet, royal blue jersey, white pants and royal blue socks, the same look they wore at Jacksonville in Week 8.

**Who won Colts vs. Texans on Thursday night?**

The Texans, 26-3, on Thursday, November 19. C.J. Stroud threw three touchdown passes and Will Anderson Jr. had a safety, per ESPN.

**What channel was the Colts vs. Texans game on?**

Prime Video. It was the Week 11 Thursday Night Football game, at 8:15 p.m. Eastern.

**Why did the Texans wear white helmets?**

It is the helmet of their new Rivalries uniform. The Texans had never worn a white helmet in a game before Thursday. Their regular helmet is dark navy, and their Battle Red games use a red one.

**What is the HOUSTON/US uniform?**

The Texans' entry in the NFL and Nike Rivalries program, revealed on August 25. It is built from Houston itself: the blue street tiles found around the city, numbers based on the "Be Someone" bridge, and a collar wordmark that reads "Houston" with the "US" in blue.

**Why did the Texans have a new field?**

To match the uniform. The midfield logo is the gothic H-Town "H" from the helmet, and the end zones are painted like Houston's street tiles with H-TOWN and TEXANS in the design.

**What logo is on the Texans' white helmet?**

A white version of the gothic H-Town "H" with a red star. The bullhead is on the jersey chest instead, the first time it has sat over the heart.

**Was Thursday the first time the Texans wore the Rivalries uniform?**

Yes. Houston named this game for the debut when it revealed the uniform in August, and won it 26-3.

**How many times will the Texans wear the Rivalries uniform in 2026?**

Once. The Colts game was the only date on the schedule. It stays in the rotation through 2028.

**What did the Colts and Texans wear the first time they played this season?**

In Week 3 in Indianapolis, the Colts debuted their Anvil Strike Rivalries uniform, anthracite with a metallic royal blue helmet, and the Texans wore white jerseys with navy pants. The Colts won 19-17.

**What are the Colts' and Texans' records?**

After Thursday, the Texans are 4-6 and the Colts are 3-8, per ESPN.

**Where can I buy the Texans Rivalries jersey?**

HOUSTON/US jerseys went on sale on September 1 at NFLShop.com, Nike, Fanatics and the Texans' team stores.

**What is the NFL Rivalries program?**

A Nike program that gives teams a city-themed alternate uniform worn at home against a division rival, and kept for three seasons. The AFC South and NFC North got theirs in 2026. Our [Rivalries explainer](/stories/what-is-the-nfl-rivalries-uniform-program) covers how it works.
