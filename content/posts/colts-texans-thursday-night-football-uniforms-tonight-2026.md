**Tonight is the night the Texans put on a white helmet for the first time.** Houston hosts the **Indianapolis Colts** on **Thursday Night Football at 8:15 p.m. Eastern (7:15 p.m. in Houston) on Prime Video**, and it is the debut of the **HOUSTON/US** Rivalries uniform: a **white helmet** with a **chrome blue facemask**, a **white jersey** with H-Town Blue numbers and Houston street-tile stripes, and **white pants**. The Texans painted a new field to match. The Colts are scheduled in **royal blue**.

## What The Texans Are Wearing Tonight

HOUSTON/US is Houston's entry in the NFL and Nike Rivalries program, and tonight is its first game. Every piece, from the team's own description:

- **Helmet:** Liberty White, the first white helmet the Texans have ever worn in a game, with a glossy finish. The side carries a white version of the gothic H-Town "H" with a red star, and the center stripe is tiled in red and blue. The front bumper reads "H-Town."
- **Facemask:** chrome, in H-Town Blue. On TV it is the first thing you will notice.
- **Jersey:** white, with the bullhead logo over the heart for the first time in team history. The numbers are H-Town Blue, drawn from the stencil lettering on Houston's "Be Someone" bridge.
- **Stripes:** the sleeves, collar and pants use Houston's blue-and-white street tiles next to a Battle Red stripe.
- **Collar:** a "Houston" wordmark inside, with the "US" picked out in blue. That is where the name comes from.
- **Pants:** white, so the look runs white from top to bottom.

The Texans have asked fans to wear H-Town Blue to NRG Stadium tonight. Every detail of the uniform is in our [HOUSTON/US Rivalries uniform story](/stories/texans-houston-us-rivalries-uniform-colts-thursday-night-football-2026).

## What The Colts Are Wearing Tonight

The Colts' published season plan has **royal blue** for this road trip, one of only two road games where Indianapolis wears blue. The other was Week 8 at Jacksonville, when the Jaguars wore their own Rivalries uniform and the Colts went white helmet, royal blue jersey and white pants. Indianapolis has not posted a separate uniform graphic for tonight, but its game-week Thursday Night Football graphic on Wednesday shows Jonathan Taylor in the royal blue jersey. We will update this once the team posts its look before kickoff.

Houston did not stop at the uniform. ## How Each Team Has Done In These Looks

From our [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026), which logs what every team actually wore, through Week 10:

- **Texans in HOUSTON/US: 0-0.** Tonight is the first game. Houston is 3-6 overall and has worn white in every game since Week 3 except the Week 7 Battle Red game against the Giants.
- **Colts in royal blue: 1-3.** Indianapolis has worn the white helmet, royal blue jersey and white pants four times: losses to Baltimore, Tennessee and Jacksonville, and a 24-22 win over Miami last Sunday. The Colts are 3-7 overall.
- **The first meeting:** Week 3 in Indianapolis, where the Colts debuted their own Rivalries uniform, Anvil Strike, and won 19-17. The Texans wore white jerseys with navy pants that day. This is the second time this season these two teams meet with one side in a brand-new Rivalries look.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the Texans wearing tonight?**

The HOUSTON/US Rivalries uniform, for the first time: a white helmet with a chrome blue facemask, a white jersey with H-Town Blue numbers and blue-and-red street-tile stripes, and white pants.

**What are the Colts wearing tonight?**

Royal blue. The Colts put royal blue on their published schedule for this road game, and their game-week graphic shows Jonathan Taylor in it. The white helmet and white pants are expected, the same look they wore at Jacksonville in Week 8.

**What time is Colts vs. Texans tonight?**

8:15 p.m. Eastern, 7:15 p.m. Central, Thursday, November 19, at NRG Stadium in Houston.

**What channel is the Colts vs. Texans game on?**

Prime Video. It is the Week 11 Thursday Night Football game.

**Why are the Texans wearing white helmets?**

It is the helmet of their new Rivalries uniform. The Texans have never worn a white helmet in a game before tonight. Their regular helmet is dark navy, and their Battle Red games use a red one.

**What is the HOUSTON/US uniform?**

The Texans' entry in the NFL and Nike Rivalries program, revealed on August 25. It is built from Houston itself: the blue street tiles found around the city, numbers based on the "Be Someone" bridge, and a collar wordmark that reads "Houston" with the "US" in blue.

**Why do the Texans have a new field tonight?**

To match the uniform. The midfield logo is the gothic H-Town "H" from the helmet, and the end zones are painted like Houston's street tiles with H-TOWN and TEXANS in blue letters.

**What logo is on the Texans' white helmet?**

A white version of the gothic H-Town "H" with a red star. The bullhead is on the jersey chest instead, the first time it has sat over the heart.

**Is tonight the first time the Texans wear the Rivalries uniform?**

Yes. Houston named this game for the debut when it revealed the uniform in August.

**How many times will the Texans wear the Rivalries uniform in 2026?**

Once. Tonight is the only date on the schedule. It stays in the rotation through 2028.

**What did the Colts and Texans wear the first time they played this season?**

In Week 3 in Indianapolis, the Colts debuted their Anvil Strike Rivalries uniform, anthracite with a metallic royal blue helmet, and the Texans wore white jerseys with navy pants. The Colts won 19-17.

**What are the Colts' and Texans' records?**

The Texans are 3-6 and the Colts are 3-7 going into tonight, per ESPN.

**Where can I buy the Texans Rivalries jersey?**

HOUSTON/US jerseys went on sale on September 1 at NFLShop.com, Nike, Fanatics and the Texans' team stores.

**What is the NFL Rivalries program?**

A Nike program that gives teams a city-themed alternate uniform worn at home against a division rival, and kept for three seasons. The AFC South and NFC North got theirs in 2026. Our [Rivalries explainer](/stories/what-is-the-nfl-rivalries-uniform-program) covers how it works.
