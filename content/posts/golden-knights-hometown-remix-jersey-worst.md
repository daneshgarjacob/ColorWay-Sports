Of all 32 Hometown Remix jerseys the NHL and Fanatics revealed on Monday, the Vegas Golden Knights' is the worst, and it is not close. We ranked it [dead last of 32](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-jerseys-ranked). It is one of the ugliest sweaters the league has put on the ice in years.

## What the Golden Knights' Hometown Remix Jersey Looks Like

Vegas built the sweater around the casino floor. The base is black. The knight shield sits on the chest in metallic gold thread inside an ornate jade frame. The shoulders are a jade yoke with a felt-table texture and the four card suits. The sleeves carry red-and-black bands cut like a roulette wheel, plus gold stripes and a "Vegas" wordmark. The numbers use a playing-card typeface, and "Since 2017" sits inside the hem.

## Why It Does Not Work

Every one of those ideas is on the sweater at the same time. Felt, cards, roulette and gold are four different themes, and the jersey tries to show all of them at once. The jade fights with the gold, the red roulette bands fight with the jade, and the black base turns the whole thing muddy from a distance. Up close it is a theme park. From the upper deck it is a mess.

It also walks away from what Vegas already does well. The Golden Knights' gold-and-steel look is one of the cleanest identities any expansion team has built. This sweater buries all of that under a costume.

The best Hometown Remix jerseys do the opposite: one idea, one strong color, a big logo in the middle of the chest. That is why [Pittsburgh's pop-art sweater](/stories/nhl-hometown-remix-jerseys-ranked) finished first and Vegas finished last.

## When Vegas Wears It

The Golden Knights wear the Hometown Remix jersey five times. It debuts Sunday, Nov. 15 against Vancouver, then returns Dec. 21 vs. Carolina, Jan. 28 vs. Buffalo, Feb. 18 vs. Columbus and Mar. 15 vs. Winnipeg. Every date is on our [Golden Knights uniform schedule](/stories/golden-knights-uniform-schedule-2026-27).
