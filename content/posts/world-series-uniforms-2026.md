What will the teams wear in the 2026 World Series? It is set: the **Tampa Bay Rays vs. the Milwaukee Brewers**. The Rays beat the Guardians 7-3 in Game 6 of the ALCS on Monday night at Tropicana Field to win the pennant 4-2, wearing the white Devil Rays throwback. The Brewers swept the Dodgers 4-0 in the NLCS for their first World Series since 1982 and their first as a National League team. Game 1 is **Friday, October 23 at American Family Field** at 8:00 p.m. ET on FOX. Below is every game date and time, which games are under the Tropicana Field roof, the 2026 World Series patch, what both teams have worn in every playoff game this October with their record in each jersey, and what we expect game by game. We confirm both jerseys at first pitch of every game.

**Why the Rays look like the 1998 Devil Rays this October:** the rainbow TAMPA BAY road gray, the Friday throwback and what is left of the sunburst, in [Are the Rays Becoming the Devil Rays Again?](/stories/rays-devil-rays-comeback-world-series-2026)

## Quick Answers

- **Brewers at home (Games 1, 2, 6, 7):** the cream, expected. They have worn it in all four home playoff games this October and won all four.
- **Brewers on the road at Tropicana Field (Games 3, 4, 5):** the navy alternate with the yellow-front ball-in-glove cap, expected. They have worn it in all four road playoff games and are 3-1 in it.
- **Rays on the road in Milwaukee (Games 1, 2, 6, 7):** the new gray with "TAMPA BAY" in a gradient script, expected. It has been their road jersey in all four road playoff games, and they are 3-1 in it.
- **Rays at home under the Tropicana Field roof (Games 3, 4, 5):** home white or the Devil Rays throwback, our expectation. They are 2-1 in the home white and 2-0 in the throwback this October.
- **The patch:** the 2026 World Series logo goes on the jersey as a small patch high on the chest, and on the side of the cap.
- **Home field:** Milwaukee, with the best record in baseball at 103-59. The Rays went 98-64.

## The 2026 World Series Patch

<img src="/images/posts/mlb-postseason-logos-ranked/world-series-2026.jpg" alt="2026 World Series logo, a white cursive World Series script on navy with the MLB logo atop a gold diamond, Capital One and a gold 2026 banner" style="width: 100%; max-width: 420px; display: block; margin: 1.5em auto 0; border-radius: 10px;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">The 2026 World Series logo. Image via MLB.</p>

The 2026 World Series logo is a white cursive "World Series" script on a navy diamond trimmed in gold, with the MLB logo at the top, Capital One under the script and a gold 2026 banner below. It is nearly identical to the 2025 mark, the first time the logo has repeated in back-to-back years since 1998 and 1999. We ranked every one in our [World Series logo history](/stories/world-series-logo-history-1986-2025).

**Where the patch goes on the jersey.** Since 2023, the World Series patch has been a small mark high on the chest, not on the sleeve. MLB moved it when sleeve sponsor patches arrived, so the Brewers keep their Northwestern Mutual patch on the sleeve. The patch only goes on for the two pennant winners, starting with Game 1.

**On the cap.** Every playoff team has worn the postseason logo on the right side of its New Era cap all October. In the World Series, the two teams switch to the World Series logo there.

**Is there a gold World Series uniform?** No. Nothing gold has been announced for this World Series. The gold-trimmed jerseys you may remember belong to the champion the following season, like the Dodgers' gold trim after their titles. The winner here would get that treatment in 2027.

**In Milwaukee: the new gray, expected.** The gray jersey with "TAMPA BAY" in a gradient wordmark, worn with the navy "TB" gradient cap, debuted September 22 and has been the Rays' road jersey for all four road playoff games. They won three of them, including Games 4 and 5 in Cleveland that turned the ALCS. That is a change: the navy alternate was their everyday road jersey in the regular season, and it has not been worn once this October.

**At Tropicana Field: home white or the Devil Rays, our expectation.** The home whites with the sunburst "Rays" script opened both series at home and are 2-1. The 1998 Devil Rays rainbow throwback has been worn twice, in ALCS Games 2 and 6, and the Rays won both. The full set is on the [Rays uniform schedule](/stories/rays-uniform-schedule-2026).

## Will the Rays Wear the Devil Rays Throwback in the World Series?

We think it is likely at least once, but the Rays have not announced anything, so this is our expectation, not a team call. Here is the evidence. The throwback is 2-0 this postseason: a 3-1 win over Cleveland in ALCS Game 2 and the 7-3 pennant clincher in Game 6. Tampa Bay went back to it for the biggest home game of its season, so the club clearly sees it as a winning playoff look. It is also the Rays' fan favorite, the regular Friday home jersey since 2023.

The wrinkle is the calendar. The Rays' home games in this Series fall on Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday (Games 3, 4 and 5), not a Friday, and they opened both home series this October in the home whites. Our read: home white is the safer bet for Game 3, and the Devil Rays throwback is the strongest candidate for Game 4 or a Game 5. It is a home jersey, so we do not expect it in Milwaukee. We will confirm the call before first pitch of every game at the Trop.

## Rays vs. Brewers: The Uniform Matchup

On paper, this is a great uniform World Series. Milwaukee's navy and gold against Tampa Bay's navy and Columbia blue are two of the cleanest color sets in baseball, and both teams arrive with a throwback story: the Brewers' ball-in-glove logo on every cap, and the Rays' 1998 Devil Rays rainbow. It also puts a powder blue road jersey (Milwaukee's, unused so far this October) and a Columbia blue team in the same Series.

**Games 1 and 2 in Milwaukee, expected:** Brewers cream with the all-navy ball-in-glove cap vs. the Rays' new "TAMPA BAY" gray. Cream against gray is a softer contrast than white against gray, but the gold on the Brewers' script and the gradient on the Rays' wordmark keep it from washing out.

**Games 3, 4 and 5 at the Trop, expected:** Brewers navy with the yellow-front cap vs. the Rays' home white, with the Devil Rays throwback as the wild card. Navy against the rainbow throwback would be the most colorful matchup of the Series.

**Games 6 and 7 back in Milwaukee, expected:** the same cream vs. gray as Games 1 and 2.

## The ALCS in Brief: How the Rays Got Here

The Guardians won ALCS Game 1 7-3 in St. Petersburg and led the series 2-1 after a 3-2 win at Progressive Field in their red alternate. Then the Rays took over: 7-3 in Game 4 (a day late after a Friday rainout) and 3-1 in Game 5, both in Cleveland in the new gray, then 7-3 in Game 6 at home in the Devil Rays throwback, with the Guardians in their navy alternate for the first time all series. Every game is logged on the [ALCS uniform guide](/stories/mlb-championship-series-uniforms-2026).

The Brewers' only other World Series was 1982, as an American League team, and they lost to the Cardinals in seven games. Their home uniform that year was a white pinstriped pullover with "Brewers" across the chest and the ball-in-glove cap, the look their current pinstripe alternate echoes. On the road they wore powder blue, and so did the Cardinals. That is why the new powder blue road jersey is the one fans most want to see this year.

## The Bottom Line

The Brewers have been the most predictable team in October: cream at home, navy on the road, 7-1. The Rays have been nearly as steady on the road, wearing the new "TAMPA BAY" gray in every road game, and have picked between home white and the Devil Rays throwback at the Trop. We expect cream vs. gray in Milwaukee and navy vs. Rays white or the throwback in St. Petersburg, all with the 2026 World Series patch on the chest and cap. The open questions are when the Devil Rays come out and whether Milwaukee's powder blue finally gets a playoff night 44 years after the last one. We log every game, both jerseys, on the [MLB uniform tracker](/stories/mlb-uniform-tracker-2026).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Who is playing in the 2026 World Series?**

The Tampa Bay Rays and the Milwaukee Brewers. The Rays beat the Guardians 4-2 in the ALCS and the Brewers swept the Dodgers 4-0 in the NLCS.

**When is Game 1 of the 2026 World Series?**

Friday, October 23, at American Family Field in Milwaukee, at 8:00 p.m. ET on FOX, FOX Deportes and FOX One. Game 2 is Saturday, October 24, also in Milwaukee.

**What are the Rays wearing in the World Series?**

We expect the new gray with "TAMPA BAY" in a gradient script in Milwaukee (Games 1, 2, 6 and 7), the same road jersey they wore in all four road playoff games, going 3-1. At Tropicana Field (Games 3, 4 and 5) we expect the home white or the Devil Rays throwback. Those are our projections; we confirm each game at first pitch.

**What are the Brewers wearing in Game 1?**

The cream home jersey with the all-navy ball-in-glove cap, expected. The Brewers have worn the cream in all four home playoff games this October and won all four.

**What are the Brewers wearing in the World Series?**

We expect the cream at home for Games 1, 2, 6 and 7 and the navy alternate with the yellow-front ball-in-glove cap on the road for Games 3, 4 and 5. That is exactly what they wore in all eight playoff games this October.

**Will the Rays wear the Devil Rays throwback in the World Series?**

Nothing has been announced. We expect it at least once at Tropicana Field, since the Rays won both games they wore it in this postseason, ALCS Games 2 and 6. It is a home jersey, so we do not expect it in Milwaukee.

**Is the World Series under a roof?**

At least Games 3, 4 and 5 are, because Tropicana Field is a fixed dome. American Family Field's roof is retractable, and on cold late-October nights it is likely to be closed.

**What is the Rays' record in each uniform this postseason?**

3-1 in the new "TAMPA BAY" road gray, 2-1 in the home white and 2-0 in the Devil Rays throwback, for a 7-2 record overall.

**What did the Rays wear in ALCS Game 6?**

The white Devil Rays throwback, the same jersey they wore in their Game 2 win. The Guardians wore their navy alternate. The Rays won 7-3 to clinch the pennant.

**Will the Brewers wear powder blue in the World Series?**

It is possible but has not happened yet. Powder blue was their most-worn road jersey in the regular season, but they have worn the navy alternate in all four postseason road games.

**Do the Brewers wear the City Connect in the playoffs?**

No. The "Wisco" City Connect has not been worn this October, and we have not found any team wearing a City Connect in a postseason game.

**What is the 2026 World Series patch?**

A white cursive "World Series" script on a navy, gold-trimmed diamond with the MLB logo, Capital One and a gold 2026 banner. It is nearly identical to the 2025 logo. It goes on the jersey as a small patch high on the chest and on the side of the cap.

**Where is the World Series patch on the jersey?**

On the chest. Since 2023, MLB has put a smaller World Series patch high on the front of the jersey because the sleeve now carries the team's sponsor patch.

**Who has home-field advantage in the 2026 World Series?**

The Brewers. Their 103-59 record was the best in baseball, so Games 1, 2, 6 and 7 are in Milwaukee.

**What did the Brewers wear in the 1982 World Series?**

White pinstriped pullovers at home and powder blue on the road, with the ball-in-glove cap. They lost to the Cardinals in seven games.

**Have the Brewers or the Rays ever won the World Series?**

No. The Brewers' only other appearance was 1982. The Rays lost in 2008 to the Phillies and in 2020 to the Dodgers, so one of these franchises will win its first title.
