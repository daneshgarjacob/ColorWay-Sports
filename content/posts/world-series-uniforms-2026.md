What will the teams wear in the 2026 World Series? The **Milwaukee Brewers** are in, after sweeping the Dodgers 4-0 in the NLCS. It is their first World Series since 1982 and their first as a National League team. Their opponent comes out of the ALCS, where the **Cleveland Guardians lead the Tampa Bay Rays 2-1**. Game 1 is **Friday, October 23 at American Family Field** on FOX. Below is every game date, the 2026 World Series patch, what all three teams have worn in every playoff game this October, and what we expect in Milwaukee and on the road. We confirm both jerseys at first pitch of every game.

## Quick Answers

- **Brewers at home (Games 1, 2, 6, 7):** the cream, expected. They are 4 for 4 in cream at home this October and won all four.
- **Brewers on the road (Games 3, 4, 5):** the navy alternate with the yellow-front ball-in-glove cap, expected. They are 4 for 4 in navy on the road.
- **Guardians, if they win the pennant:** road gray in Milwaukee. They are 4 for 4 in gray on the road this postseason.
- **Rays, if they win the pennant:** the new "TAMPA BAY" gradient gray in Milwaukee. It is 2 for 2 on the road this postseason.
- **The patch:** the 2026 World Series logo goes on the jersey as a small patch high on the chest, and on the side of the cap.
- **Home field:** Milwaukee, with the best record in baseball at 103-59.

## The 2026 World Series Patch

<img src="/images/posts/mlb-postseason-logos-ranked/world-series-2026.jpg" alt="2026 World Series logo, a white cursive World Series script on navy with the MLB logo atop a gold diamond, Capital One and a gold 2026 banner" style="width: 100%; max-width: 420px; display: block; margin: 1.5em auto 0; border-radius: 10px;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">The 2026 World Series logo. Image via MLB.</p>

The 2026 World Series logo is a white cursive "World Series" script on a navy diamond trimmed in gold, with the MLB logo at the top, Capital One under the script and a gold 2026 banner below. It is nearly identical to the 2025 mark, the first time the logo has repeated in back-to-back years since 1998 and 1999. We ranked every one in our [World Series logo history](/stories/world-series-logo-history-1986-2025).

**Where the patch goes on the jersey.** Since 2023, the World Series patch has been a small mark high on the chest, not on the sleeve. MLB moved it when sleeve sponsor patches arrived, so the Brewers keep their Northwestern Mutual patch on the sleeve and the Guardians keep Marathon. The patch only goes on for the two pennant winners, starting with Game 1.

**On the cap.** Every playoff team has worn the postseason logo on the right side of its New Era cap all October. In the World Series, the two teams switch to the World Series logo there.

**Is there a gold World Series uniform?** No. Nothing gold has been announced for this World Series. The gold-trimmed jerseys you may remember belong to the champion the following season, like the Dodgers' gold trim after their titles. The winner here would get that treatment in 2027.

## If It Is Rays vs. Brewers: The Best Uniform Matchup, Under a Roof

On paper, a Rays-Brewers World Series is the better uniform matchup. Milwaukee's navy and gold against Tampa Bay's navy and Columbia blue would be two of the cleanest color sets in baseball, with the new "TAMPA BAY" gray in Milwaukee and a chance at the Devil Rays rainbow throwback at the Trop. It would also put a powder blue road jersey and a Columbia blue team in the same Series.

The catch is the roof. Tropicana Field is a fixed dome, so Games 3, 4 and 5 would be played indoors no matter what. American Family Field has a retractable roof, and the Brewers generally want it to be 60 degrees or warmer before they open it, so cold October nights in Milwaukee would likely keep it shut. That could make this an entire World Series played indoors. A Guardians-Brewers Series would at least send three games outdoors to Progressive Field. We post the roof call for every Milwaukee game on our [American Family Field roof status](/stories/american-family-field-roof-open-brewers-2026) page.

## The Last Time: Brewers in the 1982 World Series

<img src="/images/posts/world-series-uniforms-2026/brewers-1982-yount-pinstripes.jpg" alt="Robin Yount in the Brewers' pinstriped home pullover and ball-in-glove cap in a 1982 team portrait" style="width:100%;max-width:420px;border-radius:10px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto 0;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">Robin Yount, 1982 Milwaukee Brewers team photo. Public domain.</p>

The Brewers' only other World Series was 1982, as an American League team, and they lost to the Cardinals in seven games. Their home uniform that year was a white pinstriped pullover with "Brewers" across the chest and the ball-in-glove cap, the look their current pinstripe alternate echoes. On the road they wore powder blue, and so did the Cardinals. That is why the new powder blue road jersey is the one fans most want to see this year.

## The Bottom Line

The Brewers have been the most predictable team in October: cream at home, navy on the road, 7-1. We expect the same in the World Series, with the 2026 World Series patch on the chest and on the cap. The open questions are whether Cleveland keeps riding the red at home, whether the Rays' new gray gets its biggest stage, and whether Milwaukee's powder blue finally gets a playoff night 44 years after the last one. We log every game, both jerseys, on the [MLB uniform tracker](/stories/mlb-uniform-tracker-2026) and the [ALCS uniform guide](/stories/mlb-championship-series-uniforms-2026).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the Brewers wearing in the World Series?**

We expect the cream at home for Games 1, 2, 6 and 7 and the navy alternate with the yellow-front ball-in-glove cap on the road for Games 3, 4 and 5. That is exactly what they wore in all eight playoff games this October. We confirm each game at first pitch.

**Will the Brewers wear powder blue in the World Series?**

It is possible but has not happened yet. Powder blue was their most-worn road jersey in the regular season, but they have worn the navy alternate in all four postseason road games.

**Do the Brewers wear the City Connect in the playoffs?**

No. The "Wisco" City Connect has not been worn this October, and we have not found any team wearing a City Connect in a postseason game.

**What is the 2026 World Series patch?**

A white cursive "World Series" script on a navy, gold-trimmed diamond with the MLB logo, Capital One and a gold 2026 banner. It is nearly identical to the 2025 logo. It goes on the jersey as a small patch high on the chest and on the side of the cap.

**Where is the World Series patch on the jersey?**

On the chest. Since 2023, MLB has put a smaller World Series patch high on the front of the jersey because the sleeve now carries the team's sponsor patch.

**Who has home-field advantage in the 2026 World Series?**

The Brewers. Their 103-59 record was the best in baseball, so Games 1, 2, 6 and 7 are in Milwaukee.

**When does the 2026 World Series start, and what channel is it on?**

Friday, October 23, in Milwaukee, on FOX, FOX Deportes and FOX One. A Game 7 would be Saturday, October 31.

**What did the Brewers wear in the 1982 World Series?**

White pinstriped pullovers at home and powder blue on the road, with the ball-in-glove cap. They lost to the Cardinals in seven games.

**Have the Brewers ever won the World Series?**

No. 1982 was their only other appearance.
