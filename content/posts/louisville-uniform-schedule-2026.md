adidas makes all of it. The base red and white sets share one template: an arched LOUISVILLE wordmark, black-outlined numerals, and a shoulder stripe that stacks red, white and black, the three colors the program actually owns. The only announced 2026 alternate is For The Ville, the third Louisville uniform built around Muhammad Ali, a Louisville native, after the all-white robe-inspired set of 2019 and the red and gold Rumble in the Jungle look of 2024. It is also the most divisive: the red is gone entirely, replaced by antique gold, which is why we gave it an F in our [2026 college football blackout uniform rankings](/stories/college-football-blackout-uniforms-2026-ranked). ACC home teams wear color and visitors wear white, so every road date is predictable and the home dates come down to red or a special.

**September 6 vs. Ole Miss, Nissan Stadium, Nashville.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **white helmet, white jersey, white pants**. The helmet carried the red Cardinal head and a red facemask, the jersey had LOUISVILLE and the numerals in red with black outlines, and the pants had a red side stripe with the Cardinal head on the hip. Ole Miss was the designated home team for the Music City Kickoff, a Sunday night game, and wore navy over gray, so the all-white look was the visitor's default. No. 9 Ole Miss won 41-38 on a 48-yard field goal by Lucas Carneiro after Louisville had led 13-10 in the third quarter. The Rebels' side of the matchup is on the [Ole Miss 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/ole-miss-uniform-schedule-2026).

**September 11 vs. Villanova.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **white Salute to Service helmet, red jersey, white pants**. The home opener at L&N Federal Credit Union Stadium fell on the 25th anniversary of September 11, and the helmet swapped the Cardinal head for a Cards script and a center stripe filled with the American flag, with a red facemask. The jersey was the standard red home top. The program's own post called it "Stars & stripes for our Salute to Service game." Louisville won 59-13, and it was a Friday night kickoff at 7 p.m. Eastern.

**September 19 vs. SMU.** Red, announced. Louisville posted the combination under ["Keeping it classic"](https://x.com/LouisvilleFB/status/2100585568203837775): white helmet, red jersey, white pants. It is the standard home look for a 3:30 p.m. Eastern kickoff on ESPN2, against the same opponent Louisville faced in the red and gold Rumble in the Jungle Ali set in 2024. We will star the cell once we see it on the field.

**September 26 vs. Wake Forest.** Red expected. A noon kickoff and the ACC opener at home.

**October 3 at NC State.** White expected. First ACC road game, at Carter-Finley Stadium, with the kickoff time still to be set.

**October 9 vs. Florida State.** Red expected. A Friday night game on ESPN at 7 p.m. Eastern, and the biggest home stage before Halloween. If Louisville adds a surprise helmet this fall, a Friday night national TV slot is a natural place for it. Nothing has been announced. Florida State's plan is on the [Florida State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/florida-state-uniform-schedule-2026).

**October 17 at Syracuse.** White expected. Inside the JMA Wireless Dome.

**October 24.** Bye.

**October 31 vs. Stanford.** For The Ville expected. This is the one special date the program has announced: Louisville Football said the uniform "will make its on-field debut Oct. 31 against Stanford." Black jersey and pants, the oversized white wordmark, gold stripes and gold hip circles, and red gloves that are meant to read as boxing gloves.

**November 7 at Georgia Tech.** White expected. Georgia Tech hosts in gold at Bobby Dodd Stadium, per the [Georgia Tech 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/georgia-tech-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 14 at North Carolina.** White expected. It is homecoming at Kenan Stadium, and Carolina blue is expected for the Tar Heels on the [North Carolina 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/north-carolina-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 21 vs. Pittsburgh.** Red expected. Senior Day and the final home game.

**November 28 at Kentucky.** White expected. The Governor's Cup is at Kroger Field this year, so Kentucky wears blue and Louisville comes in white. The Wildcats' side is on the [Kentucky 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/kentucky-uniform-schedule-2026).

## The Bottom Line

Two games in, Louisville has shown both sides of its base closet: all white in Nashville and red at home under a one-off flag helmet for the 9/11 anniversary. The rest of the calendar is simple. Red is the default for the other four home dates, white covers all five road trips, and the black For The Ville set is locked in for Stanford on Halloween. That game is the one to watch, because it is the first time Louisville takes the field with no red on the uniform at all. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Louisville wearing this week?**

Louisville hosts SMU on Saturday, September 19, in an announced classic home look: white helmet, red jersey and white pants. Last week against Villanova the Cardinals wore the red jersey, white pants and a white Salute to Service helmet with a Cards script and an American flag stripe. The grid above lists every game with the expected uniform.

**What are Louisville's football uniforms for 2026?**

A red home jersey with a white arched LOUISVILLE and black-outlined numerals, a white road jersey with the same design in red, white pants, and a white helmet with the red Cardinal head and a red facemask. The one announced alternate is the all-black For The Ville uniform, and a flag-striped Salute to Service helmet appeared for the Villanova game. adidas makes the full set.

**Does Louisville have a new alternate uniform in 2026?**

Yes. The For The Ville uniform, revealed in August, is all black with an oversized white wordmark in the style of Muhammad Ali's Everlast boxing gear, three gold stripes on the collar and pants, gold circles on the hips, and red gloves. It debuts October 31 against Stanford at L&N Federal Credit Union Stadium.

**What did Louisville wear against Ole Miss?**

All white: white helmet with the red Cardinal head and red facemask, white jersey with red lettering outlined in black, and white pants. Ole Miss was the designated home team at Nissan Stadium in Nashville and wore navy, and the Rebels won 41-38 on a late field goal.

**Why did Louisville wear a different helmet against Villanova?**

The September 11 home opener was the program's Salute to Service game on the 25th anniversary of 9/11. For that night Louisville replaced the Cardinal head decal with a Cards script and ran an American flag through the center stripe, paired with the standard red jersey and white pants.

**How many games does Louisville play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: six at L&N Federal Credit Union Stadium, five on the road, and a neutral-site opener against Ole Miss in Nashville, with a bye on October 24. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
