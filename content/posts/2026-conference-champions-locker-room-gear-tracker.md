## Takeaways So Far

**The NBA outclassed the NHL on the hat side.** The Knicks hat puts the next round on the front of the cap. The Knights hat puts the round just won on the front and buries the next round on the back. The Knicks front design earned the front-of-cap real estate the locker room moment matters most for. The Knights front design did not.

**The shirts are basically the same.** Both teams ended up wearing the same recycled Fanatics destination tee template, and both shirts landed at B-. The Knicks shirt earns a slight forward-looking edge for running the "Finals" wordmark in Knicks orange, but neither shirt did real concept work and neither player base wore them on camera nearly as much as the hats.

**Team-color fronts work and should be the default.** The Knights hat is half-right because the gold front matches the primary logo color and reads as a team-first piece. If the NHL or Fanatics moves the Stanley Cup Final logo to that team-color front panel for the next conference champion (or the Stanley Cup champion itself), the back-half of the design idea gets fixed and the grade jumps two full letters. The all-black default template the Knicks hat ran in is the other miss, and the version of the Knicks hat in Knicks orange or blue would have been a clean A.

**Coming up:** The NBA Western Conference Champion (Thunder leading Spurs 3-2, Game 6 in San Antonio) and the NHL Eastern Conference Champion (Hurricanes leading Canadiens 3-1, closeout chance for Game 5 in Raleigh) lock in their respective conferences over the next week. Both rounds end with the same Fanatics locker room hat and t-shirt drop, both teams will be graded here the moment the gear hits the floor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which teams have clinched the 2026 Conference Championships so far?**

The New York Knicks clinched the 2026 NBA Eastern Conference Championship with a Game 4 road sweep of the Cleveland Cavaliers at Rocket Mortgage Fieldhouse, advancing to the NBA Finals for the first time since 1999. The Vegas Golden Knights clinched the 2026 NHL Western Conference Championship with a Game 4 home sweep of the Colorado Avalanche at T-Mobile Arena, advancing to the Stanley Cup Final for the first time since their 2023 Cup win. The NBA Western Conference and the NHL Eastern Conference are still being decided.

**Which conference champion hat got the highest grade so far?**

The New York Knicks 2026 Eastern Conference Champions hat earned an A-, the highest grade on the board so far. The Knicks hat puts the "2026 NBA Finals" wordmark on the front panel alongside the team logo crest, prioritizing the next round over the conference name on the front-of-cap real estate that matters most on the trophy presentation broadcast. Full Knicks hat and shirt breakdown in the [Knicks 2026 Eastern Conference Champions review](/stories/knicks-2026-eastern-conference-champions-hat-shirt-graded).

**Which conference champion hat got the lowest grade so far?**

The Vegas Golden Knights 2026 Western Conference Champions hat earned a C+, the lowest grade on the board so far. The Knights hat runs a Knights gold front panel that matches the team's primary logo color, but the front wordmark stops at "Western Conference Champs" and the 2026 Stanley Cup Final logo is applied to the back of the cap where the broadcast cameras almost never frame it. Full Knights hat and shirt breakdown in the [Knights 2026 Western Conference Champions review](/stories/knights-2026-western-conference-champions-hat-shirt-graded).

**Why are both conference champion locker room shirts graded B-?**

Both the Knicks and Knights conference champion locker room shirts are heather-toned Fanatics destination tees from the same general template that NBA and NHL conference champions have been wearing for years. Neither shirt does real concept work to lift it above the template floor, and the Knicks players and Knights players both defaulted to wearing the locker room hats on camera for the trophy presentations rather than the shirts. The shared B- grade reflects competent execution on a tired template.
