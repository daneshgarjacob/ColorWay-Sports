**Two NFL uniforms have not lost a game in 2026: the Kansas City Chiefs' red jersey with the red helmet and white pants (6-0), and the San Francisco 49ers' scarlet jersey with the gold helmet and gold pants (6-0).** At the other end, the **Miami Dolphins are 0-8 in white**, and three more combinations are 0-4. These numbers come from our own log: ColorWay Sports records the helmet, jersey and pants every NFL team actually wore in every game, from game photos, and pairs it with the ESPN final. Through Thursday night's Chiefs-Bills game, the last of the three on Thanksgiving, that is **167 games and 334 team-games**, with nothing unlogged. We do not know of anyone else who keeps records by full uniform combination.

A combination here means all three pieces. The Bears in a navy jersey with white pants and the Bears in a white jersey with navy pants are two different uniforms, and they have two different records. We only list combinations worn at least three times.

## Quick Answers

- **Which NFL uniforms are undefeated in 2026?** Chiefs red helmet, red jersey, white pants: **6-0**. 49ers gold helmet, scarlet jersey, gold pants: **6-0**. Four more are 3-0: Bills all white, Rams white jersey with royal pants, Vikings white jersey with purple pants, Seahawks all navy.
- **Which NFL uniforms have not won?** Dolphins white jersey: **0-4 with aqua pants and 0-4 with white pants**. Titans light blue jersey with white pants: **0-4**. Buccaneers white jersey with white pants: **0-4**. Steelers white jersey with gold pants: **0-3**.
- **What is the strangest split?** The Seahawks are 3-0 at home in all navy and 0-2 at home in navy with wolf grey pants. The Buccaneers are 2-3 in white with pewter pants and 0-4 in white with white pants. Same jersey, same helmet.
- **What do the Steelers wear best?** Black with gold pants, **5-1**. The all-black Color Rush is **1-0** after a 27-5 win over Denver on Black Friday. Pittsburgh is 5-0 at home.
- **What about the Cowboys?** **1-6** in the white home jersey, **4-1** in anything else, after Thursday's 39-38 loss to the Eagles in the navy Classic throwback.
- **Does helmet color matter?** Teams in white helmets are **22-44**. Teams in navy helmets are **26-13**. That is mostly about which teams wear them.

The more interesting entry is Kansas City's: the Chiefs are **6-0 in red** and **2-3 in everything else**, white on the road included: Thursday night's 13-10 loss at Buffalo came in the red helmet, white jersey and red pants, which drops that road look to 2-2. Navy helmets are 26-13 because the Bears and Broncos are a combined 16-4 in them.

## What This Does And Does Not Prove

A uniform does not win a game. Good teams wear their home uniform at home, where teams win more often, and bad teams wear whatever they wear and lose in it. What a record by uniform does show is pattern: which looks a team trusts for its big games, which pants it pairs with which jersey, and which combinations it keeps going back to after losing. The Seahawks' and Buccaneers' pants splits and the Cowboys' home white are the three we would watch.

## How We Count

Every game is logged in the [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026) from game photos, never from a schedule or a projection, and matched to ESPN's final score. A combination is helmet color, jersey and pants color. Records are through Friday's Broncos-Steelers game (Steelers 27, Broncos 5), so all five Thanksgiving-week games are in them. Every team's full record in every uniform is on our [uniform records page](/uniform-records), and the white-at-home numbers are in [NFL teams wearing white at home](/stories/nfl-teams-wearing-white-at-home-record-2026).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which NFL uniforms are undefeated in 2026?**

Through Black Friday's Broncos-Steelers game: the Chiefs' red helmet, red jersey and white pants (6-0) and the 49ers' gold helmet, scarlet jersey and gold pants (6-0). The Bills' all-white look, the Rams' white jersey with royal pants, the Vikings' white jersey with purple pants and the Seahawks' all navy are each 3-0.

**What uniform have the Dolphins not won in?**

White. Miami is 0-4 in the white jersey with aqua pants and 0-4 in the white jersey with white pants, 0-8 in white overall. Its only win this season came in aqua.

**What is the Chiefs' record in red jerseys in 2026?**

6-0 with the red helmet and white pants: five home games and one at Miami, where the Dolphins took white at home. Kansas City is 2-3 in its other uniforms, including the 13-10 loss at Buffalo on Thanksgiving night in white over red pants.

**What is the Steelers' record in white jerseys this season?**

0-3 with gold pants and 1-0 with black pants. In the black home jersey with gold pants they are 5-1.

**What did the Steelers wear on Black Friday?**

The all-black Color Rush against the Broncos, a combination they had not worn yet in 2026. They won 27-5, so it is 1-0.

**What is the Cowboys' record in their white home jersey?**

1-6 in 2026: 1-5 with the silver-green pants and 0-1 with the silver pants. Dallas is 4-0 in the all-white Color Rush and navy, and 0-1 in the Thanksgiving Classic.

**Do the Buccaneers win more in pewter pants?**

In 2026, yes: 2-3 in the white jersey with pewter pants and 0-4 with white pants.

**Which helmet color has the worst record in the NFL?**

White, 22-44, mostly because five losing teams (the Colts, Dolphins, Cardinals, Chargers and Titans) wear white helmets. Buffalo is 8-1 in its white helmet.

**Which helmet color has the best record?**

Navy, 26-13, led by the Bears and Broncos.

**Where does this data come from?**

From ColorWay Sports' own NFL uniform tracker: every game logged from game photos and paired with ESPN's final score.

**Is the Thanksgiving Cowboys-Eagles game included?**

Yes. The Cowboys lost it 39-38 in the 1960s Classic, their first game in it this season (0-1), and the Eagles won in the white jersey with midnight green pants (now 1-2). Chiefs-Bills on Thanksgiving night is in them too: the Bills won 13-10 in blue over blue, now 2-0 in that look, and the Chiefs fell to 2-2 in white over red pants.
