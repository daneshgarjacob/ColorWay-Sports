UConn has not worn an alternate uniform since 2016, and that streak is about to end. The Huskies revealed a red jersey in August, the first primarily red jersey any UConn team is believed to have worn, and it has not been assigned a date yet. This is the full UConn 2026 uniform schedule: the navy home set, the white road set, the new red alternate, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games in Jason Candle's first season, including a Friday night game at The Rent. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

Everything is Nike, in what the program calls national flag blue and white. UConn moved to this template in 2021, and it is about as plain as a college uniform gets: two colors, clean numerals, no loud striping. That is exactly why the red jersey matters. UConn football has not had an alternate since 2016, when it wore gray sets with chromatic red trim against Syracuse and Temple, the look from the 2015 upset of undefeated No. 19 Houston at Rentschler Field. The program has spent the years since teasing fans on April Fools' Day, and this April it posted a light blue "Husky Eyes" combo that was a joke.

**September 5 vs. Lafayette.** ★ Confirmed from UConn Athletics game photography: **navy helmet with the husky head, navy jersey, navy pants**, all navy for the home opener and Jason Candle's first game. UConn won 56-7 in front of 31,831 at Pratt & Whitney Stadium and set a school record with 671 total yards.

**September 12 vs. Maryland.** ★ Confirmed from the CBS Sports Network broadcast: **navy helmet, navy jersey, navy pants** again. Maryland answered in a red helmet, white jersey and red pants and won 38-14, ending UConn's eight-game home winning streak. We track the other side on our [Maryland 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/maryland-uniform-schedule-2026).

**September 19 at Southern Miss.** White expected. The first road game of the season, 7 p.m. Eastern at M.M. Roberts Stadium in Hattiesburg on ESPN+, and the first look at the white road set in 2026.

**September 26 at Miami (OH).** White expected. A 3:30 p.m. kickoff at Yager Stadium in Oxford against a RedHawks team in red at home, so this is the one date on the schedule where a red jersey could never work.

**October 3 vs. Syracuse.** Navy expected. Homecoming at The Rent against the old Big East rival, noon on CBS Sports Network, with Syracuse expected in white. Homecoming is one of the two dates on any schedule where a new alternate is most likely to debut. Our [Syracuse 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/syracuse-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Orange side.

**October 10 at Temple.** White expected. Lincoln Financial Field in Philadelphia, kickoff time to be announced, against a Temple team in cherry. See our [Temple 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/temple-uniform-schedule-2026).

**October 23 vs. UMass.** Navy expected. A Friday night at 7 p.m. that UConn is billing as Cancer Awareness and The Rent at Night. UConn's January home schedule graphic had this game on Saturday, October 24, and it has since moved to Friday. A night game against a regional rival is the other natural spot for the red jersey.

**October 31 at Air Force.** White expected. Halloween at Falcon Stadium, 1 p.m. Mountain time, with Air Force in blue. Our [Air Force 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/air-force-uniform-schedule-2026) tracks the Falcons.

## When Will UConn Wear the Red Jersey?

UConn has not said, and nothing on the schedule is labeled as a red game. Here is how we read it. The jersey is built for the home crowd, the reveal video is about the noise at The Rent, and college road teams wear white unless the home team agrees otherwise. That points to the seven home dates. Homecoming against Syracuse on October 3 is the obvious debut: the biggest crowd of the year, an old conference rival and a noon national TV window. The Friday night game against UMass on October 23 is the backup, because night games are where alternates look best. We will star the date the moment UConn confirms it.

## The Bottom Line

Two games in, UConn has worn the same thing twice: navy helmet, navy jersey, navy pants. That is a fine look and a very predictable one, which is why the red jersey is the story of the Huskies' closet this season. White is locked in for the five road games, starting Saturday at Southern Miss, and navy is the default for the rest of the home schedule. The dates we would circle for red are homecoming against Syracuse and the Friday night game against UMass. The grid above has every game with the expected set, and we add a star as each uniform is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is UConn wearing this week?**

UConn plays at Southern Miss on Saturday, September 19 at 7 p.m. Eastern on ESPN+, and the white road jersey is expected in the Huskies' first road game of the season. UConn wore the navy helmet, navy jersey and navy pants in both home games so far. The week-by-week grid above lists every game with the expected uniform.

**What are UConn's football uniforms for 2026?**

A navy home jersey with white numerals, a white road jersey with navy numerals trimmed in gray, and a new red alternate jersey with navy numerals trimmed in white. Pants come in navy and white, and there are navy and white helmets, with the navy shell carrying the husky head logo. Everything is Nike.

**Does UConn have a red jersey?**

Yes. UConn revealed a red alternate jersey on August 26, 2026, its first alternate since 2016 and believed to be the first primarily red jersey any UConn team has worn. It uses the same design as the white road jersey. There is no red helmet or red pants. UConn has not announced when the Huskies will wear it.

**What did UConn wear against Maryland?**

The navy helmet with the husky head, the navy jersey and navy pants at Pratt & Whitney Stadium on September 12. Maryland wore a red helmet, white jersey and red pants and won 38-14.

**Who is UConn's head coach in 2026?**

Jason Candle, who came to UConn from Toledo and replaced Jim Mora after Mora left for Colorado State. Candle won his first game 56-7 over Lafayette on September 5.

**Where does UConn play home games?**

Pratt & Whitney Stadium at Rentschler Field in East Hartford, Connecticut, known to fans as The Rent. UConn has seven home games in 2026.

**How many games does UConn play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games as an FBS independent: seven at home and five on the road, with a single bye on October 17. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
