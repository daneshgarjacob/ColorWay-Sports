Kentucky is going **"ice cold"** at Tennessee on Saturday. The Wildcats announced an all-white uniform with a **chrome silver helmet** for the game at Neyland Stadium, and they posted it with players sitting in a freezer truck stacked with bags of ice. It is a fun idea, and it is a direct answer to Tennessee, which wears its all-black Dark Mode for the same game.

## What Kentucky Is Wearing at Tennessee

- **Helmet:** chrome silver with a blue stripe down the middle and the UK logo on the side.
- **Jersey:** white, with "KENTUCKY" across the chest and blue numbers.
- **Pants:** white.

Kickoff is Saturday, November 7 at 6:15 p.m. ET on ESPN. With Tennessee in black helmets, black jerseys and black pants, this will be all white against all black under the Neyland lights. Our [Week 10 college football uniforms guide](/stories/college-football-week-10-uniforms-2026) has Tennessee's look and every other Top 25 game.

## Our Grade: B-

We like what Kentucky was going for. All white against Tennessee in all black is a great TV picture, and the ice theme is a better rollout than most programs bother with.

The helmet is the problem.
- **It doesn't match.** The chrome silver is cooler and grayer than the white jersey and pants, so from head to toe it reads as two different uniforms. A white helmet, or a white chrome finish, would have finished the look.
- **The logo disappears.** On a mirror finish under stadium lights, the UK on the side washes out. From a broadcast camera you can barely tell whose helmet it is.

So it is a **B-**: a great idea that lands a little off. Swap the helmet and this is an A-.
