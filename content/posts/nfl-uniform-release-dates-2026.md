It's uniform season in the NFL. Four teams have confirmed new jersey reveals this offseason, and two have already dropped. Here's every confirmed reveal date and what we know so far.

## Tennessee Titans - March 12 (Revealed)

The Titans kicked off the 2026 NFL uniform cycle with a full rebrand on March 12. New uniforms, new logo, new identity. The jerseys are clean and modern, and the timing lines up perfectly with their new stadium and the Cam Ward era. The logo, on the other hand, was a mixed bag. We broke the whole thing down in our [full Titans rebrand post](/stories/tennessee-titans-rebrand-2026).

## Atlanta Falcons - April 2 (Revealed)

The Falcons dropped their new uniforms on April 2 and went back to basics. Clean numbers, same logo, and a return to simplicity after years of a set that tried too hard. The red jersey that leaked before the reveal ended up being the real thing.

## Washington Commanders - April 15

The Commanders announced that their new uniforms are coming on April 15. This is the one we've been waiting for. Washington has been through a lot of identity changes over the past few years, and this feels like the chance to finally get it right.

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; margin: 0 0 2em;">

</div>

We're hoping they lean into the throwback look that they wore as their alternate this past season. That set was easily the best thing they've put on the field in years. The burgundy and gold with the classic striping and the old-school feel. If the new primary set looks anything like that, Washington is going to have one of the best uniforms in the league.

## Baltimore Ravens - April 16

The Ravens confirmed their new uniforms are dropping on April 16 under the campaign name "The Next Flight." Baltimore has worn essentially the same uniform template since the franchise started in 1996, making this the most significant uniform change in Ravens history. The teaser features a real raven and the tagline "The Next Flight" with the 4.16.26 date.

We want to see the Ravens modernize without overthinking it. The purple, black, and gold color scheme is already one of the strongest in the NFL. If they clean up the chest design and update the number font while keeping the core identity intact, this could be one of the best new uniforms of the offseason.
