The Calgary Flames' black C jersey is the red home sweater from their 2004 Stanley Cup Final run, back for the final season at the Scotiabank Saddledome. It makes its regular-season debut tonight, Saturday, October 24, against the Detroit Red Wings, and Calgary wears it four times in 2026-27: once against each team it beat or faced on the way to Game 7 in 2004.

## How It Looked in the Debut

It looked exactly like the render. The Flames wore the 2004 retro for the first time on Saturday, October 24, and beat the Detroit Red Wings 4-1 at the Saddledome, with Detroit in its road white. On the ice it came with black helmets, black pants and red socks striped in black, white and yellow to match the waist, and the black C read from every camera angle. One debut, one win.

## Why 2004 Still Matters in Calgary

The 2004 Flames were the team nobody saw coming. Jarome Iginla was the captain, Miikka Kiprusoff was in net and Darryl Sutter ran the bench. Calgary beat Vancouver in seven games, Detroit in six and San Jose in six, with Martin Gelinas scoring the series winner against both the Canucks and the Red Wings.

Then came Tampa Bay. The Flames led the Final 3-2, and in Game 6 a Gelinas shot looked to cross the goal line late in regulation, but no goal was called and the Lightning won in double overtime. Tampa took Game 7 2-1. Calgary fans still argue that goal was in, and this sweater is the one that team wore at home through the whole run. That is why the schedule lines up the way it does.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the Flames black C jersey?**

It is the red home jersey the Calgary Flames wore from 2003-04 through 2006-07, including the 2004 Stanley Cup Final run, with a black Flaming C crest outlined in white and yellow. Calgary brought it back as a retro jersey for 2026-27, the last season at the Saddledome.

**When do the Flames wear the black C jersey?**

Four times in 2026-27, all at home: October 24 vs Detroit, December 26 vs Vancouver, December 29 vs San Jose and January 9 vs Tampa Bay.

**What are the Flames wearing tonight against Detroit?**

The 2004 retro jersey with the black C, confirmed. Saturday, October 24 was its regular-season debut, on the club's '04 Celebration Night, and the Flames won 4-1.

**Why did the Flames bring back the 2004 jersey?**

For the Saddledome farewell season. The 2004 team reached Game 7 of the Stanley Cup Final in that building's era, and the Flames scheduled the jersey against each of their four 2004 playoff opponents.

**Is the Flames 2004 jersey new for 2026-27?**

It is a returning design, not a new one. It is a near replica of the 2003-04 to 2006-07 home sweater, updated with the current Play Alberta patch and "1983" and "2027" stitched inside the collar.

**Is the black Blasty jersey gone?**

The Flames have not retired it, but the black Blasty third does not appear on their announced 2026-27 home jersey schedule, which is split between the classic red, the 2004 retro and the Hometown Remix.

**Can you buy the Flames 2004 black C jersey?**

Yes. Replicas went on sale at the CGY Team Store on October 23, 2026.

**Who did the Flames play in the 2004 playoffs?**

Vancouver in the first round, Detroit in the second, San Jose in the Western Conference Final and Tampa Bay in the Stanley Cup Final, which Calgary lost in seven games.

## The Bottom Line on the Flames 2004 Jersey

The black C is back. Calgary's 2004 Stanley Cup Final sweater debuts tonight against Detroit and returns three more times against Vancouver, San Jose and Tampa Bay, all at the Saddledome in its final season. It is one of the best sweaters the Flames have ever worn, and our grade is an A.
