Boston College owns one of the cleanest closets in the ACC and one of the most meaningful uniforms in all of college football. This is the full Boston College 2026 uniform schedule: the maroon home set, the white road set, the gold helmet that finally got its stripe back, the Red Bandanna uniform the Eagles wore on the 25th anniversary of 9/11, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games in Bill O'Brien's third season. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

The current look is only a year old. Boston College became the first FBS football program to suit up in New Balance in 2025, a fitting partnership for a Boston school and a Boston brand, and the reveal leaned hard on the Doug Flutie era: simple maroon, simple white, gold trim, shoulder stripes that adidas had shaved off in the previous cycle.

That 2025 launch kept the helmet as a bare gold shell. The one visible change for 2026 is the best one: a maroon stripe now runs down the center of the gold helmet, with the ACC shield on the front bumper. It is a small thing that makes the whole uniform read older and better, and you can see it in the program's own preseason photos of the maroon home set.

ACC home teams wear color and visitors wear white, so every road date on this schedule points to the white jersey and every home date is a question of which pants go under the maroon. The Eagles have gold pants, maroon pants and white pants in the rotation, which is where most of the week-to-week variety lives.

**September 5 at Cincinnati.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **gold helmet, white jersey, gold pants**, the most traditional road combination Boston College owns and the first live look at the maroon helmet stripe. Cincinnati won 34-15 at Nippert Stadium. What the Bearcats wore is a separate question, and we track it on the [Cincinnati 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/cincinnati-uniform-schedule-2026).

**September 11 vs. Rutgers.** ★ Confirmed: the **Red Bandanna uniform**, gold helmet with a paisley center stripe, white jersey with red paisley shoulders, white pants. The 25th anniversary of 9/11 fell on a Friday night in Chestnut Hill, and the annual Red Bandanna Game honors Welles Crowther, the 1999 Boston College graduate and former lacrosse player who guided at least eighteen people out of the South Tower while wearing the red bandanna he carried everywhere. The program released the look two days before kickoff, down to a sideline towel printed FOR WELLES, SEPTEMBER 11TH, 2026 and the number 19.

## The Bottom Line

Two games into 2026, Boston College has shown the two things that matter: the maroon stripe is back on the gold helmet, and the Red Bandanna uniform is still the best one-off in the sport. Everything else is mix and match. White and gold on the road, maroon at home, and a pants decision every week that quietly changes how the whole thing looks. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Boston College wearing this week?**

Boston College hosts Maine on Saturday, September 19 at 2 p.m. at Alumni Stadium, and the maroon home jersey is expected. The Eagles wore the Red Bandanna uniform against Rutgers on September 11 and the white road set at Cincinnati on September 5. The week-by-week grid above lists every game with the expected uniform, and we update it as combinations are confirmed.

**What are Boston College's football uniforms for 2026?**

A maroon home jersey with white numerals outlined in gold, a white road jersey with maroon numerals outlined in gold, and the Red Bandanna alternate. Both base jerseys carry shoulder stripes and the BC eagle logo on the chest rather than a wordmark. Pants come in gold, maroon and white, and the helmet is a gold shell with a maroon center stripe. New Balance makes all of it.

**What is the Red Bandanna uniform and when does Boston College wear it?**

It is Boston College's annual tribute to Welles Crowther, a 1999 graduate and former lacrosse player who died on September 11, 2001 after leading at least eighteen people to safety in the South Tower while wearing a red bandanna. The uniform is a white jersey covered in red paisley on the shoulders and sleeves, with a paisley BC eagle on the chest and a paisley stripe on the gold helmet. In 2026 the Eagles wore it against Rutgers on September 11, the 25th anniversary of the attacks.

**Did Boston College change its helmet for 2026?**

Yes, in the way that matters most to people who care about this. The gold shell now carries a maroon stripe down the center, after the program wore a plain gold helmet through the first New Balance season in 2025. The ACC shield sits on the front bumper and the facemask is maroon. There is still no logo on the sides.

**How many games does Boston College play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: six at Alumni Stadium, six on the road, with a bye on October 10. Home dates are Rutgers, Maine, Virginia Tech, Pitt, Florida State and Syracuse. Road trips go to Cincinnati, SMU, Georgia Tech, Duke, Notre Dame and Miami. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
