**The Houston Comets have a logo again.** The team unveiled its new identity on **Thursday, October 29, 2026**, at an event at POST Houston in downtown Houston, ahead of its return to the WNBA in **2027**. The colors are **red, navy and silver**, the same family the original Comets wore when they won the league's first four championships from 1997 to 2000.

The new primary logo is a **slanted navy H with a red basketball comet** shooting through the middle of it. **Four stars** inside the H stand for the four titles, and the one white star is a nod to Texas, the Lone Star State. The set also includes a round **HOUSTON COMETS** badge and two wordmarks. Here is every new mark, how it compares to the original Comets logos, and a grade for each.

## Quick Answers

- **When did the Comets reveal their new logo?** Thursday, October 29, 2026, at POST Houston.
- **What is the new logo?** A navy H, tilted upward, with a red basketball comet forming the crossbar and four four-pointed stars inside the letters.
- **What do the stars mean?** The team says the **four stars** honor the four straight WNBA championships (1997, 1998, 1999, 2000). Three are silver. The **white star** pays tribute to Texas, the Lone Star State. It is one of the four, not a fifth.
- **What are the colors?** Red, navy and silver.
- **When do the Comets play again?** In the **2027 WNBA season**, at Toyota Center.
- **Have the uniforms been revealed?** No. The team says more brand elements are coming in the coming months.
- **Our overall grade:** **B.** A smart, faithful update of the old Comets H, with a comet tail that looks more like a lightning bolt than it should.

## The Reveal

The Comets posted the new look on X the night of the event with the line "Inspired by legacy. Created for our future." The thread walks through the badge, the H, the comet, the four stars and the white star in the team's own words.

## The New Primary Logo: The H

<img src="/images/posts/houston-comets-new-logo-2027/primary-h-logo.png" alt="Houston Comets new primary logo 2027: slanted navy H with a red basketball comet as the crossbar, three silver stars and one white star inside, outlined in white and red" style="display:block;width:100%;max-width:460px;margin:1.4em auto 0.8em;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin-top: 0.5em;">The new primary logo, via the Houston Comets</p>

Here is what is in it, piece by piece:

- **The H.** A navy H, leaning forward and tilted upward. The team calls it the "ascending H" and says it stands for ambition and progress. Sheirr told the Chronicle the upward angle represents the team's upward trajectory.
- **The comet.** A red basketball with a long tail streaks left to right through the middle of the H and becomes the crossbar. The team describes it as the comet traveling "through the bridge of the H."
- **The stars.** Four four-pointed stars sit inside the H: one in the upper left (white), one in the upper right, one in the lower left and one in the lower right (all silver). Small dots fill in the rest of the night sky.
- **The outlines.** The whole logo is wrapped in a white outline and then a red one.

### Where it comes from

This H is not new to Houston. **The original Comets had an alternate logo of a basketball comet shooting through an H on a field of stars.** The Chronicle reported the new H draws on that original secondary logo. What changed is that the H has been promoted to primary, redrawn with sharper, slanted letters, and given a cleaner set of stars.

The original Comets **primary** logo was different: a red basketball comet flying over the word "Comets" in script, on a starfield. Bleacher Report reported the team could not bring that design back as its primary logo because of WNBA rules. So the comet and the stars carried over, and the script did not.

### Grade: B+

This is the right idea. A one-letter mark is easy to put on a hat, a court and a jersey, and it carries real history, since Comets fans already know the H with the comet through it. The stars are the best part: four titles are the whole reason the name means something, and putting them inside the logo is a clear, honest way to say so.

Two things keep it short of an A. First, the comet's tail is drawn as a jagged zig-zag, and from a distance it reads as a **lightning bolt**, not a comet. Second, the white-star story is a little confusing. The team says four stars equal four titles and the white star is for Texas, which means one star is doing two jobs. Most fans will count four stars and stop there, which is fine, but the symbolism is cleaner on paper than it is on a T-shirt.

## The New Global Logo: The Round Badge

<img src="/images/posts/houston-comets-new-logo-2027/global-badge-tee.jpg" alt="Houston Comets new round badge logo 2027 on a gray T-shirt: red ring with HOUSTON across the top and COMETS across the bottom in white, the slanted H with the comet in the navy center" style="display:block;width:100%;max-width:440px;margin:1.4em auto 0.8em;border-radius:12px;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin-top: 0.5em;">The new round badge on a Comets T-shirt, via the Comets Shop</p>

The badge puts the H on a navy circle inside a **red ring**, with **HOUSTON** curved around the top and **COMETS** around the bottom in white. A thin white line runs inside the ring, and a navy line wraps the outside.

It also matches the Rockets. In June the Rockets launched a new **global logo shaped like a space mission patch** as part of their own rebrand ([our grades here](/stories/houston-rockets-2026-rebrand-graded)). The Comets' round badge is a mission patch in spirit too, which makes sense for two teams in a city with NASA's Johnson Space Center.

### Grade: B

Clean, readable and useful. It will look good on a hat patch and at center court. It is also the most expected part of the set. Round badges with the city on top and the nickname on the bottom are everywhere right now, and nothing here would surprise you.

## The New Wordmarks: HOUSTON and COMETS

<img src="/images/posts/houston-comets-new-logo-2027/wordmarks.jpg" alt="Houston Comets new wordmarks 2027: HOUSTON with a basketball comet as the second O and stars around the letters, and COMETS with a basketball comet as the O, in white on navy, both with a lowercase-style t" style="display:block;width:100%;max-width:640px;margin:1.4em auto 0.8em;border-radius:12px;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin-top: 0.5em;">HOUSTON via the Houston Comets; COMETS from the Comets Shop logo</p>

There are two wordmarks, one for each word. In **HOUSTON**, the second O is a red basketball comet with its tail streaking in from the left, and small four-pointed stars float around the first few letters. In **COMETS**, the O is the comet. Both use rounded, heavy letters with a **lowercase-style t** (and in COMETS, a lowercase-style m), mixed in with capitals.

That lowercase t is a Houston thing. The Rockets brought back their signature lowercase t in their June rebrand, and the Comets now share it.

### Grade: B-

The comet-as-O idea is fun, works well in COMETS, and ties every piece of the set together. The mixed lowercase and capital letters are the weak spot. They look friendly and modern up close, but at a glance HOUSTON reads a little uneven, with the tall t sticking up out of the line. It will look better on a jersey chest than it does in a press graphic.

The honest summary: this is a modern version of the old Comets, not a new idea. Sheirr put it simply to the Chronicle: "We modernized it." Comets senior creative director Jose Lopez told reporters, per Bleacher Report, that "the logos, they're a memory," and that people would gravitate to the color scheme.

We think that was the right call. When a team brings back a name with four championships behind it, fans want to recognize it, and they will. The cost is the script. The old "Comets" script on the starfield was the most 1990s, most personal part of the original look, and losing it makes the new set feel more like a corporate system and less like the team Cooper, Sheryl Swoopes and Tina Thompson played for.

## The Comet Problem

Look closely at the tail on the H. It is a set of sharp zig-zags in navy, white and red, with straight red streaks closer to the ball. A real comet tail is soft and fans out. This one is angular, and from across a room the whole crossbar reads as a **lightning bolt with a basketball on the end**.

That matters because the comet is the name. On the old alternate logo the tail was a clear streak. Here it looks electric, closer to the Houston Rockets' energy than to something drifting across the night sky. It is a small thing on a big logo and a bigger thing on a small one, like a hat side patch, where the zig-zags will turn to noise.

## What Comes Next

The Comets have not revealed their uniforms. The team says more brand elements are coming in the coming months, and Rockets Wire reported the same. Every WNBA team's uniforms are made by Nike, so expect the usual set of editions when they arrive. The team will play at **Toyota Center**, the Rockets' arena, which is being renovated with upgrades for the WNBA team, according to the Chronicle.

Fans got their first chance to buy the new look at a pop-up shop at the George R. Brown Convention Center, open October 30 through November 1, and on the Comets Shop online.

## How the Comets Came Back

The original Comets were one of the WNBA's eight original teams. They won the first four championships in league history, 1997 through 2000, and the league folded the team in **December 2008** when no new owner could be found.

In 2026, Rockets owner **Tilman Fertitta** bought the **Connecticut Sun** and moved the franchise to Houston to revive the Comets for 2027. Team president Jennifer Rizzotti and general manager Morgan Tuck came over from Connecticut. The name has not been simple: a company tied to rapper Travis Scott filed for the "Houston Comets" trademark, and Front Office Sports reported the team expects to keep the name as that dispute plays out.

## The Bottom Line

The Comets did the most important thing right: the new logo looks like the Comets. The slanted H with the comet through it comes straight from the original set, the colors are back, and the four stars put the four titles right in the mark. The comet tail should look more like a comet, the wordmarks are a bit busy, and we will miss the old script. Tell us what you think: is the H the right primary logo, or should the Comets have fought to bring back the original?

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the new Houston Comets logo?**

The new primary logo is a slanted navy H with a red basketball comet streaking through the middle as the crossbar. Four four-pointed stars sit inside the H, three silver and one white, and the logo is outlined in white and red.

**When did the Houston Comets unveil their new logo?**

Thursday, October 29, 2026, at an event at POST Houston in downtown Houston. The team posted the logos on X the same night.

**What do the four stars on the Comets logo mean?**

The team says the four stars honor the original Comets' four straight WNBA championships in 1997, 1998, 1999 and 2000.

**What does the white star on the Comets logo mean?**

It is a tribute to Texas, the Lone Star State. The white star is one of the four title stars, not a fifth star. The other three are silver.

Red, navy and silver, the same color family the original Comets used from 1997 to 2008.

The Comets return for the 2027 WNBA season. The franchise is the former Connecticut Sun, bought by Rockets owner Tilman Fertitta and moved to Houston.
