Miami's base wardrobe barely changes. One white helmet covers the orange and white jerseys, and the white pants go with both, so the only real question on most Saturdays is which jersey. The black set is the exception. All-black first appeared in 2015. adidas turned it into the Miami Nights series in August 2017, when it was unveiled alongside the green State of Miami set. It was originally slated for a Thursday night game against Georgia Tech, but that game moved to a Saturday afternoon, and the black debuted in prime time against Virginia Tech that November, a 28-10 win. New Miami Nights versions followed in 2020 and 2022. The 2022 version brought neon piping to the helmet, jersey, and pants. The 2023 edition debuted against Georgia Tech, a 23-20 loss. Miami revealed another all-black set in July 2024 and wore it against Florida State that October, the first time in 69 meetings that the Hurricanes wore black against the Seminoles, and Miami won 36-14. In 2025 the special-game slot went to adidas's Honor and Support uniforms for the military appreciation game against Stanford: a black helmet with a digital camo logo, a camo jersey with black numerals, and olive pants. Miami won that game 42-7. We have not seen a new 2026 alternate announced, so the grid below works from the sets we know. In the ACC the home team wears color and the visitor wears white, and Miami follows that convention.

**September 4 at Stanford.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **white helmet, white jersey, white pants**, the all-white road set, against Stanford in cardinal jerseys. Miami won 45-6 on a Friday night in Palo Alto. ESPN lists this game under September 5 because its feed uses UTC, and a 6 p.m. Pacific kickoff falls after midnight UTC. It was played Friday, September 4.

**September 10 vs. Florida A&M.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **white helmet, orange jersey, white pants**, with Florida A&M in white. Miami won 77-7 in the Thursday night home opener at Hard Rock Stadium. It was the same home combination Miami wore seven times in 2025, going 6-1. ESPN lists this game under September 11 because of the same UTC shift.

**September 18 at Wake Forest.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **white helmet, white jersey, white pants**, the all-white road set exactly as Miami's [Week 3 graphic](https://x.com/CanesFootball/status/2100578725129544026) laid it out. Wake Forest answered in its all black Dark Mode set, matte black helmet over a black jersey and black pants, so the ACC opener in Winston-Salem was white against black with nothing shared. Miami won 33-20 on a Friday night. It is the cleanest contrast we have seen all season, and the road set keeps doing the heavy lifting.

**September 26 vs. Central Michigan.** ★ Confirmed: **white helmet, green jersey, green pants**, the first time the green alternate is out this season and the all-green look under the white U helmet that goes back to the State of Miami set. It breaks the orange-at-home pattern of the opener. Kickoff is 6:30 p.m. Eastern on The CW at Hard Rock Stadium.

**October 3 at Clemson.** White expected, since Miami is the visitor. The biggest road test of the first half, in Death Valley, against a Clemson team that wears orange at home. With Miami in white, the two oranges will not clash.

**October 10.** Bye.

**October 17 vs. Florida State.** Orange expected, since it is a home game. This is the home date we are watching most closely. Miami wore Miami Nights black for Florida State's last visit to Hard Rock Stadium in 2024, and a night kickoff would make this the natural date for black again.

**October 24 vs. Pittsburgh.** Orange expected, since it is a home game.

**October 31 at North Carolina.** White expected, since Miami is the visitor. A Halloween trip to Kenan Stadium in Chapel Hill.

**November 7 at Notre Dame.** White expected, since Miami is the visitor. This is the old rivalry renewed in South Bend, a 7:30 p.m. Eastern kickoff on NBC. Miami in white against Notre Dame's navy, gold helmets, and gold pants gives this one of the season's sharpest contrasts. The teams opened 2025 at Hard Rock Stadium, where Miami won 27-24.

**November 14 vs. Duke.** Orange expected, since it is a home game.

**November 21 vs. Virginia Tech.** Orange expected, since it is a home game. Virginia Tech is the opponent the black set debuted against in 2017, and the Hokies' November visit is one more candidate for a night game.

**November 28 vs. Boston College.** Orange expected, since it is a home game. This is the regular season finale and the last home game.

## The Bottom Line

Miami has worn nothing but its two base sets in the first three games: all white at Stanford, orange over white at home against Florida A&M, and all white again at Wake Forest in a 33-20 win that opened ACC play. With the same helmet and pants every week, the jersey color is the only thing that changes. That changes against Central Michigan on September 26, when Miami wears the green alternate with green pants under the white helmet. Expect white at Clemson, North Carolina, and Notre Dame, and orange for most of the rest of the home slate until the program decides a night deserves black. Florida State's October 17 visit is the leading candidate, based on 2024, and it now has a second candidate to choose from: Miami revealed [2001 national championship throwbacks](/stories/miami-2001-throwback-uniforms) on September 22, in both the orange home and the white road version, with no date announced for either. We grade the road whites an A- and the orange a B+. Last season ended in the national championship game at Hard Rock Stadium, a 27-21 loss to Indiana, so any 2026 special set will get plenty of attention. The grid above has every date, and we star each game once the uniform is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Miami wearing this week?**

Miami wore all white at Wake Forest on Friday, September 18: white helmet with the orange and green stripe, white jersey and white pants, confirmed on the ESPN broadcast. Wake Forest wore its all black Dark Mode set, matte black helmet, black jersey and black pants, so it was white against black with nothing shared, and Miami won 33-20 in the ACC opener. Next up is Central Michigan at home on Saturday, September 26, and Miami goes green: white helmet, green jersey, green pants. The grid above lists every game, and we update it as each combination is confirmed. Every other top game this Saturday is in our [College Football Week 4 Uniforms 2026](/stories/college-football-week-4-uniforms-2026) guide.

**What are Miami's football uniforms for 2026?**

Miami has an orange home jersey with white numerals trimmed in green, a white road jersey with green numerals outlined in orange, and a green alternate. All three go with the white helmet, which has an orange and green center stripe and the split U logo, and with white pants carrying an orange and green side stripe. The black Miami Nights set adds a black helmet and black pants. All of it is made by adidas.

**Does Miami have a new alternate uniform in 2026?**

None has been announced as of September 19. The most recent specialty uniforms were the all-black set revealed in July 2024 and worn against Florida State, and the camo Honor and Support uniforms worn against Stanford in October 2025. If Miami unveils a 2026 set, we will add it here with the game it is tied to.

**What did Miami wear against Stanford?**

All white: white helmet, white jersey, and white pants, Miami's standard road uniform, in a 45-6 win on Friday, September 4. Stanford wore cardinal jerseys as the home team. ESPN files the game under September 5 because its schedule runs on UTC.

**What is the Miami Nights uniform?**

It is Miami's all-black alternate: black helmet, black jersey, and black pants, inspired by South Beach nightlife. Black first appeared in 2015, and adidas has used the Miami Nights name since 2017, with new versions in 2020, 2022, 2023, and 2024. Miami usually saves it for night games at Hard Rock Stadium, most recently the 36-14 win over Florida State in 2024.

**How many games does Miami play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven at Hard Rock Stadium and five on the road, with a bye on October 10. The road trips are Stanford, Wake Forest, Clemson, North Carolina, and Notre Dame. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
