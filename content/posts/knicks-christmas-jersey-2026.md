## What Works

The Knicks Christmas jersey is the lightest of all eight designs, and that alone makes it stand out. While the Lakers, Celtics, and Warriors all got swallowed by black bases and neon effects, the Knicks version actually lets some color breathe. The blue and orange stripes running across the chest paired with the "New York" wordmark give this jersey an identity that the others are missing. You can actually tell this is a Knicks jersey from a distance, which is more than we can say for most of the collection.

<img src="/images/posts/nba-xmas-2026-knicks/knicks-xmas-detail1.jpg" alt="Close-up detail of New York Knicks 2026 Christmas jersey stripes and wordmark" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin-top: 0.5em;">Image via Basketball Jersey Archive</p>

The striping is the best part. It ties the jersey back to the classic Knicks look in a way that feels intentional rather than just slapping a logo onto a dark template. There is actual design thought here, and it shows.

## What Doesn't Work

The same problem that plagues the entire collection still applies here. Christmas jerseys should feel like Christmas. Think about the Adidas era, those bright, bold, monochromatic designs that made Christmas Day basketball feel like an event. These Nike jerseys look like they were designed for Halloween, not December 25th. Even the Knicks version, which is the best of the bunch, still trends darker than a Christmas jersey should.

<img src="/images/posts/nba-xmas-2026-knicks/knicks-xmas-detail2.jpg" alt="New York Knicks 2026 Christmas jersey full view showing overall dark color scheme" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 10px;" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin-top: 0.5em;">Image via Basketball Jersey Archive</p>

We would have loved to see Nike lean into a brighter palette across the board. Give us whites, golds, greens, reds. Give us something that actually screams holiday basketball. Instead, we got eight dark jerseys that blend together on the court.

## Our Grade

The Knicks got the best hand in a weak deal. The stripes and wordmark save it from the generic feel that drags down the rest of the set, and the lighter base gives it some separation. But it is still part of a collection that missed the mark on what Christmas Day jerseys should be.

**Grade: B+**
