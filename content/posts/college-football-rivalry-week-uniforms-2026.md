College football's Rivalry Week runs Friday, November 27 and Saturday, November 28, 2026, the weekend after Thanksgiving. It has the Iron Bowl, The Game, the Egg Bowl, the Old Oaken Bucket, Paul Bunyan's Axe and the 100th Territorial Cup, and below is what both teams are expected to wear in 19 rivalry games, with each program's full uniform schedule one tap away.

Every look carries one of three labels:

- **Confirmed:** the school has posted the uniform for this game.
- **Scheduled:** the uniform is on a schedule the school published before the season (Florida is the only one here).
- **Expected:** our projection from that team's uniform schedule and its home and road pattern. Most schools post their combination between Monday and game day, so these turn into Confirmed during game week, and on Saturday night we check every one against the game photos.

The NFL's holiday week is the same weekend, five games from Thanksgiving Eve through Black Friday: [NFL Thanksgiving Uniforms 2026](/stories/nfl-thanksgiving-uniforms-2026).

## The Egg Bowl: Mississippi State At Ole Miss

*Friday, November 27, noon ET on ABC, Vaught-Hemingway Stadium, Oxford.*

**Ole Miss: home color (Expected).** The Egg Bowl moves to the Friday after Thanksgiving in Oxford, so the Rebels wear a home color, red or navy. In 2025 they won it in Starkville in white with the gray and white Realtree camo helmet, so a camo encore is possible. See the [Ole Miss 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/ole-miss-uniform-schedule-2026).

**Mississippi State: white (Expected).** The visitor takes white with Ole Miss in color. Full season on the [Mississippi State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/mississippi-state-uniform-schedule-2026).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When is college football Rivalry Week in 2026?**

Friday, November 27 and Saturday, November 28, the weekend after Thanksgiving. The Egg Bowl, Texas at Texas A&M, Florida at Florida State and Paul Bunyan's Axe are on Friday; the Iron Bowl, The Game and the rest are on Saturday.

**What is Alabama wearing in the 2026 Iron Bowl?**

Crimson. The Iron Bowl is at Bryant-Denny Stadium on November 28, so Alabama is the home team, and Auburn is expected in all white.

**What is Ohio State wearing against Michigan in 2026?**

Scarlet is expected at Ohio Stadium on November 28, with Michigan in white. Ohio State has worn alternates for this game before, so watch for its game-week post.

**Why do USC and UCLA both wear their home colors?**

The schools shared the Coliseum from 1928 through 1981 and both wore home jerseys in every meeting. The NCAA's visitors-in-white rule ended that in 1982, the programs defied it in 2008, and the NCAA has allowed both teams to wear color in this game since. In 2026 it is UCLA blue against USC cardinal at the Rose Bowl.

**What is Ole Miss wearing in the 2026 Egg Bowl?**

A home color, red or navy, is expected in Oxford on Friday, November 27, with Mississippi State in white. Ole Miss won the 2025 Egg Bowl in white with a gray and white Realtree camo helmet, so a camo encore is possible.

**What is Florida wearing at Florida State?**

The blue helmet, white jersey and white pants, from the game-by-game schedule Florida published before the season. Florida State is expected in garnet at home.

**What is Arizona wearing for the Territorial Cup?**

Red is expected. It is the 100th Territorial Cup and Senior Day in Tucson, and Arizona has called a Red Out. Arizona State is expected in white.
