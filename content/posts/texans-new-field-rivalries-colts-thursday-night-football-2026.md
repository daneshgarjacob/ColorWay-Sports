**The Texans have a new field to go with their new uniform.** On Wednesday, November 18, Houston posted 15 photos of its grounds crew painting the NRG Stadium turf with the caption "new field... who dis." The field matches the **HOUSTON/US** Rivalries uniform, which debuts the next night when the Texans host the **Indianapolis Colts** on **Thursday, November 19, at 8:15 p.m. Eastern on Prime Video**. The post does not name the game, but it is Houston's next home game, and the team's own X banner for this week is the Colts game in the Rivalries uniform.

## What Is On The New Field

- **Midfield:** the gothic H-Town "H" from the Rivalries helmet, in white with H-Town Blue and Battle Red outlines and a red star to its right, painted over the 50.
- **End zones:** white, painted to look like the street-tile mosaics the uniform is built on, with **"H-TOWN"** and **"TEXANS"** spelled out in blue tile-style letters. The borders carry blue-and-white stripes and a red star, like the uniform.
- **25 Seasons logo:** the "Est. 2002, 25 Seasons" anniversary mark, redone in the same blue, white and red tile style.
- **Colors:** H-Town Blue, Battle Red and white. The photos show the crew mixing a bright light blue paint, close to the blue of the uniform numbers.

It is the same idea as the uniform itself: Houston's blue-and-white street tiles, the gothic H and the red star, all in a lighter palette than the team's usual Deep Steel Blue.

## Why It Matters

Most teams that wear a special uniform change the helmet and jersey and leave the field alone. Houston painted the whole building to match, for a national TV game, on the night the franchise wears a **white helmet** for the first time in its history. The full uniform breakdown is in our [Texans Rivalries uniform story for Thursday night](/stories/texans-houston-us-rivalries-uniform-colts-thursday-night-football-2026): white helmet with a chrome blue facemask, white jersey with H-Town Blue numbers and tile stripes, and white pants. The Colts are scheduled in royal blue.

Our take: this is the right way to do a one-night look. The tile end zones are the best part, because they turn the uniform's main idea into something you can see from the blimp shot. The light blue against green turf will be loud on TV, which is the point.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why do the Texans have a new field?**

It goes with the HOUSTON/US Rivalries uniform, which the Texans debut against the Colts on Thursday Night Football. The field uses the same street-tile design, gothic "H" and red star as the uniform.

**When will the Texans play on the new field?**

Thursday, November 19, 2026, against the Indianapolis Colts at 8:15 p.m. Eastern on Prime Video. The Texans posted the field photos the day before.

**What is at midfield on the new Texans field?**

The gothic H-Town "H" from the Rivalries helmet, white with blue and red outlines, with a red star next to it.

**What do the Texans' new end zones say?**

"H-TOWN" and "TEXANS," in blue letters painted to look like Houston's street tiles on a white background.

**Is the new field permanent?**

The Texans have not said. It is painted to match a uniform the team wears once this season, so expect it for the Colts game.

**What are the Texans wearing against the Colts?**

The HOUSTON/US Rivalries uniform: a white helmet with a chrome blue facemask, the first white helmet in team history, a white jersey with H-Town Blue numbers and tile stripes, and white pants.

**What are the Colts wearing at Houston?**

Royal blue, per the Colts' schedule.

**What does the 25 Seasons logo on the field mean?**

2026 is the Texans' 25th season. The team was founded in 2002, and the anniversary logo on the field reads "Est. 2002" and "25 Seasons."
