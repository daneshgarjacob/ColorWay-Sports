Wondering what jersey Tennessee is wearing this week? This is the full Tennessee 2026 uniform schedule, game by game. Orange at Neyland, white on the road, and two dates that break the pattern: the new Smokey Grey against Auburn on October 3, and orange against Alabama on the Third Saturday in October.

## The 2026 Smokey Grey Is a Neyland Tribute

Tennessee revealed this year's Smokey Grey on August 20, and it is the best one the series has produced in a while. The theme is General Robert Neyland, and the timing is the reason: 2026 is **one hundred years since Neyland's first season** as Tennessee's head coach in 1926.

The details are where it earns the tribute:

- **Neyland's signature** in script across the back helmet bumper.
- **A white star on the shell**, the old Tennessee helmet marking.
- **Ayres Hall printed across the shoulder yokes** in tonal grey line art, flags and all, so it reads as texture from the stands and as architecture up close.
- **The vintage interlocking UT monogram** in raised orange on the collar and the sleeves.
- Orange numbers with a white outline, arched **TENNESSEE** on the back, and cream pants with an orange, cream and grey stripe.

This is also **the first Smokey Grey of the adidas era**. Tennessee left Nike this year, and the Smokey Grey Series, which started back in 2013, carries over into the new deal with a different design each season.

**It debuts October 3 against Auburn at Neyland Stadium.** One game, as always with this series.

### Our Grade: B-

Grey jerseys are not our favorite thing in college football, and that is the ceiling this one runs into. Working against that, this is about as well as the idea gets executed. The Ayres Hall print on the sleeves is the best part of the set, a real piece of design rather than a pattern for the sake of one, and it rewards a closer look without shouting from the stands. The orange numbers give the jersey the pop it needs, which is the thing most grey sets get wrong when they go tonal and disappear.

The pants are the quietly correct decision. Cream instead of grey keeps this from turning into a single flat block of colorless uniform, and it is the difference between a grey jersey and a grey uniform. Tennessee did the version of this we would ask for. We would just rather have the orange.

## What Tennessee Wears the Rest of the Year

The SEC convention does most of the work here. The home team wears color, the visitor wears white, so Tennessee is in orange at Neyland and white everywhere else unless something is announced.

- **Orange jersey, white pants, orange helmet.** The home set.
- **White jersey, white pants, orange helmet.** The road set.
- **Smokey Grey.** October 3 vs Auburn only.
- **Dark Mode.** The all-black alternate is in the adidas rotation but **has not been assigned to a 2026 date**. We will add it here if it is.

## The Two Dates That Matter

Most of this schedule answers itself. Orange at home, white away, twelve games, no surprises in either direction. The two that are worth circling are **October 3** and **October 17**, two weeks apart, both at Neyland.

October 3 is the only alternate uniform on the calendar. October 17 is the rarer thing: a road team showing up in color, which almost never happens in this sport and has not happened in this rivalry in more than fifty years. Back to back home dates where the field looks different than it normally does.

## The Bottom Line

Tennessee's 2026 uniform schedule is orange at Neyland and white on the road, with one alternate. The Smokey Grey Neyland centennial set goes on **October 3 against Auburn**, and the Vols wear **orange against Alabama on October 17** in the first color vs color Third Saturday in October since 1970. The Dark Mode black alternate has no date yet. We update this page when one is announced.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Tennessee wearing this week?**

Tennessee wears orange at Neyland Stadium and white on the road. The exceptions are October 3 against Auburn, when the Vols wear the Smokey Grey alternate, and October 17 against Alabama, which is an orange home game with Alabama also in color.

**When does Tennessee wear the Smokey Grey uniform in 2026?**

October 3 against Auburn at Neyland Stadium. The Smokey Grey Series is a once-a-season uniform, and 2026's version is a tribute to General Robert Neyland marking one hundred years since his first season as head coach.

**What is on the 2026 Smokey Grey uniform?**

Neyland's signature on the back helmet bumper, a white star on the helmet, Ayres Hall printed in tonal grey across the shoulder yokes, the vintage interlocking UT monogram in orange, orange numbers outlined in white, and cream pants with an orange, cream and grey stripe.

**Is the 2026 Tennessee Smokey Grey uniform any good?**

We grade it a B-. The Ayres Hall sleeve print is genuinely well designed, the orange numbers keep the jersey from going flat, and the cream pants stop it from becoming an all-grey uniform. The grey jersey itself is the limit on how high it can score.

**Is Tennessee wearing adidas in 2026?**

Yes. Tennessee moved from Nike to adidas for the 2026 season, and the Neyland Smokey Grey is the first uniform in the Smokey Grey Series made by adidas.

**What does Tennessee wear against Alabama?**

Orange. The October 17 game at Neyland is a color vs color game, with Tennessee in orange and Alabama in crimson rather than white, the first time the rivalry has done that since 1970.

**Does Tennessee have a black uniform in 2026?**

The Dark Mode all-black alternate is part of the adidas set, but it has not been assigned to a 2026 game.
