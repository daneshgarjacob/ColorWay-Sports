USC has the best uniform in the Big Ten, Michigan is right behind it, and Rutgers is last. We graded all 18 teams on the full set: the home jersey, the road jersey and every alternate they actually wore this season. Then we sorted them into tiers and counted down from worst to best.

Every grade is based on what the team wore in 2026, which we track game by game on each team's uniform schedule page. Each entry has a photo the team posted of its own home uniform.

## Quick Answers

- **What is the best uniform in the Big Ten?** USC. Cardinal and gold, gold pants with both jerseys, and no alternates. Michigan is a close second.
- **What is the worst uniform in the Big Ten?** Rutgers. A scarlet jersey with a block wordmark that could belong to any school in the country.
- **Where is Oregon?** Sixth. Some of the Ducks' single combinations are the best in the sport. As a set, they have no identity.
- **Where is Indiana?** Ninth. The national champions wear a clean uniform. It is also the most boring one in the top half.

## 18. Rutgers: C-

Scarlet jersey, white block numbers, RUTGERS across the chest in block letters, white pants, a scarlet helmet with a white block R. Cover the R and you could not tell me which school this is. Nike took over in July 2025 and kept the old look almost exactly, so the most interesting thing Rutgers has worn this year is the white 9/11 tribute helmet at Boston College, a one-off. The all-black Blackout set looks fine. It looks like every other Blackout set too.

## 17. Purdue: C-

Old gold helmet with the black P, black jersey with PURDUE across the chest in gold, old gold pants with a black and white stripe. That combination is a top-half Big Ten uniform. Purdue keeps walking away from it: the black helmet over the black jersey and white pants opened the season, and an all-black look came out for Notre Dame. Old gold is the one thing nobody else in the conference has. Until Purdue wears it every week, this is the second-worst set in the Big Ten.

## 16. Northwestern: C

The base set is better than this ranking. A purple helmet with the oversized white N, a purple jersey with three white shoulder stripes and NORTHWESTERN across the chest is a strong college football look. The problem is that Northwestern wore ten different combinations last year and keeps adding costumes. This season brought the new Purple Gothics, deep royal purple with a metallic gold stripe and the Weber Arch printed across the back of the helmet, on top of two black Gothic sets, plus an all-white "Stormtroopers" look at Oregon on Halloween. The original 2014 Gothic is on our list of the worst college uniforms ever. Wear the purple.

## 15. Michigan State: C+

Spartan green is one of the best colors in college football, and this set buries it under a Greek key pattern. The key runs down the center of the helmet, around the sleeves and down the pants. Once is a detail. Three times is wallpaper. The 2026 change makes it worse: the MSU Federal Credit Union patch is on the jersey and its logo is printed on the helmet bumper. The black Shadow alternate has no chest wordmark and no reason to exist at a green school.

## 14. Illinois: C+

Orange helmet with the navy block I, navy jersey, orange pants. Navy and orange is a great pair, and the base look works. Then Illinois puts orange pants under nearly everything and rotates through orange helmets with the block I, ILLINOIS, ILLINI and script wordmarks, a navy script helmet and a military appreciation helmet. Too many helmets is how you end up with no helmet people remember. The best thing Illinois wore all year was the all-navy Blue Out against Nebraska, navy helmet over navy jersey over navy pants. Build around that.

## 13. Maryland: C+

The Script Terps helmet is good. It is a red shell with the cursive Terps script from the early 1980s and a white center stripe, and it has been the everyday helmet since 2023. Everything under it is too busy. The shoulder stripes pack black, white and gold from the state flag onto a red jersey, and the closet holds red, white, black and gold jerseys plus white and black versions of the script helmet. This year added a "Los Terps" helmet for one game against Virginia Tech. Pick a lane.

## 12. Nebraska: B-

Same grade we gave the redesign in June. The new numbers, built from the digits stamped on a steel anvil with crop-row lines inside, are the best number font in the Big Ten. The rest gave ground: the stripes moved off the sleeves to the shoulders and the TV numbers dropped to the sleeves, and the scarlet jersey looks busier for it. The helmet, white with the red Block N, was left alone, which was correct. The black alternate takes the N off the helmet for a Huskers script. That was a mistake.

## 11. Wisconsin: B-

White helmet with the red Motion W and a red facemask, red jersey with white numbers, white pants. Wisconsin has worn that helmet since the early 1990s and has the sense to leave it alone. The two alternates this season were both smart: an all-red set with silver numbers at Lambeau Field against Notre Dame, tied to the building, and the Red Out at Homecoming with a red helmet. What holds it back is that red over white pants is the most common look in college football. It edges Nebraska because the helmet never needed a redesign.

## 10. Minnesota: B-

Maroon and gold is an elite color pair, and the maroon home jersey with gold numbers and gold and white shoulder stripes uses it well. The best thing Minnesota did this year was bring back the chrome gold helmet, which made its 2026 debut against Michigan on October 3. The gold jersey is the miss. A gold jersey with maroon numbers looks like a practice top. The everyday maroon helmet with the oversized gold Block M is fine, and the chrome one is better. Make chrome the primary.

## 9. Indiana: B

Indiana won a national title in this uniform, and it is a good one. In 2024 the program stripped the black outlines off the numbers and went back to straight crimson and white, and in 2026 the only change was a trident at the collar. INDIANA arches across the crimson home jersey, HOOSIERS across the white road jersey, and the crimson helmet carries the white IU trident. It is clean, correct and boring. White pants under both jerseys flatten it, and the white helmet at Rutgers is the only time all year the champs changed anything.

## 8. Iowa: B+

Black helmet with the gold Tigerhawk, black jersey with white numbers and gold and white shoulder stripes, gold pants with a black stripe. Iowa has worn this for years with almost no changes, and the stability is the point. The gold pants carry the whole look, under both the black home jersey and the white road jersey. That is also why the all-black alternate, worn for Iowa State and Ohio State this year, is a step down. Take the gold pants away and Iowa looks like everyone else's blackout.

## 7. Washington: B+

Metallic gold helmet with the purple W, purple jersey with white numbers outlined in gold, gold pants with a purple and white stripe that matches the helmet stripe. Purple and gold is hard to get wrong, and the June 2025 adidas refresh got the details right by bringing back the gold outline on the numbers. The road look pairs the white jersey with purple pants, and there are no white pants in the core set. Good. The black Dawgs alternate, black with DAWGS in gold script, is the third blackout look in three years. Drop it.

## 6. Oregon: B+

This will make people mad. Oregon's best single combinations are as good as anything in the sport. The all-green look against Portland State, with a chrome green helmet over green jersey and green pants, is a great uniform. The trouble is the set. The Ducks pick the helmet, jersey and pants separately, post a new "uniform combo" every week (they are on Game 10), and the result is a team with no home uniform. Mummy Mode 2.0 at Ohio State was a dark chrome helmet with a white jersey and black pants. Fans cannot attach to a uniform they cannot predict.

## 5. UCLA: A-

Powder blue jersey with gold numbers, the gold UCLA shoulder loop edged in white, gold pants and a metallic gold helmet with the blue UCLA script. Blue and gold is one of the great color schemes in sports, and UCLA uses one helmet and gold pants nearly every week. The ding is the shoulder loop. The current Jordan Brand template cuts it off at the seam instead of wrapping it under the arm, which shrinks the most famous stripe in college football. The 1954 throwback, a lighter powder blue with white shoulder stripes, is better than the primary, and UCLA wore it to beat Michigan State 28-10.

## 4. Penn State: A-

A white helmet with one navy stripe and no logo. A navy jersey with white numbers at home, a white jersey with navy numbers on the road, white pants with both, and no name on the back. No alternates announced for 2026. The plainness is the whole brand, and it works because Penn State commits to it every single week. It loses a notch because navy and white with nothing else can look flat on TV, especially the road set.

## 3. Ohio State: A-

Silver helmet covered in buckeye leaf stickers, scarlet jersey, silver pants. The helmet is the only one in the sport that gets more decorated as the season goes on. For 2026 the Buckeye Stripe came back to the sleeve in metallic silver to match the helmet, which ties the whole set together. Ohio State has three alternates, all scarlet, all black and all white, and through six games it wore none of them. That is discipline. It sits behind Michigan because the silver pants make the scarlet home look slightly gray next to Michigan's maize.

## 2. Michigan: A

The winged helmet is the best helmet in college football. Maize wings on navy, in use since 1938, over a navy jersey with maize numbers and maize pants at home, and a white jersey with the same maize pants on the road. That base set is an A+. Michigan lost points this year by going off script: an all-maize uniform against Western Michigan, blue over blue for the Stripe Out against Iowa, and navy pants with the navy jersey against Indiana. The maize pants are what make Michigan look like Michigan. Keep them on.

## 1. USC: A

Cardinal jersey with gold numbers, gold pants, a cardinal helmet with a gold stripe. On the road, a white jersey with cardinal numbers and the same gold pants. That is the entire wardrobe, and USC has kept it that way for decades. The gold pants under both jerseys are why the home and road sets read as one team. No alternates announced, no blackout, no costume. USC wore the cardinal at the Coliseum on Halloween and beat No. 2 Ohio State 30-18. Best uniform in the Big Ten.

## The Bottom Line

The best uniforms in the Big Ten have one thing in common: they barely change. USC, Ohio State, Michigan and Penn State each have a helmet you can recognize from the upper deck and the discipline to wear it every week. The bottom of the list is crowded with teams that either have nothing to recognize (Rutgers) or keep adding pieces until the base set disappears (Maryland, Northwestern, Illinois). Oregon shows the cost. The Ducks have the best individual combinations in the conference and still finish sixth, because nobody can tell you what an Oregon uniform is.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best uniform in the Big Ten?**
USC, graded A. A cardinal jersey with gold numbers, gold pants with both the home and road jerseys, a cardinal helmet with a gold stripe, and no alternates. Michigan is second, also an A.

**What is the worst uniform in the Big Ten?**
Rutgers, graded C-. The scarlet jersey with a block RUTGERS wordmark and a block R helmet is generic. The best thing Rutgers wore this year was a one-game 9/11 tribute helmet.

**Where does Oregon rank?**
Sixth of 18, with a B+. Oregon's single combinations, like the all-green look against Portland State, are great. But the Ducks pick a new combination every week, so they have no home uniform at all.

**Where does Indiana rank?**
Ninth, with a B. The defending national champions wear a clean crimson and white set with the black outlines removed in 2024. It is correct and boring, and they have not worn an alternate jersey in three seasons.

**Does Michigan have the best helmet in the Big Ten?**
Yes. The maize winged helmet on navy has been in use since 1938 and is the best helmet in college football. Michigan ranks second, even after wearing all maize, blue over blue and navy pants this year.

**Which Big Ten teams have no alternate uniforms in 2026?**
USC, Penn State and Indiana have not announced an alternate jersey for 2026. Ohio State has three, but had not worn any of them through six games.

**Which Big Ten teams changed their uniforms for 2026?**
Nebraska had the biggest change, a full adidas redesign with a new anvil and crop-row number font. Ohio State added a metallic silver Buckeye Stripe to the sleeve, Indiana added a trident at the collar, Minnesota brought back its chrome gold helmet and Michigan State added the MSUFCU patch.
