Thanksgiving is the one day a year the whole country watches the same three games, and the Lions and Cowboys have hosted it for decades. That makes the holiday a uniform showcase. We picked the eight best looks ever worn in an NFL Thanksgiving week game and ranked them, counting down from 8 to 1.

Every uniform below was worn on Thanksgiving, apart from the Rams' Fearsome White, which gets its first game on Thanksgiving Eve this Wednesday. For each one: the helmet, jersey and pants in plain colors, the Thanksgivings it was worn, our take and a grade.

**How we graded.** These are Thanksgiving grades: each uniform judged against the other seven, on the look itself and on what it means on the holiday. Two of these have everyday grades in our team rankings that run higher (the Cowboys' Classic is an A in [every Cowboys jersey ranked](/stories/cowboys-jerseys-2026-ranked), and Fearsome White is an A- in [every Rams jersey ranked](/stories/rams-jerseys-2026-ranked)). Against the best Thanksgiving uniforms ever, the room is tougher.

## 8. Panthers All Blue (2015): C

<img src="/images/posts/best-nfl-thanksgiving-uniforms-bracket-2026/panthers-all-blue.jpg" alt="Carolina Panthers linebacker in the all-blue Color Rush uniform: silver helmet with a black facemask, Panther blue jersey with white numbers, Panther blue pants and blue socks" style="width:100%;max-width:520px;border-radius:10px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto 0;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">Panthers all blue. Photo via the team: <a href="https://www.panthers.com/photos/panthers-color-rush-uniforms-16333300" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Carolina Panthers</a></p>

**The colors:** silver helmet, Panther blue jersey with white numbers, Panther blue pants, blue socks. Blue from the shoulders to the shoes.

**When it was worn:** Thanksgiving 2015 at Dallas, one of the first Color Rush games, per [Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/nfl/2015/11/20/thanksgiving-color-rush-jerseys-cowboys-panthers). Carolina won 33-14 to stay unbeaten and went on to reach the Super Bowl that season.

**Our take:** The game was great. The uniform looks like a practice set somebody wore by accident. Panther blue is a good accent color, and the regular Panthers uniform uses it well. Spread over every inch of the body, it turns into one flat wash of blue with a silver helmet sitting on top that does not match anything below it. It earned a spot here because it is part of the best Color Rush matchup Thanksgiving ever had. On its own, it is the weakest look of the eight.

## 7. Lions Wolf Gray (2019, 2022): C+

<img src="/images/posts/best-nfl-thanksgiving-uniforms-bracket-2026/lions-wolf-gray.jpg" alt="Detroit Lions defensive back in the wolf gray Color Rush uniform: silver helmet with the blue leaping lion, dark gray jersey with white numbers trimmed in Honolulu blue and blue sleeve stripes, gray pants" style="width:100%;max-width:520px;border-radius:10px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto 0;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">Lions wolf gray. Photo via the team: <a href="https://x.com/Lions/status/1595609650124099585" target="_blank" rel="noopener">@Lions</a></p>

**The colors:** silver helmet with the blue leaping lion, a dark steel gray (anthracite) jersey with white numbers trimmed in Honolulu blue and blue sleeve stripes, gray pants, per the [Lions](https://www.detroitlions.com/news/lions-to-wear-alternate-uniforms-this-season-19026253).

**When it was worn:** Thanksgiving 2019 against the Bears and Thanksgiving 2022 against the Bills, per [CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/nfl/news/lions-choose-not-to-wear-traditional-uniform-on-thanksgiving-in-favor-of-this-controversial-choice).

**Our take:** Detroit has the best color in football, and on the biggest day of its year it twice chose gray. The set looks sharp in a photo and fine on a Sunday in October. On Thanksgiving, when the whole country is tuned in to see the Lions, Honolulu blue should be the first thing on the screen. The blue trim on the numbers is the one part that works. Some fans love this uniform. We think it is a good alternate worn on the wrong day.

## 6. Rams Fearsome White (Thanksgiving Eve 2026): B

<img src="/images/posts/best-nfl-thanksgiving-uniforms-bracket-2026/rams-fearsome-white.jpg" alt="Los Angeles Rams Fearsome White alternate uniform: royal blue helmet with white horns and a white facemask, white jersey with royal blue numbers and a royal stripe over the shoulder, white pants" style="width:100%;max-width:520px;border-radius:10px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto 0;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">Rams Fearsome White. Photo via the team: <a href="https://www.therams.com/photos/exclusive-photos-rams-fearsome-alternate-uniform-unveiled-2026" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Los Angeles Rams</a></p>

**The colors:** royal blue helmet with white horns and a white facemask, a white jersey with royal blue numbers and a royal stripe that runs over the shoulder like a horn, white pants.

**When it was worn:** once. Wednesday, November 25, 2026, against the Packers, the first NFL game ever played on Thanksgiving Eve, on Netflix. The Rams won 26-13, wearing it as announced. Every detail is in [what the Rams are wearing on Thanksgiving Eve](/stories/packers-rams-thanksgiving-eve-uniforms-2026).

**Our take:** This might be the best-designed uniform on the whole list, and it still lands sixth. The shoulder stripe is the one idea in the Rams closet nobody else could wear, and the white facemask is a detail we have wanted from this team for years. It also has zero Thanksgivings behind it. Every uniform above it has a holiday game, a score and a memory attached. Fearsome White has one Wednesday night. The black outline on the numbers and the black swoosh still bother us too. It looked as good as we hoped on Wednesday. Bring it back next year, and it moves up.

## 5. Bears Orange Throwback (2004): B

<img src="/images/posts/best-nfl-thanksgiving-uniforms-bracket-2026/bears-orange-2004.jpg" alt="Chicago Bears defensive lineman number 93 in the orange throwback jersey with navy numbers, navy helmet and white pants, against the Cowboys on Thanksgiving 2004" style="width:100%;max-width:520px;border-radius:10px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto 0;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">Bears orange. Photo via the team: <a href="https://www.chicagobears.com/photos/bears-in-orange-jerseys-through-the-years" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Chicago Bears</a></p>

**The colors:** navy helmet, an orange jersey with navy numbers outlined in white and navy sleeve stripes, white pants with an orange and navy side stripe, navy socks with orange stripes.

**When it was worn:** Thanksgiving 2004 at Dallas. Chicago wore orange throwbacks honoring its 1933 and 1934 teams, per the [Chicago Bears](https://www.chicagobears.com/news/chalktalk/chalk-talk-what-s-bears-record-in-orange-jerseys), against a Cowboys team in the first Thanksgiving appearance of its 1960s throwback. Dallas won 21-7. It is still the last time the Bears wore orange jerseys on the road, per the [team](https://www.chicagobears.com/news/chalktalk/chalk-talk-when-was-last-time-bears-wore-orange).

**Our take:** Orange against navy on national TV on Thanksgiving is about as loud as an NFL holiday has ever looked, and we mean that as a compliment. The Bears are a navy team, and the orange jersey is a reminder of how good their secondary color is. It ranks fifth because it is a one-game memory from more than 20 years ago, and because the Bears have worn orange at home plenty of times since, so the shock is gone. Chicago played at Detroit on Thanksgiving 2026 in white over navy pants and won 39-35. Next time, bring the orange back to Ford Field.

## 4. Packers 1967 Throwback (2003): B

<img src="/images/posts/best-nfl-thanksgiving-uniforms-bracket-2026/packers-1967-throwback-2003.jpg" alt="Green Bay Packers quarterback Brett Favre, number 4, raising a fist in the 1967 throwback uniform at Detroit on Thanksgiving 2003: gold helmet with the G and a gray facemask, white jersey with green and gold sleeve stripes, gold pants" style="width:100%;max-width:520px;border-radius:10px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto 0;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">Packers 1967. Photo via the team: <a href="https://www.packers.com/photos/throwback-thursday-packers-showcase-alternate-uniforms" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Green Bay Packers</a></p>

**The colors:** gold helmet with the G and a gray facemask instead of green, a white jersey with green numbers and three broad green sleeve stripes split by two gold ones, gold lace-up pants with thinner side stripes, and green socks with white and gold stripes, per [Packers.com](https://www.packers.com/news/packers-will-don-throwback-uniforms-against-detroit-2474380).

**When it was worn:** Thanksgiving 2003 at Detroit, honoring Vince Lombardi's 1967 team, the one that won the Ice Bowl and Super Bowl II. The Lions wore uniforms in the style of their 1934 team that day and won 22-14.

**Our take:** Here is the problem. The Packers' 1967 throwback is nice, but it does not look very different from their normal uniform. The gray facemask and the bolder sleeve stripes are the whole change, and from the couch they are easy to miss. That says something good about the everyday Packers uniform, and it is why this sits fourth. What lifts it this high is the game: a 1967 Packers throwback against a 1934-style Lions throwback, on Thanksgiving, is one of the best uniform matchups the holiday has ever had.

## 3. Cowboys Double-Star White (2015): B+

<img src="/images/posts/best-nfl-thanksgiving-uniforms-bracket-2026/cowboys-double-star-white.jpg" alt="Dallas Cowboys player number 88 in the 2015 Double-Star white Color Rush uniform: silver helmet with a navy and white center stripe, white jersey with navy shoulders carrying white-outlined stars and navy numbers, white pants" style="width:100%;max-width:520px;border-radius:10px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto 0;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">Cowboys Double-Star white. Photo via the team: <a href="https://www.dallascowboys.com/news/cowbuzz-cowboys-unveil-new-colorrush-uniform-for-thanksgiving-day-game-393151" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Dallas Cowboys</a></p>

**The colors:** the regular silver helmet with the navy star, a mostly white jersey with navy shoulders carrying stars and navy numbers, and white pants with navy piping, per the [Dallas Cowboys](https://www.dallascowboys.com/news/cowbuzz-cowboys-unveil-new-colorrush-uniform-for-thanksgiving-day-game-393151), who called it a nod to the Double-Star jersey of the 1990s.

**When it was worn:** Thanksgiving 2015 against Carolina, the other half of that Color Rush game. The original Double-Star jersey debuted on Thanksgiving too, in 1994 against the Packers, the day backup Jason Garrett led Dallas to a 42-31 win.

**Our take:** The navy shoulder yoke is what makes it. An all-white uniform usually washes out on TV, and this one has a dark band across the top with two big stars that you can read from the upper deck. It is the best Color Rush uniform the NFL ever made, and it is also why this list has a cutoff at third. Dallas lost 33-14 in it, everything below the shoulders is one shade of white, and the silver helmet was a leftover the uniform was never designed around. It is a great uniform. The two above it are great Thanksgiving uniforms.

## 2. Cowboys 1960s Classic Throwback: A-

<img src="/images/posts/best-nfl-thanksgiving-uniforms-bracket-2026/cowboys-classic.jpg" alt="Dallas Cowboys 1960s Classic throwback: white helmet with a single navy star and a gray facemask, navy jersey with white shoulders carrying stars, white pants with navy stripes" style="width:100%;max-width:520px;border-radius:10px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto 0;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">Cowboys 1960s Classic. Photo via the team: <a href="https://x.com/dallascowboys/status/1993428379585200234" target="_blank" rel="noopener">@dallascowboys</a></p>

**The colors:** white helmet with a single navy star and a gray facemask, a navy jersey with oversized stars on white shoulders, and white pants with two navy stripes. It copies the uniform Dallas wore from 1960 through 1964.

**When it was worn:** every Thanksgiving from 2004 through 2012, per the [Dallas Cowboys](https://www.dallascowboys.com/news/cowboys-unveil-alternate-helmet-for-2022-season), including Tony Romo's five-touchdown game against Tampa Bay in 2006. The NFL's one-helmet rule shelved it from 2013 through 2021. It came back on Thanksgiving 2022 against the Giants and has been the Thanksgiving uniform every year since: 14 Thanksgivings in all, counting 2026. It was on again on Thanksgiving 2026 against the Eagles, who won 39-38 on a 59-yard field goal with two seconds left: the Classic's first Thanksgiving loss since its 2022 return, so it is 4-1 on the holiday in that run. [What the Cowboys and Eagles wore](/stories/cowboys-eagles-thanksgiving-what-they-wore-2026).

**Our take:** No team owns a holiday the way Dallas owns Thanksgiving in this uniform. Fourteen times on the biggest stage of the regular season, and nobody pictures the day without the white helmet anymore. Taking the silver away leaves the cleanest Cowboys look there is: three colors, big stars, block numbers. It is our favorite Cowboys uniform. It finishes second for one reason. The Cowboys turned an old uniform into a tradition. The Lions' throwback was already part of one.

## 1. Lions 1934 Throwback: A-

<img src="/images/posts/best-nfl-thanksgiving-uniforms-bracket-2026/lions-1934-throwback.jpg" alt="Detroit Lions 1934 throwback uniform: plain silver helmet with no logo, solid Honolulu blue jersey with silver block numbers and no sleeve stripes, silver pants" style="width:100%;max-width:520px;border-radius:10px;display:block;margin:1.2em auto 0;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">Lions 1934. Photo via the team: <a href="https://x.com/Lions/status/1993504228342694090" target="_blank" rel="noopener">@Lions</a></p>

**The colors:** a silver helmet with no logo, a solid Honolulu blue jersey with silver block numbers, no sleeve stripes and no shoulder numbers, and silver pants. It copies what Detroit wore in 1934, its first season in the city. (One caveat we always add: 1935 card art shows leather-colored helmets, so the plain silver shell is a modern take.)

**When it was worn:** Thanksgiving 2017 against the Vikings, per the [Detroit Lions](https://www.detroitlions.com/news/lions-to-wear-alternate-uniforms-this-season-19026253), 2018 against the Bears and 2020 against the Texans, per the Lions' own game-day images and the Gridiron Uniform Database, and against the Packers in 2023 and 2025, per [WILX](https://www.wilx.com/2025/11/26/lions-return-classic-throwback-look-thanksgiving-game/). Green Bay won the 2025 game 31-24. Detroit lost all five ([the full record](/stories/lions-record-in-throwbacks-on-thanksgiving)). It was not worn in 2026: the Lions wore Honolulu blue with silver pants and the silver helmet against the Bears and lost 39-35.

**Our take:** This is the best Thanksgiving uniform ever because of where it comes from. The Lions' Thanksgiving game began on November 29, 1934, against the Bears, and this is a copy of what the host team, the one that started the whole tradition, wore that season. No other look on the list ties back to the first game like that. It is also just a great uniform: one blue, one silver, no stripes, no logo, nothing to take away. It stops at an A- because Detroit will not commit to it. It has been worn on five of the ten Thanksgivings since 2017, and Detroit passed on it again in 2026, against the Bears, of all teams. Wear it every year and it is an A. The whole story: [why do the Lions wear 1934 throwbacks on Thanksgiving](/stories/why-do-the-lions-wear-1934-throwbacks-on-thanksgiving).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best NFL Thanksgiving uniform ever?**

The Lions' 1934 throwback, in our ranking: a plain Honolulu blue jersey with silver numbers, silver pants and a silver helmet with no logo. It copies the uniform from the year Detroit's Thanksgiving game began. The Cowboys' 1960s Classic is second. The fan vote is in our [Thanksgiving uniforms bracket](/stories/best-nfl-thanksgiving-uniforms-bracket-2026).

**What is the worst uniform on the list?**

The Panthers' all-blue Color Rush from Thanksgiving 2015. It ranks eighth of eight with a C. The game was memorable; head-to-toe Panther blue was too much.

**What do the Cowboys wear on Thanksgiving?**

Their 1960s Classic throwback: a white helmet with a single navy star, a navy jersey with stars on white shoulders, and white pants with navy stripes. They wore it every Thanksgiving from 2004 through 2012 and have worn it every Thanksgiving since 2022.

**What are the Cowboys wearing on Thanksgiving 2026?**

The 1960s Classic, against the Eagles on Thursday, November 26, as on the uniform schedule Dallas published on September 2, confirmed from ESPN's game photos. The Eagles won 39-38, the Classic's first Thanksgiving loss since 2022 (4-1). Details: [what the Cowboys and Eagles wore](/stories/cowboys-eagles-thanksgiving-what-they-wore-2026).

**What do the Lions wear on Thanksgiving?**

It changes. Detroit has worn its 1934 throwback (2017, 2018, 2020, 2023, 2025), its wolf gray Color Rush (2019, 2022), all blue (2024) and its regular home uniform (2021). In 2026 it was Honolulu blue with silver pants and the silver helmet against the Bears, not the throwback, in a 39-35 loss. Details: [what the Lions wore on Thanksgiving 2026](/stories/lions-bears-thanksgiving-uniforms-2026).

**Why do the Lions wear 1934 throwbacks on Thanksgiving?**

The throwback copies Detroit's first-season uniform from 1934, the same year the Lions started their Thanksgiving game, against the Bears on November 29, 1934. Full story: [why do the Lions wear 1934 throwbacks on Thanksgiving?](/stories/why-do-the-lions-wear-1934-throwbacks-on-thanksgiving)

**Why do NFL teams wear throwbacks on Thanksgiving?**

Thanksgiving football is the oldest tradition in the NFL, and the two hosts, Detroit (since 1934) and Dallas (since 1966), use old uniforms to match it. The games also draw some of the biggest TV audiences of the season. The full history: [why do NFL teams wear throwback uniforms on Thanksgiving?](/stories/why-do-nfl-teams-wear-throwbacks-on-thanksgiving)

**Why did the Cowboys stop wearing their throwback from 2013 to 2021?**

The NFL did not allow teams to wear a second helmet in those years, and the throwback needs a white helmet instead of the usual silver one. The league allowed alternate helmets again starting in 2022.

**What is the Cowboys Double-Star jersey?**

A white jersey with navy stars on the shoulders that debuted on Thanksgiving 1994 against the Packers. Dallas brought back an all-white version for the Color Rush game against Carolina on Thanksgiving 2015, which ranks third here.

**What did the Cowboys and Panthers wear on Thanksgiving 2015?**

Dallas wore all white with navy shoulders and stars, and Carolina wore all Panther blue, in one of the first Color Rush games. Carolina won 33-14.

**Have the Bears ever worn orange on Thanksgiving?**

Yes, once: at Dallas on Thanksgiving 2004, in throwbacks honoring the 1933 and 1934 Bears. Dallas won 21-7, and it is still the last time the Bears wore orange jerseys on the road.

**What throwback did the Packers wear on Thanksgiving?**

Two of them, both at Detroit. In 2001 Green Bay wore a 1939 throwback with plain gold helmets and white jerseys with no stripes. In 2003 it wore a 1967 throwback with green and gold sleeve stripes, lace-up gold pants and a gray facemask.

**What did the Rams wear on Thanksgiving Eve?**

Fearsome White: a royal blue helmet with white horns and a white facemask, a white jersey with royal numbers and a royal stripe over the shoulder, and white pants. It was the uniform's only game of 2026, against the Packers on Wednesday, November 25, and the Rams won 26-13.

**Have the Lions ever worn gray on Thanksgiving?**

Yes, twice: the wolf gray Color Rush against the Bears in 2019 and against the Bills in 2022.

**Do players wear a special patch on Thanksgiving?**

Yes. Players in the three Thanksgiving Day games wear the [John Madden Thanksgiving patch](/stories/john-madden-thanksgiving-patch-2026), worn every Thanksgiving since 2023.
