## Tigers at Rangers: Globe Life Field Roof on July 4th

The Rangers host the Tigers at 4:05 PM ET (3:05 PM local) in Arlington, and the Globe Life Field roof will almost certainly be closed. The Rangers close the roof any time the temperature sits outside the 65 to 80 degree window, and an early-July afternoon in North Texas blows past that before the gates even open. Globe Life Field has stayed shut for 80 percent or more of Rangers home games every season since it opened, and summer day games are the most automatic closures on the calendar. The upside: 72 degree air conditioning on a 95 degree holiday.

Day-of confirmation: the [@GLFroof account on X](https://x.com/GLFroof) posts the roof status before every Rangers home game. Our full [Globe Life Field roof guide](/stories/rangers-roof-open-globe-life-field-2026) has the complete 2026 roof rules and history.

## Rays at Astros: Daikin Park Roof on July 4th

The Astros host the Rays at 7:10 PM ET (6:10 PM local), and the Daikin Park roof will be closed. Houston in July is the exact weather the roof exists for: mid-90s heat, tropical humidity, and pop-up thunderstorms. The Astros open the roof for roughly 9 percent of home games, almost all of them in April and late September, and a July evening start does not come close to qualifying. Expect the roof shut and the ballpark at a controlled 73 degrees.

Day-of confirmation: the Astros post the roof status on the [@astros account on X](https://twitter.com/astros) before each home game. Our full [Daikin Park roof guide](/stories/daikin-park-roof-open-astros-2026) covers the rules, the rename from Minute Maid Park, and the roof-open windows.

## Blue Jays at Mariners: T-Mobile Park Roof on July 4th

Seattle is the one place on the July 4th slate where you should see open sky. The Mariners host the Blue Jays at 4:10 PM ET (1:10 PM local), and T-Mobile Park's roof is built differently from the other six: it is an umbrella, not a sealed dome. It covers the field without enclosing it, and the Mariners only roll it out for rain, not heat. Early July is the start of Seattle's famously dry stretch, so a dry holiday forecast means an open roof for the afternoon game. If a rare Fourth of July shower rolls through, the roof closes in about 10 minutes and the game plays on.

Day-of confirmation: the [@Mariners account on X](https://twitter.com/Mariners) and the T-Mobile Park gameday feed note the roof status when weather is in play. Our full [T-Mobile Park roof guide](/stories/tmobile-park-roof-open-mariners-2026) explains the umbrella design and how often it actually closes.

## Brewers at D-backs: Chase Field Roof on July 4th

The nightcap of the holiday slate sends the Brewers to Phoenix at 9:40 PM ET (6:40 PM local), and the Chase Field roof will be closed. Phoenix on July 4th routinely sits between 105 and 110 degrees, and even after sunset the desert holds triple-digit heat into the evening. The Diamondbacks close the roof and run the air conditioning for essentially the entire summer, reserving roof-open games for April, May, and late September. No retractable roof call in baseball is easier than Chase Field in July.

Day-of confirmation: the D-backs note roof status via the [@Dbacks account on X](https://twitter.com/Dbacks) and the gameday guide. Our full [Chase Field roof guide](/stories/chase-field-retractable-roof-diamondbacks) covers how the roof works and when it opens.

And [Rogers Centre](/stories/rogers-centre-roof-open-blue-jays-2026), the roof that opens more than any other in MLB, is dark until the Blue Jays return home.

Three of the four roof-park games on July 4th, 2026 should be played indoors. Globe Life Field, Daikin Park, and Chase Field all close for summer heat as a matter of policy, and a holiday afternoon in Texas, Houston, and Phoenix is the policy's whole reason for existing. T-Mobile Park in Seattle is the exception: the umbrella roof stays retracted in dry weather, and early July is Seattle's driest stretch of the year, so the Blue Jays and Mariners should get a true open-air Fourth of July game. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which MLB stadiums with retractable roofs host games on July 4th, 2026?**

Four of the seven: Globe Life Field (Tigers at Rangers, 4:05 PM ET), T-Mobile Park (Blue Jays at Mariners, 4:10 PM ET), Daikin Park (Rays at Astros, 7:10 PM ET), and Chase Field (Brewers at Diamondbacks, 9:40 PM ET). Rogers Centre, American Family Field, and loanDepot Park are empty because the Blue Jays, Brewers, and Marlins are on the road.

**Will the Rangers game on July 4th be indoors?**

Almost certainly yes. The Rangers close the Globe Life Field roof whenever the temperature is outside 65 to 80 degrees, and a July 4th afternoon in Arlington is far above that window. Expect a climate-controlled 72 degrees inside. The @GLFroof account on X posts the official call before first pitch.

**Will the Astros play under a closed roof on July 4th?**

Yes. Houston's July heat and humidity keep the Daikin Park roof shut; the Astros open it for only about 9 percent of home games, nearly all in April and late September. The Rays-Astros holiday game will be played in air conditioning.

**Will the Mariners roof be open for the July 4th game against the Blue Jays?**

Expect it open. T-Mobile Park's roof is a rain umbrella, not a heat dome, and the Mariners leave it retracted in dry weather. Early July is the start of Seattle's dry season, so barring a rare holiday shower, the 1:10 PM local start should be open air.

**Is the Chase Field roof ever open in July?**

Effectively no. Phoenix sits at 105 degrees or hotter on a typical July 4th, and the Diamondbacks keep the roof closed with the air conditioning running all summer. Roof-open games at Chase Field happen in April, May, and late September.

**What time do MLB games start on July 4th, 2026?**

The 15-game slate runs from 11:05 AM ET (Pirates at Nationals in Washington) to 10:10 PM ET (Padres at Dodgers in Los Angeles). The national TV window is 8 PM ET, with Cardinals-Cubs or Mets-Braves on Baseball Night in America depending on your market.
