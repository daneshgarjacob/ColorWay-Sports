So what do MLB teams wear in the playoffs? Mostly the classics. In October the home team almost always wears its home white (or its pinstripes, or its cream) and the road team wears its road gray. The alternates that fill up a September slate mostly go back in the closet. Mostly. Every October a few clubs break the pattern, and those are the jerseys people remember.

The 2026 postseason opens with the Wild Card Series on **September 29**. The Division Series runs October 3 to 10, the League Championship Series October 11 to 20, and the World Series starts **October 23**, with a Game 7, if needed, on Halloween night. Below is how the October uniform call actually works, whether City Connects are allowed, what the postseason patches look like, and what each likely playoff team has been wearing lately. We will log every postseason game, both teams and both jerseys, on our [2026 MLB uniform tracker](/stories/mlb-uniform-tracker-2026).

## Who Picks the Jersey

The home team picks first. Baseball's rulebook lets a league require white at home and a different color on the road, and that is the old default that October still leans on. The home club chooses its jersey, and the visitors pick something that contrasts with it. If the home team goes white, the road team has the whole wardrobe to choose from: gray, a color alternate, even a powder blue. If the home team goes dark, the visitors stay light.

Inside the clubhouse, the call usually comes from the manager and the players, with the equipment staff carrying it out. Superstition drives a lot of it. A team that wins in a jersey tends to keep wearing it, and a team that is losing looks for a reset.

The league also has a say. When the Phillies fell behind the Dodgers 2-0 in the 2025 NLDS, the players asked to wear their powder blues on the road for Game 3 in Los Angeles. Manager Rob Thomson told reporters the club had to go through "a bunch of hoops" with MLB to get it approved. So picking an October alternate is not quite as simple as pulling it off the rack. The club makes the request and the league signs off.

## Are City Connect Jerseys Allowed in the Postseason?

Yes, they are allowed. This one comes up every fall, and the answer has been the same for years. In 2021, with the Red Sox on a five-game win streak in their yellow City Connect, WEEI's Rob Bradford reported that MLB would let Boston wear it in the playoffs if the club chose to. In 2024, a source told On Pattison that the Phillies' City Connect was also eligible for October.

Allowed is not the same as worn, though. We have not found a single postseason game in which a team actually wore its City Connect. Most clubs treat the City Connect as a Friday night regular season look, and when October arrives they go back to the uniforms their history is built on. We would not bet on a City Connect in the 2026 playoffs either, but if one shows up, it will be the first entry on the tracker we highlight.

## The Postseason Patch

The October uniform changes are small, and most of them sit on the cap.

- **The postseason cap patch.** Every playoff team wears New Era's on-field postseason cap, which adds a patch with that year's MLB Postseason logo on the right side of the cap. The front stays the same team logo fans already know.
- **The World Series patch.** The two pennant winners add the World Series logo. The cap patch has been part of the Fall Classic since 1996, and it moved from the left side to the right side of the cap in 2016 to make room for the New Era flag. The jersey patch dates to 1987. Since 2023, when MLB started selling sleeve advertising, the World Series jersey patch has been a much smaller mark high on the chest instead of on the sleeve.
- **The sponsor.** Capital One is the World Series presenting sponsor, and its logo has been built into the World Series mark itself since 2022, which means it rides along on the patch. We are not fans of that, and we explain why in our [World Series logo history](/stories/world-series-logo-history-1986-2025).

The one thing you will not see is gold. The gold-trimmed uniforms belong to the defending champion, and they come out for Opening Day and ring night, not for October. The Dodgers, the champs the last two years, already did their gold for 2026, and we compared all three of their sets in our [Dodgers World Series gold uniform breakdown](/stories/dodgers-world-series-edition-uniforms-2026).

## The October Alternates People Remember

Because almost everyone plays it straight, the exceptions stick.

- **Astros orange, 2017.** Houston won Game 7 of the 2017 World Series at Dodger Stadium in the orange alternate tops, and the orange has carried a lucky-charm reputation in Houston ever since.
- **Phillies powder blue, 2022.** A rainout pushed Game 5 of the 2022 World Series to a Thursday, and Thursday at Citizens Bank Park means powder blue. The Phillies wore it in a World Series for the first time since 1983 and lost 3-2 to the Astros.
- **Phillies powder blue, 2025.** Down 2-0 to the Dodgers, the Phillies wore the powder blues on the road for NLDS Games 3 and 4, their first postseason road game in them since the 1983 World Series. They won Game 3 and then lost Game 4, 2-1 in 11 innings, to end their season.

## The Bottom Line

October is when baseball dresses like baseball. The home team picks, the visitors contrast, and most clubs go back to home whites and road grays. City Connects are allowed, and we have not found one ever worn in a playoff game. The real October changes are a postseason patch on the cap and, for the last two teams standing, a small World Series patch on the chest. The fun is in the exceptions: Houston's orange, Philadelphia's powder blue and, in 2026, Milwaukee's new powder blue road set. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What do MLB teams wear in the playoffs?**

Most teams wear their traditional uniforms in the postseason: home white, pinstripes or cream at home and road gray on the road. Alternates show up less often in October than in the regular season, though some clubs, like the Astros with their orange tops and the Phillies with their powder blues, have worn them in big postseason games.

**Who decides what jersey an MLB team wears in the postseason?**

The home team picks first, and the road team chooses a jersey that contrasts with it. Inside each club the manager and players have a big say, and MLB signs off on the choices. In 2025 the Phillies had to get league approval before wearing their powder blues on the road in the NLDS.

**Can MLB teams wear City Connect jerseys in the playoffs?**

Yes. MLB allowed the Red Sox to wear their yellow City Connect in the 2021 postseason, and the Phillies' City Connect was reported eligible in 2024. We have not found a postseason game where a team actually wore one.

**What patch do MLB teams wear in the postseason?**

Every playoff team wears a cap with that year's MLB Postseason logo patch on the right side. The two World Series teams add the World Series logo to the cap and a small World Series patch on the chest of the jersey, where it has been since 2023.

**Do the Phillies wear powder blue in the playoffs?**

Sometimes. The Phillies wore their powder blues at home in Game 5 of the 2022 World Series and on the road in Games 3 and 4 of the 2025 NLDS. They normally wear them for Thursday home games, and Game 3 of the 2026 Wild Card Series, if it is needed, falls on Thursday, October 1.

**When do the 2026 MLB playoffs start?**

The Wild Card Series starts September 29, 2026. The Division Series runs October 3 to 10, the League Championship Series October 11 to 20, and the World Series October 23 to 31.
