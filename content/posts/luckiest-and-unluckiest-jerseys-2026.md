**The luckiest jersey in sports right now is the Tampa Bay Lightning's blue home sweater: 9-0-0. The unluckiest is the Chicago Blackhawks' white road sweater: 0-8-1.**

*Updated Nov. 18 with games through Nov. 17.*

We log what every NFL, NBA and NHL team wears in every game, then match it to the final score. Nobody else keeps this. Here are the uniforms that have not lost yet this season, and the ones that have not won. All records run through Tuesday, Nov. 17. In the NFL, that is the end of Week 10.

## Quick Answers

- **Luckiest jersey in the NHL:** Lightning blue home, **9-0-0**.
- **Luckiest jersey in the NBA:** Timberwolves blue, **8-0**.
- **Luckiest uniform in the NFL:** Chiefs red jersey with white pants, **5-0**. The 49ers in red over gold are also **5-0**.
- **Unluckiest jersey in the NHL:** Blackhawks white road, **0-8-1**.
- **Unluckiest jersey in the NBA:** Wizards white, **0-7**.
- **Unluckiest jersey in the NFL:** Dolphins white, **0-8** (0-4 with aqua pants, 0-4 with white pants). The Cowboys in white over silver-green pants are **0-5**.
- **Best same-night matchup:** the Lightning in blue beat the Blackhawks in white 4-1 on Nov. 5. Both streaks kept going.
- **Streak that just ended:** the Rockets started 8-0 in red, then lost 128-110 at Miami in red on Monday, Nov. 16.
- **How we count:** NFL uniforms need 3 or more games to make the list. NBA and NHL jerseys need 4 or more.
- **Our take (opinion):** the Dolphins should stop wearing white.

## How We Count

Every record here comes from ColorWay Sports' game-by-game uniform log: the [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026), the [NBA uniform tracker](/stories/nba-uniform-tracker-2026-27) and the [NHL uniform tracker](/stories/nhl-uniform-tracker-2026-27). We record the look each team actually wore, then pair it with the final score. Every team's full record in every uniform is on our [uniform records page](/uniform-records).

In the NFL, a "uniform" is the jersey and pants together, because teams mix them. The Chiefs in red over white pants are one uniform. The Chiefs in white over red pants are another. In the NBA and NHL, a uniform is the jersey.

NFL teams play about once a week, so we set the bar at **3 games**. NBA and NHL teams play three or four times a week, so we set it at **4 games**. In the NHL, an overtime or shootout loss counts as a loss for "winless." A jersey with zero wins is winless.

## Our Take (Opinion)

Jerseys do not win games. Home jerseys come with home crowds, and good teams win in everything. The Lightning's blue and the Blackhawks' white are partly a home-and-road split.

But some of these are hard to explain away. The Dolphins are 0-8 in white and 1-0 in aqua. The Cowboys are 0-5 in their white and silver-green and 4-1 in everything else. The Wizards are 0-7 in white and 4-4 in the rest.

If we ran the Dolphins, we would stop wearing white for the rest of the season. It costs nothing to try. Players are superstitious, fans are superstitious, and 0-8 is a long time to keep doing the same thing.

**How many games does a uniform need to make this list?**

Three games in the NFL, where teams play once a week. Four games in the NBA and NHL, where teams play several times a week.

**Does an NHL overtime loss count as a loss here?**

It does not count as a win. We show NHL records as wins, losses and overtime losses. A sweater with zero wins is winless, even if it earned a point.

**In the NFL, does a different pants color count as a different uniform?**

Yes. We track the jersey and pants together, because that is what changes week to week. The Chiefs in red over white and the Chiefs in white over red are separate uniforms.
