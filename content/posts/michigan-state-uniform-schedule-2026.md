Michigan State wears green at home and white on the road, but the Spartans reveal a new helmet, jersey and pants combination almost every week, so the base colors are only half the answer. This is the full Michigan State 2026 uniform schedule: the green home set, the white road set, the Greek key helmets, the black alternate, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games in Pat Fitzgerald's first season. Two games in, the Spartans have worn green over white pants and then green head to toe. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

The current Nike set arrived in 2023 and built everything around a Greek key pattern: it runs down the center of the helmet, along the sleeves, and down the sides of the pants, where it shows in a lighter green on the green pants. The same launch introduced the black Shadow uniform. The one visible change for 2026 is sponsorship. Michigan State signed a 10-year patch deal with MSU Federal Credit Union in June, so the MSUFCU patch now sits on the jersey and the logo is printed on the helmet bumper. We have not seen a new 2026 alternate announced. The program posts a jersey reveal video before each game, and Big Ten home teams wear color, so road dates point to white and home dates come down to which helmet and pants go with the green.

**September 4 vs. Toledo.** ★ Confirmed from game photos and the program's reveal video: **green helmet, green jersey, white pants**, with the Greek key stripe down the center of the helmet and the new MSUFCU logo on the bumper. It was Pat Fitzgerald's first game as Michigan State's head coach, and the Spartans won 30-20 on a Friday night at Spartan Stadium. One calendar note: ESPN files this game under September 5 because its feed runs on UTC and an 8 p.m. Eastern kickoff lands after midnight there. The correct date is Friday, September 4.

**September 12 vs. Eastern Michigan.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **all green**, green helmet with the Greek key stripe, green jersey, and green pants with the Greek key running down the leg in a lighter green. It is the same all-green look Michigan State chose for the Michigan game in 2025. Eastern Michigan came in with gray helmets, white jerseys and gray pants, the combination the Eagles announced during the week. Michigan State won 35-7.

**September 19 at Notre Dame.** White expected. A 7:30 p.m. Eastern kickoff on NBC at Notre Dame Stadium, where the Irish wear navy at home, so the Spartans are the team in white. The open question is the helmet: Michigan State wore a green helmet over the white set on the road at USC and Nebraska last season. Our [Notre Dame 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/notre-dame-uniform-schedule-2026) tracks the Irish side.

**September 26 vs. Nebraska.** Green expected. The Big Ten opener at Spartan Stadium, 5 p.m. Eastern on BTN, with Nebraska in white. See the [Nebraska 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/nebraska-uniform-schedule-2026) for the Huskers.

**October 3 at Wisconsin.** White expected. It is Homecoming at Camp Randall Stadium, and the Badgers are expected in red. Kickoff time is still to be announced. The [Wisconsin 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/wisconsin-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Badgers' side.

**October 10 vs. Illinois.** Green expected. This is Michigan State's Homecoming, announced in April, with a kickoff no later than 5 p.m. Homecoming is the home date we are watching most closely for an alternate or a special helmet.

**October 17 vs. Northwestern.** Green expected. Fitzgerald coached Northwestern for 17 seasons, so this one comes with a storyline before anyone picks a uniform.

**October 24 at UCLA.** White expected. The Rose Bowl, with UCLA in its blue home jerseys. The [UCLA 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/ucla-uniform-schedule-2026) is watching this date for the Bruins' 1954 throwback.

**October 31.** Bye.

**November 7 at Michigan.** White expected. The Paul Bunyan Trophy game at Michigan Stadium, with Michigan in navy under the winged helmet. Our [Michigan 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/michigan-uniform-schedule-2026) covers the Wolverines.

**November 14 vs. Washington.** Green expected, with Washington in white. The [Washington 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/washington-huskies-uniform-schedule-2026) tracks the Huskies.

**November 20 vs. Oregon.** Green expected. A Friday night game at 8 p.m. Eastern on FOX, and Oregon's first trip to East Lansing since 2015. Oregon brings more combinations than anyone in the conference, so check our [Oregon 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/oregon-uniform-schedule-2026) for the Ducks.

**November 28 at Rutgers.** White expected. The regular season ends at SHI Stadium in Piscataway, kickoff time to be announced.

## The Bottom Line

Michigan State has used the green jersey and the Greek key green helmet in both games so far, first over white pants and then in the all-green look. The road half of the schedule is predictable: white at Notre Dame, Wisconsin, UCLA, Michigan and Rutgers, with the helmet the only real variable. The home dates are where this program experiments, and Homecoming against Illinois and the Friday night Oregon game are the two most likely places for the black set or something new. The grid above has every date, and we star each game as the combination is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Michigan State wearing this week?**

Michigan State plays at Notre Dame on Saturday, September 19, and white is expected because the Irish wear navy at home. The Spartans post a jersey reveal video before each game, and we will star the cell once the full combination is confirmed. Last week against Eastern Michigan, Michigan State wore all green.

**What are Michigan State's football uniforms for 2026?**

A green home jersey with white numerals and wordmark, a white road jersey with green numerals, and a black alternate jersey, all from Nike with a Greek key stripe on the sleeves and pants. The helmets are a green shell with a white Spartan head and a Greek key center stripe, and a white shell. New for 2026, the MSUFCU patch is on the jersey and the MSUFCU logo is on the helmet bumper.

**Does Michigan State have a new alternate uniform in 2026?**

Nothing new has been announced. The black alternate that debuted as the all-black Shadow set in 2023 and returned with a white helmet against Boston College in 2025 is the alternate we expect to see. Homecoming against Illinois on October 10 and the Friday night game against Oregon on November 20 are the dates we are watching.

**What did Michigan State wear against Eastern Michigan?**

All green: green helmet with the Greek key stripe, green jersey and green pants. Eastern Michigan wore gray helmets, white jerseys and gray pants. Michigan State won 35-7 at Spartan Stadium on September 12.

**What did Michigan State wear against Toledo?**

The green helmet, green jersey and white pants in the season opener on Friday, September 4. Michigan State beat Toledo 30-20 in Pat Fitzgerald's first game as head coach. ESPN lists the game on September 5 because its schedule feed runs on UTC.

**How many games does Michigan State play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven at Spartan Stadium and five on the road, with a bye on October 31. Two of the home games are on Friday nights, the Toledo opener and Oregon on November 20. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
