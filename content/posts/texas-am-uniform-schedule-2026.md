## The Texas A&M 2026 Uniform Sets

Texas A&M runs an adidas wardrobe built on three jerseys plus the camo alternate.

- **Maroon jersey.** The home look, and the one the Aggies wear for most games at Kyle Field.
- **White jersey.** The road set, worn at SEC venues where the home team wears color.
- **Black jersey.** A situational alternate that appears rarely and has not been assigned a 2026 date.
- **Honor and Support camo.** One game, October 3 against Arkansas.

One thing to understand before the table below: **SEC home teams wear colored jerseys and visitors wear white.** That convention is what makes the road games predictable and the home games worth watching, because the home dates are where an alternate can appear.

**September 5 vs. Missouri State, Kyle Field.** Season opener at home. Expect the maroon home set.

**September 12 vs. Arizona State, Kyle Field.** Second straight home game and a non-conference opponent with real name value. Maroon expected, though a home non-conference game is exactly the kind of slot a program uses for a change-up.

**September 19 vs. Kentucky, Kyle Field.** SEC opener at home. Maroon expected.

**September 26 at LSU, Baton Rouge.** First road trip, and a night game in Death Valley if it draws the window. LSU wears white at home for some games, which is the one road date on this schedule where A&M could end up in maroon. Watch this one.

**October 3 vs. Arkansas, Kyle Field.** ✅ **Confirmed: Honor and Support camo.** Camo jersey, camo helmet, maroon pants. The 150th anniversary game.

**October 10 at Missouri, Columbia.** Road. White expected.

**October 17 vs. The Citadel, Kyle Field.** Home non-conference game. Maroon expected.

**October 24 at Alabama, Tuscaloosa.** Road. White expected.

**October 31.** Bye week.

**November 7 at South Carolina, Columbia.** Road. White expected.

**November 14 vs. Tennessee, Kyle Field.** Home, and the biggest home game left on the board after Texas. Maroon expected, and a candidate for a maroon-on-maroon or throwback treatment if A&M has one held back.

**November 21 at Oklahoma, Norman.** Road. White expected.

**November 27 vs. Texas, Kyle Field.** The rivalry game, at home, the Friday after Thanksgiving. Maroon expected. If Texas A&M is holding anything back for one night, this is the night.

## What About the LSU Game?

It is the one genuinely uncertain date on the schedule. LSU has worn white at home for selected games, and when the home team goes white the visitor wears color. If that happens on September 26, Texas A&M would break out maroon on the road, which would be the only time all season the Aggies wear color away from Kyle Field. Nothing is confirmed yet and we will update this page when it is.
