**Northwestern basketball has its own Purple Gothic uniforms.** The men's team revealed them on Tuesday, November 24, with a set of photos shot in front of a floor-to-ceiling stained-glass window, and a post that says the uniforms are "inspired by the stained glass on campus." They debut on **Tuesday, December 1, against Wisconsin at Welsh-Ryan Arena**, 8 p.m. Central on Peacock and NBCSN.

The name is not new. Northwestern football revealed its own **Purple Gothics** on June 30, the first purple version of a Gothic look the school had only ever worn in black and white. Five months later, basketball has the same idea in the same colors. Here is what the new jersey looks like, how it lines up with the football set, and where the whole Gothic line came from.

## Quick Answers

- **When do the Purple Gothics debut?** Tuesday, December 1, 2026, against Wisconsin at Welsh-Ryan Arena in Evanston, 8 p.m. Central (9 p.m. Eastern) on Peacock and NBCSN.
- **What do they look like?** A purple jersey with **Northwestern** across the chest in gold Gothic script, gold numbers outlined in black, black trim around the arm openings with gold piping, and purple shorts.
- **What inspired them?** Northwestern says the stained glass on campus.
- **Who makes them?** Under Armour. Its logo sits in gold at the collar.
- **Is this the same as the football Purple Gothics?** Same name and same purple and gold, but a different design. Football's version was inspired by the Weber Arch and campus architecture; basketball's by stained glass.
- **Our grade:** A-.

## What The Purple Gothic Basketball Uniform Looks Like

Every detail below is from the eight photos in Northwestern's reveal post above.

- **Jersey color:** purple.
- **Chest:** **Northwestern** in gold Gothic blackletter script, straight across the chest.
- **Numbers:** gold, outlined in black, with sharp pointed ends cut like Gothic lettering. On the 4, the tips come to a point instead of a flat edge.
- **Back:** the player's name in gold capital letters over the same gold number outlined in black.
- **Trim:** black panels around the arm openings with thin gold piping, and a gold trim line around the collar.
- **Logos:** the Under Armour logo in gold at the center of the collar and the Big Ten logo in gold on the right chest.
- **Shorts:** purple. The shorts are shown in the full-length photos in the post.

The photos were taken inside, under a tall stained-glass window in purple, blue, green and gold. Northwestern's post does not name the building, so we will not guess at it here. The team's wording is only that the look was "inspired by the stained glass on campus."

What carries over:

- **The colors.** Purple with gold, and black only as an outline. Both sets drop the black base that defined every Gothic uniform before 2026.
- **The numbers.** Gold numerals outlined in black on both. On football they are a textured metallic gold; on basketball they are gold with the same black edge.
- **The lettering.** Both use Gothic blackletter script for the school name. Football splits it across the shoulders, Northwestern and University. Basketball puts Northwestern across the chest, where a basketball jersey has room for it.

What is different:

- **The inspiration.** Football's Purple Gothics, like every Gothic set since 2014, point to campus architecture, and specifically the Arnold Weber Arch printed on the back of the helmet. Basketball's point to the stained glass.
- **The extras.** Football has the helmet to work with: the gold stripe, the gold N and the arch. Basketball has no helmet, so all of the Gothic identity has to live in the script and the numbers.

One more football note. The announced debut for football's Purple Gothics was October 24 against Rutgers, and Northwestern wore a purple helmet, purple jersey and purple pants that day, a 42-14 win. As we noted in the [Northwestern 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/northwestern-uniform-schedule-2026), we could not confirm from the team's halftime graphic whether that jersey was the new Gothic alternate. The basketball debut date, at least, is clear.

## The History Of Northwestern's Gothic Uniforms

The Gothic line started with football in 2014. Northwestern posted "Northwestern Gothic. Coming 10.18.14." on October 6, 2014, and wore the set at Homecoming against Nebraska on October 18. It was all black, built by Under Armour around the Gothic architecture on campus. Then-coach Pat Fitzgerald put it simply when it was announced: "The gothic look is the gothic look on campus."

From there it spread across the athletic department:

- **2014:** Football debuts the black Gothics against Nebraska on October 18.
- **By 2020:** the black Gothics, known as the "Gothic Blacks," had been worn by men's and women's basketball, lacrosse, wrestling and football.
- **February 2020:** lacrosse and softball debut the all-white "Gothic Ice," which Northwestern called the newest edition of its Gothic uniform series.
- **2023:** the men's basketball Gothic jerseys pick up extra striping in a uniform update.
- **August 2024:** the men's basketball team shows off white Gothics on its offseason trip to Italy and Greece.
- **November 2024:** football brings the black Gothics to Wrigley Field ("Bringin' the Gothics to Wrigley").
- **June 30, 2026:** football reveals the Purple Gothics.
- **November 24, 2026:** men's basketball reveals its Purple Gothics.

For twelve years "Gothic" at Northwestern meant black. 2026 is the year it turned purple, first on the football field and now on the basketball court.

## Our Take

This is the right move, and it is better on a basketball jersey than on a football uniform. We put the original 2014 black Gothic on our list of the [10 worst college football uniforms ever](/stories/worst-college-football-uniforms-ever-ranked) because all of the detail disappeared from the stands and what was left was a team in black. Northwestern is purple. It is one of the few programs in the country that owns its color.

The Purple Gothics fix that. A basketball jersey is close to the camera, so the Gothic script and the pointed gold numbers actually read on a broadcast, and the purple keeps it looking like Northwestern from the upper deck. The stained-glass idea is a better story than "another alternate," and the photo shoot sells it. Our only knock is that the stained glass shows up in the photos more than in the uniform itself: we could not find a stained-glass pattern on the jersey in the reveal images. **Grade: A-.**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When will Northwestern wear the Purple Gothic basketball uniforms?**

The debut is Tuesday, December 1, 2026, against Wisconsin at Welsh-Ryan Arena in Evanston. Tip is 8 p.m. Central, 9 p.m. Eastern, on Peacock and NBCSN, per Northwestern's schedule.

**What do Northwestern's new Purple Gothic basketball jerseys look like?**

A purple jersey with Northwestern in gold Gothic script across the chest, gold numbers outlined in black with pointed Gothic-style ends, black trim around the arm openings with gold piping, a gold Under Armour logo at the collar and a gold Big Ten logo on the chest. The back has the player's name in gold over the number. The shorts are purple.

**What inspired Northwestern's Purple Gothic uniforms?**

Northwestern's post says the basketball uniforms are "inspired by the stained glass on campus." The reveal photos were shot in front of a large stained-glass window. The football Purple Gothics, revealed in June, were inspired by the Gothic architecture on campus and the Weber Arch.

**Who makes Northwestern's basketball uniforms?**

Under Armour. The Under Armour logo is on the collar of the new Purple Gothic jersey, and Under Armour also makes Northwestern's football uniforms, including the Gothic sets.

**Are the basketball Purple Gothics the same as the football Purple Gothics?**

No. They share the name, the purple and gold colors, Gothic script and gold numerals outlined in black. The football set adds a gold-striped helmet with the Weber Arch on the back and puts the Gothic script on the shoulders; the basketball jersey puts Northwestern across the chest.

**When did Northwestern first wear Gothic uniforms?**

Football wore the first Northwestern Gothic uniform at Homecoming against Nebraska on October 18, 2014. It was all black, built around the campus architecture.

**Does Northwestern basketball have other Gothic uniforms?**

Yes. The men's and women's basketball teams have worn black Gothic uniforms, and the men's team showed off a white Gothic version on its trip to Italy and Greece in August 2024. The purple set is the newest.

**Who does Northwestern play on December 1?**

Wisconsin, at Welsh-Ryan Arena, at 8 p.m. Central on Peacock and NBCSN.

**Why does Northwestern use Gothic lettering on its uniforms?**

Because of the campus. Northwestern's Evanston campus is known for its Gothic buildings, and the Gothic uniforms have been tied to that architecture since football introduced them in 2014. Coach Pat Fitzgerald said at the time: "The gothic look is the gothic look on campus."

**Did Northwestern football wear the Purple Gothics?**

The announced debut was October 24, 2026, against Rutgers. Northwestern wore a purple helmet, purple jersey and purple pants that day and won 42-14. We could not confirm from the team's halftime graphic whether the jersey was the Gothic alternate. Every game is in our [Northwestern 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/northwestern-uniform-schedule-2026).

**What are Northwestern's colors?**

Purple and white are the everyday colors, with gold and black used on the Gothic uniforms. The new Purple Gothics in football and basketball are purple with gold, with black only as trim and outlines.
