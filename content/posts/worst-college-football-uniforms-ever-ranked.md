**College football has the best uniforms in sports and the worst ones.** A program can wear the same classic look for 60 years, then hand it over to a shoe company for one Saturday. These are my 10 worst college football uniforms ever, counting down to the worst.

Every uniform here was worn in a real game or is shown from the school or brand that made it. For each: what it was, when it was worn, and why it ranks where it does. Dates and details come from the schools' own announcements and coverage from the time.

## Quick Answers

- **What is the worst college football uniform ever?** My No. 1 is Texas Tech's red-to-black fade from November 15, 2014, against Oklahoma.
- **Which uniforms made the top 10?** Counting down: Northwestern 2014, Ohio State 2016, Georgia Tech 2012, Nebraska 2012, Notre Dame 2016, UCLA 2014, Florida 2017, Iowa State 2023, Michigan State 2019 and Texas Tech 2014.

## 10. Northwestern, 2014: Northwestern Gothic

<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/NUFBFamily/status/519172891183939584"></a></blockquote>

**What it was:** an all-black Under Armour set worn against Nebraska on October 18, 2014. The school built it around campus architecture: Gothic lettering, arches on the shoulders based on the windows of Deering Library, and ivy detailing on the shoulders and pants.

**Why it ranks 10th:** the idea is thoughtful, and that's why it's only 10th. But all of that detail disappears from the stands, and what's left is a team in black with hard-to-read lettering. Northwestern is purple. It's one of the few programs in the country that owns its color. [Northwestern uniform schedule 2026](/stories/northwestern-uniform-schedule-2026)

## 9. Ohio State, 2016: The 1916 Throwback

<img src="/images/posts/worst-college-football-uniforms-ever-ranked/ohio-state-2016.jpg" alt="Ohio State 2016 alternate uniform: scarlet and gray striped jersey with number 16, gray pants and a matte black helmet held at the player's side" style="display: block; width: 100%; max-width: 460px; margin: 1.25em auto 0.4em;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">via Ohio State Athletics</p>

**What it was:** a Nike throwback marking 100 years since the 1916 team, worn against Nebraska on November 5, 2016. Scarlet and gray striped jersey, gray pants and a matte black helmet. Ohio State won 62-3.

**Why it ranks 9th:** an old idea doesn't make a good uniform. The stripes look like a practice bib, and the matte black helmet has nothing to do with 1916 or with Ohio State. This program already has one of the best uniforms in the sport. [Ohio State uniform schedule 2026](/stories/ohio-state-uniform-schedule-2026)

## 8. Georgia Tech, 2012: The Honeycomb

<img src="/images/posts/worst-college-football-uniforms-ever-ranked/georgia-tech-2012.jpg" alt="Georgia Tech 2012 Russell Athletic uniform seen from behind: navy and gold jersey with a hexagon pattern, number 52, and white pants" style="display: block; width: 100%; max-width: 460px; margin: 1.25em auto 0.4em;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">via Russell Athletic</p>

**What it was:** a new Russell Athletic set that debuted at Virginia Tech on Labor Day 2012, with three jerseys, two pants and two helmets. The hexagon pattern was the main design element, meant to echo a yellow jacket's nest.

**Why it ranks 8th:** Bleacher Report said at the time that it looked more like bubble wrap, and that's exactly what it looks like from any distance. Georgia Tech's white and old gold is a classic combination, and it got buried under a texture. [Georgia Tech uniform schedule 2026](/stories/georgia-tech-uniform-schedule-2026)

## 7. Nebraska, 2012: adidas Night vs. Wisconsin

<img src="/images/posts/worst-college-football-uniforms-ever-ranked/nebraska-2012.jpg" alt="Nebraska players in red jerseys, red pants with black stripes and black helmets on the sideline against Wisconsin at Memorial Stadium in 2012" style="display: block; width: 100%; max-width: 680px; margin: 1.25em auto 0.4em;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">Photo: <a href="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WisconsinatNebraska2012.jpg" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Thundrplaya</a> / <a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">CC BY-SA 3.0</a></p>

**What it was:** adidas specials for both teams on September 29, 2012, in Lincoln. Nebraska wore red jerseys, red pants with heavy black stripes and black helmets.

**Why it ranks 7th:** plain red and white is the point of Nebraska. FootballScoop put this game on its worst list because adidas made it about adidas, and that's right. Two programs with simple, recognizable looks gave them up for a night. [Nebraska uniform schedule 2026](/stories/nebraska-uniform-schedule-2026)

## 6. Notre Dame, 2016: The Olive Shamrock Series

<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/NDFootball/status/756302012912840704"></a></blockquote>

**What it was:** the Shamrock Series uniform for Army at the Alamodome in San Antonio on November 12, 2016. An olive green jersey, mustard-gold pants and a hand-painted helmet, with "God, Country, Notre Dame" worked into the design. Notre Dame won 44-6.

**Why it ranks 6th:** gold helmets and navy jerseys are one of the cleanest looks in sports, and this looked muddy. Sports Illustrated joked that Grey Poupon must have been the sponsor. The helmet is the only part worth keeping. [Notre Dame uniform schedule 2026](/stories/notre-dame-uniform-schedule-2026)

## 5. UCLA, 2014: LA Steel

<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/adidasFballUS/status/489409149868838913"></a></blockquote>

**What it was:** an adidas alternate unveiled on July 16, 2014, as a tribute to Los Angeles. Dark gray from helmet to socks, with gold-and-blue striped numbers.

**Why it ranks 5th:** UCLA's powder blue is one of the best colors in college football, and this uniform takes it away. Sports Illustrated ranked it third all time in 2016 and called the numbers cheap. [UCLA uniform schedule 2026](/stories/ucla-uniform-schedule-2026)

## 4. Florida, 2017: Swamp Green

<img src="/images/posts/worst-college-football-uniforms-ever-ranked/florida-2017.jpg" alt="Florida players in swamp green gator-scale jerseys with orange numbers, darker green pants and dark helmets against Texas A&M in 2017" style="display: block; width: 100%; max-width: 680px; margin: 1.25em auto 0.4em;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">Photo: <a href="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Swamp_green_unis.jpg" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Zeng8r</a> / <a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">CC BY-SA 4.0</a></p>

**What it was:** a one-game alternate against Texas A&M on October 14, 2017, in Gainesville. Green jerseys printed with gator scales, pants in a different green, orange numbers and lettering.

**Why it ranks 4th:** Florida's orange and blue are classic, and this looked like a costume. The scales stop at the waist, so the top and bottom never look like the same uniform. Florida hasn't worn it since. [Florida uniform schedule 2026](/stories/florida-uniform-schedule-2026)

## 3. Iowa State, 2023: The Jack Trice Legacy Game

<img src="/images/posts/worst-college-football-uniforms-ever-ranked/iowa-state-2023.jpg" alt="Iowa State 2023 Jack Trice Legacy Game uniform: white helmet with AMES across the top, cardinal jersey with gray vertical stripes and number 2, and pale tan pants" style="display: block; width: 100%; max-width: 460px; margin: 1.25em auto 0.4em;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0.25em 0 1.5em;">via Iowa State Athletics</p>

**What it was:** a throwback worn against TCU on October 7, 2023, a win, marking 100 years since the death of Jack Trice, the school's first Black student-athlete, who died after being injured in a 1923 game at Minnesota. "Ames" appeared on the helmet and pants, and the "i will" on the helmet bumper came from a letter Trice wrote before that game.

**Why it ranks 3rd:** this is on the list for one reason, and it isn't the tribute. Honoring Trice was exactly right, and the helmet and jersey are good. But on TV, the pale tan pants made players look like they had forgotten them, and that's what everyone talked about. A game meant for Jack Trice deserved better than that. ## 2. Michigan State, 2019: Neon

<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/MSU_Football/status/1117062628461953025"></a></blockquote>

**What it was:** a Nike alternate revealed in April 2019 and worn against Western Michigan on September 7, 2019. A dark green jersey, STATE in highlighter green across the chest, highlighter green pants and a neon Spartan on the helmet.

**Why it ranks 2nd:** Michigan State is dark green and white, and that's a great look. This added a green that clashes with it from helmet to cleats, and the pants and the lettering don't quite match each other either. ## 1. Texas Tech, 2014: The Red-to-Black Fade

<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/TexasTechLogo/status/533696025564045312"></a></blockquote>

**What it was:** a red-to-black ombre Under Armour uniform worn on Senior Day against Oklahoma on November 15, 2014, in Lubbock. Texas Tech lost 42-30.

**Why it ranks 1st:** the color never settles. The jersey and pants look like they're melting into each other, and there isn't a single clean line on the whole uniform. FootballScoop pointed out that Under Armour had already used the same idea on its high school All-America game. Texas Tech has a great red and black look, and it gave its seniors this on Senior Day. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the worst college football uniform of all time?**

My pick is Texas Tech's red-to-black fade from 2014. Florida's 2017 swamp green is the most common answer on other lists, and it's No. 4 here.

**Why did Iowa State's pants look like skin?**

Iowa State's 2023 Jack Trice Legacy Game uniform used pale tan pants, which Uni Watch suggested were meant to evoke the canvas pants of the 1920s. On TV the color read as bare legs.

**Did Florida ever wear the swamp green uniforms again?**

No. Florida wore them once, against Texas A&M on October 14, 2017.

**What were Michigan State's neon uniforms?**

Nike alternates revealed in April 2019, with a highlighter green STATE across a dark green jersey and highlighter green pants.

**What was UCLA's LA Steel uniform?**

A dark gray adidas alternate unveiled in July 2014 as a tribute to Los Angeles.
