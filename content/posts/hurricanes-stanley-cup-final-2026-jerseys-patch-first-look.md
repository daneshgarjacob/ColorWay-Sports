## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the 2026 Stanley Cup Final patch?**

The 2026 Stanley Cup Final patch is a silver Stanley Cup trophy silhouette with "20" on the left and "26" on the right flanking the base, the "STANLEY CUP" wordmark arched across the top, and "FINAL" stacked across the bottom in white-on-red. Both Final teams wear it on the right chest of the jersey.

**What does the 2026 NHL Stanley Cup Final patch look like on the Carolina Hurricanes jersey?**

The 2026 NHL Stanley Cup Final patch on the Carolina Hurricanes jersey shows the silver Stanley Cup trophy silhouette in the center of the patch with "20" on the left side of the base and "26" on the right side, the "STANLEY CUP" wordmark arched across the top in white-on-red, and "FINAL" stacked across the bottom. The patch sits on the right chest of the Hurricanes black home alternate sweater directly above the NUCOR jersey sponsor patch. The silver-and-red patch pops against the dark black sweater body for one of the cleanest patch-on-jersey reads of the 2026 NHL postseason.

**Where is the Stanley Cup Final patch placed on the Hurricanes jersey?**

The 2026 Stanley Cup Final patch is placed on the right chest of the Carolina Hurricanes black home alternate sweater, directly above the NUCOR jersey sponsor patch. The right-chest placement matches the NHL standard Stanley Cup Final patch position from prior Cup Final years and sits opposite the captain "C" patch on the left chest of the captain's jersey.

**Who is the captain of the Carolina Hurricanes for the 2026 Stanley Cup Final?**

Jaccob Slavin is the captain of the Carolina Hurricanes for the 2026 Stanley Cup Final and has been the team's captain since the 2023 season. The Hurricanes are running the standard white-on-red "C" captain patch on the left chest of Slavin's jersey, the same captain patch format the team has used through the entire 2026 postseason. Stanley Cup Final Game 1 against the Vegas Golden Knights is Slavin's first-ever Stanley Cup Final game as the Hurricanes captain.

**Why are the Hurricanes wearing the black home alternate sweater for the Stanley Cup Final?**

The Hurricanes have worn the black home alternate sweater for every single home game of the 2026 Stanley Cup Playoffs across Round 1, Round 2, the Eastern Conference Final, and now Game 1 of the Stanley Cup Final. Carolina has not worn the iconic red home sweater at any home playoff game in 2026 despite the red home being the team's heritage primary uniform. The team has not publicly explained the choice but the black alternate has worked as the visual backdrop the team has built the entire postseason home identity around.

**Who is the Hurricanes starting goalie for Stanley Cup Final Game 1?**

Frederik Andersen is the Carolina Hurricanes starting goalie for Stanley Cup Final Game 1 against the Vegas Golden Knights and wears number 31. The Hurricanes first-look photos confirm Andersen's locker is set up with a custom Stanley Cup Final 2026 name plate at the top reading "ANDERSEN 31" with the official Stanley Cup Final 2026 wordmark, his goalie mask is in place with the number 31 prominent in white-on-red, his catcher glove has "ANDERSEN" stitched on the cuff, and his blocker pad is staged on the right of the locker.

**What does Frederik Andersen's goalie mask look like for the Stanley Cup Final?**

Frederik Andersen's goalie mask for the 2026 Stanley Cup Final carries a Carolina Hurricanes-themed design in red, black, and white with the number 31 displayed prominently on the chin in white-on-red. The mask matches the Hurricanes color story across the entire goalie equipment kit including the catcher glove with "ANDERSEN" stitching on the cuff and the blocker pad with the same red-white-black palette. The mask design is part of the goalie equipment package staged in Andersen's locker for the Game 1 first-look reveal.

**What does the Hurricanes captain (C) patch look like?**

The Carolina Hurricanes captain (C) patch is a white "C" letter on a red background with black trim and white outline, sized in the standard NHL captain patch dimensions. The patch sits on the upper left chest of the captain's jersey and the Hurricanes have used the same captain patch design through the entire 2026 NHL postseason. The Stanley Cup Final 2026 first-look photos confirm the team is running the same captain patch on the left chest of presumed captain Jaccob Slavin's black home alternate Game 1 jersey.

**What is the NUCOR patch on the Hurricanes jersey?**

The NUCOR patch on the Hurricanes jersey is the team's official on-jersey sponsor patch, the small advertising patch the NHL has allowed on team jerseys since the 2022 season. NUCOR is a North Carolina steel company headquartered in Charlotte and the Hurricanes have run the NUCOR sponsor patch on the right chest of every game jersey through the 2026 NHL Stanley Cup Playoffs. The Stanley Cup Final 2026 patch sits directly above the NUCOR sponsor patch on the right chest stack for Game 1 at Lenovo Center.

**When is Stanley Cup Final Game 1 between the Hurricanes and Golden Knights?**

Stanley Cup Final Game 1 between the Carolina Hurricanes and the Vegas Golden Knights is Monday June 2, 2026 at Lenovo Center in Raleigh, North Carolina. The Hurricanes are the home team for Games 1 and 2 of the best-of-seven Stanley Cup Final series. This is the franchise's first home Stanley Cup Final game in Raleigh since the 2006 Cup-winning playoff run.

**When was the last time the Carolina Hurricanes played in the Stanley Cup Final?**

The Carolina Hurricanes last played in the Stanley Cup Final in 2006 when the franchise won the Stanley Cup in seven games over the Edmonton Oilers, the only Stanley Cup championship in Hurricanes franchise history. The 2026 Stanley Cup Final is the team's first Cup Final appearance in 20 years.
