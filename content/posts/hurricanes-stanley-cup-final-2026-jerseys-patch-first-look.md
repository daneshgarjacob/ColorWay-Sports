## The Stanley Cup Final 2026 Patch Placement on the Right Chest

The new Stanley Cup Final 2026 patch sits on the right chest of the Hurricanes black home alternate sweater directly above the NUCOR jersey sponsor patch. The patch itself is the Stanley Cup trophy silhouette in silver with "20" on the left side and "26" on the right side flanking the base of the Cup, the "STANLEY CUP" wordmark arched across the top, and "FINAL" stacked across the bottom in white-on-red. The silver-and-red patch design pops against the dark black sweater body in a way that the same patch would not against the Hurricanes iconic red home sweater the team has chosen not to wear at any home game of the 2026 playoff run.

The right-chest placement matches the league's standard Stanley Cup Final patch position from prior years and the Hurricanes have kept the patch sized large enough to read clean on the broadcast wide angle without crowding the NUCOR sponsor patch directly below it. The on-jersey sponsor program the NHL rolled out in 2022 has changed how Cup Final jerseys frame their patch programs, and Carolina handled the right-chest stack as well as any team that has worn the patch since the league added jersey sponsors.

## The Captain (C) Patch on the Left Chest

The captain patch on the left chest of the same jersey is the second piece of patch news on the Game 1 reveal. The "C" sits in white-on-red on a black background patch with white trim, the standard captain mark format the Hurricanes have used since Jaccob Slavin took over the captaincy in 2023. This jersey is presumably Slavin's based on the captain mark, and the patch frames Slavin's first-ever Stanley Cup Final game as the Hurricanes captain after the franchise's 2006 Cup-winning roster that pre-dated his arrival.

The captain patch and the Stanley Cup Final patch on the same chest is one of the cleanest visual moments a player can wear on a jersey in any league. The first-look photos confirm that the Hurricanes are not modifying their captain mark for the Cup Final and are running the same standard "C" placement they have used through the entire 2026 postseason on top of the new Cup patch.

## The Black Home Alternate Holds for the Stanley Cup Final

The Hurricanes are sticking with the black home alternate sweater for Stanley Cup Final Game 1, the same uniform choice the team has made for every single home game of the 2026 postseason. Carolina has not worn the iconic red home sweater at any home playoff game across Round 1, Round 2, the Eastern Conference Final, or now the Stanley Cup Final, a choice we have flagged in the [2026 NHL Stanley Cup Final Jersey Tracker](/stories/nhl-stanley-cup-final-2026-jersey-tracker-hurricanes-knights) as the single biggest variable on the Hurricanes side of the Finals matchup.

The patch-on-black call is genuinely a winning visual for the broadcast read. The silver-and-red Stanley Cup Final patch pops against the dark sweater body in a way that the patch would not pop against the Hurricanes red home sweater, where the patch red would compete with the sweater red and the silver Cup silhouette would lose contrast against the brighter background. Carolina chose the broadcast-cleanest backdrop for the Cup Final patch reveal even if they passed on the most heritage-rich home color story the franchise carries.
