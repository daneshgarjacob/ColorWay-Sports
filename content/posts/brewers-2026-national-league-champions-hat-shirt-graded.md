The Milwaukee Brewers are going to the World Series. They swept the two-time defending champion Dodgers 4-0 in the National League Championship Series, closing it out 6-4 in Game 4 at Dodger Stadium on Thursday night, October 15, behind a Garrett Mitchell go-ahead home run in the sixth. It is the franchise's first National League pennant and its first trip to the Fall Classic since 1982, when Milwaukee was still in the American League. Game 1 of the World Series is Friday, October 23, at American Family Field.

That means new locker room gear. Local and national coverage of the celebration says the players pulled on the gray National League Champions T-shirt and the New Era locker room cap right after the final out. We are grading those two pieces, plus the matching locker room hoodie and the World Series side patch cap New Era released for the Fall Classic, counting down from our lowest grade to our highest.

For what the Brewers actually wore on the field all series, see the [2026 MLB Championship Series uniform guide](/stories/mlb-championship-series-uniforms-2026) and the full [Brewers uniform schedule](/stories/brewers-uniform-schedule-2026).

The Fanatics locker room hoodie carries the exact same chest graphic as the T-shirt below, printed on a heather charcoal fleece with a kangaroo pocket. "Milwaukee Brewers" sits in spaced gold capitals above a navy bar reading "National League," a big chrome-effect "Champions" script sweeps across the middle, and the ball-in-glove logo sits at the bottom between gold "20" and "26." The National League logo hangs off the right side and the small 2026 World Series logo and the Fanatics mark sit up top.

The art is the same art, so most of what we like about the shirt carries over. The problem is the canvas. The graphic is scaled down to fit between the hood opening and the pocket, and in the product photo the drawstrings hang straight through "Milwaukee Brewers" and the top of the "Champions" script. On a shirt the design has room to breathe. On the hoodie it reads cramped, and the gold numerals and the ball-in-glove, the best parts of the piece, end up just above the pocket seam. We did not find a report of the players wearing this one in the celebration, so it is graded strictly as the fan version of the shirt.

## 3. Brewers 2026 National League Champions Locker Room T-Shirt

<div style="margin: 2em 0; text-align: center;">

This is the shirt the players were wearing when the champagne started. It is a heather charcoal Fanatics tee, and the layout stacks from top to bottom: the 2026 World Series logo and the Fanatics mark, "Milwaukee Brewers" in spaced gold capitals, "National League" in white condensed block letters on a navy bar, then a huge "Champions" script in a shiny chrome finish with a navy drop shade and a long tail swash. Behind the script is a white spray-textured infield diamond. Under it, the ball-in-glove logo in navy and gold sits between "20" and "26" in Brewers gold, and the red, white and navy National League logo is tucked against the right edge of the diamond.

What works is the bottom half. The ball-in-glove is the best logo in the sport and the shirt makes it the anchor of the whole design, and the gold numerals pull the Brewers' yellow out of the logo and onto the shirt in a way that reads as Milwaukee from across a room. Putting the logo inside an infield diamond is a nice baseball-first touch too.

What does not work is the top half. The chrome "Champions" script is the trendy effect of the moment and it is the loudest thing on the shirt, louder than anything that says Brewers. The navy bar and the gold wordmark are doing the team-identity work in a much smaller size. Count the marks, too: the World Series logo, the Fanatics logo, the National League logo and the Brewers logo all on one chest. And the base is heather charcoal, not Brewers navy, so the shirt leans on the print for all of its color. It is a good shirt with one effect too many.

## 2. Brewers 2026 Locker Room 9SEVENTY Hat

This is the cap from the celebration. New Era lists it as the 2026 League Championship Series Locker Room 9SEVENTY Stretch-Snap, Fanatics lists it as the 2026 World Series Locker Room 9SEVENTY in charcoal, and in the product photos it reads almost black. The front is a big raised white "WORLD SERIES" wordmark with gray rules above and below it. Over the wordmark is a round navy patch holding the ball-in-glove logo in gold, framed by ornate gray scrollwork on both sides, with "20" and "26" tucked into the curls. The white New Era flag sits on the left side, the undervisor is gray, and the back carries a "League Champs" wordmark under the MLB logo in the same scroll frame, above a gray snap strap.

We love the priority call. When we graded the Vegas Golden Knights' conference title hat in May, our biggest complaint was that the [front looked back at the round they just won](/stories/knights-2026-western-conference-champions-hat-shirt-graded) and buried the Final on the back panel. This hat does it the right way around. The front says "World Series," the place the Brewers are going, and "League Champs" gets the back. In every celebration photo, the first thing you read is where they are headed next.

The knocks are color and personality. Charcoal is the default locker room shade, but the Brewers have one of the best two-color palettes in baseball and the only place navy and gold show up is inside that small roundel. The gray scrollwork is fussy, a little old-fashioned in a way that does not connect to anything in Milwaukee's brand, and outside the roundel nothing on the front is specific to the Brewers. Swap that charcoal crown for Brewers navy and put a gold outline on the wordmark and this is an A.

This one is not locker room gear and nobody has worn it in a game yet, so we want to be clear about that up front. It is the New Era 59FIFTY version of the all-navy ball-in-glove cap, the cap the Brewers wore at home in the NLDS and NLCS, with the 2026 World Series patch added on the right-wear side. The patch stacks the MLB logo over "2026" in raised gold block numerals and "World Series" in white script, set on a diamond. The back carries the MLB logo.

It tops the list because it does the least and gets the most out of it. The ball-in-glove, navy crown, gold outline: the team's real cap, untouched, with one small mark that dates it to the Brewers' first World Series trip in 44 years. The gold numerals in the patch pick up the gold in the logo, so the side of the cap matches the front instead of fighting it. No chrome, no scrollwork, no charcoal stand-in for navy. If you want one piece that says "October 2026" five years from now and still looks like a Brewers cap, this is it. The only thing holding it back from an A+ is that the patch is the standard World Series mark rather than anything built for Milwaukee.

## Bottom Line

The Brewers' pennant gear gets the big decision right. The locker room hat leads with "World Series" on the front and saves "League Champs" for the back, which is exactly the fix we asked for after the Golden Knights' conference title hat in May. Where the set loses points is color: charcoal on the hat, heather charcoal on the shirt and hoodie, with Brewers navy and gold squeezed into logos and numerals instead of filling the pieces. The ball-in-glove carries everything it touches. The best piece of the bunch is the simplest one, the navy ball-in-glove 59FIFTY with the World Series patch on the side, and we will be watching on October 23 to see how the Brewers dress for Game 1.
