The Milwaukee Brewers are going to the World Series. They swept the two-time defending champion Dodgers 4-0 in the National League Championship Series, closing it out 6-4 in Game 4 at Dodger Stadium on Thursday night, October 15, behind a Garrett Mitchell go-ahead home run in the sixth. It is the franchise's first National League pennant and its first trip to the Fall Classic since 1982, when Milwaukee was still in the American League. Game 1 of the World Series is Friday, October 23, at American Family Field.

That means new gear, and a lot of it. The players pulled on the gray National League Champions T-shirt and the New Era locker room cap right after the final out, and New Era has a whole run of 2026 World Series side patch caps in different fits, led by the 59FIFTY version of the on-field cap the Brewers will wear in the Series. We graded all of it: the shirt, the hoodie, the locker room hat, every World Series side patch cap we could find on New Era and Fanatics, two '47 caps and the NLDS clincher hat from last week. We count down from our lowest grade to our highest.

## 14. Brewers 2026 National League Champions Locker Room Hoodie

<div style="margin: 2em 0; text-align: center;">
  <img src="/images/posts/brewers-2026-national-league-champions-hat-shirt-graded/brewers-nl-champions-locker-room-hoodie.jpg" alt="Milwaukee Brewers Fanatics 2026 National League Champions locker room hoodie in heather charcoal with a navy National League bar, chrome Champions script, ball-in-glove logo, gold 20 26 numerals and the National League logo on the chest" style="max-width: 100%; border-radius: 12px;" />

The Fanatics locker room hoodie carries the exact same chest graphic as the T-shirt, printed on a heather charcoal fleece with a kangaroo pocket. "Milwaukee Brewers" sits in spaced gold capitals above a navy bar reading "National League," a big chrome-effect "Champions" script sweeps across the middle, and the ball-in-glove logo sits at the bottom between gold "20" and "26." The National League logo hangs off the right side and the small 2026 World Series logo and the Fanatics mark sit up top.

The art is the same art, so the shine we like on the shirt carries over. The problem is the canvas. The graphic is scaled down to fit between the hood opening and the pocket, and in the product photo the drawstrings hang straight through "Milwaukee Brewers" and the top of the "Champions" script. On a shirt the design has room to breathe. On the hoodie it reads cramped, and the gold numerals and the ball-in-glove, the best parts of the piece, end up just above the pocket seam. We did not find a report of the players wearing this one in the celebration, so it is graded strictly as the fan version of the shirt.

## 12. Brewers 2026 National League Champions Locker Room T-Shirt

<div style="margin: 2em 0; text-align: center;">
  <img src="/images/posts/brewers-2026-national-league-champions-hat-shirt-graded/brewers-nl-champions-locker-room-shirt.jpg" alt="Milwaukee Brewers Fanatics 2026 National League Champions locker room T-shirt in heather charcoal with Milwaukee Brewers in gold, National League in a navy bar, a chrome Champions script over a white infield diamond, the ball-in-glove logo between gold 20 and 26, and the National League logo" style="max-width: 100%; border-radius: 12px;" />
</div>

This is the shirt the players were wearing when the champagne started. It is a heather charcoal Fanatics tee, and the layout stacks from top to bottom: the 2026 World Series logo and the Fanatics mark, "Milwaukee Brewers" in spaced gold capitals, "National League" in white condensed block letters on a navy bar, then a huge "Champions" script in a shiny chrome finish with a navy drop shade and a long tail swash. Behind the script is a white spray-textured infield diamond. Under it, the ball-in-glove logo in navy and gold sits between "20" and "26" in Brewers gold, and the red, white and navy National League logo is tucked against the right edge of the diamond.

The chrome is the best part. That shiny "Champions" script catches the light in every clubhouse photo and gives the shirt the celebration feel it needs, and it is the first thing you notice from across the room. The bottom half backs it up: the ball-in-glove anchors the design and the gold numerals pull the Brewers' yellow onto the shirt in a way that reads as Milwaukee right away. Putting the logo inside an infield diamond is a nice baseball-first touch too.

What keeps it at a B is everything around the chrome. Count the marks: the World Series logo, the Fanatics logo, the National League logo and the Brewers logo all on one chest. And the base is heather charcoal, not Brewers navy, so the shirt leans on the print for all of its color.

## 7. Brewers 2026 Locker Room 9SEVENTY Hat

<div style="margin: 2em 0; text-align: center;">
  <img src="/images/posts/brewers-2026-national-league-champions-hat-shirt-graded/brewers-locker-room-hat-front.jpg" alt="Milwaukee Brewers New Era 2026 locker room 9SEVENTY stretch-snap hat in charcoal with a raised white WORLD SERIES wordmark, a navy and gold ball-in-glove roundel and gray scrollwork with 20 and 26" style="max-width: 100%; border-radius: 12px;" />
</div>

This is the cap from the celebration, the one that says "WORLD SERIES" across the front. New Era lists it as the 2026 League Championship Series Locker Room 9SEVENTY Stretch-Snap, Fanatics lists it as the 2026 World Series Locker Room 9SEVENTY in charcoal, and in the product photos it reads almost black. The front is a big raised white "WORLD SERIES" wordmark with gray rules above and below it. Over the wordmark is a round navy patch holding the ball-in-glove logo in gold, framed by ornate gray scrollwork on both sides, with "20" and "26" tucked into the curls. The white New Era flag sits on the left side, the undervisor is gray, and the back carries a "League Champs" wordmark under the MLB logo in the same scroll frame, above a gray snap strap.

We like the design. When we graded the Vegas Golden Knights' conference title hat in May, our biggest complaint was that the [front looked back at the round they just won](/stories/knights-2026-western-conference-champions-hat-shirt-graded) and buried the Final on the back panel. This hat does it the right way around. The front says "World Series," the place the Brewers are going, and "League Champs" gets the back. The roundel is a strong center, and in every celebration photo the first thing you read is where they are headed next.

We just wish it were more colorful. Charcoal is the default locker room shade, but the Brewers have one of the best two-color palettes in baseball and the only place navy and gold show up is inside that small roundel. Everything else is white and gray. Swap that charcoal crown for Brewers navy and put a gold outline on the wordmark and this jumps a full grade.

## Bottom Line

The Brewers' pennant gear gets the big decisions right. The locker room hat leads with "World Series" on the front and saves "League Champs" for the back, and the T-shirt's chrome "Champions" script gives the celebration the shine it deserves. Where the locker room set loses points is color: charcoal on the hat, heather charcoal on the shirt and hoodie, with Brewers navy and gold squeezed into logos and numerals.
