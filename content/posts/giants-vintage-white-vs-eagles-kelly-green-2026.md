**Both teams are in throwbacks for this one.** When the New York Giants visit the Philadelphia Eagles at Lincoln Financial Field on **Sunday, November 8, at 1:00 p.m. Eastern on FOX**, the Eagles wear **Kelly Green** and the Giants wear **Vintage White**. The two clubs announced it together on Wednesday, September 2, with the Eagles posting "Historic rivalry 🤝 Historic uniforms." Both sets are based on what the two teams wore in the late 1980s and early 1990s, when this was one of the best rivalries in the league.

## What the Giants Are Wearing: Vintage White

<img src="/images/posts/new-york-giants-uniform-schedule-2026/vintage-white-games.jpg" alt="New York Giants Vintage White uniform graphic, assigned to the Week 9 game at the Eagles and the Week 10 Thursday night game against the Commanders in 2026, showing the navy GIANTS wordmark helmet and the white jersey with blue numbers outlined in red" style="display:block;width:100%;max-width:460px;margin:0 auto 0.5em;border-radius:10px;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.75em; color: #999; margin: 0 0 2em;">Image: New York Giants</p>

Vintage White is the white version of the Giants' Legacy throwback. The Giants built it in 2016 for the NFL's Color Rush program, and the club says the white jersey is modeled on the road uniform it wore for two decades, the era of its first Super Bowl title in the 1980s. Here is the set, from the team's September 2 announcement and its uniform graphic:

- **Navy helmet** with the underlined **GIANTS wordmark** on the sides, a red center stripe and a white facemask. It is the same helmet the Giants wear with the Legacy Blue throwback.
- **White jersey** with **blue numbers outlined in red**, and blue and red stripes on the sleeves.
- **White pants.** In the Giants' game-week graphic for this matchup, the pants carry a blue stripe trimmed in red.

## What the Eagles Are Wearing: Kelly Green

Kelly Green is the Eagles' throwback to the uniforms of the Randall Cunningham and Reggie White era, which the team wore from 1985 to 1995. It came back as an alternate in 2023. Here is the set as the Eagles revealed it in July 2023, per NFL.com:

- **Kelly green helmet** with **silver wings trimmed in white** and a gray facemask.
- **Kelly green jersey** with **white numbers trimmed in black** and the old white-and-black eagle logo on each sleeve.
- **Silver pants** with a green-white-green stripe down the sides.

The Eagles have not posted a helmet-and-pants call for this specific game yet, so the helmet and pants are expected rather than confirmed for November 8. Every sign points to the full set: the team's announcement graphic shows the kelly green helmet and silver pants, and the Eagles have said they waited more than a decade to bring Kelly Green back because they did not want to pair it with the midnight green helmet. Until the NFL allowed a second helmet, there was no way to wear it properly.

## When Was It Announced?

**September 2, 2026.** Both clubs posted it that Wednesday morning: Philadelphiaeagles.com and Giants.com each published the Week 9 uniform news on September 2. Two days later, on Friday, September 4, the Eagles posted all three of their 2026 alternate dates together: Kelly Green against the Giants and the Texans, and the black set against the Seahawks. So the Giants game was announced on September 2, and the full alternate schedule on September 4.

## How Often Each Team Wears These in 2026

**Giants: twice, back to back.** Week 9 at Philadelphia on November 8, and Week 10 at home against the Washington Commanders on Thursday, November 12, on Prime Video. Those are New York's only two Vintage White games this season. The Legacy Blue throwback also gets two, October 4 against Arizona and December 6 against San Francisco. Every game is in our [Giants 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/new-york-giants-uniform-schedule-2026).

**Eagles: twice, both at home.** Week 9 against the Giants on November 8, and Week 16 against the Houston Texans on Thursday, December 24. The Eagles wore Kelly Green three times in 2025, so two is one fewer than last year. Every Eagles look is in our [Eagles 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/eagles-uniform-schedule-2026).

## Have the Giants and Eagles Worn These Against Each Other Before?

In the original era, this was a normal Giants-Eagles game. From the mid-1980s through the mid-1990s, the Eagles wore kelly green and the Giants wore white with blue numbers on the road, which is what both of these uniforms are copying.

In the modern era, we could not find a previous game with both throwbacks on the field. Kelly Green only returned in 2023, and the Eagles have worn it against the Giants before, including at the Linc in 2025, but we found no record of New York wearing Vintage White in any of those games. Neither club's announcement mentions an earlier meeting in these two sets. If one turns up, we will add it here.

## Our Take

This is the best-dressed game of the NFL season so far. Kelly green is the best color in the league, and the Giants' white set is the right opponent for it: plenty of white, with blue and red trim that does not compete with the green. Both helmets match the era, the navy wordmark helmet on one side and the kelly green helmet with silver wings on the other, and nobody is wearing a modern piece with an old jersey.

Our one complaint is the count. Both of these are better than the primary uniforms they replace, and each team is using its set twice. Our matchup grade: **A**.
