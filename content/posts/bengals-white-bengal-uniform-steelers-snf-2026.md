## What The White Bengal Is

The White Bengal starts with the helmet. The NFL began allowing a second helmet shell again in 2022, and Cincinnati used it on a **white shell with the team's black tiger stripes**, the same stripe pattern as the regular orange helmet, flipped onto white. With it come the **white jersey** and **white pants with black stripes**, and white socks, so the only color on the uniform is the black of the stripes and the orange trim on the numbers.

Most all-white alternates look like a road uniform with less contrast. This one is the opposite. Black stripes on white read harder than orange stripes on black, which is why the helmet is the most popular piece the Bengals own.

The set has changed once. In 2022 the Bengals wore the white helmet with their white Color Rush jersey, which the Cincinnati Enquirer described as having black sleeves and black numbers. Starting in 2023 they switched to the primary white jersey, with white sleeves and black numbers outlined in orange. The Bengals say the current version has been worn four times.

## What The Steelers Wear

Pittsburgh has not posted its game-day look yet, so this is our call. With the Bengals confirmed in white, the Steelers are expected in their **black jersey with gold numbers** and the black helmet. That puts black and gold across from black and white, which is the matchup the White Bengal was built for. We will update this page with what both teams actually wore after the game.

## White Bengal Vs. White Bengal Stripe

The Bengals have a second white-themed night this season, and the names are easy to mix up. Thursday, December 31 against Baltimore is **White Bengal Stripe** night. That name is for the crowd, which will be split into white and black sections so the stands look like a tiger stripe. It is not a uniform. Tonight is the only date for the full White Bengal set in 2026.

## Our Take

The White Bengal is the best alternate in the AFC and maybe the league. It does not invent a new color or borrow from the past. It takes the stripe, the one design idea the Bengals own outright, and puts it on the cleanest possible background. Under the lights on NBC, against a division rival in black and gold, it gets the stage it deserves. The only thing keeping the matchup from an A is that we would like to see more than one White Bengal game a year. **Matchup grade: A-.**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What uniform are the Bengals wearing against the Steelers tonight?**

The White Bengal: a white helmet with black tiger stripes, the white jersey and white pants. Kickoff is Sunday, November 15 at 8:20 p.m. Eastern on NBC.

**How many times have the Bengals worn the White Bengal uniform?**

Six times before tonight: against Miami and at Pittsburgh in 2022, against the Rams and at Jacksonville in 2023, against Philadelphia in 2024 and against Pittsburgh in 2025. Tonight is the seventh.

**What is the Bengals' record in the White Bengal uniform?**

5-1. The only loss was 37-17 to the Eagles on October 27, 2024. The Bengals are 3-1 in the uniform at home.

**When did the Bengals' white helmet debut?**

On Thursday, September 29, 2022, in a 27-15 win over the Miami Dolphins on Thursday Night Football in Cincinnati.

**Is White Bengal Stripe the same as the White Bengal?**

No. White Bengal Stripe is the name of the New Year's Eve game against Baltimore and the black-and-white crowd pattern in the stands. The White Bengal is the uniform, and it comes out once in 2026, tonight.

**What are the Steelers wearing at Cincinnati?**

We expect the black jersey. The Bengals are the home team and declared white, so Pittsburgh wears its dark jersey on the road.
