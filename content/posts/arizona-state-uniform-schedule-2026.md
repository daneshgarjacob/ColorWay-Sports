Arizona State does not have a home uniform and a road uniform so much as a closet. Gold, maroon, white and black helmets, pitchfork or Sparky on the side, three jersey colors and a throwback still waiting for a date. This is the full Arizona State 2026 uniform schedule: the combinations confirmed so far, the two we know the Sun Devils have worn, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games. Arizona State announces each combination in game week, so we update this page as every one lands.

The thing to understand about the Sun Devils is that the helmet, jersey and pants are chosen separately. Last season Arizona State ran the gold Sparky helmet over a white jersey at Baylor, a full blackout against TCU, and a black helmet over a white jersey at Utah, all from the same adidas wardrobe. So the grid below predicts the jersey color, maroon at home and white on the road, and leaves the helmet and pants open until the school posts the combination. The [Roses &amp; Devils throwback](/stories/arizona-state-throwback-uniforms-2026), the gold full-body Sparky helmet and maroon block-letter jersey that marks 40 years since the 1987 Rose Bowl win over Michigan, is the one uniform every home date is auditioning for. Arizona State has not said when it will be worn.

**September 5 vs. Morgan State, Tempe.** ★ Confirmed: **gold helmet, maroon jersey, gold pants**, the chrome maroon pitchfork on the shell and the traditional combination Arizona State has opened at home in since 2011. New for 2026 was the Big 12 and Monster Energy patch on the right shoulder. Arizona State won 70-7 in a game the two schools agreed to shorten to a ten-minute fourth quarter.

**September 12 at Texas A&M, College Station.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **gold helmet, white jersey, gold pants**, with Sparky on the helmet for the first time this season in place of the pitchfork. Same helmet shell and pants as the opener, white jersey swapped in for the road. Texas A&M wore its standard home set, maroon jersey, white pants, white helmet, so Kyle Field read maroon and white against gold and white. Arizona State lost 48-20. The Aggies' side of the day is on the [Texas A&M 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/texas-am-uniform-schedule-2026).

**September 19 vs. Kansas, Wembley Stadium, London.** White jersey expected. The Union Jack Classic is the first college football game played in London and the Big 12 opener for both schools, and Kansas is the designated home team, which is why Arizona State's schedule lists it as a road game. The helmet is the open question, but on Wednesday the Sun Devils [posted a gold helmet with the Sparky logo and a maroon facemask in front of Big Ben](https://x.com/ASUFootball/status/2100373682375766098), the strongest hint yet at the London helmet. It is a hint, not a full reveal, and the jersey and pants have not been announced.

**September 26.** Bye.

**October 3 vs. Baylor, Tempe.** Maroon jersey expected for the Big 12 home opener. Baylor has white pencilled in on its own page, which leaves Arizona State free to pick any helmet it likes. Combination announced in game week.

**October 10 vs. Hawai'i, Tempe.** Maroon jersey expected. A non-conference home date in the middle of October is exactly the kind of low-stakes night a program uses to try a combination, so this is a live candidate for something other than the standard set. Announced in game week.

**October 17 at Texas Tech, Lubbock.** White jersey expected. Texas Tech's homecoming, and the Red Raiders historically go red-heavy for it, so Arizona State should be in white. Announced in game week.

**October 24 vs. Kansas State, Tempe.** Maroon jersey expected. Announced in game week.

**October 31 at BYU, Provo.** White jersey expected. A Halloween night in LaVell Edwards Stadium is the kind of date that has pulled a black helmet out of the Sun Devils' closet before, but nothing is set. Announced in game week.

**November 7 vs. Colorado, Tempe.** Maroon jersey expected. Announced in game week.

**November 14 at UCF, Orlando.** White jersey expected. Arizona State's first trip to Orlando in program history. Announced in game week.

**November 21 vs. Oklahoma State, Tempe.** Maroon jersey expected. This is homecoming, and if the Roses &amp; Devils throwback is being held for a home crowd with alumni in the building, homecoming is the obvious night. That is our read, not an announcement. Announced in game week.

**November 28 at Arizona, Tucson.** White jersey expected for the Territorial Cup. The rivalry game closes the regular season on the road, and the combination is announced in game week.

## The Bottom Line

Two games in, Arizona State has worn the same gold helmet and gold pants twice and changed only the jersey and the decal, maroon with the pitchfork at home, white with Sparky on the road. The rest of the season is a jersey prediction, maroon at home and white away, with the helmet and pants left to the weekly announcement. The one uniform everyone is waiting on is the Roses &amp; Devils throwback, and until Arizona State names a date, homecoming against Oklahoma State on November 21 is the best guess we have.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Arizona State wearing this week?**

Arizona State wore a gold Sparky helmet, white jersey and gold pants at Texas A&M on September 12. The next game is against Kansas at Wembley Stadium in London on September 19, where the Sun Devils are the designated road team and a white jersey is expected. Arizona State posted a gold Sparky helmet with a maroon facemask from London on Wednesday, a strong hint at the helmet, but the full combination has not been announced. Arizona State announces each combination in game week, and the grid above is updated as each one is confirmed.

**What are Arizona State's uniforms for 2026?**

An adidas wardrobe built on maroon, white and black jerseys, gold pants as the default with maroon, white and black options, and gold, maroon, white and black helmets that carry either the chrome pitchfork or the full-body Sparky decal. The standard home uniform is the gold pitchfork helmet, maroon jersey and gold pants.

**When will Arizona State wear the Roses &amp; Devils throwback uniforms?**

Arizona State has not announced a date. The throwback, a gold helmet with the full-body Sparky and a maroon jersey with ARIZONA STATE in block letters, marks 40 years since the 1987 Rose Bowl win over Michigan. Our full breakdown is in the [Roses &amp; Devils uniform grade](/stories/arizona-state-throwback-uniforms-2026).

**Does Arizona State have a black uniform in 2026?**

Yes. The black jersey and satin black helmet with gold-trimmed pitchforks were worn as a full blackout against TCU in 2025, and both remain in the wardrobe. No 2026 game has been assigned to them yet.

**How many games does Arizona State play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games, six of them at Mountain America Stadium in Tempe, plus the neutral-site Kansas game in London and a September 26 bye. The full schedule with expected jerseys is in the grid above.
