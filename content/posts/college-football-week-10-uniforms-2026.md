Week 10 of the 2026 college football season is the biggest Saturday of the year so far. **No. 1 Miami plays at No. 5 Notre Dame** on Saturday night, **No. 10 Alabama goes to No. 15 LSU**, **Oregon visits No. 2 Ohio State**, **No. 3 Georgia plays at No. 12 Ole Miss**, **No. 8 BYU and No. 6 Utah** meet in the Holy War and **No. 11 Texas visits No. 23 Missouri**. Two uniforms are already known. **Tennessee wears its all-black Dark Mode against No. 9 Kentucky**, announced a full week early, and **Florida goes all blue against Oklahoma**, the one all-blue game on the season schedule Florida published in August. Air Force plays at Army for the Commander-in-Chief's Trophy on Saturday night, and Arizona hosts TCU on Friday in its Sandstorm-themed game.

Below is every uniform announced so far, then every game this week involving an AP Top 25 team or one of the programs we track in our college uniform schedules, with kickoff time, TV and what each team is wearing. A look is marked **Confirmed** when the school itself has posted it for this game. When a school has not posted yet, we give our projection from that team's uniform schedule and label it **Expected**. Most schools post their combination between Monday and game day, so this page fills in all week, and on Saturday we confirm every look from the game broadcasts and team photos, then add final scores. Rankings are from the October 25 AP poll; we will update them when the new poll comes out on Sunday. All times are Eastern. Last week's looks are in our [Week 9 uniform guide](/stories/college-football-week-9-uniforms-2026).

## Every Uniform Announced for Week 10

- **Tennessee: Dark Mode (Confirmed).** Black helmet with an orange Power T, black jersey with orange numbers, black pants, vs. No. 9 Kentucky. The first black game of the season and the first in the adidas version.
- **Florida: all blue (Announced in August).** Blue helmet, blue jersey, blue pants, vs. Oklahoma, from the season uniform schedule Florida published before the season. It is the only all-blue game on that schedule.

That is the list as of Friday, October 30. Most of Week 10 is still playing Week 9, and the rest of the announcements will come from Monday on.

## Games of the Week

### No. 9 Kentucky at No. 16 Tennessee: Dark Mode at Neyland

**Saturday, 6:15 p.m., ESPN.** **Tennessee: Confirmed, Dark Mode.** Tennessee announced on Friday morning, October 30, that it will wear its all-black Dark Mode uniform against Kentucky, with a one-minute video captioned "The chaos lives within" that ends on the date, 11.07.26. The Vols are off this week, which is why the announcement came with a full week to go.

This is the adidas Dark Mode set Tennessee revealed on July 9 when it launched its new adidas uniforms: a **black helmet with the orange Power T** and an orange center stripe, a **black jersey with orange numbers**, a Power T on the front of the collar, and **black pants**. It is the first time Tennessee has worn black this season. The Vols first wore black jerseys against South Carolina on Halloween night in 2009, and brought back a full black set under Josh Heupel in 2021.

### Oklahoma at No. 17 Florida: The All-Blue Game

**Saturday, 7:45 p.m., SEC Network.** **Florida: Announced, all blue.** Florida is the rare college program that publishes its uniform schedule before the season, and its 2026 grid has one all-blue game: a **blue helmet, blue jersey and blue pants** against Oklahoma in The Swamp. Florida has followed that grid in every game but one. At Texas on October 17 it wore the blue helmet instead of the listed orange, so we will still look for the game-week post before calling it confirmed.

### No. 1 Miami at No. 5 Notre Dame: Navy vs. White in South Bend

**Saturday, 7:30 p.m., NBC.** Neither team has posted yet. **Notre Dame: Expected, navy**, with the gold helmet and gold pants Notre Dame wears with everything. **Miami: Expected, white.** It is the first time since 1976 that Miami has played in South Bend in November.

The open question is Miami's 2001 throwbacks. Miami revealed both the orange home and the white road version of its 2001 national championship uniforms on September 22, and wore the orange version against Pittsburgh on October 24. The white road version, which goes with green pants, has no date yet. Miami posted its standard all white for this week's game at North Carolina, and its last three regular-season games are at home, so this is the last regular-season road game left for it. Nothing has been announced. More: [Miami's 2001 throwbacks, graded](/stories/miami-2001-throwback-uniforms), [Miami schedule](/stories/miami-hurricanes-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Notre Dame schedule](/stories/notre-dame-uniform-schedule-2026).

### No. 10 Alabama at No. 15 LSU: The Home White Rule

**Saturday, 3:30 p.m., ABC.** **LSU: Expected, home white.** LSU is the one SEC program that regularly wears white at home, and when it does, the visitor wears color. LSU has worn the home whites against Clemson and Texas A&M this season and gone purple at home against Louisiana Tech and McNeese. LSU lists the game as its "LSU Salutes" game. **Alabama: Expected, crimson** if LSU is in white, which would make it the one road game all season where Alabama wears crimson. That is the most likely outcome, but neither team has announced anything. More: [LSU schedule](/stories/lsu-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Alabama schedule](/stories/alabama-uniform-schedule-2026).

### Oregon at No. 2 Ohio State: First Meeting Since the Rose Bowl

**Saturday, 3:30 p.m., CBS.** Neither team has posted yet. **Ohio State: Expected, scarlet.** It is Ohio State's first meeting with Oregon since the 2024 Rose Bowl. Ohio State revealed three alternates in May, Scarlet Rush, Tunnel Vision and Sub Zero, and has not assigned any of them to a game. **Oregon: Expected, white jersey.** Oregon picks its helmet, jersey and pants separately each week and announces the combination in game week, so the helmet and pants are open. This week it wears a matte black helmet with white wings against Northwestern. More: [Ohio State schedule](/stories/ohio-state-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Oregon schedule](/stories/oregon-uniform-schedule-2026).

### No. 3 Georgia at No. 12 Ole Miss

Ole Miss wore white at Georgia in 2025, so the colors flip this year. **Georgia: Expected, white**, with the red helmet and silver britches. More: [Ole Miss schedule](/stories/ole-miss-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Georgia schedule](/stories/georgia-uniform-schedule-2026).

Utah went red head to toe for its Red Out against Arkansas on September 12. **BYU: Expected, white.** BYU wears all navy against Arizona State this week. More: [Utah schedule](/stories/utah-uniform-schedule-2026) and [BYU schedule](/stories/byu-uniform-schedule-2026).

### No. 11 Texas at No. 23 Missouri

**Saturday, noon, TV to be announced.** **Texas: Expected, white.** Texas wears its "icy whites" on the road and has no alternates. **Missouri: Expected, black or gold.** It is the biggest home game on Missouri's schedule. Mizzou wore its Memorial Stadium 100th anniversary throwback against Troy on September 19, and if it has another special look in reserve, this is the most likely date. More: [Texas schedule](/stories/texas-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Missouri schedule](/stories/missouri-uniform-schedule-2026).

### Air Force at Army: The Commander-in-Chief's Trophy

**Saturday, 7:30 p.m., CBS, at Michie Stadium.** It is Army's only home night game of the season. **Army: Expected, black**, with the gold helmet. Army has paired the black jersey with gold pants and with black pants this season, so the pants are the open question. **Air Force: Expected, white.** Air Force built its 2026 Air Power Legacy Series uniform around the B-21 Raider, but it already wore that set against Navy on October 3. More: [Army schedule](/stories/army-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Air Force schedule](/stories/air-force-uniform-schedule-2026).

### TCU at No. 22 Arizona: Friday Night Sandstorm

**Friday, 10:15 p.m. (8:15 p.m. Arizona), ESPN.** **Arizona: Expected, Sandstorm.** Arizona scheduled this as its first official Sandstorm-themed game, with fans asked to wear white. The Sandstorm set is a red helmet with a white jersey and red numbers. Arizona wore it with red pants at West Virginia on October 10. **TCU: Expected, white.** More: [Arizona schedule](/stories/arizona-uniform-schedule-2026) and [TCU schedule](/stories/tcu-uniform-schedule-2026).

## Dates to Watch

- **Salute to Service week.** Veterans Day is Wednesday, November 11, and a long list of schools hold military appreciation games on November 7: LSU ("LSU Salutes"), Utah, Boston College, NC State, Houston, Minnesota, Georgia Southern, Louisiana, UNLV and Utah State, plus East Carolina on Friday and App State's Heroes Day. These games usually bring a patch or a helmet decal rather than a new uniform. **NC State** is the one most likely to bring out something bigger: its camo Wolfpack Warriors set.
- **East Carolina** has tagged Friday's game against South Florida as Paint It Black and Night of the Boneyard, the night it has used for its black alternate.
- **Southern Miss** calls Thursday's game against James Madison a Blackout.
- **Coastal Carolina** has Homecoming and a stadium-wide Black Out against Old Dominion.
- **Homecoming watch:** Baylor, Houston, Purdue, Tulane, North Texas, Troy, Coastal Carolina, Georgia Southern and Washington, where it is also the Purple Reign Game.
