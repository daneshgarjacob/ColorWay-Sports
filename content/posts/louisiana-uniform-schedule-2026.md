The uniforms are Adidas, and the set is about as clean as it gets in the Sun Belt: red and white, with LOUISIANA spelled out across the chest and the stripes kept to the sleeves and the pants. The detail that makes it local is on the helmet bumper. The 337 is the area code for Lafayette and the rest of Acadiana, a small nod to home that shows up every time a player turns to face the camera.

Four games into Michael Desormeaux's fifth season, the pattern is simple. Red jersey at Our Lady of Lourdes Stadium, white jersey on the road, and the pants change from week to week. Louisiana went red over white for the opener, white over red at USC, all red against UAB and all white at Charlotte. No two games have matched yet.

**September 5 vs. Lamar.** ★ Confirmed from Louisiana's own game photography, dated to the day of the game: **red helmet, red jersey, white pants** for the opener at Our Lady of Lourdes Stadium at Cajun Field. The Ragin' Cajuns ran past Lamar 38-7.

**September 12 at USC.** ★ Confirmed from Louisiana's own game photography, dated to the day of the game: **red helmet, white jersey, red pants** at the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum. USC wore cardinal over gold. Louisiana scored 30 points on a top-15 team, but USC won 49-30. Our [USC 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/usc-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Trojans side.

**September 19 vs. UAB.** ★ Confirmed from Louisiana's own game photography, dated to the day of the game: **red helmet, red jersey, red pants**, the first all-red look of the season. UAB wore white jerseys over green pants. Louisiana made the late stops it needed and won 21-14.

**September 26 at Charlotte.** ★ Confirmed from Louisiana's own game photography, dated to the day of the game: **red helmet, white jersey, white pants** at Jerry Richardson Stadium. Charlotte wore green at home. The Cajuns defense dominated in a 34-7 win, with Brent Gordon forcing a fumble on the way to national defensive honors.

**October 3 vs. Arkansas State.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **white helmet, red jersey, red pants** for Homecoming and Gridiron Alumni Day, red over red under the white shell. Arkansas State wore a black helmet and white jersey. It was tied 20-20 late, and Tony Sterner kicked a 37-yard field goal as time expired for a 23-20 win in the Sun Belt opener. Louisiana is 4-1.

**October 10 at Louisiana Tech.** White expected. An in-state game at Joe Aillet Stadium in Ruston, 7:30 p.m. Eastern on ESPN+.

**October 17 vs. Troy.** Red expected. Family Weekend and the Hall of Fame Game in Lafayette. The TV slot is set to be announced October 5.

**October 24 at Southern Miss.** White expected. It is Southern Miss's Homecoming at Renasant Stadium in Hattiesburg, and the Golden Eagles have worn all black at home this season. Kickoff time still to be set.

**October 31.** Bye.

**November 7 vs. South Alabama.** Red expected. Louisiana Salutes, the program's military appreciation game, where a special helmet decal or patch is common.

**November 12 at UL Monroe.** White expected. A Thursday night in-state rivalry game at Malone Stadium in Monroe, 9 p.m. Eastern.

**November 21 vs. Coastal Carolina.** Red expected. Senior Day and the regular season home finale. Coastal is expected in white; our [Coastal Carolina 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/coastal-carolina-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Chanticleers.

**November 28 at Georgia State.** White expected. The regular season finale at Center Parc Stadium in Atlanta, kickoff time still to be set.

## Will Louisiana Wear an Alternate in 2026?

Not yet. Four games in, Louisiana has stayed in red and white and has not shown an alternate jersey or helmet. The open dates for one are the themed home games: Homecoming against Arkansas State on October 3, Louisiana Salutes against South Alabama on November 7 and Senior Day against Coastal Carolina on November 21. The program has not announced anything for any of them, and we will not put an alternate in the grid until it does.

## The Bottom Line

Louisiana runs one of the cleanest closets in the Sun Belt: one red helmet with 337 on the bumper, a red jersey at home, a white jersey on the road and a pants color that keeps changing. Four games have produced four different combinations, and the all-red look against UAB was the strongest of them. The questions left are what the Ragin' Cajuns wear for Homecoming and Louisiana Salutes, and whether anything beyond red and white comes out this season. The grid above has every date, and we add a star as each uniform is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Louisiana wearing this week?**

Louisiana hosts Arkansas State for Homecoming on Saturday, October 3, and the red home jersey is expected with the red helmet. The Ragin' Cajuns wore red jerseys in both home games so far, with white pants against Lamar and red pants against UAB. Louisiana has not announced its combination for Homecoming.

**What does 337 on the Louisiana helmet mean?**

It is the area code for Lafayette and the surrounding Acadiana region. The number sits on the front bumper of the red Ragin' Cajuns helmet.

**What are Louisiana's football uniforms for 2026?**

A red home jersey and a white road jersey, both with LOUISIANA across the chest, worn with red or white pants. The helmet in every game so far has been red with white center stripes and the Ragin' Cajuns logo.

**Who makes Louisiana's uniforms?**

Adidas. The Adidas logo is on the jerseys, and both the red and white sets are Adidas.

**What is Louisiana's homecoming game in 2026?**

Arkansas State on Saturday, October 3 at 8 p.m. Eastern at Our Lady of Lourdes Stadium in Lafayette, on ESPN+. It is also Gridiron Alumni Day.

**When does Louisiana play UL Monroe in 2026?**

Thursday, November 12 at 9 p.m. Eastern at Malone Stadium in Monroe. Louisiana is expected in its white road jersey.
