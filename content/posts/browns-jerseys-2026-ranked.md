The Browns have two jerseys on the 2026 schedule, brown at home and white on the road, and a lot of options around them. The all-brown Alpha Dawg look and the white alternate helmet are both in the closet, but neither has a date, and both are built on these same two jerseys. There is no Rivalries uniform yet either, because the program has not reached the AFC North. We put Cleveland twenty-ninth in our [best NFL uniforms of 2026](/stories/best-nfl-uniforms-2026-ranked) at a C+, and the gap between these two jerseys is the main reason it is not lower.

Here are both Cleveland Browns jerseys for 2026, ranked and graded, with the helmet and pants each one wears. For the week-by-week jersey schedule 2026, the [Browns 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/browns-uniform-schedule-2026) has every game.

The brown home jersey has white numbers outlined in orange and orange and white sleeve stripes, worn under the orange helmet with white or brown pants. It projects to all eight home games at Huntington Bank Field: the Carolina Panthers, Pittsburgh Steelers, Baltimore Ravens, Houston Texans, Las Vegas Raiders, Cincinnati Bengals, Atlanta Falcons and Indianapolis Colts. The Browns have not published a uniform schedule, so none of those is confirmed.

This jersey is also the base of Alpha Dawg, the all-brown look with brown pants and the brown alternate helmet. It has no announced 2026 date. Our read is the Thursday night game against Pittsburgh in Week 4, Cleveland's only home prime-time game, but that is a projection, not a team announcement.

Brown is a color nobody else would choose, which is exactly why it belongs to Cleveland, and the orange helmet makes the whole look instantly recognizable. The jersey is still the weak point of the set. Brown is dark enough that the orange trim barely registers, and on a gray December afternoon the uniform turns into a dark block under a bright helmet. It grades a C+. It is honest and tidy, but it needs the orange to do more.

The white road jersey has brown numbers outlined in orange and brown and orange sleeve stripes, worn under the orange helmet. It projects to all nine road games: at the Jaguars, Buccaneers, Jets, Titans, Steelers, Saints, Giants, Ravens and Bengals. It opened the season at Jacksonville with white pants, and the Jaguars won 34-10. The matchup still graded a B on our [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026), because the orange helmets were the only warm color on the field against Jacksonville's teal.

This jersey is also the base of the white alternate helmet look, introduced in 2023 and worn with orange pants. It has no announced 2026 date, and alternate helmets are capped at three games a season.

White is where the Browns look their best. Brown numbers on white have real contrast, the sleeve stripes read cleanly, and the orange helmet stands out more against white than against brown. It grades a B-, the top mark in the closet. It is simple, it knows what it is, and it lets the best helmet in the division carry the look.

## The Bottom Line on the Browns Jersey Rankings

The Browns jerseys rank by how much the orange helmet stands out. White gives it the most contrast and grades a B-, and brown, the jersey the franchise is known for, grades a C+.

Cleveland keeps adding helmets around a shell that is already the most distinctive in the league. The simpler fix is a brown jersey with more orange in it.
