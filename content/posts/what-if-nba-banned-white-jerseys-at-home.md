**The NBA doesn't have a home jersey anymore.** It has a closet, and the home team grabs whatever it wants. So let's play this out: what if the league banned white at home? The home team wears color. The visitor wears white. Every night, all season, playoffs included.

This is a what-if. The NBA has not proposed anything like it, and no team has asked for it. But we think it would fix the league's uniform mess overnight, and we ran the numbers on who it would hurt. Some of you are going to hate this.

## Quick Answers

- **Do NBA home teams have to wear white?** No. Since Nike took over in 2017-18, the league has had no home or road uniforms. The home team picks, and the visitor picks something that contrasts.
- **How often does the home team wear white now?** In **304 of the 1,200 games** the NBA has dated for 2026-27, the home team is scheduled in the white Association jersey. That's about **25 percent**. Our count, from the league's own schedule.
- **Who loses the most?** The **Bulls**, with 22 of 40 home dates in white. Then the **Knicks** (16), **Celtics** and **Thunder** (15 each).
- **Who barely notices?** The **Nuggets** (3 home whites), **Suns** and **Raptors** (4 each).
- **Our verdict:** do it.

## How It Works Today

When Nike became the NBA's uniform maker in 2017-18, the league dropped "home" and "away" completely. The old white home uniform became the Association Edition and the old color road uniform became the Icon Edition, [CBS Sports reported at the time](https://www.cbssports.com/nba/news/look-nba-eliminates-home-and-road-uniforms-teases-new-nike-jerseys). Add the Statement, City and Classic Editions and a team can carry five jerseys a season.

The home team chooses its jersey for every game, and the visitor works around it. We explained why teams love that setup in [why NBA teams don't wear white at home](/stories/why-nba-teams-dont-wear-white-at-home): more jerseys on the court in front of the home crowd means more jerseys sold.

So the home team already wears color three games out of four. The problem isn't white or color. The problem is that nobody knows which until the schedule comes out, and 385 games don't have an Association white on the floor at all.

## The Rule We Would Write

**Home team: any color jersey it wants. Road team: the white Association. No exceptions.**

That's not a crazy idea. It's the NHL. Hockey home teams wore white for decades, then the league flipped it for the 2003-04 season so home teams wear their dark sweaters and visitors wear white, [a change driven by the rise of colored third jerseys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHL_uniform). Every NHL fan knows what the home team is wearing before the puck drops. You can check it yourself on our [NHL uniform schedule](/stories/nhl-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

The NBA version keeps everything teams like about the current system. The Icon, the Statement, the City and the Classic all stay home, in front of the fans most likely to buy them. The only jersey that moves is the white, and it goes on the road, where it gets seen in 29 other arenas.

## The Winners

**The Lakers.** Gold at home is the oldest look the Lakers have. Before the Nike era the Lakers [traditionally wore gold at home and purple on the road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logos_and_uniforms_of_the_Los_Angeles_Lakers), one of the few teams to wear a light color other than white at home. A ban on home whites makes gold at Crypto.com Arena the law. The Sunday whites, which started in 2002-03 as a tribute to broadcaster Chick Hearn, would become a road jersey. We can live with that. - **Red on red:** the Bulls' red Icon, the Rockets' red Icon, the Raptors' red Icon and the Heat's red Statement. Bulls vs. Rockets in color is a blur.
- **Green on green:** the Celtics' Kelly green against the Bucks' darker green. Close enough to ruin a broadcast.
- **Blue on blue:** the Thunder's blue, the Mavericks' blue and the Clippers' blue Icons.
- **Black on black:** the Heat's black Icon, the Trail Blazers' black Icon and the Bulls' black pinstripe Statement.

Color against color can work when the colors are far apart. On Wednesday the Lakers wore the gold Icon at the Clippers, who were in their blue Icon, and it looked great. That's in our [NBA uniform tracker](/stories/nba-uniform-tracker-2026-27). But "far apart" is a judgment call, and judgment calls are how you get red on red. White on the road ends the argument.

## Why We'd Do It Anyway

We've argued the other side before. In May we said the NBA should [bring back white at home in the playoffs](/stories/nba-only-major-league-killed-home-jersey-tradition), and we still think any fixed rule beats no rule. But be honest about which rule could actually happen. Teams aren't giving up colored jerseys at home. The money is too good. A white-at-home mandate fights the business. A color-at-home mandate matches it, and still gives fans the one thing they've lost since 2017: certainty.

The Bulls, Knicks and Celtics lose a home look they love. Everybody else gets a league where you know what you're watching before you turn it on.

Bulls fans, Knicks fans, Celtics fans: tell us we're wrong.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do NBA teams have to wear white at home?**

No. The NBA dropped home and road uniform designations when Nike became its uniform supplier in 2017-18. The home team picks its jersey for each game and the visiting team wears one that contrasts.

**When did NBA teams stop wearing white at home?**

The rule ended with the 2017-18 season, when the white home uniform became the Association Edition and the color road uniform became the Icon Edition. Teams have mixed in color at home more every season since.

**How often does the NBA home team wear white in 2026-27?**

In 304 of the 1,200 games the NBA has dated so far, the home team is scheduled in the white Association jersey, about 25 percent. That's our count from the league's published uniform schedule. Every team's full calendar is in our [2026-27 NBA uniform schedule](/stories/nba-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

**Which NBA team wears white at home the most?**

The Chicago Bulls, scheduled in the white Association for 22 of their 40 dated home games. The Knicks are next with 16, then the Celtics and Thunder with 15 each.

**Which NBA team wears white at home the least?**

The Denver Nuggets, with 3 home whites on the schedule. The Suns and Raptors have 4 each.

**Who decides which jersey an NBA team wears?**

The home team. It sets its jersey schedule for the season, and the road team picks a contrasting jersey from its own closet. We covered it in detail in [who chooses NBA playoff jerseys](/stories/who-chooses-nba-playoff-jerseys).

**Why do the Lakers wear gold at home?**

It's tradition. The Lakers wore gold at home and purple on the road for decades, even when most of the league wore white at home. Their white "Sunday whites" started in the 2002-03 season as a tribute to broadcaster Chick Hearn.

**What is the Miami Heat's White Hot tradition?**

Heat fans wear white to home playoff games. The team launched the White Hot campaign in 2006, the year Miami won its first championship.

**Does the NHL make home teams wear color?**

Yes. Since the 2003-04 season, NHL home teams wear their dark sweaters and road teams wear white. Before that, home teams generally wore white.

**Has the NBA proposed banning white jerseys at home?**

No. This story is a hypothetical. The NBA has made no such proposal and teams still choose their own home jerseys.
