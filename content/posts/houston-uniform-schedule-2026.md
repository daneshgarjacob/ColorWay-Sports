Houston has three jerseys, three helmets and one alternate that the whole city plans a weekend around. This is the full Houston 2026 uniform schedule: the red home set, the white road set, the light blue Houston Blue alternate that comes back on October 3, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games. The base sets come from Nike's 2025 redesign, Houston's first real overhaul since 2022, and the changes were smart ones. The rigid all-caps serif wordmark gave way to a flowing script Houston. The collars and sleeves got contrast trim for the first time since 2012. Most of all, a bold center stripe came back to the primary red helmet, which had not carried one since 1999. The whole point of that project was to make the red, the white and the Houston Blue look like one family instead of three separate ideas, and it worked. Big 12 home teams wear color and visitors wear white, so most weeks the only question is which pants and which shell. The exception is the Houston Blue game, which the athletic department treats as an annual event.

## The Bottom Line

Two games in, Houston has worn red twice and shown both pants. The rest of the season sorts itself neatly: white on the road because the Big 12 says so, red at home, and two dates worth circling. October 3 brings Houston Blue back for the Built By Houston Game, and November 28 pairs Senior Day with a Wear Black crowd theme that the program has not explained yet. Friday night in Lubbock is already settled, and the answer is all white. The grid above has every date, and we add a star as each uniform is confirmed on the field.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Houston wearing this week?**

All white. Houston announced the combination on Wednesday for Friday night at Texas Tech: white helmet with the red interlocking UH and a red facemask, white jersey with the red script Houston, white pants. Kickoff is 8 p.m. Eastern on FOX, and Texas Tech is wearing its all-black Patrick Mahomes "Let's Ride" set.

**What are Houston's football uniforms for 2026?**

A red home jersey and a white road jersey, both with a script Houston wordmark and contrast collar and sleeve trim, plus the light blue Houston Blue alternate. The helmets are a red shell with a thick white center stripe and the interlocking UH, a white shell with the red UH, and the white Houston Blue shell with a light blue Houston script. Nike introduced the current set in June 2025.

**When is Houston wearing the Houston Blue uniforms in 2026?**

Saturday, October 3, at home against UCF. Houston is promoting the game as the Built By Houston Game with a Wear Houston Blue crowd theme. It will be the fourth time the Cougars have worn the light blue set since it debuted in 2023.

**What is the Houston Blue uniform?**

It is Houston's tribute to the powder blue the Houston Oilers wore, a light blue jersey with red numbers, a white helmet with a light blue Houston script and white pants with a red stripe. The original 2023 version drew a cease and desist letter from the NFL over the Oilers marks, so Houston redesigned it for 2024 and has worn the updated version since.

**Why does ESPN list the Texas Tech game on September 19?**

ESPN's schedule feed runs on UTC. A 7 p.m. Central kickoff on Friday, September 18 is already Saturday in London, so the game shows a September 19 date there. The November 13 game at Colorado has the same one-day shift for the same reason. Both are Friday night games.

**How many games does Houston play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: six at TDECU Stadium, six on the road, with a bye on October 31. Two of the road games are Friday nights, at Texas Tech on September 18 and at Colorado on November 13. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
