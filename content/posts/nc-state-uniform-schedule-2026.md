adidas outfits NC State, and the Tuffy wolf head in the sailor cap is the helmet logo, usually in an oversized version that nearly covers the side of the shell. From 2020 through 2025 the white helmet with a red facemask was the most common choice, with red and black shells in rotation. In 2025 the program opened with the City of Oaks set, a red script jersey with an acorn and a white script helmet with a center stripe. For 2026 there are two changes so far: a smaller Tuffy on the red helmet for the opener, and the Wolfpack Warriors camo set built around the program's new US Army partnership. We break that uniform down in our [Wolfpack Warriors review](/stories/nc-state-wolfpack-warriors-army-uniform-2026). ACC home teams wear color and visitors wear white, so the road dates are predictable and the home dates come down to red, black, or something special.

**August 29 at Virginia.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **red helmet with the smaller Tuffy decal, white jersey, red pants**, against Virginia in orange jerseys, orange pants, and white helmets at Scott Stadium. The downsized Tuffy was the headline before kickoff, and some fans wanted the oversized version back. Virginia won 34-8.

**September 5.** Bye.

**September 11 vs. Richmond.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **the Wolfpack Warriors set**, a black helmet with a red facemask, a black and gray camo jersey, and matching camo pants, for the home opener at Carter-Finley Stadium. It was a Friday night game on the anniversary of 9/11, and NC State won 73-0. Our full breakdown and grade are in the [Wolfpack Warriors review](/stories/nc-state-wolfpack-warriors-army-uniform-2026).

**September 19 at Vanderbilt.** White expected. A nonconference trip to FirstBank Stadium in Nashville, 12:45 p.m. Eastern on SEC Network, with Vanderbilt in its home colors.

**September 26 vs. App State.** Red expected. A 7:30 p.m. Eastern kickoff on ESPNU, and a night home game is the natural spot for the black alternate if NC State wants it.

**October 3 vs. Louisville.** Red expected. The ACC home opener, with Louisville in white per the [Louisville 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/louisville-uniform-schedule-2026).

**October 10 vs. Wake Forest.** Red expected. A third straight home game.

**October 17.** Bye.

**October 23 at Stanford.** White expected. NC State's first game ever at Stanford, a Friday night kickoff at 10:30 p.m. Eastern (7:30 p.m. Pacific) on ESPN. ESPN files it under October 24 because its feed runs on UTC.

**October 31 vs. California.** Red expected. This is Homecoming, on Halloween.

**November 7 vs. Duke.** Red expected, with a big asterisk. NC State has designated this as its Military Appreciation game, which makes it the most logical date for a second Wolfpack Warriors appearance. Nothing has been announced, so we are listing red until we see it. Duke's side is on the [Duke 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/duke-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 14 vs. Syracuse.** Red expected. The final home game of the season.

**November 21 at Florida State.** White expected. This is Florida State's Homecoming and Seminole Heritage game, so watch for garnet or a turquoise surprise on the [Florida State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/florida-state-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 28 at North Carolina.** White expected. The regular season finale at Kenan Stadium, with Carolina blue expected on the [North Carolina 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/north-carolina-uniform-schedule-2026).

## The Bottom Line

NC State has already shown its range: a traditional road look with a quieter Tuffy, then the most talked about alternate of its season in the home opener. From here the home dates default to red, the road dates to white, and the question marks sit on night games and Military Appreciation against Duke on November 7. The grid above has every date, and we add a star as each uniform is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is NC State wearing this week?**

NC State plays at Vanderbilt on Saturday, September 19, and the white road jersey is expected. Last week against Richmond, the Wolfpack wore the Wolfpack Warriors camo set with a black helmet. The week-by-week grid above lists every game with the expected set, and we update it as combinations are confirmed.

**What are NC State's football uniforms for 2026?**

A red home jersey, a white road jersey, a black alternate, and the new Wolfpack Warriors camo set. The helmets come in red, white, and black with the Tuffy wolf head, plus the black Warriors helmet with a one-color red Tuffy and a red facemask. adidas makes the uniforms.

**What is the Wolfpack Warriors uniform?**

A black and gray camo uniform from adidas honoring NC State's partnership with the US Army. It debuted in the 73-0 win over Richmond on Friday, September 11. Our [full review](/stories/nc-state-wolfpack-warriors-army-uniform-2026) has the details and the grade.

**Will NC State wear the camo uniform again in 2026?**

Nothing has been announced. The most likely date is the November 7 home game against Duke, which NC State has designated as its Military Appreciation game. We will update this page if the Wolfpack Warriors set is confirmed for a second game.

**Why did NC State change its helmet logo?**

For the opener at Virginia, NC State wore its red helmet with a smaller Tuffy decal instead of the oversized wolf head it has used in recent seasons. The program has not said whether the smaller logo is permanent, and fan reaction was mixed.

**How many games does NC State play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven at Carter-Finley Stadium and five on the road, with byes on September 5 and October 17. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
