Week 6 of the 2026 NFL season runs from Thursday, October 15 to Monday, October 19, with 14 games and four teams on bye: the Cincinnati Bengals, Detroit Lions, Miami Dolphins and Minnesota Vikings. It opens with the best-dressed Thursday night of the season so far. Denver wears its **orange throwback** under the royal blue "D" helmet, and Seattle answers in its **royal blue throwback**, both confirmed by the teams on October 13. On Sunday, New Orleans brings its **all-white alternate** under the white helmet to the Giants, the Chargers wear **white over gold pants** in Kansas City, Houston and Jacksonville play at **Wembley Stadium**, and Dallas visits Green Bay on Sunday night. It is also the third and last weekend of [Crucial Catch](/stories/nfl-crucial-catch-2026-schedule).

**Update, October 19:** Every Thursday and Sunday game is final, and the list below now shows what each team actually wore, with scores and our matchup grades. Five teams changed something from our projection: the Texans (navy pants), Falcons (white pants), Patriots (navy pants), Panthers (black pants) and Bills (royal blue pants). The best-dressed game was the opener, Seattle's royal throwback at Denver's orange throwback, an A+. Monday night is Washington in white over gold at San Francisco in scarlet, both announced.

Below is every Week 6 look we know, starting with the special ones and then all 14 games in order. **Confirmed** means the team has announced the jersey or the full combination. **Expected** is our read from that team's uniform schedule, and it gets replaced with what was actually worn in our [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026) once the game is played. Helmets and jerseys project reliably; pants are the piece teams change the week of the game, so treat every expected pants call as the least certain part of the line.

Confirmed on both sides.** Denver showed the look on Tuesday, October 13: a rack of **royal blue helmets with the orange "D" and white bronco**, an orange center stripe trimmed in white and white facemasks. The team's release describes the rest as **orange jerseys** with white numbers and Legacy Blue trim, **white pants with an orange and Legacy Blue side stripe**, and orange and blue striped socks. Seattle confirmed its side the same day: the **royal blue throwback with the silver helmet and silver pants**. Orange against royal blue is the most colorful matchup of the week, and both clubs are in retro sets at once. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What uniforms are NFL teams wearing in Week 6 of 2026?**

Most teams are in their standard sets, home in color and visitors in white. The special looks are the Broncos' orange throwback and the Seahawks' royal blue throwback on Thursday night, the Saints' all-white alternate under the white helmet at the Giants, the Chargers in white over gold pants at Kansas City, and the Jaguars hosting the Texans at Wembley Stadium in London.

**What are the Broncos wearing against the Seahawks on Thursday Night Football?**

The orange throwback, under a royal blue helmet with the orange "D" logo and a white facemask, with white pants carrying an orange and blue stripe. Denver showed the helmets on Tuesday, October 13. Kickoff is Thursday, October 15 at 8:15 p.m. Eastern on Prime Video at Empower Field at Mile High.

**What are the Seahawks wearing in Week 6?**

The royal blue throwback with the silver helmet and silver pants, at Denver on Thursday, October 15. Seattle confirmed it on October 13. It is the first of three royal blue games this season; the others are Week 7 against Kansas City and Week 13 against Dallas.

**What are the Saints wearing against the Giants?**

The white alternate under the white helmet, the all-white set with gold numbers. New Orleans published it on its 2026 schedule. The Giants wear royal blue at home, so it is all white against royal over white on Sunday, October 18 at 1:00 p.m. Eastern on FOX.

**What are the Chargers wearing against the Chiefs in Week 6?**

The white jersey over gold pants under the white helmet, as published by the team on September 1. It is one of two white-over-gold games this season. Kansas City is expected in red at home.

**What are the Texans and Jaguars wearing in London?**

Jacksonville is the designated home team at Wembley Stadium, and we expect the Jaguars in the black helmet, teal jersey and black pants they wore against Philadelphia in London last week, with the Texans in their white road set. Neither club has announced a jersey. Kickoff is 9:30 a.m. Eastern on Sunday, October 18, on NFL Network.

**What are the Cowboys wearing on Sunday Night Football in Week 6?**

The standard white jersey at Lambeau Field, from the schedule Dallas published on September 2, with the silver star helmet. Green Bay is expected in green with gold pants for its Packers Vs. Cancer game. Kickoff is 8:20 p.m. Eastern on NBC.

**What are the Commanders and 49ers wearing on Monday Night Football?**

Both teams have announced their uniforms. Washington wears its white jersey under the standard burgundy helmet with gold pants, and San Francisco wears scarlet at home with the gold helmet and gold pants. The game kicks off at 8:15 p.m. Eastern on Monday, October 19, on ESPN and ABC.

**Which NFL teams are on bye in Week 6?**

The Cincinnati Bengals, Detroit Lions, Miami Dolphins and Minnesota Vikings, so 28 teams play in Week 6, starting with Seahawks at Broncos on Thursday, October 15 and ending with Commanders at 49ers on Monday, October 19.

**Which NFL teams have Crucial Catch games in Week 6?**

Five clubs have named a Week 6 home game: the Colts, Giants, Raiders, Chiefs and Patriots. Denver's Thursday night game is its only home date in the Crucial Catch window. Crucial Catch does not change the jerseys, only the hats, gloves and sideline gear.
