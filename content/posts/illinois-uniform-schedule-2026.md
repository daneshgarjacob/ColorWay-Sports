Illinois has three jersey colors, a rack of orange helmets and a navy one, and a home crowd that picks a color theme for the big games. This is the full Illinois 2026 uniform schedule: the navy home set, the white road set, the orange alternate, the helmets, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games in Bret Bielema's sixth season. Two games in, the Fighting Illini have worn navy over orange both times, with a patriotic helmet for Duke. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

Illinois wears Nike, and the Bielema era has leaned hard on orange: orange pants with nearly everything, orange helmets almost every week, and an all-orange set for the loudest nights. In 2025 the Illini rotated through the block I helmet, wordmark and script helmets, a white jersey with both orange and white pants, the all-orange look and a navy script helmet for the Music City Bowl. We have not seen a new 2026 alternate announced. Big Ten home teams wear color and visitors wear white, so navy is the home default and white travels, but the athletic department's themed home games are worth watching. The Purdue game is a "Hail to the Orange Out" and the Nebraska game is a "Hail to the Blue Out."

**September 3 vs. UAB.** ★ Confirmed from the Big Ten Network broadcast and Illinois Athletics game photos: **orange helmet, navy jersey, orange pants**. The helmet is the orange shell with the navy block I and a navy and white center stripe. UAB wore gold helmets with white jerseys and white pants. Illinois won 42-23 on a Thursday night at Gies Memorial Stadium. One calendar note: ESPN files this game under September 4 because its feed runs on UTC and an 8 p.m. Central kickoff lands after midnight there. The correct date is Thursday, September 3.

**September 12 vs. Duke.** ★ Confirmed from Illinois Athletics game photos: **orange helmet, navy jersey, orange pants**, the same set as the opener with one change on the helmet. The block I on the side was filled with stars and stripes, and an American flag sat on the front bumper, one day after the September 11 anniversary. It was Hall of Fame Weekend and FamILLy Day in Champaign, with a 2:30 p.m. Central kickoff on FS1. Duke was in white and won 31-27.

**September 19 vs. Southern Illinois.** Navy expected, and still unconfirmed. Illinois won the nonconference finale against the FCS Salukis 48-10 in a 1 p.m. Central kickoff on Peacock, but no broadcast frame or game photograph has surfaced yet, so the cell stays unstarred until we can see the combination.

**September 26 at Ohio State.** White expected. The Big Ten opener at Ohio Stadium, 11 a.m. Central on FOX, on the Buckeyes' Hall of Fame Weekend, with Ohio State in scarlet. Our [Ohio State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/ohio-state-uniform-schedule-2026) tracks the home side.

**October 3 vs. Purdue.** Navy expected, with an asterisk. It is Homecoming and a "Hail to the Orange Out," and Illinois has the all-orange set in the closet, so orange is a real possibility for the Purdue Cannon game. Purdue will be in white. Kickoff time to be announced.

**October 10 at Michigan State.** White expected. It is Michigan State's Homecoming at Spartan Stadium, with the Spartans in green. The [Michigan State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/michigan-state-uniform-schedule-2026) has their side.

**October 17.** Bye.

**October 24 vs. Oregon.** Navy expected. Military Appreciation and Foundation Weekend. Illinois has already used a stars-and-stripes helmet this season, so a patriotic helmet could make a return for this one. Oregon brings more combinations than anyone in the conference; see the [Oregon 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/oregon-uniform-schedule-2026).

**October 31 at Maryland.** White expected. Halloween at SECU Stadium, where the Terrapins are expected in red. The [Maryland 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/maryland-uniform-schedule-2026) covers the home side.

**November 6 vs. Nebraska.** Navy expected. A Friday night game at 7 p.m. Central on FOX, which ESPN files under November 7. It is Dads Day and a "Hail to the Blue Out," so navy is the easy call, and the navy script helmet from last season's bowl game would fit the theme. Nebraska will be in white; see the [Nebraska 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/nebraska-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 13 at UCLA.** White expected. Another Friday night, 6 p.m. Pacific (8 p.m. Central) on FOX at the Rose Bowl, which ESPN lists under November 14. The [UCLA 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/ucla-uniform-schedule-2026) tracks the Bruins in blue.

**November 21 vs. Iowa.** Navy expected. Senior Day and the final home game, with Iowa in white. Our [Iowa 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/iowa-uniform-schedule-2026) has the Hawkeyes.

**November 28 at Northwestern.** White expected. The Land of Lincoln Trophy game at Ryan Field in Evanston closes the regular season. Kickoff time to be announced.

## The Bottom Line

Illinois has opened 2026 in the same base look twice, orange helmet, navy jersey, orange pants, and used the helmet to mark the occasion against Duke. Expect navy at home and white on the road at Ohio State, Michigan State, Maryland, UCLA and Northwestern. The dates that could break the pattern are the themed ones: the Orange Out Homecoming against Purdue, Military Appreciation against Oregon and the Blue Out Friday night against Nebraska. The grid above has every date, and we star each game as the combination is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Illinois wearing this week?**

Illinois beat Southern Illinois 48-10 on Saturday, September 19, and the navy home jersey was expected, though we have not seen a frame of the combination yet. Last week against Duke, the Illini wore the orange helmet with a stars-and-stripes block I, the navy jersey and orange pants. The week-by-week grid above lists every game with the expected set, and we update it as combinations are confirmed.

**What are Illinois's football uniforms for 2026?**

A navy home jersey with orange numerals, a white road jersey, and an orange alternate jersey, with orange, white and navy pants. The primary helmet is orange with a navy block I and a navy and white center stripe, and Illinois also wears wordmark and script helmets, including a navy script helmet. Nike makes the uniforms.

**What color helmet does Illinois wear?**

Orange, almost every week. The primary has the navy block I, and the alternates swap in an ILLINOIS or script wordmark. Illinois wore a navy helmet with a script logo in the 2025 Music City Bowl, and it wore a stars-and-stripes block I on the orange helmet against Duke on September 12.

**Is Illinois wearing orange for the Purdue game?**

Maybe. The October 3 Homecoming game against Purdue is a "Hail to the Orange Out," and Illinois has an all-orange uniform. The program has not announced the uniform yet, so our grid holds the navy home default until it does.

**What did Illinois wear against Duke?**

The orange helmet with a stars-and-stripes block I and a flag on the bumper, the navy jersey and orange pants, on September 12 at Gies Memorial Stadium. Duke won 31-27.

**What did Illinois wear against UAB?**

The orange helmet with the navy block I, the navy jersey and orange pants in the Thursday night opener on September 3. Illinois won 42-23.

**How many games does Illinois play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven at Gies Memorial Stadium and five on the road, with a bye on October 17. Nine are Big Ten games, and two are Friday nights: Nebraska on November 6 and at UCLA on November 13. The full schedule with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
