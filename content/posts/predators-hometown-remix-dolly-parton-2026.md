**Every Nashville Predator was named Dolly on Saturday.** The Predators debuted their Hometown Remix jerseys against the St. Louis Blues at Bridgestone Arena, and in warmups every player wore **DOLLY** across the shoulders in place of his own name. The ice had a **gold butterfly at center ice**, which the team called "a center ice logo fit for the Queen of Country." Nashville won **3-1**.

The team's caption rated the jerseys **"925/10,"** a nod to Dolly Parton's "9 to 5."

## What the Predators' Hometown Remix Jersey Looks Like

- **Color:** navy, with gold numbers outlined in white.
- **Stripes:** gold and white bands on the sleeves and the hem, and gold stripes on the navy socks.
- **Crest:** the gold sabre-tooth cat on tonal navy filigree embroidery, inspired by the rhinestone stage suits of country music.
- **Details:** gold flourishes down the lower sleeves and an iris, Tennessee's state flower, worked into the sleeve design.

## DOLLY in Warmups, Real Names in the Game

The DOLLY nameplates were a **warmup** look. The game highlights show the Predators back in their own names for the game, in the same navy Remix jersey. St. Louis wore its white road jerseys.

## The Game

Nashville beat St. Louis 3-1. Steven Stamkos scored on the power play in the Remix jersey, the 248th power-play goal of his career, which moved him past Luc Robitaille into fifth place all time. Ross Colton and Nick Perbix, on an empty net, also scored for Nashville.

## Our Grade: A

**A.** The Remix jersey is one of the best in the set: navy and gold done like a country stage suit, without losing what makes it a Predators sweater. Putting DOLLY on every back and a butterfly on the ice turned a jersey debut into a Nashville event. It only misses an A+ because the DOLLY nameplates did not stay on for the game.

## FAQ

### Why did the Predators wear DOLLY nameplates?

It was a tribute to Dolly Parton on the night Nashville debuted its Hometown Remix jerseys, which draw on country music stagewear. The team posted the photos with "For Dolly 🦋" and rated the jerseys "925/10."

### Did the Predators wear DOLLY nameplates during the game?

No. The DOLLY nameplates were worn in warmups. In the game the players wore their own names on the same navy Hometown Remix jerseys.

### What do the Predators' Hometown Remix jerseys look like?

Navy with gold numbers and gold and white stripes, the gold sabre-tooth crest on navy filigree embroidery inspired by rhinestone stage suits, and an iris, the Tennessee state flower, on the sleeves.

### What was at center ice for Predators vs. Blues?

A gold butterfly with SMASHVILLE and a signature, which the team called "a center ice logo fit for the Queen of Country."

### Who won Predators vs. Blues on November 7?

Nashville won 3-1. Steven Stamkos scored his 248th career power-play goal, passing Luc Robitaille for fifth all time.
