The ColorWay Sports team was at Crypto.com Arena on Wednesday night for the Lakers home opener against the Warriors. The Lakers won 127-105. But the score is not what we walked out talking about. It feels like the Lakers are back.

The arena music sounds right again. The old three-point sound is back. And Dieter Ruehle is playing the organ. For the first time in a long time, a Lakers game felt like a Lakers game.

## Dieter Ruehle on the Organ Makes the Whole Building Better

We graded the news an A+ when [Dieter Ruehle returned as the Lakers organist](/stories/dieter-ruehle-returns-lakers-organist-2026), and hearing it in person was even better than we expected. Dieter on the keyboard completely changes the game. It is incredible.

The arena sounds better. The breaks in play feel like part of the night instead of dead air filled with whatever is trending. Anyone who has been to a Dodgers or Kings game knows what he does to a building, and now the Lakers have it again.

## The Game

The Lakers beat the Warriors 127-105 in front of 19,057, per ESPN. Luka Doncic led the Lakers with 28 points and 7 assists. Walker Kessler added 26 points and 9 rebounds. Stephen Curry led Golden State with 23 points.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey did the Lakers wear on opening night 2026?**
The gold Icon Edition. The Lakers wore gold at home against the Warriors on October 21, 2026, as the NBA's uniform schedule listed.

**What jersey did the Warriors wear against the Lakers?**
The royal blue Icon Edition.

**Who won Lakers vs Warriors on October 21, 2026?**
The Lakers, 127-105, at Crypto.com Arena.

**Who is the Lakers organist?**
Dieter Ruehle, who returned as the Lakers organist and musical director for the 2026-27 season. He is also the organist for the Dodgers and the LA Kings.

**Did the Lakers bring back their old three-point sound?**
Yes. We heard the old-school three-point sound at the home opener on October 21, 2026.

**How many times do the Lakers wear gold at home in 2026-27?**
Nine times, per the NBA's schedule: October 21, November 2, November 18, January 7, January 26, February 16, March 6, March 11 and April 6.

## The Bottom Line

The Lakers won, and they looked like the Lakers in gold at home. But the real win was the building. Dieter on the organ, the old three-point sound and a team that is about the team again. We will be back.
