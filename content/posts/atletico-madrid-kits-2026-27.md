Atletico Madrid's home shirt is always nice, and the reason is always the same: they keep it simple. The 2026/27 version is no different and it grades accordingly. The away shirt goes the other way and pays for it.

## What Does Atletico Madrid's 2026/27 Home Kit Look Like?

Red and white vertical stripes carrying a tonal trident pattern for Neptune's fountain, with deep royal blue framing a V-neck collar. Released on June 29, one of the earliest launches in Europe.

The trident is the detail worth praising, and specifically the restraint in how it is applied. It is there if you look for it and invisible if you do not, which is exactly how a tonal pattern should behave. Compare that to the clubs this season who built an entire shirt around a concept and ended up in the bottom half of our ranking.

The royal blue framing the V-neck is the other good decision. Atletico's blue is a genuine third colour rather than a trim afterthought, and using it to define the collar gives the stripes a boundary.

Simple, correct, and it will look the same in five years.

**Grade: A-**

## What Does Atletico Madrid's 2026/27 Away Kit Look Like?

Black with neon green accents, released on May 18, the earliest launch of any kit in our ranking.

Atletico usually get change kits right by keeping them quiet, and this one goes the other way.

The neon is a lot, and more importantly it does not have a reason to be there. Neon green is not an Atletico colour, it is not referencing anything in the club's history, and it does not pair naturally with the red and blue they use everywhere else. Strip the crest off and this could belong to anyone.

Not a disaster. Just not for us, and a strange departure from a club that is normally so disciplined.

**Grade: B-**

## What Does Atletico Madrid's 2026/27 Third Kit Look Like?

Light Pumice grey, with six thin hoops in red and navy across the body and sleeves, a navy polo collar with a red edge, a retro shield crest on the chest, and grey shorts and socks. Released August 24.

Nike went to the late 1990s for the template and it lands. The hoops are thin enough to read as detail rather than as a pattern, the grey is warm rather than flat, and the navy collar gives the whole shirt an anchor so the stripes have somewhere to stop. The retro crest is the right call on a shirt this old-fashioned, and the red and navy together are the two colours Atletico should always be reaching for on a change kit.

This is the shirt the away should have been. It is restrained, it is specific to the club, and it looks like something Atletico would have worn in 1998, which for a third kit is a compliment. Of the Nike third shirts that have launched so far this season, this is the best of them.

**Grade: A-**

## Atletico Launched Earlier Than Anyone

Worth noting for anyone tracking release cycles: Atletico put their away shirt out on May 18 and their home on June 29, making them the earliest club in our entire European group. Most clubs launched through July.

That is a commercial decision rather than a design one, but it does mean Atletico fans had both shirts a full two months before Arsenal or Chelsea supporters did.

## Who Makes Atletico Madrid's Kits?

Nike, who have supplied Atletico Madrid since 2001.

## The Bottom Line

Atletico Madrid's home shirt is one of the best in Europe at an A-, built on red and white stripes with a tonal Neptune trident pattern and royal blue framing the collar. The black away shirt with neon green accents is a B- and a strange departure for a club that normally keeps change kits quiet. The grey third with its thin red and navy hoops corrects that at an A-, the best third shirt Nike have released this season. Wardrobe grade: B+.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are Atletico Madrid's 2026/27 kits?**

The home shirt has red and white vertical stripes with a tonal trident pattern for Neptune's fountain and deep royal blue framing a V-neck collar. The away is black with neon green accents. The third is light grey with thin red and navy hoops and a navy polo collar. All three are made by Nike.

**What grade do Atletico Madrid's 2026/27 kits get?**

The home shirt gets an A-, the away a B- and the third an A-. The wardrobe grade is a B+.

**What is the pattern on Atletico Madrid's 2026/27 home shirt?**

A tonal trident motif referencing Neptune's fountain in Madrid. It is applied subtly enough that it reads as texture rather than as a graphic, which is why it works.

**When were Atletico Madrid's 2026/27 kits released?**

The away shirt launched on May 18, 2026 and the home shirt on June 29, 2026, making Atletico the earliest of Europe's fifteen biggest clubs to get both kits out. The grey third followed on August 24, 2026.

**Why is the Atletico Madrid away kit neon green?**

Nike paired the black base with neon green accents. It is not a colour with any history at the club, which is the main reason it grades lower than the home shirt.

**Who makes Atletico Madrid's kits?**

Nike, who have supplied Atletico Madrid since 2001.
