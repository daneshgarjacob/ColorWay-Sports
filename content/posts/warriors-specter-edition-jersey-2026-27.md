**The Warriors' Specter Edition jersey is black with royal blue and gold.** The team posted its official photos on Monday, November 23, with the caption "A jersey worn with obsession. Our Specter Edition uniform honors 80 seasons of teamwork, perseverance, and joy that define Warriors basketball." The base is **black**, with **royal blue light streaks** running down one side from the shoulder to the hem, **black numbers outlined in gold**, a small **gold WARRIORS wordmark** and **gold trim** around the neck and arms. The Warriors wear it **three times, all at home**: November 29 vs the Clippers, December 1 vs the Jazz and December 22 vs the Mavericks. They are one of four teams that debut a Specter jersey on November 29, the first night any team wears one.

## Quick Answers

- **What does the Warriors Specter jersey look like?** Black, with royal blue light streaks down the left side, black numbers outlined in gold, a small gold WARRIORS wordmark, a gold Nike logo and gold trim.
- **When do the Warriors wear it?** November 29 vs the Clippers, December 1 vs the Jazz and December 22 vs the Mavericks. All three are home games at Chase Center.
- **When is the first game?** Sunday, November 29, against the Clippers, 5:30 p.m. Pacific. It is the first night of the Specter Edition across the league.
- **When does it go on sale?** November 30, the date the Specter jerseys go on sale (per the Hawks' official page). The Warriors have not posted their own sale details yet.
- **Why do the Warriors have one?** Nike made the Specter Edition only for the NBA's eight oldest franchises. The Warriors date back to the 1946 Philadelphia Warriors. [Why only eight teams](/stories/why-only-8-nba-teams-got-specter-edition-jerseys).
- **Our grade:** B, the same grade we gave it in April. It is No. 8 of 8 in [our Specter ranking](/stories/nba-specter-edition-jerseys-ranked).

## What The Jersey Looks Like

Here is every piece, from the Warriors' own photos:

- **The base.** Black. Like all eight Specter jerseys, it is a black jersey first, with the team colors added on top.
- **The blue.** Warriors royal blue, in long blurry streaks that run straight down the left side of the jersey, from the shoulder strap to the hem, like light caught in a long-exposure photo. Up close they look like folds of fabric or bridge cables. The right side of the jersey has no blue at all.
- **The numbers.** Black, with a thick gold outline. From a distance the number reads as a gold outline on black, the same treatment as the Lakers' Specter numbers.
- **The wordmark.** WARRIORS in gold block letters with a black outline, small, sitting below the Nike logo instead of across the chest.
- **The Nike logo.** Gold, moved toward the middle of the chest like on every Specter jersey.
- **The trim.** Gold around the V-neck and the arm holes, and a thin gold stripe down each side seam.
- **The patch.** The gold IREN patch on the right shoulder, the Warriors' new sponsor patch this season.
- **The Nellie band.** A gold "NELLIE" tag on the left shoulder strap, honoring former Warriors coach Don Nelson. The Warriors wear it on their jerseys all season ([more on this season's patches and bands](/stories/every-new-nba-uniform-logo-court-patch-2026-27)).
- **The number.** Both team posts show No. 30, and the video has Stephen Curry wearing it. The photos only show the front, so we have not seen the Warriors' back or shorts yet. On the other Specter jerseys, the player's name sits below the number on the back.

The Warriors have five jerseys this season, and the Specter makes **three black ones**:

- **White (Association), 32 games.** The most-worn Warriors jersey this season.
- **Royal blue (Icon), 18 games.** The classic royal and gold.
- **Black with gold GOLDEN STATE (Statement), 16 games.** Gold lettering and numbers, with a thin royal blue stripe cutting across the front.
- **Black with gold SAN FRANCISCO (City Edition), 11 games.** The 2023-24 set, back this season, with gold script and gold side stripes. It debuts January 22 vs the Bucks.
- **Black with royal blue streaks (Specter), 3 games.** The only one of the three black jerseys where blue is the main color on the front, and the only one where the team name is this small.

So the Specter is not the Warriors' first black jersey. It is the third. What it does have that the other two do not is a lot of blue. On the Statement the blue is one thin stripe, and the City Edition has none. The trade-off is the lettering: the Statement and City Edition both put a big gold name across the chest, and the Specter shrinks WARRIORS to a small tag under the Nike logo.

## When It Goes On Sale

The Specter jerseys go on sale **November 30**. The Hawks' official Specter page gives that date for online sales, with in-arena sales starting December 3, and the Celtics say theirs are "dropping soon on-court, online and in-store." The Warriors have not posted their own sale details yet. Nike has not announced a price, so we are not guessing at one.

## Why The Warriors Got One

The Specter Edition is only for the league's eight oldest franchises, and the Warriors are one of them. The team started in 1946 as the **Philadelphia Warriors**, one of the original BAA teams, and won the league's first championship in 1947. They moved to San Francisco after the 1961-62 season and became the Golden State Warriors in 1971. The team's posts lean on that history: "80 seasons," and "Dub Nation's rise to one of basketball's winningest franchises." The other seven are the Kings, Pistons, Celtics, Knicks, Hawks, 76ers and Lakers. ## Our Grade: B

The official photos look better than the leak image did. The blue streaks are brighter than we expected and actually look like light moving, and the gold outline on the numbers is sharp. The gold Nellie tag on the shoulder is a nice touch that the leak did not show.

The problem is the same one we had in April. All of the blue is on one side. The left half of the jersey has a big royal blue graphic and the right half is plain black, so it still looks half finished. The Warriors have one of the best color sets in the league, royal and gold, and this is their third black jersey of the season. The gold WARRIORS is so small you will not be able to read it on TV.

We gave it a **B** when it leaked in April ([our first review](/stories/warriors-christmas-jersey-2026)), and the official photos keep it there. It is still No. 8, last, in [our ranking of all eight](/stories/nba-specter-edition-jerseys-ranked).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the Warriors Specter Edition jersey?**

A fifth Warriors uniform for 2026-27, part of Nike's Specter Edition line for the NBA's eight oldest franchises. It joins the Association, Icon, Statement and City Edition jerseys.

**What color is the Warriors Specter jersey?**

Black, with royal blue light streaks down the left side, black numbers outlined in gold, a small gold WARRIORS wordmark and gold trim.

**When do the Warriors wear the Specter Edition jersey?**

Three home games: November 29 vs the Clippers, December 1 vs the Jazz and December 22 vs the Mavericks.

**When is the Warriors Specter jersey debut?**

Sunday, November 29, 2026, at Chase Center against the Los Angeles Clippers, 5:30 p.m. Pacific. The Pistons, Knicks and Kings debut theirs the same night.

**When can I buy the Warriors Specter jersey?**

The Specter jerseys go on sale November 30, per the Hawks' official page. The Warriors have not posted their own sale details yet.

**How much is the Warriors Specter jersey?**

No price has been announced by Nike or the Warriors yet. We will add it when one is posted.

**Is the Warriors Specter jersey a Christmas jersey?**

No. It leaked in April labeled as a Christmas jersey, but the Warriors wear their white Association Edition against the Nuggets on Christmas Day. Their Specter games are November 29, December 1 and December 22.

**Is the Warriors Specter jersey the Bay Bridge jersey?**

The leak was read as a Bay Bridge design, because the blue streaks look like bridge cables. The Warriors' own posts do not mention a bridge. They say the jersey "honors 80 seasons" of Warriors basketball.

**What does NELLIE on the Warriors jersey mean?**

It honors Don Nelson, the former Warriors coach. The Warriors wear the "Nellie" band on their jerseys all season, and it shows up on the Specter jersey as a gold tag on the left shoulder.

**What is the IREN patch on the Warriors jersey?**

IREN is the Warriors' new jersey patch partner for 2026-27. On the Specter jersey the patch is gold with black letters, on the right shoulder.

**How many jerseys do the Warriors have in 2026-27?**

Five: Association (white), Icon (royal blue), Statement (black with gold GOLDEN STATE), City Edition (black with gold SAN FRANCISCO) and Specter Edition. The count for each is in our [Warriors uniform schedule](/stories/warriors-uniform-schedule-2026-27).

**Is the Warriors Specter jersey any good?**

We grade it a B, the same as in April. The blue streaks look better in the official photos than in the leak, but the graphic only covers one side, and it is the third black jersey for a royal and gold team. It is last in our ranking of the eight.
