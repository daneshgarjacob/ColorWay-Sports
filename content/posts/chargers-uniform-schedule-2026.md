## Super Chargers Navy (Weeks 9, 12, and 17, Confirmed)

The all-navy Super Chargers set gets three home dates: Houston in Week 9 on Sunday, November 8, New England in Week 12 on Sunday, November 29 in the Sunday night window, and Kansas City in Week 17 to close out the home schedule. The Chargers are not shy about it, calling these "the best alternate uniforms in the NFL" in their own announcement.

The set is a modern throwback to the navy jerseys the franchise wore from 1992 to 2006, the era of the 1994 AFC Championship team, and it is built to invoke Junior Seau, LaDainian Tomlinson, Antonio Gates, and Philip Rivers. What makes it work is restraint. The navy is deep enough to make the gold bolts glow rather than blend, and the helmet carries the lightning without the set tipping into costume. It is the rare modern throwback that looks like it belongs to the team wearing it.

Three wearings is also the maximum most teams get out of an alternate, and the Chargers spent all three on high-visibility dates: a division rival to close the year, a national Sunday night window, and a Houston team that will be in white. That is good scheduling.

## Charger Power Gold (Week 13 at Buccaneers, Confirmed)

This is the best-looking game on the 2026 calendar and it is not close. The Chargers wear the gold Charger Power jersey with white pants at Tampa Bay on Sunday, December 6, and the Buccaneers wear their white 1976 creamsicle throwback. Two teams, both dressed out of the late seventies, in the same game.

It also answers a question people ask about this jersey every year. A visiting team can normally only wear a colored jersey if the home team goes white, which is why road alternates are so rare. Tampa Bay choosing the white creamsicle set is exactly what unlocks the gold for Los Angeles. Without that, this game does not happen.

Charger Power is the all-gold jersey the team introduced in 2025 as a nod to the Air Coryell era, and it debuted that October against the Colts. Gold is a genuinely hard jersey color to pull off, and most attempts read as a costume or a practice top. This one survives because the powder blue numerals and bolts give the eye somewhere to land.

## Powder Blue at Home (The Base Set, Expected)

Powder blue is the franchise's best asset and it projects to the five home dates not claimed by an alternate: the Cardinals in Week 1, the Raiders in Week 2, the Broncos in Week 5, the Jets in Week 11, and the 49ers on Thursday night in Week 15. Our [Jets 2026 schedule](/stories/jets-uniform-schedule-2026) has New York in the white road set for that Week 11 trip, which is the clean contrast this jersey deserves.

The variable is below the waist. Los Angeles added powder blue pants in 2025 as an interchangeable option alongside white and gold, so the same jersey can produce a monochrome powder look, the classic white-pant version, or the gold-pant combination. The team rotates them deliberately, and that call usually lands the week of the game.

## How the Chargers Rotation Works in 2026

Four alternates in one season is a lot, and the Chargers can do it because their closet is deep and coordinated rather than scattered. Powder blue is the identity, white is the road default, navy is the heritage set, and gold is the special occasion. Every piece of that comes from the same seventies-to-nineties well, so nothing on the schedule looks like it was borrowed from another team.

The pattern across the year is worth noticing too. The first eight weeks are entirely base uniforms, then the alternates land in a cluster from Week 9 through Week 17. That keeps the powder blue from feeling like a rerun and puts the best-looking sets in the part of the season people actually watch.
