This is the full Arizona 2026 uniform schedule: the navy home set, the white road set, the all-red Red Out look, the Sandstorm alternate that gets its own themed game against TCU, and a week-by-week outlook for all twelve games, including the 100th Territorial Cup. Arizona is a Nike program with a short list of base pieces and a long list of ways to combine them. The navy-over-white look is the one the program calls Desert Swarm, after the 1990s defenses, and it is the default at Casino Del Sol Stadium. The newest set is Sandstorm, which debuted in a 41-13 win over Oklahoma State on October 4, 2025, and went on the road to Houston later that season. Arizona also pulled out a retro look with paw print helmet stickers against Kansas State in 2025. No new 2026 uniform has been announced. What Arizona did announce is a fan color for every home game, and those calls are worth watching: a Red Out against Arizona State has usually meant the team wears all red, too. Big 12 home teams wear color and visitors wear white, which makes the five road dates easy to call.

**September 5 vs. Northern Arizona.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **white helmet, navy jersey, white pants**, the Desert Swarm look for the Native American Heritage opener in Tucson. Fans were asked to wear blue for the first Blueout since 2016, which had people wondering whether the navy helmet would come back for the first time since 2020. It did not. Northern Arizona wore white helmets, white jerseys and white pants. Arizona won 35-7. One calendar note: ESPN files this game under September 6 because its feed runs on UTC, and a 6:30 p.m. kickoff in Tucson lands after midnight in London. It was played Saturday, September 5.

**September 12 at BYU.** ★ Confirmed from the FOX broadcast and Arizona's game-week reveal: **all white, white helmet included**, with navy numerals outlined in red and the navy and red center stripe on the helmet. BYU wore white helmets over royal jerseys and royal pants, so Provo got a clean white-against-royal game, which we covered on the [BYU 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/byu-uniform-schedule-2026). Arizona led 17-14 at halftime and did not score again. BYU won 28-17 in the Big 12 opener.

**September 19 vs. Northern Illinois.** Navy expected. It is the Hispanic Heritage game, the first meeting ever between the two schools, and Arizona has asked fans to wear red, so an all-red look would not surprise us. Kickoff is 7:30 p.m. in Tucson on TNT, and ESPN lists it under September 20 for the same UTC reason as the opener.

**September 26 at Washington State.** White expected. A non-conference road trip to Martin Stadium in Pullman, on CBS.

**October 3 vs. Cincinnati.** Navy expected. The Big 12 home opener is also Family Weekend and Pacific Islander Heritage, and it is a white-clad crowd by request. Arizona won the first-ever meeting 30-24 in Cincinnati last season.

**October 10 at West Virginia.** White expected. This is West Virginia's Coal Rush game, and the Mountaineers are expected in all black, so Arizona in white should make this one of the starkest contrasts on the conference calendar. More on the home side on the [West Virginia 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/west-virginia-uniform-schedule-2026).

**October 17.** Bye.

**October 24 vs. Iowa State.** Navy expected. Homecoming, and a big one: Arizona is marking 100 years of Bear Down, the words quarterback John "Button" Salmon left for the team before his death in 1926. Fans are asked to wear red. If Arizona has a one-off uniform in its pocket this season, this is the date we would bet on.

**October 31 at Texas Tech.** White expected. Halloween in Lubbock, where the Red Raiders are expected in red or black.

**November 6 vs. TCU (Friday).** Sandstorm expected. Arizona scheduled its first official Sandstorm-themed game for this Friday night, with fans asked to wear white, and the theme is built around the uniform that debuted against Oklahoma State. We will star the cell once the team confirms. ESPN lists this game on November 7 because the 8:15 p.m. Tucson kickoff is past midnight UTC.

**November 14 vs. Utah.** Navy expected. Military Appreciation night, with fans in red. Arizona has occasionally worn a military-themed uniform for this game, though last season it stayed in its standard look. Utah should be in white; see the [Utah 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/utah-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 21 at Kansas State.** White expected. Final road game, against a purple home side in Manhattan. The Kansas State view is on the [Kansas State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/kansas-state-uniform-schedule-2026).

**November 28 vs. Arizona State.** Red or navy, with red the lean. It is Senior Day and the 100th Territorial Cup, and Arizona has called a Red Out. When Arizona calls a Red Out for this game, the team has usually worn all red, so we are not treating navy as a safe call here. Arizona State is expected in white; see the [Arizona State 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/arizona-state-uniform-schedule-2026). Arizona announces its combination in game week.

## The Bottom Line

Two games in, Arizona has worn exactly what the rules predict: the navy jersey under the white helmet at home, and head-to-toe white at BYU. The interesting part of this season is the promo calendar. Arizona has told fans what color to wear for every home game, and three of those calls point somewhere other than navy: red for Bear Down 100 at homecoming, white for Sandstorm night against TCU, and red for the 100th Territorial Cup. The road schedule should stay white all the way through. The grid above has every date, and we fill in the stars as each uniform is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is Arizona wearing this week?**

Arizona wore all white, white helmet included, at BYU on September 12. Next up is Northern Illinois in Tucson on September 19, the Hispanic Heritage game, where navy is the expected home jersey and fans have been asked to wear red. The week-by-week grid above lists every game with the expected set, and we update it as combinations are confirmed.

**What are Arizona's football uniforms for 2026?**

A navy home jersey with white numerals outlined in red, a white road jersey with navy numerals, an all-red set for Red Out games, and the Sandstorm alternate with red numerals and a red cactus helmet. The everyday helmet is white with the block A and a navy center stripe edged in red. Pants are white by default, with red pants for the all-red look.

**Does Arizona have a new alternate uniform in 2026?**

No new uniform has been announced. The newest set is Sandstorm, which debuted against Oklahoma State in 2025, and Arizona has scheduled a Sandstorm-themed home game against TCU on Friday, November 6. We will add anything new to this page as soon as it is revealed.

**What did Arizona wear against BYU?**

All white: white helmet, white jersey and white pants. BYU wore royal jerseys and royal pants under white helmets. BYU won 28-17 in Provo on September 12, holding Arizona scoreless in the second half.

**Why did Arizona wear navy against Northern Arizona if it was a Blueout?**

The Blueout was a call for fans. Arizona stayed in its standard home look, white helmet, navy jersey and white pants, rather than going all blue. The team has not worn the navy helmet since 2020 or an all-blue uniform since 2019.

**When is Arizona's Sandstorm game?**

Friday, November 6, against TCU at Casino Del Sol Stadium. It is the first official Sandstorm-themed game, and fans are asked to wear white.

**How many games does Arizona play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven at Casino Del Sol Stadium in Tucson and five on the road, with a bye on October 17. The season ends with the 100th Territorial Cup against Arizona State on November 28.
