## The Three Types of MLB Uniform Teams

Before the team-by-team tables, it helps to know that every club falls into one of three buckets. Once you know which bucket a team is in, you know how much the calendar can tell you.

- **Scheduled.** A specific jersey for specific days, every week. If it is Thursday, you already know the look. Only the Phillies, Marlins, Cardinals, and Yankees truly live here.
- **Partial.** One or two anchor days are locked, almost always a City Connect on Friday, and the rest is up to the team that night. This is where most of baseball lives in 2026.
- **No system.** The jerseys get worn with no real pattern, so the day of the week tells you very little. A handful of teams still operate this way.

## The Teams Actually Worth Scheduling Around

If you are trying to plan a trip around seeing a specific jersey in person, four teams make it easy. The [Phillies](/stories/phillies-uniform-schedule-2026) run the cleanest system in the sport: pinstripes on home nights, creams on day games, powder blues every Thursday, City Connect every Friday, grays on the road. The [Marlins](/stories/marlins-uniform-schedule-2026) give every weekend home game its own look, capped by the 1993 teal throwbacks on Sundays. The [Cardinals](/stories/cardinals-uniform-schedule-2026) run baseball's most rigid rotation around the birds-on-the-bat classics. And the [Yankees](/stories/yankees-uniform-schedule-2026) keep it simple by barely having a closet at all, with pinstripes at home, grays on the road, and a brand-new alternate they have not even worn yet.

Everyone else gives you one guarantee at most. If your team runs a City Connect, Friday night at home is almost always the safest bet to catch it. Beyond that, the choice usually comes down to the club, the pitcher's preference, and whatever promotion is running that night.

## City Connect Is Now the One Reliable Anchor

The single biggest reason so many teams have drifted from "no system" to "partial" is the City Connect program. Nearly every club now owns one, and the easiest way to guarantee attention for it is to lock it to a weeknight. Friday is the runaway favorite. If you want a deeper look at the alternates themselves, we ranked the full slate in our [2026 MLB City Connect ranking](/stories/mlb-city-connect-jerseys-2026-ranked). Boston's Fenway Green and Kansas City's Forever Fountains are early standouts, while a few teams, most notably the [Cubs](/stories/cubs-uniform-schedule-2026), still do not have one at all.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is my MLB team wearing today?**
Start with three questions: is the team home or away, is it a day game or a night game, and what day of the week is it. Almost every road game is the gray road uniform. At home, the day of the week is your best clue, because most teams lock a City Connect or alternate to a specific weeknight, usually Friday. Only the Phillies, Marlins, Cardinals, and Yankees run a full weekly schedule you can predict every day. For the rest, pick your team from the tables above and open its full 2026 uniform schedule for the exact day-by-day logic.

**Which MLB teams have a set uniform schedule?**
Four: the Philadelphia Phillies, Miami Marlins, St. Louis Cardinals, and New York Yankees. For these clubs, the day of the week reliably tells you the jersey. Every other team locks one or two anchor days, most often a City Connect Friday, and leaves the remaining games to the club's discretion.

**How many uniforms does each MLB team have in 2026?**
Most teams carry five distinct looks in 2026. The deepest closets belong to the Orioles, Blue Jays, Padres, and Giants at six, while the leanest are the Yankees and Angels at three and a small group of four-jersey clubs like the Dodgers, Cubs, Rockies, Pirates, and Athletics. The counts include home, road, alternates, City Connects, and any throwback in the regular rotation.

**What day do MLB teams wear City Connect jerseys?**
There is no league-wide rule, but Friday night home games are the most common City Connect slot in 2026. Teams like the Guardians, Tigers, Royals, Rangers, and Braves all lock their City Connect to Fridays. A few break the pattern, such as the Astros wearing their "Stros" City Connect on Monday home games.

**Do MLB teams still wear gray road uniforms?**
Almost all of them do, and the gray road set remains the single most-worn uniform for most teams. There are exceptions worth knowing: the Tampa Bay Rays currently have no gray road jersey, and the Los Angeles Angels retired their road grays and now send the red jersey on the road instead.

## The Bottom Line on the 2026 MLB Uniform Schedule

Baseball has never had more jerseys in circulation, and it has never been harder to guess which one is coming out on a given night. The honest takeaway is that only four teams, the Phillies, Marlins, Cardinals, and Yankees, treat their uniform rotation like an actual schedule you can plan around. For everyone else, the reliable move is simple: assume grays on the road, and if the team owns a City Connect, circle Friday night at home.
