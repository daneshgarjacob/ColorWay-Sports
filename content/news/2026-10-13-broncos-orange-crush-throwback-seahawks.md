Denver showed its Week 6 look on Tuesday: royal blue helmets with the orange "D" and the white bronco, an orange center stripe and white facemasks, plus orange throwback jerseys with blue and white sleeve stripes. The team says the set also has white numbers with blue trim, pants with an orange and blue side stripe, and orange and blue striped socks.

Seattle comes in wearing its royal blue throwback with silver helmets and silver pants, so both teams go retro in a color vs color game. Kickoff is Thursday, October 15, at 8:15 p.m. Eastern on Prime Video. It is the first of two throwback games for Denver this season. The second is Week 18 against the Chargers.
