The Yankees' High-A affiliate will play some 2027 games as the **Hudson Valley Mountain Gnomes**, a new alternate identity the Renegades announced Tuesday.

The name comes from a local legend about **Henry Hudson's 1609 trip up the river**: his crew finds a band of bearded gnomes in the **Catskills**, drinks their magic liquor, bowls nine-pins with them and briefly turns into gnomes. The team says the legend is the basis for a classic Washington Irving short story.

The team says every Mountain Gnomes jersey hides its gnome in a **different spot**, and the first of four games is **Friday, May 21, vs. Wilmington**. [Read the full story: the logo, the hidden HV and the four-game plan](/stories/hudson-valley-mountain-gnomes-renegades-alternate-identity-2027).
