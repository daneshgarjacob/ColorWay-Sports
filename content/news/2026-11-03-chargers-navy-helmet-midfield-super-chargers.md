For Sunday's game against the Texans, the Chargers wear their **all-navy Super Chargers** uniform at SoFi Stadium, and the field changes to match. The **navy helmet replaces last year's white bolt at midfield**.

The full look is the navy helmet, navy jersey and navy pants, the set fans have pushed the team to wear more often.
