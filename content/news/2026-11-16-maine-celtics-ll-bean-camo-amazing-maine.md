The Boston Celtics' G League team unveiled its fourth **L.L.Bean "Amazing Maine" uniform** on Monday: L.L.Bean's **classic camouflage pattern**, which the team says first appeared on the **Bean's Camouflage Parka in 1959**, redone in a **Celtics green colorway**. The jersey patch recognizes the **WinterKids Education Foundation**.

The series started with **Maine Hunting Plaid** in 2023-24, and last season's uniform was based on the **L.L.Bean Norwegian Sweater**. Maine wears the camo set for **five games**, starting **Sunday, November 22, against Capital City**, with a different Maine nonprofit recognized each night.
