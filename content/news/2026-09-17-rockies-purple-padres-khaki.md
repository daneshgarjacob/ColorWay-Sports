Colorado went to the purple alternate at Coors Field on Thursday afternoon, against a San Diego club in the khaki road uniform. It is one of the better color pairings left on the September calendar, and purple at home is a rarity this late in a season going nowhere.

Earlier in the day the Brewers wore the powder blue alternate at PNC Park, where Pittsburgh answered in the black Pittsburgh alternate.
