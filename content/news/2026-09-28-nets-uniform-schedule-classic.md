Brooklyn never announced it, but every game on the Nets' official schedule page carries a jersey. We counted all 80: 22 Association, 17 Statement, 16 Classic, 14 Icon and 11 City.

The Classic Edition gets Opening Night against Charlotte on October 21 and every Eras Night on the promotional calendar. The white is mostly a road jersey now, 14 of its 22 games away from Barclays. The Biggie City Edition waits until January 23 against Toronto. Thanks to the reader who tipped us off.
