Tampa Bay revealed a new gray road uniform with **TAMPA BAY** arched across the chest in a gradient running light blue into green into yellow, the ray mark beneath it, and navy piping, navy swoosh and a navy helmet with the TB ray. The club framed the set around its commitment to the community.

It is the first traditional road gray the Rays have carried since 2023. They abandoned it and have travelled in the navy and Columbia blue alternates ever since.

It also answers the criticism we have made twice about this wardrobe: the Columbia blue alternate has worn a small sunburst on the right chest instead of a wordmark since 2022, and we called it a gorgeous colour wearing almost nothing. This one puts a full arched wordmark back where it belongs.

It debuts **Tuesday, September 22**, in the second game of the doubleheader against the Yankees, with a week left in the 2026 season.
