Ole Miss's **"Military Appreciation Threads"** for Wofford on Saturday: a **gray and white camo helmet with the Ole Miss script filled in stars and stripes**, REBS on the front bumper and HOTTY TODDY on the back, a **navy jersey with white numbers** and **gray pants** with a red stripe. The field at Vaught-Hemingway gets a flag Ole Miss logo at midfield and red, white and blue end zones. Kickoff is noon Eastern on SEC Network+. Our grade: **B**.
