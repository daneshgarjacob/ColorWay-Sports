The Pacers posted "Debuting our ABA Classic Court today" on Sunday morning with a photo from the rafters at Gainbridge Fieldhouse. The floor has **yellow paint with plaid lanes** for coach Bobby "Slick" Leonard, the Pacers P at center court with the ABA championship trophy silhouette, and **1970, 1972 and 1973** for the three ABA titles.

It is a throwback night on both sides. The Nets visit at 5 p.m. Eastern, and both teams are scheduled to wear their **ABA Classic Edition** uniforms: the Pacers in their blue Classic Threads and the Nets in their ABA set. The same two teams met in throwbacks in Brooklyn on October 25, the first Classic Edition against Classic Edition game of the season, and Indiana won 136-109. We ranked the Pacers' jersey and court No. 1 of the four ABA teams in our [Classic Edition rankings](/stories/nba-classic-edition-jerseys-courts-2026-27).
