Navy and Under Armour revealed the Midshipmen's uniform for the 127th Army-Navy Game: a **Naval Special Warfare** tribute to the SEALs, SWCC boat crews and the WWII Frogmen, with a **white jersey**, BUD/S-style numbers, **tonal blue camo** and a hand-painted helmet with the **SEAL Trident** and a **Bone Frog** on top.

We graded it an **A-**, a step ahead of Army's Ivy Soldier.
