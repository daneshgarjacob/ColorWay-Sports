For Senior Day against Tennessee Tech, Mississippi State wore a **white helmet with the maroon interlocking MSU**, a **maroon jersey** and **white pants**, and won **34-16**. Until Saturday the white helmet had only gone with white jerseys this season. Next is the Egg Bowl at Ole Miss on Friday.
