Oregon posted its Game 8 combo on Wednesday night with the line "Bold by design." It is a **matte black helmet with white wings and a black facemask**, a **black jersey with silver numbers outlined in green** and diamond-plate texture across the shoulders, and **white pants**.

The white wings are the story. Oregon's helmets usually carry chrome, green or yellow wings, and a flat white wing on a black shell is about as stark as the Ducks get. The green only shows up in the outline around the numbers.

No. 20 Northwestern visits Autzen Stadium on Saturday, October 31, at 3:30 p.m. Eastern on Peacock. Oregon is 4-3 after losing 21-10 at Illinois. Every other Halloween weekend look is in our [college football Week 9 uniforms guide](/stories/college-football-week-9-uniforms-2026).
