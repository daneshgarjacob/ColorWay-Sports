Georgia Tech went all white at home, so Wake Forest wore **all black**: a **black helmet with the gold WF**, **black jersey** and **black pants**. The Deacons won **50-29** for their sixth power conference road win of the season, which the team says is a College Football Playoff era first. It is their third all-black game of 2026.
