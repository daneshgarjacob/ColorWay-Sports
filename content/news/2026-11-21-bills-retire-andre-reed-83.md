**"83 is heading to the rafters at Highmark Stadium."** The Bills announced Thursday that they will retire Hall of Fame receiver **Andre Reed's No. 83** at a home game during the **2027 season**, the fourth retired number in team history after Jim Kelly's 12, Thurman Thomas' 34 and Bruce Smith's 78, and the first at the new stadium. Reed holds a press conference at Highmark Stadium on Sunday before the Bills play Miami.
