Iowa State's **"bounce house threads"** for Friday night at UCF: the **cardinal helmet with "Cyclones" in white script** outlined in gold, a white and gold center stripe and a **white facemask**, with the **white jersey** and IOWA STATE across the chest in cardinal. The team's video does not show the pants.

UCF counters with its own script helmet, **Knights Script Mode**: a black helmet with the gold Knights script, black jersey and black pants. Kickoff is 6 p.m. Eastern on FS1. [UCF's look, explained](/stories/ucf-knights-script-mode-uniform-iowa-state-2026).
