The National Lacrosse League's **Las Vegas Desert Dogs** showed off a **new home, away and alternate uniform set** at a fan event Tuesday night at The George Sportsmen's Lounge at Durango Resort, co-hosted with Lexicon Bank. The team calls it a **"whole new look"** for its **fifth season** in Las Vegas. Capelli Sport makes the uniforms for every NLL team.

The **alternate jersey** is on presale in the team store and ships **January 30, 2027**, so expect it on the floor later in the season. The home opener is **Saturday, December 5**.

The team's own announcement does not describe the colors or design, so we are holding the details until the Desert Dogs publish them.
