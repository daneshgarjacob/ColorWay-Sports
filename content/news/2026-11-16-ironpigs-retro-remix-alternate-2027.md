The Phillies' Triple-A club has a new alternate in regular rotation starting in 2027: the **"Retro Remix,"** a **red jersey with navy piping** down the front and on the sleeves.

**"Lehigh Valley"** arcs across the chest in the same script as the team's Friday Fauxback uniforms, finished with a **curly pig tail**. The Fauxback swinging pig's head sits on a **right-sleeve patch**, and the **red cap with a navy brim** shows the full-bodied pig leaning on a bat, legs crossed.

The team says the whole set borrows from the Fauxbacks it introduced in 2025.
