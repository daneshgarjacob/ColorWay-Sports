UConn revealed the red jersey in August, the program's first alternate since 2016. The reveal pairs it with the white helmet and white pants. It uses the same template as the white road jersey.

No date has been announced. Homecoming against Syracuse on October 3 and the Friday night game against UMass on October 23 are the likely spots.
