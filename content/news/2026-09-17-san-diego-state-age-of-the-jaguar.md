San Diego State unveiled the Age of the Jaguar uniform on Tuesday and wears it Saturday against James Madison, 7 p.m. Pacific at Snapdragon Stadium.

The helmet is black, with the Ocelotl jaguar glyph on one side and the player's number on the other. The jersey is red with black numerals and red-on-red sublimated Aztec artwork on the sleeves, over red pants. The school says it is the first time the program has worn a red jersey and red pants together.
