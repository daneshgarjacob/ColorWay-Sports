Los Angeles posted a seven-photo **"Fearsome White Uniform Look Book"** for Wednesday's game against Green Bay: the **royal blue helmet with white horns and a white facemask, the white jersey with royal blue numbers and a royal horn stripe over the shoulder, and white pants**. It is the uniform's only date of 2026, the first NFL game ever played on Thanksgiving Eve, 8:00 p.m. Eastern on Netflix. The Packers confirmed green over gold pants on Monday.
