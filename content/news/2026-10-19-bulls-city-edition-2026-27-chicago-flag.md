The Bulls released their 2026-27 City Edition on NBA Jersey Day, and it is the 2017-18 Chicago flag set coming back: a white shirt with a red "Chicago" script, the city flag's four red six-pointed stars down the side and light blue at the collar.

Chicago wears it six times this season, starting Thursday, January 14 at the United Center against the Timberwolves. We moved the Bulls up to No. 10 in our City Edition ranking.
