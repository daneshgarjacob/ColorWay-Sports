Air Force wears its 2026 Air Power Legacy Series uniform against Navy on October 3, and it honors the B-21 Raider stealth bomber. Matte black head to toe, a dark camouflage helmet wrap with B-21 above the facemask, and chrome silver numerals.

The back plates read STRATEGIC DETERRENCE and POWER PROJECTION. The patches are Air Force Global Strike Command, the 412th Test Wing that flight tests the B-21 at Edwards, Air Force Materiel Command and the Colorado Air National Guard's 140th Wing. We graded it A-.
