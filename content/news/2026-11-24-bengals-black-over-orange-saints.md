Cincinnati's **"Week 12 Uni Combo,"** posted with the caption "Stripes set," for Sunday against New Orleans: the **striped orange helmet, black jersey, orange pants and black socks**. The Bengals wore black over white pants against Tennessee in Week 8 and black over black pants against Jacksonville in Week 4. Kickoff is 1:00 p.m. Eastern on CBS.
