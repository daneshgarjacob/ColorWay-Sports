With **Joe Burrow** sick on Thursday, **Ja'Marr Chase** took the podium for the Bengals' Week 11 press conference wearing Burrow's **orange No. 9 home jersey**. The team posted the full press conference on [Bengals.com](https://www.bengals.com/video/ja-marr-chase-press-conference-week-11-2026-commanders). Reporters in the room quoted Chase's opening line as "I'm the quarterback today."

On the field, Cincinnati has already confirmed its look for Monday night at Washington: the **striped orange helmet, white jersey and white pants**, against the Commanders' all-black Hail Raiser. Every Week 11 uniform is in our [NFL Week 11 uniform guide](/stories/nfl-week-11-uniforms-2026).
