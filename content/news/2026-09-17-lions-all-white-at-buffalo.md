Detroit goes white jersey and white pants under the silver helmet on Thursday night at Buffalo, the road look its own fans nicknamed the Marshmallows. The team listed it plainly: white jerseys, white pants, white socks.

The Bills counter in the blue primary with a 2026 inaugural season patch, for the first regular season game at the new Highmark Stadium. Buffalo also has red end zones, which Bills Mafia picked in a team poll.
