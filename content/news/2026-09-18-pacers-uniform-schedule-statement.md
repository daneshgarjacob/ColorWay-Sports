Indiana posted its jersey-by-jersey schedule for the season, one graphic per uniform. We counted it: the gold pinstriped Statement is booked for 31 games (17 home, 14 road), the white Association for 13 (6 home, 7 road) and the navy Icon for 10 (5 and 5).

That covers 54 of the 80 dated games. The other 26 were left off, with no City Edition list yet. Opening night at New Orleans is an Association game; the home opener against Dallas is gold.
