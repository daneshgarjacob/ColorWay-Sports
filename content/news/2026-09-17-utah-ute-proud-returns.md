White helmet with the tribal interlocking U's and a tribal center stripe, red jersey, red pants. Utah wears it once a year, in partnership with the Ute Tribe, and the helmet is the reason to watch.

It was one of twelve Week 3 combinations teams announced on Thursday, along with Miami in all white, Wake Forest in Dark Mode black and TCU in a new white helmet with a Frogs script.
