North Texas announced an all-white set for Saturday at Texas State: white helmet with the green eagle, white jersey with green numbers, and white pants with the green Texas logo. It is the Mean Green's first all-white look under Neal Brown.

North Texas wore green at home in last week's 44-6 win over UNLV and white with green pants at Indiana in the opener.
