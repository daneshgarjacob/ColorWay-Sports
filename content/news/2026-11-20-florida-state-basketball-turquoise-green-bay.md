Florida State men's basketball wore **turquoise** against Green Bay on Friday for its Seminole Heritage game: a **turquoise jersey with SEMINOLES and the numbers in garnet trimmed in gold**, **garnet and cream side panels**, **turquoise shorts** and **Nike N7 shoes** in turquoise with a black geometric pattern. Florida State won **87-63**. The football team wears turquoise for the first time on Saturday against NC State. Our grade: **A-**.
