Pittsburgh announced its **Color Rush** for Friday's game against Denver: the **black helmet, black jersey with gold numbers and gold sleeve stripes, and black pants**. It is the first Color Rush game of the Steelers' 2026 season and the first Black Friday game in team history, 3:00 p.m. Eastern on Prime Video. The team says it is 9-3 in the Color Rush. It is not the gold 1933 throwback, which still has no date. Denver is scheduled in Summit White.
