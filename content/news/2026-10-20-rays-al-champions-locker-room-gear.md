The Rays clinched the pennant with a 7-3 win over the Guardians in Game 6 on Monday night, and the American League champions locker room gear is out: the Fanatics 2026 AL Champions locker room T-shirt in heather charcoal and the New Era 2026 World Series locker room cap, the same template the Brewers got for winning the National League.

Both use the Rays' standard TB logo and type, not the Devil Rays throwback look Tampa Bay wore in the two biggest home games of the series, Game 2 and the Game 6 clincher. The Rays are 2-0 in the throwback this postseason. Every jersey they have worn this October is in our [World Series uniforms guide](/stories/world-series-uniforms-2026).
