Miami revealed throwbacks of the uniforms its 2001 national championship team wore, in both versions. The orange home set has a green and white shoulder yoke, white numbers outlined in green and white pants. The white road set has a green yoke with orange trim, green numbers and **green pants**. Both sit under a white helmet with the green and orange centre stripe.

Releasing both halves is the unusual part. A throwback is normally one jersey for one game.

The green pants are what separate the road set from what Miami wears now, which is all white: white helmet, white jersey, white pants, at Stanford and at Wake Forest this season.

No date announced for either. Florida State's October 17 visit is the most likely home slot for a special set.
