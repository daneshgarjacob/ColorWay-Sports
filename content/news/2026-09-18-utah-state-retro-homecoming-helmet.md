Utah State is wearing a throwback helmet with its 1992 to 1995 logo, the bull leaping through a block U, for Homecoming against Troy on Saturday, September 26. The jersey and pants have not been announced.

It will be the Aggies' fourth different helmet in four weeks, after the cow print in the opener, white at Washington, and navy at Utah this Saturday.
