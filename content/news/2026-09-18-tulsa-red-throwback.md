Tulsa posted the set on Wednesday under "Nothing like a classic look": a red jersey with white block numbers, a blue sleeve band with gold stripes, and gold pants. The photos were shot at a vintage gas station with a classic Chevy, the year Route 66 turns 100.

Tulsa has not named the game. It has also posted a "making of the helmet" teaser, so a matching helmet may be coming. Saturday against East Texas A&M is the best bet.
