Washington posted its Week 2 combination on Thursday: burgundy helmet, burgundy jersey, white pants and burgundy socks at AT&T Stadium on Sunday at 4:25 on FOX.

Dallas confirmed its side a day earlier, the Arctic White Color Rush head to toe, white socks included. So both sides of this one are locked in with no overlap, which is not something you can say about most games this week.
