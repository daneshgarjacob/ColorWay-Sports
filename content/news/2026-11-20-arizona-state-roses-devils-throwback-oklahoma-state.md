Arizona State's **Roses &amp; Devils** throwback gets its first game on Saturday against Oklahoma State, homecoming in Tempe. It is the 1986 look the Sun Devils wore to beat Michigan in the 1987 Rose Bowl: a **gold helmet with the full-body Sparky decal** and a maroon and white center stripe, a **maroon jersey with ARIZONA STATE in white block letters and gold numbers**, and **gold pants**. Kickoff is 10:15 p.m. Eastern on ESPN. Our grade: **A**.
