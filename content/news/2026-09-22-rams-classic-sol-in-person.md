The Rams wore Classic Sol for the first time Monday night against the Giants: the gold jersey honoring the 1951 champions, the multi-striped white pants and the standard royal helmet. It was the first gold jersey the Rams have worn since the all-gold Color Rush set in 2015. Rams 28, Giants 6.

It comes back once more this season, Thursday, December 3, at home against the Chiefs.
