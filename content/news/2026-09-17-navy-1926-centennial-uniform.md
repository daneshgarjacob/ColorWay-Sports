Navy will wear a commemorative Under Armour uniform against Notre Dame on October 31 at Gillette Stadium, honoring the 100th anniversary of the 1926 national championship team, the only national title in program history.

The striping is the whole idea. The jersey is a modern take on the 1926 Chevron Stripe design and carries nine stripes, four on the front and five on the back, one for each win that 9-0-1 season. The numerals use the Class of 1926 pin typography, the collar reads "Navy Blue and Gold" after the alma mater first performed in April 1926, and an anniversary patch sits on the chest. The gold helmet and gold pants are unchanged.
