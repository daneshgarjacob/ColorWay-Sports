Army and Nike unveiled the **Ivy Soldier** uniform for the 127th Army-Navy Game on **December 12**: an **ivy-green camo** build for the **4th Infantry Division** with a **IV helmet**, Steadfast and Loyal on the shell, an ivy-wrapped Athena shield and a 9/11 decal.

We graded it a **B**.
