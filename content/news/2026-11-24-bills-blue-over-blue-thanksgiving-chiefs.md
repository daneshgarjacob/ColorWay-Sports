Buffalo's **"Thanksgiving Threads"** graphic, labeled "Blue Blue White": the **white helmet, royal blue jersey, royal blue pants and white socks**. The royal blue jersey was on the home schedule the Bills published before the season; the pants are blue, not white. Kickoff is 8:20 p.m. Eastern on NBC, the first Thanksgiving game at the new Highmark Stadium.
