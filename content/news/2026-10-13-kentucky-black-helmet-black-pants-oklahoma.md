Kentucky posted a "new look for week seven" on Tuesday: a black helmet with the blue interlocking UK logo, the white jersey with KENTUCKY and the numbers in blue, and black pants with a blue and white side stripe and a blue UK on the hip.

It is the third different helmet in three weeks for the Wildcats, after the chrome shell at South Carolina and the Power K throwback against LSU. Kentucky (5-1) plays at Oklahoma on Saturday, October 17, at 7:30 p.m. Eastern.
