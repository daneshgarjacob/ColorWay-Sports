Pittsburgh's 60th season has its own mark. Five stars for the five Stanley Cups, and gold, white and black stripes for the Three Rivers, with the skating penguin inside the 6.

The team says it goes on the helmets for every home and road game this season, and on the shoulders as a patch for the home opener against Montreal on October 3. Replica patches go on sale that night through the Penguins Foundation, with proceeds going to the Foundation. The Penguins won the opener 6-5 in overtime.
