**Andrew Low Ah Kee**, CEO of Fanatics Commerce, says the Utah Mammoth and Colorado Avalanche jerseys made this the **biggest Winter Classic launch in history**, a record day at **more than double the previous best**. In his post on Friday, he added that **8 of the top 10 products across every league on Fanatics** were Winter Classic items. The jerseys went on sale November 18; the game is New Year's Eve at Rice-Eccles Stadium in Salt Lake City.
