Tulane posted "WUMBO is BACK" on Wednesday with four photos of the helmet against a dark New Orleans skyline. It is a **white helmet with an oversized green Angry Wave**, the wave's eyes and curling crest spread across the whole shell, and a **green facemask**.

Wumbo is what Tulane and its fans call the big version of the Angry Wave logo. The program used #Wumbo when the helmet first came out in 2018, and it has come back a handful of times since, including in 2024. Tulane has not shown the jersey or pants yet.

The Green Wave play at Charlotte on Friday night, October 30, at 7 p.m. Eastern on ESPN2, the night before Halloween. Every other Halloween weekend look is in our [college football Week 9 uniforms guide](/stories/college-football-week-9-uniforms-2026).
