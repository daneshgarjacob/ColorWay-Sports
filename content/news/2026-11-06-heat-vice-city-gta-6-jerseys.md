The Heat revealed their **Vice City** uniforms for **November 18 against the Bucks**: **black with neon pink trim**, a backwards Nike logo, Cactus Jack on the shorts and six pink stars.

The players wear a **HEAT** jersey on the court. The **Vice City script** version is the one fans can buy that night.
