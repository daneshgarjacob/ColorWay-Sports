Baltimore debuts its **Darkness Falls** uniform on **Monday, November 16**, against the Chargers at M&T Bank Stadium: the new **matte black Darkness helmet with the red-eyed raven**, a black jersey and black pants.

The Chargers wear **white over powder blue**.
