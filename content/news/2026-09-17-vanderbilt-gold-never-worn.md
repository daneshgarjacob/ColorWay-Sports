Vanderbilt revealed a gold uniform in August: gold helmet with the black and white center stripe, gold jersey, gold pants. For the Gold Out against Delaware on September 12, outlets reported the Commodores "went all gold."

They did not. The broadcast frames show the gold jersey worn with the **white helmet and white pants**, not the gold ones. The complete gold set from the reveal still has not been worn in a game.

It is a small thing, but it is the kind of small thing that ends up in every season recap as fact once one outlet writes it down. We check the frames.
