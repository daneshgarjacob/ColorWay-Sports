LSU will retire **Bert Jones' No. 7 jersey** on Saturday night in Death Valley, when Texas visits for the first time since 1953.

Jones was the **first LSU quarterback named first-team All-America** and the **No. 2 overall pick** in the 1973 NFL Draft.
