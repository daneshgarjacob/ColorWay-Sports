Ottawa wore its **black Hometown Remix sweater with the brushed gold crest** against the Kings on Saturday, a date the team never announced, and won **4-2**. It is the second Remix night after the November 12 debut, and the Senators are **2-0** in it. Seven of the nine home Remix games are left.
