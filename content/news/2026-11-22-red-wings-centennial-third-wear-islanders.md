Detroit wore the **solid red Centennial sweater** with the **cream 1930s Winged Wheel** for the third time this season on Saturday and lost **3-2** to the Islanders. The Red Wings are **1-2** in it: a win over Chicago on October 29, then losses to Boston on November 18 and the Islanders. No Centennial dates have been announced for the rest of the season.
