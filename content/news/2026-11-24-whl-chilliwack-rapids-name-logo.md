The Western Hockey League's newest franchise has a name: the **Chilliwack Rapids**, unveiled Monday with the City of Chilliwack. The league describes the logo as a **stylized "C"** that captures the rapids, **framed by snow-capped mountains**, with sweeping curves and angular lettering, in a palette of **green, teal and aqua**. The name comes from the fast rivers of the Fraser Valley.

The Rapids start play in **2027-28** as the WHL's **24th franchise**, at the **Chilliwack Coliseum** in the B.C. Division, the first WHL hockey in the city since 2011. Former Blackhawks head coach **Jeremy Colliton** is the governor, general manager and head coach.
