South Florida has three helmets, three jersey colors and a brand new head coach who has already changed the pattern. This is the full South Florida 2026 uniform schedule: the green home jersey, the white road set, the black alternate, the metallic gold helmet that carried the program for years, and the Gold Standard white shell the Bulls brought back for their 30th season. Two games in, USF has worn the white helmet twice, which is not what the last few years looked like. We update this page as each combination is confirmed.

The base kit is adidas, the colors are green and gold, and the wardrobe is deeper than most people outside Tampa realize. What changed this year is the headwear. USF announced in May that the Gold Standard helmet was coming back for the program's 30th season of football, and the reveal did what a good helmet reveal is supposed to do.

<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/USFFootball/status/2055045993008132328"></a></blockquote>

The original Gold Standard ran in 2015 and 2016 and again from 2019 through 2021, a plain white shell with a solid gold Bull U. The 2026 version is tighter: the logo is smaller, it carries a white outline and visible gold flake to match the finish on the metallic gold helmet, the bumper up front holds a gold bull, and the number and conference mark on the back are gold too. It is meant to go with the white jersey, which makes every road date a candidate.

The other change is on the chest. In April, USF and Tampa General Hospital announced a jersey patch deal, the first of its kind for a college football program in Florida.

<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/RHiggins_USF/status/2041879180405539233"></a></blockquote>

The patch itself showed up in July when the Bulls put out the 2026 uniform, a small TGH shield on the chest opposite the American Conference A.

<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/USFFootball/status/2080065322812383299"></a></blockquote>

One more piece of context before the grid. Brian Hartline is in his first season running the program, 2026 is the last year the Bulls play home games at Raymond James Stadium before the on campus stadium opens, and the schedule has no Power Four opponent on it for the first time in years.

<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true"><a href="https://twitter.com/CFBKings/status/2027121981271728395"></a></blockquote>

American Conference home teams wear color and visitors wear white, so every road cell below starts in white and every home cell is a question of which green, or whether the black comes out.

**September 5 vs. FIU.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **white helmet, green jersey, white pants**, the Hartline era opening in the lightest version of the home look at Raymond James Stadium. USF won 19-9. The gold shoulder yoke and the Tampa General patch both made their debut here.

**September 12 at Army.** ★ Confirmed from the broadcast: **all white**, white helmet, white jersey, white pants, on a noon kickoff at Michie Stadium. It was the standard white shell with the green Bull U and the green facemask, not the Gold Standard, so the new helmet is still sitting in the equipment room. USF won 28-24 in its American Conference opener against an [Army](/stories/army-uniform-schedule-2026) side in the gold helmet with the black jersey and black pants.

**September 19 vs. Delaware State.** Green expected. An FCS visitor on a Saturday night in Tampa, and the last non conference home date before the schedule turns.

**September 26 at Bowling Green.** White expected. The first half of a home and home with the Falcons that also brings them to Tampa in 2030.

**October 3 vs. Temple.** Green expected. Temple runs cherry and white with two diamond helmets, so this one should read cleanly in a wide shot no matter which pants USF picks.

**October 8 at UTSA.** White expected. A Thursday night in the Alamodome, and a national television window is exactly the kind of slot where the Gold Standard helmet makes sense with the white jersey.

**October 17 vs. Kent State.** Green expected. Second MAC opponent of the season, this time in Tampa.

**October 24.** Bye.

**October 31 vs. UAB.** Green expected, with a real chance of black. Halloween at home is the most obvious spot on the calendar for the black alternate, which USF last pulled out late in 2025 under the white helmet.

**November 6 at East Carolina.** White expected. A Friday night at Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium on ESPN2, against a purple home side.

**November 12 vs. Memphis.** Green expected. Thursday night at Raymond James against [Memphis](/stories/memphis-uniform-schedule-2026) in white, and probably the biggest home game of the year.

**November 21 at Florida Atlantic.** White expected. The Bulls' designated annual conference opponent, in Boca Raton.

**November 27 or 28 vs. Tulane.** Green expected. The regular season finale and the last home game at Raymond James Stadium before USF moves on campus, and the date is genuinely unsettled: the conference still has to slot the Thanksgiving weekend windows, and [Tulane](/stories/tulane-uniform-schedule-2026) lists it the same way. If any game gets a send off uniform, it is this one.

## The Bottom Line

The story of South Florida's 2026 wardrobe so far is the helmet nobody has seen. USF revived the Gold Standard in May, built a whole reveal around it, and then opened the season in the plain white shell twice. The green jersey is the home default, white is locked in on the road, the black alternate is the wild card, and the metallic gold lid that defined the last decade has not appeared once. There are six home dates and four road dates left. Halloween against UAB and the finale against Tulane are the two cells we are watching hardest, and a Thursday night at UTSA is the most likely place for the gold flake to finally show up on television. The grid above has every date, and we add a star as each combination is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What jersey is South Florida wearing this week?**

USF hosts Delaware State on September 19 at Raymond James Stadium, and green is expected under the American Conference home color convention. The Bulls wore the white helmet with the green jersey and white pants against FIU in the opener and went all white at Army in Week 2. The grid above lists every game with the expected set, and we update it as combinations are confirmed.

**What are South Florida's football uniforms for 2026?**

A green home jersey with white numerals outlined in gold and a gold shoulder yoke, a white road jersey with green numerals trimmed in gold, and a black alternate. The helmets are a metallic gold shell, a white shell with a green center stripe and the green Bull U, and the revived Gold Standard, a white shell with a reflective gold flake Bull U and a gold facemask. All of it is adidas, and every jersey carries the Tampa General Hospital patch.

**Does South Florida have a new uniform in 2026?**

Two changes. The Gold Standard helmet is back for the program's 30th season, a white shell with a gold flake Bull U that USF last wore in 2021, and every jersey now carries a Tampa General Hospital patch, the first college football jersey patch partnership in the state of Florida. The base green and white jerseys also picked up a gold shoulder yoke.

**What did South Florida wear against Army?**

All white. The white helmet with the green Bull U, the white jersey with green numerals and white pants, at Michie Stadium on September 12. USF won 28-24. Army wore the gold helmet with the black jersey and black pants.

**How many games does South Florida play in 2026?**

Twelve regular season games: seven at Raymond James Stadium and five on the road, with a bye on October 24. There is no Power Four opponent on the schedule and no game against UCF, who left for the Big 12. The full slate with expected uniforms is in the grid above.
