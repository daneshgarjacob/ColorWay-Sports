The **Minnesota Vikings** were scheduled to wear **all purple** against the Indianapolis Colts on Sunday, October 25. They didn't. Minnesota came out at U.S. Bank Stadium in the **purple helmet, purple jersey and white pants**, its standard home look, and beat the Colts **16-10**.

## Who Said All Purple

The Vikings said it first. When the team published its [2026 home game themes](https://www.vikings.com/news/2026-game-themes-us-bank-stadium) on vikings.com on July 28, the table listed a jersey and pants combination for every home date. The Colts game had no theme, and its line read **Purple/Purple**. The article said the Vikings were "scheduled to wear Purple-on-Purple (jersey and pants)." The team also [posted the themes on X](https://x.com/Vikings/status/2082481542001988082) the next day.

The night before kickoff, [Athlon Sports](https://athlonsports.com/nfl/minnesota-vikings/announcers-referees-and-jersey-combination-for-vikings-week-7-game) said the same thing in its Week 7 announcers, referees and jersey preview by Steve Silverman: the Vikings "will be wearing their All-Purple uniforms." [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/announcers-referees-jersey-combination-vikings-044315697.html) and [Yardbarker](https://www.yardbarker.com/nfl/articles/announcers_referees_and_jersey_combination_for_vikings_week_7_game/s1_17615_44470637) ran the same Athlon story. Athlon also wrote that this was the only game all season with the all-purple combination, which matches the team's table: no other 2026 date lists purple pants.

## What They Actually Wore

The Vikings' own game-day photos show purple on top and white below. The Colts wore all white: white helmet, white jersey, white pants. The ESPN game photos show the same thing.

**Why the change?** We don't know. We have not seen an explanation from the Vikings or anyone covering the team. The game was decided late, when Harrison Smith intercepted Daniel Jones, and the win kept Minnesota undefeated, per ESPN.

## Our Take: B

Purple over white against all white is clean, and it is the look Minnesota wears most at home. But this one stings. All purple against an all-white Colts side would have been the sharpest color split of the early window, and it was the one date the team gave its boldest version of the standard set. The white pants took the edge off. We grade the matchup a **B**, the same grade it carries in our [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026).

The bigger question is what happens next. The team's table has no other purple-pants date in 2026: the rest of the home slate is purple over white, the Rivalries jersey against Detroit in Week 15 and the Winter Warrior against Washington in Week 16. Unless Minnesota adds a date, the all-purple look sits out the whole season. Every Vikings game is on the [Vikings 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/vikings-uniform-schedule-2026).
