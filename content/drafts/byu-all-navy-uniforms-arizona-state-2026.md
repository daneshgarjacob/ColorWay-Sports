**BYU is going all navy against Arizona State.** The Cougars revealed their Game 8 uniform on Monday, October 26, with a Gameday Threads graphic posted as "this week's uni combo": a **navy helmet, navy jersey and navy pants**. No. 8 BYU (6-1) hosts Arizona State (4-3) at LaVell Edwards Stadium on **Saturday, October 31, at 1:30 p.m. Mountain on FOX**. The game has not been played yet, so this is what BYU announced, and we will confirm it from the broadcast on Saturday.

## What BYU Is Wearing vs. Arizona State

Everything below is visible in BYU's own graphic, titled "Gameday Threads, Game 08, vs Arizona State," presented by Entrata.

- **Helmet:** a navy shell with a navy and white center stripe, the white oval Y on the side, a navy facemask and a BYU nose bumper. Up close the shell has a glossy, flaked finish.
- **Jersey:** navy, with white numbers, white stripes on the shoulders, and the oval Y set in the V of the collar. It carries the **Big 12 patch** and the **Entrata patch**, plus the Nike swoosh.
- **Pants:** navy, with white side stripes running down the leg.

That makes it navy from top to bottom, with white as the only other color. It is a different look from the navy jersey BYU wore against Iowa State on October 9, when it went with a white helmet and white pants. This time the helmet and pants match the jersey.

## Jake's Take: The Mono Look That Actually Works

"BYU in all navy looks great. I love the helmet, and I usually don't like mono colors for the whole uniform, but this one works, and we will see it on field."

White is the only other color on the uniform: the helmet stripes, the oval Y, the numbers, the Y on the collar and the stripes down the pants.

## BYU Has Gone Navy Head to Toe Before

This is not the first all-navy BYU look. Before its Week 2 game against Southern Utah in 2023, BYU posted "NEW HELMET ALERT" with the combination listed as "Navy | Navy | Navy." BYU won that game 41-16.
