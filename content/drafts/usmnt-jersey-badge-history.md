The US Men's National Team crest has gone through a handful of distinct visual eras since the US Soccer Football Association was founded in 1913. The original heraldic shield of the earliest kits. The mid-century wordmark eras where the crest changed almost as often as the kit supplier did. The stars-and-stripes federation shield that anchored the modern era from the 1994 home World Cup through the middle of the 2010s. The 2016 rebrand that stripped the mark down to a monogram and a red-and-white bar system. And the current crest worn at the 2026 FIFA World Cup on home soil, the identity every US Soccer fan has been looking up on Google over the last week.

We tracked every one of them. Below is the full USMNT badge history, era by era, ranked from best to worst, with the case for which chest crest is the most iconic USMNT badge of all time.

The US Soccer Football Association was founded in 1913 as one of the earliest FIFA-affiliated federations outside Europe, and the original chest crest read like the federation itself: a heraldic shield with USSFA lettering, a small national flag detail, and a formal, old-world visual language borrowed from the era's British and European federations. Not much US soccer identity to speak of in these years — the sport was still finding its footing on the country — but the mark set the pattern of a shield-anchored chest crest that the federation would return to over and over across the next century.

Through the middle of the 20th century US Soccer swung between a handful of federation wordmark crests without ever really settling on one identity. The USMNT was not a serious international side yet — the country would not qualify for another men's World Cup between the 1950 US-beats-England run and the 1990 tournament — and the chest crest reflected that lack of program continuity, changing shape and style every few years as the federation cycled through logos. No design family, no visual continuity across the era.

The 1980s and early 1990s are the run-up to the modern USMNT program. The federation moved back to a shield-anchored crest, the men qualified for Italia 90 (their first World Cup since 1950), and the United States hosted the 1994 FIFA World Cup on home soil — the biggest single event in US Soccer history to that point. The chest crest during this era carried the visual weight of the moment: a federation shield with the national colors and the flag stars and stripes, worn on the denim-print 1994 home kit that is one of the most recognizable jerseys the country has ever put on the field.

The stars-and-stripes federation shield is the crest that raised a generation of US soccer fans. Anchored by a shield with the "US Soccer" wordmark, a field of stars, and red-and-white stripes running vertically or across, this is the mark worn through the entire post-1994 growth of the American men's program: Landon Donovan, DaMarcus Beasley, Clint Dempsey, Tim Howard, the 2002 quarterfinal run, "Dos a Cero" in Columbus, and the 2010 World Cup group win over Algeria. Small tweaks and refreshes through the two decades but the same core identity throughout.

The 2016 redesign was the first meaningful rebrand of the US Soccer identity in a generation. The federation stripped the crest down, simplified the stars and stripes into cleaner geometric bars, and moved the wordmark placement — the modern-federation-crest era across every kit from the 2018 qualifying cycle through the 2022 World Cup in Qatar. The mark reads sharper on a broadcast and lays flatter on a kit than the older shield ever did, at the cost of losing some of the heritage weight the previous crest was carrying.

## FAQ: USMNT Jersey Badge History

**When was the first US Men's National Team jersey badge?** The US Soccer Football Association was founded in 1913, and the earliest USMNT chest crest carried the USSFA lettering on a heraldic shield.

**How many times has the USMNT jersey badge changed?** The badge has cycled through six distinct visual eras across the program's history, with the most recent redesign landing in 2024 ahead of the 2026 FIFA World Cup on home soil.

**What is the USMNT badge for the 2026 World Cup?** The current crest was unveiled as part of the federation's home-World-Cup identity refresh in the run-up to the 2026 tournament and is the mark worn on every USMNT jersey through the group stage and knockouts.

**What was the badge on the 1994 US home World Cup jersey?** The 1994 crest was a stars-and-stripes federation shield worn on the denim-print home kit, one of the most iconic USMNT jerseys of all time.
