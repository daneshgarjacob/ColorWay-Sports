<!-- IMAGE NEEDED: 2026 MNF scorebug screenshot, caption "via ESPN" -->

**The scorebug:** ESPN soft-launched a brand new NFL graphics package and scorebug during a Raiders vs. Texans preseason game on August 20. ESPN says it runs on every NFL game across its networks this season, including Monday Night Football, and that it will keep changing through the season on the way to Super Bowl LXI in February 2027, ESPN's first Super Bowl.

This is Prime Video's fifth season of Thursday Night Football, and it opened with Lions at Bills on September 17. Herbstreit reportedly signed an extension with Prime at the end of September.

**The scorebug:** Prime's bug uses team logos instead of abbreviations, and the timeout markers are built into the shape of the bug itself.

## 3. NFL on CBS

<!-- IMAGE NEEDED: 2026 CBS scorebug screenshot, caption "via CBS Sports" -->

**The booth:** Jim Nantz on play-by-play with J.J. Watt as the lead analyst and Tracy Wolfson on the sideline. Watt moved into the No. 1 chair when Tony Romo was put on leave before the season, and CBS announced on October 2 that Romo is not coming back. The other CBS crews include Ian Eagle with Ross Tucker and Kevin Harlan with Trent Green.

**The scorebug:** CBS rebuilt its scorebug for the 2025 season and debuted it in Week 1 on September 7, 2025. It is bigger than the old one, with a larger score font, and it carries no network branding inside the bug. It got mostly positive reviews when it launched.

## 2. NFL on Fox

<!-- IMAGE NEEDED: 2026 Fox scorebug screenshot, caption "via Fox Sports" -->

**The booth:** Kevin Burkhardt on play-by-play and Tom Brady as the analyst, now in their third season together, with Erin Andrews and Tom Rinaldi on the sidelines. Fox's No. 2 crew is Joe Davis with Greg Olsen.

**The scorebug:** Fox's current bug grew out of the minimal design it debuted at Super Bowl LIX in February 2025, which fans hated. For the 2025 season Fox swapped the team abbreviations for team logos, moved the timeouts under each score and dropped the team color backgrounds. The numbers and logos float on the screen with no box behind them.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best NFL broadcast in 2026?**

We rank Sunday Night Football on NBC No. 1, with Mike Tirico and Cris Collinsworth in the booth. Fox is second and CBS is third.

**What is the worst NFL broadcast in 2026?**

Monday Night Football on ESPN and ABC finishes last in our ranking, just behind Thursday Night Football on Prime Video.

**Who are the NFL announcers for each network in 2026?**

NBC Sunday Night Football: Mike Tirico and Cris Collinsworth. Fox: Kevin Burkhardt and Tom Brady. CBS: Jim Nantz and J.J. Watt. Prime Video Thursday Night Football: Al Michaels and Kirk Herbstreit. ESPN Monday Night Football: Joe Buck and Troy Aikman.

**Who replaced Tony Romo on CBS?**

J.J. Watt. He took over as CBS's lead NFL analyst next to Jim Nantz when Romo was placed on leave before the 2026 season, and CBS announced on October 2 that Romo will not return.

**Did ESPN change its NFL scorebug in 2026?**

Yes. ESPN soft-launched a new NFL scorebug and graphics package during a Raiders vs. Texans preseason game on August 20, 2026. It is used on Monday Night Football and will be built out for Super Bowl LXI in February 2027.

**Which network has the best NFL scorebug?**

Scorebug was one of our three categories, along with vibes and announcers. ## The Bottom Line on the 2026 NFL Broadcast Rankings

Sunday Night Football on NBC is the best way to watch the NFL in 2026, with Fox close behind and CBS in the middle after a booth change at the top. Thursday Night Football on Prime and Monday Night Football on ESPN round out the bottom, and ESPN has a whole season to fix its new look before its first Super Bowl in February.
