Three NFL teams walked out on Sunday of Week 7 in something different than what was on the board. The **Minnesota Vikings** listed all purple and wore **white pants**. The **Cincinnati Bengals** went with **white pants** at Baltimore instead of the black pants we projected. The **Los Angeles Rams** wore **royal pants** in Las Vegas instead of the all-white look we expected. The kicker: all three teams won. Here is every look, checked against the ESPN game photos and each team's own posts, with the grades from our [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026).

## 1. Vikings: All Purple Listed, Purple Over White Worn

Minnesota did something most teams don't. It published a jersey for every home game of 2026, and Week 7 against the Indianapolis Colts on Sunday, October 25, was listed as the **all-purple** set: purple jersey, purple pants. That is not what showed up at U.S. Bank Stadium. The Vikings wore the **purple helmet, purple jersey and white pants**, their standard home look. The Colts wore all white: white helmet, white jersey, white pants.

Look at the team's own photos: purple on top, white below. Minnesota won **16-10** on a late Harrison Smith interception of Daniel Jones, which kept the Vikings undefeated, per ESPN. The team is calling itself the NFL's last unbeaten team.

**Our take: B.** Purple over white against all white is clean, but this is the one that stings. The team put all purple on its own schedule, and all purple against an all-white Colts side would have been the sharpest color split of the early window. Instead the white pants took the edge off. It is also the second time this season Minnesota has crossed us up on pants: in Week 5 at New Orleans, the Vikings wore the white jersey with **purple** pants when we expected white. The all-purple set still has not been worn in 2026. Every Vikings game is on the [Vikings 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/vikings-uniform-schedule-2026).

## 2. Bengals: Black Pants Projected, All White Worn

At M&T Bank Stadium, Baltimore wore exactly what it confirmed: black helmet, purple jersey, purple pants. Cincinnati was the surprise. We had the Bengals in the white jersey with **black pants**, the combination they wore at Pittsburgh in Week 3. They wore the **striped orange helmet, white jersey and white pants** instead.

It is not a brand-new look. Cincinnati also went white over white at Houston in Week 2, with the tiger stripe running down the leg. And it came with a blowout: Joe Burrow threw four touchdown passes and the Bengals beat the Ravens **35-13**.

**Our take: B.** All white with tiger stripes against full purple is a strong color split, and nobody was confusing these two teams on the field. But with white pants, the orange helmet is doing all the work. The black pants give the white road set a bottom half that looks like it belongs to the Bengals. Full grid on the [Bengals 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/bengals-uniform-schedule-2026), and the Baltimore side is on the [Ravens 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/ravens-uniform-schedule-2026).

## 3. Rams: White Pants Expected, Royal Pants Worn

The Rams published white for the Week 7 trip to Las Vegas back on September 1, and white is what they wore up top: the **royal helmet and white jersey**. We had them in white pants too. They wore **royal pants**. The Raiders wore their home set: silver helmet, black jersey, silver pants.

Los Angeles has now worn white over royal on the road twice this season, at Philadelphia in Week 4 and in Las Vegas on Sunday, and went all white at Denver in Week 3. The Rams won **35-21** at Allegiant Stadium, and Matthew Stafford set the franchise record for touchdown passes, per ESPN.

**Our take: B+, and the best change of the three.** Royal and gold against silver and black is bright against dark, and the royal pants give the Rams a much stronger road look than all white ever does. If we were running the Rams' equipment room, white over royal would be the road default. Every Rams game is on the [Rams 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/rams-uniform-schedule-2026), and the Las Vegas side is on the [Raiders 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/raiders-uniform-schedule-2026).

## Ranking the Three Twists

1. **Rams, royal pants (B+).** The only change that made the uniform better than what we expected.
2. **Bengals, white pants (B).** Clean, but the black pants are the better Bengals road look.
3. **Vikings, white pants (B).** Same grade as the Bengals, but the biggest letdown, because the team listed all purple itself and then did not wear it.

## The Bottom Line

Three Week 7 teams changed their pants from what was listed or expected, and all three won. The Vikings listed all purple and wore purple over white in a 16-10 win over the Colts. The Bengals wore all white instead of the black pants we projected in a 35-13 win at Baltimore. The Rams wore white over royal instead of all white in a 35-21 win at Las Vegas. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What did the Vikings wear against the Colts?**

The purple helmet, purple jersey and white pants, Minnesota's standard home look, confirmed from the ESPN game photos and the team's own photos. The Colts wore all white. The Vikings won 16-10 on Sunday, October 25.

**Why didn't the Vikings wear all purple in Week 7?**

We have not seen an explanation from the team. Minnesota listed the all-purple set for the Colts game on its 2026 home schedule, but on game day the Vikings wore white pants with the purple jersey. The all-purple set has not been worn in 2026 so far.

**Why did the Bengals wear white pants against the Ravens?**

We have not seen an explanation from the team. We projected the white jersey with black pants, the combination the Bengals wore at Pittsburgh in Week 3, but they wore white pants with the striped orange helmet and white jersey, the same look they wore at Houston in Week 2. The Bengals won 35-13.

**What did the Ravens wear against the Bengals?**

Baltimore wore the black helmet, purple jersey and purple pants, as the team confirmed before the game.

**What pants did the Rams wear against the Raiders?**

Royal pants, with the royal helmet and white jersey. It is the second time this season the Rams have worn white over royal on the road, after Week 4 at Philadelphia. The Raiders wore the silver helmet, black jersey and silver pants. The Rams won 35-21.

**Which Week 7 uniform changes got the best grade?**

The Rams' royal pants, part of a B+ matchup with the Raiders. The Vikings-Colts and Bengals-Ravens matchups both got a B in our [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026).

**Did all three teams that changed uniforms win?**

Yes. The Vikings beat the Colts 16-10, the Bengals beat the Ravens 35-13, and the Rams beat the Raiders 35-21.
