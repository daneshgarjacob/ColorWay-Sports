Week 11 of the 2026 college football season puts every team in the new AP Top 25 on the field. **No. 8 Texas plays at No. 24 LSU** on Saturday night on ABC, **No. 2 Utah visits No. 22 Arizona**, **No. 15 USC goes to No. 6 Indiana**, **Missouri visits No. 1 Georgia** and **Florida plays at No. 13 Kentucky**. The date that matters most on the uniform calendar is at Marshall, which hosts App State on Saturday, November 14, the anniversary of the 1970 plane crash, for its annual Play For The 75 game. The week starts with MAC games on Tuesday and Wednesday and has three Friday night games, including **Illinois at No. 4 UCLA** and **No. 14 Houston at Colorado**.

Below is every uniform announced so far, then every game this week involving an AP Top 25 team or one of the programs we track in our college uniform schedules, with kickoff time, TV and what each team is wearing. A look is marked **Confirmed** when the school itself has posted it for this game. When a school has not posted yet, we give our projection from that team's uniform schedule and label it **Expected**. Most schools post their combination between Monday and game day, so this page fills in all week, and on Saturday we confirm every look from the game broadcasts and team photos, then add final scores. Rankings are from the November 8 AP poll. All times are Eastern. Last week's looks are in our [Week 10 uniform guide](/stories/college-football-week-10-uniforms-2026).

## Games of the Week

### No. 8 Texas at No. 24 LSU: The Home White Question

**Saturday, 7:30 p.m., ABC.** **LSU: Expected, home white.** LSU wears white for selected home games, and this season it has done it against Clemson, Texas A&M and, last week, Alabama, when it paired the white jersey with the gold helmet and gold pants. It went purple at home against Louisiana Tech and McNeese. **Texas: Expected, burnt orange, if LSU is in white.** Texas wore white at Tennessee and Missouri this season. If LSU stays in white, Texas would wear burnt orange on the road, the way Alabama wore a crimson helmet, crimson jersey and white pants at Tiger Stadium last week. Neither team has posted its look for this game. More: [LSU schedule](/stories/lsu-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Texas schedule](/stories/texas-uniform-schedule-2026).

### No. 2 Utah at No. 22 Arizona: Military Appreciation Night in Tucson

**Saturday, 7:30 p.m. (5:30 p.m. Arizona), FOX.** **Arizona: Expected, navy.** Arizona has worn a navy jersey with a white helmet in home games against Northern Arizona, Cincinnati and Iowa State, and wore its Sandstorm set against TCU last Friday: a chrome red ARIZONA helmet, white jersey and white pants. This is Military Appreciation night, with fans asked to wear red. Arizona has worn a military-themed uniform for this game in some seasons and stayed in its standard look last year. **Utah: Expected, white.** Utah has also worn all red on the road this season, at Iowa State, so the road white is a projection we will check against the team's post. More: [Arizona schedule](/stories/arizona-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Utah schedule](/stories/utah-uniform-schedule-2026).

### No. 15 USC at No. 6 Indiana: Crimson vs. White in Bloomington

**Saturday, 3:30 p.m., CBS.** **Indiana: Expected, crimson.** Indiana opened the season at home in a crimson helmet, crimson jersey and white pants in all three September home games. **USC: Expected, white, with gold pants.** USC wore a cardinal helmet, white jersey and gold pants at Penn State on October 10. The Trojans last played on October 31, beating Ohio State 30-18 in a cardinal helmet and cardinal jersey, and both teams were off on November 7. More: [Indiana schedule](/stories/indiana-uniform-schedule-2026) and [USC schedule](/stories/usc-uniform-schedule-2026).

### Missouri at No. 1 Georgia

**Saturday, 3:30 p.m., ABC.** **Georgia: Expected, red**, with the red helmet and silver pants, the set it wore at home against Oklahoma and Vanderbilt. Georgia moved up to No. 1 in the new poll after wearing a red helmet, white jersey and silver pants at Ole Miss last week. **Missouri: Expected, white.** Missouri wore a black jersey under its black helmet against Texas last week. It has worn white on the road except at Mississippi State, where it went all black because the home team wore white. More: [Georgia schedule](/stories/georgia-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Missouri schedule](/stories/missouri-uniform-schedule-2026).

### Florida at No. 13 Kentucky: Orange Pants in Lexington

**Saturday, 6:15 p.m., ESPN.** **Florida: Confirmed, from its published grid.** Florida's 2026 uniform schedule lists an **orange helmet, white jersey and orange pants** at Kentucky. Orange pants appear twice on that grid, at Auburn and at Kentucky. Florida has followed the grid in every game but one. At Texas on October 17 it wore the blue helmet instead of the listed orange, so we will check the game-week post as well.

More: [Colorado schedule](/stories/colorado-uniform-schedule-2026) and [Houston schedule](/stories/houston-uniform-schedule-2026).

### App State at Marshall: Play For The 75

**Saturday, 6 p.m., ESPN+.** Marshall hosts App State on the anniversary of the November 14, 1970 plane crash that killed 75 people, including most of the football team, for the annual Play For The 75 game. Marshall opened 75 Week on Sunday with a poster reading "We Play for the 75," asking fans to wear black, and listing the Fountain Ceremony at 10 a.m. at the Memorial Student Center.

## Dates to Watch

- **Veterans Day week.** Veterans Day is Wednesday, November 11, and military appreciation games are all over the schedule: No. 21 Ohio State, No. 22 Arizona, Maryland, Rutgers, Liberty, North Carolina, Oklahoma State, UConn (Huskies & Heroes), Texas State and West Virginia (Heroes Day), Hawai'i (Military Night), Coastal Carolina (Salute to Service) and Toledo on Wednesday. These games usually bring a patch or a helmet decal. **Iowa** usually holds its game in this window with an American flag Tigerhawk decal, and **West Virginia** is expected to add a flag decal behind the Flying WV. **Liberty** has built one-off helmets for this game before.
- **Marshall's Play For The 75** against App State on Saturday, with fans asked to wear black.
- **Colorado** has a Buffs Wear Black crowd theme for Friday's game against Houston.
- **North Carolina** has Homecoming, Military Appreciation and Monogram Day against Louisville, and our [North Carolina schedule](/stories/north-carolina-uniform-schedule-2026) flags it as the likeliest date for the 1980 throwback to return for a third game.
- **Homecoming watch:** North Carolina, SMU, Rice, Tulsa, Western Kentucky and UTSA.
