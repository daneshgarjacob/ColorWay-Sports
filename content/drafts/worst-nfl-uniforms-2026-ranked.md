We have graded 116 NFL jerseys this season, every uniform in every team's closet. Most of the league is in good shape. These ten are not. Three of them earned an F, and all three come from the NFL's new Rivalries program, which was supposed to be the most exciting uniform idea of the year.

The rule for this list: current 2026 uniforms only, the ones teams are actually wearing this season, ranked by the grades we gave them in each team's jersey ranking. We are counting down from 10 to the worst uniform in the NFL.

## Why the Rivalries Uniforms Dominate This List

Three of our four worst grades belong to the Rivalries program. The problem is the same each time: a strong local story that does not survive the trip to a TV screen. Hidden textures, darker versions of colors a team already owns, and details you have to read about to notice. The good Rivalries sets pick one idea and commit to it. The bad ones stack four. Our full [Rivalries uniforms ranking](/stories/nfl-rivalries-uniforms-2026-ranked) has all 16.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the worst uniform in the NFL in 2026?**

The Minnesota Vikings' Rivalries uniform, in our ranking. It stacks a duller purple, a gold facemask, rune numbers and knots on top of the best base uniform in the division, and it finished last of all 16 Rivalries uniforms.

**Which NFL uniforms got an F in 2026?**

Three: the Vikings Rivalries uniform, the Packers Rivalries uniform and the Colts' Anvil Strike. All three are part of the NFL's new Rivalries program.

**Are the Bills' Nickel City uniforms bad?**

We graded them a C- after seeing them on the field in Week 3. Gray on gray mutes the brightest colors in the AFC, and the Charge helmet, royal sleeves and socks are what keep the look from falling apart.

**What is wrong with the Saints' black jersey?**

An oversized contrasting collar cuts it in half at the chest. It grades a D+, and it is the jersey New Orleans wears most often.

**When do the Vikings wear the Rivalries uniform?**

Sunday, December 20, on Sunday Night Football against the Detroit Lions.

## The Bottom Line

The worst uniforms in the NFL this year are not the old ones. They are the new ones, and most of them come from a program designed to be the fun part of the season. The Vikings, Packers and Colts each took a great identity and buried it under details that disappear on TV.
