Week 5 of the 2026 NFL season runs from Thursday, October 8 to Monday, October 12, with 15 games and the first byes of the year for the Kansas City Chiefs and Carolina Panthers. It is the best throwback weekend of the season so far. New England brings out the red **Pat Patriot** throwback against Las Vegas on the day it honors the 2001 Super Bowl team, Green Bay debuts its brand-new **Rivalries** uniform against Chicago at Lambeau, Tampa Bay wears **red** at Dallas on Thursday night, Arizona goes to the **black** alternate against Detroit, and Atlanta stays in **red** for Sunday Night Football against Baltimore. Jacksonville hosts Philadelphia in London, and it is the second weekend of [Crucial Catch](/stories/nfl-crucial-catch-2026-schedule).

Below is every Week 5 look we know, starting with the special ones and then all 15 games in order. **Confirmed** means the team has announced the jersey or the full combination. **Expected** is our read from that team's uniform schedule, and it gets replaced with what was actually worn in our [NFL uniform tracker](/stories/nfl-uniform-tracker-2026) once the game is played. Helmets and jerseys project reliably; pants are the piece teams change the week of the game, so treat every expected pants call as the least certain part of the line.

## The Week 5 Throwbacks and Special Looks

### New England Patriots: Pat Patriot for the 2001 Champions

**Sunday, 1:00 p.m. ET, CBS. Patriots confirmed, Raiders expected.** New England announced on August 31 that the red **Pat Patriot** throwback, with the tricorne-hatted Pat Patriot logo on a white helmet, comes out for this game. It is the day the club honors the 25th anniversary of the 2001 Super Bowl team, the group that started the dynasty, and Pat Patriot goes on the field paint at midfield. Las Vegas has not announced anything for the trip, and our read is its **classic white** look under the silver helmet with silver pants, so it is red against white.

Pat Patriot is one of the best throwbacks in the league, and it only comes out twice this season, here and on Thursday night against Minnesota on December 10. Every date is in our [Patriots 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/patriots-uniform-schedule-2026), and Las Vegas's side is in the [Raiders 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/raiders-uniform-schedule-2026).

### Green Bay Packers: The Rivalries Debut Against the Bears

<div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:1fr 1fr;gap:12px;margin:1em 0 0.6em;"><div style="background:#ececf0;border-radius:10px;height:190px;display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:center;padding:10px;box-sizing:border-box;"><img src="/images/posts/nfl-tracker-jerseys/bears-white-road.jpg" alt="Chicago Bears white road jersey, expected at Green Bay in Week 5 of 2026" style="max-height:170px;max-width:100%;object-fit:contain;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></div><div style="background:#ececf0;border-radius:10px;height:190px;display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:center;padding:10px;box-sizing:border-box;"><img src="/images/posts/nfl-tracker-jerseys/packers-green-rivalries.jpg" alt="Green Bay Packers Rivalries jersey, debuting against the Bears in Week 5 of 2026" style="max-height:170px;max-width:100%;object-fit:contain;" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></div></div>

**Sunday, 1:00 p.m. ET, FOX. Packers confirmed, Bears expected.** Green Bay's Rivalries set was revealed with the rest of the 2026 class on August 25, and the debut date came with it: this game. It is a darker hunter green jersey with cream and gold trim, numbers modeled on the lettering of paper currency, striping taken from the borders of Packers stock certificates, and an interlocking GB logo. The helmet is **cream with the first matte finish in team history** and the first matte green facemask, which means the gold helmet with the G sits this one out. Chicago is expected in its road set, **navy helmet, white jersey**, with the pants a game-week call.

Chicago gets its own turn on Christmas Day, when the Bears debut their Rivalries set against Green Bay at Soldier Field. Full details in the [Packers 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/packers-uniform-schedule-2026) and the [Bears 2026 uniform schedule](/stories/bears-uniform-schedule-2026), and every set in the class is ranked in our [NFL Rivalries uniforms ranking](/stories/nfl-rivalries-uniforms-2026-ranked).

The jerseys do not change for Crucial Catch; the hats, gloves and sideline gear do. Every date is in our [NFL Crucial Catch 2026 schedule](/stories/nfl-crucial-catch-2026-schedule).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What uniforms are NFL teams wearing in Week 5 of 2026?**

Most teams are in their standard sets, home in color and visitors in white. The special looks are the Patriots' red Pat Patriot throwback against the Raiders, the Packers' Rivalries debut against the Bears, the Buccaneers expected in red at the Cowboys on Thursday night, the Cardinals in black with the black helmet against the Lions, the Falcons in red for Sunday Night Football against the Ravens, and the Chargers in powder blue over powder blue pants against the Broncos.

**What are the Patriots wearing in Week 5?**

The red Pat Patriot throwback, with the Pat Patriot logo on a white helmet, against the Las Vegas Raiders on Sunday, October 11 at Gillette Stadium. New England confirmed the date on August 31. It is the day the team honors the 25th anniversary of the 2001 Super Bowl champions, and the throwback returns once more on Thursday, December 10 against the Vikings.

**What are the Packers wearing against the Bears in Week 5?**

The new Rivalries uniform, which debuts at Lambeau Field on Sunday, October 11. It is a darker hunter green jersey with cream and gold trim, currency-style numbers and an interlocking GB logo, under a cream helmet with the first matte finish in team history. The Bears are expected in their white road set.

**What are the Cowboys and Buccaneers wearing on Thursday Night Football?**

Dallas confirmed its standard white jersey for the Thursday, October 8 game at AT&T Stadium. Because the Cowboys wear white at home, Tampa Bay can wear color, and we expect the Buccaneers in red under the pewter helmet. Tampa has not announced its jersey yet.

**What are the Falcons wearing on Sunday Night Football in Week 5?**

Red, under the black helmet with the silver facemask, against the Baltimore Ravens on Sunday, October 11 at 8:20 p.m. Eastern on NBC. Atlanta confirmed it in the full jersey schedule it published on September 10. Red became the Falcons' primary home jersey again with the April 2026 redesign. Baltimore is expected in white.

**What are the Eagles and Jaguars wearing in London?**

Jacksonville is the designated home team at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, and we expect the Jaguars in teal with the Eagles in their white road set. Neither club has announced a jersey for the game, which kicks off at 9:30 a.m. Eastern on Sunday, October 11, on NFL Network.

**Which NFL teams are on bye in Week 5?**

The Kansas City Chiefs and the Carolina Panthers. They are the first byes of the 2026 season, so 30 teams play in Week 5, starting with Buccaneers at Cowboys on Thursday, October 8 and ending with Bills at Rams on Monday, October 12.

**Which NFL teams have Crucial Catch games in Week 5?**

Eight clubs have named a Week 5 home game: the Seahawks, Steelers, Commanders, Dolphins, Jets, Cardinals, Chargers and Titans. The Cowboys, Falcons, Saints and Rams have not named theirs, and Week 5 is one of the possible dates for each. Crucial Catch does not change the jerseys, only the hats, gloves and sideline gear.
