The group stage is over, the Round of 32 is set, and the field just got a lot smaller. So we did the only reasonable thing: we held a fantasy draft of every star still standing in the 2026 World Cup. The rules are simple. One pool of every player whose team is still alive, snake-draft order, best player available, and the goal is to build the squad you would actually want for the rest of the knockouts. This is a running draft, so as teams get knocked out their players come off the board and we keep updating the picks.

A quick word on the format. We are not just listing the most famous names, we are drafting for the rest of this tournament: who is in form, who is healthy, who carries a team that has a real path through the bracket, and who you simply do not want to bet against in a knockout game. ## Round 1: The First 12 Picks

1. **Kylian Mbappé, France.** The clear No. 1 overall. France have the deepest roster left in the field and Mbappé is the most decisive knockout player on the planet. You start a draft with the guy who wins one game by himself.
2. **Erling Haaland, Norway.** Norway are the surprise of the tournament and Haaland is the most ruthless penalty-box finisher alive. If your fantasy team needs goals, this is where you get them.
3. **Mohamed Salah, Egypt.** Egypt are through and Salah is still one of the best one-on-one attackers in the world. A high-floor, high-ceiling pick who can carry a tie on his own.
4. **Lamine Yamal, Spain.** The teenager is the most exciting player in the bracket and Spain are a genuine favorite. You draft the future and the present in the same pick.
5. **Jude Bellingham, England.** The engine of an England side built to go deep. Bellingham does everything and shows up in the biggest moments, exactly what you want in a knockout run.
6. **Vinícius Júnior, Brazil.** Brazil are loaded again and Vinícius is the kind of dribbler who turns a tight knockout game into a highlight. Pure upside.
7. **Jamal Musiala, Germany.** Germany topped their group and Musiala is the most press-resistant creator in the draft. He makes everyone around him better.
8. **Kevin De Bruyne, Belgium.** If you want one player to bend a game with a single pass, you take De Bruyne. Belgium will go as far as he carries them.
9. **Lionel Messi, Argentina.** The reigning champions are still here and so is Messi. You do not need us to explain this pick.
10. **Harry Kane, England.** The second England player off the board. Kane is the most complete center forward left, a guarantee of goals and link play for a team with a real shot.
11. **Achraf Hakimi, Morocco.** The best full-back in the world and the heartbeat of a Morocco side that knows how to win knockout games. A two-way pick that wins you points at both ends.
12. **Luis Díaz, Colombia.** Colombia quietly had one of the best group stages and Díaz is a relentless, direct winger who travels well in a one-off game.
