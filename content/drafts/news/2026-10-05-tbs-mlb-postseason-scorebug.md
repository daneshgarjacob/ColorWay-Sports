TNT Sports has the American League this October. TBS, truTV and HBO Max carry both AL Division Series, White Sox vs Guardians and Yankees vs Rays, and then the ALCS. TNT debuted a new scorebug when the ALDS started on Saturday.

It is a smaller black box with the TNT Sports logo, the team logos, scores, inning, count, outs and bases. The "ALDS, Best-of-5, Game 1" line floats above it with no background. In the debut frame there is no batter bar, so the hitter's spot in the order is not on screen.

We had TNT/TBS at No. 5 of 6 national MLB scorebugs with a D-, and the new one does not move it.
