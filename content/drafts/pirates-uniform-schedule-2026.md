The Pittsburgh Pirates run five uniforms in 2026, and the closet is about as black-and-gold as it gets. The set isn't scheduled with the published-timetable precision of a team like the Phillies, but it has two genuine anchors — the gold "We Are Family" throwback on Sunday home games and the new City Connect on Friday home games — with the pinstripes and grays handling the everyday work in between. The big change for 2026 is the City Connect itself: the bright yellow PNC Park kit from 2023 through 2025 is gone, replaced by an all-black uniform with gold gothic lettering. Here's the full Pirates wardrobe and exactly when each jersey comes out.

The Pirates carry five uniforms in 2026, and four of them are holdovers — the home white pinstripes, the road gray "Pittsburgh" script, the black alternate, and the gold 1979 throwback are all unchanged. The one new piece is the City Connect, which got a complete redesign this spring: the bright yellow 2023 kit was retired and replaced with an all-black, gold-lettered uniform that debuted on April 17. Everything else in the closet is the black-and-gold identity Pittsburgh has worn for decades.

## Home White Pinstripes (Most Home Games)

The home white is the Pirates' default and the look most fans picture: a clean white jersey laced with thin black-and-gold pinstripes, "Pirates" scripted across the chest, and the black cap with the gold "P." It's worn for the bulk of every homestand — essentially any home game that isn't claimed by the Friday City Connect or the Sunday throwback. It's a quiet, classic baseball look, and the pinstriping is exactly the kind of subtle detail that reads as old-school without trying too hard. The Pirates are smart to leave it almost entirely alone.

## Road Grays — The "Pittsburgh" Script (Most Away Games)

On the road, Pittsburgh wears the standard gray set with the cursive "Pittsburgh" wordmark arched across the chest, paired with the black cap and gold "P." This script was revived ahead of the 2020 season — script "Pittsburgh" had last appeared on the road jerseys from 1990 through 2000, and for the 19 years in between the away tops used a blocky capitalized treatment. Fans loved the throwback cursive enough that the team brought it back, and it's been the road default ever since. It's the workmanlike look for most away games, and the retro script gives it a warmth that a plain block font never had.

## Black Alternate — The Black "Pittsburgh" (Manager's Choice)

The black alternate is the wild card and one of the most popular shirts in the closet. It's a black jersey with the same retro "Pittsburgh" script across the chest in gold, worn with the black cap. Like the gray road top, this black alternate was unveiled in January 2020 as part of the "script is back" refresh. There's no fixed day attached to it — it's the jersey the Pirates reach for when they want to lean all the way into the black-and-gold, and it shows up both at home and on the road. Paul Skenes has made no secret of loving it, which is part of why it gets so much camera time on his starts. It's the darkest, boldest piece in the regular rotation, and the lack of a schedule is exactly what keeps it feeling like a choice rather than a chore.

## Gold "We Are Family" Throwback (Sunday Home Games)

This is the fan favorite. The gold throwback recreates the iconic late-1970s look from Pittsburgh's "We Are Family" era — a gold pullover-style jersey, black pants with gold striping down the legs, and the unmistakable pillbox cap ringed with gold stripes and topped by a gold "P." The originals were worn off and on from 1977 through 1984, peaking with the 1979 World Series champions, and the modern version has become Pittsburgh's standing Sunday home uniform. It's loud, it's nostalgic, and it's the one look that makes PNC Park feel like a time machine. Of everything in the closet, this is the jersey the throwback crowd circles on the calendar.

## City Connect — The New Black-and-Gold Kit (Friday Home Games)

The biggest story of the Pirates' 2026 season is the brand-new City Connect, which made its on-field debut on Friday, April 17 against the Tampa Bay Rays and now owns every Friday home game at PNC Park. It's a complete reset from the bright yellow "PGH" kit the team wore from 2023 through 2025. The new version goes all-black from cap to pants, with gold doing the heavy lifting and a sharp touch of red used sparingly throughout. The centerpiece is "Pirates" written across the chest in a bold gothic, bridge-inspired typeface — a deliberate nod to the lettering and steel architecture that define the city — replacing the old "PGH" abbreviation with a fuller wordmark. It comes with two cap options: a standout gold crown with a black brim, and an inverse black crown with a gold brim, each carrying a Jolly Roger logo with crossed swords. Tying the loudest jersey in the closet to a guaranteed weekly slot is a smart move, and early reviews of the gold-on-black look have been strong.

## How the Pirates Rotation Actually Works

Let's be straight about it: the Pirates are not a published-timetable team the way the [Phillies](/stories/phillies-uniform-schedule-2026) are. Pittsburgh really has two soft anchors — **the City Connect on Friday home games and the gold throwback on Sunday home games** — and everything else is feel and matchup. Home pinstripes cover the rest of the homestand, road grays handle most of the trip, and the black alternate gets dropped in whenever the staff (and the pitcher) wants the full black-and-gold, with no day-of-week assignment behind it.

It still works, because the two anchors cover the jerseys fans actually plan around. The Friday City Connect and the Sunday throwback are the looks people buy tickets and merch for, so handing each one a standing slot at PNC Park builds anticipation even without a rigid system. The pinstripes and grays are perfectly content to do the quiet weekday work in between.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many uniforms do the Pirates have in 2026?**
Five: the home white pinstripes, the road gray "Pittsburgh" script, the black alternate, the gold 1979 "We Are Family" throwback, and the new City Connect. Four are holdovers; only the City Connect was redesigned for 2026.

**When do the Pirates wear the new City Connect uniform?**
The 2026 City Connect debuted on Friday, April 17 against the Tampa Bay Rays and is worn for every Friday home game at PNC Park the rest of the season. It's an all-black uniform with gold gothic "Pirates" lettering and a touch of red.

**What happened to the yellow Pirates City Connect?**
It was retired. The bright yellow "PGH" kit worn from 2023 through 2025 was replaced for 2026 by an all-black, gold-lettered design. The biggest visual change is the chest, where "PGH" gave way to a fuller arched "Pirates" wordmark.

**When do the Pirates wear the gold throwback uniform?**
The gold "We Are Family" throwback — a gold pullover with black-and-gold striped pants and the striped pillbox cap — is Pittsburgh's standing Sunday home uniform, recreating the look from the 1979 World Series championship era.

**Why does the Pirates road jersey say "Pittsburgh" in script?**
The cursive "Pittsburgh" wordmark was revived ahead of the 2020 season after fans asked for it. Script "Pittsburgh" had previously appeared on the road jerseys from 1990 to 2000; the same script also lives on the black alternate.

**Do the Pirates have a strict uniform rotation like the Phillies?**
No. Outside of the City Connect on Friday home games and the gold throwback on Sunday home games, the Pirates pick their jerseys by feel rather than a published schedule, so the black alternate and the everyday whites and grays stay flexible.
